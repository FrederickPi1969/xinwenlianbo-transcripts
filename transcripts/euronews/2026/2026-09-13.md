Qeshm is the largest island in the Strait of Hormuz, a crucial oil and gas transit chokepoint that has been at the centre of tensions between Washington and Tehran.
One person has been killed and three were injured after a ship was struck in the Strait of Hormuz on Sunday, according to Iranian authorities, as Iran and the United States continue to clash over the strategic waterway.

"A merchant vessel was struck around 5:00 am (0130 GMT) this morning off the coasts of Hengam and Qeshm islands. One person was killed and three others were injured," Qeshm city governor Amir Teymouri said, according to state TV. Teymouri did not specify the origin of the strike.

Qeshm is the largest island in the Strait of Hormuz, a crucial oil and gas transit chokepoint that has been at the centre of tensions between Washington and Tehran since late February.

The UKMTO maritime agency on Sunday said it had received a report that a vessel had been struck by an "unknown projectile" while transiting the Strait.

It later reported that a fire had broken out onboard the ship and that local authorities were helping evacuate the crew.

"Vessels are advised to transit with caution and report any suspicious activity to UKMTO whilst authorities are investigating," the agency added.

The vessel, which was carrying 243 people, had been sailing between the islands of Java and Borneo when it encountered bad weather.

"We have carried out efforts to search for victims to the fullest extent. There were passengers who...had already been successfully evacuated by the units at the scene. We can inform you that the total number of evacuated passengers is 103 people," local search and rescue chief I Putu Sudayana told reporters, adding that one of the passengers had died.

Sudayana said rescuers faced waves of up to 2.5 metres as they fought to reach the ferry amid "extremely risky" conditions for vessels.

"Even our Basarnas vessel, which is 40 meters long, is at great risk," he said, referring to Indonesia's Search and Rescue Agency.

It comes after a speedboat carrying eight people, including five journalists covering the recent eruption of Indonesia's Mount Anak Krakatoa, went missing earlier this week. The search for the vessel remains ongoing.

The Southeast Asian nation is made up of more than 17,000 islands, with boats providing a common and important form of transport.
