## [Press Statement] China&#8217;s Actions Against Philippine Navy Personnel at Second Thomas Shoal — Thomas &#8220;Tommy&#8221; Pigott

The United States condemns China’s dangerous and aggressive actions against Philippine navy personnel at Second Thomas Shoal in the South China Sea on July 20, 2026, and calls on China to immediately cease its destabilizing conduct.  China’s troubling pattern of provocation against lawful Philippine maritime operations undermines regional peace and stability and directly contradicts China’s repeated commitments to resolve disputes peacefully.

We stand with our Ally, the Philippines, and commend the professionalism and restraint shown by Philippine navy personnel in this incident.  We reaffirm the July 2016 Arbitral Tribunal ruling, which found that China’s expansive maritime claims in the South China Sea have no basis in international law. That decision is final and legally binding on both China and the Philippines.  We will continue to stand with our Allies and partners in defense of a free and open Indo-Pacific.

## [Press Statement] Launch of First Pilot Zones in Lebanon — Thomas &#8220;Tommy&#8221; Pigott

Today, pilot zone operations began in the villages of Froun, Srifa, and Zawtar Al-Gharbiya in accordance with the Trilateral Framework and under the auspices of the Military Coordination Group for Lebanon. This milestone is a direct outcome of last week’s discussions between Israel and Lebanon in Rome. The United States will continue working closely with both parties to implement the Framework to a successful conclusion.

## [Press Statement] Colombia National Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the Government of the United States of America, I extend my best wishes to the people of Colombia as you celebrate your Independence Day.

The United States values its enduring partnership with the Colombian people and is committed to advancing security, prosperity, and opportunity across our hemisphere. We look forward to expanding our partnership and writing a new chapter under the leadership of President Elect Abelardo De La Espriella.

We extend our best wishes to the people of Colombia and the Colombian American community in our nation on this important national day. I am confident Colombia’s best days are ahead.
