## [Press Statement] Venezuela National Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the United States of America, I join the people of Venezuela as they mark this national day.  We hold in our hearts and are united in prayer for the Venezuelans affected by the recent devastating earthquakes.  Their courage and unity in the face of tragedy reflect the Venezuelan people’s enduring resilient spirit.  The Trump Administration’s swift response efforts in delivering life-saving relief, including the activation of urban search and rescue teams from the Fairfax County Fire and Rescue Department, the Los Angeles County Fire Department, Miami-Dade Fire Rescue, and City of Miami Department of Fire-Rescue, demonstrate our support for a full recovery.  Amid this difficult time, we look forward to continuing the work towards a stable, prosperous, and democratic Venezuela aligned with the United States.

## [Press Statement] Cabo Verde National Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the U.S. government, I extend warm wishes to Cabo Verde on its momentous 51st year of independence.

We mark this special occasion to reaffirm our shared values of democracy, economic prosperity, and maritime security. Cabo Verde stands as a model of good governance and economic progress. I send my best wishes to the people of Cabo Verde and congratulate them on the relaunch of direct flights between our two countries. We look forward to what we can accomplish together in the future.

## [Press Statement] Algeria National Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the United States of America, I offer my warmest congratulations to the Government and people of Algeria on the occasion of Algeria’s 64th National Day, July 5.

Over the last year, our bilateral relationship has made great strides in the economic, cultural, and security spheres.  We look forward to strengthening our cooperation and securing greater prosperity for both our countries.
