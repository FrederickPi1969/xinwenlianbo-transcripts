## [Press Statement] 75th Anniversary of the United States-Philippines Mutual Defense Treaty — Thomas &#8220;Tommy&#8221; Pigott

Today, we celebrate the 75th anniversary of the 1951 United States-Philippines Mutual Defense Treaty, the foundation of our ironclad Alliance. The Treaty remains an enduring testament to our unwavering commitment to each other’s defense and security. This year also marks the 80th anniversary of United States-Philippines diplomatic relations – eight decades defined by shared history, shared sacrifices, and a shared vision of a free and open Indo-Pacific, of which the Treaty has played an integral part. As we look ahead to the next eight decades of U.S.-Philippines relations, the United States reaffirms our commitment to deepening our relationship with the Philippines, to honoring our obligations in the Treaty, and to advancing our vibrant economic relationship through initiatives like the Luzon Economic Corridor. The rock-solid ties between our peoples underpin everything we do together as Allies.

## [Press Statement] Statement by Spokesman Tommy Pigott on the Passing of His Majesty King Harald V of Norway — Thomas &#8220;Tommy&#8221; Pigott

On behalf of the people of the United States, I extend my deepest condolences to Her Majesty Queen Sonja, the entire Royal Family, and the Norwegian people on the passing of His Majesty King Harald V.

The King was a close friend of the United States and steadfast supporter of the strong relationship between our two countries. America was honored to provide a safe haven for the young Prince Harald and his family during the Second World War, and his subsequent royal visits across the United States strengthened the ties between our nations throughout his reign. King Harald’s personal commitment to celebrating the proud heritage of Norwegian-Americans and kinship between the people of the United States and Norway will forever serve as part of his enduring legacy.
