## [Press Statement] The Republic of the Congo National Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the United States, I extend our congratulations to the people of the Republic of the Congo on the 66th anniversary of its independence.

We are proud of our historic collaboration, which began over six decades ago when the United States recognized the Republic of the Congo on the day of its independence. I look forward to furthering our partnership by strengthening our countries’ commercial and security relationship for the welfare of our citizens.

The United States wishes the Republic of the Congo a prosperous year ahead.

## [Press Statement] Liechtenstein National Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the United States of America, I congratulate the people of Liechtenstein on your National Day.

The United States maintains a long-standing partnership with Liechtenstein, grounded in a commitment to freedom and cooperation on business and investment. We continue to work together to advance our mutual prosperity and commercial diplomacy goals.

As we look to the year ahead, we remain dedicated to deepening our commercial ties and fostering the innovation and prosperity that benefit both our peoples. On this National Day, I extend my best wishes to the people of Liechtenstein.
