The text of the following statement was released by the Governments of the United States of America and the Republic of Portugal on the occasion of the 53rd meeting of the U.S.-Portugal Standing Bilateral Commission.
Begin Text:
The Governments of the United States of America and the Portuguese Republic convened the 53rd meeting of the Portugal-United States Standing Bilateral Commission (SBC). The dialogue focused on the following priorities:

Defense and Security Cooperation: The delegations reaffirmed NATO’s central role in Euro-Atlantic security and their shared commitment to allied defense investment in line with commitments made at the 2025 NATO Summit in The Hague. Recognizing the strong bilateral defense relationship and the successful collaboration on defense equipment programmes, both sides welcome the continued expansion of cooperation between their respective armed forces. They highlighted the ongoing implementation of the State Partnership Program between Portugal and the Illinois National Guard, including activities already underway and further engagements planned to strengthen interoperability, readiness, and the exchange of expertise.

Cooperation in the Azores/Lajes: Both sides praised the growing U.S. engagement with the Autonomous Region of the Azores. The delegations committed to expanding economic opportunity and people-to-people ties that benefit Azorean communities. The delegations acknowledged the U.S. Air Force’s efforts in environmental remediation.

Economic security/CEO Forum: The delegations reaffirmed their strong commitment to reinforcing economic ties between Portugal and the United States. Building on the 52nd SBC meeting commitments and the October 8 meeting between Secretary Rubio and Foreign Minister Rangel in Lisbon, the two governments confirmed the inaugural CEO Forum Plenary Meeting to be held on November 10, 2026, in Lisbon. With this initiative, both sides aim to identify new opportunities for trade and investment, while facilitating job creation and promoting economic growth and security, to the benefit of both the United States and Portugal.

The United States congratulated Portugal on its election to the UN Security Council for the 2027-2028 term. Both delegations agreed to cooperate within the Council to address the pressing topics of the global agenda.

The delegations reaffirmed their sustained interest in continuing and deepening the bilateral strategic partnership.

## [Media Note] Joint Statement on United States – Iceland Strategic Dialogue </a> <div class="collection-result-meta" dir="ltr"> <span dir="ltr">November 3, 2026</span> <!-- <span>date</span> --> </div> </li> <li class="collection-result"> <!-- add label if a report custom post in inculded in collection--> <p class="collection-result__date">Press Statement</p> <a href="https://www.state.gov/releases/office-of-the-spokesman/2026/11/dominica-national-day-2/" class="collection-result__link" target="_self"> Dominica National Day — Marco Rubio

The text of the following statement was released by the Governments of the United States of America and Iceland on the occasion of the United States-Iceland Strategic Dialogue.

The United States and Iceland held the annual bilateral Strategic Dialogue in Reykjavik from November 2-3.  The Dialogue was led by the U.S. Department of State Bureau of European and Eurasian Affairs Deputy Assistant Secretary Peter Andreoli, Office of the Secretary of War Principal Director for Europe and NATO Policy Alton Buland, Icelandic Director General for International Affairs and Policy Anna Hjartardóttir, and Director General for Defense Jónas Allansson with participation from U.S. Ambassador to Iceland Billy Long and Icelandic Ambassador to the United States Svanhildur Hólm Valsdóttir.

The United States and Iceland built on the momentum of Secretary Rubio’s recent visit, marking 75 years of close bilateral defense and security cooperation and reaffirming their commitment to further strengthening the strategic partnership between the two nations. Discussions addressed the evolving European security landscape and wider global security challenges, with particular emphasis on enhancing cooperation in the Arctic, reinforcing NATO’s collective deterrence and defense, and advancing bilateral security initiatives. The Dialogue also focused on improving regional domain awareness, protection of critical infrastructure, and countering malign influence and subversive activities. Participants explored new opportunities for dual-use technologies, supply chain security, innovation, and collaboration with the U.S. National Guard.

## [Press Statement] Panama National Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the United States of America, I congratulate the people of Panama on the 123rd anniversary of your independence. Panama is one of our most important partners, and we will continue to stand with you as you recover from the recent earthquake. Our strong partnership allowed us to deliver humanitarian, logistical, and technical assistance within 24 hours of the disaster.

We marked significant advances in our relationship this year. Panama joined the Shield of the Americas, Americas Counter Cartel Coalition, and Pax Silica. Together, we showed our commitment to the Panama Canal’s security during PANAMAX, the first large-scale live exercise in 14 years. In the year ahead, we will deepen our security partnership and pursue new trade and investment opportunities. Panama is making historic investments in the Canal and seeking investment in technology, cybersecurity, and other critical infrastructure, as well as health and education. I wish the people of Panama a joyous celebration.
