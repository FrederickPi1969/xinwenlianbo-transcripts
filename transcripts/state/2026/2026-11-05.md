## [Press Statement] Dismantling the Prince Group Transnational Criminal Organization — Thomas &#8220;Tommy&#8221; Pigott

Today, the United States is sanctioning four individuals and 41 entities linked to the Cambodia-based Prince Group Transnational Criminal Organization (TCO), building on the October 2025 and June 2026 designations targeting the organization’s leadership, affiliated companies, and financial facilitators.  These sanctions are being implemented by the U.S. Department of the Treasury’s Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC).

Prince Group TCO members target victims in the United States and around the world through sophisticated cyber-enabled fraud schemes, many of which are carried out from large-scale scam compounds in Southeast Asia.  Today’s designations target several individuals and affiliated companies who continue to provide material support to the Prince Group TCO.

Additionally, the United States is sanctioning Chui Kok Wa, a Malaysian individual who defrauded U.S. victims by managing a network of scammers located in Madagascar.  This network perpetrated scams targeting Americans, including schemes to defraud them of digital assets.  On October 27, 2026, charges were unsealed in the U.S. District Court for the District of Columbia charging Chui with conspiracy to commit wire fraud.

No criminal network is beyond reach. The Trump Administration will continue to expose, sanction, and dismantle the criminal networks that prey on Americans through cybercrime, fraud, and extortion.

Today’s actions were taken in furtherance of Executive Order (E.O.) 14390 “Combating Cybercrime, Fraud, and Predatory Schemes Against American Citizens” and pursuant to E.O. 13581 “Blocking Property of Transnational Criminal Organizations,” as amended by E.O. 13863, and E.O. 13694 “Blocking the Property of Certain Persons Engaging in Significant Malicious Cyber-Enabled Activities,” as further amended.

For more information about today’s actions, please see the Department of the Treasury’s press release.

## [Press Statement] Further Sanctions on the Cuban Regime’s Foreign Military Support and Illicit Revenue Networks — Marco Rubio

Cuba’s ruling elite is hoarding the island’s energy, shipping, and technology resources for its own enrichment, leaving the Cuban people to contend with blackouts, shortages, and a stagnant economy.  The Marxist regime in Havana relies on a web of state-owned enterprises, joint ventures, and offshore intermediaries to keep this system running; its foreign backers supply the military equipment that helps sustain the regime’s military forces and enable its brutal repression, while propping up its persistent efforts to threaten, infiltrate and subvert the American homeland.

Today, pursuant to President Trump’s Executive Order (E.O.) 14404, “Imposing Sanctions on Those Responsible for Repression in Cuba and for Threats to United States National Security and Foreign Policy,” I am designating ten targets including additional Cuban elites, a crude oil shipping network, state-owned import and technical-support entities, and China-based suppliers of the regime’s military forces.  I am also identifying five associated vessels as blocked property.

Among today’s targets is GUILLERMO FAUSTINO RODRIGUEZ LOPEZ CALLEJA, who is part of an extensive web of offshore shipping companies that move Cuban crude oil shipments to and from the island.  I am also designating ANY RODRIGUEZ LASTRES, daughter of the previously designated Grupo de Administración Empresarial (GAESA) President Ania Guillermina Lastres Morera.

Additionally, the Department of the Treasury issued a determination pursuant to E.O. 14404 that authorizes the imposition of economic sanctions on any foreign person operating or having operated in the electronics, manufacturing, marine, and technology sectors of the Cuban economy.

The Trump Administration will not stand by while the Cuban regime funnels the island’s resources to its military elite, while regime insiders and their family members profit from opaque offshore networks, and while Cuba continues to serve as a staging ground for a global coalition of hostile anti-American actors to threaten, spy on, and plot against the United States and its interests.

The Department’s actions are being taken pursuant to E.O. 14404, which authorizes sweeping sanctions on Cuba, including against persons who support the Cuban regime’s security apparatus and those responsible for repression in Cuba and other threats to U.S. national security.  These actions also further both E.O. 14380, Addressing Threats to the United States by the Government of Cuba and National Security Presidential Memorandum 5, which directs the Executive Branch to improve human rights, encourage the rule of law, foster free markets and free enterprise, and promote democracy in Cuba.  For more information on today’s action, see the Department of State’s Fact Sheet.
