		Continuing to Promote Accountability for the Murillo-Ortega Dictatorship — Marco Rubio

Secretary of State Marco Rubio had a productive call with Mexican Foreign Secretary Roberto Velasco to advance cooperation on shared priorities, including collaborating to halt the flow of illegal immigrants between countries, stopping the trafficking of fentanyl and other illicit drugs, and the importance of accelerating decisive actions to dismantle the cartels.
