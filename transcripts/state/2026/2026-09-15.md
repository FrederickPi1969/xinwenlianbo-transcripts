## [Press Statement] Nicaragua National Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the United States of America, I send my solidarity to the Nicaraguan people on this day commemorating your independence. More than two centuries ago, Nicaraguans forged their own path to determine their future. Today, that same conviction endures despite the Murillo-Ortega dictatorship’s brutal efforts to annihilate it.

Whether at home or in exile, Nicaraguans’ aspirations for a future free from tyranny cannot be cancelled or erased.  The United States recognizes the Nicaraguan people’s strong resolve and conviction and today celebrates the ties that bind our two peoples.

## [Press Statement] Honduras National Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the United States of America, I congratulate the people of Honduras as you celebrate your Independence Day.

This occasion honors the enduring principles of liberty and justice that continue to guide Honduras forward.  Honduras stands with us as a founding member of the Shield of the Americas, defending democratic sovereignty across our hemisphere.  Our nations share a partnership grounded in mutual respect and a commitment to the safety and prosperity of our citizens.  I commend President Nasry Asfura’s steadfast leadership in advancing our shared priorities of strengthening border security, disrupting transnational crime, and expanding economic opportunity for both of our peoples.

As Hondurans mark this proud day, the United States stands with you in pursuit of a more secure and prosperous future.

## [Press Statement] Guatemala National Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the United States, I congratulate the people of Guatemala as you celebrate your Independence Day and another year of progress in our partnership.

Under President Bernardo Arévalo’s leadership, we achieved significant milestones together this year: a historic reciprocal trade agreement opening new markets for our farmers, manufacturers, and workers; a five-year global health Memorandum of Understanding through the Trump Administration’s America First Global Health Strategy; and expanded economic cooperation in critical infrastructure.  Underpinning this progress is our steadfast security cooperation, disrupting cartels and fentanyl traffickers, and building one of our hemisphere’s most extensive migration partnerships.

This is just the beginning, as the United States and Guatemala continue building a safer and more prosperous future together.

## [Press Statement] El Salvador National Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the United States, I congratulate the people of El Salvador on your National Day and celebrate our shared aspiration for a prosperous future.

Our enduring partnership continues to grow stronger.  Under President Nayib Bukele’s leadership, El Salvador’s steadfast commitment to regional security has made our hemisphere safer, including through its participation in the Standing Group of Partners to restore stability in Haiti.  El Salvador stands with us as a founding member of the Shield of the Americas, defending democratic sovereignty across our hemisphere, and as a member of Pax Silica to drive innovation and secure regional supply chains.

On this September 15, the United States stands with the Salvadoran people to continue strengthening the bonds between our nations.

## [Press Statement] Costa Rica National Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the United States, I extend warm congratulations to the people of Costa Rica as you celebrate your Independence Day.

Our two nations share a friendship rooted in democracy, security, and economic ties.  That friendship has driven joint efforts to dismantle transnational criminal organizations, secure critical telecommunications infrastructure, disrupt illegal immigration, and bolster border security.  Costa Rica stands with us as a founding member of the Shield of the Americas, defending democratic sovereignty across our hemisphere, and as a member of Pax Silica to drive innovation and secure regional supply chains.  Under President Laura Fernández’s leadership, I am confident our partnership will continue to keep our people safe and advance shared prosperity.
