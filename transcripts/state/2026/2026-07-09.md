## [Press Statement] Argentina National Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the United States of America, I extend my warmest congratulations to the people of Argentina on the 210th anniversary of your independence.

Under President Javier Milei’s leadership, Argentina has become an indispensable partner in advancing prosperity, security and stability throughout our hemisphere. The United States is committed to expanding our collaboration in critical minerals, civil nuclear energy, trade, and investment.

Together, we are confronting the threats of narcoterrorism, transnational crime, and authoritarian regimes that undermine freedom in our region. Argentina’s principled leadership in defending liberty and opposing tyranny sets a powerful example.

As we look toward the future, I am confident our partnership will continue to deliver meaningful results for both nations and our hemisphere as a whole.

## [Press Statement] South Sudan National Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the United States of America, I extend congratulations to the people of South Sudan as you commemorate the 15th anniversary of your independence.

The United States’ support for the South Sudanese people began decades before independence in 2011. In partnership with religious organizations, civil society groups, and other grass roots movements, the United States has sought to promote a peaceful and prosperous future for the South Sudanese people. The United States looks forward to continuing our work with the people of South Sudan to build a future that benefits both our nations.
