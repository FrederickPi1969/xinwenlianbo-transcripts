## [Press Statement] North Macedonia National Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the American people, I extend my warmest congratulations to the people of North Macedonia as you celebrate 35 years of independence.

The United States is proud to stand with North Macedonia as a trusted ally and partner.  North Macedonia plays an important role in strengthening security and stability across the region through its commitment to our shared defense and collaboration on counterterrorism and cybersecurity efforts.  We welcome the continued growth of our economic partnership, including through this year’s Strategic Dialogue in Washington.  Together, we are advancing opportunities in energy, infrastructure, technology, and other sectors to strengthen prosperity in both our countries.

We wish you a joyous Independence Day and continued peace, security, and prosperity in the years ahead.

## [Press Statement] Andorra National Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the United States of America, I extend warm wishes to the people of Andorra as they celebrate their patron saint, Our Lady of Meritxell.

The United States values Andorra as a steadfast friend that shares our commitment to national sovereignty and a secure, prosperous future for its citizens. The United States is proud of the momentum built over the past year in our relationship with the Principality of Andorra, particularly Andorra’s continued collaboration with American technology companies to strengthen its digital infrastructure and financial sector security. We look forward to building on this foundation in the coming year, expanding opportunities for U.S. businesses and investment.

As the people of Andorra celebrate this important day, the United States reaffirms its partnership with the Principality and extends our warmest regards to the Andorran people in the year ahead.
