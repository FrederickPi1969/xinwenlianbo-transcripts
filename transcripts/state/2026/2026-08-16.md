## [Press Statement] The Gabonese Republic National Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the United States, I am pleased to extend warm congratulations to the people of Gabon as you celebrate the 66th anniversary of your independence on August 17.

Our growing commercial ties and deepening security cooperation reflect a shared commitment to a stable, prosperous, and sovereign Gabon.

As Gabon looks toward its future, the United States stands as a steadfast partner. We wish the Gabonese people health, peace, and continued prosperity in the year ahead.

## [Press Statement] Indonesia Independence Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the people of the United States of America, I extend my warmest congratulations on the 81st anniversary of Indonesia’s independence.

The United States and Indonesia enjoy an enduring partnership rooted in dedication to democratic governance and a shared vision for a free and open Indo-Pacific. Our Comprehensive Strategic Partnership reflects the depth of our cooperation – from defense and regional security to fair and balanced trade, energy, and technology. The United States looks forward to creating new avenues of opportunity for our nations in priority areas such as critical minerals, maritime security, and the digital economy. We offer our heartfelt congratulations to you and the people of Indonesia as we continue our journey together toward shared prosperity.

We also extend deepest condolences to those who lost loved ones in the devastating earthquake and its aftermath in East Nusa Tenggara. Our thoughts are with those who were injured and all communities affected by this tragedy.
