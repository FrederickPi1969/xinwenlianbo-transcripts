## [Press Statement] Iceland’s August 29 Referendum — Thomas &#8220;Tommy&#8221; Pigott

The United States takes note of Iceland’s August 29 Referendum on whether to resume EU accession negotiations. We respect the sovereign decision of the people of Iceland and express confidence in Iceland’s continuing trajectory as a strong and valuable NATO ally and close partner of the United States and Europe. We look forward to strengthening our partnership with Iceland on all fronts including security, energy, innovation, and economic and cultural ties, to further advance our shared vision of peace and prosperity.

## [Press Statement] Malaysia Independence Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the United States of America, I send my warmest congratulations to the people of Malaysia on the 69th anniversary of Malaysia’s independence.

For nearly seven decades, the United States and Malaysia have shared a deep and enduring partnership, rooted in strong economic ties, interpersonal bonds, and a shared commitment to freedom of navigation in a free and open Indo-Pacific. As one of Malaysia’s top investors and a valued trading partner, the United States looks forward to deepening this relationship in the years ahead.

On this special occasion, I wish all the people of Malaysia continued peace and prosperity.
