## [Press Statement] Eswatini National Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the United States of America, I offer warm congratulations to the citizens of Eswatini as you mark the 58th anniversary of your nation’s independence. The enduring bond between our two countries demonstrates a mutual dedication to stability, community health, and prosperity. We welcome further collaboration and productive exchanges that strengthen our security, resilience, and success.

Please accept our heartfelt best wishes on this joyous occasion.
