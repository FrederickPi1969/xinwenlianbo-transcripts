Secretary of State Marco Rubio met today with French Foreign Minister Jean-Noël Barrot.  The Secretary and Foreign Minister discussed U.S. fundamental concerns about the International Criminal Court.

The Secretary and Foreign Minister also addressed the situation in the Middle East, reaffirming the importance of freedom of navigation in the Strait of Hormuz.  The Secretary also called for the cessation of EU preference measures and overregulation targeting U.S. companies.

Lastly, they discussed efforts to secure a durable, long-term peace settlement to the Russia-Ukraine war.

## [Press Statement] Cambodia National Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the United States of America, I congratulate the Kingdom of Cambodia as it celebrates its 73rd Independence Day.

The United States and Cambodia have built a close relationship based on trust, mutual respect, and shared interest for more than 75 years. President Trump’s leadership in helping broker the historic Kuala Lumpur Peace Accords has opened a new chapter in our relationship, creating opportunities to advance lasting peace and expand economic prosperity for Americans and the Cambodian people alike. We look forward to working together to support a strong and independent Cambodia.

I extend my warmest wishes to the people of Cambodia as you celebrate this important milestone in your nation’s history.
