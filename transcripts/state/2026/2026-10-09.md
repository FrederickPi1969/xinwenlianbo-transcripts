Political reconciliation talks between the interim Venezuelan government and 2015 National Assembly representatives are producing tangible reforms in civil liberties and political rights, laying the groundwork for credible elections and a democratic transition.  Two rounds of talks have already taken place, producing significant results:  a wholesale reform and reconstruction of an independent Venezuelan Supreme Court, the restoration of access to more than 70 previously blocked internet domains, and the reform of a key repressive law.  The interim government has also released more than 1,000 political prisoners and announced the release of nearly 200 more.

The talks bring together representatives of Venezuela’s 2015 National Assembly, led by its President Dinorah Figuera, and representatives of the interim government, led by current National Assembly President Jorge Rodríguez.  The 2015 National Assembly was Venezuela’s last democratically elected legislative body recognized by all Venezuelan political parties and by the international community.

The delegations held two rounds of talks in Caracas in August and September, with another round scheduled to begin October 14.  The parties have decided to remain in session to maintain the momentum they have achieved.

## [Fact Sheet] Imposing Sanctions on the International Criminal Court </a> <div class="collection-result-meta" dir="ltr"> <span dir="ltr">October 9, 2026</span> <!-- <span>date</span> --> </div> </li> <li class="collection-result"> <!-- add label if a report custom post in inculded in collection--> <p class="collection-result__date">Press Statement</p> <a href="https://www.state.gov/releases/office-of-the-spokesman/2026/10/imposing-sanctions-on-the-international-criminal-court/" class="collection-result__link" target="_self"> Imposing Sanctions on the International Criminal Court — Marco Rubio

Under President Trump, the United States has acted decisively to defend U.S. sovereignty and U.S. citizens against the threat of the International Criminal Court (ICC).  To date, the United States has imposed sanctions on 17 persons pursuant to Executive Order (E.O.) 14203 “Imposing Sanctions on the International Criminal Court.”  Today, the Department of State announces additional actions to defend U.S. sovereignty and neutralize the threat posed by the ICC.

As a result of today’s sanctions-related actions, and subject to applicable licenses and exceptions, all property and interests in property of the ICC that are in the United States or in possession or control of U.S. persons are blocked and must be reported to the Department of the Treasury’s Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC).

All transactions by U.S. persons or within (or transiting) the United States that involve any property or interests in property of designated or otherwise blocked persons are prohibited unless exempt or authorized by a general or specific license issued by OFAC, including those general licenses described above. These prohibitions include the making of any contribution or provision of funds, goods, or services by, to, or for the benefit of any blocked person and the receipt of any contribution or provision of funds, goods, or services from any such person.

Petitions for removal from the SDN List may be sent to: OFAC.Reconsideration@treasury.gov. Petitioners may also refer to the Department of State’s Delisting Guidance page.

## [Press Statement] Uganda National Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the United States of America, I congratulate the people of Uganda on the 64th anniversary of Uganda’s independence and wish them a happy Independence Day.

The shared bond between the United States and Uganda is built on a foundation of respect for sovereignty and a desire to build a more prosperous future. In the past year, we have grown our commercial ties, advanced regional security and partnered to improve global public health.

I am confident the enduring partnership between the United States and Uganda will continue to flourish in the years to come bringing greater opportunity to both our peoples.

We extend our best wishes to the people of Uganda and look forward to building a stronger partnership in the coming years.
