Secretary of State Marco Rubio will travel to Samarkand, Uzbekistan; Mumbai, India; and Jamnagar, India from October 17-22.

In Uzbekistan, Secretary Rubio will participate in the C5+1 Ministerial to highlight how strategic cooperation through this diplomatic platform promotes a more peaceful and prosperous future for the United States and the countries of Central Asia. The Secretary will also participate in bilateral engagements with senior officials from all C5 countries – Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, and the Kyrgyz Republic. On this historic visit, Secretary Rubio will lead a U.S. commercial delegation comprised of over 200 business leaders, ushering in a new era of strengthened commercial cooperation for the United States and Central Asia.

The Secretary will then travel to India for his second official visit this year. He will meet with senior government officials to strengthen the U.S.-India strategic partnership, exchange views on regional and global dynamics, and discuss prospects for enhanced energy cooperation. Secretary Rubio will also meet with business leaders to promote opportunities for commercial investment and economic cooperation.

## [Press Statement] Disrupting Cartel del Noreste’s Financial Network — Thomas &#8220;Tommy&#8221; Pigott

Today, the United States is sanctioning four individuals and 11 entities tied to the Mexico-based Cartel del Noreste (CDN), crippling the financial infrastructure of one of the most violent terrorist cartels in our Hemisphere.  These sanctions are being implemented by the U.S. Department of the Treasury’s Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC).

The individuals and entities sanctioned today have been involved in quarter horse racing, construction, trucking, and the oil and gas sectors that have facilitated CDN’s operations, including the movement of illicit proceeds, logistical support, and other activities benefiting the cartel.

Cartels flood American communities with fentanyl, crystal methamphetamine, heroin, marijuana, and cocaine.  Let this serve as a notice to every CDN leader, financier, and business partner:  the United States will pursue you, your assets, and your criminal enterprise no matter where you attempt to hide.  There is no safe haven for those who finance or profit from CDN’s violence and destruction.

On February 20, 2025, the Department of State designated CDN as a Foreign Terrorist Organization (FTO) and Specially Designated Global Terrorist (SDGT).  Today’s OFAC action was taken pursuant to Executive Order (E.O.) 14059, which targets the proliferation of illicit drugs and their means of production, and E.O. 13224, as amended, which targets terrorists and their supporters.

For more information about today’s sanctions, please see the Department of the Treasury’s press release.
