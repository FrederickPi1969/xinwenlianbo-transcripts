## [Press Statement] Federated States of Micronesia National Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the United States of America, I offer my congratulations and best wishes to the people of the Federated States of Micronesia as you celebrate the 40th anniversary of your independence.

As a fellow Pacific nation, the United States and the Federated States of Micronesia share a unique partnership anchored in our Compact of Free Association. Since the establishment of bilateral ties in 1986, our nations have enjoyed a steadfast friendship, underpinned by a commitment to mutual prosperity, strong bilateral security cooperation, and flourishing people-to-people ties.

The United States reaffirms its partnership with the Federated States of Micronesia as you embark on the next 40 years and offers its warmest wishes as you celebrate this special day.

## [Press Statement] Antigua and Barbuda National Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the United States of America, I extend warm congratulations to the people and government of Antigua and Barbuda on the 45th anniversary of your independence.

The United States and Antigua and Barbuda share a longstanding friendship grounded in our common democratic values, strong people-to-people ties, and commitment to a safe, secure, and prosperous Caribbean. Since establishing diplomatic relations in 1981, our two countries have worked together on issues ranging from regional security and law enforcement to economic development, and disaster preparedness.

We value Antigua and Barbuda’s partnership in advancing security and stability throughout the Eastern Caribbean and look forward to continuing our close cooperation on issues of mutual importance. As you mark this milestone, I wish you all a happy 45th Independence Day.
