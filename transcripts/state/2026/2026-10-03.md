## [Press Statement] Iraq&#8217;s National Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the United States of America, I congratulate the people of Iraq on their National Day on October 3.

Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi’s July visit to Washington and his meeting with President Trump at the White House reaffirmed the close friendship between our two nations and our shared commitment to a strong and prosperous future.  The historic economic partnerships announced during the visit further demonstrate the tremendous potential for cooperation between American and Iraqi businesses.  We also welcome Iraq’s efforts to strengthen its sovereignty and contribute to security and stability across the region.

On this National Day, we celebrate the history, resilience, and achievements of the Iraqi people and look forward to the continued growth and partnership between our two nations.  We extend our warmest wishes to the people of Iraq as they commemorate this important occasion.

## [Press Statement] Germany National Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the United States of America, I congratulate the German people as you mark the 36th anniversary of German Unity on October 3.

The peaceful reunification of Germany in 1990 stands as one of the defining triumphs of the modern era — a testament to the courage of Germans who never wavered in their pursuit of freedom. That legacy continues to anchor the enduring partnership between our two nations.

This year, as the United States marks the 250th anniversary of its independence, we are reminded of how deeply intertwined our histories have become. Generations of Americans and Germans have helped shape each other’s nations, which today remain bound by strong shared interests. We additionally welcome Germany’s continued investment in its defense capabilities, a reflection of the clear-eyed understanding that peace and sovereignty must be safeguarded, not assumed.

As we look ahead, the United States reaffirms its commitment to working alongside Germany in pursuit of a safer, stronger, and more prosperous future for both our peoples.

Congratulations again to the Federal Republic of Germany and to all the German people on your National Day of Unity.
