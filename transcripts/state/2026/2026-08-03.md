		Continuing to Advance Venezuela’s Institutional Democratic Transition — Thomas &#8220;Tommy&#8221; Pigott

Secretary of State Marco Rubio spoke today with Paraguayan President Santiago Peña. The Secretary thanked President Peña for his support of Venezuela’s reconstruction and democratic transition, emphasizing that sustained regional unity and coordination will be important to advancing Venezuela’s path forward.

The two leaders also discussed bilateral priorities – including expanding security and economic cooperation. The call reaffirmed the strategic partnership between the United States and Paraguay.

## [Press Statement] Cook Islands National Day — Marco Rubio

On behalf of the United States of America, I extend my congratulations to the people of the Cook Islands on your 61st anniversary of self-governance.

The partnership between our two countries is strengthened by our cooperation on seabed exploration, people-to-people ties, and joint commitment to ensuring a free and open Pacific region.

The United States looks forward to advancing our engagement on maritime security and responsible ocean resource development in the years ahead. I wish the people of the Cook Islands a joyful Constitution Day.
