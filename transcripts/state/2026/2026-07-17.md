Deputy Secretary of State Christopher Landau met today in Washington, D.C., with the Foreign Minister of North Macedonia, Timčo Mucunski. They discussed ways to advance our partnership, particularly by deepening ties on energy, infrastructure, and security. They found many areas of agreement, including a shared concern that burdensome regulation is a threat to economic prosperity.
