## Presidential Message on National Diabetes Month

Presidential Message on National Diabetes Month
The White House
November 10, 2026
Nearly every American family has been touched by diabetes—a parent, a grandparent, a child, or a neighbor whose daily life is shaped by this disease.  This National Diabetes Month, we recognize the millions of Americans struggling with diabetes, and we reaffirm our commitment to reverse the chronic disease epidemic in our Nation and Make America Healthy Again.

In recent decades, the number of adults diagnosed with diabetes has doubled, with more than 40 million men and women in the United States living with the disease.  An estimated 25 percent of adults living with diabetes are unaware they have it, putting their health at serious risk every single day.  For too long, the Federal Government accepted these numbers as the cost of modern life.  My Administration refuses to accept that answer, and we are fighting every day to help patients and families receive the care and medicine they need to stay healthy, including insulin.

When I returned to office, I made a promise to pursue groundbreaking medical advancements and expand access to care for American families and patients, which is why I proudly established the Make America Healthy Again Commission to gain new insights into health conditions like diabetes.  Through my Great Healthcare Plan, unveiled earlier this year, I am continuing to deliver on my commitment.  This plan is slashing the price of prescription drugs, including insulin, by securing Most-Favored-Nation pricing so that Americans never again pay more than patients in other countries for the same medications.  It will cut insurance premiums by putting money directly into the pockets of American families instead of insurance companies.  It will hold Big Insurance accountable by requiring insurers to disclose, in plain English, how often they deny claims and how much of every premium dollar actually goes to care.  Furthermore, it will bring total price transparency to American healthcare so that patients know the cost of care before they receive it.  Together, these reforms will deliver a healthcare system that puts patients first, restores trust, and truly serves the American People.

Through platforms like TrumpRx, we are lowering the cost of medications and ensuring individuals with diabetes are not left without the care they depend on.  Under my leadership, prescription drug prices recorded their largest annual decline in more than 60 years, and we have delivered massive price cuts on GLP-1s and other drugs used to manage diabetes. Patients who buy GLP-1s through TrumpRx are saving $1,800 this year, and those savings will soar to $3,000 by 2028.  Thanks to TrumpRx, any American can also purchase insulin for $35 per month or less.  With bold and decisive America First policies, my Administration will always stand up for individuals with diabetes and ensure that their necessary medications remain affordable.

This month, our Nation unites to recognize the strength of those living with diabetes, honor the strides we have made in prevention and treatment, and renew our commitment to defeating this disease once and for all.  As we continue building a bigger and brighter future filled with unprecedented technology, research, and treatment, I encourage all Americans to take a proactive role in their health and understand their risk for diabetes.  Together, we will ensure that every American has the tools and support they need to build a healthy and prosperous life.

## Presidential Message on the Birthday of the United States Marine Corps

Presidential Message on the Birthday of the United States Marine Corps
The White House
November 10, 2026
For over two and a half centuries, the United States Marine Corps has stood as a living embodiment of the American Spirit.  With honor, courage, and unwavering commitment to preserving and advancing the interests of the United States, Marines have proven time and again that they will answer every call and complete any mission no matter the odds.  As the Marine Corps celebrates 251 years of protecting America and defeating her enemies, I extend my deepest gratitude to every Marine who has fought and sacrificed for our Nation and pay tribute to their unmatched legacy of valor.

From the dawn of our Republic, Marines have defended the pillars of freedom and liberty that America was founded upon.  During the Revolutionary War, Marines took to the sea to seize the port of Nassau and reinforce the Continental Army with large quantities of military supplies.  Throughout World War II, Marines played a critical role across the Pacific Theater and were essential to American victories at Guam, Guadalcanal, Okinawa, and Iwo Jima.  These incredible Devil Dogs have served on the front lines of countless conflicts throughout American history, never failed to protect our citizens at home and abroad, and fearlessly demonstrated American might and power in the face of our adversaries.  Today, America is made safer through the heroic and selfless service of our brave Marines.

The patriotic men and women of the United States Marine Corps are enduring reminders of the true source of American greatness and glory.  As President, I have been the strongest advocate for our Marines in the history of our country.  Through advancements in Super Intelligence and investments in missile defense, next-generation capabilities, and shipbuilding, we are providing our Marines with every resource they need to defeat America’s enemies wherever they may be.

Under my leadership, our Marines also return home to the care and support they have so rightly earned.  My Administration has restored common sense at the Department of Veterans Affairs, empowering veterans to seek the healthcare providers of their choice, lowering costs for veterans and their families, and slashing harmful backlogs.  As Commander in Chief, I will never stop standing up for our Marines and upholding their reputation as the most lethal fighting force the world has ever seen.

Guided by their motto, Semper Fidelis, meaning “Always Faithful,” the United States Marines have left their mark on the history of our Nation and all of mankind.  The steadfast strength of our Marines is a testament to the power of the American Spirit and the might of the United States Armed Forces.  Generation after generation, Marines have faithfully served as champions of America, and they will carry on as faithful defenders of liberty for centuries to come.  Semper Fi!

## Presidential Message on National Alzheimer’s Disease Awareness Month

Presidential Message on National Alzheimer’s Disease Awareness Month
The White House
November 10, 2026
This National Alzheimer’s Disease Awareness Month, we stand with every American living with this cruel disease, every family walking beside a loved one through its difficult course, and every caregiver who devotes themselves to others day after day with extraordinary patience and love.  We also pause to remember the millions of precious moments, cherished memories, and beloved lives that Alzheimer’s has taken from families across our Nation.

Few illnesses challenge the human spirit like Alzheimer’s.  It steals the memories that tell the story of a life—the names of old friends, the sound of a child’s voice, and the familiar places we once called home.  Yet no disease can erase the dignity of our citizens, and no diagnosis diminishes the love of a family that will not leave their side.  Across America, spouses become caregivers; children return the devotion once given to them by their parents; and neighbors, doctors, nurses, researchers, and volunteers come together to offer comfort and hope in moments of profound uncertainty.  These acts of love and devotion to those suffering from Alzheimer’s represent the very best of America’s heart and soul.

For too long, our Nation has accepted chronic illness as an unavoidable part of American life, but my Administration refuses to accept that answer any longer.  Through our historic Make America Healthy Again agenda, we are asking the fundamental questions about why Americans are becoming sick, pursuing the root causes of chronic disease, restoring integrity to scientific research, and placing preventive innovation at the center of our national mission.  We are unleashing the power of Super Intelligence and the genius of American scientists, doctors, and entrepreneurs to pursue medical breakthroughs that once seemed beyond our grasp.  America has never been a country that resigns itself to the impossible—we have conquered countless diseases that once seemed unbeatable and transformed modern medicine for the world.  With that same spirit of discovery, compassion, and determination, we will keep searching for better treatments, earlier answers, and—one day—a cure for Alzheimer’s.

This month, we honor those fighting Alzheimer’s and the caregivers who stand beside them.  We remember those we have lost, embrace the families who carry their memories forward, and renew our promise to build an America where longer lives are also healthier lives. Together, we will Make America Healthy Again and give future generations the priceless gift of more years, more memories, and more time with the people they love.

## Presidential Message on Critical Infrastructure Security and Resilience Month

Presidential Message on Critical Infrastructure Security and Resilience Month
The White House
November 10, 2026
America’s critical infrastructure is the foundation of our prosperity, the backbone of our national defense, and the bedrock of our resilience.  For more than two centuries, American workers, through their own ingenuity and grit, have built the infrastructure that enables the world’s greatest economy and strongest military.  During Critical Infrastructure Security and Resilience Month, we celebrate this long, rich legacy and reaffirm our commitment to expanding opportunity, fortifying our national security, and unleashing American innovation to keep our Nation free and resilient for generations to come.

My America First Resilience Strategy establishes a bold vision to secure such a future through commonsense transformation.  We are confronting the gravest threats to our homeland head-on to protect American lives, strengthen our communities, and safeguard livelihoods; upgrading our aging technology and infrastructure to guarantee dependable power, clean water, and modern communications for households nationwide while keeping our country ahead of its rivals; returning authority to the States and equipping governors, local leaders, and industries with the tools to prepare for and recover from any crisis; and streamlining government to deliver faster results and greater value for every hardworking citizen.  Our goal is clear:  to secure the supply chains and systems on which our Nation depends so that no adversary and no disaster will ever hold America or our core interests hostage.

For America to remain the champion of the world’s economy, our critical infrastructure must be built by American companies, supported by American workers, and secured against those who seek to exploit it.  Since returning to office, I have taken decisive action to reshore the manufacturing our critical infrastructure relies on.  To harness our abundant energy and natural resources, I declared a National Energy Emergency and signed an Executive Order on Unleashing American Energy, directing every Federal agency to expedite major energy projects.  I also established the National Energy Dominance Council, marshalling the full power of the Federal Government behind that vital mission, and invoked the Defense Production Act to strengthen domestic production of the transformers, transmission equipment, and other critical components essential to our electric grid and national defense.  Through this America First approach, we are expanding American oil, natural gas, and nuclear energy production and eliminating disastrous, unnecessary, or burdensome regulations to finally restore the reliable and affordable energy that powers the greatest Nation on Earth.

That America First commitment is more critical than ever as we enter a new era of digital infrastructure—the age of Super Intelligence (SI).  Across America, we are investing in SI data centers to power the American economy and ensure that we remain the global leader in this cutting-edge technology, especially as our adversaries compete against us for dominance.  Every new data center will unlock countless high-paying jobs in America’s SI industry.  Thanks to the work of my Administration, America’s largest technology companies have also signed the Ratepayer Protection Pledge, agreeing to pay for the new power generation and grid upgrades their data centers require instead of passing those costs on to American families.  As President, I will always stand up for the middle class and ensure that our companies put the American People first.

Our infrastructure is as strong as the Americans who build, operate, and defend it.  This Critical Infrastructure Security and Resilience Month, we honor the linemen, engineers, technicians, operators, cybersecurity professionals, and skilled workers whose dedication keeps America running.  Their unrivaled genius and determination represent the very best of our Nation, and together with them, we will build and maintain the infrastructure that makes America great.

## Congressional Bills H.R. 997, H.R. 1703, H.R. 1945, and H.R. 7730 Signed into Law

Congressional Bills H.R. 997, H.R. 1703, H.R. 1945, and H.R. 7730 Signed into Law
The White House
November 10, 2026
On Tuesday, November 10, 2026, the President signed into law:

H.R. 997, the “National Taxpayer Advocate Enhancement Act of 2025,” which authorizes the National Taxpayer Advocate to appoint legal counsel within the Office of the National Taxpayer Advocate that reports directly to the National Taxpayer Advocate, or delegate thereof, and further expands the authority of National Taxpayer Advocate to take personnel actions with respect to any Taxpayer Advocate Service employees;

H.R. 1703, the “Choices for Increased Mobility Act of 2026,” which modifies Medicare payment rules for certain wheelchairs;

H.R. 1945, the “America’s National Churchill Museum National Historic Landmark Act,” which designates the America’s National Churchill Museum, located in Fulton, Missouri, as a National Historic Landmark; and

H.R. 7730, the “Bankruptcy Threshold Adjustment Act,” which permanently raises bankruptcy threshold limits for small business Chapter 11 proceedings and consumer bankruptcy Chapter 13 proceedings.
