## Presidential Message on Overdose and Drug Poisoning Awareness Week

Presidential Message on Overdose and Drug Poisoning Awareness Week
The White House
August 24, 2026
During Overdose and Drug Poisoning Awareness Week, our Nation renews its resolve to defeat the scourge of illicit drugs and synthetic poisons that have devastated hundreds of thousands of American lives.  My Administration is relentlessly confronting this threat at every source, and we will always put our national security and the safety, health, and well-being of the American People first.

When I returned to office, I vowed to stop the flow of fentanyl and other deadly drugs into our country, and my Administration immediately took action.  We created the Homeland Security Task Force to bring together all federal, state and local partners to achieve the national policy goal of eliminating the cartel presence in America once and for all.  By doing so, my Administration secured our southern border and began dismantling fentanyl supply chains at every link, from the narco-terrorist cartels and gangs that traffic these deadly drugs to the foreign suppliers that enable them.  I proudly signed the HALT Fentanyl Act, permanently classifying fentanyl-related substances as Schedule I drugs, ensuring dealers and traffickers will receive the maximum force of the law.  I designated fentanyl as a weapon of mass destruction and the worst cartels as foreign terrorist organizations, unleashing our national security resources against this attack on American soil.  I have also directed military forces to conduct strikes on dozens of narcotrafficking boats to disrupt the flow of maritime supply routes.  As a result, fentanyl deaths dropped 22 percent in a single year, total drug overdose deaths fell 14 percent, and thousands of American lives have been saved.

The strength of our country rests upon the health of its people, and my Administration is restoring hope in every community touched by addiction.  Earlier this year, I launched the Great American Recovery Initiative, uniting government, healthcare, faith communities, and the private sector behind one mission of prevention, treatment, and recovery.  This program is making proven treatments more accessible, supporting Americans and families on the road to recovery, and giving every person the opportunity to build a healthy and hopeful future.

This week, we remember every American lost to overdose and drug poisoning, stand with the families who carry their memory, and support all those working to overcome addiction.  Above all, we vow to end the devastation wrought by deadly drugs and build a stronger, healthier, and safer America.

## Presidential Message on National Park Week

Presidential Message on National Park Week
The White House
August 24, 2026
America’s vast and wondrous national parks stand at the heart of our story, preserving the lands our ancestors cherished and the unmatched splendor of a continent like no other.  During National Park Week, we celebrate the rich cultural heritage, grandeur, and exceptionalism found in the more than 400 national parks and monuments across our land.

On August 25, 1916, the National Park Service was established to protect our Nation’s natural resources, historic landmarks, and magnificent landscapes.  For more than a century, our treasured parks, battlefields, and monuments have stood as hallmarks of the American Spirit, drawing enthusiasts from all walks of life to seek out places of historical significance along with some of the world’s most incredible natural wonders—from coastal landscapes, pristine glacial lakes, and rugged forests to rushing waterfalls, hiking trails, and wild game found nowhere else on Earth.

My Administration is committed to protecting every acre of our lands and preserving the cleanest air and water in the world.  During my first term, I was proud to sign the Great American Outdoors Act—the most significant conservation policy since President Teddy Roosevelt—to address prior mismanagement, maintenance backlogs, and critical infrastructure needs in our national parks and public lands.  Upon returning to office, I signed the Make America Beautiful Again Executive Order, establishing a commission devoted to promoting voluntary stewardship of our lands, expanding outdoor access, cutting needless regulations, and recovering imperiled species all while growing our economy and protecting the time-honored integrity of our national parks.  In response, the Commission issued its Make America Beautiful Again report, calling on the American people, private businesses, and non-profit groups to partner with the federal government and each other to ensure that the next 250 years of our great nation are beautiful, successful, and secure.  To mark this milestone year for the National Park Service, I am pleased to announce that on August 25 our national parks will be open free of charge to residents of the United States, giving families the opportunity to see firsthand the natural beauty of our magnificent Republic.  Under my leadership, we will make America’s Great Outdoors a source of national pride and breathtaking splendor once again, vowing always to defend our God-given heritage through responsible conservation and bold restoration efforts.

As our Nation commemorates 250 glorious years of Independence, we give thanks for the abundant natural resources of our homeland and for the generations of Americans whose labor preserved these treasures.  Together, we will ensure that our majestic national parks endure as a source of adventure, respite, inspiration, and awe for generations to come.
