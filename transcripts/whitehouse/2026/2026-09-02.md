## Presidential Message on National Preparedness Month

Presidential Message on National Preparedness Month
The White House
September 2, 2026
America has always been a nation of strong, capable, and determined people who understand that strength must be built before crisis.  Preparedness builds that strength, and readiness is the result.  This National Preparedness Month, I call on every American citizen to ready themselves and their families, homes, businesses, and communities for natural and man-made threats.

Readiness begins with understanding the risks that matter most.  Before a crisis, Americans can take practical steps to protect their homes and property, purchase affordable insurance against the risks they face, know evacuation routes, maintain an emergency kit with essential supplies, and stay informed through trusted local news and real-time alerts.  These simple investments save lives, protect livelihoods, and help families and communities return to normal.

The same principle applies to the systems that power our Nation.  American businesses and operators of critical infrastructure have an essential role and must also be ready.  The energy, communications, transportation, financial, water, food, and health systems that power American life must remain available when we need them most.  My Administration is working to strengthen the reliability of these systems, secure vital supply chains, reduce dangerous dependencies, and ensure services continue operating through crisis.

On June 23, 2026, I issued the National Resilience Strategy that articulates how the Nation, through federalism, will achieve a readiness posture.  As President, my Administration continues transforming emergency management in the United States around readiness, responsibility, and results.  States and communities must participate in this transformation by building their own fiscal and operational capacity to manage the emergencies they routinely face and support one another through mutual aid, drastically speeding up deployment and delivery of critical resources when disaster strikes.  Americans deserve state and local governments that respond quickly to their needs, enabling communities to rebuild stronger and more resilient than ever before.  This allows the Federal Government to concentrate its resources on the greatest risks and catastrophic incidents—regionally or nationally.  It also makes our country harder to threaten.  When America functions through crisis, our adversaries have fewer opportunities to harm us and fewer opportunities to achieve their objectives.

Strength begins with a Nation that is ready to act and such readiness is embodied by Americans who stand strong, who never back down, and who always put America First.  This month, we honor our brave first responders, law enforcement officers, emergency personnel, and selfless volunteers.  When the alarm sounds, they do not hesitate.  They answer the call, protect their fellow Americans, and help keep our communities and our Nation safe and secure.  Together, we resolve to build a more prepared, ready, and resilient America.

## Presidential Message on National Childhood Cancer Awareness Month

Presidential Message on National Childhood Cancer Awareness Month
The White House
September 2, 2026
This National Childhood Cancer Awareness Month, my Administration stands with the countless children facing the devastating effects of cancer.  We recognize the love, strength, and care of the families who walk beside them through every step of their journeys.  Above all, we commit to advancing lifesaving innovation and accelerating cures, giving every child the chance to live a long, healthy, and happy life.

American medicine has achieved remarkable breakthroughs in the fight to save the lives of our children, but cancer still remains the leading cause of death among children in the United States.  To overcome such devastation, my Administration is strengthening preventative efforts, developing better treatments, and ultimately finding a cure to end this scourge.

Through the Make America Healthy Again Commission, we are addressing the root cause of chronic illnesses afflicting children—including cancer—and giving every child the chance to grow up healthy.  We are also harnessing Artificial Intelligence to accelerate pediatric cancer research, identify promising solutions, and unlock new possibilities for prevention and cures.  By bringing together American innovation and cutting-edge technology, we are providing new hope to children and families fighting cancer.

Every American child deserves the chance to grow up healthy and achieve their own unique American Dream.  Together, we will fight to eradicate this vicious disease and build a future of greater hope, health, and possibility for America’s children.

## Presidential Message on National Recovery Month

Presidential Message on National Recovery Month
The White House
September 2, 2026
During National Recovery Month, we celebrate the more than 23 million Americans who have found a pathway from addiction to recovery, we recognize those who have helped them reclaim their lives, and we renew our commitment to supporting those still struggling to find hope and healing.

Throughout every community in America, the effects of drug and alcohol addiction are felt in the lives of families, friends, and neighbors.  Today, 48.4 million Americans suffer from addiction, and 69,973 Americans died last year to overdose.  Every American deserves to live free from the grip of addiction—and my Administration is wholly committed to making that possible by preventing drug use before it starts, providing treatment at the earliest opportunity, and helping more Americans lead engaged and productive lives in recovery.

In January, I was proud to establish the Great American Recovery Initiative, coordinating a national response to the disease of addiction across government, healthcare, faith communities, and the private sector while fostering a culture that celebrates recovery.  I also signed an Executive Order on Ending Crime and Disorder on America’s Streets to make our communities safer and help homeless Americans access addiction and mental health services needed to rebuild their lives.  I am directing the Food and Drug Administration to prioritize non-opioid options for chronic pain, and I signed the SUPPORT for Patients and Communities Reauthorization Act to strengthen federal grant programs for treatment, overdose response, and mental health care.  We are also stopping deadly drugs before they ever reach our shores.  I signed the HALT Fentanyl Act into law, classifying all fentanyl-related substances as Schedule I drugs; formally designated major international drug cartels as foreign terrorist organizations; sealed our border; and empowered our brave Border Patrol and Armed Forces with the resources needed to carry out massive drug seizures to keep fentanyl off our streets.

Recovery is a testament to the strength of the American Spirit and the promise of a second chance.  This month, we stand with those on the road to recovery and the families and communities who support them along the way.  Together, we will help more Americans break free from addiction and lead healthy, productive, and fulfilling lives.
