## Congressional Bills H.R. 1276, H.R. 2069, H.R. 2196 and H.R. 5366 Signed into Law

Congressional Bills H.R. 1276, H.R. 2069, H.R. 2196 and H.R. 5366 Signed into Law
The White House
September 11, 2026
On Friday, September 11, 2026, the President signed into law:

H.R. 1276, which directs the Department of the Interior to remove all deed restriction from an approximately 3.62-acre parcel of land conveyed from the Department to the City of Paducah, Kentucky;

H.R. 2069, the “Stop Secret Spending Act of 2025,” which expands Federal spending transparency requirements to include other transaction agreements, and to modify related standards, oversight, and reporting;

H.R. 2196, the “National Emergency Medical Services Memorial Extension Act,” which extends the authority of the National Emergency Medical Services Memorial Foundation to establish a commemorative work on Federal lands in the District of Columbia to commemorate the commitment and service represented by emergency medical services personnel; and

H.R. 5366, the “Doug LaMalfa Federal Disaster Tax Relief Certainty Act,” which extends and codifies the Federal tax deduction for qualified disaster-related personal casualty losses and the exclusion from gross income of qualified wildfire relief payments.

## Presidential Message on Rosh Hashanah

Presidential Message on Rosh Hashanah
The White House
September 11, 2026
Melania and I send our warmest greetings to the entire Jewish community on Rosh Hashanah—the Jewish New Year and the first of the High Holy Days.

The Jewish community around the world marks the anniversary of when the Almighty created Adam and Eve and the story of humanity began.  These two days open a solemn season of prayer, repentance, and spiritual renewal in preparation for Yom Kippur.

My Administration reaffirms our steadfast commitment to ushering in a historic renewal of faith across our country, eliminating the scourge of anti-Semitism, and defending the sacred right of religious liberty for every single American.

May you be inscribed in the Book of Life. Wishing you a Happy, Healthy, and Sweet New Year!
