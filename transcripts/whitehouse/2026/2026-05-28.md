## Congressional Bill H.R. 3497 Signed into Law

Congressional Bill H.R. 3497 Signed into Law

May 28, 2026
On Thursday, May 28, 2026, the President signed into law:

H.R. 3497, the “Medal of Sacrifice Act of 2025,” which establishes the Medal of Sacrifice to honor certain eligible law enforcement officers and first responders who are killed in the line of duty.
