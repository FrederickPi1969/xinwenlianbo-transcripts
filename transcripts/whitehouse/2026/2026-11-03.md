## Congressional Bills H.R. 1744 and H.R. 8205 Signed into Law

Congressional Bills H.R. 1744 and H.R. 8205 Signed into Law
The White House
November 3, 2026
On Tuesday, November 3, 2026, the President signed into law:

H.R. 1744, the “United States Commission on International Religious Freedom Reauthorization Act of 2026,” which extends and authorizes annual appropriations for the United States Commission on International Religious Freedom through Fiscal Year 2028; and

H.R. 8205, the “Accelerating Access to Critical Therapies for ALS Reauthorization Act of 2026,” which reauthorizes the Accelerating Access to Critical Therapies for ALS Act through Fiscal Year 2031.

## First Lady Melania Trump to Convene Russian and Ukrainian Representatives at the White House

First Lady Melania Trump will personally bring representatives of Russia and Ukraine, two nations actively at war, together at the White House. This event represents an extraordinary moment in American history.

Mrs. Trump independently initiated engagement between the two warring nations and successfully secured a ceasefire in Oleshky and the surrounding Kherson region. This achievement marks an unprecedented diplomatic initiative by an American First Lady.

First Lady Melania Trump will deliver remarks on the ceasefire in Oleshky at 11:00 am ET on Thursday, November 5, 2026, at the White House.
