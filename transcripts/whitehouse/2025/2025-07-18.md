## The President Signed into Law S. 1582

The President Signed into Law S. 1582
The White House
July 18, 2025
On Friday, July 18, 2025, the President signed into law:
S. 1582, the “Guiding and Establishing National Innovation for U.S. Stablecoins Act” or “GENIUS Act,” which provides for the regulation of payment stablecoins, and for other purposes.
