Veteran Washington Post photojournalist Heidi Levine, an American, was on the scene documenting the attack when settlers surrounded and began beating her. Video of the assault shows masked men surrounding Levine, knocking her to the ground, repeatedly beating and kicking her as she tried to protect herself. I mean, in years past, you know, whole families would come out together. There would be these beautiful picnic spreads, and, you know, it was a — there was no rush.

On Friday morning in Abu Falah, there were no women or children. There were just men rushing to pick the olives as fast as they possibly could, because just in the distance, on top of a hill, we were literally watching these settlers, who live in an outpost there that the Israeli government knows about. We watched them put on their masks, pick up their clubs and prepare for the assault. About an hour into the harvest, the settlers started charging down the hill.

And by the way, I should mention, this village coordinated with the IDF to do their olive harvest on that day, under the pretense that they would get protection from the settlers who are living on their land. The IDF didn’t show up.

So, the settlers start charging down the hill. They are all wearing masks. They’re holding, you know, these big clubs, and they’re swinging giant rocks that are — you know, can maim people when they hit them. As that’s happening, the farmers — many of the farmers start to retreat back into the village, but the shabab, or the Palestinian youth, sort of emerge and start protecting the village.

You have to keep in mind that this is not just about the olive harvest. Abu Falah, like in many communities around the West Bank, the olive fields there are the buffer zone between the violent settlers and the village itself. In other words, if the settlers were able to push past the olive groves, they would then be in the residential part of the community. They would be burning homes. They would be assaulting people. And they would basically try to, you know, expand their territorial control into the village.

So, these young Palestinians step up. All they have is rocks. They’re totally outgunned always. And they basically charge back at the settlers. And for maybe 10 minutes, the Palestinians were successfully defending their land with just stones. And then these settlers start setting the olive trees on fire. So, suddenly, there’s smoke in the air.

And then the thing that really changed things was when the IDF finally did show up. They came screaming in in a military jeep, and rather than do something to stop these masked settlers who were assaulting Palestinians on their own land, they start shooting tear gas at the Palestinians who I was with. As that’s happening — so, now there’s tear gas and smoke in the air — a truck full of settlers then speeds in. Settlers in the back, wearing all black, shooting M16s into the air like ISIS, start flying in. So, now there’s machine gun fire, too.

So, it was at that moment that everybody on the side of the village, as in the Palestinians and the journalists who were there and the activists who were there with them, just turn around and run. So, we’re all now just running back towards the village. And that is when Heidi was caught by settlers.

So, basically, once I got far enough out of the cloud of smoke and tear gas, I looked back, and I could just see a — within this massive mob of, like, probably 50-plus settlers, there is a smaller circle of maybe four or five just, like, savagely beating somebody on the ground with their clubs, with their feet, with their fists. I only discovered it was a journalist when somebody grabbed their blue press vest and pulled them up. And for a minute, I thought they were helping them, and then they held them up so that another settler could come and kick her in the stomach and knock her back down. So, we saw that this was happening, and me and a couple other journalists and a group of Palestinians, you know, knew that her life was in danger. I mean, it looked like they were killing her. In the video, you can see them, you know, raining down these blows with these clubs, that certainly could have killed her.

And so, there was really no way to get around the settlers, because they had fanned out across the fields, and they were continuing to throw rocks, and the army was tear-gassing us. They ran out ahead, and they started beating the settlers back by throwing stones at them, which is what — the only thing that allowed me to then run out front and grab Heidi.

And then, just as I’m starting to think, “We might be overrun here. You know, these settlers could potentially catch us, and then I’ll be on the bottom of that pile,” once again, the Palestinian youth created a literal perimeter around us to protect us and to beat the settlers back with stones once again, just to defend two journalists who, you know, in many cases, they had met that day or hadn’t even met at all.
