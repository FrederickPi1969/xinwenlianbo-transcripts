In Israel, prosecutors indicted settler Yinon Levi Thursday over the killing of Palestinian activist Awdah Hathaleen in the occupied West Bank last year. Levi is charged with reckless manslaughter, armed trespassing and malicious property damage. According to the Israeli rights group B’Tselem, it’s the first time an Israeli has been indicted in the killing of a Palestinian in the occupied West Bank since October 7, 2023. Hathaleen was a prominent activist against settler violence and was involved in the Oscar-winning documentary “No Other Land,” about Palestinians resisting the demolition of their villages. A historic heat wave continues to roil Europe. Austria has broken its national heat record for the second time in two days. One town near the Slovak border reached about 106 degrees Fahrenheit, topping the record Vienna set just a day earlier, according to the national weather service.

In neighboring Hungary, businesses and households are curbing electricity use amid a severe drought. Falling water levels on the Danube have forced Hungary to largely shut down its only nuclear plant, which depends on the river for cooling.

New forecasting suggests that the rapidly developing El Niño weather system could drive roughly 50 million people into acute hunger by the end of 2027. Meteorologists say this year’s El Niño is developing into the strongest in at least 70 years. El Niño is the recurring Pacific weather pattern which alters rainfall across the globe.
