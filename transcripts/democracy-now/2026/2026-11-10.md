## Pentagon Whistleblower Wes Bryant: Impeach Hegseth. He’s a “Clear & Present Danger” to Democracy

“Under Hegseth, the U.S. military has been hijacked,” says retired Air Force Master Sergeant Wes Bryant, who served as chief of civilian harm assessment at the Pentagon from 2024 to 2025. “America has portrayed itself to the world as a far-right dictatorship bent on global domination, and Hegseth and his wielding of the U.S. military with free rein from Donald Trump is really the primary enforcer of that.”

Bryant adds that “Hegseth should be the first domino,” but that Trump, as well as “many of our senior military leaders,” should also be impeached.

AMY GOODMAN: The House is moving towards a vote this week on whether to impeach Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth. REP. THOMAS MASSIE: Wherefore, Secretary of Defense Peter Hegseth, by such conduct, has demonstrated that he will remain a threat to the Constitution if allowed to remain in office, and has acted in a manner grossly incompatible with his duties and the rule of law. Peter Brian Hegseth thus warrants impeachment and trial, removal from office and disqualification to hold and enjoy any office of honor, trust or profit under the United States.

AMY GOODMAN: Congressmember Massie introduced eight articles of impeachment against Hegseth, including violating the 1973 War Powers Resolution by continuing to attack Iran after Congress ordered the Trump administration to stop, illegally waging war against Yemen, ignoring laws designed to reduce civilian casualties, overseeing extrajudicial killings by bombing the boats of alleged drug traffickers in the Caribbean and eastern Pacific, abducting President Nicolás Maduro of Venezuela without congressional authorization, and suppressing the free speech rights of Democratic Senator Mark Kelly in retaliation for his participation in a video reminding troops not to obey illegal orders.

Only a few Republican defectors in the House would be needed for a simple majority to impeach, setting up a Senate trial that could remove Hegseth from office.

You know, if they had been emplaced at the Central Command, for example, if those personnel had been overseeing the strike planning for every single target set in Iran, I promise that a strike as egregious of a failure as Minab would not have happened.

And then you have the detractions, the devolutions of operational practice and standards in general, where, you know, even aside from my unit that existed, the Civilian Harm Enterprise, the U.S. military has been violating standing doctrine and practices in targeting and civilian harm mitigation that even existed over the course of the past 20, 30 years. You know, you combine this with Hegseth’s deemphasis on the protection of civilian, his glorification of and reveling in war and violence, his direction — his directives to carry out mass bombing campaigns, and what I believe is his directives that he’s making, you know, over secured lines or in secured areas to the commanders themselves, so that they’re unrecorded, to actually have a higher tolerance for civilian casualties. We’ve got this incredible spike in civilian casualties, not just Minab in Iran. It’s upwards of 3,000-plus potential civilians killed, along with, you know, massive destruction of civilian infrastructure, even targeting of cultural heritage sites, hospitals, schools — more than just Minab — police and fire entities, vital energy infrastructure. ## “We Have to Be Brave”: Palestinian Journalist Abed Abu Shehadeh Speaks Out After Israeli Detention

“Men with ski masks knock on your door at 11 at night. They tell you that they have an arrest warrant. They raid the house. They wake your wife, your children,” says Shehadeh. “It tells how messianic and dangerous the extreme right in Israel has become, willing to use this unprecedented power against journalists.”

AMY GOODMAN: This is Democracy Now!, democracynow.org. I’m Amy Goodman, with Juan González.

The prominent Palestinian journalist Abed Abu Shehadeh has been released from Israeli custody after five days in detention. He was arrested last Wednesday when armed police and Shin Bet agents conducted a nighttime raid on his home in the Israeli city of Jaffa. His detention was related to his reporting on the Israeli elections and the role of Palestinian political parties. Ahead of the October 27th election, Abu Shehadeh traveled to Qatar, where he interviewed Azmi Bishara, a prominent Palestinian intellectual, former member of the Israeli Knesset. Israeli authorities detained Abu Shehadeh on suspicion of, quote, “contacting a foreign agent,” unquote, in connection with that interview. Azmi Bishara has lived in exile in Doha since being accused of aiding Hezbollah during the 2006 Lebanon war.

And it’s a direct outcome from what we’ve been through in the past three years. We know the Hebrew language very well. We know the Israeli political culture very well.
