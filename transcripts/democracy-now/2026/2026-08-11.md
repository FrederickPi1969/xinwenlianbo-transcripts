In Syria, a court sentenced ousted leader Bashar al-Assad to death Tuesday after a trial in absentia. He was convicted of killings, torture and arbitrary arrest during Syria’s nearly 14-year war. It was the first Syrian conviction against Assad, who fell in a rebel offensive in December 2024 that ended his family’s decades of rule and a war that killed hundreds of thousands of people.

Under Colombia’s new right-wing, Trump-supported President Abelardo de la Espriella, Colombia has recognized Morocco’s sovereignty over occupied Western Sahara. Colombia has frozen diplomatic relations with the Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic and ended prior support for the independence group, the Polisario Front. Morocco has brutally occupied Western Sahara and its Indigenous people, the Sahrawi, since 1975, in defiance of the United Nations and the international community. The first Trump administration recognized Moroccan sovereignty over Western Sahara in 2020 as part of a larger effort to normalize relations between Israel and its Arab neighbors.

Trump’s State Department has revoked more than 175,000 visas from foreign nationals as part of its sweeping immigration crackdown. This comes as congressional Democrats say they plan to investigate the Trump administration’s efforts to deport U.S. military service members and their families. The probe was announced in response to an Associated Press investigation that found the Trump administration has detained more than 50 parents and spouses of active-duty U.S. military personnel. The investigation by U.S. lawmakers, led by Senator Elizabeth Warren, also found that ICE has attempted to deport 282 veterans and their immediate family members. In April 2025, ICE rescinded its long-standing policy that protected immigrant veterans, and replaced it with a new measure that allows for the detention and deportation of noncitizen U.S. service members.

Here in New York, a Staten Island judge ordered an emergency pause on New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani’s new pied-à-terre tax. The pied-à-terre tax targets homes valued at $5 million or higher, and co-ops and condos worth $1 million or more, when the owner doesn’t live there as their primary residence. Massachusetts has expanded late-term abortion access, loosening restrictions in cases of severe or lethal fetal abnormalities or if the pregnant person’s life is at risk. The law will allow providers to perform abortions after the previous cutoff of 24 weeks when it is necessary to save the pregnant person’s life. Most abortions still happen within the first trimester. During Monday’s bill signing, Massachusetts Governor Maura Healey, a Democrat, said, “We believe that health care decisions should be made between women and families and their doctors, not politicians. … Mothers and fathers and families who experience these devastating diagnoses — and they are devastating, heartbreaking — now they’ll be able to make their own choices.” Massachusetts becomes the 10th state, along with Washington, D.C., to allow an abortion at any point in pregnancy.
