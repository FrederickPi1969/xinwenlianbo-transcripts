## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will be on a two-day visit to West Bengal and Assam beginning today. The Prime Minister will first travel to West Bengal where he will flag off India’s first Vande Bharat Sleeper Train between Howrah and Guwahati (Kamakhya) at Malda town railway station this afternoon. He will also virtually flag off the return service. By significantly reducing travel time by around 2.5 hours on the Howrah-Guwahati route, the fully air-conditioned Vande Bharat Sleeper train will give a major boost to religious travel and tourism.

“With the introduction of the sleeper version of the Vande Bharat Express, long-distance train travel in India is set to become faster, safer and more comfortable for common passengers. The Howrah-Guwahati route is among the most significant rail corridors in the country, supporting the daily movement of lakhs of passengers. The visits to major pilgrimage centres like Kalighat Temple and Kamakhya Devi Temple will become easier, safer and more convenient. The Saraighat Express takes around 17 hours to cover the distance between Howrah and Guwahati, and in comparison, the Vande Bharat Sleeper will complete the same journey in just 14 hours. With Dipendra Kumar from Guwahati, Saklen Akhtar Akashvani News.”

Prime Minister Modi will also dedicate to the Nation and lay the foundation stone of multiple rail and road infrastructure projects worth over three thousand two hundred crore rupees in a public function at Malda district in West Bengal today. The projects include, new railway lines, electrification of key sections in north Bengal, freight and Vande Bharat maintenance facilities, four new Amrit Bharat Express trains, and the four-laning of the Dhupguri-Falakata stretch of National Highway-31D.

The train originating from Guwahati will feature authentic Assamese cuisine, while the train starting from Kolkata will serve traditional Bengali delicacies, ensuring a delightful and culturally rich dining experience on board.
