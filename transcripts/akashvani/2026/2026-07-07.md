Prime Minister Narendra Modi will hold talks with Indonesian President Prabowo Subianto in Jakarta today. Union Home and Cooperation Minister Amit Shah has said that Cooperation Ministry has infused new energy into the lives of nearly 30 crore people associated with the cooperative sector over the past five years. Addressing the fifth Foundation Day celebrations of the Cooperation Ministry in New Delhi, Mr Shah said the ministry, established by Prime Minister Narendra Modi in 2021, has transformed the cooperative ecosystem into one that is modern, transparent, technology-driven and competitive.

He said the ministry has identified the key challenges facing the cooperative sector, prepared a comprehensive roadmap for its development and resolution, and introduced reforms that have modernised the entire ecosystem. He added that when the nation celebrates the centenary of its Independence in 2047, the cooperative movement will serve as the strong foundation of a Prosperous India. Recalling the creation of the Ministry on July 6, 2021, Amit Shah said the decision ended a 75-year wait for millions of people associated with the cooperative sector.

Both had stepped down after their names surfaced in the alleged Ram Temple donation embezzlement case. Krishna Mohan has been appointed Interim General Secretary until a new appointment is made. His complaint formed the basis of the FIR registered in the alleged donation irregularities case, in which eight people have been arrested. He also asserted that all donated ornaments and valuables are safe, properly accounted for, and that details of donated items are available with the Trust.

## Midday News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi held delegation-level talks with Indonesian President Prabowo Subianto in Jakarta to review the full spectrum of bilateral ties and provide fresh momentum to the comprehensive strategic partnership between the two countries. The two leaders also witnessed the exchange of Memoranda of Understanding (MOUs) aimed at enhancing collaboration in areas of mutual interest. Speaking at a joint press conference with the Indonesian President Prabowo  in Jakarta, Mr Modi said growing mutual trust has strengthened cooperation in defence, security and the maritime sector, with both countries agreeing to expand defence exchanges, disaster management cooperation and industrial collaboration.

The two leaders also reaffirmed their commitment to strengthening the India-Indonesia Comprehensive Strategic Partnership during talks in Jakarta. The two leaders witnessed the signing of several Memorandums of Understanding covering defence, critical minerals, agriculture, steel, maritime cooperation and emerging technologies. They agreed to set up a branch of IIM Bangalore in Jakarta.

The two sides also agreed to strengthen collaboration in manufacturing, food security and digital technology.

Mr. Modi expressed gratitude after being conferred Indonesia’s highest civilian honour ‘Bintang Adipurna of the Republic of Indonesia’.

He dedicated the award to the people of India and described it as a reflection of the enduring historical and civilisational ties between India and Indonesia.

Mr. Modi said the partnership is advancing across a wide range of sectors, including development, security, technology, culture and education. Expressing confidence in the future of bilateral ties, he said a new golden chapter in the India-Indonesia partnership begins today.

The Prime Minister said an agreement signed today will make India’s high-quality and affordable medicines more easily available to the people of Indonesia. He added that India will contribute to the capacity building of Indonesian doctors and healthcare professionals. Mr. Modi said both sides have reached an important understanding to strengthen supply chain resilience in critical minerals and steel. He added that companies from the two countries are beginning new partnerships in stainless steel and rare-earth magnets.

The Prime Minister said India’s Unified Payments Interface (UPI) will soon be integrated with Indonesia’s payment system, promoting both ease of doing business and ease of travel.

Earlier, Prime Minister Modi received a ceremonial welcome at the Istana Merdeka, the Presidential Palace.

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh also congratulated Prime Minister Narendra Modi on being conferred with Indonesia’s highest civilian honour, the ‘Bintang Adipurna of the Republic of Indonesia’. In a social media post, Mr. Singh said this exceptional honour is a resounding recognition of Prime Minister Modi’s visionary leadership, transformative statesmanship and steadfast commitment to advancing India’s national interests.

External Affairs Minister Dr S Jaishankar also said that the the conferment of ‘Bintang Adipurna of the Republic of Indonesia’ Medal of Honour upon Prime Minister Narendra Modi is a matter of immense pride for all Indians. In a social media post, Dr Jaishankar said, the conferment is a testament to Prime Minister Modi’s steadfast efforts towards deepening India’s Comprehensive Strategic Partnership, as well as stronger India – ASEAN ties.

On July 26, 2008, a series of 21 bomb blasts ripped through Ahmedabad in a span of 70 minutes, killing 56 people and injuring 200 others. Some hospitals in the city were also targeted. A special court in 2022 sentenced 38 Indian Mujahideen members to death. The court had also sentenced 11 other convicts to life imprisonment.
