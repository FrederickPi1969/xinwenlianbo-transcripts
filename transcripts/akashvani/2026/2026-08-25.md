## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has called upon young diplomats to build strong grassroots-level engagement in their countries of posting and deepen India’s connection with local communities. Interacting with the Officer Trainees of the 2025 Batch of Indian Foreign Service (IFS) at Seva Teerth, Mr Modi encouraged the Officer Trainees to expand their networks beyond traditional diplomatic circles and deepen engagement with people.

“Mr Modi  encouraged the officer trainees to combine India’s civilisational strengths with technology, innovation and effective communication to further strengthen India’s global engagement. The Prime Minister also called upon the Officer Trainees to serve as catalytic agents for country’s economic engagement with the world. He also emphasised the importance of strengthening the digital presence of Indian Missions. Mr Modi urged the Officer Trainees to work towards making India a more attractive global tourism destination by showcasing its civilisational, cultural and geographical strengths. Union Home Minister Amit Shah has directed all agencies to take strict measures against infiltration. Inaugurating the 9th National Security Strategies Conference-2026 in New Delhi, Mr. Shah directed that an AI-based analysis of information available on Multi-Agency Centre (MAC) platforms should be done to create SOPs to counter various national security challenges. To effectively tackle terrorism and radicalisation, Mr. Shah emphasised training for implementation of the Home Ministry’s PRAHAAR scheme, expediting trials by bringing back fugitives and utilising trial in absentia provisions, and undertaking a comprehensive and integrated approach while investigating UAPA cases.

“On the first day of the Conference, discussions focused on critical aspects of national security, and document on narcotics control, detection and deportation of illegal migrants, a policy framework to combat cybercrime, and proxy warfare by Pakistan. The two-day conference is being held in a hybrid format and is being attended by over 850 participants from across the country. On the second day today, the Conference will focus on the threat landscape from sub-aerial systems and formulating countermeasures, securing the blue economy, information warfare and its implications on national security, and the need for a biosecurity framework. WITH ANUPAM, SANYUKTA YADAV, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

The Unified Payments Interface (UPI) has completed ten years of transforming digital payments and has emerged as the backbone of India’s digital payments ecosystem. The UPI, which was launched on 25th August 2016, has surged almost 13 thousand fold in transaction volume.

“The Finance Ministry said that the annual transaction volume expanded from just 1.78 crore transactions in 2016-17 to over 24 thousand 162 crore transactions in 2025-26. The transaction value also rose sharply from seven thousand crore to nearly 314 lakh crore during the same time period. The Ministry said that UPI has also emerged as a global platform for cross-border digital payments and is currently operational in 11 countries. These are United Arab Emirates, France, Bhutan, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Singapore, Mauritius, Qatar, Cambodia, Greece and Maldives. UPI has emerged as one of the most successful pillars of India’s Digital Public Infrastructure, providing an interoperable, real-time payments platform. VISHAL KUMAR, AKASHVANI, NEW DELHI  ”

The government has lifted export restrictions on wheat flour and related products, opening the way for overseas shipments of atta, maida, semolina and other wheat-based products. According to the notification issued by the Directorate General of Foreign Trade, the government has amended the export policy for wheat flour and related products, with immediate effect. In a separate notification issued by DGFT, the government has also changed the export policy for specified wheat and durum wheat categories from prohibited to free.

The flood situation has worsened in Patna and several other districts of Bihar due to the rising levels of the Ganga and other major rivers. With the Ganga continuing to swell, breaches in ring embankments in Bhagalpur and Begusarai districts have affected hundreds of people.

“The rising waters of the Ganga and its tributaries have led to a breach in the ring embankment at Raghopur in the Naugachhia subdivision of Bhagalpur district, causing floodwaters to spread into the village. In Begusarai district, a nearly thirty-foot breach in the ring embankment near Jhamtia village has affected several panchayats. Teams of the Water Resources Department are engaged in repairing the embankments. Meanwhile, Chief Minister Samrat Chaudhary conducted an aerial survey of the flood-affected districts to assess the situation. He directed the administration to carry out relief and rescue operations with utmost promptness. Amid the rising water level of the Ganga, the West Bengal government has opened all the gates of the Farakka Barrage. Savita Pareek, Akashvani News, Patna.”

Rain activity in Odisha is likely to ease from today, though the impact of the active low-pressure system over the Bay of Bengal continues to be felt in several parts of the state. North coastal districts may receive moderate rainfall, while flood-affected areas remain under close watch as river levels respond to the recent spell of rain.

“The low-pressure area over the Bay of Bengal remains active and is continuing to influence weather conditions over Odisha. Moderate rain is likely in several areas, with north coastal districts expected to receive rainfall. However, the IMD says rainfall activity will decrease from today, though rain is likely to continue till the 30th of August. A Yellow Warning has been issued for 16 districts. Meanwhile, the Baitarani at Akhuapada is still flowing above the danger mark, though the water level is showing a declining trend. Prakash Dash for Akashvani News, Bhubaneswar.”
