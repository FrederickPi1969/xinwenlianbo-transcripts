## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has stressed the need for full utilisation of India’s diverse climate conditions to increase productivity and export strength. Mr Modi also emphasized on scaling up high-value crop produce to make the country’s agriculture products globally competitive. The Prime Minister was addressing a Post Budget Webinar on the theme Agriculture and Rural Transformation through video conferencing yesterday. He highlighted the shifting global demand and the necessity of making Indian agriculture sector export-oriented.

The Prime Minister said, today the world is becoming more conscious about health, with a strong focus on holistic healthcare and organic food. On the fisheries sector, Mr Modi said, India is the world’s second-largest fish producer. He highlighted that while approximately 4.5 lakh tonnes of fish are currently produced in various reservoirs and ponds, there exists a potential for an additional 20 lakh tonnes of production.

The Prime Minister also spoke on the technology culture in agriculture, referencing e-NAM and the development of digital public infrastructure.

Union Home and Cooperation Minister Amit Shah inaugurated and laid the foundation stone for development projects worth over 3,770 crore rupees in Odisha yesterday. These include development initiatives of the National Dairy Development Board, the Odisha State Cooperative Milk Producers’ Federation as well as various projects of the state government. Mr Shah also inaugurated Sulphuric Acid Plant-III at Paradip unit of Indian Farmers Fertiliser Cooperative Limited.
Addressing a gathering at the Cooperative Development Seminar in Bhubaneswar, Mr Shah said the projects launched during the day mark an important step in the development of Odisha. He said the sulphuric acid plant at Paradip will help strengthen domestic production and contribute to making the country self-reliant in the chemical.

Referring to internal security, Mr Shah said the Centre has intensified efforts to eliminate Left-wing extremism and expressed confidence that the country will be free from Naxalism by the end of this month.

The Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) has granted the Aerodrome License to Yamuna International Airport Private Limited for the Noida International Airport at Jewar in Gautam Budh Nagar of Uttar Pradesh. The airport has been licensed under the Public Use category for all-weather operations. The Ministry of Civil Aviation informed that the airport is being developed in four phases along with a multi-modal cargo hub. Upon completion of all phases, the Noida International Airport will be capable of handling up to 70 million passengers per year.

Civil Aviation Minister K Rammohan Naidu congratulated the Uttar Pradesh government, stating that the development of Noida International Airport is a major step toward strengthening aviation connectivity for the National Capital Region and Western Uttar Pradesh.

Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal highlighted the rapid rise of India’s startup ecosystem in the defence and space sectors. The Minister stressed that this transformation is being powered by the country’s MSMEs, which are integrating Indian innovation into global supply chains. Mr Goyal was addressing participants of the 66th cohort of the National Defence College in New Delhi on the theme ‘Boosting India’s Manufacturing Sector: Role of Government and Policy’.

President Droupadi Murmu will be on a day-long visit to Darjeeling in West Bengal today. During her visit, the President will grace the 9th International Santal Conference, being organised by the International Santal Council.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that regions connected through air connectivity experience faster and broader development. In a video message on the occasion of the groundbreaking ceremony of the new Kota-Bundi Greenfield Airport today, the Prime Minister said that before 2014, India had around 70 airports, but their number has now increased to more than 160. He also noted that several airports have now been developed around Delhi. Mr. Modi said that when airports are built, they encourage entrepreneurship and tourism in nearby smaller towns and accelerate economic activities in the region.

Speaking at the event, Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla said that there had been a demand for a Greenfield Airport in Kota for several decades, and now that dream is becoming a reality. He said Kota already has strong road and rail connectivity, along with adequate power and water resources. With the introduction of air connectivity, new avenues of development will open up.
