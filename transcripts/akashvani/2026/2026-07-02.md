## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will hold Annual Summit level Talks with his Japanese counterpart Sanae Takaichi in New Delhi today. The Summit will provide an opportunity for both sides to review and strengthen the full spectrum of bilateral cooperation as well as exchange views on regional and global issues of mutual interest. Several MoUs are expected to be exchanged following the talks between Prime Minister Modi and Prime Minister Takaichi.

The Japanese Prime Minister arrived in New Delhi last evening on a three day visit to India. This is the first official visit of Prime Minister Takaichi to India. The External Affairs Ministry said that the visit marks an important step in taking forward the Special Strategic and Global Partnership between India and Japan. Our Correspondent reports that Economic Security has emerged as a key pillar of the relationship.

“During the 1st Economic Security Dialogue held in Tokyo in November 2024, the two sides identified five sectors as priority sectors for strategic collaboration. These are semiconductors, critical minerals, pharmaceuticals, clean energy and information and communication technology. The India-Japan relationship is marked by frequent high-level political engagement. Most recently, Prime Minister Narendra Modi interacted with the Japanese Prime Minister during the G7 Summit in France. The two leaders had earlier met on the sidelines of the G20 Summit in Johannesburg in November last year. The Annual Summit remains the flagship platform driving the strategic agenda of the partnership. An Action Plan for Japan-India Human Resource Exchange and Cooperation was launched during the Annual Summit in 2025, which envisages two-way exchange of five lakh personnel in 5 years, including 50 thousand skilled and semi-skilled personnel from India to Japan. Apart from annual summits, high-level exchanges and meetings regularly take place at Foreign Minister, Defense Minister, NSA, Foreign Secretary and other levels. Suparna Saikia, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said he is looking forward to the wide-ranging discussions with Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi that will further deepen India-Japan Special Strategic and Global Partnership. In a social media post, Mr Modi said he is delighted to host her on her first visit to India. Prime Minister Modi said that through their joint efforts, India and Japan will continue to advance peace, stability and prosperity in the Indo-Pacific and beyond.

Employees’ Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) has officially notified an 8.25 percent interest rate on EPF deposits for financial year 2025-26. The ratings have been declared following approval of the Ministry of Labour and Employment as per the EPF Scheme, 1952. It has directed Regional and Zonal Offices to credit the approved interest to members’ EPF accounts.

The Government issued a notice to Meta regarding roll out of the username feature on Whatsapp in India. According to the Sources the Ministry of Electronics and Information and Technology has directed Meta to furnish a detailed explanation on the usernames feature within three days. More from our Correspondent:

“The Sources said that the Ministry has taken note of a public announcement by Whatsapp that it has commenced a phased global roll out, including in India, of its usernames feature. It said that once the feature is enabled, the recipient’s phone number will no longer be visible to a first time contact and that an optional username key may be set as an additional control. Sources said there are concerns that the feature may increase incidence of online frauds, phishing, digital arrest scams and impersonation attacks. Concerns have also been raised that the feature may facilitate impersonation and identity spoofing. The sources further said that the Ministry has directed Meta to furnish a detailed explanation on this feature within three days and not roll out the feature until consultation on the matter is over. SAKLEN AKHTAR, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.

The Delhi Government has notified a permanent Winter Pollution Master Plan that will remain in force every year from 1st November to 28th February to curb seasonal air pollution in the national capital. We have a report:

“Chief Minister Rekha Gupta said the new framework consolidates existing pollution-control measures into a single set of rules, ensuring timely implementation without issuing separate orders every winter.

Vehicles without a valid Pollution Under Control certificate will not be supplied fuel at petrol pumps, throughout the year. From 1st November to 31st January, entry of non-Delhi registered vehicles below BS-VI emission standards will also be prohibited, ambulances, fire services and other essential vehicles. The government has also announced that parking charges at authorised parking facilities will be doubled from 1st November to 28th February, while 50 per cent of employees in government and private offices will work from home from 1st November to 31st January.

Demolition work and open dust-generating construction activities will remain prohibited from 1st November to 31st January, except for essential public infrastructure projects. Large commercial buildings and major construction sites will be required to install anti-smog guns. WITH SUPARNA AND ARZOO, CHIRAG JHA FROM NEWS DESK.”

The Government has given one year extension to Foreign Secretary Vikram Misri till 14th of July next year. Mr Misri assumed charge as Foreign Secretary on 15th July 2024. He was India’s Ambassador to China from January 2019 to December 2021.

Jammu and Kashmir Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha flagged off the first batch of pilgrims of the annual Shri Amarnath Ji Yatra from the Bhagwati Nagar base camp in Jammu this morning. The 57-day annual pilgrimage officially commences across the twin tracks of Kashmir tomorrow and is scheduled to conclude on August 28. The first convoy of pilgrims was dispatched from Jammu early in the morning under tight security. The Yatra takes place via the traditional 48-km Nunwan-Pahalgam route in Anantnag district and the shorter 14-km Baltal route in Ganderbal district.

The Centre has assured full support to Assam in dealing with the recent flood situation. The assurance was given by Union Minister for Agriculture and Rural Development Shivraj Singh Chouhan and Union Minister for Parliamentary Affairs Kiren Rijiju during a flood review meeting with the Assam Government in Guwahati last evening. The Union Ministers reviewed the latest flood situation in Assam, including in the worst-affected district of Dhemaji and assessed the preliminary extent of the damage. Assam Chief Secretary Ravi Kota informed the Union Ministers that 13 districts have been affected by this year’s first major flood, impacting around 72,000 people.

Met department has also forecast heavy to very heavy rainfall over Coastal Karnataka, Gujarat, Konkan, Goa and Madhya Maharashtra during the next 2 days.

People in Delhi woke up to refreshing showers this morning. Rain lashed several parts of the city, bringing much-needed relief from the sweltering heat.

Several districts in the Sagar and Bhopal divisions received heavy rainfall yesterday. Waterlogging was reported from many parts of Indore and Mhow, where some vehicles were stranded in flooded areas. The indirect talks between Iran and the US through mediator concluded in Doha yesterday. According to a statement by the Qatari government, both sides agreed to continue discussions after the funeral ceremonies for Iran’s former Supreme Leader, Ali Khamenei on 9th of this month.

Talking to media, US Vice President JD Vance said the Doha negotiations were progressing well, with technical-level discussions already underway. He said the next phase would focus on Iran’s nuclear programme and added that the talks were being held from a position of strength after recent US military action.

A series of strong explosions hit Kyiv overnight after President Volodymyr Zelensky warned that Russia was preparing a major strike. Ukrainian officials said Russia fired missiles and drones at the capital, causing fires and damage to residential buildings. Air raid sirens sounded across the city, and many residents took shelter in metro stations.

In the FIFA World Cup, Belgium staged a stunning comeback to beat Senegal 3-2 after extra time Round of 32 and qualify for the Round of 16. Youri Tielemans scored a 125th-minute penalty to seal a dramatic 3-2 victory and extend Belgium’s unbeaten run to 17 matches.

Co-hosts US registered their first win in the knockout round after 2002 this morning. They defeated Bosnia and Herzegovina 2-nil in the Round of 32 to advance into the round of 16, wherein they will take on Belgium.

Last night, England defeated the Democratic Republic of the Congo 2-1 in Atlanta, with Harry Kane scoring twice in the final 15 minutes. England will face co-hosts Mexico in the Round of 16 on July 6.

In another Round 32 clash Mexico beat Ecuador 2-0 in Mexico City to reach the Round of 16. France defeated Sweden 3-0 in New York, while Norway edged Ivory Coast 2-1 in Texas to book their place in the last 16.

Opting to bat first, India scored 189 for 7 in stipulated 20 overs, with skipper Shreyas Iyer scoring highest 68 runs off 47 balls. Opening batter Abhishek Sharma also scored 59 runs, while Shivam Dubey remained not out at 42 runs. For England, Saqib Mahmood bagged 3 wickets.

India and Japan today exchanged several agreements in different fields including economic and energy security, pharmaceuticals, defence and AI. Prime Minister Narendra Modi held Annual Summit level Talks with his Japanese counterpart Sanae Takaichi in New Delhi this afternoon.

Addressing the Joint Press Meet following the talks, Prime Minister Modi said mutual trust is the biggest asset in today’s time and the India and Japan bilateral partnership has stood the test of time. He highlighted that India and Japan are among the world’s biggest economies today and a free and open Indo Pacific is a shared priority for both countries.

He stressed that both nations understand the importance of economic security and energy security. He added that technology partnership will be the most important pillar of India-Japan cooperation. Mr Modi said that to realize this vision, India and Japan have issued a Joint Statement in the field of AI.

The Prime Minister also informed that in the field of Defence today, both nations have signed an agreement on the first co-development project between India and Japan.

The Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Aajeevika Mission (Gramin) [VB-G RAM G] Act, 2025 marks a major step towards ensuring employment, strengthening rural livelihoods and accelerating the development of villages across the country, Union Minister for Rural Development and Agriculture & Farmers’ Welfare Shivraj Singh Chouhan has said.

Union Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan, along with Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu, today launched the VB-G RAM G Act at Mukkavaripalle village in Tirupati district of Andhra Pradesh.

Addressing the gathering, Mr. Chouhan said the scheme aims to ensure that no poor labourer remains without work and that every hand gets employment. He thanked Prime Minister Narendra Modi for introducing the scheme and said employment under the programme has been enhanced from 100 days to 125 days.

Union Environment, Forest and Climate Change Minister Bhupender Yadav today underlined that the government is promoting ease of living while ensuring environmental protection through policies that balance development with sustainability. Addressing a National Conference on Electric Mobility, Mr Yadav said the government’s approach reflects Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s vision of ensuring that ecology and economy grow together. He highlighted that despite rapid industrial development, the country’s forest cover has increased from seven lakh one thousand 673 square kilometres in 2014 to seven lakh 15 thousand square kilometres.

The Election Commission today announced the schedule for bye-elections to three Legislative Assembly constituencies across Bankipur in Bihar, Datia in Madhya Pradesh, and Manjalpur in Gujarat. The polling will take place on 30th of this month, and the counting of votes will be conducted on 3rd of next month.

The National Rainfed Area Authority-funded wild rice conservation project has achieved a major milestone in Assam. The Borjuli site in Sonitpur district has been notified as a Biodiversity Heritage Site by the National Biodiversity Authority under the project.

The project, titled In-situ Conservation and Management of Wild Rice (Oryza rufipogon) in Sonitpur District of Assam, aims to protect wild rice in its natural habitat. The recognition is an important step towards conserving India’s rich wild rice diversity and promoting climate-resilient agriculture.

Minister of State for Home Nityanand Rai today attended the flag-in-ceremony marking the success of the Mount Everest and Mount Lhotse mountaineering expeditions by the Border Security Force, Indo Tibetan Border Police and National Security Guard in New Delhi.

Speaking on the occasion, the Minister said that by conquering the world’s highest peak and singing Vande Mataram to the skies, the all women mountaineering team of BSF  has set a rare example of grit, patriotism and dedication.

India’s long-standing proposal for the Comprehensive Convention on International Terrorism has received renewed support at the United Nations General Assembly. The Ninth Review of the UN Global Counter-Terrorism Strategy, adopted by 140 votes, has urged member states to make every effort to finalise the convention proposed by India over three decades ago. Addressing the UN General Assembly, India’s Permanent Representative to the UN, P. Harish, said the absence of a universally agreed legal framework weakens global counter-terrorism efforts. He stressed that there should be no double standards in tackling terrorism and called for holding perpetrators, financiers and sponsors accountable. He added that “A terrorist is a terrorist,” and emphasised that the counter-terrorism efforts should not be weakened by false equivalences or politicised narratives.

The first batch of 4,822 pilgrims for the annual Shri Amarnath Ji Yatra was flagged off by the Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha from the Bhagwati Nagar Yatri Niwas base camp in Jammu today amid elaborate security arrangements. Pilgrims from different parts of the country have been arriving in Jammu to take part in the sacred pilgrimage.
