## Morning News

Bihar’s Deputy Chief Minister and BJP leader Samrat Choudhary will take oath as new Chief Minister of the state this morning. The swearing-in ceremony is scheduled to take place at Lok Bhavan in Patna at 11 AM. He will be the first BJP leader to assume the top post in the state.
Yesterday, Mr. Choudhary met Governor Lieutenant General (Retired) Syed Ata Hasnain at Lok Bhawan and staked his claim to form the government. He was unanimously elected as leader during NDA legislature party meeting.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will inaugurate Sri Guru Bairavaikya Mandira at Srikshetra Adichunchanagiri mutt in Mandya, Karnataka today. After arrival, the Prime Minister will visit Jwala Peetha, where Lord Shiva was believed to be in penance. Thereafter, the Prime Minister will offer prayers at Sri Kala Bhairava temple before inaugurating the new mandira built as a tribute to the spiritual lineage of the mutt in the ancient Kalabhairaveshwara temple complex.

“Elaborate arrangements are made for the visit of the Prime Minister to Sri Kshetra Adichunchanagiri, a sacred place built on a hill in Nagamangala taluk. Guru Bhairavaikya Mandira to be inaugurated by PM is built like Konark Sun temple with architectural designs from Hoysala, Cholas, Chalukyas and Gangas. The Prime Minister will be felicitated with Mysuru Peta created with Mysuru and Benaras silk and a string of pearls and small pendants. This design of peta was worn by the erstwhile King Nalwadi Krishnaraja Wadiyar of Mysuru. Sudhindra, Akashvani news, Bengaluru.”

The death toll in boiler explosion incident in Singhanterai of Chhattisgarh has risen to 14. Prime Minister Narendra Modi has announced an ex-gratia of 2 lakh rupees from Prime Minister’s National Relief Fund to the next of the kin of each deceased and 50 thousand rupees to the injured.

“The explosion occurred when a boiler tube exploded at a Vedanta Ltd. power plant in Singhanterai village of Sakti district yesterday. Sakti Superintendent of Police said, the powerful blast occurred at around 2:30 PM in a tube supplying high-pressure steam from the boiler to the turbine causing severe burns to workers. Chief Minister Vishnu Deo Sai has also announced a compensation of 5 lakh rupees each to the kin of each deceased worker and 50 thousand rupees to those injured. He also ordered the Commissioner of Bilaspur division to conduct an inquiry into the tragedy. The district administration has already ordered a separate magisterial probe into the accident. JIGYASA PANDEY, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

The advance registration for the annual Shri Amarnath Ji Yatra will commence from today via online and offline modes across 554 designated bank branches in the country. We have a report:

“The registration and issuance of yatra permits will be conducted on a first-come, first-served basis, subject to a fixed daily quota for each route at every designated branch. The registration facility will be available at 554 branches of J&K Bank, Punjab National Bank, State Bank of India, and Yes Bank across the country. Radio Frequency Identification Device (RFID) will be issued to every registered pilgrim and service provider. Registered pilgrims will receive group accident insurance with the sum assured enhanced to Rs. 10 lakh per person. Pilgrims aged between 13 and 70 years will be eligible for registration, while women with more than six weeks of pregnancy will not be permitted to undertake the pilgrimage. Pilgrims will be required to submit a valid Compulsory Health Certificate (CHC), issued on or after April 8, 2026, by an authorised doctor or medical institution. N. Gulshan Raina, Akashvani News, Jammu.”

Around 250 people, including Rohingya refugees and Bangladeshi nationals, are feared missing after a trawler carrying them capsized in the Andaman Sea while reportedly attempting to reach Malaysia.

“The vessel reportedly departed from Teknaf in southern Bangladesh and sank amid rough seas, strong winds and overcrowded conditions. The incident is believed to have occurred on April 9, though details remain unclear. So far, only nine survivors have been rescued. According to the Bangladesh Coast Guard, they were spotted drifting at sea by a passing vessel and later brought to safety. Authorities said several of the rescued individuals are suspected of being involved in human trafficking. The UN agencies expressed deep concern over the tragedy, describing it as a stark reminder of the growing risks faced by Rohingya refugees undertaking dangerous sea journeys. They noted that prolonged displacement, deteriorating conditions in refugee camps, and limited access to education and livelihoods are pushing many to seek opportunities abroad through unsafe routes. Navalsang Parmar, Akashwani News, Dhaka.”

At the same time, Israel and Lebanon have opened their first direct talks in decades in Washington, focusing on border tensions and security arrangements in southern Lebanon. A three day special sitting of the extended Budget session of Parliament will begin tomorrow. The session which will continue till the 18th of this month, will primarily focus on Constitutional Amendments to the Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam. The government will introduce three amendment bills to pave the way for the operationalisation of the Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam Bill, which was passed in 2023, proposing 33 percent reservation for women in the Lok Sabha and State Assemblies. More from our correspondent:

“The special session has been convened  to amend the Constitution so that the women’s reservation Act  can be implemented in the 2029 Lok Sabha elections after carrying out the delimitation exercise. Few days ago,  Prime Minister Narendra Modi had also written to the floor leaders of the  Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha urging all the parties  to come together in one voice to pass  amendments in this regard. Mr. Modi emphasised that it is imperative that the 2029 Lok Sabha elections and Assembly elections are conducted with women’s reservation in place. Parliamentary Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju has also assured that Women Reservation Bill is balanced and takes care of  the aspirations of every state, community and region.

Several political parties  including BJP, JD(U), LJP(R)  and Congress have issued three line whip to all its MPs of both the Houses to remain present during the proceedings of the House and support their party stand. With Anand Kumar, Suparna Saikia, Akashvani News, Delhi.”
