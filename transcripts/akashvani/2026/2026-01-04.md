The United Nations Security Council will hold an emergency meeting tomorrow to discuss the US military operation in Venezuela. The meeting has been convened at the request of Colombia, a non-permanent member of the Council, and was supported by permanent members — China and Russia. Venezuela’s Permanent Mission to the United Nations has sent a letter to the Security Council seeking the emergency session, criticising the US-action.

UN Secretary-General has described the US military action in Venezuela that led to the capture of President Nicolás Maduro as a dangerous precedent. In a statement, UN Spokesperson Stéphane Dujarric said Secretary-General António Guterres is deeply alarmed and worried that the operation could have serious implications for the region. The statement added that Mr Guterres is deeply concerned that the rules of international law have not been respected. He emphasized the importance of full respect for international law, including the UN Charter, and expressed concern that these rules may not have been followed.

Moreover, the United Nations High Commissioner for Human Rights Volker Türk said that he is alarmed by U.S. intervention in Venezuela.

Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro and his wife, First Lady Cilia Flores, arrived in New York and were deported to a Detention Center in Brooklyn. They are set to face drugs and weapons charges in Manhattan federal court. The pair have been charged with conspiracy to commit narco-terrorism and import cocaine and conspiracy to possess machine guns and destructive devices against the US.

Earlier, US President Donald Trump asserted that an extraordinary military operation was carried out in Venezuela’s capital, Caracas, under his direction and its leader, President Nicolas Maduro, who has been, along with his wife, captured and flown out of the Country. Speaking at a press conference at his Mar-a-Lago residence in Florida, President Trump said the United States is going to run Venezuela until what he described as a safe, proper and judicious transition of power takes place.

The US President also alleged that Venezuela is a major source of illegal drugs entering the United States and claimed that US actions have significantly reduced maritime drug trafficking. He said US will help rebuild Venezuela’s oil and infrastructure sectors.

The Constitutional Chamber of Venezuela’s Supreme Court ordered that Vice President Delcy Rodríguez to assume the role of acting president of the country. The decision was taken after Nicolás Maduro was detained in an operation by U.S. forces. The court ruling said that Rodríguez would assume the office in order to guarantee administrative continuity and the comprehensive defence of the Nation.

Meanwhile, the World leaders have issued mixed reactions following the capture of Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro and his wife by US forces. Several countries, including Russia, China, Iran, Brazil and Cuba, have condemned the US military action, while France and Italy advocated for power transition in Venezuela.

Prime Minister’s flagship platform for Pro-Active Governance and Timely Implementation, PRAGATI, has marked a significant milestone with the successful conduct of its 50th meeting. Since its launch by Prime Minister Narendra Modi in 2015, PRAGATI has transformed governance by enabling real-time monitoring and resolution of key infrastructure projects and public grievances through direct Prime Ministerial review. Today, in this special series, we take a look at the Lumding-Silchar Broad Gauge Conversion Project.

“The Lumding-Silchar Broad Gauge Railway Line is a strategically critical rail infrastructure corridor serving southern Assam and providing essential rail connectivity to the hilly States of Tripura, Manipur, and Mizoram. The line functions as a vital lifeline for the region, supporting the movement of foodgrains, fertilisers, petroleum products, construction materials, and other essential commodities. The Lumding-Silchar Gauge Conversion Project was sanctioned in 1996-97 and was subsequently declared a National Project in 2004. Despite its importance, the project faced prolonged implementation challenges in its initial years due to constrained fund flow, difficult terrain, and insurgency-related disruptions during 2006 to 2009. The project was reviewed by Prime Minister Narendra Modi under the PRAGATI mechanism on 25th March 2015, at which time physical progress was minimal. Following the Prime Minister’s guidance, the project received focused attention, including enhanced inter-agency coordination, close monitoring, and time-bound execution. The Phase-1 of the project achieved a major milestone with the completion of Broad Gauge works and opening for goods traffic in March 2015, followed shortly by passenger services. After this, Phase-II was completed, culminating in the commissioning of the entire corridor on 1st March 2017. With this, seamless Broad-Gauge connectivity was established across the corridor, fully integrating the Barak Valley and the adjoining North-Eastern States with the national railway network. The project reflects the impact of high-level monitoring through PRAGATI, reaffirming the Government’s resolve to strengthen connectivity and promote inclusive development across the North-Eastern Region. With Anand Kumar, Suparna Saikia, Akashvani News, Delhi

Rural Development Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan today said that MGNREGA had become a synonym for corruption, where workers were often sidelined and routinely exploited. While highlighting the shortcomings of the previous Act, the Minister said that Viksit Bharat G-RAM-G safeguards workers’ interest. He noted that provisions of increased pay and unemployment wages, interest on delayed payment, and employment guarantee of 125 days from the earlier 100 days, ensure that not only workers remain better off but they also bring meaningful contribution to their villages.

He highlighted the vision of Viksit Bharat through Viksit Gaon and said funding for rural employment has been significantly increased to support sustainable outcomes.
