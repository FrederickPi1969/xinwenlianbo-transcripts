## Midday News

India and the United States have signed the Pax Silica declaration at the India AI Impact Summit in New Delhi today. This declaration will strengthen collaboration in areas like the production of critical minerals and semiconductors, and in the rapidly evolving Artificial Intelligence space, to create a safe and strong global silicon and technology ecosystem. Presiding over the signing ceremony, Union Minister for Electronics and Information Technology Ashwini Vaishnaw has said that the goal of India is to take a leadership role in Semiconductor and Electronics industries. He added that the Semiconductor Industry requires more than 10 lakh talented pool and that will be fulfilled within the country.  He also informed that students from 315 Universities and colleges are designing chips.

At the signing ceremony US Ambassador to India Sergio Gor said that from the trade deal to Pax Silica to defence cooperation, the potential for India and the US to work together is truly limitless. Mr Gor noted that ‘Pax Silica’ aims to replace coercive dependencies with trusted industrial partnerships that empower free markets.

Our correspondent reports that the joining of India in the Pax Silica coalition marks a significant milestone in the strengthening of strategic technology and supply chain cooperation between India and the US. More from our correspondent :

Pax Silica is envisioned as a strategic coalition of trusted nations committed to securing the “silicon stack”, from critical minerals and semiconductor fabrication to advanced AI systems and deployment infrastructure. The initiative seeks to reduce overconcentration in global supply chains, prevent economic coercion, and ensure that emerging technologies are developed and governed by open, democratic societies. Pax Silica is US administration’s initiative to secure supply chains for silicon-based technologies. It was initially launched in December last year with eight members including Japan, South Korea, Singapore, Netherlands, United Kingdom, Israel, United Arab Emirates, and Australia. With Vishnu, Priya Mondal, Akashvani News, Delhi.

The Delhi High Court today said presence of a public urinal and open garbage bin right next to one’s house violates the right to live with dignity in a clean and healthy environment. Justice Amit Bansal stated that a hygienic environment was an integral aspect of a healthy life and the absence of a healthy environment frustrates the right to live with dignity.

The court passed the order while dealing with a lawyer’s petition raising a grievance against the construction of an unauthorised open garbage bin and urinal on the walls of his property.

The court directed the MCD to immediately demolish the open dustbin and the urinal next to the house of the petitioner, observing that the presence of an open garbage bin as well as public urinal was undoubtedly a nuisance.
