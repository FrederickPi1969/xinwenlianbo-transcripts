## Evening News

Parliament has passed the National Co-operative Development Corporation (Amendment) Bill, 2026 with the Rajya Sabha approving it today. Lok Sabha has already passed the Bill. Minister of State for Cooperation Murlidhar Mohol moved the bill, which aims to further amend the National Co-operative Development Corporation Act, 1962. The Bill allows the Corporation to undertake its activities in industrial goods. The legislation also allows the state governments to extend funding from the Corporation to any entity engaged in co-operative development. The Corporation can also provide loans and grants directly to any co-operative society or entity engaged in co-operative development.

The Parliament has also passed the Kerala (Alteration of Name) Bill, 2026, with the Rajya Sabha approving it today. The Lok Sabha had passed the legislation yesterday. The Bill seeks to change the name of the state of Kerala to Keralam in the First Schedule of the Constitution.

Replying to a debate on the Bill, Minister of State for Home Nityanand Rai said the Kerala Legislative Assembly had passed a resolution in 2024, requesting the Centre to change the name of the State from Kerala to Keralam. He said the request in this regard was made because the name of the State in Malayalam is Keralam, while in the Constitution, it is written as Kerala.

“Both the Houses of Parliament once again witnessed disruption following opposition uproar on various issues. Due to the ruckus, the Question Hour in both the Houses could not be taken up today. Lok Sabha could not take up the Zero Hour as well, while the Rajya Sabha took it up for a brief period. Despite government’s assurance that it is ready for a detailed discussion and reply on the issues raised, the opposition parties, including Congress, continued with their sloganeering. Post Lunch, the Rajya Sabha cleared the National Co-operative Development Corporation (Amendment) Bill, 2026 and the Kerala (Alteration of Name) Bill, 2026. Both Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha today gave their approval for referring the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Amendment Bill, 2026, to the Joint Parliamentary Committee for further scrutiny. ANUPAM MISHRA, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

The Lok Sabha has passed the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Amendment Bill, 2026. When the House met at 3 PM after the second adjournment, Union Coal and Mines Minister G Kishan Reddy moved the Bill to further amend the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957. The bill aims to make India’s mining sector more efficient, investment-friendly and well-equipped to fulfil the country’s growing demand for critical minerals. Following the passage of the bill, the Lower House was adjourned for the day.

Both Houses of Parliament today gave their approval for referring the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Amendment Bill, 2026, to the Joint Parliamentary Committee for further scrutiny. The bill aims to further amend the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act, 2010. The objective of the Bill is to make the use of foreign contributions more transparent and accountable.

Minister of State for Home Nityanand Rai moved a motion in the Lok Sabha to refer the Bill to a JPC comprising members of both the Houses for wider consultations. The committee will consist of 21 members from the Lok Sabha, nominated by the Speaker, and 10 members from the Rajya Sabha, nominated by the Chairman. The Committee will submit its report to this House by the last day of the first week of the winter session.   The Rajya Sabha also approved the motion to nominate 10 members from its side to the Joint Committee for consideration of the FCRA Amendment Bill, 2026.

The Report of the Inquiry Committee investigating allegations concerning former Allahabad High Court Judge Yashwant Varma was tabled in both the Houses of Parliament. The Judges Inquiry Committee, investigating allegations concerning Yashwant Varma, had submitted its report to the Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla in May this year. The Committee was constituted by Mr Birla on the 12th of August last year to probe the allegations levelled against Justice Varma, after a sum of burnt cash was found at his official residence in New Delhi.
