Iran is witnessing its most serious wave of unrest in three years as nationwide protests entered the tenth day. Security forces have tightened their presence across major cities, even as U.S. President Donald Trump warned that he could side with demonstrators if violence escalates. The protests were triggered by the sharp collapse of the Iranian rial and surging inflation, and have spread from Tehran’s Grand Bazaar to more than two hundred and twenty locations across at least seventy-eight cities in up to thirty-one provinces.

Iranian officials have acknowledged that the U.S. warning, reinforced by Washington’s recent seizure of Venezuelan leader Nicolás Maduro, has complicated efforts to contain the demonstrations. Reports say Iran’s Supreme National Security Council held an emergency meeting to review security tactics and contingency plans. While President Masoud Pezeshkian has called for restraint and dialogue, Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei has urged firm action against ‘rioters’.

Prime Minister’s flagship platform for Pro-Active Governance and Timely Implementation, PRAGATI, has marked a significant milestone with the successful conduct of its 50th meeting. Since its launch by Prime Minister Narendra Modi in 2015, PRAGATI has transformed governance by enabling real-time monitoring and resolution of key infrastructure projects and public grievances through direct Prime Ministerial review. Today, in this special series, we take a look at Mission Amrit Sarovar, which aims to construct and rejuvenate water bodies across the country.

The Mission Amrit Sarovar, launched in 2022, is a flagship initiative to construct and rejuvenate 75 water bodies in each district across the country. Each Amrit Sarovar is designed to have a pondage area of minimum of one acre with a water holding capacity of about ten thousand cubic meters. The progress of Mission Amrit Sarovar was regularly reviewed at the national level through PRAGATI, bringing together senior officials from Central Ministries, State governments, and district administrations on a single digital platform. This has enabled faster decision-making and effective resolution of issues such as land availability, inter-departmental coordination, and release of funds. Joint Secretary in the Department of Rural Development, Rohini Bhajibhakare, said that reviews of projects through PRAGATI by Prime Minister Narendra Modi significantly strengthen coordination among all Ministries.

Under the scheme, the target was to construct 50 thousand Amrit Sarovars by 15th August 2023. However, due to rapid progress following PRAGATI intervention, the work gained significant momentum, and over 68 thousand 800 Amrit Sarovars have been completed so far across the country. The Mission has made a significant contribution to addressing water scarcity and has played an important role in enhancing both surface and groundwater availability in various regions. With Dipendra Kumar, Suparna Saikia, Akashvani News.

The Madras High Court’s Madurai Division Bench today upheld a single judge’s order allowing lighting of a lamp claimed as Deepathoon on the Thirupparankundram hill. A Division Bench of Justices G Jayachandran, and KK Ramakrishnan delivered the judgment, making it clear that the spot on which the stone pillar Deepathoon is located belongs to the Lord Subramania Swamy Temple. The Bench said, the lamp may be lit and the number of persons permitted can be fixed, subject to consultation with the Archaeological Survey of India. Advocate and petitioner for Hindu Munnani, Niranjan S Kumar said, the Division bench has stated that the lighting of the lamp is a must, and it has to be lit at the top of the Thirupparankundram hill.

The Madurai Bench of the Madras High Court had earlier directed that the lamp be lit at the hilltop temple.

Names of a total of two crore eighty nine lakh voters have been excluded in the draft electoral roll for Uttar Pradesh released by the Election Commission today after the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) exercise in the state. Of these, 2.17 crore have moved and names of 25 lakh 47 thousands were registered in more than one place.

Even after nearly 13 years since commencement of construction, the Bogibeel Bridge project had achieved only 72 per cent physical progress while the cost escalated by five times. The Prime Minister Narendra Modi reviewed this project in PRAGATI in May 2015. Recognising its critical role in regional connectivity and national security, the Prime Minister directed that the project should be taken up in mission mode. It was only after Prime Minister Modi’s intervention, the pace of work accelerated leading to completion of the bridge on 25th December, 2018. Cabinet Secretary T V Somanathan said that PRAGATI has helped commissioning of critical projects like Bogibeel Rail cum Road Bridge over river Brahmaputra.

The Bogibeel Rail-cum-Road Bridge plays a critical role in strengthening national security and strategic preparedness in the eastern region by facilitating swift movement of defence forces and their equipment.
