## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi says, Economic Survey shows that India’s fast moving ‘Reform Express’ is on track despite global challenges. In a social media post, Mr Modi said, Economic Survey, which was presented in Parliament yesterday, showcases how India’s Reform Express is moving forward with consistency and purpose. Prime Minister said, the Survey reflects the strength of India’s economic foundations and the government’s continuous efforts to drive growth, innovation and inclusive development. He emphasised that sustained reforms over the years have helped strengthen the economy and build confidence among investors, businesses and citizens.

Country’s real GDP is estimated to grow by 7.4 per cent in the financial year 2026 and the growth is likely to range between 6.8 to 7.2 percent in the financial year 2027. This has been projected in the Economic Survey presented by the Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman in the Parliament. Briefing media on the Economic Survey, Chief Economic Advisor V Anantha Nageswaran has said real GDP growth was 6.5 per cent in Fiscal Year 2025 and this year it is projected at 7.4 per cent.

India’s innovation ecosystem has shown strong and steady progress over the past few years, with the country climbing to 38th position in the Global Innovation Index 2025 from 66th in 2019, Economic Survey said. The Survey highlights how government initiatives in manufacturing, research, startups and technology have helped India emerge as a growing global hub for innovation and industrial development.

India has also become an important global player in intellectual property. In 2024, the country ranked fourth in trademark filings, sixth in patents and seventh in industrial designs worldwide.

World Bank President Ajay Banga said India has demonstrated that it can show exceptional growth. Talking to media persons in Bhubaneswar, he said, India will absolutely achieve growth rate as projected in the Economic Survey. He said, the level of infrastructure investment that has happened in India in the last 10 years is quite admirable.

Mr Banga said, skilling population in India will be important to reach the goal of Viksit Bharat by 2047.

The International Monetary Fund has described the India-European Union Free Trade Agreement  as a positive development, noting that it reflects countries’ efforts to deepen economic integration amid rising global trade tensions and uncertainty. Responding to questions during a briefing on emerging market economies, IMF Economic Counsellor and Director of Research, Pierre-Olivier Gourinchas said, recent trade agreements show that governments are seeking pragmatic ways to expand trade ties even after a turbulent year for global commerce.

Prime Minister’s flagship platform for Pro-Active Governance and Timely Implementation, PRAGATI, has marked a significant milestone with the successful conduct of its 50th meeting. Since its launch by Prime Minister Narendra Modi in 2015, PRAGATI has transformed governance by enabling real-time monitoring and resolution of key infrastructure projects and public grievances through direct Prime Ministerial review. The platform exemplifies cooperative federalism, bringing together the Centre, States, and Union Ministries on a single digital interface. Over a decade, PRAGATI has helped accelerate decision-making, resolution of issues causing delay in key infrastructure projects, and instilled a strong culture of accountability. Today, in this special series, we take a look at the projects under Jal Shakti Ministry monitored by PRAGATI mechanism.

Ministry of Jal Shakti is responsible for the formulation and implementation of policies, programmes, and projects related to water resources management, drinking water supply, sanitation, and river rejuvenation. At present, 93 projects with a total investment of 2,86,343 crore rupees are being monitored on the Project Monitoring Group portal pertaining to the Department of Water Resources, River Development, and Ganga Rejuvenation. The project portfolio comprises 66 projects under Irrigation, 26 projects under Sewerage collection, disposal and treatment, and one project related to Water collection, treatment and supply on the PMG portal. Out of the 66 irrigation projects being monitored on the PMG portal, 10 priority projects with  investment of 43,583 crores have been reviewed by Prime Minister under the PRAGATI mechanism. Of these, one project has been commissioned, while nine projects are under implementation. The ongoing irrigation projects have demonstrated steady progress, with an average physical progress of around 70 percent, and two projects exceeding 95 percent physical progress. A total of 126 issues came up across 27 Irrigation projects. Of these, 106 issues have been resolved, including 14 issues pertaining to PRAGATI-reviewed projects. The remaining 20 issues are being actively monitored under the PRAGATI mechanism. Suparna Saikia, Akashvani News, Delhi.
