## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will attend the third-day of the mega air show, ‘Wings Over Ganges’, at the Namo Ghat in Varanasi today as the Chief Guest. The event is being organised to mark the 94th anniversary of the Indian Air Force. Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath and Defence Minister Rajnath Singh will also be present on the occasion.More from our correspondent:

“Prime Minister Narendra Modi will witness the Air Force Day Aerial Display-2026 at Namo Ghat in Varanasi today. The aerial display will feature aircraft including the Tejas, Light Combat Helicopter Prachand and Rafale, along with transport aircraft. Aircraft from the Indian Navy and the Indian Army will also participate, showcasing jointness in operations. The Indian Air Force’s premier display teams, including the Surya Kiran Aerobatic Team and the Sarang Helicopter Display Team, will also participate in the event. Before attending the air show, the Prime Minister will inaugurate a new garment manufacturing facility in Varanasi. The facility marks an important step towards strengthening India’s textile and apparel manufacturing ecosystem and promoting industrial growth in the region. Focusing on modern manufacturing practices, the facility will create meaningful employment opportunities, particularly for women, and contribute to the region’s economic progress. Prem Chandra Gupta, Akashvani News, Varanasi.”

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will inaugurate the Reserve Bank of India’s Data Centre and Cybersecurity Training Institute at Jatni, near Bhubaneswar, in Odisha this evening. Our correspondent reports that the facility is expected to strengthen data security and enhance the resilience of India’s digital financial infrastructure.

“Prime Minister Narendra Modi will inaugurate the Reserve Bank of India’s new Data Centre and Cybersecurity Training Institute at Info Valley-Two in Jatni, near Bhubaneswar, this evening. Built at an estimated cost of 170 crore rupees over an area of 18.55 acres, the facility is designed to strengthen data security, disaster recovery and the continuity of critical financial services. It will support the Reserve Bank of India’s important digital operations, including payment and settlement systems. Odisha is hosting the Reserve Bank of India’s second data centre in the country. The facility is expected to provide additional capacity for safeguarding critical financial data and improving country’s digital financial infrastructure. The accompanying Cybersecurity Training Institute will serve as a dedicated centre for specialised training, research and capacity building in enterprise computing and cybersecurity. Prakash Dash for Akashvani News, Bhubaneswar.”

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will share his thoughts with the people of the country and abroad in his monthly radio programme Mann Ki Baat on Akashvani at 11 AM today. It will be the 139th episode of Mann Ki Baat.

The programme will be broadcast across the entire Akashvani and Doordarshan network, the AIR News website, and the Newsonair mobile app. It will also be live-streamed on the YouTube channels of AIR News, DD News, the PMO, and the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting. Akashvani will broadcast the programme in regional languages immediately after the Hindi broadcast.

The fifth edition of Kashi Tamil Sangamam will be held from 28th November to 12th December this year. The programme will celebrate, reaffirm and strengthen the age-old civilizational bonds between Tamil Nadu and Kashi, the two ancient seats of learning, strengthening the spirit of Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat. The Ministry of Education said the theme of this edition is Thirukkural Payilvom – Thirukkural Abhyas Karen. More from our Correspondent:

“As part of the Sangamam, around one thousand four hundred participants from Tamil Nadu, divided into seven groups of approximately two hundred members each, will undertake an eight-day journey. The itinerary will include Varanasi, Prayagraj and Ayodhya and featuring cultural, academic, domain-specific and spiritual engagements. The emphasis on youth participation will enable a new generation to engage meaningfully with India’s classical knowledge traditions. Ministry of Education invites eligible applicants from Tamil Nadu, especially youth and students, to register for the programme and participate in this celebration of India’s cultural oneness, linguistic diversity and shared civilisational inheritance. Interested applicants can register through the official Kashi Tamil Sangamam portal, hosted by IIT Madras, at kashitamil.iitm.ac.in. AKSHIT vedyan, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

India has issued a fresh advisory for its nationals in Saudi Arabia, particularly the Umrah pilgrims, affected by ongoing flight cancellations and is coordinating with authorities of the Gulf Kingdom to facilitate their early return. The advisory comes amid escalating tensions in the region due to a series of drone and missile attacks targeting civilian infrastructure by Yemen’s Houthis. The Consulate General of India in Jeddah said its 24×7 Control Rooms in Jeddah and Makkah continued to provide necessary assistance to affected Indians, with support from community associations and volunteers. The mission also said it remained in regular contact with Saudi Arabia’s Ministry of Hajj and Umrah and Ministry of Foreign Affairs to address difficulties caused by the flight disruptions, particularly issues concerning visa validity and delayed departures.

Air India and Air India Express flights to and from Riyadh and Dammam will remain suspended until November 7. Indian Embassy in Saudi Arabia said, Air India and Air India Express flights to and from Jeddah will stay suspended until October 31.  The suspension comes amid continued attacks on civilian infrastructure in Saudi Arabia, including on airports in Riyadh and Dammam. Airline operator Indigo has also said that its flights to and from four Saudi Arabian cities will remain cancelled in view of the evolving security situation till 31st of this month.

The Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) said that the Indian Embassy and Consulate in Saudi Arabia continue to be actively engaged with concerned airlines and tour operators to facilitate the early return of Indian pilgrims who are stuck in the country. Addressing the media in New Delhi, MEA spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal said that about 3,000 stranded Umrah pilgrims have returned to India from Saudi Arabia over the past week and more than 10,000 normal Umrah pilgrims have also travelled back to India.

External Affairs Minister Dr. S. Jaishankar has said that India-Africa partnership is built on shared histories, common aspirations and Deep South-South solidarity. He said this during an interaction with the Heads of Mission from Africa in New Delhi yesterday. In a social media post, Dr. Jaishankar said they discussed various aspects of India-Africa partnership, the rise of Africa in a multipolar world, and new possibilities for people-centric cooperation.

During his visit, Prime Minister Modi and Prince Alois will hold a bilateral meeting to discuss cooperation in various areas, including trade and investment, science, technology and innovation and people-to-people exchanges, as well as exchange views on regional and global developments.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today said Mann Ki Baat has completed twelve years and that the journey has felt like a pilgrimage for him. Addressing the nation through his Mann Ki Baat programme on Akashvani, the Prime Minister noted that Mann Ki Baat began on the auspicious occasion of Vijayadashami twelve years ago. Since then, this journey has continued without a break. Mr. Modi added that this has been like a Teerth Yatra (pilgrimage trip) to closely understand the efforts of the people and to salute their contributions.

The Prime Minister noted that cleanliness drives are being run at many places ahead of festivals like Diwali and Chhath. He stressed the need to maintain cleanliness of the places where people live. He added that small, individual efforts can bring about significant change in the surroundings.

Mr Modi expressed happiness that on Gandhi Jayanti this year, sales of the products of Khadi Gram Udyog crossed 28 crore rupees in a single day. This is an increase of 1.5 crore rupees over last year. He said the popularity of Khadi continues to grow, and the benefits are reaching the people who bring Khadi and various handicrafts to life. Mr Modi urged people to prioritise products made by local artisans during the festive season. He added that this will further strengthen the ‘Vocal for Local’ movement and the dream of ‘Atmanirbhar Bharat’ will be realised sooner.

Speaking about the e- Sanjeevani scheme, Prime Minister Modi expressed satisfaction that approximately 50 crore tele-consultations have been conducted through this platform so far to make people’s lives easier.

Recalling his trip to Chennai earlier this month, Mr Modi said he met many of the nation’s scientists, researchers, and young talents. He said the nation’s youth are currently working on subjects that have the potential to transform the future of humanity. He cited ‘Brain Research’ as one such field.  Mr Modi had  inaugurated the ‘Sudha Gopalakrishnan Brain Centre’ at IIT Madras.

Touching upon the subject of organ donation, Prime Minister Modi said it plays a vital role in such advanced research. He gave the example of Shivani from Narmadapuram in Madhya Pradesh who had met with a road accident, and her family decided to donate her organs. Mr Modi praised her family, saying such a step of humanity can bring happiness back into a family’s life, while also providing significant support to research efforts.

The Prime Minister also shared some other experiences from his visit to Chennai. The first was his interaction with young people on the metro, while another was his visit to IIT Madras. Mr Modi highlighted that one of the start-ups he saw focuses on verifying the quality of gold, which can help prevent fraud in gold purchases. He added that another team is developing a platform that could enable the rapid recovery of funds lost to bank fraud. He further said that another team is exploring how AI can assist in the screening of cervical cancer. Mr Modi said that the interaction at IIT was entirely focused on ‘deep-tech.’ These start-ups are being supported by the incubation ecosystem at IIT Madras.
