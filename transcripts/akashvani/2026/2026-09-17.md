## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will inaugurate SEMICON India 2026 at Yashobhoomi in New Delhi this morning. Prime Minister will also address the gathering on the occasion. The three-day conference and exhibition, being held from today is being organised on the theme ‘Silicon to Systems: Building the Ecosystem’. It will bring together global and Indian companies, policymakers, investors, researchers, start-ups, academic institutions and students from across the semiconductor and electronics value chain. More from our correspondent:

“The conference will focus on advancing collaboration, investment, innovation and talent development in India. Parallel sessions will cover advancements in the display industry, six Country Roundtables designed to facilitate collaboration and joint ventures, a forum on women’s leadership, among other sessions. SEMICON India 2026 will adopt a comprehensive value-chain approach, showcasing the journey from semiconductor materials, equipment and chip design to fabrication, advanced packaging, electronics and systems. It will feature more than 600 participating companies and is expected to attract around 50 thousand visitors, with representatives from 52 countries, more than 400 executives and over 150 speakers. ANUPAM MISHRA, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

Bharatiya Janata Party will launch a month-long nationwide “Seva Sankalp Abhiyan” beginning today. The initiative is designed to commemorate Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s birthday today and celebrate his completion of 25 years in public service as a head of government. This includes his 13 years as Chief Minister of Gujarat and 12 years as Prime Minister of India.
To mark the occasion, a ‘Sewa Sankalp Abhiyan’ will be launched from his hometown Vadnagar in Gujarat today. Union Home Minister Amit Shah and Gujarat Chief Minister Bhupendra Patel will flag off a Vadnagar-to-Varanasi bike rally as part of the celebrations. A report:

“A series of welfare programmes will be held at PM’s birthplace, Vadnagar today on the occasion of his birthday. Union Home Minister Amit Shah and Chief Minister Bhupendra Patel will participate in a grand Maha Aarti at the historic Sharmishtha Lake, where sacred water will be collected in a kalash and handed over to bikers traveling to Varanasi. The dignitaries will lead a foot march from the lake to the Hatkeshwar Temple to offer prayers, followed by flagging off a rally of 250 bikers heading to Varanasi to spread the message of public service. Home Minister and Chief Minister will also lead a cleanliness drive at Vadnagar Railway Station. Aparna Khunt, Akashvani News, Ahmedabad.”

India has rejected the so-called Pakistan-China Boundary Joint Commission saying that Pakistan has no locus standi to enter into arrangements concerning Indian territory under its illegal and forcible occupation. Responding to media queries, Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal said, the Ministry has seen reports in this regard and India’s position on this matter is clear and consistent. He said, there is no boundary between Pakistan and China. Mr Jaiswal said, New Delhi rejects the Pakistan-China Boundary Joint Commission which is without any legal basis.

The Central Government has been organising Hyderabad Liberation Day celebrations since 2022. This year’s main programme will be held at the Secunderabad Parade Grounds with Vice President CP Radhakrishnan as the Chief Guest. Union Ministers Gajendra Singh Shekhawat, G. Kishan Reddy and Bandi Sanjay Kumar, along with several other dignitaries, will participate in the celebrations.

PM Vishwakarma Scheme has completed three years today. Launched in 2023 by Prime Minister Narendra Modi, the scheme has achieved the milestone of registering thirty lakh beneficiaries under it. The objective of the scheme is providing end-to-end support to traditional artisans and craftspeople engaged in eighteen identified trades. Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises will celebrate the third anniversary of the scheme in Kolkata today.

The US Federal Reserve has raised its benchmark interest rate for the first time since 2023 in an effort to lower persistent high inflation. The central bank also signaled that another rate hike could occur later this year. The quarter-point increase lifts the Fed’s key rate to about 3.9 percent and, over time, could result in higher borrowing costs for mortgages, auto loans, and credit cards. In a set of quarterly projections, the Fed also signaled that its rate-setting committee expects to hike rates a second time to 4.1 per cent.

In Unites States, the House of Representatives has passed a broad package of sanctions targeting Russian officials and key pillars of its economy. The move comes as lawmakers look to deprive President Vladimir Putin of the financial resources needed to wage the war against Ukraine. The bill is named after the late Senator Lindsey Graham of South Carolina, who spent more than a year negotiating it. The measure was approved in a 262 to 159 vote and now heads to President Donald Trump to be signed into law.

India’s cheetah population has risen to 52, including 32 cheetahs born in the country. More from our correspondent:

“Out of these 52 cheetahs, 49 are currently in Kuno National Park and three in Gandhi Sagar Sanctuary, both in Madhya Pradesh. This was stated in a report entitled “Four Years of Cheetahs in India – A Status Review” released by Kuno National Park. The report has been released on completion of four years of reintroduction of Cheetah in India. On this day in 2022, Prime Minister Narendra Modi released eight wild cheetahs from Namibia into special enclosures at Kuno National Park in Madhya Pradesh. Over the last four years, many important milestones have been achieved, ranging from the reintroduction of cheetahs to breeding, survival of cubs, and expansion in open forest. The next phase of the cheetah restoration programme involves expanding suitable habitat for cheetahs and establishing a comprehensive, long-term cheetah meta-population in India. Kaumudee Parik, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

In Men’s Cricket, India will play the final match of the three match T20 International series against Afghanistan in Delhi today. The match will begin at 7.30 PM Indian Standard Time.
India has already secured the series with convincing wins in the first two matches, while Afghanistan has only managed brief moments of resistance. Abhishek Sharma set the pace in the opening win, and Sanju Samson’s 22-ball 57 wrapped up the second. India’s attack has been equally dominant, boosted by the return of Jasprit Bumrah and Arshdeep Singh alongside strong overs from Nitish Kumar Reddy.

PM Vishwakarma Scheme has completed three years today. Prime Minister Narendra Modi had launched this scheme on this day in 2023 to provide end-to-end support to traditional artisans and craftspeople engaged in 18 identified trades. The Scheme provides a comprehensive package of support comprising recognition through PM Vishwakarma certificates and identity cards, skill upgradation, toolkit incentive, credit support, incentives for digital transactions and marketing support. More from our correspondent.

The PM Vishwakarma Scheme has achieved a significant milestone, with 30 lakh beneficiaries registered under the initiative. The Scheme seeks to strengthen the traditional skills of Vishwakarmas by providing them with institutional support, modern tools, access to finance, skill upgradation and market linkages and enabling them to enhance their income. Around 24 lakh 50 thousand Vishwakarmas have so far received Basic Skill Training through the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship. Under the credit component, more than six lakh Vishwakarmas have been provided loans of up to one lakh rupees at a concessional interest rate of five percent per annum. In addition, over 18 lakh modern toolkits have been delivered to Vishwakarmas at their doorsteps through India Post. With Dipendra Kumar Saklen Akhtar FOR Akashvani News Delhi.
