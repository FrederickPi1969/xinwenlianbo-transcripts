## Evening News

India and Uzbekistan today decided to elevate their bilateral relationship to Comprehensive Strategic Partnership. The two countries have also agreed to establish a long-term arrangement for the supply of uranium from Uzbekistan to India. Both sides have also set a target of increasing annual bilateral trade to five billion dollars by 2030.

The decisions were taken following delegation level talks between Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Uzbekistan President Shavkat Mirziyoyev in Tashkent. The two leaders witnessed the exchange of eleven Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs) aimed at expanding bilateral cooperation across key sectors, including mining, culture, education, tourism, economic and financial dialogue, and Ayurveda.

In his remarks during the talks, Prime Minister Modi said, a stronger India-Uzbekistan partnership would be a powerful force for peace, progress and prosperity in Central Asia.

Later, at a joint press conference with the Uzbekistan President following the talks, Prime Minister Modi said maintaining a zero tolerance policy towards terrorism, extremism and separatism will remain their shared resolve.

Mr Modi said, India and Uzbekistan will jointly organise a business summit involving business leaders from both countries.

Prime Minister Modi said the two countries will also step up cooperation in the field of sports.

Mr Modi announced 100 Lal Bahadur Shastri Scholarships for Uzbek students to promote the Hindi language in the Central Asian Country, along with the establishment of a Sanskrit Chair at Tashkent State University of Oriental Studies.

India has pledged one million dollars for afforestation in the Aral Sea region. The two sides also agreed on an arrangement allowing Indian UPI apps to scan Uzbekistan’s national UZQR codes for merchant payments. A Letter of Intent was also signed for the restoration and conservation of the Buddhist sites of Fayaz Tepa and Kara Tepa in Uzbekistan.

On the occasion, Prime Minister Modi was also conferred with Uzbekistan’s highest state honour, the Order of “Oliy Darajali Dustlik”, by President Shavkat Mirziyoyev. Prime Minister Modi dedicated the accolade to the 1.4 billion people of India and highlighted that it is a symbol of the enduring friendship between the two civilizations.

Several Union Ministers including Defence Minister Rajnath Singh, Home Minister Amit Shah, External Affairs Minister Dr S Jaishankar and Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman have congratulated Prime Minister Modi on being honoured with Uzbekistan’s supreme state award. Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla also congratulated Mr Modi on receiving the honour.
