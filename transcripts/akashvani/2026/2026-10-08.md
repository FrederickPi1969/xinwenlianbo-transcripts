The 57th GST Council meeting will be held at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi today. Chaired by Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman, the meeting will discuss policy reforms to resolve GST disputes and improve the flow of input tax credit, with the aim of reducing the time and cost of compliance. Sources in the Finance Ministry said that the government will propose a five-pronged plan for the next set of reforms to the GST system. The proposal covers process reforms, structural reforms, ease of living and doing business, exports of services, and e-commerce. These reforms are aimed at automating and easing a lot of the current administrative bottlenecks in the GST system, and reducing the compliance burden, especially for small businesses.

“The waters of Kochi have always been part of the city’s identity. Today, those same waterways are carrying a new generation of commuters aboard the Kochi Water Metro. Inaugurated by Prime Minister Narendra Modi on April 25, 2023, the Water Metro began passenger services the following day. It has since emerged as a new dimension in urban public transport, connecting Kochi’s islands with the mainland through modern electric-hybrid boats. Prime Minister Narendra Modi said the Water Metro would help ease traffic congestion in Kochi.

Kochi-based writer T. N. Venugopal says the Water Metro has transformed water transport in the city.

Kochi is the first city in India to have a Water Metro project. The system is planned across 15 routes, connecting ten island communities over a network of about 78 kilometres. The boats are air-conditioned and battery-powered, with dedicated charging facilities and modern passenger terminals. The response from commuters has been steadily growing. As of October 2, this year, the Water Metro had carried more than 77 lakh passengers. On October 2 alone, 11,670 passengers travelled on the service. For Kochi, however, the Water Metro is about more than infrastructure and numbers. It is about making better use of the city’s natural waterways while offering an alternative to road-based travel. As the electric boats move quietly across the backwaters, the waterways that once shaped Kochi’s history are now helping shape its future, connecting the city’s islands, its people and its aspirations. Smithy M Palluruthy/Akashvani News/Thiruvanathapuram.”

## Evening News

The GST Council during its 57th meeting in New Delhi today approved a series of measures to ease compliance for taxpayers. The decisions include scrapping the arrest powers of tax officers under the GST regime and raising the threshold for prosecution from five crore rupees to one crore rupees. Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman made the announcement while addressing the media in National Capital this evening.

The punishment, whether a fine, imprisonment or both, will be left to judicial discretion in each case. A taxpayer who files a return late, makes a mistake or falls behind on payment will face recovery proceedings, interest and a proportionate penalty.

The Finance Minister said no changes has been made to GST rates at the meeting and that the rate structure is settled. She said the GST Council has recommended common standards for notices and proceedings.

Ms Sitharaman said the Council has also taken steps to facilitate the smoother movement of goods.
