## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has called for sensitising Urban Local Bodies to the challenges arising from urbanisation, including population growth and future water requirements, and suggested organising similar Chintan Shivirs for Urban Local Bodies. Mr Modi chaired the valedictory session of the two-day National Departmental Summit on Water, organised by the Ministry of Jal Shakti yesterday. He called for greater adoption of water-saving technologies. He also suggested dedicated exhibitions and workshops, including exposure to global best practices, to enable Indian manufacturers and stakeholders to develop and scale efficient solutions.

“Prime Minister Narendra Modi emphasised that the actionable and time-bound action points emerging from the Summit should be pursued through a continuous process of review and learning. Appreciating the intensive two-day deliberations, Mr Modi suggested that the Summit should not remain a one-time exercise and called for a continuous process of review and follow-up.

Emphasising Jan Bhagidari, Mr Modi suggested promoting “Jal Utsav” to create greater awareness among people. Prime Minister called for making de-silting a regular programme and developing clear criteria and SOPs for de-silting. On dam safety, Mr Modi stressed rigorous preparedness before every monsoon, adherence to prescribed precautions and creating awareness among school students, including through suitable incorporation in educational curricula.

Prime Minister further called for strengthening knowledge and capacity on water management and governance in universities and higher educational institutions. He suggested structured inter-State study-visits involving public representatives, officials and farmers to facilitate practical learning and replication of successful interventions.

Mr. Modi also called for robust water data governance, with water-sector data brought together, systematically managed, analysed and effectively utilised. He recommended the use of AI for data collection and analysis through a uniform format. He also stressed anticipatory and integrated planning to address water scarcity through suitable utilisation of treated water for agriculture and industrial purposes. With Suparna Saikia, this is Vishnu P S, for Akashvani News, Delhi.”

Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Prime Minister of Belgium, Bart De Wever, will hold bilateral talks in New Delhi today. Both leaders will discuss the entire spectrum of bilateral relations, as well as regional and global issues of mutual interest. The Belgian Prime Minister arrived in the national capital last night on a three day visit to India. Prime Minister De Wever is being accompanied by Belgian Minister of Defence, Theo Francken. This is his first visit to India since taking office in February last year. The visiting dignitary will also deliver a keynote address at a Confederation of Indian Industry business event, in the context of the India-EU Free Trade Agreement. Our correspondent reports that the visit will provide an opportunity to further strengthen the India-Belgium partnership and advance cooperation in areas of mutual interest, including trade and investment, defence and security, and renewable energy.

“India and Belgium share historical ties spanning over three centuries. Belgium was among the first European countries to establish diplomatic relations with independent India, in September 1947. Belgium is an important economic partner for India in Europe. Bilateral trade reached over 13 billion dollars in 2025-26. Diamond trade, with Antwerp as a major global diamond hub, remains a significant component of bilateral commerce. Belgium is also an important source of investment in India. FDI inflows from Belgium amounted to approximately 4.2 billion dollars between April 2000 and December 2025. Science, technology and Innovation cooperation is an important pillar of the partnership and India and Belgium have developed cooperation in renewable energy, clean technologies and sustainability. Defence cooperation is also emerging as an important new dimension of the partnership, reflecting the diversification of bilateral ties into strategic and security-related areas. Belgium’s appointment of a Defence Attaché in New Delhi reflects this intent of deepening cooperation in defence. The Indian diaspora is an important bridge between the two countries with around 35 thousand people of Indian origin in Belgium, contributing substantially to economic, social and cultural life in that country. Suparna Saikia, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

ISRO Chairman V. Narayanan has said that the EOS-05 Earth observation satellite will be launched tomorrow. The satellite is scheduled to lift off at 2:55 AM aboard the GSLV-F17 rocket from the Satish Dhawan Space Centre at Sriharikota. Mr. Narayanan said EOS-05 is a state-of-the-art Earth observation spacecraft and India’s first imaging satellite to be placed in a geosynchronous orbit.

Japan Credit Rating Agency (JCR) has upgraded India’s Long-Term Foreign Currency and Local Currency Issuer Ratings by one notch from ‘BBB+’ to ‘A-’, while maintaining the Stable Outlook. JCR has also raised India’s country ceiling by one notch to ‘A’. Finance Ministry said that the upgrade reflects India’s solid economic growth, the effectiveness of economic policies in strengthening the foundations for growth, and the improved soundness of the financial system.

“The Ministry said that the upgrade comes against the backdrop of a challenging global environment and underscores the continued strengthening of India’s economic fundamentals, supported by sustained growth, effective economic policies, improved fiscal quality and a stronger financial system. In a statement, JCR noted that the Indian economy has maintained a high growth rate, supported by robust private consumption and public investment.The agency also highlighted the Indian Government’s continued implementation of policies including the development of digital public infrastructure and implementation of the Goods and Services Tax, which have strengthened India’s economic foundations. The agency also recognised the improvement in the quality of fiscal expenditure, with greater emphasis on capital expenditure, particularly infrastructure investment. JCR noted that the Central Government’s fiscal deficit declined from 4.7 percent in Financial Year 25 to 4.4 percent in Financial Year 26, while capital expenditure remained high. JIGYASA PANDEY, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

The Bombay High Court has ordered a CBI probe into the death of Disha Salian, former manager of late actor Sushant Singh Rajput. Disha Salian died on June 8, 2020, after falling from the 14th floor of a residential building in the Mumbai suburbs.

“In the Disha Salian death case, a Division Bench of Justices Sarang V Kotwal and Ranjitsinha R Bhonsale yesterday directed the CBI to register an FIR based on the complaint filed by her father, Satish Salian. The Court has further clarified that the direction to register the FIR does not imply any finding of guilt against anyone. The bench said that nobody shall be treated as an accused unless, in the opinion of the investigating officer, there are sufficient grounds to raise reasonable suspicion against him, depending on the material collected during the investigation. Prarthna, Akashvani News, Mumbai.”

## Midday News

India and Belgium today exchanged several agreements including Letter of Intent on Defence Cooperation, and MoUs in the fields of Renewable Energy, cyber crime, and diplomatic training. Prime Minister Narendra Modi held bilateral talks with his Belgian counterpart Bart De Wever in New Delhi this afternoon. Both leaders discussed the entire spectrum of bilateral relations, as well as regional and global issues of mutual interest.

In his press statement, Prime Minister Modi stated that shared democratic values, market economy, and people to people ties make India and Belgium natural partners. Mr Modi recalled that he visited Belgium ten years ago, and expressed pleasure that the partnership has made remarkable progress over the past decade. The Prime Minister said, both countries will achieve doubling of trade in next five years.

Prime Minister Modi said a clean energy future and reliable supply chain are shared priorities for both nations. He went on to add that the MoU signed today on Renewable Energy will give new momentum to sustainability cooperation. The Prime Minister said, in critical minerals, both nations have decided to increase cooperation in processing and recycling. Mr Modi said that in semiconductors, Belgium’s expertise and India’s scale are complementary to each other. In this direction, both countries will work together to connect Belgium’s IMEC Institute with India’s semiconductor ecosystem.

Mr Modi said, the world is passing through a phase of great upheaval and situations of conflict persists in many parts of the world. He added that uncertainty is affecting every sector, be it food, fuel or supply chains. He emphasised that India’s economy has maintained its momentum even in this challenging environment.

Earlier this morning, the Prime Minister of Belgium visited Rajghat and paid tributes to Mahatma Gandhi.

The Belgium Prime Minister arrived in New Delhi last night on a three day visit to India. He is accompanied by Belgian Minister of Defence, Theo Francken.

Earlier today, Defence Minister Rajnath Singh held a delegation level talks with his Belgian counterpart Theo Francken. He said India and Belgium have warm and enduring ties, built on mutual respect, democratic values and a shared commitment to a rules-based global order.

The Belgian Defence Minister Theo Fracken stressed on taking India-Belgium friendship forward.

During the meeting, the two ministers agreed to expand bilateral ties through defence cooperation dialogue, high-level visits, training and capacity building.
