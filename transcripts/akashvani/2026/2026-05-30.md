## Morning News

The Government has asked public sector oil marketing companies to set up Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG) reserves for at least 30 days as the West Asia conflict persist disrupting global energy supplies. Joint Secretary at the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas, Sujata Sharma stated this while talking to reporters yesterday during the inter-ministerial press briefing on the West Asia conflict. Outlining the precautionary measures to safeguard against supply shocks, Ms Sharma said, India was also working to increase crude oil storage capacity.

Additional Secretary (Gulf) in the Ministry of External Affairs, Aseem Mahajan, said that the government is closely monitoring developments in the Gulf and West Asian region. He said Indian Embassies and Consulates in the region are operating round-the-clock helplines to provide timely assistance to Indian nationals.

Director in the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways, Opesh Kumar Sharma, informed that the merchant vessel Nissos Keros, a crude oil tanker, has safely transited the Strait of Hormuz and is expected to arrive in Visakhapatnam on the 3rd of June. He said, the vessel is carrying around 2 lakh 70 thousand metric tonnes of crude oil.

Hindi Patrakarita Diwas is being observed today. The day is celebrated every year on May 30th in India to commemorate the beginning of journalism in Hindi. The day marks the publication of ‘Udant Martand’, the first Hindi newspaper in India, which was launched on May 30th, 1826, from Kolkata. The newspaper was founded and edited by Pandit Jugal Kishore Shukla, who is regarded as a pioneer of Hindi journalism.

On the occasion, Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla today extended his greetings to journalists, writers, and enlightened individuals dedicated to the Hindi language. In a social media post, Mr Birla said that Hindi journalism has served as a powerful medium for awakening social consciousness, strengthening democracy and reflecting the sentiments of the people.

The Khet Bachao Abhiyan to promote balanced and judicious use of fertilisers will be launched  across the country on 1st June and will be carried out throughout the month. Union Minister for Agriculture and Farmers Welfare Shivraj Singh Chouhan chaired a high level meeting regarding the preparations of the campaign in New Delhi today. He said that the Campaign should be made a comprehensive national drive connecting farms, farmers and villages. The Minister said the focus of the campaign will be on balanced and judicious use of fertilisers, timely advice to famers in  view of weather related challenges, active participation at panchayat level and delivering the benefits of the schemes directly to the villages. A report:

“The Khet Bachao Abhiyan will be carried out across the country from Monday till 30th June. The Krishi Vigyan Kendra’s have been assigned the role of key coordinators for all participating institutions in the campaign. Over one thousand six hundred teams have been formed and total five hundred teams have been constituted for hundred districts of excessive fertiliser use. During the campaign, the focus will be on saving the farms and providing timely and right guidance to farmers. Agriculture Ministry emphasized that minimizing the reckless use of chemical fertilisers is the prime objective of the campaign. The farmers will be sensitized about the balanced use of fertilisers and other agricultural inputs based on soil tests, motivating them to increase the use of green manures, organic and bio-products. Anupam Mishra, Akashvani News Delhi.”
