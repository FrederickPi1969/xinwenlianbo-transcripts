## Morning News

India has called for WTO-compliant approach to correcting global trade distortions at the concluding day of G20 Trade Ministers’ Meeting in Milwaukee, US. Speaking on structural excess capacity and production, Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal said India shares the concerns of many Members that trade-distorting support in some economies can lead to dumping and predatory pricing. He said such concerns are addressed through WTO-consistent measures, including anti-dumping and countervailing duties, based on evidence and subject to judicial review. He emphasised that industrial capacity in itself is not the issue, while distortions can arise where geographical concentration of production is driven by hidden subsidies and support.

“Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal highlighted that India’s trade with G20 members depends on a transparent, non-discriminatory and rules-based trading system. He said Most-Favoured-Nation treatment is a key guarantee of this system, ensuring that the same terms apply to the smallest trader and the largest trader. Speaking on ending forced labour in global supply chains, Mr Goyal said India’s commitment to the elimination of forced labour is absolute and unconditional. The Minister reaffirmed India’s commitment to a rules-based, fair, equitable, non-discriminatory, transparent, open, inclusive and multilateral trading system with the WTO at its core. Suparna Saikia, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

The Group of Seven (G-7) nations have agreed to release 100 million barrels of diesel and crude oil over four months in an effort to ease soaring fuel prices, with a substantial release of diesel planned in the first 20 days. The decision came as diesel prices in the United States climbed to record levels, adding pressure on President Donald Trump and his administration ahead of November’s midterm elections. Mr Trump said that European countries had agreed to release large quantities of diesel from their stocks and that the process would begin “immediately.”

The announcement followed an overnight conversation between Trump and French President Emmanuel Macron.  According to a statement issued by France after the meeting, the International Energy Agency will coordinate the release.

Rural women in India are no longer confined to traditional farm work. They are increasingly becoming operators and service providers of modern agricultural technology. Today, in our special series, we bring you a story that highlights how the ‘Namo Drone Didi’ scheme is playing an important role in this transformation. Here is a report by our correspondent:

“The scheme was approved by the Union Cabinet in November 2023 with the objective of connecting women with drone technology in agriculture. An outlay of one thousand 261 crore rupees was provided for the scheme in the last 3 years. Under the scheme, the government aims to provide 15 thousand agricultural drones to selected women. The scheme seeks to create new livelihood opportunities for women by enabling them to work as drone service providers. They can offer farmers drone-based spraying services for liquid fertilisers and pesticides on a rental basis. This can help improve efficiency in farm operations and reduce operational costs. Under the scheme, selected women are eligible for central financial assistance of up to 80 percent of the cost of the drone and its associated equipment, subject to a maximum of eight lakh rupees. Loans are also available to meet the remaining cost, along with interest support. The drone package also includes training for the beneficiaries.

In Rajasthan, 50 women have been provided drones. Pooja, a Namo Drone Didi from Sawai Madhopur district of Rajasthan, says that the training has brought a positive change in her life and has also provided financial support to her family.

The ‘Namo Drone Didi’ scheme is thus not only promoting the use of technology in agriculture, but is also helping rural women acquire new skills, take on new responsibilities and explore new avenues of livelihood and income. Jitendra Dwivedi, Akashvani News, Jaipur.”

“In the field of health security, Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana, or AB-PMJAY, is a fully government-funded health assurance scheme. Under this, eligible families receive a health cover of up to 5 lakh rupees every year. The scheme offers cashless treatment facilities for secondary and tertiary care in empaneled public and private hospitals across the country. Pre-hospitalization expenses for up to 3 days and post-discharge expenses for up to 15 days are also included. Pre-existing conditions are covered from day one of the scheme. There is no limit on family size, age, or gender under the scheme. Eligible beneficiaries can avail of the benefits at any empaneled hospital in any state across the country—meaning beneficiaries are not restricted to their home state for treatment. Furthermore, all senior citizens aged 70 years and above, regardless of their income or socio-economic status, are eligible for a health cover of up to 5 lakh rupees under Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY.
