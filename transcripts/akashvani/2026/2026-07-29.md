## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi chaired the second high-level meeting with Secretaries to the Government at his residence yesterday. During the meeting, the Prime Minister reviewed the future action agenda of Ministries and Departments dealing with Finance and Economy, Commerce and Industry, and Technology sectors. The meeting focused on accelerating progress towards the goals of Viksit Bharat.

“During the meeting, Mr Modi called for a strong focus on team building across Ministries and Departments, urging officers to work with a shared sense of purpose. He emphasised the need to break both horizontal and vertical silos within the Government so that departments function in close coordination and deliver integrated outcomes.

Highlighting the importance of citizen-centric governance, the Prime Minister said that the people of India must remain at the centre of governance. He reminded that every decision and policy should be guided by the interests and aspirations of the common citizen. Mr Modi stressed that while India is making significant investments in infrastructure, equal priority must be given to developing indigenous technologies. The Prime Minister underscored the importance of technological self-reliance and building domestic capabilities in critical sectors.

The meeting featured detailed discussions by Secretaries on the progress, priorities and implementation issues across their respective domains. The meeting was attended by the Cabinet Secretary, Secretaries of key Ministries and Departments, and senior officials from the Prime Minister’s Office. ADITYA PRATAP SINGH, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

The Lok Sabha will resume the discussion on the Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Amendment Bill, 2026 today. Union Minister Dr Jitendra Singh moved the Bill for consideration and passing in the House yesterday. The Bill seeks to amend the Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024. The legislation incorporates several significant provisions, including stringent penalties such as imprisonment for up to ten years, fines of up to ten crore rupees, and the seizure of the properties of convicted offenders.

Moving the Bill, Union Minister Dr Jitendra Singh said, the new legislation reaffirms the government’s deep commitment to safeguard the welfare of the students and the youth of this country. He said, at the same time, it is also a reiteration of Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s resolve not to allow anyone to compromise with the future of the youth of the country.

BJP MP Bansuri Swaraj termed the legislation as a transformative step and said such legislation is the need of the hour to ensure fair and transparent examination.

Samajwadi Party MP Akhilesh Yadav expressed concern over the paper leak of various examinations. Mr Yadav said that the NEET UG paper was leaked this year, despite the new act – Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) was made in 2024.

Abhisekh Banerjee of AITC, Lavu Sri Krishna Devarayalu of TDP, Dr Shrikant Shinde of Shiv Sena supported the Bill. The TDP MP also called for decentralised question paper setting committees.

Dayanidhi Maran of DMK asserted that the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test NEET is the core issue, which is not uniform and is against students.

Congress MP Priyanka Gandhi Vadra accused the government of failing to protect the interests of the students and weakening the education system.

The discussion remained inconclusive. The Presiding Officer Sandhya Ray adjourned the House to meet at 11 AM today.

Delhi Government has approved the Delhi Lakshmi Yojana (DLY), marking a significant step towards the economic empowerment and social security of women. Under the scheme, eligible women will receive financial assistance of two thousand five hundred rupees every month. The scheme’s guidelines were approved at the Cabinet meeting chaired by Delhi Chief Minister Rekha Gupta. A provision of five thousand 110 crore rupees has been made for the scheme in the 2026-27 Budget. The scheme aims to strengthen women economically while empowering them socially.

Talking to media in New Delhi, Delhi CM Rekha Gupta said that the portal for the scheme will open on 1st of August. The Chief Minister said the scheme will cover eligible women between 21 and 60 years of age whose family income is up to 2.50 lakh rupees per annum.

## Evening News

The Lok Sabha today passed the anti-Paper leak bill, proposing tougher penalties and fast-track courts to curb exam malpractices. The Bill seeks to amend the Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024. The Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Amendment Bill, 2026 incorporates several significant provisions, including stringent penalties such as imprisonment for up to ten years, fines of up to ten crore rupees, and the seizure of the properties of convicted offenders. The legislation mandates that every State Government and Union Territory Administration establish Special Fast Track Courts for day-to-day trial.

Replying to the discussion on the bill, Minister of State for Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions Dr Jitendra Singh said, the amendment in the bill has been brought to make the law more stringent and ensure speedy justice in paper leak cases.

Dr Singh criticised Leader of the Opposition and Congress MP Rahul Gandhi for using unparliamentary language, saying it is inappropriate and unfortunate. Replying to Mr Gandhi’s accusation against Union Home Minister Amit Shah, Dr Singh said, the government repeatedly clarified that no shots were fired and only tear gas was used.

Parliamentary Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju strongly objected to the remarks of Mr Gandhi and demanded an apology from him in the House.

Earlier, participating in the discussion, Congress MP Rahul Gandhi said the Students’ protest on the NEET issue was not violence or hatred but a deep expression of the youth, and all political parties must respect this expression.

After the passage of the bill, the Lower House was adjourned till tomorrow.

On the passage of the anti-paper leak bill in the Lok Sabha, Education Minister Pralhad Joshi said, the legislation aims to dismantle the paper leak ecosystem and increase transparency, credibility and fairness in the examination process.

Home Minister Amit Shah has congratulated every student in India on the passage of the bill. In a social media post, Mr Shah assured that the Narendra Modi government will spare no one who attempts to imperil the future of students and will leave no stone unturned to ensure that they face the full wrath of the law.

The Rajya Sabha has passed the Prevention of Insults to National Honour (Amendment) Bill, 2026. The bill will further amend the Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act, 1971. The proposed legislation seeks to give Vande Mataram the same statutory protection as the national anthem, Jana Gana Mana and making insult to the National song a criminal offence.These offences are punishable with imprisonment up to three years, a fine, or both.

Replying to a discussion on the Bill, Minister of State for Home Nityanand Rai said, the amendment in the Bill reflects the collective commitment to the soul of India, national consciousness, cultural heritage, and the ideals of the freedom struggle.
