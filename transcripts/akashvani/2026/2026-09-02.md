## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has renewed his call to embrace Swadeshi goods and avoid unnecessary purchase of gold and destination weddings abroad. Mr Modi congratulated the country on recording 7.8 percent economic growth and spoke of the need to promote self-reliance and give greater emphasis to swadeshi products. The Prime Minister said India is progressing rapidly despite global uncertainty, wars, crises and disruptions to supply chains.

Taking a swipe at his critics, he said some people within the country, “drowned in an atmosphere of despair”, are spreading the “lies” all around.

The Government has reduced the stock holding limit for sugar dealers from four thousand quintals to two thousand quintals effective from the 15th of this month till 30th of November this year. Our Correspondent reports that the new stock limit is aimed to curb hoarding and speculative trading, protect consumer interest by ensuring adequate availability and price stability.

“Currently a stock holding limit of four thousand quintals on sugar dealers across the country is effective from 1st of the last month. Under the amended provisions a sugar dealer shall not hold any stock for a period exceeding 30 days from the date of receipt of such stock. The sugar dealer shall not also keep sugar in stock at any time and in any place throughout the country in excess of two thousand quintals. However, the stock holding limit shall remain at four thousand quintals for Kolkata and its extended metropolitan areas considering the specific market requirements of the region. The Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution said that the Kolkata area is sourcing sugar from Uttar Pradesh and Maharashtra and supplying to the eastern part of the country, including the North-Eastern region. With Bhanu, Saklen Akhtar, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

The Government said that the country has adequate availability of onion and it has begun a calibrated and targeted release of buffer stocks through Kanda Express and road transport to ensure moderation of seasonal price pressures. The Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution Ministry said that retail intervention efforts have expanded significantly across 19 cities, supported by the dispatch of over 30 trucks to ensure widespread availability.

Agriculture and Farmers Welfare Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan reviewed the progress of the Prime Minister Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana at a meeting in New Delhi yesterday. More from our correspondent:

“The Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare said that the average score of the 100 districts across the six objectives of the scheme increased from 22.54 in April 2026 to 38.85 in July 2026, an improvement of 16.31 points. The Ministry said that the scheme is being implemented through the convergence of 36 Central schemes of 11 Ministries and Departments, along with State schemes and private-sector participation. The review emphasized on strengthening the monitoring framework to move beyond tracking activities and expenditure towards measuring outcomes and the actual benefits reaching farmers. Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana was approved by the Union Cabinet last year and was launched on 11th October 2025. Shivang Misra, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

## Evening News

India has stepped up its relief operation in flood-hit Nepal, with New Delhi delivering a fifth tranche of emergency material. India’s Ambassador to Nepal in a social media post shared that the fifth Indian aircraft carrying relief and rescue assistance arrived in the Himalyan Nation this afternoon. The aircraft carried an 11-member rescue team equipped with specialized tunnel-rescue equipment, along with five-point-five tonnes of essential medicines.

The flash floods, triggered by the collapse or avalanche of ice and rock near the Nepal-Tibet border, devastated towns and villages in northern and central Nepal on the 26th of last month.

The Indian Air Force has carried out several sorties to transport essential supplies to affected areas. India has also offered specialised search and rescue support. Indian teams have been involved in operations around hydropower tunnels and in forensic efforts to identify victims amid the high number of casualties and missing people.

Meanwhile, Nepal Prime Minister Balendra Shah while speaking in Parliament today thanked India for its quick response to the devastating flash floods, as rescue teams continue searching for survivors and missing people in some of the worst-affected areas. The death toll from devastating flash floods in the Himalayan Nation has risen to 1,200 with over 4000 people still missing, as rescuers battled rain and debris.

Addressing the House of Representatives, Mr Shah acknowledged the emergency assistance provided by India and China, including relief supplies, technical support and rescue personnel.

The Prime Minister said, the devastation caused by the flash floods, stands as a stark warning of escalating climate risks in the Himalayan region and called on the international community to share responsibility for mitigating its impacts.

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) today said its special US dollar-Indian rupee (USD-INR) forex swap facility has received forex inflows of over 136 billion US dollars till August 31, 2026. Of the total inflows, Foreign Currency Non-Resident (Bank) [FCNR-B] deposits accounted for the overwhelming majority, amounting to 127.2 billion US dollars. Meanwhile, Overseas Foreign Currency Borrowings (OFCBs) brought in 5.26 billion US dollars and External Commercial Borrowings (ECBs) contributed 3.89 billion US dollars.

The Reserve Bank introduced the special USD-INR forex swap facility covering FCNR(B) deposits, ECBs and OFCBs inflows on June 8, 2026 to boost the inflow of dollars amid a weakening rupee.
