## Morning News

Government has said that all refineries are working at 100 percent capacity and refineries have adequate inventory of crude. Prime Minister Narendra Modi launched various development projects worth 23 thousand 550 crore rupees from Silchar in Assam today. Mr Modi took part in the ‘bhoomi poojan’ for 22,864-crore rupees access-controlled expressway between Silchar and Shillong in Meghalaya, the first of its kind in the Northeast. The 166-km four-lane greenfield high-speed corridor will reduce the distance between Guwahati and Silchar from 295 km to 252 km, cutting travel time from 8.5 hours to around 5 hours. Prime Minister also laid the foundation stone for an elevated corridor on NH-306, from Trunk Road near Capital Point to Rangirkhari Point in Silchar. This project aims to alleviate congestion on one of Silchar’s busiest roads, improve links with neighbouring states such as Mizoram, Tripura and Manipur, and contribute to the economic development of the Barak Valley. Mr Modi also laid the foundation stone for 122-crore rupees College of Agriculture at Patharkandi in Karimganj district.

Speaking on the occasion, Prime Minister said, North East is now leading India’s Act East Policy. Mr Modi said, Barak Valley, which was neglected during Congress rule, is now emerging as gateway to Southeast Asia. Prime Minister said, the Barak Valley once served as an important trade centre but lost its strategic advantage due to decisions taken during the Congress era. He alleged that despite ruling the country and the state for decades, the Congress failed to focus on the development of Barak Valley.

But today, the double-engine government is working with commitment to transform Barak Valley and make it a developed region, Prime Minister said.

Highlighting infrastructure development, Mr Modi said, a historic step was taken with the foundation stone being laid for the Silchar-Shillong high-speed corridor.

The Prime Minister also highlighted the Centre’s focus on improving railway infrastructure in the Northeast. He said, under the double-engine government, major work has been undertaken to strengthen railway connectivity in the region. Mr Modi said, more than 2,500 kilometres of railway lines have already been electrified in the Northeast. He added that railway lines in Barak Valley will also be electrified, which will further enhance connectivity and support economic growth in the region.

On the conditions of war prevailing all around the world, the Prime Minister said BJP government is making every possible effort to ensure that the citizens of our country face the fewest hardships possible. Mr. Modi said the objective of the government is to minimise the impact of this war on the nation’s citizens.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will visit Kolkata today and he will inaugurate and lay the foundation stone for multiple development projects worth around 18 thousand 6 hundred 80 crore rupees. In a major boost to road infrastructure in the region, the Prime Minister will inaugurate and lay the foundation stone for several National Highway projects with a combined length of more than 420 kilometers, worth around 16 thousand 9 hundred 90 crore rupees.

Mr. Modi will also lay the foundation stone for several new National Highway projects. These include five packages of the 231-kilometer long four-lane Kharagpur-Moregram economic corridor section of NH-116A.

In the Railways sector, Prime Minister will flag off the Purulia-Anand Vihar Terminal (Delhi) Express, strengthening rail connectivity between West Bengal, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Jharkhand and the national capital region.

The Consulate General of India in Dubai has announced extension of regular Indian Visas and E-Visas and waived of over stay penalty to foreigners stuck in India due to ongoing developments in West Asia. In a social media post, the Consulate General said that all types of Visas and e-Visas expiring or due for expiry soon would be extended for a month on gratis basis, for affected nationals on case-to-case basis. The Consulate General said exit permits would be provided on gratis basis to the affected foreign nationals, and non-application of exit permit and extension of Visas by affected foreign nationals would not be treated as violation of migration legislation.

The Iranian state broadcaster reported that a suicide drone was used to target the radar system at the US embassy in Baghdad. The report indicated that the mission specifically focused on the electronic detection equipment situated within the diplomatic mission’s compound. Iraq has long been a proxy battleground between the United States and Iran.

Lebanon’s Health Ministry said, this was the second attack on the health sector within hours, after another Israeli strike on the southern village of Souaneh killed two paramedics when it hit a paramedic centre.
