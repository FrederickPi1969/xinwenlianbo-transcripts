India’s water management initiatives have emerged as a model for developing countries. World Bank Global Director for Water, Saroj Kumar Jha, has described India’s progress as a remarkable success story. Speaking ahead of the 2026 United Nations Water Conference, Mr Jha praised initiatives such as the Jal Jeevan Mission, Namami Gange and Swachh Bharat Mission. He said the Jal Jeevan Mission had provided improved drinking water access to 800 million people in less than seven years. He also lauded efforts to clean the Ganga and strengthen the safety of nearly 900 dams. Mr Jha urged India to share its experiences globally at the conference, scheduled in Abu Dhabi from December 8 to 10.

Union Minister of Ports, Shipping and Waterways Sarbananda Sonowal today launched a recreational river cruise service along the Yamuna in New Delhi. The cruise named Namo Yamuna River Safari will cover a six-kilometres area along the river Yamuna between Sonia Vihar-Jagatpur. Speaking on the occasion, Mr Sonowal said that the Delhi government is committed to cleaning the Yamuna and making its water beneficial for people’s lives.

Delhi Chief Minister Rekha Gupta said that for the first time the people of Delhi will experience the Cruise Service named as Namo Yamuna River Safari. She invited the people of Delhi to experience the cruise service with their family members which provides a positive image of the national capital Delhi.  Mrs Gupta added that Ministry of Road Transport and Highways has implemented projects worth over one lakh and 25 thousand crore rupees in the national capital to decongest the city.

Our correspondent reports that the introduced cruise service offers visitors a six-kilometres round-trip experience between Sonia Vihar-Jagatpur.

The initiative aims to promote river-based tourism and offer visitors a new recreational experience in the national capital. The facilities at the site will include a musical fountain, a children’s play zone, shopping facilities, a food court and adventure sports activities. The development of the recreational river cruise service in Delhi marks an important step towards harnessing the potential of this National Waterway for recreational navigation and river-based tourism. The initiative is expected to offer Delhi residents and tourists a unique experience while fostering greater public engagement with the Yamuna. The use of electric-powered cruise vessels will also add a new recreational attraction to the National Capital Region. With Shivang and Anand, Rishu Jaiswal, Akashvani News, Delhi.

India and the United States have reaffirmed commitment to strengthen bilateral counter-narcotics cooperation towards ensuring the safety and well-being of their communities. US lawmakers have reintroduced a bill in the House of Representatives seeking to declare Diwali a federal holiday. The legislation, known as the Diwali Day Act, is led by Congresswoman Grace Meng and has the support of 19 lawmakers, including five Indian-origin representatives. The bill recognises Diwali, also known as the Festival of Lights, as an important celebration for Hindus, Sikhs, Jains and Buddhists. Meng said the move would promote cultural inclusion and recognise America’s diverse traditions. If passed by Congress, Diwali would become the twelfth federally recognised holiday in the United States.

The Saudi-led coalition said that it has intercepted multiple missiles and a drone launched towards Saudi Arabia by Yemen’s Houthi rebels. The interceptions included two missiles targeting the capital Riyadh, a ballistic missile aimed at the southern region, and a drone over the oil-rich Eastern Province. Saudi Civil Defence issued precautionary alerts in Riyadh and southwestern cities, including Abha and Khamis Mushait. Meanwhile, Houthi media reported Saudi airstrikes on targets in the capital Sanaa, including a gas station and a state oil company building. The reports have not been independently confirmed. The developments come amid escalating hostilities between the Houthis and the Saudi-led coalition, raising concerns over regional security and international shipping through the Red Sea.

The Supreme Court has asked WhatsApp and other intermediaries to apprise it about their proposed measures to prevent digital arrest scams and related cybercrimes in the country. A bench headed by Chief Justice of India Surya Kant asked WhatsApp and similar intermediaries to simultaneously submit their proposals to the I4C (Indian Cybercrime Coordination Centre). The order regarding it uploaded on the website with regard to the hearing conducted on 29th of last month in a suo motu case titled ‘Victims of Digital Arrest Related to Forged Documents’,

The Election Commission has said that restrictions and prohibitions applicable to serving government employees under the Model Code of Conduct (MCC) will also apply to retired government officials and other individuals engaged in various capacities by State and Union Territory governments.

The Commission has written a letter to the Chief Secretaries and Chief Electoral Officers of Goa, Manipur, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh and Uttarakhand in this regard where Assembly elections wil be held next year. We have a report –

“The Election Commission has said that such individuals must maintain political neutrality and refrain from participating in election campaigns or canvassing. It also said that such individuals shall not summon any election related officer of the constituency or district where elections have been announced, for any official discussions or meetings or hold video conferences with any such officer. The Poll Body added that the provisions of the MCC apply to all organizations, corporations and commissions funded wholly or partially by the Government and their employees. It said, the relevant Conduct Rules, in the States, prohibit the Government servants from taking part in political activities and elections. With Anand Kumar, Anupam Mishra, Akashvani News, Delhi.”
