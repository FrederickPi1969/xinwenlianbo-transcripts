“The peace agreement will be signed on June 19 in Switzerland. Trump said the Strait of Hormuz will open on Friday after the official signing of the agreement. The agreement calls for the US and Iran to negotiate over Iran’s nuclear enrichment and the disposal of its highly enriched uranium during a 60-day window.
