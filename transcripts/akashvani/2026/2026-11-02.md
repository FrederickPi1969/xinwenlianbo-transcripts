The Government has notified the Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin): VB-G RAM G – Grievance Redressal Rules, 2026, establishing a structured, multi-tier and time-bound mechanism for addressing grievances relating to the implementation of the Scheme. The Ministry of Rural Development said that the rules aim to make grievance redressal more accessible, transparent and accountable, while ensuring that citizens’ concerns receive timely attention and appropriate action. A report :

“The Ministry said that the Rules provide for the establishment of a time-bound, multi-tier grievance redressal mechanism by State Governments at the Gram Panchayat, Block and District levels. It said that designated Grievance Redressal Officers at the appropriate levels will be responsible for receiving, registering, enquiring into and disposing of grievances. To facilitate easy access, any person may submit a grievance relating to any matter arising out of or connected with the implementation of the Scheme. The Rules provide for submission of grievances in person before the designated Grievance Redressal Officer, through speed post, with proof of delivery, through the Digital Public Infrastructure for Redressal of Grievances designated by the Central Government and through telephone. The Rules also prescribe clear timelines to ensure prompt examination and resolution of grievances. The enquiry into a grievance is required to be completed within seven working days and resolved within 15 days from the date of its receipt. With Anupam Misra, Rishu Jaiswal, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today said, the ongoing global crises have highlighted that energy security was directly linked to economic security. He emphasised that the future of energy and economic security lies in clean energy. More from our correspondent.

“The establishment of these FRRO offices will help foreigners visiting India in obtaining various consular services such as visa extension, conversion, immigration compliance and legal safety. The expansion will strengthen local-level institutional capacity, improve coordination with State authorities and facilitate legitimate foreign visitors. The Foreigners Regional Registration Office regulates, tracks and facilitates the lawful stay of foreign nationals visiting or residing in India. Overall, the FRRO monitors compliance with Indian immigration laws, handles cases of overstay and visa violations, and coordinates legal action or penalties where necessary.
