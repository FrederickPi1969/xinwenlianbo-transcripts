India and Uzbekistan have agreed to cooperate in addressing the threats and challenges to national and regional security, and further strengthen the framework of the India-Uzbekistan Joint Working Group on Counter Terrorism. The tenth meeting of India-Uzbekistan Joint Working Group on Counter Terrorism was held in New Delhi yesterday. The two sides discussed the threats posed by terrorist groups globally and in their respective regions. Both sides exchanged views on current and emerging challenges to counter-terrorism. These include countering extremism and radicalization, combating financing of terrorism, preventing use of technology for terrorist purposes, and the nexus between transnational organized crime and terrorism.

In Tibet, 16 people have been killed and 54 others injured after a magnitude 7.0 earthquake struck Baqen County in Nagqu City last evening. Hundreds of homes collapsed and rockfalls were triggered. Rescue operations are underway with more than 1,600 personnel deployed to assist those affected and provide emergency relief.
The Nagqu media office said, at least 339 homes and 20 livestock sheds collapsed in the earthquake. Communication services were disrupted in parts of the region, while railway authorities halted train services as a precaution.
Meanwhile, Intermittent snowfall is forecast in the affected region over the next three days, raising concerns for residents displaced by the earthquake and those involved in rescue operations.
India’s National Centre for Seismology said the earthquake struck Nagqu City at 8:03 PM Indian Standard Time, at a depth of 173 kilometres, around 400 kilometres from Lhasa.

The Allahabad High Court has ruled that a survivor of sexual assault under the POCSO Act cannot be denied compensation merely because the medical report does not record physical injuries. A division bench of Justice Shekhar B Saraf and Justice Amitabh Kumar Rai directed authorities to pay three lakh rupees to the minor under the Uttar Pradesh Rani Lakshmi Bai Mahila Samman Kosh scheme within ten days of submission of a certified copy of the order. The court set aside the district steering committee’s decision to withhold compensation, observing that physical injuries are not a necessary condition for establishing penetrative sexual assault under Section 3 of the POCSO Act. It held that the absence of injury marks cannot, by itself, negate an allegation of sexual assault. The bench noted that once the FIR and chargesheet indicate an offence under Section 4 of the Act, compensation cannot be denied solely on the basis of a medical report that records no injuries.

The two satellite-tagged Amur Falcons, Apapang and Alang, have returned to Manipur after travelling across 23 countries in Asia and Africa, marking the successful completion of their nearly year-long tracking journey. In a social media post, Union Environment Minister Bhupender Yadav said that the birds arrived in Manipur yesterday, 11 days after departing from their breeding grounds in northeastern China. He noted that the two falcons were tagged by scientists of the Wildlife Institute of India in November 2025. More from our correspondent.

The return of the Amur falcons marks the completion of a round-trip migration journey, highlighting the remarkable long-distance movements of the migratory birds. The falcons are now expected to spend two to three weeks at select stopover sites in the mountainous areas of Tamenglong district in Manipur. During this period, they will feed on termites and build up essential fat reserves for their next migratory leg. From Manipur, the birds are expected to undertake a six-day non-stop flight of nearly six thousand kilometres to Somalia, including a challenging three thousand kilometre crossing of the Arabian Sea. The annual arrival of the Amur Falcons is eagerly awaited by local communities in Manipur, who have played a significant role in protecting the migratory birds. Nitika Gupta, for Akashvani News, Delhi.

According to the India Meteorological Department (IMD), the system is likely to intensify into a depression by tomorrow, a deep depression by the day after tomorrow, and a cyclonic storm by this Monday morning. Odisha is likely to witness increased rainfall activity starting this Monday, with widespread rain and isolated heavy rainfall expected between this Tuesday and Thursday.
