US Senate has backed President Donald Trump’s military campaign against Iran. Senators voted to block a bipartisan resolution aiming to stop the military campaign ​without authorization by Congress  last night. The Senate voted 53 to 47 not to advance the resolution, largely along party .

“Debate about Trump’s buildup of military assets in the Middle East, and American and Israeli strikes on Iran has centered on whether Trump is pulling the country into another “forever war” like ​the long conflicts in Iraq and Afghanistan. Opponents of the resolution rejected this, insisting that Trump’s action was ​legal and within his right as commander in chief to protect the United States by ordering limited strikes. The vote came amid mounting frustration among Democrats who say Trump has increasingly sidelined Congress on matters of war. During Trump’s second term, the US military has struck seven other countries without authorization from lawmakers. The War Powers Resolution, passed in 1973 in the aftermath of the Vietnam War, was designed to check precisely that kind of unilateral action. It requires the President to notify Congress within 48 hours of introducing US forces into hostilities and bars armed forces from remaining in such conflicts for more than 60 days-with a possible 30-day extension-without a declaration of war or specific authorization for the use of military force. It also allows any member of Congress to force a vote on a resolution directing the removal of US forces. ASHISH P. ABRAHAM, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

The President said, Mr. Stubb’s presence at the Raisina Dialogue is a matter of pride for India. She expressed confidence that his views on the contemporary global situation will be valuable for the participants. President Murmu expressed confidence that the recently announced India-EU Free Trade Agreement will provide fresh momentum to bilateral trade and investment.

The President said, Finland is a global leader in advanced digital technologies. She appreciated the role of Finland’s companies in the country’s journey towards a Viksit Bharat.

MEA Secretary (West) Sibi George said that Finland’s advanced capabilities in digital innovation align well with India’s digital transformation initiatives. Briefing media in New Delhi today, He said, the two sides have established a cross-sectoral joint working group on digitalization to advance cooperation in new and emerging technologies.

The conflict in West Asia is escalating rapidly as several countries are becoming involved. The United States and Israel are carrying out joint air strikes against Iran while Tehran is responding with missiles and drones. Fighting is spreading across different parts of the region, and Iran backed armed group Hezbollah has joined the conflict.

As per latest reports, Azerbaijan today strongly condemned drone attacks launched from Iranian territory targeting the city of Nakhchivan and its airport, resulting in damage to infrastructure and injuries to two civilians. However, Iran’s general staff of the armed forces denied that it had launched any drone toward Azerbaijan.

Qatar has said the country has also been subjected to a missile attack and that its air defence systems were actively intercepting the incoming missiles. Saudi Arabia also said its air defense system intercepted and destructed several drones. The UAE claimed, one ballistic missile and six drones hit the country’s territory today.

Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps has said country launched its large Khorramshahr-4 missiles in an attack targeting Israel. It claimed attacks in Bahrain, Kuwait and the United Arab Emirates as well.

In the meantime, Israel announced multiple incoming missile attacks and air sirens sounded in Tel Aviv and Jerusalem. Iran has threatened to target Israel’s Dimona nuclear site if Israel and the US seek to topple the Iranian regime.

On the other hand, Israeli military said, it has launched another wave of strikes on Iran as explosions were heard in multiple locations in Tehran. Israel also conducted targeted attacks in Lebanon at several command centers of the Iranian-backed Hezbollah, including one being used by the air unit of the armed group.

According to media reports, the war has so far claimed over one thousand two hundred lives in Iran, 77 people have been killed in Lebanon and around a dozen in Israel. It has disrupted the supply of the world’s oil and gas, snarled international shipping and stranded hundreds of thousands of travellers in West Asia.

Sri Lanka has conveyed its concerns over the escalating conflict in West Asia during a discussion between Foreign Minister Vijitha Herath and Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi. In a message posted on social media, Herath said the discussion took place yesterday as tensions in the region continue to intensify. More from our Colombo correspondent:

“A day after a US submarine attack sank the Iranian naval vessel IRIS Dena in waters south of Sri Lanka, reports have emerged that Sri Lanka and Iran have held discussions on the rapidly evolving situation. Sri Lanka’s Foreign Minister Vijitha Herath held a discussion with his Iranian counterpart Abbas Araghchi on March 4, amid escalating tensions in the Middle East. In a message on social media, Herath said Sri Lanka conveyed its grave concern over the growing hostilities and stressed the importance of diplomatic dialogue to restore regional peace. Meanwhile, another Iranian vessel, the support ship IRIS Bushehr, currently near Sri Lanka’s Exclusive Economic Zone, has requested permission to dock at a Sri Lankan port. The government is considering the request. Ahmed Muyeen Farooqi, Akashvani News, Colombo.”

West Bengal Governor CV Ananda Bose has resigned from his post ahead of the upcoming State Assembly elections. Mr Bose, was appointed as West Bengal governor in November 2022. Speaking to a news agency, Mr Bose confirmed his resignation from the post this evening.

Ladakh’s Lieutenant Governor Kavinder Gupta today resigned from his post, eight months after assuming the office. Mr Gupta had taken the oath in July last year, becoming the third Lieutenant Governor of the Union Territory.

In Chhattisgarh, a Maoist was killed in an encounter between police and Maoists in the forest area along the Dantewada-Bijapur border. The killed Maoist has been identified as Rajesh Punem, an Area Committee Member of the Bhairamgarh Area Committee of Maoist organisation. A reward of five lakh rupees had been announced on him.

In Nepal, counting of votes have begun after country’s first general elections since violent Gen Z protests toppled the K P Sharma Oli-led coalition government. Voting took place today to elect 275 members of the House of Representatives. More from our Kathmandu correspondent:

“With 60 percent of voters turnout in today’s election in Nepal, with sparse incidents of disputes in Dolakha, sarlahi and Rautahat, the overall election was held in a peaceful manner. Voters exercised their voting rights with spirit to strengthen democracy in Nepal. Youth who participated during the Gen Z protest which toppled K P Sharma Oli government, voted enthusiastically. Voting process started at 7 am at all polling stations under security provided by Nepali Army, Nepali Police, election police and officials from National Investigation Department. Voters have casted votes for 165 seats under the First-past-the-post system and 110 seats under Proportional representation system. Results for the First-Past-the-Post ballots are expected within 24 hours, while the Proportional Representation results may take another few days. Akashvani News, Kathmandu.”
