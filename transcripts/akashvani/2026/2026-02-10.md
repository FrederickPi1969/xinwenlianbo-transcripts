## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has announced a Special Economic Package of 175 million dollars for the Seychelles. Addressing the Joint Press Conference after meeting the President of the Seychelles, Dr Patrick Herminie in New Delhi yesterday, the Prime Minister said, this package will support Social Housing, E-Mobility, Vocational Training, Health, Defence, and Maritime Security.

The Prime Minister added that as a maritime neighbour and trusted partner, Seychelles holds a special place in India’s Vision MAHASAGAR.

India and Seychelles also signed 7 Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs) across various sectors including Health, Meteorology, Electronics and Information Technology and Good Governance.

Moreover, President Droupadi Murmu hosted a banquet in honour of visiting Seychelles President Patrick Herminie at Rashtrapati Bhavan. Welcoming him yesterday, she said that India’s relationship with Seychelles is based on mutual trust and respect, and shared belief in democracy and pluralism. Earlier, Vice-President C P Radhakrishnan called on the President of Seychelles.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi had a telephonic conversation with his Mauritian counterpart Dr. Navinchandra Ramgoolam. In a social media post, Mr Modi said that during the conversation yesterday, they reaffirmed commitment to strengthen the special, historic and people-centric ties that unite both nations. Both leaders also reviewed progress in the wide-ranging cooperation. The Prime Minister further said that India and Mauritius will continue to work together to achieve shared objectives of peace and stability in the Indian Ocean Region. Mr Modi also said that he looks forward to welcoming Dr Ramgoolam for the AI Impact Summit next week.

Union Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chauhan has said that the India-US trade deal is the best agreement in the national interest, in which the interests of farmers have been fully safeguarded. Talking to media after Govardhan parikrama at Mathura in Uttar Pradesh, the Agriculture Minister said, major grains including wheat and paddy have been completely protected in this agreement.

Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman has said that the Union Budget 2026-27 reflects the Government’s commitment for continuity, certainty and predictability from a policy point of view for businesses and industry. She said that it will enable them to foster long-term plans and investment commitments. She said this while addressing the Board Members of the US-India Strategic Partnership Forum in New Delhi yesterday.

Electronics and IT Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw met US Ambassador to India, Sergio Gor in New Delhi. They discussed strengthening strategic ties, with a focus on semiconductors, electronics manufacturing and supply chain resilience. In a social media post yesterday, Mr Vaishnaw said, he looks forward to strong participation from the United States in the AI Impact Summit 2026.

The first BRICS Sherpa Meeting commenced in New Delhi yesterday. Secretary for Economic Relations and India’s BRICS Sherpa Sudhakar Dalela highlighted key priorities of India’s Chairship based on Prime Miniter Narendra Modi’s vision for people-centric and humanity-first approach. He also underlined the overall theme of Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability. India will chair BRICS for the fourth time this year.

Russia has said it will actively support India’s chairmanship of BRICS, describing New Delhi’s agenda as modern and highly relevant, with strong focus on counter-terrorism and energy security. Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov said that India, which assumed the chair on 1st of January, this year, is prioritising issues that address current global challenges while preparing for the future. In an interview with TV BRICS, he highlighted the relevance of counter-terrorism amid persistent threats in the region.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah will deliver the keynote address at the National Conference on Tackling Cyber-Enabled Frauds and Dismantling the Ecosystem in New Delhi today. On this occasion, he will also inaugurate the new Cyber crime Branch of the Central Bureau of Investigation. He will also launch the State Cyber Crime Coordination Centre Dashboard of the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) under the Ministry of Home Affairs.

The Rajya Sabha will resume discussions on the Union Budget 2026-27 today. Lok Sabha is also scheduled to take up the General Discussion on Union Budget. Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman had presented the Union Budget in Parliament on the 1st of this month. The first phase of the Budget session commenced on 28th of January and will continue till the Friday. The second phase will take place from 9th March to 2nd April.

Uttar Pradesh has become the first state in the country to present its own ‘Economic Survey’ report showcasing the pace of development and acceleration in industrialization. Uttar Pradesh’s Finance Minister Suresh Khanna yesterday tabled the Economic Review Report for the year 2025-26 during the Budget Session of the Legislative Assembly.

The state’s contribution to the national economy has increased from 8.6 per cent in the year 2016-17 to 9.1 per cent in the year 2024-25. The survey also states that size of state economy has more than doubled from rupee 13.30 lakh crore rupees in 2016-17 to 30.25 lakh crore rupees in 2024-25, and it is poised to reach rupees 36 lakh crore in 2025-26.

According to the Survey, the state government has consistently increased spending on rural development. Compared to 1,814.34 lakh ‘Manav diwas’ in 2017-18, UP generated 3,363.97 lakh ‘Manav Diwas’ in 2024-25, an increase of 85.41 percent. SUSHIL TIWARI, AKASHVANI NEWS, LUCKNOW.

The BJP-led Mahayuti has recorded a sweeping victory in the Maharashtra Zilla Parishad elections, winning 552 of the 731 seats whose results were declared by the State Election Commission yesterday. Within the ruling alliance, the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) emerged on top with 225 seats, followed by the Nationalist Congress Party (NCP) with 165 seats and the Eknath Shinde-led Shiv Sena with 162 seats.

In the opposition Maha Vikas Aghadi, the Congress won 55 seats, the Uddhav Thackeray-led Shiv Sena (UBT) secured 43 seats, while the Sharad Pawar-led NCP (SP) won 26 seats. Independents and smaller parties accounted for the remaining seats.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has hailed the resounding success of the BJP and Mahayuti in the Zilla Parishad elections in Maharashtra. In a social media post, Mr Modi said that across rural and urban Maharashtra, the people want good governance and an alliance which works in the spirit of the state’s glorious culture.

The Election Commission of India has appointed Ashok Kumar as the new Chief Electoral Officer of  Delhi. He will assume the charger replacing R Alice Vaz. In a letter to Chief Secretary of Delhi, the Commission mentioned that Mr Ashok Kumar has to assume the charge of the office of the CEO, NCT of Delhi immediately. In January, the poll panel asked the Delhi government to furnish a panel of three IAS officers to select the CEO.

Union Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw flagged off a new passenger train service connecting Sairang in Mizoram and Silchar in Assam. He also inaugurated the Blended Hospitality Service at Gauhati Railway Station. The newly introduced train will operate five days a week, improving rail connectivity between Mizoram and Assam.

On to sports, The International Cricket Council (ICC) has confirmed the participation of Pakistan in all their scheduled ICC Men’s T20 World Cup 2026 matches, including their match against India on 15th of February in Colombo. In a statement released last light, the ICC said that the dialogue between ICC and Pakistan Cricket Board took place as part of a broader engagement with both parties recognising the need for constructive dealings and being united, committed and purposeful in their aspirations to serve the best interests of the game with integrity, neutrality and cooperation.

Opposition parties including Congress, DMK, Samajwadi Party, RJD and Left parties have signed the notice submitted to the secretary General. However TMC has not signed it.

In the Lok Sabha the uproar continued today as well as the opposition did not let the Question Hour function following which the Presiding officer adjourned the house till noon. The house has been adjourned twice without doing any business. When the Lok Sabha assembled for the day, the presiding officer P C Mohan tried to take up Question Hour but the opposition members including Congress, TMC and DMK among others trooped into the well and started sloganeering. Amidst noisy scene, Minister of State for Animal Husbandry and Dairying, S P Singh Baghel could not complete his reply. As the pandemonium continued, the Presiding officer adjourned the House till 12 noon.

When the House assembled at 12 noon, the concerned Ministers laid the papers related to their Ministries. After that presiding officer P C Mohan tried to take up discussion on General Budget 2026-27, but the opposition members continued with their sloganeering. Intervening, Parliamentary Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju urged the opposition members to allow the proceedings of the House, as the precious time of Parliament is being lost due to the disruption.

Despite the appeal from the presiding officer and Minister, the opposition members continued with their protest resulting in the adjournment of the House till 2:00 PM.

Minister for New and Renewable Energy Pralhad Joshi has said, India has achieved a total renewable energy capacity of 272 Gigawatts till date. He said this while replying to a Question during Question Hour in the Rajya Sabha today.

Union Minister of Ports, Shipping and Waterways Sarbananda Sonowal has said that due the transformative policies of the NDA government, India achieved a record 146 million tonnes of cargo movement on National Waterways in  2024-25, up from 18.1 million tonnes in 2013-14. Replying during the question hours in Rajya Sabha, the Minister said the total investment in the in-land waterways has reached over 1700 crore rupees in the last 10 years, which was only 183 crore rupees during the UPA regime. The Minister informed that 111 waterways are currently operational, which help in reducing the logistic cost in the country.

Parliamentary Affairs Minister  Kiren Rijiju has accused the opposition MPs of disrupting the proceedings of Lok Sabha, saying that their conduct reflects that they don’t have any respect for the dignity of the House. Talking to the Media outside the Parliament Mr Rijiju said the Congress Women MPs had crossed over the treasury bench, even reached near the Prime Minister’s seat in the house last week, is a shameful act.

Minister of State for Rural Development Kamlesh Paswan has said that Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act is not merely an employment programme but it is a comprehensive framework designed to accelerate rural development. Mr Paswan said this in a written reply to a Question in Lok Sabha today. He said, the act guarantees one hundred and twenty-five days of wage employment in a financial year for every rural household, instead of hundred days earlier.

In another written reply in the Lok Sabha, Minister of State for Agriculture and farmer Welfare Ramnath Thakur said the Government has disbursed over four lakh crore rupees through 21 instalments since the inception of the PM-KISAN scheme. The Minister said, a farmer-centric digital infrastructure has ensured that the benefits of the scheme reach all  farmers across the country without involvement of any intermediaries. Mr Thakur informed that all payments under the scheme are made through the Direct Benefit Transfer mechanism into Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts of the beneficiaries.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah will deliver the keynote address at the National Conference on Tackling Cyber-Enabled Frauds and Dismantling the Ecosystem in New Delhi this afternoon. On the occasion, he will inaugurate the new Cyber crime Branch of the Central Bureau of Investigation. He will also launch the State Cyber Crime Coordination Centre Dashboard of the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) under the Ministry of Home Affairs.

The Ministry of Home Affairs said the primary objective of the conference is to build a shared understanding of the scale, trends and evolving nature of cyber-enabled frauds in India. During the two day meet, the conference will witness participation from senior officials and experts representing Central and State Law Enforcement Agencies, the Department of Telecommunications, the Department of Financial Services and the Reserve Bank of India.

## Evening News

Rajya Sabha today resumed the discussion on the Union Budget 2026-27. Participating in the discussion, Shiv Sena MP Milind Deora said, during this very politically and economically tumultuous time, India stands alone as a rare island of macroeconomic growth and stability.

Surendra Singh Nagar of BJP said, the Budget prioritizes, increasing economic growth rate, fulfilling people’s expectations and ensuring that in the social sector all schemes reach the people. Kanimozhi of DMK termed the budget anti-poor saying it is detached from the country’s economic realities. Sukhendu Sekhar Ray of TMC said, the budget has allowed for 100 per cent FDI in the insurance sector which endangers the business of nationalised insurance companies.

Masthan Rao Beedha of TDP said, this budget combines macroeconomic stability, infrastructure growth, technological transformation and social inclusion. Later, the house was adjourned for the day, after completing today’s scheduled business.  The discussion will continue tomorrow.

Lok Sabha also took up discussion on the Union Budget 2026-27. Participating in the discussion, Aparajita Sarangi of BJP said, the Budget reflects commitment, conviction and consistency of the Government. She lauded the Government for signing free trade agreements with several nations saying that India has shown its ability to cleverly navigate in a different changing world order.

Earlier, initiating the discussion, Congress MP Shashi Tharoor criticised the Budget saying that government spending has reduced and tax receipts have remained flat.

However, the discussion remained inconclusive. Later, the House was adjourned till 11 AM tomorrow.

Government has set a three-hour deadline for social media platforms to take down AI-generated or deepfake content once it is flagged by the government or ordered by a court. The Government today notified amendments to the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, that formally define AI-generated and synthetic content. The official notification also barred digital platforms from allowing the removal or suppression of AI labels or associated metadata once they have been applied. The new rules will come into force from 20th of this month.

The Election Commission today published the final voter list for the Special Revision (SR) 2026 covering all 126 Assembly constituencies in Assam, with the total electorate standing at 2,49,58,139.

According to the Chief Electoral Officer (CEO), Assam, a total of 2,43,485 voters names were dropped from the draft list, which had recorded 2,52,01,624 voters. The draft voter list for Special Revision 2026 was published on December 27, 2025. Prior to this, a house-to-house verification drive was conducted across the state between November 22nd and December 20th, 2025.  People can appeal to the District Magistrate within 15 days, with a second appeal to the CEO within 30 days.

In compliance with the Supreme Court’s order, the Election Commission of India has extended the final publication of electoral rolls in West Bengal in relation to the Special Intensive Revision SIR till 28th of February. Earlier, yesterday the Supreme Court ordered to extend the scrutiny period by seven days.

Home Minister Amit Shah has emphasized the need to spread more awareness about cyber security among the countrymen. On the occasion, Mr Shah also inaugurated the new Cyber Crime Branch of the Central Bureau of Investigation. A bench headed by Chief Justice of India Surya Kant sought responses from the states after the Union home ministry submitted a comprehensive status report.
