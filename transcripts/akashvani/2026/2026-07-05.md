Prime Minister Narendra Modi launched the next phase of regional air connectivity scheme Viksit UDAN in Jodhpur, Rajasthan yesterday, to give fillip to air travel in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities and remote regions. Addressing a massive gathering, the Prime Minister said that under the new phase of UDAN scheme, smaller cities and remote regions will be connected through air connectivity.

Prime Minister Modi also inaugurated the new terminal building at Jodhpur Airport, marking a major push to India’s civil aviation infrastructure and regional connectivity. Mr Modi highlighted that the architecture and interior of the new terminal are trending on social media.

Civil Aviation Minister Ram Mohan Naidu also said that the launch of the Viksit UDAN Scheme will mark the next chapter of India’s regional connectivity journey. Mr Naidu said, the scheme, launched by Prime Minister with an outlay of over 28,840 crore rupees, will facilitate the development of over 100 new aerodromes and 200 helipads, bringing the dream of affordable air travel closer to millions and connecting the aspirations of a rising India.

Later in the day, Prime Minister Modi inaugurated CG Semi’s state-of-the-art Outsourced Semiconductor Assembly and Test (OSAT) plant in Sanand, Gujarat. The event was attended by Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw and Gujarat Chief Minister Bhupendra Patel, along with global tech leaders. Developed with a massive investment of over 7,500 crore rupees, the advanced facility will have a annual production capacity of up to 5 billion semiconductor chips.

Addressing a large gathering, Prime Minister Modi said that the CG Semi OSAT facility in Sanand will not only strengthen the domestic chip manufacturing ecosystem but also boost technological self-reliance and elevate India’s position in the global semiconductor value chain.

Mr Modi also said, the Sanand plant is geared to produce 20 crore chips annually. He congratulated CG Semi on setting an ambitious future target of scaling production to 500 crore chips per year.

Earlier upon reaching Sanand, the Prime Minister toured the high-tech plant and inspected the production process. He also interacted closely with engineers and operators working inside the facility.

Renowned Pandavani singer and Padma Vibhushan awardee Dr Teejan Bai of Chhattisgarh has passed away. She had been suffering from a prolonged illness and was undergoing treatment at AIIMS Raipur. Dr Teejan Bai will be laid to rest with full State honours this evening at her native village, Ganiyari, in Durg district. With her powerful voice, compelling stage presence and distinctive style of narration, Teejan Bai gave Pandavani a new identity not only across India but also internationally. Her remarkable ability to bring the stories of the Mahabharata to life on stage made her one of the most celebrated exponents of Indian folk culture.

In recognition of her outstanding contribution to Indian folk art, she was honoured with Padma Shri, Padma Bhushan and Padma Vibhushan. President Droupadi Murmu, Vice President C P Radhakrishnan and Prime Minister Narendra Modi have expressed grief over her demise. More from our correspondent:-

“Teejan Bai was born on 24 April 1956 in Ganiyari village of Durg district in Chhattisgarh, into a humble family. From her childhood, she had a deep passion for listening to and singing the stories of the Mahabharata. Despite social opposition and financial hardships, she never gave up her passion. At that time, it was considered inappropriate for women to perform Pandavani in the ‘Kapalik’ style. However, Teejan Bai broke this convention and carved a distinct identity for herself on the stage. With her powerful voice, compelling acting and unique style of presentation, Teejan Bai gave Pandavani recognition not only across India but also on the international stage. Her remarkable ability to bring the stories of the Mahabharata to life made her one of the most distinguished exponents of India’s folk culture. Vikalp Shukla, Akashvani News, Raipur.”
