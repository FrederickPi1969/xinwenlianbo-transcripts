Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) will launch PSLV-C62 / EOS-N1 mission at 10:17 AM today from Satish Dhawan Space Centre in Sriharikota. Our Correspondent ha the details:

This mission will mark the 64th flight of PSLV, India’s most reliable and successful launch vehicle. The mission is being carried out by NewSpace India Limited and represents the ninth dedicated commercial mission to build and launch an Earth Observation satellite. Along with the primary payload EOS-N1 or Anvesha satellite, the rocket will also deploy 15 co-passenger satellites from both Indian and international customers. Developed by Defence Research and Development Organisation, Anvesha satellite is designed to provide cutting-edge imaging capabilities, enabling India to map enemy positions with pinpoint accuracy. Beyond the strategic payload, this mission is historic for another reason – India’s private space sector is stepping into the limelight. For the first time, a single Indian private company, Dhruva Space, based in Hyderabad, is contributing seven satellites to the mission. AASHISH ABRAHAM,AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.

Prime Minister’s flagship platform for Pro-Active Governance and Timely Implementation, PRAGATI, has marked a significant milestone with the successful conduct of its 50th meeting. Since its launch by Prime Minister Narendra Modi in 2015, PRAGATI has transformed governance by enabling real-time monitoring and resolution of key infrastructure projects and public grievances through direct Prime Ministerial review. Over a decade, PRAGATI has helped accelerate decision-making, resolution of issues causing delay in key infrastructure projects, and instilled a strong culture of accountability. All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS), Nagpur has been established as a strategically significant tertiary healthcare institution in the eastern part of Maharashtra for strengthening advanced medical care delivery for the Vidarbha region and the city of Nagpur. The project commenced on 7th of  October,2015 and was taken up as a major healthcare infrastructure initiative with a sanctioned project cost of  one thousand 577 crore rupees. Recognising its importance for public health outcomes in the region, the project was reviewed by Prime Minister Narendra Modi under the PRAGATI mechanism on 30th of August,2017. In this special series, today, our correspondent takes a look at the 4.5 kilomtere long Patna-Ganga Rail cum Road Bridge in Bihar.

“The Patna-Ganga Rail cum Road Bridge, popularly known as J P Setu, was conceived as a transformational multimodal infrastructure intervention across the River Ganga at Patna. The project was designed to resolve a long-standing structural bottleneck in north-south connectivity of Bihar by integrating rail and road transportation on a single bridge structure. The project was sanctioned in November, 2001. The project encountered significant on-ground challenges . Due to the delay in execution its cost increased by five times. The project was reviewed during the PRAGATI meeting by Prime Minister Narendra Modi in 25TH March 2015 and directed to handover the land to railway and expeditiously disburset the compensation. With focused monitoring the project achieved 100 percent  physical progress and was completed on 4th March 2022. The Bridge transformed connectivity across the river Ganga, reduced logistics costs for agricultural produce, industrial goods, and essential commodities and strengthens regional supply chains and market access. With Dipendra Kumar, Anand Kumar, Akashvani News Delhi.”
