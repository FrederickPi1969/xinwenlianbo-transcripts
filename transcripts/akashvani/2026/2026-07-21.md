## Morning News

The National Democratic Alliance (NDA) Parliamentary Party’s weekly strategy meeting, now known as Mangal Milan, will be held in the Parliament House this morning. Prime Minister Narendra Modi is scheduled to address the meeting. The meetings are traditionally held on Tuesdays during Parliament sessions with an intend to demonstrate complete alliance unity. NDA alliance partners will pass a congratulatory motion during the meeting, honouring Mr Modi for becoming the longest-serving elected Prime Minister of India.

President Droupadi Murmu will hold talks with her North Macedonian counterpart Gordana Siljanovska-Davkova in Skopje today. President Murmu is also scheduled to meet Prime Minister Hristijan Mickoski and Speaker of the Assembly Afrim Gashi, and address the Parliament. She will also address the India-North Macedonia Business Forum.

The visit reflects the importance India attaches to strengthening bilateral ties with the three countries and its engagement with the wider Eastern European region. India condemned the attack on the vessel MV GOLDEN LEO while departing the port of Odesa in Ukraine. External Affairs Ministry said four Indian nationals have tragically lost their lives, while one is hospitalized in a critical condition. The Ministry said that at the time of the incident, there were 17 crew members on board, including 5 Indian nationals. The Ministry  has reiterated that targeting commercial shipping and endangering innocent civilian crew members or otherwise impeding freedom of navigation and commerce is deplorable and should be avoided. The Ministry said that the Indian Mission in Ukraine is closely monitoring the situation and is making every effort to extend all possible assistance to those affected. The Government of India extended deepest condolences to the families of the deceased Indian nationals and wished them speedy and full recovery.

External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar met his Panamanian counterpart Javier Eduardo Martinez-Acha Vasquez in New Delhi. During the meeting, Dr Jaishankar underscored the importance of deepening India’s engagement with Panama as a gateway to Latin America and the Caribbean region. Both leaders held wide-ranging discussions, including advancing trade, business, development, maritime, health, agriculture, education and digital cooperation. They also spoke about engagement at the multilateral fora.

Earlier, External Affairs Minister expressed confidence that the visit of Panamanian Foreign Minister Javier Eduardo Martinez-Acha Vasquez will further strengthen bilateral ties across all sectors.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has congratulated Andy Burnham on assuming office as Prime Minister of the United Kingdom. In a social media post, Mr Modi said that India and the UK are bound by shared democratic values and enjoy wide-ranging cooperation across trade, investment, technology, defence and people-to-people relations. He noted that as India-UK Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA) enters into force this month, the bilateral partnership between the two nations is poised to grow even stronger. The Prime Minister added that he is looking forward to working closely with Mr Burnham to deepen the India-UK Comprehensive Strategic Partnership further and advance the shared Vision 2035.

In an official order released yesterday, the government said that the enhanced assistance will be disbursed to all eligible beneficiaries through the existing Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) mechanism.

The Election Commission is organising an international conference in Kolkata today to discuss the process of making the postal ballot system more inclusive, secure, and accessible. The conference is being jointly organised by the India International Institute of Democracy and Election Management (IIIDEM) and International IDEA. We have a report :

Election officials from all 24 electoral districts of West Bengal will attend the event, along with election administrators, policymakers, academics, and election experts from India and several other countries. The discussions will focus on ways to make postal ballots and special voting arrangements more inclusive, accessible, and secure. West Bengal Chief Electoral Officer Neelam Meena will inaugurate the conference. The programme will feature thematic sessions on postal ballots, maintaining electoral integrity, preventing fraud, audit mechanisms, the legal framework, technology-enabled special voting systems, and the future of remote voting. During the international session, election experts from Nepal, Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, and other countries will share their experiences virtually. Alongside India’s experience, international best practices will also be discussed. Sandipon Dasgupta, AKASHVANI, Kolkata.

## Midday News

The National Democratic Alliance (NDA) Parliamentary Party meeting, now known as Mangal Milan, was held at the G.M.C. Balayogi Auditorium in Parliament House today. During the meeting, NDA alliance partners passed a congratulatory motion honouring Prime Minister Narendra Modi for becoming the longest-serving elected Prime Minister of India.

Talking to media, Parliamentary Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju said that during the meeting, Prime Minister Modi called for coordinated efforts by all stakeholders to prevent paper leaks in future examinations across the country. He said, Mr Modi reiterated the government’s commitment to ensuring strict action against those involved in examination paper leaks and safeguarding the interests of the country’s youth. The Prime Minister said all those involved in the recent NEET paper leak case have been arrested, with 13 accused currently lodged in jail.

Mr Rijiju said Prime Minister Modi also spoke about the Free Trade Agreements that India has signed with several countries. During the meeting, Mr Modi said that in every Free Trade Agreement, the interests of Indian farmers have been given the highest priority.

Rashtriya Lok Morcha chief Upendra Kushwaha said that the Prime Minister reaffirmed the government’s commitment to ensuring transparency and fairness in the conduct of competitive examinations during the NDA Parliamentary Party meeting. Talking exclusively to Akashvani News at Parliament Premises, Mr. Kushwaha said the re-examination for NEET was conducted with complete integrity and future examinations will also be held in the same transparent manner.

Both the Houses of Parliament were adjourned till 2 PM noon over the opposition uproar on various issues, including the NEET paper leak case. When the Lok Sabha reassembled after the first adjournment at 12 noon, the House took up motions for election to different committees of the Parliament amid opposition uproar. Presiding Officer Sandhya Ray urged the Members to return to their seats and allow the house to function smoothly. She said, Zero Hour belongs to the Members and the entire country is watching the proceedings of the House. Despite her appeal, the opposition members came to the well with placards and carried on sloganeering over NEET and student protests. It led to adjournment of the Lok Sabha till 2 PM.

Earlier, when the House began its proceedings at 11 AM, Speaker Om Birla tried to take up the Question Hour, but the opposition members raised the issue of the paper leak of the NEET examination. .

When the Upper House met after the first adjournment at 12 noon, Chairman CP Radhakrishnan appealed to opposition Members to go to their seats and allow functioning of the House, but the Opposition did not pay heed to his advice. Amid Uproar, the Rajya Sabha was adjourned till 2 PM.

Leader of the Opposition in the Lok Sabha Rahul Gandhi today met Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla and said the Opposition wants a discussion on the issue of students. Talking to reporters outside Parliament, Mr Gandhi said the issue concerns students and the future of the youth.

The Government has refuted several social media posts claiming that a girl died on the spot during the protests in Delhi yesterday. The fact check unit of Press Information Bureau (PIB) has clarified that the claim is fake and said that no protest-related death has occurred in Delhi.

India today summoned the Russian Chargé d’Affaires, Vladimir Ladanov, following the attack on the commercial vessel MV Golden Leo on the 19th of this month, which resulted in the tragic loss of four Indian lives. Conveying grave concerns and unequivocal condemnation of the attack, the Ministry of External Affairs said that such attacks undermine the safety, security, and stability of international maritime commerce. The Ministry said that Mr Ladanov was requested to convey India’s strong concerns to his authorities that the targeting of commercial shipping and the resulting loss of innocent civilian lives is unacceptable and must be avoided.

The Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation has granted marketing authorization for India’s first dengue vaccine, the attenuated dengue vaccine (Qdenga®), to strengthen national dengue prevention efforts.
