## Morning News

Both Houses of Parliament have given their approval to refer the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Amendment Bill, 2026, to the Joint Parliamentary Committee (JPC) for further scrutiny. The bill aims to further amend the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act, 2010. The objective of the Bill is to make the use of foreign contributions more transparent and accountable.

Minister of State for Home Nityanand Rai moved a motion in the Lok Sabha to refer the Bill to a JPC comprising members of both the Houses for wider consultations. The committee will consist of 21 members from the Lok Sabha, nominated by the Speaker, and 10 members from the Rajya Sabha, nominated by the Chairman. The Committee will submit its report to this House by the last day of the first week of the winter session.

The Rajya Sabha also approved the motion to nominate 10 members from its side to the Joint Committee for consideration of the FCRA Amendment Bill, 2026.

Today is the last day of the Monsoon Session of Parliament. The session which began on the 20th of last month will conclude today. The Rajya Sabha is scheduled to take up the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Amendment Bill, 2026 today for consideration and passage. Both Houses of Parliament are also scheduled to take up Private Member’s Business today.

The Calcutta High Court has cancelled OBC all certificates issued under the OBC-A and OBC-B classifications introduced by the previous Trinamool Congress government in West Bengal.

”A division bench of Justice Rajasekhar Mantha and Justice Anuj Singh yesterday while delivering the judgement said the two classifications no longer exist in the state and certificates issued under the earlier system have lost their validity. Holders of these certificates will now be treated as belonging to the general category.

The controversy dates back to 2010, when the state divided OBC communities into OBC-A and OBC-B categories. In 2024, the High Court had struck down the inclusion of 66 communities in the OBC list and ordered a fresh survey.

After the BJP government came to power in 2026, it abolished the OBC-A and OBC-B classification and introduced a single OBC category. SANDIPON DASGUPTA RNU KOLKATA.”

The Punjab and Haryana High Court has stayed the appointment process for the posts of 454 Pharmacy Officers over allegations of a paper leak in the recruitment. The examination for which was held on July 19th , this year. The posts were advertised by Baba Farid University of Health Sciences, Faridkot. The next hearing of the case has been scheduled on September 17.

”The stay orders of Punjab and Haryana High Court have come amid a political storm over the examination. Within a few hours of the alleged paper leak through high-tech pen cameras and other instruments, Punjab Police claimed to have busted an interstate cheating racket after its crackdown across 25 examination centers in three districts – Faridkot, Kotkapura and Ferozepur. The police arrested seven key conspirators and 28 candidates who were directly involved in this case.  The probe revealed that the cheating racket charged desperate candidates exorbitant sums ranging between 3.5 lakh rupees and 13 lakh rupees to guarantee passing marks. Nearly 7 thousand candidates had appeared for the post. RAJESH BALI/AKASHVANI NEWS/JALANDHAR.”

BJP spokesperson Sambit Patra has alleged that the Punjab government misled the public over the alleged paper leak in the Pharmacy Officers recruitment examination conducted by Baba Farid University of Health Sciences in July. Talking to the reporters, he said that the Punjab and Haryana High Court has stayed the examination and made stern observations, including questioning why the exam was not stopped earlier and whether it should be conducted afresh.

The Monsoon Session of Parliament has concluded, with both Houses being adjourned sine die today. The session began on July 20 and saw a total of 19 sittings over 25 days. In all 12 bills were passed by the Parliament including the Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Amendment Bill. More from our correspondent:

“Several Bills including of national importance were passed during the monsoon session. The Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Amendment Bill, 2026, the Prevention of Insults to National Honour (Amendment) Bill, the Tribunals Reforms Bill and the Kerala (Alteration of Name) Bill, 2026, were passed. The Parliament also referred the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Amendment Bill, 2026, to the Joint Parliamentary Committee for further scrutiny. The objective of the Bill is to make the use of foreign contributions more transparent and accountable. However, the session also saw repeated disruptions over the issue of students’ protests over the NEET paper leak, alleged police action against protesting students and alleged anomalies in Ayodhya Ram Temple donation.  Anand Kumar, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

In his concluding remarks, Rajya Sabha Chairman C P Radhakrishnan expressed concern over the repeated disruptions in the House. He said, the House managed to fulfil its core legislative duties by passing and returning 12 Bills. Mr Radhakrishnan informed that the House functioned for only 37 hours and 49 minutes.

Earlier, the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Amendment Bill, 2026 was passed by the Parliament, with the Rajya Sabha approving it today. Coal and Mines Minister G Kishan Reddy moved the Bill to further amend the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957.

Union Parliamentary Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju has accused the opposition of running away from discussions in Parliament when the government was ready for it. He said, the conduct of opposition MPs is not good for democracy, especially for parliamentary democracy. Addressing a press conference in New Delhi, Mr Rijiju said that Parliament is meant for debate, discussion and legislative business, but continuous sloganeering and protests had affected the functioning of the House in the Monsoon session. Mr Rijiju said that 12 Bills were passed during the Monsoon Session, but only one of them was discussed in both Houses. He stated that during this session, productivity stood at 19 per cent in the Lok Sabha and 39 per cent in the Rajya Sabha. The Minister expressed concern that several first-time MPs were unable to gain parliamentary experience because of repeated disruptions.

Country’s exports including merchandise and services registered a robust growth of 13.3 per cent in July this year by reaching to 80.14 billion US dollar. The country’s exports was 70.72 billion US dollars in the same month last year.

According to the provisional data released by the Commerce Ministry, the export performance was led by merchandise shipments, which jumped over 19.6 per cent to over 44 billion US dollars last month from 36.98 billion in the same month last year. More in this report:

“Among India’s major export destinations, the United States, China, Singapore, Kenya and Malaysia recorded positive growth in July.  India has also recorded positive export growth to the UAE, the Netherlands, Germany, South Africa, Tanzania, Australia, Sri Lanka, Italy, and Vietnam during this period. During first four months of the current financial year, Singapore, China, Tanzania, South Africa and Sri Lanka were among the major destinations registering significant increases in export value. On the import side, Russia, China, Oman, Taiwan and the United States recorded growth in July, while Russia, China, Oman, the United States and Brazil registered higher import values during April-July period.

India’s exports to the West Asian region rose by 8.62 per cent year-on-year to 5.7 billion dollars in July, despite global uncertainties. Export to the West Asian countries stood at 5.2 billion dollars in the same month last year.

India’s exports to the United States increased 12.85 per cent to 9.02 billion dollars in July. Country’s shipments to China jumped 64.57 per cent to 2.2 billion dollars. Swati Rakheja, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

A terrorism monitoring team of the United Nations Security Council (UNSC) has declared the deadly Red Fort suicide car bombing in Delhi that killed 11 people the work of Al-Qaida in the Indian Subcontinent (AQIS). The report by the Analytical Support and Sanctions Monitoring Team on terrorism to the Security Council said, the attack in last November was officially attributed to AQIS.

The report said AQIS has continued to evolve from a fragmented group into a regional terrorist organisation. It has established logistical and financial networks, while operating through small, decentralised and scattered cells rather than large units. The report also highlighted that AQIS is attempting to exploit Bangladesh as a base to establish new cells.

Against this backdrop, the team warned that terrorist threats intensified in South Asia.

India emerged as the overall champions at the Commonwealth Fencing Championship by winning an impressive 35 medals after four days of competition held at Lagos in Nigeria. Indian contingent secured thirteen gold, eight silver, and fourteen bronze medals at the event across the Under 23 and senior competitions.

The senior Indian fencers claimed five gold medals in six events on the fourth and final day of the competition, securing honours in Men’s Sabre, Women’s Sabre, Men’s E’pée, Women’s Epée and Women’s foil, as well as a silver in Men’s foil.

The performance also helped India win the Wilkinson Sword Trophy, awarded by the Commonwealth Fencing Federation to the country with the best overall results.
