## Morning News

External Affairs Minister Dr S Jaishankar has called for greater cooperation among Global South countries to address rising geopolitical tensions, economic challenges, climate shocks and pressures on the multilateral system. The Minister was addressing the meeting of Global South Countries on the sidelines of the 81st UN General Assembly in New York yesterday. Dr Jaishankar said the global situation was marked by increasing conflicts, pressure on supply chains and trade, fragmented investment flows, growing debt vulnerabilities and inadequate development finance. He said Global South countries should work together to address key challenges, including supply chain, finance and connectivity bottlenecks.

Dr Jaishankar also met Italy’s Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Antonio Tajani on the sidelines of the United Nations General Assembly. In a social media post, the Minister said their discussions focused on the Gulf situation, the India-Middle East-Europe Economic Corridor (IMEC), and the Ukraine conflict. He said the two countries aimed to expand collaboration and increase trade ties.

Addressing the India-CELAC Foreign Ministers’ Meeting during the UN High-Level Week 2026 in New York, the External Affairs Minister called for stronger economic and development partnerships between India and the Community of Latin American and Caribbean States (CELAC). He said, India-CELAC trade has now crossed 50 billion US dollars annually. Dr Jaishankar said, India emerged as one of the world’s major economies over the past decade, with its GDP more than doubling and growth remaining robust. He added that India today combines a large and increasingly skilled human resource base with increasing capabilities, growing market, strong digital infrastructure and expanding technology capacities. He said India’s engagement with the Global South went beyond its national interest and reflected its commitment to shared prosperity.

US President Donald Trump has said his administration strongly supports the India-Middle East-Europe Economic Corridor. He said, there is a need to shift Middle East energy infrastructure away from Iranian checkpoints amid multiple strikes on West Asian countries during the war. President Trump made the remarks while addressing a meeting with Gulf Cooperation Council and Arab leaders on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly in New York yesterday. President Trump said, attacks on commercial shipping in neighbouring countries had highlighted the need to shift the region’s energy infrastructure. He said the region was close to overcoming longstanding challenges that have plagued this region for decades.

Addressing the inaugural session of the BRICS Tax Heads meeting in New Delhi today, Ms Sitharaman said, the BRICS countries are large developing economies and source jurisdictions that have built significant domestic tax capacity from a low base and their perspective is essential to making these negotiations fair and lasting.

Indian Railways has achieved a significant milestone in railway safety with the successful commissioning of KAVACH Version 4.0 for the first time on South Central Railway. Railway Ministry said, the upgradation from KAVACH Version 3.2 to Version 4.0 has been successfully completed over 108 Route Kilometres on the Malkajgiri-Kamareddi section of Hyderabad Division.

KAVACH 4.0 brings enhanced safety, advanced technology, higher reliability and safer train operations. More from our correspondent.

“KAVACH is an indigenously developed Automatic Train Protection system designed to enhance operational safety. The system assists the Loco Pilot in maintaining trains within prescribed speed limits and can automatically apply brakes when required if the Loco Pilot fails to do so. KAVACH is designed to help prevent Signal Passing at Danger, control train speed in critical situations and reduce the risk of collisions. The system also supports safer train operations during inclement weather conditions. The successful commissioning of KAVACH 4.0 on South Central Railway represents an important step towards strengthening indigenous railway safety technology and expanding advanced Automatic Train Protection capabilities across the Indian Railways network. With Dipendra Kumar, Ankit Kumar, Akashvani News Delhi.”
