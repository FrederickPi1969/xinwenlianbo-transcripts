Pradhan Mantri Skilling and Employability Transformation through Upgraded ITIs (PM-SETU) has completed one year today. The PM-SETU scheme of the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship was launched on this day last year. The initiative  seeks to strengthen industry-led governance, upgrade training infrastructure and promote demand-driven courses in line with evolving workforce requirements. More from our correspondent:

PM SETU aims to enhance the overall quality and relevance of vocational training by transforming Industrial Training Institutes (ITIs) into modern, industry-aligned training institutions. The scheme has a total outlay of over 60 thousand crore rupees, comprising 30 thousand crore rupees from the Centre, 20 thousand crore rupees from States and 10 thousand crore rupees from industry. ITIs provide vocational training to equip youth with practical skills for employment and industry. Under the Craftsmen Training Scheme (CTS), training is offered through Government and Private Industrial Training Institutes in engineering and non-engineering trades. Its course duration varies from six months to two years, depending on the trade. The Directorate General of Training is the apex organisation responsible for the development and coordination of vocational training at the national level. The total number of ITIs, including Government and Private, in the country has increased to over 13 thousand in 2026 from over nine thousand in 2014, indicating an increase of 42 percent. Further, enrolment in ITIs across the country has also increased to 14.70 lakh in the academic session 2025-26 from 9.51 lakh in the academic session 2014-15, indicating an increase of 54 percent. At the national level, the Ministry has constituted a National Steering Committee (NSC) to provide overall direction for the scheme. With Anand and Rishu, Saklen Akhtar, Akashvani News Delhi.

The curtain falls on the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games 2026 today, bringing more than two weeks of action packed competition across 43 disciplines to an end. India finishes fourth in the overall medal standings. We have a report:

The closing ceremony of the 20th Asian Games will be held at Paloma Mizuho Stadium in Nagoya, Japan, beginning at 2:30 PM IST. It will feature the traditional closing proceedings, including the grand parade of athletes, the lowering of the Asian Games flag, and the formal handover to the next host city, Doha, Qatar, for the 2030 edition. Proudly carrying the Indian Tricolour will be India’s women’s flag-bearer and shooting star, Neeru Dhanda. The double gold medallist will lead India’s delegation. India finishes fourth in the overall medal standings, behind China, Japan, and Korea, with an impressive 85 medals- including 21 gold, 27 silver, and 37 bronze. This is India’s second-largest medal haul in Asian Games history, behind only the record performance at Hangzhou 2023. India’s women’s hockey team made history by winning gold for the first time since 1982, ending a 44-year wait, while the men’s hockey team won back-to-back Asian Games titles for the first time. In archery, India’s women’s recurve team secured the country’s first-ever Asian Games gold in the event, while Kumkum Mohod became the first Indian woman to win individual recurve gold. In golf, Pranavi Urs became India’s first woman golfer to win an Asian Games gold medal. India’s boxing contingent scripted history with three gold medals, the nation’s first-ever triple-gold haul in a single Asian Games edition. In weightlifting, Mirabai Chanu’s silver gave India its first medal in the sport since 1998. India proudly signs off from Aichi-Nagoya 2026, etching another glorious chapter into its sporting history. Kaumudee Pareek, Akashvani News, Delhi.
