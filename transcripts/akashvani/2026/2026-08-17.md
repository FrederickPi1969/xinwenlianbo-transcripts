## Morning News

Self-enumeration for Census 2027 will begin today in Ladakh and identified snowbound areas of Jammu and Kashmir, and continue till August 31. Our correspondent has filed this report:

“Ladakh would become the first Union Territory in the country to complete the Census 2027 exercise, with Population Enumeration scheduled from September 1 to 30. The self Enumeration is beginning today. Around 637 Enumeration Blocks (EBs) would be created for complete coverage of Ladakh.

Census  Operations for Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh Amit Sharma said the September schedule had been planned in view of the region’s harsh winter conditions, difficult terrain and limited accessibility of Ladakh. Population Enumeration will collect detailed information on population distribution, demographic composition, education, economic activity, migration, disability, language, fertility and other population characteristics.

Census 2027 is being conducted digitally, with House Listing Operations completed from June 1 to 30. The first phase achieved 100 per cent digital data collection.Yangchan Dolma for AkashVani from Leh Ladakh.”

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has announced re-test for three papers of the UGC-NET examination – English, Commerce and Sociology following complaints about several errors in these paper. NTA said, the English paper will be held on 9th September from 9 am to 12 noon in Shift 1, while the Commerce paper will be conducted the same day from 3 pm to 6 pm in Shift 2. The Sociology paper will be held on 10th September from 9 am to 12 noon in Shift 1.

The NTA had conducted the UGC-NET June 2026 examination from 22nd to 30th June across 87 subjects for the award of Junior Research Fellowship, eligibility for assistant professor, and admission to PhD programmes.

NTA had formed a committee to enquire into and look into these errors. The Committee recommended that, in the interest of holding a fair and error-free examination, these three papers should be re-conducted.
