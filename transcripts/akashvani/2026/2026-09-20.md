## Morning News

Government has said that Biometric Aadhaar authentication is mandatory for subsidised domestic LPG refills from 1st October this year. Domestic LPG consumers will need to have completed Biometric Aadhaar Authentication to book refills at the regulated Retail Selling Price with applicable subsidy. More from our correspondent:

Biometric Aadhaar Authentication ensures that subsidised LPG reaches only genuine and deserving households. Linking each connection to the Aadhaar-authenticated consumer will help prevent diversion of subsidised domestic cylinders for commercial and industrial use. It will remove duplicate and ineligible connections, and make subsidy delivery transparent and targeted. The P&G Ministry said the process is not meant to deny LPG to any genuine household, and every eligible consumer has several easy ways to complete it. It said 27.43 crore consumers have already completed authentication and do not need to do anything further. Authentication can be completed at the time of delivery, at the distributor’s showroom, or on Oil Marketing Company (OMC) mobile apps. The Petroleum and Natural Gas Ministry has urged consumers who have not yet completed Biometric Aadhaar Authentication to do that before 1 October 2026. With Suparna, Saklen Akhtar, Akashvani News, Delhi.

The Nasha Mukt Yuva for Viksit Bharat- 100 Weeks Nasha Mukt Yuva Abhiyan will witness a major nationwide youth mobilisation today. The Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports said that Rashtriya Sankalp Samaroh is being organised across 1,067 locations in the country. It added that this programme will be organised across 471 District MY Bharat Offices, along with 596 locations of partner organizations. The Samaroh will bring the campaign’s message directly to young people through Nasha Mukt Pledge, Signature Campaign, thematic street plays, slogan writing and youth-led discussions.

Bharatiya Janata Party Punjab’s “Nasha Mukt Punjab Yatras”, which the party National President, Nitin Nabin launched from the border district of Pathankot on September 18 is getting a remarkable response from the people of the state.

Yesterday, Delhi Chief Minister, Rekha Gupta, while launching the second out of four phases of the Yatras from Fatehgarh Sahib, termed it as a movement of new beginning in Punjab. She said ‘Nasha Mukt Punjab Yatra’ is not merely a political programme but a call for collective action to free Punjab from drugs, crime and fear. More from our correspondent:

This 13-day long ‘Nasha Mukt Punjab Yatra’ which has started with a tagline of – ‘Bhajpa Da Naara – Nasha Mukana Saara’ (भाजपा दा नारा – नशा मुकाना सारा) is focusing on three major challenges presently confronting Punjab. These are growing drug menace, deteriorating law and order, and corruption at every level of governance. The third yatra will be launched today by Union Minister of State for Law and Justice, Arjun Ram Meghwal from Fazilka’s Sadiqi border and tomorrow, the fourth phase will be flagged off by Union Agriculture Minister, Shivraj Singh Chauhan and Haryana Chief Minister, Nayab Singh Saini from Talwandi Sabo in Bathinda. The ‘Nasha Mukt Punjab Yatra’ will conclude on September 30th at Jalandhar in which Union Home Minister, Amit Shah is scheduled to participate. Rajesh Bali, Akashvani News, Jalandhar

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has distributed more than 51 thousand appointment letters to newly appointed youth in various Government Organisations at the 20th Rozgar Mela through video conferencing yesterday. Speaking on the occasion, Mr Modi emphasized the pivotal role of youth in building a developed and self-reliant India. He said that in recent years, the Central government has consistently focused on equipping the youth with the skills and opportunities needed to prepare for the future.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi is completing 25 years in public service, a journey defined by the mantra of Jan Seva Se Desh Seva, and a relentless commitment that continues today.

Guided by this vision for Seva and Sushasan, the Government has been ceaselessly working towards achieving the goal of a Viksit Bharat by 2047. Through inclusive development and social empowerment, this dedication is transforming lives.

Today, in this special series, Seva Sankalp Abhiyan, we bring you an instance of how this vision has been translated into action through the introduction of the Goods and Services tax or the GST.

“The GST was introduced in 2017 with the aim of consolidating a fragmented internal market divided by multiple central and state taxes, cascading taxation and border check-posts. Seventeen taxes and 13 cesses were brought under a common framework, making One Nation, One Tax, One Market a practical reality. Before GST, interstate movement could involve repeated tax checks, paperwork and long waits. The recent reforms in GST rates have been welcomed by both industry and the common people. Speaking to Akashvani News, Chairman of MVIRDC World Trade Center Mumbai and President of All India Association of Industries Dr. Vijay Kalantri said that the rationalisation of tax rates has encouraged citizens to pay taxes.

Neha Kulkarni, a working professional from Mumbai, said that the reduction in GST on essential items has lowered her family’s monthly expenses and resulted in significant savings.

Over the last nine years, GST reforms have simplified taxation, eased compliance and brought greater relief to consumers and businesses, contributing to a significant transformation in the lives of citizens. Prarthna, Akashvani News, Mumbai.”

The Election Commission will hold special camps across all polling station locations in the national capital today. The day-long drive aims to assist citizens with fresh inclusions, objections for proposed inclusions or deletions of name in electoral rolls, and corrections to particulars in the electoral rolls. Booth Level Officers will remain present at designated polling booths to receive applications. People can submit Form 6 for new voter enrollment, Form 7 for objections or deletion of entries, and Form 8 for any corrections or updating of details in the electoral rolls.

Addressing the media regarding the ongoing SIR process in the national capital, Delhi Chief Electoral Officer Ashok Kumar has clarified that Electoral Registration Officers (EROs) can consider documents other than those listed in the notice during hearings on the case to case basis.

Akhil Bharatiya Vidyarthi Parishad (ABVP) has secured three major posts in the Delhi University Students’ Union (DUSU) elections. ABVP’s Yash Dabas won the post of President, while Riya Chhawdi secured victory for Secretary and Lakshya Raj Singh for Joint Secretary. However, independent candidate Deepanshu Shokeen emerged victorious for the position of Vice President.

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh and Home Minister Amit Shah have congratulated ABVP for their success in the DUSU elections.

Iran has communicated its conditions to international mediators for restarting negotiation with United States. It has also warned that Tehran is prepared for a decisive war if Washington rejects its demands. Mohsen Rezaei, secretary of Iran’s Supreme National Security Council, said Iran’s conditions included an end to the war on all fronts, the release of frozen Iranian funds and an end to the naval blockade. In a separate post on X, Rezaei said Iran has announced seven conditions for beginning negotiations with Washington and described Tehran’s position as clear and unambiguous.
