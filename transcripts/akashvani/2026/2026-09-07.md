## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that over the past several years, the relationship between India and France has reached new heights. He said, together with President Macron, both countries are building a Special Global Strategic Partnership. Speaking during inauguration of Swaminarayan Hindu Mandir in Paris through video conferencing, Mr Modi said, From technology, AI and defence to space and climate action, both nations are moving forward together with new momentum and renewed energy. He said, both sides also celebrating this year as the India-France Year of Innovation. The Prime Minister said, the India-France Strategic Partnership is special because it rests upon the strong foundation of the longstanding cultural and political connections.

He said, history bears witness to the sacrifices of Indian soldiers in France, a history that continues to live in the collective memory and hearts. Mr Modi said, those interested in languages also know the important role French scholars have played in promoting and popularising Sanskrit. He added that now, through the magnificent BAPS Swaminarayan temple, a new chapter is being added to the India-France deep cultural relationship.

Delhi Chief Minister Rekha Gupta has ordered a magisterial inquiry into the building collapse incident in Satya Niketan area of New Delhi, and directed registration of an FIR against the building owner. The Chief Minister’s Office said the decision was taken following an on-site review and a preliminary assessment of the incident. The CMO said those found responsible for the grave incident will be held fully accountable and dealt with under the strictest provisions of law.

Death toll in the incident has risen to six following the recovery of one more body late last night. Search and rescue operations continued at the site as teams work to clear the debris and look for any trapped person. Teams from the NDRF, Fire Service, Delhi Police, and other agencies are working at the site to clear the debris.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah spoke to Delhi Chief Minister, Delhi Police Commissioner and Director General of NDRF and assessed the situation on the ground. Mr Shah said, the injured are being provided with all necessary medical assistance.

The building was being used as a paying guest accommodation. A senior police officer said repair work was underway in the basement when the structure collapsed at around 1.30 pm yesterday.

Sri Venkateswara College of the University of Delhi will remain closed today in the wake of the tragic building collapse.

The All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS) said that it received eleven patients who sustained injuries in the building collapse at Satya Niketan in New Delhi.

Of the eleven patients, five were brought dead to the Trauma Centre, while another patient, who was in critical condition, later succumbed to his injuries.

According to the AIIMS, three patients have been admitted and are undergoing treatment. One patient underwent limb surgery, while another, who sustained minor injuries, was discharged after being kept under observation.

Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) has issued a clarification on reports circulating in the media regarding the speculations and misinformation that there is an attempt to privatise ISRO or reduce its role. In a social media post, ISRO said that it will neither be privatised nor will its importance be diminished. It added that participation of the private sector in space is aimed at enabling ISRO to focus even more strongly on the next generation of India’s space programme, while industry scales up mature technologies and capabilities.

ISRO said the space-sector reforms aim to create a larger, stronger and globally competitive Indian space ecosystem. It said the reforms should therefore be seen as ecosystem expansion and capability multiplication, rather than privatisation.

The seven core OPEC+ countries –  Saudi Arabia, Russia, Iraq, Kuwait, Kazakhstan, Algeria, and Oman met virtually yesterday to review global market conditions and outlook. They decided to keep its oil output policy unchanged for October amid global uncertainty. A report :

The decision comes after OPEC+ increased production by around 188-thousand barrels per day for September, completing the rollback of a voluntary production cut of around 1.65 million barrels per day that had been introduced in 2023.

The meeting comes against the backdrop of continuing disruptions to oil exports, including those linked to the conflict involving Iran and disruption around the Strait of Hormuz. These disruptions have limited the impact of OPEC+ production decisions on global oil supplies and prices. It is worth nothing that the United Arab Emirates (UAE) had left the alliance in May this year, citing national interest and commitment to contributing effectively to meeting the market’s pressing needs.

OPEC+ is expected to meet again in October to review market conditions and production levels. ISHANI, AKASHVANI NEWS.

Iran’s top security official has announced that a new restricted zone will be declared outside the Strait of Hormuz in the coming days, and that any vessel entering the area without coordination with them will be placed on an Iranian sanctions list. Major General Mohsen Rezaei, secretary of the Supreme National Security Council (SNSC) said that the zone will begin from the US Navy’s blockade line and extend into parts of the Persian Gulf. Ships that move toward the strait from either side and enter this area will face sanctions, the Iranian State Media reported.

The flood situation in Bihar remains serious, with the Ganga and several other rivers flowing above the danger mark. However, there is some relief from the Sone River. Discharge from the Indrapuri Barrage has dropped sharply from 5.54 lakh cusecs on Wednesday to 1.96 lakh cusecs by Sunday afternoon. In football, India qualified for the AFC U20 Asian Cup main tournament for the first time in 20 years, after a 3-nil win over Bangladesh in their final qualifying match in Tashkent yesterday.

The victory, India’s second in the qualifiers, took them to six points from three matches. India then waited for the result of the Syria-Uzbekistan match.

Uzbekistan’s 3-nil win over Syria confirmed India’s qualification as one of the seven best runners-up across the eight groups. India last featured in the AFC U20 Asian Cup in 2006.
