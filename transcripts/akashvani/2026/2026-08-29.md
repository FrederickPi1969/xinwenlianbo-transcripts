## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will embark on a four-day visit to Uzbekistan and Kyrgyz Republic beginning today. On the first leg of his two-day visit, he will reach Uzbekistan. The Prime Minister’s Office in a statement said during his visit, he will hold delegation-level talks with President Mirziyoyev to further strengthen the strategic partnership between India and Uzbekistan. Our Correspondent has the details.

“During the visit, both Leaders will have discussions on the entire gamut of issues of bilateral Importance in Tashkent. India has longstanding and wide-ranging cooperation with Uzbekistan in the field of defence. A Joint Working Group on Defence was established in 2019. India and Uzbekistan regularly participate in joint military exercises “DUSTLIK”, and the seventh edition of this exercise was conducted in Uzbekistan in April 2026. In View of this, the visit of PM Modi becomes very important and is expected to lay the foundation for further cementing strategic and business partnership between both countries. The government has said that more than 115 lakh hectares of farmland have been brought under micro-irrigation under the Per Drop More Crop scheme as of July this year. The initiative aims to promote efficient use of water at the farm level and improve water-use efficiency in agriculture. The initiative began in January 2006 as the Centrally Sponsored Scheme for Micro Irrigation. From 2015-16 to 2021-22, it was officially named Per Drop More Crop and implemented as a core component of the Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana.

Under this scheme, the government provides financial assistance of up to five hectares per beneficiary to install micro irrigation systems. Small and marginal farmers receive 55 per cent, and other farmers receive 45 per cent financial assistance. Central assistance of over eight thousand 123 crore rupees has been released in the last three years. The scheme promotes precision water management to enhance crop productivity and increase farmers’ incomes.
