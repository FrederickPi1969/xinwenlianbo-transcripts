## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will be arriving in Nice, France, this evening, embarking on a two-nation European tour spanning France and Slovakia. This is the Prime Minister’s seventh official visit to France since 2014 and carries special diplomatic weight – Prime Minister Modi and French President Emmanuel Macron are set to hold their first bilateral summit since the elevation of India-France ties to a Special Global Strategic Partnership earlier this year. The Prime Minister will also participate in the G7 Summit in Evian, reaffirming India’s standing as an indispensable voice of the Global South on the world stage.

“On arrival this evening, Prime Minister Modi will proceed to his hotel, where he will attend a community reception bringing together members of the Indian diaspora from across the south of France.

The engagements on 14th June open on a note of innovation and ambition. Prime Minister Modi, alongside President Macron, will inaugurate Bharat Innovates – India’s flagship innovation showcase – at the Palais des Expositions de Nice. More than 120 Indian startups and companies will come together with leading investors and business leaders from France and across the world, spotlighting India as a global destination for deep-tech, artificial intelligence and entrepreneurship.

Thereafter, the two leaders will proceed to the historic Villa Kerylos for their bilateral summit, where defence, space, civil nuclear energy, technology and people-to-people ties are expected to feature prominently on the agenda.

India and France share over 25 years of Strategic Partnership, with deep cooperation in defence, space and a fast-growing economic relationship, with bilateral trade crossing USD 15.82 billion in 2025-26. SHAILESH MENON FOR AKASHVANI NEWS, FROM NICE FRANCE.”

After concluding his engagements in Nice, the Prime Minister will visit Slovak Republic from 14 to 16th June. Our Correspondent Smithy M Palluruthy from Bratislava has filed this report…

“The Slovak capital, Bratislava, is all set for the maiden visit of the Indian Prime Minister. Prime Minister Narendra Modi will hold talks with Slovak Prime Minister Fico during the visit and explore new avenues of cooperation. The Prime Minister will also meet President Pellegrini during the visit. The Prime Minister’s state visit assumes greater significance in view of the continuous increase in bilateral trade, which crossed USD 1 billion in 2024 and reached USD 1.8 billion last year. The visit will further reinforce India’s commitment to strengthening cooperation with European Union member states. Smithy M Palluruthy, Akashvani News, From Bratislava in Slovak Republic.”

The Five-day BRICS Agriculture Conference concluded at Indore in Madhya Pradesh today. At the end of the conference, the ‘Indore Declaration’ was adopted unanimously. The declaration outlines key decisions on food security, farmers’ welfare, climate-friendly farming, agricultural trade and use of smart technology in farming.

Union Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan said that BRICS nations represent nearly half the world’s population and contribute around 42 percent of global agricultural output. He said, Indore Declaration is not just a document of agreement but a symbol of the collective will and shared responsibility of BRICS nations. We have a report:

“Four new institutional initiatives were announced at the conference. Under the first initiative, a Network of Centres of Excellence will be set up for joint research on natural and regenerative farming, with the Indian Institute of Farming Systems Research playing a key role. The second initiative involves setting up a BRICS Digital Agriculture Network to bring Artificial Intelligence and digital technology into farming, which will be coordinated by IIT Delhi. The third initiative will establish a Global Forum to protect farmers’ rights related to seeds and to preserve traditional seed varieties. The fourth initiative will set up the BRICS AgriN Network for sharing agricultural inputs and genetic resources among member countries. With ABHINAV GOEL’S REPORT PRASHANT KUMAR SINHA FOR AKASHVANI NEWS.”
