## Morning News

President Droupadi Murmu has appointed Governors for six States. Mr. OP Mathur, who is currently serving as the Governor of Sikkim, will be the new Governor of Keralam. He will replace the current Governor of Keralam Rajendra Vishwanath Arlekar, who has been appointed as the Governor of Tamil Nadu. Mr. Dushyant Gautam has been appointed as the new Governor of Sikkim. Besides, the President has appointed Mr. Braj Mohan Ram as the Governor of Odisha, Dr. Sudha Yadav as the Governor of Karnataka, and Dr. Dhal Singh Bisen as the Governor of Uttarakhand. Their appointments will take effect from the dates on which they assume charge of their respective offices.

India will host first-ever BIMSTEC Home Ministers’ Meeting in New Delhi today. Union Home Minister Amit Shah will chair the high level meeting. The meeting underscores the shared resolve to combat terrorism, transnational organised crime, narcotics radicalisation, trafficking, illicit financial flows and cyber threats.

Extending a warm welcome to all the participating Home Ministers and member state delegations, Union Home Minister Amit Shah said that the historic meeting underscores the shared resolve of the BIMSTEC members to strengthen cooperation in tackling common security challenges, including terrorism and organised crime. In Jammu and Kashmir, four terrorists, including a top Lashkar-e-Taiba (LeT) commander, were killed in a landmine blast along the Line of Control when they were trying to infiltrate into Poonch district yesterday. The deceased terrorists have been identified as top LeT commander Mohammad Faizan, Mohammad Ishtiyaq, Mohammad Waqas, and Mohammad Asim. All of them were residents of Mandla, Madarpur, in Hajira tehsil of PoJK’s Poonch district. The group of terrorists was trying to sneak across the LoC in the Nangi Takri area of Krishna Ghati Sector when they triggered a mine. The powerful blast killed all four terrorists on the spot. The exact explosion site is in the Satwal-Naki/Nangi Takri area, which is a traditionally known infiltration path used by cross-border operatives trying to sneak into Indian territory.

The Election Commission of India (ECI) has stepped up efforts to ensure that every eligible citizen is included in the electoral rolls, with special camps and voter assistance campaigns being organised across several states and Union Territories.

Chief Electoral Officers (CEOs) and District Election Officers (DEOs) are taking proactive measures to help voters register their names, correct personal details and resolve discrepancies in the voter lists, in line with ECI instructions. Special camps are being organised at polling booths to make voter registration and correction services more accessible. Eligible citizens can also approach Booth Level Officers (BLOs), Assistant Electoral Registration Officers (AEROs), Electoral Registration Officers (EROs) and District Election Officers for assistance. The campaign also aims to address grievances related to voter enrolment, the hearing process and discrepancies in electoral records. Through these initiatives, the ECI aims to ensure that no eligible Indian citizen is left out of the electoral rolls and that voters receive timely assistance in resolving their concerns. The Centre has informed the Supreme Court that it has decided to regulate trade margins on anti-cancer medicines by capping them at 30 per cent of the maximum retail price (MRP), while the apex court called for a broader review of medicine pricing across the pharmaceutical sector.

Solicitor General Tushar Mehta, appearing for the Union government, told a bench of Justices Vikram Nath and Sandeep Mehta that the Centre would notify a list of cancer drugs to which the trade margin cap would apply.

The Centre’s second highest law officer submitted that the list could be periodically updated to include additional anti-cancer medicines, depending on market developments and public health considerations, while excluding inexpensive drugs.

The development came after the apex court repeatedly questioned the substantial disparity between the price at which medicines are supplied to retailers and the MRP printed on them, raising concerns over the financial burden on patients and the need for an effective regulatory framework.

The Karnataka High Court has struck down provisions of a 2022 state law that increased reservation for Scheduled Castes from 15 to 17 per cent and Scheduled Tribes from 3 to 7 per cent. A two-judge bench ruled that Sections 3 and 4 of the Karnataka Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes Reservation Act, 2022, were unconstitutional, as the enhanced quota breached the 50 per cent reservation ceiling laid down by the Supreme Court in the 1992 Indra Sawhney case. The law had increased the overall reservation for SCs, STs and Other Backward Classes from 50 to 56 per cent.

UPI Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) on select Person-to-Merchant transactions will be implemented after Diwali. In a social media post, National Payments Corporation of India said that UPI and Services Steering Committee today decided that, keeping in mind the festive season, MDR on select Person-to-Merchant  UPI transactions will be implemented immediately post Diwali. It said, the date will be notified separately.

All person to person UPI transactions will remain free regardless of amount. The MDR charges will apply only to person to merchant transactions exceeding two thousand rupees. Around 96 per cent of person-to-merchant UPI transactions will remain unaffected. Small merchants including street vendors receiving up to one lakh rupees per month through UPI QR codes under the Person-to-Person-Merchant category will continue to enjoy zero MDR on all transactions.
