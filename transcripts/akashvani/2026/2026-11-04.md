Vote counting is underway across the United States following the 2026 midterm elections, with Democrats taking an early lead over President Donald Trump’s Republicans as results continue to come in.

Democrats have secured ten Senate victories, two House win and six gubernatorial victories, while Republicans have won three Senate seat and one gubernatorial victories, when report last come in.

In the US Senate races, Democratic incumbent Mark Warner secured a fourth term representing Virginia, defeating Republican Bert Mizusawa. In Georgia, Democrat Jon Ossoff won re-election to the Senate, defeating Republican Mike Collins.

In Delaware, Democratic incumbent Chris Coons defeated Republican Mike Katz to secure a third full Senate term, while Rhode Island Senator Jack Reed won a sixth term after defeating Republican Raymond McKay.

In New Jersey, Democratic Senator Cory Booker also retained his seat, defeating Republican challenger Justin Murphy.

In Florida, Republican Ashley Moody won the US Senate race, defeating Democrat Angie Nixon.

In the gubernatorial contests, Democrats JB Pritzker of Illinois, Kathy Hochul of New York, Phil Weiser of Colorado, Helena Foulkes of Rhode Island, Josh Shapiro of Pennsylvania and Wes Moore of Maryland won the election. Republican Eric Barlow won the Wyoming gubernatorial race, defeating Democrat Kenneth Casner.

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh has said that India aims to achieve defence production worth 3 lakh crore rupees and defence exports of 50 thousand crore rupees by 2029. Addressing the Consultative Committee of Defence Ministry of Defence in Jaisalmer, Mr Singh said the government is committed to building a technologically empowered military capable of meeting present-day and emerging security challenges. He said defence production had increased from 46 thousand 429 crore rupees in 2014-15 to a record 1.78 lakh crore rupees in 2025-26.Defence exports has risen from less than 2,000 crore rupees in 2014 to approximately 40,000 crore rupees.

Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) has successfully test-fired an indigenous Pralay quasi-ballistic missile from the Integrated Test Range at Chandipur, off the Odisha coast. The missile was launched from Launch Complex-Three yesterday. It successfully engaged two simulated targets at a reported distance of around 500 kilometers. As a safety precaution, authorities evacuated more than 2,500 people from nearby villages before the test. The Pralay missile is capable of carrying different types of warheads and has a reported strike range of up to 500 kilometers.

In the US midterm elections, the Democratic Party secured 54 seats out of 100 seats in the Senate, gaining control of the upper chamber of Congress following a series of electoral victories. In the House of Representatives, the Democratic Party have also crossed the threshold of 218 seats required to win the majority as it won 224 seats, while the Republican Party secured 135 of the 435 seats. The counting of ballots is underway. The Democratic Party registered significant gains in several traditionally Republican states. In South Carolina, Democratic candidate and doctor Annie Andrews defeated Republican Darline Graham to win a Senate seat. The state had not elected a Democrat to the Senate since 1998. More in this report:

“The United States midterm elections take place halfway through the four-year presidential term and determine the composition of the US Congress. The US Congress consists of two chambers, the House of Representatives and the Senate. All 435 seats in the House of Representatives and 35 of the 100 Senate seats are being contested in this elections. Members of the House serve two-year terms, while senators serve six-year terms. The electoral process includes primary elections, through which political parties select candidates, followed by general elections. The two major political parties are the Democratic Party and the Republican Party. Unlike India, elections in the United States are administered by individual state and local authorities. Voters can cast their ballots through in-person voting, early voting and postal voting, according to state laws. The elections also include State Governor elections and several state and local elections. The newly elected members of Congress are scheduled to take office on January 3, 2027. The results will determine party representation in both chambers of Congress for the next two years. SHAGUN CHOPRA, AKASHVANI NEWS.”

Indian Railways carried over 64 crore passengers, compared to around 63 crore passengers during the corresponding month last year. With Dipendra Kumar, Akshit Kumar Vaidyan, Akashvani News Delhi.”
