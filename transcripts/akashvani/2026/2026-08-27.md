## Morning News

Nearly 160 people have been killed and over 750, including 133 Indian nationals, remain missing after a massive flash flood swept through central Nepal yesterday. Originating in Tibet, the flood devastated several towns and villages, washing away critical bridges, and crippling a dozen hydropower projects. Nepal Foreign Minister Shisir Khanal told the parliament that an earthquake hit the Tibet region at 8:37 AM (local time) yesterday and the flash floods were reported three minutes later, suggesting a glacial outburst due to tremors, which triggered the floods. More from our correspondent:

Nepal’s National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority said, the massive flood entered the Trishuli River through the Bhote Koshi in Timure, a border-town in Rasuwa district, due to the rockslide and swelling of the Lhende River situated along the Nepal-Tibet border. It then spread along the river course to other districts like Nuwakot, Chitawan, Gorkha, Tanahu and Nawalparasi East, covering areas ranging to nearly 200 kilometres from the capital Kathmandu.

Nepal Police said, 157 people died in the flash flood. The Nepal government said, around 500 people are missing. According to China’s state-run news channel, 3 people were killed and 265 are missing on the Chinese side of the border.

Videos from the affected areas showed a powerful surge of muddy water, rocks and debris sweeping through the border region. Officials said that around 100 people have been rescued from different parts of the affected areas.
Prime Minister Narendra Modi spoke to his Nepalese counterpart Balendra Shah and assured him of all possible humanitarian assistance. Mr Shah thanked Prime Minister Modi for his warm words of solidarity and for offering humanitarian assistance. New Delhi has already sent the first tranche of relief supplies to the neighbouring nation. Indian Embassy in Kathmandu issued emergency numbers for assistance and information related to Indian nationals.
The Embassy of India in Beijing also issued a helpline number, which can also be contacted through WhatsApp. At least 50 of the Indians missing were pilgrims for the Kailash Manasarovar Yatra.
Besides Indians, missing tourists are from more than two dozen countries including US, Malaysia, UK, South Africa, Australia and Netherlands. KAUMUDEE PAREEK, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.

Bihar and Uttar Pradesh are on high alert after several districts in neighbouring Nepal were devastated by flash floods in the Gandak river system. NDRF has been placed on high alert along with the local administrations and relief-rescue teams in the border areas where its impact may be felt. Union Home Minister Amit Shah spoke to Bihar Chief Minister Samrat Choudhary and Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath on the situation and the possible rise in river water levels.

Bihar government has asked districts along the Gandak river, including West Champaran, East Champaran, Gopalganj, Saran, Muzaffarpur and Vaishali, to remain on alert. However, water level in Gandak River has started receding. A report:

At the Valmikinagar Gandak Barrage in West Champaran, the water level of the river is falling. Around eighty thousand cusecs of water was released from the barrage this morning, about half the discharge recorded last night. At present, the situation is under control.

However, as a precautionary measure, the district administration has already shifted more than five thousand people living in low-lying areas to safer locations.The situation remains similar in other vulnerable districts as well. All necessary arrangements have been put in place to deal with any emergency. The Bihar government has appealed to people to remain alert, not to panic, and to avoid rumours. Savita Pareek, Akashvani News, Patna.

Government has commenced calibrated and targeted release of onions from the buffer stock to ensure adequate availability and moderate seasonal price pressures in the coming months. Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and  Public Distribution said that onion availability is comfortable to meet domestic demand in the coming months as estimated production of onion for 2025-26 is over 307 Lakh Metric Tonnes. It added that in view of the comfortable production outlook, government has fixed a procurement target of two lakh metric tonnes of Rabi onion for the Price Stabilization Fund (PSF) buffer during 2026-27. The Ministry said that onion will be sold at 35 rupees per kilogram through mobile vans and retail outlets of National Cooperative Consumers Federation (NCCF) and National Agricultural Cooperative Marketing Federation (NAFED), as well as through Safal and Kendriya Bhandar outlets.

India has stepped up efforts to trace and rescue Indian nationals stranded in Nepal due to the flash flood tragedy.
