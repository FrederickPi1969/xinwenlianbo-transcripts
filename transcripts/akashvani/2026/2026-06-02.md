## Morning News

Myanmar President U Min Aung Hlaing has assured Prime Minister Narendra Modi that Myanmarese territory will not be allowed to be used against India’s security interests. The two leaders held wide-ranging talks to boost bilateral ties in trade, defence, energy, and connectivity in New Delhi yesterday. Mr Aung Hlaing is on a five-day visit to India. Briefing media after the talks, Foreign Secretary Vikram Misri said, President of Myanmar reiterated the assurance that Myanmar’s territory will not be permitted to be used against India’s security interests. Mr Misri said, Prime Minister Modi reaffirmed India’s support for the sovereignty and territorial integrity of Myanmar and both sides underscored the importance of preventing the misuse of sovereign territory for activities “inimical” to their security interests.

The Foreign Secretary said that both sides expressed interest in forging closer cooperation, particularly in areas of trade and investment, health, education, energy, and critical technologies like artificial intelligence.

The Myanmarese leader accompanied by a high-level delegation will travel to Mumbai today for business and industry interactions and site visits.

The talks will take place in New Delhi from today and it will last till Thursday. India’s chief negotiator is Darpan Jain, Additional Secretary in the Department of Commerce, while the US team is led by its chief negotiator Brendan Lynch. President Droupadi Murmu has said that education is a powerful means of empowerment as it enables individuals to become self-reliant, aware and capable. The Ministry of Home Affairs has notified the Immigration and Foreigners (Amendment) Rules, 2026, introducing key changes in registration timelines and the appeals mechanism under the Immigration and Foreigners Rules, 2025. The amendment allows registration to be completed at any time before the expiry of 180 days from arrival in India. Previously, registration had to be completed within 14 days after the end of the 180-day period. However, late registration beyond this period will now be permitted only under emergent circumstances.

The Chief Justice of India Justice Surya Kant administered the oath of office to five new judges of the Supreme Court today. Justices Sheel Nagu, Shree Chandrashekhar, Sanjeev Sachdeva and Arun Palli and senior advocate V. Mohana took oath as judges of the apex court. The Centre had cleared their appointments yesterday on the recommendation of the Supreme Court Collegium.

The appointments were made weeks after President Droupadi Murmu promulgated the Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Ordinance, 2026, increasing the sanctioned strength of judges in the apex court from 34 to 38, including the Chief Justice of India.

The Ministry has advised that anyone, who has travelled from or transited through an Ebola-affected country in the last 21 days and develop symptoms such as fever, headache, muscle pain, diarrhoea, or unexplained bleeding, isolate themselves and inform local health authorities. It emphasized that early reporting is crucial for saving lives and preventing the spread of the disease.

## Evening News

The government has constituted a one member committee to probe into matters relating to procurement of services for On-Screen Marking (OSM) system by Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE).

The OSM is a new digital evaluation system introduced by CBSE this year for Class 12 board examinations, replacing traditional paper checking.

The committee will be chaired by Chairperson of the Capacity Building Commission, S Radha Chauhan. The committee will submit its report within a month to the Department of Personnel and Training.

In another development, Senior IAS officer Lokhande Prashant Sitaram, has been appointed as the new Chairperson of the CBSE. Mr Sitaram was earlier serving as an Additional Secretary in the Home Ministry. His appointment was made in place of Rahul Singh who has been transferred to the Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare as Additional Secretary.

The government has also appointed Varun Bhardwaj as Secretary in CBSE. His appointment has been made in place of Himanshu Gupta who was serving as the Secretary in CBSE.

Meanwhile, the CBSE today officially activated online portal for verification of marks and re-evaluation of answer sheets of Class 12th students. Our correspondent reports that the facility is available only to those students who have obtained scanned copies of their evaluated answer books.

“Soon the portal was made live malicious actors attempted to disrupt services through a barrage of cyber attacks. The CBSE said that the most recent being a denial of service attack attempt causing 1.5 million hits on the portal within a matter of 2 minutes and more than 1 lakh attempts of unauthorised file access. The board said that its teams remain vigilant and responsive to ensure that the students are facilitated in all ways possible. Based on student feedback, CBSE has further refined the platform, including extending session time limits to make the process more convenient and seamless. The CBSE said that the portal is currently supporting over 8 thousand concurrent users, and more than 16 thousand students have successfully completed their submissions this evening. The portal will remain open till June 6th, and no offline applications or requests submitted after the deadline will be accepted. With Anand Kumar, Saklen Akhtar, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

A Delhi court today extended the judicial custody of five accused in the NEET paper leak case till 15th of this month. Yesterday, two other accused were sent to 14 days of judicial custody. The CBI has arrested 13 accused in the case, so far. Last month, the National Testing Agency cancelled the National Eligibility-cum-Entrance Test held on 3rd May amid allegations of paper leak. A re-examination has been scheduled for 21st of this month.
