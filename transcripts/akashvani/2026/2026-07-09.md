Prime Minister Narendra Modi and his Australian counterpart, Anthony Albanese, participated in the India-Australia CEO Forum in Melbourne this morning. Both leaders also met the leading business leaders from India and Australia. Speaking on the occasion, Prime Minister Modi said the world is currently navigating a period marked by uncertainty, supply chain disruptions, and an energy crisis. He said that in such times, it is both natural and essential for India and Australia to move forward as natural and trusted partners.

Prime Minister further said, Economic Cooperation and Trade Agreement (ECTA) concluded in record time in 2022, has further strengthened the economic ties between the two countries.

Prime Minister Modi also noted the complementarities between the Indian and Australian economies, underlining vast opportunities available in India across a number of sectors.

Mr Modi invited Australian investors to take advantage of the opportunities for long-term investments in the country and also welcomed the growing presence of Australian universities in India.

Following the CEOs Forum, Prime Minister Modi also addressed the Economic Roadmap business event, a larger gathering of over 200 CEOs and business leaders from both sides. Speaking about natural synergies between India and Australia, he highlighted that shared democratic values, a common vision for the Indo-Pacific, vibrant people-to-people ties and strong political understanding created fertile ground for the business partnership between the two countries to grow and prosper together.

The Centre has approved the procurement of Khopra and Totapuri mangoes from farmers in Tamil Nadu to safeguard farmers’ interests. Union Agriculture and Farmers’ Welfare Ministry said, this will ensure better price realization for producers and protect them from distress sales caused by market fluctuations. More from our Correspondent:

“The approval is based on the proposal submitted by Tamil Nadu. Under the approval, over 87 thousand metric tonnes of Khopra will be procured in the state. The entire procurement will be undertaken at the Minimum Support Price (MSP) under the Price Support Scheme. The total MSP value of the approved procurement is over one thousand crore rupees. Further, procurement of over 96 thousand metric tonnes of Totapuri mangoes in Tamil Nadu during this year has received approval under the Market Intervention Scheme (MIS). The procurement will be carried out under the MIS, with the Market Intervention Price fixed over one thousand rupees per quintal. The intervention is aimed at ensuring that Totapuri mango growers receive fair and remunerative prices for their produce and are protected from having to sell at a loss due to sudden declines in market prices. The measure is expected to provide price stability, safeguard farmers’ incomes, and ensure better returns for their produce. With Anupam, Saklen Aktar, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

## Evening News

India and Australia today adopted a new Joint Declaration on Defence and Security Cooperation and a Maritime Security Collaboration Roadmap to promote a free, open and inclusive Indo-Pacific. The deals were signed following delegation-level talks between Prime Minister Narendra Modi and his Australian counterpart, Anthony Albanese during the third Australia-India Annual Leaders’ Summit in Melbourne.

In a joint press statement after the talks, Prime Minister Modi said, the Indo-Pacific is not just a meeting point of two oceans but showcases the shared aspirations of like-minded democracies like India and Australia.

Later briefing the media, Foreign Secretary Vikram Misri said both sides finalized the administrative arrangement for a long-term supply of Australian uranium to India. The agreement is expected to strengthen India’s clean energy programme and support its net-zero goals.

The Foreign Secretary said, the two leaders also reviewed progress in economic cooperation. Following the India-Australia CEOs Forum, they agreed to accelerate negotiations on the Comprehensive Economic Cooperation Agreement, building on the gains achieved under the Economic Cooperation and Trade Agreement, which has led to a 55 percent increase in bilateral trade. India and Australia also expanded cooperation in emerging technologies through the operationalization of the Australia-India Partnership for Cyber Critical Technologies and Supply Chains. Australia also reaffirmed its support for India’s Gaganyaan human spaceflight mission. The two leaders also reiterated their commitment to the Quad as an important platform for regional cooperation, including counterterrorism and disaster relief.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today said, India’s aspiration to become one of the world’s top three economies at the earliest is driven by the motto of “Grow more, achieve more. Mr Modi was addressing the Indian community at the Marvel Stadium in Melbourne.

Speaking at the “Melbourne Meets Modi” event, Prime Minister Modi also referred to Operation Sindoor. He said the operation demonstrated the effectiveness of India’s homegrown defence systems in carrying out anti-terror operations.

Mr Modi said the India-Australia partnership has reached new heights, driven by shared values, strong people-to-people ties, and expanding cooperation across key sectors. He described the one-million-strong Indian diaspora as a vital pillar of bilateral relations.

Speaking on the occasion, Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese described the Indian community as a living bridge between the two countries.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah today chaired a conference of Superintendents of Police (SPs) from border states and Union Territories in New Delhi. Superintendents of Police from Jammu and Kashmir, Punjab, Uttarakhand, Rajasthan, Gujarat, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar and West Bengal participated in the conference. Apart from infiltration, illegal immigration and demographic changes, border security, drone threats and narcotics trafficking were discussed during the meeting.

The National Commission for Women (NCW) today constituted a high-level Expert Committee amid growing concerns over irregularities in the rapidly expanding Assisted Reproductive Technology (ART) sector. The panel will undertake a comprehensive review of the regulatory framework and concerned laws governing IVF clinics, ART centres and gamete banks, with a focus on protecting the reproductive rights, dignity and safety of women. Former Judge of the Delhi High Court, Justice Asha Menon will chair the expert committee.
