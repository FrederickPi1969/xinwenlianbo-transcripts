## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that a strong maritime capability is the deciding factor for a country’s economic and strategic influence, and India is preparing for it. He said, a country with maritime capabilities is strong and has more economic and strategic influence.

Prime Minister Modi made these remarks after commissioning three indigenously built Indian Navy platforms – INS Dunagiri, INS Sanshodhak and INS Agray at the Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port in Kolkata yesterday. Designed by the Navy’s Warship Design Bureau and built by Garden Reach Shipbuilders and Engineers, the vessels are intended to strengthen combat operations, hydrographic surveying and anti-submarine warfare.

Prime Minister Modi has expressed gratitude to people around the world for making the International Day of Yoga 2026 a global celebration. The Prime Minister said it is encouraging to see Yoga bringing together people from all walks of life, across nations, in the shared pursuit of wellness and good health. He expressed hope that the day would inspire more people to make Yoga a part of their daily lives in the times to come.

President Droupadi Murmu visited Madhya Pradesh’s Kuno National Park yesterday and reviewed the progress of Project Cheetah, India’s flagship initiative to reintroduce the species in the wild after its extinction in the country. During the visit, the President toured the park’s Cheetah Management Area and was briefed by officials on the status of the project. Kuno, the country’s first cheetah reintroduction site, has been developed under Project Cheetah because of its suitable habitat, adequate prey base and relatively low human disturbance.

“President Droupadi Murmu went on a safari at Kuno National Park this morning. The President will also interact with members of the Sahariya tribe working as Cheetah Mitras, cheetah trackers, local forest personnel and tourist guides to learn about their experiences and role in the project. Earlier, on Sunday, President Murmu visited the Cheetah Command and Control Centre at Kuno and viewed an exhibition related to the cheetah project. Officials informed her that India currently has 52 cheetahs, of which 49 are in Kuno National Park, while three have been shifted to the Gandhi Sagar Wildlife Sanctuary. She was also briefed about the monitoring and tracking system used for the animals. Prime Minister Narendra Modi launched India’s ambitious cheetah reintroduction project in 2022 by releasing eight cheetahs into Kuno National Park on his birthday. Subsequently, more cheetahs were brought from South Africa and Botswana. Abhinav Goel, Akashvani News, Bhopal.”

She is known for her artistic range and strong contralto voice, working in a variety of genres including musicals, comedies, and dramas. Her career and personal life, marked by both public fascination and private struggle, made her a cultural icon. Garland began her career at the age of two, performing with her two older sisters as a vaudeville act called The Gumm Sisters. In 1935, aged 13, she signed a contract with Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer (MGM) and was initially cast in supporting roles in ensemble musicals such as Broadway Melody of 1938 (1937) and Thoroughbreds Don’t Cry (1937). She achieved international recognition for her portrayal of Dorothy Gale in the musical film The Wizard of Oz (1939). She followed this with leading roles in MGM musicals including Meet Me in St. Louis (1944), Easter Parade (1948), and Summer Stock (1950). She expanded her range with dramatic performances in A Star Is Born (1954) and Judgment at Nuremberg (1961), both of which earned her Academy Award nominations.

Garland’s music career was kickstarted with her signature song “Over the Rainbow” from The Wizard of Oz. She recorded 11 studio albums between 1939 and 1962.  Her live album, Judy at Carnegie Hall (1961), made Garland the first woman to win the Grammy Award for Album of the Year. Also in 1961, she became the first female recipient and youngest honoree of the Golden Globe Cecil B. DeMille Award. Garland developed dependencies on prescription medications that affected her physical and mental well-being. She died from an accidental barbiturate overdose at age 47 in 1969.

Several of Garland’s performances are preserved in the National Film Registry and the National Recording Registry by the Library of Congress, and six of her recordings have been inducted into the Grammy Hall of Fame. Her numerous accolades include one Golden Globe Award, two Grammy Awards, the Academy Juvenile Award, the Special Tony Award, and nominations for three Emmy Awards. In 1997, she was posthumously awarded the Grammy Lifetime Achievement Award. In 1999, the American Film Institute ranked her the eighth-greatest star of classic Hollywood cinema.

Fifteen people, most of them students, lost their lives in a tragic fire incident in the Aliganj area of Lucknow in Uttar Pradesh, this afternoon. The tragedy occurred when a massive fire broke out at an animation coaching centre located inside a commercial complex. Several injured persons have been admitted to nearby hospitals. Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath has said, high-level committee comprising the Director General of Police and the Additional Chief Secretary (Home) will investigate the incident and those found responsible would face strict action. More from our correspondent –

“The blaze reportedly erupted inside the institute building and spread rapidly, trapping people inside. it was a shopping complex having coaching, game zone and showrooms in it. Fire brigade teams, police personnel and district administration officials rushed to the spot and launched rescue and evacuation efforts. Firefighters broke through the wall and entered the building.Talking to media Deputy CM of UP Brijesh Pathak said that the high level committee comprising DGP and additional Chief Secretary (Home) of the state will investigate the matter and find the reasons behind the fire so that such kind of incidents never happen in future.

Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath said that the culprits will not be spared.

He left his visit to Aligarh and Firozabad districts incomplete and returned back to Lucknow. He inspected the incident site and also visited the injured in the hospital. SUSHIL CHANDRA TIWARI, AKASHWANI NEWS, LUCKNOW”

President Droupadi Murmu, Vice President C. P. Radhakrishnan and Prime Minister Narendra Modi have expressed grief over the loss of lives in the fire. In a social media post, Mr Modi announced an ex gratia of two lakh rupees from the Prime Minister’s National Relief Fund for the next of kin of each deceased. The injured will receive 50 thousand rupees each.

The two-day BRICS National Security Advisers meeting began in New Delhi today. The meeting being chaired by National Security Advisor Ajit Doval brings together security chiefs from the bloc’s 11 member countries. These include India, Brazil, China, Egypt, Ethiopia, Indonesia, Iran, Russia, Saudi Arabia, South Africa and UAE. The gathering takes place as India holds the BRICS Chairship for the fourth time in 2026. The discussions cover the rapidly evolving nature of national security challenges, along with the role of new technologies in emerging security threats.

On the sidelines of the meeting, Mr Doval today met Iran’s Deputy Secretary of the Supreme National Security Council, Ghadir Nezamipour. The Ministry of External Affairs said that both sides reviewed the ongoing situation in West Asia. They also discussed cooperation under the BRICS platform and India-Iran bilateral ties.

Mr Doval also met the Ethiopia’s Executive Director of Analysis at the National Intelligence and Security Service, Million Lema Tadesse and Brazil’s Secretary of Multilateral and Political Affairs, Carlos Cozendey.

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh has said that India is exporting defence equipments to over hundred countries and becoming self-reliant in the sector. Addressing an event in New Delhi today, the Defence Minister said that during Operation Sindoor country’s armed forces targeted only those who were responsible for killing the Indian citizens. He added that the government is committed to build an empowered India by laying emphasis on both capabilities and culture.

Shiv Sena leader and Maharashtra Deputy Chief Minister Eknath Shinde today announced the induction of six Lok Sabha MPs from the Shiv Sena (UBT) faction into the party. Addressing a press conference in Mumbai, Mr. Shinde welcomed the MPs – Sanjay Dina Patil, Sanjay Deshmukh, Sanjay Jadhav, Bhausaheb Wakchaure, Nagesh Patil-Ashtikar and Omprakash Raje Nimbalkar into Shiv Sena.

Mr. Shinde said, the induction of the six MPs marked the second phase of the movement that began in June 2022, when 40 Shiv Sena MLAs rebelled. He said the uprising was aimed at preserving the ideology of Shiv Sena founder Balasaheb Thackeray and safeguarding the party’s Hindutva principles.

The BJP-led West Bengal government today presented its first full Budget for 2026-27, with a strong focus on social welfare, infrastructure development and employment generation. The Budget also announces several women-centric initiatives. Presenting a deficit Budget of 6,070 crore rupees, Finance Minister Swapan Dasgupta described it as a roadmap for building a developed Bengal. He said the state’s outstanding debt has crossed 8 lakh crore rupees, making financial discipline a key priority.

The Centre today informed the Supreme Court that the CBSE has come up with a new policy for the evaluation of private students, who were affected by the cancellation of Class 12th board examination in Gulf countries due to the recent Iran-US war. The top court were informed by the Solicitor General Tushar Mehta that under the new policy, which was notified yesterday, a distinct formula for evaluating the private students appearing for the board exam was developed. Giving details, he said, for the subjects for which examination could not be held, the performance will be assessed based on the marks obtained by the private candidate in Class X and the last-attempted Class XII board examination.

Indian Railways has approved the deployment of Automatic Train Protection system Kavach on 631 route kilometres of East Coast Railway to enhance safety and operational efficiency. The Railways Ministry said the project covers six important railway sections of East Coast Railway including Baghuapal-Budhapank, Haridaspur-Paradeep, Khurda Road-Balangir, Naupada-Gunupur, Lanjigarh Road-Junagarh and Bobbili-Salur. The ministry said the project is expected to benefit both passenger and freight train operations across key sections of Odisha and neighbouring regions served by East Coast Railway.

Chemicals and Fertilizers Ministry has said that four cargo ships, carrying shipments of Urea, Di-Ammonium Phosphate (DAP), and Sulphur have successfully crossed the Strait of Hormuz last week. These vessels are currently heading toward their designated destination ports across the country, including Krishnapatnam and Kakinada ports in Andhra Pradesh, Paradeep port in Odisha, and the Mundra port in Gujarat. The Ministry said these fresh imports will immediately offload to supplement the nation’s existing fertilizer buffers and secure ongoing agricultural requirements.

In Qatar, 13 people including Indians were killed in an explosion at the Ras Laffan Industrial City last evening. Energy minister of Qatar Saad bin Shreida Al-Kaabi said 66 people were injured in the fire caused by the explosion at the Barzan local gas supply facility. The exact number of Indian victims was not immediately known. In a social media post, the Indian embassy in Doha said it is in constant touch with Qatari authorities and will render all help to the families of those who have lost their lives or have been injured.

UK Prime Minister Keir Starmer has announced his resignation from the post and also as the leader of the Labour Party, ending his tenure at the Downing Street amid growing internal pressure from his own party.

Delivering his resignation statement, Mr Starmer recalled his arrival at the Downing Street two years ago as the proudest moment of his life.

Meanwhile, senior politician Andy Burnham has officially launched his campaign to succeed Keir Starmer as the leader of the Labour Party. The race to determine the next occupant of Downing Street is set to begin next month.

External Affairs Minister Dr S Jaishankar today met his Mongolian counterpart, Battsetseg Batmunkh in Ulaanbaatar. In a social media post, Dr. S. Jaishankar said the talks underscored the warmth, depth and potential of the India-Mongolia Strategic Partnership. The two leaders reviewed progress in key areas of bilateral cooperation, including development projects, capacity building, culture, education and security. They also explored opportunities to expand collaboration in mining, clean energy and agro-processing sectors.
