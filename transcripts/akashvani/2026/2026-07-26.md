## Morning News

The CJP has withdrawn its agitation after the government agreed to its demands. Union Ministers J P Nadda and Dr Jitendra Singh held the third round of discussion with the CJP delegation yesterday focusing on its demands for reforms in the examination system and measures to prevent paper leaks. Addressing a joint press conference with CJP delegation in New Delhi, Mr Nadda said that during the meeting, the government agreed to the demands of CJP that no action should be taken against the protesters and no FIRs should be filed against them.

On the five point charter given by the CJP for reforms in examinations, Mr Nadda said that the government will deeply think about the charter and will do whatever is possible after holding deliberations with the CJP.

CJP’s Chief Spokesperson Saurav Das announced to withdraw the agitation in good faith expressing hope that the agreed-upon terms will be executed within the agreed-upon timelines.

Union Minister Pralhad Joshi will be the new Education Minister following the resignation of Dharmendra Pradhan. Mr Joshi has been assigned the charge of the Ministry of Education, in addition to his existing portfolio. Mr Joshi is currently serving as the Minister of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution and New and Renewable Energy. Talking to media after the announcement, Mr. Joshi said, he will try his level best to meet the expectations from him.

President Droupadi Murmu has accepted the resignation of Dharmendra Pradhan from the Union Council of Ministers, with immediate effect.

The government will introduce the Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Amendment Bill, 2026 in the Lok Sabha tomorrow. The Bill is listed in the business of the House for Monday. The Bill seeks to amend the Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024. It will be introduced by Union Minister Dr Jitendra Singh.

On Friday, The Union Cabinet had approved a draft Bill to set up fast-track courts and to ensure stricter punishment in cases of paper leaks and exam malpractices.

## Evening News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today announced the constitution of a high-powered task force on examination reforms, headed by renowned technology expert Nandan Nilekani. The task force will also consist of Former IB Director Tapan Deka, Former ISRO Chairman S Somnath, IIT Chennai Director V Kamakoti. Besides, Former Education Secretary Anita Karwal, and Logistics Expert Amrit Lal Meena are also part of the task force.

In a video message, Mr Modi said the task force will focus on strengthening the examination system, and based on its recommendations, necessary steps will be taken at the earliest to ensure the credibility of future examinations. The Prime Minister added that the government is also moving towards introducing a new law in Parliament tomorrow with stringent provisions to deal with examination-related irregularities. Mr Modi reaffirmed the Government’s commitment to protecting the integrity of competitive examinations and safeguarding the future of millions of students.

Union Minister Pralhad Joshi today assumed charge as the Education Minister. Mr Joshi was appointed to the post yesterday following the resignation of Dharmendra Pradhan. Mr Joshi also continues to hold the portfolios of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution, and New and Renewable Energy. At the ongoing Commonwealth Games (CWG 2026), Indian weightlifter Mirabai Chanu won the gold medal in the women’s 48 kilogram event in Glasgow today. The 31-year-old lifter produced a record-breaking performance, lifting 85 kilogram in the snatch to set a CWG record in the category. She then lifted 105 kilogram in the clean and jerk, another Games record to finish with a total of 190 kilogram. With this victory, Mirabai Chanu secured her third consecutive Commonwealth Games gold medal, having also bagged the yellow metal in the 2018 and 2022 editions.

Earlier in the day, another Indian weightlifter Rishikanta Singh Chanambam won the silver medal in the Men’s 60 kilogram event. Rishikanta lifted 121 kilogram in the snatch, setting a new CWG record before Malaysia’s Mohamad Aniq Kasdan equalled the mark. Prime Minister Narendra Modi congratulated Rishikanta Singh Chanambam by saying that his determination and outstanding performance have brought immense joy to the nation. Mr Modi also wished that the Indian weightlifter continues to scale greater heights and inspire many young athletes.

In Men’s Cricket, India defeated hosts Zimbabwe by 35 runs in the third and final T20 International to clean sweep the series 3-nil in Harare.
