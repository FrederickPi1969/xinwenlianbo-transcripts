## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi spoke with the President of United Arab Emirates (UAE) Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan yesterday. In a telephonic conversation, both leaders discussed the current situation in West Asia and security of the Strait of Hormuz. Mr Modi said in a social media post that he reiterated India’s strong condemnation of all attacks on the UAE that have resulted in loss of innocent lives and damage to civilian infrastructure. The Prime Minister said both sides agreed on the importance of ensuring safe and free navigation through the Strait of Hormuz. During the conversation, both leaders reiterated the commitment to work together for the early restoration of peace, security and stability in the region.

Iran has confirmed the killing of National Security chief Ali Larijani and the head of Basij force Gholam Reza Soleimani in an Israeli strike. Larijani was one of Tehran’s most prominent figures in the ongoing war against the US and Israel and close confidant of slain leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei and his son Mojtaba. The confirmation comes hours after Israel said it eliminated two of the top Iranian security official, including Larijani, in an Israeli strike aimed at weakening the Iranian leadership amid the ongoing conflict. Iran’s Fars news agency said Larijani was targeted by American and Israeli fighter jets while he was at his daughter’s house in the Pardis district east of Tehran.

Iraq’s capital, Baghdad, has been rocked by a series of explosions close to the US embassy yesterday in the city’s heavily fortified Green Zone as the rapidly escalating US-Israeli war on Iran continued to spill over the border. Security sources said that at least three explosive drones also targeted a US diplomatic facility near Baghdad International Airport, activating C-RAM air defence systems. A fire also broke out near the Central Bank in the al-Jadriyah district, where debris from an aerial object had fallen close to the building’s main gates, citing Iraqi authorities. There were no immediate reports of casualties from the attacks.

US President Donald Trump has said that most NATO allies have declined to join the US military operation against Iran, criticising the alliance and asserting that Washington does not need foreign support.​ He added that despite their reluctance, almost every country strongly agreed with what US is doing, and that Iran cannot, in any way, shape, or form, be allowed to have a nuclear weapon.

French President Emmanuel Macron has said his country is ready to help secure the Strait of Hormuz, but only as part of a mission separate from the current Middle East war. Speaking ahead of a security meeting at the Elysee presidential palace, he stressed any mission would require discussions and de-escalation with Iran and must be entirely separate from the ongoing military operations and bombings.

The Election Commission has deployed one thousand one hundred 11 Central Observers for the assembly elections of Assam, Keralam, Puducherry, Tamil Nadu and West Bengal and the bye-elections in 6 States. Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar has reiterated that the polls will be conducted in a free, fair, violence-free and inducement-free manner, ensuring that every voter can exercise their franchise without fear or favour. “Out of the one thousand one hundred eleven Observers, 557 are General Observers, 188 Police Observers and 366 Expenditure Observers. General Observers will oversee the overall conduct of polls, Police Observers will supervise law and order and security arrangements, while Expenditure Observers will keep a strict vigil on election spending to curb the misuse of money power. In Gujarat, the high-level committee for the Uniform Civil Code submitted its final draft report to Chief Minister Bhupendra Patel in Gandhinagar yesterday. The committee, led by retired Supreme Court Judge Justice Ranjana Prakash Desai, prepared this report after conducting an extensive study and gathering public feedback across the districts of Gujarat. The draft report proposes a common legal framework for all religions and communities on matters including marriage, divorce, inheritance, and adoption. A primary focus of the recommendations is to ensure equal rights and security for women. The committee highlighted that the draft also takes into account the unique geographical and cultural diversity of the state.

## Midday News

Rajya Sabha today bid farewell to 59 members from 20 states including 9 women members. Speaking during the farewell, Prime Minister Narendra Modi said that  their contribution to parliamentary democracy will  always be remembered. He said that in politics there is never a full stop, as their experience and contribution to the nation will always remain relevant and valuable.

The Prime Minister said, there are also some bitter and sweet experiences but, when such an occasion arises, it is natural that members rise above party lines and a common sentiment emerges among all.

Mentioning Deputy chairman Harivansh who is also retiring Mr Modi said he had the opportunity to discharge his responsibilities in this House for a long time.

Earlier, addressing the House, the Chairman of the House C. P. Radhakrishnan said the retirement of one-third of Rajya Sabha members every two years reflects the constitutional design of the Council of States, which allows experienced lawmakers to make way for new members while preserving institutional stability.

Making the remarks on the bid farewell to retiring MPs in the Rajya Sabha, Leader of Opposition and Congress MP Mallikarjun Kharge said those in politics, public life, neither get tired nor retire for the passion to serve the country.

The Lok Sabha has extended the tenure of the Joint Parliamentary Committee examining the legislation related to one nation, one election, until the first day of the last week of the Monsoon session, 2026. The bill seeks to introduce simultaneous polls to the Lok Sabha and State Legislative Assemblies. The Chairman of the committee, P.P. Chaudhary moved the motion to extend the tenure of the Joint Committee on the Constitution (129th Amendment) Bill, 2024, and the Union Territories Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2024. The motion was adopted by a voice vote in the House.

Railways Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw has said that Vande Bharat trains are equipped with several advanced safety features to enhance passenger safety. Replying to supplementaries during Question Hour in the Lok Sabha today, Mr Vaishnaw highlighted that Anti-climbing features have been introduced in the Vande Bharat Express for passenger safety.

Lok Sabha has taken up discussion on the Demands for Grants under the control of the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare for 2026- 27. Amrinder Singh Raja Warring of the Congress initiated the discussion. He said, the farmers of the country are not just foodgrain producers but are also the strongest base of the country’s economy and food security.

Konda Vishweshwar Reddy of BJP said that agriculture is the country’s cultural identity and stability. He said, since 2014, the agricultural growth in India has been phenomenal compared to other sectors.

The government has directed the airlines to allocate minimum 60 per cent of seats for selection on any flight free of charge to ensure fair access for the passengers. The Ministry of Civil Aviation today issued directions through the Directorate General of Civil Aviation to strengthen passenger convenience, transparency and uniformity of practices across airlines. The Ministry directed that passengers travelling on the same PNR must be seated together, preferably in adjacent seats. It added that carriage of sports equipment and musical instruments should be facilitated in a transparent and passenger-friendly manner. The Airlines have also been instructed to bring out clear, transparent policies for carriage of pets. Strict adherence to passenger rights framework, particularly in cases of delays, cancellations and denied boarding has also been directed by the government.

A large Indian vessel named ‘Jag Ladki’ arrived at Mundra Port in Gujarat this morning carrying a significant consignment of crude oil. The ship is transporting approximately eighty-one thousand metric tonnes of ‘a high-quality oil from the United Arab Emirates.

It follows the earlier arrival of the vessel ‘Shivalik’ at Mundra and ‘Nandadevi’ at the Vadinar port. Authorities at the port have ensured smooth offloading operations to facilitate the distribution of the crude oil to refineries.

At least six people were killed and 24 others injured after two Israeli strikes hit central Beirut this morning, according to Lebanon’s health ministry. Authorities said human remains were recovered from the debris, with identification to be carried out through DNA testing. The attacks struck densely populated areas without prior warning, marking a further escalation in strikes on the Lebanese capital. Local media reported that one strike hit an apartment in the Zuqaq al-Blat district, near government buildings and foreign embassies. Another strike hit the Basta neighbourhood.

In a separate overnight incident, Israeli forces demolished a 15 storey residential building in central Beirut, claiming Hezbollah was storing funds in the basement. Overall, four attacks were reported overnight in central parts of the city, many of them in residential zones.

Meanwhile, in Israel, two people were killed near Tel Aviv during an Iranian missile barrage. Security services reported multiple impact sites, bringing the total number of people killed by Iranian missile attacks on Israel to 14.

The United States has also expanded its military involvement, announcing strikes on Iranian missile facilities near the Strait of Hormuz. More from our West Asia Correspondent:

“Al Minhad Air Base, located about 24 kilometers south of Dubai and long used by the UAE Air Force, allied forces including the Royal Air Force, and a small Australian contingent, came under attack again this morning. An Iranian projectile, likely a missile or drone, struck a road just outside the base around early morning local time). It sparked a fire causing minor damage to an accommodation block and a medical facility in the Australian-operated section. UAE defenses were active, but this marks the second Iranian hit on the base since the conflict escalated, following an initial drone strike in the opening days.”

Saudi Arabia is hosting an emergency meeting of foreign ministers from Arab and Islamic countries in Riyadh this evening, as tensions across West Asia continue to intensify. Saudi authorities say the meeting aims to strengthen coordination and support regional security and stability.

In Madhya Pradesh, 7 people have lost their lives in a fire incident in Indore early this morning. The incident occurred near Bengali Square, where a massive fire broke out in a three-storey house around 4 AM. According to initial reports, the fire started after a short circuit in an electric car that was being charged outside the house, leading to a blast.

In New Delhi, a fire broke out in a multi-storey building in the Palam area today. The Delhi Fire Service has reported 7 casualties in the incident. The department informed that 31 fire tenders were deployed at the place after a call was received at 7 this morning. More details are awaited.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has expressed grief over the fire mishaps in New Delhi and Indore. In a social media post, the Prime Minister extended his condolences to the families of the deceased and prayed for the speedy recovery of the injured.

He announced an ex gratia of two lakh rupees from the PMNRF for the next of kin of each deceased, and fifty thousand rupees for the injured in both incidents.

India Meteorological Department (IMD) has issued an orange alert for thunderstorms accompanied with hailstorm over Chhattisgarh, Himachal Pradesh and Vidarbha for today. Thunderstorms accompanied with lightning and gusty winds are also predicted over Uttarakhand, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, Maharashtra and Interior Karnataka for today.

According to local meteorological observations, several parts of Ladakh, including Leh, Kargil, and surrounding high-altitude areas, have recorded fresh snowfall over the past 24 to 48 hours.

The National Centre for Disease Control (NCDC) has issued an advisory for the citizens amid the heat waves situation in the country. The NCDC advised drinking plenty of water and avoiding heavy work during peak heat in order to stay safe from heat stroke. The Centre also advised people to wear light coloured clothes.

The Union Cabinet has approved the Bharat Audyogik Vikas Yojna with an allocation of 33 thousand 660 crore rupees for developing 100 plug-and-play industrial parks in the country. Briefing media in New Delhi after the Cabinet meeting this afternoon, Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said, the scheme aims to develop world-class industrial infrastructure, unlocking manufacturing potential and driving the country’s growth story.

Mr Vaishnaw said, in a significant step towards enhancing farmers’ welfare and advancing the vision of Aatmanirbhar Bharat, the government has given the nod to Minimum Support Price (MSP) funding of over 1,718 crore rupees to the Cotton Corporation of India for cotton season 2023-24.

The Minister informed that the Cabinet also cleared the Small Hydro Power Development Scheme for the period 2026-27 to 2030-31 with an outlay of more than two thousand 584 crore rupees.

Mr Vaishnaw said, the Cabinet has approved the construction of over 101 km long four-lane access-controlled National Highway-927 from Barabanki to Bahraich in Uttar Pradesh at a cost of more than six thousand 969 crore rupees.

The Lok Sabha today passed the Appropriation (No 2) Bill, 2026. The legislation was moved by Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman. The Bill seeks to authorize payment and appropriation of certain sums from and out of the Consolidated Fund of India for the services of the financial year 2026-27.

Before the passage of the bill, the House also approved demands for grants for 2026-27 under the control of the different Ministries by applying the guillotine. The House negated the cut motions moved by the opposition members with a voice vote. Later, the Lower House adjourned for the day to meet on Monday.

Earlier, the Lower House also gave its nod for the demands of grants under the control of the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare for 2026-27. Replying to a discussion, Agriculture and Farmers Welfare Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan said that the government is moving forward with the objectives of ensuring food security, increasing farmers’ income, and providing nutritious food to the people.

Several members including, Konda Vishweshwar Reddy of BJP, Amrinder Singh Raja Warring of Congress, and Sanjay Uttamrao Deshmukh of Shiv Sena (UTB) participated in the debate.

The Rajya Sabha today bid farewell to 59 members from 20 states including 9 women who are set to retire from April to June this year. Speaking in the House on the occasion, Prime Minister Narendra Modi said that their contribution to Parliamentary democracy will always be remembered. After the farewell, the Upper House was adjourned for the day to meet again on Monday.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today held a telephonic conversation with the Crown Prince of Kuwait, Sheikh Sabah Al-Khaled Al-Hamad Al-Mubarak Al-Sabah on the evolving situation in West Asia. In a social media post, Mr Modi said, during their conversation, he reiterated India’s condemnation of attacks on Kuwait’s sovereignty and territorial integrity. They also emphasized that ensuring safe and free navigation through the Strait of Hormuz remains a foremost priority. Both sides agreed that sustained diplomatic engagement is essential for maintaining regional peace and stability. The Prime Minister thanked the Crown Prince for his continued support towards the safety and wellbeing of the Indian community in Kuwait. Mr Modi also conveyed greetings to the Crown Prince of Kuwait on the upcoming Eid festival.

The meeting of the Parliamentary Standing Committee on External Affairs was held at Parliament House Annexe this evening. The Committee was briefed by representatives of the Ministry of External Affairs on the safety, security and repatriation of the Indian Diaspora during the ongoing conflict in West Asia. Senior Congress MP Shashi Tharoor who is the Chairman of the Committee chaired the meeting. The Ministry of External Affairs team was led by Secretary (West) Sibi George. Speaking to media after the meeting, Mr Tharoor said, the discussion on West Asia was the most comprehensive.

In view of the ongoing situation in West Asia, the government has offered an additional 10 per cent allocation of commercial (Liquefied Petroleum Gas) LPG to all States and Union Territories, raising it to 30 per cent, subject to their support in the long-term transition from LPG to Piped Natural Gas (PNG). Joint Secretary, Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas, Sujata Sharma, shared this information during an inter-ministerial briefing on recent developments in West Asia held in New Delhi this afternoon. She said, the Government has also urged commercial LPG consumers in  major cities and urban areas to switch to PNG. The government also assured that crude availability is normal and refineries are operating at maximum capacity.

Special Secretary for Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways, Rajesh Kumar Sinha said, all Indian vessels remain safe, with no incidents reported.
