## Morning News

The Election Commission of India has barred both factions of the Trinamool Congress from using the party’s name and election symbol in the upcoming Assembly bypolls in Nandigram and Rejinagar in West Bengal.

“In an interim order issued yesterday, the Commission said neither the Mamata Banerjee-led group nor the Arup Roy-led group can use the name All India Trinamool Congress or the party’s reserved twin-flower symbol.Both groups will have to contest the bypolls under different names and with election symbols allotted by the Commission. This arrangement will continue until the dispute is finally settled.The Commission said documents and claims submitted by both sides show that there are currently two rival groups within the Trinamool Congress, with both claiming to be the real party.As the dispute cannot be settled before the upcoming bypolls, the Commission has taken this temporary step to decide who can use the party’s name and symbol for now.Sandion Dasgupta, RNU Kolkata.”

The first meeting of the Joint Parliamentary Committee on the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Amendment Bill, 2026, will be held in New Delhi today. The Committee will receive a briefing from representatives of the Ministry of Home Affairs on the provisions of the Bill.

“Both Houses of Parliament, on 12th August, gave their nod to referring the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Amendment Bill, 2026, to a Joint Parliamentary Committee for further scrutiny. The Bill seeks to amend the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act, 2010, with the objective of making the use of foreign contributions more transparent and accountable.

The Committee comprises 21 members from the Lok Sabha, nominated by the Speaker, and 10 members from the Rajya Sabha, nominated by the Chairman. Lok Sabha MP Sanjay Jaiswal will head the Joint Panel. Prominent members from the Lok Sabha include Tejaswi Surya, Nishikant Dubey, A. Raja and Supriya Sule. From the Rajya Sabha, Harsh Vardhan Shringla, Sanjay Kumar Jha, Praful Patel and Menaka Guruswamy are among the members of the Committee. The Committee is required to submit its report to the House by the last day of the first week of the Winter Session. Vishnu P S, for Akashvani News, Delhi.”

Candidates who appeared in the examination can now log in to the registration portal using their application number and password to view the final answer keys of their respective subjects. The NTA has advised all candidates to visit the official UGC-NET website regularly for the latest updates related to the examination. For any clarification, candidates may contact the NTA Help Desk through the contact details provided on the website.

External Affairs Ministry has notified the Passports (Amendment) Rules, 2026, amending the Passports Rules, 1980. ## Midday News

External Affairs Minister Dr. S. Jaishankar has reaffirmed India’s support for Bhutan’s developmental goals and priorities. During his official visit to Bhutan from 16th to 18th September, Dr. Jaishankar and his Bhutanese Counterpart Lyonpo D. N. Dhungyel reviewed all aspects of bilateral cooperation, besides regional and international issues of mutual importance. The two sides appreciated the progress in strengthening the special and multi-faceted India-Bhutan partnership.

“Dr. Jaishankar and his Bhutanese counterpart jointly inaugurated and launched several development projects. These included the 65-bedded Gyaltsuen Jetsun Pema Mother and Child Hospital in Mongar, the Jaganathan Bridge on the Samdrup Jongkhar-Trashigang Highway, and the transfer of 43 jersey cows from India to the National Cattle Breeding Centre in Bumthang. India also handed over 54 electric vehicles to the Bhutanese Government. The ministers also laid the foundation stone for the 500-kilowatt Lunana Hydropower Project. During the meeting, the two sides also expressed satisfaction at the implementation of the Joint Vision on Energy Partnership of March 2024, and recalled the recent strides in energy cooperation, including the inauguration of the 1020 MW Punatsangchhu-II Hydroelectric Project. It also includes signing of its Tariff Protocol, and the resumption of dam construction of 1200 MW Punatsangchhu-I HEP. Both the ministers also welcomed the signing of the Agreement on concessional Line of Credit of 4000 crores rupees for energy projects in Bhutan. They also welcomed the active engagement of Indian companies in hydropower projects in Bhutan, adding dynamism to the existing energy cooperation. Ankit Kumar,Akashvani News, Delhi.

The Ministers also welcomed the operationalisation of a UPI-based facility, enabling Bhutanese nationals to make digital payments in India using Bhutanese mobile applications.

During his visit, Dr. Jaishankar also called on the King of Bhutan Jigme Khesar Namgyel Wangchuck and Bhutanese Prime Minister Dasho Tshering Tobgay.

The Government has relaxed the sugar stock holding limit for bulk consumers from fifteen to thirty days. The relaxation is subject to  condition that the quantity of stock held beyond the existing fifteen days’ limit, shall be sourced exclusively from sugar imported under Advance Authorisation Scheme and Tariff Rate Quota.

The Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution Ministry said, the stockholding limit for purchase from the open market shall remain unchanged and will be restricted to fifteen days’ consumption only. The Ministry said, the measure is intended to strike a balance between the interests of bulk consumers and the need to maintain stability in the domestic sugar market.
