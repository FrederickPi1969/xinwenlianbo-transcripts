## Morning News

A two-day meeting of BRICS Foreign Ministers’ will begin today in New Delhi. The meeting will be chaired by External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar. BRICS Foreign Ministers and Heads of Delegation from Member and Partner Countries will participate in the meeting. They will also call on Prime Minister Narendra Modi. Global leaders have arrived in the national capital ahead of the BRICS Foreign Ministers’ Meeting. The leaders who arrived here yesterday include Foreign Minister of Iran, Seyed Abbas Araghchi, Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister of Thailand Sihasak Phuangketkeow, Foreign Minister of Cuba Bruno Rodriguez Parrilla and Deputy Minister of Foreign Affairs of Vietnam Nguyen Minh Hang. Other leaders who arrived were Foreign Minister of Malaysia Dato’ Seri Utama Mohamad bin Haji Hasan, and the UAE’s Minister of State, Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Khalifa Shaheen Al Marar. A report:

“During the meeting, the Foreign Ministers of BRICS Member Countries will exchange views on global and regional issues of mutual interest. On the second day, BRICS Member and Partner Countries will participate in a session themed “BRICS@20: Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability”. This will be followed by a session on the “Reforms of Global Governance and Multilateral System”.

The BRICS Foreign Ministers held their last meeting on the margins of 80th Session of the United Nations General Assembly (UNGA 80) on 26 September 2025. The meeting was chaired by India in its capacity as the incoming BRICS Chair for 2026. Aman Yadav, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

The Assam Government will introduce the Uniform Civil Code (UCC) Bill in the State Assembly on the 26th of this month. Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma made the announcement while addressing the media after chairing the first Cabinet meeting of his second term as Chief Minister. Mr Sarma said a special session of the Assam Assembly will be held from the 21st to 26th of May, during which the newly elected MLAs will take oath. He said the proposed legislation aims to address issues including the legal age of marriage, polygamy, inheritance, and live-in relationships. He clarified that religious practices would remain outside the scope of the Bill.

With this move, Assam is set to become the third State, after Uttarakhand and Gujarat, to table a Uniform Civil Code Bill.

The government has prohibited the export of sugar, including raw sugar, white sugar, and refined sugar, with immediate effect till September 2026 or further orders. According to a notification, Directorate General of Foreign Trade has amended the export policy from Restricted to Prohibited. This prohibition shall not apply to sugar being exported to the EU and USA under CXL and TRQ quota, the Advance Authorisation Scheme, and Government-to-Government shipments to meet food security needs of other countries. Consignments already in the physical export pipeline are also exempted.

The West Bengal government has lifted all restrictions on the inter – state movement of potato, onion, fruits, vegetables, oil seeds, food grains, and other essential products. The restrictions had been imposed by the erstwhile TMC government. On the other hand in a significant development, the government of West Bengal decided to hand over the cases related to teachers recruitment, municipal recruitment and cooperative services recruitment scams to the CBI. The West Bengal government has also terminated the services of all persons who were re-employed by the TMC government after their superannuation. More in this report:

”In a major relief for farmers and traders, the government of West Bengal has lifted restrictions on the export of agricultural goods to other states. According to a notification issued by the Department of Agricultural Marketing, items such as potatoes, onions, vegetables, fruits, food grains, and oilseeds can now move freely across state borders without any barriers. District administrations, police, and transport authorities have been directed to ensure smooth implementation of this order. Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari, speaking at Nabanna yesterday, said the decision will benefit small and marginal farmers as well as traders. He emphasized that farmers will now receive fair prices for their produce. The Chief Minister also warned that strict action will be taken against officials if goods-laden vehicles are unnecessarily stopped or harassed at interstate borders. This move is expected to ease the way for agricultural exports and reduce the difficulties faced by farmers and traders in West Bengal. SANDIPON DASGUPTA, AKASHVANI NEWS, KOLKATA.”

India has asserted that expectations are growing for BRICS to play constructive role amid global uncertainty and economic challenges. External Affairs Minister Dr S Jaishankar stated this in his opening remarks on the commencement of two-day BRICS Foreign Ministers’ meeting in New Delhi today. The meeting is being chaired by Dr Jaishankar. Foreign Ministers and Heads of Delegation from BRICS Member countries and Partner nations are participating in the meeting.

Speaking on the occasion, External Affairs Minister Dr S Jaishankar said, many countries continue to face challenges on energy, food, fertilizer and health security and BRICS can help them respond more effectively.

Dr S Jaishankar said, recent conflicts only underline the importance of dialogue and diplomacy.

Dr Jaishankar said today’s BRICS meeting provides a platform to exchange perspectives and develop convergences.

During the meeting, the Foreign Ministers will exchange views on global and regional issues of mutual interest.

The theme for India’s BRICS Chairship this year is ‘Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability’.

Foreign Ministers of several countries are participating in BRICS meeting. They include, Brazil’s Mauro Vieira, Russia’s Sergey Lavrov, Iran’s Seyed Abbas Araghchi, Indonesia’s Sugiono, Dato’ Seri Utama Mohamad bin Haji Hasan from Malaysia, UAE’s Khalifa Shaheen Al Marar and Ronald Lamola from South Africa, among others.

The Foreign Ministers will also call on Prime Minister Narendra Modi this afternoon.
On the second day tomorrow, BRICS Member and Partner Countries will participate in a session themed “BRICS@20: Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability”. This will be followed by a session on the “Reforms of Global Governance and Multilateral System”.

In a social media post, Dr Jaishankar said, they reviewed the progress of the Comprehensive Strategic India-Indonesia Partnership and the cooperation with the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN).
