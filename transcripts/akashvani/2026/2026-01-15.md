## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that Kashi-Tamil Sangamam celebrates the living unity of India’s many traditions while honouring their unique identities. In an OPED published in many newspapers today, Mr Modi said, what can be a better place than Kashi to host such a Sangamam. He said, Kashi has remained a civilisational anchor from time immemorial, where people have come from all over the world in search of knowledge, meaning and Moksha for thousands of years. Prime Minister Modi wrote that Kashi’s connection with Tamil people and culture is very deep. He said it is in Kashi that Baba Vishwanath resides, while Tamil Nadu has Rameswaram.

Earlier, Prime Minister Narendra Modi emphasized that harvest festivals like Pongal strengthen the spirit of “Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat”. Mr Modi said this while speaking at the Pongal celebrations yesterday, organised at the residence of Union Minister of State for Information and Broadcasting, Dr L. Murugan, in New Delhi. The festival of harvest, Pongal is being celebrated today by Tamil speaking people all over the world. This is also the beginning of spring in the Tamil calendar. Thai month brings about new beginnings in all aspects of life in Tamil society. It is considered auspicious for weddings and new business. On this day, the harvested grain is offered to the Sun which helps in agriculture.

“Thai piranthal vazhi pirakkum is an old saying which means the birth of this month will make a way for things to be accomplished. The streets in villages are festive to the eyes as a mini pandal would be made with sugarcanes and a new mudpot would be kept in the midst of the Rangoli. The mudpot will be decorated with turmeric plants and pongal is cooked with rice, milk and jaggery. The fun part is when the mudpot overflows the family shout pongalo pongal which means bounty after bounty. The people also preserve a set of seeds during this day after offering the produce to the Sun. The State Government operated special buses from Capital Chennai to various parts of the State. Chennai Sangamam, a folk and cultural festival, will be a part of the spectacle as folk dancers will dance to the tunes of the reverberating drums in more than 20 public places. Joy, Akashvani News, Chennai.”

The Tamil community in Sri Lanka is also celebrating Thai Pongal today with fervour and gaiety.

“As part of the celebrations, the Tamil Hindu community which numbers upwards of 2 and a half million in the island will visit temples today to thank for the abundance of previous year’s harvest and pray for a successful one in the coming year. The multi day celebration of Pongal, marks the beginning of the sun’s journey north ward, signalling the end of the winter solstice. Celebrations have started early in the morning with making of beautiful designs of Kolam before every house. As devotees pray to the sun god, chants of Pongalo pongal and overflowing of Pongal from the pots will be notable. Dr. Ahmed Muyeen Farooqi, Akashvani News,Colombo.”

On the other hand, both elders and children are seen enjoying kite flying in urban areas like Hyderabad. Lakshmi, Akashvani News, Hyderabad.”

Minister for Earth Sciences Dr Jitendra Singh today announced major expansion of Automatic Weather Stations.
