Today is the first Monday of the holy month of Sawan as it brings a special gift for devotees. In Ujjain, the city of Mahakal is decked up today, waiting for the palanquin. We have a report:

The first procession of the Sawan month will begin today from the world-famous Mahakaleshwar temple in Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh. During this, Lord Mahakal will sit in a palanquin in his Chandramauleshwar form. He will travel through the city and give darshan to devotees. A special prayer will be held at the assembly hall at three thirty in the afternoon. The procession will then leave the temple at four in the evening. The procession will pass through Gudri Chouraha, Bakshi Bazaar and Kaharwadi before reaching Ramghat. There, Lord Mahakal will be bathed with water from the Shipra river, followed by an aarti. After this, the procession will pass through different routes and return to the temple by around seven in the evening. The Mahakal temple administration has made special arrangements for the convenience of devotees. Medical teams and round-the-clock ambulance services have also been arranged along the route. The countdown to the 2030 Ahmedabad Commonwealth Games has officially begun, with the curtain falling on the Glasgow Commonwealth Games last night. A report;

As the curtain came down, India offered the world a vibrant invitation to  Ahmedabad, blending ancient philosophy, contemporary artistry and cultural grandeur in a handover presentation that officially launched the journey towards the centenary edition of the Games in 2030. Prince Edward, Duke of Edinburgh, representing King Charles III, formally declared the Games closed in Glasgow. He called up on athletes of all countries and territories to visit Ahmedabad, in four years time to celebrate the 24th Commonwealth Games. The formal handover began with the Commonwealth Games flag making its symbolic journey across the arena. Led by bearers holding the Scottish and Indian flags, the procession lowered the folded Games flag. Glasgow’s Lord Provost Jacqueline McLaren then passed it to Commonwealth Games Scotland vice-chair Susan Jackson, two-time Olympic medallist Neeraj Chopra, Indian Olympic Association president PT Usha, and finally Gujarat Deputy Chief Minister Harsh Sanghavi. This marked the official transfer of hosting responsibilities from Scotland to India. Indian athletes departed from Glasgow as a satisfied bunch after winning 39 medals at the 2026 Games, including 13 gold, 17 silver, and nine bronze, to end in fourth position on the standings. ## Evening News

The Lok Sabha today passed the Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Bill. The bill aims to increase the number of Judges of the Apex Court from the present 33 to 37 (excluding the Chief Justice of India). In May this year, President Droupadi Murmu had promulgated an ordinance in this regard. Union Minister of Law and Justice Arjun Ram Meghwal moved the Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Bill. He said, the Bill is an important step in that ongoing reform process within the judicial sector.

The Lok Sabha was adjourned several times today amid protests by opposition parties over several issues. Earlier, when the House began its proceedings at 11 AM, Speaker Om Birla congratulated the athletes for their outstanding performance at the 23rd Commonwealth Games at Glasgow. After that, Mr Birla tried to take up the Question Hour, but the opposition members raised the issue of alleged police action during students’ protest. Mr Birla expressed concern over the deliberate disruptions by some opposition members.

Minister of Statistics and Programme Implementation, Rao Inderjit Singh introduced the Indian Statistical Institute Bill, 2026. Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman introduced the Bankers’ Books Evidence Bill, 2026.

The Rajya Sabha today passed the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development (Amendment) Bill amid ruckus. The Bill aims to further amend the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development Act, 2006. It aims to address payment delays and simplify dispute resolution. Our correspondent has more details:

“Both the Houses of Parliament once again faced disruptions following opposition uproar over several issues, including alleged police action during students’ protest over the NEET issue. Lok Sabha was adjourned thrice while Rajya Sabha was adjourned twice. Due to the ruckus, Question Hour in both the Houses could not be taken up. However amidst uproar, the Lok Sabha passed the Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Bill, 2026. The bill aims to further amend the Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Act, 1956, for increasing the number of Judges of the Apex Court from the present 33 to 37 (excluding the Chief Justice of India). The Rajya Sabha passed the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development (Amendment) Bill 2026. It will amend the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development Act, 2006 to address payment delays and simplify dispute resolution. Besides, The Indian Statistical Institute Bill, 2026 and the Bankers’ Books Evidence Bill, 2026 were introduced in the Lok Sabha today. Anand Kumar, Akashvani News, Delhii.”
