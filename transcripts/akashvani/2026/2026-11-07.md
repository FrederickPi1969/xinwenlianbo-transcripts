## Morning News

Electronics and Information Technology Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw has said, India remains focused on becoming the artificial intelligence (AI) “application capital” of the world. Speaking to the media in New Delhi yesterday, Mr. Vaishnaw said the country is working towards expanding the use of  Artificial Intelligence across key sectors, including agriculture, healthcare and education. He added that Indian industries are making significant progress in developing AI-based applications.

Responding to questions on the recent global fall in technology stocks amid concerns over the future of AI investments, Mr. Vaishnaw said the AI industry is undergoing a period of rapid change and uncertainty. He said India is focusing on expanding the use of AI across various sectors and developing applications that can bring large-scale transformation.

India and the European Union have successfully concluded negotiations on New Delhi’s association with Horizon Europe, the bloc’s 93.5-billion-euro flagship research and innovation funding programme. Under the arrangement, Indian researchers, businesses and research organisations will be eligible to receive direct funding from Horizon Europe and lead international collaborative research projects under its second pillar, which focuses on global challenges and European industrial competitiveness. Once signed and ratified by both sides, the agreement is expected to come into effect in January next year.

“The Horizon Europe programme covers key areas such as healthcare, artificial intelligence, digital technology, space, climate, energy and industrial development. The European Commission said the agreement marks a significant milestone in India-EU relations and will strengthen scientific cooperation, create new research opportunities and deepen their strategic partnership. Horizon Europe currently includes 23 associated non-EU countries, ranging from the United Kingdom, Canada, and Japan to South Korea and Switzerland. The association will enable Indian institutions to receive grants directly from the European Commission and allow Indian scientists to lead and coordinate major international research projects, on terms similar to those available to organisations in EU member states, in the covered areas. The agreement is expected to give a fresh impetus to India-EU scientific and technological cooperation and strengthen collaboration in emerging technologies and innovation. Rishu Jaiswal, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

The National Health Authority, under the Union Ministry of Health and Family Welfare reviewed the progress of two flagship healthcare initiatives, Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB PM-JAY), and the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM). It focused on expanding beneficiary coverage, improving healthcare delivery, strengthening financial protection and accelerating the adoption of digital health services. The two-day National Review Meeting which concluded in Kolkata yesterday, brought together senior officials from States and Union Territories to assess the progress of both schemes and discuss priorities for their next phase of implementation.
The discussions also highlighted the role of technology, data analytics, automation and Artificial Intelligence in improving operational efficiency, transparency and accountability, as well as healthcare outcomes. Addressing the meeting, National Health Authority Chief Executive Officer Dr. Sunil Kumar Barnwal said the next phase of implementation must go beyond expanding coverage and focus on ensuring better-quality healthcare for beneficiaries.

In a statement, Road Transport and Highways Ministry said, the guidelines will ensure that upcoming and ongoing National Highway projects are equipped to support Multi-Lane Free Flow (MLFF)-based barrier-free toll collection, allowing vehicles to pass through toll plazas without stopping.

The Ministry of Railways has stepped up preparations to manage the heavy passenger rush during the Diwali and Chhath festivals. As part of its crowd-management measures, the Railways is operating 18,000 special trains, deploying additional Railway Protection Force personnel and installing Artificial Intelligence-enabled cameras at major railway stations. Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw stated this while inspecting the passenger holding areas at New Delhi Railway Station last evening. The Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) has released the date sheet for the Class 10th and Class 12th board examinations 2027. The examinations for both classes will begin on the 16th of February next year, with papers commencing at 10:30 AM. According to the official timetable, the Class 10 examinations will conclude on the 9th of March, while the Class 12 examinations will continue until the 9th of April. As per the notification, Class 10 students will appear in examinations for 83 subjects, while Class 12 students will take examinations in 121 subjects. The Board said the date sheet was prepared after carefully considering more than 40 thousand subject combinations to ensure that students do not face any clash between examinations of the subjects they have opted for.

The Government has clarified that E20 Petrol supplied by PSU Oil Marketing Companies meets prescribed quality standards. The clarification came following a press release issued by the Karnataka government’s Department of Food, Civil Supplies, Consumer Affairs and Legal Metrology claiming that petrol sold at certain retail outlets in the State contains between 26 percent and 31 percent ethanol. The Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas noted that E20 petrol supplied by the three OMCs across the country, including Karnataka, conforms to IS 17021, the Bureau of Indian Standards specification for ethanol-blended motor gasoline. It also said that 893 samples across 27 States and UTs, including 56 from Karnataka, meet prescribed specifications. The Ministry added that all 56 samples tested in Karnataka also conform to IS 17021 specifications, with ethanol content ranging from 19 per cent to 20 per cent.

Earlier, the three public sector Oil Marketing Companies, Indian Oil Corporation Limited, Bharat Petroleum Corporation Limited and Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited have clarified that E20 petrol supplied by them across the country conforms to the Bureau of Indian Standards specification for ethanol-blended motor gasoline.

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh has called upon Air Force personnel stationed at Bagdogra to maintain a high level of operational preparedness and vigilance, considering the strategic importance of the region in safeguarding the security of northeastern India.

Mr Singh was speaking to Air Force personnel at Bagdogra Airport in Siliguri today, before proceeding on a visit to Sikkim.

He said the nature of modern warfare was changing rapidly and was no longer confined to conventional battles along the borders. Cyberattacks, drone warfare, proxy wars and information warfare, including the spread of misinformation through social media, had emerged as major security challenges.

The Defence Minister stressed that military personnel must remain technologically aware and well-equipped to deal with these emerging threats. He said advanced technology alone could not ensure victory without courage, discipline, high morale and a strong sense of responsibility.

Mr Singh also emphasised the need to strengthen cordial relations between the armed forces and local communities, saying that public support was an important pillar of national security.

Highlighting the vision of Aatmanirbhar Bharat, he said Indian startups and industries were making significant contributions to the development of drones, anti-drone systems and other advanced defence equipment. The armed forces were also adopting indigenous technologies and modern training methods to prepare for future challenges.

The Defence Minister said that every successful military operation was the result of collective teamwork, dedication and professional commitment. He added that the entire nation stood firmly behind its armed forces.
