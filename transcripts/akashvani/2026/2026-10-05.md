## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Swiss President Guy Parmelin will hold bilateral discussions in New Delhi today. President Parmelin will be on a three-day state visit to India, accompanied by a high-level delegation comprising senior officials and business leaders.

Prime Minister Modi and President Parmelin will review progress in bilateral cooperation across a wide range of areas, including trade and investment, science, technology and innovation, mobility, culture, and people-to-people contact. The leaders will also exchange views on regional and global developments, including cooperation in multilateral fora.

President Parmelin will meet President Droupadi Murmu this evening. External Affairs Minister Dr. S. Jaishankar will also call on President Parmelin.

“Earlier, in February this year, the Swiss President visited India to participate in the AI Impact Summit in New Delhi. The visit assumes added significance in view of his participation in the 2nd India-EFTA Prosperity Summit on 7th of this month to mark the first anniversary of the India-EFTA Trade and Economic Partnership Agreement coming into force. Among EFTA countries, Switzerland is the largest trade and investment partner of India. India and Switzerland share longstanding friendly relations. This visit will further strengthen bilateral relations, building upon the regular high-level exchanges and deepen cooperation in new areas of mutual interest. Suparna Saikia, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

In Assam, Union Minister of Ports, Shipping and Waterways Sarbananda Sonowal will flag off the maiden international cargo voyage from the newly constructed Bogibeel Terminal in Dibrugarh today. Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma will also join the flag-off ceremony.

“With the flagging off of the cargo voyage today, Dibrugarh is set to restore its pre-Independence legacy as a major inland waterway trade hub. For the first time in more than eight decades, an international cargo consignment will sail directly from Assam to a foreign port. Union Minister Sarbananda Sonowal will flag off the cargo voyage at 11 a.m. today. The inaugural shipment consists of 1,080 tonnes of methanol produced by Assam Petro-Chemicals Limited at Namrup. Two renovated cargo vessels will transport the consignment to Pangaon Port in Dhaka, Bangladesh. Rakesh Doley, Akashvani News, Guwahati.”

Four airports are set to come up in West Bengal as the state government moves to speed up regional air connectivity. A tripartite MoU between the Centre and the state for the four airports will be signed today under the UDAN scheme.

“West Bengal is set to take a major step towards strengthening air connectivity, with the state government signing the agreement with the Centre today for the construction of four new airports in the state.

The agreement will be signed at Kolkata in the presence of Union Civil Aviation Minister K Ram Mohan Naidu, Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari and representatives of the Airports Authority of India. Chief Secretary Manoj Agarwal and Transport Minister Arjun Singh are also expected to attend the ceremony.

Under the Centre’s regional connectivity scheme, the proposed airports will come up at Charra in Purulia, Kalaikunda in Paschim Medinipur, Balurghat in South Dinajpur and Hasimara in Alipurduar.

The projects are expected to give a boost to regional economic activity, investment and tourism, besides creating employment opportunities and further improving air connectivity across the state. SandIpon Dasgupta, akashvani news, Kolkata.”

The Chhattisgarh Government has taken special initiatives to provide artificial limbs and assistive devices to persons with disabilities, particularly those affected by IED blasts, in the Bastar division of Chhattisgarh. Free camps are being organised from today for this purpose.

“The Bastar division of Chhattisgarh remained affected by the problem of Left Wing Extremism for decades. During this period, Maoists planted a large number of  improvised explosive devices, in the interior and remote areas of the region, targeting security forces.

Many innocent villagers and tribal people in Bastar were killed or suffered disabilities after coming in contact with these IEDs.

Free camps are being organised in Jagdalpur from the fifth to the seventh of October and in Dantewada from the fifteenth to the seventeenth of October. The district administration is also providing free transport and food facilities for persons with disabilities attending the camps.

State Deputy Chief Minister and Home Minister Vijay Sharma said that through these camps, the state government is making efforts to reach out to all those who became disabled due to IED blasts across the Bastar division and reduce the suffering they have faced for years. Vikalp Shukla, Akashvani News, Raipur.”

Today, in our special series, we bring you a report from our Aizawl Correspondent on Centre’s One District One Product (ODOP) initiative.

“Banana growers in Mizoram’s Lunglei district have earned around Rs 3.78 crore from banana-based products under the Centre’s One District One Product (ODOP) initiative, officials said.

Banana has been selected as Lunglei’s ODOP product, with the state government promoting its cultivation and the processing of bananas into products such as banana chips, juice and other food items.

Lunglei Deputy Commissioner Navneet Mann said the banana crop was selected after the district administration, found that bananas grow well in the area.

Banana cultivation is currently being carried out in around 17 villages in Lunglei district, involving about 300 to 400 households. Products made from the crop have so far generated an estimated income of Rs 3,78,28,000, officials said. Isaac Zoramsanga, Akashvani News, Aizawl.”

Flydubai flight’s Omani co-pilot tried to crash the aircraft in Saudi desert after the hijack attempt was foiled, reports Italian newspaper Corriere Della Sera. The paper says, Omani co-pilot Hammam al-Hammami attacked the Indian pilot Captain Smit Machchhar to take control of the plane and crash it into a building at Tel Aviv in Israel. Al-Hammami’s original plot was to kill Flight Captain Smit Machchhar, fly the plane to Tel Aviv, and crash it into one of the city’s skyscrapers, the report claimed, citing Israeli intelligence. According to the newspaper, Hammami realised his original plan could not be carried out, so he decided to crash the plane in the desert. He switched off the autopilot and pushed the control stick. Within 30 seconds, the aircraft lost 16,000 feet of altitude and was on its way to hit the ground. Al-Hammami tried to damage the controls of the aircraft as well before the passengers overpowered him. Yaniv Hayoun, a plumber by profession, pulled the control stick towards him and prevented the plane from losing altitude further.

India and Switzerland today inked several agreements in the field of promotion and collaboration on transportation technologies, mobility systems, and infrastructure. A Letter of Intent was also signed between the Department of Science and Technology and the Swiss National Science Foundation. An Implementation Arrangement for cooperation in innovation, technology transfer and entrepreneurship between the Department of Science and Technology and the Zurich University of Applied Sciences was also signed. These agreements were signed after the bilateral talks between Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Swiss President Guy Parmelin in New Delhi.

In a joint press meet, Prime Minister Modi appreciated Swiss President Guy Parmelin’s commitment to India-Switzerland relations. He said that India implemented the historic Trade and Economic Partnership Agreement with the European Free Trade Association, EFTA, countries last October. He said that the agreement was India’s first Free Trade Agreement with a European economic bloc.

Prime Minister Modi said that India and Switzerland have taken several important decisions to enhance market access for Indian exports in Switzerland and further facilitate investment between the two countries. He said, this will increase exports of Indian agricultural products, pharmaceuticals, textiles and engineering goods to Switzerland.

Mr Modi said that at the same time, India will attract new Swiss investments in important sectors, such as biotechnology, life sciences, banking, insurance, food processing and sustainability. Prime Minister said, through these initiatives, the two nations will work together to develop trusted supply chains and reliable solutions for the entire world.

President Parmelin arrived in New Delhi this morning on a three-day state visit to India, accompanied by a high-level delegation comprising senior officials and business leaders. The visiting dignitary was accorded a ceremonial welcome at the forecourt of Rashtrapati Bhavan.

The Reserve Bank of India’s Monetary Policy Committee began its three-day meeting in Mumbai today. The RBI’s policy review comes amid renewed tensions in West Asia, elevated crude oil prices and concerns over food inflation. The Committee will also assess the inflation outlook and prevailing economic conditions before announcing its decision at the end of the three-day meeting. The last repo rate hike was in February 2023, when the RBI raised the rate by 25 basis points to 6.50 per cent. It subsequently kept the rate unchanged through 2023-24 before beginning its rate-cut cycle in 2025. The policy repo rate currently stands at 5.25 per cent.
