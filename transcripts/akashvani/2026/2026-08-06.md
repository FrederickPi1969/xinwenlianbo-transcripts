## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has underscored that India has a rich tradition of storytelling and an extraordinary pool of creative talent, which can be leveraged to strengthen the nation’s position as a global hub for content creation. In a social media post, Mr Modi informed that he met the Co-CEO of Netflix, Ted Sarandos in New Delhi. The Prime Minister also noted that during the meeting, they discussed the immense potential of India’s media and entertainment sector. Addressing a conference of content creators in New Delhi last year, Mr Modi called upon them to share India’s stories to the World.

Meanwhile, Netflix India said that during the meeting, the two leaders discussed their shared vision of taking India’s media and entertainment industry to the global stage. The streaming platform also announced the launch of the Netflix India Storytelling Initiative, an investment aimed at nurturing the next generation of India’s creative talent through skilling and education partnerships with the Indian Institute of Creative Technologies (IICT) and the National Film Development Corporation (NFDC).

The Government has extended the tenure of Dr. T.V. Somanathan as the Cabinet Secretary by one year. The Appointments Committee of the Cabinet (ACC) has approved the one-year extension in the service of Dr. T.V. Somanathan, as Cabinet Secretary beyond 30th August 2026. The ACC has also approved the tenure extension of Govind Mohan as the Home Secretary by one year.

In the Lok Sabha, the Taxation and Other Laws ( Amendment) Bill, 2026 is listed for consideration and passing in today’s business of the House. The Bill seeks to amend the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007, the Income-tax Act, 2025, and the Finance Act, 2026.

In the Rajya Sabha, the Appropriation (No.3) Bill, 2026 is listed in the business of the House for consideration and return. The legislation provides for the authorisation of appropriation of moneys out of the Consolidated Fund of India to meet the amounts spent on certain services during the financial year ended on the 31st of March, 2023, in excess of the amounts granted for those services and for that year. The Lok Sabha has already given its approval to the Bill.

Students intensified their protest over alleged irregularities in recruitment examinations in Jharkhand. Ahead of the monsoon session of the state assembly, set to begin today, five protesters, including two women, joined Jharkhand Loktantrik Krantikari Morcha leader Devendra Nath Mahto, who has been on a hunger strike for the past four days. Mahto’s health became a matter of concern after his blood sugar and pressure levels dropped significantly. Chief Minister Hemant Soren yesterday asserted that his government was committed to solving students’ problems. In a post on social media, he said, his government is working on this matter with complete seriousness.

The protest began on July 25 under the banner of the JPSC-JSSC Reforms Manch at Jaipal Singh Munda Stadium in Ranchi. The protesters said they had been forced to adopt the extreme form of agitation after repeated appeals to the government failed to produce any concrete action. The demonstrators are demanding cancellation of the 14th Jharkhand PSC Civil Services Examination and an independent probe into the alleged irregularities in the recruitment test either by the Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) or by a panel of retired high court judges from outside the state. They also stressed comprehensive reforms in the Jharkhand Public Service Commission (JPSC) and the Jharkhand Staff Selection Commission(JSSC).

He has asked the SDRF, NDRF, district administration and other agencies to maintain close coordination and ensure prompt relief and rescue operations in case of any emergency.

India has urged Sri Lanka to cooperate in the early release of Indian fishermen and their boats detained by its authorities, while reiterating that a humanitarian approach should guide the handling of the long-pending issue. The matter figured prominently during Foreign Secretary Vikram Misri’s discussions with the Sri Lankan leadership during his official visit to Colombo. Mr Misri arrived in Colombo yesterday for a day-long visit. He held separate meetings with President Anura Kumara Dissanayake, Prime Minister Harini Amarasuriya, Foreign Minister Vijitha Herath and Foreign Secretary Aruni Ranaraja. During the talks, Mr Misri discussed issues pertaining to fishermen and requested Sri Lanka’s cooperation for the early release of fishermen and boats detained in the island nation. The fishermen issue is a contentious one in the ties between India and Sri Lanka, with Lankan Navy personnel even firing at Indian fishermen in the Palk Strait and seizing their boats in several alleged incidents of illegally entering Sri Lankan territorial waters.

The Government has said that 60 merchant vessels carrying cargo were safely moved through the Strait of Hormuz during West Asia crisis despite the heightened security situation in the region. Replying to a question in Parliament, the government also disclosed that 3,972 Indian seafarers were repatriated from the Gulf region, while a round-the-clock crisis management system handled tens of thousands of distress calls and emails.

The government’s response highlights India’s maritime response during the ongoing West Asia crisis, underscoring how New Delhi managed to keep one of the country’s most critical trade arteries functioning even as tensions threatened shipping through the Strait of Hormuz. The strategic waterway connects the Persian Gulf to the Gulf of Oman and the Arabian Sea, and serves as the world’s most critical maritime chokepoint for energy supplies.

The Bombay High Court today convicted former Tehelka editor Tarun Tejpal in the 2013 sexual assault case involving a former junior colleague. A division bench of Justices Neela Gokhale and Amit Jamsandekar of the Bombay High Court’s Goa bench convicted Tejpal under provisions of the Indian Penal Code related to rape by a person in a position of trust or authority, sexual harassment, and assault with intent to disrobe a woman. The court said it would pronounce the sentence later in the afternoon. During the hearing, Tejpal sought leniency describing himself as a political victim and the father of two daughters. The case stems from allegations made by a former junior colleague, who accused Tejpal of sexually assaulting her inside a hotel elevator during the ThinkFest event in Goa in November 2013. The Union Cabinet today approved GOBARdhan, the National Circular Bioenergy Scheme, with a total outlay of 23 thousand 731 crore rupees to boost clean energy and rural economy. Briefing media in New Delhi this afternoon, Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said, the scheme will be implemented from 2026-27 to 2035-36 and establish Compressed Biogas as a major pillar of India’s future energy mix.

Mr Vaishnaw said, the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs has also approved the construction of four Lane Highway from Guwahati to Tezpur along the National Highway-15 in Assam.

On Cabinet’s approval of GOBARdhan Scheme, Prime Minister Narendra Modi in a social media post, said, the decision is a vital step towards clean energy, rural prosperity and realising the Country’s collective dream of becoming a self-reliant nation.

Further, the Prime Minister highlighted the Guwahati-Tezpur Corridor project in Assam will reduce travel time, improve road safety and ease congestion through major bypasses at various locations.

Civil Aviation Minister K Rammohan Naidu has debunked the claims stating that the Government plans to blend ethanol with Aviation Turbine Fuel (ATF) are completely false and irresponsible. In a social media post, Mr Naidu today said that there is no such proposal. He said, Ethanol and Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF) are entirely different and should not be conflated. Mr Naidu said that Sustainable Aviation Fuel is an internationally certified aviation fuel and adopted globally which undergoes rigorous testing and meets stringent global safety standards.

Meanwhile, Commerce and Industry Ministry in a statement refuted the claims in media that India is importing and intends to import large quantities of ethanol from the United States for fuel blending, as baseless and factually incorrect. The Ministry said, ethanol used for fuel blending under the Ethanol Blended with Petrol Programme is sourced entirely from domestic producers as per the domestic policy framework.
