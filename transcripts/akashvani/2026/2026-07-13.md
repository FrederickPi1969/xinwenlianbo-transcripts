The Indian Space Research Organisation says, it has successfully carried out three major tests of the Gaganyaan crew module systems. More from our correspondent:

“The first test pertained to ensuring an upright position for the crew module after splashdown in the sea, considered one of the most important crew safety requirements. To do this, a stored cold-gas-based uprighting system was developed and tested. The second test involved examining the separation of the umbilical mechanism that serves as a link between the crew module, where astronauts live, and the service module, which provides power and propulsion.  The third test validated the structural integrity of the crew module during the apex cover separation event. The Supreme Court today ruled that the process of determining citizenship or declaring an individual a foreigner must be carried out through a fair, lawful and reasonable process. The Apex court was hearing a batch of petitions filed by 27 people who had been declared “foreigners” by the Foreigners Tribunals in Assam. The Supreme set aside the judgments of the Gauhati High Court that had upheld declarations of 27 appellants as foreigners. A bench of Justice Vikram Nath and Justice Sandeep Mehta acknowledged that the State has a legitimate and compelling interest in ensuring that those not legally entitled to Indian citizenship do not obtain it through false claims, misuse of legal process, or delay, but stressed that due process must prevail.

The bench directed that no coercive action be taken against the petitioners until the tribunals rehear their cases. The petitioners had challenged the tribunal orders before the Gauhati High Court and later the Supreme Court, relying on documents such as pre-1971 legacy records, electoral rolls and land records to establish their Indian citizenship.

The Supreme Court today directed the Special Investigation Team, SIT constituted by the Uttar Pradesh Government on alleged misappropriation of donations in Ram Janmbhumi Temple, to submit a status report on the investigation. The Court also issued notice to the Union Government, the Uttar Pradesh Government and the Shri Ram Janmbhoomi Teerth Kshetra Trust on petitions seeking a CBI probe into alleged embezzlement of donations meant for the Ayodhya Ram Mandir. A Bench headed by Chief Justice of India, Surya Kant and Justices Joymalya Bagchi and V. Mohan heard the petitions.

Earlier on June 13, the UP government constituted the three-member SIT at the request of the Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra Trust following allegations of misappropriation of temple donations.

In  Cricket, Indian team led by Harmanpreet Kaur today recorded a historic win in one-off women’s Test against hosts England at the Lord’s. India defeated England comfortably by 270 runs in first-ever Women’s Test held at Lord’s. Chasing a target of 457 runs, the hosts were all out for 186 in their second innings before lunch on day 4. For India, Sneh Rana took 4 wickets, while Sayali Satghare, Kranti Gaud and Deepti Sharma bagged two wickets each.

India had declared their second innings at 341 for 7 yesterday at the stroke of tea, giving a target of 457 runs.
