## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will interact today via video conference with participants of Seva Yatra travelling from Varanasi in UP to Vadnagar in Gujarat under Seva Sankalp Abhiyan.

“The interaction with the Prime Minister will give the Seva Yatris a chance to share their experiences from the journey, which is intended to connect youth participation and community service across the locations covered by the teams. The Seva Yatra began on September 17 and is part of the wider ‘Seva Sankalp Abhiyan’ to highlight PM Modi’s 25 years of public life. The participants are travelling through towns, districts, talukas and villages, with the programme focusing on taking community service and public engagement activities to the grassroots. The journey is not limited to a motorcycle expedition, but is designed to bring together service, communication, youth participation, social welfare, cultural connect and community mobilisation at different locations along the route. Yashasvi Kiryal, Akashvani News, Delhi.

The Prime Minister yesterday interacted with participants of the Seva Yatra, travelling from Vadnagar to Varanasi and praised their ongoing journey as a significant step toward grassroots nation-building. During the interaction, Mr Modi asked the participants about their experiences on the road, their interactions with people and the new learnings they had gathered during the journey.

Today marks 12 years of Make in India. It was on this day in 2014 when the initiative was launched with the ambition of becoming a global hub for manufacturing, design and innovation. The initiative has transformed India’s manufacturing landscape by expanding production, investment and capabilities across industries.

“Over the years, Make in India has opened new opportunities for businesses and investors to participate in India’s manufacturing journey. The initiative has subsequently been expanded under Make in India 2.0. The initiative now focuses on 27 sectors, which include 15 in manufacturing and 12 in services. Guided by ‘Minimum Government, Maximum Governance’, it has also sought to modernize processes and policies. Under the initiative, India’s electronics production increased nearly sevenfold, from 1.9 lakh crore rupees in 2014-15 to over 13 lakh crore in 2025-26. During this period, Indian Railways manufactured nearly 55 thousand coaches over 2014-24. Further, under the Make in India initiative, country’s indigenous defence production increased from 46 thousand 429 crore in 2014-15 to a record 1.78 lakh crore in Fiancial Year 2025-26, the initiative now goes beyond producing more goods and focuses on building the skills, technology and capacity needed to make them in India. With Priya Mondal, Bhanu Pratap Singh, Akashvani News, Delhi.

The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) has proposed nationwide ban on manufacture and sale of analogue product as paneer. In a notification, the FSSAI stated that the products already licensed or registered under ‘Analogue in Dairy Context’ category shall discontinue the use of the term Paneer in their nomenclature, labeling or marketing. The FSSAI has proposed the amendment in the Food Safety and Standards (Prohibition and Restrictions on Sales) Regulations, 2011 for Analogue Paneer.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi is completing 25 years in public service, a journey defined by the mantra of Jan Seva Se Desh Seva, and a relentless commitment that continues.

Guided by this vision for Seva and Sushasan, the Government has been ceaselessly working towards achieving the goal of a Viksit Bharat by 2047. Through inclusive development and social empowerment, this dedication is transforming lives.

Today, in our special series, we bring you an instance of how this vision has been translated into action through Shree Anna or Millets.

“Millets such as pearl millet, sorghum, finger millet, kodo, little millet, barnyard millet and amaranth have been part of India’s traditional diet for centuries. The Government of India is now promoting these grains as Shree Anna. The observance of 2023 as the International Year of Millets also helped raise their global profile.

Millets provide dietary fibre, protein, iron, calcium, magnesium and other micronutrients. They can form part of a balanced diet and are particularly relevant for managing lifestyle diseases such as diabetes and obesity. Senior faculty  at Jaipur’s Sawai Man Singh Medical College, Dr Puneet Saxena, says millets are naturally gluten-free and nutritionally rich.

Millets also need less water, making them suitable for water conservation and climate-resilient farming. Rajasthan has traditionally been a major millet-growing state, particularly for pearl millet. The focus is now shifting from production to processing, value addition and better market access. Jitendra Dwivedi, Akashvani News, Jaipur.”

The fourth edition of the UP International Trade Show (UPITS) will begin today at the India Expo Centre and Mart in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh. Union Home Minister Amit Shah and Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath will inaugurate the five-day event.

“The event is expected to witness participation of over 2,400 exhibitors, 1.5 lakh B2B buyers and more than 4.5 lakh general visitors. Over 550 international buyers from 85 countries are also expected to attend the event. Partner countries for this edition include Austria, Japan, Russia, Singapore, Belarus and Vietnam. Additionally, reflecting the prominent role of women in trade and industry, around 300 women representatives will actively participate in the event. The exhibition will showcase various industries and sectors, including agriculture, automobiles, defence, IT, health, tourism and renewable energy. A major highlight this year is the introduction of the ‘One District, One Cuisine’ initiative, which will be featured alongside the ‘One District, One Product’ scheme to present the state’s traditional culinary heritage to the world. With Ankit Kumar, Akshit Vedyan, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

Anant Chaturdashi is being celebrated across Mumbai today, marking the culmination of the ten-day Ganeshotsav that began on Ganesh Chaturthi.

“Mumbaikars will bid a tearful adieu to their beloved Ganpati Bappa amid the beats of dhol-tasha, pushpavrishti and chants of “Ganpati Bappa Morya”. Thousands of devotees are expected to participate in the grand farewell to Lord Ganesh, as they gather to catch a glimpse of their favourite deity. The visarjan processions of Lalbaugcha Raja and Mumbaicha Raja are among the most iconic sights of the city.

The Maharashtra Tourism Department has set up a special viewing pavilion at Girgaon Chowpatty for foreign tourists to witness the Ganesh immersion ceremony and experience Mumbai’s vibrant cultural traditions.

Major immersion sites, including Girgaon Chowpatty, Dadar, Juhu and other locations, have been equipped with special arrangements for devotees. The BMC has deployed civic personnel, lifeguards, ambulances and motorboats to facilitate safe and smooth immersions. C PRARTHNA, AKASHVANI NEWS, MUMBAI”

The deep depression over north coastal Andhra Pradesh and adjoining south Odisha has weakened into a depression, but its impact continues to trigger heavy rain in several parts of Odisha. Southern and western districts have been particularly affected after three days of incessant rainfall, with flooding, landslides and disruption of road communication reported from several areas. Schools and Anganwadi centres remain closed today in six districts as authorities continue to monitor the situation.

“Heavy rain continues in several parts of southern and western Odisha under the influence of the weakened depression. Koraput, Rayagada, Malkangiri, Ganjam, Nabarangpur, Gajapati and Kalahandi remain among the worst affected districts. According to the state government, rain-related impacts have been reported across dozens of blocks and villages, while more than eight thousand people have been shifted to safer locations. Road communication has been disrupted in many parts of southern and western Odisha. Flood conditions are also being monitored in northern Odisha. The Jalaka river at Mathani in Balasore district is in a severe flood situation, with several low-lying areas of Basta facing inundation. The India Meteorological Department has forecast continued rainfall in Odisha, although the weather system is gradually weakening. Prakash Dash for Akashvani News, Bhubaneswar.”

Heavy rain is likely in several districts of West Bengal today. The Alipore Meteorological Office has warned of heavy rain in East and West Midnapore, Bankura, Purulia, West Burdwan and Birbhum.

The deep depression is moving north-northwestwards and is expected to weaken further over the next 24 hours. It brought intermittent rain to several districts overnight.

Strong winds of 40 to 50 kilometres per hour may affect South 24 Parganas and East and West Midnapore.

The Indian duo won 11-6, 13-15, 11-9, 11-5 in the quarterfinals to secure India’s first table tennis medal at the ongoing Games.

Defending champions India held Azerbaijan to a 2-2 draw in the eighth round of the FIDE Chess Olympiad in Samarkand yesterday. R Praggnanandhaa, Arjun Erigaisi, D Gukesh and Vidit Santosh Gujrathi were all held to draws by their Azerbaijani opponents. Gukesh drew with Muradli Mahammad after 56 moves, while Praggnanandhaa drew with Shakhriyar Mamedyarov, Arjun with Aydin Suleymanli and Vidit with Rauf Mamedov. India remained in joint second place in the Open section with 13 match points. Uzbekistan and Germany share the lead with 14 match points after Germany defeated the hosts 2.5-1.5.
