## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will launch the nationwide Human Papillomavirus (HPV) Vaccination Programme for girls aged 14 years from Ajmer in Rajasthan today. The nationwide roll-out marks a decisive move in the country’s public health care journey and fulfills the government’s commitment to advancing the vision of Swastha Nari, ensuring prevention, protection and equity at the core of women’s healthcare. The programme will target approximately 1.15 crore girls aged 14 years every year across all States and Union Territories. The vaccine will be provided free of cost at designated government health care facilities. The Vaccination will be voluntary and informed consent from parents or guardians will be obtained prior to administration.

“India’s national programme will use a quadrivalent HPV vaccine -Gardasil. HPV vaccines are among the most extensively studied vaccines globally with more than 500 million doses administered worldwide since 2006. Scientific evidence demonstrates almost 100 percent  effectiveness in preventing cervical cancer caused by vaccine-covered HPV types. With this launch, India will join over 160 countries that have introduced HPV vaccination into their immunization schedules. The special campaign will run in mission mode over a three-month period, during which eligible girls can receive the vaccine daily at designated facilities. Thereafter, the vaccine will continue to be available on routine immunization days.The nationwide HPV vaccination programme reflects delivery-oriented governance where scientific evidence, policy commitment and implementation readiness converge to produce measurable public health impact. Anand Kumar, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

The Indian Medical Association (IMA) has hailed the central government’s decision to provide free HPV vaccination for adolescent girls aged 14 years across India. In a letter to Prime Minister Narendra Modi, IMA said that this initiative will contribute in the prevention of cervical cancer, safeguarding the health and lives of millions of women in India.

Moreover, Prime Minister Modi will also launch, inaugurate and lay the foundation stone of multiple development projects worth over 16 thousand 680 crore rupees in Ajmer, Rajasthan today. The projects span key sectors, including urban development, drinking water supply, roads, irrigation, energy, and industrial infrastructure. The Prime Minister will also address a public gathering on the occasion.

“The Prime Minister will inaugurate several National Highway projects, including the 4-lane Greenfield Expressway from Bandikui to Jaipur, along with other key infrastructure projects. With the completion of the Bandikui–Jaipur Greenfield Expressway, Jaipur has now been directly connected to the Delhi–Mumbai Greenfield Expressway. In addition, the Prime Minister will lay the foundation stone for important elevated road projects at Sanganer in Jaipur and in Jodhpur city. In line with the Government’s commitment to ensuring safe and clean drinking water for all, the Prime Minister will lay the foundation stone for four packages of the Nonera Major Drinking Water Project and five packages of the Parwan Akawad Major Drinking Water Project. To ensure seamless power transmission from generation centres, the Prime Minister will inaugurate transmission systems for evacuation of power from renewable energy zones in the state. On the occasion, the prime minister will also distribute appointment letters to more than 21,000 youth selected in various departments of the State Government. – Jitendra Dwivedi, Akashwani News, Ajmer.”

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will also visit Gujarat this afternoon. During his visit, Mr Modi will inaugurate Micron Technology’s state-of-the-art Semiconductor Assembly, Test and Packaging (ATMP) facility in Sanand. This landmark event marks the official commencement of commercial production and shipment of the first Made-in-India semiconductor memory modules. Union Minister of Electronics and IT Ashwini Vaishnaw will also be present on the occasion.

“The inauguration of Micron’s Sanand facility today marks a transformative leap for India’s technology ecosystem. With a total investment of over ₹22,500 crore, the project represents the first proposal approved under the India Semiconductor Mission. This project, which saw its ground-breaking just in September 2023, is now ready to convert advanced DRAM and NAND wafers into finished products for markets worldwide. A key highlight of this facility is its nearly 500,000 square feet of cleanroom space, ranking it among the largest raised-floor cleanrooms in the world. By commencing commercial shipments of the first indigenous memory modules, India is taking a decisive step toward self-reliance in the semiconductor sector. Aparna Khunt /Akashvani News /Ahmedabad.”

The Election Commission has published the incomplete final voter list in West Bengal after Special Intensive Revision of electoral roll. The list has been released in accordance with the Supreme Court’s directive and the Election Commission’s guidelines. As per the incomplete roll, the total number of voters stands at 7 crores, 4 lakhs 59 thousand and 284. Altogether, 63 lakhs 66 thousand 952 names have so far been removed from the state’s voter list. The concerned voter can submit claims and objections within the next 15 days.

In Andhra Pradesh, twenty-one people were killed and 13 others were injured in a massive explosion at a firecracker manufacturing unit in Vetlapalem village of Samarlakota in Kakinada district today. More from our correspondent:

“The blast occurred around 2 pm near the Godavari canal, when 34 workers were inside. Police, fire, health, and emergency teams rushed to the site and launched rescue operations. Police have so far identified 20 of the deceased. The injured were shifted to Government General Hospital, Kakinada, where several are reported to be in critical condition.Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu visited the spot, ordered immediate medical aid, and directed relief measures for affected families. Allu Murali, Akashvani News, Vijayawada.”

The President, Vice President, Prime Minister and Home Minister have expressed grief at the loss of lives in the incident. Prime Minister Narendra Modi in social media post also announced an ex-gratia of two lakh rupees from Prime Minister’s National Relief Fund to the next of kin of each deceased. The injured will be given fifty thousand rupees.

In Cricket, Jammu and Kashmir have created history by clinching their maiden Ranji Trophy title, defeating Karnataka through first innings lead in the final at Hubballi. After opting to bat, Jammu and Kashmir posted a mammoth 584 in their first innings. With the ball, the Union Territory bowled out Karnataka for 293, taking the decisive first innings lead. Jammu and Kashmir then extended the subsequent lead of 291 in the first innings to 633 in the second innings. The match ended in a draw on Day 5, with Jammu and Kashmir winning the match and ending a 67-year wait for a Ranji Trophy title.

The Vice President, Prime Minister and Home Minister congratulated the Jammu and Kashmir Cricket Team on their triumph and scripting a new chapter in region’s sporting history. Jammu and Kashmir Chief Minister Omar Abdullah has announced a cash reward of 2 crore rupees for the team.

In the Men’s T20 Cricket World Cup, Pakistan have set a target of 213 runs for Sri Lanka in a Super 8 group 2 match at Pallekele. Pakistan posted 212 for eight wickets in stipulated 20 overs. In reply, Sri Lanka were 8 for one in 1.3 overs, when reports last came in.
