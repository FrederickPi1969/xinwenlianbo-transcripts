## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will be on a day-long visit to Rajasthan and Gujarat today to inaugurate, and lay the foundation stone of several development projects. The Prime Minister will first travel to Rajasthan, where he will inaugurate the new terminal building of Jodhpur Airport this morning. The project worth 480 crore rupees, is designed to handle up to 20 lakh passengers annually. He will also launch the Modified UDAN Scheme in Jodhpur. Thereafter, Mr Modi will travel to Balotra where he will dedicate, inaugurate, and lay the foundation stone for development projects worth approximately 1.06 lakh crore rupees this afternoon. These projects span multiple sectors, including urban transport, railways, roads, renewable energy, and power transmission.  He will also dedicate India’s first greenfield integrated refinery-cum-petrochemical complex to the nation at Pachpadra in Balotra, marking a landmark achievement in the country’s energy and petrochemical sector. Prime Minister Modi will also lay the foundation stone for Phase 2 of the Jaipur Metro Rail Project, which has a total cost of over 13 thousand crore rupees. More from our correspondent:

“The refinery and petrochemical complex is expected to open new avenues for investment, employment, and industrial growth in the state’s western districts. The Rajasthan Refinery Project will also boost sectors such as MSMEs, packaging, textiles, auto components, and agri-films, providing a fresh push to the state’s economy. Built on Rajasthan’s challenging desert terrain, the project is a remarkable feat of engineering. The second phase of the Jaipur Metro Rail Project will develop the North-South Corridor, significantly enhancing the city’s multimodal connectivity. The corridor will integrate metro services with the airport, railway station, and bus terminal, providing seamless and more convenient public transport for citizens. Jitendra Dwivedi, Akashvani News, Jodhpur.”

Later in the evening, Prime Minister Modi will travel to Gujarat where he will inaugurate the CG Semi Outsourced Semiconductor Assembly and Test facility at Sanand in Ahmedabad. The project is one of the first four approved under the India Semiconductor Mission and has been developed with a total investment of over 7 thousand 500 crore rupees. Our correspondent has the details:

“The newly operational CG Semi Outsourced Semiconductor Assembly and Test-or OSAT-facility represents a major milestone in India’s journey toward building a self-reliant technology ecosystem. Once fully ramped up, the plant will have an annual production capacity of up to 5 billion semiconductor chips. It will offer comprehensive, end-to-end packaging and testing services, including wafer sorting, package design, and failure analysis. Driven by rapid advancements in Artificial Intelligence and high-performance computing, the facility is designed to meet the global demand for advanced memory and storage solutions. The high-capacity production at Sanand will directly cater to global and domestic customers across critical industries, including automotive, industrial automation, 5G communications, and the Internet of Things. Aparna Khunt, Akashvani News, Ahmedabad.”

Gujarat Anti-Terrorist Squad (ATS) has arrested eight people associated with the Pakistan-based banned terror outfit Jaish-e-Mohammad. They were arrested from various districts of Gujarat and Madhya Pradesh in simultaneous operations yesterday.

A total of 254 pieces of literature, including the flag of Jaish-e-Mohammad, were recovered from the accused. The investigation also revealed financial links to the Pakistan-based terror group. The arrested individuals have been sent to a 14-day Police remand.

Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal has said that India is on track to achieve its one trillion US dollar export target, with nearly 15 per cent of the annual exports already recorded in the first quarter of the current financial year. The claims circulating in social media that E20 fuel, which is a blend of 20 per cent ethanol and 80 per cent petrol, causes a drop in vehicle mileage are misleading. According to extensive field trials led by the Automotive Research Association of India, in collaboration with Indian Oil Corporation Limited, Indian Institute of Petroleum, Dehradun, and the Society of Indian Automobile Manufacturers, no significant negative impact on vehicle fuel efficiency has been demonstrated. The trials covered approximately 40 thousand kilometres on passenger cars and 20 thousand kilometres on two-wheelers. It said, the mileage is influenced by many factors beyond fuel type including driving habits, maintenance, tyre pressure and alignment, and air-conditioning load.

In Jammu and Kashmir, a total of 12 thousand 168 pilgrims paid obeisance at the Holy Cave Shrine on the opening day of the annual Amarnath Yatra yesterday, as the pilgrimage began from the twin base camps of Baltal and Pahalgam. The Yatra commenced in the early hours from both the traditional 48-km Nunwan-Pahalgam route and the shorter 14-km Baltal route, despite intermittent rainfall.

Pilgrims, including men, women, and sadhus, began their journey to the 3,880-metre-high Holy Cave Shrine amid chants of “Bum Bum Bhole” after being flagged off by the respective Deputy Commissioners and Senior Superintendents of Police from the two base camps. The 57-day pilgrimage will conclude on 28th of August.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today inaugurated CG Semi’s state-of-the-art Outsourced Semiconductor Assembly and Test (OSAT) plant in Sanand, Gujarat. The event was attended by Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw and Gujarat Chief Minister Bhupendra Patel, along with global tech leaders. Developed with a massive investment of over 7,500 crore rupees, the advanced facility will have an annual production capacity of up to 5 billion semiconductor chips.

Addressing a large gathering, Prime Minister Modi emphasised that this development is the next step in India’s massive electronics revolution, which has already established the country as the world’s second-largest mobile phone manufacturer. He stated that the CG Semi OSAT facility in Sanand will not only strengthen the domestic chip manufacturing ecosystem but also boost technological self-reliance and elevate India’s position in the global semiconductor value chain.

Lauding the diverse workforce at the plant, the Prime Minister described the complex as a “Mini India,” where engineers and professionals from various states are working under one roof to power the global digital economy.

The Prime Minister also said, the Sanand plant is geared to produce 20 crore chips annually. He congratulated CG Semi on setting an ambitious future target of scaling production to 500 crore chips per year.

Addressing at the occasion, Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw announced that India will have five operational semiconductor plants by the end of 2026. He emphasized that chips produced at the Sanand plant will cater to diverse industrial applications and will be exported to global markets, including the United States, Japan, and Europe.

Upon reaching Sanand, the Prime Minister toured the high-tech plant and inspected the production process. He also interacted closely with engineers and operators working inside the facility.

Earlier, Prime Minister Modi inaugurated and laid the foundation stone for development projects worth over 1 lakh 5 thousand crore rupees in Rajasthan, marking a major push to infrastructure, energy, transport and employment generation in the state. During his day-long visit, the Prime Minister inaugurated the new terminal building at Jodhpur Airport. Thereafter, he dedicated the country’s most modern Greenfield Refinery-cum-Petrochemical Complex to the nation in Pachpadra in Balotara district, which is an industrial processing plant built from scratch on undeveloped clean land. He also inspected the refinery’s state-of-the-art control room, interacted with engineers and technical experts, flagged off fuel tankers, and virtually laid the foundation stone for Jaipur Metro Phase-II.

Addressing the programme, the Prime Minister said the conflict in West Asia had triggered the biggest energy crisis of the 21st century, but India successfully overcame the challenge through timely decisions, effective diplomacy and prudent management of national resources.

Mr. Modi also handed over appointment letters to around 54 thousand youth recruited across various departments of the Government of Rajasthan.
