## Morning News

The Nation celebrates its 77th Republic Day today. President Droupadi Murmu will lead the Nation in celebrating the Day from Kartavya Path in New Delhi. The Republic Day parade will start at 10:30 AM and run for a duration of around 90 minutes.

“The Republic Day Parade at Kartavya Path today will be a unique blend of India’s cultural diversity and military prowess. The theme this year is ‘Swatantrata ka Mantra: Vande Mataram’ and ‘Samriddhi ka Mantra: Aatmanirbhar Bharat’. The Republic Day Parade will start with Prime Minister Narendra Modi laying a wreath at the National War Memorial. President Droupadi Murmu will arrive at Kartavya Path, receiving salute during a ceremonial march past. President of the European Council Antonio Costa and the President of the European Commission Ursula von der Leyen will be the Chief Guest at the parade. Thirty tableaux from various States, Union Territories, Central Government Ministries and Departments will be showcased during the parade. The tableaux will exhibit a unique mix of 150 years of national song Vande Mataram and the nation’s rapid progress on the back of growing self-reliance across sectors. The Tri- services tableau would represent the collective strength, unity, and integration of the Indian Army, Indian Navy, and Indian Air Force. The event will conclude with a flypast by 29 aircrafts, including Rafale, Su-30, C-295, Mig-29, Apache, among others. To honour their contributions in nation and society building, 2 thousand 500 artists will present cultural performances. With ANUPAM MISHRA FROM KARTAVYA PATH, PRIYAL MONDAL, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has extended best wishes to countrymen on the occasion of Republic Day. In a social media post, Mr Modi wished that the occasion renew energy and enthusiasm in citizens to build a Developed Nation. He said Republic Day is a powerful symbol of independence, Constitution, and the democratic values.  Mr Modi added that this festival gives us new energy and inspiration to move forward unitedly with the resolve to build the nation.

Vice President CP Radhakrishnan also extended greetings to citizens in India and abroad on the occasion of the 77th Republic Day. In a social media post, the Vice President said that the day celebrates India as a vibrant Republic, founded on the timeless ideals of justice, liberty, equality, and fraternity.He added that the day stands not only as a tribute to the vision and foresight of the framers of the Constitution, but also stands as a solemn reaffirmation of the duties and responsibilities of the citizens to uphold the democratic values.

President Droupadi Murmu has said that Atma-nirbharata and Swadeshi are the guiding principles in shaping the country’s economic destiny. She lauded India’s young entrepreneurs, sportspersons, scientists, and professionals for energising the nation and making their mark globally. President Murmu made these remarks while addressing the nation on the eve of the 77th Republic Day yesterday. Ms Murmu said that the country has the world’s largest young population and its youth possess immense talent. She added that a large number of country’s young population is presenting impressive instances of success through self-employment.

Urging everyone to work together with the spirit of ‘Nation First’ and make India even more glorious, President Murmu said that Republic Day is an opportunity to strengthen the strong feeling of patriotism. She also noted India’s precision strikes against terror infrastructure through Operation Sindoor last year.

The Government has announced 131 Padma awards for the year 2026. The awards comprise five Padma Vibhushan, 13 Padma Bhushan and 113 Padma Shri. Among the awardees, 19 are women and 16 honours are being conferred posthumously.  Actors Dharmendra & Mammootty, Supreme Court judge, KT Thomas and political VS Achuthanandhan are the prominent figures selected for Padma Vibhushan, while singer Alka Yagnik and cricketer Rohit Sharma are the prominent Padma awardees. The list of Padma Bhushan awardees includes playback singer Alka Yagnik, former Uttarakhand Chief Minister Bhagat Singh Koshyari, Kallipatti Ramasamy Palaniswamy from Tamil Nadu, and Malayalam actor Mammootty from Kerala.

President Droupadi Murmu approved gallantry awards for 70 Armed Forces personnel, including six posthumous awards, on the eve of the 77th Republic Day. IAF Group Captain Shubhanshu Shukla, who made history by becoming the first Indian to visit the International Space Station, has been honoured with the Ashoka Chakra, the country’s highest peacetime gallantry award. The President has also approved 301 military decorations for the Armed Forces and other personnel.  On the eve, the President also approved 98 Mentioned-in-Despatches to the Armed Forces personnel, including five posthumous.

Delhi Police has issued a detailed traffic advisory to ensure the smooth conduct of the ceremonial parade and other associated cultural events.  Kartavya Path will be closed for traffic until the parade ends. No crossing will be allowed on Kartavya Path between Vijay Chowk and India Gate till 2 pm today. In addition C-Hexagon near India Gate will remain closed to traffic from 9:15 am onwards. Traffic on Tilak Marg, Bahadur Shah Zafar Marg and Netaji Subhash Marg will be diverted from 10:30 am onwards. Further, Delhi Police has urged commuters to avoid the parade route and nearby areas unless travel is essential.

As part of tour programme, PMIS interns interacted with Union Minister Harsh Malhotra and shared their learning experiences, aspirations, and feedback on the scheme. Addressing the interns, Mr Malhotra said to enable India’s youth, PMIS is designed to open new opportunities and serve as a bridge between theoretical learning and practical experience.

Prime Minister’s flagship platform for Pro-Active Governance and Timely Implementation, PRAGATI, has marked a significant milestone with the successful conduct of its 50th meeting. Today, in this special series, we take a look at the Nerul-Belapur – Uran Railway Line located in Maharashtra.

“Nerul-Belapur – Uran Railway Line is a strategically significant suburban rail infrastructure project. The project was sponsored by the Ministry of Railways with a revised project cost of over 2 thousand 800 crore rupees. The railway line was planned for execution in two phases: Phase I from Nerul – Belapur-Seawood to Kharkopar, and Phase-II from Kharkopar to Uran. The project involved multiple statutory and coordination dependencies. Recognising the project’s importance for port-based mobility, urban expansion, and regional economic integration, these issues were taken up for structured review under the PRAGATI mechanism on 22nd February 2017.Guided by the directions of   Prime Minister Modi, a coordinated and time-bound governance framework was activated. This leadership-driven intervention translated into visible execution momentum on the ground. Phase I from Nerul / Belapur-Seawood to Kharkopar was successfully commissioned on November 2018. The Kharkopar-Uran section (Phase-II) was subsequently inaugurated for passenger services by   Prime Minister Narendra Modi on 12 January 2024.   AKSHIT VEDYAN AKASHVANI NEWS DELHI.”

Election Commission has directed the Chief Secretary of West Bengal to suspend Assistant Electoral Registration Officer (AERO)  and Block Development Officer (BDO) Sumitra Pratim Prodhan of Basirhat-II with immediate effect and remove from any electoral roll-related work. In a letter to the Chief Secretary, the commission stated that the officer legally assigned four officials as Additional AEROs to handle hearings about discrepancies in voter lists. It said that these officials were never officially designated as AEROs under Section 13C of the Representation of the People Act, 1950.

India’s foreign exchange reserves rose sharply, by 14.167 billion US Dollars in the week that ended January 16, to 701.360 billion dollars, driven by a jump in both gold reserves and foreign currency assets. The country’s forex reserves has been hovering near its all-time high of 704.89 billion dollars.

The Nation is celebrating the 77th Republic Day today. President Droupadi Murmu led the Nation in celebrating the Republic Day from Kartavya Path in New Delhi. The ceremony commenced with the visit of Prime Minister Narendra Modi to the National War Memorial, where he led the nation in paying tributes to the fallen heroes by laying a wreath.

President Droupadi Murmu arrived at Kartavya Path in a ceremonial buggy with the chief guests of the parade, the President of the European Council, Antonio Costa and the President of the European Commission, Ursula von der Leyen. The National Flag was unfurled, followed by the national anthem with a 21-gun salute using 105 mm Light Field Guns.

The Republic Day Parade at Kartavya Path today highlighted a unique blend of India’s cultural diversity and military prowess. The theme this year is ‘Swatantrata ka Mantra: Vande Mataram’ and ‘Samriddhi ka Mantra: Aatmanirbhar Bharat’. Thirty tableaux from various States, Union Territories, and Central Government Ministries and Departments were displayed during the parade. These tableaux showcased the cultural diversity of the country by exhibiting a unique mix of 150 years of national song Vande Mataram and the nation’s rapid progress on the back of growing self-reliance across sectors. A tri-services tableau showing replicas of major weapon systems used by the Indian military during Operation Sindoor was one of the key attractions at the parade. BrahMos and Akash weapon systems, the rocket launcher system ‘Suryastra’ with deep-strike capabilities and the Main Battle Tank Arjun were among the main military platforms that India presented during the Parade at Kartavya Path. President Murmu conferred the Indian Air Force Group Captain Shubhanshu Shukla with India’s highest peacetime gallantry award, the Ashoka Chakra. The event concluded with a flypast by 29 Indian Air Force aircraft, including Rafale, Su-30, C-295, Mig-29, Apache, among others. Over 2 thousand and 500 artists presented cultural performances during the parade, with special guests from different walks of life attending the ceremony.
