## Morning News

The Election Commission has taken the extraordinary step of canceling the entire voting process in the Falta Assembly constituency of South 24 Parganas district, West Bengal. Fresh polling will be held on 21st of this month across all 285 polling stations, including auxiliary booths. Counting of votes will be held on 24th of May.

“The Election Commission has taken the extraordinary step of canceling the entire voting process in the Falta Assembly constituency of South 24 Parganas district, in West Bengal. Fresh polling will be held on 21st of May 2026 across all 285 polling stations, including auxiliary booths. Counting of votes is scheduled for 24th May.

In its statement, the Commission explained that the decision followed reports of severe electoral offences and large-scale subversion of the democratic process during voting on 29th April.

Observer reports highlighted serious irregularities, including tampering with electronic voting machines, intimidation of voters, and violations of polling rules. Our correspondent tells us more about Bengaluru-based space-tech startup:
