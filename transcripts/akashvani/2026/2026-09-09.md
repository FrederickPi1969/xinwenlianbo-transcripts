## Morning News

Praising the remarkable achievement of India’s rise in fintech, Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that the country’s Unified Payments Interface (UPI) is the undisputed champion of this success story. Inaugurating the ‘Global Fintech Fest 2026’ in Mumbai, Mr Modi said, this is such a massive transformation that whenever fintech is discussed, it will remain incomplete without UPI.

Reiterating his core philosophy of technological empowerment, Prime Minister praised UPI as ultimate tool for financial inclusion, successfully penetrating areas ignored by traditional banking models. Prime Minister pointed out that over 24 billion UPI transactions occurred in August alone. Highlighting the global expansion of India’s flagship digital payment system, Prime Minister said that UPI is now live in 11 countries, turning what was once unimaginable into an everyday reality for international payments.

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh has described Indian community in Sri Lanka as a bridge between the two countries, saying they play a crucial role in strengthening friendship between the two nations. Mr Singh, who reached Colombo yesterday on a three-day visit to Sri Lanka, made the remarks while interacting with members of the Indian diaspora. The Defence Minister said that India will always stand firmly with Sri Lanka in times of crisis, underlining that the relationship between the two countries is based on shared history, culture and faith. In his address, Mr Singh highlighted India’s rapid economic transformation, saying the country is among the fastest-growing major economies in the world and contributes more than 16 per cent to global growth.

India and Kuwait have agreed to deepen their strategic partnership across various areas. The 8th India- Kuwait Foreign Office Consultations between both the countries, were held in Kuwait yesterday. The meeting was co-chaired by Additional Secretary of  Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) Aseem Mahajan and Assistant Foreign Minister for Asia Affairs for Kuwait’s Foreign Affairs Ministry, Nawaf Abdullatif Sulaiman Al- Ahmed.

During the meeting, both sides discussed avenues to further deepen the strategic partnership across a range of areas including political relations, trade, investment, defence, security, energy, technology, agriculture, health, culture and people to people ties. They also reviewed the progress of meetings of Joint Working Groups under the Joint Commission for Cooperation mechanism.

External Affairs Ministry said that both sides agreed to hold the next round of Foreign Office Consultations in New Delhi at a mutually convenient date. It said that India and Kuwait enjoy long-standing ties which were elevated to Strategic Partnership in December 2024. The Ministry added that the 1.05 million-strong Indian community in Kuwait continues to serve as an important bridge between the two countries. SHIVANG MISRA, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.

The Union Ministry of Women and Child Development will launch the 9th National Nutrition Month 2026 in Varanasi today. Union Women and Child Development Minister Annapurna Devi will launch the campaign at the Rudraksh Convention Center in the presence of Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath.

The campaign will be conducted throughout the month of September in Anganwadi centers, schools, panchayats, and homes across the country with community participation. This year, the theme of National Nutrition Month is “Our Anganwadis, Our Responsibility.” Nutrition Month 2026 will highlight dietary diversity and adequate protein, fresh, hot cooked meals at Anganwadi centers, community ownership and accountability, education for nutrition and early childhood development, and the completion of ten years of the Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana. The inaugural event in Varanasi will feature a nutrition fair, a video presentation based on beneficiaries’ experiences, and a dialogue between the Union Minister and Anganwadi workers. Union Minister of State Savitri Thakur and the state’s Minister for Women’s Welfare, Child Development, and Nutrition, Baby Rani Maurya, will also be present. Union Minister for Road Transport and Highways Nitin Gadkari will inaugurate the Porvorim Elevated Corridor in Goa today. The six-lane, over five-kilometre project on NH-66, built at a cost of around 641 crore rupees, is expected to ease traffic and improve connectivity between Panaji and northern part of the state. The corridor will open for regular traffic from September 11.

It is expected to carry around 75,000 to 80,000 vehicles a day, taking nearly 60 percent of the traffic off the existing ground-level road.The project is aimed at easing congestion on the busy Mapusa-Panaji route.It is also expected to improve the movement of tourists and commercial vehicles along this important corridor. Tensions across West Asia have escalated sharply as the United States and Iran continue to exchange strikes, raising fears of a wider regional conflict. The United States said its military destroyed five Iranian crude-oil tankers following what Washington described as attacks by Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) on a US warship over the past two days. Iran, in retaliation, launched missiles toward US targets in Jordan. US Central Command said in a social media post that the strikes came in response to two Iranian attempts over the previous two days to target the US warship with ballistic missiles. Meanwhile, IRGC vowed retaliation against tankers near Bahraini and Kuwaiti ports. It urged tanker crews to immediately leave vessels located either at anchor or inside ports, warning that the ships could be targeted.

The tariffs targeting US merchandise officially came into force last night. Canadian commercial enterprises operating under the federal government’s newly implemented dollar-for-dollar tariffs, covering approximately 28 billion US Dollars worth of American imports. In Chhattisgarh, a Kanker Court has sentenced five Maoists, including dreaded  Prabhakar and his wife Raje Kange, to imprisonment in a case related to an attack on security forces in 2015. Prabhakar, a cadre of the Dandakaranya Special Zonal Committee, and his wife Raje Kange, associated with the Divisional Committee Member rank, were each sentenced to seven years’ in prison. The three other Maoists involved in the case were awarded five years’ imprisonment each. The case originates from 2015, when a group of Maoists attacked a team of security forces that was conducting a search operation in the Korar region.

India could witness one final surge of monsoon activity before the southwest monsoon begins its seasonal retreat. Rains are expected to return across large parts of Central, South, East and West India between September 12 and 21. Weather models indicate that the coming spell could provide a temporary revival to the fading monsoon circulation, bringing showers and thunderstorms to several parts of the country during the second half of September. The latest satellite imagery shows a noticeable reduction in cloud cover over large parts of the Indian subcontinent, particularly over northwest India, while convective cloud clusters remain active over eastern and southern regions.
