## Morning News

Government has clarified that there will be no charges at all for person-to-person (P2P) UPI transactions, irrespective of the amount transferred. Finance Ministry said that payments to merchants up to two thousand rupees along with transactions covered under the zero Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) framework for small merchants, will also remain free. The Ministry said that consequently, approximately 96 percent of all P2M transactions will remain unaffected. It said that MDR will apply only to specified merchant transactions above two thousand rupees. The Ministry said that MDR is neither a tax nor a charge collected by the government or National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI). It is distributed among payment ecosystem participants, including banks and payment application providers, to support the operation and continued expansion of the UPI ecosystem. NPCI has said that a revised UPI Merchant Discount Rate framework has been introduced on select merchant transactions, effective from 15th of next month, while keeping UPI free for consumers. Our correspondent reports said that a dedicated fund for promoting use of UPI by small merchants will be set up with contribution of 5 percent of total MDR collections.

The Finance Ministry said that charges will apply only to person to merchant (P2M) transactions exceeding two thousand rupees. It said that UPI app providers are explicitly prohibited from levying platform fees or hidden charges. The Ministry said that banks have been advised to ensure that merchants do not pass Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) charges to customers for UPI payments. A nominal MDR of 0.4 percent will be levied on Person to Merchant transactions above two thousand rupees while for high-value transactions of 75 thousand rupees and above, MDR will be capped at three hundred rupees per transaction. The Ministry informed that small merchants receiving up to one lakh rupees per month via UPI QR codes under the Person to Person Merchant (P2PM) classification will continue to enjoy mandatory zero MDR for all transactions. Akshit Vedyan, Akashvani News Delhi.

Maharashtra Cabinet has approved soft loans of around 750 crore rupees for cooperative sugar mills to clear pending Fair and Remunerative Price (FRP) dues to sugarcane farmers for 2025-26 crushing season and support operations for the 2026-27 season. More from our correspondent:

The decision to approve soft loans of around 750 crore rupees for cooperative sugar mills was taken at a Cabinet meeting chaired by  Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis yesterday. The Chief Minister said the state government will bear the interest on these loans. Mr. Fadnavis said the move will enable cooperative sugar mills to commence operations and procure sugarcane for the current crushing season, benefiting both mills and farmers. Speaking to reporters after the meeting, Mr. Fadnavis said that Maharashtra has achieved nearly 99.5 per cent payment of FRP to sugarcane farmers and is now first in the country in terms of FRP clearance. Prarthna, Akashvani News, Mumbai.

Delhi Government has announced to double the fixed monthly incentive for ASHA workers from three thousand rupees to six thousand rupees. The decision was taken at a cabinet meeting chaired by Chief Minister Rekha Gupta in New Delhi last evening. More from our correspondent:

The increase in the monthly incentive will benefit over six thousand ASHA workers currently working in Delhi. Delhi Chief Minister Rekha Gupta said that the increase in the monthly incentive is an important step towards recognising ASHA workers’ contribution and ensuring their financial security. The Chief Minister said that it will boost their enthusiasm for work and help them continue their services. In another decision, Delhi government has  decided to build hostels for over ten thousand students studying in colleges and universities across the national capital.

Delhi Chief Minister said that the government will also conduct a survey of colleges that have sufficient space for hostel construction. With Akshit Vedyan, Rishu Jaiswal, Akashvani News, Delhi.

The opening ceremony of the 22nd edition of the India-US joint military exercise ‘Yudh Abhyas’ was held at Auli in Chamoli district of Uttarakhand. The ceremony was conducted at the Auli Foreign Training Centre, with contingents of 600 personnel each from the two countries participating. The exercise is being conducted at Auli as well as the Mahajan Field Firing Range in Rajasthan. More details from our correspondent:

The exercise aims to enhance the capabilities of both armies to conduct joint military operations in mountainous and semi-mountainous areas, with special emphasis on infantry-based operations. During the exercise, the two armies will exchange operational methods and strengthen joint planning and operations.

The training will include the use of drones and autonomous systems for surveillance and reconnaissance. Modern weapon systems will also be demonstrated at the Mahajan Field Firing Range. Experts from both armies will exchange views on emerging technologies and developments related to multi-domain warfare. Sakshi Singh, Akashvani News, Dehradun.

New Zealand’s Parliament has passed legislation to implement its free trade agreement with India, paving the way for significant reductions in tariffs and expanded trade between the two countries. The legislation was approved by a vote of 93-29, with support from the opposition Labour Party. New Zealand Trade Minister Todd McClay said the agreement would deliver “immediate and substantial” benefits for exporters. He said the high-quality trade deal is expected to enter into force later this year. Under the agreement, tariffs on about 95 percent of New Zealand’s exports to India will be eliminated or significantly reduced. More than half of the products covered by the deal will become duty-free from the day the agreement enters into force. All Indian goods will receive duty free access to the New Zealand market. Wellington has also agreed to invest 20 billion US dollar in ​India over ​the next ⁠15 years.

External Affairs Ministry today summoned the Charge d’Affaires of the High Commission of Pakistan in New Delhi and lodged a strong protest over the unacceptable conduct of Pakistani naval units at sea, which led to a collision with a unit of the Indian Navy. In a statement, the Ministry said, while the incident which took place in international waters, did not cause any major damage, the conduct was in direct contravention of the Agreement between India and Pakistan on Advance Notice on Military Exercises, Manoeuvres and Troops Movements of April 1991. The Ministry asked the Charge d’Affaires of Pakistan to convey to the concerned Pakistani authorities, the need for all military units to observe due care and respect the provisions of the relevant agreements between the two sides in order to prevent  any such incidents in future.

Delhi has been ranked top performer in States and UT in Electric Mobility adoption as per the India Electric Mobility Index (IEMI) 2025. The Index was released by the NITI Aayog in New Delhi today, which outlines both the policy framework and implementation outcomes for e-mobility at the sub-national level. After Delhi, the other top performing States and UTs are Maharashtra, Karnataka, Chandigarh and Goa. This is the second edition of the Index and it measures the progress of States and Union Territories in achieving their Electric Mobility goals.

Speaking at the release of the index, Niti Aayog Member, Rajiv Gauba said that transition to electric mobility is an economic, environmental and a strategic imperative to the ambition of Viksit Bharat by 2047.
