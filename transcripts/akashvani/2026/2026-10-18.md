In West Bengal, 19 people lost their lives and 25 others were injured in a stampede at the Chorbagan Durga Puja pandal in north Kolkata last evening. The incident occurred at around 8 PM, during the Maha Saptami celebrations.

President Droupadi Murmu, Vice President C. P. Radhakrishnan, Prime Minister Narendra Modi and other senior leaders have expressed deep sorrow over the loss of lives in the stampede in Kolkata. Prime Minister Modi has announced an ex-gratia of two lakh rupees from the Prime Minister’s National Relief Fund to the next of kin of each person who lost their life in the stampede. The injured will receive fifty thousand rupees each.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah spoke to Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari and took stock of the situation following the stampede. Mr. Shah who is currently in Kolkata will visit the hospital to meet the injured today. Here is a report from our Kolkata correspondent:

“According to police and eye witnesses heavy rain and a sudden rush of visitors led to chaos at the crowded pandal. Several people reportedly fell amid the rush, triggering a stampede-like situation. The injured were rushed to Kolkata Medical College and Hospital, R-G Kar Medical College and Hospital, and N-R-S Medical College and Hospital for treatment. West Bengal Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari, the state Health Minister and senior government officials visited the hospitals to assess the situation. The Chief Minister also announced compensation of 25 lakh rupees and a  government job for the family of each deceased person. Later, speaking to reporters, the Chief Minister said police have detained the Durga Puja organisers, private security guards and staff on duty for questioning.

The Chief Minister announced that a Special Investigation Team, or SIT, headed by a sitting judge of the Calcutta High Court, will investigate the stampede at the Durga Puja pandal in Kolkata. The SIT will include members of the Special Task Force, the Criminal Investigation Department and other government departments. The Chorbagan community puja pandal has been closed, and the site has been handed over to forensic experts, the police Special Task Force and the Fire Brigade for investigation. Meanwhile, Union Home Minister Amit Shah, is monitoring the situation and is in constant touch with the Chief Minister and other senior state officials. SandIpon Dasgupta, Akashvani News, Kolkata.”

West Bengal Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari has announced the formation of a high-level committee to review safety arrangements at major Durga Puja pandals across the state following the deadly stampede. The committee will review crowd management, emergency exit routes and structural safety at prominent puja venues and recommend measures to prevent similar incidents in the future.

The Chief Minister has directed organisers to strictly follow safety guidelines issued by the authorities, emphasising that public safety must take precedence over elaborate pandal designs and themes.

The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) has notified draft amendments proposing Maximum Residue Limits (MRLs) for two pesticides, acetamiprid and imidacloprid, in tea. The draft Food Safety and Standards (Contaminants, Toxins and Residues) Amendment Regulations, 2026, propose maximum residue limits of 20 milligrams per kilogram for acetamiprid and 50 milligrams per kilogram for imidacloprid in tea. In another move, FSSAI has published a draft amendment to the Food Safety and Standards (Foods for Infant Nutrition) Regulations, 2020, proposing to prohibit the addition of sucrose and fructose to food products intended for infants up to six months of age. More from our correspondent:

The proposed limits over the pesticides in tea aim to establish clearly defined regulatory benchmarks for pesticide residues, facilitating compliance, monitoring and enforcement of food safety requirements, subject to the finalisation of the draft regulations. The proposed standards also recognise the importance of effective pest management in agricultural production. The Ministry has highlighted that India’s average pesticide use remains below 0.5 kilograms per hectare, substantially lower than the global average of 2.38 kilograms per hectare. For the tea sector, the proposed limits are expected to provide greater regulatory clarity to tea growers, processors, food businesses, testing laboratories and enforcement authorities. Further, FSSAI’s amendment to the Food Safety and Standards (Foods for Infant Nutrition) Regulations, 2020 aims to promote appropriate infant nutrition and support healthy growth and development during the crucial first six months of life. It seeks to ensure that infant nutrition products meet the nutritional needs of this age group without the unnecessary addition of these sugars. With Anand Kumar, Rishu Jaiswal, Akashvani News, Delhi.

The Indian squad features several prominent para-athletes, including javelin thrower Sumit Antil, para-badminton player Pramod Bhagat, para-shooter Avani Lekhara, para-archer Sheetal Devi, sprinter Simran Sharma and para-table tennis player Bhavina Patel among others.  SANYUKTA YADAV, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI

The National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA), under the Department of Pharmaceuticals, has undertaken trade margin rationalisation (TMR) for 137 identified non-scheduled anti-cancer drugs in the public interest. The initiative aims to curb excessive trade margins, make cancer treatment more affordable and improve access to anti-cancer medicines. The move is expected to reduce the maximum retail prices (MRPs) of the identified medicines by up to 70 per cent, depending on their existing price structures. The Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers has said that the revised prices will come into effect on the 9th of next month.

Consumers can check the revised prices of medicines through the Pharma Sahi Daam app and the NPPA website.

Hundreds of thousands of people have taken to the streets across Belgium, France, Italy and Portugal to protest against rising living costs, low wages and the growing difficulty of accessing affordable housing. The demonstrations have put governments across the European Union under pressure.

France witnessed widespread unrest, with more than five lakh fifty thousand people participating in demonstrations, according to the Interior Ministry.
