## Morning News

The 18th BRICS Summit is set to begin at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi today. The two-day grand event will bring together eleven major emerging and developing economies, including Brazil, China, Egypt, Ethiopia, Indonesia, India, Iran, Russia, Saudi Arabia, South Africa, and the UAE. During the high-level event, leaders of the BRICS member countries are expected to discuss key issues, including global governance, multilateralism, trade and investment, technology, food and energy security, healthcare, disaster resilience, and critical supply chains. The Summit is being held under India’s 2026 BRICS chairship, with the theme, “Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability.”

Several key world leaders will participate in the high-profile Summit. Prime Minister Narendra Modi will be joined by Russian President Vladimir Putin, Chinese President Xi Jinping, South African President Cyril Ramaphosa, Egyptian President Abdel Fattah El-Sisi, Indonesian President Prabowo Subianto and Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian.

The Summit will begin with a closed session for BRICS member countries on “Inclusive Global Governance and Strengthening Multilateralism.” Leaders of the BRICS member countries will also visit the Bharat Innovates exhibition at Bharat Mandapam. This will be followed by a joint tree-planting ceremony and a gala dinner hosted by Prime Minister Modi.

The Prime Minister is scheduled to hold a series of bilateral talks with BRICS leaders on the sidelines of the Summit today. These include meetings with Chinese President Xi Jinping, Crown Prince of Abu Dhabi Sheikh Khaled bin Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan, Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed Ali, Malaysian Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim and Vietnamese Prime Minister Le Minh Hung.

“New Delhi has taken centre stage for BRICS 2026, with world leaders converging at a time when there is global churn and instability. The Leaders meet at the backdrop of conflicts in West Asia and the Black Sea region, energy crisis, and climate change. BRICS has emerged as a key platform for coordinating economic policy among major emerging economies. It serves as the principal forum where these nations shape a common position on global governance. Through sustained dialogue, it has become a channel for advancing the interests of the Global South within multilateral institutions. Its agenda spans development, finance, technology, trade and people-to-people exchanges. India’s Chairship is guided by the theme “Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability.” The theme places development, sustainability and innovation at the heart of the agenda. With Anand Kumar, Suparna Saikia, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has called for secure sea lanes, open supply routes and the safety of seafarers to sustain global trade. He also urged BRICS countries to work towards removing trade barriers and expanding opportunities for businesses. Mr Modi stated this while addressing Leaders’ Session of BRICS Business Forum at Bharat mandapam in New Delhi last evening. The Prime Minister said, India was a strong advocate of freedom of navigation and the safety of seafarers, stressing that secure maritime routes were essential for the smooth movement of global trade.

Amid rising trade barriers globally, Prime Minister Modi said India was seeking to build economic bridges. He said, India today offers a source of confidence for growth and stability.

Earlier, Prime Minister Modi held bilateral meetings with Russian President Vladimir Putin, Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian and UN Secretary-General António Guterres in New Delhi yesterday ahead of the 18th BRICS Summit. During his meeting with President Vladimir Putin, the two leaders reviewed progress in India-Russia cooperation across a wide range of areas, including politics, trade and economy, defence, energy, space, skill mobility and people-to-people ties. Both sides also shared perspectives on key regional and global issues of mutual interest, including the conflicts in West Asia and the Black Sea region.

In view of the BRICS Summit in New Delhi, traffic diversions and regulated movement will be implemented on key routes across National Capital from 9:30 AM to 9 PM today. The Traffic Police have advised the commuters to plan their journeys in advance and allow extra travel time.

The Government has approved 18 lakh 77 thousand houses under the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana-Urban (PMAY-U) 2.0, giving further impetus to the implementation of the scheme and advancing the goal of Housing for All in urban areas. The decision was taken at the ninth meeting of the Central Sanctioning and Monitoring Committee, held in New Delhi on Thursday.

The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Maandhan Yojana has completed seven years today. The scheme was launched on this day in 2019 with the aim of providing social security to small and marginal farmers. Under the scheme, eligible landholding small and marginal farmers are assured a minimum monthly pension of three thousand rupees from the age of 60.

“Since its launch, Pradhan Mantri Kisan Maandhan Yojana has emerged as an important pillar of the social security for the farming community. Over these seven years, the scheme has steadily expanded its reach among farmers across the country.  It reflects the government’s continued commitment to ensuring that farmers can look towards their later years with greater financial security and dignity. By February 2026, over 24.96 lakh farmers have enrolled under the scheme across the country. Haryana state leads with around 5.75 lakh enrolments, followed by Bihar with over 3.46 lakh. Since 2019, more than five hundred forty crore rupees has been utilised to support the scheme’s implementation and outreach, as of February 2026. It brings the promise of financial security closer to millions who spend their working lives securing India’s food security. SHIVANG MISRA, Akashvani  NEWS, DELHI.”
