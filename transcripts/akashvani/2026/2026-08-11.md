## Morning News

Parliament has passed the Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Bill 2026 after Rajya Sabha returned the Money Bill to Lok Sabha following discussion. The Lok Sabha had earlier passed the Bill, which was then sent to Rajya Sabha for discussion. Since it is a Money Bill, Rajya Sabha can only make recommendations on its provisions and return it to the Lok Sabha, which may accept or reject those recommendations.

Replying to the discussion on the bill in the Rajya Sabha, Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman made it clear that the legislation does not impose any tax on UPI and the digital payment system will remain free for consumers. The Finance minister stated that the UPI-related amendment is only an enabling provision and does not itself impose any tax or transaction charge on users. Ms Sitharaman also said that the government doesn’t intend to impose any MDR charges on small merchants as they remain central to UPI’s inclusive growth.

The Government has notified eleven more international ports, enabling foreign nationals holding e-visas to enter India through these designated points. The new additions include nine land ports and two airports. With this, the total number of international ports facilitating entry of e-visa holders has risen to 88. A report:

“The Home Ministry said the newly notified points include Bhopal and Tirupati airports, and nine land ports at Agartala, Attari, Darranga, Gede, Ghojadanga, Haridaspur, Jaigaon, Dawki and Moreh. The Ministry said the move will further enhance the attractiveness and utility of the e-visa facility for international travellers. Around 95 percent of e-visa applications are processed in less than 72 hours. Introduced in 2014 for nationals of 43 countries, the e-visa facility has now been extended to citizens of 172 countries. It is currently available under 17 sub-categories, including tourist, business, medical, student, transit, cruise and mountaineering visas. The Ministry said India now issues roughly 78 percent of its visas electronically. Ishani Yadav, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

Union Home Minister Amit Shah is scheduled to move the Kerala (Alteration of Name) Bill, 2026 and the National Co-operative Development Corporation (Amendment) Bill, 2026 for consideration and passing in the Lok Sabha today. Mr Shah will move a motion for consideration of the Bill seeking to alter the name of the State of Kerala. He will also move the National Co-operative Development Corporation (Amendment) Bill, 2026, which seeks to further amend the National Co-operative Development Corporation Act, 1962.

Rajya Sabha is scheduled to take up the Tribunals Reforms Bill, 2026 for consideration and passing. Minister of State for Law and Justice Arjun Ram Meghwal will move Bill. The bill aims to improve efficiency, ensure independence, transparency, and uniformity in the qualifications, appointment of service of Chairpersons and Members of various Tribunals as well as the administration and functioning of the Tribunals. The legislation also proposes to establish a National Tribunals Commission.

At least 132 people were killed following a powerful 7.4 magnitude earthquake hit western Colombia yesterday. More than 570 others have been injured. According to US Geological Survey, the epicentre of the quake was near San Jose del Palmar in the Choco region, about 400 kilometres west of the capital, Bogota. The quake damaged hundreds of buildings and trapped several people under debris. Rescue teams and local residents searched through the rubble for survivors. Colombian President Abelardo de la Espriella has declared an emergency to speed up funding for relief and recovery operations. The tremors were also felt in neighbouring Ecuador and Panama, though no major damage was reported there.

## Evening News

The Parliament passed the Tribunals Reforms Bill, 2026, with the Rajya Sabha approving it today. Lok Sabha has already passed the bill. The legislation aims to improve efficiency, ensure independence, transparency, and uniformity in the qualifications, appointment of service of Chairpersons and Members of various Tribunals, as well as the administration and functioning of the Tribunals. It also proposes to establish a National Tribunals Commission.

When the Upper House met at 2 PM after the second adjournment, Law and Justice Minister Arjun Ram Meghwal moved the Bill.

Replying to discussion on the bill, Mr Meghwal said, the new bill has a provision to establish the National Tribunals Commission which will be chaired by a former Judge of the Supreme Court or a former Chief Justice of a High Court.

The Rajya Sabha was adjourned for the day after completing its business.

The Lok Sabha has passed the Kerala (Alteration of Name) Bill, 2026. The Bill seeks to change the name of the state of Kerala to Keralam in the First Schedule of the Constitution. A resolution in this regard was passed by the Kerala Legislative Assembly in June 2024.

Speaking on the bill, Minister of State for Home Nityanand Rai said, the Union Cabinet in February this year approved the proposal to change the name of the state to Keralam and to introduce the necessary legislation for an amendment in this regard.

The Lower House has also passed the National Co-operative Development Corporation (NCDC)(Amendment) Bill, 2026. The legislation aims to further amend the National Co-operative Development Corporation Act, 1962. The Bill allows the Corporation to undertake its activities in industrial goods. The legislation also allows the state governments to extend funding from the Corporation to any entity engaged in co-operative development. The Corporation can also provide loans and grants directly to any co-operative society or entity engaged in co-operative development.

Minister of State for Cooperation Murlidhar Mohol moved the bill in the House. Speaking on the bill, Mr Mohol said, keeping in view the changing requirements and the expansion of the cooperative sector, it had become necessary to widen the scope of functioning of National Cooperative Development Corporation (NCDC).

Both the Bills were passed without discussion amid uproar by the opposition over several issues. After completing the business, the Lok Sabha was adjourned for the day.
