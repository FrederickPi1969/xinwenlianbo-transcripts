## Morning News

India has called upon all parties to exercise restraint, de-escalate tensions, ensure protection of civilians, and uninterrupted flow of energy and commerce during ongoing conflicts. Addressing the UN General Assembly in New York last night, External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar pointed out that the repercussions of wars are experienced far outside the borders of directly engaged nations. He said, hostilities continue to destabilise global security, threatening progress and slowing development far beyond their immediate regions. Dr. Jaishankar said, India is prepared to do everything possible to help improve the situation, particularly in the areas of food and energy security.

Referring to the protracted Russia-Ukraine conflict, the minister said the ongoing war remains a critical international concern and echoed Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s call for an end to hostilities.

Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov has strongly backed India’s claim for a permanent seat in the United Nations Security Council. In his address at UN General Assembly in New York last night, Mr Lavrov said, Russia is in favour of broadening the representation of Asia, Africa, and Latin America and Moscow supports India’s and Brazil’s candidatures for a permanent seat. He advocated for more seat allocation to the Global South and voiced strong support for India and Brazil. Mr Lavrov said, UN Security Council reform cannot involve any additional seats being allocated to Western countries, especially to Germany and Japan, who are now on the path to militarism and revanchism.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will share his thoughts with the people of the country and abroad in his monthly radio programme Mann Ki Baat on Akashvani at 11 AM today. It will be the 138th episode.

The programme will be broadcast across the entire Akashvani and Doordarshan network, the AIR News website, and the Newsonair mobile app. It will also be live-streamed on the YouTube channels of AIR News, DD News, the PMO, and the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting. Akashvani will broadcast the programme in regional languages immediately after the Hindi broadcast.

All public sector banks and Regional Rural Banks will remain open today for the convenience of customers. This step has been taken in view of the proposed three-day nationwide bank strike called by the United Forum of Bank Unions from tomorrow. Reserve Bank of India has granted approval for all bank branches, offices, ATM-link branches and Currency Chests to remain fully operational today. Meanwhile, several public sector banks have decided to waive ATM transaction charges above the free monthly limit during the three-day bank strike.

## Midday News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today stated that the surgical strike by the Indian Army on the terrorist hideouts in Pakistan demonstrated a new example of India’s counter-terrorism policy to the world. Addressing the nation in his Mann Ki Baat programme on Akashvani, Mr Modi said India is set to mark the tenth anniversary of the surgical strike tomorrow (28th of September). He said the world was astonished to see the manner in which brave Indian soldiers crossed the Line of Control to take action against terrorism.

Prime Minister Modi said the surgical strike was India’s decisive response to the long-standing pain the nation had endured due to terrorist attacks. He stated that over the past decade – from the Balakot airstrike to ‘Operation Sindoor ’, India has repeatedly sent the message that terrorist strikes will be met with a befitting response.

Prime Minister Modi said this year also marks 25 years since the 9/11 terrorist attacks. He said that the highest number of victims at the World Trade Center in the US, after Americans, were Indians. He recalled paying homage to those innocent citizens during his US visit after becoming Prime Minister in 2014. He once again extended his condolences to the bereaved families on behalf of crores of Indian citizens.

Touching upon the topic of the ongoing census, Mr Modi said the first phase of this exercise is nearly complete across all States and Union Territories. Data of over 32 crore families has already been recorded. Work is currently underway also in the remote, snow-clad regions of Ladakh, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, and Uttarakhand. The Prime Minister noted that many inspiring experiences have emerged from these areas. In Uttarakhand, the census process began in the village of Mana in Chamoli, which is known as the country’s first village.

Prime Minister Modi informed that so far, more than 2 crore 60 lakh people have already entered their details on the census portal via mobile, completing the self-enumeration. He said accurate information about citizens and their families helps create a precise picture of the country’s future.

The Prime Minister said today marks the 100th birth anniversary of the revered late Ashok Singhal, who was the former International Working President of the Vishwa Hindu Parishad. He called him a great personality who dedicated his entire life to culture, society, and the nation.

The Prime Minister said the nation began this year with a grand celebration of national pride, which is the Somnath Swabhiman Parv. He said that Mahmud of Ghazni had demolished the Somnath Temple for the first time a thousand years ago in 1026 AD. He highlighted that the Somnath Temple still stands in all its grandeur by the sea, bestowing the blessing of immortality upon India. He added that this is why the Somnath Swabhiman Parv was celebrated with immense pride this year.

The Prime Minister also mentioned Mahatma Gandhi’s birth anniversary, which will be celebrated on the 2nd of October. He said cleanliness is one of many guiding principles for life given by Bapu. Citing several examples of initiatives to maintain cleanliness, Mr Modi said young people in Madhya Pradesh are working to clean up a river.

The Prime Minister pointed out that all these efforts start with a single individual first, and a movement grows when others join in. He said this is what Jan Bhagidari is all about. He stated that cleanliness is the most beautiful tribute the nation can pay to Mahatma Gandhi.

Prime Minister Modi noted that millions of young people across the country have recently taken a significant resolve against substance abuse and drugs, under the Nasha Mukt Yuva Abhiyan. They have vowed to stay away from drugs and have also taken up the responsibility of spreading this message to others. Mr Modi said Nasha Mukt Yuva Abhiyan will run for 100 weeks.

Mr Modi had urged young people through ‘Mann Ki Baat’ to join this campaign using their creativity. He expressed delight that thousands of youth have stepped forward in response to this call. Various competitions, including anthem, slogan writing, and street plays, have been organised on the ‘MY Bharat’ platform. Similarly, young people are sharing creative content on social media using the hashtag – NashaMuktYuva. Prime Minister Modi noted that in essence, the message, the medium, and the driving energy behind it all come from the youth themselves. He added that in the coming days, activities such as health check-ups and meditation camps will also be conducted as part of this campaign.
