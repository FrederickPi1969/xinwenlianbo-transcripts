The Parliament has passed the Taxation and Other Laws Amendment Bill 2026 today with Rajya Sabha returning it to Lok Sabha after discussion. The Lok Sabha has already passed the Bill. Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman had moved the bill.

The Bill seeks to amend the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007, the Income-tax Act, 2025, and the Finance Act, 2026. The legislation will replace the Income-tax (Amendment) Ordinance, 2026, which was promulgated on 5th June this year.

Replying to the discussion, the Union Finance Minister said that the objective of the Bill is to strengthen India’s manufacturing ecosystem, attract further investment, generate employment, and promote manufacturing activities. She said the second major reform relates to providing tax exemptions to foreign companies procuring data services from specified data centres in India and enhancing ease of doing business. The Minister clarified that the Bill does not impose any tax or transaction charge on UPI users.

The Parliament also passed the Bankers’ Books Evidence Bill, 2026, with the Rajya Sabha approving it today. Lok Sabha has already passed the Bill. Union Finance Minister had moved the Bill, which has a provision that an electronic or digital record of a banker’s book will be admissible, valid, and legally enforceable as evidence.

Replying to discussion on the bill, Finance Minister Sitharaman said, this bill is a very reformative step, which will help the country move forward at a faster pace.

After completing its business, the Rajya Sabha was adjourned for the day.
