## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will address the Post Budget Webinar on “Technology, Reforms and Finance for Viksit Bharat” at 11:30 this morning through video conferencing. The webinar will deliberate on key themes including public capital expenditure, infrastructure development, banking sector reforms, financial sector architecture, deepening of capital markets and ease of living through tax reforms. The event is part of a series of Post Budget Webinars being organised on priority areas emerging from the Union Budget 2026-27.

“The webinar is the first in a series aimed at facilitating effective and outcome-oriented implementation of the Budget announcements for the financial year 2026-27. It is designed to draw lessons from past experiences and obtain structured feedback from stakeholders to further strengthen policy execution and governance mechanisms. The discussions will see participation from representatives of industry, financial institutions, market participants, government departments, regulators and academia. The deliberations are expected to focus on enhancing public investment efficiency, strengthening financial systems, expanding access to capital and promoting reforms that improve ease of living for citizens. Through these consultations, the government seeks to ensure coordinated action across sectors and accelerate progress towards the goal of Viksit Bharat. Rohan, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

President Droupadi Murmu will witness the Indian Air Force’s Exercise ‘Vayu Shakti’ at the Pokhran Field Firing Range in Jaisalmer, Rajasthan this evening. President Murmu reached Jaisalmer last evening on a two-day visit to the state. The Defence Ministry has informed that prior to the exercise, the President will fly a sortie in indigenous Light Combat Helicopter (LCH) – ‘Prachand’. The mission will be undertaken as a two aircraft LCH formation, with Chief of the Air Staff, Air Chief Marshal Amarpreet Singh, flying in the second LCH. Notably, the LCH ‘Prachand’ is India’s first indigenously designed and developed combat helicopter, manufactured by Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL). The helicopter has been designed to operate across diverse terrains, ranging from deserts to high-altitude regions. After this, the President will attend the Indian Air Force’s mega demonstration ‘Vayu Shakti’ at the Pokhran Field Firing Range near the India-Pakistan border. During the event, various aircraft of the Indian Air Force will showcase their operational capabilities. Besides the President, Defence Minister Rajnath Singh and Chief Minister Bhajanlal Sharma will also be present at the programme.

“Vayu Shakti is the Indian Air Force’s premier combat exercise, showcasing the strike capability, precision targeting, and operational readiness of fighter aircraft, helicopters, transport planes, and missile systems. During the exercise, coordinated air operations will be demonstrated under simulated war-like conditions, both during the day and night. A total of 77 fighter jets and 43 helicopters will participate in this mega event, including the Rafale, Sukhoi-30 MKI, Mirage-2000, Jaguar, and the indigenously developed Tejas. Jitendra Dwivedi, Akashvani News, Jaipur.”

Afghanistan launched a retaliatory attack against Pakistan last night following Pakistani airstrikes on Afghan border regions earlier this week. In a press statement, Afghanistan’s Ministry of National Defense said that the posts of the Pakistani troops were captured under heavy attacks on the Durand Line. It stated that a total of 55 Pakistani soldiers were killed, two bases and 19 posts were captured.
