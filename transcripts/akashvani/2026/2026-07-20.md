## Morning News

The Monsoon session of Parliament will begin today. It will continue till 13th of August. The 25-day session will have 19 sittings. A report:

“During the session, several important legislations are expected to be taken up for discussion and passage. Today the Lok Sabha is scheduled to take up the Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Bill 2026 and Rajya Sabha is scheduled to discuss the Prevention of Insults to National Honour (Amendment) Bill 2026. Apart from these Bills, Registration of Births and Deaths (Amendment) Bill, Income Tax (Amendment) Bill, Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development (Amendment) Bill, 2026 are also expected to be discussed during the Monsoon Session. ANUPAM MISHRA, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

On the eve of the Monsoon session, Government yesterday convened an all-party meeting at Parliament House. The meeting was chaired by Defence Minister Rajnath Singh. Over 50 leaders from 40 political parties attended the meeting.

Speaking to media after the meeting, Parliamentary Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju urged all political parties to ensure the smooth functioning of Parliament and engage in constructive discussions on key issues.

Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla yesterday chaired the meeting of the Business Advisory Committee of the Lok Sabha. During the meeting, members representing the Government as well as various political parties exchanged views on the legislative and other business proposed to be taken up during the Session. Mr Birla emphasized the importance of constructive deliberations, meaningful participation and adherence to the highest parliamentary traditions.

Delhi Police has clarified that no permission has been sought or granted for any protest march and procession in the New Delhi District in view of Parliament Session beginning today. In a social media post, the police said that Section 163 of Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 is in force in the area, hence protest marches, processions, demonstrations and assemblies of five or more persons are strictly prohibited in the area, except Jantar Mantar.

President Droupadi Murmu will hold delegation level talks with her Moldovan counterpart Maia Sandu in Chisinau today. President Murmu will also meet the President of the Parliament of Moldova, Igor Grosu and will interact with members of the Moldova-India Parliamentary Friendship Group. Further, the President will address a Business Forum and interact with the members of the Indian Community. This is the first ever visit by an Indian President to Moldova. President is on a three-nation state visit to Moldova, North Macedonia, and Romania.

After concluding her visit to Molodova, the President in the second leg of her visit, will reach North Macedonia tomorrow.

Madhya Pradesh cabinet has approved the Uniform Civil Code Bill during a special meeting held at Jagdishpur, a historic town near Bhopal. Our correspondent reports that tribal communities have been kept outside the scope of this law.

“Madhya Pradesh has taken a major step towards implementing the Uniform Civil Code, or UCC. A cabinet meeting was held yesterday at Jagdishpur in Bhopal district, chaired by Chief Minister Dr. Mohan Yadav. At this meeting, the draft of the UCC Bill 2026 was unanimously approved. The bill will now be tabled in the monsoon session of the state assembly, which begins today. The session will run until 24 July. After the cabinet meeting, Chief Minister Yadav told reporters that the UCC will give equal status to everyone in the state. He said multiple marriages will no longer be valid in the state. According to the UCC bill, those in live-in relationships will have to register within one month. Marriage registration will also be made mandatory down to the village level. However, tribal communities have been kept out of its scope. Abhinav Goel, Akashvani News, Bhopal.”

## Midday News

Monsoon session of parliament begins; Both houses witness multiple adjournments amid opposition uproar.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi calls for productive monsoon session of parliament, says it will lead to welfare of the Nation.

Supreme Court to hear pleas seeking probe into Ram temple donations theft case today.

President Droupadi Murmu holds delegation level talks with her Moldovan counterpart Maia Sandu in Chisinau.

And, IMD issues red alert warning for heavy to very heavy rainfall over parts of Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh and Jammu and Kashmir.

The monsoon session of Parliament began today. The Lok Sabha and the Rajya Sabha have been adjourned till 2 PM amid opposition protest over the NEET paper leak issue. When the House met after the third adjournment at 1 PM, the opposition members continued with their protest over the NEET issue. Presiding officer Dilip Saikia tried to take up the scheduled business but the opposition members did not pay heed to the Chair’s advice and continued with their protest. Amid the uproar, the Lok Sabha was adjourned till 2:00 PM.

Earlier, when the Lok Sabha met at 11 AM, the House paid tributes to six of its former members who passed away recently. When the Speaker tried to take up the Question Hour, the opposition members including from the Congress and the Samajwadi Party, raised the issue of paper leak of NEET examination. Mr Birla urged the opposition to cooperate in running the House and maintain decorum, but the opposition members continued their protest, which led to adjournment of the Lok Sabha till 12 noon.

The Rajya Sabha was also adjourned till 2:00 PM following the opposition protest over the NEET issue. When the Upper House met after the third adjournment at 12:50 PM, the opposition MPs again raised slogans over the NEET Paper leak issue. Chairman C P Radhakrishnan urged the members to maintain decorum and allow the House to function. Amid the ruckus, the Upper House was adjourned till 2:00 PM. Earlier, the House was adjourned thrice over the issue.

In the Rajya Sabha the proceedings began at 11:00 AM with the oath of the newly elected and re-elected Members. During the proceedings, Leader of the opposition Mallikarjun Kharge tried to raise the issue of NEET examination paper leak.

However due to uproar, Rajya Sabha Chairman C P Radhakrishnan adjourned the House till 12 noon then till 12:30 and again till 12:50 PM.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that be it the monsoon or the monsoon session, if both are proactive, they prove to be highly productive. Speaking to the media before beginning of the Monsoon Session of Parliament, he said, when both are productive, it leads to the welfare of the nation and the well-being of all living beings. Mr Modi said, it is a powerful message that the potential and aspirations of the country’s youth are as boundless as space itself.

He said, in just one month, the nation has achieved numerous milestones, a series of accomplishments that fill every citizen with pride. The Prime Minister said, be it on the national stage, the international arena, or now, even in space, these have truly been moments of pride for India.  Mr Modi said, the entire world is concerned about the persistent threat of war in various regions. He said, the conflict in West Asia posed a major crisis for countries like India that rely on others for energy.

Union Minister of Law and Justice Arjun Ram Meghwal today introduced the Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Bill, 2026 in the Lok Sabha. The bill aims to further amend the Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Act, 1956 for increasing the number of Judges of the Apex Court from the present 33 to 37 (excluding the Chief Justice of India). In May this year, President Droupadi Murmu had promulgated an ordinance to increase the number of Supreme Court Judges from 33 to 37.

Delhi Police has refuted claims circulating in some sections of the media about the alleged use of force and detentions at Jantar Mantar, in New Delhi. In a social media post, Delhi Police clarified that no such incident has taken place and said the protest is being handled professionally. Meanwhile, Delhi Metro Rail Corporation (DMRC) has informed that the interchange facilities and gates are now open at Seva Teerth, Rajiv Chowk and Central Secretariat metro stations. Earlier DMRC said that Janpath, Rajiv Chowk, Patel Chowk, Central Secretariat and Seva Teerth metro stations have been closed till further instructions due to security reasons.

The Supreme Court is set to hear a batch of pleas today seeking a fair and time-bound investigation into the alleged embezzlement of donations collected for the Ram Temple in Ayodhya. According to the Supreme Court’s cause list, four separate petitions related to the matter are scheduled to come up for hearing before a bench comprising Chief Justice Surya Kant and Justices Joymalya Bagchi and V. Mohan. Earlier, The Special Investigation Team constituted by the Uttar Pradesh Government has filed its status report before the Supreme Court in the Ram Mandir donation theft case. The status report was submitted in a sealed cover before a three-judge Bench headed by Chief Justice of India Surya Kant.

The Calcutta High Court today refused to grant interim permission to the Mamata Banerjee led faction of the Trinamool Congress to operate three of the party’s bank accounts that were frozen by the Enforcement Directorate (ED). The ED froze the three bank accounts of the Trinamool Congress with balances amounting to 440.42 crore rupees in connection with its probe based on an FIR lodged by the state police in alleged dishonest financial dealings, unlawful collection of money, and routing of suspected funds through certain bank accounts of the party.

Tripura Director General of Police (DGP) Anurag Dhankar was found dead at the Police Headquarters in Agartala today. The cause of his death has not yet been officially confirmed. Further details are awaited.

President Droupadi Murmu has begun her engagements in the Republic of Moldova on the first leg of her three-nation visit. After receiving a ceremonial Guard of Honour at the Presidential Palace in Chisinau, President Murmu held bilateral talks with Moldovan President Maia Sandu. The two leaders first met one-on-one, followed by delegation-level discussions aimed at strengthening bilateral ties and expanding cooperation in areas of mutual interest. President Murmu will then visit the Ștefan cel Mare și Sfânt Monument, one of Moldova’s most important national landmarks dedicated to the medieval ruler Stephen the Great. The President will pay homage by laying a wreath at the monument. The visit is historic, as it marks the first-ever visit by an Indian President to Moldova, reflecting the growing partnership between the two countries.

Union Environment, Forest and Climate Change Minister, Bhupender Yadav today met with United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change Executive Secretary, Simon Stiell in New Delhi. In a social media post, Mr Yadav said that the discussions during the meeting focused on adaptation, technology transfer, climate finance, global stocktake, and just transition. He noted that under the leadership of Prime Minister Narendra Modi, India has not only submitted its enhanced Nationally Determined Contributions, but also achieved it successfully way earlier than its stipulated timeline. Mr Yadav said that India is also taking strides in other allied sectors to combat the climate change crisis.

US strikes targeted the town of Khormoj in southwestern Iran, while Jordan, Kuwait, and Bahrain reported fresh Iranian attacks as US-Iran hostilities spilled into neighbouring countries. Iran’s semi-official news agency, citing a local official, reported that the US strike triggered power outages across parts of Khormoj, the administrative centre of Dashti County in Bushehr province.

In Assam, the Indian Army has launched ‘Operation Jal Rahat’ to support the government in large-scale rescue and relief operations as worsening floods continue to inundate several parts of eastern Assam. The flood situation has worsened as incessant rain continues across the state, affecting thousands of people in several districts of Upper Assam.

In Sivasagar district alone, 510 people have been rescued so far by teams of the SDRF and Fire and Emergency Services. A total of 22 revenue circles and 298 villages remained affected, impacting more than 57 thousand people. Over 3,874 hectares of crop land have been inundated.

Amid a heavy rainfall warning in several districts of Uttarakhand and reports of landslides at various locations along the Char Dham Yatra route, the authorities decided to halt the pilgrimage temporarily. Garhwal Commissioner Anand Swaroop has directed the District Magistrates concerned to ensure that pilgrims are accommodated at safe locations wherever roads are blocked and that the yatra resumes only after the routes are declared completely safe.

The Weather Department has also predicted thunderstorms accompanied with lightning and gusty winds over Andhra Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh, North Interior Karnataka, Odisha and Tamil Nadu Puducherry and Karaikal today.

The Supreme Court today observed that the issue of alleged embezzlement of donations at the Ram temple in Ayodhya, Uttar Pradesh should not be politicize. It said that the Ram temple donation row is a simple case of commission of crime and the court is just to ensure proper investigation. The apex court asked the Solicitor General appearing for the Uttar Pradesh government to take instructions on whether the Special Investigation Team (SIT) which had examined the entire issue before the registration of the FIR, could be entrusted with the responsibility to probe the case instead of the local police.
