## Morning News

The Union Cabinet has approved two elevated highway projects worth over 25 thousand crore rupees for Varanasi in Uttar Pradesh. The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA), chaired by Prime Minister Narendra Modi, approved these projects to improve road connectivity and reduce congestion and travel time in Varanasi. The projects include a 6-lane Greenfield Elevated Corridor covering over 46 kilometer between National Highway-19 and Varanasi Ring Road on Hybrid Annuity Model. It will be build at a total capital cost of over 14 thousand 4 hundred crore rupees.

Briefing media in New Delhi, Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said, the other project is related to the development of around 43-kilometer-long, 6 lane and 4-lane Elevated Corridor along Varuna River Bank in Varanasi city at a cost of around 11 thousand crore rupees.

The Union Cabinet also approved the Semicon 2.0 with a budgetary outlay of 1 lakh 27 thousand 5 hundred crore rupees to strengthen India’s semiconductor design and manufacturing ecosystem. Mr Vaishnaw said, building on the momentum of Semicon 1.0, the scheme aims to further the government’s commitment to putting India on the global semiconductor map.

The Minister said, the Cabinet also cleared the Mobile Phone Manufacturing Scheme with a budgetary outlay of 62 thousand 500 crore rupees, aimed at scaling up domestic mobile phone production, deepening local value addition and strengthening India’s position as a global electronics manufacturing hub.

The Cabinet also gave nod to two multitracking projects covering four districts across Odisha and Jharkhand, expanding the Indian Railways network by about 145 kilometers. It also approved the National Investment Policy for Urea-2026 for Atmanirbhar Bharat.

The Railway Ministry has launched a new beta version of Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation (IRCTC) website to make train ticket booking simpler and more user-friendly. The IRCTC has made four major improvements in this new beta version. First, it does not have unnecessary captchas, pop-ups, flashing graphics, or other distracting elements. Second, passengers can view seat availability across all classes. Third, it offers a faster checkout process, significantly reducing the number of steps required to book tickets. And fourth, the website will now save passengers’ details, making repeat bookings easier. The website can be accessed at www.irctc.co.in/eticket/.

Delhi’s iconic Red Fort complex has been closed to the public till the 80th Independence Day celebrations on 15th of August. An order in this regard has been issued by the Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) in view of the ongoing preparations for the Independence Day celebrations at the complex. A senior ASI official said that such orders are issued every year ahead of Independence Day.

The first international flight service from Madhya Pradesh has been launched. Chief Minister Dr. Mohan Yadav flagged off the first direct flight from Indore to Abu Dhabi, the capital of the United Arab Emirates.

World famous Rath Yatra of Lord Jagannath and his 2 siblings – elder brother Lord Balabhadra and younger sister Devi Subhadra will begin today at Puri in Odisha. During the annual festival of Rath Yatra, Lord Jagannath and his two siblings give darshan to the public in the Chariots and visit Gundicha Temple, revered as the maternal aunt’s abode of Lord Jagannath. Lakhs of devotees from across the country and abroad have converged to witness the divine procession of the Holy Trinity. Our Correspondent has filed this report:

“Jai Jagannath. Jai Jagannath. Jai Jagannath. With the first rays of dawn illuminating the sacred Bada Danda of Puri, an ocean of devotion is enveloping the pilgrim city as Lord Jagannath, Lord Balabhadra, Devi Subhadra and Lord Sudarshan are embarking on their annual sojourn to the Sri Gundicha Temple, revered as the maternal aunt’s abode of the sibling deities. Despite heavy rain lashing Puri since early morning, lakhs of devotees are remaining undeterred, patiently and anxiously awaiting the ceremonial Pahandi Bije of the Holy Trinity to their respective chariots. The Supreme Court today voiced concern over the introduction of a third language in Class 9 under the CBSE curriculum, observing it would increase the stress level of students. The Apex Court has suggested that it be introduced in Class 5 or Class 6 so that students can cope with it.

A bench of Justices BV Nagarathna and R Mahadevan orally asked the Centre to not introduce a third language in Class 9 level. The remarks were made during the hearing of the Tamil Nadu government’s plea against a Madras High Court directive to facilitate the establishment of Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas (JNVs) in every district of the State. The Tamil Nadu government has consistently opposed the setting up of Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas, over concerns with regard to the three-language policy curriculum followed by the schools.

The Supreme Court today directed all States and Union Territories to formulate and implement a comprehensive policy for the early or premature release of prisoners who are of advanced age or are terminally ill. The Court ordered that the policy be framed and notified within three months to ensure a uniform mechanism for considering such cases across the country. A bench of Justices Vikram Nath and Sandeep Mehta issued the directions by invoking its extraordinary powers under Article 142 of the Constitution while deciding a public interest litigation plea seeking a humane and uniform framework for the release of elderly and seriously ill prisoners.

Mr. Wangchuk is on an indefinite fast at Jantar Mantar in New Delhi. Solicitor General Tushar Mehta, who appeared for the Centre as well as the Delhi government, submitted that life of every individual is precious and there was no objection to undertaking Wangchuk’s regular medical checkup.

India has crossed the one billion mark in Social Protection Coverage. In a social media post, Union Labour and Employment Minister Dr. Mansukh Mandaviya said, India’s social protection coverage has expanded from 19 per cent in 2015 to 68.4 per cent in 2026, bringing more than one hundred crore citizens under the umbrella of social protection framework. He said this reflects the Narendra Modi government’s commitment to Sabka Saath Sabka Vikas. India’s social protection system has expanded significantly through various welfare programmes aimed at providing financial security, healthcare, and food assistance to millions. These initiatives have played a crucial role in improving livelihoods and reducing poverty across the country.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will flag off India’s first Hydrogen train between Jind and Sonipat at Jind railway station during his visit to Haryana tomorrow. Designed, engineered and integrated in India, the train has been developed using indigenous technology, reflecting the country’s growing capabilities in advanced railway engineering. With the introduction of this train, India joins the select group of countries that have operational hydrogen-powered trains. These include Germany, Japan, China and the United States. The train has been designed to run at a speed of 110 kilometers per hour but on this section, it will operate at a maximum speed of 75 kilometers per hour.

“India’s hydrogen train features a 10-coach configuration, making it among the longest hydrogen-powered passenger trains developed so far. It is powered by a 3,200 Horse Power  propulsion system, making it one of the most powerful hydrogen-powered trainsets in operation. The train is powered by hydrogen fuel cell technology, which converts hydrogen into electricity to propel the train. The process produces only water vapour as a by-product, resulting in zero carbon emissions during operation, supporting India’s transition to sustainable transport. The train is equipped with multi-layer safety systems capable of detecting hydrogen leaks, heat, flames and smoke. Anand Kumar for Akashvani News, Delhi”
