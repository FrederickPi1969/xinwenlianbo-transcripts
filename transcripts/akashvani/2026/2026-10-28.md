## Morning News

The very severe cyclonic storm Arnab has commenced its landfall process along the Andhra Pradesh and Yanam coasts near Kakinada. According to India Meteorological Department (IMD), the landfall process started at 1:20 AM today. The crossing began earlier than originally expected due to the rapid movement of the storm. The storm hit the coast packing sustained maximum wind speeds of 115-125 km per hour, with gusts reaching up to 140 km per hour.

Heavy rainfall and strong winds have disrupted normal life across several coastal districts of Andhra Pradesh.

“Cyclone Arnab remained practically stationary near the Andhra Pradesh and Yanam coasts, south of Yanam. Extremely heavy rainfall has lashed several coastal districts, with Kannuripalem in Anakapalli district recording 259 millimetres of rainfall. Strong winds reaching 130 kilometres per hour were recorded at Maruteru, while uprooted trees, power disruptions and flooding have affected normal life in several areas. The state government is closely monitoring the situation, Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu reviewing relief operations with district collectors and disaster management officials. More than 84 thousand people have been evacuated to 290 relief shelters, while NDRF teams remain deployed in affected areas. Train and flight services have been disrupted, with several cancellations and rescheduling of services. Authorities have warned of storm surges up to two-and-a-half metres, particularly near Kakinada and Yanam, and advised residents of vulnerable coastal and low-lying areas to remain alert. R. Sai Gireesh, for Akashvani News, Vijayawada.”

In Puducherry, normal life has been affected in the coastal region of Yanam as cyclonic storm Arnab began making landfall near Kakinada in the early hours of this morning.

“Strong winds uprooted trees and electric poles, disrupting power supply in several areas. Seawater entered several houses in coastal villages of Yanam. Residents have been shifted to 19 cyclone relief centres opened in the region.

Around 300 personnel, including Puducherry Police, India Reserve Battalion and the National Disaster Response Force, have been deployed in the region for rescue and relief operations.

Puducherry Public Works Minister Malladi Krishna Rao and Regional Administrator Sivaraj Meena visited the affected areas  and assured residents of all necessary assistance. With K Sheshu Babu, Bibin S Nath, Akashvani News, Puducherry.”

Parliamentary election in Israel remained deadlocked, with exit polls from all three major television networks giving opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot’s bloc a narrow two-seat lead over Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu’s camp. The projections put Eisenkot’s bloc at 52 seats and Netanyahu’s Likud-led alliance at 50, leaving neither side with the 61 seats needed for a majority in the 120-member parliament-Knesset.

“The results could give Eisenkot the first chance to form a government, but his path to power remains uncertain. The remaining 18 seats were projected to go to Arab parties not aligned with either bloc, and Eisenkot has largely ruled out forming a government with them. Netanyahu, meanwhile, could remain caretaker prime minister if coalition talks fail.

Israel’s Central Elections Committee said, voters turnout reached 77.7%, the highest in more than two decades and around seven percentage points above the 2022 election. The vote was the first since the Hamas-led October 7, 2023, attacks and the ensuing wars in Gaza, Lebanon and Iran.

Netanyahu has served as prime minister for nearly 19 of the past 30 years and has repeatedly survived political crises. His opponents removed him from office only once since 2009, but he returned to power 18 months later.

With the latest exit polls showing a narrow advantage for Eisenkot’s bloc but no clear majority for either side, the final count and subsequent coalition negotiations will determine who can form Israel’s next government. Kaumudee Pareek, Akashvani News.”

Very Severe Cyclonic Storm Arnab has completed its landfall along the Andhra Pradesh coast. The India Meteorological Department said the storm crossed the coast between Bapatla and Visakhapatnam, near Narsapur and southwest of Yanam, in earlier this morning. At the time of landfall, the cyclone brought maximum sustained winds of 125 to 135 kilometres per hour, with gusts reaching 150 kilometres per hour. The landfall process was completed by 8 AM, with the storm’s central region moving entirely over land. The system is likely to move north-northwestwards and gradually weaken into a cyclonic storm within the next three hours and further into a deep depression during the following six hours.

Meanwhile, Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu has written to Union Home Minister Amit Shah, seeking early deployment of central teams to assess the damage caused by Cyclone Arnab in the state. In his letter, the Chief Minister said the cyclone had damaged crops, houses, roads and electricity infrastructure. He said 16 NDRF and 11 SDRF teams had been deployed across 14 districts, while more than 28 thousand people had been shifted to rehabilitation centres. No loss of life has been reported so far. The state government has issued emergency helpline numbers 1070 and 112. Mr. Naidu also sought continued support from central agencies for relief and rehabilitation. The authorities have evacuated over 1 lakh 80 thousand people to safer zones and relief shelters across the states of Andhra Pradesh and Odisha. More than 100 train services have been cancelled or rescheduled.

India and Mexico have taken a significant step towards strengthening their economic partnership with the signing of the Terms of Reference for a Preferential Trade Agreement. The government today approved several key initiatives aimed at strengthening domestic manufacturing and promoting rural governance. The decisions were taken at a Union Cabinet meeting chaired by Prime Minister Narendra Modi. Briefing the media in New Delhi, Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw announced the details.

Mr Vaishnaw said, the Cabinet has approved the Container Manufacturing Promotion Scheme with a total outlay of ten thousand crore rupees over 13 years, up to the financial year 2039-40. The scheme aims to establish a globally competitive container manufacturing ecosystem in India.

The Cabinet has also given nod to the Khelo India Sports Goods Manufacturing Scheme to position India as a global hub for high-quality sports goods.

The Cabinet has also cleared Pradhan Mantri Viksit Gram Swaraj Yojana as a Centrally Sponsored Scheme, with a total financial outlay of seven thousand 395 crore rupees. The scheme will be implemented over five years, from 2026-27 to 2030-31 to empower Gram Panchayats and strengthen grassroots governance.

Meanwhile, the Cabinet has also approved a three per cent increase in Dearness Allowance for Central Government employees and Dearness Relief for pensioners, with effect from the first of July, this year.
