## Midday News

India-AI Impact Summit 2026 begins in New Delhi; Five day Summit is first-ever global AI summit to be hosted in the Global South.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi says, it is a matter of immense pride that people from all over world are coming to India for AI Impact Summit.

Violence in Kashmir, North-East and Maoist-affected areas has been reduced by 80 per cent, says Union Home Minister Amit Shah.

Eight people killed after a massive fire broke out in a factory in Rajasthan.

And, in ICC Men’s T20 World Cup Match between Afghanistan and United Arab Emirates underway in the Group ‘D’ clash in Delhi.

The India-AI Impact Summit 2026 is begins in New Delhi. The five day Summit is the first-ever global AI summit to be hosted in the Global South. The Summit will strengthen India’s role as a key platform for shaping the global AI agenda. It also positions India as a convenor and partner in global AI cooperation, supporting shared standards, collaborative frameworks, and scalable solutions for public good. Over a hundred countries have been invited to the Summit and more than two lakh people have registered online.

World leaders from twenty nations are scheduled to attend the event. They include President of France, Emmanuel Macron, Brazil President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, President of Spain, Pedro Sánchez Pérez-Castejón, President of Switzerland, Guy Parmelin, Netherlands Prime Minister Dick Schoof, UAE’s Crown Prince of Abu Dhabi, Sheikh Khaled bin Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan, Prime Minister of Mauritius Dr Navinchandra Ramgoolam, Sri Lanka President Anura Kumara Disanayaka, Vice President of Seychelles, Sebastien Pillay and Bhutanese Prime Minister Tshering Tobgay.

The External Affairs Ministry said that leaders from Bolivia, Croatia, Estonia, Finland, Greece, Guyana, Kazakhstan, Liechtenstein, Serbia, and Slovakia, are also set to participate in the Summit. Ministerial delegations from over 45 countries would be participating in the Summit. The UN Secretary General and Senior Officials from several International Organizations will also join the deliberations.

The India-AI Impact Summit 2026 is guided by three foundational pillars, or Sutras, which articulate the core principles guiding global cooperation on AI. More from our correspondent:-

“Artificial Intelligence is a key enabler of India’s development journey, strengthening governance and improving public service delivery. The five-day Summit will bring together global leaders, policymakers, technology companies, innovators, and experts to showcase and deliberate on the transformative potential of AI across governance, innovation, and sustainable development. On the first day of the India AI Impact Summit today, panel discussions are being held on a range of topics at Bharat Mandapam and Sushma Swaraj Bhawan. This includes the Future of Employability in the Age of AI, Harnessing AI to Transform India’s Judicial Ecosystem, AI for Road Safety: Data-Driven Solutions for Enhancing Road Safety in India and AI for Smart and Resilient Agriculture. Recognising the strategic importance of AI, India has accorded high priority to building a robust and inclusive AI ecosystem. Initiatives such as the India AI Mission, development of AI compute infrastructure, promotion of indigenous AI models, and large-scale capacity-building programmes are laying a strong foundation for responsible and trusted AI adoption in the country. With Aman Yadav, Ankit Kumar, Akashvani News Delhi.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today said that it is a matter of immense pride for the countrymen that people from all over the world are coming to India for the India AI Impact Summit. In a social media post, Mr Modi extended warm welcome to world leaders, industry leaders, innovators, policymakers, researchers and tech enthusiasts from across the world for the Summit. Mr Modi emphasized that the occasion is proof that the country is progressing rapidly in the field of science and technology, making an important contribution to global development. The Prime Minister added that the occasion also shows the capability of the country’s youth. He also highlighted that the Summit’s theme of Sarvajana Hitaya, Sarvajana Sukhaya or welfare for all, happiness for all, reflects the country’s shared commitment to harnessing Artificial Intelligence for human-centric progress.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will inaugurate the India AI Impact Expo 2026 in New Delhi this evening. The five-day expo is being held alongside the India AI Impact Summit 2026, and will serve as a national demonstration of Artificial Intelligence in action. It will feature 13 country pavilions showcasing international collaboration in the AI ecosystem.

Chief Economic Advisor V Anantha Nageswaran today said that India can lead globally by aligning AI adoption with mass employability through education reform. Mr Nageswaran made these remarks during the Future of Employability and AI session at the AI Impact Summit 2026 in New Delhi. He said, India could become one of the largest societies where machine learning and human resources complement each other rather than conflict.

CEO of Edge Verve Sateesh Seetharamiah said AI will act as a faster capability multiplier and life-long learning ability will be the core mantra to tide over the AI wave.

Information and Broadcasting Ministry has set up a dedicated Pavilion at the India AI Impact Summit 2026 in New Delhi, highlighting India’s growing technological capabilities, startup depth, and policy-led vision for responsible AI adoption. The Pavilion, which houses the WAVES Creators Corner, is designed as a media and dialogue space where policymakers, startups and industry leaders converge to discuss how artificial intelligence can be aligned with India’s developmental priorities, cultural diversity, and national security considerations. The Ministry said, the Pavilion aims to project India’s AI ecosystem not merely as a consumer of global technology, but as a creator of indigenous, scalable and socially relevant AI solutions.

The Indian Army will highlight its expanding integration of Artificial Intelligence to strengthen sustainment, enhance operational readiness and build future-ready defence capabilities at the AI Impact Summit 2026. In a social media post, the Army said visitors can explore cutting-edge, dual-use technologies at the Indian Army AI Stall at the Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will meet French President Emmanuel Macron in Mumbai tomorrow. During the meeting the two leaders will review progress made in India-France Strategic Partnership and exchange views on issues of regional and global importance. Mr Macron will be on a three day official visit to India from tomorrow at the invitation of Prime Minister Narendra Modi to participate in the AI Impact Summit.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah has said that after the abrogation of Article 370, the violence in Kashmir, North-East and Maoist-affected areas has been reduced by 80 per cent. He said, the day is not far when these areas will be completely free from violence. Speaking on the occasion of the 79th Raising Day celebrations of Delhi Police, Mr Shah said, the Maoist terrorism, spread across 11 states, has always posed a significant challenge to the country’s internal security.

Mr Shah said, the foundation stone for a Delhi Police Special Cell Integrated Headquarters has been laid today to further strengthen policing in the national capital.

The spice sector has hailed the Interim Trade Agreement achieved between India and the United States of America. Talking to Akashvani News, Chairman of Federation of Indian Spice Stakeholders Tejus Gandhi said that the deal will help in generating new opportunities of enhancing market competitiveness. Mr Gandhi added that the reduction of tariffs will boost exports of Indian goods.

In Rajasthan, eight people have been killed after a massive fire broke out in a factory in Tapukara of khairthal-Tijara district. Police and administrative authorities began relief and rescue operations. Firefighters are attempting to douse the flames. More people are feared to be trapped inside the factory. The incident is reported to have occurred at a cardboard factory in the Khushkhera industrial area. According to eyewitnesses, a fire broke out early morning following a loud explosion in the factory, and within a short time it took a severe and uncontrollable form. ADM Sumitra Mishra stated that the bodies of all eight workers have been recovered. The rescue operation is underway.

Vice President C P Radhakrishnan and Prime Minister Narendra Modi have expressed grief at the fire mishap in Bhiwadi, Rajasthan. In a social media post, Mr Radhakrishnan extended condolences to the bereaved families, and prayed for the speedy recovery of those injured. Prime Minister Narendra Modi described the incident as tragic and deeply saddening. In a social media post, Mr Modi condoled the loss of lives in the mishap and prayed for the speedy recovery of the injured.

The International Shivratri Festival of Mandi district in Himachal Pradesh is beginning today. This time, more than 216 deities have been invited to the fair, who will stay in Mandi for an entire week. The festival will commence with prayers and the traditional jaleb ceremony at the Shri Raj Madhav Rai Temple.

In China, eight people were killed and left two others with minor burns following an explosion and fire at a fireworks shop, ahead of the Lunar New Year.

Four people were killed after an Israeli airstrike targeted a vehicle near Lebanon’s border with Syria, underlining continued hostilities along Israel’s northern front despite an existing truce. According to Lebanon’s National News Agency an Israeli drone struck a car in the Majdal Anjar area near the Lebanese-Syrian border last night. The Israeli military said that it had targeted operatives belonging to the Palestinian militant group Islamic Jihad.

Members of Civil Society Organisations from Chandel and Tengnoupal districts of Manipur called on President Droupadi Murmu at Rashtrapati Bhavan in New Delhi today. In a social media post, President Murmu said that they visited Rashtrapati Bhavan as part of the National Integration Tour.

Central Industrial Security Force (CISF) today conducted a Joint Counter-Terrorist Mock Exercise at Delhi Airport to enhance inter-agency coordination, preparedness and response to security threats. In a social media post, CISF said that the exercise focused on strengthening threat response, sharpening tactical readiness and ensuring seamless inter-agency coordination.

The match is set to begin at 7 PM Indian Standard Time.

President Droupadi Murmu has said, efforts should be made to preserve and promote the Ol Chiki script of Santhali language. Addressing the centenary celebrations of Ol Chiki script in New Delhi, President Murmu said, Ol Chiki is a powerful introduction to the Santhali community.

The Ol Chiki is the official script of the Santhali language, one of India’s prominent tribal languages spoken widely across Jharkhand, Odisha, West Bengal, Assam, and Bihar.

The President said, the Ol Chiki script is expanding not only in the country but also worldwide.

The Government also released a commemorative coin and commemorative postage stamp to mark 100 years of the Ol Chiki script. The Ol Chiki script was created in 1925 by Raghunath Murmu for the Santali language .

The agreement ensures robust safeguards to protect the interests of Indian farmers and domestic producers, marking a transformative phase in trade relations between the two countries.
