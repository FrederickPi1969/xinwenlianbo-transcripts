The Election Commission has issued directions to implement the Supreme Court’s order on the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls in West Bengal. It mandates public display of names under Logical Discrepancies and Unmapped categories and provides affected voters opportunities to submit documents and be heard within a specified timeline.

The Election Commission has directed West Bengal authorities to strictly implement the Supreme Court’s this month’s order related to the ongoing Special Intensive Revision (SIR) 2026. It has instructed the state administration and police to ensure adequate manpower, smooth conduct of hearings, and maintenance of law and order, with strict action for any non-compliance. The ECI has directed DGP of West Bengal, Police Commissioner of Kolkata, all District Collectors, all other Police Commissioners and all SPs to ensure strict law and order during SIR. Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar has assured the people of West Bengal that no one will be allowed to take law in their hands and strict action will be taken in all such cases. US President Donald Trump has said that he will not use force to take control of Greenland, but reiterated his demand that Washington be given ownership of the Arctic Island. Addressing World Economic Forum in Davos, US President said, he is seeking immediate negotiations to discuss the acquisition of Greenland. Greenland is a semi-autonomous territory of Denmark, a US ally. The US and Denmark are among 32 North American and European countries, which form the military alliance, NATO. Trump has repeatedly raised the idea of acquiring Greenland from Denmark.

US President Donald Trump has announced that he is cancelling his planned tariff on US allies in Europe over US control of Greenland after he and the leader of NATO agreed to a framework of a future deal on Arctic security. The abrupt about-face emerged hours after Trump had insisted that he wants to get Greenland, including right, title and ownership, but said he would not use force to do so while deriding European allies and vowing that NATO should not try to block US expansionism. In an extraordinary speech at the World Economic Forum, the president said he is asking for territory that was cold and poorly located.

The European Parliament has decided to suspend work on the European Union’s trade deal with the United States in protest at US President Donald Trump’s demands to acquire Greenland and threats of tariffs on European allies who oppose his plan. The EU assembly has been debating legislative proposals to remove many EU import duties on US goods, a key part of the agreement struck in Turnberry, Scotland, at the end of July, as well as to continue zero duties for US lobsters, initially agreed with Trump in 2020.

Prime Minister’s flagship platform for Pro-Active Governance and Timely Implementation, PRAGATI, has marked a significant milestone with the successful conduct of its 50th meeting. Since its launch by Prime Minister Narendra Modi in 2015, PRAGATI has transformed governance by enabling real-time monitoring and resolution of key infrastructure projects and public grievances through direct Prime Ministerial review. Over a decade, PRAGATI has helped accelerate decision-making, resolution of issues causing delay in key infrastructure projects, and instilled a strong culture of accountability. Today, in this special series, we take a look at the Hyderabad Metro Rail Project.

Hyderabad, the capital of Telangana, has emerged over the last two decades as one of India’s most dynamic metropolitan economies, driven by rapid growth in information technology, pharmaceuticals, manufacturing and services. The Hyderabad Metro Rail Project was conceived as a high-capacity mass rapid transit system to provide a reliable, efficient and sustainable urban mobility backbone for Hyderabad. Phase-I of the Hyderabad Metro consists of three high-density corridors spanning 69.2 Km. The project was reviewed at the highest level under the PRAGATI mechanism chaired by Prime Minister Narendra Modi on 30th December 2015. Prime Minister issued a set of decisive directions to ensure that institutional and financial bottlenecks did not hinder commissioning. Given the project’s PPP structure, the Government of Telangana, the Department of Economic Affairs and the Ministry of Urban Development were directed to closely coordinate to ensure smooth and timely commissioning of the metro. This led to coordinated efforts across multiple agencies to guarantee that a project of this magnitude was implemented through focussed efforts. These PRAGATI-driven interventions brought clarity and momentum at a crucial stage of the project. These ensured that financial support, regulatory decisions and inter-governmental coordination moved in parallel with construction activity, preventing delays that could have affected commissioning. As a result, the Hyderabad Metro was able to progress in a coordinated manner across all three corridors, with successive sections becoming operational and rapidly attracting ridership. With Suparna Saikia, Anupam Mishra, Akashvani News, Delhi.
