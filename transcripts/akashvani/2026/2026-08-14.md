Today is Partition Horrors Remembrance Day. The nation is paying homage to all of those who sacrificed their lives and suffered unimaginable pain during the partition in 1947. A report:

“To mark the Partition Horrors Remembrance Day, various programs are being organized across the country including in the National Capital. In a fitting tribute to all those who lost their lives due to the partition of the nation and were displaced from their roots, in 2021, the Government had decided to observe 14th August every year as the day to commemorate their sacrifice. It also ensures that future generations remember the pain and suffering endured by the people, as the birth of independent India was marked by the violent partition, which left lasting scars on millions. The Partition Horrors Remembrance Day keep reminding people of the need to remove the poison of social divisions, disharmony and further strengthen the spirit of oneness, social harmony and human empowerment. The partition caused amongst the largest migrations in human history affecting about 20 million people. While celebrating the Independence, a grateful nation also salutes those people who had to sacrifice their lives in the frenzy of violence. ANUPAM MISHRA, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.

On Partition Horrors Remembrance Day, Prime Minister Narendra Modi has remembered the courage of all those who were impacted by Partition. In a social media post, Mr Modi termed the partition as a moment in history that tore apart several lives, where families were uprooted, loved ones were lost and immense suffering was endured. Mr Modi added that at the same time, people overcame this and rebuilt their lives from nothing, turned adversity into achievement and contributed immensely to the nation’s progress.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah has paid homage to all those who lost their lives in the partition and to those who struggled against the atrocities on Partition Horrors Remembrance Day. In a social media post, Mr Shah said that the day of 14 August 1947 stands witness to one of the most brutal chapters in history, in which millions of people had to lose their lives and endure inhuman suffering.

## Evening News

President Droupadi Murmu has said that Indian Economy is the fastest growing major economy despite warfare and instability prevailing in several parts of the world. Addressing the nation on the eve of the 80th Independence Day this evening, the President highlighted that the growth rate of the Indian economy is expected to be more than twice the average global growth rate.

President Droupadi Murmu highlighted that making the country Naxal-free is a major achievement, as for decades, Naxalism posed a serious challenge to the nation. She said, today, the areas once affected by Naxalism are filled with enthusiasm.

The President said that students are the architects of India’s future and emphasised that the Government is undertaking comprehensive steps to reform public examinations.

Speaking about the country’s young population, the President said, 65 per cent of the country’s population is below the age of 35, which is the most valuable asset. She said, many of our youth are embracing the culture of job creation as they pursue the path of self-employment.

President Murmu said, India is moving ahead towards building a Viksit Bharat by the centenary year of Independence in 2047. She said, guided by the spirit of Antyodaya, it is the resolve of the country to ensure that the fruits of development reach every individual.

In her address, President Murmu highlighted the government’s efforts to uplift the deprived sections of the society, saying over 25 crore people have been lifted out of poverty.

More than 80 crore people are receiving free rations, while 60 crore people have access to free healthcare coverage of up to five lakh rupees annually under Ayushman Bharat. The President also highlighted support to farmers through Kisan Samman Nidhi and Kisan Credit Cards.

Speaking about the country’s digital infrastructure, President Droupadi Murmu said that India has developed the world’s largest and best digital infrastructure and more than half of the world’s real-time digital transactions take place in India. She said, together, the people of India and the Government have presented an example of digital revolution to the global community.

President Droupadi Murmu also noted that national interest is the foundation of India’s foreign policy and peace, cooperation, dialogue, and the ideal of Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam are the pillars of its foreign policy. The President said that India is playing a leading role among the countries of the Global South and the country has grown in stature on the global stage.

The President also reminded that future generations have as much right over natural resources as the present generations. She emphasised to ensure that none of our actions result in the slightest encroachment upon the exercise of this right by future generations.

President Droupadi Murmu also expressed happiness over the exceptional performance of girls in the field of education. She said, women’s participation is increasing across diverse sectors, from the Drone Didi who contributes to enhancing agricultural productivity, to the Fighter Combat Leader in the Air Force.

Speaking about sports promotion, President Murmu said, sports are being adopted as a medium for promoting the development of the youth and for nation-building.

The President said, embracing the rich heritage and the ideals of the freedom movement, the nation is marching ahead on the path of modern development.
