## Morning News

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh has urged Army Commanders to closely monitor geopolitical and regional developments and remain prepared to meet future challenges. Addressing the Army Commanders’ Conference in New Delhi yesterday, Mr Singh emphasized the need for a long-term strategic vision spanning the next 20 to 30 years. He called upon the Indian Army’s senior leadership to remain equally prepared to respond to immediate and emerging challenges. He stressed that strategies must be dynamic and flexible to allow timely adjustments to changing circumstances. More from our Correspondent:

“Commending the armed forces for adapting to evolving operational requirements, the Defence Minister said, Operation Sindoor demonstrated innovative thinking, enhanced synergy and superior coordination, supported by advanced weaponry. He added that the operation sent a clear message to the world that India would not hesitate to take necessary measures to safeguard its national interests. Mr Singh called for an integrated approach to modern warfare, emphasizing that success would depend on multi-domain capabilities and seamless coordination in joint operations. The Defence Minister also highlighted that defence production reached a record one lakh 78 thousand crore rupees in the financial year 2025-26, while defence exports touched an all-time high of around 38 thousand crore rupees. On the occasion, Mr Singh released the Land Warfare Doctrine, which provides an overarching framework to prepare the Indian Army for the challenges posed by the evolving nature of warfare and future conflicts. He also unveiled the annual United Nations Journal and felicitated Indian Army personnel for their notable contributions while serving as United Nations peacekeepers. India has emerged as the third-largest exporter of arms to Europe, driven by rising demand for defence equipment under the European Union’s ‘ReArm Europe’ initiative and the expansion of the country’s domestic manufacturing sector. Defence Secretary Rajesh Kumar Singh made the remarks while talking to media persons yesterday. He said India’s defence exports have reached an all-time high of 38 thousand 424 crore rupees, equivalent to around 3.6 billion euros. He said the increase in exports to Europe was largely driven by the growing demand for standard ammunition, artillery shells and other large- and small-calibre ammunition.

Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman has urged financial sector regulators and other stakeholders to expedite the refund of unclaimed assets to their rightful owners. These include bank deposits, dividends, shares, post office savings, insurance proceeds and pension funds. Ms Sitharaman stated this while chairing the 30th meeting of the Financial Stability and Development Council (FSDC) in New Delhi yesterday. Ms Sitharaman also called for simplifying Know Your Customer (KYC) procedures and strengthening cybersecurity preparedness amid rising geopolitical uncertainties. The Council reviewed efforts under the ‘Your Money, Your Right’ campaign, which aims to facilitate the return of unclaimed financial assets to their rightful owners. It urged the concerned ministries and regulators to intensify their efforts to ensure timely refunds. The Council also deliberated on issues relating to macroeconomic and financial stability, as well as the country’s preparedness to address emerging challenges. Discussions included measures to promote innovation in the fintech sector and explore the potential of tokenisation of financial assets and other real-world assets.

The State Bank of India (SBI) has individually notified customers whose personal data was identified in a recent suspected cyber incident, assuring them that their account balances and funds remain safe. In a statement issued yesterday, the country’s largest public sector lender said, the affected customers have been informed. The bank also advised customers to follow safe digital banking practices and remain alert to suspicious calls, messages and emails. SBI initiated a detailed investigation after receiving alerts on 16th of this month from its Threat Intelligence Service Provider, the Defence Cyber Agency and the Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In) about suspected customer data exposure on the dark web.

The Union Home Ministry will launch a first-of-its-kind pilot initiative to strengthen civil defence preparedness by providing specialised training to Civil Defence Master Trainers through the Indian Army. The training will be conducted by the Directorate General of Fire Services, Civil Defence and Home Guards, with the aim of establishing a robust civil defence network across the country. The Ministry said the three-day training programme will be conducted in 12 States and Union Territories in the first phase.  A total of 600 Civil Defence Master Trainers, with 50 from each participating State and Union Territory, are proposed to be trained by the second week of November this year.

Electronics and Information Technology Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw has expressed confidence that India will have mobile phones designed and manufactured in the country and sold under Indian brands by October 2028. Speaking at a summit organised by a media house in New Delhi yesterday, Mr Vaishnaw said, India will become the world’s design capital for semiconductors. He added that India lost four decades, from 1950 to 1990, during which manufacturing was never considered an important sector of the Indian economy.

Railways Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw will inaugurate the Satna-Devendra Nagar and Rewa-Churhat rail sections today, covering a total length of more than one hundred kilometres. He will also flag off a new train service between Devendra Nagar and Churhat. Madhya Pradesh Chief Minister Dr Mohan Yadav will be present on the occasion. India has advised its nationals in France, Italy and other parts of Europe to remain vigilant and exercise caution amid protests and unrest in several countries. The External Affairs Ministry said, Indian missions in France, Italy and Belgium are closely monitoring the situation and maintaining regular contact with members of the Indian community to ensure their safety and well-being. The advisory comes amid large-scale demonstrations across parts of Europe, reportedly fuelled in part by the rising cost of living. Protests in France last week turned violent, with incidents of arson and vandalism reported in several areas.  Addressing the media, External Affairs Ministry spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal said Indian nationals had been advised to avoid areas where protests are taking place, exercise due caution while travelling and follow advisories issued by local authorities.

## Midday News

The government has achieved its target of creating 11 crore Farmer IDs across the country. Agriculture and Farmers Welfare Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan said this today while sharing a video message. The Minister said the target of creating 11 crore Farmer IDs by March 2027 was set by Prime Minister Narendra Modi, which has been achieved five months ahead of schedule. Mr Chouhan informed that this target was achieved by yesterday night. He said that Farmer IDs will ensure transparent delivery of benefits under various government schemes.

The Ministry of Home Affairs will collaborate with the Indian Army to establish a civil defence network of around 60 thousand volunteers across 12 states and Union Territories under a pilot project. According to the Ministry of Home Affairs, the first phase will involve specialised three-day training for civil defence master trainers from the participating states and Union Territories. A total of 600 master trainers, 50 from each participating state and Union Territory, will undergo training at designated Indian Army training establishments. The programme is scheduled to be completed by the second week of November 2026.

The initiative follows Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s Independence Day address, in which he outlined a vision for a vibrant civil defence network equipped with modern systems, advanced technologies and contemporary preparedness capabilities.

The Central Government has approved a proposal to increase 320 postgraduate medical seats across six medical colleges in Himachal Pradesh. Union Health and Family Welfare Minister Jagat Prakash Nadda shared this information in a video message. The Health Minister said the approval is part of the government’s initiative to expand medical education and strengthen healthcare services across the country. Mr Nadda said the government will provide a grant of approximately 415 crore rupees for this project.

Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal has said the government is willing to bear the entire cost of expensive certifications for micro and small enterprises under the Export Promotion Mission, to help Indian businesses access global markets. Addressing reporters in Berlin on his two-day visit to Germany, Mr Goyal urged the existing and prospective exporters to approach the Directorate General of Foreign Trade for assistance with certifications and regulatory compliance. He said the government would also support micro and small enterprises in meeting European Union requirements, including the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism and the EU Deforestation Regulation. Support for other businesses would be provided on a graded basis.

The fifth edition of Kashi Tamil Sangamam is scheduled to be held from the 28th of next month till the 12th of December. The programme will celebrate, reaffirm and strengthen the age-old civilizational bonds between Tamil Nadu and Kashi, the two ancient seats of learning, strengthening the spirit of Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat. The Ministry of Education said the theme of this edition is Thirukkural Payilvom – Thirukkural Abhyas Karen.

As part of the Sangamam, around one thousand four hundred participants from Tamil Nadu, divided into seven groups of approximately two hundred members each, will undertake an eight-day journey. The itinerary will include Varanasi, Prayagraj and Ayodhya and featuring cultural, academic, domain-specific and spiritual engagements. The emphasis on youth participation will enable a new generation to engage meaningfully with India’s classical knowledge traditions. Ministry of Education invites eligible applicants from Tamil Nadu, especially youth and students, to register for the programme and participate in this celebration of India’s cultural oneness, linguistic diversity and shared civilisational inheritance. Interested applicants can register through the official Kashi Tamil Sangamam portal, hosted by IIT Madras, at  https://kashitamil.iitm.ac.in/. Registration has commenced on the 22nd of this month and will remain open until the 13th of next month. The Ministry informed that applications are invited under seven categories, including students, teachers, writers and media professionals, agriculture and allied sectors, professionals and artisans, women, and spiritual leaders.

Odisha is gearing up for intense rainfall as a developing system over the Bay of Bengal is expected to strengthen into a cyclonic storm and move towards the eastern coast. The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has issued red, orange and yellow warnings for several districts, with extremely heavy rainfall possible in isolated areas  this Tuesday and Wednesday. The state government has stepped up preparedness, keeping evacuation arrangements, cyclone shelters and emergency response teams ready.

The weather system over the east-central and adjoining southeast Bay of Bengal is expected to intensify into a depression today, a deep depression tomorrow, and a cyclonic storm by Monday morning. If it reaches cyclonic-storm intensity, it is expected to be named ‘Arnab’. The IMD has issued a red warning for Gajapati, Ganjam and Puri for this Tuesday. On Wednesday, the red alert covers Rayagada, Koraput, Malkangiri, Gajapati and Ganjam, where extremely heavy rainfall is possible at isolated places. Orange and yellow warnings have also been issued for several other districts; the rainfall threat is likely to extend into northern and western Odisha on Thursday. Meanwhile, Odisha Chief Minister Mohan Charan Majhi convened a high-level meeting at Bhubaneswar this evening to review the administration’s cyclone preparedness. The state government has already directed district administrations to prepare evacuation plans and keep cyclone shelters ready. The NDRF, ODRAF and Fire Services are on standby, while health facilities are preparing for emergencies. Fishermen have been advised against venturing into the sea as authorities closely monitor the system moving towards the north Andhra Pradesh-south Odisha coast.
