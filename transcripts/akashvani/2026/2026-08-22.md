## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that reforms are not a compulsion but a commitment and conviction for his government, and that its policies are pro-people, pro-growth and pro-development.

Addressing the Economic Times World Leaders Forum in New Delhi last evening, Mr Modi said his government has created a clear roadmap for reforms, with the primary focus being on reforms at the governance level. He said India is undertaking next-generation reforms at the policy level to secure the country’s future.

The Prime Minister pointed out that his government is not merely changing regulations but is changing the entire philosophy behind them. He said that earlier, the approach to regulations was Prohibited unless Permitted . Mr Modi said the licence raj under earlier governments contributed to unemployment, while his government’s Production Linked Incentive model is creating new opportunities.

The Prime Minister also spoke about the global economic environment, saying the weaponisation of resources and growing mistrust were creating new challenges. He said that even in such circumstances, the world continues to see India as a bright spot of hope and growth.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah has lauded West Bengal Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari for handing over 1,129 acres of land to the Border Security Force  and Sashastra Seema Bal  for fencing along the India-Bangladesh border in the state within 45 days of the new government coming to power. He said the request for the land required for the border fence had been pending since 2014. Mr Shah was speaking while attending a programme at the Sashastra Seema Bal  frontier headquarters in the Ranidanga area at Siliguri in West Bengal yesterday. The Home Minister said completing border fencing is crucial to stopping infiltration and protecting national security. He said the government will continue its zero-tolerance policy towards illegal immigration.

The BRICS Tourism Ministers’ Meeting, held under India’s Chairship, concluded yesterday with the unanimous adoption of the Jaipur Declaration, which provides a framework for strengthening tourism cooperation among BRICS member countries. The declaration outlines key areas of cooperation, including the use of artificial intelligence in tourism, sustainable tourism practices, skill development and seamless travel facilitation.

The meeting was presided over by Union Minister for Culture and Tourism Gajendra Singh Shekhawat at Jaipur in Rajasthan and marked the culmination of the BRICS Tourism Track under India’s Chairship in 2026. The Tourism Track was taken forward under the theme, “Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability.”

Addressing the meeting, Mr Shekhawat said tourism will play an important role in India’s journey towards Viksit Bharat by 2047 and had the potential to extend economic growth beyond major urban centres to towns, villages and remote regions.

India will host the BRICS Youth Ministers’ Meeting and the BRICS Sports Ministers’ Meeting at Visakhapatnam in Andhra Pradesh. The BRICS Youth Ministers’ Meeting will be held today, while the BRICS Sports Ministers’ Meeting will take place tomorrow.

The meetings will be chaired by Youth Affairs and Sports Minister Dr Mansukh Mandaviya, with Minister of State for Youth Affairs and Sports Raksha Khadse also participating.

According to the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, the Youth Ministers’ Meeting will focus on strengthening cooperation in youth development and empowerment, with discussions on education, skill development, entrepreneurship, wellbeing and sustainable development.

President Droupadi Murmu will confer National Teachers’ Awards to 48 school teachers on the 5th of next month at Vigyan Bhawan in New Delhi. The Education Ministry said, school teachers have been selected through a three-stage selection process at the District, State and National levels by the Department of School Education and Literacy. The 48 selected school teachers are from 27 States, 7 Union Territories and 6 Organizations of the Central Government. Out of the 48 selected teachers, 26 are male and 22 are female.

India and Nepal have reviewed the entire spectrum of bilateral cooperation and reaffirmed their commitment to strengthening cooperation on border management and security. The discussion came up during the 14th meeting of the India-Nepal Joint Working Group, which concluded in Dehradun yesterday.

“Discussions covered measures to address trans-border crime and criminal activities, and strengthening an effective utilisation of border infrastructure, particularly Integrated Check Posts, roads and railway networks. The two sides also discussed the removal of encroachments and maintenance of boundary pillars. They exchanged views on strengthening institutional coordination and capacity building across various sectors and agreed to sustain engagement in these areas. Both sides also agreed that the next meeting of the Joint Working Group will be held in Nepal at a mutually convenient date. Ishani Yadav, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

During the meeting, both the countries reviewed the ongoing defence industrial cooperation with emphasis on Research and Development through the India-UK vision 2035 and the Ten year Defence Industrial Roadmap. The government today imposed restrictions on the manufacturing, sale and distribution of all fixed dose combinations (FDCs) containing Chlorpheniramine Maleate and Phenylephrine Hydrochloride for children below four years of age. The Health Ministry, in a notification, mandated a clear warning that these medicines shall not be used in children below four years of age, in a significant step to safeguard the health and safety of young children. The government directed manufacturers to clearly display the warning, “Fixed Dose Combination shall not be used in children below four years of age,” on the label, package insert and promotional literature associated with these formulations. The government said that the use of such combinations may pose risks to children below four years of age and that safer alternatives are available.

A major industrial accident has been reported from Jharkhand. Three engineers were killed and one person was seriously injured after a turbine exploded and triggered a massive fire at a cement and steel power plant in Ramgarh, late last night. A report:

A massive fire broke out late last night following a powerful explosion in a turbine at the Maa Chhinnamastika Cement and Steel Power Plant in Hehal, under the Barkakana OP area of Ramgarh district. Three engineers were killed in the incident, while one person sustained serious injuries. The deceased have been identified as Sanjeev Kumar Kesari of Ranchi, Vineet Mahto of Bokaro, and Abhishek Pandey of Garhwa. According to preliminary information, the plant’s protection system failed due to excessive temperatures, causing the turbine to overspeed and resulting in the explosion. Plant employees have alleged that the management had already been informed about excessive temperatures in the control room and inadequate cooling arrangements, but no timely action was taken. Several fire trucks rushed to the site after receiving information about the explosion and fire and after considerable efforts, firefighters managed to bring the blaze under control. Angered by the incident, villagers and local residents blocked the Ramgarh–Patratu road today, demanding compensation and jobs for the affected families. Mahvish Rahman, Akashvani News, Ranchi.
