The US Federal Reserve has kept its benchmark interest rate unchanged at a target range of 3.75% to 4.00%, keeping the average effective rate at approximately 3.9%. More from our correspondent:

“Led by Chairman Kevin Warsh, the Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) opted to maintain the status quo following a quarter-percentage-point hike in September, which had marked the central bank’s first interest rate increase in three years. The decision was not unanimous. Fed Governor Lisa Cook and Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack dissented from the hold, voting to raise the target range by a quarter percentage point due to persistent inflation concerns. The central bank stated that keeping rates unchanged allows for the “accumulation of more information” to ensure a timely return of inflation to its 2% target. The decision comes less than a week before the US midterm elections. Financial markets and economists continue to anticipate a potential final quarter-point rate hike at the next FOMC meeting scheduled for 8th and 9th December 2026. KAUMUDEE PAREEK, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”
