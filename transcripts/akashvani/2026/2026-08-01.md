## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that abuse and hatred never solve anything. He appealed for compassion toward misguided youth who have gone astray, saying they should be guided onto the right path in the spirit of nation-building and national interest, rather than be punished. In a video message posted on his social media handles, the Prime Minister referred to the recent incident at Jantar Mantar in National Capital, where abusive language was used against him and his late mother. He said the episode had been witnessed by people across the country and the world, adding that while the anger and disappointment in society over such conduct were understandable, childhood is also a phase of learning, making mistakes and correcting them.

President Droupadi Murmu has given assent to the Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Amendment Bill, 2026. The Bill was passed by Parliament on Thursday. The Act aims to curb unfair practices in public examinations by introducing stricter punishments and establishing fast-track courts. It seeks to strengthen transparency, fairness, and integrity in the examination system by creating a robust legal framework while protecting the future of students. The Act includes several key provisions, including stringent penalties such as imprisonment of up to ten years, fines of up to ten crore rupees, and the confiscation of properties belonging to convicted offenders.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will be on a day-long visit to Andhra Pradesh and Karnataka today. Mr Modi will first travel to Andhra Pradesh, where he will lay the foundation stone, inaugurate and dedicate to the nation multiple development projects worth nearly 18,000 crore rupees at Bhogapuram in Vizianagaram district.  More from our Correspondent:-

“The Prime Minister will take a walk-through of the passenger terminal before inaugurating the Greenfield Alluri Sitarama Raju International Airport at Bhogapuram. Built under the Public-Private Partnership mode at a cost of over 5 thousand 640 crore rupees, the airport has the capacity to handle six million passengers annually.

The Prime Minister will also lay the foundation stone for Andhra Pradesh’s first semiconductor manufacturing facility under the India Semiconductor Mission at Visakhapatnam. The ASIP project, involving an investment of over 460 crore rupees, will have the capacity to produce about 96 million chips every year.

In addition, he will dedicate to the nation and lay the foundation stone for renewable energy transmission projects worth about 9 thousand 920 crore rupees and National Highway projects worth over 1 thousand 880 crore rupees. Allu Murali, Akashvani News, Visakhapatnam.”

West Bengal begins the process of Census today, which will be conducted digitally in two phases. The exercise aims to collect updated demographic data through a technology-driven approach, with the two-phase process designed to ensure comprehensive coverage across the state. More from our correspondent:

“In the first phase, citizens can complete the Self-Enumeration process online from today until the 15th of August by submitting their details through the official Census portal. Citizens can log on to se.census.gov.in and complete their own details through the self-enumeration system.

The second stage of this phase will run from the 16th of August to the 14th of September. During this period, Census officials will visit households for house-listing and preliminary enumeration.

The final phase is scheduled for February next year, when enumerators will collect detailed information on every member of each household, including name, age, gender, caste and religion, for the actual population count. Sandipon Dasgupta and Shyamal Goswami RNU Kolkata.”

India and China will resume border trade through the Nathu La Pass in Sikkim today, marking the revival of a key trade route between the two neighbouring countries. The resumption of trade will end a six-year suspension that had been placed since 2020 due to the COVID-19 pandemic. The revival of trade is expected to boost cross-border commerce, benefiting registered traders, transport operators and allied businesses. Trade through Nathu La had resumed in 2006 after remaining suspended for more than four decades.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today said Andhra Pradesh is moving ahead at a rapid pace and that the Centre is fully committed to realising the vision of Swarna Andhra and Viksit Bharat through large-scale infrastructure and industrial development.

Addressing a public meeting after inaugurating the Alluri Sitarama Raju International Airport at Bhogapuram, the Prime Minister said development projects worth about 18 thousand crore rupees, including airport, road and energy infrastructure, reflect the State’s fast-paced progress under the NDA governments at the Centre and in the State.

Mr. Modi described the new airport as the runway for the growth of Andhra Pradesh, particularly North Andhra, and a launch pad for the aspirations of the State’s youth. He announced that the airport has been named after freedom fighter Alluri Sitarama Raju, saying the name would inspire future generations with the spirit of patriotism and public service.

Mr. Modi said the Centre is transforming Andhra Pradesh into a major growth engine through ports, highways, railways and logistics under the PM Gati Shakti National Master Plan. He said railway projects worth nearly one lakh crore rupees are under implementation, while more than 70 railway stations are being modernised.

The Prime Minister expressed confidence that under the leadership of Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu, Andhra Pradesh would play a major role in achieving the goal of Viksit Bharat 2047.

Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu praised Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s leadership for helping rebuild Andhra Pradesh and transform North Andhra. He said the inauguration of the Alluri Sitarama Raju International Airport at Bhogapuram was a historic milestone that would bring investment, industries, jobs and new opportunities to the region.

Indian Railways has approved the multitracking of the 29 kilometre Danapur to Fatuha section of East Central Railway at a cost of 9 hundred 76 crore rupees. The Railway Ministry said, the project will decongest one of the busiest stretches on the Patna rail corridor and create room for a large increase in both passenger and freight services. The Ministry said that in the first year of operation, the project will allow 22 additional passenger trains and 18 additional freight trains every day.

In Jammu and Kashmir, the second labourer injured in the terrorist attack in the Kelam area of Kulgam district succumbed to his injuries this morning, taking the death toll to two. A senior police official said, the injured person succumbed to his injuries during treatment at a hospital in Srinagar.

The two labourers working at a brick kiln were fired upon by unidentified terrorists last evening. Soon after the incident, the area was cordoned off and a massive search operation was underway to trace the terrorists.

Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha and Chief Minister Omar Abdullah have strongly condemned the killing of the labourers and offered condolences to the families of the deceased. Mr Sinha has directed the security forces to intensify operations in the area to neutralise the terrorists involved in the attack.

Mr Abdullah announced an ex-gratia of 10 lakh rupees each from the Chief Minister’s Relief Fund for the next of kin of the deceased, in addition to 6 lakh rupees immediate assistance to be provided by the Kulgam District Administration.

The Election Commission has revised the schedule for the Special Intensive Revision of the electoral rolls in Telangana. It said, the revision of the schedule has been carried out following a request from the Chief Electoral Officer of the state. According to the revised schedule, Booth Level Officers will conduct house-to-house verification till the 10th of this month. Rationalisation and re-arrangement of polling stations will also be completed by the same date. The poll body said that the draft electoral roll will now be published on the 17th August.

The period for filing claims and objections will continue from the 17th of August to the 16th of September, while their disposal will be completed by the 15th of October. The final electoral roll will be published on the 19th of October.

The Election Commission has extended the last date for filing claims and objections under the Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls in Odisha to the 19th of this month. The deadline for the notice phase and disposal of claims and objections has also been extended to the 17th of next month.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah has approved the advance release of 2 thousand 117.85 crore rupees from the Central share of the State Disaster Response Fund (SDRF) to support the States affected by floods during the on-going southwest monsoon. We have a report:

“The assistance approved for the affected States includes 44.55 crore rupees to Arunachal Pradesh as advance release of the first instalment for the financial year 2026-27. For Assam 379.35 crore rupees has been sanctioned as first instalment, while Gujarat has been approved 5 hundred crore rupees. Himachal Pradesh, which had already received the first instalment, has been sanctioned 193.95 crore rupees as advance release of the second instalment, while Odisha has been sanctioned the second instalment of 5 hundred crore rupees. Further, Maharashtra has been approved 5 hundred crore as advance release of the first instalment.

The Home Ministry noted that since the onset of the monsoon, the Government has been continuously monitoring the flood situation in close coordination with the State Governments. It added that the Government has also extended all possible assistance, including pre-deployment of National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) teams and logistical support from the Ministry of Defence, wherever required. Inter-Ministerial Central Teams (IMCTs) have already been deputed to Arunachal Pradesh and Assam to assess the damages.
