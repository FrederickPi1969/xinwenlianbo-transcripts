## Morning News

President Droupadi Murmu will present the 72nd National Film Awards at Ekta Nagar in Kevadia, Gujarat this evening. Instituted in 1954, these awards represent India’s highest cinematic honours, recognizing artistic and technical excellence across various categories, namely Feature, Non-Feature, and Writing on Cinema. As part of a major policy initiative by the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting, the awards are being held outside the national capital. The ceremony at Ekta Nagar brings together cinematic talent from across the country, embodying the spirit of ‘Ek Bharat, Shreshtha Bharat’. The president will also honour the veteran actor Anant Nag with the Dadasaheb Phalke Award for 2024. A report from our correspondent:

“The stage is set and the lights are ready in the iconic Ekta Nagar as Kevadia prepares to host the historic 72nd National Film Awards for the very first time. Renowned actors, acclaimed filmmakers, and technicians from across the country have started arriving at the venue for this grand celebration of Indian cinema. The ceremony honours outstanding cinematic achievements for the year 2024. Among the major feature film announcements, Article 370 was selected for Best Feature Film, with Yami Gautam winning Best Actress for her role in the film.

The Best Actor honour is shared by Mammootty for Bramayugam and Kartik Aaryan for Chandu Champion. Rajkumar Periasamy earned Best Direction for Amaran, while Kalki 2898 AD was named Best Popular Film Providing Wholesome Entertainment. Randeep Hooda won Best Debut Director for Swatantrya Veer Savarkar. In the non-feature category, the Marathi film Bhangaar was named Best Non-Feature Film, while Director Aanand L Rai was selected for Best Direction for Statue of Unity – Ekta ka Prateek. Ravi Raj Murmu took the Best Debut Director (Non-Feature) honour for the Santhali film, Angen. With Chirag Bhoraniya from Ekta Nagar, Aparna Khunt, Akashvani News, Ahmedabad.”

Union Home Minister Amit Shah will inaugurate the Third National Conference of Anti-Narcotics Task Force (ANTF) Heads of States and Union Territories in New Delhi today. The Ministry of Home Affairs has informed that during the event Mr Shah will also lay the foundation stones for the new complexes of Narcotics Control Bureau Zones in Lucknow and Dehradun. A Report:

“The two day conference based on the theme ‘United Resolve, Shared Responsibility, will focus on the progress of targets assigned to the ANTF and initiatives undertaken in combating the menace of drug trafficking and substance abuse across the country. During the event, Mr Shah will also release Television Commercials to spread awareness against drug abuse. The Ministry of Home Affairs said that the heads of Anti-Narcotics Task Forces from 36 states and Union Territories will participate in this conference. This discussion will help the participating states, departments, and agencies to move forward with a renewed commitment towards effectively combating drug trafficking across the country over the next three years. The national event will also seek to further deepen a future-ready partnership between Narcotics Control Bureau and Anti-Narcotics Task Force to combat drug abuse and realise the vision of a Drug-Free India. Saumya Sharan, for Akashavni News, Delhi.”

The Election Commission plans to increase the number of tribunals in West Bengal to ensure speedy disposal of applications from people whose names have been excluded from the electoral rolls during the SIR process. The state currently has 19 tribunals. The Commission is expected to tell the Supreme Court today that the number should be increased to at least 42, with one tribunal for each Lok Sabha constituency in the state. The Supreme Court is scheduled to hear 13 petitions today relating to the Special Intensive Revision, or SIR, of electoral rolls. The Commission is likely to apprise the court of the need for more tribunals during the hearing.

External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar held talks with his counterparts from France, Ecuador, Slovakia and Switzerland on the sidelines of UN General Assembly in New York last night and discussed regional and global issues. Mr Jaishankar arrived in New York City  on Sunday, leading the Indian delegation for the 81st session of the UN General Assembly. Mr Jaishankar will address world leaders at the General Debate from the UNGA podium on September 26.

His engagements, commenced with a meeting with French Foreign Minister Jean-Noel Barrot. In a social media post Mr Jaishankar said the regular meetings and telecons enable both countries to maintain the momentum of Special Global Strategic Partnership.

Karam festival, a vibrant celebration of Jharkhand’s rich tribal culture, nature and the bond between brothers and sisters, is being celebrated across the state today with traditional fervour and enthusiasm. Special cultural and state-level programmes are being organised across Jharkhand to mark the auspicious occasion. Here is a report from our correspondent:

“Karam festival, one of the most significant celebrations of Jharkhand’s folk culture and nature worship, is being celebrated across the state today with great enthusiasm. Governor Santosh Kumar Gangwar attended the program on the eve of Karam festival organised in Bero, Ranchi, as the chief guest and extended greetings to the people of the state. He also delivered a message on nature conservation. Yesterday, a seminar-cum-cultural programme was organised at Audrey House in Ranchi where the entire premises came alive with Jharkhand’s cultural spirit to the beats of drums, nagadas and mandar, accompanied by traditional Karam songs. A programme was also organised yesterday evening at the Deekshant Mandap in Morabadi, where a tribal fashion show was the main attraction. Karam festival is considered a symbol of crop protection, respect for nature, and the enduring bond between brothers and sisters. On this occasion, sisters observe a Nirjala fast, praying for the well-being and long life of their brothers. Mahvish Rahman, Akashvani News, Ranchi.”

## Midday News

Vice President C P Radhakrishnan today inaugurated 9th India International Water Week 2026 in New Delhi. Addressing on the occasion, he stressed upon the precise usage of water and highlighted that it should be conserved. He underlined that Water week is a significant platform to bring all stakeholder to deliberate upon processing challenges of water management. He said that no single institution, sector or State can address the challenges related to water in isolation and called upon a greater cooperation and a coordinated approach.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah today inaugurated the 3rd National Conference of Anti-Narcotics Task Force Heads of States and UTs in New Delhi. The two day conference serves as a powerful platform to safeguard future generations from the menace of drugs. Heads of ANTFs from 36 States and Union Territories are attending the conference, being organised by the Narcotics Control Bureau. Based on the theme of the United Resolve, Shared Responsibility, the conference is focussing on progress of targets assigned to State and UTs ANTFs in recently released Vision Document on Narcotics Control (2026-29). During the conference, deliberations will also focus on identifying and adopting appropriate strategies to further strengthen the Central Government’s measures for supply reduction, demand reduction, and harm reduction.

Government has urged bank employees to refrain from resorting to strikes and work towards resolving issues through dialogue, and ensure that banking services remain uninterrupted. The appeal comes ahead of a three-day nationwide strike called by the United Forum of Bank Unions and certain other Unions and Associations in the Public Sector Banks from 28th to 30th September. They have called a  strike in support of two principal demands – implementation of five-day banking, and withdrawal of the Performance Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme.

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has forecast Heavy to Very Heavy Rainfall at isolated places over Coastal Andhra Pradesh and Odisha during the day. Assam, Meghalaya, Chhattisgarh, Gangetic West Bengal and Jharkhand will experience heavy rainfall.

Odisha has stepped up preparedness, with the IMD issuing warnings for extremely heavy rainfall, while schools and Anganwadi centres in many districts have been closed as a precaution.

“The IMD has forecast extremely heavy rainfall at isolated places of Odisha tomorrow and the day after, with very heavy rain today and on this Friday. Thunderstorms, lightning and squally winds are also likely during this period under the influence of the deep depression. The IMD has also issued an Orange warning for vulnerable districts and warned of squally winds along the south Odisha coast. The State Government has kept shelters, rescue teams and machinery ready and directed district administrations to closely monitor vulnerable areas. All schools and Anganwadi centres in several districts have been ordered closed from today for three days as a precaution. Fishermen have also been advised not to venture into the sea. Prakash Dash for Akashvani News, Bhubaneswar.”

Heavy rain will continue in Madhya Pradesh for the next few days. The weather department has issued an alert for several districts.

“A fresh spell of heavy rain will begin in Madhya Pradesh from the 23 of September. According to the weather department, the system will be more active on the 24 and 25th of September. In the next two days, heavy to very heavy rain is likely in seventeen districts. Jabalpur, Rewa, Shahdol and Sagar divisions will be affected the most. In Women’s cricket, India thrashed Sri Lanka to win gold medal in the final in Asian Games 2026 at the Aichi Prefectural Korogi Sports Park, Japan. Smriti made this record during the Asian Games final against Sri Lanka at Korogi Sports Park in Nisshin. Mandhana smashed three sixes and three fours in the first four overs to get India off to a blistering start.  She surpassed former Indian captain Mithali Raj’s record of 10,868 runs across all formats.
