## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi and his Japanese counterpart Sanae Takaichi have unequivocally and strongly condemned terrorism and violent extremism in all its forms and manifestations, including cross-border terrorism from Pakistan. The 16th India-Japan Annual Summit Joint Statement said, both leaders condemned in the strongest terms the terrorist attack in Pahalgam, Jammu and Kashmir on 22nd April last year. The leaders also took note of the United Nations Security Council Monitoring Team Report of 29th July 2025 mentioning The Resistance Front.

“Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi also strongly condemned the terrorist attack in Delhi on 10th November last year. The two leaders called for the perpetrators, organisers and financiers of the reprehensible act to be brought to justice without delay. They also called for concerted action against all UN-listed terrorist groups and entities, including Al Qaeda, ISIS (Daesh), Lashkar-e-Tayyiba, Jaish-e-Mohammad and their proxies. The two Prime Ministers stressed the need for resolute action to eliminate terrorists’ safe havens, dismantle terror financing networks and their nexus with transnational crime, and halt the cross-border movement of terrorists. Towards the shared goal of a resilient and prosperous Indo-Pacific, the two Prime Ministers reiterated their commitment to advancing cooperation among like-minded countries. They welcomed the steady progress under the Quad framework and reaffirmed their commitment to enhancing practical cooperation. SAKLEN AKHTAR FOR AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI”.

India and Japan have reaffirmed their shared commitment to the salience of economic security as one of the foundational pillars of the India-Japan Special Strategic and Global Partnership. The India-Japan Joint Declaration on Economic Security Cooperation said, the economic security partnership is underpinned by mutual trust, shared values, and aligned interests and is a recognition that the prosperity, security, and economic futures of the two sides are deeply intertwined. It added that this declaration is a recognition that the synergy between the updated Free and Open Indo-Pacific and the Mutual and Holistic Advancement for Security and Growth across Regions (MAHASAGAR) initiative provides both sides with the required impetus to furthering economic security cooperation.

Election Commission of India (ECI) has sought responses from rival factions of All India Trinamool Congress (AITC) regarding competing claims about the party’s organisational elections and authorised signatories.

“The Commission said in a statement that it has written separate letters to Mamata Banerjee and party leader Ritabrata Banerjee regarding the claims and counter claims by both factions with respect to organisational elections and authorised signatories of the AITC. The Commission said both sides have been directed to submit their responses by 5.30 pm on Monday. The development comes amid a dispute within the Trinamool Congress over the party’s organisational structure and the authority to act on its behalf before the Election Commission. Such disputes are governed by the Election Symbols (Reservation and Allotment) Order, under which the Commission examines rival claims, supporting documents and submissions from the contending factions before taking a decision, where necessary. The ECI has not expressed any view on the merits of the rival claims and has sought written responses from both factions before proceeding further in the matter. CHIRAG JHA, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

The annual Shri Amarnath Yatra began this morning from both Pahalgam and Baltal routes in Jammu and Kashmir. Lakhs of devotees from across India are expected to undertake the sacred journey to the Amarnath cave shrine during the 57-day long pilgrimage. Pilgrims travel to the holy Amarnath Cave, located at an altitude of nearly 3,888 metres, to seek the blessings of Lord Shiva and witness the naturally formed ice Shivling. The Yatra will culminate on August 28, coinciding with the festival of Raksha Bandhan. The first batch of pilgrims, comprising more than 4,800 people, including women and children, left for Amarnath cave shrine from twin base camps of Nunwan and Baltal this morning. More from our correspondent:

”Amid chants of Bam Bam Bhole and Har Har Mahadev, thousands of Yatris embarked on a tough but spiritually enriching journey to the 3,880-metre-high cave shrine this morning. During the 57-day pilgrimage, lakhs of devotees are expected to take up the 48-kilometre route from Nunwan-Pahalgam in Anantnag district, and the shorter but steeper 14-kilometre route from Baltal in Ganderbal district to pay obeisance at the holy shrine. Along the twin routes, authorities have put in place comprehensive facilities including medical camps, resting points, langars, drinking water stations, and real-time weather monitoring. Meanwhile, the second batch of Yatris left Jammu this morning and is expected to reach the valley later this afternoon. Ishfaq Shah, Akashvani News, Srinagar.”

In Ukraine, 27 people have been killed and 90 others were injured in massive drone and missile strikes by Russia on capital Kyiv. Damage was recorded in 30 locations across Kyiv, mainly residential buildings and civilian infrastructure. Russia’s Defense Ministry said in a statement the deadly bombardment on Wednesday night was in response to Ukraine’s long-range strikes on its civilian infrastructure. Ukraine’s increasingly frequent and large-scale attacks on Russia, described by President Volodymyr Zelenskyy as a 40-day blitz, have primarily targeted oil refineries, triggering a fuel crisis in Russia more than four years after the war between Kyiv and Moscow began.

In Thailand, nine monks were killed yesterday when an 11-year-old boy crashed a pickup truck into a group of monks on a pilgrimage walk in northeastern Mukdahan province. Officials said a total of 35 monks from Mukdahan province were on the pilgrimage. Five monks were killed at the scene, while four others died at a hospital.

In Syria, an explosive device detonated in a Damascus cafe yesterday, killing at least nine people. Interior Ministry reported the device was planted inside the cafe, located 100 meters from the Palace of Justice in the capital’s Hejaz district.

Gujarat ATS has arrested eight people associated with the banned terrorist organization Jaish-e-Mohammed. Banaskantha, Navsari and Patan police of Gujarat and Dewas police in MP made the arrests after joint raids in these districts.

According to the ATS all the accused were in touch with Pakistani handlers Abdullah and Mohammad Umar. They were also working to translate the literature of the banned terrorist organization Jaish-e-Mohammed into Gujarati and spreading its ideology. A total of 254 pieces of literature, including the flag of Jaish-e-Mohammed, were recovered from the accused.
