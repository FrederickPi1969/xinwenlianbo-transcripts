India marks 10th anniversary of surgical strikes that demonstrated to the world a new example of Narendra Modi government’s counter-terrorism policy. On September 28-29, 2016, Indian Army carried out targeted surgical strikes on terrorist launch pads across the Line of Control (LoC) in Pakistan-occupied Jammu Kashmir (PoJK). The operation was launched as a direct and decisive military response to the Uri terror attack earlier that month, where 19 Indian soldiers lost their lives. The anniversary highlights a decade of evolved national security measures.

## Midday News

The Supreme Court today refused to stay the Centre’s decision to impose Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) on specified person-to-merchant UPI transactions above two thousands rupee, with the charges set to come into effect from the 15th of next month. A bench comprising Chief Justice of India Surya Kant and Justice Joymalya Bagchi and Justice V. Mohana sought responses from the Centre, the Reserve Bank of India and other respondents, directing them to file their counter-affidavits within four weeks. The apex court observed that the issue is less legal and more technical.

Appearing for the Centre, Additional Solicitor General N. Venkataraman, told the bench that 96 per cent of users of the payment gateway were remain exempt. The public interest litigation (PIL) challenging the move was filed by advocate Anjan Datta. Earlier, the Centre had announced its decision to impose MDR on specified person-to-merchant UPI transactions above two thousands rupee.

The Supreme Court today took suo motu cognisance of the alleged gang-rape of a minor in a moving bus in the Delhi-NCR region, observing that such cases underline a systematic failure on the part of law enforcement authorities. A bench comprising Justice J.B. Pardiwala and Justice K. Vinod Chandran observed that public spaces, including parks and buses, cannot be allowed to turn into high-risk zones because of inadequate lighting, poor surveillance and the lack of other essential administrative facilities. Last month, a 16-year-old girl, who boarded a sleeper bus in Greater Noida, was allegedly gang-raped by its conductor and driver and abandoned near Kashmere Gate, Delhi. The two accused have been arrested.

## Evening News

Union Home Minister Amit Shah today inaugurated the Conference on ‘PRAHAAR – National CT Policy and Strategy’ in New Delhi. The Conference deliberated upon the seven pillars of PRAHAAR including Prevention, Response, Aggregating Internal Capacities, Human Rights, Attenuating Conditions for Terrorism, Aligning International Efforts and Recovery and Resilience through a whole-of-society approach.

Speaking on the occasion, the Home Minister said, the Government has adopted a ‘zero-tolerance’ policy against all forms and manifestations of terrorism. He highlighted that from 2019 to 2026, the Narendra Modi government successfully brought back 288 fugitive criminals to India from 36 countries. Mr Shah said, as a result of better coordination among agencies, more than 40 modules of Shahzad Bhatti network involved in cross-border smuggling of weapons, explosives and narcotics were busted, more than 100 FIR’s were registered and 350 individuals were arrested across 15 states. He said, at the same time, the government declared Shahzad Bhatti network a terrorist organisation under Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act – UAPA.

Mr Shah also launched a new Extradition Portal, which will synergise and expedite the efforts to bring back fugitives from abroad.

India and the UAE have agreed to take steps for the timely implementation of initiatives relating to settlement of trade in local currency, integration of payment and messaging systems, and central-bank digital currencies. The two sides reached the agreement during the 14th meeting of the India-UAE High Level Joint Task Force on Investments in Mumbai today. It was co-chaired by Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal and Managing Director of Abu Dhabi Investment Authority (ADIA) Sheikh Hamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan.

Addressing media after the meeting, Mr Goyal said, India has set a target of raising its total exports of goods and services to one trillion dollars this year, from 863 billion dollars last year. He said, the two countries aim to take bilateral trade to 200 billion dollars by 2032.

The Centre will dispatch teams to Karnataka and Maharashtra to assess the drought situation in the two states. He said, a Central team will be sent to Karnataka on 3rd October to assess the drought situation.
