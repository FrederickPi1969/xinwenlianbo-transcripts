Government has invited fresh applications under the Production Linked Incentive Scheme for Promoting Domestic Manufacturing of Medical Devices (PLI-MD), which has now been extended up to the year 2035-36. An outlay of approximately two thousand 700 crore rupees available under the scheme will be utilized for the purpose. The applicants can submit applications through an online portal, which will be operational in the next 10 days. The Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers said that on the basis of learnings in the first phase of the Scheme, a number of simplifications have been done in the eligibility conditions as well as procedure for claiming incentive by the applicants.

The applicants shall be allowed three years for setting up their manufacturing facilities in any of the four Target Segments (TS) which are medical consumables, disposables and implants, Critical components, high-end medical devices which are presently at a nascent stage of domestic manufacturing and high-end medical devices that are presently only assembled in the country.

The US House of Representatives has rejected a resolution seeking the impeachment of Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth over his conduct in office and handling of the ongoing war in Iran. The resolution, moved by Republican Congressman Thomas Massie of Kentucky and supported by Democrats, was defeated last night after President Donald Trump expressed strong support for the Defence Secretary. Most Republican lawmakers stood by Hegseth.

The vote comes amid growing political challenges for the Republican Party following significant losses in the midterm elections. The war in Iran and the resulting rise in fuel prices have emerged as major issues of concern within the party.

The rejection of the resolution may, however, provide only temporary relief to Hegseth, as Democrats are expected to gain control of both chambers of Congress in January, potentially renewing efforts to hold the Defence Secretary accountable.

Union Health Minister J P Nadda today addressed the inaugural BIMSTEC Health Ministers’ Meeting in Colombo highlighting India’s progress towards Universal Health Coverage through Ayushman Bharat, strengthened public health infrastructure and digital healthcare initiatives.

Emphasizing regional solidarity, the Minister called for enhanced cooperation in health security, pandemic preparedness, One Health and traditional medicine, while reaffirming India’s readiness to share its expertise and best practices with BIMSTEC member nations.

He also highlighted India’s commitment to hosting the BIMSTEC Centre of Excellence in Traditional Medicine at Jamnagar, Gujarat, to promote joint research, capacity building and knowledge exchange.Guided by PM Modi’s vision of ‘One Earth, One Family, One Future’, India remains committed to building a healthier, more inclusive and resilient BIMSTEC region.

Established in 1997, BIMSTEC (Bay of Bengal Initiative for Multi-Sectoral Technical and Economic Cooperation) is a regional grouping of seven countries – India, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Bhutan, Nepal, Myanmar and Thailand – aimed at strengthening cooperation between South and Southeast Asia.
