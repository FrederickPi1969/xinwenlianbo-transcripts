## Morning News

The Government has approved the an Incentive Scheme for Promotion of Domestic PNG Connections, aimed at bringing clean, safe and affordable piped cooking gas to households across the country. The scheme will be effective from 1st of next month. The Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas said that the scheme is designed to fast-track the expansion of active PNG connections ensuring that more families can start using piped natural gas in their kitchens at the earliest. Currently, there are 1.74 crore Domestic PNG connections across the country. The Ministry added the new Scheme directly incentivises the City Gas Distribution companies to actively convert unbilled connections into working gas connections, and expand the PNG network to new areas. It said that Piped Natural Gas offers households a safer, cleaner and more convenient alternative to LPG cylinders and traditional cooking fuels. The Scheme will be implemented over two Tranches spanning six months.

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has revamped its examination team by removing 6 hundred experts and on boarding new experts. In a social media post, Ministry of Education said that a four-tier question paper checking system will be put in place by NTA to strengthen the scrutiny of examination papers. The NTA will also induct 20-25 officials in the next two to three weeks. Further, NTA offices will be shifted to the new premises, where Security will be reinforced with CISF teams.

Jharkhand government last night released a list of 44 recruitment exams that have been cancelled due to irregularities, amid students’ protests over the issue. Here is a report:

“The Jharkhand government has cancelled 44 recruitment examinations conducted by JPSC, JSSC and outsourced agencies, including the JSSC-CGL and examinations conducted by TDPL. The cancelled examinations include the JPSC 11th to 13th Civil Services examinations, CDPO, Drug Inspector, Civil Judge, Assistant Conservator of Forest, Forest Range Officer, Assistant Professor and other recruitment examinations. The government has also cancelled examination results where the preliminary work was conducted by TDPL, even if another agency subsequently handled the process. The notifications state that the JSSC-CGL examination was cancelled based on findings emerging from the SIT probe into alleged irregularities and the ongoing CID investigation. The government has also decided to postpone six examinations and order CID investigations into 17 examinations. A fast-track court will be sought from the High Court for cases related to alleged irregularities in JPSC and JSSC examinations. Internal vigilance cells will also be constituted in both commissions, while a committee headed by senior IAS officer Amitabh Kaushal will recommend reforms in the examination system. The committee is expected to submit its report to the government within two months. The decisions follow a 25-day student agitation demanding cancellation of disputed examinations and investigation into alleged irregularities, with students earlier announcing a proposed protest outside the Chief Minister’s residence on August 20. While students had welcomed the cancellation of the JSSC-CGL and other examinations, they have maintained that their demand for a CBI probe must also be addressed. Mahvish Rahman, Akashvani News, Ranchi.”

Israel has carried out air strikes on the Abu Duhur air base in Syria’s northwestern Idlib province, saying the action was aimed at preventing the deployment of Turkish troops at the base. The Supreme Court today reiterated the need to institutionalise the National Testing Agency (NTA), with secure infrastructure, dedicated testing centres, cyber-security systems, institutional expertise and a permanent mechanism for examination-related operations to prevent future paper leaks. While hearing pleas concerning the NEET-UG 2026 paper leak, the Court directed the Union Secretary for the Ministry of Education to file an affidavit setting out the steps being taken to implement the Radhakrishnan Committee’s recommendations. The Court said its objective was to ensure that the country has a robust examination institution capable of retaining expertise and adapting to technological changes.

Solicitor General appearing for the Centre and NTA, explained the safeguards currently in place for the preparation, printing, transportation and distribution of question papers. The K. Radhakrishnan Committee was constituted under the chairmanship of former ISRO chairman Dr K. Radhakrishnan after the NEET-UG 2024 paper leak controversy. It submitted its report to the Ministry of Education in October 2024, making 101 recommendations to overhaul the NTA and strengthen the security and integrity of public examinations.

The apex court  issued notices to the Ministry of Home Affairs and the commission. The petition sought directions to the Centre and all the states and union territories to create a centralised system, backed by statutory safeguards, for DNA and biometric identification of the missing and rescued children.

The ED said the searches were carried out under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002. It added the investigation relates to the alleged diversion of government contract funds through private contractors and their subsequent use, including for creation of assets for the University or the Trust. The Lucknow-based Maulana Mohammad Ali Jauhar Trust, of which former Uttar Pradesh Cabinet minister Azam Khan is the main trustee along with family members and associates, runs the Muhammad Ali Jauhar University and a public school in Rampur district. The ED said that the searches were aimed at tracing the alleged proceeds of crime and recovering financial records, documents and digital evidence related to the diversion and utilisation of government funds.

The Union Cabinet today approved five projects worth over 13 thousand crore rupees including, four railway and one highway project. Briefing media after the meeting in New Delhi today, Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said, the Union Cabinet, under the leadership of Prime Minister Narendra Modi, has cleared four multitracking projects of Railway covering eight districts across four states of West Bengal, Odisha, Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh.
