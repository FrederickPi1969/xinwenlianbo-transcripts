## Morning News

President Droupadi Murmu will be on a three day visit to Gujarat beginning today. She will preside over the presentation ceremony of the 72nd National Film Awards at Ekta Nagar, Kevadia tomorrow. More from our correspondent:

National Film Awards, recognising achievements in Indian cinema for the year 2024, will be presented by the President tomorrow. Instituted in 1954, the National Film Awards are among the country’s highest honours for excellence in cinema. Presented annually by the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting, the awards recognise creative and technical achievements across feature films, non-feature films and writing on cinema. The winners of the 72nd edition were announced on July 18, 2026, by the chairpersons of the three juries. The awards are divided into three main sections – Feature Films, Non-Feature Films and Best Writing on Cinema – along with the Dadasaheb Phalke Award, the highest honour in Indian cinema. ESHIKA SHAW, FROM AKASHVANI NEWS.

External Affairs Minister Dr. S. Jaishankar will lead the Indian delegation for the High Level Week at the 81st Session of the United Nations General Assembly (UNGA) in New York, USA. The External Affairs Minister is scheduled to address the High Level Session of the 81st UNGA on 26th of this month. During the visit, Dr Jaishankar will also attend various multilateral and bilateral meetings. More from our correspondent:

Dr Jaishankar’s visit to New York comes as world leaders prepare to gather at the UN headquarters for the annual high-level General Debate, scheduled from September 22 to 28. The 81st session of the UN General Assembly is being held under the theme “Restoring trust, managing transformation: A United Nations that delivers for all.” The General Assembly will bring together presidents, prime ministers and senior representatives from all 193 UN member states, who will outline their priorities and positions on major international issues. UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres has called on world leaders to strengthen international cooperation as they gather in New York for the General Assembly. In a video message, Guterres said, “Come together for peace, dignity, justice, and humanity. Only with international cooperation we can build a better world. Guterres is attending his final UN General Assembly as Secretary-General, with his tenure set to conclude later this year. ASHISH ABRAHAM, FOR AKASHVANI NEWS.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi is completing 25 years in public service. His journey is guided by the principle of “Jan Seva Se Desh Seva” and a continued commitment to public service. Inspired by this vision of service and good governance, the government is working towards the goal of a developed India by 2047. This commitment, through inclusive development and social empowerment, is bringing about a transformation in people’s lives. In this context, we bring you a report on the PM-KISAN scheme.

“In Madhya Pradesh, a large number of farmers are benefiting from the PM-KISAN scheme. Under the scheme, eligible farmers receive financial assistance of six thousand rupees every year in three equal installments, directly into their bank accounts. This support is useful for farmers to meet their needs for fertilizers, seeds and other farming activities. Prime Minister Narendra Modi said that the government is continuously working keeping the needs of farmers in mind.

The scheme was started in 2019. So far, more than four lakh 47 thousand crore rupees have been transferred to farmers through 23 installments. More than nine crore 49 lakh farmers benefited from the 23rd installment.The Central Government has now approved the continuation of the PM-KISAN scheme from 2026-27 to 2030-31. Chhindwara farmer Basant Patel said that farmers are happy with the decision to continue the scheme for five more years.

Women farmers have also benefited from the scheme. The scheme uses digital verification, Aadhaar authentication and Direct Benefit Transfer to send the money directly to farmers. Vipin Mishra, Akashvani News, Bhopal.”

After the wait of centuries, the construction of the grand Ram Temple has ushered in a new chapter for Ayodhya, the city of Lord Ram, placing it prominently on the global map of religious tourism and development. With the efforts of the Central and State governments, Ayodhya has witnessed new era of development and has once again emerged as a major global centre of Sanatan Dharma. A report:

The transformation of Ayodhya, the city of Lord Ram, gained momentum after the change of government at the Centre in 2014 and the formation of the Yogi Adityanath government in Uttar Pradesh in 2017. While the Supreme Court verdict paved the way for the construction of the grand Ram Temple, initiatives such as the Deepotsav celebrations also began restoring Ayodhya’s lost cultural and religious grandeur.

Following the consecration of Ram Lalla in Ayodhya in the presence of Prime Minister Narendra Modi, the city has increasingly emerged on the global stage as a symbol of religious tourism, Sanatan culture and development.

It was from this very city of Lord Ram that Prime Minister Narendra Modi called upon the people to awaken the values of Lord Ram within themselves if India is to become a developed nation by 2047.

Mayor of Ayodhya Municipal Corporation Girish Pati Tripathi said that following the construction of the Ram Temple, Ayodhya has witnessed significant changes in the cultural, social and economic areas in line with Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s vision.

Today, Ayodhya is not merely a centre of faith, but is also setting new benchmarks in religious tourism, connectivity and employment opportunities. Sushil Chandra Tiwari, Akashvani News, Lucknow.

US President Donald Trump has said that he is in deciding mode over the Iran war, warning that big things are going to happen. His remarks came after Tehran said it has shared its conditions with Washington, through mediators, for reopening the Strait of Hormuz. Trump’s comments come ahead of Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian’s expected visit to New York to attend the Uited Nations General Assembly. A senior delegation is likely to accompany him. Trump told a TV channel that very big things are going to be happening in the not-so-distant future if the Iranian regime does not behave. He reportedly discussed three possible options for the war – “wiping Iran out,” “letting them rot economically”, or “making a deal.”

The United States has issued a security alert for its mission in Saudi Arabia, requiring US government employees working in the country to obtain special authorisation for all official and personal travel to the cities of Taif and Yanbu. US Mission in Saudi Arabia said the restrictions were imposed “due to the ongoing security situation” and urged American citizens in the kingdom to remain vigilant and follow recommended security measures. The mission said, regular and emergency consular services remained available for US citizens. The alert comes after Yemen’s Houthi group launched attacks towards Saudi Arabia, including a ballistic missile directed at Riyadh.

Union Minister of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare Shivraj Singh Chauhan will flag off the fourth phase of the Nasha Mukt Punjab Yatra from Talwandi Sabo in Bathinda, today. He will be accompanied by Haryana Chief Minister, Nayab Singh Saini.

The Yatra is focusing on three major challenges presently confronting Punjab. These are growing drug menace, deteriorating law and order, and corruption at every level of governance.

Yesterday, Union Minister Arjun Ram Meghwal while addressing the gathering at Fazilka from where he flagged off the third phase of the Nasha Mukt Punjab Yatra, said that it is not a political campaign but an initiative for social reform to make the state Viksit Punjab. He said it should not be viewed through a political lens.

India Meteorological Department (IMD) has forecast heavy rainfall in Chhattisgarh, Coastal Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, Karnataka and Madhya Maharashtra today. Weather Agency said that conditions are favourable for further withdrawal of Southwest Monsoon from some more parts of Rajasthan and some parts of Punjab, Saurashtra, Kutch during the next two days.

Indian men’s chess team secured an emphatic 3-1 victory over Holland, while the Indian women’s team suffered a shocking defeat to Poland in the fifth round of the 46th Chess Olympiad in Samarkand, Uzbekistan. The Indian men extended their perfect run to five consecutive match wins, placing them at the top of the leaderboards alongside Uzbekistan and Germany.

R Praggnanandhaa bounced back from a string of draws to score a brilliant victory over an in-form Anish Giri. Arjun Erigaisi fought hard to secure a draw against Jorden Van Foreest. Nihal Sarin comfortably held a draw against veteran Loek Van Wely. D Gukesh continued his sublime form by defeating Erwin l’Ami, picking up his fourth individual win in five games.

In the Asian Games, India has clinched third silver medal in the men’s 10m air rifle team event in Nagoya, Japan. Indian shooters Rudrankksh Patil and Himanshu Dhillon also qualifying for the individual final.

The silver added to India’s medal tally in shooting, which had begun with another second-place finish in the women’s 10m air rifle team.

Earlier, Elavenil Valarivan, Vidarsa Vinod and Sonam Maskar won silver in the women’s 10-metre air rifle team event, while Elavenil also claimed silver in the Women’s 10m Air Rifle Individual event, finishing just behind Japan’s Hinata Taichi.

In a historic first, Angh, directed by Naga filmmaker Theja Rio, has won the Platform Prize 2026 at the Toronto International Film Festival, TIFF. Angh was one of 12 films selected for this year’s Platform programme, TIFF’s only competitive section.Beginning with the 2026 prize, winning the TIFF Platform Prize gives a non-English-language film a direct pathway to eligibility for consideration for the Academy Award for Best International Feature Film. Announcing the award, Jury President Pablo Trapero praised Angh for its refreshing perspective, strength of filmmaking and deeply resonant portrait of a world.

Union Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal today said that the free trade agreement between India and New Zealand come into force on 20th of next month on the occasion of Dussehra festival. Addressing a conference in New Delhi, Mr Goyal said that the strategic partnership reflects the shared ambition of both the countries to deepen the cooperation and includes the goal of doubling bilateral trade to almost 35 thousand crore rupees in the next four to five years.

Free Trade Agreement  will open new opportunities for Indian exports including textiles and apparel, leather and footwear, engineering goods, pharmaceuticals, agriculture and processed food products.  At the same time, New Zealand will gain enhanced and  access to the Indian market for products including wood, wool, sheep meat, and raw leather hides.

Minister of State for Health and Family Welfare Anupriya Patel today said that India’s journey in Pharmacovigilance has been a remarkable example of self reliance over the years. She said this while releasing the 7th Edition of National Formulary of India in New Delhi. The Minister said that currently more than one thousand one hundred Adverse Drug Reaction Monitoring Centres are operational in the country and the Government aims to increase this number up to the primary health facility level. She also said that 25 countries in the world recognize and accept the Indian pharmacopoeia and added that the country now provides pharmacovigilance data to WHO patient safety database. The Minister highlighted that India has risen from the 123rd rank in 2014 to 8th rank in providing this data to WHO.  India achieved this milestone in the year 2025.

The Department of Telecommunications has cautioned citizens against fraudulent issuance of SIM cards, misuse of telecom identifiers, or tampering with IMEI numbers. It has asked citizens not to share or transfer SIM cards issued in their name to others. The Department has also advised citizens to refrain from procuring SIM cards through fake documents. Communication Ministry has advised citizens to regularly verify their mobile connections through the Sanchar Saathi Portal and Mobile App.

The BJP today slammed the Congress over a recent remark by former Vice President Hamid Ansari. Addressing a press conference at the party headquarters, BJP spokesperson Sudhanshu Trivedi claimed that Mr Ansari had quoted a book written in 2019, which states that former Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru considered Hindu nationalism a greater threat than communism. Mr Trivedi alleged that the Congress today has moved towards the left and is deeply influenced by communist ideology. He added that the former Vice President’s remarks reflect the mindset of the Congress.
