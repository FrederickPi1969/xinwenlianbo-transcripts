## Morning News

BRICS leaders have condemned the April 2025 Pahalgam terrorist attack in Jammu and Kashmir. In a joint statement, BRICS leaders have called for zero tolerance towards terrorism, reaffirming the regional bloc’s commitment to combating terrorism in all its forms. The New Delhi Declaration adopted unanimously at the opening day of the summit said that all acts of terrorism are criminal and unjustifiable, regardless of their motivation and who committed such an act.

The New Delhi declaration condemned in the strongest terms the terrorist attack in Jammu and Kashmir on 22 April 2025, during which 26 people were killed and many more injured. The attack was later traced to terrorists backed and trained in Pakistan. The declaration also called for action against the cross-border movement of terrorists, terrorism financing, and safe havens. BRICS leaders also urged the member nations to ensure zero tolerance for terrorism and reject double standards in countering terrorism. They further called for concerted actions against all UN-designated terrorists and terrorist entities. The joint declaration also expressed deep concern over the continued escalation of tensions in West Asia and called for exercising maximum restraint as well as avoiding actions that could further aggravate the situation. It said, the BRICS countries will increase efforts, through dialogue and diplomacy, towards a long-term understanding that will contribute to lasting peace, security and stability in the region. BRICS leaders also stressed the need to work together to maintain smooth flow of global trade, supply chains and energy, in accordance with applicable international law. The BRICS bloc expressed concern about the ongoing conflicts in many parts of the world and the current state of polarisation and fragmentation in the international order. With Brics team, Saklen Akhtar, Akashvani News, Delhi.

In his remarks yesterday, Prime Minister Narendra Modi noted that the New Delhi Declaration provides a clear direction for the shared resolve of the member countries. He added that it is now their shared responsibility to translate these resolutions into tangible results.

Mr Modi has proposed a BRICS Continuity and Implementation Mechanism to ensure that the grouping’s institutional momentum is maintained despite the Chairship changing every year. The Prime Minister said the mechanism would enable follow-up of all initiatives and outcomes through the Troika.

India will get another weapon and missile testing range in Bengal’s Junput coastal village in Purba Medinipur district. West Bengal Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari has approved Defence Research and Development Organisation’s (DRDO) proposal to set up weapon and missile testing range in the state. The Chief Minister said, this will be a big project for West Bengal’s development. The proposed testing range in Junput will be the third major hub for DRDO after the Chandipur sites in Odisha and the proposed testing centre in Andhra.

India has one missile testing facility, known as the Integrated Test Range, which operates across two launch complexes in Odisha. The proposed Junput range, located approximately 70km along the Bay of Bengal from Chandipur, will share the workload of the Odisha testing range and will give a boost to the country’s defence testing capabilities.

In Nepal, the death toll from flash floods has risen to 1,386. According to the National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority, 104 of the recovered bodies have so far been identified and handed over to families. Around 5,130 people are still reported missing.

Security forces continue search and rescue operations. More than 13,700 people have been rescued so far, while 339 people are undergoing treatment in hospitals.

Meanwhile, over 3,700 people are staying in temporary shelters across Rasuwa, Nuwakot and Dhading districts.

The floods, triggered by an ice-rock avalanche near the Nepal-Tibet border on 26th of the last month, devastated towns and villages in northern and central Nepal.
