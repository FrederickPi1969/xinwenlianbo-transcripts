Renowned singer Suman Kalyanpur passed away at her residence in Mumbai yesterday. She was 89. Let’s dive into her melodious career with this report from our Correspondent:

“Born on 28 January 1937 in Dhaka, then part of British India and now in Bangladesh, Suman Kalyanpur made her Hindi film debut in the mid-1950s and went on to record hundreds of songs with legendary composers and singers, especially Mohammed Rafi. Her most popular songs include “Aajkal Tere Mere Pyaar Ke Charche”, “Na Na Karte Pyaar” and “Tumne Pukara Aur Hum Chale Aaye”. She also made a significant contribution to Marathi music. A notable aspect of Suman Kalyanpur’s career was that her voice closely resembled that of the legendary Lata Mangeshkar, leading many listeners to mistake her songs for Lata’s. Despite this comparison, Suman Kalyanpur established a distinct legacy of her own and is remembered for her melodious, refined singing style. With her passing, another golden chapter in Indian film music has come to a close. Ankita Apte, Akashvani News, Mumbai.”

The India-Oman Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA) has come into force today. Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal said, the CEPA will deepen the economic and strategic ties between the two nations. In an article published in a national daily, Mr Goyal said, the Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement immediately gives Indian exporters 100 percent duty-free market access in Oman to 98 per cent of tariff lines covering 99.38 per cent of exports. This marks a dramatic improvement over the pre-CEPA system that allowed only 15.3 per cent of India’s exports to enter Oman at zero duty. The Minister said that goods from India that currently face a 5 per cent import duty in Oman will become more competitive.

Mr Goyal added that the agreement will significantly benefit the country’s students, artisans, women, farmers, fishermen, and Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises by opening new markets, boosting exports, attracting investments, and accelerating job creation.

## Evening News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi and President of Myanmar U Min Aung Hlaing held detailed talks on the entire gamut of bilateral issues in New Delhi today. The discussions covered trade, defence, border management, culture among other areas of interest. Briefing media after the talks, Foreign Secretary Vikram Misri said both sides expressed interest in forging closer cooperation, particularly in the areas of trade and investment, development cooperation, health, education and energy. Foreign Secretary said Prime Minister reaffirmed India’s support for the sovereignty and territorial integrity of Myanmar and both sides underscored the importance of preventing the misuse of territory for activities inimical to their security interests.

Mr Misri said both sides underlined the importance of connectivity and agreed to work as quickly as possible towards the completion of Kaladan Multi Modal Transit Transport project.

President Droupadi Murmu has said, Myanmar is an important partner in India’s Neighbourhood First, Act East, and MAHASAGAR policies. Welcoming Myanmar President U Min Aung Hlaing at Rashtrapati Bhavan today, President Murmu said, both the countries share deep cultural, civilizational, and spiritual ties that go back to centuries. The President said, Myanmar is India’s gateway to Southeast Asia, as it is the only ASEAN country that shares a land border with India. Congratulating President Hlaing on assuming office as the President of Myanmar, President Murmu said that India looks forward to working closely with the new Government to strengthen the bilateral partnership further.

The India-Oman Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA) has come into force today. Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal said, the CEPA will deepen the economic and strategic ties between the two nations. Addressing media in New Delhi, Mr Goyal said that this partnership agreement is not simply a trade agreement, it is a statement of civilisational trust. The Union Minister informed that from day one, India will secure duty free access on 98 percent of Oman’s tariff lines covering over 99 percent of India’s export to Oman.

Mr.Goyal informed that multiple consignments are being shipped today from Mumbai, Kolkata and Chennai to mark the operationalization of the agreement.

Mr Goyal underlined that the India-Oman agreement is part of India’s nine Free Trade Agreements, covering 38 nations, finalized in the last three and a half years. He added, India is negotiating agreements with several countries including, Israel, Chile and Canada.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today shared an article written by Mr Goyal on the India-Oman Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement. Malaysia has become the latest country to impose age-based restrictions on social media use by children. So far, Australia, Indonesia and Malaysia have introduced restrictions on social media access for children, while Brazil has mandated stricter parental oversight and age-verification measures.
