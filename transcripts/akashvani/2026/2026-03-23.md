## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that the conflict in West Asia is an evolving situation and the entire world is affected in some form and in such a situation, adding that all efforts must be made to safeguard the citizens from the impact of this conflict. The Prime Minister said this while chairing the meeting of the Cabinet Committee on Security to review the situation and mitigating measures in the context of ongoing West Asia Conflict.

“During the meeting, the Cabinet Secretary Dr T V Somanathan gave a detailed presentation on the global situation and mitigating measures taken so far and being planned by all concerned Ministries and Departments. According to Prime Minister Office’s statement, the expected impact and measures taken to address it across sectors like agriculture, fertilisers, food security, petroleum, and other affected sectors were discussed in the meeting. The meeting also made a detailed assessment of availability for critical needs of the common man, including food, energy and fuel security. Furthermore short term, medium term and long term measures to ensure continued availability of essential needs were also discussed in the meeting.

The meeting, also involved discussion on several measures to diversify sources of imports required by chemicals, pharmaceuticals, petrochemicals and other industrial sectors. The PMO informed that  several measures proposed by different ministries will be prepared and implemented in the coming days after consultation with all stakeholders. Samarth Singh for Akashvani News Delhi.”

Iran has threatened to completely close Strait of Hormuz if US attacks Iranian power plants. In a statement, Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) said that the Strait of Hormuz will be completely closed and will not be opened until destroyed Iranian power plants are rebuilt. The IRGC’s statement comes after US President Donald Trump warned that if Iran does not open the Strait of Hormuz within 48 hours, America will hit and obliterate various Iranian power plants, starting with the biggest one first.

Moreover, the IRGC has announced the 74th wave of its Operation True Promise 4, saying it involved the use of heavy missiles targeting military and security sites in Tel Aviv, Petah Tikva, Holon and Ramat Gan.

“In Tehran, the tone is defiant and the threats are escalating. Iran’s Revolutionary Guard Corps announced the 74th wave of its Operation True Promise 4, describing it as involving super-heavy missiles and claiming strikes on military and security targets in Israel. Iran’s parliament speaker  went further on social media, warning that if Iranian power plants are attacked, the critical infrastructure, energy infrastructure, and oil facilities throughout the region will be considered legitimate targets and will be destroyed in an irreversible manner, and the price of oil will remain high for a long time. Meanwhile, Iran’s ambassador to the United Kingdom and permanent representative to the International Maritime Organization said the strait will remain open to all nations – except for Iran’s enemies  and that safe passage is possible once  necessary security arrangements with Tehran are made. Vinod Kumar/Akashvani News/Dubai.”

Meanwhile, Air India and Air India Express will operate a total of 30 scheduled and non-scheduled flights to and from West Asia today even as services on several routes remain limited. Both carriers will continue their scheduled operations to Jeddah, with a total of 10 flights between India and the Saudi Arabian city. Air India will operate one return service each from Delhi and Mumbai, while Air India Express will run services from Bengaluru, Kozhikode and Mangaluru.

Union Coal and Mines Minister G. Kishan Reddy will launch the 7th Tranche of Auction of Critical and Strategic Mineral Blocks today. The nation today pays homage to legendary freedom fighters Bhagat Singh, Rajguru and Sukhdev on their martyrdom day. On this day in 1931, British rulers had executed them in Lahore for their revolutionary activities against the empire.

”The Britishers, after hanging Shaheed-E-Azam Bhagat Singh, Rajguru and Sukhdev, a day before the scheduled date, secretly brought their bodies to the bank of the river Sutlej at Hussainiwala in Ferozepur and unceremoniously cremated them. A National Martyrs Memorial here depicts their irrepressible revolutionary spirit. Border Security Force, Punjab, in its unique initiative as part of its Diamond Jubilee celebrations organised Ferozepur-Hussainiwala Border Marathon, here yesterday, to honor the supreme sacrifice of the legendary trio. Ferozepur Railway Division has started 6 pairs of special Shahidi-Mela trains today to ferry the visitors from Feerozepur Cantt to Hussainiwala and back. People are visiting the memorial to pay tributes to the Martyrs. A state level programme will be held at the ancestral village of Shaheed Bhagat Singh at Khatkar Kalan to remember the supreme sacrifice of these Sons of the soil for the Nation.Rajesh Bali/Akashvani News/Jalandhar.

The Mera Yuva (MY) Bharat will organise a nationwide Padyatra today, marking the Shaheed Diwas. The initiative will be conducted under the theme MY Bharat, My Responsibility.

“The Padyatra will be a tribute to the sacrifice of India’s young freedom fighters – Bhagat Singh, Rajguru, and Sukhdev. Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports said that the initiative aims to channelise the energy, idealism, and aspirations of youth towards responsible citizenship. The Padyatras will be organised across all 763 districts across country, with a major event to be organised in Faridabad, Haryana, with participation of over 10 thousand youth. Minister of State for Youth Affairs and Sports Raksha Khadse and Sports Minister of Haryana Gaurav Gautam will be present on the occasion.

Every year, Shaheed Diwas serves as a powerful reminder that India’s freedom was earned through sacrifice and courage. It reinforces the idea that freedom is not merely inherited, but must be strengthened and preserved through continuous collective effort. In this context, the Shaheed Diwas Padyatra seeks to transform remembrance into action by encouraging youth to actively participate in nation-building. WITH AMAN’S REPORT, THIS IS CHIRAG JHA, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

## Evening News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi addressed the Lok Sabha today on the current situation in West Asia, outlining key aspects of the ongoing US-Israel conflict with Iran and explaining India’s position on the issue. Speaking in the House, the Prime Minister said, there is no energy security crisis in India despite the on-going conflict. He said, the government is ensuring smooth supplies of petrol, diesel, oil, gas and fertilizers.

The Prime Minister said, the government is in constant contact with suppliers from different countries and effort is to ensure that oil and gas supplies continue from the sources wherever possible.

The Prime Minister said that in this grave situation, the safety of Indians in the country and abroad has remained the government’s top priority.

Mr Modi reiterated that India has consistently expressed deep concern over the conflict and has urged all sides to reduce tensions. He added that India is in favour of a peaceful resolution to this crisis.

The Prime Minister said, all agencies of the country, be it coastal security, border security, cyber security and strategic installations have been put on high alert.

Later, addressing a summit organized by a private media in New Delhi, Prime Minister Modi said, amid West Asia conflict, India has demonstrated its capacity for relationship-building, decision-making, and crisis management.

The Government today reassured that all refineries are operating at the highest capacity and natural gas for domestic and transport purposes supply is 100 percent maintained. Joint Secretary in the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas, Sujata Sharma stated this while addressing an inter-ministerial briefing in Delhi on recent developments in West Asia today.

According to sources, two Indian Flag LPG carriers, Jag Vasant and Pine Gas, carrying more than 92 thousand 612 MT of LPG, have transited through the Strait of Hormuz this evening. The vessels have 33 and 27 Indian seafarers on board. These vessels are destined for India and are likely to reach Ports between 26th and 28th of this month.

Meanwhile, US President Donald Trump has announced an extension to his deadline for Iran to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, saying the US will hold off strikes on power plant for five days. Mr. Trump made this announcement in a social media post, just hours ahead of the earlier stated 48 hour-deadline. He noted that recent discussions between US and Iranian officials have been very good and productive, expressing optimism that they could lead to a complete and total resolution of the conflict.
On the other hand, Iranian state media reported that Tehran has firmly rejected the prospect of talks with Washington, emphasizing that no direct dialogue is currently taking place between the two sides. In a statement, Iran’s Foreign Ministry dismissed recent remarks by US President Trump, suggesting they are intended to buy time for potential military planning and ease pressure on global energy markets. Despite these diplomatic signals, the situation on the ground remains volatile.

“The latest developments open a narrow window for de-escalation, but the risk of a wider conflict remains high. The United States’ decision to delay strikes signals a tactical pause rather than a breakthrough, as divisions between Washington and Tehran continue. Israeli forces are expanding operations in Lebanon, while threats to Gulf infrastructure are increasing the risk of regional spillover. Gulf states are now prioritising long-term security guarantees, particularly to counter missile and drone attacks on critical energy systems. As global oil markets react sharply, the coming days will determine whether diplomacy stabilises the situation or tensions escalate further across West Asia. Vinod Kumar, Akashvani News, Dubai.”

External Affairs Ministry Spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal while talking about US President Donald Trump’s latest social media post said that the Ministry is closely following these developments. Mr Jaiswal was addressing an inter-ministerial briefing in Delhi on recent developments in West Asia.
