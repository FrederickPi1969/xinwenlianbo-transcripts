The Har Ghar Tiranga campaign will begin today, and will run through 17th of this month. The initiative is aimed at encouraging citizens to hoist the National Flag and participate in activities celebrating patriotism, national unity and India’s cultural heritage. The campaign, which began in 2022 as part of the Azadi Ka Amrit Mahotsav celebrations, has since evolved into one of the country’s largest public participation movements. Over the years, it has emerged a true people’s movement that inspires citizens across regions and communities to celebrate the Tricolour as a symbol of India’s unity, honour and collective national identity. A report:

“This year’s campaign holds special significance as it coincides with the 150th anniversary of India’s National Song, “Vande Mataram”, bringing together the National Flag and the National Song in a unique celebration of patriotism. As part of the celebrations, mass singing of Vande Mataram will be organised across the country. As part of the Independence Day commemorations,the Government will also observe Partition Horrors Remembrance Day on August 14 across the country to remember the suffering and displacement caused by the Partition of India. The observance will include silent marches, exhibitions on Partition, documentary screenings and other commemorative programmes across States and Union Territories. Inviting citizens to participate in the campaign, the Ministry of Culture has urged people to hoist the National Flag with pride, learn and sing Vande Mataram, take part in community activities and join the nation in remembering the sacrifices that shaped India’s journey to Independence. More than 200 Indian Embassies, High Commissions and Consulates will organise a series of events to mark the campaign. A key highlight this year will be the Surya Path Tiranga digital relay, in which 54 Indian Missions and Posts, selected according to the sunrise sequence across time zones, will participate. “The Baitarani river system has entered its third flood wave this season, with fresh inundation reported in parts of Jajpur and Bhadrak districts. The Salandi river is also rising, aggravating the situation in low-lying areas of Bhadrak, while the Kani river, a branch of the Baitarani, has caused fresh flooding in Jajpur district. The Mahanadi is also swelling, though the immediate focus remains on the northern river systems. The IMD has issued more rain under the influence of a low-pressure system over north Odisha,  some parts of western Odisha and adjoining West Bengal. Odisha has recorded above-normal monsoon rainfall this season. Large areas of agricultural land have been inundated, while road communication has also been disrupted in several pockets. Prakash Dash for Akashvani News, Bhubaneswar.”

Typhoon Dolphin has made landfall along China’s eastern coast this afternoon, affecting Zhejiang and Fujian provinces. Following the typhoon’s landfall, country’s National Meteorological Centre has issued a red alert for heavy rainfall in the country. Meanwhile, Shanghai’s two airports cancelled more than 1,300 flights, while authorities relocated around 3 lakh people in surrounding regions. Heavy rain is expected across Zhejiang, Fujian, Shanghai, Jiangsu, Anhui, Jiangxi, Hubei, Henan and Shandong till Wednesday. The typhoon is expected to move northwestward and gradually weaken after landfall.

Indian weightlifter Maharajan Arumugapandian clinched the silver medal in the men’s 65 kg category at the Asian Junior Weightlifting Championships in Tashkent, Uzbekistan today. Maharajan produced a new personal best total of 285 kilograms, lifting 128 kg in the snatch and 157 kg in the clean and jerk to secure silver. Vietnam’s Hoang Thach claimed the gold medal with a total lift of 291 kilograms, finishing six kilograms ahead of Maharajan.
