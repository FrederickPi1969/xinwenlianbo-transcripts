## Morning News

In a rare confluence of constitutional leadership and developmental vision, Prime Minister Narendra Modi is visiting Odisha this morning, where he will join President Droupadi Murmu in her home district of Mayurbhanj. The visit coincides with the President’s 68th birthday and the celebrations marking two years of the Odisha Government. Our Correspondent reports from Rairangpur:

“In a rare and significant engagement, the President and the Prime Minister will jointly offer prayers at the sacred Santhali Jahera and Ho Jahera groves and visit local educational and skill-development institutions, underscoring the nation’s commitment to the empowerment of tribal communities. Later, the two leaders will participate in a grand public programme at Rairangpur marking the completion of two years of the Mohan Charan Majhi-led Odisha Government. Development projects worth more than 47,000 crore rupees will be inaugurated by the Prime Minister and foundation stones laid across key sectors including energy, transportation, health, education, irrigation and industrial infrastructure. President Murmu and Prime Minister Modi are also scheduled to address a massive public gathering. Prakash Dash from Rairangpur Akashvani news.”

Prime Minister Modi has extended greetings to President Murmu on her birthday. Mr. Modi said that the President’s journey is marked by courage, simplicity, humility and an unwavering commitment to public service that continues to inspire people across the country.

Later in the day, Prime Minister Modi will release over 18 thousand 8 hundred 80 crore rupees to the bank accounts of 9 crore 44 lakh farmers nationwide under the PM-Kisan scheme from Hoogly in West Bengal today. This also includes over 900 crore rupees to the accounts of more than 45 lakh farmers of West Bengal. We have more details on the Prime Minister’s visit from our Correspondent:

“Prime Minister Narendra Modi is arriving in West Bengal today on a two days visit for the first time since the BJP formed the government in the state. During his stay he will attend the West Bengal Day celebrations in Tarakeswar in Hooghly, lead the International Yoga Day program on Kolkata’s Red Road and dedicate three indigenously built warships-INS Dunagiri, INS Sanshodhak, and INS Agray-to the nation. He will also inaugurate and lay foundation stones for several key projects. These include railway initiatives worth nearly rupees 590 crore , a new 300-bed railway hospital in Howrah, and a rail overbridge in East Midnapore. He will also launch 49 rural road projects under the Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana, strengthening connectivity across more than 315 kilometers.

In agriculture, Mr Modi will formally introduce the Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana in West Bengal, providing insurance coverage to nearly 50 lakh farmers. He will also unveil new initiatives under the Digital Agriculture Mission, including Agri Stack and the Dhan-Dhanya Krishi Yojana.

The Prime Minister’s program extends to fisheries and animal husbandry, with projects such as a modern fishing harbor at Fraserganj, a fish market in Birbhum, and eastern India’s first goat semen research laboratory in Nadia. Sandipon Dasgupta, AKASHVANI NEWS, Kolkata.”

Prime Minister Modi has extended greetings to the people of West Bengal on the occasion of Paschimbanga Divas. Mr Modi said, this day celebrates a state that has made immense contributions to the country’s history through literature, music, art, spirituality, science, trade, commerce and social reform.

Senior Indian bureaucrat Vivek Aggarwal has been appointed as the next Vice-President of the Financial Action Task Force (FATF) for the 2026-27 term. He will succeed Giles Thomson of the United Kingdom. Mr Aggarwal, currently serving as Secretary in the Ministry of Culture, earlier headed the Indian delegation to the FATF and played a key role in India’s engagement with the global anti-money laundering and counter-terror financing watchdog.

The Ministry of External Affairs has termed Mr Aggarwal’s selection a major win for India. In a social media post, the Ministry said the appointment reinforces India’s commitment to combating terrorism financing and illicit financial networks.

The government has banned the manufacture, sale and distribution of 16 fixed-dose combination (FDC) drugs for human use with immediate effect. The move follows a Supreme Court-directed review by an expert committee set up by the Drugs Technical Advisory Board, which found the drug combinations lacked therapeutic justification and could pose health risks. The banned products include certain dermatological, pain-relief, antispasmodic and antibiotic-based medicines.

The Ministry of Health and Family Welfare said the action aims to ensure only safe, effective and scientifically validated medicines remain available. State drug regulators and enforcement agencies have been directed to strictly implement the ban.
