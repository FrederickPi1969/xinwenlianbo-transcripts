## Morning News

The Union Cabinet has approved a draft Bill to set up fast-track courts and to ensure stricter  punishment in cases of paper leaks and exam malpractices. The Cabinet meeting chaired by Prime Minister Narendra Modi at the Parliament House complex approved the draft bill. Sources told Akashvani News that the proposed legislation is expected to be introduced in the Parliament next week.

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has issued advertisements for a set of senior professional appointments at the level of General Manager. It has also opened the engagement of sixteen Young Professionals through the Pratibha Setu portal of the Union Public Service Commission.The NTA in a statement said that these measures represent the first concrete step in the institutional rebuild of the agency that is being implemented in line with the recommendations of the High-Level Committee of Experts chaired by Prof. K. Radhakrishnan.

NTA has notified four Positions for General Manager on contract, each for an initial term of three years. It said that the engagement of Young Professionals is being undertaken for the first time.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi thanked everyone for the responses and positive suggestions received to his video, where he announced the government’s decision to bring a strict law to punish those involved in paper leaks. In the video message, Modi said that a new bill incorporating provisions for strong action against paper leaks will be introduced in the ongoing session of Parliament next week.

The draft bill, as announced by the Prime Minister in the Instagram video shared by him, was approved by the Union Cabinet yesterday. In a fresh video message released through his Instagram handle, yesterday, Mr Modi said the relations will continue with more bonding.

Mr Modi created a new world record with the most views on a reel in 24 hours, garnering 303 million views as of now. The earlier record was 300 million views.

Delhi Police has constituted a Special Task Force (STF) to investigate offences relating to paper leaks and other examination-related offences under the Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024. Our correspondent has the details.

Delhi Police in a statement said that the Task Force will be responsible for conducting prompt, professional, and comprehensive investigations into offences pertaining to public examinations, conducted by different authorities. These include Union Public Service Commission, Staff Selection Commission, Railway Recruitment Boards, Institute of Banking Personnel Selection, National Testing Agency as well as Ministries or Departments of the Central Government and their attached and subordinate offices for recruitment of staff. In addition to investigation, the Special Task Force will also ensure effective coordination with the prosecuting agencies to facilitate expeditious prosecution to ensure that trials in such cases are conducted on a day-to-day basis. With Anand Kumar, Shivang Misra, Akashvani News, Delhi

Union Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan has resigned today and tendered his resignation to Prime Minister Narendra Modi. In a social media post, Mr Pradhan said that he was pained by the prevailing situation following students’ protest during the past ten days  saying that it is not a matter of prestige for him. He said he had taken into account the situation at Jantar Mantar in New Delhi, as well as the situation across the country. Mr Pradhan also expressed his gratitude to the Prime Minister for his guidance, trust, and constant support. He said that service to the nation is the highest priority of his life and he will remain forever dedicated to it.

Mr Pradhan said, from day one, he took full responsibility and never turned away from the situation. He said, the NEET-UG results declared on July 16 were satisfactory, with many meritorious students from underprivileged backgrounds achieving success. He said however, even during this time, individuals holding responsible positions attempted to create obstacles and misled students, calling it an act that caused him deep anguish.

Union Minister Pralhad Joshi will be the new Education Minister following the resignation of Dharmendra Pradhan. Mr Joshi has been assigned the charge of the Ministry of Education, in addition to his existing portfolio. Mr Joshi is currently serving as the Minister of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution and New and Renewable Energy.
