## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that India is emerging as a trusted partner to the world. Addressing Indian diaspora in Paris last night, Mr Modi said, there was a time when relations between countries were determined solely by trade, but today, trust has become just as important alongside trade.

Mr Modi further said, the country is investing across sectors that will drive growth, innovation and opportunity for generations to come.

Mr Modi added that at the G7 meeting in Evian, he emphasised the importance of partnerships based on trust. He said, India’s message to the G7 Summit was clear that global governance will only be effective when it is inclusive.

Moreover, Mr Modi said he arrived in France when his government had completed 12 years and India has witnessed transformative growth across sectors in these years.

Earlier, the Indian community in France welcomed Prime Minister Modi with the message “Bonjour Modi”, or Hello Modi, and chants of “Bharat Mata Ki Jai”.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will disburse incentives worth 2 thousand 4 hundred crore rupees under Pradhan Mantri Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana in New Delhi today. This incentive has supported the creation of 15 lakh employment opportunities across the country. Labour and Employment Minister Dr Mansukh Mandaviya said that since August last year, more than 63 lakh employees have been brought into the formal workforce under the scheme, of whom nearly 30 per cent are women.

“The Scheme has been designed to encourage both workers and employers to participate in the formal economy. Under the scheme, first-time employees are eligible for an incentive of up to 15 thousand rupees, gaining crucial support as they enter the workforce. Employers generating additional employment are eligible for incentives of up to 3 thousand rupees per month per additional employee. It aims to facilitate the creation of over 3.5 crore jobs across the country over a period of two years. Priya Mondal, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will lay the foundation stone of a coal gasification project worth over 25 thousand crore rupees at Lakhanpur in Jharsuguda district of Odisha tomorrow. The Ministry of Coal informed that the project marks a historic milestone as the country’s first commercial-scale coal-to-ammonium nitrate facility. The project is being developed by Bharat Coal Gasification and Chemicals Limited, a joint venture of Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited and Coal India Limited. It will produce two thousand tonnes per day of ammonium nitrate using indigenously developed coal gasification technology.

The Supreme Court today ruled that the right to walk on a demarcated footpath is a fundamental right. A bench of justices PS Narasimha and AS Chandurkar held that a citizen’s fundamental right to walk on a demarcated footpath is primary and shall have priority over movement by motorised vehicles. In a significant verdict, the apex court said, it forms part of the right to movement guaranteed under Article 19 (1) (d) and other fundamental rights including Article 21- Right to life and liberty. The apex court called for a regulatory body to effectuate the fundamental right to walk on demarcated footpaths. It said such regulator will maintain institutional integrity by taking independent and objective decisions without governmental or industrial control.

The declaration by the top court came in an unfortunate motor accident compensation case where a father lost his five-year-old son while taking him to school.
