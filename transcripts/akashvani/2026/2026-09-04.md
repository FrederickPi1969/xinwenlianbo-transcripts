## Morning News

Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) has successfully launched its earth observation satellite EOS-05 onboard GSLV-F17 rocket from Sriharikota in Andhra Pradesh early this morning. After a 27-hour countdown, GSLV-F17 rocket, with satellite EOS-05, lifted off at 2.55 AM from the second launch pad at Satish Dhawan Space Centre. ISRO Chairman V. Narayanan said the state-of-the art satellite was successfully placed into sub- geosynchronous orbit about 18 minutes after it lifted off from the spaceport.

“ISRO said the 2,367-kilogram satellite was placed in its intended Sub-Geosynchronous Transfer Orbit about 18 minutes after lift-off. EOS-05 is a state-of-the-art Earth observation spacecraft and India’s first imaging satellite to be placed in a geosynchronous orbit. It is designed to provide frequent, near-real-time imagery of large areas, strengthening India’s capability to monitor the Earth. The satellite’s data will support applications in agriculture, natural resource monitoring and disaster management. It can provide timely information during disasters and help authorities plan and respond to emergencies. ISRO Chairman V. Narayanan said the mission will further strengthen India’s Earth observation capabilities and improve the delivery of Earth observation services. The mission also demonstrates India’s growing capability in indigenous launch vehicle technology. Allu Murali, Akashvani News.”

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has commended the successful launch of India’s first-ever imaging satellite from the Geosynchronous orbit. In a social media post, Mr Modi called the successful launch a reflection of the excellence, innovation and growing capabilities that define India’s space sector. The Prime Minister said the growing partnership between ISRO and Indian industry is adding new strength and scale to country’s space programme and expanding India’s capabilities across the entire space ecosystem. He also termed it an outstanding achievement by ISRO and a proud moment for the nation.

Government has constituted a 31-member Joint Parliamentary Committee (JPC) on Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Amendment Bill, 2026. Lok Sabha MP Sanjay Jaiswal will head the joint panel to examine the amendment bill. We have a report:

“Prominent names from Lok Sabha in the committee include Bhartruhari Mahtab, Tejaswi Surya, Nishikant Dubey, A. Raja and Supriya Sule. From Rajya Sabha, Harsh Vardhan Shringla, Sanjay Kumar Jha, Praful Patel and Menaka Guruswamy are in the committee. Both Houses of Parliament on 12th August gave their approval for referring the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Amendment Bill, 2026, to the Joint Parliamentary Committee for further scrutiny. The bill aims to further amend the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act, 2010. The objective of the Bill is to make the use of foreign contributions more transparent and accountable. with aman yadav, BHANU, AKASHVANI NEWS.”

The office of the Chief Electoral Officer of Delhi has informed that the electors who were unable to submit their filled-in enumeration forms within the specified time that is from 30th June to 17th Aug 2026 may file Form 6 along with the prescribed Declaration Form for inclusion during the claims and objections period that is 31 August to 30th September this year. The Supreme Court of India will host the BRICS Chief Justices’ Forum in New Delhi beginning today. The forum will bring together Chief Justices, Presidents of Supreme Courts and senior judicial leaders from BRICS Member States and Partner Countries. The Forum will provide a platform for dialogue among the participating judiciaries and for exchange of views on contemporary issues relating to justice delivery, international dispute resolution, technology and sustainable development.

Chief Justice of India Surya Kant will hold a series of bilateral meetings with heads of judicial delegations on the opening day. He will meet judicial leaders from Russia, China, Egypt, Indonesia, the UAE, Belarus, Kazakhstan, South Africa, Iran and Uzbekistan.

The bilateral meetings will provide an opportunity for direct interaction and exchange of views on matters of mutual judicial interest and will further strengthen engagement among the participating judiciaries.

Festival of Janmashtami, which marks the birth of Lord Krishna, is being celebrated across the country today. Devotees throng the temples to offer prayers to Lord Krishna.

Our correspondent reports that Mathura and Vrindavan in Uttar Pradesh is fully geared to celebrate the birth of Lord Krishna.

“The streets of Mathura and Vrindavan, steeped in devotion to Lord Krishna, are reverberating with the  chanting of Jay Shri Krishna. Today is Lord Krishna’s birthday, and preparations for a grand celebration are underway throughout the city. Lord Krishna’s birth anniversary is being celebrated with great devotion and enthusiasm throughout Brij Mandal and other parts of state also. but the grand celebration is in Mathura Vrindavan, where all temples are decked up for midnight celebration. flowers petals and colorful lights are decorated everywhere. On this occasion, millions of devotees are already doing darshans of Lord Krishna. In view of the huge crowd of devotees coming from India and abroad, extensive security arrangements have been made in the entire Mathura city and police are also  gearing themselves for traffic management. For the first time in Mathura, on Janmashtami today, 500 drones will bring Lord Krishna’s birth and his various pastimes to life in the sky. SUSHIL CHANDRA TIWARI ,AKASHvANI NEWS, LUCKNOW.”

The auspicious festival of Janmashtami is being celebrated across Gujarat with immense grandeur and deep devotion. Grand celebrations and special religious rituals are underway at the major Krishna shrines including the world-famous Dwarkadhish, Dakor, and Shamalaji temples. More from our correspondent:

“A large number of devotees from across the country have gathered at the holy city of Dwarka to take part in the Janmashtami celebrations. The rituals commenced early this morning with the auspicious Mangala Aarti and will continue through the day with elaborate offerings, special darshan, and traditional kirtans. The grand festivities will culminate tonight at midnight with the spectacular Janmotsav Aarti, as the Dwarkadhish temple erupts in joyful chants of ‘Jai Kanhaiya Lal Ki’ to welcome the divine birth of Lord Krishna. The holy shrine has been beautifully illuminated and adorned with vibrant floral arrangements, while the deity of Lord Dwarkadhish has been embellished in magnificent attire and sacred ornaments. Along with Dwarka, grand celebrations are also taking place at the historic Ranchhodraiji Temple in Dakor and the Shamalaji Temple. Elaborate arrangements have been made by the local administration and temple authorities to manage the heavy rush of pilgrims and ensure smooth darshan throughout the day and night. Aparna Khunt, Akashvani News, Ahmedabad”.

India has sent two more aircraft with relief materials to Nepal to support the ongoing relief and rescue efforts in flood-affected areas. External Affairs Ministry said, both C-130J aircraft carrying 21.5 tonnes of humanitarian assistance landed in Kathmandu. The relief material was handed over to the Minister for Science, Technology and Innovation, Mahabir Pun.

The Ministry of Home Affairs will make available services of Central Forensic Science Laboratories, State Forensic Science Laboratories, and laboratories of the National Forensic Sciences University for DNA profiling of first-degree biological relatives of the missing persons from India following the flash flood in Nepal. Ministry of External Affairs said, first-degree biological relatives residing in India may have their biological samples collected and DNA profiles prepared at certain facilities.

In Nepal, the death toll from the devastating flash floods has risen to 1,259, while more than 5,000 people remain missing more than a week after the disaster.

According to Nepal Police, Chitwan recorded the highest number of recovered bodies at 355, including unidentified human remains, followed by Nawalparasi East with 218 and Nawalparasi West with 212.

Prime Minister Balendra Shah said the disaster’s link to climate change must be strongly raised with the international community, as symbolic funerals were held by families of locals still missing.

Meanwhile, joint rescue teams led by the Nepal Army continue their search, and an Indian tunnel rescue team has established a base in Syabrubesi to speed up treacherous operations at the Chilime Power House alongside Nepali teams.
