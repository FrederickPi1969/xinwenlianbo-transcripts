## Morning News

The Nation celebrates its 80th Independence Day today. Prime Minister Narendra Modi hoisted the National Flag at Red Fort in Delhi and addressed the nation. The celebrations this year also commemorates Yuva Shakti, acknowledging the contribution of young India in building a Viksit Bharat by 2047.

Addressing the nation from the ramparts of Red Fort, Prime Minister Modi said that this is the first time since independence that Vande Matram has resonated from ramparts of Red Fort.

Prime Minister said, a nation becomes great when it moves forward on the strength of its resolve and capabilities.

Mr Modi said that over the past twelve years, crores of citizens of the country, with their determination and dedication, made every possible effort to take the country to new heights. He said, due to these efforts, 25 crore people have come out of poverty.

He said, India which was once among the Fragile Five economies has now emerged as a major and the fastest-growing economy in the world. Mr Modi said that India has set a big goal, to become a developed nation by 2047.

Highlighting the achievements made in different sectors, the Prime Minister said, over the past 12 years, defence production has increased nearly four-fold.

He said, internet users increased by nearly four-fold and digital transactions by 100 times. Mr Modi said, the NDA government has worked with equal seriousness towards the welfare and amenities for the common man.

Prime Minister said, Energy security is today’s need, and that’s why we have already taken steps in this direction. Mr. Modi said the government is moving forward with the aim of 100 gigawatts of nuclear power capacity by 2047. He added that India has set a target of commissioning five new nuclear reactors during the current decade.

Prime Minister said India has already begun production at three semiconductor plants, and five to eight more plants are expected to come up in the coming years.

Prime Minister said India must move towards self-reliance and not remain dependent on other countries. He asserted that the country needs to strengthen its own capabilities and protect its national interests.

Mr Modi unveiled a seven-point reform framework, or ‘Sapta Dhara’, aimed at accelerating India’s journey towards becoming a Viksit Bharat by 2047. He said, work that could not be completed in the past five to seven decades must be achieved within the next five to seven years.

Prime Minister said that government has decided that one crore youth will be given training in AI skilling in the next one year. He said, this will enable them to take the lead in the global AI sector. Mr Modi said, the country has to focus on three things – cost, quality and skill.

President Droupadi Murmu has said that the government is undertaking comprehensive steps to completely curb the use of unfair means in public examinations. Addressing the nation on the eve of the Independence Day, she said students are the architects of India’s future and government is making the system more transparent, secure and trustworthy for the youth.

President Droupadi Murmu has approved 78 Gallantry awards to the personnel of the Defence Forces and Central Armed Police Forces on the eve of Independence Day. 13 Awards have been given posthumously. These are nine Kirti Chakras, one Bar to Shaurya Chakra, 19 Shaurya Chakras, five Bar to Sena Medals, 36 Sena Medals, three Nao Sena Medal, and five Vayu Sena Medals.

A powerful 7.7 magnitude earthquake hit the ocean near Indonesia early in the morning, damaging homes and public buildings while triggering a tsunami warning. There are no immediate reports of casualties. The quake was felt across much of Flores Island.

Authorities have issued a tsunami warning for part of East Nusa Tenggara, West Nusa Tenggara, South Sulawesi and Southeast Sulawesi provinces, and urged residents to stay away from beaches and riverbanks. Residents along affected coastlines have been advised to move to higher ground.

The first Test of two-match series between India and Sri Lanka will begin today at the Galle International Stadium. The match will begin at 10 AM IST.

After suffering 0-2 defeat to South Africa at home, Shubman Gill and Co. cannot afford any more slip ups and need a sweep to stay alive in the ICC World Test Championship 2025-27.

A slew of injuries have hampered India in the lead up but an extended training period in Sri Lanka would put the side in position to perform well and aim for a win.

Prime Minister Modi said that over the past twelve years, crores of citizens of the country, with their determination and dedication, made every possible effort to take the country to new heights.

## Evening News

The Nation is celebrating its 80th Independence Day today. Prime Minister Narendra Modi led the Independence Day celebrations from the iconic Red Fort in Delhi. Addressing the nation from the ramparts of Red Fort, the Prime Minister said, over the past twelve years, crores of citizens of the country, with their determination and dedication, made every possible effort to take the country to new heights. He said a nation achieves greatness when it moves forward on the strength of its own dreams and resolve. The Prime Minister stressed that higher dreams and stronger resolutions raise the overall scale and capability of the nation’s efforts.

Prime Minister Modi said energy security is one of the major requirements of the present times and the government has taken several significant steps in this direction. He said nuclear energy is an important means of ensuring a country’s energy security.

Prime Minister Modi said, the government has opened up 99 per cent of offshore areas that were once designated as no-go zones and converted them into go-ahead areas. He said, the government is now actively pursuing exploration beneath the sea and making efforts to discover new reserves.

Stressing on empowerment of youth and women for a Viksit Bharat, Prime Minister Modi announced to provide free online coaching for poor and middle class families for various public examination. He also announced that the government has decided that one crore youth will be given training in AI skilling in the next one year. He said, this will enable them to take the lead in the global AI sector.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi urged all parties to support the implementation of women’s reservation in Parliament and state assemblies without making it a political fight. He said, women should be given the opportunity to contribute to shaping India’s policies.

On naxalism, the Prime Minister said the people of the country gave the opportunity to serve the nation in 2014 and he resolved to make the country free from Naxalism.

In his address, the Prime Minister called upon the nation to harness the seven streams of strength called as Sapt Dhara. He said, in the next five to seven years, the country will achieve all these that could not be achieved in the past five to seven decades.

Prime Minister Modi said that in the next seven to eight years, another 5 to 8 semiconductor plants are set to commence operations. He said, India has started production at three semiconductor manufacturing plants.

Prime Minister highlighted the government’s focus on critical minerals saying that the modern technology depends entirely on critical minerals, and India is actively working to secure its position in this sector.

World leaders and foreign missions have extended Independence Day greetings to India, praising its democratic journey, civilizational heritage and growing role in regional and global cooperation. U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio highlighted the strengthening India-U.S. strategic partnership, while Singaporean President Tharman Shanmugaratnam stressed growing cooperation in skills development, digitalization, sustainability and advanced manufacturing.  French President Emmanuel Macron conveyed his warm wishes on India’s 80th Independence Day, while Israeli President Isaac Herzog congratulated President Droupadi Murmu, Prime Minister Narendra Modi and the people of India in a message shared in Hindi. In a unique global celebration of India’s Independence Day, the SURYAPATH TIRANGA Global Relay is underway, in which the Indian Tricolour is traveling across the world following the path of the rising sun. The Suryapath Tiranga journey began at 1:30 AM at Suva, the capital of Fiji, as the Tiranga was hoisted at the first point of the relay. From there it moved westward. The Culture Ministry said in a statement that at every mission and post, the National Flag was hoisted, creating a continuous visual relay of the Tiranga across time zones and continents.

In Indonesia, the death toll has risen to 47 after a powerful 7.7 magnitude earthquake struck off the coast of Flores today. The earthquake was followed by dozens of aftershocks as rescue teams continued efforts to reach the worst-affected areas. Hundreds of buildings in the region have been damaged by the quake and the dozens of aftershocks that followed. A 6.4 magnitude earthquake was also felt in North Sumatra later in the day, at a depth of 163 kilometres. Tsunami waves of less than one metre were reported in several parts of the country. The tsunami alert was withdrawn around three hours after the initial earthquake. Officials said people are still believed to be trapped inside buildings in several affected areas. Landslides have also been reported, with debris blocking roads and hampering rescue and relief operations.

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has issued an orange colour warning for heavy to very heavy rainfall over Arunachal Pradesh, Gangetic West Bengal, Odisha, Uttar Pradesh, and Uttarakhand till tomorrow. Heavy rainfall conditions are also likely to prevail over Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh, Jharkhand, Konkan, Goa, Madhya Maharashtra, and Madhya Pradesh over the next two to three days. Similar weather conditions are likely to prevail over the northeastern region until the 21st of this month.
