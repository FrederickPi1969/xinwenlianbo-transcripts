Payments Council of India PCI has announced that UPI will continue to be free for consumers. We have a report:

“In a statement, the PCI affirmed that every Indian can continue to make instant digital payments without any transaction charges. It also noted that small merchants will not be charged for accepting digital payments. The PCI added that banks, fin-tech companies, NPCI, and the RBI have been investing in technology, cyber-security, fraud prevention, and innovation to maintain UPI’s reliability.

The PCI clarified that any applicable merchant service charges, which are commercial arrangements solely between merchants and payment providers, do not mean that consumers pay to use digital payments. It highlighted that UPI has become a critical national digital infrastructure, used daily by many citizens. The PCI further noted that as transaction volumes continue to grow, sustained investment in security, resilience, innovation, fraud prevention, and infrastructure will remain essential to ensure UPI reliably serves consumers and businesses for years to come. The US Senate has voted to approve a bill to sanction Russia and key buyers of its petroleum products, such as China and other countries, claiming that such trade helps fuel the Ukraine war. The bill, renamed the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026, was approved by the Senate by an 86-11 vote. This bill will allow President Trump’s administration to impose 100 per cent tariff on goods from countries that are top five importers of Russian oil and gas. With that the bill would also extend the expiration date of the Iran Sanctions Act of 1996 till 2031, which penalises companies that invest in Iran’s energy sector.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today urged the students of the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Delhi to keep the national interest at the centre of their decisions and ask themselves how their actions can benefit the country and address its needs. Mr Modi made these remarks while addressing the 57th Convocation of IIT Delhi. The Prime Minister said that new sectors are emerging in the country, offering fresh opportunities for the youth. He cited sectors such as semiconductors, space and drones, and said India is working towards becoming self-reliant across all sectors. Mr Modi said this drive towards self-reliance will serve as a strong foundation for building a Viksit Bharat.

Further elaborating on India’s efforts in the semiconductor sector, Prime Minister Modi said that production has begun at India’s first semiconductor unit.

Mr. Modi also spoke about distractions in the age of social media. He said students should avoid comparing themselves with others on the basis of salary packages, jobs or the sectors in which they are working. Mr Modi said life is not a leaderboard and one’s journey is not a competition with others, but with oneself.

Union Education Minister Pralhad Joshi, Chairperson Board of Governors of IIT Delhi, Harish Salve, and Director IIT Delhi Professor Rangan Banerjee were also present in the ceremony. On the occasion, the Prime Minister also inaugurated a high performance computing facility ‘Param Pragya’ at IIT Delhi.
