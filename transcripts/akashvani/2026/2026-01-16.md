## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will participate in a programme marking a decade of the Startup India initiative at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi today. During the programme, the Prime Minister will interact with members of India’s vibrant startup ecosystem.

“Startup India was launched on 16 January 2016 by Prime Minister Narendra Modi as a transformative national programme to nurture innovation, promote entrepreneurship and enable investment-driven growth. The objective of this initiative is to make India a nation of job creators rather than job seekers. As India advances towards a 7.3 trillion dollar economy by 2030 and the broader vision of Viksit Bharat 2047, startups are poised to remain central to the country’s development trajectory serving both as catalysts of growth and as enduring symbols of India’s future-ready, innovation-led economic model. With Dipendra Kumar, Saklen Akhtar for Akashvani News, Delhi.”

Select startup representatives will also share insights from their entrepreneurial journey. Bharat Sethi, Founder of Rage Coffee shared how start up india mission has regained the faith of the common man.

The Election Commission has extended the schedule for the ongoing Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls in Goa, Lakshadweep, Rajasthan, Puducherry and West Bengal up to the 19th of January. With this, electors can file claims and objections till 19th of this month. In a letter to the Chief Electoral Officers of these states and Union Territories, the Election Commission said the revised schedule has been decided after considering the requests from respective CEOs and other relevant factors.

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has decided that all candidates scheduled to appear for JEE (Main) 2026 Session 1 examination in West Bengal on the 23rd January this year will be allotted another date of examination among the advertised dates. The decision was taken in view of the Saraswati Puja celebrations in the state on 23rd of this month. NTA has advised candidates to e-mail at jeemain@nta.ac.in, or call at 011-40759000 for any query.

Union Power Minister Manohar Lal has said that India has more than doubled its installed power generation capacity from 249 gigawatt in 2014 to over 510 gigawatt in 2025. Addressing the curtain raiser of the Bharat Electricity Summit 2026 in New Delhi, the Minister said that India has entered a new era of energy abundance defined by reliability, accessibility and resilience.

The International Monetary Fund (IMF) has said that India is a major growth engine for the global economy. The IMF noted that India’s economic growth in the third quarter was stronger than expected. Addressing a press conference, Director of the IMF’s Communications Department, Julie Kozack said, India’s growth remains robust and is largely driven by strong domestic consumption.

The IMF had earlier projected India’s growth at 6.6 percent for the 2025-26 fiscal year.

Indian Coast Guard has intercepted a Pakistani fishing boat with 9 crew inside Indian waters near the International Maritime Boundary Line in the Arabian Sea. According to the Indian Coast Guard, the Pakistani boat named AL-MADINA, was sighted by an ICG ship on Wednesday. On being challenged, the boat attempted to flee towards Pakistan side. However, the ICG Ship intercepted and boarded the boat in Indian waters and a total of 9 crew were found in the Pakistani boat. The boat was then towed to Porbandar port by the ICG Ship for a thorough rummaging and joint interrogation by the concerned agencies.

In Jammu and Kashmir, the Police have attached the immovable property of a Pakistan-based terror handler in Poonch district. The action followed court directions in connection with a case registered at Mandi Police Station. The attached property consists of 10 kanal and 14 marlas of land in Mandi tehsil, with an estimated value of 22.05 lakh rupees. The land belongs to Abdul Aziz, a resident of Chamber Kanari who is currently operating as a terror handler in Pakistan. The officials said the accused had earlier exfiltrated to Pakistan and PoK. Since then, he has been involved in activities inimical to the security and sovereignty of India.

## Evening News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said, the Startup India programme is not just a scheme but a ‘Rainbow Vision’ that connects diverse sectors with new opportunities. Addressing an event marking a decade of Startup India on National Startup Day in New Delhi today, the Prime Minister said, the Startup India Mission has become a revolution in just ten years, fostering a new culture across the country.

The Prime Minister said courage is essential for enterprise, praising the youth for taking risks, and noted that risk-taking, once discouraged, is now respected in the country.

The Prime Minister also lauded the role of women in the startup ecosystem, noting that over 45 percent of recognized startups in India have at least one female director or partner.

On the occasion, the Prime Minister also visited the exhibition area at the event and interacted with exhibitors. Union Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal said, in the last decade a big change has been brought in the startup ecosystem under the leadership of the Prime Minister.

Startup India was launched on 16 January 2016 by Prime Minister Narendra Modi as a transformative national programme to nurture innovation, promote entrepreneurship and enable investment-driven growth. The objective of this initiative is to make India a nation of job creators rather than job seekers.

The BJP-led Mahayuti poised for a big victory in Maharashtra, with a massive edge on most of the 29 municipal corporations, including Mumbai, Pune, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Kalyan-Dombivali, Nashik, Thane and Navi Mumbai. Addressing media persons in Mumbai, the Chief Minister of Maharashtra said that the Mahayuti alliance had contested the civic polls with a clear focus on governance and infrastructure under the leadership of Prime Minister Narendra Modi. He thanked the people of the state for record-breaking mandate. In the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation till last reports, the BJP has won 70 seats, while the Shiv Sena has secured 21. The Shiv Sena-UBT (Uddhav Balasaheb Thackeray faction) has managed 48 seats, Raj Thackeray’s MNS has won 5, and the Congress has secured 19 seats.

In Thane, Shiv Sena has won 34 seats, BJP 17, and NCP 4. The Shiv Sena UBT could manage only one seat. In the Pune Municipal Corporation, BJP crossed the majority mark of 83 seats in the 165-member house, paving the way for a single-party government.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has expressed happiness and gratitude to the people of Maharashtra on the victory of National Democratic Alliance in the local body elections. In a social media post, Mr Modi said the results of various municipal corporation elections indicate that NDA’s bond with the people of Maharashtra has further deepened. Prime Minister Modi added that NDA’s track record and vision for development have struck a chord. He also thanked NDA workers for their cooperation, who worked tirelessly among people across Maharashtra.

A Delhi court today sent five accused in the Red Fort blast case to judicial custody till 13th of next month. The accused include three doctors and a preacher. The National Investigating Agency’s plea was allowed for Dr Adeel Rather, Dr Shaheen Saeed, Dr Muzammil Ganaie, Maulvi Irfan Ahmad Wagay and Jasir Bilal Wani who were arrested for allegedly aiding terror attacks ahead of the 10th November blast that killed 15. Judicial custody of two others Yasir Ahmed Dar and Naseer Bilal Malla was also extended. Nine accused have been arrested in the case so far.

The Government has blocked 242 illegal betting and gambling website links. Sources said that so far, over 7 thousand 800 illegal betting and gambling websites have been taken down, with a significant increase in enforcement actions after the passage of the Online Gaming Act. Sources added that today’s action reflects the Government’s commitment to protect users especially youth, and to curb financial and social harm caused by illegal online betting and gambling platforms.

The Enforcement Directorate (ED) has conducted searches at 26 locations across several States today. These states include Goa, Maharashtra, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Odisha, and Delhi. In a statement, ED said that these searches are part of an investigation into illegal international narcotics trafficking and money laundering under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002. ED said, the distribution of narcotic substances on a business-to-business basis has been traced across multiple states.

External Affairs Ministry has said, India is keeping a close eye on the prevailing situation in Iran. During a media briefing in New Delhi, MEA spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal said that India is committed to take whatever steps required in the interest of the Indian citizens living in that country.

Regarding Chabahar Port, Mr. Jaiswal said the US Treasury issued guidance on a conditional sanctions waiver in October last year which is valid until 26 of April this year. He said, India continues to engage with the US on the arrangement.

External Affairs Minister Dr. S. Jaishankar has said that the Special Strategic and Global Partnership between India and Japan is on an upward trajectory. Mr Jaishankar said this after co-chairing the 18th India-Japan Strategic Dialogue with his Japanese counterpart, Toshimitsu Motegi, in New Delhi today. In his opening address ahead of the talks with the Japanese Foreign Minister, Dr. Jaishankar said that over the last two decades, India and Japan have successfully transformed their relationship from a purely economic one into a comprehensive and strategic partnership.

In a social media post, the External Affairs Minister said that both countries discussed a wide range of issues during the discussions, including deeper economic cooperation, maritime security, supply chain resilience, critical minerals.

The Government will bring a legislation amending the Seed Act 1966 in the Upcoming Budget Session of Parliament. Briefing media in New Delhi, today, Agriculture and Farmers welfare Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan said the legislation aims to curb the sale of poor-quality, fake, and unauthorised seeds. He said that a nationwide seed traceability system will be established, under which every seed packet will carry a QR code enabling farmers to access complete information about its origin and helping prevent the sale of fake or substandard seeds. He further said that every seed company will now have to be registered.
