Prime Minister Narendra Modi will inaugurate the International Conference on “The Future of Environment and Climate Dynamics today in New Delhi. The two-day conference is being organised by the National Green Tribunal. It will bring together environmental experts, judges of the Supreme Court and High Courts, and District Court judges. Secretaries of various ministries and senior government officials will also participate in the event. Representatives from State Judicial Academies, State Legal Services Authorities, State Expert Appraisal Committees, State Environment Impact Assessment Authorities and State Wetland Authorities will attend the conference.

The Conference will provide a platform to discuss key environmental and climate challenges. These include gaps in policy formulation, implementation and enforcement. The conference will also explore ways to strengthen environmental governance and institutional cooperation.

US President Donald Trump said that the United States has reached a deal with Denmark to strengthen the American military presence in Greenland, ending months of tension over his repeated calls to acquire the Arctic island. The agreement leaves Greenland under Denmark’s sovereignty while giving the US what Trump described as a larger security role. In a social media post, Trump said the agreement gives the United States permanent control over security, and all other needs, in Greenland, completely addressing all of US concerns.

Denmark said the agreement will be signed by the governments of the US, Denmark and Greenland next week during the United Nations General Assembly, although it will still need parliamentary approval in Denmark and Greenland before it can take effect.

In this special series, we look at the opportunities being provided to the youth through the Agnipath Scheme. More from our correspondent:-

“The government launched the Agnipath Scheme in 2022 to provide young people with an opportunity to serve the nation. Under the scheme, youth inducted into the Armed Forces are known as Agniveers. Agniveers serve for four years, during which they receive military training and gain experience in discipline and various skills. Uttarakhand has a strong military tradition, with its youth playing an important role in serving in the Armed Forces. Young people from the state are also participating in the recruitment process under the Agnipath Scheme. A large number of candidates participated in the Agniveer recruitment rally held at Banbasa in Champawat in February and March this year. After completing four years of service, up to 25 percent of each batch of Agniveers will be inducted into the regular cadre of the Armed Forces through the prescribed process. The remaining Agniveers are provided a Seva Nidhi package and a skill certificate. In Uttarakhand, a 10 percent horizontal reservation has been provided for ex-Agniveers in various uniformed Group ‘C’ services under rules issued in 2025. These include posts in the police, fire services, prisons, forest, excise and Secretariat security. Subject to the prescribed conditions, ex-Agniveers are also provided relaxation in the upper age limit and exemption from the physical efficiency test. Thus, the Agnipath Scheme is providing young people with an opportunity to serve the nation while gaining military training and experience, while Uttarakhand has also made provisions for employment opportunities for them after their service. Santosh Thapliyal, Akashvani News, Dehradun.”
