## Morning News

The nation is paying homage to the great freedom fighter Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, on his 151st birth anniversary today. The day is observed as Rashtriya Ekta Diwas, or National Unity Day. President Droupadi Murmu and Vice President C. P. Radhakrishnan today paid floral tributes to the Iron Man of India, Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, at his statue at Patel Chowk in New Delhi. Prime Minister Narendra Modi also paid tributes to saying Sardar Patel dedicated his life to building a strong, prosperous and capable India.  Union Home Minister Amit Shah, Lieutenant Governor Taranjit Singh Sandhu, Delhi Chief Minister Rekha Gupta and BJP MP Bansuri Swaraj also paid floral tributes to the great national leader. Later Union Home Minister Amit Shah flagged off the ‘Run for Unity’ at the Major Dhyan Chand National Stadium to mark Rashtriya Ekta Diwas. Speaking at the event, Mr Shah said He was a great personality who not only transformed the nation’s history but also shaped its very geography. He said India stands proudly on the global stage today because of the strong foundations laid by Sardar Patel.

“Popularly known as the Iron Man of India, Sardar Patel served as country’s first Deputy Prime Minister and Home Minister and played a historic role in integrating more than 560 princely states to build a united and strong India.Rashtriya Ekta Diwas celebrates his immense contribution to the political integration and unity of the country. The occasion also serves as a reminder of the importance of national unity, integrity, security and harmony in India’s diverse and vibrant society.

Government institutions, schools and colleges are organising unity pledges, cultural programmes and awareness activities across the country to commemorate Sardar Patel’s ideals and legacy. On the occasion, the Run for Unity is also being organised at various places across the country to spread the message of national integration, peace and brotherhood. SANJEEV JASROTIA, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

The government has constituted a High-Level Committee on Banking for Viksit Bharat to review the country’s banking sector and recommend reforms in line with the vision of Viksit Bharat 2047. The Finance Ministry said, the Committee will recommend structural, institutional, regulatory and legislative reforms to make the Indian banking sector globally competitive, financially inclusive and resilient. The Committee will be chaired by former Central Vigilance Commissioner Suresh N. Patel.

“The High level Committee on Banking for Viksit Bharat  will have a tenure of six months. Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman had announced the setting up of the Committee in her Budget speech this year. The Committee will assess existing banking practices and prudential norms, identify global best practices and examine their suitability for adoption in the Indian banking system. It will also review the existing regulatory and legal frameworks governing banks, including Regional Rural Banks and Cooperative Banks, and suggest reforms to improve efficiency and ease of banking. In addition, the Committee will prepare a roadmap for the adoption of artificial intelligence and digital innovations in banking institutions, while recommending safeguards against cyber risks. With Rishu Jaiswal, Shivang Misra, Akashavni News, Delhi.”

The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) has successfully launched its next-generation navigation satellite, NVS-03, marking a significant step towards strengthening the country’s indigenous satellite navigation capabilities. The Geosynchronous Satellite Launch Vehicle, GSLV-F18, carrying the satellite, lifted off from the Second Launch Pad at the Satish Dhawan Space Centre in Sriharikota, Andhra Pradesh, last night.  NVS-03 is the third satellite in ISRO’s second-generation navigation satellite series. It is designed to ensure continuity and improve the availability of navigation services under the Navigation with Indian Constellation (NavIC), country’s indigenous alternative to the Global Positioning System (GPS).  Speaking to media later, Dr Narayanan said ISRO has placed its third indigenous atomic clock in orbit aboard the NVS-03 navigation satellite.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has congratulated the scientists, engineers and the entire team of the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) on the successful launch of the GSLV-F18 rocket carrying the NVS-03 satellite.

The exemption, which was earlier valid until the 31st of October, has been extended to ensure adequate availability of cotton for the Indian textile sector. The extension is expected to reduce input costs across the textile and apparel industries, providing relief to manufacturers and consumers while safeguarding the interests of domestic farmers.

India has called on the United Nations Human Rights Council to maintain sustained attention to the threat of terrorism, describing it as one of the gravest threats to human rights, particularly the right to life. Addressing the UN General Assembly in New York, First Secretary at India’s Permanent Mission to the United Nations, Bhavika Mangalanandan, said terrorism continues to pose a serious challenge to the enjoyment of human rights. The Meteorological Centre has issued a yellow warning for heavy rainfall in 14 districts today. Meanwhile, the rain-related situation in Odisha remains critical, with four people reported dead in separate incidents. A major landslide has also struck Gidipabali in Bhanjanagar, Ganjam district, where 12 people remain missing under the debris, according to official estimates. According to the preliminary assessment, Cyclone Arnab has caused losses estimated at four thousand seven hundred and sixty-four crore rupees across twelve districts. Konaseema and West Godavari are among the worst-affected districts. Meanwhile, relief and restoration operations are continuing across the affected districts. The State Government has announced an ex-gratia payment of five lakh rupees to the families of those who lost their lives in cyclone-related incidents.
