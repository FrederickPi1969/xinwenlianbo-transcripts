## Morning News

US President Donald Trump does not rule out sending ground troops into Iran.
Prime Minister Narendra Modi discusses West Asia situation with Saudi Crown Prince, and Kings of Bahrain and Jordan.

Conflict in West Asia has intensified as Iran and joint Israeli-US forces launched new waves of missile and drone attacks. Iranian-backed militias targeted US assets across Gulf countries, including the United Arab Emirates, Kuwait, and Bahrain, following the killing of Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei and other top officials. Israel and the US struck dozens of targets in Tehran, hitting Iranian military and intelligence sites.  Speaking at the White House, US President Donald Trump said their objectives are destroying Iran’s missile capabilities, annihilating their navy and ensuring that Iran can never obtain a nuclear weapon.  Later in a interview to private media channel, US President said the big wave is yet to come in conflict with Iran. In separate interview, Mr. Trump did not rule out sending US troops to Iran if it was necessary.

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio also said that hardest hits are yet to come from the US military and the next phase will be even more punishing on Iran than it is right now. He said that the world will be a safer place after completion of this operation.

During a Pentagon press briefing, US Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth  said more than 1,250 targets have been struck in the first 48 hours. The US military said B-2 stealth bombers struck Iran’s ballistic missile facilities with two thousand pound bombs. Meanwhile, Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu visited Beit Shemesh city, and met civilians hit by missile from Tehran. After meeting the locals, Mr Netanyahu said, this is the 3rd day of Operation Roaring Lion to thwart existential threats to Israel, and great threats to America and the entire world.

Meanwhile, the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) said, the recent losses of its supreme leader and other top officials have not shaken Iran, but rather made it stronger adding the enemy will no longer be safe anywhere in the world. Meanwhile, Iran’s president Masoud Pezeshkian has appointed Revolutionary Guards general Majid Ebnelreza as acting defence minister after his predecessor was killed in Israeli-US strikes.

On the other hand, United Arab Emirates (UAE)’s Ministry of Defence said that its air defenses successfully intercepted 9 ballistic missiles, 6 cruise missiles, 148 drones yesterday. UAE said that since the beginning of the Iranian attack, 174 ballistic missiles launched toward the country have been detected, of which 161 were destroyed, while 13 fell into the sea. A total of 689 Iranian drones were also detected, with 645 intercepted, while 44 landed within the country’s territory. Moreover, Iran’s Foreign Minister Seyed Abbas Araghchi said that over 160 young girls were killed in the US-Israeli bombing on a primary school.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has discussed the evolving situation in West Asia with Crown Prince and Prime Minister of Saudi Arabia, Mohammed bin Salman during a telephonic conversation. Mr Modi condemned the recent attacks on Saudi Arabia, describing them as a violation of its sovereignty and territorial integrity. In a social media post, he said that both sides agreed that the earliest restoration of regional peace and stability is of utmost importance. The Prime Minister also thanked Mr. Salman for looking after the well-being of the Indian community during these difficult times.

Mr. Modi also held a telephonic conversation with the King of Bahrain Hamad Bin Isa Al Khalifa. Condemning the attacks on Bahrain, Mr Modi said that India stands in solidarity with its people in this difficult hour. He thanked the King of Bahrain for the steadfast support extended to the Indian community in the country.

The Prime Minister also spoke to King Abdullah II of Jordan and conveyed deep concern at the evolving situation in the region. Mr Modi reaffirmed India’s support for peace, security and the well-being of the people of Jordan. In a social media post, Mr Modi said he thanked the King for taking care of the Indian community in Jordan in this difficult hour.

Flight operations from Dubai and Abu Dhabi to India resumed last night. In a major relief to thousands of Indians stranded in the UAE due to conflict in the West Asian region, flights to Delhi, Mumbai, and Bengaluru took off after days of suspension. Dubai Media Office said in a social media post that the Dubai Airports confirmed a limited resumption of flight operations last evening, with a small number of flights permitted to operate from Dubai International and Dubai World Central – Al Maktoum International. Travellers have been advised not to proceed to the two airports unless they have been contacted directly by their airline with a confirmed departure time.

Civil Aviation Ministry says, IndiGo plans to operate 10 special relief flights from Jeddah to India today to facilitate the return of stranded passengers, subject to required approvals and prevailing airspace conditions. In a statement the ministry said, IndiGo is coordinating with the Consulate General of India at Jeddah for passenger facilitation.  Union Minister for Civil Aviation Rammohan Naidu also reviewed the situation at a high-level meeting, attended by Civil Aviation Secretary Samir Kumar Sinha and all senior officials. The Ministry said it is closely monitoring the evolving airspace situation in parts of the Middle East and its impact on international flight operations. It said the special arrangements are being made to facilitate the movement of stranded passengers and the Airlines are deploying additional capacity where required. It further added that airlines are coordinating closely with foreign aviation authorities and Indian missions abroad to ensure safe and orderly passenger movement. The Ministry also advised passengers to check the latest flight status directly with their respective airlines before proceeding to the airport and to rely only on official sources for updates.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will address the Post-Budget Webinar today through video conferencing on the theme ‘Sustaining and Strengthening Economic Growth.’ The Prime Minister’s Office informed that the Post-Budget Webinar will highlight the forward-looking reform push to expand industrial scale, deepen technological leadership and strengthen the foundations of critical sectors. It said that the deliberations will focus on translating these priorities into effective action. They will be guided by insights from industry participants whose on-ground experience will support more effective implementation.

In Kerala, capital city of  Thiruvananthapuram is fully prepared for the annual Attukal Pongala festival today. Lakhs of women devotees from across the state have already started arriving to take part in the ritual at the Attukal Bhagavathy Temple. More from our correspondent:

Lakhs of women devotees have gathered in Kerala’s capital, Thiruvananthapuram, for the renowned Attukal Pongala festival. Devotees will prepare pongala, a sweet offering made of rice, jaggery and coconut, in earthen pots set up on temporary brick hearths across streets and open spaces in the city. As per tradition, only women offer pongala. The Adappu Vettu ritual and lighting of the ceremonial flame will take place at 9.45 this morning, marking the beginning of the offering. The pongala offering ritual  will be held at at 2.15 this afternoon. The festival will end by midnight with the offering of Kuruthi. Authorities have put in place elaborate arrangements, including traffic regulations, drinking water facilities, medical camps and enhanced security. A local holiday has been declared for government offices in the district. Smithy M Palluruthy/Akashvani News/ Thiruvananthapuram.

All England Badminton Championships will begin today in Birmingham. India will open their campaign today, with a 12-member squad across all five disciplines. Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that India continues to be a beacon of hope for the world with its strong economy. He said, as India emerges as a beacon of hope for the world, rapid economic development has become the cornerstone for realizing the vision of Viksit Bharat. The Prime minister was addressing a post-budget webinar on the theme Sustaining and Strengthening Economic Growth this morning.

The Prime Minister said, today, the world is looking for reliable and resilient manufacturing partners. He said, India has the opportunity to become a key player in this direction.

Prime Minister Modi said, government intention and resolve are clear to Build more, produce more, connect more and Export more. He said, it is everyones responsibility to never compromise on quality and the products should be of global standard.

The Prime Minister Modi said the global economy is undergoing a major transformation, with markets now focusing on sustainability alongside cost. He added that early investment in clean technologies will help industries access emerging markets. Mr. Modi highlighted the positive impact of government MSME reforms and stressed the need for high-capacity transport, multi-modal connectivity, and better coordination between railways, highways, ports, airports, and waterways to reduce logistics costs.

The Centre has set up two more empowered committees in West Bengal to decide on applications seeking Indian citizenship under the Citizenship Amendment Act, (CAA). In a gazette notification, the Union Home Ministry said, the empowered committee will be headed by an officer not below the rank of Deputy Secretary, to be nominated by the Registrar General and Census Commissioner of India. The decision has been taken to constitute two additional panels to fast-track the processing of large number of applications. The West Bengal already has two such committees.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has crossed the significant milestone of 30 million subscribers on YouTube today and continues to be the most-followed world leader on the platform. PM Modi joined Youtube in 2007. As per the rankings, he is comfortably ahead of others in the category. Mr Modi has more than seven times the number of subscribers compared to US President Donald Trump.

The Israel Defense Forces (IDF) said its soldiers are operating in southern Lebanon and are positioned at several points near the border area as part of an enhanced forward defense posture. It further said that Israel is conducting targeted strikes against Hezbollah’s infrastructure. Israel also alleged that Hezbollah chose to attack Israel on behalf of the Iranian regime. A report:

“Israel has confirmed that the Commander of Palestinian Islamic Jihad (PIJ), Abu Hamza Rami, has been killed in Beirut. Moreover, the United States Central Command has said that the US forces have destroyed Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRCG) command and control facilities, Iranian air defense capabilities, missile and drone launch sites, and military airfields during sustained operations. The U.S. embassy in Saudi Arabia’s Riyadh was also hit by two drones from Tehran that resulted in a limited fire and minor material damage. CHIRAG JHA, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

US President Donald Trump will meet German Chancellor Friedrich Merz at the White House in Washington, D.C. today amid the ongoing conflict in the West Asia region. The engagement between both the leaders will be followed by a bilateral meeting in the Oval Office.

Normal life remains affected across Kashmir for the second consecutive day as authorities impose restrictions following protests over the killing of Iran’s Supreme Leader Ayatullah Syed Ali Khamenei. Schools and colleges remain closed, and examinations have been postponed, with precautionary measures aimed at maintaining law and order and preventing untoward incidents.

The Directorate General of Shipping has said that three Indian seafarers, serving on board foreign-flagged vessels, were killed and one person was injured amid the escalating tensions in the West Asia region. The Directorate, IFC-IOR, MRCC and other authorities are maintaining close coordination with all stakeholders to ensure their continued safety, well-being and timely support.

The Central Board of Secondary Education has postponed Class 10th and Class 12th examinations in the West Asia region scheduled for the 5th and 6th of this month. The board informed students and schools that the decision has been taken due to the current situation in Bahrain, Iran, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia and the UAE. The Board will further review the situation on Thursday and will take appropriate decisions.

Afghan forces have seized key Pakistani military posts along the border as clashes along the Durand Line entered the fifth consecutive day. Afghanistan’s Defence Ministry said its forces carried out retaliatory strikes on Pakistani bases following recent air raids by the Pakistan Air Force inside Afghan territory. Afghan officials claimed that multiple Pakistani posts were captured in Kandahar province, including three in the Spin Boldak district and two in the Ali-Sher district.

The escalation marks the latest surge in tensions along the Durand Line, where hostilities have intensified since Pakistan’s strikes on the 21st and 22nd of last month.

Crude oil prices steadied today after a sharp surge in the previous session amid escalating tensions in West Asia. According to media reports, US crude futures rose 1.4 per cent to 72.23 dollars a barrel, while Brent crude gained nearly 1.9 per cent to trade at 79.2 dollars in the early trade.

The spike follows Iran’s retaliatory strikes on oil and gas facilities and concerns over supply disruptions through the strategic Strait of Hormuz, a key route for global energy shipments. Around 20 per cent of global oil flows pass through the strait, including over 40 per cent of India’s crude imports.

Investment bank Morgan Stanley has warned that Brent crude could touch 120 dollars per barrel in case of prolonged disruption.

Indian stock markets remained closed today on account of Holi. Trading was halted at both the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) and the National Stock Exchange (NSE) across all segments, including equities, equity derivatives, currency derivatives, interest rate derivatives and securities lending and borrowing Scheme. The currency and commodity markets also remained closed.

A total lunar eclipse will take place across the globe in several time zones today. In India, this first rare celestial event is going to occur at 3:20 pm and will end at 06:47 pm. According to the India Meteorological Department, this will be a major event of the year and sky watchers and astronomers are extremely excited for this event.

On the occasion of Holi tomorrow, services of Delhi Metro will not be available till 2:30 PM on all lines of the Delhi Metro including the Airport Express Line. According to the Delhi Metro Rail Corporation, train services will start at 2:30 PM from terminal stations on all Lines and will continue normally thereafter.

In a social media post, Mr Radhakrishnan urged every citizen to reaffirm their commitment to not only to conserve the rich biodiversity of India but also to restore critical species and ecosystems.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi reaffirmed the government’s commitment to wildlife conservation on World Wildlife Day. In a social media message, he said the day celebrates the planet’s rich faunal diversity and recognises the efforts of those working to protect wildlife. He reiterated India’s commitment to conservation, sustainable practices and habitat protection.

All England Badminton Championships will begin in Birmingham this evening. India will open their campaign, with a 12-member squad across all five disciplines. The responsibility of leading the charge has shifted to Lakshya Sen as two-time Olympic medallist PV Sindhu is stranded in Dubai following the closure of Gulf airspace amid the US-Iran conflict. India Meteorological Department (IMD) has forecast thunderstorms accompanied by lightning over Arunachal Pradesh, Assam and Meghalaya today. ## Evening News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today spoke with leaders of Kuwait, Qatar, and Oman on West Asia Conflict. During his telephonic phone conversations, Mr Modi strongly condemned the violation of sovereignty and territorial integrity of these countries. He appreciated their continued support to ensuring safety and well-being of the Indian community. The Prime Minister also stressed the significance of dialogue and diplomacy for earliest restoration of regional peace and stability.

In a social media post, Prime Minister Modi said, he spoke to Crown Prince of Kuwait, Sheikh Sabah Al-Khaled Al-Hamad Al-Mubarak Al-Sabah. Mr Modi said India stands with Kuwait’s people in this difficult hour.

The Prime Minister also spoke to the Amir of Qatar, Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani and expressed firm solidarity with Qatar.

Mr Modi also spoke with Sultan of Oman, Haitham bin Tarik and exchanged views on recent developments in West Asia.

External Affairs Minister Dr S Jaishankar held a telephone conversation today with EU High Representative for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy, Kaja Kallas on the ongoing conflict in the Gulf region.

Earlier, in a statement, External Affairs Ministry spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal strongly reiterated India’s call for dialogue and diplomacy in the ongoing conflict in the West Asia region. Mr Jaiswal said, there are almost one crore Indian citizens who live and work in the Gulf region and their safety and well-being is India’s utmost priority. He expressed grief at the loss of several lives in the conflict. The spokesperson assured that government will continue to closely monitor the evolving situation and take relevant decisions in the national interest. Our correspondent has filed this report:

“The spokesperson stressed that there are almost one crore Indian citizens who live and work in the Gulf region and their safety and well-being is of utmost priority. He said India cannot be impervious to any development that negatively affects them. Stating that trade and energy supply chains also traverse this geography, Mr Jaiswal underlined that any major disruption has serious consequences for the Indian economy. As a country whose nationals are prominent in the global workforce, India is also firmly opposed to attacks on merchant shipping. The spokesperson said some Indian nationals have lost their lives or are missing as a result of such attacks in the last few days. The spokesperson said these developments evoke anxiety as a proximate neighbour with critical stakes in the security and stability of the region. Suparna Saikia, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

The Embassy of India in Iran has relocated most Indian students who were in Tehran to safer locations outside the city due to the heightened risk perception in the Iranian capital. In an advisory, the Embassy said only a small number of students who declined the Embassy’s offer remain in Tehran. They have been asked to exercise due caution at all times and avoid areas of protests or demonstrations.

Besides, the first flight of Air India, carrying 149 passengers and 8 operating crew members arrived in New Delhi from Dubai today.

In view of the evolving global energy situation, the government has ensured both availability and affordability of energy for its population by diversifying its sources. The Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas said, India has sufficient energy reserves to deal with the ongoing situation. Highlighting the country’s preparedness, the Ministry said that India is well stocked with crude oil and inventories of key petroleum products, including petrol, diesel and ATF, to deal with short-term disruptions. The Ministry has also established a 24×7 Control Room to continuously monitor the supply and stock position of petroleum products across the country.

Union Minister of Commerce and Industry Piyush Goyal today held a consultation meeting with all stakeholder Ministries, key logistics and trade facilitation partners to review the emerging geopolitical situation and its potential impact on India’s Exports and Imports. In a social media post, Mr Goyal informed that in this context, the Inter-Ministerial Group for Supply Chain Resilience composed of members from Department of Financial Services, Ministry of External Affairs, along with other Ministry’s has been created to facilitate effective coordination, monitoring and follow-up.

Meanwhile, the West Asia conflict has escalated sharply as the United States and Israel intensified strikes on Iran. Tehran and its allies retaliated with missile and drone attacks targeting Israel, Gulf countries and key energy infrastructure, widening the regional conflict. The killing of Iranian Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei has further deepened uncertainty. As per latest reports, Israel is currently conducting simultaneous targeted strikes against military targets in Iranian capital, Tehran and against Iran backed armed group Hezbollah’s infrastructure in capital of Lebanon Beirut. The International Atomic Energy Agency confirmed structural damage at Iran’s nuclear site in Natanz, following a review of the most recent satellite images. In a statement, the UN nuclear watchdog said, there were no signs of radiation leaks and no further impact on the main underground enrichment plant. A 4.3-magnitude earthquake struck Iran’s Gerash region, amid the escalating conflict in the Islamic Republic. There were no immediate indications of major damage or injuries.

On the other hand, the Iranian state media said that a command building at a US air base in Bahrain has been destroyed. Bahrain also said that its air defences intercepted 70 Iranian ballistic missiles and 59 drones targeting the Kingdom. The U.S. embassy in Saudi Arabia’s Riyadh was also hit by two drones from Tehran that resulted in a limited fire and minor material damage.
