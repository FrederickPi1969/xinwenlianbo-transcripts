Chhattisgarh Assembly has passed the Chhattisgarh Freedom of Religion Bill, 2026, by voice vote. The legislation seeks to prohibit religious conversions carried out through deceit, force, allurement, or digital means. Under the provisions of the new Bill, any attempt at religious conversion through fraudulent means, coercion, or digital platforms will be a cognizable and non-bailable offence. The legislation prescribes stringent punishment, including imprisonment ranging from seven to ten years. Convicts will also face a minimum fine of five lakh rupees. The Bill also makes it mandatory for individuals intending to change their religion to file a formal application with the competent authority.

The Bill was passed in the absence of the opposition, who staged a walkout after their demand to refer the legislation to a Select Committee was declined.
