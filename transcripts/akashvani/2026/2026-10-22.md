## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will hold talks with his Polish counterpart Donald Tusk in New Delhi today. Both leaders will review the entire spectrum of bilateral relations during their talks and also exchange views on regional and global issues of mutual interest. Several agreements are expected to be signed after the talks.

Prime Minister Tusk is being accompanied by Minister of Finance and Economy, Andrzej Domanski, and a large business delegation. This is his second visit to India in his capacity as the Prime Minister of Poland. He first visited India during his earlier tenure in 2010. Prime Minister Tusk has also visited New Delhi in 2017 for the India-EU Summit in his capacity as President of the European Council.

Prime Minister Tusk will also travel to Bengaluru during his visit, where he will engage with various business stakeholders including from the IT and space sectors. Our correspondent has filed this report:

“India-Poland bilateral relations were elevated to a Strategic Partnership during the visit of Prime Minister Modi to Poland in August 2024. Both countries celebrated 70 years of diplomatic relations in 2024. Bilateral trade was pegged at over 6 billion dollars last year. Poland is India’s largest trading and investment partner in Central and Eastern Europe. Indian investments in Poland are value at over 3 billion dollars. Around 3500 Indian students are pursuing higher education in that country. Poland is home to a strong Indian diaspora of over 30 thousand. The visit will provide the opportunity to further strengthen the India-Poland Strategic Partnership and advance cooperation in areas of mutual interest, including trade and investment, defence, emerging technologies, renewable energy, education, culture and people-to-people ties. It would also serve to deepen the India-EU strategic partnership. Suparna Saikia, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

President Droupadi Murmu will be on a two-day visit to Chhattisgarh beginning today. During her visit, she will attend a series of programmes in Raipur and Jagdalpur. President Murmu will participate in events for the Nuakhai festival in Raipur today, and attend the Muriya Darbar and a public programme celebrating Bastar Dussehra in Jagdalpur tomorrow. More from our correspondent:

“President Murmu will arrive at the Swami Vivekananda Airport in Raipur this morning and will proceed to Lok Bhavan, where she is scheduled to release a commemorative coin of two hundred rupees honouring Pujya Mata Hasi Devi Ji. Union Agriculture and Farmers Welfare Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan will launch the nationwide ‘Khet Bachao Abhiyan-Rabi 2026’ from Karnal in Haryana today. The campaign will continue until November 30th. The initiative is aimed at promoting sustainable agriculture, improve soil health and encourage the judicious use of agricultural resources. The campaign aims to create awareness among farmers about the balanced and judicious use of fertilizers and other agricultural inputs based on soil testing. As part of the campaign, farmers’ conferences, Kisan Chaupals, training sessions and field demonstrations will be organized at the state, district, block and Gram Panchayat levels. Agriculture departments, Krishi Vigyan Kendras, ICAR institutes and agricultural universities will work together to ensure wider participation to strengthen public outreach.

The Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM) has imposed stage one measures in Delhi-NCR under the Graded Response Action Plan (GRAP) as air quality slipped into the ‘poor’ category. A report:

“In a statement, CAQM said, Air Quality lndex (AQI) of Delhi which was observed 179 (Moderate category) on Tuesday, has exhibited a sharp rise with AQI of 244 at 2 pm on Wednesday (Poor category). It added that the forecast by the India Meteorological Department (IMD) and the Indian Institute of Tropical Meteorology have predicted AQI to remain in the ‘poor’ category in the coming days. Under GRAP-1, restrictions are placed, such as banning the use of coal and firewood as fuel in tandoors in hotels, restaurants, and open eateries, and allowing the use of diesel generators only in emergency or essential situations. GRAP is a set of emergency measures that kick in to prevent further deterioration of air quality once it reaches a certain threshold. The first stage, GRAP-1, kicks in when the AQI is between 201 and 300; the second stage, GRAP-2, is invoked when the AQI is between 301 and 400; GRAP-3 kicks in between 401 and 500; and GRAP-4 is invoked when the AQI is more than 450. WITH SHIVANG’S REPORT, KAUMUDEE PAREEK,  AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI”

Delhi government has proposed capping the number of e-rickshaws in the national capital at 2.5 lakh. In this regard, the government has placed the draft E-Rickshaw Policy in the public domain for one month, inviting suggestions and feedback from stakeholders before finalising the regulatory framework.

The proposed policy aims to streamline e-rickshaw operations, improve road safety and strengthen last-mile connectivity while addressing traffic congestion. Delhi Transport Minister Dr Pankaj Kumar Singh said that the government is considering a cap of 2.5 lakh e-rickshaws in Delhi, with mandatory compliance certification, fitness requirements, valid licensing and vehicle identification to ensure that only eligible vehicles operate on city roads. Dr Singh said that following consultation and finalisation of the policy, the government proposes to initiate its implementation process within 90 days.

The Delhi Metro Rail Corporation (DMRC) has announced that it will operate 20 additional train trips on weekdays from today following the implementation of GRAP-I in Delhi. The DMRC will also intensify anti-pollution measures at its construction sites and surrounding areas across Delhi to help curb dust and pollution. It said that this decision has been taken to encourage more and more people to use public transport rather than their personal vehicles. DMRC said that in total, over 250 anti-smog guns and mist guns have been installed at the construction sites, stations and office buildings to decrease dust levels. Meanwhile, daily inspections are being conducted by DMRC’s Environment department to ensure that all environmental norms are adequately followed.

State Election Commission of Ladakh has announced that the elections to all panchayat halqas in the Union Territory will be held on November 21. Prime Minister Narendra Modi today held bilateral talks with his Poland’s counterpart Donald Tusk in New Delhi. During their talks, both the leaders reviewed the entire spectrum of bilateral relations and also exchange views on regional and global issues of mutual interest. Addressing the joint press meet, Prime Minister Modi said that the world is undergoing a period of uncertainty and transformation and despite these challenges, India and Poland are emerging as important centres of growth, technology and innovation. He said, today, both nations have agreed to boost cooperation in areas of defence, space and nuclear energy. Mr Modi invited companies from Poland to engage in co-development and co-production with India in all these strategic sectors.  He expressed confidence that companies from both countries will work together to develop trusted supply chains for the world and achieve the shared goal of doubling bilateral trade by 2030.

Mr Modi said, the India-European Union Free Trade Agreement has created new opportunities for the India-Poland partnership. He said, Once the agreement comes into effect, it will open up new opportunities for Indian exports to Poland in machinery, chemicals, plastics and engineering goods.

Prime Minister Modi said, they also held detailed discussions on regional and global developments. He said, both India and Poland believe in democratic values and the rule of law. Mr Modi said, both the countries believe that reforms are necessary to make global governance institutions stronger and more effective. He said whether it is Ukraine or West Asia, India will continue to support every meaningful effort towards peace.

Prime Minister Modi said that the two nation also agreed to establish the India-Poland Startup Bridge. He said, these initiatives will give fresh momentum to cooperation in AI, cyber, fintech and other emerging technologies. The Prime Minister Tusk arrived in New Delhi last evening on a three day visit to India. He is being accompanied by Minister of Finance and Economy, Andrzej Domanski, and a large business delegation. This is his second visit to India in his capacity as the Prime Minister of Poland.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah today inaugurated and laid the foundation stones for projects worth over eight thousand 941 crore rupees related to the rejuvenation of the Yamuna River. The expansion of sewer networks and improvements to the drainage system mark significant steps towards cleaning the Yamuna River. Speaking on the occasion, Union Home Minister Amit Shah said that the BJP government is committed to cleaning and rejuvenating the Yamuna by May 2029. He said the Centre and the governments of Delhi, Haryana and Uttar Pradesh had prepared coordinated, time-bound action plans to tackle pollution in the river. Mr. Shah accused the previous Aam Aadmi Party government of making excuses instead of addressing the issue. He reiterated that cleaning and rejuvenating the Yamuna remained a firm commitment of the BJP government. The Minister said the projects inaugurated and launched today will strengthen wastewater treatment, improve drinking water facilities and expand sewer connectivity across Delhi.

Union Minister for Chemicals and Fertilisers J. P. Nadda has said that India aims to develop a one-trillion-dollar chemicals market by 2040. Inaugurating the 14th edition of India Chem 2026 at the Bombay Exhibition Centre, NESCO, in Mumbai today, he said a favourable environment is emerging in the country for the growth of the chemicals and petrochemicals sector. He added that clear policies, government support and positive industry participation are opening up new opportunities for investment and growth in the sector. Mr. Nadda said that under the government_s proposed _Bhavya Rasayan_ scheme, the Centre would provide one thousand crore rupees for each chemical park, while the concerned state would have to contribute a minimum of 500 crore rupees. He said the development of world-class infrastructure, including port, rail, air and road connectivity, would be crucial to the selection and success of these parks.

President Droupadi Murmu released a commemorative two hundred rupees coin to mark the 200th birth anniversary of Pujya Mata Sahib Hasi Devi Ji at Lok Bhawan in Raipur, Chhattisgarh, today. Hundreds of members of the Sindhi community attended the event. Pujya Mata Hasi Devi Ji was the fifth head of the historic Shadani Darbar, a revered spiritual and cultural center for the Sindhi community.

Following this programme, President Murmu will attend the ‘Seva Samman Parv’ and ‘Yuva Prabodhan’ events at the Sardar Balbir Singh Juneja Indoor Stadium in Raipur as part of the Nuakhai celebrations.

The theme of this edition is Thirukkural Payilvom – Let Us Learn the Thirukkural. Union Agriculture and Farmers Welfare Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan will launch the nationwide ‘Khet Bachao Abhiyan-Rabi 2026’ from Karnal in Haryana today.
