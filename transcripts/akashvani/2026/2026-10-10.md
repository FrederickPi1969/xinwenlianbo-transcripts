## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has called for greater focus on the health, nutrition and overall development of adolescent girls. The Prime Minister made the remarks while chairing the valedictory session of the two-day National Departmental Summit on Women and Child Development in New Delhi yesterday. Mr Modi emphasized the importance of Jan Bhagidari (people’s participation) and called for Anganwadi Centres to be developed as centres of community participation. He said families living near Anganwadi Centres should be encouraged to contribute on special occasions by sharing nutritious food, toys and other locally relevant items for children.

Discussing issues related to women’s safety, Mr Modi advised the development of age-appropriate modules for children on women’s safety, dignity and empowerment. He also stressed the importance of preventive measures through coordinated efforts across various departments.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has stressed the need for young people to continuously upgrade their skills and adapt to emerging technologies amid rapid technological advancements. He made these remarks while interacting with members of Team India who participated in the WorldSkills Competition Shanghai 2026 at his residence in New Delhi yesterday.

Congratulating the participants on representing India on the global stage, Mr Modi urged them to develop skills in line with the requirements of the international market. He also encouraged them to contribute to strengthening India’s skill training ecosystem by sharing their experiences and suggestions, and urged them to become ambassadors of skill development.

The Delhi Police has denied permission for the proposed protest by the CJP at Jantar Mantar in the national capital today, citing previous violations of guidelines and public safety concerns. Addressing the media in New Delhi yesterday, Delhi Police Joint Commissioner Nupur Prasad said, applications seeking permission to hold protests or other programmes at Jantar Mantar must be submitted at least 10 days in advance. The Joint Commissioner said the decision to deny permission was taken in view of the party’s previous failure to adhere to Delhi Police guidelines.

The Joint Commissioner warned that Delhi Police will take appropriate legal action against anyone found violating the law.

“Ahead of the proposed protest at Jantar Mantar today, elaborate security arrangements have been made across the national capital. DCP Railway B. Meanwhile, due to security reasons, the entry and exit at 45 metro stations will remain closed today till 06:00 PM. These include Janpath, Patel Chowk, Central Secretariat, Lok Kalyan Marg, Rajiv Chowk, New Delhi, Seva Teerth and Mandi House. The Delhi Metro Rail Corporation (DMRC) said that the situation will be reviewed by the security agencies and necessary updates will be shared accordingly. Namo Bharat train services between Sarai Kale Khan and New Ashok Nagar Namo Bharat stations, in Delhi, will also remain closed today till 10:00 pm. The United States has temporarily lifted sanctions on the sale and import of Russian-origin diesel fuel until 7th of April, 2027, following a conversation between US President Donald Trump and Russian President Vladimir Putin. Speaking to media persons outside the White House, President Trump said, Russia will immediately supply more than three lakh tonnes of diesel, followed by five lakh tonnes in November, one million tonnes thereafter and an additional three million tonnes, depending on the condition of Russian refineries.

The Government has debunked an article claiming that Indian Air Force suffered losses during the Balakot airstrike. The Fact Check Unit of the Press Information Bureau (PIB) informed that the article misrepresents the nature and execution of the operation, presenting a misleading narrative that distorts the facts. The Unit added that Balakot airstrike was a deliberate, targeted operation conducted by the Indian Air Force against a designated terrorist facility. PIB said that the operation underscored the IAF’s precision-strike capability, operational preparedness and resolve to deliver a calibrated response to terrorism.

Mahalaya is being observed today, marking the traditional invocation of Goddess Durga and heralding the arrival of Durga Puja, Bengal’s biggest annual festival. Here is report from our Kolkata correspondent:

The day marks the culmination of Pitru Paksha, the fortnight dedicated to paying homage to departed ancestors, and the beginning of Devi Paksha, according to traditional Hindu belief. Devotees across the state are offering tarpan on the banks of rivers and other water bodies, praying for the peace of their ancestors’ souls. Mahalaya is also synonymous with the timeless radio programme Mahishasuramardini, broadcast by Akashvani in the early hours of the day. The programme, featuring Sanskrit chants, devotional songs and narration by Birendra Krishna Bhadra, has remained an integral part of Bengal’s cultural heritage for generations. With the invocation of Goddess Durga, the countdown to Durga Puja begins, filling the air with devotion, anticipation and festive spirit. Sandipon Dasgupta/Kolkata.

The portals of Shri Hemkund Sahib, the renowned Sikh pilgrimage site in Chamoli district of Uttarakhand, were closed for winter today. The final Ardas, or prayer, was performed at 12:30 PM, following which the portals were closed at 1 PM in accordance with religious traditions.

The portals of Shri Hemkund Sahib were opened to pilgrims on May 23 this year. During the pilgrimage season, around two lakh eighty thousand devotees paid obeisance at the sacred shrine.
