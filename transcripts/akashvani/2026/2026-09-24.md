## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will interact today via video conference with participants of Seva Yatra travelling from Vadnagar to Varanasi under Seva Sankalp Abhiyan. The interaction with the Prime Minister will give the Seva Yatris a chance to share their experiences from the journey, which is intended to connect youth participation and community service across the locations covered by the teams. A report:-

The Seva Yatra began on September 17 and is part of the wider ‘Seva Sankalp Abhiyan’ to highlight PM Modi’s 25 years of public life.  The participants are travelling through towns, districts, talukas and villages, with the programme focusing on taking community service and public engagement activities to the grassroots. The journey is not limited to a motorcycle expedition, but is designed to bring together service, communication, youth participation, social welfare, cultural connect and community mobilisation at different locations along the route. Ten groups are undertaking the Vadnagar-to-Varanasi journey. The two-way motorcycle yatras between Vadnagar and Varanasi involve young participants travelling across multiple states, districts and villages. During the journey, the Seva Yatris have engaged with local communities and participated in service-oriented activities. These include cleanliness drives and interactions with beneficiaries and residents, as well as activities at schools, hospitals, nursing homes, and other community locations. The journey is scheduled to continue until October 7. HARSHITA OJHA, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

The Delhi Cabinet has approved the DURGA E-Auto scheme aimed at providing safe, clean and dignified employment opportunities to women and transgender persons in the national capital. Under this scheme, one thousand three hundred pink electric autos will be allocated in the first phase. More details in this Report:

“This scheme was approved in the cabinet meeting chaired by Chief Minister Rekha Gupta in New Delhi yesterday. Under this scheme, one thousand 300 pink electric autos will be allocated in the first phase of these, one thousand one hundred 70 autos will be reserved for women and 130 for transgender beneficiaries. The Chief Minister said the scheme will give women and transgender persons an opportunity to become owners and drivers of electric autos. She said that a maximum of 90 electric autos for women and up to 10 electric autos for transgender beneficiaries will be allocated in each revenue district. Ms Gupta said the scheme is an initiative towards making Delhi a safer, greener and more inclusive city. With Akshit, Saklen Akhtar, FOR Akashvani News, Delhi.”

Finance Ministry has informed that all Public Sector Banks and Regional Rural Banks will remain open on Sunday, 27th of this month for convenience of customers. The Ministry said that this step has been taken in view of the proposed three-day nationwide bank strike called by the United Forum of Bank Unions (UFBU) from 28th to 30th of this month, coinciding with the preceding weekend holidays of 26th and 27th September. It said that the Reserve Bank of India has granted approval for all bank branches, offices, ATM-link branches and Currency Chests to remain fully operational on Sunday. The Ministry said that the Government and bank managements have appealed to unions to defer the proposed strike, and have also taken measures to ensure that essential banking services continue un-interrupted.

The Government has reduced the Basic Customs Duty on major imported crude edible oils with a view to moderating domestic edible oil prices. This provides relief to consumers and mitigate inflationary pressures arising from the sharp increase in international edible oil prices.
