The Ministry of Civil Aviation (MoCA) has initiated a series of reforms to make aviation security processes simpler and quicker for the people working at Indian Airports. Briefing the media on the Bureau of Civil Aviation Security (BCAS) Reforms to Improve Operational Efficiency and Ease of Doing Business in New Delhi, Civil Aviation Secretary Samir Kumar Sinha said that these sweeping changes aim to digitize workflows, making processes simpler, and transition towards accountability-driven governance while maintaining the highest security standards.

The Ministry informed that BCAS has streamlined the security clearance framework by moving towards a category-and contract-agnostic security clearance which is applicable across the nine identified categories of aviation entities. It is proposed to remain valid for five years as part of these reforms.

India’s women’s recurve archery team has created history by winning its maiden Asian Games gold medal, defeating world champions South Korea 5-3 in a thrilling final today.

Ankita Bhakat, Kirti Sharma and Kumkum Mohod held their nerve in windy conditions to edge past the 10-time Olympic champions.

After the first two sets were tied, India seized the advantage in the third set with a 55-point score against Korea’s 52. In the fourth set, a crucial score correction turned Kirti Sharma’s 8 into a 9, taking India to 54 points and sealing the gold.

Earlier, India had defeated China 5-1 in the semifinal to reach its first-ever Asian Games women’s recurve final.

This is India’s first Asian Games gold in women’s recurve team event.
