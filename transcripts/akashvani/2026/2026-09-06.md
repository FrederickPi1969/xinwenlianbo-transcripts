## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has urged young Indians to dream even bigger than previous generations and build global consulting, accounting, financial and legal firms capable of leading the world. Mr Modi said, Today, Indians are leading the top companies of the world, so why can’t companies built by Indians lead the world? Prime Minister was addressing centenary celebrations of Shri Ram College of Commerce (SRCC) at Delhi University yesterday evening.

Mr Modi said, India’s 7.8 percent quarterly growth amid global uncertainty reflects its economic strength. He said, India has come a long way, from policy paralysis to policy dynamism. He said the world is watching India become the fastest growing major economy. Mr Modi shared that smartphone exports now exceed 2.5 lakh crore rupees, while domestic defense production has hit an all-time high of nearly two lakh crore rupees.

The Prime Minister said the SRCC has given generations of students the opportunity to learn, grow and make a difference over the years. Mr Modi said the college has distinguished itself over the years, making a mark for academic excellence. He stated that former Finance Minister Arun Jaitley was an alumnus of SRCC. He said SRCC alumni have left their mark in every sector, from arts to politics. Mr Modi added that together, 140 crore Indians will make India both Viksit and Aatmanirbhar.

The Prime Minister highlighted the profound significance of this 100-year milestone for both the college and the nation’s education sector, and paid tribute to founder Shri Ram and bowed to his enduring vision.

Education Minister Pralhad Joshi, in his speech, praised SRCC for maintaining its high standard over the years. He said today India is the third largest Start Up ecosystem under the leadership of Prime Minister Modi. He said there is requirement of skilled human resources in the global market.

Dr Sharma will succeed Admiral Devendra Kumar Joshi who was serving as Lt Governor of the Islands since October 2017. Dr Sharma is presently a BJP Rajya Sabha MP from Uttar Pradesh has also served as Deputy Chief Minister of the state.

In Chhattisgarh, the National Investigation Agency’s special court in Jagdalpur has convicted all ten accused in Jhiram Valley Maoist attack case under various charges. A total of eleven accused were tried in the case, of whom one died during the trial.

The court found all ten accused, including two women, guilty of the murder of twenty-nine people and attempt to murder fifteen others. The order on the quantum of punishment has been reserved for September 16th.

In May 2013, Maoists attacked a convoy of Congress leaders in Jhiram Valley. 29 people including senior Congress leaders were killed in this dastardly attack.

More than 500 institutional investors, finance ministers and business leaders will participate in the inaugural iBRICS Summit, on the sidelines of BRICS Summit in New Delhi on 12th and 13th of this month. The two-day summit is designed to connect sovereign capital with bankable infrastructure, energy, and digital capacity projects across 21 BRICS member states and partner nations. Sovereign wealth funds, pension funds and family offices attending the iBRICS Summit manage a total wealth of one trillion US Dollars. They will discuss cross-border investments, non-dollar settlements and payment systems among BRICS economies.

The summit comes at a critical juncture as BRICS economies adapt to shifting trade dynamics, US tariff pressures, and high dollar-clearing costs.

As BRICS Chair 2026, India will be hosting the 18th BRICS Summit on September 12-13 at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi.

The Melghat and Pench tiger reserves in Maharashtra have emerged as India’s most successful sites for the reintroduction of captive-bred vultures, recording encouraging survival rates. Details from our correspondent:

“According to the Bombay Natural History Society, or BNHS, 29 vultures released across the two reserves between December 2025 and January 2026 recorded survival rates of up to 78 per cent. BNHS said the success demonstrates the viability of shifting bird release enclosures to prey-rich and inviolate tiger habitats. The results are a significant boost to ongoing efforts to conserve and restore India’s declining vulture populations. The encouraging survival rates offer hope that such conservation efforts will help revive vulture populations and ensure a stronger future for these important birds. With C Prarthana, Madhuri Pange, Akashvani News, Mumbai.”

The Prime Minister also highlighted that the world now praises India’s policy dynamism and its status as the fastest-growing major economy. He asserted that achieving a 7.8 percent quarterly growth amid the West Asia war and global trade hurdles demonstrates India’s intrinsic capability.

The Prime Minister also celebrated the success of the Make in India initiative in transforming the nation from a massive importer to a leading exporter. During his address, he shared that smartphone exports now exceed 2.5 lakh crore rupees, while domestic defense production has hit an all-time high of nearly 2 lakh crore rupees. Mr Modi also noted that the defence exports which were just 600 to 700 crore rupees in 2013-14 has reached 40 thousand crore rupees now.  Mr Modi also highlighted India’s unprecedented speed in infrastructure development and policy execution, with projects that earlier took decades now being completed in just a few years. He said rapid electrification of the railway network and a growing number of Free Trade Agreements are opening up new opportunities for the youth of the country.

The External Affairs Ministry responded to the UN General Assembly resolution on equal-area world map. Spokesperson of Ministry of External Affairs Randhir Jaiswal said that India has voted in favour of the United Nations General Assembly resolution on equal-area cartographic representation. Mr Jaiswal further added that India’s sovereign territory, including the Union Territories of Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh, must be depicted in accordance with India’s official map. He also clarified that any inaccurate or misleading depiction is unacceptable. He said the government’s position on India’s sovereignty and territorial integrity is clear, consistent and non-negotiable.

Mr Jaiswal said that the UN General Assembly adopted a resolution titled “Correct the Map Rebalancing Global Cartographic Representation and Promoting Equitable Representation of the World’s Regions, Particularly Africa” on 4th of this month. He said, the purpose of this is to encourage the use of equal-area map projections that preserve the relative sizes of continental landmasses. The Spokesperson said, India voted in favour of the resolution and also made an explanation of vote stressing on the underlying principle of promoting equal – area cartographic representation. He highlighted that the resolution does not constitute endorsement of any specific map, projection, or depiction of national boundaries.

Ten people have been confirmed dead after allegedly drinking suspected spurious and illegal liquor in Sagar district of Madhya Pradesh. Sagar Collector Pratibha Pal confirmed the deaths. Around 35 people were brought to hospitals. Some of them are still in serious condition. Serious patients have been shifted to Bundelkhand Medical College and Sagar District Hospital. The case is reported from Naoroja and Ghughra Khurd villages under Banda police station area. According to the Collector, the first information was received at around 4:30 AM yesterday. Screening has now started in the affected villages. People who consumed the liquor are also being checked.

The Collector said that initial findings from two to three post-mortems suggest possible consumption of methyl alcohol. Some people have been taken into custody for questioning. The administration has ordered some liquor shops in the area to be sealed as a precaution and their liquor samples to be collected. Chief Minister Dr. Mohan Yadav said he has directed the minister in charge and officials to immediately investigate the matter.
