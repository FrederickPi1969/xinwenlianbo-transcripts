## Midday News

BRICS leaders have stressed that energy security is a crucial foundation for social and economic development, national security and the welfare of all nations. During the two day BRICS summit held in New Delhi, the member nations highlighted the need to enhance energy security by ensuring energy market stability and maintaining undisrupted flows of energy from diverse sources and protection of critical energy infrastructure. During the Summit, Prime Minister Narendra Modi cautioned that the weaponisation of technology and critical minerals could become a hurdle to progress. More from our correspondent.

India hosted the summit under the theme Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability, placing people-centred development and the interests of the Global South at the heart of its presidency. The most significant outcome of the BRICS summit was the adoption of the New Delhi Declaration, which outlined the shared position on major geopolitical and economic issues. On the situation in West Asia, BRICS leaders called for maximum restraint, dialogue and diplomacy, and stressed the need to protect civilians and civilian infrastructure. The leaders strongly condemned terrorism in all its forms and manifestations and reiterated their commitment to a policy of zero tolerance towards terrorism. A consensus was reached on a ‘BRICS Integrated Early Warning System’ for the prevention of and response to infectious diseases. This summit was highly significant from the perspective of global politics, the economy, and multilateral cooperation. With Dipendra Kumar, Aman Yadav, Akashvani News Delhi.

The Government has said that the security situation in Jammu and Kashmir remains firmly under control due to the relentless efforts of the army.  In its latest annual report 2025-26, the Defence Ministry said that these efforts have led to a significant decline in violence, a reduction in protests, and zero incidents of stone pelting.

According to the report, the situation within Jammu and Kashmir and along the Line of Control had significantly altered during Operation Sindoor. However, after the Director Generals of Military Operations level talks last year, the situation has stabilised to a large extent, though the unpredictability continues.

The report also highlighted the overall situation with China. The year 2025 witnessed a significant reduction in the deployment of China’s People Liberation Army  Forces opposite the Northern Borders, with 10 Combined Arms Brigade-sized forces each deployed in areas along the Line of Actual Control (LAC) and in traditional training areas. The bilateral interactions between India and China at political, diplomatic and military levels facilitated positive developments along the Northern Borders, leading to stability. According to the report, the deployment of the Indian Army in all sectors along the LAC is robust, well-poised, and prepared to deal with any emerging contingency.
