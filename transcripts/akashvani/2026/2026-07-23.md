## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that welfare of citizens must remain at the heart of all reforms, and governance initiatives should be designed around people’s needs. He said, reforms should not only improve the functioning of the government but also accelerate India’s journey towards becoming a developed nation. We have a report:

“Prime Minister Modi made these remarks while chairing a high-level meeting with Secretaries to review the progress of rural development, agriculture, social welfare and allied sectors in advancing the vision of Viksit Bharat. The meeting, held at the Prime Minister’s residence focused on strengthening governance, improving inter-ministerial coordination and accelerating the implementation of reforms aimed at enhancing citizens’ welfare.

Highlighting the rapidly evolving governance landscape, Mr Modi stressed the need to modernise governance processes in line with changing times.

The Centre has approved two Electronics Manufacturing Clusters (EMCs) in Tamil Nadu with a combined investment of over 1 thousand 12 crore rupees. Mobile phone production has grown more than thirty-fold to 6.27 lakh crore rupees, with exports touching 2.59 lakh crore rupees. National Broadcasting Day is being observed today. On this day in 1927, the first ever radio broadcast in the country went on air from the Bombay Station under, the Indian Broadcasting Company. On the 8th of June, 1936, the Indian State Broadcasting Service became All India Radio (AIR). The day commemorates nearly a century of broadcasting’s contribution to informing, educating, entertaining and uniting the nation. More from our Correspondent:

“This day highlights the pivotal role broadcasting has played in India’s development, educational outreach, and cultural preservation. From the freedom movement to nation-building, radio has played a vital role in disseminating information, promoting literacy, creating health awareness, and sharing agricultural knowledge, particularly in rural India. In 1956 the name AKASHVANI was adopted for the National Broadcaster. The Vividh Bharati Service was launched in 1957 with popular film music as its main component. For generations, AKASHVANI has been serving to inform, educate and entertain the masses truly living up to its motto – Bahujana Hitaya, Bahujana Sukhaya. National Broadcasting Day celebrates not just technological progress, but also radio’s enduring power to inform, educate, entertain, and unite a diverse nation. With Priya Mondal, this is Dilnashi Arzoo, Akashvani News, Delhi.”
