The Government has increased the Special Additional Excise Duty (SAED) on the export of diesel and Aviation Turbine Fuel (ATF) in its latest fortnightly review. With effect from today, the export duty on diesel has been raised to 14 rupees per litre from 13 rupees 5 paisa per litre, while the levy on ATF exports has been increased to 12 rupees 5 paisa per litre from 9 rupees 5 paisa per litre. The export duty on petrol remains unchanged at 1 rupee 5 paisa per litre.

The government has notified an amendment to the Drugs Rules, 1945, saying that exemption given for sale of cough syrups in small villages has been withdrawn. Now, sale and dispensing of cough syrups in smaller villages will be required to take place only through duly licensed pharmacies.

“The Ministry of Health and Family Welfare has issued a Gazette notification saying that the word ‘Syrup’ from Schedule K of the drugs rules under the heading ‘Class of Drugs’ has been omitted. Now with the omission of the word Syrup”, the exemption will no longer be available for cough syrups. Earlier, the sale of cough syrups in villages with a population of less than one thousand was permitted without requiring compliance with certain retail sale licensing provisions. The amendment was made to strengthen regulatory oversight of syrup formulations and to align the exemption framework with contemporary public health and safety requirements. The measure is expected to promote responsible distribution and sale of cough syrups while ensuring greater compliance with regulatory standards across the country. Anand Kumar, Akashvani News, Delhi”

Agriculture and Farmers’ Welfare Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan today held a high-level agriculture review meeting in New Delhi and conducted a detailed review of preparations across the country for the Kharif 2026 season. The Agriculture Ministry said in a statement that in the context of possible El Niño conditions, he placed special emphasis on increasing cotton production, achieving self-reliance in pulses and advance contingency plans for low-rainfall districts. Our correspondent has filed this report;

“The Agriculture Minister gave a clear message that protecting farmers’ interests is the government’s top priority under the guidance of Prime Minister Narendra Modi. The meeting focused on ensuring that all necessary steps are taken well in advance to safeguard agriculture and farmers from any adverse weather conditions that may arise during the season. Mr Chouhan gave clear directions that full preparations should be made in advance in those districts where there is a possibility of low rainfall or uneven rainfall. He said that such districts should be clearly identified and crop-wise contingency plans should be prepared in coordination with state governments so that farmers can be provided immediate alternatives, advice and support, that in any weather challenge situation.
