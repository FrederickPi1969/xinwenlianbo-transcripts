National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA) capped trade margins on MRP of 137 anti-cancer drugs, based on the recommendations of Expert Committee constituted by the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS) under Ministry of Health & Family Welfare. The revised prices of these drugs come into effect from today. As on date, 76 pharmaceutical companies have reported revised MRP for 1225 anti-cancer formulations, covering 121 drugs. Reduction in the MRP have been reported in 1031 formulations of anti-cancer medicines, with 600 brands reporting above 50 percent reduction and the highest reduction being 96.45 percent. Further, out of the 137 identified anti-cancer drugs, there are 10-15 drugs which are yet to be launched in India. However, Trade Margin Rationalization (TMR) will be applicable on these drugs also as and when they are launched.  As a result of the price reductions to be effected from today, the estimated annual savings to consumers would be 3,502.31 crore rupees.

Leaders, scientists, civil society representatives, activists and others from nearly 200 countries, including India, are gathering for two weeks in Turkey’s Mediterranean resort city of Antalya for the 31st Conference of Parties to the United Nations Climate Change Conference (COP31), beginning today, with aim to accelerate clean energy, scale up climate finance and strengthen resilience. The summit will continue till November 20.

The Conference of the Parties (COP), the highest-level global climate summit held annually under the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC), is bringing together heads of state, Ministers, scientists, business leaders, and civil society representatives from around the world and serves as the primary platform for negotiating and advancing collective action to combat climate change. At COP31, the World Metrological Organisation (WMO) will support countries by providing authoritative scientific observations and information on the state of the global climate system, including through the launch of its flagship State of Climate update 2026, a science-based annual assessment of climate indicators and impacts.

Eisenkot, a former chief of the Israel Defence Forces (IDF), has 28 days to form a government and secure the approval of the Knesset — Israel’s parliament. He may seek an additional 14-day extension from Herzog for government formation.

The October 27 elections, seen as a referendum on Netanyahu’s leadership, produced no clear majority but divided the political landscape into two competing blocs, with the anti-Netanyahu alliance recommending Eisenkot.

The Delhi Government has notified the Delhi (Right of Citizens to Time-Bound and Easy Delivery of Services) Act, 2026. The Act aims to ensure that government departments deliver services within prescribed timelines and officials responsible for unnecessary delays are held accountable. It will replace the existing time-bound service delivery law of 2011 with a more modern and accountable system. Delhi Chief Minister Rekha Gupta said that the government is committed to making Delhi’s governance system citizen-centric, responsive and accountable, ensuring that every eligible citizen receives services on time and without difficulty. More from our correspondent

“The new system will ensure that citizens do not have to wait indefinitely for government services as the citizens will have a statutory right to receive government services within a prescribed timeframe. For unnecessary delays in delivery of services, the concerned officer can be fined 250 rupees per day, up to a maximum of five thousand rupees. There is also a provision for a fine for improperly rejecting applications under the new framework. Once an application is received, the designated officer will be responsible for either delivering the service within the prescribed timeline or communicating the reason for its rejection in writing. The Delhi government said that every application will be assigned a unique application number, allowing applicants to track its status online.  WITH ANAND KUMAR , SHIVANG MISRA, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”
