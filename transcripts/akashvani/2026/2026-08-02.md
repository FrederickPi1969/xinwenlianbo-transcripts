## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will launch the nationwide campaign Nasha Mukt Yuva for Viksit Bharat Sankalp Abhiyan today through video conferencing. The launch will mark the beginning of a 100-week nationwide Jan Bhagidari campaign against substance abuse. More from our correspondent:

“Youth across the country from over 10 thousand locations will participate in the Nasha Mukt Yuva for Viksit Bharat Sankalp Abhiyan. MY Bharat Volunteers, MY Bharat National Service Scheme volunteers, youth clubs, schools, colleges, universities, NGOs, youth wings of industry associations and more than 125 spiritual organisations will also join the programme virtually. Under the campaign, weekly activities will be organised every Sunday across the country to promote awareness and encourage public participation in building a drug-free society. The activities will include sports events, walkathons, meditation sessions, cultural programmes, awareness campaigns, nukkad nataks, discussion forums, art competitions and community engagement programmes. The initiative aims to inspire youth to stay away from substance abuse and encourage them to become ambassadors of positive social change in their communities. WITH SAUMYA, SAKLEN AKHTAR for AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI”

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said, the potential of youth becomes a driving force only when they find the right opportunities and the right direction to dream big with confidence to realise their goal. He was speaking after inaugurating the Swami Vivekananda Cultural Youth Centre Viveka Smaraka in Mysuru, Karnataka yesterday. The Prime Minister highlighted that today the youth in India are leading the world’s third largest startup ecosystem, atmanirbhar Bharat mission and Make in India initiative.

Union Minister for Health and Family Welfare Jagat Prakash Nadda has reviewed the preparedness for the National Eligibility-cum-Entrance Test for Postgraduate (NEET-PG) 2026 to ensure the smooth, secure, transparent and technology-enabled conduct of the examination. The exam is scheduled to be held on the 30th of this month. A report:

“During the meeting, the Minister assessed the preparedness of the National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences, technology partners and other stakeholders involved in conducting the examination. He was briefed on examination logistics, candidate facilitation measures, the security architecture and the technology-driven interventions introduced for NEET-PG 2026.The Minister stressed that every effort must be made to ensure a fair, transparent and candidate-friendly examination while upholding the highest standards of integrity and security. The Minister also reviewed the comprehensive security arrangements put in place for the examination. WITH PRIYA’S REPORT, JIGYASA PANDEY, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

President Droupadi Murmu flagged off a run ‘PBG Soldierathon’ at a ceremony held at the Forecourt of Rashtrapati Bhavan this morning. Governor of Mizoram General VK Singh who is the patron of the initiative was also present on the occasion. In a social media post, the President said that the Soldierathon serves as a unique platform to strengthen the bond between civilians and the Armed Forces, guided by the vision of ‘Run for Soldiers, Run with Soldiers’.

Telangana Police have rescued 6,307 children in a month-long special drive called Operation Muskaan-12, aimed at ending child labour and child trafficking across the state. The operation ran from 1st to 31st of last month.

Around 620 police personnel from 124 sub-division teams took part. After training on laws and procedures, teams raided places where child labour is common, including railway stations, bus stands, temples, brick kilns, mechanic shops, construction sites, and tea stalls.

Of the rescued children, 5,737 were reunited with their families, while 570 were sent to child care centres.

In Gujarat, the weather department has issued a Red Alert for heavy to very heavy rainfall across six districts today, including Morbi, Surendranagar, Rajkot, Ahmedabad. The rain spell has now shifted towards Saurashtra, causing widespread waterlogging in several areas. We have a report from our correspondent:

“Heavy rainfall continues to lash Gujarat as the monsoon spell shifted towards Saurashtra yesterday, Morbi recorded eight and a half inches of rainfall, causing severe waterlogging in low-lying areas and disrupting normal life. The intense spell has pushed 20 reservoirs to overflow, with 30 dams placed on high alert. The Sardar Sarovar Narmada Dam, considered the lifeline of the state, has reached nearly 75 percent capacity, taking the state’s total seasonal average rainfall past 64 percent. In view of strong winds, fishermen have been advised not to venture into the sea for the next two days, and a local cautionary signal number 3 has been hoisted at all major ports across the state. Meanwhile, the India Meteorological Department has predicted that the intensity of the rainfall is likely to decrease gradually starting tomorrow. Aparna Khunt, Akashvani News, Ahmedabad.”

In Odisha, the flood situation has shown signs of gradual improvement, but authorities remain on high alert as swollen rivers continue to threaten low-lying areas in the Mahanadi basin. The state government continues to closely monitor the situation in downstream districts, including Cuttack, Puri, Jagatsinghpur, Kendrapara and Khordha, where floodwaters are yet to fully recede. Authorities have urged people in vulnerable coastal and riverine areas to remain alert as restoration efforts continue.

Meanwhile, Heavy rain continue to wreak severe havoc across Keralam, disrupting normal life and causing widespread damage across multiple regions. Orange Alert has been declared for 12 districts today excluding Thiruvananthapuram and Kollam, urging the public to exercise extreme caution as heavy downpours persist. Due to worsening weather and rough sea conditions, authorities have also placed a strict ban on fishing activities along the coasts of Keralam.

Relief and rescue operations are underway. ​So far, six deaths have been officially reported, while four individuals remain missing.

The IMD forecast heavy rainfall conditions over Delhi, Haryana, Punjab, Chandigarh, Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, and Ladakh during the next 2 days.

Meanwhile, the Weather agency has also advised fishermen not to venture into parts of the north Arabian Sea till tomorrow, and few parts of the east-central Arabian Sea from 3rd to 6th of this month.

Out of the seven affected States, Himachal Pradesh, Odisha and Nagaland had earlier received the first instalment of SDRF for the financial year 2026-27.

Iranian State media quoted senior Iranian security official saying Tehran has prepared plans to target critical installations in Israel and US energy assets across the region if any military action is launched against the country. India climbed to fourth place in the Commonwealth Games 2026 medal standings in Glasgow following winning 16 medals across four disciplines on a memorable Day 10th.  This surge brought India’s tally to 39 medals, comprising 13 gold, 17 silver, and 9 bronze. The boxing team led the charge with 10 medals. India’s athletes added two medals each in the men’s para shot put and men’s triple jump, while one each in judo and athletics. A report:

“Indian boxers delivered a historic performance at the Commonwealth Games, securing unprecedented seven gold medals, highest by any nation in a single edition of the Games, along with three silver medals. This marks India’s best-ever boxing campaign at the Commonwealth Games. Boxers Preeti Pawar, Jaismine Lamboria, Sakshi Chaudhary, Priya Ghanghas, Arundhati Choudhary, Sachin Siwach, and Ankush Panghal clinched the gold medals, while Jadumani Singh, Lovlina Borgohain and Narender Berwal won silver medals in their respective weight categories. India also enjoyed success in para athletics, producing a memorable one-two finish in the men’s F57 shot put event. Soman Rana claimed the gold medal, while Shubham Juyal secured silver. The athletics contingent added three more medals. In the men’s triple jump, Praveen Chithravel won silver and Selva Prabhu Thirumaran took bronze, while long-distance runner Gulveer Singh earned his second medal of the Games by clinching bronze in the men’s 5000m final. Judoka Unnati Sharma also added to the glory by bagging bronze medal in the women’s 63kg event. RUPESH RATHORE FOR AKASHVANI NEWS.”

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today said that as India moves towards becoming a Viksit Bharat, the nation’s youth must be physically fit, mentally strong, and full of self-confidence. Speaking on the launch of ‘Nasha Mukt Yuva for Viksit Bharat Sankalp Abhiyan’ virtually in New Delhi today, Mr Modi said young people should always stay away from drugs and embrace a healthy lifestyle.

He added that the launch will mark the beginning of a 100-week nationwide Jan Bhagidari campaign against substance abuse.

Mr Modi said drug addiction diverts people from their goals and distances them from achieving their dreams. He said that addiction not only destroys families but also weakens the strength of the nation. Stressing the need to protect the youth from the menace of drugs, he said young people must understand the serious consequences of addiction. Mr Modi reminded that the drugs ultimately ruin careers, disrupt family life, and destroy the future of individuals.

The Prime Minister urged families not to hide cases of substance abuse due to social stigma. He said they should seek timely help, communicate openly, consult experts and make use of medical support to help affected family members recover. He stressed that families must foster a culture of open dialogue to identify warning signs at an early stage and provide timely support to children.

The United States has agreed to hold off a planned military attack on Iran after Tehran and several West Asian countries requested a pause, claiming that the framework of a potential agreement had been reached. In a social media post, US President Donald Trump today said the US remained fully prepared for military action but had decided to suspend the planned strike to allow diplomacy to move forward. According to Trump, the proposed agreement would include the Immediate, Complete, and total opening of Hormuz Strait, and an end to Iran’s nuclear threat. Iran had not officially confirmed the claims made by the US President.

Pakistan had recently convened a conference in Islamabad to protest India’s decision to hold the Indus Waters Treaty in abeyance. Ambassador Kwatra stated that threatening war against India, to distract from its internal problems, has become a standard policy option for Islamabad.

In badminton, India’s Tanvi Sharma claimed her maiden BWF World Tour title, defeating Vietnam’s sixth-seeded Nguyen Thuy Linh 21-16, 21-16 to win the Taipei Open Super women’s singles title today.

Seventeen-year old Tanvi became the youngest Taipei Open champion surpassing Taiwan’s Tai Tzu Ying. She is also the first Indian woman to win the title since Saina Nehwal in 2008.

India’s economy is likely to grow around 7 per cent in the first quarter of current fiscal, higher than earlier expectations, supported by stronger economic indicators, improved industrial activity and a recovery in exports. According to an SBI Research Pre-Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) report released yesterday, the growth estimate marks an improvement from the Reserve Bank of India’s earlier projections, which were lowered because of geopolitical uncertainties. The report attributed the improved outlook to a broad-based pickup in high-frequency indicators during the April-June quarter. SBI Research also noted that the global environment remains uncertain amid the West Asia crisis, but India’s domestic economy has shown resilience.

The Delhi Government has notified a scheme to provide compensation to the next of kin or legal heirs of prisoners who die an unnatural death while in custody within the prisons of the National Capital Region. Delhi Government’s Home Department issued a notification in this regard today.

Under this scheme, compensation of up to 7.5 lakh rupees will be awarded, depending on the specific circumstances. Compensation of 5 lakh rupees has been fixed for cases involving negligence in the discharge of duties by prison officials or staff, negligence by medical or paramedical personnel, and cases of prisoner suicide. According to the notification, the scheme will apply only to cases involving the unnatural death of a prisoner in any Delhi prison occurring on or after the date of its notification. Delhi Chief Minister Rekha Gupta stated that this scheme, notified in accordance with the guidelines of the National Human Rights Commission, will not only provide timely relief to the affected families under specified circumstances but also further strengthen administrative accountability. Aman Yadav, Akashvani News, Delhi.
