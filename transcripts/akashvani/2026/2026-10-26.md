The 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum will commence in New Delhi from today. External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar has said that the use of terrorism as an instrument of statecraft is simply unacceptable, and it can never be justified or rationalised. He said, terrorism continues because not all states display zero tolerance for the menace. Dr Jaishankar was addressing an event held in New Delhi to mark 75th anniversary of Chinmaya Mission. He said, the world must develop sharper awareness about this common threat, and it must come together with stronger resolve to counter terrorism in all its forms and manifestations.

He underlined the importance of peace, especially amid the ongoing conflicts in the world, and cautioned that war anywhere threatens peace everywhere. Dr Jaishankar cited the two major conflicts going on in the world, in Ukraine and in West Asia, and said societies and economies and people far away who have little say in the matter are actually paying the cost.

ISRO Chairman V Narayanan has said that India will expand its space operations significantly with four functional launch pads by the year 2029. Mr. Narayanan said, these include a 2 thousand acre dedicated Small Satellite Launch Vehicle (SSLV) complex at Kulasekarapattinam in Tuticorin district of Tamil Nadu and a third launch pad at Sriharikota, to meet the global demand for satellite launches. He was addressing convocation of a private university at Porur near Chennai.

The ISRO chief said that the upcoming Kulasekarapattinam spaceport facility will feature crucial infrastructure such as integration buildings, launch pads, rail-track systems, telemetry, tracking, and command systems.

Mr. Narayanan stated that ISRO plans to conduct the first uncrewed Gaganyaan test within the next two months, followed by two subsequent uncrewed tests before the crewed mission.

India Meteorological Department (IMD) has said that the Deep Depression over Bay of Bengal will intensify further into a Cyclonic Storm today. IMD has advised the fisherman not to venture into West central and adjoining Northwest and Southwest Bay of Bengal during the next two days.

Talking to Akashvani News, Director General of IMD, Dr Mrutyunjay Mohapatra said, due to influence of depression the sea condition is very rough and will deteriorate further. He said that the wind speed may lead to large scale damage of structures and standing crops. He said that heavy to extremely heavy rainfall may hit parts of Andhra Pradesh, Odisha and Chhattisgarh during the next two days. Dr Mohapatra added that due to heavy rainfall can cause flooding in low-lying areas and landslides in hilly areas and urged the people living in these regions to be cautious.

In Brazil, Leftist President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva has defeated right-wing Senator Flavio Bolsonaro in Brazil’s intense presidential race. With this victory, Lula has become the only politician in modern Brazilian history who successfully rebound and win the second round after falling behind in the first. A report:

“Incumbent Lula won the final runoff election with 51 percent of the vote and more than 51.49 million ballots. Meanwhile, Bolsonaro received 48.68 percent of the vote, which amounted to 48.8 million ballots. Just over 2.6 million votes separated the two candidates. Because of the close and highly contested race, voting stations across the country remained open two hours longer than originally planned.

The former metalworker union leader, at the age of 80 will be sworn into office on 5th of January next year, breaking his own record as the oldest president to take the oath of office since Brazil returned to a democracy. This victory marks an unprecedented fourth non-consecutive term for Lula, who has run for the presidency a total of seven times. KAUMUDEE PAREEK FOR AKASHVANI NEWS DELHI.”

Mr Birla expressed confidence that the deliberations at this forum will give a new direction to the mutual trust and cooperation and further strengthen the shared commitment to people-centric development.
