## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will inaugurate the centenary celebrations of revered Jain spiritual leader and social reformer Acharya Vidyanand Ji Maharaj in New Delhi today. Mr Modi will also address the gathering on the occasion. The programme will mark the formal commencement of a year-long national tribute, being organised by the government, in collaboration with Bhagwan Mahaveer Ahimsa Bharti Trust to honour the 100th birth anniversary of Acharya Vidyanand Ji Maharaj. Acharya Vidyanand Ji played an instrumental role in the restoration and revival of ancient Jain temples across India and worked for education, especially in Prakrit, Jain philosophy, and classical languages.

India has rejected a ruling issued by the so-called Court of Arbitration on the Kishenganga and Ratle hydroelectric projects situated in Indian Union Territory of Jammu and Kashmir. In a statement, the External Affairs Ministry dismissed the supplemental award given by the illegal Court of Arbitration purportedly constituted under the Indus Waters Treaty 1960.

The Ministry said, India has never recognised the legal validity of this Court of Arbitration terming its constitution a serious breach of the Indus Waters Treaty. It said, any proceedings before this forum and any decision taken by it, is illegal.

The Ministry said, the Indus Waters Treaty was placed in abeyance by the country after the Pahalgam terrorist attack. The decision was taken in view of Pakistan’s support to cross-border terrorism. The Ministry stressed that India is no longer bound to perform any of its obligations under the Treaty.

The External Affairs Ministry said this latest charade at Pakistan’s behest is yet another desperate attempt by it to escape accountability for its role as the global epicenter of terrorism.

A total of four thousand 415 Indian nationals have been evacuated so far under Operation Sindhu, from conflict zones in Iran and Israel amid the prevailing security situation in West Asia. These included 3,597 from Iran and 818 from Israel in special evacuation flights. The External Affairs Ministry in a statement said, 14 Overseas Citizenship of India or OCI card-holders, 9 Nepali nationals, 4 Sri Lankan nationals and 1 Iranian spouse of an Indian national were also evacuated from Iran.

Bihar has become the first state in the country to launch e-Voting facilities through mobile applications in municipal bodies elections and by-polls which are underway in the state today. Polling is taking place in Six Nagar Panchayat and 36 other municipal bodies. Voting in the state began at 7 am and will conclude by 5 pm. For e-Voting, voters can cast their votes till 1 pm from their homes. State Election Commissioner Deepak Prasad called it a historic day.

“In Bihar, by rolling out e-Voting feature today in municipal bodies polling the State Election Commission has added another feather in its hat, by incorporating a big technological innovation to enhance voter turnout. It will support the motto of inclusive voting which is sacrosanct in democracy. It may prove a solution to voter apathy which has been witnessed in urban areas. The State Election Commission has so far introduced FRS- Face Recognition System for voter verification, OCR Optical Character Recognition system in counting of votes and digital lock for EVM making it a pioneer body in adoption of wide range of technology in election process of Panchayati Raj and Urban local bodies. Dharmendra Kumar Rai Akashvani News Patna.”

India’s Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders Limited (MDL) has decided to acquire a 51 per cent controlling stake in Sri Lanka’s largest and most established shipyard Colombo Dockyard PLC under a 442 crore rupees deal from Japan’s Onomichi Dockyard. The State-run MDL has already signed definitive agreements with Onomichi in this regard.

Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders’ acquisition of Colombo Dockyard is a strategic win for both India and Sri Lanka. For India, it strengthens maritime influence in the Indian Ocean, expands shipbuilding and repair capacity, and boosts economic and diplomatic ties with Sri Lanka. The deal offers MDL access to a prime location for servicing international shipping routes, while freeing up its Indian facilities for defence projects. For Sri Lanka, it rescues a critical, debt-ridden asset, safeguards over 3,000 jobs, and stabilises investor confidence. However, the deal enhances India’s strategic presence in Sri Lanka’s maritime sector, offering a counterbalance in a region where China has already established a significant foothold through its investments in the Hambantota Port. Dr. Ahmed Muyeen Farooqi/Colombo.

Mahanadi Coalfields Limited (MCL) set a new record by producing over 225 million tonnes of coal in financial year 2024-25, marking a 9 per cent increase compared to the previous financial year. MCL is a subsidiary of Coal India Limited and one of India’s leading coal-producing companies.

In the financial year 2024-25, MCL supplied 210 million tonnes of coal to various power stations and other consumers. According to MCL, 98% of this coal was extracted through environmentally friendly surface mining methods. Talking Exclusively to the Akashvani News the Chairman Cum Managing Director of MCL Uday A Kaole said that while last year the company loaded an average of 10 rakes per day, the number has now increased to 27 rakes per day.

He also mentioned that initiatives like PM Gati Shakti have significantly accelerated the transportation of coal to end-users.

To ensure smooth and transparent business operations, MCL is integrating IT-based solutions into its internal systems. With Akshit from Sambhalpur, Adarsh for Akashvani News.

The Government has issued model rules for the felling of trees on agricultural land in a bid to promote agroforestry.
