## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi held a telephonic conversation with the President of Sri Lanka Anura Kumara Dissanayake yesterday and assured him India’s continued support to Sri Lanka under the ongoing Operation Sagar Bandhu in providing rescue and relief to distressed persons. Mr Modi assured that India, in line with its Vision MAHASAGAR and its established position as the First Responder, will continue to extend all necessary assistance in the coming days as Sri Lanka undertakes rehabilitation efforts, resumes public services, and works toward restoring livelihoods across the impacted regions. During the conversation, President Dissanayake also conveyed his deep gratitude for India’s assistance in the wake of the disaster and appreciated the swift deployment of rescue teams and relief material. He also conveyed the appreciation of the people of Sri Lanka for India’s timely and effective response efforts. The Prime Minister also expressed his condolences on the loss of lives and widespread devastation in Sri Lanka in the wake of Cyclone Ditwah.

India has intensified its humanitarian mission in Sri Lanka as the island reels from the devastating impact of Cyclone Ditwah, which has triggered massive floods and landslides. Authorities now confirm 390 deaths and 352 people still missing, while rescue and relief efforts continue across hard-hit districts as access gradually improves. Under Operation Sagar Bandhu, India has expanded medical, rescue, and logistics support, reinforcing its role as Sri Lanka’s First Responder. A C-130J aircraft brought around 10 tonnes of medical supplies, including BHISHM Modular Trauma Cubes, alongside an IAF medical team. Meanwhile, INS Sukanya arrived at Trincomalee port in the eastern province and delivered 12 tonnes of essential relief for immediate distribution.

“Rescue operations intensified across Sri Lanka as Indian teams worked alongside Sri Lankan authorities and forces. Indian Air Force MI-17 helicopters evacuated 96 stranded people, including three critically injured survivors from flood hit areas, while delivering over 5.5 tonnes of urgent rations and medical supplies to cut-off communities. On the ground, NDRF teams continued life-saving missions, pulling 43 people-among them infants, pregnant women, and the elderly-from areas unreachable by road, with search efforts still underway. As major rivers threaten to overflow again, India’s Operation Sagar Bandhu remains fully engaged, reinforcing responders and supporting Sri Lanka through its most perilous hours. Ahmed Muyeen Farooqi, Akashvani News, Colombo.”

As Sri Lanka continues to deal with heavy flooding and destruction caused by Cyclone Ditwah, former Sri Lanka cricketer Angelo Mathews has expressed gratitude to India for the support. In a social media post, Mathews thanked the Indian armed forces for risking their lives to save people in Sri Lanka. Mathews said all Srilankans truly appreciate the efforts.

The North East monsoon has become vigorous as deep depression located near North Tamil Nadu and South Andhra Coast remained stationary during the past six hours about 50 km east of Chennai, 160 km east of Cuddalore and 140 km south of Nellore. It is very likely to recurve slowly south southwestwards and weaken into a depression during the next 12 hours. The minimum distance of the centre of the deep depression from North Tamilnadu Puducherry coast is about 35 km. The weather department has raised a red alert to Chennai, Tiruvallur, Chengalpattu and Kanchipuram districts for another 24 hours. Light to moderate rains are likely to continue at one or two places at Ranipet, Tirupattur, Villupuram and Cuddalore districts.

The Government has issued directions for pre-installation of Sanchar Saathi App in mobile handsets to verify the genuineness of mobile handsets.

“In order to safeguard the citizens from buying the non-genuine handsets, enabling easy reporting of suspected misuse of telecom resources and to increase effectiveness of the Sanchar Saathi initiative, Department of Telecommunications has directed the manufacturer and importers of mobile handsets to pre-install Sanchar Saathi mobile application on all mobile handsets manufactured or imported for use in India. The department has said that pre-installed Sanchar Saathi application should readily be visible and accessible to the end users at the time of first use or device setup. The functionalities of the application should not be disabled or restricted. The department also directed mobile handset manufacturers and importers to make efforts to push the app through software updates to devices that have already been manufactured and are in the sales channels in India. The Department has asked the manufactures to complete the implementation within 90 days and submit the report in 120 days. SHIVANG MISRA, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

The Atal Pension Yojana has crossed 8.34 crore enrolments. This was stated by Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman in a reply to a question in Lok Sabha yesterday. Women constitute 48 percent of the total subscribers, reflecting a significant participation from female beneficiaries across the country.

Launched in May 2015, the Atal Pension Yojana aims to build a universal social security system for citizens, particularly workers in the unorganised sector. The scheme is open to individuals aged 18 to 40 years who have a savings bank or post-office account. Subscribers receive pension benefits after turning 60, and benefits are expected to begin disbursal from 2035 onward.

The bill seeks to amend the Manipur Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017. It will replace the Manipur Goods and Services Tax (Second Amendment) Ordinance, 2025 which was promulgated in October this year. The Lok Sabha has already passed the Bill.

Union Minister of State for Home Affairs Nityanand Rai has said, Government has decided to conduct Census in 2027. In a written reply in the Lok Sabha today, Mr Rai said, in the first phase, House Listing and Housing Census will be conducted from April to September next year followed by second phase. He said, Population Enumeration will be done in February 2027 with reference date of 1st March, 2027 except for the Union territory of Ladakh and snow-bound non-synchronous areas of the Union territory of Jammu and Kashmir and the States of Himachal Pradesh and Uttarakhand, where it will be done in September, 2026 with reference date of 1st October, 2026.
