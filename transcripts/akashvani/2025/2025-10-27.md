## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi and ASEAN leaders jointly reviewed the progress in the ASEAN-India relations and discussed initiatives for strengthening the Comprehensive Strategic Partnership. In his address, the Prime Minister reiterated India’s support for ASEAN Unity, ASEAN Centrality, and the ASEAN Outlook on the Indo-Pacific. He complimented ASEAN on adoption of the ASEAN Community Vision 2045.

He emphasised that early review of the ASEAN-India FTA can unleash the full economic potential of our relationship for the benefit of our peoples and further strengthen regional cooperation. Mr Modi also said that terrorism is a serious challenge to global peace and security, and stressed the importance of unity in the fight against terrorism.

During his address, the Prime Minister announced extended support for implementation of the ASEAN-India Plan of Action to implement the ASEAN-India Comprehensive Strategic Partnership (2026-2030). He also Proposed to organise the Second ASEAN-India Defence Ministers’ Meeting and the Second ASEAN-India Maritime Exercise for a secure maritime environment.

External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar will represent India at the 20th East Asia Summit at Kuala Lumpur in Malaysia today. The East Asia Summit will provide an opportunity to deliberate on the challenges to peace, stability and prosperity in the Indo-Pacific region and also exchange views on regional and international developments.

Dr Jaishankar met Singapore’s Foreign Minister Vivian Balakrishnan and exchanged views on the evolving global and regional landscape while exploring new avenues to deepen India-Singapore cooperation. The meeting took place on the sidelines of the 47th ASEAN Summit in Kuala Lumpur yesterday, where both ministers participated in high-level regional diplomatic engagements.

The 8th Session of the International Solar Alliance Assembly will begin today at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi. 124 countries will participate in the four-day event and over 40 Ministers from across the globe will attend the session. The event will bring the global community together to deepen cooperation and accelerate solar adoption for a brighter and sustainable future. Our correspondent reports that the member countries will review the progress, address challenges, and align actions towards one vision – affordable solar energy for all.

The Solar Alliance will take forward the vision of Prime Minister Narendra Modi of harnessing the power of solar energy. India has achieved 50 per cent of its total installed electricity generation capacity from non-fossil fuel sources five years ago, ahead of its target. India is the 3rd world largest Solar Producer and world 2nd largest market for renewable energy growth. More than 20 lakh households have already been benefiting from solar power on their rooftop under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Yojana. The International Solar Alliance is an international organisation with 124 Member and Signatory countries. It works with governments to improve energy access and security worldwide and promote solar power as a sustainable transition to a carbon-neutral future. It also promotes the use of solar energy in the agriculture, health, transport, and power generation sectors. Akshit from Akashvani News, from Delhi.

The four-day long Chhath Puja entered its third and main day today, known as Sandhya Arghya. Sandhya Arghya is dedicated to the setting sun. The sacred moment signifies gratitude for the light, warmth, and energy the sun provides throughout the day.

On this day, families gather at riverbanks or water bodies, carrying the soop or bamboo basket filled with traditional prasad like Thekua, sugarcane, fruits and lit diyas. Devotees wade into the water and offer Arghya to the setting sun, praying for the prosperity and well-being of their children and family.

In Bihar, millions of devotees and worshippers will offer arghya or sacred water offering to the setting sun.

In view of the large gathering, extensive security arrangements have been made at 78 ghats along the Ganga. The ghats have been barricaded, and teams of divers and the NDRF and SDRF have been deployed for safety.

Tomorrow morning, with the offering of arghya to the rising sun, the four-day Chhath Mahaparv will conclude. Meanwhile, a large number of devotees have gathered for worship at famous Sun temples across the state.

In view of the Bihar Assembly elections, voter awareness messages are also being displayed at various Chhath ghats, urging people to participate enthusiastically in the upcoming polls. Dharmendra Kumar Rai, Akashvani News, Patna.”

The GST Bachat Utsav kicked off on 22nd September as the Narendra Modi government slashed GST rates in many sectors, benefitting citizens across the country. Today, our correspondent brings a report on how the new GST rates on food items are helping the common man.

The Next Generation GST Reforms have provided significant relief to citizens by reducing taxes on essential food items. Under the new tax structure, pizza bread, khakhra, and chapati have been made tax-free, which earlier attracted a 5 percent tax. Similarly, paratha and parotta, previously placed in the 18 percent tax slab, have also been exempted. To ease the tax burden on the common man, the government has further reduced GST on soups, broths, chocolates, cocoa products, as well as tea and coffee extracts. All of these items now attract only 5 percent GST under GST 2.0. Talking exclusively to Akashvani News, Vice Chairman of the CII North Bengal Zonal Council and a tea factory owner, Satish Mitruka, welcomed the reduction in GST rates.

This rationalization aims to improve the affordability of food, ensure daily nutrition, encourage consumption and provide economic relief to families. Saklen Akhtar, Akashvani News.

Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Shah said, India’s maritime vision is anchored in Safety, Stability, and Self-Reliance. The GST Bachat Utsav kicked off on 22nd September as the Narendra Modi government slashed GST rates in many sectors, benefitting citizens across the country. Today, our correspondent brings a report on how the next gen GST reforms are boosting the economy of Jammu and Kashmir.

Under the revised GST structure, the government has provided a major boost to Jammu and Kashmir’s economy by offering significant relief across various sectors. Under GST 2.0, lowered tax rates on handicrafts, tourism and speciality products have widened markets, strengthened exports, and enriched livelihoods. The Election Commission today announced that the second phase of Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of Electoral Rolls will be conducted in 12 states and Union Territories. The states and Union Territories are: the Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Lakshadweep, Chhattisgarh, Goa, Gujarat, Kerala, Madhya Pradesh, Puducherry, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal. Briefing Media in New Delhi today, Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar said that this revision is aimed at making electoral rolls more transparent and error-free. He said, the phase two of SIR will cover 51 crore voters. Mr. Kumar said, printing of enumeration forms and training of Booth Level Officers will begin from tomorrow.

The Chief Election Commissioner said, while the enumeration process will begin on 4th November for one month, the draft rolls will be published on 9th December and final electoral rolls on 7th February, next year.
