## Morning News

Donald Trump will take oath as 47th President of United States today. The presidential oath will be held in the presence of United States Chief Justice John Roberts in Washington DC. The swearing-in ceremony is scheduled to start at 8:30 pm IST. Mr Trump will also deliver his inaugural address as part of the official transfer of power. JD Vance will be sworn-in as US Vice President.
Several foreign leaders are expected to attend the swearing-in ceremony. External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar will represent India at the ceremony.
Ahead of his oath, 78-year old Trump addressed supporters at a victory rally in Washington DC last night. He told a packed arena that he plans to sign a host of executive orders on the first day of his presidency.

The 55th World Economic Forum (WEF) Annual Meeting will begin today at Davos in Switzerland. During the five day event, India’s model of inclusive growth and digital revolution will be highlighted. Indian delegation will be spearheaded by Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw along with Jal Shakti Minister C R Patil, Civil Aviation Minister K. Rammohan Naidu, Food Processing Industries Minister Chirag Paswan and Minister of State for Skill Development and Entrepreneurship Jayant Chaudhary.

The annual meeting is a forum for global leaders to consider key global and regional challenges. India’s participation in World Economic Forum aims to strengthen partnerships, attract investment, and position the country as a global leader in sustainable development and technological innovations. A high-level representatives from six states – Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh, and Kerala, will showcase the region’s industrial advancements, investment opportunities, and success stories as part of India’s ongoing economic transformation. During the five day event, there will be a discussion about inclusive growth, investment in social, physical and digital infrastructure and on democratizing technology. With Dipendra Kumar, Saklen Akhtar, Akashvani News Delhi.

The first three hostages released from Hamas captivity in Gaza have arrived in Israel. Three Israeli women were finally freed yesterday after 471 days in captivity, on the first day of a ceasefire agreement between Israel and Hamas. Emily Damari, a British-Israeli; Doron Steinbrecher, a veterinary nurse; and Romi Gonen, had been abducted from the Nova music festival by Hamas on October 7, 2023.  The released Israeli women were handed over via the Red Cross and reunited with their families after medical checks. They are the first of 33 Israeli hostages expected to be released. Israel has also released 90 Palestinian prisoners as part of the ceasefire deal with Hamas.

The 85th session of the prestigious All India Presiding Officers’ Conference (AIPOC) will begin today in Patna. Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla will inaugurate the two-day conference, which is being held in Bihar after an interval of 43 years. Presiding officers from different legislative bodies across the country, Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha will discuss the theme, 75th Anniversary of Constitution: Contribution of Parliament and State Legislative Bodies in Strengthening Constitutional Values.

Bihar is hosting the prestigious conference for the third time. Bihar Assembly Speaker Nand Kishore Yadav said Lok Sabha Speaker will Chair the inaugural session of the Conference at Central Hall of Bihar Legislative Assembly. The plenary session will be held in assembly this afternoon. Around 300 delegates including Speakers, Deputy Speakers, Chairman and Deputy Chairman of different legislative bodies have reached Patna to  attend the conference. Dharmendra Kumar Rai, Akashvani News, Patna.

## Midday News

Mr. Donald Trump will be sworn in as the 47th President of the United States today. The presidential oath will be administered by Chief Justice John Roberts at the Capitol Rotunda in Washington, DC.

Ahead of the ceremony, the 78-year-old Trump addressed a victory rally in Washington, DC, last night. Speaking to a packed arena, Mr Trump promised to act with historic speed to address every crisis facing the United States.

The inaugural ceremony has been moved indoors due to forecasts of intense cold weather in Washington, DC. The last time an inauguration was relocated indoors due to severe cold was in 1985, during former Republican President Ronald Reagan’s second swearing-in.

As part of the official transfer of power, Mr. Trump will also deliver his inaugural address during today’s swearing-in ceremony. JD Vance will take the oath as the Vice President.

External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar will represent India at the event. The ceremony will be attended by several foreign leaders, including Argentina’s President Javier Milei and Italy’s Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni.

In a social media post, Dr Jaishankar said, they discussed the progress in their bilateral cooperation and developments pertaining to Quad. Dr Jaishankar also met Foreign Affairs Minister of Australia Penny Wong.

In Uttarakhand, the State Cabinet has given its nod to the Uniform Civil Code (UCC) manual, paving the way for its implementation in the state. This decision is expected to set a precedence for other states of country, reflecting the state’s proactive approach towards governance and social reform.

Speaking to the media, Chief Minister Pushkar Singh Dhami expressed his optimism about the UCC’s impact. He stated that Uttarakhand’s adoption of UCC will serve as an inspiration for the rest of India.

The Uniform Civil Code seeks to ensure that all citizens are governed by a common set of laws in matters of personal relationships, promoting equality and justice.
