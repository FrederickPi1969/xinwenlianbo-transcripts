Prime Minister Narendra Modi will interact with Mudra Yojana beneficiaries in New Delhi today. The event is scheduled to be telecast at 9 this morning.

Over 52 crore loans worth more than 32 lakh crore rupees have been sanctioned under Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana till date. The scheme was launched in 2015 by the Prime Minister Narendra Modi to provide collateral-free loans of up to ten lakh rupees to non-corporate, non-farm, small and micro enterprises, aiming to facilitate financial inclusion and empower entrepreneurs.

“Mudra Yojana launched in 2015 is completing 10 years today. three categories of loans are being provided Under the scheme. This include Shishu of loan up to 50 thousand rupees, Kishor upto five lakh rupees and Tarun upto ten lakh rupees. Nearly 70 per cent of Mudra loans have been availed by women entrepreneurs, enhancing their financial independence and contributing to gender equality. The scheme has fostered a sense of self-reliance among marginalized groups by enabling them to sustain their livelihoods through entrepreneurship. SC, ST and OBC entrepreneurs have received over 50 per cent of all Mudra loans. Over the years, Mudra Yojana has reshaped India’s entrepreneurial landscape, drawing global recognition as a model of financial inclusion. SAKLEN AKHTAR, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

The Supreme Court ruled that Tamil Nadu Governor R.N. Ravi acted “illegal and erroneous” by reserving 10 bills, re-adopted by the state assembly, for the President’s approval.

A bench of Justices J.B. Pardiwala and R. Mahadevan ruled that it was not open for the Governor to reserve a bill for the President, which was re-presented by the state government after being passed again by the Assembly. Once the bills were re-presented to the Governor, he should have approved them, as they were deemed assented to.  The bench also made it clear that under Article 200 of the Constitution, the Governor does not have discretionary power to withhold assent and must act on the advice of the Chief Minister and the Council of Ministers.

Chief Minister M.K. Stalin hailed the Supreme Court’s ruling as a historic victory for Tamil Nadu and all state governments across India. Speaking in the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly, CM Stalin said the verdict vindicated the state’s stand on legislative autonomy.
