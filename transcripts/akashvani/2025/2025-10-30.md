The GST Bachat Utsav kicked off on 22nd September as the Narendra Modi government slashed GST rates in many sectors, benefitting citizens across the country. Today, our correspondent brings a report on how the new GST rates in the Apparel and  footwear sector are helping citizens.

“The next-generation GST reforms have provided major relief to the apparel and footwear sector. The reforms aimed at easing the burden on young manufacturers, have made production more cost-effective, and ensured that quality Readymade garments and footwear becomes more affordable for consumers. Under the new tax structure, GST has been reduced to 5 percent for upto 2,500 rupees per piece on Readymade Garments. This rationalisation has stimulated domestic demand in small towns and rural regions. Similarly, the tax rates on man-made yarns has been reduced from 12 percent to 5 percent, reducing reliance on imports and boosting exports. In addition, footwear priced up to 2 thousand and five hundred rupees  per pair now attract only 5 percent GST instead of 12 percent, making stylish and durable footwear more accessible to young and middle-income consumers. This rationalisation has significantly encouraged the country’s manufacturing base, benefit consumers and make these products more affordable. With Ishani and Drishti’s report, Aman Yadav, for Akashvani News, Delhi.”

Twenty-eight people have lost lives in destruction brought by Hurricane Melissa in the Caribbean. Melissa is now passing through the Bahamas as a Category 1 hurricane after making landfall in Cuba this morning. Cuba suffered significant damage from the storm and around 140,000 people were cut off by rising river levels. Melissa hit Jamaica yesterday as one of the strongest Atlantic hurricanes on record, caused major damage to public infrastructure and left most of the island without power. Jamaican Prime Minister Andrew Holness said the damage is “heartbreaking”, and one town he toured had been “completely destroyed”. According to the reports, at least 20 people died in Haiti, after a river flooded by Melissa burst its banks. Four bodies were recovered in Jamaica’s badly hit St. Elizabeth Parish, and another three died on the island during storm preparations. One person died in the Dominican Republic.
