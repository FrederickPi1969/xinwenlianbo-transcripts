GST Bachat Utsav, which was announced by Prime Minister Narendra Modi, and commenced on 22nd of last month, is benefiting all sections of society. Today, our correspondent brings a report on the GST reforms that will boost the economy of the state of Haryana.

The new GST reform is a big relief for Haryana’s farmers, manufacturers, and small enterprises. Under the reformed tax structure, GST on a wide range of goods has been reduced, making them more affordable and strengthening the state’s industrial base.  With the reduced GST from 28 percent  to 18 percent on automotive  components, the new reforms will make vehicles more affordable. This step will also improve Haryana’s export potential to automotive markets in Africa, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia.

In agriculture and allied sectors, farm machinery, implements, and fertilisers that earlier attracted 18 percent GST are taxed at 5 percent, reducing costs for farmers and encouraging greater mechanisation.  These changes are expected to stimulate higher sales, preserve and create employment, and energise both rural and industrial economies, especially during the festive season and in the periods ahead. With Samarth, Shivang Misra for Akashvani News Delhi.

“The new GST reform is providing a major boost  to Manipur’s artisans, farmers, and small businesses. Under the revised tax structure,  GST 2.0 many essential goods in the state have now come under a reduced tax slab making local products more affordable and giving a boost to regional industries and livelihoods. Along with this, the tax cut on packaged coffee from 18 percent  to 5 percent brings substantial relief across Manipur’s coffee industry. With this move, districts such as Ukhrul, Senapati, and Chandel which are important hubs for coffee cultivation are being benefited as sector generates additional employment in processing, packaging, and distribution networks. The next generation GST reforms have geared to stimulate greater demand, preserve employment, and invigorate Manipur’s rural and craft economies during festive times and beyond.
