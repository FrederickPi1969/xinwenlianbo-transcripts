## Morning News

Bharatiya Janata Party has completely rejected and distanced itself from controversial remarks made by the party MPs Nishikant Dubey and Dinesh Sharma on the Supreme Court and the Chief Justice of India. The two MPs have also been asked to refrain from making such remarks. In a social media post, BJP President JP Nadda said  the Bharatiya Janata Party has nothing to do with the statements made by them. He said, these are their personal statements, but the BJP neither agrees with nor supports such statements.

Mr Nadda added, Bharatiya Janata Party has always respected the judiciary and gladly accepted its orders and suggestions. He said the party believes that all the courts of the country are an integral part of our democracy and are strong pillars of the protection of the Constitution.

The campaign will continue till 5th of next month. Under the campaign, BJP leaders and workers will visit Muslim communities and make them aware about the amended act.

Mr. Nadda had also accused the opposition parties of misleading Muslims over the provisions of the amended law as part of its vote-bank politics. He asserted that the Prime Minister Narendra Modi-led government is determined to make welfare programmes of the Waqf with transparent and efficient management of its properties.

## Midday News

In Jammu and Kashmir, at least three people lost their lives due to cloud burst in Seri Bagna area of Ramban district in Jammu region today. Ramban region witnessed heavy hailstorm, multiple landslides and gusty winds throughout last night. Over 100 people were rescued after heavy rain triggered a flash flood in Dharam Kund village in the district early this morning. 40 residential houses were damaged in the village due to violent weather condition in the region. Talking exclusively to Akashvani News, Deputy Commissioner Baseer-ul-Haq Chaudhary, said multiple vehicles were swept away in the flood caused by overflowing of river streams.

The Deputy Commissioner said, vehicular movement along the strategic Jammu-Srinagar National Highway is blocked due to landslides and mudslides following heavy rainfall.

Srinagar-Sonamarg-Gumri Road, historic Mughal Road and Sinthan top road are also closed.

Talking to media, Deputy Traffic Inspector Javed Kataria said, restoration work is underway but is being hampered by adverse weather conditions. He advised commuters not to travel on the arterial road till the weather improved and the roads are cleared.

Ramban district administration has released emergency contact numbers for 24×7 help at District Control Room 01998-295500, 01998-266790.

Meanwhile, Union Minister Dr Jitendra Singh and Jammu and Kashmir Chief Minister Omar Abdullah expressed grief over the loss of lives in the incident. The Union Minister in a social media post said, he is in constant touch with Deputy Commissioner of the district. He said, every kind of relief, both financial and otherwise, is being provided.

Taking to the social media, Jammu and Kashmir Chief Minister said, they are in touch with the local administration to ensure immediate rescue efforts wherever needed. The Chief Minister informed that he will be reviewing restoration, relief, and repair plans later in the day.
