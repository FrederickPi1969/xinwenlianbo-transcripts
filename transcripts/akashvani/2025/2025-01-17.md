## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will inaugurate the Bharat Mobility Global Expo 2025, the largest mobility expo in India, at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi today. The theme of this Expo is Beyond Boundaries: Co-creating Future Automotive Value Chain. This vision aims to foster collaboration and innovation across the automotive and mobility sector, emphasizing sustainable and cutting-edge technological advancements. Our correspondent reports that the Expo aims to unite the entire mobility value chain under one umbrella.

“The six day Bharat Mobility Global expo will be held across three separate venues –  Bharat Mandapam and Yashobhoomi in New Delhi, and India Expo Center and Mart in Greater Noida. The Expo will offer a unique platform to showcase innovations in automotive and mobility sector. This year’s expo will have a special emphasis on the global significance with participation from across the globe as exhibitors and visitors. Over five thousand international participants and eight hundred exhibitors will showcase their products in the expo. In addition, the Expo will also feature states sessions to showcase policies and initiatives in the mobility sector to enable collaboration between industry and regional levels. The entry to the Bharat Mobility Global Expo is free for all visitors from 19th to 22nd  of January. With Dipendra Kumar, Pallavi Parmar, Akashvani News Delhi.”

In view of the improvement in air quality, the Commission for Air Quality Management has revoked the stage four of Graded Response Action Plan (GRAP) across the National Capital Region with immediate effect. The decision has been taken by the Sub-Committee on GRAP after reviewing the situation in its meeting held yesterday. The Committee said that AQI of Delhi is continuously improving and has been recorded as 302 yesterday, which is about 98 AQI points below the benchmark as directed by the Supreme Court for invoking Stage-IV. However, all actions under Stages III, II and I of revised GRAP will remain invoked and be implemented, monitored and reviewed by all agencies concerned in the entire NCR to ensure that the AQI levels do not slip further.

Over seven crore pilgrims have taken a holy dip at the Triveni Sangam in Prayagraj in just six days, from 11th of this month till yesterday, during the ongoing Maha Kumbh Mela. The Sangam is witnessing a remarkable display of devotion and faith, with saints, Kalpvasis, and devotees gathering in large numbers for the sacred dip. Yesterday alone, more than 30 lakh devotees bathed in the holy water, seeking virtue and divine blessings.

“The entire Maha Kumbh Mela area is witnessing a continuous stream of pilgrims. Devotees from various states of India and countries around the world are immersing themselves in the spiritual atmosphere of the holy confluence. On the first day of Mahakumbh on 13th of January, a record 1.70 crore pilgrims took the ritual while the next day, during the Makar Sankranti Amrit Snan, over 3.50 crore people took a holy dip. The state government has anticipated that over 45 crore people will attend the Maha Kumbh this time. The early figure of 7 crore pilgrims in the initial days of the Mahakumbh strongly indicates this projection to be true. Mahakumbhnagar offers a vibrant glimpse of the nation’s diverse cultural heritage, uniting faith and traditions under one spiritual roof. The 45 day Mahakumbh Mela will continue till 26th of next month. Anand Kumar for Akashvani News, Prayagraj.”

India’s electronics exports surged by 35.11 percent to a two-year high of 3.58 billion dollars in December 2024 from 2.65 billion dollars in the same month of the previous year. Commerce Ministry said, this reflects the increase in foreign demand for high value Indian goods and rising domestic production capacities. The country’s electronic exports jumped by 28.6 percent to 26.11 billion dollars in April-December period of current fiscal year, from 20.3 billion dollars during the same period in 2023-24. Electronics goods have emerged as the fastest-growing segment in India’s export basket as new manufacturing capacities have come up in the country driven by the success of the Centre’s production-linked incentive (PLI) scheme.

The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs, CCEA has approved 11 thousand 440 crore rupees revival package for Rashtriya Ispat Nigam Limited (RINL). Information and broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw told media in New Delhi today, the revival package was cleared in yesterday’s cabinet meeting. He said, RINL is an important company in the country’s steel sector, located in Andhra Pradesh. Mr Vaishnaw said, with the help of this package, all the employees of RINL as well as all the people connected with the economic activities of RINL will be immensely benefited.

Home Minister Amit Shah today chaired a review meeting in New Delhi to assess the implementation of three new criminal laws in Madhya Pradesh. In a statement, the Home Ministry said  Mr. Shah appreciated the efforts made so far by the Madhya Pradesh government in implementing the laws. The Home Minister emphasized the need for  hundred per cent implementation of the laws in the state at the earliest. In the meeting, Mr. Shah mentioned that before registering cases under sections related to terrorism and organized crime, senior police officials should examine whether the case qualifies for the application of those sections. He said that any misuse of these legal provisions would undermine the sanctity of the new criminal laws.
