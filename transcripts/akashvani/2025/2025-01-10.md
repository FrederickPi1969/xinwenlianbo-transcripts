## Morning News

President Droupadi Murmu will attend the 18th Pravasi Bharatiya Divas Convention this morning being held at the Janata Maidan in Bhubaneswar. President Murmu will also confer the Pravasi Bharatiya Samman to distinguished members of the Indian diaspora for their excellence in different fields.

“Today is the last day of the 3-day long 18th Pravasi Bharatiya Divas Convention. President Droupadi Murmu is scheduled to preside over the valedictory function of the Convention and address the Indian diaspora this morning. She will also confer the Pravasi Bharatiya Samman 2025 to select Indian diaspora members. The Samman is the highest honour conferred on a Non-Resident Indian, Person of Indian Origin or an organisation or institution established and run by Non-Resident Indians or Persons of Indian Origin, who have made significant contribution within India or abroad for social and humanitarian work, for contributions made towards India’s development for building bridges between India and their host country or for enhancing India’s prestige in their home country. Prakash Dash, Akashvani News, Bhubaneswar.”

The Indian Navy has received the sixth and last Scorpene submarine, Vaghsheer of Project P-75 from Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders Limited. The submarine will subsequently get commissioned into the Indian Navy as INS Vaghsheer. According to an official statement, the advanced technology used in the Scorpene ensures superior stealth features and the capability to launch precise attacks using guided weapons. Vaghsheer can launch attacks with both torpedoes and tube-launched anti-ship missiles, whether submerged or on the surface. The submarine is capable of performing a variety of missions, including Anti-Surface Warfare, Anti-Submarine Warfare, Intelligence Gathering, and Area Surveillance.

India’s industrial output accelerated to a six month high of 5.2 percent in November as compared to 3.5 percent in October, according to the data released by the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation.

Among the three major components of Index of Industrial Production (IIP), manufacturing output climbed 5.8 percent in November, while electricity generation grew 4.4 percent and mining activity increased by 1.9 percent, as per the data.

The corresponding growth rates of IIP as per Use-based classification in November 2024 over November 2023 stood at 2.7 percent in Primary goods, 9 percent in Capital goods, 5 percent in Intermediate goods, 10 percent in Infrastructure or Construction Goods, 13.1 percent in Consumer durables and 0.6 percent in Consumer non-durable.
