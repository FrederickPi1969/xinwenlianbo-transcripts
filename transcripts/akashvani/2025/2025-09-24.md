## Morning News

Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw has said, two Vande Bharat Sleeper trains will be launched soon. Briefing the media in New Delhi yesterday, Mr Vaishnaw said, the first train has cleared all the tests and trial runs and the second train is likely to be completed by the middle of next month. He said, the two trains have to be launched together to ensure regular services on an overnight journey.

Manufactured by BEML using Integral Coach Factory technology, the Vande Bharat Sleeper train will be able to carry 1,128 passengers. It can operate at a top speed of 180 kmph, making it one of the fastest overnight train services in the country.
The Railway Minister also announced twelve thousand special trains to be operated during Diwali and Chhath festivals from 1st October till 15th November.

Uttar Pradesh celebrated ‘Ayushman Bharat Diwas’ yesterday, marking seven years of the Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana. Special camps were organized across the state, where Ayushman Cards were issued, making UP the top state in the country for card distribution. The CEO of State Agency For Comprehensive Health and Integrated Services, Archana Verma said, out of 9 crore targeted beneficiaries in Uttar Pradesh, 5.38 crore Ayushman Cards have already been issued, marking a historic achievement for the state.

Today, in this Special Series – ‘Seva Parv’, we bring to you how the Modi government is shaping India’s future through the India Semiconductor Mission.

Semiconductors are the essential building blocks of modern electronics, acting as the hidden brains that make devices work. Since the launch of the India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) in 2021, India has transformed its semiconductor journey from vision to reality under the leadership of Prime Minister Narendra Modi. While addressing the Semicon India 2025 in New Delhi this month, Prime Minister Modi described semiconductor chips as Digital Diamonds.

The India Semiconductor Mission is driving the 76,000 crore rupee Semicon India Programme, offering up to 50% support for semiconductor and display manufacturing. Through the Design Linked Incentive Scheme, it has engaged over 280 academic institutions and 70+ startups, while the SPECS scheme provides 25% support for electronic component manufacturing. India’s Semiconductor journey reached a milestone at the SEMICON India 2025, where Union Electronics and Technology Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw officially presented the first set of Made-in-India chips to Prime Minister Modi. Together, these initiatives reflect the country’s steady progress towards building a self-reliant semiconductor industry. As PM Modi has said, “today’s India inspires confidence in the world, and when the chips are down, the world can bet on India”.

In Bihar, the Congress Working Committee (CWC) meeting will be held in Patna today. This is the first time in more than eighty years that the Working Committee meeting is being held in Patna. Bihar in-charge of the party, Krishna Allavaru, informed that the meeting will deliberate on various national and international issues, including the Bihar Assembly elections and increasing the reservation limit.

The meeting, and chaired by Congress National President Mallikarjun Kharge, will be attended by senior leaders of the party, including Sonia Gandhi, Rahul Gandhi, and Priyanka Gandhi. Apart from Working Committee members, Chief Ministers of Telangana, Karnataka, Himachal Pradesh, state party presidents from all states, and leaders of the legislative parties will attend the meeting.

Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis will visit the flood affected areas of the state to review relief and rescue measures. Heavy rainfall and the release of water from the Lower Terna project have caused flooding in Terna river, severely affecting a village in Ausa taluka of Latur district. Several houses have been submerged and citizens have been shifted to safer locations as a precautionary measure. The Latur-Solapur National Highway was also partially closed due to floodwaters.

In Parbhani district, floods caused by heavy rains and water discharge have affected 36 villages. A total of 578 citizens have been safely evacuated with the assistance of the Indian Army and SDRF teams. In Beed district, 59 circles recorded excessive rainfall, and NDRF teams are engaged in rescuing 84 stranded citizens. In Dharashiv district, relief operations are ongoing in Bhoom and Paranda talukas. In Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar district, all 27 gates of the Jayakwadi dam have been opened, releasing around 85,000 cusecs of water into the Godavari river. As a result, all river ghats in Paithan have been submerged. Citizens residing along the riverbanks have been alerted. One casualty each has been reported in Vaijapur and Kannad talukas due to flooding. Samarjit thakur, Akashvani news, sambhaji nagar.

Back home, The next-generation GST reforms represent a significant step towards simplifying India’s indirect tax system. Now people can avail the benefit of GST reforms, which already came into effect from the 22nd of this month. Under the new structure, the GST council has simplified the earlier four-tier structure of 5, 12, 18 and 28 per cent into a two tier structure of 5 per cent and 18 per cent. By reducing the tax rate on a wide range of goods and services, the reforms are bringing substantial relief to citizens. A report:

The government has made significant tax reduction in the healthcare sector to provide relief to the common man. The GST rates on Health and life insurance premiums, which earlier attracted 18 per cent, have now been made completely  tax-free. This move is expected to encourage wider adoption of health insurance, reducing out of pocket medical expenses for middle class families.The government has also waived off taxes on 33 life saving drugs used for the treatment of cancer and severe chronic diseases to provide affordable and long-term treatments for chronic illnesses. Along with that, the GST on various medicines and medical equipment such as gauze, bandages, diagnostic kits, glucometers and thermometers have also been slashed to 5 percent from the previously taxed 12 percent. Talking to the, Akashvani News, Suyogya Shukla, a resident of Lucknow Uttar Pradesh, said that the reduced GST on medicines and insurance are going to benefit people and ease the financial burden.

This rationalisation in the healthcare sector marks a decisive step towards a healthier India. The decision of lowering the cost of medical services has directly eased the financial burden on the people. With Drishti, and Saklen Akhtar, Rohan for Akashvani News, Delhi.

External Affairs Minister Dr S Jaishankar hosted a High-Level Meeting of Global South Countries in New York on the sidelines of the 80th UN General Assembly session. During the meeting, Dr Jaishankar called for utilizing existing forums to strengthen consultations among the Global South with a view to enhance solidarity and encourage collaboration. He also made a proposal on reforms in the UN and multilateralism as whole.

In a social media post, Dr Jaishankar informed that on the sidelines of the High-Level meeting, he also met Singapore’s Foreign Minister Vivian Balakrishnan, St Lucia’s Foreign Minister Alva Baptiste and Foreign Minister of Somalia Abdisalam Ali. He also interacted with his Dutch counterpart David van Weel where he had an insightful conversation on European strategic positioning and India’s approach.

Union Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal has said that India is in a great spot to expand its clean energy over the next five years. Addressing the US-India Strategic Partnership Forum in New York, Mr Goyal said the government aims to grow its clean energy capacity from over 250 Gigawatts to 550 Gigawatts. The Minister said the government expects to increase its trade with the US on energy products in the upcoming years. Mr Goyal said India and the US can plan to collaborate on Nuclear power.

She highlighted that Indian art is continuously evolving and presenting new dimensions.

The Government has approved six major proposals with a total investment outlay of around 95 thousand crore rupees. Briefing media in New Delhi, Union Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said, the decisions taken in today’s Union Cabinet meeting cover several sectors.

The Minister said, the Cabinet has approved a comprehensive package of nearly 70 thousand crore rupees to revitalize India’s shipbuilding and maritime sector.

The government also cleared payment of Productivity Linked Bonus PLB of 78 days to 10.91 lakh railway employees.

Payment of PLB to eligible railway employees is made each year before Durga Puja and Dussehra holidays. In Chhattisgarh, seventy-one Maoists, including twenty-one women surrendered before the security forces in Dantewada district today. Police said, thirty of them were carrying a combined reward of sixty-four lakh rupees. The Maoists surrendered under the influence of the ‘Lone Varratu’ or ‘Return to Home’ campaign being run in the district. Authorities have imposed strict restrictions in Leh following violent protests demanding the advancement of proposed talks with the Centre on extending the Sixth Schedule and granting statehood to Ladakh. Our correspondent has the details:

“District Magistrate Leh has issued an order under Section 163 of Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS) 2023, for maintaining the Law and Order in district Leh. As per the Order, No Procession, rally or March shall be carried out without prior approval from Competent authority in Leh. No use of Loudspeaker mounted on vehicles, No one is allowed to make Statements which has potential to disturb the public tranquility and which may led to law and order problem in the district. Meanwhile, Lieutenant Governor of Ladakh, Kavinder Gupta has strongly condemned the violent incidents and appealed for peace. He said that the Center is ready to talk and the violent incidents that occurred today in Leh are against the history and legacy of Ladakh.
