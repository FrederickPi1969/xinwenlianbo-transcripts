## Morning News

Prime Minister and Senior BJP leader Narendra Modi will launch the party campaign for Bihar Assembly elections with an interactive session with party’s booth level workers today. He will interact with the party workers under the Mera Booth Sabse Majboot campaign. President Droupadi Murmu has said, India attaches the highest priority to its development and capacity building partnership with Mongolia. She said, India  is fully committed to the timely completion of all the projects currently underway, which will emerge as lasting symbols of India-Mongolia friendship and cooperation. India will resume international postal services to the United States from today. In a release, the Ministry of Communications said, India Post has now established a compliant mechanism for Delivery Duty Paid processing after extensive system development and coordination with the US Customs and Border Protection. The Ministry highlighted that the new set-up ensures full regulatory compliance, faster customs clearance, and seamless delivery to addressees in the US without any additional duty or delay. Postal services to the US were earlier suspended, following an Executive Order issued by the US Administration.

The Central Bureau of Investigation, CBI conducted searches at multiple locations across Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, and Kerala in online job fraud case. During the action carried out under Operation Chakra V yesterday, three persons were arrested. In this case, thousands of innocent citizens across India were allegedly cheated of crores of rupees through deceptive online schemes.

The Central agency said, the accused persons and their associates created a complex network of shell companies across India, primarily in Bangalore, to channel and launder the proceeds of crime. The fraudsters, including foreign operatives, were using forged credentials and fraudulently obtained digital signatures and incorporated multiple shell companies and opened numerous bank accounts in their names to collect funds from victims. Several Indian nationals were operating under the direction of foreign-controlled entities involved in illegal online gambling and investment fraud operations. The CBI traced substantial financial flows to accounts associated with these entities, indicating an extensive transnational cyber fraud network. With Anand’s report, Aishwarya Rai Nigam, Akashvani News, Delhi.

Pakistan-based smugglers are devising new ways to spread their network to smuggle narcotics in India through the Punjab border. Quoting highly placed sources in BSF, Punjab Frontier, our correspondent reports that Pakistani smugglers are preferring fresh faces with no prior smuggling record to escape legal scrutiny. They are also asking their Indian counterparts to recruit minors as couriers to escape arrest and conviction.

The Border Security Force, in an intensifying crackdown, has nabbed over 203 Indian drug smugglers and 16 Pakistani nationals during this year till date in Punjab, mainly from the high-risk border districts of Amritsar, Tarn Tarana and Ferozepur in Punjab. 3 Pakistani intruders have also been neutralized by the BSF. The Force has deployed anti-drone systems, motion sensors and night vision devices and has seized 200 Pak drones along with 287 kgs of heroin, 13 kgs of ICE drug, 174 weapons including AK- 47s, 12 hand grenades and over 10 kgs of high explosives these drones have carried inside the Indian territory. BSF spokesperson, Deputy Inspector General, AK Vidyarthi said that voluntary help by some villagers and joint operations with the Punjab Police and other central agencies are proving fruitful in tracing the local conduits of transnational drug cartels. Rajesh Bali/Akashvani News/Jalandhar.

In Rajasthan, the death toll in the Bus fire accident on Jaisalmer- Jodhpur highway has risen to 20. The incident took place around 3:30 PM yesterday when an AC sleeper bus caught fire, on the Jaisalmer-Jodhpur highway. According to reports, 19 passengers were charred to death inside the bus, while one person succumbed to burn injuries during treatment. Over 50 people were on board the bus at the time of the accident. Among 15 passengers who were injured, many suffered burns of up to 70 percent.  Officials said, the bodies of the victims were badly burnt and their identities will be confirmed through DNA testing. President Droupadi Murmu, Vice President C P Radhakrishnan and Prime Minister Narendra Modi have expressed condolences over the tragic deaths of several people. The Central Government has announced an ex-gratia of 2 lakh rupees each for the next of kin of the deceased and 50,000 rupees each for those injured.

The GST Bachat Utsav, announced by Prime Minister Narendra Modi which commenced on the 22nd of last month, is benefiting the country as a whole. Today, our correspondent brings a report on the GST reforms that are set to empower various industries in Telangana.

Telangana is one of India’s leading pharmaceutical manufacturing hubs. The new GST reforms hold special significance for the state’s pharmaceutical manufacturing sector. Under GST 2.0, 30 cancer drugs will now attract 0 per cent tax instead of 12 percent, improving accessibility for consumers. Similarly, GST on all drugs and medicines for personal use has been reduced to 5 per cent from 12 per cent. The reduction will make healthcare more affordable and accessible while boosting the growth and innovation of Telangana’s pharmaceutical and life sciences industry. Meanwhile, the GST on food products including, paneer, cheena, ultra-high temperature milk and Indian breads will now attract zero percent tax. Other items such as butter, ghee, dry fruits, pickles, namkeen, will now be under the 5 percent tax category which previously was the part of 12 percent tax slab. These rate cuts will reduce shelf prices by 6 to 7 percent, making processed foods more affordable across rural and semi-urban markets. Shivang Misra, Akashvani News, Delhi.

Israel has received the bodies of four hostages after they were handed over to the Red Cross by Hamas in Gaza. The statement of the office of Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said, the bodies were transferred to Israeli Defense Forces and Shin Bet personnel which is one of the three major intelligence organizations of Israel inside the Gaza Strip. They were later transferred across the border into Israel. The remains are being taken to the National Center of Forensic Medicine in Tel Aviv for identification.

Unlike a similar handover on Monday, Hamas did not disclose the identities of the bodies in advance, and Israeli authorities said their identities remain unclear.

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh has approved a hundred per cent increase in financial assistance for Ex-Servicemen and their dependents under schemes implemented by the Department of Ex-Servicemen Welfare. The penury grant has been doubled from four thousand rupees to eight thousand rupees per month per beneficiary. This will provide sustained lifetime support to aged and non-pensioner Ex-Servicemen and their widows above 65 years of age with no regular income. The Ministry said, the education grant has been increased from one thousand to two thousand rupees per month per head for up to two dependent children. The marriage grant has also been raised from 50 thousand to one lakh per beneficiary.

The Ministry said, the revised rates take effect for applications submitted from 1st November this year onwards, with an annual financial implication of approximately 257 crore rupees.
