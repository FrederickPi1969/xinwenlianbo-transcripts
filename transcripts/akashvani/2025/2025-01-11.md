## Morning News

Union Home Minister Amit Shah will chair a regional conference on Drug Trafficking and National Security in New Delhi today. The conference, which is being organised by the Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB), aims to address the growing concern of drug trafficking and its impact on national security, with a special focus on eight states and Union Territories of northern India. During the event, Mr. Shah will launch the Drug Disposal Fortnight drive and inaugurate the new office complex of the NCB’s Bhopal zonal unit and extension of the MANAS-2 helpline to all states and Union Territories.
During the drug disposal fortnight drive, which will continue till 25th of this month, more than 44 thousand kilograms of seized narcotics worth 2411 crore rupees, will be disposed of.

Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal has said that India has the potential to reach 100 billion US dollars in combined exports from the Food & Beverage (F&B), agriculture, and marine products industries in the next five years. Addressing on the sidelines of Indusfood 2025 in New Delhi, the Minister emphasised that the target is achievable, requiring a combined annual growth rate of 14 to 15 percent.

The Minister urged Indian companies to invest in innovation, better packaging, sustainability and to mechanise their processes to enhance hygiene and efficiency.

Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal has said that India aims to establish itself as world’s largest destination for data centres. Speaking on the sidelines of the Bharat Climate Forum in New Delhi yesterday, Mr Goyal said, India can realize this goal due to its interconnected grid, clean power, data security and trained workforce.
The Minister mentioned that the country is is one of the best performing countries under the Paris Agreement and is well ahead on every goal. He added that India has achieved its proposed 2030 target of having 40 to 45 percent of installed capacity as renewable or clean energy 8 years ahead of the schedule by 2022 itself.

Union Minister for Electronics and Information Technology Ashwini Vaishnaw laid the foundation stone for a new assembly line to produce laptops at the state-of-the-art Chennai facility of leading electronic systems design and manufacturing company Syrma SGS Technology Ltd in Tamil Nadu.

Speaking on the occasion, the Minister said this facility marks a significant milestone in the ‘Make in India’ initiative, as Syrma SGS collaborates with Taiwanese brand Micro Star International to produce laptops for the Indian market.

## Midday News

Union Home Minister Amit Shah has said that drugs worth more than sixteen thousand nine hundred crore rupees were seized in year 2024, marking the largest figure since independence. Chairing the regional conference on Drug Trafficking and National Security in New Delhi today, the Minister said, If we aim to make India supreme, it cannot be achieved without a drug-free India. He said, this battle must be fought collectively by the central and state governments on a united platform.

Mr Shah highlighted that over last ten years, nearly 24 lakh kilograms of drugs were seized, while drugs worth more than 56 thousand 800 crore rupees were disposed off. During the event, the Home Minister inaugurated the Drug Disposal fortnight and the extension of the MANAS-2 helpline to all states and Union Territories. Mr Shah also inaugurated the new office complex of the Narcotics Control Bureau’s Bhopal zonal unit. The Drug Disposal fortnight drive will begin from today to 25th of this month. During this period, a total of 44 thousand seven hundred ninety two kilograms of seized narcotics worth 2 thousand four hundred eleven crore rupees in market value will be disposed of.

Highlighting the Fortnight, the Minister asserted that it will deliver a strong message to the public against drug trafficking.

In Uttar Pradesh, a grand three-day celebration has commenced in Ayodhya to commemorate the first anniversary of the consecration ceremony of Ram Lalla’s idol at the Ram temple. Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath inaugurated the programme.

The Shri Ram temple was decorated with more than 50 quintals of flowers. Ram Lalla was anointed with Panchamrit and water from Saryu and 56 dishes were offered as prasad and Maha Aarti was performed. More from our correspondent:

As this is the first anniversary of the consecration of Ram Lalla in the Shri Ram Janmabhoomi temple, the devotees from all over the country and the world have reached Ayodhya. On this special occasion, Ram Lalla is giving darshan to the devotees wearing a pitamber woven with gold and silver. Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath inaugurated the three-day Pratishtha Dwadashi festival. During the Pratishtha Dwadashi festival, 21 Vedic Acharyas will chant 6 lakh Shri Ram Beej Mantras on all three days. Also, there will be a bhajan programme for Lord Ram Lalla every day from 6:00 pm to 9:00 pm. Apart from this, there will be Ram Katha, discourses and cultural programs every day at Angad Tila, in which common people can participate. In view of the first anniversary program, no passes will be made for darshan on all three days. Also, live telecast is being done on Doordarshan so that more people can have darshan of Ram Lalla. Tight security arrangements have also been made for the convenience and safety of the devotees. Om Awasthi, Akashvani News, Lucknow.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has greeted the countrymen on the occasion. In a social media post, the Prime Minister said, this temple was built after centuries of sacrifice, penance, and struggle. He expressed confidence that Ram temple will become a great source of inspiration in the accomplishment of the resolution for a developed India.

“Cloudy weather persists in the higher and mid-hill regions of the state, further intensifying the cold wave. In the higher reaches of the tribal districts of Kinnaur and Lahaul-Spiti, the minimum temperature is being recorded below the freezing point in many areas. Meanwhile, in the plains like Kangra, Bilaspur, and Una, dense fog has reduced morning visibility to just 50 meters, further affecting traffic. The Meteorological Center in Shimla has predicted rain and snowfall in the hilly areas of the state over the next 24 hours. A yellow alert has also been issued for dense fog in the plains and lower regions during this period. Prabha Sharma, Akashvani News, Shimla.”

In Ladakh, temperatures have plunged to extreme lows, with Drass recording close to minus 25 degree Celcius, followed by Kargil at minus 14.6 degree Celcius and Leh at minus 13.3 degree Celcius. Passengers are advised to travel on Jammu-Srinagar National Highway during day time and follow lane discipline.
