His vision was realized on the 3rd of this month, when the GST Council reduced tax rates in many sectors to ease the lives of citizens. Today, we take a look at the reforms in the agriculture sector.

“Under the Next Generation GST Reforms, the government has provided relief to the farmers through significant reduction in the GST in agricultural sector. The tax rate on the tractors below 1800 cc will be reduced from 12 percent to 5 percent. Tractor components including tyres and tubes, hydraulic pumps and others will be slashed down to 5 percent slab which previously was the part of 18 percent slab.Several other utilities including Specified Bio-Pesticides, Micro Nutrients, Drip Irrigation System and Sprinklers, alongside various Agricultural machines for cultivation, harvesting and threshing have witnessed reduction from 12 percent to 5 percent. Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman said that labour-intensive industries have been given strong support, while farmers and the agriculture sector will also benefit from the decisions.

The changes will particularly benefit small and medium farmers. The reduction in GST rates on agricultural equipment will lower the cost of farming, New tax rates will come into effect from 22nd of this month . Shivang, Akashvani News, Delhi.“
