Prime Minister Narendra Modi and French President Emmanuel Macron addressed the 14th India-France CEOs Forum in Paris last evening, reaffirming their commitment to strengthening business ties and fostering innovation. The forum brought together top industry leaders from both countries, focusing on key sectors like Defence, Aerospace, Artificial Intelligence, Infrastructure, and Sustainable Development.

In his address, Prime Minister Modi highlighted India’s growing attractiveness as a global investment hub, emphasizing its stable policies and recent economic reforms. He said, this is the right time for businesses to come to India as the country works towards the goal of becoming a ‘Viksit Bharat’ by 2047 while providing a robust business-friendly environment and policy continuity. Mr Modi spoke about opening up the insurance sector to 100 per cent FDI and simplifying tax structures to enhance ease of doing business.

The Prime Minister also informed about allowing private participation in civil nuclear energy.

Mr Modi invited French companies to explore new investment opportunities in sectors like defence, energy, aviation, space, fintech, and healthcare.

The Prime Minister stressed India’s leadership in emerging technologies such as AI, semiconductors, quantum computing, and hydrogen energy, urging stronger Indo-French collaboration for mutual growth.

External Affairs Minister Dr. S. Jaishankar also addressed the forum, emphasising the importance of a global, inclusive approach to AI and innovation. He spoke about strengthening India-France and India-EU cooperation in key areas, diversifying production, and building resilient supply chains.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi also held a meeting with US Vice President JD Vance and his family in Paris. In a social media post, Mr Modi said they discussed various subjects. He also expressed delight over joining them in celebrating the birthday of their son.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi met with President of Estonia, Alar Karis on the sidelines of the AI Action Summit in Paris yesterday. They underlined that warm and friendly relations between India and Estonia are based on their shared commitment to democracy, rule of law and the values of freedom and pluralism. Mr Modi invited the Estonian government and companies to explore the opportunities offered by the India growth story and take advantage of programmes such as Digital India.

## Evening News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi and French President Emmanuel Macron have reaffirmed their strong commitment to the India-France Strategic Partnership. During their bilateral talks in Paris, the two leaders reviewed cooperation in the strategic areas of Defence, Civil Nuclear Energy and Space. They also held discussion on boosting technology, innovation, and investment ties, with special focus on the upcoming India-France Year of Innovation in 2026.

Mr Modi along with the French President reached the historic French city of Marseille this morning after concluding a series of high-level engagements including the co-chairing of the Artificial Intelligence Action Summit. The two leaders also held delegation level talks after their arrival in Marseille.

A Joint Statement outlining the roadmap for future cooperation was adopted, with ten key agreements signed in areas including civil nuclear energy, environment, and culture.

This afternoon, Prime Minister Modi and French President Macron jointly inaugurated the new Indian Consulate in Marseille. This new consulate will serve four regions in southern France. A large number of Indian diaspora members gathered to witness the historic occasion.

The two leaders also visited International Thermonuclear Experimental Reactor (ITER) project, in Cadarache. Both leaders also paid tribute to Indian soldiers who sacrificed their lives during the first and second World Wars at the Mazargues War Cemetery. Further deepening maritime ties, Prime Minister Modi and President Macron visited the Marseilles Port. The leaders also reviewed the India-Middle East-Europe Economic Corridor in Marseille, highlighting its role in enhancing regional connectivity and economic integration.

Briefing media in Marseille today, Foreign Secretary Vikram Misri said, Prime Minister Modi and French President Macron during their talks exchanged views on the recent geopolitical developments in Europe, West Asia and Indo-Pacific.

The Foreign Secretary said Artificial Intelligence (AI) was a major area of bilateral discussion between the two sides.

Prime Minister Modi has since left for Washington DC on a two-day visit to United States after wrapping up his successful visit to France. Mr Modi will reach Washington DC in the wee hours tomorrow. where he will hold bilateral talks with US President Donald Trump.
