## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will visit Bihar and West Bengal today. Our correspondent reports that in Bihar, Mr Modi will lay the foundation stone and inaugurate multiple development projects worth around 13 thousand crore rupees at Gaya at around 11 AM.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will inaugurate and lay the foundation stones of several projects that are expected to boost economic activities, strengthen infrastructure, and promote tourism in Bihar. This bridge has made commuting between North and South Bihar much easier. After addressing the program in Bodh Gaya, the Prime Minister will also visit Simaria to see this mega bridge. Local residents have urged the government to name the bridge after the national poet Ramdhari Singh Dinkar, who was born in this region. Under the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana, Mr. Modi will also facilitate housewarming for more than 16,000 beneficiaries. Dharmendra Kumar Rai, FOR PATNA ,YASHASVI Akashvani News , DELHI

The Punjab Police’s Cyber Crime Wing has busted an inter-state mule account racket involved in duping thousands of people of crores of rupees across the country. Four persons running the racket have been arrested in coordination with the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) of the Ministry of Home Affairs. Police have also recovered cash amounting to 10.96 lakh rupees, nine mobile phones, 32 debit cards and 15 bank passbooks from the accused. Our correspondent reports that a mule account is a bank account used by criminals to receive, transfer, or launder illicit funds without the knowledge or sometimes with the complicity of the account holder.

Special Director General of Punjab Police’s, Cyber Crime Department, Ms V Neerja said the racket was involved in obtaining bank accounts, especially from economically weaker sections in lieu of money. They use the accounts to transfer fraudulent funds acquired through various cybercrimes. They were actively operating this criminal ecosystem through social media for the last two years, using hundreds of mule accounts from different banks across Punjab to transfer illicit money abroad through cryptocurrency exchanges. Rajesh Bali, Akashvani News, Jalandhar.

In the women’s 200m canoe singles, Odisha’s Rasmita Sahoo won gold.

Union Sports Minister Mansukh Mandaviya inaugurated the event via video message. The opening ceremony was attended by several dignitaries, including J&K’s Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha. The three-day festival, running until tomorrow features over 500 athletes from 28 states and UTs.

The Supreme Court modified its earlier directions for capture and complete relocation of stray dogs in the Delhi-NCR region. In a fresh order, the three-judge Bench of Justices Vikram Nath, Sandeep Mehta and N.V. Anjaria directed that stray dogs picked up must be released in their own territory after sterilisation and immunisation, except those infected with rabies or exhibiting aggressive behaviour.

The Justice Nath-led Bench emphasised the creation of dedicated feeding spaces for stray dogs while restricting public feeding. Expanding the scope of proceedings beyond Delhi-NCR, the apex court impleaded all Union Territories and state governments for creation of a pan-India policy on stray dogs and proposed transferring to itself similar petitions pending in various High Courts.

The Supreme Court today directed the Election Commission to accept claim forms of voters online, whose names are missing, without insisting on the physical submission of documents. The bench also clarified that the claim form could be submitted with any of the 11 documents originally listed by the ECI, or with the Aadhaar card. The order was passed by a bench of Justices Surya Kant and Joymalya Bagchi while hearing a batch of pleas challenging the June 26 order issued by the ECI directing the SIR in Bihar. The bench asked the political parties and their booth-level agents across the state to assist people who were unable to submit their enumeration forms and, as a result, had their names omitted from the voters’ list.
