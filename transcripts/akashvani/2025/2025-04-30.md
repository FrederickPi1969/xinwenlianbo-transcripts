## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi chaired a meeting with Defence Minister Rajnath Singh, and National Security Advisor Ajit Doval at his residence in New Delhi yesterday. CDS Anil Chauhan and Chiefs of three services also attended the meeting. The meeting was held in the wake of the Pahalgam terror attack. Later, RSS Chief, Mohan Bhagwat also met Prime Minister at his resedence in New Delhi.

External Affairs Minister Dr. S Jaishankar received a call from UN Secretary General Antonio Guterres and appreciated his unequivocal condemnation of the terrorist attack in Pahalgam. During the conversation, they agreed on the importance of accountability. In a social media post, Dr. Jaishankar said that India has resolved that the perpetrators, planners and backers of this attack are brought to justice.

External Affairs Minister expressed appreciation after speaking with the UAE’s Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister, Sheikh Abdullah bin Zayed Al Nahyan. In a social media post, Dr. Jaishankar stated that their discussion focused on the need to counter terrorism effectively in all its forms and manifestations. Earlier, Dr. Jaishankar had spoken with the Foreign Ministers of Greece, Cyprus, Panama, Somalia, Slovenia, and Guyana regarding the recent terrorist attack in Pahalgam.

The Indian Army effectively responded to firing by Pakistan military along Line of Control in Jammu and Kashmir last night. Defence sources said, Pakistan Army posts fired unprovoked across the Line of Control in areas opposite Naushera, Sunderbani and Akhnoor sector. Pakistan Army posts also fired in Baramulla and Kupwara districts as well as across the international border in the Pargwal sector. The Indian troops responded swiftly and effectively.

Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis has announced 50 lakh rupees financial assistance for families of victims from the state in the Pahalgam terror attack. At a Cabinet meeting in Mumbai, the government also assured educational and job support for victims’ families, including a government job for Asawari Jagdale, daughter of Santosh Jagdale who died in the attack.

BJP has sharply reacted to an image shared by Congress on its social media handle targeting Prime Minister Narendra Modi. Briefing media in New Delhi, party spokesperson Gaurav Bhatia alleged that Congress is trying to weaken the country through such post in this crucial time after the Pahalgam terror attack. Mr. Bhatia alleged that Congress is giving message to Pakistan that it is with Islamabad.

Union Minister of State for Home Affairs, Bandi Sanjay Kumar, has alleged that the Congress’s post, which features the Prime Minister alongside an objectionable image with the caption ‘Gayab’, is highly irresponsible.

Supreme Court  Judge, Justice Bhushan Ramkrishna Gavai will be the next Chief Justice of India. Union Minister of Law and Justice Arjun Ram Meghwal said that President Droupadi Murmu has  appointed  Justice Gavai as the next Chief Justice of India and his appointment will be effective from 14th of May. Justice Gavai will be the 52nd Chief Justice. He will succeed Chief Justice Sanjiv Khanna whose tenure will end on 13th of next month.

The Anti-Corruption Branch (ACB) of Delhi Police has registered a case against senior Aam Aadmi Party leaders and former Ministers Manish Sisodia and Satyendar Jain, in connection with an alleged 2,000-crore rupees classroom construction scam. As per the complaint, the scam is related to the construction of more than 12 thousand classrooms and buildings during the tenure of the AAP government. In a statement, head of ACB Madhur Verma said the contracts were awarded to 34 firms, and most of them were found to be associated with the AAP.

Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar inaugurated a two-day capacity-building training programme for Electoral Registration Officers (EROs) and Booth Level Officers (BLOs) in New Delhi today. EROs and BLOs from Bihar, Haryana, Delhi, and Uttar Pradesh are participating in the two day programme. The training programme is part of the EC’s ongoing preparations for the upcoming Elections to the Legislative Assemblies. A total of 369 grassroots election officials are taking part in the mixed-batch training programme.

The Government today decided to include the caste enumeration as part of the next census. The Cabinet Committee on Political Affairs chaired by Prime Minister Narendra Modi has taken a decision in this regard. Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw informed about the cabinet decision at a media briefing today.

Taking on opposition Congress, Mr Vaishnaw said, the move demonstrates that the Narendra Modi government is committed to the values and interests of the society and country, like in the past when it had introduced ten per cent reservation for the economically weaker sections  without causing stress in any section of the society.

The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) has approved a new highway from Shillong to Silchar at a cost of 22 thousand 864 crore rupees. Mr Vaishnaw informed that the length of the highway will be over 166 kilometers and it will improve connectivity to Tripura, Mizoram, Manipur and the Barak valley region of Assam.

The CCEA also approved fair and remunerative price of sugarcane for sugar season 2025-26 at 355 rupees per quintal for farmers. Mr. Vaishaw added that  that decision will benefit five crore sugarcane farmers:

Union Home Minister Amit Shah has decribed the decision to include caste enumeration in the upcoming census as a message of strong commitment towards social equality and rights of every section. In a social media post, Mr Shah said, the decision will empower all economically and socially backward classes, promote inclusion and pave new paths for the progress of the deprived.

BJP national president Jagat Prakash Nadda has said, the decision is extremely important for bringing the economically, socially and educationally backward castes into the mainstream and restoring the dignity to the people who have been deprived of their rights for a long time.  Reacting on the decison, Congress President Mallikarjun Kharge, in a social media post, said the announcement of caste census is a right step which the Congress party has been demanding from day one.

Senior Congress leader Rahul Gandhi, while briefing the media in New Delhi said, his party completely support it but they want a timeline to know by when this will happen.

Lok Janshakti (Ram Vilas) leader and Union Minister Chirag Paswan said, it is a huge and historic decision. Talking to media in New Delhi, Mr Paswan said, the Prime Minister has fulfilled a demand that was pending for a long time.

Telugu Desam Party Chief and Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister N Chandrababu Naidu said, the decision reflects Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s deep commitment to inclusive governance.

Communist Party of India CPI general seceretary D Raja said, his party has consistently demanded a caste census.

Union Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan told media in Bhopal that the caste census will be conducted with complete transparency.

Chief Minister Nitish Kumar has welcomed the Centre’s decision to conduct the Caste Census in the country. Mr. Kumar said, it is a positive initiative. He said by caste enumeration it will exactly establish the number of various classes and the outcome of Caste Census will help in formulation of development plans and their upliftment.

RJD leader and former Deputy Chief Minister Tejashwi Prasad Yadav said the Centre’s decision is a victory of his party’s demand.
