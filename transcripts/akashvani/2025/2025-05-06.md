## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi chaired a meeting of the Committee for the appointment of the CBI Director.The meeting was held at the Prime Minister’s Office in New Delhi yesterday. It was attended by Chief Justice of India, Justice Sanjiv Khanna and Leader of the Oppostion in the Lok Sabha, Rahul Gandhi.

During the meeting, the discussion took place about the appointment of the next CBI Director.

The meeting comes ahead of the end of the two-year term of incumbent CBI Director Praveen Sood on 25th of this month. The appointment of the CBI Director is done by the Central Government on the recommendation of a three-member appointment committee, which is headed by the Prime Minister and comprises the Leader of Opposition in Lok Sabha and the Chief Justice of India.

The Union Home Ministry has asked the states to conduct mock drills tomorrow to evaluate civil defence preparedness. It has decided to organize the Civil Defence Exercise and Rehearsal across the 244 categorized Civil Defence Districts of the country. The conduct of the exercise is planned up to the village level.

Union Home Secretary Govind Mohan is also scheduled to hold a meeting today with Chief Secretaries of States  through video-conferencing on Civil Defence Mock Drill. Our Correspondent reports that the Civil Defence Exercise is aimed to assess the operational efficacy and coordination of various Civil Defence measures.

“This exercise aims to assess and enhance the readiness of Civil Defence mechanisms across all States and Union Territories. Its primary objective is  to assess the effectiveness of air raid warning systems, operationalization of Hotline or Radio Communication Links with the Indian Air Force, as well as to test the functionality of control rooms. The exercise also includes training of civilians and students, on the civil defence aspects to protect themselves in the event of hostile attack. Besides the provision of crash blackout measures, provision for early camouflaging of vital installations and update of the evacuation plan and its rehearsal are also included in the Mock Drill. Saklen Akhtar for Akashvani News, Delhi.”

The Indian Army effectively responded to firing by Pakistan military along Line of Control in Jammu and Kashmir last night. Defence sources said, the Pakistan Army resorted to unprovoked small‑arms fire across the Line of Control opposite Kupwara, Baramulla, Poonch, Rajauri, Mendhar, Naushera, Sunderbani and Akhnoor in Jammu and Kashmir. The Indian troops responded promptly and proportionately. There are no reports of any casualties.

UN Secretary-General António Guterres has strongly condemned the Pahalgam terror attack and extended condolences to the families of the victims. He said that targeting civilians is unacceptable and those responsible must be brought to justice through credible lawful means. He told this to journalists at the UN Headquarters in New York, ahead of a closed-door Security Council meeting to address rising tensions between India and Pakistan. He noted that relations between the two South Asian neighbors were at their ‘highest in years.’ Mr.Guterres assured that the United Nations is ready to support any initiative that fosters de-escalation, diplomacy, and a renewed commitment to peace.

Russian President Vladimir Putin has spoken to Prime Minister Narendra Modi and strongly condemned the terror attack in Pahalgam. During the telephonic conversation yesterday, Mr Putin conveyed his deepest condolences on the loss of innocent lives and expressed full support to India in the fight against terrorism. He emphasised that the perpetrators of the heinous attack and their supporters must be brought to justice.

In a social media post, External Affairs Ministry spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal said, both leaders reiterated their commitment to further deepen the Special and Privileged Strategic Partnership between the two countries. Prime Minister Modi conveyed greetings to President Putin on the celebration of the 80th anniversary of Victory Day and invited him for the Annual Summit to be held in India later in the year.

Leading global airlines including Air France and Lufthansa, have stopped flying over Pakistani airspace to ensure the safety of their flights. German airline Lufthansa, in a statement, said that its aircraft are avoiding Pakistani airspace until further notice. The flight tracking data showed that flights operated by British Airways, Swiss International Air Lines, and Emirates have been turning away from Pakistani airspace, opting for longer routes to reach their destinations.

Minister of State for Science and Technology Jitendra Singh underscored the importance of building an indigenous India AI Open Stack foundational AI architecture embedded with science and engineering models tailored for Indian researchers. Chairing a comprehensive review of the Department of Science and Technology (DST) in New Delhi yesterday, he called for new and bold directions in science-driven growth, including AI-led innovations, deep tech startups, and enhanced infrastructure sharing.

The Ministry of Science and Technology, in a statement, said that during the meeting, Dr. Singh particularly focused on the evolving role of the newly constituted Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF), as well as ongoing national missions like the Geospatial Initiative. The Minister further asked the ANRF to explore the possibility of helping medical colleges in establishing their own medical research parks with an aim to boost clinical innovation and local biotech entrepreneurship.
