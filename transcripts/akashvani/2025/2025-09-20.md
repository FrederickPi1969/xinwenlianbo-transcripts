India has strongly cautioned all its citizens to exercise the strictest vigilance regarding employment promises or offers in Iran. In a statement, the External Affairs Ministry said, there have been a number of cases recently involving Indian citizens who have been lured to travel to Iran on false promises of employment, or with assurances that they would be sent further to third-countries for employment. The Ministry said, the Government of Iran allows visa-free entry to Indians only for tourism purposes. It said, any agent promising visa-free entry into Iran for employment or other purposes may well be in connivance with criminal gangs.

India has voted in favour of a UN General Assembly resolution that allows Palestinian President Mahmoud Abbas to address its upcoming high-level session through video. It comes after the US denied visas to Palestinian officials, preventing their participation in person.

The 193-member General Assembly adopted the resolution titled ‘Participation by the State of Palestine’ during its 80th session, with 145 nations voting in favour, five against and six abstentions. The US and Israel opposed the measure, while India was among those supporting it.

Two Jawans of the Assam Rifles lost their lives after a column of the paramilitary force came under unprovoked automatic weapon fire from unidentified terrorists in Bishnupur district of Manipur last evening. Five jawans were also injured in the incident and admitted to the hospital. They are currently stable. As of now, no group has claimed responsibility. Search operations are ongoing to eliminate the terrorists involved in the incident.

In a landmark move towards simplifying the tax structure and easing the financial burden on citizens, Prime Minister Narendra Modi in his Independence Day address this year announced the next generation GST reforms. The new tax rates will be implemented from Monday. Today, we take a look at the potential impact of these reforms on the electronic appliances sector.

“The Tax rate on items like  Air Conditioners and Dishwashers has been brought down from 28 per cent to 18 per cent. The significant step will not just support localisation efforts but will also reduce import dependence. Similarly cut down of tax on Television and Non TV monitors has been slashed to 18 percent. The move will directly boost demand for these appliances, helping domestic producers expand the market size. Talking to Akashvani, a resident of Delhi, Shubhanan Misra, welcomed the new reforms.

This rationalization under the next generation GST reforms is expected to stimulate demand for electronic items such as AC and large TVs, expanding the market size for domestic producers. This move will further expand opportunities for MSMEs in plastics, wiring, cooling systems, LED panels, and assembly services. With Rohan and Nitika Saklen Akhtar, FOR Akashvani News, Delhi.”

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has asserted that India’s dependence on foreign countries remains one of its biggest enemies. He said it is high time that India stands before the world as a self-reliant nation to realise the vision of a developed India by 2047. The Prime Minister was addressing the ‘Samudra Se Samriddhi’ programme in Bhavnagar today.

Unveiling his government’s vision to make India a maritime superpower, Prime Minister Modi said the country aims to triple its share in global maritime trade by 2047. He said, for global peace, stability, and prosperity, the world’s most populous country must become self-reliant.

During the programme, the Prime Minister inaugurated and laid the foundation stone for multiple projects worth over 32,000 crore rupees. Mr. Modi also inaugurated Mumbai International Cruise Terminal along with several major maritime projects valued at more than 7,800 crore rupees.

Later, Prime Minister Modi visited the ancient town of Lothal near Ahmedabad and reviewed progress of the National Maritime Heritage Complex, NMHC.  He inspected completed works and chaired a review meeting with officials. The NMHC is being developed at a cost of 4,500 crore rupee at the historic site which was once a significant trading centre of the Indus Valley Civilization. It aims to showcase India’s rich maritime legacy to the world. The Prime Minister also undertook an aerial survey of Dholera smart City.

She asserted that the Government would render all assistance for the benefit of  the fireworks and match sticks manufacturers.

Today, in this Special Series – ‘Seva Parv’, we bring to you how the Modi government has strengthened the digital health ecosystem, making healthcare more accessible and connected.

“India’s healthcare landscape is undergoing a digital transformation, driven by government initiatives, policy reforms, and technological advancements. With a rapidly growing population and increasing demand for quality healthcare, digital health solutions are playing a crucial role in enhancing accessibility, affordability, and efficiency. Launched by Prime Minister Narendra Modi in September, 2021, the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission aims to establish a robust digital health ecosystem through active collaboration between public and private sectors. Launching the mission the Prime Minister said that  it will now connect the digital health solutions of hospitals across the country with each other.

With a planned outlay of 1,600 crore rupees over five years from 2021 to 2026, the mission aims to create the digital backbone necessary to support India’s evolving healthcare infrastructure. Since its launch, over 80 crore Ayushman Bharat Health Accounts have been created, with more than 71 crore health records linked to them. Ayushman Bharat Health Accounts, a key part of the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission is designed to support India’s integrated digital health infrastructure. It streamlines interactions with verified healthcare providers and facilitates seamless access to lab reports, prescriptions, and other health records. eSanjeevani, a cohesive part of Ayushman Bharat Digital Health Mission, is  a cutting-edge cloud-based telemedicine platform. It has redefined the landscape of healthcare accessibility in India. This revolutionary platform, designed to transcend geographical limitations, facilitates remote consultations between healthcare providers and patients, as a National Telemedicine Service.”
