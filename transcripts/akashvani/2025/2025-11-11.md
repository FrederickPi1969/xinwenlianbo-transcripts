## Morning News

A powerful blast in a car near Red Fort in Delhi killed eight people and injured several others yesterday evening. Union Home Minister Amit Shah said that investigators are keeping all options open while probing the blast, which took place in a Hyundai i20 car at a traffic signal at around 7 pm. It damaged three to four other vehicles and injured pedestrians and people travelling in autorickshaws. Mr Shah said, teams of the Delhi Police, NIA, NSG and forensics have started the probe and the exact details of the blast would be unearthed soon.

Delhi Police Commissioner Satish Golcha said earlier that a slow-moving vehicle stopped at a red light in the area near Red Fort Metro Station, and the explosion occurred in that vehicle. Delhi Police has registered a case under various Sections of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA), Explosives Act, and Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi took stock of the situation in the wake of the blast and spoke to Home Minister Amit Shah. Mr Shah visited the site of the car explosion and met the injured at LNJP Hospital, promising a thorough investigation into the blast. Home Minister will review the situation at a high-level meeting with top officials today.

President Droupadi Murmu, Vice President C P Radhakrishnan and Prime Minister Narendra Modi have conveyed their condolences to the families and friends of those who lost their lives in the blast. In a social media post, the President also prayed for quick recovery of those injured.

In his message, Vice President extended his condolences to the families who lost their loved ones and also prayed for the speedy recovery of those injured.

Prime Minister, in a social media post, expressed condolences to those who have lost their loved ones in the blast. Mr Modi hoped for the speedy recovery of those injured. Prime Minister added that those affected are being assisted by authorities.

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh called the incident extremely painful and disturbing. In a social media post, he prayed for the speedy recovery of the injured.

Condolences have poured in from ambassadors of several countries after the Delhi blast.

Japanese Ambassador Ono Keiichi expressed deep sorrow over the loss of lives, offering his condolences to the bereaved families and praying for the speedy recovery of the injured.

British High Commissioner to India, Lindy Cameron said her thoughts are with all those affected by the explosion and shared an updated travel advisory.

The US Embassy in India has issued a security alert for its citizens, advising them to avoid areas around Delhi’s Red Fort and Chandni Chowk, stay alert in crowded places, and monitor local media for updates.

The Embassy of Iran also conveyed its sympathy, expressing deep sorrow over the loss of lives and injuries, and extending condolences to the Government and people of India.

Polling has begun for second and final phase of assembly elections in Bihar. Voting is taking place for 122 constituencies spread over 20 districts in this phase. More than 3 crore 70 lakh voters will decide the fate of one thousand 302 candidates including 136 women. The voting will run till 6 in the evening, except for 7 constituencies, where it will end at 5 pm for security reasons.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that the conspirators behind the Delhi car blast will not be spared.
