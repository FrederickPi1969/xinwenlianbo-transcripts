NECTAR, an autonomous body under Department of Science and Technology has unveiled an innovative Aerostatic Drone for enhanced surveillance and disaster management. The Ministry of Science and Technology in a statement today said that it is the first of its kind in the country.

Aerostatic drones are aerial platforms that derive their lift from both buoyancy and aerodynamics. This technology provides a silent aerial platform that can persistently stay afloat for surveillance with an endurance of over 4 hours. These drones can serve as temporary communication relays in remote areas or during emergencies. They can also be integrated into systems designed to detect unauthorized drone activity, enhancing security measures at sensitive locations such as airports and military bases. These drones can be utilized to monitor traffic conditions in urban areas, providing valuable data for traffic management systems. With Pallavi Parmar, Adarsh for Akashvani News, Delhi.
