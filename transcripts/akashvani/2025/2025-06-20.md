## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will visit Bihar today. He will address a public meeting at Jasauli under Pachrukhi block in Siwan district around 12 noon. The Prime Minister will inaugurate and lay the foundation stone for multiple development projects related to urban development, sewage treatment, PM Awas Yojna and railways worth over 5900 crore rupees via video conferencing from Jasauli.

Boosting railway infrastructure in the region, the Prime Minister will inaugurate the new Vaishali-Deoria railway line project connecting Bihar and Uttar Pradesh worth over 400 crore rupees and flag off a new train service on this route. For better rail connectivity to North Bihar and Purvanchal region of Uttar Pradesh, the Prime Minister will also flag off the Vande Bharat Express between Patliputra and Gorakhpur via Muzaffarpur and Bettiah. The introduction of the new Vande Bharat Express train between Patliputra Junction to Gorakhpur fulfills the much awaited demand of people of Western Bihar and Eastern Uttar Pradesh. He will flag off a state-of-the-art locomotive built at the Marhowra ( मढ़ौरा) Plant in Saran district for export to the Western African country Republic of Guinea. This is the first export locomotive manufactured in this factory in Bihar.

In line with his commitment to conservation and rejuvenation of river Ganga, the Prime Minister will inaugurate six Sewage Treatment Plants (STPs) under the Namami Gange project The Prime Minister will also release the first instalment to more than 53,600 beneficiaries of PM Awas Yojana Urban in Bihar. With Dharmendra’s report from Siwan, aishwarya rai nigam, Akashvani news.

Later in the day, The Prime Minister will visit Odisha to attend the state-level function at Janata Maidan in Bhubaneswar to commemorate the completion of one year of the first BJP government in Odisha led by Chief Minister Mohan Charan Majhi. He will also inaugurate and lay the foundation stone of multiple development projects worth over 18,600 crore rupees on the occasion.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi is scheduled to hold a roadshow and participate in a ‘Tiranga Yatra’ soon after his arrival at Bhubaneswar this afternoon. The roadshow and ‘Tiranga Yatra’ will culminate at Janata Maidan, the venue of the Odisha government’s first-anniversary function. President Droupadi Murmu will inaugurate and lay the foundation stone of various projects at Rashtrapati Niketan in Dehradun, Uttarakhand today. During the visit, President Murmu will also lay the foundation stone of the President’s Park.
