## Morning News

Maharashtra government has signed 54 MoUs worth 15.70 lakh crore rupees during the World Economic Forum (WEF) Annual Meeting 2025 in Davos. A statement from Chief Minister’s Office said that these agreements are expected to generate approximately 16 lakh jobs across various sectors. One of the key agreements involves MSN Holdings Ltd, which plans to establish an advanced lithium battery and cell manufacturing project under the state’s ‘Ultra Mega Project’ initiative.

“The project, requiring an investment of rupees 14,652 crore, aims to create 8,760 direct and indirect jobs, with a focus on development in the Vidarbha region. The largest MoU signed with Reliance Group, which has committed more than 3 lakh crore rupees for investments across petrochemicals, polyester, renewable energy, bioenergy, green hydrogen, hospitality, and real estate etc. Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis has emphasized the state’s goal of achieving a 1 trillion dollar economy. These investments are part of efforts to attract global capital and expand Maharashtra’s industrial base. Bhavana Gokhale, Mumbai.”

Parakram Diwas 2025 is being celebrated today to honour the legacy of Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose on the occasion of his 128th Birth anniversary. The three day celebration organized by the Ministry of Culture, will be held in Cuttack, Odisha, the birthplace of Netaji. Recently, in his Mann ki Baat program, Prime Minister Narendra Modi also mentioned Parakram Diwas and remembered the contributions of Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose. Terming Subhash Babu a visionary leader, Mr. Modi said, courage was ingrained in Netaji’s nature. He urged the country’s youth to read as much as possible about Netaji and derive inspiration from his life.

Our correspondent reports that the Parakram Diwas 2025 event will be inaugurated by the Chief Minister of Odisha Mohan Charan Majhi.

“On the occasion several dignitaries will pay homage to Netaji and hoist the National Flag at the house where Netaji was born, which has now been converted to a Museum dedicated in the memory of Subhas Chandra Bose. The Parakram Diwas celebration at Barabati fort will begin with a video message by Prime Minister Narendra Modi and will feature a Book, Photo and Archival exhibition focused on Netaji’s life, showcasing rare photographs, letters and documents from his remarkable journey. A sculpture photoshop and a painting competition-cum-workshop are also being planned on the occasion. The event will also feature cultural performances honouring Netaji’s legacy and highlighting the rich cultural tradition of Odisha. Films on Netaji’s life will also be screened during the event. Adarsh for Akashvani News, Delhi.”

A full dress rehearsal of Republic Day parade will take place on Kartavya Path in the national capital today. The parade rehearsal will start at 10:30 AM from Vijay Chowk and will proceed to Red Fort. To ensure a smooth parade, elaborate traffic arrangements have been made in the National Capital. According to the Delhi Police, Delhi Metro rail services will be available for passengers during the rehearsal, and all stations are open to the public.

The 70 member Delhi assembly will go to polls on 5th of next month. Akashvani News brings you a special series in view of Delhi assembly elections. Today we will listen to a report on Badli constituency.

“Delhi’s Badli seat is one of the high profile seats of the national capital, which is going to face a tough triangular contest this time. Congress state president Devendra Yadav is contesting against Aam Aadmi Party candidate Ajesh Yadav, while, BJP has fielded former Delhi University president Deepak Chaudhary from this seat. In Maharashtra, 12 people lost their lives and several others were injured in a train accident in Jalgaon district yesterday evening. President Droupadi Murmu, Prime Minister Narendra Modi, Union Home Minister Amit Shah and Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnav have expressed condolences to the bereaved families and wished speedy recovery for the injured.

“The accident took place near Pachora railway station in Jalgaon district at around 4 PM on Wednesday. According to the Chief Public Relations Officer of Central Railway, Dr Swapnil Nila, the Pushpak Express experienced an alarm chain-pulling incident following a rumour of a fire in one of the adjacent coaches. As a result, some passengers disembarked from the train. While on the tracks, several of them were struck by the Karnataka Express, coming from the opposite direction. In a tweet, Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis expressed his condolences and announced financial assistance of 5 lakh rupees to the families of the deceased. He also assured that the state government would bear the full medical expenses of the injured. Mr Fadnavis has updated Union Home Minister Amit Shah about the incident. Ankita Apte, Akashvani News, Mumbai.”

“The chatbot will provide accurate details about parking spaces, food courts, and hospitals within a one kilometer radius of the user. Additional Mela Officer Vivek Chaturvedi said that the AI chatbot is enabling pilgrims to access vital information about parking, transportation, banking,  water ATMs, and other services within seconds. ## Midday News

The nation is celebrating ‘Parakram Diwas’ today to mark the birth anniverary of Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose. This year, the ‘Parakram Diwas’ celebration is being organised for the first-time at the historic Barabati Fort in Cuttack, the birth place of one of India’s most indomitable leaders, Netaji for three days from today.  Netaji’s memorials from all over the country have been brought and displayed at the celebration venue in Cuttack. Many beautiful large-scale paintings of Netaji have also been included in the  exhibition being organised by the Lalit Kala Academy.

Remembering the Netaji, Prime Minister Narendra Modi today reiterated the country’s commitment to become the third largest economy in the world by taking inspiration from Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose. In his video message, on the 128th birth anniversary of Netaji, the Prime Minister said, the countrymen must strive to work towards achieving the goal of developed India.

Mr. Modi said, people must come out from their comfort zone and adopt excellence by focussing on efficiency like Netaji, who adopted challenges for the freedom of the country.

On the country’s rising stature in the world, the Prime Minister said, there is a favourable environment for country’s progress and the world is looking towards India.

Meanwhile, President Droupadi Murmu, Vice President Jagdeep Dhankhar, and various leaders have paid tributes to Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose on his birth anniversary. a social media post, the President said that Netaji is among the most resolute and inspiring figures in India’s freedom struggle. In his message, the Vice-President said, Netaji’s exemplary dedication to India’s Independence and the formation of Azad Hind Fauj stand as testament to his extraordinary vision and valour.

External Affairs Minister Dr. S Jaishankar Dr Jaishankar said that India’s stand on legitimate return of undocumented Indians living abroad, including in the United States stating that the country is open for their return. Speaking to the press in Washington DC yesterday he emphasized that India strongly supports legal mobility and wants skilled and talented Indians to have great opportunities at the global level.

Highlighting the strong bond between India and US, Dr Jaishankar emphasized that both nations share a very strong degree of trust and convergence. He also underlined that two nations share a sense of global good and are committed to serving their national interests while building their bilateral partnership.

The External Affair Minister has said that there have been no talks with Pakistan on resumption of trade with India. He has clarified that it was Pakistan’s administration which made the decisions in 2019 to discontinue trading with India.

Campaigning for 70 member Delhi Assembly polls is in full swing. Prominent leaders of BJP, Aam Aadmi Party and Congress are trying to woo the voters and seek support in favour of their candidates by conducting road shows, door-to-door campaigns and public meetings.

Senior Congress leader Rahul Gandhi will hold a mega rally in Mustafabad constituency in favour of party candidate Ali Mehdi. Delhi Congress President Devendra Yadav and other prominent leaders of the party will release the theme song of the party for the Delhi polls this afternoon.

The Election Commission of India organized a two-day international conference in New Delhi today. Chief Election Commissioner Rajiv Kumar attended the inaugural session of the conference. Around 30 representatives from 13 election management bodies and international organizations, including the President and CEO of the International Foundation for Electoral Systems (1987) and the Secretary General of the Association of World Election Bodies, are participating in this conference.

During the conference, the heads and representatives of election management bodies will deliberate on major issues faced in election management worldwide. The theme of the conference is Global Election Year 2024: Reiteration of Democratic Spaces and Takeaways for Election Management Bodies.
