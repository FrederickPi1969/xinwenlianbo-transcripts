The next-generation GST reforms represent a significant step towards simplifying India’s indirect tax system. Now people can avail the benefit of GST reforms, which already came into effect from the 22nd of this month. Under the new structure, the GST council has simplified the earlier four-tier structure of 5, 12, 18 and 28 per cent into a two tier structure of 5 per cent and 18 per cent. Our correspondent reports that by reducing the tax rate on a wide range of goods and services, the reforms are bringing substantial relief to citizens.

Under the Next Generation GST Reforms, the government has reduced GST on services provided by Common Effluent Treatment Plant from 12 percent to 5 percent. Reducing taxes on Treatment Plants will motivate industries to use centralized waste treatment methods, resulting in a pollution-free environment and supporting sustainable development in industrial areas.

The reduced GST rates will create green jobs in areas like waste segregation, plant operations, and maintenance. Meanwhile, the GST reduction on biodegradable bags from 18 percent to 5 percent will make them more affordable which will encourage consumers and retailers to move away from single-use plastics thereby reducing plastic pollution. By lowering the GST rates, it will help in promoting investment in research and development, expansion of bio-polymers, starch-based, and compostable materials. In order to support farmers and make solar pumps more affordable, the GST council has slashed down the tax rate on solar and wind devices from 12 percent to 5 percent. Devices including solar cookers, solar power generator, wind mills and wind operated electricity generator will now attract 5 percent tax. Shivang, Akashvani News, Delhi.
