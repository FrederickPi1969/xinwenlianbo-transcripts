## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will be on a day-long visit to Gujarat today. During his visit, the Prime Minister will participate in a programme at Dediapada in Narmada district on the occasion of Janjatiya Gaurav Divas, marking 150th birth anniversary celebration of legendary tribal leader and freedom fighter Bhagwan Birsa Munda. Mr Modi will also inaugurate and lay the foundation stone of various development projects worth over 9 thousand 700 crore rupees aimed at uplifting tribal communities and improving infrastructure in rural and remote areas of the region.

Mr Modi will participate in the Grih Pravesh of one lakh houses built under Pradhan Mantri Janjati Adivasi Nyaya Maha Abhiyan (PM-JANMAN) and Dharti Aaba Janjatiya Gram Utkarsh Abhiyan (DA-JAGUA).

The Prime Minister will also offer prayers at Devmogra Temple in Narmada district.

Mr Modi will also visit the under-construction Bullet Train Station in Surat this morning to review the progress of the Mumbai-Ahmedabad High-Speed Rail Corridor which is one of India’s most ambitious infrastructure projects symbolizing the nation’s leap into the era of high-speed connectivity.

Centre has marked 15th November as Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas, recognizing the importance of keeping tribal history alive. Over the years, this observance has expanded into Janjatiya Gaurav Week, celebrated across ministries and states with cultural programs, exhibitions, and educational activities that bring the legacy of tribal heroes to life.

Over the past decade, the vision of tribal development has expanded into a nationwide mission. Today, 42 ministries actively contribute to tribal welfare through the Development Action Plan for Scheduled Tribes. The government has continuously emphasised the development of museums and memorials dedicated to tribal freedom fighters. Eleven museums have been approved across ten states, with three already inaugurated, including the Bhagwan Birsa Munda Memorial Park-cum-Museum in Ranchi. Public spaces like Rani Kamalapati Railway Station, Tantya Bhil University and statues of Alluri Sitarama Raju and Birsa Munda reflect the ongoing effort to embed tribal legacy across the country. The publication of books, comics and digital material narrating the stories of tribal heroes has transformed the way the country remembers its tribal freedom fighters, ensuring their stories reach every generation. Pallavi Parmar, Akashvani News, Delhi.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Home Minister Amit Shah paid tributes to iconic tribal freedom fighter Bhagwan Birsa Munda on his 150th birth anniversary today. In a social media post, Mr. Modi said that on this sacred occasion of Janjatiya Gaurav Divas, the entire nation reverently remembers the unparalleled contribution of Birsa Munda in protecting the pride of the motherland.

Home Minister Amit Shah described Birsa Munda as a symbol of tribal identity, Indian pride, and a great freedom fighter.

Jharkhand is today celebrating the 150th birth anniversary of its revered tribal freedom fighter Bhagwan Birsa Munda. The state is also celebrating Silver Jubilee of its Foundation Day. The state was officially carved out of the southern part of Bihar, becoming India’s 28th state on this day in 2000.

The 150th birth anniversary of Dharti Aba Bhagwan Birsa Munda and the silver jubilee celebrations of Jharkhand State formation are being celebrated with enthusiasm, pride, and cultural diversity across the state. A series of events will take place today in Ulihatu, the birthplace of Bhagwan Birsa Munda. The grand ceremony will be attended by Chief Minister Hemant Soren, Governor Santosh Kumar Gangwar, and Union Tribal Affairs Minister Jual Oram. The ceremony will begin with the laying of wreaths at the statue of Bhagwan Birsa Munda. Descendants of Bhagwan Birsa Munda will be honoured during this event. The main programme of Jharkhand Foundation Day celebration will be held at Morabadi Ground in Ranchi. Chief Minister Hemant Soren will today inaugurate and lay foundation stone of 166 schemes worth Rupees 6,000 crore. Shilpi, Akashvani News, Ranchi.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has extended greetings to people of Jharkhand on the state’s foundation day. In a social media post, Mr Modi said, the land of Bhagwan Birsa Munda, enriched with tribal culture, has a history filled with tales of courage, struggle, and self-respect. He wished for the continued progress and prosperity of the state and all its people.

The ruling National Democratic Alliance (NDA) has secured a thumping majority in the Bihar Assembly elections, winning 202 seats in the 243-member House. The results for all the constituencies were declared by the Election Commission last night. A party or an alliance needs 122 seats to form the government in the state.

The BJP emerged as the largest party, winning 89 seats. Its ally, Chief Minister Nitish Kumar-led Janata Dal (United), won 85 seats, while Union Minister Chirag Paswan-led Lok Janshakti Party (Ram Vilas) bagged 19 seats. Hindustani Awam Morcha (Secular), headed by former Chief Minister Jitan Ram Manjhi, won five seats, while Upendra Kushwaha-led Rashtriya Lok Morcha bagged four seats.
The RJD-led Mahagathbandhan managed to win only 35 seats.

Hyderabad MP Asaduddin Owaisi-led AIMIM won five seats, while Bahujan Samaj Party (BSP) got one seat.

In Bihar, an informal meeting of the coalition partners of National Democratic Alliance (NDA), including JD(U) and BJP, is expected to take place today in Patna to discuss the formation of the new government. Our correspondent reports, early indications suggest that Nitish Kumar is likely to be sworn in as Chief Minister of Bihar for a record 10th term.

In the Bihar assembly elections, 28 out of 29 ministers in the Nitish government secured victory. However, Cabinet Minister Sumit Kumar Singh, of state minister from the Chakai Assembly constituency in Jamui district, lost the election. All 17 ministers from the BJP quota emerged victorious.

This time, the largest victory margin was recorded in Purnia, where JD(U)’s Kaladhar Mandal defeated his nearest rival Beema Bharti by a margin of 73,500 votes in the Rupauli Assembly constituency.

In Bhojpur district’s Sandesh Assembly seat, the victory margin was narrowest with only 27 votes. In this neck-and-neck contest, JD(U)’s Radha Charan Shah defeated the RJD candidate.

In the Assembly elections, the Mahagathbandhan’s constituent parties suffered heavy losses. The Rashtriya Janata Dal, which had won 75 seats in 2020, lost 50 seats this time. Dharmender Kumar Rai Akashvani News Patna.

Senior BJP Leader and Prime Minister Narendra Modi stated that the victory in the Bihar Assembly Elections is a mandate for the politics of development and a rejection of the politics of nepotism. Addressing party workers at the BJP Headquarters in New Delhi last evening, Mr. Modi said that this is a victory for the women of Bihar, who had to face the wrath of the RJD’s jungle raj.

Congress President Mallikarjun Kharge has said that they are grateful to those voters in Bihar who supported the Mahagathbandhan.

In a social media post, Mr Kharge said, they will study the election results and will present a detailed perspective after understanding the reasons for the outcomes.

The Election Commission has informed that over 46 crore enumeration forms have been distributed among electors since the launch of phase two of Special Intensive Revision (SIR) drive of electoral roll on 4th of this month. It is 91 percent of the total 51 crore electors to be covered in this phase. Maximum of over 13 crore 74 lakh forms have been handed over in Uttar Pradesh. Over 7.4 crore forms have been given in West Bengal.

In Jammu & Kashmir, multiple casualties are feared in an accidental explosion at the Nowgam police station in the outskirts of Srinagar city, late last night.

Official sources said that the blast occurred during extraction of samples from a massive cache of explosives recently seized in connection with the ‘white-collar’ terror module case.

The accidental explosion occurred while police personnel and forensic experts were handling the explosive material that had been brought from Haryana’s Faridabad, following extensive multi agency probe into recent Red Fort Delhi blast case. Further details are awaited.

In Men’s Cricket, Hosts India will resume their first innings at the overnight score of 37 for 1 against South Africa on day 2 of the first Test match at Eden Gardens in Kolkata today. The match will begin at 9:30 AM.

KL Rahul on 13 and Washington Sundar on six were at the crease when stumps were drawn last evening. Earlier in the day, opting to bat after winning the toss, South Africa were bundled out for 159 runs in their first innings. Indian bowlers dominated the innings with Jasprit Bumrah taking five wicket, while Mohammad Siraj and Kuldeep Yadav picked up two wickets each.

In Shooting, former Asian champion Esha Singh clinched a bronze medal in the women’s 25-meter pistol event at the ISSF World Championship Rifle and Pistol in Cairo, Egypt yesterday. This is her first individual senior world championship medal.

Esha finished with a score of 30 in the final, behind South Korea’s reigning Olympic champion Yang Jiin, who claimed gold with 40, and China’s Yao Qianxun, who won silver with 38.

This marks Esha’s third individual ISSF medal of the year, following gold and silver at World Cup stages earlier.

However, two-time Olympic medallist Manu Bhaker drew a blank in the event.

Overall, India are at the third position on the medal table with three gold, five silver, and four bronze medals, behind China with 10 golds, and South Korea with 6 golds.

In Badminton, star Indian shuttler Lakshya Sen has stormed into the semi-finals of the Kumamoto Masters Japan tournament. Paris Olympics 2024 semifinalist, Lakshya secured a win over former world champion Loh Kean Yew of Singapore, 21-13, 21-17, in the quarterfinals yesterday.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi visited the under-construction bullet train station in Gujarat’s Surat to review the progress of the Mumbai-Ahmedabad High-Speed Rail Corridor. Upon completion, the Bullet Train will reduce travel time between Mumbai and Ahmedabad to nearly two hours. The project is expected to boost business, tourism, and economic activity along the entire corridor, catalyzing regional development.

In Jammu and Kashmir, nine people lost their lives in an accidental explosion at Nowgam Police Station last night. J&K DGP Nalin Prabhat said the deceased include one personnel of State Investigating Agency, three personnel of Forensic Science Laboratory team, 2 crime scene photographers, two revenue officials who were part of the Magistrate’s team and one Tailor who was associated with the team.

Addressing the media in New Delhi, Home Affairs Joint Secretary (J&K Division) Prashant Lokhande said the explosion occurred while processing a huge cache of explosive substances and chemicals recovered during the investigation of a terror module. He said that the explosives were kept securely in an open area of the police station and were being processed for forensic examination. Mr Lokhande said that the police station building, along with some surrounding structures suffered severe damage. He added that the cause of the accident is being investigated, and any other speculation is unnecessary.

The National Medical Commission has removed the names of four doctors from the Indian Medical Register or National Medical Register. The Commission said that this action has been taken in view of the FIR registered under relevant sections of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act against Dr Muzaffar Ahmad, Dr Adeel Ahmad Rather, Dr Muzamil Shakeel, and Dr Shaheen Saeed following the Delhi blast. It added that the practitioners will not be permitted to practice medicine or hold any medical appointment until further orders.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi reaffirmed the government’s unwavering resolve to end the injustices faced by tribal communities and ensured that the benefits of development should reach them. He asserted that tribal welfare has always been the highest priority for their government. Mr Modi made these remarks while launching development projects worth over 9,700 crore rupees in Dediapada town of the tribal-dominated Narmada district of Gujarat this afternoon. These projects were launched on the occasion of Janjatiya Gaurav Divas, marking the 150th birth anniversary celebration of legendary tribal leader and freedom fighter Bhagwan Birsa Munda. Addressing a gathering, he said tribal pride has been an integral part of India’s consciousness for thousands of years.

The Prime Minister stated that the government is working with a comprehensive approach to address every aspect of tribal life. He informed that the number of minor forest produce items has been increased from 20 to nearly 100, and the MSP on forest produce has been raised. He emphasized that the country cannot forget the contribution of tribal society in the freedom movement.

Earlier in the day, Mr. Modi visited the under-construction bullet train station in Gujarat’s Surat to review the progress of the Mumbai-Ahmedabad High-Speed Rail Corridor. The Prime Minister landed at the Surat airport in the morning and reached the Antroli area, where a bullet train station is under construction.

Later in the evening, addressing the Bihari community living in Surat, Gujarat, Mr. Modi expressed gratitude to the people of Bihar for giving the NDA, a historic victory in Assembly elections. He said, now Bihar is showing readiness to reach new heights of development, which has been seen in this election.
