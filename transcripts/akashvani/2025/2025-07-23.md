## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will embark on a 4-day official visit to United Kingdom and Maldives today. In the first leg of his visit, he will hold discussions with his UK counterpart Keir Starmer, and call on King Charles III. Briefing media in New Delhi, Foreign Secretary Vikram Misri said, Prime Minister Modi will also interact with business leaders of both the countries.

Talking to Akashvani News, India’s High Commissioner to UK, Vikram Kumar Doraiswami, said that the upcoming visit will be beneficial for both countries.

Mr Doraiswami said an agreement on free trade will have a positive impact on both economies, calling it an ambitious document.

Our correspondent covering the Prime Minister’s visit has filed this report:

“Calling it an ambitious document, Mr Doraiswami said that the Free Trade Agreement would build a new partnership more focussed on futuristic stuff.  He said Prime Minister Modi is so much committed to the relationship between both the countries  and wants to take it forward. On meeting with King Charles, Mr. Doraiswami said that Mr. Modi and the King has lot many things in common including holistic health and environment and will be meeting on July 24th.The signing of the FTA would not only strengthen bilateral economic ties but may also serve as a major factor for India to have more such agreements with other developed countries in future. Rajesh Bali, Akashvani News, London.”

In the second leg of his visit, Prime Minister will undertake a state visit to Maldives from 25th to 26th July on the invitation of Maldivian President Mohamed Muizzu. Foreign Secretary said that Prime Minister will be the ‘Guest of Honour’ at the celebrations of the 60th anniversary of the Independence of Maldives on 26th of July. Mr Modi will hold meeting with President Muizzu and inaugurate infrastructure projects. Foreign Secretary Misri said that Maldives is a very important partner in India’s Neighbourhood First policy and Vision MAHASAGAR.

US President Donald Trump has announced a long-awaited trade agreement with Japan. As part of the deal, US importers will pay 15 per cent reciprocal tariffs on Japanese goods exported to the United States. US President said that US and Japan just completed a massive deal, perhaps the largest deal ever made. Mr Trump said Japan also will invest 550 billion dollars into the United States. He added that the US will receive 90 per cent of the profits. Now Japan will open their country to trade including cars and trucks, rice and certain other Agricultural Products, and other things.

United States and the Philippines also concluded a trade deal last night after US President met his Philippine counterpart, Ferdinand Marcos Jr. at the White House. Mr Trump said, as per the deal, the Philippines is going to open its market with the United States. He said under the trade deal, the Philippines will pay a 19 per cent Tariff.

In a major decision aimed at promoting women’s empowerment, Uttar Pradesh Cabinet has approved a one per cent discount in stamp duty on property worth up to one crore rupees, if it is purchased in the name of a woman. Till now, this exemption was applicable only on property up to 10 lakh rupees in the state, in which a maximum discount of 10 thousand rupees was available. The decision in this regard was taken at a Cabinet meeting chaired by Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath in Lucknow. In a statement, UP government said a total of 37 proposals were passed in the meeting. Giving information about the decision, UP’s Labour Minister Anil Rajbhar said that the government believes this decision will help middle class women become property owners, which will not only increase their confidence but they will also become financially more capable and respected in the society.

In a major push to youth empowerment, Delhi government has announced a significant hike in cash awards for the medal winners of Olympic and Paralympic to 7 crore rupees from currently 3 crore rupees. This decision was taken at a cabinet meeting chaired by Chief Minister Rekha Gupta in New Delhi yesterday. Under the newly-launched Mukhyamantri Khel Protsahan Yojana, Olympic and Paralympic medalists will receive 7 crore rupees for a gold medal, 5 crore rupees for silver, and 3 crore rupees for bronze. In addition, athletes who have won Olympic gold and silver medals will now be eligible for Grade A government posts. Similarly, Asian and Para Asian Games gold medallists will be given 3 crore rupees while silver medallists will get  2 crore rupees and bronze medallists 1 crore rupees.

Jammu & Kashmir Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha has launched a dedicated web portal to support families affected by terrorism. The initiative will streamline and expedite the process of providing relief and other forms of assistance to those who have suffered from terrorism in the Union Territory. The portal, which was launched yesterday, will centralize district-wise data, track relief efforts, and ensure no genuine case will be missed. The initiative aims to streamline financial aid, ex-gratia compensation, and compassionate employment.The Lieutenant Governor personally monitoring the redressal process, emphasized transparency and timely support. Encroachments on victims’ properties will also be recorded.
