## Morning News

International Women’s Day is being celebrated across the globe today. President Droupadi Murmu will preside over a national-level conference on ‘Nari Shakti Se Viksit Bharat’ in New Delhi today to mark the occasion. The conference will be followed by a high-level panel discussion and three technical sessions. Minister of State for Women and Child Development Annapurna Devi will also be present at the event.

To mark the occasion, Prime Minister Narendra Modi is handing over his social media accounts for a day to inspiring women on the occasion. Earlier in his Mann Ki Baat address, Mr Modi had encouraged women across India to share their inspiring life journeys on the NaMo App Open Forum.

Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla will also inaugurate an Exhibition on Women Members of the Constituent Assembly in the Parliament House Complex today. The exhibition seeks to commemorate 75 Years of the Constitution of India.

To commemorate the Day, the National Commission for Women in collaboration with the Akhil Bharatiya Adhivakta Parishad, will organise the All India Women Advocates’ Conference this afternoon. The program will be focused on strategizing the enhanced role of women for Resurgent Bharat with women lawyers leading the way.

“The theme of International Women’s Day this year is ‘For All Women and Girls: Rights. Equality. Empowerment. The theme calls for action to unlock equal rights, power, and opportunities for all and an inclusive future where no one is left behind. The central government has been actively working towards women’s empowerment and gender equality through various policies, schemes, and legislative measures. India has undertaken several initiatives to ensure that girls have equal access to quality education from primary schooling to higher education. These include Right to Free and Compulsory Education Act, 2009, Beti Bachao Beti Padhao initiative and Samagra Shiksha Abhiyan.

For the first time, Women priests, or batuk kanyas, performed the Ganga aarti at the Maha Kumbh Mela in Prayagraj. This practice broke centuries-old tradition. Talking exclusively to Akashvani News, one of the seven batuk kanyas shared her experience.

As part of International Women’s Day 2025 celebrations, the government recently launched Model Women-Friendly Gram Panchayats. The initiative primarily aims to establish at least one Model Women-Friendly Gram Panchayat in each district, serving as a beacon for gender-sensitive and girl-friendly governance practices.

Talking to Akashvani News, District Panchayat member from Goa, Anita Thora, appreciated the government’s initiative to support Panchayats.

The year 2025 is a pivotal moment as it marks the 30th anniversary of the Beijing Declaration and Platform for Action. The document is the most progressive and widely endorsed blueprint for women’s and girls’ rights worldwide. Priya Mondal, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will be in Navsari district of Gujarat today to attend the ‘Lakhpati Didi Sammelan’ at Vansi-Borsi. Mr Modi will disburse 450 crore rupees in financial assistance to more than 2.5 lakh women from over 25,000 self-help groups. Prime Minister will also felicitate 5 Lakhpati Didis with Lakhpati Didi Certificates. Mr Modi will launch the G-SAFAL (Gujarat Scheme for Antyodaya Families for Augmenting Livelihoods) and G-MAITRI (Gujarat Mentorship and Acceleration of Individuals for Transforming Rural Income) programme of the Government of Gujarat at the event.

“Over 1 lakh women from Navsari, Valsad and Dang districts will participate in the Lakhpati Didi Sammelan to be held at Vansi Borsi in Navsari today. During the programme, the PM will provide assistance worth over ₹450 crore to the women from over 25 thousand self-help groups in Gujarat. The PM will also launch two new initiatives of the Government of Gujarat namely G-SAFAL and G-MAITRI. G-SAFAL will provide financial assistance and entrepreneurial training to SHG women of Antyodaya families in two Aspirational districts and thirteen Aspirational Blocks of Gujarat while the G-MAITRI scheme will provide financial assistance and handholding support to Startups which are working to create a conducive environment for rural livelihoods. It is worth mentioning that following the widespread efforts of the government, today there are, about 1.5 lakh women who have an annual income of one lakh or more and have become Lakhpati Didi in Gujarat. Aparna Khunt, Akashvani News, Ahmedabad.”

In Bihar, the demographic landscape has undergone significant changes across various sectors, including government posts, police establishments, and Panchayati Raj institutions, highlighting the rise of women power. In the police force, Bihar leads all states with 30 thousand female personnel. Out of a total of One lakh 10 thousand police officers in the state, the number of women is the highest in the country. Providing strong representation for women is also contributing to the demographic dividend, especially in the police force. Previously, the landscape was lopsided in terms of women’s representation. The data of women representation changed in favour of women due to 35 percent reservation given by Nitish Kumar led government in 2013 for women in police jobs.

Talking to Akashvani News, former Director General of Police (DGP) Abhayanand says it is overall a very positive change and it has wiped out gender bias in the state police force.

Mr. Abhayanand said, with more representatives in police forces it will give a facelift to the enforcement agency and it is quite helpful during investigation and law & order maintenance needs.

Telangana government will be celebrating the International Women’s Day at Parade Ground in Secunderabad this evening. Chief Minister A. Revanth Reddy will launch several schemes that are aimed at financially empowering women’s self-help groups.

“The state will launch the‘Indira Mahila Shakti Mission’, marking a major initiative integrating the achievements of women’s SHGs and the government’s future plans for the empowerment of women.The government is also launching a first-of-its-kind initiative allowing women SHGs to own and operate Road Transport Corporation buses on a rental basis. As part of the Indira Mahila Shakti Mission, the government will introduce 150 RTC buses in the first phase, each assigned to a mandal women federation. This pilot project will be implemented in the undivided districts of Warangal, Khammam,Karimnagar, and Mahbubnagar.The government will sign agreements for 600 buses between women federations and RTC. With the report of Lakshmi from Hyderabad, this is Deepmala Kaushik, Akashvani News.”

The Election Commission has said that it will resolve the longstanding issue of duplicate Electors Photo Identity Card (EPIC) numbers within the next three months. In a statement, the Commission said, it has decided to resolve this long-pending issue after detailed discussions within the technical teams and concerned Chief Electoral Officers in the next three months, ensuring a unique national EPIC number for existing electors having duplicate EPIC numbers. The Commission had already taken cognizance of the issue of duplicate EPIC numbers. Irrespective of an EPIC number, an elector who is linked to the electoral roll of a particular polling station can cast their vote at that polling station only and nowhere else.

## Midday News

President Droupadi Murmu today stressed that there should be more women in the workforce as India is heading towards becoming the third biggest economy in the world. Addressing a day long conference on the theme ‘Nari Shakti Se Viksit Bharat’ in New Delhi today to mark the International Women’s Day, the President emphasized that the daughters of the nation are playing a crucial role in realizing the vision of a developed India. She highlighted the importance of society creating a supportive environment to help them progress and fulfill their potential.

President Murmu stated that the Central government is continuously working to ensure that women in the country are politically, economically, and socially empowered, and are able to lead the nation towards the vision of a developed India. She described the provision of reservations for women in the Lok Sabha and State Assemblies through the Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam as a historic step towards political empowerment.

Union Ministers Arjun Ram Meghwal and Annpurna Devi also addressed the gathering.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today emphasized that his government is dedicated to the empowerment of women, highlighting the significant steps taken to improve their lives. Speaking at the ‘Lakhpati Didi Sammelan’ at Navsari in Gujarat on the occasion of International Women’s Day today, Mr Modi emphasized that India is advancing towards women-led development, with the government placing utmost importance on dignity and well-being of women at every step.

Prime Minister Modi stated that the banner of women’s empowerment is soaring across all sectors of society, with the power of Indian women now acknowledged globally. He praised the role of Self Help Groups in the nation’s development and announced that the government is aiming to create three crore Lakhpati Didis over the next five years as part of its ongoing efforts to empower women.

The Prime Minister also highlighted the government’s achievements in enhancing women’s security over the past decade, citing the implementation of new criminal laws designed to protect women’s rights.

During the ‘Lakhpati Didi Sammelan’, the Prime Minister also disbursed 450 crore rupees in financial assistance to over 2.5 lakh women from over 25,000 Self-Help Groups. Mr Modi also felicitated five Lakhpati Didis with Lakhpati Didi Certificates. He also launched the two initiatives G-SAFAL (Gujarat Scheme for Antyodaya Families for Augmenting Livelihoods) and G-MAITRI (Gujarat Mentorship and Acceleration of Individuals for Transforming Rural Income) programme of the Gujarat Government.

Earlier in the day, Prime Minister Modi interacted with ten beneficiaries of ‘Lakhpati Didi’ scheme at Navsari district who shared their experiences.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has handed over his social media accounts for a day to inspiring women to mark the International Women’s Day. In a social media post, Mr Modi said, since morning, there have been inspiring posts by extraordinary women sharing their own journeys and inspiring other women. He said these women belong to different parts of India and have excelled in different areas, with the underlying theme of India’s Nari Shakti. He added that their determination and success reminds everyone of the boundless potential women hold. He further stated that the nation is celebrating their contributions in shaping a Viksit Bharat.

Through the Prime Minister’s social media account on X, Dr. Anjlee Agarwal who is the founder of Samarthyam- Centre for Universal Accessibility, said, she wants to ignite a spark of transformation, and seek a call to action.

Elina Mishra, a nuclear scientist and Shilpi Soni, a space scientist said they were thrilled to be helming the Prime Minister’s social media on Womens Day.

The Central Railway commemorated International Women’s Day in an innovative way by operating an all-women train today. The Vande Bharat Express, train no. 22223, departed from the renowned Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus bound for Shirdi, with an all-women crew onboard, including the loco pilot, assistant loco pilot, train manager, ticket examiners, and on-board catering staff. The iconic Vande Bharat Express reached Shirdi as scheduled, showcasing a significant milestone in women’s empowerment.

Union Health Minister JP Nadda today announced that the government plans to add 75,000 new seats in medical education over the next five years. Mr Nadda was addressing the inaugural session of the International Symposium on Health Technology Assessment (ISHTA) 2025 at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi. Mr. Nadda also highlighted that the number of medical colleges in the country has increased by 101 per cent since 2014. To strengthen secondary and tertiary healthcare, 22 All India Institutes of Medical Sciences (AIIMS) have been inaugurated. He further emphasized the government’s focus on expanding the healthcare sector by providing training to paramedics, nurses, and frontline workers.

Pakistan has ordered all documented Afghan migrants to leave the country by March 31st or risk deportation. Local media reported that this affects nearly 900,000 documented Afghan economic migrants residing in Pakistan.
