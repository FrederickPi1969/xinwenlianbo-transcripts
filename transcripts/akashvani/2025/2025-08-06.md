## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will inaugurate Kartavya Bhavan-3 at Kartavya Path in New Delhi today. On the occasion, he will also address a public programme. Kartavya Bhavan-3 is part of the broader transformation of the Central Vista. Our correspondent reports that it is the first among several upcoming Common Central Secretariat buildings that aim to streamline administrative processes and enable agile governance.

“Kartavya Bhavan-3 is a modern office complex of about 1.5 lakh square meters spread over two basements and seven floors. It will house key ministries including Home, External Affairs, Rural Development, MSME, DoPT, and office of the Principal Scientific Adviser. It will lead in sustainability with various power saving and recycling facilities resulting in 30 percent energy saving and also generating over five lakh units of electricity annually. The new building will exemplify modern governance, infrastructure, featuring IT-ready and secure workspaces, ID card-based access controls, electronic surveillance, and a centralized command system. It will also lead in sustainability,  with double-glazed façades, rooftop solar, solar water heating, advanced Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning systems, and rainwater harvesting. WITH RAHISUDDIN, ANUPAM MISHRA, AKASHVANI NEWS, NEW DELHI.”

After a devastating cloudburst triggered a flood yesterday in Kheerganga river, Dharali, located in Uttarkashi district of Uttarakhand, relief and rescue operations are ongoing at a war footing. The Army, NDRF, SDRF, District Administration, and other concerned agencies are actively involved in rescue and relief efforts. Over 130 people have been safely evacuated so far. Under the leadership of Colonel Harshvardhan, Commanding Officer of the 14th Rajputana Rifles, approximately 150 soldiers are engaged in relief operations in the affected area. Additionally, three SDRF teams are on-site, and around 70 to 80 people have been relocated to safer areas in Gangotri.  SDRF Commander Arpan Yaduvanshi informed that additional teams equipped with advanced gear and dog squads are en route to the disaster site.

To strengthen relief efforts, two Inspectors General, three Superintendents of Police, 11 Deputy SPs, and nearly 300 police personnel have been deployed. Special units of the PAC and the Indian Reserve Battalion- IRB are also assisting in relief work. The Public Works Department and the Border Roads Organisation (BRO) are focused on clearing the blocked roads.

Meanwhile, Chief Minister Pushkar Singh Dhami reviewed the situation at the Dehradun Disaster Operations Center last night. He directed all relief agencies to work in coordination, ensure the immediate evacuation of affected people, and provide all necessary assistance.

Our correspondent reports that hospitals in the affected region have been placed on alert to strengthen healthcare facilities.

“Following  the directives from Health Secretary Dr. R. Rajesh Kumar, three teams of specialist doctors have been dispatched to Uttarkashi. Additional beds have been reserved at Harsil and Bhatwari PHC, District Hospital Uttarkashi, AIIMS Rishikesh, and Doon Hospital. The ambulance service has been placed on high alert, and all doctors’ leaves have been canceled with immediate effect. To expedite relief efforts, MI-17 and Chinook helicopters from the Army, along with helicopters from UCADA, have been requested. Aerial support will commence once weather conditions improve. Meanwhile, rainfall continues across various parts of the state for the third consecutive day. The Department has issued orange and yellow alerts for heavy rain in different regions. In Rudraprayag, the Kedarnath Yatra has been temporarily halted for safety reasons. Sakshi Singh, Akashvani News, Dehradun.”

## Midday News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today inaugurated Kartavya Bhavan-3 at Kartavya Path in New Delhi. The Prime Minister also inspected the new building and took note of its features. Union Minister of Housing and Urban Affairs Manohar Lal was also present during the occasion.

Kartavya Bhavan-3 is part of the broader transformation of the Central Vista. It is the first among several upcoming Common Central Secretariat buildings that aim to streamline administrative processes and enable agile governance. Mr Modi will also address a public programme later in the evening.

Relief and rescue operations are being carried out on a war footing in the Dharali region of Uttarkashi district in Uttarakhand, following a devastating flood caused by a cloudburst over the Khirganga River yesterday. The Army, NDRF, SDRF, District Administration, and other teams are continuously engaged in rescue efforts. The administration has ensured proper arrangements for food, water, medicines, and shelter at relief camps to provide immediate aid to those affected.

Road connectivity has been disrupted in the Bhatwari area due to damaged roads, and efforts to clear landslides on the Uttarkashi-Harsil route are ongoing at a rapid pace.

Meanwhile, Chief Minister Pushkar Singh Dhami conducted an aerial survey of the disaster-affected areas today and carried out a detailed review of the relief and rescue operations at the disaster control room in Uttarkashi.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi spoke with Chief Minister Dhami over the phone to get an update on the relief efforts and assured all possible assistance from the central government.

NDRF Deputy Inspector General Mohsin Shahidi reported that around 150 people have been relocated to safer areas.

Meanwhile, the Met Department has predicted continued rainfall across the state in the coming days. Our correspondent has filed this report;

Normal life has been disrupted across various parts of Uttarakhand due to continuous rainfall over the past three days. In the hilly regions, landslides have blocked roads in several locations. Water levels in major rivers of the state have risen significantly, with some crossing the danger mark. The administration is alerting the public and relocating them to safer areas. The Met Department has issued a red alert for heavy to very heavy rainfall across all districts of the state today. Meanwhile, in Rudraprayag district, due to continuous rain, the Kedarnath Yatra has been temporarily suspended for the next 24 hours for safety reasons. Sakshi Singh, Akashvani News, Dehradun.

Both houses of Parliament were adjourned till 2 pm following opposition parties’ uproar over various issues. When the Lok Sabha met at 11:00 AM, Speaker Om Birla paid homage to the victims of the 1945 Hiroshima and Nagasaki atomic bombings in Japan. He said, 80 years ago, on 6th and 9th August 1945, atom bombs were dropped on the cities of Hiroshima and Nagasaki due to which a large number of people lost their lives, and many were wounded.

The House also observed silence for a while as a mark of respect to the people who lost their lives. After that, the Speaker tried to run the Question Hour but the opposition members started sloganeering demanding discussion on the Special Intensive Revision issue. As the noisy scene continued, the Speaker adjourned the House till 12 noon.

Similar scenes were also witnessed in the Rajya Sabha. When the House assembled at 11 AM, it paid tributes to Satyapal Malik, a former member of the House, who passed away in New Delhi yesterday.  Following this, the opposition members started protest raising their issues. Deputy chairman Harivansh said that as it is zero Hour several members want to raise the matter of urgent public importance and creating disruption in the House is not appropriate.

The Opposition MPs today held a protest at Parliament premises against the Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls in Bihar, demanding a discussion over this issue in Parliament. Later briefing media, Congress President and leader of Opposition in the Rajya Sabha Mallikarjun Kharge said the opposition parties want a discussion on the SIR exercise. He said the discussion on SIR is important to protect the voting rights of the people.

The Monetary Policy Committee, MPC of the Reserve Bank of India today kept the repo rate unchanged at 5.5 per cent, maintaining a neutral stance.

Consequently, the standing deposit facility rate will be at 5.25 percent and the marginal standing facility rate and the Bank Rate at 5.75 per cent.

The RBI retained its Financial Year 2025-26 GDP growth forecast at 6.5 per cent despite US tariffs on Indian imports. Inflation for the year is projected at 3.1 per cent.

A landmark Indian study has influenced global policy on tuberculosis. The World Health Organization, WHO, has said that India’s study that showed the positive impact of nutrition on the outcome of tuberculosis, TB, contributed to global guidance on the world’s most infectious disease. The study, led by the Indian Council of Medical Research, ICMR, and conducted in Jharkhand, provided the first evidence that extra nutrition can significantly reduce TB cases and deaths.

Lok Sabha has resumed its proceeding. Parliamentary affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju criticised the opposition for not allowing the house to function.

The Directorate of Enforcement, ED, is conducting search operations at 11 locations in Delhi, Noida, Gurugram and Dehradun today in a global cyber fraud case. According to the ED, foreign and Indian nationals were duped by fraudsters impersonating as the police, investigating officers and tech support service agents. The victims were threatened with arrests and extorted with their monetary assets, which were converted into crypto currencies. The Investigation further revealed that the accused duped around 260 Crore rupees in crypto currencies, which were further liquidated into cash through multiple hawala operators and individuals at UAE.

The spell of heavy rainfall continues in Himachal Pradesh. In the past 24 hours, the highest rainfall of 145 millimeters was recorded in Kasauli, Solan district. Rivers and streams across the state are in spate. In view of the bad weather, the Kinnaur district administration has suspended the sacred Kinner Kailash Yatra until further notice. Due to intense rainfall, multiple incidents of landslides have been reported in various districts. A total of 1,491 power transformers have gone out of service, severely impacting electricity supply in many areas. Water levels in the state’s dams are also rising steadily due to the rainfall and extra water is being released from the Nathpa Dam, Kol Dam, and Pandoh Dam as per prior notice. India’s premier classical chess tournament, Chennai Grand Masters 2025, has been postponed till tomorrow after a fire incident at the Hyatt Regency Chennai last night. The third edition of Chennai Grandmasters was initially scheduled to kick off today, with a opening round clash between Indian Arjun Erigaisi and American prodigy Awonder Liang. A fire at the Hotel forced the event to be postponed. After the fire incident, all players in both the Masters and Challengers sections, along with coaches and officials, were quickly evacuated and moved to a safe place.

The nine-round, ten-day event, featuring 19 top Grand Masters and an International Master from India and around the world, will run until 15th August, offering a total prize pool of 1 Crore Rupees and valuable FIDE circuit points.

Benchmark domestic equity indices, Sensex and Nifty, were trading marginally down in a choppy afternoon trade today. Sensex was near flatline, down by 44 points, to 80 thousand 666 and Nifty slipped 45 points to 24 thousand 605, when reports last came in. The broader market at the Bombay Stock Exchange was underperforming the frontline indices.

New Delhi today hit out after United States announced additional tariffs on Indian imports. This comes after US President Donald Trump imposed a additional tariff of 25 per cent on India. National Security Advisor (NSA) Ajit Doval today held a meeting with senior Russian officials to discuss bilateral defence and security cooperation in Moscow. Mr Doval’s visit to Russia comes at a time when US President Donald Trump has increased tariffs on Indian goods because of New Delhi’s oil purchases from Russia. The NSA is also likely to hold talks on defence industry cooperation.

India has a long standing and wide-ranging cooperation with Russia in the field of defence. As time-tested partners, both countries have been involved in several bilateral projects, including the supply of S-400, licensed production of T-90 tanks and Su-30 MKI production of AK-203 rifles in India and BrahMos missiles.

India and Russia today reaffirmed their strategic partnership and shared commitment to deepen industrial and economic cooperation. The Parliament has passed the Carriage of Goods by Sea Bill, 2025, with the Rajya Sabha approving it today. The Bill has already been passed by the Lok Sabha. The Bill seeks to replace the Indian Carriage of Goods by Sea Act, 1925. The Act establishes the responsibilities, liabilities, rights, and immunities in case of goods carried from a port.

The legislation empowers the central government to issue directions for carrying out provisions of the Bill, and amend the schedule specifying rules applicable to bills of lading.

Consequently, the standing deposit facility rate will be at 5.25 percent and the marginal standing facility rate and the Bank Rate at 5.75 per cent.
