Union Home Minister Amit Shah will inaugurate the ‘Fast Track Immigration – Trusted Traveller Programme’ at Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, bengaluru, Hyderabad, kochi and Ahmedabad airports today. This will provide world-class immigration facilities to travellers, making international travel seamless and secure.

According to the Home Ministry, applicants are required to complete online registration by filling in their details and uploading the required documents on the portal ftittp.mha.gov.in to enrol in this programme. Registered travellers will need to scan their airline boarding pass at the e-gate, followed by scanning their passport. At both the arrival and departure points, the passenger’s biometrics will be authenticated at the e-gates. Once this authentication is successful, the e-gate will automatically open, and immigration clearance will be deemed as granted.

The Home Minister launched the Fast Track Immigration Trusted Traveller Program in June last year from Terminal-3 of the Indira Gandhi International Airport in New Delhi.

Amid the deteriorating air quality in National Capital Delhi, the Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM) has decided to impose all actions under the Graded Response Action Plans (GRAP) 3 and 4 in Delhi-NCR. According to the Central Pollution Control Board’s (CPCB) data, Delhi  registered an average Air Quality Index (AQI) above the 350 mark at 7 AM.

With the implementation of actions under GRAP 3 and GRAP 4, non-essential construction work, road construction activities, stone crusher operations, and mining activities have been banned across the entire National Capital Region. The restrictions have also been imposed on plying of BS -3 petrol and BS – 4 four wheelers diesel vehicles in Delhi and the districts of Gurugram, Faridabad, Ghaziabad and Gautam Budh Nagar. Besides this, GRAP 3 and GRAP 4 also mandate that the Delhi government and state governments in the NCR conduct classes in hybrid mode for children up to class 11. The concerned agencies, including state Pollution Control Boards and the Delhi Pollution Control Committee have been directed to implement these measures promptly and strictly. Pallavi Parmar, Akashvani News, Delhi

## Midday News

The Indian Space Research Organisation, ISRO today scripted history by successfully completing docking of two satellites named Chaser and Target under the SpaDeX mission. With the stupendous success of the indigenously developed Bharatiya Docking System by ISRO, India has become the fourth country to use this technology after the USA, Russia and China. Docking technology is critical in assembling the space station and in crewed missions. It provides crucial facilities including in-orbit refueling and assembling heavy infrastructure in microgravity. ISRO Chairman Dr. V. Narayanan congratulated the team on the feat. More from our Correspondent:

“It is a historic moment in the Indian space programme today, ISRO has successfully docked two space crafts in Earth orbit. The Bharatiya Docking System developed indigenously by ISRO has evidently accomplished the most important mission that has bearing on future missions like Chandrayaan 4 and having our own Bharatiya Space Station. The Docking technology is essential in space when multiple rocket launches are aimed at achieving common mission goals. The technology involves development of docking sensors, power transfer technology, inter satellite communication link and inbuilt intelligence. In the coming days ISRO will demonstrate the transfer of electric power between the docked spacecrafts before undocking. The spacecraft after separation will be used for the application missions. Sudhindra, Akashvani News, Bengaluru.”

President Droupadi Murmu extended her wishes to ISRO and the entire scientists and engineers communities of the country for boosting India’s space capabilities”. In a social media post, the President said, this achievement paves the way for country’s future endeavours in space exploration such as Chandrayaan-4, India’s planned space station and Gaganyaan.

Lauding the achievement, Prime Minister Narendra Modi in a social media post, said it is a significant stepping stone for the country’s ambitious space missions in the years to come.

Union Minister of State for Science and Technology, Dr. Jitendra Singh highlighted that it is an entirely indigenous Bharatiya Docking System.
