## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will launch the Swasth Nari, Sashakt Parivar Abhiyaan and the 8th  Rashtriya Poshan Maah  Campaign from Dhar district in Madhya Pradesh today. He will also inaugurate the ‘Aadi Seva Parv’  in the state. The Prime Minister will lay the foundation stone for India’s first ‘PM Mitra Park’ in the village of Bhainsola in Dhar district.

This park is expected to give a new boost to the textile industry of Madhya Pradesh and create nearly three lakh new job opportunities for the youth. On this occasion, the Prime Minister will also launch several major initiatives, including ‘Swasth Nari, Sashakt Parivar’ and the 8th National Nutrition Month campaign, aimed at improving the health of women and children. The Prime Minister will further inaugurate ‘Aadi Seva Parv’, to promote service-oriented activities in tribal areas of MP. Along with this, he will also unveil innovative projects such as the ‘Suman Sakhi Chatbot’ and ‘Ek Bagiya Maa Ke Naam’. In addition, under the Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana, direct financial assistance will be transferred to the bank accounts of ten lakh women. Vipin Mishra, Akashvani News, Dhar.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah is scheduled to inaugurate fifteen developmental works this afternoon at Thyagraj Stadium in New Delhi. This includes hospital blocks, 101 Ayushman Arogya Mandirs, 150 dialysis centres, 75 drones for policing, and laying the foundation stone for two waste-to-energy plants, among others. The launch of these development projects comes under the Delhi government’s 15-day ‘Sewa Pakhwada’ campaign, being organised on the occasion of Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s 75th birthday today. Commencing today, the Campaign will continue till the 2nd of October, the birth anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi and Lal Bahadur Shastri.

His vision was realized on the 3rd of this month, when the GST Council reduced tax rates in many sectors to ease the lives of citizens. Today, we take a look at some items on which the GST has been slashed from 12 to 5 percent.

“The GST Council on the 3rd of this month announced a series of Next Generation GST reforms, providing big relief to the common man through significant tax reduction in diverse sectors. The Council has reduced the tax rate from 12 percent to 5 percent on various household articles including kitchenware, tableware, and toilet ware made up of iron, steel and wood. Similar reduction has been made for kerosene and wood burning stoves made up of iron or steel. In Jammu and Kashmir, the pilgrimage to Vaishno Devi shrine atop Trikuta hills in Reasi district resumed today, after a suspension of 22 days due to a devastating landslide that claimed multiple lives and injuries to several people. Pilgrims have started arriving at Katra town, the base camp for the pilgrimage in large numbers.

“The Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Shrine Board (SMVDSB) announced the reopening of the yatra subject to favourable weather conditions from this morning, bringing cheers on the faces of many devotees who were camping in Katra town for visiting the shrine. Hundreds of pilgrims gathered at Banganga Darshani gate, the starting point of the yatra, in the early hours of the day, expressing immense happiness and relief on the commencement of the pilgrimage. The yatra began at 6 AM from both the routes leading to the hilltop shrine after a temporary suspension necessitated by inclement weather conditions and essential maintenance of the track leading to the shrine. The pilgrims are advised to carry valid identification, follow designated pathways and cooperate with on-ground staff. The Radio Frequency Identity Card (RFID)-based tracking remains mandatory for transparency and traceability. The shrine board expressed its gratitude to all devotees for their patience and understanding during the temporary suspension. The pilgrims termed the resumption of the yatra as a very special day and said; visiting the shrine is a blessing. The shrine board urged all pilgrims to strictly follow safety guidelines and remain updated through official communication channels. With the path now declared safe, the pilgrimage is expected to see a large number of devotees in the coming days, especially during the upcoming Navratri scheduled to start from September 22 till October 1. The Yatra was suspended on August 26 after a massive landslide triggered on the yatra route due to heavy rains near the Inderprastha Bhojnalaya at Adhkuwari which claimed multiple lives, mostly pilgrims and injuries to several others. This is N. Gulshan Raina, For Akashvani News, From Jammu.”
