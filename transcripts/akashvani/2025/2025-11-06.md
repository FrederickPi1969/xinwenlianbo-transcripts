## Morning News

The polling has begun for the first phase of Assembly elections in Bihar. Voting is taking place for 121 constituencies spread over 18 districts in this phase. These districts include Patna, Vaishali, Nalanda, Bhojpur, Munger, Saran, Siwan, Begusarai, Lakhisarai, Gopalganj, Muzaffarpur, Darbhanga, Madhepura, and Saharsa. Polling, which began at 7 AM, will continue till 6 in the evening. However, due to security reasons, voting will conclude at 5 PM in Simri Bakhtiyarpur, Mahishi, Tarapur, Munger, Jamalpur, and in 56 polling booths of the Suryagarha Assembly segment.

Elaborate security arrangements have been made to ensure free, fair, and peaceful polling. A state-of-the-art Command and Control Centre has been set up to monitor the poll process, with webcasting from all 45,341 polling stations. Central Armed Police Forces, along with state police, have been deployed across all polling stations. To promote women’s empowerment, 926 all-women-managed booths and 107 booths managed by Divyangjan (specially-abled) personnel have been established. Additionally, 320 model booths have been set up with enhanced facilities for voters.

A special control room has been established at the Chief Electoral Officer’s office in Patna. Voters can register complaints on phone number 0612-2824001, fax 0612-2215611, or email ceo_bihar@eci.gov.in and ceobihar@gmail.com. Several voter-centric initiatives, including mobile deposit facilities, will also be available during the elections.

Now we go over live to our correspondent Dharmendra Kumar Rai in Mokama for the latest update on polling.

Campaigning for the second and last phase of Bihar Assembly elections is underway in full swing. Star campaigners and senior leaders from both the NDA and the Mahagathbandhan and other political parties will address multiple election rallies in poll bound constituencies. From the NDA side, senior BJP leader and Prime Minister Narendra Modi will address election rallies in Bhagalpur and Araria.

Senior BJP leader and Union Home Minister Amit Shah will hold election rallies in West Champaran, East Champaran and Madhubani districts.

Senior BJP leader and Defence Minister Rajnath Singh is scheduled to address public meetings in Sitamarhi and East Champaran districts.

From Mahagathbandhan, Chief Ministerial candidate of coalition Tejashwi Prasad Yadav is scheduled to address 18 election rallies today in Madhubani, Supaul, Kishanganj, Katihar and Purnea districts.

The commissioning ceremony will be held in the presence of Chief of the Naval Staff, Admiral Dinesh K Tripathi. The Ministry of Defence informed that Ikshak will enhance the Indian Navy’s hydrographic survey capabilities along with the third vessel of the Survey Vessel class and the first to be based at the Southern Naval Command.

The typhoon’s onslaught affected nearly 2 million people and displaced more than 560,000 villagers, including nearly 450,000 who were evacuated to emergency shelters.

## Evening News

Polling concluded peacefully for the first phase of assembly elections in Bihar today. Voting was held in 121 constituencies spread over 18 districts. According to Chief Electoral Officer, 64.46 percent voter turnout was recorded in the first phase.

Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar today congratulated the electors of Bihar for the historic voter turnout in Phase I of Assembly Elections. The Commissioner thanked the electors for expressing their faith in the Election Commission and turning out to vote in such large numbers with fervor and enthusiasm. Mr Kumar has also thanked the entire election machinery for working with full transparency and dedication.

The electoral fate of a total of the 1,314 candidates has now been sealed in the Electronic Voting Machines. Our correspondent has the details:

“Among the 18 districts, voter turnout was good in sixteen districts, except for Patna and Sheikhpura. In rural areas of various districts, voters showed great enthusiasm for casting their votes. Today the electoral fate of 57 Janata Dal (United) candidates from the NDA has been sealed in the EVMs. The fate of 48 BJP and 14 Lok Janshakti Party R candidates has also been locked by the voters. From the Mahagathbandhan, 73 Rashtriya Janata Dal candidates and 24 Congress candidates were in the fray. Among the seven-party Mahagathbandhan, CPI (ML) had fielded its candidates on 14 seats. In the 2020 Assembly elections out of 121 seats 23 were won by Janata Dal (United) and 32 by BJP, Lok Janshakti Party had one seat. On the other hand the Rashtriya Janata Dal had won 42 seats, while its ally Congress had secured four seats. The Vikassheel Insaan Party had also won four seats. In today’s polling, the key figures were BJP candidates and Deputy Chief Ministers Samrat Choudhary and Vijay Kumar Sinha. From the Mahagathbandhan, Chief Ministerial candidate Tejashwi Prasad Yadav is contesting from Raghopur. The electoral fate of a total of 16 ministers of the NDA government was sealed today in the EVMs. Dharmendra Kumar, Rai Akashvani News, Patna.”

The Indian Navy today enhanced its indigenous capabilities with the formal commissioning of the new survey vessel, ‘Ikshak. The ship, the third in the Survey Vessel (Large) class, was inducted into service at a ceremony held at the Naval Base in Kochi. Chief of the Naval Staff, Admiral Dinesh K. Tripathi, presided over the event. Speaking on the occasion, the Navy Chief called INS Ikshak a symbol of India’s maritime confidence and technological self-reliance.

Named after the Sanskrit word for ‘Guide,’ the ship is equipped with advanced hydrographic and oceanographic tools, including multi-beam sonars and Autonomous Underwater Vehicles. Its main role will be to conduct full-scale coastal and deep-water surveys to ensure safe navigation for all maritime traffic.
