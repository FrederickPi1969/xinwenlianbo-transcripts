## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has laid foundation stones and dedicated development projects worth 10 thousand crore rupees in Haryana. On the occasion of the birth anniversary of Dr Bhimrao Ambedkar, Mr Modi laid the foundation stone for the third 800 MegaWatt unit at Deenbandhu Chhotu Ram Thermal Power Plant in Yamunanagar. This project is an expansion of the existing plant that currently operates 2×300 MegaWatt units. The project is targeted to be completed within 52 months.

The Prime Minister also laid the foundation stone for a GOBAR DHAN Plant in Yamunanagar worth 90 crore rupees. The plant will have an annual production capacity of 2 thousand 600 metric tonnes of compressed biogas. Mr. Modi also inaugurated the Rewari Bypass, which has been built at a cost of one thousand 69 crore rupees.

Lauding the hard work of Haryana’s farmers, the Prime Minister emphasized that the governments at Center and state stand as a steadfast partner in joys and challenges of farmers.

During his Visit, he also laid the foundation stone of the new terminal building of Maharaja Agrasen Airport in Hisar and flagged off the first flight for Ayodhya from the Airport.

Supreme Court Justice B R Gavai has said that the Constitution has stood the test of time and made the country strong, stable and united. He was speaking at the first ‘Dr Ambedkar Memorial Lecture’ organised at Dr. Ambedkar International Centre in New Delhi yesterday. Justice Gavai said, in the past years, the country faced various external aggressions and internal disturbances, yet it remained united and strong, with the Constitution being amended from time to time to adapt to the changing needs.

Minister of State for Social Justice and Empowerment Ramdas Athawale outlined several flagship initiatives undertaken in India to translate the ideals of Dr BR Ambedkar into transformative action.Delivering the keynote address at a discussion at the UN, Mr. Athawale underlined the relevance of Dr Ambedkar’s vision in the context of Sustainable Development Goals. Meanwhile, New York City Mayor’s Office announced that the Mayor has proclaimed 14th April 2025 as Dr. Bhimrao Ramji Ambedkar Day.

QNu Labs, one of the startups selected by the Department of Science and Technology (DST) under the National Quantum Mission has launched world’s first and unique platform, Q-Shield. It empowers enterprises to protect their critical infrastructure. It also enables seamless cryptography management across any environment including cloud, on-premises, or hybrid, on the occasion of World Quantum Day.

The Ministry of Science and Technology in a statement said that Q-Shield enables various services like Qosmos for key generation, QConnect for secured connectivity and QVerse for secured collaboration. The launch of this platform adds one more step in India’s journey towards global leadership in quantum technology.

The sixth edition of the National Highway Excellence Awards 2023 will be held today in New Delhi, aiming to encourage innovative practices and recognize professionals for their excellence in National Highway development. The awards serve as a platform to celebrate outstanding achievements in the National Highway infrastructure sector. It honors both individuals and stakeholders for their contributions to the construction and maintenance of high-performing highway assets across the country.

Telangana Chief Minister A Revanth Reddy launched the Bhu Bharathi revenue portal in Hyderabad last evening. The portal is aimed at strengthening land security and bringing transparency to land records in the state. Addressing the officials on the occasion, Mr. Reddy said the Bhu Bharathi portal will provide permanent solutions to land issues through a dispute-free revenue policy.

The Bhu Bharathi revenue portal has been rolled out on a pilot basis in four mandals – Khammam, Mulugu, Kodangal, and Kamareddy blocks in the state. Mandal Revenue Officers will visit every revenue village in these mandals to resolve all pending land-related issues by May 1.

The second round of talks between the United States and Iran on Tehran’s nuclear programme is expected to be held in Rome on Saturday.

“Italian Foreign Minister Antonio Tajani, during his visit to Japan, announced that Rome has agreed to host the meeting following requests from the negotiating parties and Oman, which is acting as a mediator. He said that the Italian government is ready to do everything necessary to support negotiations that would help resolve the nuclear issue and promote peace. However, there has been no official confirmation of the venue from either Iran or the United States.

The first round of indirect talks between US and Iranian officials took place in Oman on April 12. Both countries have said that they had held “positive” and “constructive” talks although the negotiations remain indirect with Oman acting as a mediator.

Meanwhile, Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi will travel to Russia this week for consultations regarding the nuclear talks with the US. VISHNU PS, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI”.

The British Government is scheduled to host a conference of foreign ministers from nearly 20 nations and organisations at Lancaster House in London today, in an attempt to move Sudan closer towards peace. The conference comes on the second anniversary of the start of a civil war in Sudan that became one of the world’s biggest humanitarian crisis, leaving half of Sudan’s population short of food and over 1 crore people displaced internally. Ahead of this conference, the United Kingdom has also called for an urgent United Nations Security Council meeting over conflict in Sudan.

Himachal Pradesh is celebrating its 78th Foundation Day today. On April 15, 1948, the state was formed by merging 30 small and large princely states. Since then, April 15 has been observed every year as ‘Himachal Day’. For the first time, the state-level Himachal Day celebration is taking place in Pangi Valley, generating great enthusiasm among the local residents.

India’s Haj quota has been increased from 1 lakh 36 thousand in 2014 to 1 lakh 75 thousand in 2025. Registrations for the 52-day long upcoming annual Amarnath Yatra have started. The  Pilgrims can register online via Shri Amarnathji Shrine Board website, jksasb.nic.in. More from our correspondent:

“Registrations can also be done at over 540 authorized bank branches across the country and designated centers such as Panchayat Bhawan, Vaishnavi Dham, and Mahajan Hall. Registration is mandatory for all pilgrims and a Compulsory Health Certificate (CHC), issued by an authorised medical body, is required to confirm fitness for the high-altitude trek. To manage the number of participants, only about 15 thousand yatris will be allowed per day. The registration fee for each pilgrim is 220 rupees. A Radio Frequency Identification, or RFID card is mandatory for all pilgrims. The Yatra will commence on July 3 this year from the Pahalgam track in Anantnag district and Baltal route in Ganderbal district. The Yatra will culminate on August 9 on the occasion of Raksha Bandhan. With Gulshan Raina, Sanjeev Jasrotia, Akashwani News, Jammu.”

The Enforcement Directorate, ED has filed a chargesheet in Delhi’s Rouse Avenue Court, naming Congress leaders Sonia Gandhi and Rahul Gandhi in a money laundering case related to the National Herald newspaper. The chargesheet also names Congress Overseas Chief Sam Pitroda, Suman Dubey and others. Special judge Vishal Gogne has posted the matter for further proceedings on the 25th of this month. A prosecution complaint has been filed under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act for the offence of money laundering.

“ED began its probe in the National Herald case after member of Parliament Subramanian Swamy filed a complaint in 2014, accusing Sonia Gandhi, Rahul Gandhi, and other senior Congress leaders of taking over properties of the Associated Journals Limited for a mere sum of 50 lakh rupees. He alleged that the Associated Journals Limited properties were worth over 2,000 crore rupees. Sonia Gandhi and Rahul Gandhi were questioned in 2022 as part of the probe. The National Herald is published by Associated Journals Limited, which is owned by Young Indian Private Limited. Sonia Gandhi and Rahul Gandhi each hold a 38 per cent stake in Young Indian, making them its majority shareholders. Young Indian is under the ED scanner over charges of acquiring Associated Journals Limited and its assets for 50 lakh rupees against an alleged loan extended by the Congress. On 12th of this month, ED served notices to take possession of immovable assets worth 661 crore rupees in Delhi, Mumbai and Lucknow, that it had attached as part of a money laundering probe linked to National Herald newspaper and Associated Journals Limited.“

The Congress has reacted to the Enforcement Directorate’s chargesheet against Sonia Gandhi and Rahul Gandhi in the National Herald money laundering case, alleging that the agency’s move is nothing but politics of vendetta. In a social media post, party General Secretary Jairam Ramesh criticized the move. Reacting to it, the BJP hit out at the opposition party for calling the ED’s action “vendetta politics.” Speaking to Akashvani News, BJP spokesperson Nalin Kohli said that merely labelling it vendetta politics does not negate the core issue that is under investigation.

The Supreme Court today laid down stringent guidelines for the prevention and expedited trial of child trafficking related offences across the country.  The Bench of Justices JB Pardiwala and R Mahadevan also directed High Courts across the country to issue directions to lower courts to complete trial in child trafficking cases in six months. The top court issued the directions while hearing anticipatory bail pleas filed by the accused in a child trafficking case from Uttar Pradesh. It also mandated that such cases be heard on a day-to-day basis to prevent delays in justice. The court criticised the Uttar Pradesh government’s handling of child trafficking cases, and the Allahabad High Court for issuing bail to child trafficking accused. In a significant direction, the Court said that if a new born is stolen from hospital, the first step to be taken is to cancel the license of the concerned hospital. The case involved a stolen baby being delivered to a couple who were desirous of having a son.
