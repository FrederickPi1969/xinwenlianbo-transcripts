## Midday News

Indore has once again retained its title as India’s cleanest city for the eighth consecutive year. Surat secured the second position, while Navi Mumbai ranked third in the central government’s annual cleanliness survey. The ninth edition of Swachh Survekshan 2024-25 Awards were conferred by President Droupadi Murmu at a ceremony held in New Delhi today. Speaking on the occasion, the President highlighted that cleanliness is one of the most important aspect of Viksit Bharat 2047 vision and urged countrymen to move forward in making the country clean, healthy and developed.

In the category of cities with a population between 3 and 10 lakh, Noida emerged as the cleanest city, followed by Chandigarh and Mysore. Ahmedabad received first position in the category of cities with a population of over 10 lakh.

During the event, a total of 78 awards were presented across various categories. This year, the awards were presented across four categories, including Super Swachh League Cities and the top three cleanest cities in five different population categories. More from our correspondent:

“Eight cities of Madhya Pradesh have received awards in various categories in Swachh Survekshan 2024. Indore city has become the cleanest city of the country for the eighth time in a row. It has got the first place among the cities of Super Swachh League. As soon as this news reached, there is a festive atmosphere in the city and people are congratulating each other. Bhopal city has got second place among the cleanest cities. Apart from this, Ujjain, Budhni, Dewas, Shahganj, Jabalpur and Gwalior cities have received awards in various categories. Indore Mayor Pushyamitra Bhargava has congratulated the citizens and appealed to everyone to welcome Safai Mitras.Prashant Sharma Akashvani News Bhopal.”

The Bihar government today announced free electricity up to 125 units to consumers in the state from the 1st of next month. State Chief Minister Nitish Kumar announced the decision  ahead of the Bihar assembly polls,  saying it will benefit one crore 67 lakh families in the state. He added that over the next three years, with the consent of these domestic consumers, solar power plants will be installed on their rooftops and at nearby public places. Mr. Kumar also said the Bihar government will fully bear the cost of installing solar power plants for extremely poor families under the ongoing Kutir Jyoti scheme.

Union Road Transport and Highways Minister Nitin Gadkari said the logistics cost of the country will come down to  nine percent by the end of this year. Addressing an event of a private news channel in New Delhi today, Mr Gadkari said the reduction in logistics cost will increase India’ exports 1.5 times. He noted that increased exports will create more employment opportunities and lead the to an Atmanirbhar Bharat. The Minister also highlighted that the government is on a mission to make India free from air, noise, water, and fossil fuel pollution.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will visit Bihar and West Bengal tomorrow. During his visit to Bihar, the Prime Minister will lay the foundation stone, inaugurate and dedicate to the nation multiple development projects worth over seven thousand crore rupees in Motihari. These projects pertain to the Railways, Roads, Rural Development, Fisheries, Electronics, and Information Technology sectors. He will also flag off four new Amrit Bharat trains. Mr Modi will also release 400 crore rupees to around 61 thousand 500 Self-Help Groups in Bihar under the Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana-National Rural Livelihoods Mission.

On the same day,  Prime Minister Modi will visit West Bengal, where he will lay the foundation stone, inaugurate, and dedicate to the nation multiple development projects worth over five thousand crore rupees, covering the Oil and Gas, Power, Road, and Rail sectors.

The Delhi government has ordered an Anti-Corruption Branch (ACB) probe into alleged irregularities in the Jai Bhim Chief Minister Talent Development Scheme during the tenure of the previous Aam Aadmi Party government. Addressing a press conference in New Delhi, Education Minister Ashish Sood accused the Aam Aadmi Party of a 145 crore rupees scam during the COVID-19 period under the scheme.

He said that Delhi Chief Minister Rekha Gupta had recommended an investigation into the matter, following which Lieutenant Governor Vinai Kumar Saxena directed the ACB to initiate an inquiry.

Union Home and Cooperation Minister Amit Shah today participated in the Sahkarita Evam Rozgar Utsav being held near Jaipur in Rajasthan. Mr. Shah flagged off 100 new police vehicles to be used for police stations, armed forces, troop carriers and training purposes. He distributed certificates and cheques to the beneficiaries of various schemes and inaugurated various infrastructure projects of cooperation. Mr. Shah also inaugurated 24 warehouses built under the world’s largest grain storage scheme and 64 millet outlets for the promotion of Shri Anna.

Addressing the event, Mr. Shah said that cooperatives have contributed a lot to the country’s development in the last 100 years. He added that but the next 100 years belong to cooperatives. He said cooperatives have an active role in 98 percent of rural areas.

US President Donald Trump has said that the ongoing negotiations between India and the United States for a much-anticipated interim trade deal could be sealed soon. Mr Trump said the talks are on track and the proposed deal could provide American companies greater access to the Indian market in line with the trade pact between the US and Indonesia.

Meanwhile US President has announced plans to impose a unified tariff on over 150 countries and regions, mainly targeting those with limited trade ties with the US. Regarding Japan, Mr. Trump said negotiations are underway but expressed doubt about the outcome.

India Meteorological Department (IMD) has issued red alert for extremely heavy rainfall over parts of Madhya Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh today. IMD also issued orange alert for heavy rainfall in Kerala, Mahe and South Interior Karnataka for the next three days. It added that strong surface winds are expected at isolated places over Andhra Pradesh, Kerala, and Lakshadweep today.

IMD has issued orange alert for Kasargod, Kannur, Wayanad, and Kozhikode and yellow alert for several other Kerala districts. All educational institutions, including professional colleges and Kendriya Vidyalayas in these areas, will remain closed today.

In Karnataka, orange alert is sounded for Dakshina Kannada, Udupi and Kodagu that are hit by copious rainfall since the last few days. The district administration in these districts have declared holiday for the schools and anganwadi kendras. More from our Correspondent:-

“Relentless rain in Dakshina Kannada, Udupi, Chikmagalur and Kodagu has disrupted normal life. Landslides have damaged houses and obstructed vehicular movement in these districts. The rivers are flowing at higher levels and precaution is advised by the administration. The tourists in these districts are advised to not venture near water falls and hilly terrains due to landslides. The vehicle movement between Bengaluru and Mangaluru was disrupted due to a landslide on national highway 75 near Kadaba. Rainwater entered houses in these districts and people had to move to safer places. Power Cuts and flooding were reported in several places due to the torrential rainfall. Sudhindra Akashvani News Bengaluru.”

Due to active monsoon conditions in Himachal Pradesh, intermittent rainfall is occurring across most areas of the state, and it  is expected to intensify further in the coming days. The MeT Department has issued a yellow alert for heavy rainfall today and tomorrow, and an orange alert for heavy to very heavy rainfall in several districts from 21st to  23rd of this month. More from our Correspondent:-

“The continued monsoon rains in Himachal Pradesh have led to several incidents of landslides, severely affecting the road network. According to the State Emergency Operation Centre, a total of 226 roads, including one national highway, are currently blocked. In Sirmaur district, the Paonta Sahib–Shillai National Highway remains obstructed. Additionally, 52 electricity transformers and 137 drinking water schemes have also been affected, and restoration work is underway on a war footing. Since June 20, a total of 109 people have lost their lives due to rain-related incidents in the state, while 35 people are still missing. Ritesh Kapoor, Akashvani News, Shimla.”

Continuous rains in Palamu, Garhwa, Deoghar, Koderma, Sahibganj and other districts in Jharkhand have stalled daily life. The Ranchi Met Centre has issued an orange alert for four districts and yellow alert for 11 districts.

In Jammu and Kashmir, the Shri Amarnath Ji Yatra has been temporarily suspended due to heavy rainfall along the Baltal and Pahalgam routes. No convoys were allowed from the Jammu base camp today. Meanwhile,  the Indian Army has come to the immediate rescue of hundreds of Amarnath Yatris stranded due to a landslide triggered by relentless rainfall in the higher reaches of Jammu and Kashmir.

In Chess, Teenage Indian Grandmaster Rameshbabu Praggnanandhaa defeated world number one Magnus Carlsen to qualify for the quarterfinals of the Freestyle Grand Slam chess event in Las Vegas. The 19-year-old outplayed the Norwegian champion in just 39 moves in a dominant Round 4 clash last night.

Domestic equity benchmarks, Sensex and Nifty, were trading lower in the afternoon session today as the market showed mixed sentiment. This came amid US President Donald Trump’s statement that a US-India trade deal is nearing finalisation and subdued sentiment across Asian markets, as investor caution grew due to uncertainty surrounding the future of US Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell.

The 30 share index at BSE, sensex, was trading 200 point down at 82 thousand 433 while the Nifty 50 index  nearly 50 points down to trade at 25 thousand 163 a short while ago.

## Evening News

The Ministry of External Affairs has said the Nimisha Priya case in Yemen is a sensitive matter and the government of India has been offering all possible assistance in the case. Addressing media in New Delhi this afternoon, MEA spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal said, the local authorities in Yemen have postponed carrying out her sentence scheduled for the 16th of this month.

On NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte’s statement, the spokesperson said India is closely following the developments. He reiterated that securing the energy needs of the people is the overriding priority for India.

Petroleum and Natural Gas Minister Hardeep Singh Puri today asserted that India is not concerned about sanctions on Russian crude as there are enough supplies in the market. Speaking at Urja Varta 2025 in New Delhi, Mr Puri stated that India will import oil from wherever it is available at the cheapest price. He highlighted that this is the new India where the government is not driven by pressure, but by impact.

India has achieved a significant milestone by successfully destroying two Aerial High Speed Unmanned targets at high-altitude in Ladakh by Akash Prime, which is the upgraded variant of Akash Weapon System for the Indian Army. The Weapon System is customised to operate in altitude and above 4,500 metres and has latest upgrades including indigenously developed Radio Frequency seeker.

Army Air Defence and DRDO, in conjunction with Defence PSUs have successfully validated the indigenously designed and developed Akash Prime Weapon System.

This achievement carries added significance as it follows the exceptional performance of India’s indigenously developed air defence systems during Operation Sindoor. Defence Minister Rajnath Singh has complimented the Indian Army, DRDO, and industry on achieving the milestone.

The National Investigation Agency, NIA has arrested two more individuals in connection with the recovery of an Improvised Explosive Device (IED) planted by the banned ULFA (I) terror group. The NIA said, the device was discovered near Dispur Last Gate in Guwahati and was part of a larger conspiracy to carry out multiple blasts in Assam on Independence Day last year.

Indore has once again retained its title as India’s cleanest city for the eighth consecutive year. Surat secured the second position, while Navi Mumbai ranked third in the central government’s annual cleanliness survey. Among the cities with a population of 3 to 10 lakhs, Noida emerged as the cleanest, followed by Chandigarh and Mysore. She complimented the Ministry for promoting the Reduce, Reuse, Recycle (3R) approach and commended the waste to wealth memento presented to her.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will visit Bihar and West Bengal tomorrow. In Bihar, the Prime Minister will lay the foundation stone, inaugurate and dedicate to the nation multiple development projects worth over seven thousand crore rupees in Motihari.

In West Bengal also, the PM will lay the foundation stone, inaugurate, and dedicate to the nation multiple development projects worth over five thousand crore rupees.

In a major step to improve connectivity and support pilgrimage, the Ministry of Railways has sanctioned a Final Location Survey (FLS) for a new 77.96 kilometers rail line between Jammu and Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Katra. The project aims to ease travel for devotees and enhance infrastructure in Jammu and Kashmir.

Aircraft Accident Investigation Bureau- AAIB has appealed to the media to stop selective and unverified reporting of an Air India plane crash in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, last month. AAIB said that it has come to its attention that certain sections of the international media are repeatedly attempting to draw conclusions through selective and unverified reporting. AAIB said, such actions are irresponsible, especially while the investigation remains ongoing.

A delegation of Special Envoys from South Korea met Prime Minister Narendra Modi in New Delhi today. Prime Minister Modi reaffirmed India’s commitment to advancing the India-South Korea Special Strategic Partnership. He recalled his meeting with South Korean President Lee Jae Myung last month.  Prime Minister Modi said close collaboration between the democracies contributes to peace, stability, and prosperity in the Indo-Pacific region.

In Iraq, at least 60 people were killed and several others injured in a massive fire at a hypermarket in the Al-Kut city today. The province governor confirmed that the fire broke out in a hyper market and a restaurant and that firefighters rescued several people. He added, lawsuits have been filed against the owner of the building and the mall. An investigation has been launched into the incident.

In Bihar, at least 19 people were killed and several injured due to lightning in the past 24 hours. Maximum five casualties have been reported in Nalanda district followed by four in Vaishali district this afternoon. Two fatalities were reported each in Patna and Banka districts.  All the fatalities occurred during rains while people were working in the fields.

Chief Minister Nitish Kumar has announced an ex gratia of 4 lakh rupees each to the next of the kin of the deceased.

The Met department has issued a warning of lightning, gusty winds accompanying rainfall in several districts.

The India Meteorological Department has forecast extremely heavy rainfall over Jammu and Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Uttar Pradesh during the next 5 days. Punjab, Haryana and Rajasthan will also have similar conditions till Saturday. Senior IMD Scientist, Dr. RK Jenamani told Akashvani that a red alert has been issued for the entire Madhya Pradesh and South East Uttar Pradesh.

Orange alert has been issued for Kerala, Mahe and South Interior Karnataka for the next three days.

In Uttar Pradesh, along with capital Lucknow many districts in eastern and western parts received moderate to heavy rains today.

“All major ghats in Varanasi have been submerged as the Ganga’s water level keeps rising due to continuous rainfall in the region. State Water Power Minister Swatantra Dev Singh has said that the government is being cautious about floods in the state. Continuous monitoring is going on from flood posts

The weather department has predicted heavy to very heavy rainfall in Prayagraj division and some districts of Bundelkhand region during the next 24 hours. At the same time, strong winds can also blow in the central and southern parts of the state including the capital Lucknow. Om Awasthi, Akashvani News, Lucknow.”

Most parts of Uttarakhand are likely to experience light to moderate rainfall with thunder and lightning over the next few days.

“The Met Department has warned of heavy rains at some places in Dehradun, Tehri, Rudraprayag, Nainital, Bageshwar and Pithoragarh districts during the next 24 hours. Whereas, some other districts may also witness intense showers along with thunder and lightning. During this period, strong winds at a speed of 30 to 40 kilometers per hour are expected in isolated areas across all districts of the state. Meanwhile, intermittent rain continues in various districts. Most of the districts of the state including the capital Dehradun remained cloudy after the rain this morning. Sakshi Singh, Akashvani News, Dehradun.”

In Pakistan, torrential monsoon rains and associated incidents have left at least 124 people dead and 264 others injured throughout the country over the past three weeks. In Punjab province, 44 people lost their lives in rain-related incidents due to collapsed roofs and electrocution while 16 fatalities were reported in Balochistan from similar rain-induced disasters.

Lakshmi Jadala, Asia’s top-ranked para lawn tennis player with an intellectual disability, clinched the gold medal at the National Tennis Championship 2025 (Disability Sports) held in Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh. With this triumphant win, 15-year-old Jadala has officially qualified for the Special Olympics World Games 2027, set to take place in Cairo, Egypt.

Competing in the U-17 junior category, Jadala was unstoppable, winning all three of his matches in straight sets. Talking to media persons, an elated Jadala said, to win this tournament and represent India at the Special Olympics was a dream come true.

Youth Affairs and Sports Minister Dr. Mansukh Mandaviya today outlined India’s strategy towards finishing among the top 10 nations in the 2036 Summer Olympics and Paralympics. Speaking at the Khelo Bharat Conclave in Delhi, the Minister said sports is a public movement and goals can be achieved only if everyone works together. He asserted that only a united force can make India a global sporting powerhouse. He noted that Prime Minister Narendra Modi believes in a united force when it comes to sports.
