## Morning News

India has taken series of strong measures following Pahalgam terror attack including suspension of Indus Waters Treaty, 1960 and closing of the Integrated Check Post Attari with immediate effect. The Cabinet Committee on Security, CCS reviewed the overall security situation in its meeting at Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s residence last evening and directed all forces to maintain high vigil. Briefing media in New Delhi, Foreign Secretary Vikram Misri informed these decisions.

The Foreign Secretary said that Pakistani nationals will not be permitted to travel to India under the SAARC Visa Exemption Scheme (SVES) visas.

Mr Misri said, the overall strength of the High Commissions will be brought down to 30 from the present 55 through further reductions by 1st May. He said, the CCS chaired by the Prime Minister was briefed in detail on the terrorist attack that took place on Tuesday in Pahalgam. In the briefing to the CCS, the cross-border linkages of the terrorist attack were brought out.

The CCS condemned the attack in the strongest terms and expressed its deepest condolences to the families of the victims and hoped for the early recovery of the injured.

Government has convened an all party meeting in New Delhi today in view of Pahalgam terror attack incident. Sources said, the meeting will take place at Parliament house complex at 6 PM.

Maharashtra has announced financial assistance of five lakh rupees for the families of six victims who were shot dead during the Pahalgam terrorist attack. Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis said a financial assistance of fifty thousand will also be given to those injured in the incident.

Speaking to reporters in Mumbai, Mr. Fadnavis said such a despicable act of terrorism will not go unpunished, adding that Prime Minister Narendra Modi would track down the mastermind and retaliate with extreme steps.

United Nations Secretary-General Antonio Guterres has strongly condemned the terrorist strike at Pahalgam in Jammu and Kashmir that killed 26 people. He stressed that attacks against civilians are unacceptable, under any circumstances. The Secretary-General’s spokesperson Stephane Dujarric said that Mr. Guterres offered his heartfelt condolences to the bereaved families of the victims. The targeted attack by a front organisation of the Pakistan-based Lashkar-e-Tayyiba killed tourists at a popular tourism spot.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will visit Bihar today and participate in a programme at Madhubani marking the National Panchayati Raj Day. On the occasion, Mr. Modi will address Gram Sabhas Nationwide from Madhubani. The Prime Minister will also inaugurate, lay the foundation stone and dedicate to the nation multiple development projects worth over 13 thousand 480 crore rupees. The public meeting has been organised at Bideshwar Sthan at Jhanjharpur where he will launch these projects. Mr. Modi is scheduled to address the function at 11.30 am at the venue.

This year the National Panchayati Raj Day is being observed as a major national programme through a “Whole-of-Government” approach, involving participation of six Union Ministries -the Ministry of Rural Development, Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas, Ministry of Power, Ministry of Railways, and Ministry of Road Transport and Highways. The Prime Minister will also confer Special Category National Panchayat Awards 2025 on the occasion in different categories.

“Prime Minister Narendra Modi will present several gifts to the people of Bihar and the country from Madhubani today. On the Panchayati Raj Day, he will launch several initiatives to empower Panchayats and present awards to Mukhiyas and Sarpanches for innovation in Panchayat areas. Marking the significance of the Mithila region, these awards will also feature the specially designed imprint of Madhubani paintings.

Additionally, Prime Minister Modi will distribute assistance funds of 930 crore rupees among two lakh self-help groups under the Deendayal Upadhyaya Rural Livelihoods Mission. He will also lay the foundation stone and inaugurate several schemes worth 6200 crore rupees to support Bihar in the power sector.

To ensure easy availability of cooking gas, the Prime Minister will lay the foundation of an LPG bottling plant in Hathua in Gopalganj, at a cost of 340 crore rupees. To fulfill the basic needs of poor people, the Prime Minister will grant 15 lakh approvals under the PM Awas Yojana for rural beneficiaries and release installment to 10 lakh people.  He will also hand over constructed houses to one lakh 50 thousand beneficiaries from urban and rural areas under PM Awas Scheme. Dharmendra Kumar Rai, Akashvani News, Jhanjharpur, Madhubani.”

The Prime Minister will also flag off four trains, three rail lines and two rail over bridges through video conferencing during the event at Madhubani.

“The Prime Minister will flag off Namo Bharat Rapid rail between Jaynagar to Patna and Amrit Bharat express between Saharsa to Lokmanya Tilak Terminus in Mumbai. This will be the second Namo Bharat train in the country while the introduction of Amrit Bharat  will be the third train service.

Talking to Akashvani News, Executive Director, Ministry of Railways Dilip Kumar said that the fully air conditioned Namo Bharat train is equipped with improved passengers amenities and advanced security system.

During the event, rail lines between Supaul to Pipra, Hasanpur to Bithan and Khagaria-Alauli will also be dedicated to the nation. These projects will improve connectivity and lead to overall socio-economic development of the region. Anand Kumar for Akashvani News, Jaynagar.”

Governor Arif Mohammad Khan, Chief Minister Nitish Kumar and several other dignitaries will attend the programme.

He will address the premier Steel Industry event through video-conferencing. The three-day international exhibition and conference will bring together leading stakeholders from across the global steel value chain to discuss the future trajectory of the sector.

## Midday News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi said today that India will identify, trace, and punish every terrorist and their backers. He said that India will pursue them to the ends of the Earth. The Prime Minister said, terrorism will not go unpunished. He said that every effort will be made to ensure that justice is done, and the entire nation is firm in this resolve. The Prime Minister said this while addressing a public gathering in Madhubani district of Bihar on the occasion of National Panchayati Raj Day.

Prime Minister Modi further said that the country’s spirit will never be broken by terrorism.

The Prime Minister also emphasized that everyone who believes in humanity is with India. He stated that the country is saddened and in pain after this incident, and stands with the families of the victims.

Before beginning his address, the Prime Minister observed a moment of silence to pay tribute to those who lost their lives in the Pahalgam terrorist attack.

The Prime Minister inaugurated, laid the foundation stone, and dedicated to the nation multiple development projects worth over 13,480 crore rupees during his visit to Bihar.

The project inaugurated and work started today include an LPG bottling plant at Gopalganj, electrification projects, housing schemes, railway infrastructure, and road development.

PM Modi also inaugurated railway projects worth 870 crore rupees and flagged off four trains during his visit to Bihar. He flagged off the Namo Bharat Rapid Rail between Jaynagar and Patna, and the Amrit Bharat Express between Saharsa and Lokmanya Tilak Terminus, Mumbai, through video conferencing during an event organized at Madhubani marking National Panchayati Raj Day. This is the second Namo Bharat train in the country, while the introduction of the Amrit Bharat Express marks the third such train service. Additionally, the Prime Minister flagged off two passenger trains-one from Saharsa to Pipra and another from Samastipur to Saharsa.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today stated that India’s steel industry is brimming with renewed confidence about its future. Speaking virtually at the inauguration of the INDIA STEEL – 6th International Exhibition and Conference being held in Mumbai, he highlighted the ambitious goals set under the National Steel Policy, which aims to increase the country’s steel production to 300 million tonnes by 2030.
