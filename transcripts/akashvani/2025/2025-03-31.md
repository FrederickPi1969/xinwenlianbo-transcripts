## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has launched projects worth 33 thousand 700 crore rupees in Chhattisgarh. Addressing a public meeting in Bilaspur, Mr Modi said that these initiatives will create thousands of jobs and accelerate state’s economic development. The Prime Minister emphasized that the projects are aligned with his government’s broader agenda of infrastructure expansion, housing, education, and rural empowerment.

The projects unveiled by  the Prime Minister include three lakh new houses under the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana, 130 PM-SHRI schools equipped with smart classrooms and modern laboratories, a 540-km petroleum pipeline to enhance regional energy security, and an extensive network of rural roads aimed at improving connectivity in the Maoist-affected areas. Mr Modi said, these projects are part of a larger effort to integrate Chhattisgarh into India’s economic mainstream, reducing its historical dependence on mining and agriculture by fostering industrial and educational hubs.

Taking aim at the Opposition, Mr Modi accused the Congress of failing Chhattisgarh’s youth through recruitment scams, misgovernance, and stalled development projects. He alleged under the Congress rule, corruption was rampant, recruitment exams were marred by paper leaks, and government funds meant for the poor were misused.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will flag off the first Vande Bharat Express to Kashmir from Katra on April 19, marking the completion of the of the 272-kilometre Udhampur-Srinagar-Baramulla rail link. Speaking to reporters in Jammu yesterday, Union Minister Jitendra Singh said, Prime Minister Modi will arrive in Udhampur on April 19. During his visit, he will inaugurate the world’s highest railway bridge before flagging off the Vande Bharat train from Katra.

The much-awaited rail project, which began in 1997, faced multiple delays due to tough terrain. It includes 38 tunnels, 927 bridges, and the 1,315-meter-long Chenab Bridge, which is taller than the Eiffel Tower.

The Ministry of Home Affairs announced  the extension of the Armed Forces (Special Powers) Act (AFSPA) for another six months in parts of Manipur, Arunachal Pradesh, and Nagaland, starting tomorrow. In Manipur, AFSPA will continue in all districts except for those under the jurisdiction of 13 police stations in five districts – Imphal West, Imphal East, Thoubal, Bishnupur, and Kakching. The state has been grappling with violence since May 2023.

The law has also been extended in eight districts of Nagaland and parts of five others, while in Arunachal Pradesh, AFSPA applies to Tirap, Changlang, Longding districts, and certain areas in Namsai district.

The National Investigation Agency (NIA) arrested a key accused in connection with the illegal trafficking of a victim to the United States, who was deported back to India earlier this month. The agency said, according to the victim’s complaint, he had paid approximately 45 Lakh rupees to the accused agent for the illegal immigration process.

NIA investigations revealed the accused, who lacked any license, legal permit, or registration to send individuals abroad, had trafficked the victim to the US via Spain, El Salvador, Guatemala, and Mexico using the donkey route.

President Droupadi Murmu will be on a two-day visit to Mumbai beginning today. The President will reach the city this evening. She will attend the closing ceremony of the Reserve Bank of India’s 90th anniversary celebrations in Mumbai tomorrow.

In Uttar Pradesh, in a major administrative reshuffle, the Allahabad High Court has transferred 582 judicial officers. The transferred officers include 236 Additional District and Sessions Judges, 207 Civil Judges (Senior Division), and 139 Civil Judges (Junior Division). The order for the Annual Transfer-2025 was issued last evening by Satish Kumar Pushkar, Joint Registrar of the Allahabad High Court. All transferred judges have been directed to join their new postings immediately.

Kanpur saw the highest number of transfers, with 13 judges reassigned. Besides, 11 judges from Aligarh and five from Bareilly have also been transferred.

In Chhattisgarh, a female Maoist has been killed in an encounter between security forces and Maoists in Dantewada district. According to the information received, a joint team of security forces had gone on an anti-Naxal operation in the forest area located on the Bijapur-Dantewada district border.

During the operation, an encounter broke out this morning. Security forces have recovered the body of the killed Naxalite. A rifle and some explosives have also been recovered from the spot. Search operation is underway in the area.

Union Minority Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju has asked Opposition parties not to mislead people over Waqf amendment bill. In a statement released today, the CBCI said that over the past three years, this issue has escalated into a complex legal dispute. A French court has imposed a jail sentence on far-right leader Marine Le Pen and barred her from seeking public office after finding her guilty of embezzling European Union funds. The Paris court ruled that Ms Le Pen used more than 3.3 million dollars of European Parliament funds to pay members of her National Rally (RN) party.
