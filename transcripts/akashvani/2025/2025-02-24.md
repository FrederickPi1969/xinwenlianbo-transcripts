## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will release the 19th installment of the Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) scheme from Bhagalpur in Bihar today. The Prime Minister installment will be released to 9.8 crore beneficiary farmers via Direct Benefit Transfer. More than 22 thousand 700 crore rupees assistance will be directly released to the bank accounts of beneficiaries of the scheme. The Prime Minister will also inaugurate and dedicate various development projects in Bihar and  address a public meeting at Airport Ground in Bhagalpur in the afternoon. More from our correspondent :

Prime Minister will also interact with farmers during his address at Bhagalpur. Several farmers related schemes will also be inaugurated on the occasion. Prime Minister Modi will inaugurate a Centre for Excellence for breeding of indigenous varieties of  livestock under Gokul Mission at Motihari. He will also dedicate a milk processing unit at Barauni in Bihar. Prime Minister will also dedicate a newly doubled rail section of Nawada -Warisaliganj-Tilaiya. Dharmendra Kumar Rai Akashvani News, Bhagalpur.

Sharing the details of the programme, Union Agriculture and Farmers Welfare Minister Shivraj Singh Chauhan said, more than 3.46 lakh crore rupees have been disbursed so far to more than 11 crore farmer families in the country under the PM-KISAN scheme.

All is set for the Global Investors Summit (GIS) to be inaugurated by Prime Minister Narendra Modi in Bhopal today.  The capital of Madhya Pradesh will host hundreds of guests from all over the world at the newly inaugurated tent city, constructed near Kaliyasot Dam. The Prime Minister will address investors, industrialists, NRIs and start-ups from the country and abroad and launch the industrial policies of Madhya Pradesh.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will today address investors from all over the world in the picturesque backdrop of upper Lake and the unique landscape of the Rashtriya Manav Sangrahalaya, besides releasing more than 18 new policies of Madhya Pradesh. These include policies related to industry, food, export, MSME, start-ups, GCC, semiconductors, drones, tourism and film production. This is the eighth Global Investors Summit in Madhya Pradesh and the first to be held in Bhopal. Chief Minister Dr. Mohan Yadav will preside over the conference. More than 25 thousand registrations have been done for participation in the Global Investors Summit. More than 100 foreign delegates from more than 50 countries are also arriving in Bhopal. Three major industrial exhibitions will be held during the Summit. The Auto Show, the Textile and Fashion Expo and the “One District-One Product” (ODOP) Village.

In Telangana, the situation at the Srisailam Left Bank Canal Tunnel, is still serious with no access gained to reach the stranded individuals. The rescue efforts are underway in full vigour. State Irrigation Minister Uttam Kumar Reddy informed the media that all out efforts are being made to bring out the trapped individuals to safety. He said water seepage and accumulated mud became an obstruction to rescue operation despite the rescue teams travelling 12-13 kilometres inside the tunnel, several times to reach out to the trapped individuals. Arrangements have been made to continuously draw out water and supply of oxygen in the tunnel.
A part of the roof collapsed on Saturday morning injuring about a dozen people and trapping eight individuals including two engineers and six workers inside the tunnel near Domalapenta in Nagarkurnool district.
