In Telangana, the rescue operation at the SriSailam Left Bank Canal (SLBC) Tunnel at Domalapenta in Nagarkurnool district gained further momentum with restoration of the conveyor belt. Officials announced that the restoration of the conveyor belt has enabled the removal of 800 tonnes of debris an hour.  Disaster Management Special Chief Secretary Arvind Kumar and Collector Badavath Santosh held a review meeting at the site, assessing the rescue operation at the SLBC project.

The Authorities said that excavation is underway in areas identified by ground-penetrating radar, with two escalators kept ready for assistance. The final components of the tunnel boring machine are set to be cut using a gas cutter and removed through a loco train. The water inside the tunnel is being pumped out. Meanwhile, the National Centre for Seismology’s team has also arrived at the site to assist in the rescue operation.

US President Donald Trump launched a trade war against America’s three biggest trading partners, drawing immediate retaliation from Mexico, Canada and China. Just after last midnight, Trump imposed 25 percent taxes, or tariffs, on Mexican and Canadian imports, though he limited the levy to 10 percent on Canadian energy. Trump also doubled the tariff he slapped last month on Chinese products to 20 percent.

Canadian Prime Minister Justin Trudeau speaking at a press conference rejected the US justification for the tariffs. He said his country would impose tariffs on more than $100 billion of American goods over the course of 21 days.

China also announced retaliatory action yesterday which included 10 percent to 15 percent tariffs on certain agriculture imports from the US. This is key because China is the biggest market for these goods, such as American corn, wheat and soybeans.

Addressing a joint session of both Houses of Congress this morning for the first time during his second term he said that the people elected him to do the job, and he is doing it.  Trump said he has already done much to address what he calls a national emergency at the US-Mexico border.

Senator Elissa Slotkin of Michigan will give the Democratic Party response. President Trump last spoke to a joint session of Congress when he delivered his State of the Union address in February 2020 – weeks before the COVID-19 pandemic brought the world to a halt.

In his first comments since the US paused military aid to Ukraine, Zelenskyy emphasized Ukraine’s willingness to negotiate a peace deal and sign a minerals agreement, which was not finalized during the meeting.

The Indian Meteorological Department has forecast that Delhi-NCR will experience strong surface winds gusting to 20-30 kilometer per hour over the next two days. Arab leaders have approved a 53 billion dollar reconstruction plan for Gaza, that aims to avoid displacing Palestinians from the enclave. The plan was accepted at the closing of the emergency Arab summit in Cairo yesterday with full support from participating Arab leaders. Egyptian Foreign Minister Badr Abdelatty told that the plan includes the establishment of a seaport and an airport in the Gaza Strip and the recycling of the rubble left by the destruction in Gaza.

Arab leaders issued a warning that any attempts to displace the Palestinian people or to annex any part of the occupied Palestinian territory would lead the region into a new phase of conflict, undermine opportunities for stability, and expand the conflict to other countries in the region. They demanded that Israel should completely withdraw from the Gaza Strip, including the Philadelphi Corridor between Gaza and Egypt.

The Defence Bio-Engineering and Electro Medical Laboratory, a Bengaluru-based lab under the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) has successfully conducted high-altitude trials of the Indigenous On-Board Oxygen Generating System (OBOGS) based Integrated Life Support System for the LCA Tejas aircraft.

The OBOGS-based Integrated Life Support System is a cutting-edge system designed to generate and regulate breathable oxygen for pilots during flight, eliminating dependence on traditional liquid oxygen cylinder-based systems.
