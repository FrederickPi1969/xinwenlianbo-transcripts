Today we remember, Param Vir Chakra awardee Rifleman Sanjay Kumar who displayed exemplary bravery during the Kargil War.

A native of Bilaspur District of Himachal Pradesh, Rifleman Sanjay Kumar joined the 13th Battalion, Jammu and Kashmir Rifles in 1996. During the Kargil War on 4th July 1999, Rifleman Sanjay Kumar was the leading scout of a team tasked to capture Area Flat Top of Point 4875 in the Mushkoh Valley. During the attack when enemy automatic fire from one of the sangars posed stiff opposition and stalled the column, Rifleman Sanjay Kumar, realizing the gravity of the situation and with utter disregard to his personal safety, charged at the enemy. In the ensuing hand-to-hand combat, he killed three of the intruders and was himself seriously injured. Despite his injuries, he charged onto the second sangar. Taken totally by surprise, the enemy left behind a Universal Machine Gun and started running. Rifleman Sanjay Kumar picked up the UMG and killed the fleeing enemy. Although bleeding profusely, he refused to be evacuated. The brave action on his part motivated his comrades and they overcoming the treacherous terrain and charged onto the enemy and wrested the area Flat Top. Rifleman Sanjay Kumar displayed most conspicuous gallantry, cool courage and devotion to duty of an exceptionally high order in the face of the enemy. The Nation salutes him. In February 2022, he received the rank of Subedar Major and is posted to the National Defence Academy in Pune. ANUPAM MISHRA, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.

Indian economy will grow at 6.3 percent during the current financial year, ahead of China, US, and European Union, a UN report said. India continues to shine, even as the global economy stands on shaky ground. United Nations said that India will remain the fastest-growing major economy in the world this year. While the growth figure has been slightly revised from the 6.6 percent forecast in January, India is still ahead of other big economies like China, US, and the European Union. Next year, India’s economy is expected to grow by 6.4 percent. On the global front, the picture is far less rosy. UN said, the world economy is at a precarious moment, pointing to rising trade tensions and policy uncertainty. Many countries are now expected to grow slower than previously thought. China is expected to grow at 4.6%, the US at 1.6%, Japan at 0.7%, and the European Union at just 1%. Germany may even see a negative growth of -0.1%. The total number of Aadhaar authentication transactions have crossed 150 billion marks, making it a milestone moment in the journey of the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) and for the broader Aadhaar ecosystem. We have a report:

The landmark highlights the extensive usage and utility of Aadhaar, and the growth of digital economy in the country. Ministry of Electronics and  IT in a statement said that the cumulative number was achieved by the end of April 2025, since inception. The Ministry added that in April alone, almost 210 crore Aadhaar authentication transactions were carried out which is  nearly eight  percent  more than the same month in 2024. It added that Aadhaar based authentication has been playing a stellar role in aiding ease of living, in effective welfare delivery, and voluntarily availing various services offered by service providers. The Agency further informed that the two accused had been on the run for the past two years and also had non-bailable warrants issued against them by the NIA Special Court, Mumbai. It added that the two men, already chargesheeted along with the other arrested accused, had been engaged in assembling IEDs from a rented house in Pune. The investigations in the case are continuing.

India is set to achieve a significant milestone with the 101st mission of its space programme. ISRO’s PSLV-C61 rocket will take-off with Earth Observation Satellite, EOS-09 at 5:59 AM tomorrow from the Satish Dhawan Space Centre at Sriharikota in Andhra Pradesh.

The mission will place the EOS-09 into a Sun Synchronous Polar Orbit. The EOS-09 is designed to provide continuous and reliable remote sensing data for operational applications across various sectors. The mission carries a Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) payload capable of providing images for various earth observation application under all-weather condition. Adhering to the ISRO’s commitment to the community, the spacecraft will be a debris-free mission. The count down for this launch began this morning.
