## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will share his thoughts with the people of the country and abroad in the ‘Mann Ki Baat’ programme on Akashvani at 11 AM today. It will be the 118th episode of the monthly radio programme.

The programme will be broadcast on the entire network of Akashvani and Doordarshan, AIR News website and newsonair mobile app. It will also be live streamed on the YouTube channels of AIR News, DD News, PMO and Information and Broadcasting Ministry. Akashwani will broadcast the programme in regional languages immediately after the Hindi broadcast.

In a social media post, Mr. Modi said, it is also a testament to the deep cultural, linguistic, historical and civilisational bonds between the people of India and Sri Lanka.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah will participate in the 20th Raising Day Celebrations of the National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) in Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh today. The NDRF is the largest force globally dedicated to responding to disasters embodying the moto आपदा सेवा सदैव सर्वत्र. The Home Minister will also inaugurate and lay the foundation stone for projects worth 220 crores rupees. Our correspondent has filed this report:

“To mark the 20th Raising Day of the NDRF the Union Home Minister and Minister of Cooperation, Amit Shah, will inaugurate the Southern Campus of the National Institute of Disaster Management (NIDM) in Kondapavuluru in Krishna District of Andhra Pradesh. This new campus will further education, research, and training in building disaster resilience and lead India towards Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s vision of ‘zero-casualty approach’ for disaster management. The Home Minister will also inaugurate the 10th Battalion campus of the NDRF in Vijayawada and virtually inaugurate the Regional Response Centre of the 9th Battalion in Supaul, Bihar. Mr Amit Shah will also virtually lay the foundation stone for a new state-of-the-art ‘Integrated Indoor Shooting Range’ at the National Police Academy (NPA) in Hyderabad.”  Sri Sai for Akashvani News Vijayawada.”

The Centre has proposed a meeting with the protesting farmers on February 14 in Chandigarh to discuss their demands. Think Mobility Report, revealed that, the growth in the mobility sector will be driven by both traditional and emerging revenue pools, marking a departure from global trends.  According to the report, women now influence 52 per cent of decision-making in the electric vehicles space, surpassing the 38 per cent for internal combustion engine (ICE) vehicles.

The World Bank has projected 6.7 per cent growth for India, surpassing global growth of 2.7 per cent. The January 2025 edition of the World Bank’s Global Economic Prospects (GEP) report stated that India is set to dominate the global economic landscape, maintaining its status as the fastest-growing large economy for the next two fiscal years.

## Midday News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that the country is setting new benchmarks in space technology, with visionary scientists and innovators providing solutions to future challenges. Addressing the nation in the 118th episode of his Mann Ki Baat programme on Akashvani today, Mr. Modi commended the nation’s recent achievements in the space sector.

Lauding the Bengaluru-based Indian Space-Tech start-up, ‘Pixxel’ for successfully launching the country’s first private satellite constellation ‘Firefly’, the Prime Minister called it a significant step towards self-reliance.

The Prime Minister also applauded the space docking of satellites, saying that this technology is important for sending supplies to space stations and crew missions in space. He said, India has become the fourth country to achieve this success. Mr. Modi extended his best wishes to scientists, innovators and young entrepreneurs on behalf of the entire nation.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi extended his greetings in advance for the 75th anniversary of Republic Day saying that this year’s celebration is particularly special as this year marks 75 years of the constitution coming into force.

Mr. Modi saluted the members of Constituent Assembly who contributed in framing the Constitution. The Prime Minister remembered Baba Saheb Ambedkar, Dr. Rajendra Prasad and Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee saying that citizens must take inspiration from thoughts of these great leaders.

Prime Minister Modi also highlighted the importance of National Voters Day which will be celebrated on 25th January. This day is important because on this day the Election Commission of India was established. Lauding the role and contributions of the Election Commission, Mr. Modi said, constitution makers have accorded a very important place to the Election Commission in the Constitution and to the people’s participation in democracy.

On the on-going Maha Kumbh in Prayagraj, Uttar Pradesh, the Prime Minister said, this is an extraordinary confluence of equality and harmony.

Highlighting the amazing bonding between humans and animals, Mr. Modi said, Indian culture and heritage teaches us to live with love with the surrounding animals and birds.

On the completion of nine years of Start-Up India, the Prime Minister said, the country’s Start-Up culture is not limited to big cities and more than half of the start-ups are from Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities. He said, Waste Management, Non-Renewable Energy, Biotechnology and Logistics are the sectors in which the most number of Start-Ups are being noticed.

Remembering the contribution of Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose, the Prime Minister said, the country will celebrate the 23rd of January as Parakram Diwas on Netaji’s birth anniversary. Terming Subhash Babu a visionary leader, Mr. Modi said, courage was ingrained in Netaji’s nature.

Pointing towards good work done by the citizens for the betterment of society and environment, Mr Modi lauded the efforts of KG Mohammad of Lakshadweep saying that the marine ecosystem of Minicoy Island is improving with his hard work.

The long-awaited ceasefire deal between Israel and the Palestinian armed group Hamas commenced today in the Gaza Strip, marking a potential turning point in the 15-month conflict that has claimed thousands of lives. The truce’s implementation was delayed by nearly three hours due to last-minute complications over hostage lists, highlighting the delicate nature of the agreement. Mediator Qatar confirmed the start of the truce.

Israel has confirmed receiving from Hamas the names of the three captives to be freed on the first day of the implementation of the agreement. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu on social media platform posted that the security establishment is now checking the details. Israeli Prime Minister’s office said, four other female hostages could be freed in seven days.

“Under the terms of the deal, brokered by Qatar, Egypt and the United States, Hamas will release 33 hostages over a six-week period, with three female captives scheduled for release on the first day. In exchange, Israel has agreed to free hundreds of Palestinian prisoners. The agreement also enables increased humanitarian aid to enter Gaza through multiple crossing points. World Food Programme trucks have begun delivering essential supplies to Gaza, with convoys of wheat flour and ready-to-eat food parcels moving into Gaza. Looking ahead, significant challenges remain unresolved. The question of Gaza’s future governance remains a critical point of contention. Israel has maintained its position that it will not end the war with Hamas in power, though this issue has been deliberately excluded from current negotiations due to its complexity.This is Vinod Kumar for Akashvani news, from Dubai.”
