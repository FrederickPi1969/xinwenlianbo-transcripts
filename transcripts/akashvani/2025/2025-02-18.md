## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will hold talks with the Amir of Qatar, Sheikh Tamim Bin Hamad Al-Thani in New Delhi today. The talks between the Prime Minister and the Amir will focus on various aspects of bilateral relations between India and Qatar. Sheikh Tamim will be accorded a ceremonial welcome at the forecourt of Rashtrapati Bhawan today. During his visit, the Amir will hold discussions with President Droupadi Murmu, who will also host a banquet in his honour.

Sheikh Tamim Bin Hamad Al-Thani reached New Delhi yesterday on a two days official visit. Prime Minister Modi received him at the airport. The Amir was accompanied by a high-level delegation, including Ministers, senior officials and a business delegation.

Trade, investment, energy and technology collaboration will be the top agenda during the visit of the Amir of Qatar. India and Qatar share a strong economic partnership, with bilateral trade expanding across various sectors. Qatari firms have made significant investments in the country’s technology, infrastructure, and manufacturing industries, while Indian companies have established a strong presence in Qatar. As India consolidates its position as a global manufacturing and technology hub, several sectors are offering a promising scope for expansion of bilateral trade in the field of logistics, ports, airports, railways and highways, semiconductors, food security, tech and innovation, electric vehicles, and renewable energy. Bhupendra Singh, Akashvani News, Delhi.

Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar has been appointed as the new Chief Election Commissioner (CEC), as the tenure of incumbent poll body Chief Rajiv Kumar ends today. CEC Gyanesh Kumar is a former IAS officer from the 1988 batch of the Kerala cadre. He has served as secretary in the ministry of parliamentary affairs.

Dr.Vivek Joshi, has been appointed as new Election Commissioner. He is an IAS officer from the 1989 batch of the Haryana cadre.President Droupadi Murmu has appointed both commissioners.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah will review the status of the implementation of new criminal laws in Jammu and Kashmir in the presence of Chief Minister Omar Abdullah and Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha today. The meeting will focus on the current status of various provisions related to police, jails, courts, prosecution, and forensics outlined in the three new criminal laws: the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023; Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), 2023; and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 2023. These laws replaced the Indian Penal Code (IPC), the Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC), and the Indian Evidence Act, respectively. The meeting will also be attended by the Union Home Secretary, Jammu and Kashmir Chief Secretary, state Director General of Police, Director General of the National Crime Records Bureau, and several senior officials from both the Union Home Ministry and the Union Territory government.

The Department of Land Resources under the Ministry of Rural Development is launching a pilot programme called National Geospatial Knowledge-based Land Survey of Urban Settlements- NAKSHA in 152 urban local bodies across 26 states and 3 Union Territories from today. Union Agriculture and Farmers Welfare and Rural Development Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan will launch the programme at Raisen in Madhya Pradesh today.

Union Minister of State for Rural Development and Communications Dr. Chandrashekhar Pemmasani and Madhya Pradesh Chief Minister Dr. Mohan Yadav will also be present on the occasion.

“The aim of the Naksha programme is to create land records in urban areas to ensure accurate and reliable documentation of land ownership. Survey of India is the technical partner for the Naksha programme. This initiative will empower citizens, make life easier, enhance urban planning and reduce land-related disputes. The cost of the Naksha pilot programme is around Rs 194 crore which will be fully funded by the Government of India. Watershed Yatra will also be flagged off on this occasion. Sanjeev Sharma, Akashvani News, Bhopal.”

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology will release the Digital Brand Identity Manual (DBIM) in New Delhi today, as part of its efforts to bring uniformity across government websites and digital platforms. The DBIM defines key elements of a consistent digital identity, including visual identities. These identities are as logos, color palettes, typography, and imagery, as well as verbal identities like brand voice, messaging frameworks, and taglines. These guidelines aim to enhance citizen engagement and improve the overall user experience in service delivery.

The Ministry said that this initiative aligns with Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s vision to transform governance through technology, ensuring accessibility, efficiency, and a more citizen-friendly digital experience.

## Evening News

India and Qatar today agreed to elevate their bilateral relations to a Strategic Partnership. An agreement in this regard was signed between the two countries following delegation-level talks between Prime Minister Narendra Modi and the Amir of Qatar, Sheikh Tamim Bin Hamad Al-Thani, in New Delhi.

Briefing the media in the National Capital this evening, Secretary for Consular, Passport, Visa and Overseas Indian Affairs, in the External Affairs Ministry, Arun Kumar Chatterjee, said, the talks between Prime Minister Modi and the Amir of Qatar focussed on strengthening trade, energy, investments, innovation, technology, food security, culture, and people-to-people ties. During the talks, both leaders also exchanged views on regional and global issues of mutual interest.

Mr Chatterjee, said, two agreements and five MoUs were signed during the State visit of the Amir of Qatar. He said the first agreement is on the establishment of a bilateral strategic partnership. A revised double taxation avoidance agreement was also signed between the two countries. Mr. Chatterjee said both sides have also signed MoUs on economic partnership, cooperation in the area of archives and documentation, and youth affairs and sports.

The Secretary said, the two countries have also agreed to double trade between two countries to 28 billion dollars annually in the next five years.

Mr. Chatterjee said, both the leaders today identified a number of areas in which Qatar’s sovereign wealth fund Qatar Investment Authority can increase investments in India.

The Amir of Qatar was accorded a ceremonial welcome on the forecourts of Rashtrapati Bhawan this morning.

Later in the evening, Vice President Jagdeep Dhankhar called on the Amir of Qatar, in New Delhi. The discussions between the two leaders highlighted various aspects of bilateral relations, including energy, trade, investment, culture, and people-to-people ties.

US and Russia today decided to appoint teams to negotiate an end to the war in Ukraine during high-level talks held in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. The talks marked the first discussions between the two countries aimed at paving the way for a potential peace deal to end the Ukraine war. During the talks, the Russian side was represented by Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov and Chief Foreign Policy Adviser Yuri Ushakov while US delegation consisted of Secretary of State Marco Rubio, National Security Adviser Mike Waltz, and West Asia Envoy Steve Witkoff. However, Ukraine and European leaders were not part of the talks.

During the meeting, both countries agreed on four principles. Talking to media after the talks, US Secretary of State said, the two countries will appoint a high-level team to help negotiate and work toward the end of the conflict in Ukraine. He said, both sides also agreed to reestablish the functionality of their respective missions in Washington and Moscow. Mr. Rubio said the five people present in today’s meeting will remain engaged in this process to ensure it moves forward productively.

Speaking to media, Russian Foreign Minister said the talks went well and there was a serious conversation on all issues.
