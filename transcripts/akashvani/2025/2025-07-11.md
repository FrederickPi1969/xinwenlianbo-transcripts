## Morning News

The Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls in Bihar has achieved 66 per cent voter coverage so far, with the final deadline set for the 25th of this month. As of 6 PM yesterday, over 5.22 crore enumeration forms, that is 66.16 per cent of the total 7.89 crore electors in the state have been collected since the voter list revision drive began on June 24. The Election Commission of India has credited this progress to strong voter participation and the efforts of nearly 78,000 Booth Level Officers, along with over 4 lakh volunteers helping the elderly, differently-abled, and vulnerable citizens.

The Supreme Court yesterday permitted the Election Commission to proceed with its Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls in Bihar. However, the apex court also directed the Commission, in the interest of justice to consider accepting key identity documents such as Aadhaar, Ration Card, and Voter ID card during the ongoing revision process.

“While observing that revision of Electoral Rolls by the Election Commission in Bihar is within the mandate of the constitution, the bench of Justices Sudhanshu Dhulia and Joymalya Bagchi questioned the timing of the SIR. The top court asked, why the exercise is being connected to Bihar Assembly elections and cannot be conducted for the whole country irrespective of the elections. During the hearing, the petitioners questioned the exclusion of the Aadhaar Card from the list of documents. On the poll body’s reply that Aadhaar can not be used as proof of citizenship, the top court remarked that the electoral body should have acted earlier if it was to check citizenship under the exercise. The Apex Court listed the matter for further hearing on 28th of July before the regular Bench. It asked the Election Commission to file its counter-affidavit within a week and told the petitioners to file their rejoinder affidavit, if any, before the next hearing. SHAGUN CHOPRA, NEWS DESK.”

NASA has announced that the return journey of Indian astronaut Group Captain Shubhanshu Shukla and three other crew members of the Axiom-4 mission will begin on July 14. Speaking at a press conference, Steve Stitch, the Manager of NASA’s Commercial Crew Program, said that undocking from the International Space Station is scheduled for July 14, with a splashdown expected several hours later in the Pacific Ocean, off the coast of California.

Group Captain Shukla is currently on a 14-day mission aboard the International Space Station. His mother Asha Shukla has expressed pride and joy as he prepares to return after successfully completing his space mission.

Assam Cabinet, chaired by Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma, has approved several decisions aimed at addressing human-elephant conflicts, improving healthcare regulations, and enhancing welfare schemes for students and traditional workers.

These teams will operate during peak conflict months, particularly around paddy cultivation season, to ensure safe elephant movement and protect local livelihoods. The Assam cabinet has also approved the proposal to enhance the remuneration of village heads from Rs 9,000 per month to Rs 14,000 per month with effect from October 1 this year.
