## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will release the 20th instalment of the Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) scheme from his parliamentary constituency, Varanasi in Uttar Pradesh today. In this instalment, 20,500 crore rupees will be disbursed to over nine crore seventy lakh farmers.

On the occasion, the Prime Minister will also inaugurate and lay the foundation stone of multiple development projects worth around 2,200 crore rupees. The projects cater to multiple sectors, including infrastructure and urban development.

“Prime Minister Narendra Modi will transfer ₹20,500 crore directly to the bank accounts of over 9 crore 70 lakh farmers across country under the 20th instalment of the PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi scheme from Varanasi today. PM will also inaugurate key infrastructure projects, including the widening and strengthening of the Varanasi-Bhadohi road and Chhitauni-Shool Tankeshwar roads, and a Railway Overbridge at Hardattpur to ease traffic on the Mohan Sarai-Adalpura road. Strengthening the power infrastructure in the region, Prime Minister will lay the foundation stone of various works under the Smart Distribution Project and undergrounding of electrical infrastructure worth over 880 crore rupees. PM Modi will also inaugurate the redevelopment of 8 riverfront and lay the foundation stone for water purification and maintenance works at various kunds, along with the installation of four floating pujan platforms. Under the Jal Jeevan Mission, 47 rural drinking water schemes will also be inaugurated. In addition, Varanasi MP will also launch several new projects related to education, healthcare, tourism, and cultural heritage. Om Awasthi, Akashvani News, Varanasi.”

Railways Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw has informed that 73 major stations facing rush situations will have a Station Director empowered to take “on-the-spot decongesting decisions ” during the festive rush. Mr Vaishnaw stated this in a written reply in the Rajya Sabha yesterday. He said Railways will enhance crowd management with wider Foot Over Bridges and creation of Permanent Holding Areas outside the stations. He informed that the pilot project for Holding Areas is in progress at five stations. CCTV surveillance, walkie-talkies, announcement systems and war rooms are being set up at major stations to help decongest the heavy crowd situations. Mr Vaishnaw added that only confirmed ticket holders will be given direct access to platforms.

Union Minister of State for External Affairs Kirti Vardhan Singh has said that India’s political relations with countries of the Indian Ocean Region are strong, and trade and investment ties are being augmented. The Minister stated this in a written reply in the Lok Sabha yesterday. Mr Singh said, the Government closely monitors all developments in the Indian Ocean Region impinging on India’s security and economic interests and takes appropriate measures to safeguard India’s interests.

The External Affairs Ministry has said the sourcing of India’s defence requirements is determined solely by the country’s national security imperatives and strategic assessments. The Ministry’s spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal said this while briefing media in New Delhi last evening in reply to a query on defense procurements. On energy sourcing, the spokesperson asserted that New Delhi is guided by what is on offer in the markets, and by the prevailing global circumstances.

Responding to a query on India Russia ties, the spokesperson said bilateral relationships with various countries stand on their own merit and should not be seen from the prism of a third country. He stressed that India and Russia have a steady and time-tested partnership.

Meanwhile, the Embassy of India in Dublin has advised all Indian citizens in Ireland to take reasonable precautions for their personal security and avoid deserted areas, especially at odd hours. In an advisory issued yesterday, the Embassy said there has been an increase in instances of physical attacks reported against Indian citizens in Ireland recently. The Embassy is in touch with the authorities concerned in Ireland in this regard.

India’s digital payment backbone, Unified Payments Interface (UPI), posted a fresh record in July 2025 with 1,947 crore transactions amounting to a staggering 25.1 lakh crore rupees. The figures mark a robust 35 per cent year-on-year jump in transaction volumes and a 22 per cent increase in value, underscoring the continued dominance of UPI in the digital payments ecosystem.

On a daily basis, average UPI transaction count rose to 62.8 crore, up from 61.3 crore in June. The average daily transaction value also grew, reaching 80,919 crore rupees in July from 80,131 crore rupees the previous month, indicating sustained momentum in consumer and merchant adoption.

India has received 16 Airbus C-295 military transport aircraft, marking a significant upgrade in its defence capabilities. The handover took place today in Seville, Spain, with Indian Ambassador Dinesh K Patnaik and senior Indian Air Force officials in attendance. The delivery came two months ahead of schedule.

On October 28 last year, a milestone in India’s “Atmanirbhar” mission was achieved. Prime Minister Narendra Modi, along with Spain’s Pedro Sanchez, inaugurated the C295 final assembly line in Vadodara, Gujarat. This Tata-Airbus factory will produce the C-295 aircraft for the Indian Air Force. The deal, worth 21,935 crore rupees, will see 56 C-295s replace the aging Avro fleet.

16 aircraft have been received from Spain while 40 will be assembled right here in India. The first locally assembled aircraft will be ready by 2026, with all 40 set to be delivered by 2031.

This is Airbus’ first-ever full-scale aircraft production outside its home country. The C-295, a versatile transport aircraft, is crucial for tactical operations, cargo, and medical evacuation.

India will soon be the largest operator of the C-295, enhancing its military capabilities. This is a proud moment for India and a step closer to self-reliance in defense. Desk report Akashvani,Nabamita Adhikari.
