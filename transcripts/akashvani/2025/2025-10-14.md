## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will hold talks with President of Mongolia Khurelsukh Ukhnaa in New Delhi today. The two leaders will review the entire gamut of bilateral relations and exchange views on regional and global issues of mutual interest. Several MoUs are expected to be signed following the talks.

The Mongolian President will meet President Droupadi Murmu at Rashtrapati Bhavan this evening. President Murmu will host a banquet in honour of the visiting dignitary.

President Khurelsukh arrived in New Delhi yesterday on a four-day visit to India. He was received at the airport by Union Minister of State for Civil Aviation, Murlidhar Mohol.

The President is accompanied by a high-level delegation comprising Cabinet Ministers, Members of Parliament, senior officials, business leaders, and cultural representatives. This is President Khurelsukh’s first visit to India as President.

Diplomatic relations between India and Mongolia were established in 1955. Over the past seven decades, the two countries have developed a close and multifaceted partnership, anchored in shared cultural and spiritual links and democratic values. The partnership spans across sectors such as defence and security, parliamentary exchanges, development partnership, energy, mining, information technology, education, healthcare, and cultural cooperation. India and Mongolia are Strategic Partners, Spiritual Neighbours, and Third Neighbours. The State Visit of Mongolian President will provide an opportunity for the leadership of both nations to review progress in bilateral relations, set the vision for strengthening the strategic partnership, and exchange views on regional and global issues of mutual interest. ANUPAM MISHRA, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.

The GST Bachat Utsav, announced by Prime Minister Narendra Modi and commencing on the 22nd of last month, is benefiting the country as a whole. Today, our correspondent brings a report on the GST reforms that are set to empower various industries in Mizoram.

The new GST reform is a major boost for Mizoram’s artisans, farmers, and tourism industry. With the reduced GST rates, the traditional and regional products are now more affordable, boosting local production. The GST on packaged and processed Mizo chillies, ginger, turmeric, bamboo, cane products, and fruits has been brought down to 5 percent from the earlier 18 percent. The reduced GST has made the local goods cheaper and also supported employment across rural and semi-urban areas. Along with this, the tourism sector in the state is a vital source of employment and economic development. The GST reduction to 5 percent on rooms priced up to 7 thousand 500 rupees has made travel more affordable, boosting occupancy and supporting local businesses. This reform is set to enhance opportunities for entrepreneurship and youth employment across the state’s hospitality sector.

The reforms are designed to boost sales, protect livelihoods, and strengthen Mizoram’s tourism sector, paving the way toward inclusive and sustainable growth. With Ishani and Arzoo, Saumya Sharan, Akashvani News, Delhi.
