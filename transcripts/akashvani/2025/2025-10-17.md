## Morning News

President Droupadi Murmu will attend the National Conclave on the Adi Karmayogi Abhiyan in New Delhi today. The Ministry of Tribal Affairs is organising the conclave under the vision of Viksit Bharat, as enunciated by Prime Minister Narendra Modi. The conclave is being held in conjunction with PMJANMAN and Dharti Aba Abhiyan. Union Minister for Tribal Affairs Jual Oram, Ministers of State for Tribal Affairs, Union and State Secretaries, District Magistrates, Master Trainers, and Adi Sahyogi representatives from across the nation will also attend the event.

The conclave will focus on Tribal Village Vision 2030 From Ideas to Implementation. A key highlight of the event will be conceptualizing Tribal Village Vision 2030, aimed at finalizing policy inputs, implementation frameworks, and project execution strategies emerging from nationwide consultations with tribal communities based on over 50 thousand Village Action Plans. During the conclave, five thematic groups will deliberate on priority sectors including education and skill development, health and nutrition, livelihood and entrepreneurship, infrastructure and governance and institutional strengthening. With Pallavi Parmar, Bhanu Pratap Singh for Akashvani News, Delhi.

Defence minister Rajnath Singh will unveil the first Tejas LCA MK1A fighter jet from Hindustan Aeronautics Limited’s new Nashik production line today. During the event, Defence Minister will also inaugurate a second production line for the HTT-40 trainer aircraft at the same facility. The production will enhance the overall strength and capability of the IAF. Rajnath Singh will witness the first flight of these fighter jets today. The LCA Mk1A is a more advanced, multi-role fighter jet designed to replace Indian Air Force’s MiG-21s.  The aircraft is a 4.5-generation multi-role fighter jet which is capable of air defence, ground attack, and maritime strike missions.  The Nashik line is the third production facility. The facility was established with an investment of more than 150 crore rupees, which will add eight more jets on a yearly basis, enhancing HAL’s production capacity to 24 aircraft annually.

Union Minister for Agriculture and Farmers’ Welfare Shivraj Singh Chouhan held a high-level meeting to deliberate on strategies for making Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs) across the country more effective and empowered. During the meeting held in New Delhi, Mr Chouhan highlighted that there are currently 731 KVKs in the country and while expanding their network, it is essential to strengthen them for the benefit of small farmers. He emphasised that KVKs serve as the primary medium to reach farmers, playing a crucial role in technology dissemination, training, and delivering agricultural innovations. The Union Minister instructed  officials of Indian Council of Agricultural Research to ensure that KVKs are provided with adequate financial, human resource, and technical support to streamline their functioning and enhance their efficiency.

GST Bachat Utsav, which was announced by Prime Minister Narendra Modi, and commenced on 22nd of last month, is benefiting all sections of society. Today, our correspondent brings a report on the GST reforms that will boost the economy of Kerala.

The new GST reform is a major boost for Kerala’s farmers, small businesses, and industries. The Kerala’s spice industry will benefit from the reduction of GST on Green Cardamom and Betel Leaf from 18 per cent to 5 percent. This move is expected to reduce spice prices by about 11 percent, making local products more affordable for consumers and boosting the state’s global competitiveness.  Similarly, the GST on jams, pulp, and pickles has been reduced from 12 per cent to 5 per cent, which will benefit Kuttiattoor Mango growers and processors in Kannur.

Meanwhile, Hotels and homestays up to 7,500 rupees per night will now attract 5 per cent GST. The move will make stays more affordable and supports the growth of Kerala’s tourism industry.

With the revised rates, the new GST reforms has strengthen Kerala’s agriculture, food processing, and tourism sectors with lowering costs, improving incomes, and expanding market opportunities for smallholders and MSMEs. With Ishani Yadav, Shivang Misra , Akashvani News, Delhi.

The US Chamber of Commerce has filed a lawsuit challenging President Donald Trump’s one lakh dollar fee on new H-1B visas for highly skilled foreign workers. The Chamber stated that the fee will force businesses that rely on the H-1B programme to choose between dramatically increasing their labor costs or hiring fewer highly-skilled workers. Employers who sponsor H-1B workers currently typically pay between 2,000 and 5,000 dollars in fees, depending on the size of the company and other factors. The rise in H-1B fee is also being challenged by unions, employers, and religious groups in a California federal court. The measure has raised serious concerns regarding its implications on Indian tech professionals and the flow of remittances, with nearly 71 per cent of all H-1B visas being granted to Indian citizens.

GST Bachat Utsav, which was announced by Prime Minister Narendra Modi, and commenced on 22nd of last month, is benefiting all sections of society. Today, our correspondent brings a report on the GST reforms that will boost the economy of Assam.

The new GST reform is a big relief for Assam’s weavers, farmers, and small businesses. Under the new tax structure , GST has been reduced on traditional and regional goods of the state. The reform especially benefits Assam’s tea industry, which now attracts only 5 per cent GST, making its products cheaper and more competitive in both domestic and international markets. Meanwhile, the GST on culturally significant item including, Muga Silk, Gamosa has been reduced from 12 percent to 5 percent. This will bring relief to weavers, who can now sell in competitive markets and earn better margins. The tourism sector has gained from this reform, as hotel rooms priced up to seven thousand five hundred rupees per night will now be under the 5 percent GST slab, making Assam a more affordable destination for visitors. The reforms are designed to drive sales, safeguard employment, and strengthen rural economies during the festive period and in the long run. With Shivang Misra,  Saumya Sharan for Akashvani News, Delhi.

The Supreme Court has taken a serious view on the digital arrest scams seeking response from the centre and the Central Bureau of Investigation. The apex court took suo motu cognisance of rising cases of digital arrest scams, where fraudsters impersonate law enforcement agencies or judicial authorities to extort money from citizens, particularly senior citizens.

The action was taken on a complaint received from a 73-year-old woman from Ambala in Haryana, who alleged that scammers used forged Supreme Court orders to confine her in a so-called digital arrest and extort more than one crore rupees.

More than 200 naxalites surrendered today in Jagdalpur, the headquarters of Chhattisgarh’s Bastar division.These Naxalites surrendered 153 weapons, including 19 AK-47 rifles, and 17 SLR rifles. According to the officials, the surrendered group represented various ranks of the outlawed CPI (Maoist) organisation. Talking to the media, Chhattisgarh Chief Minister Vishnu Deo Sai said the government is taking several welfare measures for their rehabilitation and skill development. More details from our Raipur correspondent:

This is the largest surrender by Naxalites in Chhattisgarh’s history. 210 Maoists active in various areas of the state’s Bastar division, including Abujhmad and Kanker, surrendered in Jagdalpur today. These include several top leaders of Maoist organisation. These Naxalites surrendered today at a ceremony in Jagdalpur by handing over their weapons to senior police and Central Armed Police Force officers. Senior police officials presented a copy of the Indian Constitution to these Naxalites who had renounced the path of violence. On this occasion, leaders of various tribal communities in Bastar also welcomed these Naxalites for returning to mainstream of society. Over 2,000 Naxalites have surrendered in Chhattisgarh in the past 22 months. Today has proved to be a very important day in fulfilling the Central Government’s resolve to make the country Naxal-free by March 2026. Vikalp Shukla, Akashvani News, Raipur.
