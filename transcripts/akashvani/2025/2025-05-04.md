## Midday News

President of Angola Joao Manuel Goncalves Lourenco has said India and Angola are committed to further advancing bilateral ties. Speaking at the India Angola Business Forum in New Delhi today, he said India is a key partner which has expertise in generic medicines.

Addressing the gathering, Union Minister of State for External Affairs Kirti Vardhan Singh said that India Angola bilateral ties are underpinned by a vibrant energy partnership.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today met Chief of Air Staff, Air Chief Marshal AP Singh at his residence in New Delhi. The meeting came amid rising tensions between India and Pakistan following the Pahalgam terror attack.

In a significant development, following the recent Pahalgam terror attack, the Punjab Police have arrested two individuals in Amritsar for allegedly spying for Pakistan. The accused, identified as Palak Sher Masih and Suraj Masih, were allegedly involved in leaking sensitive information and photographs of military installations, including Army cantonments and air bases in Amritsar. According to senior police sources, preliminary investigations point to their links with Pakistani intelligence operatives. Authorities believe the arrests could lead to crucial revelations as the probe continues.

The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) successfully carried out maiden flight-trials of its Stratospheric Airship Platform from Sheopur Trial site in Madhya Pradesh yesterday. Developed by Aerial Delivery Research and Development Establishment at Agra in Uttar Pradesh, the airship was launched carrying an instrumental payload to an altitude of around 17 kilometers.

Raksha Mantri Rajnath Singh has congratulated the DRDO for the successful maiden flight-trial of the system. He stated that this system will uniquely enhance India’s earth observation and Intelligence, Surveillance and Reconnaissance capabilities. The Minister added that this airship will also make the country one of the few countries in the world having such indigenous capabilities.

The United States has approved the sale of HawkEye 360 technology to India to boost the country’s surveillance capabilities. The move will enhance India’s maritime domain awareness in the Indo-Pacific region. In a statement, the US Defence Security Cooperation Agency said the 131 million dollar deal will improve India’s capabilities to meet the current and future threats by bolstering its maritime domain awareness, analytical abilities and strategic posture.

External Affairs Minister Dr S Jaishankar today stated that the Arctic region has a global significance in itself and India has a growing involvement with the Arctic. The Minister was speaking at Arctic Circle India Forum 2025 in New Delhi.

Replying to a question on Europe, Dr Jaishankar stressed that India is looking for partners, and not preachers who do not practice at home what they preach abroad.

Union Minister of State for Home Affairs Bandi Sanjay Kumar today ruled out any possibility of holding talks with Maoists, stating that dialogue cannot happen with those who wield guns and kill innocent people. Speaking in Telangana’s Karimnagar district, the Minister stressed that talks can only be considered if the Maoists lay down their arms.

The Election Commission will soon launch a single-point mobile Application, ECINET to provide a unified digital platform to stakeholders for all electoral-related activities. The Commission said in a statement, the new one-stop platform, ECINET, will integrate and reorient over 40 of existing mobile and web applications of the poll body. The new user-friendly digital interface is being developed for the electors and its other stakeholders such as the election officials, political parties and the civil society.

ECINET will have an aesthetic User Interface and a simplified User Experience. The move is also designed to alleviate the burden on the users for downloading and navigating multiple Apps and remembering different logins.

The National Testing Agency (NTA) is conducting the National Eligibility-cum-Entrance Test- NEET (UG) 2025 today across over five thousand centres in the country and 13 cities abroad. Over 22.7 lakh candidates have registered for the examination. The NTA in a statement said that all preparations and logistical arrangements for organising a safe and secure examination have been made.

The sacred portals of Badrinath Dham opened with traditional rituals in Chamoli district of Uttarakhand this morning. The event was highlighted by the harmonious sounds of a military band, as the temple complex echoed with the chanting of “Jai Badri Vishal.”

Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan today launched India’s first two Genome-edited Rice varieties by ICAR in New Delhi. Addressing the event, the Minister said, its a historic day for India. He said these two varieties will reduce the cost of production and increase the yield of rice in the country. Mr. Chouhan congratulated agriculture scientists who were involved in this project and said that the country has faith in the capability of the country’s agricultural scientists. Our correspondent reports that these varieties have been developed for the states of Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, Tamilnadu, Chhattisgarh, Maharashtra, West Bengal and Madhya Pradesh. A report –

“The first rice variety named DRR Dhan 100 Kamala has been derived from Samba Mahsuri which matures 15 to 20 days earlier and yields 25 percent more production. The second variety, named Pusa DST Rice 1, is salinity and alkalinity tolerant and yield 30 percent higher production under saline conditions.  Speaking at the occasion the Agriculture Minister termed the launch of these varieties as historic and said that these two varieties will reduce the cost of production and increase the yield.

ICAR said that these varieties mark a major step in climate-resilient and high-yield agriculture. Akshit Vedyan Akashvani News Delhi.”

The Baloch Liberation Army (BLA) launched a major assault in the Mongochar area of Kalat district, Balochistan, blocking the Quetta-Karachi National Highway (N-25). BLA set multiple government buildings on fire. Balochistan is a province in the Southwestern region of Pakistan.

The attack, claimed by the BLA’s Fateh Squad, began with armed militants halting traffic and searching buses and private vehicles. Militants then seized and set ablaze several key government offices. The BLA described the raid as part of its ongoing “struggle for liberation.” Security forces later moved in, restoring traffic and launching a counter-operation.

In a related incident, BLA militants intercepted a police van transporting prisoners from Gadani jail to Quetta. At least 10 prisoners were freed, and five policemen were taken hostage.
