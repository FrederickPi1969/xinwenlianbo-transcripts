## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will visit Uttar Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh today. He will lay the foundation stone and inaugurate various development projects worth over three thousand 880 crore rupees in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh.These projects encompass developments in road infrastructure, electricity, education, and tourism, aiming to enhance the region’s overall growth. Mr Modi will also address a public meeting at Mehdiganj in Varanasi. He will be welcomed by Governor Anandiben Patel and Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath.

“Prime Minister Narendra Modi will inaugurate 19 completed projects worth Rs 1,629 crore and lay the foundation for 25 new projects worth Rs 2,255 crore during his visit to Varanasi today. With the foundation laying by the PM, work on ambitious projects such as the construction of an underpass road tunnel on the Varanasi-Lucknow highway (NH-31), construction of the MSME Unity Mall, a road bridge between Varanasi Ring Road and Sarnath, and flyovers at Bhikharipur and Manduadih crossings will begin. The list of projects to be inaugurated by the Prime Minister includes 132 rural drinking water schemes under the Jal Jeevan Mission by UP Jal Nigam Rural worth rupees more than Rs 345 crore and several other projects. Om Awasthi, Akashvani News, Lucknow.”

Later in the day, the Prime Minister will arrive at Anandpur Dham of Isagarh Tehsil in Ashoknagar district of Madhya Pradesh.

“In line with his commitment to furthering the cultural and spiritual heritage of India, Prime Minister Narendra Modi will visit Anandpur Dham today. Anandpur Dham has been established for spiritual and philanthropic purposes. Spanning 315 hectares, it houses a modern gaushala with over 500 cows. The trust also runs agricultural activities under Shri Anandpur Trust campus. The trust has been operating a charitable hospital in Sukhpur village, schools in Sukhpur and Anandpur and various Satsang Centers across the country.More than 12 hundred students are studying in these schools. Sanjeev Sharma, Akashvani News, Bhopal.”

The National Investigation Agency (NIA) formally arrested Tahawwur Hussain Rana, the key conspirator in the deadly 26/11 Mumbai terror attacks, immediately after his arrival in New Delhi yesterday evening, following his successful extradition from the United States. Rana, a Canadian national of Pakistani origin, was taken for medical examination and was produced before special NIA Judge in Patiala House Court last night. The court sent him to 18-day custody of the National Investigation Agency.

Meanwhile, the US State Department has reiterated its commitment to combating global terrorism in collaboration with India after the US extradited Tahawwur Rana to India. During a press briefing, US State Department spokesperson Tammy Bruce stated that the US has consistently supported India’s efforts to hold those responsible for the attacks accountable.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah has said that the Modi government is mowing down drug cartels with full might. In a social media post, lauding the efforts of agencies, Mr. Shah said that in vision to build a drug-free Bharat, country’s agencies have launched massive operations strangling drug cartels and seized 30.4 kilograms of methamphetamine tablets worth 24.32 crore rupees, while arresting three people in Assam. He added that government’s offensives against drugs will continue with ruthless force.

US President Donald Trump has said that he will impose reciprocal tariffs again on countries that fail to reach an agreement with America by July 9, when the 90-day pause ends. As many as 80 human lives perished in Bihar and Uttar Pradesh in rain related incidents, high speed winds and lightning.

In Bihar, rain related incidents claimed 58 lives during the past 24 hours. The highest number of casualties were reported from Nalanda district, where 22 people lost their lives due to uprooting of trees and collapse of walls, while, 35 people died in such incidents in other parts of the state.

“In Bihar, standing crops of rabi, Masoor, Maize, onion, and several horticultural crops like mango and litchi have been damaged due to heavy rains and gusty winds in several parts of the state. Gusty winds with a speed of 50 to 60 kilometers per hour damaged several thatched houses. In Buxar and Bhojpur pontoon bridges were damaged, large trees were uprooted and electricity supply was affected due to storms and rains. The operation of trains remain affected in Jamui, Arrah and Danapur due to felling of trees on tracks. Dharmendra Kumar Rai, Akashvani News, Patna.”

At least 22 people, including five women, lost their lives in rain-related incidents in Uttar Pradesh yesterday. According to the Relief Commissioner’s office, 13 died after being hit by lightning while the rest perished due to injuries after walls or ceilings collapsing on them due to heavy winds.

Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath announced four lakh rupees ex gratia to the family of the deceased.

The annual fishing ban will come into force for a 61-day period in Puducherry, Karaikal and Yanam regions from 15th of this month and in Mahe from 1st June. The seasonal ban is aimed at allowing fish to breed and replenish, ensuring long-term sustainability of the marine ecosystem. During this time, all mechanized fishing boats, including trawlers and motorized fiber boats, are strictly prohibited from operating. Only traditional boats like kattumarams and country boats will be allowed.

Another Hurriyat-affiliated organization, the Jammu and Kashmir Mass Movement, has rejected separatism, declaring its complete commitment to the unity of India. So far, as many as 12 Hurriyat-linked organizations have broken away from secessionism, placing their trust in the Constitution. The Defence Research and Development Organisation has successfully conducted the release trials of Long-Range Glide Bomb Gaurav from the Su-30 MKI aircraft. According to the Defence Ministry, the trials successfully demonstrated a range close to 100 kilometres with accuracy.

Gaurav is a one thousand kilogram class glide bomb, designed and developed indigenously by Research Centre Imarat, Armament Research and Development Establishment and Integrated Test Range situated at Chandipur. Senior officials of DRDO and the Indian Air Force participated and reviewed these trials.

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh complimented DRDO, IAF and industry for successful development trials of Gaurav. He said, the development of the Long Range Glide Bomb will further enhance the capabilities of the Armed Forces to a great extent.

Union Health Minister JP Nadda today launched the Ayushman Bharat Yojana in Odisha. Launching the scheme at Bali Yatra Ground in Cuttack, he said as many as 61 crore people in the country are availing of the scheme’s benefits. Mr. Nadda said the Centre has spent 1.26 lakh crore rupees over the last six years on the treatment of poor people. The Ayushman Bharat Yojana will be implemented alongside the Odisha government’s Gopabandhu Jan Arogya Yojana in the state.

“The Odisha government had inked an MoU with the Centre for the integration of Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana with the Gopandhu Jan Arogya Yojana of Odisha. With this integration, around 3.52 crore people of 1.08 crore families including a significant percentage of tribal people in the state will be covered under the combined scheme, Ayushman Bharat-Gopabandhu Jan Arogya Yojana. The combined scheme will provide health cover of 5 lakh rupees per family per annum with an additional 5 lakh rupees for women members. In addition to this, senior citizens aged above 70 will get free treatment under the Ayushman Vaya Vandana Yojana. PRAKASH DASH, AKASHVANI NEWS, BHUBANESWAR.”
