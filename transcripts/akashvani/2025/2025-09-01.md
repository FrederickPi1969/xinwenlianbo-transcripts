Prime Minister Narendra Modi will address the plenary session of Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) Summit in Tianjin, China today. Foreign Secretary Vikram Misri told media persons in Tianjin that during his address, Mr Modi will outline India’s approach to fostering regional cooperation under the SCO umbrella. After his address to the plenary session of the Summit, Prime Minister Modi will hold bilateral meeting with Russian President Vladimir Putin.

Earlier yesterday, PM Modi and Chinese President Xi Jinping held wide-ranging talks with a focus on rebuilding the bilateral ties between India and China. Mr Misri said, in the meeting Mr Modi underlined the importance of peace and tranquility on the border areas for continued development of bilateral relations.

The Foreign Secretary said, Prime Minister Modi conveyed it very clearly to the Chinese President that peace and tranquility at the border is like an insurance policy for India-China ties. Mr Misri said that there was an understanding on the need to maintain peace on the borders using the existing mechanisms and avoid disturbances to the overall relationship going forward. Cross-border terrorism was mentioned by the prime minister as a priority and Mr Modi underlined the fact that this is something that impacts both India and China.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah arrived in  Jammu last night to review the ongoing relief and rescue operations and assess the damage caused by recent cloudbursts and floods, particularly in Katra, where landslides along the Vaishno Devi pilgrimage route have caused several deaths. Mr Shah will also chair a high level review meeting. A report:

During his day long visit, the Home Minister is scheduled to conduct an aerial survey of flood-affected areas in Jammu and hold meetings with officials at Raj Bhawan where he will be briefed by the Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha, senior officials from the Ministry of Home Affairs and the UT administration about the rescue, relief, and restoration works so far. The meeting will cover the damages, ongoing relief and rescue operations, and the restoration of infrastructure across Jammu. He will also meet the affected families. Following the meeting, The Home Minister will visit various areas, including Katra, the base camp for Shri Mata Vaishno Devi and is likely to visit Narayana Hospital in Kakrayal, Katra to meet victims injured in the recent landslide incident at Mata Vaishno Devi pilgrimage route. The Home Minister will also visit Kishtwar to assess the damage caused by the recent flash floods and cloudbursts. This is N. Gulshan Raina For Akashvani News From Jammu.

In a statement, the Ministry said that on the directions of Home Minister Amit Shah, these teams will conduct on-the-spot assessments of the situation and review the relief work carried out by the respective State Governments. The Central Teams will visit the flood and landslide-affected districts of Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Punjab, and Jammu and Kashmir.

## Midday News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today stated that double standards on terrorism are unacceptable. Delivering his statement at the SCO Summit in Tianjin, China, Mr Modi termed terrorism as a huge challenge for humanity, saying terrorism in all its forms should be condemned. Mr Modi said India has been dealing with terrorism for the last four decades. He expressed gratitude to the countries that showed solidarity with India after the Pahalgam terror attack.

The Prime Minister highlighted that India has played a very positive role as a member of the SCO. He added that India’s vision and policy for the SCO is based on three important pillars – Security, Connectivity and Opportunity.

Prime Minister Modi also exchanged perspectives with Russian President Vladimir Putin and Chinese President Xi Jinping during the SCO Summit.

The Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) declaration strongly condemned the terrorist attack in Pahalgam and sent a clear and unified message against terrorism. The declaration released after the Heads of States Council summit also recognized India’s growing contributions to regional cooperation and innovation. The ‘Tianjin Declaration’ marks a significant step in reinforcing collective efforts against terrorism and reflects the growing importance of India’s role in shaping regional cooperation under the SCO umbrella. Reaffirming their unwavering commitment to combating terrorism, separatism, and extremism, the SCO nations rejected the use of such groups for mercenary purposes, and highlighted the sovereign right and responsibility of states to counter extremist threats. More from our correspondent:

“In a strong and unified message from Tianjin, the Member States have  condemned the terrorist attack in Pahalgam on the 22nd of April, 2025. They expressed deep condolences to the families of the victims and affirmed that the perpetrators, organizers, and sponsors of such heinous acts must be brought to justice. Reaffirming their commitment to combat terrorism, separatism, and extremism, the Member States emphasized the inadmissibility of using such groups for mercenary purposes. The declaration condemned terrorism in all its forms, rejected double standards in the fight against terror, and called for global cooperation to curb the cross-border movement of terrorists. The Tianjin Declaration also recognized and resonated with India’s global vision of ‘One Earth, One Family, One Future. Ritesh Kapoor Akashvani News Tianjin.”

Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Russian President Vladimir Putin today discussed bilateral cooperation, including in the economic, financial, and energy sectors and expressed satisfaction with the sustained growth in bilateral ties in these areas. Both leaders held bilateral talks at Tianjin in China on the sidelines of the SCO Summit.  ​The two leaders also discussed regional and global issues, including the latest developments concerning Ukraine. The two leaders reaffirmed their support for further strengthening of the Special and Privileged Strategic Partnership between the two countries. The Prime Minister conveyed to President Putin that he was looking forward to receiving him in India for the 23rd Annual Summit later this year. In his opening remarks, Mr Modi said India and Russia have always stood shoulder to shoulder even during the toughest of times. He said the close cooperation between the two nations is also important for world peace, stability and prosperity.

Saying that New Delhi and Moscow have been continuously discussing the ongoing conflict in Ukraine, the Prime Minister welcomed all the recent efforts for peace. He exuded hope that all parties will move forward constructively.

Meanwhile, the Prime Minister has since emplaned for Delhi after concluding his visit to China.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today called upon Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) member countries to take decisive action against terrorism in all its manifestations. Addressing the SCO Summit at Tianjin in China, Mr Modi emphasized that there should be no double standards in dealing with terrorism and urged the group to hold countries who perpetrate and support cross-border terrorism accountable.

The Prime Minister highlighted India’s approach to strengthening cooperation under the SCO framework. In this regard, Mr Modi noted that India seeks greater action under three pillars – Security, Connectivity and Opportunity. Highlighting the role of connectivity in fostering development and building trust, Prime Minister stated that India strongly supported projects such as Chabahar port and International North-South Transport Corridor.

Prime Minister Modi also exchanged perspectives with Russian President Vladimir Putin and Chinese President Xi Jinping during the SCO Summit.

Meanwhile, in a major diplomatic win for India, in the the Tianjin Declaration which was adopted after the conclusion of the SCO summit, the member states unequivocally condemned the Pahalgam terrorist attack took place on 22nd April this year and called for justice against those responsible.
