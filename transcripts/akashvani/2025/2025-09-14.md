## Midday News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today inaugurated and laid the foundation stone of major infrastructure and industrial development projects worth over 6,300 crore rupees in Assam. Addressing people at Mangaldoi in Darrang district on this occasion. The Prime Minister said India is the fastest growing country in the world and Assam has become one of the fastest growing states in the country. Mr Modi said the country is moving ahead unitedly to build a developed India and North East has a very big role in the achievement of this resolution.

He said 25 years of the 21st century have passed and now this next part of the 21st century belongs to the global East and North East of the country.

The Prime Minister said his government has been focusing a lot on connectivity in the North East as fast connectivity is very important for the rapid development of any region.

Mr Modi also urged people to be brand ambassadors of Vocal for Local and to take pride in Swadeshi products.

Admiring Bhupen Hazarika Mr Modi said that Bhupen Da gave voice to India’s unity. He said Bharat Ratna for Bhupen Da reflects his government’s commitment to the North East.

In Darrang, Prime Minister Modi was felicitated by Union Minister Sarbananda Sonowal and Assam CM Himanta Biswa Sarma. The development projects include Darrang Medical College & Hospital and GNM School and B.Sc. Nursing College, strengthening medical education and healthcare delivery in the region.

Later today the Prime Minister will also inaugurate and lay the foundation stone of infrastructure and industrial development projects worth over 9,230 crore rupees in Assam.

Mr. Modi will inaugurate Assam Bio-ethanol Private Limited at Numaligarh Refinery Plant at Golaghat aimed at promoting clean energy and reducing dependence on fossil fuels. He will also lay the foundation stone of the Polypropylene Plant at Numaligarh Refinery Limited adding significant value to Assam’s petrochemical sector.

He said the Central Government has introduced initiatives like the Sarathi Platform and Hindi Shabd Sindhu Kosh to make Hindi more accessible. He underlined that Hindi should not remain only a language of conversation, but should also become the language of science, technology, justice, and policing.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi also extended his greetings to the people of the country on the occasion of Hindi Diwas today. In a social media post, Mr Modi said that Hindi is not merely a medium of communication, but a vibrant heritage of the country’s identity and values. He also highlighted that the growing respect for Hindi language on the global stage is a matter of pride and inspiration for the nation.

On this Independence Day, from the ramparts of the Red Fort, Prime Minister Narendra Modi had announced the next generation of GST reforms. His vision was realized on the 3rd of this month, when the GST Council reduced tax rates in many sectors to ease the lives of citizens. Today, we take a look at the reforms for the health sector.

Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman on 3rd of this month announced the decisions of the 56th GST council meeting. A significant highlight of the announcement was the tax cut on health insurance premiums. Now all health insurance premiums, which till recently attracted 18 percent GST, will be fully exempted from any tax from 22nd of this month. Now the insured will pay an amount which would simply be the base premium. The reform will particularly be beneficial for seniors and retirees, who often hesitate to take health insurance due to high premiums. The change is also expected to increase insurance penetration across the country, providing financial security for families and individuals. Talking to media, Hemant Rai Singhani, a resident from Bengaluru said that the simplification of tax slabs under the recently announced GST reforms will benefit the ordinary citizens.

The tax cut is expected to make premiums cheaper for middle-class families and encourages wider adoption of health insurance, reducing out-of-pocket medical expenses. With Rishu Jaiswal, Samarth Singh for Akashvani News, Delhi.
