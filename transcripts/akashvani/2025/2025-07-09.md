## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi held talks with Brazilian President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva in Brasilia yesterday. Speaking at a joint press conference alongside the Brazilian President, Mr Modi said that India and Brazil have agreed to raise bilateral trade to 20 billion US Dollars over the next five years. Mr Modi said, this target is not difficult to achieve if the two countries work together like one team.

The Prime Minister said, we want India-Brazil relations to be as colourful as carnival, as passionate as football. He said that both sides will also work to expand the India-MERCOSUR Preferential Trade Agreement. Mr Modi said, India and Brazil will work to expand cooperation in trade, clean energy, defence, Artificial Intelligence, and digital public infrastructure.

PM Modi said, our thinking on the fight against terrorism is aligned – zero tolerance and zero double standards. He said, our clear stance is that there is no place for double standards on terrorism.

Brazil conferred its highest honour ‘The Grand Collar of the National Order of the Southern Cross’ on Prime Minister Modi. He expressed his gratitude for being conferred with Brazil’s highest national honour.

Secretary (East) in Ministry of External Affairs, P Kumaran has said that during the talks between Prime Minister Narendra Modi and President Lula da Silva, both leaders acknowledged the importance of the agriculture sector and its significant contribution to the economies on both sides. Briefing media in Brasilia, Mr Kumaran said, the Prime Minister proposed enhanced collaboration in sharing knowledge, best practices, and technology transfer in various areas.

The Secretary said, three agreements were signed and exchanged in front of the President of Brazil and Prime Minister Narendra Modi.  Mr Kumaran said, these were the agreements on cooperation in combating international terrorism and transnational organised crime, an MoU on cooperation for the sharing of successful large-scale digital solutions for digital transformation and MoU on cooperation in renewable energy.

In the final leg of his five-nation visit, Prime Minister Narendra Modi will reach Windhoek in Namibia today at the invitation of Namibian President Netumbo Nandi-Ndaitwah. This will be the first visit of PM Modi to the country, and the third ever Prime Ministerial visit from India to Namibia, which is coming after nearly three decades. On arrival, The Prime Minister will be accorded a ceremonial welcome, which will be followed by the delegation-level talks between the two countries to deepen the economic partnership.

Several MoUs on expanding cooperation in various critical areas such as energy security, health and pharma are expected to be exchanged between the two countries. During the visit, Prime Minister, Narendra Modi, will be conferred the highest civilian award of Namibia. The PM will also pay homage to the Founding Father and the first President of Namibia, Late Dr. Sam Nujoma. Later in the day Mr. Modi is scheduled to deliver an address at the Parliament of Namibia.

For more updates, we will go live to our correspondent covering the Prime Minister visit

The Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM) has amended its earlier directive to enforce a phased ban on refuelling End-of-Life vehicles. In a statement, The CAQM said that All End of Life vehicles will be denied fueling from 1st November, 2025 in Delhi, Gurugram, Faridabad, Ghaziabad, Gautam Budh Nagar, and Sonipat. The ban will extend to the remaining NCR districts from 1st April, 2026. The new implementation timeline for Delhi and five high-density NCR districts came after the Delhi government raised some operational concerns and requested the authority to review it.

Delhi Cabinet has approved over 900 crore rupees for Smart Classrooms in Delhi Government’s schools. It has also approved implementation of National Education Policy (NEP)-2020 in the national capital. The decisions were taken yesterday in the 10th Cabinet Meeting chaired by Delhi Chief Minister Rekha Gupta.

Briefing media in New Delhi, Education Minister Ashish Sood unveiled a comprehensive digital classroom expansion plan. He added that under the plan two thousand 446 smart blackboards will be installed across the 75 ‘Chief Minister Shri Schools’.

“Rishabh Yadav and Jyothi Surekha Vennam led India’s impressive performance in the compound qualification rounds, helping the country grab the top seedings in both the individual and team events at the fourth and final stage of the Archery World Cup in Madrid, Spain.

As a result, India also bagged the top seeding in the compound mixed team event, with Yadav and Jyothi combining forces.

Gujarat Chief Minister Bhupendra Patel has ordered an investigation into a bridge collapse near Padra in Vadodara district early this morning, in which 9 people were killed and several others injured. A portion of the Gambhira bridge, which connects Anand and Vadodara districts, gave way, plunging five vehicles into the Mahisagar River.
The issue was discussed in today’s cabinet meeting held in Gandhinagar under Chief Minister’s chairmanship. Mr. Patel has sought a preliminary report from the officials regarding the collapse of the bridge. The Chief Minister also held a meeting with the officials of the Roads and Building Department, in which details of other dilapidated bridges in the state were presented.
Prime Minister Narendra Modi has expressed grief over the loss of lives in the incident. He has announced an ex-gratia of two lakh rupees each for the next of the kin of those killed, from the Prime Minister’s National Relief Fund. The injured will be given 50 thousand rupees each.

A court of inquiry has been constituted to ascertain the cause of crash of Indian Air Force Jaguar Trainer aircraft. The aircraft met with an accident during a routine training mission and crashed near Churu in Rajasthan today. Both pilots were killed in the accident. No damage to any civil property has been reported. Indian Air Force has expressed regret for the loss of lives and has said, it stands firmly with the bereaved families in this time of grief.

In Bihar, the bandh called by the RJD-Congress led Grand Alliance today witnessed a mixed impact. The bandh and chakka jam were called in protest against Special Intensive Revision, SIR by the Election Commission underway in the state. During the bandh, protesters disrupted vehicle movement on national and state highways in various parts of the state. Attempts were also made to disrupt train operations at railways stations such as Madhubani, Darbhanga, Jehanabad, Arwal. Congress leader Rahul Gandhi, RJD leader Tejashwi Prasad Yadav, CPI leader D. Raja , Dipankar Bhattacharya of CPI-ML led the bandh supporters in Patna.

The BJP has alleged that the Grand Alliance is trying to undermine constitutional bodies. Former State BJP President, Dr. Sanjay Jaiswal questioned the motive behind bandh when the Special Intensive Revision is already challenged in Supreme Court.

Meanwhile, addressing the media in New Delhi, BJP MP Ravi Shankar Prasad accused the Grand Alliance of playing politics over the Special SIR.

The Election Commission of India today said that active participation of electors, tireless efforts of election officials, volunteers and Booth Level Agents has resulted in the collection of over 57 per cent Enumeration Forms in Bihar in the Special Intensive Revision, SIR. The Commission in a statement said, over 4.53 crore Enumeration Forms out of nearly 7.90 crore have been collected in the first 15 days of the exercise. It said, in the last 24 hours over 83 lakh Enumeration Forms have been collected. The Poll Body hoped that the exercise of collecting the Enumeration Forms could be completed well before the stipulated date of 25th of this month.

Meanwhile, the Commission today posted Article 326 of the Constitution on its social media platforms. The Article lays down the principle of universal adult suffrage, meaning every Indian citizen aged 18 and above has the right to vote, provided they are not disqualified under any law due to non residence, criminal conviction, unsound mind, or corruption.

The nationwide strike called by joint trade unions disrupted normal life in Puducherry today. Private transport services including autos, tempos, and buses were suspended, while government buses operated under police protection. Over 1,500 police personnel were deployed to maintain law and order during the strike.

A Delhi court today extended the judicial custody of 26/11 Mumbai terror attack mastermind Tahawwur Rana till 13th of next month. The development came after the National Investigation Agencyfiled a supplementary chargesheet against Rana.

In Chhattisgarh, twelve Maoists surrendered before the police in Dantewada district today. District SP, Gaurav Rai said, a total reward of twenty-eight lakh rupees was declared on nine of these Maoists.

The surrendered Maoists will be provided incentives of 50,000 rupees each along with other facilities under the new rehabilitation policy of the state government.

The U.S. Supreme Court has allowed Trump Administration to move forward with sweeping federal job cuts. The ruling overrode lower court orders that had paused the layoffs, which are being driven by the Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE). The decision which came yesterday, allows the terminations to proceed while legal challenges continue.

By April this year, DOGE-led efforts led to over 2,60,000 federal workers leaving their posts.

India Meteorological Department (IMD) has issued orange alert for heavy rainfall accompanied by thunderstorms over Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh and Vidarbha for the next three days. Heavy to moderate rainfall is also expected over northern and eastern parts of the country till 15th of this month. The IMD said that Assam, Meghalaya, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram and Tripura are likely to witness heavy rainfall tomorrow.

Meanwhile, heavy rainfall and thunderstorm lashed parts of Delhi-NCR today, bringing respite from heat in the city. Due to sudden weather change, several airlines have issued advisory asking passengers to keep a check on their flight status before arriving at the airport.

The Indian women’s cricket team will be aiming to seal the T20 International series against England in the fourth match tonight in Manchester, U.K. The match will begin at 11 PM Indian standard Time. The Women in Blue hold a 2-1 lead in the five match series.

In Wimbledon, several Quarter-final matches are lined up for this evening at the All England Club in London.
