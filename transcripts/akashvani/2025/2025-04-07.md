## Morning News

President Droupadi Murmu arrived in Lisbon early this morning on the first leg of her state visit to Portugal and Slovakia.  President Murmu will hold delegation level talks with her Portugal counterpart Marcelo Rebelo de Sousa. She will also meet Prime Minister Luis Montenegro and the Speaker of Parliament, Jose Pedro Aguiar Branco. The Mayor of Lisbon will also host a special event in her honour. President will also meet members of Indian community in Portugal and she is likely to meet some Indian researchers from various universities and academic institutions.

“The visit of President is Coming at a time when India and Portugal are celebrating 50 years of  re establishment of diplomatic ties. President will meet and hold delegation level talks with President and Prime Minister of Portugal. To celebrate the 50th anniversary of the re-establishment of diplomatic relations President will also launch commemorative stamp showcasing  the artistic and cultural heritage that unites India and Portugal. Pooja P. Vardhan, Akashwani News, Lisbon.”

From April 9 to 10, President Murmu will visit Slovakia at the invitation of the Slovakia President Peter Pellegrini. It will be the first visit by the President of India to Slovakia in 29 years.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah has assured BJP MLAs and senior party leaders that terror-related situation in the Jammu region will be brought under control soon. He reiterated Central Government’s stand on Statehood to Jammu and Kashmir that it will be restored at an appropriate time. Mr Shah held two hour-long meeting with all 28 BJP MLAs at party headquarters in Trikuta Nagar in the presence of Union Minister Dr Jitendra Singh. Mr Shah arrived in Jammu yesterday evening on a three-day visit to Jammu and Kashmir to review the security situation, interact with security personnel and inaugurate and lay foundation stone of multi-crore projects virtually in the Union Territory and check on the progress of development work.

“The Home Minister will be chairing two crucial and separate security review meetings. One of the meeting will be the Unified Headquarters in which Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha, top brass of Home Ministry, Army, paramilitary forces, J&K Police, Intelligence agencies and civil administration will be present. The Unified Headquarters will review security situation in J&K with special reference to the Jammu region where infiltration attempts and movement of terrorists in upper reaches of Kathua, Udhampur and other districts has become a cause of concern. Besides security, other arrangements to be made for the Annual Amarnath Yatra will also be discussed. This is N. Gulshan Raina, For Akashvani News, Jammu.”

Over fifty countries have contacted the White House to begin trade talks after US President Donald Trump introduced sweeping new tariffs. Top Trump administration officials defended these tariffs, even though they caused US stocks to lose nearly 6 trillion Dollars in value last week.

US stocks fell nearly 10 per cent in the two days following Trump’s announcement of a more aggressive global tariff regime than expected by analysts and investors.

Australian blue-chip stocks, a benchmark index of the country’s largest 200 listed companies, also sank 6 per cent after trading opened today. The Taiwan stock index also plunged 9.8 per cent at the open.
