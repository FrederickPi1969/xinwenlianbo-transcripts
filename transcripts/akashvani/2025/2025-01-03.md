## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will inaugurate and lay the foundation stone of multiple development projects in Delhi today. He will inaugurate around 1700 newly constructed flats for the dwellers of JJ clusters and also hand over keys to eligible beneficiaries in Ashok Vihar, in the national capital. The inauguration of newly constructed flats will mark the completion of the second successful In-Situ Slum Rehabilitation Project by the Delhi Development Authority. The project is aimed at providing the residents of the JJ clusters with a better and healthier living environment equipped with proper amenities and facilities.

“Prime Minister Modi will inaugurate two urban redevelopment projects- the World Trade Centre at Nauroji Nagar and the General Pool Residential Accommodation Type-II Quarters at Sarojini Nagar. The World Trade Centre has transformed the area by replacing over 600 dilapidated quarters with state-of-the-art commercial towers, offering around 34 lakh square feet of premium commercial space with advanced amenities. The GPRA Quarters include 28 towers that house over two thousand five hundred residential units. Besides, the Prime Minister will also inaugurate the Central Board of Secondary Education’s Integrated Office Complex at Dwarka built at around  300 crore rupees. It includes offices, auditorium, advanced data center, among others. Suparna Saikia, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

Prime Minister Modi will also lay the foundation stone of three new projects in Delhi University worth over 600 crore rupees. He will lay the foundation stone of Veer Savarkar College at Roshanpura in Najafgarh.

Talking to Akashvani News, Delhi University Vice Chancellor Prof. Yogesh Singh said, the Prime Minister will lay the foundation of an academic block in the Eastern Campus at Surajmal Vihar in East Delhi and an academic block in the Western Campus at Dwarka.

Union Road Transport and Highways Minister Nitin Gadkari has announced projects worth 12,500 crore rupees to improve the transport system and reduce congestion and pollution in Delhi. He also announced a fund of 1200 crore rupees from the Central Road and Infrastructure Fund for Delhi.

Under the project, Delhi-Katra Expressway will provide direct connectivity to vehicles coming from Jammu and Kashmir and Punjab to the Indira Gandhi International Airport and Delhi-Mumbai Expressway. Vehicles coming from Dehradun will get alternate connectivity to the Indira Gandhi International Airport through Dwarka Expressway, reducing the travel time from one and a half hours to 45 minutes.

The BJP has appointed election officers for the election of the State presidents and National council members of the party. The list includes the names of Union Ministers and senior party leaders.

Union Minister Bhupendra Yadav has been appointed as the election officer for Gujarat, while Union Minister Shivraj Singh Chauhan has been given the responsibility of Karnataka. Besides, Union Minister Piyush Goyal has been appointed as election officer for Uttar Pradesh, Union Minister Manohar Lal has been tasked with Bihar, while Union Minister Dharmendra Pradhan will oversee election in Madhya Pradesh and Union Minister Kiren Rijiju has been given the responsibility of Sikkim.

Department of Telecommunications in coordination with telecom service providers has strengthened telecom services for Maha Kumbh Mela 2025. The Department of Telecommunications has made extensive arrangements to ensure seamless communication across the Mela area and key public spaces in Prayagraj, Uttar Pradesh. The Maha Kumbh Mela 2025 will be held in Prayagraj from 13th January to 26th of February.

“As the stage is set for the Maha Kumbh Mela 2025, preparations are ramping up to ensure seamless connectivity for the millions of visitors expected. Telecom service providers are rolling out advanced technology and optimizing networks across Prayagraj. To enhance connectivity and ensure uninterrupted service, 126 kilometers of optical fibre have been laid throughout the city, while 328 new towers and 575 Base Transceiver Stations have been installed. Telecom services are also being optimized at key public spaces, including major transport hubs, parking areas, and highways leading in and out of the city. PALLAVI PARMAR, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

The country’s first-ever ‘Coastline-Waders Bird Census’ will begin at Marine National Park and Sanctuary in Jamnagar today. The three-day census which has been jointly organized by the Department of Forest and the Bird Conservation Society will also host significant events focusing on counting wader and coastal bird species.

“Jamnagar is often regarded as a paradise for coastal birds. The district is especially known for the Waders’ Birds, which is considered as the Jewels of Jamnagar. Marine National Park and Sanctuary Area of Jamnagar is the Home to Around 300 Species of Resident and Migratory Birds. The Country’s first-ever ‘Coastline-Waders Bird Census’ is all set to begin today. It will cover the selected locations along the estimated 170 km stretch of coastline and 45 islands from Okha to Navlakhi, spanning the districts of Devbhoomi Dwarka, Jamnagar, and Morbi. Along with the bird counting activities, the three-day program will also feature expert talks by specialists in forestry and wildlife and also knowledge-sharing sessions. Aparna Khunt, Akashavni News, Ahmedabad.”

Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis has announced that all infrastructure projects in the state will have a unique identification which will help streamline the system and bring transparency.

This decision was taken in the cabinet meeting chaired by the Chief Minister yesterday.

“Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnvis said several departments are working on the same projects in the same location and duplication of work leads to massive expenditure and losses. Like the Aadhaar card helped weed out bogus beneficiaries, similarly Unique Identification for basic amenities and infrastructure projects will help to streamline the system and stop several agencies from attempting the same work repeatedly.

Mr. Fadnavis further said whenever a project is taken up, all details, along with the work timeline, will be uploaded on the PM Gatishakti portal, and Maharashtra Remote Sensing Application Centre. Bhavana, Aakashwani news, Mumbai.”

External Affairs Ministry today said that India has urged the Chinese side to ensure that the interests of downstream states of the Brahmaputra are not harmed by activities in upstream areas. Replying to a query on hydropower project on the Yarlung Tsangpo River in the Tibet Autonomous Region of China, Ministry spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal said that as a lower riparian state with established user rights to the waters of the river, New Delhi has reiterated its concerns.

Replying to another query, the spokesperson said they have seen the announcement pertaining to the establishment of two new counties in Hotan Prefecture of China. Parts of jurisdiction of these so-called counties fall in India’s Union Territory of Ladakh.
