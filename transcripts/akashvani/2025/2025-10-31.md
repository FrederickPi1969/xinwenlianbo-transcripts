## Morning News

The Nation pays homage to Iron Man Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel on his 150th Birth anniversary today. The day is also celebrated as Rashtriya Ekta Diwas or National Unity Day, highlighting the monumental role Sardar Patel played in uniting India after independence.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will participate in the Rashtriya Ekta Diwas celebrations and offer floral tribute to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel. He will administer the Ekta Diwas pledge and witness Ekta Diwas Parade. The parade will include contingents from BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP and SSB along with various state police forces.

“Today, a magnificent display of the nation’s power and unity will unfold at Ekta nagar in front of the sky-high statue of the Iron Man Sardar Patel. The Rashtriya Ekta Diwas Parade,  a major attraction of the Ekta Divas celebrations , will feature contingents from the BSF, CRPF, and various state police forces. This year, the event has become even more special with the ‘Rashtriya Ekta Parade’ organized on the lines of Republic Day. A standout feature this year is the BSF marching contingent exclusively comprising Indian breed dogs, such as the Rampur and Mudhol Hounds, demonstrating the ‘Aatmanirbhar Bharat’ spirit. The parade Will also honour bravery, recognizing Shaurya Chakra and gallantry medal awardees from the CRPF and BSF for their exceptional courage in anti-Naxal and counter-terror operations. Ten vibrant tableaux, designed to reflect the theme ‘Unity in Diversity’, will also be showcased by various states and central agencies. After the parade, Prime Minister will interact with Officer Trainees of the 100th Foundation Course at the culmination of Aarambh 7.0. The 7th edition of Aarambh is being held on the theme of “Reimagining Governance.” Aparna Khunt//Akashvani news//Ahmedabad.”

President Droupadi Murmu paid floral tributes to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel on his 150th birth anniversary his statue at Patel Chowk, in New Delhi, this morning. The GST Bachat Utsav bagan on the 22nd of September as the Narendra Modi government slashed GST rates in many sectors, benefitting citizens across the country. Our correspondent brings a report on how the next generation GST reforms are boosting the economy of Maharashtra.

Under the next-generation GST reforms, Maharashtra is gaining significantly in its agriculture, industry and services sector. In GST 2.0, tax on sugar, fruit juice, jams and sauces have been reduced to 5 per cent from the earlier 12 per cent. This has encouraged higher consumption, promoted value addition, and supported farmers. The government has also reduced the GST on footwear priced upto two thousand 500 rupees per pair to 5 percent. This will boost sales and exports of Maharashtra’s famous Kolhapuri Chappals. Under the new tax structure, GST rates on solar panels, solar water heaters, and other renewable devices have been slashed down to 5 per cent. Maharashtra is also famous for its film and entertainment industry. In GST 2.0, tax on cinema tickets priced upto 100 rupees has been reduced from 12 per cent to 5 per cent. Lower ticket prices are expected to reduce costs for the common person, making cinema more accessible.

The new GST reforms are benefitting Maharashtra by easing tax burdens, lowering costs for consumers and creating a balance between affordability and competitiveness. With Rishu and Arzoo Saklen Akthar for Akashvani News.
