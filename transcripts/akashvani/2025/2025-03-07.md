## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that India’s achievements and successes have sparked a new wave of hope across the globe. He said, today the whole world is saying that it is the era of India. Addressing an event organised by a private news channel in New Delhi, Mr Modi said, India is driving global growth today and moving at a faster pace on the path of development.

Prime Minister Modi said, today’s India thinks big, sets ambitious targets and delivers remarkable results. He said, in the past decade, India has become the fifth-largest economy and is now rapidly moving towards becoming the third-largest economy in the world.

Mr Modi further highlighted that in the past ten years, India has become the world’s second-largest steel producer, the second-largest mobile phone manufacturer, and the third-largest startup ecosystem. He said, India is among the top five countries in solar energy capacity.

Today is Jan Aushadhi Diwas. The day is celebrated to raise awareness about the scheme and promote the use of generic medicines. With an objective of making quality generic medicines available at affordable prices to all, Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBJP) aims to make quality generic medicines available at affordable prices. Our correspondent reports that the Chemicals and Fertilizers Ministry organised week-long activities across the country to support this initiative.

“Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana was launched in November 2008  to provide quality medicines at affordable rates to the public through dedicated outlets called Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Kendras. The scheme provides medicines at 50 to 80 percent less rates than the market. It also provides medicines procured only from the certified manufacturers for ensuring the quality of the products. One of the major initiatives launched includes the Suvidha Sanitary Napkins at one rupee per pad only.  Over 15 thousand Janaushadhi Kendras are now operational across all districts of the country and the scheme has created a promising avenue for self-employment, offering a sustainable and regular earnings. With Dipendra, Bhanu Pratap Singh, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

## Midday News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will inaugurate and lay the foundation stone for a slew of development projects worth 2,587 crore rupees at Silvasa in the Union Territory of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu in a short while from now. These projects aim to improve connectivity, promote industrial growth, encourage tourism, create employment opportunities and aim at enhancing public welfare initiatives in the region. In the evening, the Prime Minister will travel to Gujarat to launch the Surat Food Security Saturation Campaign. More from our correspondent:

“Gujarat government has decided to extend the Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana PMGKAY Benefits to Other Underprivileged Families who are the beneficiaries of other social security schemes which include Ganga Swaroopa (widowed) women, elderly individuals, Divyangs, and marginalized daily wage earners. Under the Surat Food Security Saturation campaign, nearly 2.3 lakh such beneficiaries have been identified who will receive National Food Security Act (NFSA) benefits from the Prime Minister today. This initiative is a part of the government’s ongoing efforts to extend the reach of food security measures and improve access to essential nutrition for the people. The Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (PMGKAY) was launched by the central government to provide additional free food grains to NFSA beneficiaries during the COVID-19 pandemic. The government has extended the scheme for additional five years upto Jan 2029.”

The US Supreme Court has rejected the plea of 26/11 Mumbai terror attack accused Tahawwur Rana against his extradition to India.

Tahawwur, a Canadian national of Pakistani origin, had filed an “Emergency Application For Stay in the Supreme Court of the United States. He argued in his plea that his extradition to India will violate the US law and the United Nations Convention Against Torture. The petition also cited medical records showing that he has multiple acute and life-threatening diagnoses.

Rejection of Tahawwur Rana’s plea comes following US President Donald Trump announcement of the approval of the extradition process during a joint press conference with Prime Minister Narendra Modi.

Tahawwur Rana is wanted in India for his involvement in the 2008 Mumbai terror attacks carried out by Pakistan-based Lashkar-e-Taiba (LeT) terrorists which claimed the lives of over 174 people.

People in Mizoram are celeberating one of their most popular festivals, Chapchar Kut today with traditional fervour and enthusiasm. Deeply ingrained in Mizo culture, Chapchar Kut is celebrated annually in the month of March and marks the period when freshly cut bamboo and trees are left to dry before being burned for jhumming, a traditional slash-and-burn farming method. We have a report:

“Chapchar Kut – the biggest festival of Mizoram has been celebrated since time immemorial. The festival brings people together across all sections of society and fosters the spirit of love and unity. In olden days, the festival was celebrated for a week. However, in recent times, the festival is being celebrated for a day. Chapchar Kut signifies and honour the “Dignity of labour” as it is celebrated marking the completion of the jhum operation. Chapchar Kut also brings alive the cultural richness of the Mizo people. Chief Minister Lalduhoma attended the State Level Celebration at Lammual in Aizawl as “Kut Pa”. A large number of Kutters attended the celebration with enthusiasm and festivity” Gin Gangte, Akashvani News, Aizawl.”
