Prime Minister Narendra Modi has called on citizens to adopt the spirit of Swadeshi and support locally made products. Addressing a function at Varanasi in Uttar Pradesh, Mr Modi stressed that true service to the nation lies in promoting indigenous goods amid global economic uncertainties.

The Prime Minister stressed that India, poised to become the world’s third-largest economy, must prioritize its economic interests, particularly the welfare of farmers and small industries. He urged citizens and traders to embrace “Make in India” products, especially during the upcoming festival season, as a tribute to Mahatma Gandhi.

In a 55-minute speech, the Prime Minister invoked Lord Shiva’s “Rudra roop” (fierce form) to describe India’s resolute action against terrorism. Mr Modi said the world saw the capabilities of India’s indigenous weapons during Operation Sindoor. He said India’s air defence systems, missiles, and drones proved the strength of ‘Atmanirbhar Bharat’ or self-reliant India. Prime Minister said Brahmos missiles have instilled fear in the enemies of India.

Mr Modi criticized opposition parties for questioning the operation, accusing them of trivializing the bravery of India’s armed forces by calling it a “tamasha” and engaging in vote-bank politics.

The Prime Minister also laid the foundation stone and inaugurated development projects worth around 2,200 crore rupees in Varanasi. Mr Modi also releasing 20th instalment of the PM-KISAN scheme, transferring over 20,500 crore rupees to more than 9.7 crore farmers across India. The Prime Minister also emphasized the government’s commitment to transforming the lives of farmers, boosting their income, and reducing farming costs.

The Election Commission has doubled the remuneration for Booth Level Officers (BLOs) and enhanced the remuneration of BLO Supervisors involved in the preparation and revision of electoral rolls. In a statement, the poll body informed that as per the revised structure, the annual remuneration for BLOs has been doubled from six thousand rupees to twelve thousand rupees. Additionally, the incentive for BLOs for the revision of electoral rolls has been increased from one thousand rupees to two thousand rupees.

BLO Supervisors, who play a crucial role in overseeing the electoral roll preparation process, will now receive eighteen thousand rupees annually, up from twelve thousand rupees.

The Election Commission has provided an honorarium for the Electoral Registration Officers (EROs) and the Assistant Electoral Registration Officers (AEROs) for the first time. EROs will be given 30 thousand rupees while AEROs will get 25 thousand rupees. The poll body has also approved a special incentive of six thousand rupees for BLOs for the Special Intensive Revision-SIR starting from Bihar.

The Election Commission has said that not a single claim or objection has been filed by any political party in the first 24 hours of the publication of the draft electoral rolls in Bihar. In a statement the Commission said, all political parties put together have filed zero claims and objections for the addition of the names of eligible electors and deletion of the names of ineligible electors.

The Poll Body had released the draft electoral rolls for Bihar following the completion of the Enumeration Phase under the Special Intensive Revision SIR 2025. It said, the public has one month to submit claims and objections and assured that no name will be removed from the draft voter list without a stated reason. The draft list, covering all 243 Assembly Constituencies and 90 thousand 712 polling booths was shared with all political parties by the 38 District Election Officers across the state.

The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI), in consultation with the Ministry of Ayush, has released a definitive list of Ayurvedic food preparations under the category of Ayurveda Aahara. This significant step brings India’s time-honoured food wisdom into the mainstream, following the introduction of the Food Safety and Standards (Ayurveda Aahara) Regulations in 2022. More from our correspondent:-

These regulations recognise foods based on recipes, ingredients, and processes from authoritative Ayurvedic texts, and the new list brings unprecedented clarity and confidence to consumers and businesses alike.

This initiative aims to assist Food Business Operators by providing a clear and credible reference for the manufacture of Ayurveda Aahara products.

Minister of State for Ayush Prataprao Jadhav has urged citizens to incorporate Ayurveda Aahara into their daily lives to experience its long-term health benefits. He emphasised that these time-tested dietary practices not only nourish the body but also strengthen immunity, support digestion, and promote overall well-being.

This initiative marks a pivotal moment in enhancing regulatory clarity for industry stakeholders while promoting the widespread adoption of Ayurveda-based nutrition for better public health outcomes. SANYUKTA YADAV, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI

## Midday News

Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw flagged off the Bhavnagar-Ayodhya Cantt weekly train from Bhavnagar railway station in Gujarat today. The Minister also virtually inaugurated two other trains between Rewa and Pune and Jabalpur and Raipur.

The Minister said that with 34,000 kilometres of new railway tracks laid in the past 11 years, Indian railways is witnessing a massive transformation. Addressing the gathering at Bhavnagar railway station, he reaffirmed the commitment of the government to developing, modernising and expanding the railway network and ensuring safety and cleanliness in railways. The Minister said the redevelopment of 1,300 stations in the country is the largest ever station redevelopment project in the world.

Union ministers Mansukh Mandaviya and Nimu Ben Bambhaniya were also present on the occasion. Madhya Pradesh Chief Minister Mohan Yadav, and Chhattisgarh Chief Minister Vishnu Deo Sai participated in the programme virtually. On the occasion, the railway minister also made a slew of announcements for Gujarat. A report from our correspondent:

“In a significant boost to infrastructure in Rajkot, Porbandar, and Junagadh districts, new train services was announced between Porbandar and Rajkot. A long-awaited demand has been fulfilled with the announcement of a new railway line between Saradia and Vansjalia. Additionally, a modern coach maintenance hub will be built at Ranavav station at a cost of ₹135.64 crore. The Minister also announced establishment of new container terminal at Bhavnagar port and promised to start new Vande Bharat train between Bhavnagar and Surat in coming time.  Aparna Khunt Akashvani News, Bhavnagar.”

A powerful 6.8 magnitude earthquake struck Russia’s Kuril Islands this morning. The quake triggered a tsunami warning for parts of Russia’s Kamchatka Peninsula. This is the second major earthquake near Russia in a week. On July 30, an 8.8 magnitude quake struck the region, followed by several strong aftershocks.

Just hours before the latest tremor, the Krasheninnikov Volcano in Kamchatka erupted for the first time in 600 years. According to Olga Girina, head of the Kamchatka Volcanic Eruption Response Team, the eruption may be linked to the earlier earthquake, which had triggered tsunami warnings as far as the French Polynesia and Chile.

The death toll from the partial collapse at Chile’s El Teniente copper mine has risen to two, after human remains were found during ongoing search efforts for five trapped workers. The discovery comes days after the collapse, triggered by a seismic event on Thursday, trapped six workers more than 900 metres below ground at the world’s largest underground copper mine. One miner was previously confirmed dead, halting operations at the century-old mine.

Chile is the world’s largest copper producer, generating about 5.3 million tonnes in 2024. Chile’s mining industry is considered one of the safest globally. The country, however, lies along the earthquake-prone Pacific Ring of Fire.
