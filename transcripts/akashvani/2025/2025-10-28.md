The 4-day long Chhath Puja culminated today with oblation to the rising Sun, known as Usha Arghya.  Usha Arghya is considered the final and most significant ritual of the festival. On this day, devotees gather at rivers, ponds, or water bodies to offer prayers to the rising Sun, expressing deep gratitude and seeking blessings for health, prosperity, and wellbeing. Once the final offering to the rising Sun is made, devotees break their 36-hour-long fast by consuming Prasad and water – a ritual known as Parana. This act marks the successful conclusion of the Chhath Vrat.

In Bihar, devotees gathered at various riverbanks across the state – including Ganga, Gandak, Kosi, Bagmati, and Kamla Balan – to offer prayers to the rising Sun.

“Amid a reddish sky and a slight cold, millions of devotees standing waist-deep water at different Chhath ghats across Bihar offered Ushakalin Arghya to the Sun God. After the ritual of Paran, devotees performed prayers and broke their 36-hour-long rigorous fast, marking the completion of the sacred Nirjala Vrat or fast without water. Prasad was distributed among people at the Chhath ghats. In the state capital Patna, lakhs of devotees offered Arghya to the Sun God and Chhathi Maiya along the banks of Ganga river. In view of the upcoming elections, voter awareness campaigns were also carried out at several Chhath ghats through posters, hoardings, banners, and video messages – appealing to people to actively participate in the festival of democracy, the Bihar Assembly Elections. Dharmendra Kumar Rai, Akashvani News, Patna.”

The GST Bachat Utsav kicked off on 22nd September as the Narendra Modi government slashed GST rates in many sectors across the country. Today our correspondent brings a report on how the reduced GST rates on stationery items are helping citizens.

“Under the Next Generation GST reforms, the government has provided significant relief for stationery items. As part of the new tax structure, pencils, sharpeners, crayons, exercise books, graph books, maps, and charts have been made completely tax-free. These items earlier attracted a 12 percent GST. This step has also reduced out-of-pocket education expenses for families, encouraging higher school enrolment, and supporting better learning outcomes. The GST reduction has not only made the learning tools cheaper, but has also further enhanced access to education across the country. Shivang Misra, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

The GST Bachat Utsav kicked off on 22nd September as the Narendra Modi government slashed GST rates in many sectors, benefitting citizens across the country. Our correspondent brings a report on how the next gen GST reforms are boosting the economy of Punjab.

Under the next gen GST reforms, the government has provided a major boost to Punjab’s economy by providing significant relief across various sectors. Under the new tax structure, GST on agro and food commodities, including milk, butter, ghee, curd, cheese, UHT milk, infant food, dairy whitener, and chocolate, have been slashed to 5 percent from the previously taxed 12 percent. This tax relief on key products of Punjab has encouraged greater production, supported livelihoods, and strengthened the state economy. Similarly, GST on dairy-based mithai, which is a major centre for Ludhiana and Amritsar, has also been lowered to 5 percent from earlier taxed at 12 per cent, resulting in a drop of nearly 5 percent to 7 percent per kilogram and making mithai more affordable for consumers as well as supporting small sweet makers. Talking to Akashvani News, Virender Kumar, a farmer from Chandigarh, has hailed the government’s decision

The rationalisation under the new GST reforms has eased the tax burden on essential goods and services, enhanced competitiveness, and created growth and employment opportunities in the state. With Rohan Saklen Akhtar, Akashvani News, Delhi.
