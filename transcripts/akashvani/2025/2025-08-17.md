## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will inaugurate two major National Highway projects worth around 11 thousand crore rupees in New Delhi today. These projects include the Delhi section of the Dwarka Expressway and Urban Extension Road-II (UER-II). On the occasion, the Prime Minister will also address the gathering.

These projects have been developed as part of the government’s comprehensive plan to decongest the national capital. The projects aimed to significantly improve connectivity, reduce travel time, and ease traffic in Delhi and its surrounding areas. The 10.1-kilometer-long Delhi section of the Dwarka Expressway has been developed at a cost of over 5 thousand 300 crore rupees. This section will provide multi-modal connectivity to Yashobhoomi, the DMRC Blue and Orange lines, the upcoming Bijwasan railway station, and the Dwarka cluster bus depot. The Alipur to Dichaon Kalan stretch of UER-II, along with new links to Bahadurgarh and Sonipat, is being constructed at a cost of around 5,580 crore rupees. It will ease traffic on Delhi’s Inner and Outer Ring Roads, as well as at busy points like Mukarba Chowk, Dhaula Kuan, and NH-9. In a social media post, Prime Minister reaffirmed the government’s unwavering commitment to enhancing the ease of living for citizens by giving a significant boost to infrastructure development in the National Capital Region. India’s space hero, Group Captain Shubhanshu Shukla, received a warm welcome upon his arrival at Delhi’s International Airport early this morning. He was warmly received at the airport by Union Minister Dr. Jitendra Singh, Delhi Chief Minister Rekha Gupta, and ISRO Chairman Dr. V. Narayanan.

After making history as the first Indian to visit the International Space Station, Shubhanshu Shukla underwent rehabilitation in the United States to recover from the physical demands of his groundbreaking mission.

Mr Shukla piloted NASA’s Axiom-4 mission, which launched from Kennedy Space Centre in Florida on 25th June, this year. During the mission, he conducted groundbreaking microgravity experiments aimed at advancing scientific research in space. He safely returned to Earth on 15th of last month.

Shukla, is also one of the astronauts shortlisted for India’s first human spaceflight mission, Gaganyaan, which is set to launch by the end of next year.

The Election Commission of India (ECI) has said that utmost transparency is the hallmark of electoral roll preparation as per law, rules and guidelines. In a statement, the poll body said that political parties were involved at every stage of preparing electoral rolls, which is a multi-stage exercise.

The ECI has criticised political parties and individuals for raising concerns over alleged errors in electoral rolls from previous elections. The poll body said that the appropriate time to raise any issue with the Electoral Rolls would have been during the Claims and Objections period of that phase, which is precisely the objective behind sharing the Electoral Rolls with all Political Parties and the Candidates. The Election Commission clarified that had such issues been flagged during the stipulated time, they could have been examined and, if genuine, corrected by the Electoral Registration Officers before the elections.
