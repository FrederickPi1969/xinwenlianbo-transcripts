## Morning News

Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar has announced the introduction of 17 new initiatives for upcoming assembly elections in Bihar. Addressing a press conference in Patna, he said, some of these initiatives will be implemented in the conduct of elections, while others in counting. Mr Kumar said, these measures are intended not only to enhance the electoral process in Bihar but also to serve as models for nationwide implementation in the future.

Chief Election Commissioner outlined some of the key initiatives, including a new Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) that aims to ensure delivery of Elector Photo Identity Cards within 15 days of voter registration. Additionally, a mobile deposit facility will be available at polling booths to streamline the voting process. Bihar has become the first state in the country where every polling station will now serve fewer than 1,200 voters, following a significant move by the Election Commission aimed at easing voter access and reducing queue lengths. Chief Election Commissioner also highlighted that the poll-bound Bihar will be the first state to feature larger fonts and colour photographs of candidates on Electronic Voting Machine (EVM) ballots, replacing the current black-and-white images that often make candidate identification difficult despite the clarity of election symbols. Mr Kumar also announced that all polling stations in Bihar will have complete webcasting coverage to ensure greater transparency during the elections.

Bihar Chief Minister Nitish Kumar will inaugurate the first phase of the Patna Metro Rail Service today. It will open for public tomorrow. The metro rail services will be available from 8 am morning to 10 pm night on this route. The first phase of the approximately 3.6-kilometre-long elevated metro corridor has been named the Priority Corridor. In this phase, metro services will operate from the Inter-State Bus Terminal (ISBT) to Bhootnath Station.

The coaches of Patna metro rail have been designed with meticulous blending of attractively aesthetic Madhubani paintings featuring illustrations of Bihar’s historical landmarks such as Golghar, Mahabodhi Temple, Buddha Smriti Park, Nalanda Mahavihara and other heritages.

On the priority corridor of Patna Metro Rail passengers will have to pay 15 rupees for a short journey between two stations, and a maximum fare of 30 rupees for traveling across all stations. The train services on the metro line will be available after the interval of every 20 minutes. Dharmendra Kumar Rai, Akashvani News, Patna

Union Health Ministry has advised all States and Union Territories to ensure the rational use of cough syrups, especially among children, citing that most coughs are self-limiting and do not need medication. The move follows recent reports of children’s deaths in Chhindwara, Madhya Pradesh, allegedly linked to contaminated cough syrups.

In a high-level meeting chaired by Union Health Secretary Punya Salila Srivastava, the Centre reviewed compliance with drug quality norms and emphasized strict adherence to the Revised Schedule M by all drug manufacturers. The Union Health Minister had earlier directed this matter be urgently discussed with States.

Authorities confirmed that risk-based inspections have begun at 19 drug manufacturing units across six states to identify quality lapses. States were also asked to boost surveillance, improve reporting from health facilities, and enhance outbreak response coordination.

A Central team found that a sample of the cough syrup Coldrif, consumed by affected children, contained toxic Diethylene Glycol (DEG) beyond permissible limits. The syrup was traced to a manufacturer in Kancheepuram, Tamil Nadu. Regulatory action has followed, including the cancellation of the company’s manufacturing license and the initiation of criminal proceedings.

Indian Navy is set to commission Androth, the second Anti-Submarine Warfare Shallow Water Craft (ASW-SWC) in Visakhapatnam today. The ceremony will be presided over by Vice Admiral Rajesh Pendharkar. The Ministry of Defence said that the commissioning of Androth, marks yet another milestone in the Indian Navy’s steady stride towards capability enhancement and indigenisation. It added that the commissioning of Androth will significantly bolster the Navy’s ASW capabilities, especially in countering threats in littoral waters. Built by Garden Reach Shipbuilders and Engineers (GRSE), Kolkata, Androth is a testimony to India’s growing maritime self-reliance with over 80 percent indigenous content.

GST Bachat Utsav, which was announced by Prime Minister Narendra Modi, and commenced on 22nd of last month, is benefiting all sections of society. Today, our correspondent brings a report on the GST reforms that will boost the economy of Assam.

The new GST reform is a big relief for Assam’s weavers, farmers, and small businesses. Under the revised rates, taxes have been reduced from several traditional and regional goods of the state. The reform especially benefits Assam’s tea industry, which now has only 5 per cent GST, making its products cheaper and more competitive in both domestic and international markets. Assam’s rich handloom and craft sector will also see major relief as GST on this sector has also been reduced to 5 per cent. Apart from this, agricultural products such as Joha rice, Boka Saul, and Tezpur litchi are also now taxed at 5 per cent. This will help local farmers improve their margins and reach larger markets. The tourism sector will also gain from this reform, as hotel rooms priced up to seven thousand five hundred rupees per night will attract only 5 percent GST, making Assam a more affordable destination for visitors. The next generation GST reforms seek to make products more affordable for consumers while improving earnings for artisans. The reforms are designed to drive sales, safeguard employment, and strengthen rural economies during the festive period and in the long run. With Samarth Singh, Akshit Vedyan for Akashvani News, Delhi.

## Evening News

The Election Commission of India announced the Election schedule for the Bihar Assembly today. Briefing media in New Delhi today, Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar said, the Election for the 243 members Assembly will be conducted in two phases on 6th and 11th November this year. In the first phase, the voting will be held on 121 Assembly seats while in the second phase, 122 constituencies will go to polls.

“The assembly elections in Bihar will be held in two phases. The gazette notification for the first phase will be issued on the 10th of October, and for the second phase on the 13th. The last date for filing of nominations for the first phase will be the 17th, and for the second phase, the 20th. Scrutiny of nominations will take place on the 18th and 21st. The last date for withdrawal of nominations will be 20th October and 23rd October. The date of polling will be 6th of November, Thursday for the first phase and 11th of November, Tuesday for the second phase. Counting of votes will be held on 14th November, and the entire election process will be completed by 16th November.”

With the announcement of poll schedule, the Model Code of Conduct has come into force in Bihar. The Chief Election Commissioner said, over 90 thousand 712 polling booths will be set up for seven crore 43 lakh voters in the state.

“In Bihar, there are a total of 243 assembly seats. Out of this, 38 seats are reserved for scheduled caste and two for scheduled tribe. The total number of electors is 7 crore 43 lakh, of which 3.92 crore are male and 3.5 crore are women. Four lakh are senior citizens, and 14,000 have completed 100 years of age. Around fourteen lakh voters are first-time voters. The poll panel is ensuring that this time, no polling station will have more than 1,200 voters. This time, for the convenience of people, the Election Commission has set a goal to ensure that no polling station has more than 1,200 voters. As a result, the number of polling stations has increased to 90,712 in Bihar.”

Mr Kumar said, for the first time one General observer will be deployed in each constituency to work as the eye and ear of the Commission. Mr Kumar added that webcasting will be done at all polling stations to thoroughly monitor the electoral process. The Chief Election Commissioner also announced By-election for eight assembly seats in seven states. These assembly constituency are Budgam and Nagrota in Jammu and Kashmir, Anta in Rajasthan, Ghatsila in Jharkhand, Jubilee Hills in Telangana, Tarn Taran in Punjab, Dampa in Mizoram and Nuapada in Odisha.
