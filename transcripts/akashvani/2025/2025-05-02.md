Prime Minister Narendra Modi will dedicate the Vizhinjam International Deepwater Multipurpose Seaport, India’s first semi-automated port, to the nation in Kerala today. The Supreme Court has said that doctors should only prescribe generic medicines and not any particular brand, aligning with the recent directive issued in Rajasthan. Hearing a petition seeking regulation of unethical marketing practices by pharmaceutical companies, the apex court said, if this directive is implemented across the country, it will bring about a huge change. The three-judge bench of the Supreme Court comprises Justices Sandeep Mehta, Vikram Nath and Sanjay Karol. The apex court remarked that the issue of pharmaceutical companies allegedly bribing doctors will be resolved if there is a statutory mandate for doctors to prescribe generic medicines.

Thousands of pilgrims from across India and abroad gathered to witness this divine moment. The air resonated with spiritual fervor, as the sacred valley of Kedarnath came alive with devotion, faith, and celebration.

To mark this auspicious occasion, the Kedarnath temple was adorned with 108 quintals of vibrant flowers. This elaborate floral decoration amplified grandeur of the temple premises, creating a mesmerizing sight for arriving devotees.As per tradition, the Panchmukhi Doli of Lord Kedarnath-carried in a ceremonial palanquin-began its spiritual journey from the Omkareshwar Temple in Ukhimath on April 27. After halting at Guptkashi, Phata, and Gaurikund, the revered procession reached Kedarnath Dham last evening.  This morning, the temple doors were formally opened in the presence of a large gathering of devotees. To ensure a smooth and safe pilgrimage experience this year, the administration has implemented comprehensive arrangements.
