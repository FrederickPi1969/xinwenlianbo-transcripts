## Morning News

Mrs Rekha Gupta will be sworn in as the new Chief Minister of Delhi today. Her name was announced as the new Chief Minister following Delhi BJP legislative party meeting in New Delhi last evening. She met Lieutenant Governor Vinai Kumar Saxena at the Raj Niwas and staked claim to forming the new government. Mr Saxena accepted the claim and invited her to form the new government. Our correspondent reports that Rekha Gupta will take oath as the Chief Minister at the iconic Ramlila Maidan in Delhi.

“Rekha Gupta will be the fourth woman Chief Minister and the second woman BJP Chief Minister of Delhi. The oath of office and secrecy will be administered by Delhi LG VK Saxena at around 12:35 PM. Prime Minister Narendra Modi, Home Minister Amit Shah and Defence Minister Rajnath Singh among others will attend the swearing in ceremony. Chief Ministers and Deputy chief Ministers of around 20 states have also been invited to attend the event. Delhi Police has issued a traffic advisory in anticipation of large crowds. To manage the traffic during the event, certain diversions and restrictions will be in place. Suparna Saikia, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

In a social media post, Mrs Gupta thanked the top leadership of the BJP for entrusting her with the responsibility of the post of Chief Minister. She pledged to work with full honesty, integrity and dedication for the welfare and development of the people of Delhi.

Rekha Gupta is the first time MLA, who has been elected from Shalimar Bagh seat. She began her political journey with ABVP at Daulat Ram College, Delhi University, in 1992. She became the President of the Delhi University Students’ Union. She has also served as the General Secretary of BJP Mahila Morcha in Delhi.

After the announcement of Rekha Gupta’s name as next Chief Minister of Delhi, several BJP leaders including Home Minister Amit Shah and other Union Ministers have congratulated her. In a social media post, Home Minister Amit Shah said that he has full faith in Rekha Gupta. He added that BJP’s New Chief Minister in Delhi will work with dedication in the direction of the resolution taken by Prime Minister Narendra Modi to make Delhi one of the top capitals of the world.

Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw also congratulated Mrs Gupta. In a social media post, Mr Vaishnaw wrote that he looked forward to working with her on developing IT ecosystem and Railways in Delhi, under the leadership of Prime Minister Narendra Modi.

Outgoing Delhi Chief Minster Atishi and Aam Aadmi Party National Convenor Arvind Kejriwal have congratulated Mrs. Gupta on being elected as the new Chief Minister and expressed hope that she would fulfil all promises made during the election campaign.

A symposium on spirituality and culture- “Mahakumbh Dialogue: Spiritual and Cultural Heritage of India” was organized at the Kala Kumbh Complex during the Mahakumbh in Prayagraj. The symposium highlighted the significance and references of the Mahakumbh throughout history, featuring delegates from UNESCO, Allahabad University and other educationists.

“The program was inaugurated by UNESCO Director of the South Asia Region, Tim Curtis along with Prof. Heramb Chaturvedi, former Head of Department of History at Allahabad University. In his address, Mr Curtis highlighted the significance of Kumbh and mentioned that UNESCO recognized the Kumbh Mela as an intangible cultural heritage of humanity back in 2017. He added that its social, economic and environmental impacts were being monitored since 1923. He said that the Kumbh Mela provides a new vision to the world as a natural and cultural heritage.
Prof. Chaturvedi, while highlighting the historical aspect of Kumbh, said that Kumbh Mela is also mentioned in the travelogue of the 7th century Chinese Buddhist monk, Hiuen Tsang.
The symposium was organised by the Department of Culture, Uttar Pradesh and Prayagraj Mela Authority. With Abhishek Kapil, Adarsh for Akashvani News, Prayagraj.”

The Puducherry government has announced that organ donors will be accorded state honours as a mark of respect for their life-saving contributions. Guidelines have been formulated to implement this initiative. The government has directed hospitals to promptly report brain-dead cases to district collectors and regional administrators. As per the new guidelines, district collectors and regional administrators will visit the residences of organ donors and pay homage. Additionally, on occasions such as the Republic Day and the Independence Day, the Lieutenant Governor or the Chief Minister will invite the families of organ donors to official functions and honor them publicly.

## Midday News

BJP’s first-time MLA Rekha Gupta was sworn in as the new Chief Minister of Delhi at the iconic Ramlila Maidan in the national capital this afternoon. Delhi Lieutenant Governor VK Saxena administered the oath of office and secrecy to Ms Gupta.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi, Union Home Minister Amit Shah, Defence Minister Rajnath Singh and several Union Ministers were present during the oath-taking ceremony. Chief Ministers and Deputy Chief Ministers of several states also attended the event.

After taking oath, Rekha Gupta has become the fourth woman Chief Minister after Sushma Swaraj, Sheela Dixit, Atishi and the second woman BJP Chief Minister of Delhi. She was elected as the new Chief Minister of Delhi in the BJP legislative party meeting in the National Capital last evening. Earlier, talking to media ahead of the swearing-in ceremony, Rekha Gupta thanked the top leadership of the party for entrusting her with the responsibility of the post of Chief Minister.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi congratulated Rekha Gupta on taking oath as Delhi’s Chief Minister. In a social media post, Mr Modi said, she has risen from the grassroots, being active in campus politics, state organisation, municipal administration and now MLA as well as the Chief Minister. The Prime Minister expressed confidence that Ms Gupta will work for Delhi’s development with full vigour.

The 55th Director General level Border Co-ordination Conference between Border Security Forces (BSF) and Border Guard Bangladesh (BGB) was concluded in New Delhi today. During the conference, issues like infiltration, trans-border crimes, fencing at the border, smuggling and other issues were discussed at length between the two nations.

Briefing media after the conference, Director General of Border Security Force Daljit Singh Chawdhary said, infiltration has gone down substantially after 5th August last year along the Indo-Bangla border. He said, the surveillance mechanism at the border has been strengthened with the deployment of state of the art surveillance systems to check illegal activity.

Rail India Technical and Economic Service (RITES) Limited has been tasked to design a permanent holding area at New Delhi, Anand Vihar, Ghaziabad, Varanasi and Ayodhya Railway stations. According to the Railway Ministry, once the model is ready, it will be expanded to 60 high density footfall railway stations. All unauthorised access points to these stations will be closed and these holding areas will be integrated with the Amrit Stations redevelopment programme.

The Centre today launched an intensified special Non-Communicable Diseases screening campaign to ensure hundred per cent coverage of all individuals aged 30 years and above. The Union Health Ministry said, the initiative aims to achieve screening of all individuals for prevalent Non-Communicable Diseases, including Diabetes, Hypertension, and three common cancers – Oral, Breast, and Cervical. Our correspondent reports that the campaign aims to ensure early detection and timely intervention for Non-Communicable Diseases.

“The campaign will be executed across Ayushman Arogya Mandirs and various healthcare facilities nationwide, under the National Programme for Prevention and Control of Non-Communicable Diseases. During the 40 day campaign trained ASHAs and frontline workers will conduct door to door visits to ensure maximum screening coverage, reaching individuals in their homes. The data on screening, treatment, and follow-ups will be uploaded daily on the NP-NCD Portal, ensuring transparency and accountability. The initiative is expected to lower healthcare costs and enhance the overall quality of life for individuals across the nation. This special drive also marks a significant step toward a healthier and Non-Communicable Disease -free India, empowering citizens to take charge of their health and well-being. Bhupendra Singh, Akashvani News Delhi.”

The ongoing Maha Kumbh Mela in Prayagraj has evolved into not only a spiritual gathering but also a large-scale movement focused on cleanliness and environmental preservation.
