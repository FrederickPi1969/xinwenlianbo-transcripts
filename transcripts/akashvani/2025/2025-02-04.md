## Morning News

Both the Houses of the Parliament will resume the discussion on the Motion of Thanks on President’s Address today. Moving the motion in the Lok Sabha yesterday, BJP MP Ramvir Singh Bidhuri, listed the achievements of the Narendra Modi-led government, including public welfare schemes. He attacked the AAP-led Delhi government for allegedly failing to implement central schemes like Ayushman Bharat and the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana in the capital. Mr.Bidhuri also raised issues of poor road conditions, air pollution, and public transport in the city. He further accused former Delhi CM Arvind Kejriwal of lavish spending on his official residence.

Later, Leader of Opposition and Congress MP Rahul Gandhi criticized the President’s speech, calling it a repeat of previous years and a ‘laundry list’ of government achievements. Rahul Gandhi raised concerns about unemployment  and said the government has failed to address the unemployment issue despite the country’s growth. He pointed out that manufacturing’s share of GDP has dropped from 15.3 percent to 12.6 percent, the lowest in the past 60 years.

BJP MP Ravi Shankar Prasad alleged that Congress is uncomfortable whenever the Maha Kumbh is mentioned, noting that thirty five crore people have participated in the event so far. He accused Congress of feeling entitled to govern the country.

Other MPs also raised concerns during the discussion. Naresh Chandra Uttam Patel of the SP pointed out the absence of a mention of the Minimum Support Price, MSP guarantee in the President’s address.

Dr. Kakoli Ghosh Dastidar of the TMC criticized the omission of food inflation and farm loan waivers.

Arvind Ganpat Sawant of Shiv Sena, UBT spoke about schemes benefiting farmers but raised concerns about disparities in the education system.

The discussion saw participation from several other MPs, including K. Kanimozhi of DMK, JD(U)’s Dileshwar Kamait, IUML’s ET Mohammed Basheer, RLD’s Rajkumar Sangwan, BJP’s Ganesh Singh, Pratap Chandra Sarangi, Rodmal Nagar, and RJD’s Abhay Kumar Sinha. However, the debate remained inconclusive. The House was adjourned and will reconvene today at 11 AM.

External Affairs Minister Dr. S. Jaishankar has accused Leader of the Opposition and Congress MP Rahul Gandhi of deliberately speaking falsehoods about Dr.Jaishankar’s  visit to the U.S. in December last year. In a social media post, Dr. Jaishankar said he went to meet the Secretary of State and NSA of the Biden administration. He also chaired a gathering of Indian Consuls General and met the incoming NSA-designate.

The External Affairs Minister stressed that at no stage was an invitation regarding the Prime Minister discussed. He added that it is common knowledge that Prime Minister Narendra Modi does not attend such events, and India is generally represented by Special Envoys. He claimed that Mr. Gandhi’s lies may be intended politically, but they damage the nation abroad.

BJP MPs met Rajya Sabha Chairman Jagdeep Dhankhar and submitted a notice of breach of parliamentary privilege against senior Congress leader and MP Sonia Gandhi for her derogatory remarks against President Droupadi Murmu. In the notice, it was stated that her statement is inconsistent with the established Rules and Conduct and Parliamentary ethics and etiquettes. The BJP MPs have urged the Rajya Sabha Chairman to take cognizance of the matter and initiate disciplinary action against Mrs. Gandhi. Talking outside Parliament, BJP MP Faggan Singh Kulaste said that the remarks are in violation of Parliamentary traditions and conduct.

All arrangements are being made for the smooth conduct of elections for the 70 member Delhi Assembly to be held tomorrow. The polling will be held from 7 AM till 6 PM on Wednesday. Over one crore 56 lakh voters will decide the fate of the 699 candidates in the fray. Campaigning came to an end last evening. The counting of votes will take place on Saturday.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah will chair a security review meeting on Jammu and Kashmir in New Delhi today. The meeting is expected to be attended by Jammu and Kashmir Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha, Home Secretary Govind Mohan, and chiefs of central agencies like IB and RAW, along with other senior officials.

The last such review meeting was held on December 19 2024, in which Mr Shah directed all security agencies to continue working in a coordinated manner to eliminate terrorism in Jammu and Kashmir.

Meanwhile, an ex-serviceman was killed in a terrorist attack in South Kashmir’s Kulgam district yesterday.

Security sources said terrorists attacked the ex-serviceman along with his wife and daughter in the Behibagh area of Kulgam. His wife and daughter, who were injured in the attack, are under treatment and are reported to be in stable condition. Security forces have cordoned off the area to nab the attackers.

He said that the ongoing redevelopment of Jammu Railway Station once completed will ensure direct railway connectivity between Jammu and Srinagar.

U.S. President Donald Trump has announced a one-month delay in U.S. tariff hikes on Canada and Mexico, after the two neighboring countries agreed to strengthen border security efforts. However, tariffs against China are still scheduled to go into effect today.

In a social media post, Canadian Prime Minister Justin Trudeau said during a call with Trump, he pledged additional cooperation on border security. Earlier, Trump revealed that he had a positive conversation with Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum, who agreed to deploy 10,000 soldiers to the U.S.-Mexico border.

The Reserve Bank of India has informed that the total value of 2000 rupee banknotes in circulation dropped to 6,577 crore rupees as of the 31st of last month. In a statement, the RBI has clarified that 98.15 percent of the 3.56 lakh crore worth of currency notes in circulation in May 2023 have been returned to the system.

The RBI had announced the withdrawal of 2000 rupee notes on 19th May 2023, urging citizens to deposit or exchange them at banks by 7th October 2023.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that his government has taken a slew of measures for the welfare of the poor people resulting in upliftment of 25 crore people out of poverty. Replying to the discussion in Lok Sabha today on the motion of thanks to the President’s address to the joint sitting of the Parliament, the Prime Minister said, it has been possible only because of this government’s commitment, dedication and hardwork. Mr. Modi assured that his government is working to achieve the vision of Viksit Bharat by emphasising on 100 percent saturation of schemes. He termed the President’ address as inspiring which strengthens the resolve of a Viksit Bharat, gives new hope and inspires people.

The Prime Minister said, budgetary allocations for capital expenditure has witnessed massive hike during his tenure and it has been raised to over 11 lakh 21 thousand crore rupees. He said, the government has brought transparency in the governance resulting in saving of crores of rupees. The Prime Minister also recalled various steps which have been taken to provide tax incentives to the middle class.

The Prime Minister said, his government has increased job opportunities for the youth of the country.

Mr. Modi added that initiatives like the development of Artificial Intelligence in India have been prioritised by his government. He said, the NDA government has created a separate Ministry for Tribals.  He said that it was his government that started the Ayushman Bharat for the poor and elderly. India has made significant progress in mobile and electronics manufacturing in the last 10 years. In a social media post, Electronics and IT Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said that there were only 2 mobile manufacturing units in 2014 and today the country has over 300 units. Mr Vaishnaw said that the value of mobile phone manufacturing has grown substantially from 18 thousand 9 hundred crore rupees in financial year 2014, to 4 lakh 22 thousand crore rupees in financial year 2024. The Minister highlighted that mobile phone manufacturing in India has generated 12 lakh direct and indirect jobs in the past decade. Mr Vaishnaw noted that the Make in India program is driving self-reliance, boosting production, and creating jobs.
