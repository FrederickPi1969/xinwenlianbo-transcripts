## Morning News

The government has given approval for the development of Sonprayag to Kedarnath ropeway with the length of 12.9 kilometres. Briefing media in New Delhi, Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said, the total cost of the project will be over four thousand crore rupees.

Mr Vaishnav also informed about 12.4 km Hemkund Sahib Ropeway that is to be developed at a cost of two thousand 730 crore rupees.

Mr. Vaishnaw said the government has taken decisions for livestock health and disease prevention with the cost of three thousand 880 crore rupees. He said the vaccination programme will be a major thrust area of this programme. He said, this will help the farmers. He said, good quality and affordable generic veterinary medicines will be provided through Pashu Aushadhi.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will arrive in Uttarkashi on a day-long visit to Uttarakhand this morning. Mr Modi will address a public meeting in Harsil today.

“During his tour, the Prime Minister will visit Mukhwa village, the winter abode of Goddess Ganga, where he will offer prayers. Following this, he will take in the panoramic view of the Himalayas from Mukhwa Viewpoint and inspect the Uttarakhand Winter Tourism Exhibition in Harsil. Additionally, he will flag off adventure sports rallies and trekking expeditions while also addressing a public gathering.

Security and administrative arrangements have been finalized ahead of the visit. This tour is seen as a significant step toward boosting tourism in Uttarakhand and giving national recognition to its winter destinations.

Chief Minister Pushkar Singh Dhami stated that the Prime Minister’s visit will contribute to the state’s economic growth.Sakshi Singh, Akashvani News, Dehradun.”

Union Minister for Labour and Employment Dr. Mansukh Mandaviya has said, India’s social security coverage has doubled from 24.4 percent to 48.8 percent. Addressing the post-budget webinar on the theme ‘Investing in People’, the Minister emphasized that a nation’s greatest asset is its people. He added that ten new Employees’ State Insurance Corporation (ESIC) medical colleges have been approved, with plans for 10 more in the pipeline. The Minister outlined the government’s mission to skill and empower India’s youth, ensuring that Indian talent makes a global impact. He further highlighted the roadmap for Viksit Bharat by 2047. Mr. Mandaviya stated that investing in people is not just an economic decision but a social, moral, and cultural commitment toward an educated, healthy, and empowered society.

Jammu and Kashmir Chief Minister Omar Abdullah, who holds Finance portfolio, will present Economic Survey in the Legislative Assembly today. The Economic Survey for the year 2024-25 will be tabled in the House a day ahead of presentation of general budget by Mr Abdullah in the Assembly.

The Budget Session of Arunachal Pradesh Legislative Assembly will begin today with the Governor’s Address. Four bills The Arunachal Pradesh Goods & Services Tax (Amendment) Bill, 2025, The Arunachal Pradesh Arun Parivar Patra Authority Bill, 2025, The Arunachal Pradesh Public Premises (Eviction of Unauthorized Occupants) (Amendment) Bill, 2025 and The Arunachal Pradesh Flood Plain Zoning Bill, 2025 will be introduced in the state Assembly today. The five-day assembly session will conclude on March 12.

Sahitya Akademi will organize its annual Festival of Letters, Sahityaotsav, Asia’s Largest Literature Festival, at Rabindra Bhavan in New Delhi from the 7th to the 12th of March. Addressing the media in New Delhi, Secretary of Sahitya Akademi Dr. K. Sreenivasarao said that this six-day event will be inaugurated by the Union Minister for Culture, Gajendra Singh Shekhawat. Over 700 writers from different parts of the country, representing more than 50 languages, will participate in the festival. The event will feature nearly 120 sessions. The theme of this festival is Indian Literary Traditions. It will feature Young Writers, Women Writers, North East and Tribal writers, LGBTQ writers and poets, alongside renowned writers, translators, publishers, poets, and distinguished personalities from various fields. The Festival of Letters is open and free for all literary lovers and those who wish to experience the flavor of India’s longest-running Literature Festival.

In Uttar Pradesh, an active terrorist of Babbar Khalsa International (BKI) with alleged links to Pakistan’s ISI was arrested from Kaushambi district in the early hours today in a joint operation by UP STF and Punjab Police. The suspected terrorist, Lajar Masih, a resident of Kurlian village in Ramdas area of Amritsar, Punjab, was nabbed around 3.20 am. Additional Director General of Police UP Special Task Force, Law and Order Amitabh Yash said that the operation was carried out in the Kokhraj police station area of Kaushambi. He said, as per available information, the arrested terrorist works for Swarn Singh alias Jeevan Fauji, the head of the Germany-based module of Babbar Khalsa International and is in direct touch with Pakistan-based ISI operatives. UP STF has recovered some explosives material and illegal arms from the terrorist. The ADG said, this terrorist had escaped from judicial custody in Punjab on September 24 last year.

External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar has said that the US administration under President Donald Trump is moving towards multipolarity which suits India’s interests. Dr Jaishankar said that the United States’ foreign policy shift under President Trump was expected and it suits India in many ways. External Affairs Minister was addressing a session titled ‘India’s rise and role in the world’ at the Chatham House think tank in London yesterday. Dr Jaishankar is on a six-day visit to the UK and Ireland.

Responding to a question on resolving issues in Kashmir, Dr Jaishankar said removing Article 370 was step number one, restoring growth and economic activity and social justice in Kashmir was step number two, and holding elections with a very high turnout was step number three. He said, India is waiting for the return of the stolen part of Kashmir which is under illegal Pakistani occupation. When that is done, Kashmir issue will be solved.

On China, Dr Jaishankar noted some positive movement since October 2024, including the opening of the Mount Kailash pilgrimage route in Tibet.

US has paused intelligence sharing with Ukraine alongside a military aid freeze, part of a pressure campaign to force its government to cooperate with the White House’s plans to end the country’s war with Russia. The C.I.A. director, John Ratcliffe, and the national security adviser, Michael Waltz, both confirmed the pause in intelligence support but suggested it could be short-lived if Ukraine quickly came back to the negotiating table.

In U.S, a 27-year-old student from Telangana was shot dead by unidentified persons in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. Praveen, a native of Keshampet Mandal in Shadnagar Constituency, Rangareddy district, was in his second year of a Master’s program while working a part-time job. The tragic incident has left his family and community deeply shocked. His family members informed that the circumstances leading to his death were not clear. More details are awaited.

US President Donald Trump issued a stern ultimatum to Hamas, demanding the immediate release of all hostages held in Gaza, along with the return of the bodies of those killed. His warning came just hours after the White House confirmed it was engaged in direct negotiations with the militant group, a move that marks a shift in US policy. He further pledged to provide Israel with everything it needs to eliminate Hamas, warning that not a single Hamas member will be safe if the hostages are not released. In a social media post, Trump described the situation as urgent, saying that Hamas should not delay the release of the hostages or the bodies of those killed. He condemned their actions, calling them “sick and twisted” for holding on to the bodies of victims.

In Pakistan, at least four people were killed and five others injured in an explosion in a market in the restive Balochistan province yesterday. Police said, an improvised explosive device (IED) was attached to a motorbike which was parked in the Naal Bazaar of Khuzdar. According to the reports, some vehicles were also destroyed in the blast. In a statement, Balochistan Chief Minister Mir Sarfraz Bugti strongly condemned the blast saying that orders were issued to provide the injured with the best medical care available.

Eyal Zamir has taken over as the 24th Chief of the Israel Defense Forces (IDF). He has warned that Israel’s multi-front conflict would be ongoing and vowed to strike its enemies with tremendous force. Zamir assumed his new role during a change-of-command ceremony at the Kirya military base in Tel Aviv following government approval of his appointment.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has emphasized on promoting ’round the year’ tourism in Uttarakhand to strengthen economy and create jobs for the youth. Addressing a public meeting at Harsil in Uttarkashi district of the state today, Mr Modi stressed on the need for diversification of the tourism sector in the state, appealing to the people to visit Uttarakhand in winter season. The Prime Minister said, there should be no off-season in the picturesque hill state. He said, it will allow visitors to experience state’s divine landscapes all throughout the year.

Highlighting the state’s winter beauty, Prime Minister Modi noted that vacant hotels during this season cause economic imbalance, even though winter showcases Uttarakhand’s true charm.

The Prime Minister also outlined major infrastructure projects approved for the state.

The Prime Minister further advocated promoting Uttarakhand as a destination for weddings, calling it a perfect place for memorable celebrations. Mr Modi arrived in Uttarkashi this morning on a day-long visit to Uttarakhand.

Earlier in the day, the Prime Minister flagged off a trek and bike rally in Harsil as part of efforts to promote tourism and boost the local economy. He then admired the panoramic view of the Himalayas from Mukhwa Viewpoint. Prime Minister Modi also offered prayers at Mukhwa temple — the winter abode of Goddess Ganga in Uttarkashi district.

Electronics and Information Technology Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw has stressed that India is adopting a pragmatic and techno-legal approach to Artificial intelligence (AI) governance. In an article written by him in a national daily, the Minister said, the Government is investing in AI-driven safeguards against deepfakes, cybersecurity threats, and privacy risks, ensuring responsible AI deployment. He said, India wants to leverage technology for inclusive growth while maintaining a regulatory framework that fosters innovation.

The Minister said, India has long been a global leader in software development, but now, the nation is making rapid strides in hardware manufacturing. He said, with five semiconductor plants under construction, India is strengthening its position in the global electronics sector. Mr Vaishnaw said, the country will soon reach a major milestone with the launch of India’s first Make in India semiconductor chip this year.

Mr Vaishnaw asserted that the nation is shaping its digital future with a strong focus on Digital Public Infrastructure, Artificial Intelligence, Semiconductors, and Electronics manufacturing.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has emphasized on the need for fostering partnerships with global cooperative organizations to expand the Indian cooperative sector. He also stressed on promoting organic products through cooperative organizations. The Prime Minister today chaired a high-level meeting to review the progress of the cooperative sector in the country.

During the meeting, Mr. Modi also suggested focusing on export and developing a soil testing model to improve agricultural practices. Prime Minister highlighted the importance of integrating UPI with RuPay, KCC cards to facilitate financial transactions. He also talked about promoting cooperative farming as a more sustainable agricultural model recommending the use of digital public infrastructure (Agristack) to expand agriculture and related activities in the Cooperative Sector. Mr. Modi proposed to introduce cooperative courses in schools, colleges, and IIMs, as well as promoting successful cooperative organizations to inspire future generations.

During the meeting, the Prime Minister was briefed about National Cooperation Policy and key achievements of the Ministry of Cooperation over the past three and a half years. With Bhupendra Bhanu Pratap Singh Akashvani News, Delhi

The award was received by Minister of State for External Affairs Pabitra Margherita on behalf of Prime Minister Modi in a ceremony held in Bridgetown, Barbados.

Electronics and IT Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw has said that India will develop its own Graphics Processing Unit (GPU) within the next 3 to 4 years. He said the cost of India’s AI model computation GPU is the lowest in the world, at around 67 rupees per hour after a 40 percent government subsidy. Launching the AI Kosh, AI Compute Portal, and other AI-based initiatives in New Delhi, Mr. Vaishnaw said that one year ago, the India AI mission was approved by the cabinet, and the country has achieved a lot in the field of Artificial Intelligence. He said that earlier, the government had set a target of ten thousand GPUs but has surpassed the target and reached 14 thousand.

The Minister said that in the coming five years, India will be among the top five nations in the world in key technology sectors like Semiconductors, Artificial Intelligence, and Telecom.

India is taking a major stride in Artificial Intelligence by creating world-class AI infrastructure and a robust ecosystem for start-ups and innovators. Initiatives like the IndiaAI Mission and the establishment of Centres of Excellence for AI are strengthening the country’s Artificial Intelligence ecosystem, paving the way for innovation and self-reliance.

These efforts align with the vision of Viksit Bharat by 2047, leveraging cutting-edge technology for economic growth, governance, and societal progress. With the approval of the IndiaAI Mission in 2024, the government allocated 10,300 crore rupees over five years to strengthen AI capabilities.

On the occasion, Minister of State for Health and Family Welfare Anupriya Patel highlighted several initiatives being implemented by the government for the welfare of society.
