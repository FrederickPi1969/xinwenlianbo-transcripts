## Morning News

The Monsoon session of Parliament will begin today and will run until August 21, with 21 sittings.This is the first session after Operation Sindoor. Parliamentary Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju said key legislative and other business will be taken up.

“During the session, seventeen Bills are expected to be taken up for discussion and passage. Important legislations including Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Bill – 2025, the National Sports Governance Bill – 2025 and the Merchant Shipping Bill – 2024 will be taken up. The Bills of Lading Bill- 2024, The Carriage of Goods by Sea Bill – 2024, The Coastal Shipping Bill – 2024, The Indian Ports Bill – 2025, The Income-tax Bill – 2025, are also likely to be taken up for discussion. The Government has said it is ready to discuss important issues to be raised in Parliament. With SUPARNA SAIKIA, AMAN YADAV AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

Yesterday, the government held an all party meeting of both the Houses of Parliament to seek cooperation from opposition parties for the smooth conduct of the session. Union Minister and Leader of the House in the Rajya Sabha JP Nadda chaired the meeting. Parliamentary Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju stated that the Government is prepared and ready to discuss any other important issue in the two Houses as per rules of both Houses.

Leaders of different political parties expressed their views on various issues likely to be raised during the Monsoon Session and assured the Government of providing full cooperation. Replying to a media query on Justice Varma impeachment matter, Mr Rijiju said, the process will be undertaken together by all parties as it is not a move by the Government alone. The motion for the removal of the Justice Varma has already gathered significant backing from lawmakers and the number of signatures of MPs for the motion has already crossed 100.

Congress leader Gaurav Gogoi demanded Prime Minister Modi’s statement on the floor of the House on Trump’s claims, the Pahalgam terror attack and the Special Intensive Revision in Bihar.

BJD leader Sasmit Patra said, his party will raise the law and situation and atrocities against women in Odisha during the session.

In Bihar, the Monsoon session of the state legislature will begin today. It will go on till July 25, with five sittings scheduled. It will be the last session of the 17th Bihar Assembly, ahead of expected elections in October-November. The First Supplementary Budget demand for the current financial year will be tabled today. Over 10 legislative and amendment bills are expected to be introduced.

The monsoon session of the Goa Legislative assembly will also begin today and  will continue until August 8. The government will introduce 16 bills in the assembly, and 15 private member resolutions will be discussed.

The Assam government has launched a scheme to provide a subsidy of 5 rupees per litre of milk to farmers in a bid to boost the dairy sector in the state.

“Assam Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma speaking at  the ‘bhoomi pujan’ programme for doubling the processing capacity of a plant of West Assam Milk Producers’ Cooperative Union Ltd at Guwahati informed that State government has formally rolled out the subsidy scheme for dairy farmers. He said that from now onwards the dairy farmers will receive subsidy of Rs 5 per litre for the milk they give for processing at cooperative societies.

According to the Chief Minister, this scheme will encourage  dairy farmers to increase production, boost their income and bring a dairy revolution in the rural areas. Aminul Haque Jwadder, Akashvani News, GUWAHATI.”

The International Monetary Fund (IMF) says, India leads the world in fast payments with the Unified Payments Interface (or UPI) recording 18.39 billion transactions in June this year. According to a recent IMF note titled ‘Growing Retail Digital Payments: The Value of Interoperability’, UPI has revolutionized India’s payment ecosystem.

“Launched in the year 2016 by the National Payments Corporation of India, UPI has revolutionised the country’s payment ecosystem by enabling users to link multiple bank accounts to a single mobile app and make instant transactions with ease, IMF said.  With 491 million users and 65 million merchants onboard, UPI connects 675 banks through a single digital framework. This shift has taken India away from cash and card-based payments and pushed it towards a digital-first economy. Millions of individuals and small businesses now rely on UPI for safe and low-cost transactions. AISHWARYA RAI NIGAM, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

India’s electronics exports have surged by 47 percent in the first quarter of the current fiscal.

“According to Commerce Ministry, US, UAE, and China have emerged as top three export destinations for India’s electronics sector during April-June quarter. The Netherlands and Germany are other major export destinations for the country’s electronic exports. This geographical spread highlights India’s growing integration into the global electronics supply chain and underscores the country’s emergence as a credible alternative manufacturing hub in Asia. The data also showed that the US remains the dominant export destination for India’s ready-made garments. The US is followed by UK, UAE, Germany and Spain. These figures refleect India’s continued competitiveness in the global apparel market. YASHASVI KIRYAL, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

## Midday News

The Monsoon session of Parliament has begun today. Prime Minister Narendra Modi has expressed hope that the Session will be productive and filled with enriching discussions, which will strengthen the country’s democracy. Addressing the media outside Parliament this morning, before the beginning of the Session, the Prime Minister said, the session stands as a proud moment for the nation, a true celebration of our collective achievements.

The Prime Minister said, the whole world has witnessed the strength of India’s military power. He said, during Operation Sindoor, Indian soldiers achieved their objective with 100 per cent success, demolishing the masterminds behind terrorism in their hideouts.

Prime Minister Modi said, rising above party lines and prioritizing the nation’s interest, representatives from most political parties travelled to various countries and collectively led a highly effective campaign to expose Pakistan’s role on the international stage.

The Prime Minister said, India has endured many violent challenges, be it terrorism or Naxalism, but today, the influence of Naxalism and Maoism is shrinking rapidly. He said, the red corridors of the past are now transforming into green zones of growth and development. He said, today, India is moving ahead at a fast pace.

The Prime Minister remarked that the monsoon symbolizes innovation and renewal, highlighting that current weather conditions across the country are progressing favourably, bringing beneficial forecasts for agriculture.

On the first day of the Monsoon session, both Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha witnessed adjournments amid sloganeering by the opposition members over various issues including discussion on Operation Sindoor. The presiding officer repeatedly appealed to the opposition members to take their seats and participate in the zero hour. The two elected Members are Birendra Prasad Baishya of AGP and Kanad Purkayastha of BJP from Assam. The nominated Members are Dr Meenakshi Jain, C Sadanandan Master and Harsh Vardhan Shringla. The Bombay High Court today acquitted 12 persons convicted in the 2006 Mumbai train blasts case, citing lack of conclusive evidence. A special bench of Justices Anil Kilor and Shyam Chandak quashed all convictions, including five death penalties and seven life sentences, stating the prosecution completely failed to prove the accused committed the crime. The court directed that the accused will be released if not wanted in other cases. The blasts, on 11 July 2006, killed over 180 and injured hundred others, marking one of Mumbai’s deadliest terror attacks. The verdict comes 19 years after the tragedy.

## Evening News

The Monsoon Session of Parliament began on a stormy note today with both Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha witnessing adjournments amid ruckus by the opposition parties over various issues including demand for immediate discussion on Pahalgam terror attack and Operation Sindoor. Later, both the Houses were adjourned for the day.

“On the first day of the Monsoon Session, both the Houses witnessed uproar in the morning after the opposition MPs raised slogans over several issues, including the recent terrorist attack in Pahalgam and India’s response through Operation Sindoor. The opposition continuously demanded a discussion over these  these matters. The  Parliamentary Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju and Defence Minister Rajnath Singh assured that the government is ready to discuss every issue in Parliament. But due to the uproar by the opposition, the Lok Sabha was  adjourned multiple times. When the House  reassembled at 4 PM after being adjourned  at 2:00 PM, the scene was no different forcing the Chair to adjourn the Lok Sabha for the day. While the  Rajya Sabha transacted smooth business except one adjournment in the beginning when it was assembled for proceeding. It took up  the Question Hour and Special mention and also passed the Bills of Lading Bill 2025 after discussion. With Anand Kumar, Bhupendra Singh, Akashvani News, Delhi”

Meanwhile, a meeting of Business Advisory Committee of Parliament was held at Parliament House in which it was decided to discuss Operation Sindoor in the Lok Sabha which was launched by the Armed Forces in response to the Pahalgam terror attack. The Meeting held at Parliament House, has allocated 16-hours for the debate in the Lok Sabha over this matter. Besides, 12 hours have been allocated in the Lower House for the discussion on the Income Tax Bill, 2025 and nine hours for the debate over it in the Rajya Sabha.

On the other hand, several Members of Parliament today submitted a memorandum to Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla to remove Justice Yashwant Varma. A total of 145 Lok Sabha members have signed the impeachment motion against Justice Verma under Articles 124, 217, and 218 of the Constitution.

MPs from various parties, including from BJP, Congress, TDP, JDU, JD(S), and CPM have signed the memorandum. These include BJP MP Anurag Singh Thakur, Congress MP and Leader of the Opposition Rahul Gandhi and Supriya Sule of NCP-SCP among others.

Earlier, Prime Minister Narendra Modi expressed hope that the Monsoon session will be productive and filled with enriching discussions, which will strengthen the country’s democracy. Mr Modi was addressing the media outside Parliament this morning, before the beginning of the session. The Prime Minister said, the session stands as a proud moment for the nation, a true celebration of the country’s collective achievements. He added that the world witnessed the strength of India’s military power during Operation Sindoor.

Parliament has passed the Bills of Lading Bill 2025 with the Rajya Sabha approving it today. The Lok Sabha had already passed the legislation in March this year. It aims to update and simplify the legal framework for shipping documents.  It will replace the Indian Bills of Lading Act, 1856. A bill of lading refers to a document issued by a freight carrier to a shipper. It contains details including the type, quantity, condition, and destination of goods being carried.

Replying to a debate on the bill in the Rajya Sabha, Union Minister of Ports, Shipping and Waterways Sarbananda Sonowal said that after the Narendra Modi Government came to power in 2014, the vision plan of the Ministry was developed under which Sagarmala programme was undertaken and more than 805 projects have been identified.

Earlier, speaking outside Parliament, Congress leader Rahul Gandhi alleged that ministers are only allowed to speak in the House, but Opposition members are not allowed to speak.
