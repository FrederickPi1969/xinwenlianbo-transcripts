Defence Minister Rajnath Singh and Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath will flag off the first batch of BrahMos missiles manufactured at the BrahMos Aerospace unit in Lucknow today.

Lucknow is set to make history in defence production today as the first batch of BrahMos missiles manufactured at the BrahMos Aerospace Unit in Sarojini Nagar area will roll out.

The Lucknow BrahMos unit is the first facility in the Uttar Pradesh Defence Corridor where the entire process, from missile system manufacturing to final testing, is carried out indigenously.

During the ceremony, the defence minister and chief minister will inaugurate the Booster Building and witness a demonstration of the booster docking process. A court in Belgium has approved the extradition of fugitive diamond trader Mehul Choksi to India. Mehul Choksi is wanted in the 13,000 crore rupees Punjab National Bank (PNB) fraud case. The court in Antwerp yesterday ruled that Choksi’s arrest by Belgian police, based on India’s request, was valid. This marks a major step toward bringing the fugitive diamond trader back to India to face justice. Mehul Choksi was arrested on 11th of April by Antwerp police following a formal extradition request from the Central Bureau of Investigation. He has remained in custody since then, with multiple bail pleas denied due to concerns he might flee.

In Afghanistan, at least ten civilians were killed and a dozen more were injured last night after Pakistan launched air strike on a border province of Paktika, breaking a ceasefire that had brought two days of calm to the border.

The 48-hour truce paused nearly a week of border clashes that killed dozens of troops and civilians on both sides. A senior Taliban official said, Pakistan broke the ceasefire and bombed three locations in Paktika province. The official said, Afghanistan will retaliate. Among the dead include at least three local cricket players. The killing of the three cricketers prompted Afghanistan to pull out of a tri-nation series with Pakistan and Sri Lanka scheduled for next month. Afghanistan’s cricket board in a social media post said the players had traveled to Paktika Province to take part in a friendly match.

Media reports said, Pakistani drones also struck Khandar, a village in the Afghan province’s Argun district.

The countries had reached an initial 48-hour cease-fire on Wednesday, hours after Pakistan carried out air strikes in Afghanistan and the two sides exchanged artillery and gunfire along their disputed border.WITH KAUMUDEE PAREEK, PAYAL SHARMA, AKASHVANI NEWS.

The GST Bachat Utsav, announced by Prime Minister Narendra Modi and launched on the 22nd of last month, is benefiting people across all sections of society. Today, our correspondent brings a report on the GST reforms that will boost the economy of Tamil Nadu.

The new GST reform is a significant boost for Tamil Nadu’s farmers, artisans, industries, and exporters. By cutting tax rates across various sectors, the reforms have reduced prices for consumers and strengthened the margins of traders. Tamil Nadu’s textile and handloom sector now witness strong gains. The reduced GST on zari from 12 percent to 5 percent has  made the state’s popular Kanchipuram sarees affordable.

Similarly, the coir industry units of the state, benefit from the new GST rates of 5 per cent on coir mats, ropes, and geo-textiles. This move will significantly support women and artisans working in the sector.

In order to support small processors and women of fisherfolk population, the GST on processed fish and seafood has been slashed from 12 percent to 5 percent. By easing costs for both households and industries, the reforms will strengthen rural livelihoods while boosting Tamil Nadu’s position as an industrial hub. With Samarth, Ishani Yadav, Akashvani News, Delhi.

The GST Bachat Utsav, announced by Prime Minister Narendra Modi and launched on the 22nd of last month, is benefiting people across all sections of society. Today, our correspondent brings a report on the GST reforms that will boost the economy of Andhra Pradesh.

The new GST reform is a major boost for Andhra Pradesh’s farmers, artisans, industries and exporters. By lowering tax rates across vital sectors, the changes aim to reduce costs, improve margins, and strengthen the state’s economy. Under the revised GST structure, traditional craft and agro-based industries witness major benefits now, as products such as  leather puppetry crafts, and temple-stone carvings are now taxed at 5 percent instead of the previous 12 percent. This has made local art and culture-based products more affordable, increasing sales and ensuring better earnings for rural artisans.The coastal fisheries sector of the state now attracts only 5 percent GST, reduced from the previous 12 percent. It has made Andhra’s marine exports more competitive while supporting employment in coastal communities. Similarly, the automobile and auto-components industry now sees GST on three-wheelers, small cars, and motorcycles up to 350 cc reduced from 28 percent to 18 percent. This 10 percent reduction has made vehicles more affordable for buyers and improved competitiveness for manufacturers. The reforms strengthen the state’s economy and, ensures inclusive fair market for the people. With Samarth, Rohan, Akashvani News, Delhi.

The boat, which was carrying a total of 14 Indian nationals, overturned in the waters off Beira. The Indian High Commission expressed its condolences over the loss of lives. The Commission said, five Indian nationals were successfully rescued from the mishap and are safe. One of the survivors is currently receiving medical treatment at a hospital in Beira. The High Commission said that search and rescue operations are underway to locate the five missing Indian nationals. Local authorities, maritime agencies, and the Indian Mission are jointly coordinating the efforts. The Indian High Commission assured that it is closely monitoring the situation and remains in close contact with local officials as search operations continue.
