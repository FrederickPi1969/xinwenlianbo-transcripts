## Morning News

Delhi BJP legislative party meeting will be held in New Delhi today. All the newly elected BJP MLAs and the party’s senior leaders will participate in the deliberations. Prime Minister Narendra Modi has lauded the inauguration of the Inland Waterways Transport, IWT Terminal on the Brahmaputra, National Waterway-2 at Jogighopa in Assam. In a social media post, he described it as a significant step toward improving infrastructure and promoting inland waterways for the progress and prosperity of the region.

Union Minister of Ports, Shipping, and Waterways Sarbananda Sonowal, along with Bhutan’s Minister of Finance Lyonpo Namgyal Dorji, inaugurated the IWT Terminal at Jogighopa. The terminal is located at a distance of 91 km from Gelephu in Bhutan, 108 km from Bangladesh border and 147 km from Guwahati.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah has asked the administration in Jammu and Kashmir to ensure the full implementation of the three new criminal laws in the Union Territory by April this year. He said this while chairing a review meeting on the implementation of the three new criminal laws in Jammu and Kashmir, in the presence of Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha and Chief Minister Omar Abdullah in New Delhi yesterday. Mr. Shah said that optimum use of technology should be made to ensure speedy justice under the three new criminal laws.

Speaking to the media after the meeting, Chief Minister Omar Abdullah said efforts will be made to raise awareness among the people of Jammu and Kashmir about these new laws.

The Uttar Pradesh government and Mahakumbh Mela authority have dismissed rumours claiming the Prayagraj Mahakumbh has been extended beyond the 26th of this month. Prayagraj District Magistrate Ravindra Mandhad clarified that the rumour, circulating widely on social media, is entirely false.

“The Mahakumbh will conclude on its scheduled date on 26th February. During the remaining days, arrangements are being made for a smooth visit and to ensure that people return to their destinations after taking a holy dip in the Sangam. District Magistrate Mandhad said that the schedule of the Mahakumbh fair is released according to the auspicious time and is decided in advance. Mr Mandhad appealed to the devotees not to pay attention to any kind of rumour, as there is no proposal from the government and the district administration to extend the fair. He added that traffic management is a high priority with work being done to balance the movement of devotees without affecting the everyday life in Prayagraj. The DM also clarified that no railway station has been closed without prior notice. He added that since the station is adjacent to the fair, the Prayag Sangam Railway station has been permanently closed to avoid large crowd gathering. Meanwhile, all other stations in Prayagraj are operational. With Abhishek Kapil, Adarsh for Akashvani News, Prayagraj.”

India and Qatar have agreed to elevate their bilateral relations to a Strategic Partnership. An agreement in this regard was signed between the two countries following delegation-level talks between Prime Minister Narendra Modi and the Amir of Qatar, Sheikh Tamim Bin Hamad Al-Thani, in New Delhi.

Briefing the media in the National Capital last evening, Secretary in the External Affairs Ministry, Arun Kumar Chatterjee, said, two agreements and five MoUs were signed between both the nations. He said the first agreement is on the establishment of a bilateral strategic partnership. A revised double taxation avoidance agreement was also signed between the two countries.

The Secretary said, the two countries have also agreed to double their trade to 28 billion dollars annually in the next five years.

President Droupadi Murmu has said that India’s relations with Qatar are steeped in history, spanning centuries, and that Qatar has been an integral part of West Asia’s commercial and cultural linkages with India. Welcoming the Amir of Qatar, Sheikh Tamim Bin Hamad Al Thani, at Rashtrapati Bhavan last evening, the President emphasized that the multi-faceted engagement and cooperation between the two countries are marked by a deep sense of comfort and time-tested goodwill. Both leaders agreed that elevating the India-Qatar relationship to the level of a ‘strategic partnership’ will create a roadmap for even closer engagement.

## Midday News

The High-Level Committee, under the Chairmanship of Union Home Minister Amit Shah, has approved additional Central assistance of over one thousand 554 crore rupees from the National Disaster Response Fund (NDRF) to five states affected by natural calamities such as floods, flash floods, landslides, and cyclonic storms during the past year.

According to the Home Ministry, 608 crore rupees has been approved for Andhra Pradesh, over 170 crore for Nagaland, over 255 crore for Odisha, more than 231 crore for Telangana and over 288 crore rupees for Tripura.

The Home Ministry said, this additional support is separate from the funds already allocated to the states by the Centre under the State Disaster Response Fund (SDRF). During the financial year 2024-25, the Central government released more than 18 thousand 322 crore rupees to 27 states under the SDRF and over four thousand 808 crore rupees from the NDRF to 18 states.

The 395th birth anniversary of the great Maratha Warrior-King Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj is being observed today. Born on this day in 1630 at the Shivneri Fort  in Pune district in Maharashtra, Chhatrapati Shivaji laid the foundation of the Maratha Empire and challenged the Mughal rule. He is widely admired for his progressive leadership, military acumen, bravery, wisdom, and the relentless pursuit of Swarajya.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi paid homage to Chhatrapati Shivaji on his Jayanti. In a social media post, Mr. Modi said, the valour and visionary leadership of Chhatrapati Shivaji laid the foundation for Swarajya and inspired generations to uphold the values of courage and justice.

Speaking on the occasion, Mr Mandaviya emphasized that, just as Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj laid the foundation for Hindavi Swarajya through ideal governance and respect for women, the Narendra Modi government is working to build a developed India, inspired by his principles.

Millions  of devotees continue to arrive at the ongoing Mahakumbh Mela in Prayagraj, Uttar Pradesh, with more than 65 lakh devotees having taken a holy dip in the Sangam by 12 noon today. Our Correspondent reports that the iconic Bade Hanuman temple holds immense cultural and religious significance, drawing countless devotees every day, especially after their ritual dip in the holy waters.

“The temple, also known as the Lete Hanuman Temple, located on the banks of Sangam in Prayagraj is very popular with the statue of Lord Hanuman in a lying posture. A huge crowd of devotees is seen everyday in this temple during the kumbh, while earlier the crowd used to be only during its conventionally popular visiting days of Tuesdays and Saturdays. According to religious believes, visit to the temple after bathing in Sangam bears great fruits. It is said that when Lord Hanuman was returning after the conquest from Lanka, he felt tired. Upon the request of Mother Sita, he lied down on the banks of Sangam, after which the Lete Hanuman temple was built. Talking to Akashvani News, Aditi, a devotee from West Bengal, shared her experience of her visit to the temple after taking a bath in the Triveni Sangam with her mother.

According to mythology, after the water level in the Sangam rises, the temple gets filled with water and the statue drowns. It is said that this action bathes Lord Hanuman in the holy water. With Abhishek Kapil, Adarsh for Akashvani News, Prayagraj.“

The illegal Indian immigrants who have been deported from the United States in the last 10 days are a dejected lot. Some of them were unsuspecting victims of unscrupulous travel agents who duped them first by showing green pastures abroad and then sending them through illegal means, some others are such who just wanted to go abroad by whatever means. Most of them belong to middle class families settled in small towns and villages having less employment opportunities. According to sources, the last plane that landed at Amritsar in Punjab carrying 112 immigrants had 23 females as well along with 14 kids, both boys and girls. Our Jalandhar correspondent has filed this report:

“All the deportees are having harrowing tales to tell. As reported, many of them have travelled long distances in four wheelers packed like sardines, crossed Panama Canal by dangerous boat journeys, walked through jungles without proper food, jumped over high walls to enter the US. The mental trauma after experiencing all this and then deportation seems to have shattered them emotionally. They have urged the state government to take action against the travel agents who have duped them.

Almost everyone has spent over Rs. 40 Lakh to sneak into the US by selling their family farm lands, mothers’ jewellery, taking money on loan from banks and friends, that have all gone down the drains now. Rajesh Bali, Akashvani News, Jalandhar.”
