The Government has launched Bharat Taxi on the first of this month for customers which is a cooperative-based mobility initiative aimed at providing a fair, transparent, and driver-owned alternative in India’s ride-hailing sector. Guided by the vision of the Union Minister of Home Affairs and Cooperation, Amit Shah, the initiative places drivers at the centre of ownership, governance, and profit-sharing.

“The launch of Bharat Taxi was announced on 25th March this year during a Parliament session, in response to persistent challenges faced by drivers and commuters. It operates under Sahakar Taxi Cooperative Limited, registered under the Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act, 2002. Earlier, drivers often grappled with lack of platform ownership, unclear incentive structures, long working hours, and absence of social security, while riders used to face unreliable services, frequent cancellations, surge pricing, and limited transparency in fares. But now, this platform provides reasonable fares, profit redistribution to drivers, and democratic decision-making. In terms of progress, the driver app was launched in Delhi-NCR on 14th November, registering over 1.1 lakh drivers, while the customer app saw more than 80 thousand registrations within ten days. A Pakistani court today sentenced the jailed former prime minister Imran Khan and his wife Bushra Bibi to 17 years imprisonment each in the Toshakhana 2 corruption case. The case is about alleged fraud in state gifts, which the former first couple received from the Saudi government in 2021. Special court judge Shahrukh Arjumand announced the judgment in the case in Rawalpindi’s high-security Adiala Jail. Imran Khan and his wife were awarded 10 years’ rigorous imprisonment under Section 409 of the Pakistan Penal Code and seven years under different Sections of the Prevention of Corruption Act. India stands proudly as the world’s third-largest domestic civil aviation market, reflecting the rapid growth of the sector. He was speaking at a seminar organised by the Directorate General of Civil Aviation under the Ministry of Civil Aviation. He said that over the past decade, passenger traffic within the country has grown at an impressive average rate of 9 per cent annually, while cargo volumes have risen by 2.9 per cent.

The Ministry of Civil Aviation has directed all airlines to strictly comply with passenger facilitation norms in view of the prevailing fog and low-visibility conditions affecting flight operations.
