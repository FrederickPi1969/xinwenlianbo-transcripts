## Morning News

The Budget session of Parliament will begin today with President Droupadi Murmu addressing a joint sitting of both Houses this morning. Following the President’s address, Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman will present the Economic Survey during a brief session of both Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha.

Our correspondent reports that the presentation of the Economic Survey marks the official start of the Budget session in Parliament.

The Economic Survey is a comprehensive review of the country’s current economic state and future outlook. It is prepared by the Economic Division of the Department of Economic Affairs under the guidance of the Chief Economic Advisor. Finance Minister Sitharaman will present the Union Budget tomorrow. The Lok Sabha and the Rajya Sabha will discuss the Motion of Thanks on the President’s Address on Monday. 16 Bills including the Finance Bill 2025, the Waqf (Amendment) Bill, 2024, the Immigration and Foreigners Bill, 2025, the Banking Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2024 and the Railways (Amendment) Bill, 2024 will be taken up for discussion and passage. The first part of the Budget session will be held from today till 13th February while the second leg of the session will be held from 10th March to 4th April. Suparna Saikia, Akashvani News, Delhi.

The Government has announced that India is all set to launch its own safe and secure indigenous Artificial intelligence (AI) model at an affordable cost. Briefing the media in New Delhi yesterday, Union Minister for Electronics and Information Technology Ashwini Vaishnaw highlighted that compared to global models costing 2.5 to 3 dollars per hour usage, India’s AI Model will cost less than 100 rupees per hour after 40 per cent government subsidy. The Union Minister expressed hope that the Indian AI model is likely to be ready within 6 months.

On a query on privacy concerns around DeepSeek, Mr Vaishnaw informed that it will be hosted on Indian servers soon after security protocol checks so that users, coders, developers can benefit from its Open Source Code.

Senior BJP leader and Prime Minister Narendra Modi will address a rally in Dwarka this afternoon. Other senior party leaders including Union Minister Nitin Gadkari and Uttarakhand CM Pushkar Singh Dhami will also be holding public meetings at different constituencies.

The judicial commission set up by the Uttar Pradesh government to find the reasons behind the Maha Kumbh incident will visit Sangam Ghat, Prayagraj today.

The commission will investigate the cause and circumstances of the incident and submit its report within one month. They will also recommend measures to prevent such incidents in the future. The three-member commission is headed by a retired Allahabad High Court Judge, Harsh Kumar.

Uttar Pradesh Chief Secretary Manoj Kumar Singh and DGP Prashant Kumar yesterday visited Sangam Ghat at Mahakumbh and inspected the incident area which claimed 30 lives. They urged the administration to stay motivated and adopt a build it better approach, ensuring that the upcoming Amrit Snan is flawless, with zero error in arrangements. They also emphasized that maximum officers must be deployed in high-density areas.

District Magistrate of Prayagraj, Ravindra Kumar Mandar, has denied media reports about a ban on vehicle entry due to road diversions. A viral social media post claimed that vehicle entry into Prayagraj would be restricted until 4th of February.

In a video message, Mr. Mandar clarified that there will be no diversions on 31st January, 1st February, and February 4th. He explained that vehicle entry will be restricted in Prayagraj only on 2nd February and 3rd February, due to the Amrit Snan on Vasant Panchami.

United States President Donald Trump has confirmed that there are no survivors in the mid-air collision between an American Airlines regional jet, and an Army Black Hawk Helicopter in Washington, DC. Addressing a press briefing on the aviation disaster last night, Mr. Trump said that the administration will find out how the disaster occurred and ensured nothing like this will ever happen again.

The crash, which occurred near Ronald Reagan National Airport, involved an American Airlines regional jet carrying 60 passengers and 4 crew members, and a US Black Hawk helicopter carrying three soldiers. Meanwhile, the Kremlin confirmed that two renowned former Russian figure skaters, Evgenia Shishkova and Vadim Naumov, were on board the passenger plane. Kremlin spokesman Dmitri Peskov also stated that other Russian nationals were also aboard the crashed flight.

President Droupadi Murmu has said that amidst global instability, India is setting an example to the world by emerging as a pillar of economic, social and political stability. The President said this while addressing the joint sitting of the Lok Sabha and the Rajya Sabha in the Parliament House today. She expressed confidence that the future generations of the country will definitely witness a developed, strong, capable and prosperous India by 2047.

The President said that her government is working with unprecedented pace for the development of the country and to ensure welfare of the people with a vision of Reform, Perform and Transform.

President Murmu said that the capital expenditure budget witnessed a record allocation of 11 lakh crore rupees which was only two lakh crore rupees 10 years ago. Highlighting the steps taken for building a robust infrastructure in the country, she said, the country is moving forward from Make in India to Make for the World which is creating employment opportunities. She said, the government has sanctioned 70 thousand crore rupees to connect 25 thousand habitations under the fourth phase of the Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana. She said, massive work has been done to extend the metro network in the country which has crossed the one thousand kilometer milestone. She said, the Udhampur-Srinagar-Baramulla rail link project has been completed and now the country will be connected by railway line from Kashmir to Kanyakumari. She said, the government is working to develop a modern and self-reliant agricultural system with the focus on doubling the income of farmers and giving appropriate rates to farm produce. She said, the government has started ‘Mission Mausam’ with the outlay of two thousand crore rupees to make the country weather-ready and climate-smart. She said, 41 thousand crore rupees has been disbursed to farmers in recent months under the PM Kisan Samman Nidhi scheme. The President said, the government has taken historic steps to protect the country’s borders and ensure internal security. She said, development of the border areas of the country is an important part of the nation’s strategy along with protecting the borders. She said the government has also made swift progress on critical issues like One Nation-One Election and the Waqf Act Amendment.

India’s real GDP is estimated to grow by 6.4 per cent in the financial year 2025-26 and the growth is likely to range between 6.3 and 6.8 per cent in the next financial year. This was projected in the Economic Survey presented by the Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman in both the Houses of Parliament today.

The Economic Survey highlights that the fundamentals of the domestic economy remain robust, with calibrated fiscal consolidation and stable private consumption. It mentioned that the country has displayed steady economic growth supported by the agriculture and services sector. Rural demand is witnessing improvement on the back of record Kharif production and favourable agricultural conditions. It says, the manufacturing sector faced pressures due to weak global demand and domestic seasonal conditions.

Chief Economic Advisor (CEA) Dr. V. Ananth Nageswaran has said that agriculture is the sector of the future, as there is enough scope to raise the contribution of agriculture by 0.75 per cent to one per cent in terms of GDP growth. Briefing the media in New Delhi after the tabling of the Economic Survey in Parliament, Mr. Nageswaran said the area under micro-irrigation has increased threefold.

The CEA said, in terms of infrastructure, India has done well with new airports, trains, and reduced turnaround times at ports. He said, there has also been an expansion in telecom and power sector capacity.

President Droupadi Murmu today said, amid global instability, India is setting an example to the world by emerging as a pillar of economic, social and political stability. Addressing the joint sitting of both houses of Parliament at the beginning of the Budget session this morning, the President said, her government is working with unprecedented pace for the development of the country and to ensure welfare of the people with a vision to Reform, Perform and Transform. President Murmu also expressed confidence that the future generations of the country will witness a developed, strong, capable and prosperous India by 2047.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said, President Droupadi Murmu’s address was a resonant outline of India’s path towards building a Viksit Bharat. In a social media post, the Prime Minister said, President Murmu highlighted initiatives across sectors and underscored the importance of all-round as well as futuristic development.

Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman will present the Union Budget for FY25-26 tomorrow, marking her eighth consecutive budget.

“The Lok Sabha and the Rajya Sabha will discuss the Motion of Thanks on the President’s Address on Monday. 16 Bills including the Finance Bill 2025, the Waqf (Amendment) Bill, 2024, the Immigration and Foreigners Bill, 2025, the Banking Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2024 and the Railways (Amendment) Bill, 2024 will be taken up for discussion and passage. The first part of the Budget session will be held till 13th February while the second leg of the session will be held from 10th March to 4th April. Suparna Saikia, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

The equity markets will be open from 9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m., while the commodity derivatives market will extend its trading until 5:00 p.m. In addition to equity and commodity derivatives trading, the Multi Commodity Exchange of India will also conduct a live trading session on the same day.
