## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will dedicate to the nation 2 Naval Ships and one submarine in Mumbai today. For the first time, three vessels of the Indian Navy are scheduled to be commissioned simultaneously. Our correspondent reports that the commissioning of these naval combatants marks a significant leap forward in realizing India’s vision of becoming a global leader in defence manufacturing and maritime security.

“The Prime Minister will dedicate naval ships INS Surat, INS Nilgiri, and submarine INS Vaghsheer to the nation during their commissioning at the Naval Dockyard in Mumbai. INS Surat, the fourth and final ship of the P15B Guided Missile Destroyer Project, ranks among the largest and most sophisticated destroyers in the world. INS Nilgiri is the first ship of the P17A Stealth Frigate Project. INS Vaghsheer, the sixth and final submarine of the P75 Scorpene Project. It is one of the most silent and versatile diesel-electric submarines in the world. Equipped with modern aviation facilities, Nilgiri and Surat can operate a range of helicopters, including Chetak, ALH, Sea King, and the newly inducted MH-60R, during both day and night operations. These ships also include specific accommodations to support a sizable complement of women officers and sailors. All three platforms have been designed and constructed entirely at Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders Limited Mumbai. Additionally, in the evening, the Prime Minister will inaugurate the Sri Sri Radha Madanmohanji Temple, an ISKCON project in Navi Mumbai. Jeevan Bhawasar, Akashvani News, Mumbai.”
