## Morning News

India beat Pakistan by five wickets to win their ninth Asia Cup at the Dubai International Cricket Stadium in Dubai, UAE, with Tilak Varma taking them with an unbeaten innings that proved decisive in the tournament’s dramatic conclusion.

The victory extended India’s perfect record against Pakistan in this tournament, having defeated them in all three encounters including group stage and Super Four matches on September 14 and 21.

India won the toss and elected to field first, with Pakistan openers Sahibzada Farhan and Fakhar Zaman providing a solid foundation with an 84-run partnership in 9 overs before being separated. However, Pakistan’s innings faltered thereafter, eventually being bowled out for 146 runs in the last over, setting India a modest target of 147 runs for victory. India’s chase began with lose of early wickets , slumping to 20/3 within the fourth over as Pakistan’s bowlers, led by Faheem Ashraf, struck repeatedly. Sanju Samson joined Varma to forge a crucial 57-run partnership that reignited Indian hopes, in the 13th over Sanju Samson  was dismissed. Shivam Dube’s arrival marked the turning point as he partnered with Tilak Varma to score 28 runs off the 15th and 16th overs, reducing the equation to 36 runs from the final four overs. The duo maintained their composure through the closing stages, with Tilak Varma remaining unbeaten on 69 runs from 53 , stealing the Asia cup in style in a last over six, and guiding  India home with two balls remaining. This is India’s record-extending ninth victory at the Asia Cup, This is Vinod Kumar for Akashvani , from Dubai.

The Centre has issued operational guidelines for rollout of nearly 72 thousand 300 public electric vehicle.

As per the guidelines, government offices, residential colonies, hospitals, and educational institutions will be eligible for 100 per cent subsidy on upstream infrastructure and EV charging equipment, provided they ensure free public access. For high-traffic public locations such as railway stations, airports, bus terminals, metro stations, municipal parking lots, public sector ports, retail outlets of state-run oil companies, and toll plazas operated by NHAI or state governments, the subsidy will cover 80 per cent of upstream infrastructure costs and 70 per cent of charging equipment costs. Shopping malls, markets, and roadside facilities along highways and expressways will also qualify for an 80 per cent subsidy on upstream infrastructure. Similarly, battery swapping or charging stations set up at any location will be eligible for 80 per cent support on upstream costs. KAUMUDEE PAREEK AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.
