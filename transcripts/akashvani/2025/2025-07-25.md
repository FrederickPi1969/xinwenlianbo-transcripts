## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has left for the Maldives after concluding his landmark visit to the United Kingdom last night. Mr Modi will be on a two-day visit to the Maldives beginning today at the invitation of Maldivian President Mohamed Muizzu.

Male is abuzz with preparations to welcome Prime Minister Narendra Modi. Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Maldives President Dr. Mohamed Muizzu will jointly review the progress of ‘Comprehensive Economic and Security Maritime Partnership’. The Prime Minister is also scheduled to meet other Maldivian leaders for discussions on a range of bilateral issues. Some MoUs, agreements in areas of bilateral cooperation are expected to be signed in this visit alongwith inauguration of various infrastructure projects. Tomorrow, Prime Minister will be the ‘Guest of Honour’ at Maldives 60th Independence day celebrations. This year also marks the 60th anniversary of establishment of diplomatic relations between India and Maldives. Jeevan Bhawasar, Akashvani News, Male, Maldives.

Talking to Akashvani News, Indian High Commissioner to the Maldives, G. Balasubramanian said that Prime Minister Modi and Maldivian President Muizzu will take stock of the progress in the implementation of the India-Maldives Joint Vision for a ‘Comprehensive Economic and Maritime Security Partnership.

Earlier yesterday, Prime Minister Modi held talks with his UK counterpart Keir Starmer in London. The two leaders discussed regional and global issues of mutual interest. India and the UK also signed a landmark Free Trade Agreement, which is expected to enhance bilateral trade, investment, and job creation.

After the signing of the FTA, Prime Minister Modi said, this is a historic day in bilateral relations between India and the UK. He said, this agreement will be beneficial to the youth, farmers, MSME and sectors in India.

Mr Modi also expressed gratitude to Prime Minister Starmer and his Government for strongly condemning the Pahalgam terror attack.

In his statement, UK Prime Minister Starmer said the Free Trade Agreement is the biggest and most economically significant trade deal that the UK has made since leaving the EU. He said, the deal will bring huge benefits to both the countries.

Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal has said that the India-UK Free Trade Agreement is a landmark deal that will boost trade, investment and employment. Talking to our Correspondent in London, Mr Goyal said that this unprecedented agreement will benefit farmers, small businessmen, MSME sectors, startups and other service sectors.

Mr Goyal said, the FTA will open UK markets to Indian goods, professionals, education, agriculture and food processing products.

Industry bodies have welcomed the signing of the India-UK free trade agreement. They have described it as a transformative step, anticipating expanded exports and increased investment across various sectors.

Sunil Bharti Mittal, Co-Chair of India-UK CEO Forum, said the agreement will stimulate innovation, ease market access, and foster investment.

Confederation of Indian Industry (CII) echoed the similar sentiment, noting that the agreement will deepen bilateral cooperation in clean energy, digital technologies, life sciences, and advanced manufacturing. CII Director General Chandrajit Banerjee said that this FTA marks a defining moment in our bilateral relationship, reflecting a shared commitment to inclusive growth, economic resilience, and industrial transformation.

Federation of Indian Chambers of Commerce and Industry (FICCI) said the agreement promises significant opportunities across diverse sectors, including agriculture, pharmaceuticals, services, auto components, and more.

Kirit Bhansali, Chairman of the Gem & Jewellery Export Promotion Council (GJEPC), called the deal a landmark accord that could nearly triple exports to the UK.

British companies also welcomed the deal, particularly those in the aerospace and spirits industries. Rolls-Royce CEO Tufan Erginbilgic said the FTA will help deepen co-development in power and propulsion technologies with Indian partners.

India-UK FTA is expected to help double bilateral trade to 120 billion dollars by 2030. CHIRAG JHA, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.

The Election Commission has said that over 99 percent electors have been covered in Bihar under the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) initiative. In a statement, the EC said that according to local BLOs, one lakh electors are untraceable. The Poll body said, 21.6 lakh electors have been found deceased, over 31.5 lakh permanently migrated and 7 lakh electors found enrolled at more than one place. Despite door-to-door visits by local BLOs, forms of around 7 lakh electors have still not been received. The Draft Electoral Roll will be published on the 1st of next month. The Election Commission has reiterated that any elector or political party can file a claim in case of missing names or raise an objection in case of wrong inclusion till the 1st of September this year.

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has issued a red alert in Odisha, West Bengal, Chhattisgarh and parts of Maharashtra for extremely heavy rainfall today. The IMD said heavy to very heavy rainfall will continue in Madhya Pradesh, Vidarbha, Chhattisgarh, Odisha, Gangetic West Bengal and Jharkhand till the 28th of this month. These conditions will also prevail in Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, East Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Konkan and Goa till the end of this month.

In the southern part of country, heavy to very heavy rainfall conditions will be present over Kerala, Mahe and Coastal and South Interior Karnataka during the next 5 days. Over Delhi-NCR, very light to light rain accompanied by thunderstorms and lightning is expected today.

French President Emmanuel Macron has said that the France will officially recognise a Palestinian state during the United Nations General Assembly in September. In his social media post, the French President said this decision to recognise the State of Palestine has been taken in accordance with the historic commitment of France to a just and lasting peace in the Middle East. He said, the urgent need today is for the war in Gaza to end and for the civilian population to be rescued.

Saying that peace is possible, he pointed out that we need an immediate ceasefire, the release of all hostages, and massive humanitarian aid to the people of Gaza. He further added that the world must also guarantee the demilitarisation of Hamas, and secure and rebuild Gaza. Meanwhile, Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has criticized this decision in the wake of the  October 7 massacre. He has called this plan a grave mistake, accusing the French President of ignoring the risks posed by Hamas and other groups.Currently, the State of Palestine is recognised by more than 140 of the 193 member states of the UN.

Veteran Wrestler Hulk Hogan passed away due to cardiac arrest at his home in Florida, United States yesterday. He was 71. Hogan had won six World Wrestling Entertainment (WWE) Championships. In a social media post, the WWE said that its Hall of Fame Hulk Hogan helped WWE achieve global recognition in the 1980s. Moreover, Hogan also starred in Hollywood movies, television shows, and his own animated series.

In Cricket, at Old Trafford in Manchester, England ended Day 2nd of the 4th Test of the Anderson-Tendulkar Trophy at 225 for 2. They’re trailing by 133 runs after India posted a solid 358. England captain Ben Stokes took his first five-wicket haul in eight years, while Rishabh Pant made a brave comeback with a 54-run knock despite a foot injury. In chess, history is being made in Batumi, Georgia as two Indian players, Divya Deshmukh and Koneru Humpy, will face off in the final of the FIDE Women’s Chess World Cup.

Divya Deshmukh outclassed China’s Grandmaster Tan Zhongyi, while Koneru Humpy defeated another Chinese Grandmaster, Lei Tingjie, in their semifinal matches.

In Tennis, India’s Vaishnavi Adkar has scripted history by clinching the bronze medal in women’s singles tennis event at the FISU World University Games 2025 in Germany. She has become the first ‘Indian woman’ to ever win a medal in tennis at the World University Games.

And in squash, Anahat Singh has clinched a spot in the semi-finals at the 2025 World Squash Junior Championships in Cairo and thus secured her first-ever medal at the tournament. She is the first Indian female World Squash Junior Championships semi-finalist since 2010. Aishwarya Rai Nigam, Sports Desk, Akashvani Delhi.

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh, informed over social media today, that the flight trials were carried out in Kurnool. More from our correspondent;

The 12.5-kg fire-and-forget air-to-surface missile is powered by a compact dual-thrust solid propulsion unit, enabling it to reach a maximum range of 4 km during daytime operations and 2.5 km at night. It also incorporates a two-way datalink for communication and offers multiple warhead options to engage a variety of static and moving targets. Designed, developed and tested by DRDO, the ULPGM-V3, also referred to as ULM-ER (extended-range variant), is an advanced version of DRDO’s UAV-launched precision munition, and features passive homing using an imaging infrared seeker, allowing for accurate engagement in both day and night conditions.
