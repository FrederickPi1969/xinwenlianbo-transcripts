## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that Swadesi should be life mantra for every Indian. Speaking at Maruti Suzuki’s Hansalpur plant in Ahmedabad yesterday, Mr Modi said, investment might come from anywhere in the world, but if production takes place in India, it is ultimately Swadesi.

Prime Minister said that a new chapter is being added to India’s Make in India journey, marking an important day in the nation’s goal of ‘Make in India’ and ‘Make for the World’. He pointed out that companies like Maruti Suzuki have become brand ambassadors of Make in India.

Stressing the importance of clean energy and clean mobility, Prime Minister Modi said, “Clean mobility is the future, and India is set to emerge as a reliable centre for clean mobility solutions.”He noted that proactive government measures have resulted in a 500-fold growth in electronics production in the country over the past decade. He added that “Going forward, India’s focus will be on futuristic industries,”. Reiterating India’s commitment to development, he said that alongside Mission Manufacturing, the government has also launched the National Critical Mineral Mission to meet the auto industry’s growing demand for rare earth magnets.

Earlier, Prime Minister Narendra Modi flagged off the Made in India battery electric vehicles (BEVs) ‘E-Vitara’ from Hansalpur. The vehicles will be exported to over 100 countries, including key markets like Europe and Japan.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will be on a four day official visit to Japan and China. During his Japan visit on 29th and 30th of this month, Prime Minister Modi will hold 15th India-Japan Annual Summit with his Japanese counterpart Shigeru Ishiba. Foreign Secretary Vikram Misri shared details of Prime Minister Modi’s Japan visit, with the media in New Delhi.

In China, the Prime Minister will attend the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation’s 25th meeting of Council of Heads of State in Tianjin on 31st of August and 1st September. Secretary (West) in Ministry of External Affairs Tanmaya Lal briefed Media about Prime Minister Modi’s upcoming visit to China.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah has said that demographic changes in border areas directly impact the security of the country and its borders. Mr Shah said this while addressing the inaugural session of the two-day Vibrant Villages Programme workshop in New Delhi. He said that the vibrant villages program was started by the Modi Government to stop migration from border villages and achieve 100 per cent saturation. The Minister said that the Vibrant Villages Programme is boosting employment by promoting infrastructure, preservation and promotion of culture and tourism in border villages. He highlighted that after implementing the Vibrant Villages Program in Arunachal Pradesh, the population in many border villages has increased.

The flood situation has deteriorated in Jammu and Kashmir. Heavy downpour has disrupted mobile telephone and internet services in the Kashmir Valley, while all major rivers in the Jammu division are flowing above the danger level, inundating many areas. A bridge on the Jammu-Pathankot highway collapsed, forcing the closure of the highway, as shooting stones blocked the Jammu-Srinagar highway. Tawi River is flowing above the evacuation level in Jammu city and Udhampur districts. The authorities have closed the Tawi Bridge in Jammu city. Basantar River in Samba, the Uhj River in Kathua, Chenab River in Doda, Ramban and Akhnoor areas and rivers in Reasi, Kishtwar and Doda districts are all flowing above the danger level.

All educational institutions including Schools, Colleges, Universities to remain closed today in 6 districts of Kashmir region. These districts include, Anantnag, Kulgam, Pulwama, Shopian, Budgam and Srinagar.

The festival of Ganesh Chaturthi is being celebrated today across the country with great devotion. The day marks the birth of Lord Ganesha, who is a symbol of wisdom, prosperity and good fortune. Also known as Vinayaka Chaturthi or Ganesh Utsav, the day marks the beginning of ten day long festivities during which people place clay idols of Lord Ganesha at their homes and worship them.

Ganesh Chaturthi is a major attraction in Maharashtra, where people celebrate it with great zeal.

“Amid chants of “Ganpati Bappa Morya, Mangal Murti Morya,” devotees across Maharashtra have started welcoming their beloved Ganpati Bappa into their homes. Public or Sarvajanik idols are being brought into pandals with great enthusiasm, accompanied by the beats of Dhol-Tasha Pathaks, vibrant music, and fireworks. The ceremonial Pran Pratishtha of Lord Ganpati will be performed, followed by elaborate Pooja and Aarti rituals. This ten-day festival is an integral part of Maharashtra’s cultural identity, celebrated each year with a host of cultural activities-ranging from performances and competitions to live visual displays known as Dekhava. This year, the Bombay High Court has mandated that Ganpati idols made of Plaster of Paris, up to six feet in height, must be immersed in artificial water bodies. Prarthna, Akashvani News, Mumbai.”

Many people have lost their lives after heavy rains triggered a massive landslide on the route to Vaishno Devi shrine in Katra, Jammu and Kashmir yesterday. The Union Cabinet today approved the restructuring of Prime Minister Street Vendor’s Atma Nirbhar Nidhi (PM SVANidhi) Scheme. The Cabinet also gave approval for extension of the lending period beyond the 31st of December 2024. The lending period has now been extended until the 31st of March, 2030. The total outlay for the scheme is seven thousand 332 crore rupees. The key features of the restructured scheme include enhanced loan amount across the first and second tranche, provision of UPI-linked RuPay Credit Card for beneficiaries who have repaid the second loan and digital cashback incentives for retail and wholesale transactions. The first tranche loan has been increased up to 15 thousand rupees from ten thousand rupees and second tranche loans increased up to 25 thousand rupees from 20 thousand rupees, while the third tranche remains unchanged at 50 thousand rupees. We have a report:

“The restructured scheme aims to benefit one crore 15 lakh beneficiaries including 50 lakh new beneficiaries. The scheme’s coverage is being expanded beyond statutory towns to census towns and peri-urban areas in graded manner. The introduction of UPI-linked RUPAY Credit Card would provide immediate access of credit to the street vendors in order to meet any emergent business and personal requirements. In order to give boost to digital adoption, the street vendors can avail cashback incentives upto one thousand 600 rupees on making retail and wholesale transactions. The Government had initially launched PM SVANidhi Scheme on 1st June 2020 to support street vendors who faced unprecedented hardships during the COVID-19 pandemic. Anupam, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs today cleared four major railway projects worth around 12,328 crore rupees aimed at boosting connectivity, freight movement, and regional development in the country. The projects include one new rail line to connect far flung areas of Kutch in Gujarat and multi-tracking works to enhance connectivity to over three thousand villages, benefitting the states of Karnataka, Telangana, Bihar and Assam. These initiatives will improve travel convenience besides reducing logistic cost and decrease dependence on oil imports.

The completion timeline for the 173 km long Secunderabad (Sanathnagar)-Wadi 3rd and 4th Line, having cost of 5012 crores, is five years. The timeline for the 53 km long Bhagalpur-Jamalpur 3rd line in Bihar is three years for which the cost is 1156 crore rupees. The work for the 194 km long Furkating – New Tinsukia Doubling, with a cost of 3634 crore rupees, will be completed in four years. The completion timeline of the new line to connect far-fetched area of Kutch region at an estimated cost of 2526 crore rupees is 3 years.
