## Morning News

Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman will chair the 52nd GST Council meeting in New Delhi this morning. The Finance Ministry said, the meeting will be attended by Finance Ministers of States and Union Territories and Senior officials from Centre and States. Union Minister of State for Finance Pankaj Chaudhary will also take part in the deliberations.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah is all set for a day's visit to Uttarakhand today. During his stay, he will be presiding over the 24th meeting of the Central Regional Council at Narendra Nagar in Tehri district. The meeting is likely to be attended by the Chief Ministers of Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, and Uttarakhand.

Later on, the Home Minister will also be participating in the 49th All India Police Science Congress, which is being held at the Forest Research Institute in the State capital of Dehradun. The Congress will focus on policing in the 5G era, narcotics, internal security, social media challenges, and community policing. It is being organised under the aegis of the Police Research and Development Bureau.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah has said that Left Wing Extremism (LWE) will be totally eliminated from the country in two years. He chaired a review meeting on LWE in New Delhi yesterday. While calling for resolve to uproot LWE in all its forms, Mr. Shah said that Naxalism is a curse to humanity. He said, the year 2022 witnessed the lowest number of incidents of violence and deaths in Naxal-hit areas in the last four decades. He added that success has been achieved in curbing left-wing extremism in the last few years and this fight has now reached a decisive phase.  Chief Ministers, Deputy Chief Ministers, and other concerned officials of Left Wing Extremism affected States also attended the meeting. Mr. Shah said that under the leadership of Prime Minister Narendra Modi and the cooperation of all the States affected by Left Wing Extremism, major successes have been achieved against it in 2022 and 2023.

The Home Minister said that as a result of the Government's zero-tolerance policy, the lowest level of violence and deaths has been recorded in 2022 in the last four decades.

The two-day Anti-Terror Conference organised by the National Investigation Agency concluded in New Delhi yesterday with a resolve by the NIA and all Anti-Terror Squads and Counter Terrorism units of all the states to intensify coordinated efforts to counter terrorism threat. The key directions given by Home Minister Amit Shah while inaugurating the on Conference on Thursday included design of a common training module, and multidimensional use of the various national databases to Investigate, Prosecute, and Prevent crimes. He said that all anti-terrorism agencies must adopt such a ruthless approach so that a new terrorist organization cannot be formed.

Chairing the Concluding session, Home Secretary Ajay Kumar Bhalla underlined the need for teamwork and collaborative and concerted efforts by Central and State agencies to try and prevent the establishment of terror ecosystems.

Chief Election Commissioner (CEC) Rajiv Kumar has directed the Commission’s Observers to ensure a level playing field by working in a coordinated manner to ensure free, fair, and inducement-free elections. He was addressing yesterday a Meeting for Observers to be deployed for the forthcoming elections to the Legislative Assemblies of Mizoram, Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, and Telangana. He said their job is to ensure a level playing field and purity of elections. Mr Rajiv Kumar said the Commission is increasingly concentrating on having a humane face, by facilitating and including Persons with Disabilities (PwD), Senior Citizens and Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups with the help of special provisions, such as Home Voting,  and Accessible Polling Stations.

Election Commissioner Anup Chandra Pandey said that the election should not only be fair but also seen to be fair. He instructed the observers to keep an eye on social media and ensure corrective actions.

Election Commissioner Arun Goel asked observers to ensure their work is carried out in letter and spirit and ensure the rule of law. He said observers are the eyes and ears of ECI and should deal with complaints promptly.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will inaugurate the 9th G20 Parliamentary Speakers' Summit, P-20 in New Delhi on 13th October. The two-day Summit will be held at the newly constructed India International Convention and Expo Center, Yashobhoomi, in Dwarka.

Briefing the media in New Delhi about the summit, Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla said, 25 speakers and 10 Deputy Speakers along with 50 members of Parliament among others will participate in this global event.

## Midday News

In Asian Games, India has amassed a medal tally of 100 medals including  25 gold, 35 silver and 40 bronze at the Hangzhou in China. This is the first time that India has crossed the historic milestone of the hundred medal mark. This is also only the second time India have won more than 100 medals in any of the three major Games – The Olympics, Commonwealth Games and Asian Games. They had won 101 medals in the 2010 Commonwealth Games in New Delhi.

The latest medals were won in women's kabaddi and the men's individual archery compound events. The country is currently ranked at the fourth place on the scoreboard.

The hundredth medal for the country was won by the women's Kabaddi team. In a closely fought match, India beat Taipei by 26-25 in a thrilling match. The Prime Minister applauded the team on their historic win. He also said that the victory is a testament to the indomitable spirit of our women athletes. The men’s Kabaddi team, led by Pawan Sehrawat, will battle for the yellow metal against Iran this afternoon.

In men's individual compound archery final, Ojas Deotale grabbed the gold while Abhishek Verma settled for the silver medal. Deotale won just by two points, with the final score of 149. With this, India's archery campaign comes to a close at the Games.

Starting on a high early morning, India bagged both gold and bronze in women's individual Archery Compound matches. Jyothi Surekha Vennam scored a superb 149 in the final to clinch the gold. She played the final against South Korea’s Chaewon So, breezing through all the five rounds to secure her third victory at the ongoing Games. Jyothi expressed her joy at clinching the historic gold today.

Moreover, Aditi Gopichand Swami showed exemplary performance to successfully grab the bronze medal. Aditi, the reigning world champion, defeated Ratih Zilizati Fadhly of Indonesia by 146-140.

Archery coach, Sanjeev Singh, expressed his gratitude to the support given by the country.

The wrestling mat will also witness fierce competition with Yash competing in the men’s 74kg 1/8 final against Cambodia’s Chheang Chheoeun. Eyes will also be on the famed duo of Chirag Shetty and Satwiksairaj Rankireddy, as they aim to clinch a gold in men's doubles in badminton. They will play against South Korea’s Choi Solgyu and Kim Won-ho.
