## Morning News

Prime Minister and Senior BJP leader Narendra Modi will address two election rallies in Jharkhand today to garner support for BJP-led NDA candidates for assembly elections. The Seventh Session of the International Solar Alliance (ISA) Assembly will be inaugurated at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi today. The ISA Assembly will deliberate on initiatives of International Solar Alliance that impact energy access, security and traditions with a focus on empowering member countries to adopt solar energy and mobilise finance to speed  up solar deployment.

The seventh session of the International Solar Alliance is going to be held under the presidency of India and co-presidency of the Republic of France. Minister, mission heads, and delegates from 120 Member and Signatory Countries, along with partner organisations and stakeholders, will come together in the Assembly. During this three day session major focus will be on the initiatives to improve energy access, security, and transition. At this assembly, the fulcrum of the discussions will be the means and modes that will be adopted to accelerate solar deployment across Member Countries, especially in regions with limited energy access. Additionally, updates on ISA’s flagship initiatives for entrepreneurs, skill enhancement and capacity building, mobilising finance, and advocacy for solar as energy as a choice will be presented. Sumit Saha, Akashvani News, Delhi.

## Evening News

India has condemned the attack on a Hindu temple in Canada’s Ontario perpetrated by extremists and separatists at Brampton yesterday. New Delhi has expressed deep concern about the safety and security of Indian nationals in Canada.

Strongly condemning the attack Prime Minister Narendra Modi said in a social media post, that cowardly attempts to intimidate the country’s diplomats are equally appalling. He asserted that such acts of violence will never weaken the country’s resolve. Mr Modi called on the Canadian government to ensure justice and uphold the rule of law.

Earlier, External Affairs Ministry spokesperson, Randhir Jaiswal asked the Canada government to ensure that all places of worship are protected from such attacks. He said, India also expects that those indulging in violence will be prosecuted.

In a statement, the Indian High Commission in Ottawa expressed disappointment at the violent disruption outside the consular camp in Brampton, Ontario.

On the disruption, Mr. Jaiswal said, the outreach of Indian Consular officers to provide services to Indians and Canadian citizens alike will not be deterred by intimidation, harassment and violence.
