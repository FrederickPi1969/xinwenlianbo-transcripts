## Midday News

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has kept the repo rate unchanged for the seventh time in a row. Announcing the first bi-monthly monetary policy for the current financial year, beginning on 1st of this month, RBI Governor Shaktikanta Das this morning said the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) has decided to keep the repo rate unchanged at 6.5 per cent. Repo Rate is the interest rate at which the central bank of a country lends money to commercial banks.

The Marginal Standing Facility rate and bank rate remain at 6.75 per cent. Mr Das said, the MPC also decided to remain focused on withdrawal of accommodation to ensure that inflation progressively aligns to the target, while supporting growth

The RBI Governor said, the real GDP growth for the current financial year 2024-25 is projected at 7 per cent while CPI inflation is expected to be at 4.5 percent. He said, India’s foreign exchange reserves an reached all-time high of 645.6 billion dollars as of 29th of last month. He added that the Indian Rupee was the most stable among major currencies during the past three years and this reflects sound economic fundamentals of the Indian economy.
Meanwhile, the RBI has proposed the deposit of cash in cash deposit machines through UPI. Till now UPI is mainly used for money withdrawal or Money transfer. The Central Bank has also proposed to permit the use of third-party UPI apps for making UPI payments from Prepaid Payment Instruments wallets.

Briefing media after the announcement, Governor Shaktikanta Das said, with a view to facilitate wider non-resident participation in Sovereign Green Bonds, a scheme for investment and trading in these Bonds in the Indian Financial System Code will be notified soon.

The Election Commission today held a meeting with Municipal Commissioners and District Election Officers from nine states which had the lowest voter turnout in the last General Elections. The meeting was chaired by Chief Election Commissioner Rajiv Kumar along with Election Commissioners Gyanesh Kumar and Sukhbir Singh Sandhu in New Delhi.

Chief Election Commissioner Rajiv Kumar has directed Municipal Commissioners and District Election Officers from select districts to prepare a booth-wise action plan for enhanced voter participation and behaviour change. He also asked them to prepare different strategies for urban and rural areas and plan interventions accordingly for different targeted audiences. The ‘Conference on Low Voter Turnout’ was held to increase voter turnout in the 2024 Lok Sabha Elections.

The Chief Election Commissioner 215 Rural and 51 Urban Parliamentary Constituencies with low voter turnout have been identified to reach out to voters in a targeted manner. He urged the authorities to act in a manner that instils pride among the voters in participating in the democratic festivities. He also called for a movement in which people are self-motivated to vote. For this, he emphasized a three-pronged strategy. These are: providing facilitation at polling stations like queue management, targeted outreach and communication and involvement of critical stakeholders, local icons and youth influencers to persuade people to come to polling stations.

## Evening News

The Reserve Bank of India has said, the Consumer Price Index inflation for 2024-25 is projected at 4.5 per cent. RBI Governor Shaktikanta Das while announcing the bi-monthly monetary policy today said, the CPI inflation had peaked at 7.8 per cent in April 2022. He added, that even though Inflation has come down significantly it remains above the 4 per cent target given by the Government.

The Governor added, real GDP growth for 2024-25 is projected at 7.0 per cent with Quarter 1 at 7.1 per cent; Quarter 2 at 6.9 per cent; Quarter 3 at 7.0 per cent; and Quarter 4 also at 7.0 per cent.

The apex bank has kept the repo rate unchanged for the seventh time in a row.

The Governor informed that, India’s Foreign Exchange Reserves reached an all-time high of 645.6 billion dollars as of March 29th this year.

The RBI will soon allow to deposit cash through Cash Deposit Machines using UPI. Till now UPI is mainly used for money withdrawal or Money transfer. RBI Governor today said, this measure will further enhance customer convenience and make the currency handling process at banks more efficient. The apex bank has also proposed to permit the use of third-party UPI apps for making UPI payments from Prepaid Payment Instruments (PPI) wallets. This will further enhance customer convenience and boost adoption of digital payments for small value transactions.

The RBI has said that currently around 50 lakh users in the country are using Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC). Out of which 46 lakh are retail customers while 4 lakh are Merchants. On Cumulative basis 2.2 crore transactions have been completed on CBDC. RBI’s Deputy Governor T. Rabi Shankar has said that testing of offline use of CBDC is going on. On a pilot basis, programmability of CBDC has begun today. Under which a certain amount in the form of CBDC has been transferred to a farmer who can use it only for the purchas of agriculture related products.

The University Grants Commission has instructed all higher educational institutions to create awareness amongst the students about the availability of toll-free mental health helpline. The commission requested the higher educational institutions to widely publicise the Tele MANAS helpline, stating that it is free of cost and available 24×7.

Young students entering colleges and institutions of higher learning encounter challenging situations and are at risk for developing stress, anxiety and other issues related to mental health. Tele Mental Health Assurance and Networking (Tele MANAS) initiative aims to provide round-the-clock support and assistance to those struggling with mental health issues. Tele MANAS provides toll free services with full confidentiality available on the website of the Health Ministry. The service is currently catering to over 13 hundred calls per day in 20 languages.
