## Midday News

Preparations are in full swing for the second phase of Lok Sabha Elections where voting will be held tomorrow, the 26th of April. Campaigning for this phase ended last evening.

Elections will be held for 88 Lok Sabha seats in 12 States and a Union Territories including the remaining part of the Outer Manipur Lok Sabha constituency. Twenty seats of Kerala, 14 seats of Karnataka, 13 seats of Rajasthan, eight seats each of Uttar Pradesh and Maharashtra, six seats of Madhya Pradesh, five seats each in Assam and Bihar, three seats each of West Bengal and Chhattisgarh and one seat each in Manipur, Tripura and Jammu and Kashmir will go to polls in this phase.

The polling for the Betul (ST) Parliamentary Constituency of Madhya Pradesh which was scheduled in the second phase will now be held in the third phase due to the demise of the BSP Candidate.

Senior BJP leader and Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that BJP is a party that runs on the mantra of ‘Sabka Saath, Sabka Vikas’. For BJP, nothing is bigger than the country. Addressing the gathering in support of the BJP candidate at the Police Parade Ground in Morena, Madhya Pradesh, Mr Modi said that the government is giving free ration to crores of needy people without any discrimination of caste and religion. He said, the government gave permanent houses to 4 crore poors. These houses have been given to people of every religion without any discrimination. Similarly, drinking water connections were provided to 11 crore houses and no casteism was allowed.

Mr Modi said that the demand of One Rank One Pension was implemented as soon as the government was formed. He added that the soldiers were given free rein. Mr. modi also highlighted the role of Self Help Group.

Filing of nomination for the fourth phase of the Lok Sabha polls will end today. The nominations will be scrutinized tomorrow and the candidates cleared will be allowed to withdraw their candidature by the 29th of this month. 96 Lok Sabha seats spread over nine States and a Union Territory will go to polls in this phase on the 13th of May.

In this phase, the states where voting will be held include Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Bihar, Jharkhand, Odisha, West Bengal, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, and Jammu and Kashmir.

The Election Commission of India has taken cognizance of alleged the model code of conduct violations by BJP and Congress. The ECI has sent letters to BJP President JP Nadda and Congress President Mallikarjun Kharge to seek response by 11 AM on 29th of this month.

In the letters, ECI has directed that all star campaigners to set high standards of political discourse and observe provisions of MCC in letter and spirit. In their complaints, Congress, CPI and CPI(M) had raised objections on the speech made by Senior BJP leader Narendra Modi during his election speech in Baswara, Rajasthan. While, in their complaint, BJP had raised objection on the election speech made by Congress leader Rahul Gandhi at Kottayam in Kerala.

Congress today alleged that BJP is misquoting Congress’s manifesto. Senior party leader Jairam Ramesh in a press briefing in New Delhi said, Congress has prepared its manifesto after listening to the voice of the people during ‘Bharat Jodo Yatra’ and ‘Bharat Jodo Nyaya Yatra’. He also accused BJP of raising points that are nowhere mentioned in Congress’s manifesto.

In the series of key Lok Sabha constituencies of the country, let us talk about the Rajgarh Lok Sabha seat of Madhya Pradesh today. More from our correspondent:-

Situated on the ghats of Newaz and Kali Sindh River, Rajgarh lok sabha seat is one of the important seats of Madhya Pradesh. A total of 15 candidates are in the fray in this seat. There are more than 18 lakh 69 thousand voters in Rajgarh parliamentary constituency. The Election Commission has set up 2 thousand 236 polling stations in Rajgarh. Rajgarh includes 8 assembly constituencies in three districts, two of which are held by Congress and the rest by BJP.  Rajgarh has been in the hands of persons associated with royal families eight times out of 18 elections held so far for this seat. Congress candidate Digvijay Singh is also a member of the Raghogarh royal family and was elected MP from Rajgarh in 1984 and 1991. But the picture has changed in Rajgarh since 2014. In 2014, BJP’s Rodmal Nagar had won the election by 2 lakh 28 thousand votes, whereas in the 2019 elections, his victory margin has increased to 4 lakh 31 thousand votes. -Sanjeev Sharma, Akashvani News, Bhopal.

Rajya Sabha Chairman Jagdeep Dhankhar administered oath to the elected Members of Rajya Sabha in the Parliament House today. He administered the oath to Devendra Pratap Singh, Tejveer Singh, and Mahendra Bhatt.

Aam Aadmi Party today held a protest in New Delhi against the arrest of CM Arvind Kejriwal in the alleged Delhi excise policy scam. The party has been campaigning under the ‘Jail Ka Jawab Vote Se’ slogan and will launch its campaign song today for the Lok Sabha elections.

US President Joe Biden has signed a 95 billion US dollar package of aid for Ukraine, Israel and Taiwan.

He also signed a bill that could ban TikTok. China’s ByteDance has been given nine months to remove the tik-tok app from the market or sell the app to a US company. The law was passed in response to worries that TikTok might provide the Chinese authorities access to user data. The US Senate approved the controversial landmark bill yesterday.

TikTok called the law unconstitutional and said, it will challenge the same in court.

National Security Advisor Ajit Doval has emphasized that India will continue to cooperate in countering misuse of information and communication technologies by terrorists and criminals for resisting terror funding. Mr. Doval said this while participating at the 12th International Meeting of High Ranking Officials Responsible for Security Matters in St Petersburg, Russia.

In his intervention at the plenary session on Ensuring Information Security in the Polycentric World, Mr. Doval underscored India’s policy to use digital technology for inclusive economic development. He called for international cooperation for an open, stable, secure, reliable and inclusive framework for ensuring information security.

In Telangana, ten persons died in road accidents that took place at separate locations. Four youths died when a bike hit a private bus on Warangal-Khammam highway last night. Police informed that three of them died on the spot while one person passed away during treatment.

In a separate incident that took place early this morning at Durgapuram Stage in Suryapet district, six persons died and four were injured when a car hit a standing truck on the national highway. According to primary information, the truck was kept on the side of the highway due to breakdown.

Heat Wave Conditions are likely over Bihar, Jharkhand, Coastal Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and East Uttar Pradesh. Deepanshu Sharma won the gold medal in the men’s javelin throw while three other Indian athletes bagged silver on the opening day of the Asian under-20 Athletics Championships in Dubai.

In the men’s javelin throw, Deepanshu clinched the gold medal with a throw of 70.29 meter, while Rohan Yadav claimed silver with a distance of 70.03 meter. In men’s 1500 meter, Priyanshu secured silver medal. Ritik Rathee became the first athlete to win a medal for India at Championships after bagging the silver medal in men’s discus throw with a best throw of 53.01 meter.

A total of 60 Indian athletes as many as 31 men and 29 women are competing at the Championships in Dubai. The Championships is also a qualifying event for the World under-20 Athletics Championships which will be held in Lima, Peru in August this year.

## Evening News

Stage is set for voting for the second phase of Lok Sabha Elections tomorrow in 88 constituencies spread over 12 States and the Union Territory of Jammu and Kashmir. Voting will also take place for the remaining part of Outer Manipur Lok Sabha constituency.

The seats going for polls tomorrow are: All 20 seats in Kerala, 14 in Karnataka, 13 seats in Rajasthan, eight seats each in Uttar Pradesh and Maharashtra, six in Madhya Pradesh, five seats each in Assam and Bihar, three seats each of West Bengal and Chhattisgarh and one seat each in Manipur, Tripura and Jammu and Kashmir.

The polling for Betul Parliamentary Constituency of Madhya Pradesh which was scheduled in the second phase has been postponed for the third phase due to demise of BSP Candidate.

A total of one thousand 202 candidates along with four candidates from the Outer Manipur Lok Sabha constituency are in the fray in this phase.

Voting will begin at 7 AM and end at 6 PM. Polling time has been extended till 6 PM in many polling stations in Banka, Madhepura, Khagaria and Munger constituencies in Bihar to facilitate voters in hot weather conditions.

Over 16 lakh Polling officials will welcome over 15.88 crore voters across 1.67 lakh polling stations. Our correspondent reports that 34.8 lakh first time voters are registered to cast their votes in this phase.

“Meticulous arrangements have been made at all polling stations for convenience of voters, including facilities to deal with hot weather conditions. Assured Minimum Facilities like water, shed, toilets, ramps, volunteers, wheelchairs, and electricity are in place to ensure that every voter, including elderly and persons with disabilities, can cast their vote with ease. Three Helicopters, four Special trains and nearly 80 thousand vehicles have been deployed to ferry polling and security personnel. Webcasting will be done in more than 50 percent of the polling stations along with deployment of micro-observers in all polling stations. Election Commission has called upon voters to turnout in greater numbers at polling stations and vote with responsibility and pride. Suparna Saikia, Akashvani News, Delhi”.

Some of the prominent candidates whose political fate will be sealed in the EVMs tomorrow are Congress leader Rahul Gandhi from Wayanad in Kerala, CPl’s Annie Raja also contesting from Wayanad, Union Minister Rajeev Chandrasekhar from Thiruvananthapuram, Congress leader Shashi Tharoor also from Thiruvananthapuram, Tejasvi Surya of BJP from Bengaluru South, Hema Malini of BJP from Mathura. Other key candidates are BJP’s Arun Govil from Meerut, ex-Karnataka CM HD Kumaraswamy from Mandya and Pappu Yadav from Purnea.

In Bihar, polling will be held in five Lok Sabha constituencies of Kishanganj, Katihar, Purnea, Bhagalpur and Banka. Around 94 lakh electorates will decide the fate of 50 candidates in these constituencies.

Elaborate security arrangements have been made at polling booths of poll bound constituencies of Kishanganj, Katihar and Purnea which fall under Seemanchal  region. Inter-state border along West Bengal in Kishanganj has been sealed as a precautionary measure.

Polling will conclude before normal time in naxal affected areas of Katoria and Belhar in Banka parliamentary constituency.

From NDA side JD(U) is contesting on all five seats on the other hand from Grand Alliance Congress has fielded 03 candidates and RJD is contesting 2 seats.

In West Bengal, voting will take place in Darjeeling, Balurghat and Raiganj, witnessing multi-cornered contest.

Thirteen assembly segments of Outer Manipur Parliamentary Constituency spread across eight districts will go to polls tomorrow from 7 AM to 4 PM.

Tripura East Parliamentary Constituency where the polling will be held tomorrow, there are more than 16 thousand Bru-Reang voters, who were displaced from Mizoram and have been residing mainly in Dhalai, north Tripura and parts of South Tripura districts.

In Karnataka, the Election authorities have made special arrangements for the convenience of the Physically Challenged voters tomorrow. There are 2.76 lakh physically challenged voters in these constituencies. As on date, 756 PWD voters have used the Saksham mobile app to book Wheelchair or transport facilities. The app can be used till 5.00pm tomorrow to avail the facilities.

Filing of nominations for the fourth phase of Lok Sabha elections ended this evening. Scrutiny of nomination papers will be taken up tomorrow and candidates can withdraw names by the 29th of this month. 96 Lok Sabha seats spread over nine States and a Union Territory will go to polls in this phase on the 13th of next month.
