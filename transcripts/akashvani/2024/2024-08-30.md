## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will visit Mumbai and Palghar in Maharashtra today.  At around 11 AM, Prime Minister will address Global Fintech Fest 2024 at the Jio World Convention Centre in Mumbai. Thereafter, at around 1:30 PM, Mr Modi will inaugurate and lay the foundation stone of various development projects at CIDCO ground, Palghar. He will also lay the foundation stone of Vadhvan (वाढवण) Port. The cost of this project is around 76 thousand crore rupees. It aims to establish a world-class maritime gateway that will boost the country’s trade and economic growth by catering to large container vessels, offering deeper drafts, and accommodating ultra-large cargo ships.

Vadhvan Port, located near Dahanu town in Palghar district, will be one of India’s largest deep-water ports and will provide direct connectivity to international shipping routes, reducing transit times and costs.

“In the first cabinet meeting after Prime Minister Narendra Modi took oath for the third time, the central government had approved the Vadhavan port. It will be developed as an all-weather Greenfield deep draft major port. The Port will comprise nine container terminals, four multipurpose berths, four liquid cargo berths, a Ro-Ro berth, and a Coast Guard berth. The Project will create a cumulative capacity of 298 million metric tons (MMT) per annum, including around 23 million TEUs of container handling capacity. It is expected to generate around 12 lakh direct and indirect employment opportunities. On completion Vadhavan Port will be one of the top ten ports of the world. This  port will enhance India’s maritime connectivity and further strengthen country’s position as a global trade hub. Jeevan Bhawasar, Akashvani News, Palghar.”

Prime Minister will also inaugurate and lay the foundation stone of 218 fisheries projects worth around one thousand 560  crores rupees. These initiatives are expected to generate more than five lakh employment opportunities in the fisheries sector. Mr Modi will also launch the National Roll Out of Vessel Communication and Support system at a cost of around 360 crore rupees.

Union Minister of Commerce and Industry Piyush Goyal and Union Minister of Railways Ashwini Vaishnaw flagged off the inaugural trip of the new Bandra Terminus – Madgaon Bi-Weekly Train from Borivali railway station, yesterday.

In his address via video conferencing, the Union Minister mentioned that the long-standing demand for a train service from the western suburbs to Konkan has now been fulfilled. He emphasized that the budget outlay for Maharashtra in FY 2024-25 is 15 thousand 940 crore rupees, which is 13 times more than the amount allocated before 2014. The Union Minister noted that this regular train will provide direct connectivity from the western suburbs to the Konkan region.

The registration window for the Joint Admission Test for Masters (JAM) 2025, to be organised by IIT Delhi, will open  from 3rd of next month. Candidates can apply online through the JAM application portal for admissions to various Masters programmes for the academic year 2025-26. The registration window will close on 11th of October.  The examination will be held on 2nd of February, next year. The JAM Exam is a common admission test conducted every year for admission into Master of Science and other post-graduate science programs at Indian Institutes of Technology.

The government has launched the She-Box portal to make workplaces safer for women. Women and Child Development Minister Annpurna Devi launched the portal in New Delhi yesterday. She-Box portal is a centralized platform for registering and monitoring complaints of sexual harassment of women at the workplace. This platform provides facility to file complaints, track its status and ensure a time-bound processing of complaints by Internal Committees.

Speaking on this occasion, WCD Minister Annpurna Devi said that with the help of She-box portal, complaints can be safely registered without disclosing personal information at shebox.wcd.gov.in.

Moody’s Ratings, the New York-based global rating agency, has raised its real GDP growth projections for India. It has forecast a robust 7.2 percent growth for the fiscal year 2024, up from a previous estimate of 6.8 percent for 2024. The global rating agency has also revised its projection for 2025 to 6.6 percent.

The report attributes these revised projections to strong, broad-based growth, driven by resilient private consumption and improved business conditions. Moody’s noted that the Indian economy grew 7.8 percent year-over-year in the first quarter of 2024, despite tight monetary policy and efforts toward fiscal consolidation. It also highlighted that both the industrial and services sectors are thriving, as evidenced by the Purchasing Managers’ Index, PMI remaining above 60 since the beginning of the year.

## Midday News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today highlighted India’s fintech prowess at the Global Fintech Fest (GFF) 2024 at Mumbai in Maharashtra. Speaking at the event, Mr Modi said, the Fintech sector played a significant role in democratising financial services. He said, in the last 10 years, the fintech space has attracted investments of more than 31 billion dollars.

Mr Modi added that digital transactions have diminished the menace of parallel economy and increased transparency in the banking system.

He said, the country’s FinTech adoption is unmatched in speed and scale and half of the world’s real time digital transactions take place in India. He said, in just a decade, India’s broadband users have increased from six crore to 94 crore.

Speaking on the occasion, Reserve Bank of India Governor Shaktikanta Das opined that India now stands on the cusp of a transformation where technology will act as the engine to meet the aspirations of every citizen.

It aims to establish a world-class maritime gateway that will boost the country’s trade and economic growth by catering to large container vessels.

The Waqf (Amendment) Bill, 2024 is intended to overcome the shortcomings and to enhance the efficiency of the administration and management of the waqf properties.

According to the Lok Sabha Secretariat, All India Sunni Jamiyatul Ulama of Mumbai and Indian Muslims for Civil Rights of New Delhi will put forth their views before the committee today. The Joint Committee of Parliament on the Waqf (Amendment) Bill, 2024, constituted under the Chairmanship of Lok Sabha MP Jagdambika Pal, had invited views and suggestions from the stakeholders including the public, NGOs, experts, and institutions.

The first meeting of the Joint Committee of Parliament on the Waqf (Amendment) Bill was held on the 22nd of this month. The 31 member committee consists of 21 members from the Lok Sabha and 10 members from the Rajya Sabha.

India’s core sector has posted a 6.1 percent growth in July. According to data released by the Commerce Ministry today, the combined Index of Eight Core Sector Industries measures the output of key sectors, including cement, coal, crude oil, electricity, fertilizers, natural gas, refinery products, and steel, which together have a 40 percent weight in the Index of Industrial Production. The growth in steel production rose to a three-month high of 7.2 percent. Cement output reached a four-month high of 5.5 percent, reflecting a pick-up in the construction activity. Petroleum production increased to an eight-month high of 6.6 percent, while fertilizer output was at a seven-month high of 5.3 percent as kharif sowing gathered momentum due to a better monsoon this year. The coal industry output increased by 6.8 percent, while electricity generation rose by 7.0 percent in July.

The Indian economy has clocked a GDP growth of 6.7 per cent in the first quarter of the financial year 2024-25. Figures released by the Ministry of Statistics showed that the crucial manufacturing sector that provides quality jobs to the young workforce posted a 7 per cent growth, while the construction and electricity sectors posted a double-digit growth during the quarter.

National Security Adviser to the Prime Minister of India, Ajit Doval, participated in the signing of the Founding Documents of the Colombo Security Conclave. The signing event marked a significant step in regional security collaboration among India, Maldives, Mauritius, and Sri Lanka. Following discussions at the 6th NSA-level meeting held in December 2023 in Mauritius, the Charter and Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) of the Conclave were signed in Colombo, which will pave the way for establishing the CSC Secretariat.

The agreement aims to enhance the grouping’s role in addressing security and stability challenges in the Indian Ocean Region.

“The signing event of the Charter and MoU for establishment of CSC secretariat, gives a strong boost to regional security collaboration in the Indian Ocean. Colombo Security Conclave also known as CSC is a regional grouping with India, Sri Lanka, Maldives, Mauritius and Bangladesh as members and Seychelles as observer. At the signing event held today in Colombo, National Security Adviser Ajit Doval stressed the importance of collective efforts in addressing traditional, non-traditional, and emerging hybrid challenges in the Indian Ocean Region. With the establishment of CSC secretariat, the regional grouping is expected to be strengthened in dealing with transnational threats and challenges of common concern to the member states. During his visit, Mr. Doval met with the Sri Lankan leadership as well as other prominent political leaders. Dr. Ahmed Muyeen Farooqi, Akashvani News, Colombo.”
