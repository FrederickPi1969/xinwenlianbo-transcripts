## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has proposed a comprehensive  human-centric Global development compact on behalf of India. The Prime Minister has proposed the compact in his closing remarks of the 3rd Voice of the Global South virtual Summit, yesterday. He said, the outcomes of the summit paved the way to move forward together and expressed confidence that it will boost the efforts to achieve goals set up by Global South nations.

Earlier, Prime Minister Modi called on the nations of the Global South to stand united in one voice to become each other’s strength. He also expressed concern on the current global atmosphere of uncertainty and challenges.

The Indian Medical Association (IMA) has written to Prime Minister Narendra Modi regarding the incident at Kolkata’s RG Kar Medical College and has sought his intervention to bring a central law to check violence against healthcare personnel. The IMA has demanded that the security protocols of all hospitals should be in lines with airports. It also demanded that the hospitals should be declared as safe zones with mandatory security entitlements. In its letter, the IMA has requested for meticulous and professional investigation of the crime within a specific timeframe and prompt justice. Additionally, the IMA has also called for appropriate and dignified compensation for the bereaved family and has asked for improved working conditions for doctors.

Union government has decided to form a committee to suggest all possible measures for ensuring the safety of healthcare professionals. The government made the decision after considering the concerns expressed by various healthcare stakeholders, including FORDA and IMA. It has also invited the state governments to present their suggestion to the committee on the matter. Earlier on Friday, the central government released a notice, directing government hospitals to register an FIR within six hours against any verbal abuse or physical violence meted out to healthcare staff. The government also said the head of the institution shall be responsible for filing an FIR within the stipulated time period. The central government’s notice comes amid massive uproar across the nation over the rape and murder of a trainee doctor at Kolkata’s RG Kar Medical College.

The Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) will conduct a psychological assessment of Sanjay Roy, the accused in the rape-and-murder case of a Kolkata doctor that has triggered nationwide protests. A CBI official said, a team of psychological and behavioural analysts from the Central Forensic Science Laboratory in Delhi has arrived in Kolkata to conduct the necessary tests. CBI has already taken Roy into custody.

Meanwhile, the former principal of RG Kar Medical College and Hospital, Dr Sandip Ghosh, was questioned for several hours by the CBI for the second consecutive day yesterday. He has been summoned again at the CBI office for another round of questioning.

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh will inaugurate a new state-of-the-art Maritime Rescue Coordination Centre of the Indian Coast Guard during his visit to Chennai today. He will also inaugurate the Marine Pollution Response Centre in Chennai and the Coast Guard Air Enclave in Puducherry. The Pollution Centre, located at the Chennai port, represents a pioneering step in marine pollution management. It will play a crucial role in coordinating responses to marine pollution incidents, particularly oil and chemical spills in the waters adjoining coastal states. The new facilities will ensure robust maritime security and efficient responses to emergencies.

External Affairs Minister Dr S Jaishankar will be on an official visit to Kuwait today. During the visit, Dr Jaishankar will meet his Kuwait counterpart, Abdullah Ali Al-Yahya. He is also expected to call on the leadership of Kuwait. Dr Jaishankar’s visit will enable both sides to review various aspects of bilateral relations between the two countries including political, trade, investment, energy, security, cultural, consular and people-to-people contacts. The two sides will also exchange views on regional and international issues of mutual interest, potentially addressing the evolving geopolitical landscape in the Middle East and South Asia.

Union Minister of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry, and Dairying Rajiv Ranjan Singh reviewed the measures taken by the department towards achieving the goal of a Foot & Mouth Disease (FMD)-free India by 2030 through vaccination. Mr. Singh highlighted that the livestock sector is crucial not only for the Indian economy but also for the livelihoods of farmers.

The declaration as FMD-free zones can be prioritized where vaccination is already advanced. This progress will facilitate opportunities for export.

In a statement, the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) said, this visit aligns with the tradition of regular high-level exchanges between India and Nepal. A US court has ruled that 26/11 Mumbai terror attacks accused Tahawwur Hussain Rana can be sent to India. The US Court of Appeals in California has said that the Indo-US extradition treaty permits extradition of Rana. Tahawwur Hussain Rana is wanted in India for his role in the 2008 Mumbai terror attacks that killed over 160 people.

India Meteorological Department (IMD) has forecast heavy to very heavy rainfall over Eastern part of the country for the next 2-3 days.

A low Pressure Area over south Bangladesh is likely to move north-northwestwards and become a well-marked Low pressure area over Bangladesh and adjoining Gangetic West Bengal by tomorrow. The IMD said, under this influence, heavy to very heavy rainfall is expected over Gangetic West Bengal, Odisha, Jharkhand, Bihar, Sub-Himalayan West Bengal, Sikkim, Assam, Meghalaya, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram and Tripura till 20th of this month.  The weather department also forecast isolated heavy to very heavy rainfall over Kerala, Tamil Nadu and Lakshadweep during the next two days. Meanwhile, the IMD said, Delhi is expected to witness a generally cloudy sky with light rain followed by rain or thundershowers throughout the week. Pallavi Parmar, Akashvani News, Delhi.

US Secretary of State Antony Blinken will arrive in Tel Aviv in Israel today as part of Washington’s diplomatic push to achieve a ceasefire in Gaza. This is his ninth trip to the West Asia since Hamas terrorists attacked southern Israel in October last year. The top US diplomat will oversee the indirect peace talks between Israel and Hamas to be continued in Cairo. After the Doha peace talks held on Thursday and Friday, at the behest of the US, there are hopes of a ceasefire.

Blinken, according to Israeli officials will meet the President of Israel, Issac Herzog, Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and defence minister Yoav Gallant.

Sources also said that the US Secretary of State will meet David Barnea, Mossad director and Shin Bet chief, who were part of the indirect peace talks in Doha, Qatar.

In Thailand, King Maha Vajiralongkorn has appointed Paetongtarn Shinawatra as the new Prime Minister. Secretary-General of the House of Representatives Arphat Sukanan announced this morning that Paetongtarn has been appointed premier as she received a majority vote from the Parliament on the 16th of this month.

Thirty-seven year old Paetongtarn becomes the country’s youngest and second female Prime Minister after receiving the royal endorsement from the Thai King.

Paetongtarn is now the fourth member of the Shinawatra family to ascend to the role of Prime Minister.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has congratulated Paetongtarn Shinawatra for being elected as the Prime Minister. In a social media post, the Prime Minister wished her a successful tenure. Mr Modi said, he is looking forward to working with her to further strengthen the bilateral ties between the two countries.

According to the county’s emergency management department rescue operations are currently underway at the site and further details are awaited.

The Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA) has imposed a penalty of three lakh rupees on Sriram’s IAS for publishing false and misleading advertisement regarding the result of UPSC Civil Service Exam 2022. The Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution said that the decision was taken to protect and promote the rights of consumers as a class and ensure that no false or misleading advertisement is made of any goods or services.  In view of the violation of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, the CCPA, headed by Chief Commissioner Nidhi Khare and Commissioner Anupam Mishra has issued an Order against Sriram’s IAS to discontinue the misleading advertisement with immediate effect.
