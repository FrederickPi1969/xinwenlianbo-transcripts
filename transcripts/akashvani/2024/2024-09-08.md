## Morning News

Union Home Minister Amit Shah has said that only Central government and Prime Minister Narendra Modi can restore statehood to Jammu and Kashmir. Addressing a rally of BJP workers in Jammu, Mr Shah said, he himself had assured the Parliament in August 2019 that statehood would be restored at an appropriate time after the Assembly elections.

He said that these elections are going to be historic as it will be the first Assembly polls since the abrogation of Article 370 in the union territory. The Home Minister said, for the first time since Independence, the electorate of Jammu and Kashmir will cast their votes under one flag – the Tricolour and under one Constitution drafted by Babasaheb Ambedkar. Mr Shah accused the opposition parties for wanting to bring back the old system in Jammu and Kashmir and start talks with Pakistan. He asserted that till there is no peace, there will be no talks with Pakistan.

External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar will travel to Riyadh in Saudi Arabia today to attend the First India-Gulf Cooperation Council Foreign Ministers’ Meeting. During the two-day visit, he is also expected to hold bilateral meetings with Foreign Ministers from the Gulf Cooperation Council member countries.

On the second leg of the visit, Dr Jaishankar will travel to Berlin, Germany for a two-day visit from Tuesday. This will be his third bilateral visit to Berlin. Both India and Germany share a robust Strategic Partnership and Germany is one of India’s prominent trading partners and among the largest Foreign Direct Investors. During the visit, the External Affairs Minister will meet the German Federal Foreign Minister as well as the leadership and other Ministers from the German government with an objective to review the entire gamut of bilateral relations between India and Germany.

Dr Jaishankar will thereafter travel to Geneva, Switzerland for an official visit from the 12th of this month. During the visit, the External Affairs Minister will meet with the heads and representatives of the  international organizations with whom India is actively engaged. He will also meet the Swiss Foreign Minister to review the close partnership between the two countries and explore avenues to enhance the bilateral relationship further.

Abu Dhabi’s Crown Prince, Sheikh Khaled bin Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan will embark on an official visit to India beginning today. During the visit, the crown prince will meet  with Prime Minister Narendra Modi in New Delhi.

The visit aims to further strengthen the already robust ties between the United Arab Emirates and India. Sheikh Khaled will be accompanied by a high-level delegation, including ministers, senior officials, business leaders, and prominent economic partners from the UAE. During his stay, the Crown Prince is scheduled to engage in discussions with senior officials in India. These talks will explore opportunities to enhance cooperation across key economic sectors, building upon the significant milestones already achieved for the mutual benefit of both countries and their people. In addition to high-level meetings, Sheikh Khaled will participate in several activities that showcase the strong economic and cultural bonds shared by the UAE and India.

India has sent humanitarian aid to three African countries -Zimbabwe, Zambia, and Malawi to help address the food security needs of people in the drought-affected regions.

In a social media post, the External Affairs Ministry announced that a consignment of 1,000 metric tons of rice departed from Nhava Sheva Port for Zimbabwe yesterday.

India also dispatched a shipment of 1,300 metric tons of maize as humanitarian assistance to Zambia. According to the Ministry, this aid will help meet the food and nutritional needs of the Zambian people.

Additionally, 1,000 metric tons of rice have been sent to drought-affected Malawi. The Ministry highlighted that this humanitarian assistance aims to address the severe drought caused by the El Niño phenomenon.

Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni has said that countries like India and China can play a role in resolving the conflict in Ukraine. Ms. Meloni’s remarks came less than 48 hours after Russian President Vladimir Putin also acknowledged India’s efforts in addressing the ongoing crisis. The Italian Prime Minister made these comments following her meeting with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky on the sidelines of the Ambrosetti Forum in Cernobbio, northern Italy, yesterday. During their meeting, the two leaders discussed the latest developments on the ground and Ukraine’s most pressing needs as winter approaches.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi visited Kyiv last month during which he conveyed to Mr Zelenskyy that both Ukraine and Russia should sit together without wasting time to end the ongoing war and that India is ready to play an active role to restore peace in the region. Mr Modi said India has been on the side of peace since the beginning of the conflict and he would even like to contribute personally for a peaceful resolution of the crisis. Mr Modi’s visit to Ukraine on August 23, the first by an Indian prime minister since its independence in 1991, came six weeks after he held summit talks with Russian President Vladimir Putin in Moscow.

The Government has designated senior IAS officer Tuhin Kanta Pandey as Finance Secretary. An officer of 1987 batch Odisha Cadre, Mr Pandey is currently serving in the Finance Ministry as Secretary in charge of two key domains, the Department of Public Enterprises, and of  Investment and Public Asset Management.

He has been given the notice in relation to his involvement in financial irregularities and corruption in the management of the medical college.

At the Paralympic Games in Paris, India extended their dominance by securing two more medals before the conclusion of Day 10. Navdeep Singh claimed gold in the men’s javelin throw F41 event after the Iranian athlete who had initially finished first was disqualified for unsporting or improper conduct. Meanwhile, sprinter Simran Sharma won a bronze medal in the women’s 200m T12 track event. India have climbed to the 16th spot in the medal tally, with a total of 29 medals, including 7 gold, 9 silver, and 13 bronze.

“Before the curtains are finally drawn on the Paralympic Games in Paris, Indian athletes once again proved their mettle by securing two more medals in athletics. Navdeep Singh, who had finished fourth at the Tokyo Paralympic Games, overcame his disappointment to clinch gold for his country. Navdeep surpassed the 45-meter mark in all his legal attempts, with his third throw of 47.32 meters setting a new Paralympic record and securing him the top spot on the podium. His record-breaking throw in the javelin F41 event earned India its first-ever medal in this category. However, the victory was not without controversy. Navdeep had initially won a silver medal, but the medal’s color was later upgraded to gold after Iran’s Sadegh Beit Sayah was disqualified for violating Rule 8.1 of the World Para Athletics Rules and Regulations. While the exact reason for Sadegh’s disqualification has yet to be revealed, he was issued two yellow cards. Meanwhile, in the track event, Simran Sharma secured a bronze medal in the women’s 200m T12 race with a personal best time of 24.75 seconds, redeeming herself after a heartbreaking fourth-place finish in the Women’s 100m T12 final earlier. With one last event lined up for India at the Paris Paralympic Games, Pooja Ojha is set to compete in the semi-finals of the Women’s Kayak Single 200m KL1 event in canoe today. MANIK SHARMA, SPORTS DESK, AKASHVANI.”

President Droupadi Murmu has congratulated Navdeep Singh on winning a gold medal in the men’s javelin throw F41 event at the Paris Paralympics. In a social media post, President Murmu said that Navdeep has made the nation proud.

President Murmu, Vice President Jagdeep Dhankhar, and Prime Minister Narendra Modi have also congratulated Simran Sharma on winning the bronze medal in the women’s 200m T12 event.

At the US Open, second seed Aryna Sabalenka of Belarus outclassed home favourite Jessica Pegula in straight sets to claim her maiden US Open title. With this win, Sabalenka clinched her third Grand Slam title, following her back-to-back triumphs at the Australian Open.

This year the theme for International Literacy Day is “Promoting multilingual education: Literacy for mutual understanding and peace”. Department of School Education & Literacy, Government of India, organized International Conference “Spectrum of Literacy” on the eve of International Literacy Day yesterday.

In Vietnam, at least 14 people have died and 176 have been injured after Typhoon Yagi struck the country yesterday. Vietnam’s meteorological agency issued a downgrade in the typhoon today as the storm moved westward. However, it caution about the ongoing risk of flooding and landslides.

Vietnamese officials described Yagi as one of the most deadly typhoons to hit the region in the recent decade, leaving more than three million people without electricity in northern Vietnam.

According to government sources, the typhoon destroyed nearly 3,300 buildings, 1.2 lakh hectares of rice and other crops, more than 5,000 hectares of fruit trees, and over 1,000 aquaculture cages in the region. Hundreds of flights were cancelled as four airports were closed.

The Typhoon hit Hainan in China on Friday flooding roads, uprooting trees and cutting power to more than 800,000 homes.Yagi had already claimed 16 lives in the Philippines, the first country it hit earlier in the week.
