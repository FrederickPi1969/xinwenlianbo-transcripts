India has voted in favour of a draft UN General Assembly resolution supporting Palestine’s bid to become full time UN member. The 193-member General Assembly yesterday met for an emergency special session where the Arab Group resolution in support of the State of Palestine’s full membership in the United Nations was presented by the United Arab Emirates. The resolution said Palestine is qualified and should be admitted as full member of the UN and recommended that the Security Council reconsider the matter favourably. The resolution got 143 votes in favour, nine against and 25 abstentions. Currently, Palestine is a non-member observer state at the UN. The status was granted to Palestine by the General Assembly in 2012.

Emir of Kuwait Sheikh Meshal Al Ahmad Al Sabah has announced the dissolution of Parliament and the suspension of parts of the constitution for up to four years citing political turmoil. In a televised address yesterday, the Emir stated that recent upheavals had compelled him to take these actions to safeguard the nation’s interests. The Emir declared that all facets of the democratic process will be thoroughly examined during the period of constitutional suspension. Last month, the Gulf country conducted its fourth elections, with 39 of the 46 members from the previous parliament retaining their seats.

According to Kuwait’s constitution, a new government must be formed within two weeks of an election. However, tensions between the elected Parliament and the appointed Prime Minister have hindered such a formation since the 4th of April.
