## Morning News

President Droupadi Murmu will address the nation on the eve of 78th Independence Day at 7 this evening. The address will be broadcast on the entire network of Akashvani and telecast over all channels of Doordarshan in Hindi, followed by the English version. On Doordarshan, the broadcast of the address in Hindi and English will be followed by broadcasts in regional languages by regional channels. Akashvani will broadcast regional language versions at 9:30 PM on its respective regional networks.

The Government has invited four hundred elected representatives of Panchayati Raj Institutions and over 160 field functionaries from States and Union Territories, as special guests for the Independence Day celebrations at the Red Fort in Delhi tomorrow.

“These invitees include women representatives of Panchayati Raj and field functionaries of the Ministry of Women and Child Development, who are implementing different schemes in the country. Invited functionaries under the Women and Child Development Ministry belong to several schemes of the ministry, while all invitees of Panchayati Raj Ministry are from all Panchayat across the country. The special guests have invited in recognition of their efforts towards empowering the country and provide a lifetime experience to be part of a historic national celebration, fostering a sense of pride and patriotism. OMVESH UPADHYAY WITH RAHISUDDIN RIHAN , AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has expressed happiness over a Tiranga rally carrying six hundred feet long Indian National Flag under the Har Ghar Tiranga Abhiyan in Arunachal Pradesh. In a social media post, the Prime Minister said that Arunachal Pradesh is a land where patriotism is deeply rooted in the heart of every citizen, and it clearly reflects in the state’s vibrant cultural heritage. Mr. Modi said that he is delighted to see such enthusiasm towards the Har Ghar Tiranga Abhiyan.

In Jammu and Kashmir, a Tiranga Rally was taken out with a 750-metre long tricolour on the world’s highest railway bridge on the Chenab River in the Reasi district yesterday. Reasi Deputy Commissioner Vishesh Paul Mahajan said, the tricolor flag was made by the locals in just two days.

Haryana Chief Minister, Nayab Singh Saini participated in the ‘Tiranga Yatra’ organised at Karnal. A large number of people joined the yatra, reciting “Bharat Mata Ki Jai” moved through different chowks of the city. Before the yatra commenced, Mr Saini in a address called on the people to pledge to hoist the tricolour at their homes.

In Rajasthan, Chief Minister Bhajan Lal Sharma attended the Tiranga Yatra under the ‘Har Ghar Tiranga Abhiyan’ held in Jaipur. Speaking to reporters, he said, the country stands united and undivided because of the freedom fighters.

The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) successfully carried out maiden test flight of Long Range Glide Bomb (LRGB), GAURAV from Sukhoi-30 MK-I of the Indian Air Force. The Ministry of Defence said in a statement, the test was conducted off the coast of Odisha. GAURAV is an air launched one thousand kilogram class glide bomb, capable of hitting targets at long distance.

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh complimented DRDO, IAF and industry for the successful flight test. He termed it a major milestone in the country’s effort in developing indigenous defence technologies for further strengthening the capability of the Armed Forces.

On 23 August last year, India became the fourth country to land on the moon and the first to reach its southern polar region. To honour this landmark achievement, Prime Minister Narendra Modi announced August 23 as “National Space Day”. The country will celebrate its maiden National Space Day with the theme “Touching Lives while Touching the Moon: India’s Space Saga. A myriad of events will be held highlighting India’s remarkable achievements in space, profound benefits to the society, and boundless opportunities for people from all walks of life to engage with the Indian space programme. A distinguished Scientist of ISRO and Director of U R Rao Satellite Centre in Bengaluru, M Sankaran spoke exclusively to Akashvani News on the relevance of the National Space Day. He informed that the President will inaugurate the main event in New Delhi.

The Federation of Resident Doctors Association (FORDA) has decided to call off their strike over Kolkata doctor’s murder incident. A decision in this regard was taken after a delegation of FORDA met Union Health Minister Jagat Prakash Nadda in New Delhi. The doctors from across the country were protesting over the incident since Monday. Talking to media after the meeting, FORDA President Aviral Mathur said, the Health Minister has assured that their demands would be addressed in a time-bound manner.

The Health Minister has welcomed FORDA ‘s decision to call off the strike. Mr. Nadda assured doctors that his Ministry will address all their concerns to ensure a safer and better work environment.

Meanwhile, a CBI team has begun investigation into the case after collecting the FIR copy from the Tala police station in Kolkata in West Bengal. The team yesterday interrogated four doctors who were with the deceased doctor before the incident and other persons related to the case.       The Calcutta High Court ordered a CBI investigation into the case. The division bench of Chief Justice T.S. Sivagnanam and Justice Hiranmoy Bhattacharya passed the order. A delegation of the National Commission for Women visited R G Kar Medical College  yesterday.

The Election Commission will hold a meeting with Union Home Secretary Ajay Bhalla today to review the security situation in Jammu and Kashmir. The Commission, led by Chief Election Commissioner Rajiv Kumar recently visited the Union Territory and reviewed the poll preparations. During a press conference in Jammu, Mr. Kumar emphasized that the Commission is committed to holding the elections in J&K at the earliest. He asserted that no outside or internal forces can derail the electoral process in the Union Territory.

Chief Election Commissioner, Rajiv Kumar along with Election Commissioners, Gyanesh Kumar and SS Sandhu reviewed the poll preparedness for the forthcoming Assembly Elections in Haryana. The term of the State Assembly is due to expire on 3rd of November this year. During the two-day review visit to Chandigarh, the Commission met with the representatives of national and state political parties including BJP, Aam Aadmi Party, Congress, CPI(M), Indian National Lok Dal, and Jannayak Janta Party.

India and Australia have strongly condemned terrorism in all its forms and emphasized the need for strengthening international cooperation to combat terrorism in a comprehensive and sustained manner. The statement comes after the 14th meeting of the India-Australia Joint Working Group on Counter-Terrorism held in New Delhi yesterday. The External Affairs Ministry said, the two sides condemned the use of terrorist proxies for cross-border terrorism. Both the countries also exchanged views on domestic, regional, and global terrorism threat assessments.

Joint Secretary for Counter-Terrorism at the Ministry of External Affairs, K.D. Dewal and Ambassador for Counter-Terrorism at the Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade of Australia Richard Feakes led their respective delegations.

United States has approved a 20 billion dollars arm sales to Israel, including Fighter jets and advanced Air-to-Air Missiles. The State Department announced this yesterday. It said, the possible sale will include more than 50 F-15 fighter jets, advanced medium range Air-to-Air Missiles, 120 mm tank ammunition and high explosive mortars and tactical vehicles. It is a long term deal in which F-15 jets are scheduled to be deliver by 2029. The Pentagon said, the US is committed to the security of Israel, and it is vital to assist Israel in developing a strong and ready self-defense capability.

The application form for Haj-2025 has been opened. People can apply till the 9th of next month. Minister of Minority Affairs Kiren Rijiju along with Minister of State for Minority Affairs George Kurian launched the Haj Application-2025 in New Delhi yesterday. Addressing the media following the launch, Mr. Rijiju said, Saudi Arabia has allotted a quota of over 1.75 lakh to India for Haj-2025.

President Droupadi Murmu will inaugurate Amrit Udyan Summer Annuals 2024 at Rashtrapati Bhavan today. It will open to the public from friday and visitors can visit it till 15th September. For the first time this year, Sports persons have been invited to witness Amrit Udyan on 29th August on the occasion of National Sports Day.

Union Minister for Electronics and Information Technology Ashwini Vaishnaw planted a sapling as part of Ek Ped Maa Ke Naam campaign. The Secretary of the Ministry of Electronics and IT S.Krishnan and other senior officials also participated in the plantation drive yesterday.

Delhi Metro will commence its services at 4 in the morning tomorrow to facilitate the public to attend the Independence Day ceremony. The train services will be available at a frequency of 15 minutes on all the Lines till 6 AM and thereafter, regular timetable will be followed for the rest of the day.

The India Meteorological Department, IMD has issued Orange alert in Pathanamthitta and Idukki districts of Kerala today, indicating isolated heavy to very heavy rainfall. Yellow alert has been issued for eight districts in the state.

The MeT Department has forecast heavy rainfall over Western Himalayan Region, Uttar Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh and East India during next seven days.

## Midday News

The nation will celebrate its 78th Independence Day tomorrow. Prime Minister Narendra Modi will lead the country in celebrating Independence Day from the iconic Red Fort in Delhi. He will hoist the national flag and address the nation from the ramparts of Red Fort.

On the eve of Independence Day, President Droupadi Murmu will address the nation today. The address will be broadcast at 7 PM on the entire national network of Akashvani and telecast over all channels of Doordarshan in Hindi, followed by the English version. The broadcast of the address in Hindi and English on Doordarshan will be followed by broadcasts in regional languages. Akashvani will broadcast regional language versions at 9:30 PM on its respective regional networks.

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh will also address the Armed Forces on the eve of Independence Day today. The address will be broadcast at 05.10 PM on FM Gold channel and Indraprastha Channels and AIR Live News 24×7.

The government has invited around six thousand distinguished guests from across the nation to attend the 78th Independence Day celebrations at Red Fort, in New Delhi tomorrow. The gathering, which showcases the diversity of the country’s grassroots initiatives, features individuals from 24 distinct categories, highlighting their significant contributions to the nation.

A total of 250 farmers have been invited as special guests who have benefitted from the Prime Minister Kisan Samman Nidhi. These invitees represent the millions of farmers whose lives have been positively impacted by the government’s direct income support.

A national workshop on Women’s Leadership in Panchayats is underway in New Delhi. Over 160 Panchayat representatives across the country are participating in the workshop. Addressing the workshop, former Lieutenant Governor of Puducherry, Kiran Bedi highlighted the issues of Panchayat at ground level and suggested their solutions to Panchayat representatives.

Talking to Akashvani News, elected representatives of Panchayats shared their experience of the workshop. One of Gram Pradhans from Uttrakhand, Tabassum Imran said that this workshop will help in improving the work.

Elected representatives of Panchayat form Madhya Pradesh, Manju Chouksey said that she is doing work on the ground in her village as Prime Minister Narendra Modi is doing for the country.

Our correspondent reports that these elected representatives will also participate as special guests in the Independence Day celebrations at the Red Fort in Delhi tomorrow.

Union Minister for Youth Affairs and Sports Dr Mansukh Mandaviya today interacted with the volunteers of National Service Scheme, NSS and MY Bharat who have been invited as Special guests to witness the 78th Independence Day Celebrations in Delhi. Speaking on the occasion, Dr Mandaviya said that discipline and work with dedication and commitment are the keys to achieve success in any field. He  said, youth is the future of the country and  Prime Minister Narendra Modi has set a target of making India a developed nation by 2047. He said, when  the country will be developed then  the biggest beneficiaries will be the youth of the country.

Our correspondent spoke to some of the volunteers who said that they are very excited to be a part of the Independence Day celebrations in Delhi.

One thousand 37 personnel of Police, Fire, Home Guard and Civil Defence and Correctional Services have been awarded Gallantry and Service Medals on the occasion of the Independence Day. Head Constable of Telangana Police, Chaduvu Yadaiah was awarded the President’s Medal for Gallantry.

The President’s Medal for Distinguished Service is awarded for a special distinguished record in service whereas the Medal for Meritorious Service is awarded for valuable service characterized by devotion to duty.

The Home Ministry said 213 personnel have been awarded the Medal for Gallantry. It said, out of the 94 President’s Medal for Distinguished Service, 75 have been awarded to Police Service, eight to Fire Service, eight to Civil Defence and Home Guard Service and three to Correctional Service. Seven hundred twenty nine personnel have been awarded the Medal for Meritorious Service.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah today hoisted the Tricolour at his residence in New Delhi, under Har Ghar Tiranga campaign as part of the Independence Day celebration. In a social media post, Mr. Shah said, by hoisting the Tiranga at his residence he remembered the heroes who sacrificed everything for the freedom of the country.

The campaign is being conducted across the country till tomorrow to bolster a sense of patriotism within the citizens. The government has urged people to hoist the  Tiranga at their homes and take a selfie with the Tiranga and upload it on the Har Ghar Tiranga website.

Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu has emphasized the importance of safeguarding the nation’s integrity and called on the citizens to participate in the ‘Har Ghar Tiranga’ campaign with fervor. Mr Naidu highlighted the significance of flying the national flag at every home.

In his statement, the Chief Minister expressed pride in the fact that the tricolor flag, designed by the distinguished Telugu freedom fighter Pingali Venkayya, will adorn homes across the state. He urged citizens to display the national flag not only atop their homes and offices but also on their social media profiles as a mark of respect and to foster a sense of national unity.

Today is Partition Horrors Remembrance Day. It commemorates the victims and sufferings of people during the Partition of India in 1947. In a social media post, Prime Minister Narendra Modi recalled the countless people who were impacted and greatly suffered due to the horrors of Partition. Mr. Modi said this is also a day to pay tributes to their courage, which illustrates the power of human resilience. He said that a lot of those impacted by Partition went on to rebuild their lives and attain immense success. The Prime Minister emphasized the nation’s commitment to protect the bonds of unity and brotherhood in the country.

The Supreme Court today clarified that States can recover past tax dues on mineral rights, but not for the period before 1st of April, 2005. The apex court rejected the argument that its judgment delivered in July upholding the powers of the States to tax mineral rights should be given prospective effect only. On the 25th of July, the Apex Court had ruled that States have the power to levy tax on mineral rights and that the Mines and Minerals Development and Regulation Act 1957 – does not limit such power of the States. After the judgment was pronounced, the Union and certain assessees made a demand that the judgment should be given only prospective effect.

The Apex Court today refused interim bail to Delhi Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal in a corruption case linked to the national capital’s now scrapped liquor policy. The court sought the CBI’s reply on the Aam Aadmi Party leader’s bail plea and posted the matter for 23rd of August.

The AAP chief was arrested by CBI on 26th of June, while he was in custody of the Enforcement Directorate in the money laundering case arising out of the alleged liquor policy scam.

In West Bengal, health care services at all the medical colleges and hospitals remain hampered due to the strike of junior and resident doctors over the Kolkata doctor’s murder incident. Senior doctors are also participating in the protest. Emergency and OPD services are partially functioning.

Opposition parties and common people are also on the road in Kolkata and other parts of the state. Students of various educational institutions and schools are also protesting by taking out rallies.

Earlier, the Federation of Resident Doctors Association (FORDA) had decided to call off their strike after assurance from the Health Minister Jagat Prakash Nadda that their demands would be addressed in a time-bound manner.

Meanwhile, CBI has taken custody of Sanjay Roy the person arrested in the case from Kolkata police today.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah has expressed gratitude to the people of Tripura for blessing the BJP with a historic victory in the local body elections. In a social media post, Mr Shah congratulated Chief Minister of Tripura Dr Manik Saha, state BJP President Rajiv Bhattacharjee and party workers for their untiring efforts to carry BJP government’s pro-development initiatives to the people.

President Droupadi Murmu inaugurated the Amrit Udyan Summer Annuals 2024 at Rashtrapati Bhavan today. It will open to the public from Friday and visitors can visit it till 15th September. To ignite young minds with the spirit of Amrit Udyan, button badges will be distributed among the school children. Another new physical setup is an interactive Sound Pipe exhibit.

For the first time this year, Sports persons have been invited to witness Amrit Udyan on the 29th August on the occasion of National Sports Day.

A free shuttle bus service from Central Secretariat Metro Station will also be available for the convenience of the visitors. The tickets can be booked at Rashtrapati Bhavan’s website. Entry is free for Amrit Udyan.

The process for registration of Haj-2025 has started. People can apply till the 9th of next month. Minister of Minority Affairs Kiren Rijiju along with Minister of State for Minority Affairs George Kurian launched the Haj Application-2025 in New Delhi yesterday.

Japanese Prime Minister Fumio Kishida has said he would step down in September. Announcing his decision not to seek re-election at a press conference today, Mr Kishida said the ruling Liberal Democratic Party (LDP) needs a new start.

Support for Mr Kishida, who has been Prime Minister since 2021, has fallen in the wake of a corruption scandal involving his party. The party has been in power almost continuously since 1955.

Domestic Benchmark Indices Sensex and Nifty are trading steady in Green. The indices were displaying range-bound moves in intra-day trades today. The BSE Sensex was trading up by 165 points at 79,121 and the NSE Nifty rose by 17 points at 24,156 in the afternoon session.

## Evening News

President Droupadi Murmu today said India is moving together as a cohesive nation with diversity and plurality and tendencies that stoke discord based upon perceived social hierarchies have to be rejected. In her address to the nation on the eve of Independence Day, the President said the spirit of inclusion pervades every aspect of the country’s social life. She stressed that affirmative action must be strengthened as an instrument of inclusion. President Murmu highlighted that social justice is a top priority of the Government.

Social justice is a top priority of the Government, and it has taken a number of unprecedented initiatives for the welfare of the Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes and other marginalised sections of society. Pradhan Mantri Samajik Utthan evam Rozgar Adharit Jankalyan, that is, PM-SURAJ, aims to provide direct financial assistance to the people from the marginalised communities.

The President quoted architect of the Indian Constitution Dr BR Ambedkar saying political democracy cannot last unless social democracy lies at its base.

We must remember the words of Dr. B. R. Ambedkar, the architect of our Constitution. He had rightly said, and I quote, “We must make our political democracy a social democracy as well. Political democracy cannot last unless there lies at the base of it social democracy.”

President Murmu stated that India has been among the fastest growing major economies, with an average growth rate of 8 percent annually. She said this has not only put more money in the hands of people, it has also drastically reduced the number of people living below the poverty line. She exuded pride that India has become the fifth largest economy in the world, and is also poised to become one of the top three economies soon. The President attributed this to the hard work of farmers and workers, and the farsightedness of planners and wealth-creators.

It is a matter of pride for all that India has become the fifth largest economy in the world, and we are also poised to become one of the top three economies soon. This has been made possible only by tireless hard work of farmers and workers, by the farsightedness of planners and wealth-creators, and by the visionary leadership.

President Murmu noted that this rapid but equitable progress has given India a higher stature in global affairs. She emphasised that after the successful completion of its G-20 Presidency, India has consolidated its role as the voice of the Global South. She further stated that India intends to use its influential position to expand the scope of world peace and prosperity.

President Murmu noted that the government has reduced one more relic of the colonial era by adopting the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita from July this year. She added that the new code is oriented towards ensuring justice for the victims of crime instead of focusing on punishment alone.

In her address, President Droupadi Murmu termed the General Elections held this year as the largest electoral exercise humankind has ever witnessed.

Noting that Vibhajan Vibhishika Smriti Diwas is being observed today, President Murmu said this Day recalls the horrors of Partition. She said the nation recalls the unparalleled human tragedy and stands with the families that were torn apart.

President Murmu noted that the nation is celebrating the 75th year of the Constitution. She said that remaining firm on the Constitutional ideals of justice, equality, liberty and fraternity, the government is on the mission to enable India to reclaim its rightful position on the global stage.

President Murmu highlighted that India has started celebrating the birth anniversary of Bhagwan Birsa Munda as Janjatiya Gaurav Divas.

President Murmu also stressed on gender justice and climate justice. She expressed happiness that the Government has given equal importance to women’s welfare and women’s empowerment.

The Government has given equal importance to women’s welfare and women’s empowerment.  Their participation in the labour force has increased. A variety of special government schemes have also been designed keeping the women at the centre. Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam is aimed at ensuring real empowerment of women.

On climate change, the President observed that climate change has become a reality. She expressed pride that India is at the forefront of humankind’s battle to save the planet from the effects of global warming. She urged everyone to make small but effective changes in their lifestyle and contribute to the cause of dealing with the challenge of climate change. President Murmu opined that the Amrit Kaal is going to be shaped by the youth of today.

The Amrit Kaal, the ongoing period of a quarter century leading to the centenary of our Independence, is going to be shaped by the youth of today. It is their energy and enthusiasm that will help the nation scale new heights.

The President appreciated the dedication and hard work of the Indian contingent at the recently concluded Paris Olympic Games. She emphasised that the Government has rightly prioritised the development of sports infrastructure, and it is showing results. She said that Indian youngsters are making a mark on the world stage in every sphere of sports. The President added that their achievements have inspired the next generation as well.
