## Morning News

The severe cyclone ‘Dana’ formed over the east-central Bay of Bengal is set to make landfall along the Odisha coast somewhere between Bhitarkanika and Dhamra late tonight. India Meteorological Department (IMD) has predicted wind speeds of 100 to 120 km per hour during the landfall of ‘Dana’ and the landfall process will continue till early morning of tomorrow with widespread impact to the region.The severe cyclone is 280 km from Paradip and 310 km from Dhamra off Odisha coast and it is moving at a speed of 15 km per hour in the Bay of Bengal.

Kendrapara, Balasore, Mayurbhanj, Jagatsinghpur and Bhadrak districts of Odisha will be worst affected by  ‘Dana’ along with very heavy to extremely heavy rainfalls, while the 9 other districts of the state will also experience heavy rainfalls under its impact. All air services from and to Biju Patnaik International Airport at Bhubaneswar have been cancelled from 5 this evening till 9 tomorrow morning.

People living in low lying areas in coastal areas are being shifted to multipurpose cyclone centers. All schools, colleges, universities and many government institutions have been closed till tomorrow. Leaves of doctors and government employees have been cancelled, while a large number of passenger trains have also been cancelled. Fishermen have been banned from entering the sea.

“The Bay of Bengal along Odisha coast has been rough since this morning with high tides and heavy winds. Rains have started in many coastal districts including Balasore, Bhadrak, Kendrapara, Jagatisingpur and Puri as severe cyclone ‘Dana’ is set to landfall tonight on the Odisha coast. The IMD has said rainfalls and wind speed will increase on and off Odisha coast this evening, while high tidal waves upto 2 to 3 meters are expected along the coast. The state government has opened cyclone control rooms in various districts , while ministers have been given charge of red zone districts predicted by the IMD. The government plans to shift 90 percent of the population living in low lying areas to multipurpose cyclone centres by 11 this morning. More than 40 flights and about 200 trains have been cancelled today and tomorrow as all coastal districts and some interior districts are likely to experience extremely to very heavy rainfalls from this evening till tomorrow morning. Prakash Dash, Akashvani News, BhubaneSwar.”

In view of the severe cyclone ‘Dana’ nine round-the-clock dedicated war rooms will be established to promptly restore rail services amid red alert along east coast. The decision was taken in the meeting chaired by the Railways Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw to review the preparedness by the concerned Zonal Railways which includes East Coast Railway and South Eastern Railway. Meanwhile, Indian Coast Guard has also implemented a series of preventive measures to safeguard lives and property loss ahead of the landfall of Cyclone Dana. The ICG has been closely monitoring the situation and has taken proactive steps to ensure preparedness for dealing with any emergency arising from the cyclone’s impact.

## Evening News

The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) has approved two rail projects with an estimated cost of  6,798 crore rupees to provide connectivity, facilitate ease of travel, and minimize logistics costs. The first project involves the doubling of 256 kilometre of railway line from Narkatiaganj to Raxaul, Sitamarhi, and Darbhanga and the Sitamarhi-Muzaffarpur section. This project will strengthen connectivity to Nepal, Northeast India, and border areas by facilitating the movement of passenger trains alongside goods trains. Briefing the media in New Delhi after the Cabinet meeting, Union Minister, Ashwini Vaishnaw highlighted the importance of project in Bihar.

Mr. Vaishnaw also informed that the CCEA has given its nod to a new railway line for Amaravati, which is the new capital city of Andhra Pradesh. He said a new railway line will be constructed between Errupalem and Namburu via Amaravati, covering 57 kilometers. He informed that the estimated cost of this project is 2 thousand 245 crore rupees and will provide direct rail connectivity to Hyderabad, Chennai and  Kolkata.

The Union Cabinet has approved setting up of a 1000 crore rupees Venture Capital Fund dedicated to the space sector. The deployment period of the proposed Venture Capital Fund is planned to be up to five years from the actual date of start of the fund operations. Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw told media that the fund will promote startups in the space sector.

Mr Vaishnaw said that over the period of about ten years, fund will be provided to 30 to 35 space startups under it.

Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister Nara Chandrababu Naidu expressed happiness over the Union Cabinet’s approval for the new Amaravati railway line. He said this project will connect Amaravati with major cities like Chennai, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, and Kolkata. The Chief Minister said that railway projects worth 72,000 crore rupees are underway in the state and that the long-pending railway zone issue has been resolved.

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh today said, India and China have been involved in talks at both diplomatic and military levels to resolve their differences in certain areas along the Line of Actual Control, LAC. In his address at the inaugural session of the Chanakya Defence Dialogue 2024 in New Delhi, the Defence Minister said, following the talks, a broad consensus has been achieved to restore the ground situation based on the principles of equal and mutual security.

Mr. Singh said, India has a distinctive vision regarding development and security, which has made self-reliance in defence a prominent national goal. He said that transforming India’s defence sector into a self-reliant entity will have far-reaching ripple effects.

The Ministry of Defence has signed a contract for the procurement of six Air Cushion Vehicles for the Indian Coast Guard at over 387 crore rupees. The ministry said, these amphibious vessels, also called ‘hovercrafts,’ will be procured under the Buy Indian category. The procurement of these platforms aims to boost the Indian Coast Guard’s capabilities and reinforces the increased focus on maritime security.
