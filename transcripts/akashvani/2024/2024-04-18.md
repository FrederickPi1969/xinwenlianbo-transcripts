## Midday News

Groundwork for the first phase of Lok Sabha election be held tomorrow is complete. Voting in this phase will be held for 102 constituencies spread over 17 states and four Union Territories. One thousand 625 candidates are in the fray for the first phase of Lok Sabha polls. High octane campaigning for this phase ended last evening.

All parliamentary constituencies of Arunachal Pradesh, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Lakshadweep, Puducherry, Uttarakhand and Tamil Nadu will go to polls in the first phase.

Besides voting for the first phase of Lok Sabha elections, polling will also be held to elect new Legislative Assemblies in Arunachal Pradesh and Sikkim in a single phase tomorrow.

The notification for the fourth phase of Lok Sabha elections has been issued today. 96 Lok Sabha seats spread over nine States and a Union Territory will go to polls in this phase on 13th of May. The candidates can file their nominations till 25th April. April 29 will be the last date for the withdrawal of candidature.

Meanwhile, the filing of nominations for the third phase of Lok Sabha elections will end tomorrow. 94 Parliamentary seats in 10 States and two Union Territories will go to polls in this phase on the 7th of May.

The 2024 general elections are being held in seven phases, from April 19 to June 1, to elect 543 members for the 18th Lok Sabha. The counting will take place on June 4.

The notification for Andhra Pradesh Legislative Assembly Elections was also issued today.  All 175 seats will go to polls in a single phase on 13th of next month. With the issuance of notification, the nomination process has commenced in the state. The last date for filing of the nominations is April 25. Scrutiny of nominations will take place on April 26 and the last date for the withdrawal of candidature will be April 29. The counting of votes will take place on June 4, along with Lok Sabha elections.

Several candidates from YSR Congress Party and Telugu Desam Party-Bhartiya Janata Party-Jena Sena Party Alliance have already filed their nominations in different parts.

Top leaders of various political parties continue their campaign for subsequent phases of Lok Sabha elections. Senior BJP Leader and Union Home Minister Amit Shah held two roadshows at Sanand and Kalol in Gandhinagar Lok sabha constituency in Gujarat today. Later in the day, Mr Shah is scheduled to hold a road show and address an election rally in Vejalpur area of Ahmedabad which falls in Gandhinagar Loksabha constituency.

Mr. Shah who is a BJP candidate for Gandhinagar Loksabha seat will file his nomination tomorrow. Gujarat will go for polls in the third phase on 7th May.

In Assam, Bharatiya Janata Party’s national president, JP Nadda also held an election rally in Kokrajhar, support of NDA candidate Jayanta Basumatary. Addressing the gathering at Kokrajhar Green Field, the BJP national President said, Prime Minister Narendra Modi can take strong decision and under his initiative Bodo Peace Accord was signed which paved the way for peace and development in the Bodo Territorial Region (BTR) area after decades of unrest. Mr. Nadda will address two more rallies in Assam today.

In Kerala with days left for the Lok Sabha polls on April 26th, national leaders of all the three fronts are  campaigning in full swing in the State. Union Minister and Senior BJP leader Rajnath Singh will attend election public meetings in Kollam today for NDA. He will also address a public meeting in Keralapuram in the evening. BJP National President JP Nadda will campaign for NDA candidate Tushar Vellappally  tomorrow. Congress leader Rahul Gandhi will participate in election campaign events in Palakkad and Kottayam for UDF.  CPIM General Secretary Sitaram Yechury is attending public meetings  in Kozhikode for LDF. CPI general secretary D Raja is also campaigning in the state.

BJP today released its 13th list for the Lok Sabha polls. The party has fielded Union Minister Narayan Rane from Ratnagiri-Sindhudurg Parliamentary Constituency in Maharashtra.

Meanwhile, Congress today released a list of four candidates for Odisha Assembly polls. Prakash Chandra Jena has been fielded from Bhubaneswar Central and Manoj Rath from Satyabadi.

Ahead of the first phase of Lok Sabha elections tomorrow, Chief Election Commissioner Rajiv Kumar has appealed to the voters to realise their democratic responsibility saying elections belong to  people and the choices belong to them. He added that  they do  it for their sake, for their families, children, villages, towns, and for the country. Talking to Akashvani News, Mr. Kumar emphasised that the countrymen should never underestimate the power of one vote as there are many instances, when one vote has mattered in a critical contest. He added that India’s track record of smooth, regular, and high-quality elections owes to people’s judicious and enthusiastic participation.

Highlighting the arrangements made by the Election Commission to ensure basic facilities to voters, the Chief Election Commissioner said that every polling station has been set up on the ground floor and they all are equipped with the facilities like drinking water, washrooms, sheds, ramps, wheelchair and helpdesk and volunteers. He also  said, voters who are eighty five years or above and persons with disabilities can opt to vote from the comfort of their homes.

Rajya Sabha Chairman Jagdeep Dhankhar today administered oath to the elected Members of Rajya Sabha in Parliament House. Mr. Dhankhar administered oath to Bhim Singh, Parmar Jasvantsinh Salamsinh, Sangeeta, Naveen Jain, and Dr. Sudhanshu Trivedi.

The Government has notified amended rules under the Foreign Exchange Management Act to operationalise its decision to allow up to 100 per cent FDI for the space sector.

As per the notification issued by Ministry of Finance, 100 per cent Foreign Direct Investment has been allowed for manufacturing and operation of satellites, satellite data products and ground segment and user segment, out of which up to 74 per cent will be through the automatic route and government approval will be required for investment beyond 74 per cent.

Finance Ministry said, automatic FDI has also allowed up to 49 per cent for launch vehicles and associated systems or subsystems, and creation of spaceports for launching and receiving spacecraft. Government nod will be required for investments beyond 49 per cent.

Chief of Defence Staff, General Anil Chauhan has expressed his belief that space will cast its influence on air, maritime, and land domains in the future. He said, space is an uncharted territory where concepts of combat are yet to evolve and mature.

Addressing the Indian Defspace Symposium in New Delhi through virtual mode today, the Chief of Defence Staff stressed that space must be used for the benefit of mankind. He said, India has proved its capabilities in space by developing several niche technologies.

In Kerala, Avian Influenza H5N1 has been confirmed in ducks in two panchayats, Edathva and Cheruthana in Alappuzha district. District authorities have initiated steps to cull all farm birds within a one km radius of the affected area.

The presence of  the bird flu was confirmed by the National Institute of High Security Animal Diseases in Bhopal.

Enforcement Directorate (ED) has attached properties worth around 98 crore rupees of businessman Raj Kundra. The action has been taken under the provisions of Prevention of Money Laundering Act.

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has forecast severe heat wave conditions over Odisha, Tamil Nadu, Coastal Andhra Pradesh and West Bengal during the next three days. The temperature is expected above 40 degree celsius in these areas. Heat Wave conditions are also likely over Jharkhand, north Konkan, Saurashtra, Madhya Maharashtra, Telangana, Tamil Nadu and Puducherry. Talking to Akashwani News, senior scientist at the IMD, R K Jenamani advised people to take precaution and hydrate. He said that North India does not have heat waves and Delhi, Rajasthan, Punjab and Haryana are expected to observe light rain. Heavy rain is likely in Himachal Pradesh.
