## Morning News

The Central forces will implement ten per cent reservation for ex-Agniveers. Amid debate over the Agnipath scheme, the central forces have announced that they will implement ten per cent reservation for former Agniveers. The chiefs of the central forces such as Border Security Force, Central Reserve Police Force, Central Industrial Security Force, and Sashastra Seema Bal said that former Agniveers will get relaxation in age and there will also be no Physical Efficiency Test for them.

Director General, CISF, Nina Singh has said, the Home Ministry has taken a big step of recruiting Ex Agniveers in Central Armed Police Forces. Talking to media, she said CISF has also made all arrangements in this regard.

Director General, Border Security Force, Nitin Agrawal said they are getting ready soldiers, and nothing can be better than that.

Director General, Railway Protection Force, Manoj Yadava said, RPF is excited to welcome Ex Agniveers.

Director General, CRPF Anish Dayal Singh said, Agniveers will prove to be an asset as they have gained discipline while being in the Army.

The Central Bureau of Investigation, CBI has arrested Rakesh Ranjan, alias Rocky, who is believed to be the kingpin in the NEET paper leak case. He has been sent to the CBI’s custody for 10 days. Rocky is also linked to another key figure in this racket, Sanjeev Mukhiya, who has been involved in this scam for two decades and is on the run. The arrest of Ranjan, or Rocky, could help CBI wide open the NEET paper leak case. The probe agency has so far arrested over a dozen people, including the principal and vice-principal of a school in Jharkhand’s Hazaribagh. The CBI and various state police forces are investigating a national racket that involves leaking of question papers for competitive exams like NEET.

The Central Government has set up a single-member committee to investigate the candidature claims and other details of Puja Khedkar, a probationary IAS officer, who has been recently embroiled in a controversy over alleged misuse of power as a civil servant. Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions has announced that the committee, headed by an Additional Secretary-level officer, will submit its findings within two weeks. This inquiry comes in the wake of allegations surrounding Ms Khedkar’s conduct and qualifications. She is a 2023-batch IAS officer of the Maharashtra cadre, who secured an All India Rank (AIR) of 841 in the Union Public Service Commission exam. Pooja sparked a controversy after she used her private Audi car with a red-blue beacon light and VIP number plate. She allegedly demanded facilities not typically available to probationary officers. A report by Pune Collector Suhas Diwse detailed Ms Khedkar’s repeated demands for special treatment, including a separate cabin, car, residential quarters, and a peon, even before officially beginning her training.

Further allegations suggest Ms Khedkar may have submitted fake disability and Other Backward Class certificates to clear the civil services exam. Reports also claim she provided a mental illness certificate but failed to report to AIIMS Delhi for verification when summoned in April 2022, citing a Covid infection.

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh was admitted to AIIMS, Delhi yesterday following some complications. He is stable.

Chief Economics Commentator at an English Daily, Financial Times, Martin Wolf has estimated India to be a superpower by 2047. In a newspaper column, the London based economist, Mr Wolf said, with four times more population, India will match the economic output of the US by 2047. He said, if India’s GDP were to grow even at 5 per cent a year by 2047, and US GDP were to grow at 2.3 per cent, India’s economy would equal that of the US in Purchasing Power Parity terms.

Mr Wolf added that India has strengths and it has good relations with the West, to which it is strategically important. He said that India could be what the IMF calls a ‘connector country’ in the world economy.

Defence Ministry said, these project sanctions are a testimony to the continuing endeavour of DRDO in nurturing Industries, especially MSMEs and start-ups, in defence and aerospace domains.

In Telangana, an aspirational block, Pembi Mandal has achieved hundred per cent target set for Agriculture sector under Sampoornatha Abhiyan taken up by the Niti Ayog. District Agriculture Officer Anji Prasad told Akashvani News that all 2,900 Soil Health samples have been collected as target set. A farmer from Itikyal village of Pembi block, Guguloth Reddy informed that getting the soil test done has helped him a lot and the cotton yield in his four acres has almost doubled.

Chardham Yatra pilgrims can now create their Ayushman Bharat Health Account, (ABHA) on the eSwasthya Dham portal. The Uttarakhand Government’s Department of Health and Family Welfare has launched the portal called eSwasthya Dham. The portal helps in monitoring the Char Dham Yatra pilgrim’s health parameters.

Union Health Ministry said in a release that Pilgrims can easily create their ABHA 14-digit number at the eSwasthya Dham portal in a couple of minutes.

Till date, over 65 crore ABHA have already been created. ABHA comes with several benefits for citizens including storing and managing their health records safely.

## Midday News

India and Bhutan have agreed to work together in the areas of climate change, air quality, forest, wildlife management, and capacity building in the field of environment and climate change.

A delegation from Bhutan, headed by Minister of Energy and Natural Resources, Gem Tshering met Union Minister of State for Environment, Forest and Climate Change, Kirti Vardhan Singh in New Delhi. Both the leaders discussed issues related to air quality, climate change, forests, natural resources, wildlife and renewable energy. Mr. Singh thanked the Bhutanese Minister on joining the International Big Cat Alliance, a global initiative of India. He stated that both countries share same geography, ecosystem, as well as common values of democracy. He further stated that climate change is a common concern for both the countries. Mr. Tshering informed that Bhutan is already a carbon negative country and obtains its major share of energy from hydropower.

Vice-President Jagdeep Dhankhar today addressed the faculty and students of Narsee Monjee Institute of Management Studies (NMIMS) in Mumbai. The Vice-President delivered the keynote address in a discussion titled ‘Empowering India: The Role of Higher Education Institutions (HEIs) in Making Viksit Bharat 2047’.

Addressing the students, the Vice President said that a sea change has happened in the country particularly for the benefit of the youth as a consequence of series of affirmative governance steps and initiatives.

The Union Minister Sarbananda Sonowal said that Vizhinjam port will become an important milestone for the development of the maritime sector in the country and is a major step towards Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s dream of creating world-class ports in the country. The Union Minister was addressing  the official welcome programme of the arrival of first container ship ‘San Fernando” in Vizhinjam Port in Thiruvananthapuram today.

Speaking at the function, Chief Minister Pinarayi Vijayan said that Vizhinjam will be well equipped as a mother port. He further said that ports are the driving force of economic growth and with Vizhinjam becoming a reality, a new chapter has been opened in the development chapter of Kerala.

The landing of the ship is a significant milestone in the State’s ambitious economic infrastructure development initiative and fulfillment of a long waited wish of nearly 3 decades.

Housing and Urban Affairs Minister, Manohar Lal has emphasised on ensuring quality and timely completion of projects of Central Public Works Department, CPWD. Addressing a function to Celebrate 170th CPWD Annual Day in New Delhi, Manohar Lal also stressed on the need for greater transparancy and cost effectiveness in the projects. The Minister asked the engineer and officers to adopt new technology.

The report of the Uniform Civil Code in Uttarakhand is available to the general public from today. Chairman of the Rules and Implementation Committee, Shatrughan Singh released the report of the Expert Committee Uniform Civil Code Uttarakhand. It is available in 4 volumes. The report of the Expert Committee is available on the website www.ucc.uk.gov.in. Speaking on the occasion Chief Minister Pushkar Singh Dhami congratulated the people of the state.

It may be recalled that the Uttarakhand government passed the Uniform Civil Code Bill on 7th of February  after a discussion of about ten hours in the assembly and the UCC bill was approved by the president on 12th of March.

The Supreme Court today granted interim bail to Delhi Chief Minister and Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) National Convenor, Arvind Kejriwal in the money laundering case linked to the alleged liquor policy scam. A bench comprising Justices Sanjiv Khanna and Dipankar Datta referred to a larger bench the Delhi Chief Minister’s petition challenging the arrest by the Enforcement Directorate (ED). Mr Kejriwal will however remain in the Tihar Jail as he has also been arrested by the Central Bureau Of Investigation (CBI) under the Prevention of Corruption Act in relation to the same liquor policy case.

The Delhi Chief Minister was arrested by the ED on the 21st of March in the case. He was released on 10th of May by the Supreme Court to campaign for the Lok Sabha polls. He returned to jail on 2nd of June after the Apex Court denied him any further relief.

The Enforcement Directorate has detained Karnataka former minister B. Nagendra in the money laundering case today. The Ballari Rural assembly constituency MLA from Congress was taken into custody this morning in Bengaluru to probe the alleged scam in the Karnataka Maharishi Valmiki Scheduled Tribes Development Corporation, where funds worth 187 crore rupees were transferred illegally. ED had conducted raids at 20 locations in Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana yesterday as part of the registered case under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act. Subsequent to which the former minister was taken to ED office for interrogation today.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that Samvidhaan Hatya Diwas will serve as a reminder of what happens when the Constitution of India was trampled over. In a social media post, Mr Modi said that this day is also a day to pay homage to each and every person who suffered due to the excesses of the Emergency, as Congress unleashed dark phase of Indian history.
