## Midday News

Senior BJP leader and Prime Minister Narendra Modi said that this election is not just an election to form a government, but this election is to make a developed India. In the last five years, India has become the fifth-largest economy in the world. Mr. Modi said this while addressing the election rally in Saharanpur today in support of the BJP candidate from Saharanpur Raghav Lakhanpal and the party’s Kairana candidate Pradeep Choudhary. Senior BJP leader and Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath was also present on the occasion.

Akashwani Correspondent reports that speaking on the occasion, Mr. Modi said that if the intention is right then the results are right. BJP government works without any discrimination, we want our policies to reach everyone and for this, we have worked for 10 years, that’s the real secularism and social justice.

Mr. Modi said that in the last 10 years, the government has continuously worked for the development of farmers. Talking about Vocal for Local, he praised the One District One product scheme. He added that what you have seen so far is just a trailer, a lot more needs to be done for a developed India.

Senior BJP leader and CM Yogi Adityanath said that we are fortunate that we have seen a new India, which is becoming known as a global leader. He added, today India is known for its world-class infrastructure and India is giving the model of development to the world.

Prime Minister and senior BJP leader Narendra Modi will address an election rally in Pushkar in Ajmer district of Rajasthan this afternoon. This rally is being organised in support of the BJP candidates of Ajmer and Pushkar. This is the third public meeting of Mr Modi for the Lok Sabha elections in the State. On the other hand, Congress is holding a big public meeting today in Jaipur regarding its manifesto ‘Nyay Patra’. Congress National President Mallikarjun Kharge and senior party leaders Sonia Gandhi and Priyanka Gandhi have reached Jaipur to address this rally.  Many other Senior leaders of congress including Sukhjinder Singh Randhawa, Ashok Gehlot, and Pramod Tiwari are also participating in this public meeting.

BJP National President J P Nadda will arrive in Kozhikode today to campaign for the NDA contestants in the General Elections. He will stage a roadshow in the city this evening. The roadshow will take place from Ariyidathupalam to Muthalakkulam.

Senior BJP leader and Prime Minister Narendra Modi along with senior BJP leader and Union Minister, Amit Shah and BJP President Jagat Prakash Nadda wished their Party workers on BJP’s 45th Foundation Day.

Mr. Modi in his address in Saharanpur said that a record number of our countrymen have joined the BJP as it has won the trust and hearts of the people. The biggest reason for this is that for the BJP, the nation is first, this is not a slogan but our faith.

Meanwhile, Mr Shah also thanked the party workers for making BJP the biggest political party in the world. Mr Nadda paid respects to the senior party leaders for expanding the party nationwide.

The labyrinth of India’s bustling democracy has been the torchbearer of Universal Adult Franchise. However, the journey towards gender equality in electoral participation has been both remarkable and challenging. A report:

“Reflecting on the inaugural General Elections held in 1952, out of nearly 80 lakh women voters around 2.8 lakh could not provide their identity details. Fast forward to the present, and the landscape has transformed significantly. In the 2019 Lok Sabha elections, a record-breaking 67.18 per cent of women turned out to cast their ballots, emblematic of a seismic shift in societal norms and awareness. Recognising the gender gap in electoral participation post-2009, the Election Commission launched the SVEEP initiative. From organizing ‘Mahila Matdaar Rallies’ in tribal districts to launching the “Mother India” campaign in Jammu and Kashmir, tailored strategies were implemented to engage and educate women voters. In the 2024 Lok Sabha elections, the winds of change are palpable as 12 States boast more women voters than men. Over 47.1 crore female are set to cast their votes this time. ARPITA CHOWDHURY, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

Lakshadweep, the country’s smallest Lok Sabha constituency, in terms of number of voters, will go to polls in the first phase on the 19th of April. The UT has one Lok Sabha constituency, reserved for Scheduled Tribes (ST). A report:

“There are 4 candidates in fray for General Election in Lakshadweep constituency. The Incumbent MP and NCP Sharat Pawar party candidate Mohammad Faisal, Former MP and Indian National Congress candidate Hamdulillah Sayed, the BJP supported NCP Ajit Pawar party candidate TP Yusuf and independent candidate K. Koya are in competition for the single Loksabha seat in Lakshadweep. 55 booths are there across 10 islands. 57,784 voters are currently present in the electoral roll. In the last election in 2019, the NCP candidate Muhammad Faisal won with a majority of just 823 votes. Many parties such as JDU, CPIM, CPI who had contested in 2019 is not competing this time. Their votes and the nearly 2000 first time voters will be crucial in the island’s verdict. Mayusha for Akshavani News from Thiruvananthapuram.”

A Delhi court has extended the judicial custody of Aam Aadmi Party leader and former Delhi Deputy CM Manish Sisodia till 18th of this month in the alleged money laundering case related to the Delhi Excise Policy Scam case. Special Judge Kaveri Baweja of Rouse Court extended the AAP leader’s judicial custody today after he was produced before the court on the expiry his judicial custody granted earlier. AAP leader Sanjay Singh, a co-accused in the case, also appeared before the court for the proceedings. Sanjay Singh recently got bail by the Supreme Court in the case.

The Central Bureau of Investigation as well as Enforcement Directorate have charged AAP leaders with involvement in irregularities in the Excise Policy, leading to losses for the State exchequer.

The National Investigation Agency (NIA) has arrested two key conspirators in West Bengal’s Bhupatinagar blast case that killed three persons in December 2022. NIA in a statement today said that the accused were arrested amid stiff resistance from an unruly crowd in the state’s East Medinipore district. One team member of NIA suffered minor injury and the agency’s official vehicle was also damaged as some miscreants in the crowd assaulted them. The aggressive crowd tried to stop the NIA team and its security component from moving towards Bhupatinagar police station to complete the arrest formalities. The NIA has lodged a complaint at the local police station in this regard. The arrested individuals were identified as Balai Charan Maity and Manobrata Jana. The two men had actively participated in the conspiracy to manufacture and explode crude bombs to spread terror.

Three Naxalites of the banned CPI (Maoist) party were killed in an alleged exchange of fire with security forces in a dense forest of Chhattisgarh’s Bijapur district bordering Telangana this morning. According to a police official, the encounter took place in the Pujari Kanker forest along the interstate border in the Mulugi district of Telangana. A team of Greyhounds, Telangana’s elite anti-Naxal force noticed the Naxalites while combing the forest area after receiving the discreet information. The alleged encounter between them resulted in the death of three Naxalites. The Greyhounds police recovered one AK 47, one LMG and a 12-bore gun. One of the deceased Santosh alias Sagar was the DVCM commander, and hailed from Ankushapur village of Jayshankar Bhupalpalli district, said police sources.

In the Union Territory of Jammu and Kashmir, security forces yesterday foiled an infiltration attempt by terrorists at Sabura Nala along the Line of Control (LoC) in the Uri sector of Baramulla in north Kashmir and killed two intruders.

According to an official in Chinar Corps, the terrorists attempted to infiltrate the Indian side of the LoC, which led to an exchange of fire with security forces. The Army, however, effectively retaliated, resulting in the elimination of two infiltrators. Following the encounter, a thorough search operation was conducted in the area leading to the recovery of warlike stores.

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has forecast heatwave conditions across parts of east and peninsular India for today and tomorrow. Heatwave will prevail over some parts of Odisha, West Bengal, Jharkhand, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Puducherry and Telangana today. The IMD added hot and humid weather will persist over Coastal Karnataka and Goa till tomorrow, and over Kerala, Tamil Nadu and Puducherry till the 9th of this month.

East and Northeast India will witness enhanced rainfall and thunderstorms in the upcoming days. The IMD added that light to moderate rainfalls will descend over Lakshadweep and Rajasthan today, and over Kerala and Puducherry till the 11th of this month.
