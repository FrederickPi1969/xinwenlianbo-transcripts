## Morning News

Voting is taking place for the fifth phase of Lok Sabha elections today in Fourty Nine constituencies spread over six States and two Union Territories. Voting began at 7 in the morning and will end at 6 PM. 14 seats of Uttar Pradesh, 13 seats of Maharashtra, seven seats of West Bengal, five seats each of Odisha and Bihar, three seats of Jharkhand and one seat each in Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh are being covered in this phase. The Election Commission has made all arrangements at the polling stations. So far in the first four phases, polling concluded smoothly in 379 Parliamentary Constituencies of 23 States and Union Territories. These elections have registered a total voter turnout of about 66.95 per cent.

The prominent candidates whose fate will be decided in the 5th phase include BJP leaders and Union Ministers Rajnath Singh, Piyush Goyal, Smriti Irani, Congress leaders Rahul Gandhi and KL Sharma, Omar Abdullah of the National Conference, and Chirag Paswan of LJP (Ram Vilas).

In Odisha, five parliamentary constituencies where voting is taking place today are Kandhamal, Sundargarh, Bargarh, Balangir and Aska. Polling is also being held in 35 assembly segments in the second phase today. A total of 40 candidates are in the fray for the five Lok Sabha seats and 265 candidates for the 35 Assembly seats which include heavyweight leaders from the BJD, BJP and Congress.

Political fate of Odisha Chief Minister and BJD chief Naveen Patnaik, who is seeking a record sixth term, will be decided in this phase. He is contesting from two constituencies-Hinjili in Ganjam district and Kantabanji in Balangir district. The other prominent candidates who are in the fray today include former Union Ministers and senior BJP leaders Jual Oram and Dilip Ray, former Indian hockey captain Dilip Tirkey besides many state ministers, sitting MPs and MLAs.

As many as 102 companies of the Central Armed Police Forces have been deployed for security of the polling booths. Of the total 9,162 polling booths, 20 percent are sensitive or critical polling stations, including some in the Left-Wing Extremism-affected districts of Odisha.

In Bihar, polling is underway in five Lok Sabha constituencies: Hajipur, Muzaffarpur, Saran, Madhubani, and Sitamarhi under the fifth phase of Lok Sabha elections. More than 95 lakh voters in these five parliamentary constituencies will decide the electoral fate of 80 candidates.

Elaborate security arrangements have been made in riverine areas and polling booths of the constituencies of Saran, Muzaffarpur, and Hajipur. In the Madhubani and Sitamarhi parliamentary constituencies, the border has been sealed along the Indo-Nepal border until the end of the polling.

Chirag Paswan, son of former Union Minister Ramvilas Paswan, from Hajipur Lok Sabha seat, Dr. Rohini Acharya, daughter of RJD chief Lalu Prasad, and former union minister Rajiv Pratap Rudy from Saran are prominent candidates in this phase.

Campaigning for the remaining two phases of parliamentary elections are in full swing. While the sixth phase of polling will be held on 25th of this month, the seventh and the final phase of polling will take place on the 1st of June.

Prime Minister and senior BJP leader Narendra Modi reached Puri in Odisha this morning where he is holding a mega roadshow for the party candidates in Puri Lok Sabha constituency and assembly constituencies coming under its purview. He offered prayers at Sri Jagannath Temple before going for the roadshow at the Grand Trunk Road of Puri.

The Prime Minister will also address two election rallies at Angul under Dhenkanal Lok Sabha constituency and Cuttack under Cuttack Lok Sabha constituency today. All these constituencies are going for polls in the sixth phase of the general elections.

Odisha Chief Minister and BJD Chief Naveen Patnaik is also scheduled to hold a roadshow in Bhubaneswar to campaign for party candidates.

With just four days left for campaigning for Lok Sabha polls in the  National Capital Delhi, all political parties are trying their best to woo the voters.

Iranian Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei has urged the people to pray for the return of Iranian President Ebrahim Raisi and others on board the helicopter, which made a hard landing and has not yet been located. He also asserted that Iran doesn’t need to be worried and said that the country’s administration will not be disrupted at all.

President Ebrahim Raisi was travelling back to Iran following a visit to Azerbaijan when his helicopter came down in bad weather conditions yesterday afternoon. On board the chopper were Iran’s Foreign Minister Hossein Amirabdollahian and other senior officials.

Iran has intensified search operation for the crashed helicopter

Ace Indian para shuttlers Sukant Kadam, Tarun and Suhas have sealed their berths for the upcoming Paralympics in Paris. This is the first time Kadam will be taking part in the Paralympics, and he will be playing in the men’s SL 4 category. SL4 is for players with movement affected at a low level on one side of the body, in both legs, or in the minor absence of one limb. Apart from him, Tarun and Suhas have also qualified from the same category.

French Open 2024 tennis tournament gets underway at the Stade Roland Garros in Paris, France today. The  main draw matches at the second grand slam of the year begin on 26th of this month.

## Evening News

Voting concluded peacefully for the fifth phase of Lok Sabha elections today. Overall, nearly 57 per cent voter turn out was recorded till 5 PM. Forty-nine Parliamentary Constituencies spread over six States and two Union Territories were covered in this phase. These include, 14 seats in Uttar Pradesh, 13 in Maharashtra, seven in West Bengal, five each in Odisha and Bihar, three in Jharkhand along with one each in Union Territories of Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh. Polling also took place in Odisha for the second phase of assembly elections for 35 Constituencies.

Assembly by-elections were also held for Gandey seat in Jharkhand and Lucknow East constituency in Uttar Pradesh.

Six hundred and ninety-five candidates were in the fray in this phase. Prominent candidates whose fate was decided today include BJP leaders and Union Ministers Rajnath Singh, Piyush Goyal and Smriti Irani, Congress leaders Rahul Gandhi and KL Sharma, Omar Abdullah of the National Conference and Chirag Paswan of LJP (Ram Vilas).

Maximum 14 seats went to polls in Uttar Pradesh in this phase. Voting was also held for the by- election in Lucknow East Assembly seat today. Our UP correspondent has filed this report:

” At some places polling started tittle late due to glitches in EVMs. First time voters were very enthusiastic to cast their votes. In Raibareli at some places, villagers boycotted the polling on the other hand Solda village of Lalitpur district set an example of 100% polling. Barring some sporadic incidents polling was peaceful on all 14 seats spread over Awadh and Bundelkhand areas. In last elections BJP won 13 of these 14 seats. The only seat that the saffron party lost was Rae Bareli seat, which was won by former Congress chief Sonia Gandhi. Many stalwarts are in fray on these 14 seats including BJP leaders Rajnath Singh, Smriti Irani, Kaushal Kishor, Sadhwi Niranjan Jyoti, Congress leaders Rahul Gandhi, Tanuj Puniya and Samajwadi party leader Awdhesh Prasad.  Sushil Chandra Tiwari, Akashvani News, Lucknow.”

In the fifth and final phase of polls in Maharashtra, an average of 48.66 percent voting was reported till 5 pm. Dindori registered maximum 57.06 percent voting while Kalyan recorded the lowest of 41.70 percent polling. Key contestants from the BJP include Union Ministers Piyush Goyal, Bharati Pawar, and Kapil Patil, along with lawyer Ujjwal Nikam. Shiv Sena’s Shrikant Shinde and Mumbai Congress chief Varsha Gaikwad are also among the prominent candidates. The primary contest is between the ruling Mahayuti alliance comprising the Shiv Sena, BJP, and NCP, and the opposition Maha Vikas Aghadi alliance comprising Shiv Sena – UBT, Congress, and NCP – SP.

In Bihar, polling was held in Hajipur, Madhubani, Saran, Muzaffarpur and Sitamarhi Parliamentary Constituencies amid tight security arrangements. According to the Chief Electoral Office, voters from two segments in Aurai and Gaighat in Muzaffarpur parliamentary constituency staged protest and boycotted the election at two polling booths. More from our correspondent:

“Voters from all walks of life participated today enthusiastically in the great festival of democracy. Women and elderly voters were seen at different polling booths in rural areas to cast their vote as early as possible to avoid sweltering heat and humidity. In the parliamentary constituencies of Madhubani, Muzafffarpur and Hajipur several places received showers this afternoon providing much needed relief from scorching heat.

Volunteers of Scout and Guide, NCC and security personnel helped elderly voters, Pwd Electorates and mothers with their kids facilitating to exercise their right of franchise. Several voters who were indisposed and not able to come to the polling booths availed conveyance facilities provided by the Election Commission of India. Dharmendra Kumar Rai, Akashvani News, Patna.”

In Jharkhand, the three parliamentary constituencies namely Chatra reported 60.26 per cent voting, 61.60 per cent polling was recorded in Koderma and Hazaribagh recorded 63.66 per cent voting. With this, the fate of 54 candidates have been sealed in the EVMs.

In Jammu and Kashmir, Election Commission today said that Baramulla parliamentary constituency is heading for the highest voter turnout in the last 8 Lok Sabha elections. Voter turnout of 54.21 per cent was recorded as of 5 PM in the districts of Baramulla, Kupwara, Bandipora and Budgam.

Voting was peaceful in 7 constituencies of West Bengal, where polling was held today. Our correspondent reports that people participated in large numbers to vote.

“Voting has ended peacefully in all the 7 Parliamentary constituencies covering 3 districts  of West Bengal today under the 5th phase of election. They are Bongaon, Barrackpore, Howrah, Uluberia, Sreerampur, Hooghly and Arambagh. There were reports of influencing voters and banishing of polling agents from some booths in some constituencies. Security forces have promptly taken all those situations under their control. Rain had disrupted voting for some time in the noon in all the constituencies but Commission had taken corrective measures very fast. 90 preventive arrests have been made in four constituencies today. One fake voter and one fake polling agent were arrested in Howrah constituency. 88 candidates including 14 fame candidates today contested in the poll in Bengal. Bongaon’s BJP candidate Shantanu Thakur, Barrackpur’s BJP candidate Arjun Singh and Trinamool Congress’s Partha Bhowmik, Howrah’ s TMC candidate Prasun Banerjee, Sreerampur’ s TMC candidate Kalyan Banerjee, Hooghly’s BJP candidate Locket Chatterjee’s fate will be decided on 4th June in the counting day. Kalyan Laha, Akashvani News, Kolkata.”

In Odisha, more than 60 per cent voting was recorded by 5 this evening. More from Our correspondent:

“Polling for five parliamentary constituencies and 35 Assembly segments under these constituencies in Odisha went for polls in the fifth phase of general elections today. The five Parliamentary constituencies where polling was held are Kandhamal, Sundagarh, Bargarh, Balangir and Aska and assembly seats under these constituencies. A total of 40 candidates contested for the five Lok Sabha seats and 265 candidates for the 35 Assembly seats.The voting was by and large peaceful. Many voters waited even after 6 in the evening to cast their votes as rain and technical snags in EVMs were reported from some areas. More than 60 percent of votes have been reported  by this evening. The final figure will be given tomorrow by the Election Commission. There was lots of enthusiasm among the rural voters, both men and women, this time. In many places, transgenders also turned out to vote in large numbers. The Election Commission had made elaborate arrangements for the voters to beat the heat as Odisha is going through heatwave conditions. Prakash Dash, Akashvani News, Bhubaneswar.”

In latest reports, the Election Commission has said that voters came out in large numbers braving hot weather in many parts of the states that went for polls today. Polling was closed at 6 PM, but a large number of voters were still in the queue at polling stations. Till 8 p.m.over- 57.58 per cent polling was recorded.

The Prime Minister paid rich tributes to former senior BJP leader and former Deputy Chief Minister of Bihar Sushil Kumar Modi, who passed away recently. He visited the residence of the late senior BJP leader Sushil Kumar Modi at Rajendra Nagar in Patna and consoled bereaved family members.

The Gujarat ATS has arrested four terrorists at Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International Airport in Ahmedabad. According to the local authorities, all the arrested terrorists are residents of Sri Lanka and are associated with the Islamic State. They travelled from Sri Lanka to Ahmedabad via Chennai. The ATS is interrogating the terrorists to determine their purpose for arriving in Ahmedabad. It has also been revealed during the investigation that these terrorists were awaiting orders from their handlers in Pakistan to carry out their operations.

Based on the evidence, Gujarat ATS has registered a case under UAPA against them.

In Chhattisgarh, 19 people have been killed in a road accident. The accident took place under Kukdur police station area of ​​Kabirdham district, when a vehicle lost control and fell into a ditch near Bahpani village. There were 25 people in the vehicle, who were returning to their village after plucking tendu leaves. 13 people died on the spot, while 12 injured persons were admitted to hospital. 6 people succumbed to the injuries during treatment.

President Droupadi Murmu, Vice President Jagdeep Dhankhar and Prime Minister Narendra Modi have extended condolences on the loss of lives in the Kabirdham accident.

Offline registration for the Chardham Yatra in Uttarakhand will remain suspended till 31st of this month due to overwhelming turnout of devotees.

Chief Minister, Pushkar Singh Dhami issued these instructions during a meeting in Dehradun today. He directed the authorities to devise guidelines for tour operators to manage the influx of devotees.

Our Correspondent reports that the movement of pilgrims continues smoothly across all four Dhams.

Iran’s Supreme Leader, Ayatollah Ali Khamenei has appointed Vice President Mohammad Mokhber as the Acting President after the death of President Ebrahim Raisi in a helicopter crash. The Supreme Leader also ordered five days of national mourning for Mr. Raisi, who had held the Presidency since 2021.

According to protocol, Mokhber had been expected to become Interim President. New elections will now have to be held within 50 days.

The helicopter carrying Raisi and other senior officials was forced to make a hard landing due to dense fog in the mountainous terrain.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that he is deeply saddened and shocked by the tragic demise of President Raisi. In a social media post, Mr Modi said, his contribution to strengthening India-Iran bilateral relationship will always be remembered. The Prime Minister said, India stands with Iran in this time of sorrow.

India has announced state mourning for tomorrow as a mark of respect on the death of Iran’s President and Foreign Minister in a helicopter crash. The National Flag will be flown at half mast throughout the country on all buildings where the National Flag is flown regularly. Also, there will be no official entertainment on the day.

In Athletics, the Indian Mixed team has won gold in the 4x400m event and also broke the national record at the ongoing Asian Athletic Relay Championships at Bangkok in Thailand today. The team comprised Muhammad Ajmal, Jyothika Sri, Amoj Jacob and Subha Venkatesan.

The Indian quartet are out of the two spots available for Paris Olympics via the rankings route at the moment. They are on the 21st spot in the Road to Paris list of World Athletics whereas the aim was to be either on the 15th or 16th spot.

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has issued a red alert for severe heatwave conditions over the plains of Northwest India, north Madhya Pradesh and Gujarat State for the next four days. The IMD said, heat wave to severe heat wave conditions are likely to prevail over Punjab, Haryana, Chandigarh, Delhi, West Rajasthan, West Uttar Pradesh, East Rajasthan and northwest Madhya Pradesh till the 24th of this month.
