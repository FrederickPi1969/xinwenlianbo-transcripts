## Midday News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi laid the foundation Stone and dedicated to the nation, a slew of Railway projects worth over 85,000 crore rupees from Ahmedabad in Gujarat this morning. These include two sections of dedicated freight corridor, 51 Gati Shakti Multi-Modal Cargo Terminals, and more than 1500 One Station One Product Stalls. During the programme, Mr Modi also dedicated to the nation and laid the foundation stone for Petrochemical projects worth 20 thousand crores.

The Prime Minister also flagged off ten new Vande Bharat trains including Ahmedabad-Mumbai Central, Secunderabad-Visakhapatnam, Mysuru- Dr. MGR Central (Chennai), Patna- Lucknow, New Jalpaiguri-Patna, Puri-Visakhapatnam, Lucknow – Dehradun, Kalaburagi – Sir M Visvesvaraya Terminal Bengaluru, Ranchi-Varanasi and Khajuraho- Delhi (Nizamuddin). Mr. Modi also flagged off the extension of four existing Vande Bharat trains including the Ahmedabad-Jamnagar Vande Bharat which will be extended till Dwarka.

The Prime Minister said, the development of the Indian railway has been one of the top priorities of the NDA government.

Mr Modi said, the Indian Railway which used to be a major victim of the political selfishness of the previous governments, has witnessed a massive transformation during the last ten years. Mr Modi added that after coming to power, his government is tirelessly working on enhancing the rail network, modernising the rail infrastructure, increasing the facilities for the passengers and promoting the sale of Make In India Products on railway platforms.

The Prime Minister also called Indian railways one of the powerful mediums for self-reliant India.

Mr Modi said his government undertakes developmental works as part of its mission to build the nation and not to win elections as perceived by some people.

During his one-day visit to Gujarat , Mr. Modi also inaugurated the redeveloped Kochrab Ashram and launched the Master Plan of the Gandhi Ashram Memorial.

The Prime Minister  is witnessing Bharat Shakti’ – a synergised demonstration of indigenous defence capabilities in the form of a Tri-Services Live Fire and Manoeuvre Exercise at Pokhran in Rajasthan. The exercise ‘Bharat Shakti’ is showcasing an array of indigenous weapon systems and platforms as a demonstration of the prowess of the country.

The Government has launched a new portal for persons who are eligible for Indian citizenship under Citizenship Amendment Act-2019. People who want to apply can log on to <<https://indiancitizenshiponline.nic.in>>. Union Home Ministry is also going to launch a mobile app ‘CAA-2019’ to facilitate applications through mobile app. Yesterday, The Citizenship (Amendment) Rules, 2024 under the CAA-2019 were notified.

The Citizenship Amendment Act will allow persecuted minorities like Hindu, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi and Christian communities from Afghanistan, Bangladesh and Pakistan, eligible for Indian citizenship. CAA had received the assent of the President on 12th December, 2019.

Haryana Chief Minister Manohar Lal resigned this morning after meeting Governor Bandaru Dattareya at Raj Bhawan in Chandigarh. Mr Manohar Lal’s resignation with his entire cabinet comes amid speculation of a split in the ruling alliance over seat-sharing agreements for the 2024 Lok Sabha election. The alliance between BJP and JJP comes to an end after four and a half years.

Meanwhile, BJP has claimed that it has enough numbers to form a government in the state. The BJP had won all 10 Lok Sabha seats in Haryana in the 2019 elections.

The National Investigation Agency (NIA) is carrying out extensive searches at 30 locations in different states in connection with a  terrorist-gangster nexus case.  The searches are being conducted in Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh as well as the Union Territory – Chandigarh.

India has rejected China’s comment over Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s visit to Arunachal Pradesh. External Affairs Ministry spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal asserted Arunachal Pradesh was, is, and will always be an integral and inalienable part of India. Mr. Jaiswal said, Indian leaders visit Arunachal Pradesh from time to time, as they visit other States of India. He added that the Chinese side has been made aware of this consistent position on several occasions.

India has said that blocking evidence-based proposals in the UN Security Council to blacklist global terrorists without justification smacks of double-speak in dealing with terrorism. India’s Permanent Representative to the UN Ambassador Ruchira Kamboj said this while referring to the sanctions committee of the UN Security Council. She  delivered India’s statement at the open debate yesterday on the Working Methods of the 15-nation UN body.

In June last year, China had blocked a proposal by India and the US to designate Lashkar-e-Taiba terrorist Sajid Mir, wanted for his involvement in the 26/11 Mumbai terrorist attacks, as a global terrorist under the 1267 Sanctions Committee of the Security Council.

President Droupadi Murmu has asserted that India is marching ahead to become the third largest  economy in the world and urged Mauritian people to become a part of New Delhi’s growth journey. Addressing the Indian Diaspora at the Mahatma Gandhi Institute and Rabindranath Tagore Institute in Mauritius, she lauded the role of India origin people in the growth and economic prosperity of Mauritius. Earlier in the day, President Murmu was conferred with the upon Degree of Doctor of Civil Law during  an event in the University of Mauritius. Speaking on the occasion, she highlighted the importance of close India-Mauritius ties and said the two countries are cherished partners in the Indian Ocean Region. The President said, India is giving priority to education and empowering the youth to become a knowledge economy. She said, steps have been taken to set up new Universities and colleges especially in rural areas.

Doordarshan National will now live telecast the daily Aarti from Shri Ramlala Temple in Ayodhya every day at 6:30 AM. Devotees will be able to take a divine Darshan of Lord Shri Ramlala every day.

The Pran Pratishtha of Ramlala was performed by Prime Minister Narendra Modi on the 22nd of January. The newly built temple witnesses heavy rush of devotees every day to offer prayers and have Darshan of Ramlala.

The fasting holy month of Ramzan has begun across the country today. Ramzan is the ninth month of Islamic lunar calender and Eid-ul-Fitr is celebrated at the culmination of the fasting month.

Union Minister of Sports and Youth Affairs Anurag Singh Thakur today launched Khelo India Rising Talent Identification (KIRTI) programme from Chandigarh. Rajeev Verma, Advisor to Administrator of union territory Chandigarh Banwarilal purohit was present on this occasion.

KIRTI aligns with the vision of the Prime Minister, emphasizing the importance of talent hunt in every corner of the country. Speaking on the occasion, Mr Thakur said, the primary objective of KIRTI is to identify and support youth sporting talents at the grassroot level across various disciplines.

In Cricket Women’s Premier League, Mumbai Indians will take on Royal Challengers Bangalore at Arun Jaitley Stadium in New Delhi this evening. The match will start at 7:30 PM.

Yesterday, Gujarat Giants beat UP Warriorz by eight runs in their must-win game in New Delhi.

Opting to bat first, Gujarat posted 152 for 8 wickets in the stipulated 20 overs riding on Captain Beth Mooney’s unbeaten 74 runs off 52 balls.

In reply, UP could score only 144 for five in 20 overs despite Deepti Sharma’s unbeaten 88 off 60 balls. For Gujarat Giants, sixteen-year-old pacer Shabnam Shakil claimed three wickets.

Gujarat are still at the bottom, though they have their second win in seven matches and four points now. UP are at fourth spot with three wins and five losses, with a total of six points.
