## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will hold bilateral talks with Chinese President Xi Jinping on the sidelines of the BRICS  summit in Kazan, Russia today. The meeting assumes significance in the backdrop of the two countries arriving at an agreement on patrolling arrangements in areas along the Line of Actual Control (LAC). The meeting between Prime Minister Modi and the Chinese President will be the first structured meeting in last five years. The two leaders met last in 2023 on the sidelines of the BRICS summit in South Africa and Mr. Modi had highlighted India’s concerns on the unresolved issues along the LAC in the western sector of the India-China border areas. The Prime Minister will also address the BRICS sessions.

Mr. Modi held bilateral talks with the Presidents of Russia and Iran wherein he stressed the need for peaceful resolution of issues and said that war does not bring solutions. In his meeting with Mr. Putin, Mr. Modi asserted that India is ready to assist in bringing a peaceful resolution to the Russia – Ukraine conflict. Foreign Secretary Vikram Misri informed media persons that Prime Minister Modi briefed President Putin about his engagements with the Ukrainian leadership and noted that India stood ready to contribute in bringing peace to the region.

“India’s transformation from a peace loving nation to a global peace maker is gaining momentum. Prime Minister Narendra Modi in recent bilateral talks at the BRICS Summit in Kazan underscore this emergence. India offering to help resolve the Russia-Ukraine conflict has impressed President Puttin. It highlights India’s growing global influence. India’s leadership also expressed concern over west-asia escalating tensions advocating for civilian protection and diplomatic dialogue. India and Pakistan have agreed to extend the validity of the Agreement on Sri Kartarpur Sahib Corridor for a further period of five years. The Agreement had been signed on 24th of October in 2019 to facilitate the visit of pilgrims from India to Gurdwara Darbar Sahib Kartarpur, Narowal, Pakistan through the Kartarpur Sahib Corridor and was valid for a period of five years.

Ministry of External Affairs in a statement said that extension of the validity of this Agreement will ensure uninterrupted operation of the Corridor for use by the pilgrims from India to visit the holy Gurdwara in Pakistan.

The Supreme Court today said that the Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM) Act provision on penalty for stubble burning is not being implemented. The apex court further said the Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM) Act was enacted without creating required machinery for implementing a law to curb air pollution. Additional Solicitor General (ASG) Aishwarya Bhati, representing the Centre, assured the court that rules would be finalised within 10 days and the Act would be made fully operational. The SC also took strong exception on Punjab and Haryana governments not taking any action against people for stubble burning.

Complying with the directions of the Supreme Court and Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM) to bring down stubble burning cases, Punjab Police has initiated a massive campaign to educate farmers about harmful effects of stubble burning and also taking legal action against defaulters. Special Director General of Police, Law and Order, Arpit Shukla has been assigned the task to monitor and take appropriate action. More from our correspondent:

“Arpit Shukla along with concerned district’s civil and police officials is conducting joint tours at identified villages and holding public awareness meetings with various farmers/kisan unions at district and sub-divisional level motivating them to shun stubble burning. Shukla said joint teams are inspecting on the spot and police teams have registered FIRs in over 900 cases. Apart from that, penalties worth over Rs 11 lakh have been imposed in about 400 cases and red entries have also been made in revenue records of hundreds of farmers. Rajesh Bali, Akashwani News, Jalandhar”.

## Evening News

The Prime Minister today underscored the importance of properly handling differences and disputes between India and China and not allowing them to disturb peace and tranquility. Mr. Modi held a bilateral meeting with Chinese President Xi Jinping on the sidelines of the 16th BRICS Summit at Kazan. During the meeting, the two leaders affirmed that stable, predictable, and amicable bilateral relations between India and China will have a positive impact on regional and global peace and prosperity. It will also contribute to a multi-polar Asia and a multi-polar world. Mr Modi expressed confidence that the two nations will hold talks with an open heart.

Later, Mr.Modi said, India-China relations are important for the people of our countries. He said in a social media post that mutual trust, mutual respect and mutual sensitivity will guide bilateral relations.

Briefing media about the meeting in Kazan, Foreign Secretary Vikram Misri said this meeting happened close on the heels of the disengagement and patrolling agreement and the resolution of issues that had arisen in the India-China border areas in 2020. He said, both sides noted that special representatives on the India-China boundary question have a critical role to play in its resolution.

During the bilateral meeting with Mr Modi, the Chinese President said that it is important for both sides to have more communication and cooperation, to properly handle the differences and disagreements and to facilitate each other’s pursuit of development aspirations.

Earlier, the Prime Minister asked BRICS nations to unite and cooperate strongly to deal with terrorism and terror financing. He added that there is no place for double standards on such a serious issue. At the closed Plenary Session of the 16th BRICS Summit in Kazan, Mr. Modi stressed on the need to take active steps to prevent radicalization among the youth. He called for working together on the pending issue of the Comprehensive Convention on International Terrorism in the UN. The Prime Minister also highlighted that work should be done for global regulations for cyber security, safe and secure AI. He said that there are a lot of expectations from BRICS in view of the new challenges of cyber security, deep fake and disinformation in the age of technology.

Later, addressing the BRICS leaders’ Summit, Mr. Modi said BRICS represents 40 percent of the world’s humanity and around 30 percent of the economy and the grouping has achieved several milestones in the last two decades. He expressed confidence that this organization will emerge as a more effective medium to face global challenges in the coming times. Mr Modi also extended the support to Brazilian President Lula Da silva as the next President of BRICS.

Talking about India’s Unified Payment Interface (UPI), the Prime Minister said it is a big success story of India which has also been adopted in many countries.

He also talked about initiatives including International Solar Alliance, Coalition for Disaster Resilient Infrastructure and Ek Ped Maa Ke Naam.

The BRICS (Brazil, Russia, India, China, and South Africa) Summit  today adopted the Kazan Declaration recognising reforms for the UN and multilateral  Institutions. Briefing media in Kazan, Secretary, Economic Relations, Dammu Ravi said the declaration reaffirmed the support for member countries’ aspiration to be permanent members of the UN Security Council.  He said the cooperation in the financial sector was another element of the declaration which also includes elements like payment settlements in national currencies.
