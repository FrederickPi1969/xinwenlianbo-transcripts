## Morning News

In Kerala, the death toll in the massive landslide that devastated Wayanad yesterday has risen to 135. The  toll is likely to rise further. 186 persons are undergoing treatment at various hospitals in the region. Nearly 200 people are missing, however, the exact number of those missing is yet to be ascertained. A joint rescue and relief operation by the Army, Navy, Air Force, NDRF and other agencies has begun.

45 relief camps have been opened in Wayanad, where 3,069 persons have been accommodated. Minister of State for Minority Affairs George Kurian reached Wayanad last night and took stock of the relief operations. He interacted with the senior officials of NDRF and the Army. He also visited a relief camp at Kalpetta. Speaking to media persons in Kozhikode, the Minister said the search and rescue operations will be intensified from today. He said three more NDRF teams and a Dog Squad of the Indian Army will also join the operations. Kerala Governor Arif Mohammed Khan and Chief Minister Pinarayi Vijayan will be visiting Wayanad today.

Meanwhile, the IMD has declared Orange alert in the districts of Malappuram, Kozhikode, Wayanad, Kannur and Kasaragod, indicating the possibility of isolated heavy to very heavy rainfall. The authorities have declared holiday for educational institutions in 12 districts except Thiruvananthapuram and Kollam.

Kerala  is facing the biggest landslide disaster in its history. The mishap hit Churalmala and Mundakai in Wayanad is in ruins. The rescue mission under the armed forces, NDRF teams and State forces has saved over 400 people till last night. The rescue workers were able to reach the Mundakai area only after the arrival of the Air Force as the region was cut from the mainland. 118 relief camps are opened in the State housing over 5000 rescued people. Various State ministers are camping at the disaster site and leading the rescue operations. The State mourning which has been announced on account of the disaster will continue today in Kerala. Mayusha for Akashavani News with reports from Arun from Wayanad.

Prime Minister of Vietnam, Pham Minh Chinh has arrived in New Delhi for a three-day state visit to India. He is accompanied by a high-level delegation, including several ministers, deputy ministers, and business leaders.

Prime Minister Chinh is scheduled to hold bilateral discussions with Prime Minister Narendra Modi, who will host a luncheon in his honor. Prime Minister Chinh will also meet President Droupadi Murmu and Vice President Jagdeep Dhankhar. External Affairs Minister Dr. S. Jaishankar will also call on the visiting Prime Minister.

According to the Ministry of External Affairs, India and Vietnam’s long-standing historical and civilizational ties were elevated to a Comprehensive Strategic Partnership during Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s visit to Vietnam in 2016. The Ministry emphasized that India views Vietnam as a key pillar of its Act East Policy and an important partner in its Indo-Pacific vision.

Union Minister for Education Dharmendra Pradhan launched the National Apprenticeship and Training Scheme 2.0 Portal and disbursed 100 crore rupees stipends to apprentices through the Direct Benefit Transfer mode in New Delhi. These apprentices are undergoing training in various sectors like Information Technology, manufacturing, automobile among others. This initiative aligns with the Government’s focus on skilling and employability of youth.

Addressing the event, Mr. Pradhan said,  this year’s budget also lays great emphasis on boosting skilling and employment. The Minister appealed to all educational institutions and industries to join the National Apprenticeship and Training Scheme 2.0 portal.

On the occasion, UGC chairman Prof. M. Jagadesh Kumar appreciated the government’s initiatives. He said that this portal is the need of the hour which will help youth to support the country’s growth.

Israel says, it has killed a top Hezbollah commander after carrying out a strike on a southern suburb of the Lebanese capital Beirut yesterday. At least one person was killed and a number of others wounded in the explosion in Dahiyeh, a stronghold of the Lebanese armed group. The Israeli military says, Fuad Shukr was the target of an intelligence-based elimination by fighter jets. He was responsible for a rocket attack on the Israeli-occupied Golan Heights on Saturday which killed 12 people, mostly children. Hezbollah has denied any involvement in that attack. Lebanon’s Prime Minister Najib Mikati condemned the blatant Israeli aggression. However, it is not yet clear if Fuad Shukr was killed in the attack. Security sources in Beirut say the intended target was not in the building. Hezbollah has not yet made a statement.

Crowds of travellers were stranded at the airport as airlines cancelled their flights on the back of Saturday’s attacks on the Israeli-occupied Golan Heights and Israeli retaliation.

The Bharatiya Vayuyan Vidheyak 2024 was introduced in the Lok Sabha today by the Union Civil Aviation Minister Kinjarapu Rammohan Naidu. While introducing the Bill, the Minister said, Aircraft Act was established in 1934 and a lot of amendments have been made over the years. He said the amendments were incorporated in the Act in bits and pieces. He said, this has created confusion in the Act. He said, The Bharatiya Vayuyan Vidheyak has been brought to clear out the confusion.

The Bharatiya Vayuyan Vidheyak empowers the Central Government to make rules regulating the design, manufacture, maintenance, possession, use, operation, sale, export or import of any aircraft or class of aircraft and for securing the safety of aircraft operations.

## Evening News

The Central government has reiterated unwavering support to Kerala to deal with the devastating landslide in Wayanad district. Replying to a calling attention in the Lok Sabha today regarding loss of lives and property due to landslides and floods in various parts of the country, Home Minister Amit Shah assured the people of Kerala that the Central government will provide all possible assistance following the Wayanad tragedy. Mr Shah said, Prime Minister Narendra Modi is himself monitoring the situation.

Intervening in the discussion, Minister of State for Home Nityanand Rai said the Centre has issued 33 advisories towards the management of high risk disasters including floods and landslides. He said the State governments can make preparations according to the advisories.

Initiating the discussion K C Venugopal of the Congress said, the Wayanad tragedy is a painful incident and some strong measures should be taken for assistance, rescue operations and rehabilitation.

Earlier, intervening in the calling attention motion in the Rajya Sabha today on the devastation in the district, Home Minister Amit Shah said that an early warning about heavy rainfall and landslides in Wayanad district of Kerala was given to the State government on the 23rd of this month and again on the 24th and 25th of July.

The Home Minister stated that the losses in Wayanad could have been minimized if the Kerala government had become alert following the arrival of the NDRF teams.
