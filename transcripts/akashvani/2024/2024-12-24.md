## Morning News

Union Home Minister Amit Shah has stressed the need to develop an intelligence coordination strategy with friendly nations to detect anti-India outfits and networks. Delivering the 37th Intelligence Bureau centenary endowment lecture in New Delhi, Mr Shah said, out-of-the-box thinking is required to find solutions in the face of challenges such as attacks on critical infrastructure, cyber attacks, information warfare and radicalization of youth.

Praising the Intelligence Bureau, the Home Minister said, due to the working methodology, vigilance, alertness and the tradition of sacrifice and dedication of the IB, the country is safe today. Mr Shah said that under the leadership of Prime Minister Narendra Modi, the security agencies have established dominance over various threats to the nation by fighting a decisive battle over the last five years.

The Home Minister explained that the success of any security agency is based on its workforce and its ability to train its personnel, and also highlighted the need to build a robust ecosystem to counter misinformation. In the coming days, he said, intelligence and law enforcement agencies must increase use of AI and machine learning. Mr Shah said, to achieve a fully developed India by 2047, security agencies must visualise all potential threats and prepare a comprehensive roadmap to safeguard the nation.

National Consumer Day is being celebrated today. It is celebrated on the 24th of December to highlight the significance of the consumer movement in the country. It was on this day that the Consumer Protection Act, 1986, received presidential assent and was enacted. This year the theme is Virtual Hearings and Digital Access to Consumer Justice.

On the Occasion of the National Consumer Day, Consumer Affairs, Food, and Public Distribution Minister Pralhad Joshi will launch various initiatives in New Delhi today. It includes the Jago Grahak Jago app, the Jagriti app, and the Jagriti Dashboard, which are aimed at identifying dark patterns on e-commerce platforms and empowering the consumers. He will also launch the e-Mapp portal to streamline licensing, verification, and registration processes for businesses, reduce compliance burdens, and promote transparency. The Consumer Affairs department will also launch its revamped National Consumer Helpline Portal, incorporating advanced features. It offers enhanced functionality, improved navigation, and faster grievance resolution. A safety pledge will be signed by 13 major e-commerce platforms to ensure the safety of goods sold online and respect the consumer rights. Two state-of-the-art facilities of the National Test House in Guwahati and Mumbai will also be inaugurated on the occasion. SUMIT SAHA FOR AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.

The Central Government has scrapped the No Detention Policy for classes five and eight. Speaking to the media, Education Secretary Sanjay Kumar said that students, who failed in the annual examination of class five and eight will have a chance to retake the test within two months. After the retest if they fail again they will not be promoted. He added that these students will not be expelled from any school till the completion of their elementary education. The secretary added that this decision has been taken to improve the learning outcome among children.
