In a diverse democracy like India, the “None of the Above’’(NOTA) option stands as a symbol of citizen’s right to voice their discontent and demand better governance. It reinforces the principles of democracy and accountability in the electoral process. Here is a report from our correspondent:

The “None of the Above” (NOTA) option in Indian elections is a crucial democratic tool, allowing voters to express dissent when they find none of the candidates suitable for office. Introduced in 2013 by the Supreme Court, NOTA empowers citizens to participate meaningfully in the electoral process by rejecting all candidates if they deem them unfit or unsuitable. The origin of the NOTA option can be traced back to a public interest litigation filed by the People’s Union for Civil Liberties in 2009. The PIL sought the right of voters to reject all candidates, highlighting the need for an alternative to the existing choices on the ballot. Recognizing the importance of providing voters with a mechanism to express their disapproval, the Supreme Court ruled in favour of introducing the NOTA option. Since its introduction, NOTA has played a significant role in Indian elections, serving as a reflection of public sentiment and dissatisfaction with the available candidates. It serves as a reminder to political parties and candidates to uphold integrity, competence, and accountability, thereby encouraging them to nominate worthy representatives. ARPITA CHOWDHURY, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.

The US House of Representatives has passed a 95 billion dollar legislative package including 60 billion providing security assistance to Ukraine. The Senate is expected to take up the Bill for passage this coming week. President Joe Biden has promised to sign the package as soon as he gets it.

The Bills provide 60.84 billion dollars to address the conflict in Ukraine, including 23 billion dollars to replenish US weapons, stocks and facilities; 26 billion dollars for Israel, including 9.1 billion dollars for humanitarian aid for Gaza, and 8.12 billion dollars for the Indo-Pacific region, including Taiwan.

Expressing gratitude, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said that democracy and freedom will always have global significance and will never fail as long as America helps to protect it. However, in Russia, Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said that the package would make the United States richer, further ruin Ukraine and result in the deaths of even more Ukrainians.
