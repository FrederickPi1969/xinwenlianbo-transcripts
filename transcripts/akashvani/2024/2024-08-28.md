## Morning News

External Affairs Minister Dr. S. Jaishankar and Foreign Affairs of Chile Alberto van Klaveren will co-chair the 2nd India-Chile Joint Commission Meeting in New Delhi today. Apart from this, Mr. Klaveren will also participate in Chile⁠-India Business (Agriculture) Summit today and later proceed to Mumbai. Chile is a key partner of India in the Latin American region. The forthcoming visit of Minister Klaveren will provide both sides an opportunity to review the progress in bilateral relations and explore new avenues for cooperation, further strengthening bilateral ties.

The delegation led by Special Secretary and Financial Adviser Anurag Agarwal, had productive engagements with Maldivian Ministers and senior officials of the Government. The delegation also undertook site visits and reviewed the progress of the Indian projects. The delegation is in Maldives till today.

The Joint Committee of Parliament on the Waqf (Amendment) Bill, 2024 has invited views and suggestions from the public, NGOs, experts, stakeholders and institutions.

In an official statement the Lok Sabha Secretariat said, those desirous of submitting written suggestions to the Committee can send two copies either in English or in Hindi to Joint Secretary Lok Sabha Secretariat, and they can also email to jpcwaqf-lss@sansad.nic.in.

The suggestions should reach within 15 days from the date of the publication of the advertisement. The Waqf (Amendment) Bill which was introduced in Lok Sabha during the Budget Session, was referred to the Joint Committee of  Parliament for examination. The Committee is scheduled to report on the Bill to the House by the last day of the first week of the Winter Session of Parliament this year.

Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said India would soon become the third largest economy in the world. The minister reeled out the growth rates compiled in the IMF’s World Outlook report, which shows that India is the fastest-growing economy in 2024 with a growth rate of 7 per cent, followed by China with an estimated growth rate of 5 per cent. The International Monetary Fund had last month raised India’s GDP growth forecast for 2024-25 to 7 per cent from 6.8 per cent earlier.

The growth rate for the US, which is currently the largest economy in the world with a GDP of around 28.8 trillion dollars, is estimated at 2.6 per cent.

The country has reported significant progress in Kharif crop sowing this year, with the total coverage area surpassing more than 1065 lakh hectares so far. During same period last year, approximately 1044 lakh hectares of area was sown under Kharif. The Department of Agriculture and Farmers’ Welfare released the progress report on area coverage under Kharif crops as of yesterday.

The area sown with paddy has reached over 394 lakh hectares. It was over 378 lakh hectares during the corresponding period last year.

India has developed its own indigenous RT-PCR testing kit for Mpox. The Central Drugs Standard Control Organization (CDSCO) has approved Siemens Healthineer’s IMDX Mpox Detection RT-PCR test for the viral disease. The test delivers result in just 40 minutes, significantly faster than traditional methods which takes 1-2 hours.

Indian medical scientists have developed a new smart sensor for adjusting drug dosage to manage Parkinson’s disease. The sensor is a Smartphone based fluorescence turn-on system which is affordable and user-friendly. Institute of Advanced Study in Science and Technology said, the sensor would help in accurately detecting the concentration of L-dopa in the body, which will aid in determining the required dosage for effective control of the disease. L-dopa is a chemical which is converted to dopamine in human body and acts as anti-Parkinson’s drugs.

A novel single-strain Oral Cholera Vaccine (OCV) has been launched by the Bharat Biotech International Limited (BBIL) in Hyderabad. The vaccine named, HILLCHOL was developed under license from Hilleman Laboratories, and is funded by Merck, USA and Wellcome Trust, UK.

The Paralympics Games 2024 will officially get underway in Paris today, and will go on till 8 September. This is for the first time that France will stage a summer Paralympics. The country previously hosted the Winter Paralympics in 1992 in Tignes and Albertville.

A total of 84 Indian para athletes will compete in this edition of the games. It is India’s largest contingent in Paralympic history. India will compete in 12 of the 22 sports at Paris 2024, including making a debuts in para cycling, para judo and para rowing. Para archery, para cycling, para taekwondo, para swimming and para table tennis events are other events where Indians will be seen in action from tomorrow. Para athletics, will kick off  from friday , where 38 Indian athletes will participate. The spotlight will be on javelin thrower Sumit Antil, the Tokyo 2020 gold medallist in the F64 category and a world record-holder.Bhavina Patel in the women’s singles event category. ## Midday News

The government flagship financial inclusion scheme Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana today completed 10 years. More than 53 crore beneficiaries have been banked under the scheme so far. Prime Minister Narendra Modi has congratulated all beneficiaries and thanked those who made this scheme a success. In a social media post, the Prime Minister said, Jan Dhan Yojana has been paramount in boosting financial inclusion and giving dignity to crores of people, especially women, youth, and the marginalized communities.

Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman said, the scheme integrates the poor into the economic mainstream and plays a crucial role in development of marginalised communities. She said, Jan Dhan-Mobile-Adhaar trinity enabled swift, seamless and transparent transfer of benefits of welfare schemes to eligible beneficiaries and promoted digital payments. She said, 67 percent of the accounts have been opened in rural or semi-urban areas, and 55 percent of accounts have been opened by women. More from our correspondent:

Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana has proved instrumental in providing affordable access to formal banking services. The success of the scheme can be measured by the astonishing numbers. More than 53 crore Jan Dhan accounts have garnered a deposit balance of two lakh thirty thousand crore rupees. Over 36 crore free-of-cost RuPay cards having accident insurance cover facility of two lakh rupees have been issued to beneficiaries so far. The account holders are also eligible for getting an overdraft of up to 10 thousand rupees in emergency situations. One of the key features of this scheme is that the beneficiary need not to pay any bank account opening fees or maintenance charge and there is no requirement to maintain a minimum balance. ## Evening News

The centre has approved twelve new greenfield industrial smart cities under the National Industrial Corridor Development Programme to transform the country’s industrial landscape. Addressing media after the Cabinet meeting in New Delhi, Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said, projects span across ten states and strategically planned along six major corridors.

The government has also approved a proposal to conduct e-auctions for 730 Private FM channels in 234 new cities. The estimated reserve price for this auction is over 784 crore rupees.

The Cabinet has also accorded approval for progressive expansion of the Central Sector Scheme of Agriculture Infrastructure Fund. This will significantly enhance and strengthen the agricultural infrastructure in the country and support the farming community. The Union Cabinet has decided to provide financial assistance to the state governments of North Eastern Region for development of Hydro Electric Projects. This scheme has an outlay of four thousand 136 crore rupees, which will be implemented from 2024-25 to 2031-32. Under the scheme, a cumulative hydro capacity of about 15 thousand MegaWatt will be supported. The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs has approved two new lines and one multi-tracking project of Indian Railways at estimated cost six thousand 456 crore rupees.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today chaired the meeting of the 44th edition of PRAGATI, the ICT-based multi-modal platform for Pro-Active Governance and Timely Implementation, involving the Centre and State governments. It was the first meeting of PRAGATI in the third term of NDA Government. In the meeting, seven significant projects were reviewed including two projects relating to road connectivity, two rail projects and one project each of the Coal, Power and Water Resources sectors. The cost of these projects totalled more than 76 thousand 500 crore rupees. The projects relate to Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Jharkhand, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Gujarat, Odisha, Goa, Karnataka, Chhattisgarh and Delhi. During the meeting, Mr. Modi emphasized the fact that every official in the government must be sensitized. During the interaction, the Prime Minister also reviewed AMRUT 2.0 and public grievances related to Jal Jeevan Mission. These projects together inter-alia address water issues in urban and rural areas. Mr. Modi advised Chief Secretaries to personally monitor the works under AMRUT 2.0 and States should make plans keeping in mind the growth potential and future needs of cities. Mr. Modi said that one needs to take benefit of initiatives such as PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana to meet the growing energy needs of cities.
