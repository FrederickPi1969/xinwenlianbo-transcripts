## Morning News

Principal Secretary to Prime Minister Dr. PK Mishra chaired a high-level meeting yesterday to review the status of preparedness for Mpox in the country and related public health measures. He said, Prime Minister Narendra Modi has been continuously monitoring the MPox situation.

During the meeting, Dr Mishra directed the states to enhance surveillance and take effective measures for prompt detection of cases. As of now, there is no reported case of Mpox in the country.

The World Health Organization has again declared Mpox a Public Health Emergency of International Concern on 14th of August in view of its prevalence and spread across many parts of Africa.

Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw has termed the Congress criticism of lateral entry in bureaucracy as hypocrisy. Recently, the Union Public Service Commission issued an advertisement to appoint 45 Joint Secretaries, Directors and Deputy Secretaries in Central Ministries which has been questioned by the opposition parties. In a social media post, Mr. Vaishnaw said that it was the Congress led UPA government which developed the concept of lateral entry. He added that the second  Administrative Reforms Commission chaired by Congress leader Veerappa Moily in 2005 had recommended recruitment of experts to fill the gaps in roles that require specialised knowledge. The Minister asserted that the NDA government has created a transparent method to implement this recommendation of the previous government and the recruitment will be done through UPSC in a transparent and fair manner.

The Supreme Court has taken suo motu cognisance of the Kolkata rape and murder case and will hear the matter tomorrow. A bench comprising Chief Justice of India, DY Chandrachud, Justices JB Pardiwala and Manoj Misra will hear the case. A post-graduate trainee doctor was allegedly raped and murdered at RG Kar Medical College and Hospital in Kolkata on the 9th of this month. A day after the incident, the Kolkata Police arrested a civic volunteer in connection with the case. The incident has triggered nationwide protests, with doctors from across the country demanding action against people involved in the case and safety of doctors at work place. Earlier, The Indian Medical Association had written a letter to the Prime Minister seeking his intervention to bring a central law to check violence against healthcare personnel. Meanwhile, the CBI interrogated the former principal of RG Kar Medical College and Hospital in Kolkata yesterday.

Over 70 Padma-awardee doctors have written to Prime Minister Narendra Modi seeking his personal intervention in the case connected to the rape and murder of a junior doctor at the RG Kar Medical College and Hospital in Kolkata last week. Doctors said, such acts of brutality shake the very foundations of service by medical professionals and highlight the urgent need to address violence, particularly against women, girls, and healthcare professionals. The doctors also urged the Prime Minister to take strong measures to prevent such atrocities from re-occurring. They called upon the law enforcement agencies, policymakers, and the society at large to take immediate and decisive action.

West Bengal Governor CV Ananda Bose has sent a communique to the authorities of all the medical colleges and hospitals across the state, directing them to beef up security on their premises. The move comes after the ghastly rape and murder of a junior doctor at the state-run RG Kar Medical College and Hospital in Kolkata last week. The Governor also directed the hospital authorities to ensure that women doctors and paramedics are provided adequate protection.

The annual Shri Amarnath Ji Yatra ended this morning in Jammu and Kashmir. More than 5 lakh pilgrims drawn from various parts of the country paid their obeisance at the holy cave shrine this year.

“The holy Mace of Lord Shiva called “Chhari Mubarak” reached Shri Amarnath Ji Cave Shrine in South Kashmir Himalayas early this morning making the formal end of the 43-day-long Shri Amarnath Ji Yatra. The Yatra had commenced on June 29, this year. A group of sadhus led by Custodian of the holy Mace Mahant Deependra Giri carrying Chhari Mubarak reached  Shri Amarnath Ji cave after spending a night at Panchtarni. The Yatris visited the Holy Cave from both the longest Pahalgam track in Anantnag and the shortest but steep Baltal track in Ganderbal district. on the occasion of Shravan-Punima coinciding Raksha Bandhan today, the Chhari Mubarak was taken to the holy cave of Shri Amarnath ji before sunrise, and the  pooja began with the rising sun and with its culmination, this Yatra has ended peacefully. Sunil Koul, Akashvani News, Srinagar.”
