## Midday News

The Government today convened an all-party meeting ahead of the Budget Session of Parliament in Delhi. During the meeting, the government sought cooperation from all political parties to ensure the smooth functioning of both the Houses of Parliament. Union Ministers Rajnath Singh, Kiren Rijiju, Arjun Ram Meghwal, JP Nadda, Chirag Paswan and Ramdas Athawale, and Congress leaders Jairam Ramesh and Gaurav Gogoi, NCP leader Praful Patel, DMK leaders TR Baalu and Tiruchi Siva, Aam Aadmi Party leader Sanjay Singh and AIMIM MP Asaduddin Owaisi were among those who attended the meeting. “The Budget Session of Parliament will begin tomorrow. Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman will table the Economic Survey in Parliament tomorrow. The Economic Survey provides statistical information and analysis on various sectors of the economy . It also provides data on employment, GDP growth, and inflation. Suparna Saikia, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will inaugurate the 46th Session of the World Heritage Committee at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi this evening. Mr Modi will also address the gathering on the occasion. India is hosting the World Heritage Committee Meeting for the first time. The session will continue till the 31st of this month. The World Heritage Committee meets once a year and is responsible for managing all matters pertaining to World Heritage and deciding on sites to be inscribed on the World Heritage list. During this meeting, proposals for nominating new sites on the World Heritage list, State of Conservation reports of 124 existing World Heritage properties, International Assistance and Utilisation of World Heritage Funds among others will be discussed. Our correspondent has the details:

“Over two thousand international and national delegates from more than 150 countries will attend the meeting. Various exhibitions will also be set up at Bharat Mandapam to showcase India’s culture. The Return of Treasures Exhibition will showcase some of the retrieved artefacts brought back to the country. So far, more than 350 artefacts have been brought back. While the ‘Incredible India’ exhibition will display India’s rich cultural heritage, age-old civilization, geographical diversity, tourism destinations along with modern developments in the field of Information Technology and infrastructure. Along with the World Heritage Committee Meeting, World Heritage Young Professionals’ Forum and World Heritage Site Managers’ Forum will also be held on the sidelines. With Anand Kumar’s report, Swati Rakheja, Akashvani News, Delhi

In his message, Prime Minister Modi said that it is a matter of immense joy that India is hosting meeting of World Heritage Committee. In a social media post, Mr Modi said this is an important forum to exchange views on ways to preserve the country’s heritage.

In Kerala, the 14-year-old boy from Malappuram district undergoing treatment for Nipah virus infection passed away, this morning. The child was on ventilator support at Kozhikode Medical College hospital. The funeral will be held as per Nipah protocol. Nipah infection was confirmed in the boy yesterday through tests conducted at the Kozhikode Virology Lab and the National Institute of Virology, Pune. The Bombay High Court has directed social media intermediaries to take prompt action against deepfake videos of National Stock Exchange (NSE) managing director and CEO giving stock recommendations. The court directed social media platforms like Facebook, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram to remove all accounts infringing upon the trademark of NSE. The court also directed the social media intermediaries to file their affidavits giving details of all those accounts publishing the fake videos or using the NSE’s registered trademark in three weeks. The court posted the matter for further hearing on 19th of next month.

Guru Purnima, is being celebrated today to honour pivotal role of teachers and spiritual mentors in the society. It falls on the full moon day of Ashadha month. On this day, People express gratitude to teachers and spiritual mentors who guided and shaped their lives with their wisdom and blessings. This day is also called as Vyas Purnima to commemorate the birth anniversary of sage Ved Vyas.

Lord Buddha had given his first sermon on this occasion after achieving enlightenment under the bodhi tree at Bodhgaya. He had travelled to Sarnath from Bodhgaya and gave his sermon on the full moon day. Thus, the followers of Lord Buddha worship him on this day.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has conveyed his greetings to the nation on the occasion of Guru Purnima.
