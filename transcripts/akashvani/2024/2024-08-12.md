## Morning News

Paris Olympics 2024 comes to an end with the glittering closing ceremony at Stade de France.

International Olympic committee president Thomas Bach called the Games a success. The Olympic flag was duly handed over to Karen Bass, the mayor of Los Angeles. The American city will host the 34th edition of the quadrennial Games. Renowned Hollywood actor Tom Cruise was seen emerging from the stadium with the Olympic flag in a motorcycle among other notable performances. The flag bearers for India at the event were Manu Bhaker and PR Sreejesh. The Games provided many iconic moments on the field as many world records toppled and the United States of America once again topped the medals table scraping past China on the final day with 40 gold, 44 silver, 42 bronze and a total of 126 medals. They have topped the table for seven times in eight Games.

Japan, Australia and France finished third, fourth and fifth respectively. French swimmer Leon Marchand was the most successful male athlete at these Games, becoming the first French sportsperson to win four gold medals along with one bronze win. American swimmer Torri Huske was the most decorated female Olympian at Paris with three gold and two silver wins. Now the focus shifts to Los Angeles where sporting greats will converge in four years’ time, and India will look to build on the medal wins in Tokyo and Paris to reach greater heights This is SHAURYA CHAUDHARY Reporting for AKASHVANI from Paris.

India, with a total of 6 medals, including one silver and five bronze medals, finished at the 71st spot in the overall medal tally, which is significantly lower than their 48th overall finish at the 2020 Tokyo Olympics, where they won a total of 7 medals, including one gold, two silver, and four bronze medals.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that all athletes have given their best and every Indian is proud of them. Prime Minister took to social media to laud the performances of Indian athletes at the 2024 Paris Olympics. In a post on X, Mr Modi said, as the Paris Olympics conclude, he appreciates the efforts of the entire Indian contingent through the games. Prime Minister also wished our sporting heroes the best for their upcoming endeavours.

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has refuted the allegations made by Hindenburg Research  and emphasized that it has adequate internal mechanisms for addressing issues relating to conflict of interest, which include disclosure framework and provision for recusal. It also noted that relevant disclosures required in terms of holdings of securities and their transfers have been made by the Chairperson from time to time. It also highlighted  that the chairperson has also recused herself in matters involving potential conflicts of interest.The SEBI has also asked investors to remain calm and exercise due diligence before reacting to such reports.  In a statement, the country’s stock market regulator also asked investors to take note of the disclaimer in the report that states that readers should assume that Hindenburg Research may have short positions in the securities covered in the report. Highlighting about the claims made in the Hindenburg research report, SEBI said that these issues warrant an appropriate response. It further said that the allegations made by Hindenburg Research, against the Adani Group, have been duly investigated by SEBI. The SEBI also said that it remains committed to ensuring the integrity of India’s Capital markets and its orderly growth and development.

In a stock exchange filing, Adani group said that the US short-seller’s latest report is  devised to arrive at predetermined conclusions for personal profiteering with wanton disregard for facts and the law. Adani Group said, it completely rejects these allegations which are a recycling of discredited claims that have been thoroughly investigated, proven to be baseless and already dismissed by the Supreme Court.

The Government has issued operational guidelines for the implementation of the Model Solar Village under the PM-Surya Ghar-Muft Bijli Yojana. The New and Renewable Energy Ministry, in a statement today, said that under the scheme, emphasis has been placed on creating one Model Solar Village per district across the country. The Ministry said that a total financial outlay of 800 crore rupees has been allocated for this component. It said in order to be considered under this scheme, a village must be a revenue village with a population size above 5,000 or 2,000 for special category states. The Ministry said, the villages will be assessed on their overall distributed renewable energy capacity installed within six months after the declaration of potential candidates by the District Level Committee.
