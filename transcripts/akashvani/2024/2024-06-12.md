## Morning News

The newly elected Chief Minister of Odisha, Mohan Charan Majhi will take oath in Bhubaneswar this evening. Mr Majhi will take oath along with two deputy Chief Ministers, Kanak Vardhan Singh Deo and Pravati Parida. This is the first-time, the BJP is going to form a government in the state on its own, ending the 24-year rule of Biju Janata Dal led by Naveen Patnaik. The 52-year-old Majhi was unanimously elected the leader of the BJP legislature party in presence of central BJP observers Union Ministers Rajnath Singh and Bhupendra Yadav. More from our Bhubaneswar Correspondent:

Odisha is going to write history with the swearing-in of firebrand tribal leader Mohan Charan Majhi this evening as the first BJP chief minister of the state. Odisha Governor Raghubar Das invited Mr Majhi to form the next government after he staked claim for the same with the support letter of 78 party MLAs and three independent legislators. Few other BJP legislators may also take oath today as ministers. The chief minister-elect Majhi is scheduled to visit the Lord Jagannath Temple at Puri before taking the oath to seek the blessings of the Lords. Elaborate security arrangements have been made at the Janata Maidan of Bhubaneswar where the swearing-in ceremony will take place at 4:55 this evening. Prime Minister Narendra Modi, several Union Ministers including Union Home Minister Amit Shah, BJD supremo and outgoing chief minister Naveen Patnaik and a galaxy of leaders and industrialists will be attending the swearing-in ceremony.

After being elected as the chief minister, Mr Majhi said his government will do its best to meet the expectations of the people of the state for which they voted BJP to power for the first time.

Lieutenant General Upendra Dwivedi has been appointed as the next Chief of Army Staff. He will take charge on 30th of this month. The present Chief of the Army Staff, General Manoj C Pande will demit office on 30th June. Here is more in this report:

Born on 1st July, 1964, Lieutenant General Upendra Dwivedi was commissioned into the Infantry of the Indian Army in December, 1984. During his long and distinguished service spanning nearly 40 years, he has served in different capacities. In the rank of Lieutenant General, the officer has tenanted important appointments including Director General Infantry and General Officer Commanding in Chief before getting appointed as the Vice Chief of the Army Staff.

An alumnus of the Sainik School, Rewa, National Defence College and US Army War College, Lieutenant General Upendra Dwivedi has an M Phil in Defence and Management Studies and two Master’s Degrees in Strategic Studies and Military Science. He has been decorated with the Param Vishisht and, Ati Vishisht Seva Medals and three GOC-in-C Commendation Cards. Tripti Srivastava, News Desk.

Russian and Belarusian armed forces have begun the second phase of tactical nuclear weapon exercises. The Russian Defence Ministry said, the exercises are aimed at maintaining the readiness of personnel and equipment of units for the combat use of non-strategic nuclear weapons of two countries, to unconditionally ensure the sovereignty and territorial integrity of the Union State. According to an agency report, in the first stage of drills, Russian forces conducted combat training tasks involving the Iskander operational-tactical missile system, specifically equipping launch vehicles with ammunition. Russian Aerospace forces conducted exercises involving the Kinzhal hypersonic aeroballistic missiles.

Ministry of Information and Broadcasting has announced the third edition of Antarashtriya Yoga Diwas Media Samman (AYDMS). This year 33 awards will be given under three categories, Print, Television and Radio in twenty-two Indian languages and English. The Ministry said, the awards are open to all Media Houses and Companies having registration or permission of the Ministry. The last date for entries is 8th of July.

In Jammu and Kashmir, a CRPF jawan was martyred and six security personnel were injured in two overnight encounters with terrorists in Kathua and Doda districts of Jammu division. Additional security personnel have been rushed to the area to intensify the operation against the terrorists. Meanwhile, more than 5 thousand people, mostly members of the Kashmiri Pandit community, started their journey from Jammu today for the annual Kheer Bhawani mela in Kashmir amid tight security. Akashvani Jammu Correspondent reports that the four-day pilgrimage started today with Divisional Commissioner, Jammu Ramesh Kumar, Relief Commissioner (Migrants), Dr. Arvind Karwani and prominent Kashmiri Pandit leaders flagging off the pilgrimage from the Nagrota area in the outskirts of Jammu city.

The annual Kheer Bhawani Mela, celebrated on Zeisht Ashtami, will be held on June 14 at the shrines of Tulmulla in Ganderbal, Tikker in Kupwara, Laktipora Aishmuqam in Anantnag, Mata Tripursundri Devsar in Kulgam, and Mata Kheerbhawani Manzgam also in Kulgam. This is the biggest festival of Hindus in Kashmir after Amarnath yatra and holds immense significance in J&K.

National Testing Agency (NTA) has issued frequently asked questions on post declaration of the NEET (UG) 2024 result. The FAQ have been issued in the wake of alleged discrepancies in the NEET results which was announced on 4th of this month.

On the role of the Grievance Redressal Committee in giving the compensatory marks to candidates for time loss, the NTA said, a Committee was formed which considered the grievances and representations based on factual reports. On a query that how can any candidate score 718 and 719 marks in the exam, the NTA clarified that due to the compensatory marks, two candidates happened to secure 718 and 719 marks.

Union Education Ministry had constituted a four-member panel to review grace marks awarded to more than one thousand 500 candidates in NEET-UG exam.

City Water Minister Atishi has written to Delhi Chief Secretary directing him to ensure that these teams monitor and inspect the main water distribution network from water sources to the water treatment plants and from water treatment plants to primary Underground Reservoirs.

Supreme Court asked about the measures that have been taken against them. A vacation bench presided over by Justice P.K. Mishra was hearing the Delhi government’s plea for the release of surplus water from Himachal Pradesh, as the people of the national capital are facing a shortage of water.

The top court asked the Delhi government to file a report stating the action taken for the loss of water. The matter will be taken up tomorrow.
