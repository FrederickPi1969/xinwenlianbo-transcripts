Prime Minister Narendra Modi will attend the celebrations of Abhidhamma Divas and recognition of Pali as a classical language in New Delhi today. The International Buddhist Confederation in association with the Culture Ministry is observing the International Abhidhamma Divas. The Prime Minister will share his insights on the significance of Abhidhamma Divas, the importance of the Pali language and the government’s ongoing efforts to preserve and promote the rich heritage of Buddha Dhamma.

The recent recognition of Pali as a classical language, along with four other languages, enhances the significance of this year’s Abhidhamma Divas celebrations as Buddha’s teachings on Abhidhamma are originally available in Pali language. Abhidhamma Divas commemorates the descent of Lord Buddha from the celestial realm of the thirty-three divine beings to Sankassiya, known today as Sankisa Basantapur in the Farrukhabad district of Uttar Pradesh.

In Assam, pre-harvest festival Kati Bihu is being observed across the State today. It is one of the three Bihu festivals of the state and is observed for a better harvest and agrarian prosperity, gratitude, and community bonding. More from our Correspondent:

Assamese people celebrate three Bihu festivals and all are closely connected to agriculture. Kati Bihu is celebrated on the first day of Kati month in the Assamese calendar. Kati bihu is also called Kongali because there is not much to eat at this time of year. This bihu is celebrated in the evening by lighting earthen lamps called Saki with mustard oil in the paddy fields and at homes. In the paddy fields, there is a special type of lamp which is called Akash. These earthen lamps are placed on the top of tall bamboo and the cultivators pray for the good health of their crops. Scientifically, these lamps help crops to be healthy and free from insects. The light attracts the harmful insects of the paddy fields and the insects fall prey to the burning fire. Aminul Haque Jwadder,Akashvani News Guwahati.
