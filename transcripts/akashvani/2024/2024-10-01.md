Voting is underway for third and final phase of Assembly Elections in Jammu and Kashmir. Elaborate security arrangements have been put in place for smooth, peaceful and incident free polling in forty Assembly constituencies spread over 7 districts. The polling, which began at 7 this morning will conclude at 6 PM.

The seven districts where polling is being held today are Baramulla, Kupwara and Bandipora in Kashmir division and Jammu, Udhampur, Kathua and Samba districts in Jammu Division. More than 39 lakh voters will decide the fate of 415 candidates in this phase.

Prominent candidates in the fray are former Deputy Chief Minister Muzaffar Hussain Beigh, Basharat Bukhari, Sajad Gani Lone and Nasir Aslam wani in Kashmir Division and Davinder Sing Rana, Choudhary Lal Singh and Raman Bhalla in Jammu Division.

Polling for first two phases in the Union Territory was held on 18th and 25th of September. In  the first phase over 61 per cent and in the second phase 57 per cent polling was recorded. The counting for all the phases will be held on 8th of this month along with Haryana. More from our Correspondent:

“The polling began at 7:00 AM in all the 5060 polling stations. The political fate of 415 candidates of various political parties and independents would be decided by more than 39.18 lakh eligible electors in this phase. More than 20 thousand polling staff has been deployed on duty and every polling station has been provided with Assured Minimum Facilities like drinking water, electricity, toilet, ramp, furniture, veranda/shed besides wheelchairs will also be provided to the needy. A voter help desk has also been established to extend necessary help whenever required. There will be 50 polling booths managed by women, known as Pink Polling Stations, 43 polling stations manned by specially abled persons and 40 polling stations manned by youths. Also, there will be 45 green polling stations and 33 Unique Polling stations. 29 polling stations are established near the Line of Control (LoC) and International Border. “

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has urged the voters to cast their votes in the third and final phase of voting in Jammu and Kashmir. In a social media post today, Mr. Modi expressed confidence that first-time young voters and women will cast their votes in huge numbers.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will hold delegation level talks with his Jamaican counterpart Dr Andrew Holness in New Delhi today. To bolster bilateral ties, a number of MoUs and agreements are expected to be signed between the two countries.

This is the first visit of Dr. Holness to India. During his visit, he will also call on the President and the Vice President. The Jamaican Prime Minister will also meet with other dignitaries and interact with trade and industry leaders during his four days visit. He will also visit Varanasi on 2nd of October. More from our correspondent:

“India and Jamaica, bound by strong historical and cultural ties, are set to further strengthen their bilateral relations with the visit of Jamaican Prime Minister. The visit will deepen economic cooperation between the two nations, enhancing collaboration across key sectors. India has been playing an active role in supporting Jamaica’s developmental projects. A rural development project in Kitson Town in Jamaica is being implemented with a one million US dollars grant announced by Prime Minister Narendra Modi in 2019. This initiative highlights India’s commitment to aiding Jamaica’s sustainable growth. On the trade and investment front, both nations have seen significant growth. Bilateral trade between India and Jamaica has surpassed 66 million US dollars, with India’s exports increasing consistently. Indian companies have also expanded their footprint in Jamaica, particularly in the IT and BPO sectors, further enhancing economic engagement. “

The Centre has approved the release of 675 crore rupees to flood-affected States of Gujarat, Manipur and Tripura as an advance from National Disaster Response Fund (NDRF). Of the total amount, 600 crore rupees has been approved for Gujarat, 50 crore rupees for Manipur and 25 crore rupees for Tripura. These states have been affected by extremely heavy rainfall, floods and landslides during the south-west monsoon this year. Ministry of Home Affairs said in a statement that the Central Government is fully committed to provide all possible assistance to flood-affected States.

The Health Ministry has issued draft guidelines outlining conditions for the withdrawal of life support in terminally ill patients. The draft guidelines have been released for public comments. Terminal illness has been defined as an irreversible or incurable condition in which death is inevitable in the foreseeable future. Severe brain injuries with no recovery after 72 hours are included in this category.

Housing and Urban Affairs Minister Manohar Lal has said that the government has taken major steps in the 100 days of Modi 3.0 government to improve the urban infrastructure and housing landscape. Addressing a press conference in New Delhi, the Minister informed that the government has approved Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana-Urban (PMAY-U) 2.0 in its first 100 days. He said, this initiative aims to provide financial assistance to one crore urban poor and middle-class families in urban areas with the investment of two lakh 30 thousand crore rupees.

The Indian Army has signed the eighth procurement contract through the government’s flagship initiative Innovations for Defence Excellence (iDEX) in New Delhi. The Ministry of Defence said, the contract for procurement of Generation of Quantum Secure Key was signed with QuNu Labs in the presence of Vice Chief of the Army Staff Lieutenant General NC Raja Subramani. This contract will replace the algorithm-based encryption systems and enable greater security and optimise manpower commitment.

It involves hand holding of 77 Startups for development of latest state of the art solutions for the Indian Army.

The Supreme Court has granted relief to a Dalit student who missed the chance of admission to the Indian Institute of Technology, Dhanbad, for being late in paying the online admission fee by a few minutes. A bench comprising Chief Justice of India DY Chandrachud, Justice JB Pardiwala and Justice Manoj Misra directed yesterday that the petitioner should be admitted to the seat at the Electrical Engineering course at IIT Dhanbad which was allotted to him.

Reserve bank of India has released its monthly data on sectoral deployment of bank credit. The data released yesterday shows that the growth in credit to agriculture and allied activities continued to be robust with the growth of 17.7 per cent in August this year as against the corresponding period last year. Credit growth to industries also exhibited a growth of nearly 10 per cent during the same period.

In services sector, however the decrease in credit growth was registered. It decelerated to 15.6 per cent in August this year from 21 per cent a year ago.

The Madhya Pradesh government has classified heatwaves as natural disasters. This decision will come in to effect in summer 2025. With this initiative, people affected by heat wave will also be able to get financial help. More from our correspondent:

“Following directives from the central Ministry of Home Affairs, the state government has officially notified heatwaves as a local disaster recently. The new regulation is set to take effect in the summer of 2025. As a result, individuals affected by heatwaves will be eligible for the same financial assistance that is currently available for those impacted by floods, earthquakes, and lightning strikes. The regulation aims to provide financial assistance to the citizens affected by extreme heat conditions. Sanjeev Sharma, Akashvani News, Bhopal.”

The weather agency predicted similar conditions for Assam, Meghalaya, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, and Tripura during the week. According to the IMD, no significant rainfall is expected over the northwest, west, and central parts of the country.

Israel appears to be on the brink of launching a ground offensive into Lebanon. This development follows two weeks of intensive airstrikes and the high-profile assassination of Hezbollah leader Hassan Nasrallah. More from our West Asia correspondent:

“Tensions are escalating along the Israel-Lebanon border as indications grow that Israel may be preparing for a ground incursion into Lebanon. This development comes just days after Israel’s assassination of Hezbollah leader Hassan Nasrallah, marking a significant escalation in the conflict. Israeli Defence Minister Yoav Gallant has hinted at an imminent next phase of the war, promising to use all available means to support the return of Israeli citizens who fled Hezbollah rockets. The situation has raised international concern, with US President Joe Biden expressing opposition to Israeli ground operations in Lebanon and calling for a ceasefire. However, Israel appears determined to proceed, with sources indicating the operation may focus on border security. This is Vinod Kumar, Akashvani News, Dubai.”

Hezbollah, for its part, claims readiness for a ground engagement. The group’s deputy leader, Naim Qassem, made this statement in his first public speech since Nasrallah’s assassination.

The ongoing conflict has already taken a heavy toll on Lebanon. Israeli airstrikes have continued, resulting in significant civilian casualties and displacement. The Lebanese government reports that about 1,000 civilians have been killed and one million forced to flee their homes.

## Midday News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi held delegation level talks with his Jamaican counterpart Dr Andrew Holness in New Delhi today. After the talks, India and Jamaica exchanged Memoranda of Understanding in the fields of Digital Infrastructure, Cultural Exchange and Cooperation in the area of Sports.

In a joint press statement later, Prime Minister Modi expressed confidence that Jamaican Prime Minister’s visit will give new energy to bilateral relations as well as their engagement with the entire Caribbean region.

Prime Minister Modi said, trade and investment between India and Jamaica are growing. He said, India has always been a reliable and committed development partner in Jamaica’s development journey. He further stated that India is ready to share its experience with Jamaica in areas such as Digital Public Infrastructure, small scale industries, biofuel, innovation, health, education and  agriculture.

Speaking about organized crime, drug trafficking and terrorism, the Prime Minister said that these are the common challenges and both sides have agreed to face these challenges together.

Mr Modi said, both sides will continue their efforts to ensure global peace and stability.

Prime Minister Modi further informed the Government of India has decided to name the road in front of the Jamaica High Commission in New Delhi as ‘Jamaica Marg’. He added that India and Jamaica may be separated by vast oceans, but our people, our culture and our history are linked to each other.

In his address, Prime Minister of Jamaica said, for almost two centuries now, Indians have made their mark in Jamaica, contributing to the development  of Jamaica in various spheres.

Earlier in the day, Prime Minister of Jamaica visited Rajghat and paid homage to the Father of the Nation, Mahatma Gandhi. Dr Holness arrived in New Delhi yesterday on a four-day official visit to India.

This is the first visit of Dr. Holness to India. During his visit, the Jamaican Prime Minister will also call on the President and the Vice President. He will also visit Varanasi tomorrow.

Voting is in progress for the third and final phase of Assembly Elections in Jammu and Kashmir. Forty constituencies across seven districts are going to polls in this phase. Over 39 lakh voters are likely to exercise their franchise to decide the fate of 415 candidates in this phase. Long queues of enthusiastic voters can be seen outside polling stations, waiting for their turn to cast their votes.
