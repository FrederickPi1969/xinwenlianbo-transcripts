## Morning News

The 10th edition of International Day of Yoga is being celebrated across the globe today. The main function in India was held at Srinagar in Jammu and Kashmir, where Prime Minister Narendra Modi performed yoga this morning at Sher-i-Kashmir International Conference Centre (SKICC), located on the banks of Dal Lake.

On the occasion, the Prime Minister participate in the Common Yoga Protocol session, highlighting Yoga’s significance in nurturing physical, mental, and spiritual growth.

Addressing the gathering, Mr Modi extended greetings to people performing yoga in the country and every corner of the world on International Yoga Day. He said, International Yoga Day has completed a historic journey of 10 years. In 2014, he proposed International Yoga Day at the United Nations which was supported by 177 nations and this was a record in itself. He said, since then, Yoga Day has been creating new records.

The Prime Minister said, this year a 101-year-old woman Yoga teacher from France was accorded the Padma Shri. She had never come to India but she dedicated her entire life to creating awareness about Yoga.

Jammu and Kashmir Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha, Union Minister Prataprao Ganpatrao Jadhav and many other dignitaries took part in the event. Globally, Embassies and Indian Missions abroad joined the celebrations, reflecting yoga’s widespread influence.
This year’s theme is Yoga for Self and Society,” which emphasizes the vital role the practice plays in fostering both individual well-being and societal harmony.

Various mass yoga demonstrations are being held across the country and around the world on the International Yoga Day.

Ministers and officials in the National Capital performed yoga asanas to emphasise the importance of wellness and mindfulness.

Health Minister JP Nadda participated in a mass yoga demonstration at Yamuna Sports Complex. Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw along with I&B secretary Sanjay Jaju took part in the Yoga session at Lodhi Garden. External Affairs Minister Dr S Jaishankar along with other diplomats also performed yoga.

Union Minister of Parliamentary Affairs Kiren Rijiju participated in the celebrations at Millennium Park. Union Ministers Prahlad Joshi, HD Kumaraswamy, and B.L. Verma practiced yoga at separate locations across Delhi. Army Chief Designate Lt General Upendra Dwivedi and Indian Navy Chief Admiral Dinesh K Tripathi also performed Yoga exercises this morning.

In Madhya Pradesh, Chief Minister Dr. Mohan Yadav performed yoga at Lal Parade Ground in Bhopal. On this occasion, the Chief Minister also launched Shri Anna Sanvardhan Abhiyan ie Millets promotion campaign.

In Bihar, Union Minister of State for Home Affairs Nityanand Rai took part in Yoga Day event at Sarairanjan in Samastipur district. The Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB) Bihar Unit has organized public programmes in several cities in the state. A special Yoga session has also been organised at Akashvani Patna premises.

Talking to Akashvani News on the eve of International Yoga Day, Mr. Kovind said, the practice of Yoga unites people and communities and it also has potential to connect one nation with another nation.

## Midday News

The 10th International Day of Yoga is being celebrated across the globe today. The main function was held at Sher-i-Kashmir International Conference Centre in Srinagar, Jammu and Kashmir. Prime Minister Narendra Modi led the celebrations from Srinagar. J&K Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha, Union Minister Prataprao Ganpatrao Jadhav and many other dignitaries joined the programme. Globally, Embassies and Indian Missions abroad are joining the celebrations, reflecting yoga’s widespread influence.

On the occasion, the Prime Minister addressed the gathering and participated in the Common Yoga Protocol session, highlighting Yoga’s significance in nurturing physical, mental, and spiritual growth. He underlined that the number of people practicing Yoga is growing worldwide and its utility is also being recognized by the people. He stated that there is barely any world leader who has not discussed Yoga during his interactions.

He recalled watching a video about a competition organized in Egypt regarding photographing or making a video on yoga at the country’s iconic tourism centers and praised the efforts of those taking part in it. “Similarly, yoga and tourism can become a major source of employment in Jammu & Kashmir”, the Prime Minister highlighted.

On expansion of Yoga in the last 10 years, the Prime Minister talked about a new Yoga economy and mentioned the growing attraction for Yoga tourism and people’s desire to visit India to learn authentic yoga. He also mentioned yoga retreats, resorts, dedicated facilities for Yoga at airports and hotels, Yoga apparel and equipment, personal Yoga trainers. He said all these developments are creating new avenues of employment for the youth.

Talking about the theme of this year’s International Yoga Day -‘Yoga for Self and Society’, PM Modi said that the world is looking at yoga as a powerful agent of global good and it enables us to live in the present without the baggage of the past.

The Delhi High Court has said the trial court order granting bail to Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal will not be given effect till it hears the ED’s plea challenging the relief granted in the money laundering case linked to the alleged excise scam.

Every year, June 21 is marked as the day of the summer solstice in the northern hemisphere of the world.

The solstices, which mark the changing of seasons, occur twice a year and indicate the year’s shortest and longest daylight hours, depending on the hemisphere in which one is located, during which the length of day and night differ significantly.
