## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will flag off three Vande Bharat trains this afternoon through video conferencing. Realizing the vision of ‘Make in India’ and Aatmanirbhar Bharat, the state-of-the-art Vande Bharat Express will improve connectivity on three routes, Meerut-Lucknow, Madurai-Bengaluru, and Chennai-Nagercoil. These new Vande Bharat trains will provide people with world-class means of travel, offering speed and comfort while catering to the three states of Uttar Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, and Karnataka. The introduction of these Vande Bharat Express trains will herald a new standard of rail service to meet the needs of regular travelers, professionals, businesses, and the student community in immense measure. Our correspondent reports that the Vande Bharat Express between Meerut and Lucknow will have eight coaches and cover the journey in around 7 hours 15 minutes.

“The   Meerut – Lucknow Vande Bharat Express, will begin its regular service on Sunday from Lucknow, and  from Meerut City  Junction, it will start on Monday. The train will operate six days a week except  Tuesday . It will depart from Meerut City at 6:35 am  and from Lucknow at 2:45PM. This state-of-the-art, semi-high-speed train is designed with modern amenities to offer passengers a faster and more comfortable travel experience. The Train will significantly improve connectivity and reduce travel time between Meerut City and Lucknow. Anand Kumar, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

The Vande Bharat Express between Chennai Egmore to Nagercoil will enhance links to key industrial, business, and tourist hubs, including Trichy, Madurai, Dindigul, and Tirunelveli.

“The first Vande Bharat Rail Service will connect Chennai to Nagercoil, a land of ancient temples and also tourism . Passing through 12 districts, the train will cover a distance of 726 kilometers and will provide a modern and faster travel experience with world class amenities and advanced safety features including Kavach technology, CCTV cameras for enhanced security and 360° rotating chairs. Carrying the name of Kovil meaning temple, Nagercoil also has interesting tourist places like the ancient Padmanabhapuram Palace and temple. The new Vande Bharat train will help to boost the economic growth as it is also a spice trade centre. Joy, Akashvani News, Chennai.”

India, the Maldives, Mauritius and Sri Lanka have signed a Charter and an MoU for the establishment of the Colombo Security Conclave (CSC) Secretariat in Colombo to address security and stability challenges in the Indian Ocean Region. The signing ceremony was hosted in Colombo by the Government of Sri Lanka.
National Security Advisor Ajit Doval and the representatives of the Maldives, Mauritius and Sri Lanka signed the documents yesterday on behalf of the respective Member States.

Speaking on the occasion, National Security Advisor Ajit Doval emphasized the importance of the Colombo Security Conclave in addressing traditional, non-traditional, and emerging hybrid security challenges. He highlighted the need for sustained regional cooperation across various security pillars. The CSC’s core objective is to promote regional security by addressing transnational threats and challenges of common concern to the Member States.

In Punjab, water scarcity has reached a critical point amid rapid depletion of groundwater level in most of the districts. Out of the total 23 districts of the state, the situation is extremely worrying in about eight districts, while in nine districts the water-level has reached a critical state. According to a Central Ground Water Board’s report, about 17 billion cubic meters of ground water is available in the state while 28 billion cubic meters of water is being exploited.

“The worst situation is in the Chief Minister’s own district Sangrur where the water level has gone down by more than 44 meter. The water level is continuously going down in Patiala, Pathankot, Jalandhar, Hoshiarpur, Ropar, Kapurthala, Tarn Taran and Shaheed Bhagat Singh Nagar districts and it is between 28 to 43 meters in these districts. Due to excessive water exploitation, about 78 percent of the state’s area is in the dark zone. Shishu Sharma Shantal, Akashvani News, Jalandhar.”

In the World  Under 20 Athletics Championships, Aarti won a bronze medal in the women’s 10,000 metre race walk event with a national U20 record time to open India’s medal account at Lima in Peru yesterday. She clocked 44 minutes 39.39 seconds to finish third in the gruelling event on the penultimate day of competitions. Surpassing world’s leading digital payments platforms, India’s Unified Payments Interface (UPI) processed nearly 81 lakh crore rupees transactions in the April-July period this year, which is a staggering 37 per cent increase (year-on-year).

According to the latest data by global payments hub Paysecure, UPI processed 3,729.1 transactions per second, which shows a 58 per cent surge over the  2,348 transactions every second registered in 2022- surpassing China’s Alipay, Paypal and Brazil’s PIX in the number of transactions.

In July, UPI transactions crossed 20.6 lakh crore rupees the highest-ever during a month. The value of UPI transactions processed remained above 20 lakh crore rupees for three consecutive months.

Heavy rains are lashing Andhra Pradesh due to a low-pressure system that has formed over the Bay of Bengal. Since last night, continuous downpours have affected normal life in various regions of the state. Cities and towns are witnessing waterlogged roads, and low-lying areas are inundated. Streams and canals in many places are overflowing due to the relentless rain. District administrations across the state have declared a holiday for schools today. The water accumulation on roads is disrupting vehicular movement.

In Vijayawada, a landslide occurred at Mogalrajapuram, resulting in the death of one person and injuries to several others.

Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu held a review meeting with officials regarding the heavy rains across the state. He directed the officials to remain vigilant at all times and to undertake necessary relief measures.

In Telangana, the met officials have issued a Red alert of heavy to very heavy rain in 7 districts today and 9 districts tomorrow due to a depression over west central and nearby areas in the Bay of Bengal. The officials informed that the formation lay centred this morning 80 kms south-southeast of Kalingapatnam in Andhra Pradesh. The district authorities have been asked to remain alert to ensure no human loss.

In Gujarat, the flood situation has been normalised after the significant decrease in the rainfall in the last 24 hours. The relief and rescue operation in the flood affected districts has also been concluded. Barring a few places in Kutch and parts of Saurashtra, no region in the state recorded significant rainfall during the last 24 hours.

The Centre has cut windfall tax on domestically produced crude oil to one thousand 850 rupees per tonne from two thousand 100 rupees per tonne. In an official notification, the Government said that it has come into effect from today. Windfall tax on export of diesel and aviation turbine fuel has been retained at nil. The tax rates are reviewed every fortnight based on average oil prices in the previous two weeks.
