Prime Minister Modi said, within the first hundred days of the formation of the new government in Odisha, investment worth 45 thousand crore rupees has been approved. Mr Modi stated that Droupadi Murmu, a daughter of Odisha from the tribal community serving as President of India is a great source of pride for all of us.

On the occasion, Union Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan said, on the Prime Minister’s advice, the Pravasi Bharatiya Divas will be held in Odisha next year.

Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said, over ten thousand crore rupees has been given to Odisha for development of Railway in the State.

The three-day Odisha Parba 2024, which began on Friday concluded today.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi addressed the nation in the 116th Edition of Mann Ki Baat on Akashvani today. In the programme, Mr Modi emphasized the role of young people in protecting the elderly from Digital scams and assisting them in navigating the digital world. Stressing that the elderly are most prone to cybercrimes, Mr Modi said, it is everyone’s responsibility to guide them in understanding digital technology and its risks.

The Prime Minister urged youth from non-political background to join politics. He announced that in the next few months many special campaigns will be organized in the country to connect one lakh such young people to politics.

Mr Modi announced that on the 162nd birth anniversary of Swami Vivekananda in January next year, a special event ‘Viksit Bharat Young Leaders Dialogue’ will be held at Bharat Mandapam in Delhi.

On the occasion of the National Cadet Corps (NCC) Day today, Mr Modi noted with satisfaction that the enrolment of girl cadets in the NCC has risen to almost 40 percent from 25 percent.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will inaugurate the ICA Global Cooperative Conference at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi tomorrow. For the first time, in the 130 year long history of the International Cooperative Alliance (ICA), the Global Cooperative Conference and General Assembly are being organized in India. On the occasion, Mr. Modi will launch a commemorative stamp on the International Year of Cooperatives 2025. Our correspondent reports that Bhutan Prime Minister Dasho Tshering Tobgay, and the Deputy Prime Minister of Fiji, Manoa Kamikamica will also attend the conference.

“The International Cooperative Alliance was founded in 1895. It represents thirty lakh cooperative enterprises worldwide and has more than one billion cooperative members. The six day conference at Bharat Mandapam will bring together cooperatives from around the globe for a series of events and discussions. Around three thousand foreign and Indian delegates are expected to attend this event. The conference will explore the role of cooperatives in creating a collective, peaceful, and prosperous future for all. Dipendra Kumar, Akashvani News Delhi. “

Now some news from the 55th International Film Festival of India (IFFI) underway in Goa.

At the IFFI today, Bollywood star Ranbir Kapoor paid tribute to the legendary actor and filmmaker Raj Kapoor as part of his centenary celebrations. During an engaging masterclass session, Ranbir shared that films of his grandfather Raj Kapoor were powerful stories that connected deeply with the audience.

Ranbir Kapoor also announced a special nationwide film festival in December, showcasing restored versions of Raj Kapoor’s classic movies to honour his birth centenary.

Our correspondent reports that a variety of activities were held at IFFI today, including film screenings and events, with an ever-growing number of visitors.

“Today at the International Film Festival of India, two key events concluded: the Creative Minds of Tomorrow 48-hour filmmaking challenge and the Film Bazaar. Film Bazaar wrapped up after vibrant and dynamic sessions of film screenings, networking, and collaborations.The day also featured several masterclasses, attracting audiences of all ages who were eager to gain valuable insights into the art and craft of filmmaking. In the evening, cultural performances at IFFIesta energized the atmosphere, drawing hundreds of attendees to celebrate a vibrant blend of culture, cuisine, and entertainment. With Omvesh Upadhay, Durgesh Bhadauria, Akashvani News, Goa.”
