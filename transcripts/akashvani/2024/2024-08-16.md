## Morning News

Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) will launch an earth observation satellite EOS-8 onboard its Small Satellite Launch Vehicle (SSLV) this morning. The launch will take place at 9:17 AM from Satish Dhawan Space Centre in Sriharikota. Six-and-a-half-hour countdown for the launch began at 2:47 AM today. The mission is the third and final developmental flight for SSLV after which the rocket will get into full-fledged operational mode.

All arrangements are in place at the Satish Dhawan Space Centre Sriharikota for the launch. Scientists at ISRO have been doing the last minute checks for a perfect launch. The small satellite launch vehicle SSLV D3 is capable of launching Mini, Micro and Nano satellites weighing 10 to 500 kilograms into 500 km orbit. The objective of the mission is to demonstrate repeatable flight performance of SSLV vehicle systems and injection of EOS 08 satellite and SR- 0 DEMOSAT  into a 475 km circular orbit. The rocket carries three payloads consisting of a global navigation satellite system. This will send infrared  images during day and night for applications like surveillance, disaster monitoring, environmental monitoring, fire detection, volcanic activities and industrial and power plant disasters. It will also be used for calculation of ocean surface winds, soil moisture, Cryosphere applications over Himalayan Region, Flood detection and  in land water body detection.Joy/Chennai.

The Indian Medical Association, IMA has announced a 24-hour nationwide withdrawal of non-emergency services from 6 am tomorrow in protest of the alleged rape and murder of a trainee woman doctor at the state-run R G Kar Medical College and Hospital in Kolkata and the subsequent vandalism at the facility. The medical body said in a statement that out-patient departments (OPD) will not function and elective surgeries will not be conducted. However, essential services will be maintained and casualty wards will remain operational.

Our Goa correspondent reports that over 1,000 doctors in the state will refrain from attending OPD)services in protest. The IMA Goa Branch president, Dr. Sandesh Chodankar said, discussions are underway with hospital support staff to join the protest.

Meanwhile, resident doctors and PG medical students in Kerala will observe a statewide strike across hospitals today demanding quick action against the guilty in the Kolkata doctor rape and murder case.

According to the Kerala Medical Post Graduates Association, KMPGA PG doctors will boycott all non-emergency services, including duty in the OPD and wards today. They are demanding implementation of the Central Protection Act and arrest of the perpetrators of the brutal rape-murder in Kolkata within 48 hours. The KMPGA has threatened to continue their protests if their demands remain unaddressed.

Bharatiya Janata Party’s Mahila Morcha, the women’s cell of BJP, will take out a silent candle march in Kolkata today to condemn the ghastly act of rape and murder of a resident doctor. The march will be taken out at 6 p.m. outside the R.G. Kar MCH under the leadership of its national president, Vanati Srinivasan. BJP Rajya Sabha member Darshan Singh, and national office-bearers like Sangeeta Yadav, Geeta Shakya, Indu Bala Goswami, and its West Bengal in-charge Pooja Kapil Mishra will also take part in the march.

The state unit of the BJP has also called for a two-hour cease-work at all state government establishments across West Bengal and called for road-blockades in all the districts from 2 p.m. today.

Union Agriculture and Farmers Welfare Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan inaugurated the National Pest Surveillance System (NPSS) in New Delhi yesterday. Mr Chouhan highlighted that through NPSS, the farmers will be able to know different types of insecticides and pesticides and save their crops from their harmful effect. On the occasion, Mr Chouhan also interacted with a number of farmers who are the beneficiaries of various schemes of the Central Government. Around Five Hundred farmers were present in the event.

The Agriculture Minister highlighted that recently, the Central Government released 109 high-yielding, climate-resilient, and bio-fortified crop varieties.

India will host the 3rd Voice of Global South Summit tomorrow. The theme of the Summit is – An Empowered Global South for a Sustainable Future. External Affairs Ministry said, it will act as a platform to expand discussions held in previous Summits on a range of complex challenges that continue to affect the world, such as conflicts, food and energy security crises and climate change.

The 3rd Summit will be held in virtual format structured into Leaders’ Session and Ministerial sessions. The inaugural session would be at Head of State and  Government level and will be hosted by Prime Minister Narendra Modi.

Dr Jaishankar’s visit will enable both sides to review various aspects of bilateral relations including political, trade, investment, energy, security, cultural, consular and people-to-people contacts. The two sides will also exchange views on regional and international issues of mutual interest.

Renowned missile scientist Dr Ram Narain Agarwal passed away yesterday. Honoured with Padma Shree and Padma Bhushan, Dr Agarwal was instrumental in the development of Agni, India’s long range missile.

Sweden reported its first mpox case yesterday, a day after the World Health Organization (WHO) declared the infection a global public health emergency. With this, Sweden has also become the first country outside the African continent to report the more contagious strain of mpox virus. The patient is believed to have contracted the virus during a visit to a region in Africa currently experiencing a major outbreak of mpox clade I.

On Wednesday, the WHO declared mpox a global public health emergency for the second time in two years, following an outbreak in Democratic Republic of Congo that has spread to other countries.

In Tennis, Alexander Zverev of Germany has become the first player to earn 50 tour-level wins this season after defeating Karen Khachanov at the Cincinnati Open last night. Zverev beat Khachanov in straight sets for an opening win at the ATP Masters 1000 event. Five times in his career, Zverev has earned the feat of 50 tour-level wins in single season, having also hit the milestone in 2017, 2018, 2021, and 2023. Zverev leads this year’s stats for most wins this season with 50 wins with Jannik Sinner placed second with 45 wins and Casper Ruud at third with 44 wins followed by Carlos Alcaraz who has a 38 wins this season so far.
