## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will address the inaugural session of ‘Journey Towards Viksit Bharat: A Post Union Budget 2024-25 Conference’ in New Delhi today.

The Conference is being organised by the Confederation of Indian Industry (CII) and is aimed at presenting the outline for Government’s larger vision for growth and industry’s role in this endeavour.

Delhi Lieutenant Governor Vinai Kumar Saxena has announced financial assistance of 10 lakh rupees to families of the students who lost their lives in the IAS coaching centre flooding incident in the National Capital Delhi. Three IAS aspirants had lost their lives due to water logging in the basement of a coaching center in Delhi’s Old Rajinder Nagar on Saturday.

In Jharkhand, many passengers have been injured following the derailment of 18 boggies of Howrah – Mumbai mail this morning between Badabambo and Kharsawa railway station near Potobeda village under Chakradharpur division. Quad Foreign Ministers have warned of terrorists using new technology and demanded international action against UN-listed terrorist groups including Lashkar-e-Tayyiba, Jaish-e-Mohammad, as well as Al-Qa’ida and the Islamic State outfit. In a joint statement after their meeting in Tokyo yesterday, the Quad ministers said that they are committed to working with international and regional partners to forestall threats posed by the use of new and emerging technologies for terrorist purposes. External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar, US Secretary of State Antony Blinken, and Foreign Ministers Penny Wong of Australia and Yoko Kamikawa of Japan reiterated the call for concerted action against all UN-listed terrorist groups. Recalling the 26/11 Mumbai and the 2016 Pathankot terrorist attacks, the leaders called for bringing the perpetrators of these attacks to justice without delay.

India has sent humanitarian assistance to Haiti containing nine tons of medical supplies. The consignment left New Delhi yesterday for Port-au-Prince. It includes blood transfusion essentials, reagents for Immunohematology, Rapid tests and ELISA, among others.

The Supreme Court dismissed a plea filed by the Enforcement Directorate (ED) challenging the Jharkhand High Court’s decision to grant bail to Chief Minister Hemant Soren in a money laundering case linked to an alleged land scam. A bench headed by Justice B.R. Gavai declined to interfere with the order of the Jharkhand High Court, which had granted Mr. Soren regular bail on June 28th with certain conditions. Just a week after his release from jail, Mr. Soren took oath as the Jharkhand Chief Minister for the third time earlier this month. The ED moved a special leave petition before the apex court, seeking that CM Soren’s bail be cancelled.

India Meteorological Department (IMD) has forecast active monsoon conditions over most parts of northwest India for the next 3-4 days. According to IMD, the monsoon trough is active and south of its normal position at mean sea level, it is likely to shift gradually northwards during the next 3-4 days.

Under the influence of active monsoon trough, widespread rainfall with isolated heavy falls is expected over Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Jammu-Kashmir-Ladakh-Gilgit-Baltistan-Muzaffarabad, Punjab, Haryana, Chandigarh-Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, and West Rajasthan during the next 4 days. The IMD also forecast widespread rainfall with isolated heavy falls over West, Central, South Peninsular, East, and Northeast India for the next few days. The weather department said that very heavy rainfall is expected at isolated places over Saurashtra, Kutch, Konkan, Goa, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Chhattisgarh, Coastal & South Interior Karnataka, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, Tripura, Arunachal Pradesh, and Odisha until the 2nd of next month. Pallavi Parmar, Akashvani News, Delhi

In Venezuela, security forces have used tear gas and rubber bullets to disperse protesters demonstrating against the results of contentious elections. Thousands of individuals, including many who had traveled long distances from the surrounding mountain slums, gathered in central Caracas and marched towards the presidential palace yesterday evening. The unrest followed President Nicolás Maduro’s announcement of his electoral victory.

Leaders across the America and beyond responded to the Venezuelan electoral authority’s announcement yesterday that President Nicolas Maduro had won a third term, despite numerous exit polls suggesting an opposition victory. Expressed serious concern, US Secretary of State Antony Blinken said that the announced result does not seem to reflect the true will or the votes of the Venezuelan people.

Amidst allegations of election irregularities, the US administration has called for the Venezuelan electoral authority to release detailed polling data to substantiate its claim that Maduro has secured another six-year term-a result that could have far-reaching implications for the region.

Indian shooters Manu Bhaker and Sarabjot Singh will compete for India’s second medal at Paris Olympics in 10 metre Air Pistol Mixed Team event today. The duo qualified for Bronze medal final match of the 10m Air Pistol Mixed Team event yesterday. They will be facing Oh Ye Jin and Lee Wonho of South Korea this afternoon.

Para swimmer Jiya Rai has become the world’s youngest and fastest female para swimmer to successfully swim solo across the English Channel. The 16-year-old girl covered the 34 km stretch, from Abbots Cliff in England to Pointe de la Courte-Dune in France, in 17 hours 25 minutes on Sunday and Monday. Jiya is an international open water para swimmer and holds a world record in open water swimming.

## Midday News

In Paris Olympics, Manu Bhaker and Sarabjot Singh today clinched the Bronze in the mixed 10 meter air pistol shooting event by defeating South Korean pair of Oh Ye Jin and Lee Wonho 16-10. With this win, Manu etched her name in the history of Indian sports by becoming the first Indian athlete to win two medals at the same edition of the Olympics. Earlier, Manu won a bronze in the women’s 10m Air Pistol event. Overall, Manu is the third Indian to win more than one individual medal in the Olympics.

Shuttler P V Sindhu and wrestler Sushil Kumar are the only two other Indians to have won multiple Olympic medals but in separate Olympic games.

We now go over to our correspondent Shaurya Chaudhary, who is in Paris to cover the games.
