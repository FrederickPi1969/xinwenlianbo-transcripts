## Morning News

Campaigning has picked up momentum for the first phase of assembly elections in Jharkhand and the Single phase polling in Maharashtra. Leaders of various political parties are conducting intense campaigning, in both the states.

In Jharkhand, voting for the first phase of assembly polls will be held on the 13th of this month. Defence Minister and Senior BJP leader Rajnath Singh held a public rally at Lohardaga in Ranchi yesterday. He highlighted that Jharkhand was formed during the BJP-led government, with the primary aim of making the state a model of development. Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath also addressed a poll meeting at Domchanch in Koderma. He accused Senior Jharkhand Mukti Morcha leader and Chief Minister Hemant Soren of ruining the state and said that election is the right time to change the government.  Congress President Mallikarjun Kharge sought vote in favour of I.N.D.I.A bloc candidates at Mandu in Hazaribagh while senior JMM leader Hemant Soren also addressed a series of meetings across the state.

Polling for the second phase election in Jharkhand will take place on the 20th of this month.

In Maharashtra, Leader of Opposition in the Lok Sabha and Congress leader Rahul Gandhi will be campaigning in Nagpur and Mumbai today. Prime Minister Narendra Modi is expected to address a series of rallies between 8 to 14 November in the state. More from our correspondent:

“Congress leader Rahul Gandhi will kick off his campaign for Maharashtra Assembly Elections by paying homage at Deekshabhoomi in Nagpur. He is also scheduled to address Samvidhan Sammelan in Nagpur. Popular Folk Singer, Sharda Sinha passed away in New Delhi last night. She was 72. She breathed her last at All India Institute of Medical Sciences in the National Capital. Known for her Bhojpuri and Maithili Folks Songs, Sharda Sinha was honoured with Padma Shri, Padma Bhushan and Sangeet Natak Akademi Awards. Akashvani News pays tribute to the Swar Kokila of Bihar Sharda Sinha :

Doyen of folk music of Bihar, Sharda Sinha served several generations with her lilting voice in different regional languages including Maithili, Bhojpur, Magahi. One of the greatest cultural ambassadors of Bihar and the eastern region, Sharda Sinha was born in Supaul on October 1, 1952. Her journey from a housewife in typical Bihari family to an acclaimed folk singer is an interesting one as she revolted against the orthodox warp and weft of the society. The echo and reverberation of melodious songs of Sharda Sinha associated with the great festival of faith, Chhath, Vivah Geet and other rituals will continue for the generations ahead WITH THE REPORT OF DHARMENDRA KUMAR RAI FROM PATNA.

The Government has approved the PM-Vidyalaxmi scheme to provide financial support to meritorious students. Briefing media in New Delhi today on the decisions taken in the Cabinet meeting, Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said, students will be eligible to get collateral free and guarantor free loans to pursue higher education. He said, the scheme has been introduced so that financial constraints do not prevent any youth of India from pursuing quality higher education.

As per the scheme, for loan amount up to 7.5 lakh rupees, 75 percent credit guarantee will be provided by the government to support banks to expand coverage. Three percent interest subvention for loan up to 10 lakh rupees will also be provided during moratorium period for students with annual family income of upto eight lakh rupees. This scheme is expected to benefit seven lakh students. To avail the benefit of the scheme, the students can apply on unified portal PM Vidyalaxmi for education loan.

Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan has said that Cabinet’s approval to PM Vidyalaxmi with an outlay of 3,600 crore rupees will remove obstacles to higher education and enable the country’s youth to pursue their dreams. In a social media post, Mr Pradhan said, this scheme will ensure that financial constraints do not prevent students from pursuing education.

The government has also decided to infuse fresh equity of 10,700 crore rupees for the Food Corporation of India. Briefing the media, Information and Broadcasting Minister Vaishnaw said that FCI plays a big role in the procurement of food and that it has been decided to significantly strengthen the FCI. He said, the infusion of equity is a significant step towards enhancing the operational capabilities of FCI.

This infusion will help lower the interest burden and will ultimately reduce the subsidy burden on the government.

Minister of State for Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions Dr. Jitendra Singh today launched the Nation-wide Digital Life Certificate Campaign 3.0 in New Delhi. Speaking on the occasion, Dr Singh said that Digital Life Certificate, Jeevan Praman is the vision of Prime Minister Narendra Modi for Digital Empowerment of Pensioners.

This year, 1900 DLC Camps will be held in 800 cities and towns in with more than 1100 nodal officers. 1.8 lakh Gramin Dak Sewaks will provide services at the India Post Payment Bank Camps to be held in 785 cities and towns. The Pension Disbursing Banks will conduct DLC Camps in 157 cities and towns. Alternative non-digital methods will remain available for those who are unable to access the digital platform. Pensioners can still submit their life certificates through traditional means, such as visiting pension offices or using post offices. ANUPAM MISHRA, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.
