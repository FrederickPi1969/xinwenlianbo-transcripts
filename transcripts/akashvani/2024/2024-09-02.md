## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has assured Andhra Pradesh and Telangana chief ministers of all possible help from the Centre to deal with the crisis following heavy rains in their states. Officials said, Mr Modi spoke to the two chief ministers — N Chandrababu Naidu of Andhra Pradesh and A. Revanth Reddy of Telangana — over phone in the wake of heavy rains and floods. Heavy downpour continued to lash many parts of Telangana, including Hyderabad, yesterday. The situation in Khammam, Mahabubabad and Suryapet districts of Telangana is particularly dire, as over Hundred villages submerged under floodwaters and many others were marooned.

“Heavy rain continued to lash several parts of Telangana including Hyderabad. Kamareddy and Nizamabad recorded the highest rainfall of over 22 cms yesterday while the situation in Khammam is particularly dire. Hundreds of people are stranded in flood waters on hillocks, rooftops and on highways due to swollen rivulets and streams following heavy rains for the past two days. Nine National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) teams have been pressed into service to assist in the rescue operations. Meanwhile, the Telangana state government declared a holiday for all primary and secondary schools in Hyderabad and several other affected districts today, as a precautionary measure to ensure the safety of children. Lakshmi, Akashvani News, Hyderabad.”

Unprecedented rainfall in the past two days in Andhra Pradesh has halted life at several places, especially in Vijayawada and vicinity, while 17,000 rain-affected people were evacuated from across the state.

Twenty-six National Disaster Response Force teams are being deployed for flood relief and rescue operations in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh. While 12 teams are already deployed in the two states, 14 more are being dispatched. Out of the 14 teams, eight are being airlifted from various locations across the country.

President Droupadi Murmu will be on a three-day visit to Maharashtra starting today. On the first day of her visit, she will attend the golden jubilee celebration of Shri Warana Women Co-operative Group at Kolhapur’s Warananagar. On the second day, she will address the 21st convocation of Symbiosis International (Deemed University) at Pune. Along with that, she will also be present at the centenary year function of the Maharashtra Legislative Council in Mumbai. On the last day of her tour, she is scheduled to inaugurate the Buddha Vihar at Latur’s Udgir and will also meet the beneficiaries of the ‘Shasan Aplya Dari’ and ‘Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojna’.

Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) is set to start its membership drive from today. The drive will be launched with Prime Minister Narendra Modi getting the certificate for the renewal of his membership from BJP president Jagat Prakash Nadda. All existing members will need to renew their membership. According to BJP’s constitution, membership drives are conducted afresh every five to six years.

The drive will be held in various phases from today till 10th of November. The first phase of this membership drive will conclude on the 25th of September. Individuals can sign up through a missed call, or on the party’s website or other digital platforms.

Prominent Bengali actors and other personalities from the film industry took part in the overnight protests in Kolkata, demanding justice for a trainee doctor who was raped and murdered at RG Kar hospital last month. At a mega rally that kicked off in the city’s College Square, actors and renowned personalities, including Aparna Sen, Swastika Mukherjee, Sudipta Chakraborty, Chaiti Ghosal, Sohini Sarkar and others, marched along Central Avenue, demanding justice for the trainee doctor.

The participants, including the actors, held sit-ins and remained at the protest sites till early this morning. They demanded that a representative from the ruling Trinamool Congress or anyone from the administration meet them to address their demands for a swift investigation and arrest of those involved in the case.

Uttar Pradesh government will construct a Vedic-3D museum in Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s parliamentary constituency Varanasi at the main building of the Sampurnanand Sanskrit University. The chief minister Yogi Adityanath made the announcement during his maiden visit to the university in Kashi yesterday.

“The museum will showcase the evolution of Vedic literature, highlighting the 16 Sanskars, 64 arts, and 18 Vidya Sthans as well as contributions of Indian sages and serve as a platform to revive the tradition of ‘Shastrartha’.  The Chief Minister saw the rare manuscripts preserved in the historic Saraswati Bhawan, including the “Raas Panchadhyayi,” Shrimad Bhagwat Gita, and Durgasaptashati, all inscribed with gold letters and adorned with gold artistry on special cloth. Currently the conservation work is being carried out in the university by the National Manuscript Mission with the backing of Government of India. SUSHIL CHANDRA TIWARI, AKASHVANI NEWS, LUCKNOW.”

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has condoled the passing away of senior journalist and writer Umesh Upadhyay. The Goods and Services Tax (GST) collections stood at 1.75 lakh crore rupees in the month of August, reflecting a 10 percent increase compared to 1.59 lakh crore rupees in the same month, last year. Central government collected 30 thousand 862 crore rupees, while states collected  38 thousand 411 crore rupees, before adjusting for refunds. The collection of Integrated GST on imports and inter-state sales was reported at 93 thousand 621 crore rupees.

Moreover, the gross GST revenues from domestic transactions grew 9.2 percent to about 1.25 lakh crore rupees. Revenues from import of goods were up 12.1 percent to  49 thousand 976 crore rupees, compared to the previous year’s figures.

Continuing their positive investment for the third month in a row, foreign investors pumped over 25,000 crore rupees into the Indian capital markets in August. As per the depositories data, Foreign Portfolio Investors or FPIs, invested 7,320 crore rupees into equity markets and 17,960 crore rupees into the debt markets.

The Union Cabinet today approved seven major schemes to improve farmers’ lives and increase their income at a total outlay of over 14 thousand crore rupees.

Addressing media in New Delhi this afternoon, Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said, key projects include the Digital Agriculture Mission, Crop Science for Food and Nutritional Security, strengthening agriculture education, sustainable livestock health, horticulture development, Krishi Vigyan Kendras and Natural resource management. He said, the Digital Agriculture Mission is being developed on lines of the structure of Digital Public Infrastructure for agriculture.

Mr Vaishnaw said, a 309-kilometre long new rail line project has also been approved by the Cabinet to provide the shortest connectivity between two major commercial hubs – Mumbai and Indore. The project will be completed by the year 2028-29.

The Cabinet has also cleared a proposal to setup a semiconductor unit at Sanand in Gujarat. The proposed unit will be setup with an investment of 3,300 crore rupees.

“The capacity of this unit will be 60 Lakh chips per day. The chips produced in this unit will cater to a wide variety of applications which include segments such as industrial, automotive, electric vehicles, consumer electronics, telecom, mobile phones and others.The Centre had notified the Programme for Development of Semiconductors and Display Manufacturing Ecosystem in December 2021 with a total outlay of 76 thousand crore rupees. In June, last year, the Union Cabinet had approved the first proposal for setting up a semiconductor unit in Sanand, Gujarat. In February this year, three more semiconductor units were approved. Construction of all four semiconductor units is progressing at a rapid pace. The cumulative capacity of these units is about seven crore chips per day. ANUPAM MISHRA, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

Heavy rain has disrupted normal life in Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra and Gujarat.

In Telangana, at least 16 people have lost their lives in rain related incidents amid the current spell of heavy rainfall in the state. Talking to media in Hyderabad today, State Minister D Sridhar Babu said, the situation is grim in the worst affected districts like Khammam, Mahabubabad and parts of Suryapet.

Meanwhile, Chief Minister A. Revanth Reddy visited the Khammam and Suryapet districts and took stock of the situation. The Chief Minister announced ex-gratia of 10 thousand rupees to the families affected by the floods. He also urged Prime Minister Narendra Modi to declare the situation as a national calamity.

“Several villages remained waterlogged due flooding. The local authorities have stepped up measures to bring normalcy in the affected areas in Khammam district and are making all out efforts to restore the road linkages and repair the railway lines, which were washed away. They initiated works worth 650 crore rupees to repair the retaining wall of Munneru stream. Traffic on the Hyderabad – Vijayawada National Highway has been diverted through other routes. NDRF teams are carrying out the rescue operations in the rain hit areas. Meanwhile, the Met officials have issued an alert that moderate rain is likely in some districts for the next 24 hours. Lakshmi, Akashvani News, Hyderabad “

In Andhra Pradesh, all efforts are being made to restore normalcy in the flood-affected regions.

“36 teams of disaster response force, 152 motorboats and 280 active swimmers have been pressed into action to undertake rescue operations in the flood impacted areas in NTR and Guntur districts. Helicopters and Drones have been deployed to provide food and medicines to the inaccessible areas. Chief Minister N Chandrababu Naidu visited the severely impacted Singh Nagar area in Vijayawada in a motorboat this evening to oversee the relief operations. With the water inflow at Prakasam barrage on Krishna River in Vijayawada reaching alarmingly high level, people residing near the retaining wall, Krishna Lanka and Tadepalli are being evacuated. With breaches in Khazipet-Vijayawada and Guntur Vijayawada railway sections as many as 275 trains were cancelled and 149 trains diverted. Sri Sai for Akashvani News, Vijayawada.”

In Maharashtra, Parbhani, Nanded, Beed, Jalna and Hingoli districts have been the most affected due to heavy rains, leading to overflowing of rivers causing closure of roads and highways.

In Parbhani district, an Indian Army column comprising 60 soldiers and medical teams was mobilized today to assist the district administration in rescue and relief measures. As per a preliminary assessment, floods have affected people in at least 63 villages, damaging some houses and crops.

In Gujarat, the India Meteorological Department has forecast heavy rainfall over the next four days. A red alert for very heavy rainfall has been issued for Surat and Bharuch district tomorrow.

“After a short break, the south Gujarat region has once again recorded heavy rainfall today. Torrential rains led to the rise in the water level of Ambika Olan, Valmiki, Zakhari rivers of South Gujarat. Flood like situation occurred in Dolvan tehsil in Tapi district which recorded the highest 8 inches of rainfall today. More than 250 children and staff stranded in water in the ashram school of Dolvan were rescued by NDRF. Normal life was affected due to waterlogging and closure of roads and highways. Aparna Khunt, AKASHVANI NEWS, AhmedabaD.”
