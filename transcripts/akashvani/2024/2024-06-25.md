## Morning News

The oath taking of newly elected Members of Parliament will continue today. On the first day of the 18th Lok Sabha, yesterday several MPs, including Prime Minister Narendra Modi took oath. Prime Minister Narendra Modi, who is the leader of the Lok Sabha, was the first to take oath administered by Pro-tem Speaker Bhartruhari Mahtab. Then the panel of chairperson to assist Mr. Mahtab in running the proceedings of the Lok Sabha till the election of the Speaker took oath. BJP MPs Radha Mohan Singh and Faggan Singh Kulaste are the members of the Chairperson panel who took oath yesterday. Other members of Chairperson panel are K Suresh from Congress, TR Balu from DMK and Sudip Bandyopadhyay from TMC. However they skipped the oath taking proceedings as panel members.

Prominent among those who took oath as MP include Union Ministers Rajnath Singh, Amit Shah, Nitin Gadkari, Shivraj Singh Chouhan, Manohar Lal, Piyush Goyal, Jitanram Manjhi, Rajiv Ranjan, and Annapurna Devi. Newly elected MPs from Delhi Bansuri Swaraj, Manoj Tiwari and others also took oaths.

From Himachal Pradesh, former Union Minister Anurag Thakur and actress Kangna Ranaut were among the prominent faces who took oath as Lok Sabha MPs. BJP MPs Parshottam Rupala from Gujarat and Naveen Jindal from Haryana also took oath besides Deepender Singh Hooda of the Congress.

Union Minister JP Nadda has been appointed as Leader of the House in the Rajya Sabha. He succeeds Piyush Goyal, who is now a member of the Lok Sabha from Maharashtra’s North Mumbai. Mr Nadda was nominated by BJP to Rajya Sabha from Gujarat in February this year.

The Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) has taken over five cases from Bihar, Gujarat and Rajasthan to investigate the alleged irregularities in the NEET-UG examinations. According to officials, the CBI has re-registered one case each from Gujarat and Bihar, and three cases from Rajasthan. The CBI is also expected to take up another case from Latur in Maharashtra. The officials also said that while the FIR filed by Bihar police pertained to alleged paper leak, the other four cases were related to alleged malpractices such as impersonation and cheating involving the candidates, local officials and invigilators.

The special CBI team from New Delhi has also taken over the investigation of the case of alleged NEET UG examination paper leak scam from the Economic Offence Unit (EOU) of Bihar police. The EOU had registered the case on 5th May this year on the day of NEET UG entrance examination after getting leads about the alleged paper leak scam.

In Gujarat, a team of Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) began the investigation with local police in Godhra city in connection with the alleged irregularities in the NEET exam. The CBI team reached Godhra yesterday to investigate the case. Our correspondent reports that three days after the exam was conducted on May 5, an FIR was registered over irregularities in the exam at an exam centre in Godhra. So far, the Police have arrested five accused for their alleged role in helping at least 30 students solve their question papers in Godhra. Meanwhile, the sessions judge of the Panchmahal district court in Gujarat rejected the bail plea of the accused on Friday.

The NEET-UG results were declared on the 4th of this month where 67 candidates topped the examination after scoring the maximum possible 720 marks which sparked a huge outcry over the integrity of the exam.

Anti-Terrorism Squad (ATS) Nanded in Maharashtra has registered a case in Shivajinagar Police Station in Latur against 4 persons who were allegedly running the racket of directly and indirectly providing illegal assistance to the candidates of Medical Entrance Examination NEET-2024 examination. Among them, a teacher at Latur Zilla Parishad School, Jaleel Khan Pathan was arrested on Sunday. He has been remanded to police custody till the 2nd of next month after he was produced before the court yesterday. Interrogation of other two accused, Sanjay Jadhav and Eranna Kongulwar who are teachers in Zila Parishad School in Solapur, is being carried out. Police sources said that the Latur Police is also looking for the accused Gangadhar from Delhi and a team will be dispatched to arrest him.

The Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences Vice Chancellor Dr. M K Ramesh has said that the Central Government is carrying out impartial inquiry against NEET exam paper leakage incident. Speaking exclusively to Akashvani News in Bengaluru yesterday, he said, all the Stakeholders must take up the responsibility of ensuring that the examinations are conducted in a fair and transparent manner.

Union Minister of Coal and Mines, G. Kishan Reddy along with Minister of State for Coal and Mines, Satish Chandra Dubey launched the 4th tranche of auction of critical and strategic minerals blocks at a programme in New Delhi yesterday. The auction included 21 blocks of critical mineral. Out of these, 11 are fresh blocks, spread over six States, Arunachal Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh. Addressing the programme, Mr. Reddy informed that the first tranche of auction of Offshore Mineral Blocks will be launched within 100 days of the new government.

Health and Family Welfare Minister Jagat Prakash Nadda has launched the National STOP Diarrhoea Campaign 2024 at a function in New Delhi. He launched the campaign by distributing oral rehydration salts (ORS) and zinc tablets to children, in presence of Minister of States for Health Anupriya Patel and Patraprao Jadhav. The two month campaign will begin from 1st of July and continue till 31st of August. The goal behind the STOP Diarrhoea Campaign is to attain zero child deaths due to childhood diarrhoea.

Speaking on the occasion, the Health Minister said that various initiatives taken by the Centre have cumulatively helped reducing childhood mortality due to diarrhoea.

India will host the 64th International Sugar Organisation – ISO Council Meeting in New Delhi from today. Delegates from more than 30 countries and representatives of many international organisations will participate in the three day Meeting to deliberate on critical issues of sugar and bio-fuel sector.

“India is starting the series of events with an industrial tour for the international delegates to one grain-based distillery at Muzaffarnagar in Uttar Pradesh. This visit will showcase India’s adoption of latest technology in production of biofuels and other by-products. A workshop titled ‘Sugar and Biofuels – Emerging Vistas’ is also being organised today at Bharat Mandapam. Union Minister of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution, Pralhad Joshi, will inaugurate the workshop.

The ISO Council had nominated India as the Chair of the organisation for 2024 as India is the world’s largest consumer and second largest producer of sugar. ISO has about 85 countries as members covering almost 90 percent of sugar production in the world. Adarsh Kumar, Akashwani News, New Delhi.”

The Indian Meteorological Department (IMD) has predicted favourable conditions for further advance of Southwest Monsoon into some more parts of the country. It will cover parts of Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh and Uttarakhand during the next 2 to 3 days. The IMD added that monsoon is likely to advance into remaining parts of Chhattisgarh, West Bengal, Jharkhand and Bihar during next 3 to 4 days.

Union Jal Shakti Minister, CR Paatil has said that proper access to water is a strong step towards women empowerment. Addressing the Jal Shakti Abhiyan: Catch the Rain 2024 workshop in Delhi yesterday, Mr Paatil noted that Jal Shakti is Nari Shakti as women are the most affected from water scarcity due to their household responsibilities in rural India.

A group of armed men launched shooting attack in the cities of Makhachkala and Derbent on Sunday.

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has announced that his country is “committed” to the ceasefire deal proposed by US President Joe Biden in exchange for the release of hostages. Speaking at a special Knesset plenum session summoned by the opposition, he emphasized that their position remains unchanged.

In ICC Men’s T20 World Cup Cricket, India have advanced to the semi-finals after defeating Australia by 24 runs in their Super 8 clash at St. Lucia last evening. India concluded their campaign at the Under-17 Asian Wrestling Championships in Amman, Jordan on a resounding note. The young Indian contingent clinched a total of 11 medals, including four gold, two silver, and five bronze medals.

Women wrestlers grabbed all the four gold medals. Dipanshi in 46 kg, Muskan in 53 kg, Rajnita in 61 kg and Mansi Lather in 69 kg delivered stellar performances in their respective weight categories to clinch the yellow metal.

## Midday News

Senior BJP MP Om Birla, who was also the Speaker in the last Lok Sabha,  will be the NDA nominee for the post this time as well, whereas the Opposition INDIA bloc has fielded Congress MP K. Suresh for the post. Both the candidates today filed their nominations for the Speaker’s post for which the election is scheduled to be held tomorrow.

Earlier, the Government and the Opposition failed to reach to a consensus for the post. Talking to media outside Parliament, Union Minister Piyush Goyal said senior Union Ministers Rajnath Singh, Amit Shah and JP Nadda had a meeting with Congress leader KC Venugopal and DMK MP TR Baalu over the issue. However, they were insisting to announce the name of the deputy speaker before endorsing Mr. Birla’s candidature. Mr. Goyal said they were informed that the deputy Speaker issue will be dealt at the time of its election. He said the Opposition was putting conditions which was rejected by the Government.

Earlier, Congress MP Rahul Gandhi said that the Opposition is ready to extend support to NDA nominee for the speaker post, but the Deputy Speaker post must be given to the opposition candidate. Talking to reporters outside Parliament, Mr. Gandhi said, Union Minister Rajnath Singh had called the Congress president Mallikarjun Kharge seeking support for the Speakers post. He said Mr. Kharge had told him that the Opposition is ready to extend support to the NDA nominee but, as per the convention the Deputy Speaker post should be given to the opposition.

Our correspondent reports that the Lok Sabha Speaker has been elected without any contest since Independence and this would be probably for the first time there will be a contest for the post.

Union Minister and LJP (R) leader Chirag Pawan today questioned the opposition INDIA bloc’s approach over the Lok Sabha Speaker post. Talking  to media outside Parliament, he said that the way the opposition has come up with conditions to support the NDA nominee for the speakers post in lieu of the Deputy Speaker’s post is not right.

Meanwhile,the Opposition nominee for the Lok Sabha Speaker post, K Suresh said  there has been a convention that the Speaker will be of the ruling party and the Deputy Speaker will be given to the opposition. He added that the BJP led government denied the opposition the Deputy Speakers post in the last two terms of the Lok Sabha, saying that Congress is not the recognised opposition. He added that  now Congress is a recognised opposition and the Deputy Speakers post is the oppositions right, but  the government is not ready to give it.

## Evening News

The Speaker for the 18th Lok Sabha will be chosen tomorrow. Senior BJP MP Om Birla, who was also the Speaker in the last Lok Sabha, is the N.D.A. nominee for the post this time as well. On the other hand, the Opposition I.N.D.I.A. bloc has fielded Congress MP K Suresh for the post. Both the candidates today filed their nominations for the Speaker’s post for which the election is scheduled to be held tomorrow.

Despite hectic parley, the government and the Opposition failed to reach a consensus for the post. Parliamentary Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju said, the Lok Sabha speaker should be elected unanimously and unopposed. He appealed to the opposition bloc to reconsider their decision.

On his nomination for the post of Lok Sabha Speaker, Congress MP K Suresh said, there is a convention in Lok Sabha that the Speaker will be from the ruling party and the Deputy Speaker will be from the opposition.

“As per the Article 93 of the Constitution, the Speaker of the Lok Sabha is elected on simple majority. It means that a candidate who receives more than half the votes from the members present in the House will be elected as the Speaker. Although the ruling NDA has numbers in its favour, it is likely that Om Birla will get a second term this time as well. However, the resurgent opposition is trying to put up a fight by fielding their nominee. Om Birla, who was also the Speaker in the last Lok Sabha, is representing Kota Bundi Lok Sabha seat in Rajasthan for the third term. On the other hand, Congress MP and Opposition candidate K Suresh is currently representing Mavelikkara Lok Sabha seat of Kerala.”

Housing and Urban Affairs Ministry is rolling out Safai Apnao, Bimaari Bhagao initiative under Swachh Bharat Mission-Urban 2.0 from 1st July to 31st August. This initiative seeks to enhance the preparedness of Urban Local Bodies to handle the challenges posed by heavy rainfall and heightened health risks during the months of June to August. It aligns with the Health Ministry’s ‘STOP Diarrhoea Campaign’. The Safai Apnao, Bimaari Bhagao initiative calls for comprehensive interventions focusing on cleanliness, advocacy and inter-departmental convergence to be implemented by all Urban Local Bodies.

The Equestrian Federation of India (EFI) has selected Anush Agarwalla to represent the country in the dressage event at the upcoming Paris Olympics. EFI selected him over Shruti Vora based on a thorough assessment of their recent performances, where Anush demonstrated a superior average. It will be India’s first ever entry at the dressage event at the Olympics as largely riders in past editions competed in eventing categories only. Agarwalla has been consistent since the Qualification period began last year and achieved Minimum Eligibility Requirement four times while veteran Shruti Vora earned the required two MERs this month. Anush emerged winner with an average score of 67.695 per cent against Shruti’s 67.163 per cent.
