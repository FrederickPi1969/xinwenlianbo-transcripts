## Morning News

President Droupadi Murmu has said that education is a medium of social transformation and nation building. The Indian Space Research Organisation, ISRO will celebrate this Independence Day in space by launching an Earth Observation Satellite into orbit. The Small Satellite Launch Vehicle will be used to launch the satellite on August 15th at 9.17 am from Sriharikota.

“The Small Satellite Launch Vehicle developed by ISRO will have its third and final demonstration flight on August 15 from the spaceport in Sriharikota. SSLV D3 rocket will launch Earth Observation Satellite EoS 08 to Low Earth Orbit. EoS 08 carries advanced payloads useful for disaster monitoring, remote sensing, fire and flood detection and UV radiation measurement. The Small launch vehicle developed by ISRO can be produced at low cost, has low turnaround time, can accommodate multiple micro satellites and can be launched on demand in less time with minimal infrastructure requirements. It can carry a 500 kg satellite to a 500 kms orbit. Once the demonstration flight is successful, it will be transferred to the private sector for production. Sudhindra, Akashvani News, Bengaluru.”

## Midday News

The Waqf (Amendment) Bill, 2024 is being introduced in the Lok Sabha. Minority Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju has moved the Bill for introduction in the Lower House to further amend the Waqf Act, 1995. The bill seeks to address issues related to the powers and functioning of the State Waqf Boards as well as registration and survey of Waqf properties.

Opposing the introduction of the bill, Congress, TMC, Samajwadi Party, DMK, NCP (SP), Left and others termed it as against the constitution and freedom of religion. Congress MP K C Venugopal objected to introduction of the bill alleging that it is draconian and an attack on federal structure of the country.

Samajwadi Party leader Habibbullah also opposed the bill saying Waqf is a religious matter and this legislation is an interference in the religious practice. Sudip Bandhopadhya of TMC termed this legislature unconstitutional and against the federal structure. DMK MP Kanimozhi Karunanidhi also opposed the legislation. She alleged that the government is blatantly going against the constitution, federalism and minorities. NCP (Sharad Pawar) MP Supriya Sule demanded the withdrawal of the bill or sending it to the Standing Committee for further scrutiny.

On the other hand Senior JD(U) leader and Union Minister Rajiv Ranjan Singh alias Lalan Singh rejected the allegations that this bill is against a particular community. He said, Waqf board is an institution set up through the Act and this legislation has been brought to bring transparency in it. He underlined that Waqf board is not a place for worshipping and allegation of interfering in the matter of faith does not arise.

Labour and Employment Minister Mansukh Mandaviya has said that the government is running 15 schemes which are dedicated for employment generation. Replying to supplementaries during the Question Hour in Rajya Sabha, Mr Mandaviya said, the government is running several schemes for employment generation and as a result, the unemployment rate has decreased from six per cent to 3.2 per cent now. He said that one crore 21 lakh people got employment under the Pradhan Mantri Rojgar Protsahan Yojana, since the launch of the scheme in 2016.

The Government has provided tap water connections to over 77 per cent rural households across the country under the Jal Jeevan Mission. Replying to supplementaries during the question hour in Lok Sabha Union Jal Shakti Minister C R Patil said, Narendra Modi government has started this scheme in 2019 and 15 crore households have been provided tap water connection so far. Mr Patil said, according to a study, clean and safe drinking water provided to households have saved over eight lakh crore rupees by eliminating disease.
