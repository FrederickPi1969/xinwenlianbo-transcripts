## Midday News

Voting is underway for the sixth and penultimate phase of Lok Sabha elections. In this phase polling is being held in 58 Parliamentary Constituencies spread over six States and two Union Territories. These include 14 seats in Uttar Pradesh, all 10 seats in Haryana, 8 seats each in Bihar and West Bengal, all 7 seats in Delhi, six in Odisha, four in Jharkhand and one Parliamentary Constituency in Jammu and Kashmir. Voting began at 7 in the morning and will conclude at 6 PM. Over 39 per cent voters had already cast their vote by 1 PM.

Vice President Jagdeep Dhankhar has said that voting is both a responsibility and a power. Talking to reporters in New Delhi after casting his vote, the Vice President said that India is the world’s most vibrant, active and effective democracy.

Chief Election Commisisoner  Rajiv Kumar has appealed to the voters to participate in the largest festival of democracy. Talking to reporters after casting his vote in Delhi in the sixth phase of polling of Lok Sabha elections,, Mr Kumar said that  adequate arrangements  are in place in view of hot weather conditions.

Senior Congress leaders Sonia Gandhi, Rahul Gandhi and Priyanka Gandhi and Union Ministers Dr S Jaishankar and Hardeep Puri, Delhi Minister and AAP leader Aatishi also exercised their franchise at different polling booths in Delhi.

A total of 889 candidates are in the fray in this phase. The prominent candidates whose fate will be locked in EVMs include, senior BJP leaders Dharmendra Pradhan, Maneka Gandhi, Manohar Lal Khattar and Manoj Tiwari and Congress leaders Deepender Singh Hooda, Kumari Selja, Raj Babbar and Kanhaiya Kumar. Samajwadi Party leader Dharmendra Yadav and PDP chief Mehbooba Mufti are also in the fray.

Over 11 crore voters are eligible to vote in this phase across 1.14 lakh polling stations. Simultaneously, polling is being held for 42 seats of Odisha Assembly in the third phase. In addition, Assembly by- elections for Karnal seat in Haryana and Gainsari constituency in Uttar Pradesh are also taking place.

Campaigning continues for seventh and final phase of Lok Sabha elections. Top Political leaders are leaving no stone unturned to garner support in their party’s favour. Polling in this phase will be held for 57 Parliamentary Constituencies across seven states and a Union Territory on 1st of June. A total of 904 candidates are in the fray in this phase.

Prime Minister and senior BJP leader Narendra Modi today accused INDI-Alliance parties of being opportunistic and a coalition of dynastic parties. Addressing a public meeting at Bikram in Patliputra parliamentary constituency, Mr. Modi said, INDI-Alliance is communal, casteist and dynastic coalition. Mr. Modi said family-centric parties like Congress, Samajwadi Party, National Conference, RJD, NCP and AAP want to make the Prime Minister’s post a musical chair.

wife of Samajwadi Party chief, Dimple Yadav and Congress leader Priyanka Gandhi Vadra will hold a road show in Varanasi.

India Meteorological Department (IMD) today said that the “Deep Depression” over the East-central Bay of Bengal is likely to intensify into a cyclonic storm by today evening. The IMD in a statement said, cyclone Remal is likely to cross between the Bangladesh and West Bengal coasts tomorrow midnight and become a severe cyclonic storm.

In Papua New Guinea, over three hundred people are feared dead after a massive landslide levelled a remote village in northern part of the country. More than 1,100 houses were also buried in the landslide that hit Kaokalam village in Enga Province, about 600 km northwest of capital Port Moresby yesterday.

In Archery, India bagged a gold and a silver medal at World Cup (Stage two) at Yecheon in South Korea today. The Indian Compound Women’s Team comprising Jyothi Surekha Vennam, Parneet Kaur and Aditi Swami defeated Turkey’s Hazal Burun, Ayse Bera Suzer and Begum Yuva in the final to won the gold.

The Indian Compound Mixed team of Jyothi and Priyansh clinched the silver medal after losing to American pair of Olivia Dean and Sawyer Sullian in the Gold medal match.
