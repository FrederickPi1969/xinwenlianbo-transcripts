## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will launch, dedicate to the nation and lay the foundation stone for multiple developmental projects related to the road, railways and ports sector worth more than 29 thousand 400 crore rupees at Mumbai in Maharashtra this evening.

“At around 5:30 in the evening, Prime Minister Narendra Modi will reach the NESCO Exhibition Centre in Goregaon, where he will lay the foundation stone for multiple projects including Thane Borivali Tunnel Project, Tunnel work of Goregaon Mulund Link Road, Kalyan Yard Remodelling and Gati Shakti Multi Modal Cargo Terminal at Turbhe in Navi Mumbai. Health Minister JP Nadda yesterday reviewed the Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana, PMJAY and Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission, ABDM with senior officials of the National Health Authority in New Delhi. In a social media post, Mr Nadda said these schemes are successfully fulfilling their intended purposes.He added that around 34.7 crore Ayushman cards have been issued, and around 7.35 crore hospital admissions have been facilitated under these initiatives amounting to more than one lakh crore rupees. Mr Nadda also directed the officials to ensure that the benefits of these visionary schemes reach the most needy and vulnerable sections of society.

The Central Government has sanctioned several road projects for Tripura and Assam to strengthen rural connectivity and accelerate economic growth in the North-Eastern region. The Ministry of Rural Development has sanctioned 78 roads measuring 563 kilometers and 14 bridges under Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana with an estimated investment of over 378 crore rupees for Assam. The Ministry has also sanctioned 42 roads measuring 118 kilometers with an estimated investment of 114 crore rupees for Tripura. The Ministry said, these projects will improve access to essential services like healthcare, education, and markets. It will also enhance connectivity in rural areas, bridging the gap between remote villages and urban centers.

Counting of votes has begun for by-elections in 13 assembly seats spread over seven states. The counting began at 8 this morning. Voting was held on Wednesday for Raiganj, Ranaghat Dakshin, Bagda and Maniktala in West Bengal; Dehra, Hamirpur and Nalagarh in Himachal Pradesh and Badrinath and Manglaur in Uttarakhand; Rupauli in Bihar, Amarwara in Madhya Pradesh, Vikravandi in Tamil Nadu and Jalandhar West in Punjab.

Here are the details on counting of votes in three assembly constituencies of Himachal Pradesh.

“The election results will be declared today immediately after the completion of counting of votes. First of all the postal ballots are being counted. Counting of votes of Hamirpur constituency is being done at Government Senior Secondary School in Hamirpur and counting of votes of Nalagarh Assembly constituency is being done at Government Senior Secondary School, Nalagarh. Similarly, counting of votes of Dehra assembly constituency is being done in Government College, Dhaliyara. These election results will decide the fate of 13 candidates in three assembly constituencies in Himachal Pradesh.”

In Madhya Pradesh, Three-tier security arrangements have been made for counting of votes in Amarwara assembly constituency.

“In Madhya Pradesh, The counting is being conducted in two halls of Government PG College Bhawan, Chhindwara. 17 tables have been set up for counting the votes cast in EVMs and 4 tables for counting of postal ballots in 332 polling stations of Amarwara assembly seat. Counting will be completed in about 20 rounds. More than 78 percent of voters had cast their votes in Amarwara on July 10. A total of nine candidates are in the fray in Amarwara. However, the main contest is between Kamlesh Shah of BJP and Dhiren Shah Inwati Congress.”

IIT Delhi and Prasar Bharati will host the two-day Robot Competition ‘DD-Robocon’ India 2024 from today at the Thyagaraj Stadium in Delhi. More than 750 students from over 45 colleges, institutions, and universities in the country will be participating in the event. During the competition, robots will face off each other to execute complex tasks within precise windows of time. The winning team from DD-Robocon will represent India at the international Asia-Pacific Broadcasting Union Robocon 2024, which will take place in QuangNinh, Vietnam.

IIT-Delhi said in a release that DD-Robocon India is aimed at promoting technical skill development, innovation, and teamwork among engineering students by providing visibility and recognition in the field of robotics. IIT-Delhi Professor S K Saha said, the competition is an esteemed platform for showcasing and advancing the students’ engineering and robotics capabilities.

In the Union Territory of Jammu and Kashmir, one of the ancient temples in Anantnag district is being reopened after 34 years. It was closed in 1990 due to unfavourable conditions in the Kashmir Valley. However, with the active support of the local Muslim population in the area, the temple will reopen tomorrow.

“In addition to the installation of Goddess Uma Bhagwati,  the idol of Panchmukhi Hanuman Ji will also be installed at Brari Aanghan in the temple premises. Uma Bhagwati temple at Brari Aanghan is an ancient shrine where devotees used to come in large numbers from different parts of the J&K. The Goddess is the presiding deity of Kashmiri Pandits and every year a grand religious function used to be held over there spanning for days together. The devotees from length and breadth of Kashmir Valley and Jammu used to visit the shrine to pay obeisance to Goddess whose temple was situated in five springs which are in the shape of Omkar. They are named as Brahma Kund, Vishnu Kund, Rudhra Kund and Shiv Shakti Kund. With its restoration, this grand temple will now be opened to devotees tomorrow on the auspicious occasion of “Harei Ashtami” after a gap of 34 long years which is a dream come true for the Kashmiri Pandit Community.”

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today said that NDA government’s development model is to prioritise under-privileged sections of Society. While addressing the gathering after inaugurating, launching, and laying the foundation stone for various road and railway projects totaling over 29,400 crore rupees in Mumbai, the Prime Minister said his government is prioritizing those who were neglected for many decades. Mr Modi said the NDA government is working to empower the Poor, Farmer, Women, Youth and other marginalized sections of society.

Touching upon the investors’ mood in Mumbai, the Prime Minister said that both small and big investors have enthusiastically welcomed the third term of the government. He noted that a stable government will work with triple speed in its third term.

Prime Minister Modi also launched the Maharashtra Government’s Chief Minister Youth Internship Scheme, aimed at providing opportunities for the youth. He said, Maharashtra is writing a new script of development and we are committed to improve the quality of life of Mumbaikars.

The projects launched today include the Thane-Borivali tunnel, Goregaon-Mulund link road, remodeling of Kalyan Yard, and the Gati Shakti Multi Modal Cargo Terminal at Turbhe in Navi Mumbai. Prime Minister Narendra Modi today said that the media should support the government in promoting citizen-friendly initiatives. Citing the example of environment-conservation initiative ‘Ek Ped Maa Ke Naam’, the Prime Minister informed that the initiative has been praised at the global level, and said that the media could help  make it a mass movement. He underlined the role of newspapers and magazines in the next 25-year journey to Viksit Bharat and appreciated the Media stating that it makes people aware of their strength. He said this after inaugurating the Indian Newspaper Society Towers at the Bandra Kurla Complex in Mumbai. Maharashtra Governor Ramesh Bais, Chief Minister Eknath Shinde, Deputy Chief Minister Devendra Fadanvis and Ajit Pawar, along with INS President Rakesh Sharma, were present on the occasion.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah today reviewed the implementation of the Vibrant Villages Programme at a high-level meeting in New Delhi. During the meeting, Mr Shah said, the government is committed to the all-round development of the border villages. He stressed on the need to increase employment opportunities to local residents in these areas and increase connectivity with villages to prevent migration.

The Home Minister also said, Central Armed Police Forces and Army deployed around the border villages should encourage purchase of local agricultural and handicraft products through the cooperatives. He added that their healthcare facilities should also be made available to the residents of nearby villages. The meeting was attended by senior officers of the Ministry of Home Affairs and Border Management and Director General, ITBP.

The Chief Justice of India, DY Chandrachud has emphasized the need to teach law in regional languages. Addressing the convocation ceremony of Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia National Law University, RMLNLU, in Lucknow today, Justice Chandrachud said, if the principles of law cannot be explained in simple terms to the general public, then there is a flaw in the legal profession and legal education. The CJI said, in teaching law, regional languages should also be considered. He said, RMLNLU should start an LLB course in Hindi. He said, laws related to regional issues should also be taught in the universities.

Justice Chandrachud and Chief Minister of Uttar Pradesh Yogi Adityanath distributed degrees to the students. Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath said, the rule of law is the precondition for good governance. He added that India is now recognised for its rule of law. It plays a crucial role in changing perceptions of the country.

The Special Task Force of Haryana Police has arrested Rakesh alias Kala Khairampuria, a notorious criminal involved in serious and violent crimes. A police spokesperson said, Kala had been absconding after being released on parole in 2020. During this time, he committed crimes in India and abroad. In early 2023, he fled the country and committed crimes in countries like the UAE, Armenia and Thailand in association with local gangs. The spokesperson said, Kala was brought to India in coordination with various agencies abroad and with the cooperation of the Home Ministry, the STF arrested him from the Delhi airport

In Gujarat, four children have died during treatment of suspected Chandipura virus infection at Himmat Nagar Civil Hospital in Sabarkantha. Chief District Health Officer Raj Sutariya told Akashvani that  two of the deceased children were from Rajasthan and two from the neighbouring Arvalli district. He said, two other children are undergoing treatment at the same Hospital. The officer added that the blood samples of all these children have been sent to National Institute of Virology for confirmation and the reports are still awaited.

According to Hamas-run health ministry, more than 289 people were injured in the strike. On the other hand, the Israeli military said it is looking into the incident. Meanwhile, Israeli Army Radio said that its military had targeted Hamas’ military chief Mohammed Deif in the strike on Khan Younis. However, it was unclear whether the Hamas military chief was killed or not.
