## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will attend the second Asia-Pacific Ministerial Conference on Civil Aviation at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi today. The Prime Minister will proclaim the adoption of the “Delhi Declaration,” by all the member states. The Delhi Declaration is a visionary roadmap to elevate the region’s aviation sector to new heights.

Civil Aviation Minister Rammohan Naidu inaugurated the second Asia-Pacific Ministerial Conference on Civil Aviation in New Delhi yesterday. Mr Naidu was elected the Chairman of Asia-Pacific Ministerial Conference on Civil Aviation. He called for strategic investments in Asia-Pacific aviation infrastructure to accommodate 3.5 billion annual passengers by 2035. He emphasised that India aims to increase women’s participation in aviation workforce to 25 percent by 2025.

This conference and the adoption of the Delhi Declaration represent a significant step forward in advancing safety, security, and sustainability in the Asia Pacific civil aviation sector. It highlights the spirit of cooperation that exists among the countries of this region.India is the fastest growing aviation market in the world and is currently the third largest in the domestic segment. In the last decade, the number of aircraft in India has increased from 400 to more than 800 and airports have exponentially grown from 74 to 157. The government’s initiatives like UDAN (Ude Desh ka Aam Nagrik) have enhanced regional connectivity, ensuring that even remote areas are integrated into the aviation network. Suparna Saikia, Akashvani News, Delhi.

Union Cabinet has given its nod to giving health coverage to all senior citizens who are above 70 years irrespective of income under Ayushman Bharat PM Jan Arogya Yojana with an outlay of 3 thousand 437 crore rupees. Briefing the media after the Cabinet meeting last evening in New Delhi, Union Minister for Information and Broadcasting  Ashwini Vaishnaw said the senior citizens will get the facility of free treatment up to 5 lakh rupees. He highlighted that this will cover about 4.5 crore families in the country, and include about 6 crore senior citizens.

Union Cabinet  has given its approval to the proposal of Department of Rural Development for the implementation of the Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana -IV during Financial Year 2024-25 to 2028-29. The total outlay of the scheme will be 70 thousand 125 crore rupees. Addressing media in New Delhi, Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said that under this scheme, financial assistance will be given for the construction of 62 thousand 500 Kilometres of road for providing new connectivity to the eligible 25 thousand unconnected habitations. Mr Vaishnaw said that it will create 40 crore humandays employment. Under the scheme, the  road alignment planning will be undertaken through the PM Gati Shakti portal.

The Cabinet has also approved  the “PM-eBus Sewa-Payment Security Mechanism scheme” for procurement and operation of e-buses by Public Transport Authorities with an outlay of around  3 thousand 435 crore rupees. Addressing media in New Delhi, Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said that this scheme will support deployment of more than 38 thousand electric buses (e-Buses) from Financial Year  2024-25 to 2028-29.

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh will observe the ongoing Tarang Shakti exercise at Jodhpur Air Force Station. Mr Singh will also inaugurate the Defence Aviation Expo being organised at Jodhpur Air Force station. This edition of IDAX at Jodhpur, scheduled from 12 to 14 Sep 2024, will have a grand participation from industry and host a wide range of products, and technologies. IDAX aims to showcase indigenous skills and indomitable spirit of Indian Aviation Industries to a wide spectrum of decision-makers and end users from Global Air Forces participating in Tarang Shakti 2024. During this visit, the Defence Minister will also observe the exercise.

The Defence Minister, the three Service Chiefs of India and the Chiefs of foreign Air Forces will witness the air show to be held today. In this air show, Suryakiran, Sarang, indigenous fighter aircraft Tejas and Sukhoi-30 will showcase their validities. Exercise Tarang Shakti- is one of the largest multinational air exercise being conducted by the Indian Air Force. Air forces of seven major countries USA, Japan, Australia, Singapore, Sri Lanka, UAE and Greece are participating in this exercise. 16 countries are also participating in the exercise as observers. Air Force Chiefs of some of these countries may also join today’s event. Jitendra Dwivedi, Akashvani News, Jaipur

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh today asserted that the country’s defence sector is moving ahead rapidly with new resolve of self-reliant India. He called upon friendly nations to elevate partnership to a new height in view of the evolving global challenges

## Evening News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that the aviation sector has played an important role in making India the fastest-growing economy in the world. Through this sector, the government is working to connect people, culture, and prosperity. Addressing the Asia-Pacific Ministerial Conference on Civil Aviation in New Delhi today, Mr. Modi said that an increasing middle class, leading to higher demand, are driving forces behind the progress of this sector.  He added that India’s aviation sector is undergoing a remarkable transformation, enhancing ease of travel for citizens and boosting connectivity.

The Prime Minister proclaimed the adoption of the “Delhi Declaration,” by all the member states. The Delhi Declaration is a visionary roadmap to elevate the region’s aviation sector to new heights. Mr. Modi said that the Delhi Declaration will advance the resolve for regional connectivity, innovation, and sustainable growth in aviation.

Civil Aviation Minister Rammohan Naidu inaugurated the second Asia-Pacific Ministerial Conference on Civil Aviation in New Delhi yesterday. Mr Naidu was elected the Chairman of Asia-Pacific Ministerial Conference on Civil Aviation.

External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar has said, about 75 percent of the disengagement issues at the Line of Actual Control (LAC) with China have been resolved. Speaking at the Global Centre for Security Policy in Geneva today, Dr. Jaishankar emphasized that the Galwan Valley clash of June 2020 had a profound impact on India-China relations. He further pointed out that violence at the border cannot be isolated from the broader relationship between the two countries.

Dr. Jaishankar was in Geneva on the final leg of his three-nation tour, which also took him to Saudi Arabia and Germany.

The Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) today said that since Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s visit to Russia, 35 Indian nationals have been released from the Russian Army. MEA Spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal said that around 50 Indian nationals are still with the Russian Army, and New Delhi is working diligently to secure their release as soon as possible. This brings the total number of released Indians to 45, as 10 Indian nationals were discharged before the Prime Minister’s visit.

National Security Advisor Ajit Doval met with Russian President Vladimir Putin on the sidelines of the BRICS National Security Advisers’ meeting in St Petersburg, Russia. In a statement released on Telegram, the Russian Embassy in India said, during his meeting with Mr. Doval, President Putin praised the Special and Privileged Strategic Partnership between India and Russia and emphasised the crucial role of security issues in bilateral relations.

Russia’s President proposed to hold a bilateral meeting with PM Narendra Modi on October 22, on the sidelines of the BRICS Summit in Kazan. The meeting will summarise the outcomes of joint work on the implementation of the agreements reached during his visit to Moscow and to outline prospects for the near future.

Minister of State for Science and Technology, Dr. Jitendra Singh, today said that India is ushering in an exciting time for a biotechnology-based bio-revolution. Addressing the Global Bio-India 2024 at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi, Dr. Singh said that a bio-revolution could have the same impact for India that the IT revolution once had for the Western world.

The Global Bio-India 2024 is a major international congregation of biotechnology stakeholders showcasing India’s biotech growth and opportunities to the world.

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has issued a red alert for heavy rain in Uttarakhand and Uttar Pradesh for today and tomorrow. Talking to Akashwani News, senior IMD scientist R K Jenamani said that the monsoon depression over Northwest Madhya Pradesh is slowly moving North-ward and Northeast-ward which will result in extremely heavy rainfall around various parts of the country.

Our Dehradun correspondent reports,  heavy to extremely heavy rainfall is expected in Pauri, Dehradun, Bageshwar, Champawat, Nainital, Udham Singh Nagar, and Haridwar. The district administrations have declared a holiday today for all schools and Anganwadi centers up to Class 12.

Our Correspondent reports from Gorakhpur that red and orange alert has been issued for many parts of Uttar Pradesh for the next 24 hours.

Many cities in the state are witnessing torrential rain for the last 24 hours. In Jhansi, Aligarh, Farrukhabad, Agra and Mathura districts normal life has been disrupted as the streets are submerged in water.  As many as five deaths have been reported in Mathura Bulandshahar and Moradabad districts in rain related accidents.

In Rajasthan the normal life has been badly affected by heavy rain in the eastern parts of the state.

Rainfall in Rajasthan has broken the record of 49 years in this monsoon season. 664 mm rainfall has occurred in the state so far, which is 61 percent more than normal. Earlier, 665 mm rainfall was recorded in the year 1975. Life has been disrupted due to waterlogging in low-lying areas of many cities and towns in the eastern districts. Most of the dams are overflowing and rivers are in spate. Due to excessive rainfall, Kharif crops have suffered heavy losses in many districts. Many people have lost their lives in rain-related incidents in the state.

In Madhya Pradesh, seven members of a family died after the collapse of a wall due to continuous rain in Datia district today. District Collector Sandeep Makin said that the incident occurred when a 400-year-old wall of the Rajgarh Fort collapsed in the Khalkapura locality. Chief Minister Dr. Mohan Yadav expressed grief over the incident and announced that compensation of 4 lakh rupees to give to the families of the deceased.

CPI(M) General Secretary Sitaram Yechury passed away today after a prolonged illness. The 72-year-old veteran leader breathed his last at the All India Institute of Medical Sciences, New Delhi. He had been on respiratory support over the past few days. A statement from CPI(M) said that, on Saturday, the body of Mr. Yechury will be kept at the party headquarters, A.K. Gopalan Bhavan, Gole Market, New Delhi, for public viewing and paying homage between 11 AM and 3 PM.

Born in Chennai in 1952, Sitaram Yechury was the topmost CPI(M) leader. He was thrice elected as President of the JNU Students Union. Mr Yechury joined the CPI(M) in 1975 and was arrested during the emergency for his political activities. He was elected as the General Secretary of the CPI(M) in 2015. Mr Yechury was elected to the Rajya Sabha for two terms from 2005 to 2017 from West Bengal. He served as the leader of the CPI(M) group in the Upper House of Parliament. In both the period of the United Front government and later the UPA government, Sitaram Yechury was one of the key interlocutors for the CPI(M), which was supporting these coalitions.

President Droupadi Murmu, Vice President Jagdeep Dhankhar, Prime Minister Narendra Modi, Home Minister Amit Saha have expressed grief over the demise of CPI (M) General Secretary Sitaram Yechury. In a social media post, the President said that, first as a student leader and then in national politics and as a parliamentarian, Sitaram Yechury had a distinct and influential voice.

India’s equity benchmark indices registered record high closing levels in today’s trading session amid positive global cues. In Hockey, defending champions India defeated Korea 3-1 to register their fourth consecutive win at the Men’s Asian Champions Trophy tournament in Hulunbuir, China. Araijeet Singh Hundal and skipper Harmanpreet Singh were the goal-scorers for India against the Koreans. Korea’s lone goal came from Yang Jihun off a penalty corner. India had already secured a semifinal spot after defeating China and Japan before thrashing last year’s runner-up, Malaysia. India will next take on arch-rival Pakistan on Saturday in its final league match.
