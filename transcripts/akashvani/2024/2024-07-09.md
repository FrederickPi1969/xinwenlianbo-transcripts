## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will co chair the 22nd Annual Summit between India and Russia with Russian President Vladimir Putin in Moscow today. During the Annual Summit, Prime Minister Modi and President Putin will review the whole range of bilateral issues, including defence, trade linkages, investment ties, energy cooperation, education, culture, and people-to-people exchanges. They will also share perspectives on regional and global developments of mutual interest. Both leaders will also assess the status of bilateral engagements in groupings such as BRICS, Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO), G20, East Asia Summit, and the UN.

Mr Modi reached Moscow last evening on a three day official visit to Russia and Austria. On arrival, Prime Minister was received by First Deputy Prime Minister of Russia, Denis Manturov and accorded a ceremonial welcome. Russian artists performed Indian songs to welcome Prime Minister in Moscow.

President Putin also hosted a private dinner for the Prime Minister last evening.

Today, Prime Minister Modi will interact with the Indian community in Russia. He will also lay a wreath at the Tomb Of The Unknown Soldier in Kremlin and visit the Rosatom Pavilion at the exhibition venue in Moscow. These engagements will be followed by restricted level talks between the two leaders, which will then be followed by delegation level talks.

“India and Russia have a Special and Privileged Partnership since 2020, which has remained resilient in the wake of multiple geopolitical challenges. The annual summit between the two leaders is the highest mechanism to steer and drive the cooperation between India and Russia. India-Russia bilateral trade has seen a sharp increase in 2023-24. It has since touched close to 65 billion dollars, primarily due to strong energy cooperation between India and Russia. Trade however remains imbalanced with Indian exports of 4 billion dollars and Indian imports being close to 60 billion dollars. It is a matter of priority in India’s discussions with the Russian side. Investment ties between the two countries are also growing, including in the field of energy, banking, railways, and steel. Russia also remains an important partner for India’s energy security and defence. Suparna Saikia, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

In the second leg of his two nation tour, Prime Minister will be visiting Austria later today at the invitation of the Chancellor of Austria. This will be Prime Minister Modi’s first visit to Austria. He will hold restricted delegation-level talks with the Chancellor of Austria as well as a high-level business engagement.

Dr Radha Anjali, a Bharatanatyam dancer and president of Austro – Indian Association said that the PM Modi’s visit to Austria is significant for the relation between the two countries.

In Jammu and Kashmir, five army soldiers including a JCO were killed and five others injured in a terrorist attack in Kathua district of Jammu division yesterday. Akashvani Jammu Correspondent reports that the attack took place in the Badnota village in Billawar sub division of Kathua district. The injured soldiers have been shifted to Sub District Hospital Billawar for advanced treatment. The terrorists threw a grenade and then opened fire with automatic weapons on the army vehicles that were on routine patrol duty. The security forces retaliated, but the terrorists escaped from the spot. Elite soldiers group were air dropped in Mandli sector in the evening close to the attack site area. A massive Cordon & Search Operation has been launched in the area by the security forces after the injured soldiers were removed to the hospital for treatment. Further details are awaited.

The Supreme Court has asked the Centre and National Testing Agency (NTA) to file a report before it on the NEET UG paper leak case. The top court also directed the CBI to file a status report on its investigation into the case so far. The apex court has asked the NTA, Centre and the CBI to submit their reports before it by 5 PM tomorrow.

Telecom Regulatory Authority of India, TRAI has notified amendments to regulatory framework for broadcasting and cable services and releases. TRAI has removed the ceiling on network capacity fee, NCF of 130 rupees for 200 channels and 160 rupees for more than 200 channels. The fee will now be driven by market forces. In order to ensure transparency, the regulator said that all charges have to be mandatorily published by service providers and communicated to consumers, besides reporting to it. The primary objective of these amendments includes facilitating growth of the broadcasting sector.

BJP national president JP Nadda will visit Kerala today marking his first visit to the state following the Lok Sabha elections. He will be participating in an expanded executive meeting of the state BJP in Thiruvananthapuram and other key meetings. Party National Media Head Anil Baluni said, this visit marks Mr Nadda’s first visit to Kerala following the Lok Sabha elections. He said, in the recent Lok Sabha elections, BJP not only opened its seat tally in Kerala but also increased its vote share significantly. Mr. Nadda’s visit is considered highly significant from an organizational perspective eyeing the upcoming local body polls and assembly elections.

The Uttar Pradesh government has suspended six officials in the case of the Hathras stampede after the submission of the report by the Special Investigation Team (SIT).

In the SIT report filed today, it is stated that negligence by event organizers was the main reason behind the incident. Based on the SIT report, 6 persons including SDM, CO and Tehsildar of Hathras district have been suspended.

Aakashvani correspondent reports that the SIT has also held the local administration accountable. The two-member SIT states in its report that a conspiracy is not denied, but an in-depth investigation is needed. It said, the event organizers did not make adequate arrangements for the crowds gathered.

SIT visited the accident place on the 2nd 3rd and 5th July and questioned about 125 people to file it’s report.

Mr Goel said, the accused have been running the organ racket since 2019.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has expressed confidence that the India-Russia ties will see further strengthening in the coming years. During his meeting with Russian President Vladimir Putin today, he said, in about 10 years, they have met 17 times. The Prime Minister said, it reflects the depth of the relations.

Mr Modi said, for the last 40-50 years, India is facing terrorism. The Prime Minister condemned all kinds of terrorism.

The Prime Minister said, the World has faced multiple challenges in the last five years, first due to Covid-19 pandemic and after that because of various conflicts. Mr Modi hailed India Russia cooperation saying it has helped Indian Farmers and Indian Consumers.

The Prime Minister said the world should accept that when it was facing the fuel challenge, the India-Russia agreement regarding fuel played a big role in bringing stability to the international market.

On the occasion, Mr Putin thanked Prime Minister Modi for making efforts to find a solution to end the conflict in Ukraine. During their bilateral meeting, Mr Modi had reiterated New Delhi’s readiness to assist Moscow in any possible way to establish peace in the region.

India and Russia have agreed to set the trade target to 100 billion dollars by 2030. Briefing media in Moscow Foreign Secretary Vinay Mohan Kwatra said, during the meeting between the two leaders agenda of discussion was principally focused on economic cooperation.

The two countries signed nine documents for cooperation in different fields. It includes MoUs in the ares of Environment and Climate Change, Cartography, Polar Research, Investment,Trade and Pharma. New Delhi and Moscow have also inked MoU on coperation in the field of Broadcasting between Prasar Bharati and TV Novosti.

Mr Kwatra informed that the Prime Minister strongly raised the issue of early discharge of Indian nationals who have been misled into the service of the Russian army. He added that the Russian side promised early discharge of all Indian nationals from the service of the Russian army.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi was today honoured with Russia’s most prestigious civilian honour, the Order of St Andrew the Apostle. President of Russia Vladimir Putin presented it to Mr Modi. The award recognizes Prime Minister Modi’s distinguished contribution to developing a privileged strategic partnership between Russia and India and fostering friendly ties between the two countries. The award was established in 1698 by Tsar Peter the Great in honor of Saint Andrew, the first apostle and patron saint of Russia.

Earlier, Prime Minister Modi also visited the ‘All-Russian Exhibition Centre’ in Moscow today. He was accompanied by Russian President Vladimir Putin. Both leaders toured the Rosatom Pavilion at the Exhibition Centre.

Mr Modi witnessed a photo exhibition dedicated to India-Russia cooperation in the field of civil nuclear energy. He was also shown the “Atomic Symphony” – a permanent working model of the VVER-1000 reactor which is the heart of the Kudankulam Nuclear Power Plant in India.

Meanwhile, Prime Minister Narendra Modi has left from Moscow for the second leg of his visit to Vienna, Austria.
