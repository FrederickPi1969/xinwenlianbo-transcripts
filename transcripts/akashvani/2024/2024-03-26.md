## Evening News

Tomorrow is the last day for filing nominations for the first phase of Lok Sabha elections. While, in Bihar, the last date for filing nominations is Thursday.

The elections will be held in 102 Lok Sabha seats in 17 States and four Union Territories in this phase on the 19th of April. Tamil Nadu, Arunachal Pradesh, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Lakshadweep, and Puducherry are among those states going to polls in a single phase during the first phase.

Scrutiny of nominations will take place on the 28th of this month, and the last date for the withdrawal of candidature is the 30th of March. In Bihar, the scrutiny of nominations will be done on the 30th of this month and the last date for withdrawal of candidature is the 2nd of the next month. Voting for this phase will be held in Bihar on the 19th of April.

Notification for the second phase for 89 Lok Sabha seats in different States and Union Territories will be issued on Thursday.

Several prominent candidates across the country filed their nomination papers today for the first phase of Lok Sabha polls.

In Uttarakhand, BJP candidate Malarajya Laxmishah submitted her papers for the Tehri parliamentary constituency in Dehradun. Laxmishah led a vibrant roadshow in the presence of BJP leader and Chief Minister Pushkar Singh Dhami and other party workers ahead of filing her nomination.

Congress candidate Jot Singh Gunsola also filed his nomination for the Tehri seat.

In Pauri district, BJP’s Anil Baluni submitted his papers for the Garhwal seat.

BJP’s Trivendra Singh Rawat filed his nomination from the Haridwar seat.

In Arunachal Pradesh, Senior BJP leader Kiren Rijiju filed nomination from the Arunachal West Parliamentary Constituency.

Congress candidate from the constituency, former Chief Minister of the state Nabam Tuki also submitted his papers today.

In Assam, senior BJP leader Sarbananda Sonowal filed nomination from the Dibrugarh seat. Party leader and Lakhimpur MP Pradan Baruah also submitted papers seeking re-election from his seat. Congress leader Gaurav Gogoi filed his nomination from the Jorhat Lok Sabha constituency. Eight more candidates also submitted their nominations today.

In Nagaland, People’s Democratic Alliance (PDA) candidate Dr Chumben Murry filed his nomination for the lone Lok Sabha seat.

Indian National Congress candidate, S Supongmeren Jamir also filed his papers for the seat.

In Puducherry, eight candidates filed nominations from the lone Loksabha constituency.

In Jammu and Kashmir, two candidates filed their papers from Udhampur seat.

The BJP has declared three more candidates for the ensuing Lok Sabha polls. The party has fielded Kanhaiya Lal Meena from Dausa and Indu Devi Jatav from Karauli-Dholpur Lok Sabha seat in Rajasthan. Thounaojam Basanta Kumar Singh will be the BJP candidate from Inner Manipur Parliamentary constituency.

Earlier today, the party had released its second list of nine candidates for Assembly Elections in Sikkim. Pema Wangzal Rinizing is the party candidate from Gangtok and Aruna Munger will contest from the Namchi-Singhithang seat. Assembly Elections in Sikkim will be held in a single phase simultaneously with the first phase of Lok Sabha Elections.

The BJP also announced 14 candidates for Assembly By-Polls in Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh, Karnataka, and West Bengal.

The Central Election Committee of the BJP also announced candidates for the by-elections to 6 Assembly seats of Himachal Pradesh. All the 6 rebel MLAs who recently left the Congress and joined the BJP have been made candidates for the assembly by-elections.

Meanwhile, senior BJP leader and former minister Ramlal Markanda raised objection to Ravi Thakur being declared a BJP candidate from Lahaul Spiti and hinted at contesting the elections from the Congress.

Amid reports of a tussle in the Grand Alliance in Bihar over seat-sharing agreements for the Lok Sabha polls, Congress and RJD leaders are holding a meeting in New Delhi.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today spoke to BJP candidate from Basirhat in West Bengal and one of the Sandeshkhali victims over phone. He called her Shakti Swaroopa. Mr Modi asked her about campaign preparations for the Lok Sabha election. She shared the ordeals faced by women in Sandeshkhali and said she will work hard to improve the living conditions of people there.

The Bhartiya Janat Party today moved the Election Commission over alleged varied responses of the electoral officers on the same nature of the complaints in different states. The party cited action against BJP leader and Union Minister Shobha Karandlaje for her alleged objectionable remarks and said, no action was taken in similar kind of comments made by a Tamil Nadu Minister. A party delegation comprising senior party leader Ashwini Vaishnaw and  BJP leader Vinod Tawde met the Commission over the issue in New Delhi. Talking to the media after the meeting, Mr. Tawde urged the poll panel that such remarks need to be checked.

If you look at the constituencies, Malkajgiri Constituency with over 31 lakh electors is the largest constituency by the sie of electorate and with 14 lakh 23 thousand, Mahabubnagar is the smallest one. In view of the heatwave, the Election Commission has issued an advisory to Chief Electoral officers of all states and Union Territories. It said, the India Meteorological Department has indicated the probability of above normal temperatures in the forthcoming summer season, which may translate into stronger and longer spells of heatwave especially from March to June this year. The Commission has shared a copy of Do’s and Don’ts issued by the National Disaster Management Authority regarding minimization of Heat wave impact. We have a report:

“Election Commission has directed that every polling party should be supplied with Oral Rehydration Salts, ORS for their own use as well as for any voter needing ORS due to heatstroke. There should preferably be permanent arrangement of tap facility for drinking water. As an alternative, drinking water pots having sufficient capacity on the day of poll may be provided. There should be proper provision for furniture like tables, chairs and benches inside polling stations. There should also be separate toilets in sufficient numbers for male and female voters . Tented arrangement for shade with adequate size canopy should be made available as waiting area. It must be ensured that sufficient number of medical kits along with a village level health worker are available for emergency use of voters and polling staff. Suparna Saikia, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today spoke with Belgian Prime Minister Alexander De Croo and discussed ways to further strengthen bilateral ties in diverse sectors. These include trade, investment, clean technologies, semiconductors, pharmaceuticals, green hydrogen, IT, defence, ports, among other. In a telephonic conversation, Prime Minister Modi congratulated his Belgian counterpart on the successful hosting of the First Nuclear Energy Summit in Brussels. Both leaders reviewed the excellent relations between India and Belgium. The two leaders affirmed their commitment to further bolster the India – EU Strategic Partnership under the ongoing Belgian Presidency of the Council of the European Union.

External Affairs Minister Dr S Jaishankar today called on Philippines President Bongbong Marcos at Manila. Dr Jaishankar conveyed the greetings of President Droupadi Murmu and Prime Minister Narendra Modi to the Philippines President. In a social media post, the External Affairs Minister said Mr Marcos’ guidance for taking India-Philippines to new areas will strengthen collaboration between the two democracies.

Dr Jaishankar also visited Indian Coast Guard Ship Samudra Paheredar during its port call to Manila. He said his visit and the ship’s presence signifies the deepening of bilateral relations between the two nations.

Dr Jaishankar also held a meeting with Defense Secretary Gilbert Teodoro and discussed strengthening India-Philippines defense partnership, and the convergences in the Indo-Pacific.

The External Affairs Minister concluded his Manila visit by meeting National Security Adviser Eduardo Año.

The deadline for online submission of the application form for the CUET-UG – 2024 has been extended to 31st of this month. UGC Chairman M. Jagadesh Kumar said, the decision has been taken on the request received from candidates and other stakeholders. The CUET (UG) – 2024 is being offered in 380 cities including 26 cities outside India.

Five Chinese nationals and a Pakistani were killed in a suicide attack in Pakistan’s Khyber Pakhtunkhwa’s Shangla today. According to a police official, a suicide bomber rammed an explosive-laden vehicle into a convoy that was carrying Chinese engineers, who were going to Dasu camp from Islamabad.  The provincial police have reached the spot and commenced relief operations.

In the US a major portion of Francis Scott Key Bridge collapsed after being struck by a container ship in Baltimore today. The Baltimore city fire department officials described the collapse of the Bridge as a “mass casualty multi agency incident. The conditions were unsafe for rescuers due to freezing conditions and limited visibility. The rescue workers are searching for around 20 people and multiple vehicles are believed to have fallen into the river.
