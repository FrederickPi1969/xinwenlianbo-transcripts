## Midday News

Campaigning has intensified for the remaining phases of Lok Sabha Polls as the Prime Minister and Senior BJP leader Narendra Modi held two rallies Telangana.

Addressing a public meeting at Vemulawada in Karimnagar Lok Sabha Constituency this morning, Prime Minister and Senior BJP leader Narendra Modi attributed the credit for achievements of the NDA government during the past ten years to the people. He said the vote given to him made the country a defence exporter from a defence importer and removed article 370 from Kashmir.

Mr Modi launched a scathing attack on BRS and Congress in Telangana and emphasised BJP’s commitment to ‘nation first’ policy comparing it with Congress and BRS’ ‘family first’ approach. Mr Modi further said these political parties are like ‘by the family, for the family, of the family’ and corruption and appeasement link them.

In Uttar Pradesh, many star campaigners of different political parties are canvasing for their party candidates today. Congress General secretary Priyanka Gandhi today addressed her first political rally  in Raebareli for this election. Targeting BJP she said that the saffron party is misleading people and if her party comes to power then wages of Asha workers, Anganwadi workers and MGNREGA workers will be doubled.

Samajwadi Party Chief Akhilesh Yadav today came down heavily on BJP in a rally in Shahjahanpur and said that party does false promises. He will later address a political rally in favour of Party candidate in Farrukhabad also. Senior BJP leader and Union Home Minister Amit Shah also addressed the political rally in Lakhimpur khiri district in favour of the Party candidate and Union Minister of state for Home affairs Ajay Mishra Teni. He appealed to the people to vote for BJP as this election is not only for making Narendra Modi third time Prime Minister but also to continue the welfare schemes of Central Government.

In Haryana, three independent MLAs have  withdrawn their support from the BJP government and extended it to the opposition Congress party. The independent MLAs Randhir Golan from Pundri, Dharampal Gonder from Nilokheri, and Sombir Singh Sangwan from Dadri had sent a letter to Haryana Governor Bandaru Dattatreya  informing him about their decision. They announced their decision during a press conference in Rohtak yesterday evening. They said that they have withdrawn support in the  interest of the farmers.  The Media Secretary of Haryana CM Parveen Atrey said the withdrawal of support by the independent MLAs would not impact the government as it still enjoys the support of 47 MLAs in 90 members Assembly.

In our series of key constituencies for Lok Sabha polls, today we bring you a report on Guntur parliamentary seat in Andhra Pradesh.

The Guntur Lok Sabha constituency stands as a prominent electoral region with 18 lakh voters, having women voters on slightly higher side. Guntur Lok Sabha has been a center of politics since Independence. The giant personalities like Acharya NG Ranga and Kotha Raghuramaiah represented the Lok Sabha for a long time. The congress has won 12 times and TDP has won 4 times here from 1952 to 2019. Currently, contesting from NDA alliance is Dr. Pemmasani Chandrasekhar from Telugu Desam Party. Meanwhile, the main rival, YSR Congress Party candidate Kilari Venkata Rosaiah is aiming to secure the support of the electorate with the hope of welfare schemes delivered by the YSRCP government. The capital Amaravati aspect remains an important electoral issue in the constituency. This time there are 46,300 first-time voters. The Election Commission is leaving no stone unturned in ensuring a smooth and transparent election on May 13. Allu Murali, Akashvani News, Vijayawada

In our series of key constituencies for Lok Sabha polls, today we bring you another report on Pune parliamentary seat in Maharashtra.

Regarded as the cultural capital of Maharashtra Pune is a major and historical city after Mumbai in the state situated on the banks of twin rivers Mula and Mutha.  Pune’s ancient temples, monuments, museums and parks are worth a visit.

This time, facilities of independent polling stations have been made available from 35 large housing societies in Pune city to draw urban voters in large numbers.  Although a total of 35 candidates are in the fray in the constituency, it will be a three-way fight between BJP-Shiv Sena Mahayuti, Mahavikas Aghadi and Vanchit Bahujan Aghadi.  BJP’s Muralidhar Mohol is the Mahayuti candidate while Mahavikas Aghadi’s candidate is Ravindra Dhangekar of Congress.

There are six assembly constituencies in Pune Lok Sabha Constituency. They include  Vadgaon Sheri, Parvati, Kothrud, Kasba Peth, Shivajinagar, and Pune Cantonment. It has one MLA from NCP Ajit Pawar group, four from BJP and one from Congress. Considering this, BJP looks more dominant. So this time again the battle is not easy for the Congress. Manoj Kshirsagar, Akashvani News, Pune.

A voter turnout of 64.58 per cent was recorded in the third phase of Lok Sabha Election yesterday. In this phase, 93 Lok Sabha seats of ten States and one Union Territory went to polls.  Polling remained largely peaceful with voters from all walks of life participated enthusiastically despite the heat wave condition in several parts of the country.

According to the Election Commission, Assam recorded approximately 81.61 per cent of voting. While Bihar witnessed the voting percentage of 58.18 percent, 71.06 percent voting was recorded in Chhattisgarh, 69.87 percent in Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu. Goa recorded 75.20 percent voting, Gujarat 58.98 percent, Karnataka 70.41 percent, Madhya Pradesh 66.05 percent, Maharashtra 61.44 percent, Uttar Pradesh 57.34 percent and West Bengal 75.79 percent.

Aam Aadmi Party today said that Arvind Kejriwal’s arrest has only strengthened and not weakened them. Senior AAP leader Gopal Rai, speaking to the media in New Delhi, said that despite their leader being in jail, the party continues to campaign strongly for Lok Sabha elections. The party also unveiled its election strategy, including door-to-door campaigning and roadshows planned for the final ten days of campaigning. Rai claimed that the Delhi government’s policies have benefited all sections of society, including women, the elderly, businessmen, and the working class.

BJP today slammed Congress leader Sam Pitroda over his alleged objectionable comments on Indians. Briefing media in New Delhi, party Spokesperson Sudhanshu Trivedi said, Mr. Pitroda has insulted India’s culture and identity as well as the people of the country. He said as Lok Sabha election is moving ahead, the Congress is getting exposed . Mr Trivedi alleged, the whole ideology of Congress is about dividing people. Party leader and Union Minister Rajeev Chandrasekhar alleged that Mr Pitroda’s comment reveals the mindset of Congress.

Earlier, Mr Pitroda had compared Indians from different parts of the country to Chinese, Africans, Arabs and Whites.

Senior BJP leader and Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman today rejected Congress leader Rahul Gandhi’s allegations that Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs) are being dismantled and are in disarray. Countering the allegations levelled by Mr Gandhi, Ms Sitharaman in social media post claimed that PSUs are thriving under the Narendra Modi government. She also alleged that it was under the Congress-led UPA government that PSUs suffered and claimed that the present government’s focus on capital expenditure has also led to substantial growth in their stock performance.

The Central Bureau of Investigation, CBI has arrested four accused in a case related to trafficking of Indian nationals for combat roles in the Russian Army. The CBI said that it has busted a major human trafficking network running across the country targeting gullible youths on the pretext of lucrative jobs abroad. In the case, the CBI arrested the two accused who are residents of Kerala, and two other accused of Mumbai.

The agency said, these traffickers have been operating as an organized network to lure Indian nationals through social media platforms and their local agents for highly paid jobs in Russia.

Air India Express has cancelled more than 80 flights and scores of flights have been delayed as a section of cabin crew members have reported sick to protest against certain policies of the airline management. An Air India Express spokesperson said, the airline is engaging with the cabin crew members to understand the reasons for reporting sick and also apologised for the flight disruptions.

Several Air India Express flight services from the four international airports in Kerala have been disrupted since last night, as many cabin crew employees of the carrier have collectively taken sick leave.

Meanwhile, the Civil Aviation Ministry has called for a report from Air India Express regarding cancellation of flights and asked them to resolve issues promptly. Sources said, the ministry has also asked them to ensure facilities to passengers as per DGCA norms.

Kerala government has instructed all districts to remain vigilant following reported cases of West Nile fever in three districts of the state.

Health Minister Veena George confirmed that the fever, transmitted by Culex mosquitoes, has been identified in Thrissur, Malappuram, and Kozhikode districts.

The Minister directed all districts to take necessary measures for mosquito control and urged the public to seek immediate treatment if experiencing symptoms such as headache, vomiting, muscle aches, and itching.

She emphasized that symptomatic treatment and prevention are crucial in addressing the spread of the fever, as there is currently no medicine or vaccine available against the virus.

In Jammu and Kashmir, the Cordon and Search Operation (CASO), launched in Poonch forests after terrorists’ attack on an Indian Air Force (IAF) convoy at Shaistar on 4th of this month, continued on the fifth consecutive day today. Army and police carried out search operations in areas surrounded by dense woods including Sanai, Shashtar, Gursai Top and Sheen Dhara areas of Poonch.

India has once again called upon Canada to stop providing criminal and secessionist elements a safe haven and political space in the country. In response to media queries regarding a float in the Nagar Kirtan parade in Malton, Canada, External Affairs Ministry spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal said New Delhi has repeatedly raised strong concerns regarding violent imagery being used by extremist elements in Canada against India’s political leadership. He pointed out that last year, a float depicting the assassination of India’s former Prime Minister was used in a procession. He added that display of posters of Indian diplomats have also been put out across Canada threatening violence against them.

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has issued an orange alert for moderate rain and thunderstorm with lightning and gusty winds of up to 40-50 kilometres per hour over Gangetic West Bengal and Odisha today. The IMD said, heatwave prevailing over eastern and south peninsular regions of the country will abate from today. As the region is expected to witness a wet spell accompanied by thunderstorms for the next few days. A fresh spell of heatwave is also expected over Rajasthan and west Madhya Pradesh for the next two days.
