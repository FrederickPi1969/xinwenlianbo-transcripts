## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will visit his parliamentary constituency Varanasi today and launch projects worth around 6,100 crore rupees. He will reach Kashi at around 2 PM and will inaugurate the RJ Sankara Eye Hospital. Thereafter, at around 4:15 PM, he will inaugurate and lay the foundation stone of multiple development  projects at Sigra Stadium in Varanasi. Our correspondent has filed this report:

Prime Minister will inaugurate the RJ Sankara Eye Hospital. The hospital will offer comprehensive consultations and treatments for various eye conditions. Prime Minister will also address the gathering on the occasion. In line with his commitment to boost connectivity, Prime Minister will lay the foundation stone for expansion of airport runway and construction of a new terminal building and allied works of Lal Bahadur Shastri International Airport, Varanasi worth around Rs 2870 crore. He will also lay the foundation stone of New Civil Enclave at Agra, Darbhanga and Bagdogra airport. and will inaugurate new terminal buildings of Rewa Airport, Maa Mahamaya Airport, Ambikapur and Sarsawa Airport virtually. Prime Minister will inaugurate Phases 2 and 3 of redevelopment of Varanasi Sports Complex worth over Rs 210 crore under Khelo India scheme and the Smart City Mission. SUSHIL CHANDRA TIWARI, AKASHVANI NEWS, VARANASI.

The project at sigra Stadium which will be inaugurated by Mr. Modi today aims to create a state-of-the-art sports complex featuring a National Centre of Excellence, players’ hostels, sports science centre, practice fields for various sports, indoor shooting ranges and combat sports arenas among others. Talking to Akashwani News, Lalil Upadhyay, the star hockey player and member of India’s medal winning squad at the Paris olympics, said that the efforts of the state and central governments are going to boost sports in the Poorvanchal region.

The Prime Minister will inaugurate the newly built Rewa airport in Madhya Pradesh through video conferencing today. More from our correspondent:

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will virtually inaugurate Rewa airport. Rewa is the 6th airport of the state. Rewa airport has been built at a cost of Rs 450 crore. Built on 102 hectares of land, this airport is in accordance with the needs for up to 50 years. Its runway is 30 meters wide and 800 meters long. Aircraft like ATR-72 can be operated from this airport. With the start of flights at Rewa airport, more than 65 lakh people of the state will benefit. This airport will prove to be a game changer for the entire Vindhya region. This will also promote investment along with tourism in Rewa region. Sanjeev Sharma, Akashvani News, Bhopal.

Mr. Modi will virtually inaugurate Maa Mahamaya Airport in Ambikapur, Chhattisgarh today. More from our correspondent:

Maa Mahamaya Airport, located in the tribal-dominated Surguja region of Chhattisgarh, marks a significant milestone. Its commencement fulfills a long-standing demand of the local population. Enhanced transportation facilities in Surguja division will boost commercial activities, tourism and create job opportunities.  This airport turns the vision of air connectivity in the region into a reality.

Union Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan has said that the government will establish a committee to address the stark disparity between farm-gate prices and retail costs of horticulture produce. Addressing an agricultural conference in New Delhi, he said, while farmers sell vegetables at 5 rupees a kilo, consumers end up paying 50 rupees and this gulf needs to narrow.

Mr Chouhan also unveiled plans to launch the ‘Krishi Chaupal’ next month, an initiative aimed at bridging the lab-to-land gap in agricultural research.

To cater to the heavy rush of passengers travelling to northern and eastern states due to the ensuing festivals of Diwali and Chhat Puja, Central Railway has announced a total of the 570 special services from various places of Maharashtra including Mumbai, Pune and Nagpur.  Out of 570 festival special train services, 108 services are being operated by Central Railway for passengers traveling to Latur, Sawantwadi Road, Nagpur, Pune, Kolhapur, Nanded and other places in Maharashtra. These trains include AC Specials, trains with mixed combination of AC, Sleeper and General coaches and also Unreserved Specials. For North bound passengers, the Central Railway is running 378 trips to various places like Danapur, Gorakhpur, Chhapra, Banaras, Samastipur, Asansol, Agartala, Santragachi and other places enroute.We spoke to Central Railway chief public relation officer Swapnil Nila.

A detailed report by the United Nations Human Rights Committee has raised serious concerns over the spread of religious intolerance, forced kidnappings and conversion of Hindu and Christian minority girls, curbs on media and freedom of expression in Pakistan. The UN committee also raised questions over the Pakistan government’s inability to tackle the dangerously-increasing numbers of attacks and threats on Shia Muslims, Christians, Ahmadis, Hindus and Sikhs.

The first ever International Marathon was held at Srinagar in the Kashmir Valley today. More from our correspondent:

In a landmark and one of the first ever international events in the Kashmir Valley, Kashmir Marathon was flagged off by Chief Minister Omar Abdullah this morning at 6:15 a.m from Polo View Srinagar. The Marathon marks a significant moment for the region since it witnesses the participation of about 2000 athletes including 59 international athletes. Bollywood Actor Sunil Shetty is also participating in the Marathon.

The Athletes in the Marathon will run 42 Kms full Marathon and 21 Kms half Marathon. This is Sunil Koul for Akashvani News from Srinagar.

Today is the International Day of the Air Traffic Controller (ATC). First time the day was celebrated was on 20th October in 2012 and after that it is celebrated on the 20th of October every year, to salute the unsung heroes of the Aviation Industry, who ensure people’s air travel is safe and efficient. These officers juggle thousands of flights daily, coordinating everything from takeoff to landing. Their efforts keep the skies safe, making them vital to both public safety and the country’s economy.

Talking exclusively to Akashvani News, Joint General Manager of Air Traffic Management, Airports Authority of India, A. K. Srivastava highlighted that IGI Airport Delhi is among the top ten airports in the world. He added that the airport has four runways and manages smooth arrival and departure of nearly 1400 flights daily because of its efficient traffic controlling staff.

Union Minister for Education Dharmendra Pradhan will be on a seven-day visit to Singapore and Australia beginning today. The visit is expected to foster collaboration, participation, and synergy in critical areas of mutual interest in education. On the first leg of his two-day visit to Singapore, Mr Pradhan will address members of the Indian diaspora today. Mr Pradhan will meet the Prime Minister of Singapore Lawrence Wong and Deputy Prime Minister Gan Kim Yong and other senior leaders tomorrow.

Indian Ambassador to Russia Vinay Kumar met with over 80 industry leaders from India and Russia, exploring trade and investment potential and strategic partnerships.The BRICS Business-to-Business engagement is conducted through the BRICS Business Council and the BRICS Women Business Alliance.

The BRICS Business Forum concluded in Moscow was attended by the business leaders from Brazil, Russia, India, China, South Africa, Egypt, Iran, UAE, and Ethiopia.

The victory also marked a significant milestone for Olympian PR Sreejesh, who made a successful debut as coach of the Indian junior men’s team. India will next take on Great Britain this afternoon. The match will start at 1.35 PM, IST.

South Africa entered the final, after defeating Australia in the semifinal, ending Australia’s hopes of winning their fourth T20 World Cup in a row. While New Zealand won a thrilling semifinal against the West Indies. The match will start at 7.30 PM, IST.

In Men’s Cricket, New Zealand will resume their second innings against India this morning on the fifth day of  the opening Test in Bengaluru. The eye hospital has been established by Sankara Eye Hospital, one of India’s leading eye care service providers, in collaboration with leading investor Rakesh Jhunjhunwala family and Sankara Eye Foundation, USA. It will be inaugurated in the presence of Sri Sankara Vijayendra Saraswati, Jagadaguru of Kanchi Kamakoti Peetham Tamilnadu.This hospital in Varanasi is the 14th hospital of Shankar Netralaya in the country, capable of performing more than 30,000 surgeries on the poorest patients every year. This world-class eye care facility has been set up with an investment of Rs 110 crore, the hospital will have 9 operation theatres along with other necessary facilities. To ensure smooth operations and to provide eye care and treatment to the poorest patients, the hospital will operate on a cross-subsidisation model, whereby funds received from paying patients will be used to treat patients in need.

In the Kashmir Valley, an encounter broke out between security forces and terrorists after a suspicious movement was spotted by security forces along LoC in Uri area of north Kashmir’s Baramulla district.

According to a social media post of Srinagar based Chinar Corps, the joint anti-infiltration Operation in the area was launched by Indian Army and Jammu & Kashmir Police. When terrorists were challenged by security forces, they opened indiscriminate fire, which was effectively retaliated resulting in the encounter.

The operation was in progress till the reports last came in. Further details are awaited.

An explosion has been reported outside a Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF) school in Delhi’s Rohini this morning. Delhi police has reached the spot, where the school wall was found damaged with a foul smell. The glasses of a nearby shop and a car parked near the shop were found damaged. Police said that no one was injured in the incident.

Talking to reporters, Delhi Police PRO, Sanjay Tyagi said, the experts from the Forensic Department, Crime Team and the Special Cell have reached the spot and are investigating the matter. He said, the cause of the incident is being ascertained.

The National Investigation Agency, NIA team has also reached the spot where the blast was heard.

In Rajasthan, twelve people lost their lives in a road accident last night on National Highway in Dholpur. The incident occurred, when a sleeper bus and an auto-rickshaw collided. Most of the deceased were children and women. The bus driver and conductor are in critical condition at the district hospital.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has expressed grief over the loss of lives in the accident. In a social media post, Mr Modi said, under the supervision of the state government, the local administration is engaged in all possible help to the victims. He wished for the speedy recovery of all the injured.

The Prime Minister has announced an ex-gratia of two lakh rupees to the next of kin of each deceased from the Prime Minister’s National Relief Fund. The injured will be given 50 thousand rupees.

Rashtriya Janta Dal (RJD) has expressed displeasure over seat allocation among allies of I.N.D.I.A block. Senior Party leader Manoj Kumar Jha said, the party was not consulted before seat distribution.

On the other hand, former state Congress Chief Rajesh Thakur refuted allegations of RJD and said, all allies of I.N.D.I.A block were consulted before seat sharing.

In seat sharing formula of I.N.D.I.A block 70 seats were given to Jharkhand Mukti Morcha and Congress, while   11 seats were given to RJD and CPI-ML.

The 19th edition of the Delhi Half Marathon was held in the national capital today. Prime Minister Narendra Modi today laid the foundation stone and inaugurated multiple development projects worth over 6,700 crore rupees from his parliamentary constituency Varanasi. Speaking on the occasion, the Prime Minister emphasized that development projects pertaining to Education, Skill Development, Sports, Healthcare and Tourism among other sectors have been presented to Varanasi today which will not only improve quality of life of the people but also create employment opportunities for the youth.

Mr Modi highlighted that the government has initiated a huge campaign for infrastructural development in the country in the last 10 years with objectives of improving services for the people and generating jobs for the youth through investments.

Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath, who was also present at the event expressed gratitude to the Prime Minister for presenting this Diwali gift of development to the people.

The Prime Minister also inaugurated tourism development works of Buddhism related areas in Sarnath. He also launched multiple other initiatives like tourism development works at Banasur Temple and Gurudham Temple and beautification and redevelopment of parks.

Earlier in the day, Prime Minister Modi inaugurated the RJ Sankara Eye Hospital in Varanasi in the presence of Jagadaguru Sri SankaraVijayendra Saraswathi Swamigal of Kanchi Kamakoti Peetham, Tamil Nadu.

Speaking at the event, Prime Minister Modi announced that the government will add 75 thousand more medical seats in the next five years to further strengthen healthcare infrastructure in the country.

The Prime Minister listed out various initiatives of the central government in healthcare, providing cheap and affordable treatment to people. He cited the establishment of over 5,500 Ayushman Arogya temples in Poorvanchal in last ten years, which have made medicines more affordable, reducing average spending on drugs by 25 percent.

During the event, the Kanchi Shankaracharya, lauded Prime Minister Modi’s developmental efforts.

Union Health Minister Jagat Prakash Nadda has said the government is taking necessary steps to ensure high quality and affordable healthcare facilities to all. Addressing the Bihar and Jharkhand Medical Forum Conference in New Delhi today, Mr Nadda highlighted that government is committed to implementing a comprehensive health policy that provides holistic, inclusive, preventive, curative and rehabilitative care to all. The Minister said, the two states have taken significant leap in preventative healthcare in the past few years.

The BJP has released the first list of 99 candidates for the upcoming Maharashtra Assembly polls. Deputy Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis will contest from Nagpur South West assembly seat, while Maharashtra BJP chief Chandrashekhar Bawankule will be the party candidate from Kamthi. The names of the candidates were approved by the BJP Central Election Committee.

Elections to the 288 member state assembly will be held in a single phase on 20th of next month. Counting of votes will take place on 23rd of November.

For Jharkhand Assembly elections, 70 seats will be shared by Jharkhand Mukti Morcha and the Congress under the I.N.D.I.A. block seat sharing formula while 11 seats will be given to RJD and CPI-ML.

In Jharkhand, illegal materials and cash worth over three crore 15 lakh rupees have been seized from different parts of the state since the announcement of assembly elections. Chief Electoral Officer K. Ravi Kumar stated that authorities have been instructed to strictly enforce the Model Code of conduct. According to the guidelines of the Commission, individuals are allowed to carry up to 50,000 rupees during the election period.

In Bihar, the opposition Grand Alliance has announced its candidates for the upcoming by-election in four assembly seats. According to the seat sharing formula RJD will contest Imamganj, Belaganj and Ramgarh seat whereas CPI-ML will contest Tarari seat.

On the other hand, NDA has also declared its candidates for all four seats of the by-election. JD(U) to contest  from Belaganj and Hindustani Awam Morch from Imamganj seat. Yesterday BJP had declared its nominees for Tarari and Ramgarh assembly segments.

The Delhi Police has registered a FIR in connection with an explosion outside a Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF) school in Rohini area of the National Capital this morning. Police in a statement said, the case was filed at Prashant Vihar Police Station. Police confirmed that an investigation is underway.

On receiving a call about the explosion, a Delhi Police team reached the spot, where they found the school wall damaged. No one was injured in the incident. Teams from multiple agencies, including the National Investigation Agency, National Security Guard visited the site.

Meanwhile Delhi Lieutenant Governor Vinai Kumar Saxena has said that a multi-agency probe is underway. In a social media post, he said that he is  keeping a constant watch on the progress of investigation. Mr Saxena also urged people to remain calm.

Again this October, Odisha is bracing up to face another possible severe cyclonic storm ‘Dana’ . National Disaster Management Authority and Odisha State Disaster Management Authority teams have been alerted for the possible severe cyclonic storm. The IMD has predicted coastal districts of Odisha  to experience heavy rainfalls on Wednesday, while the entire state will experience very heavy rainfalls on Thursday and Friday.
