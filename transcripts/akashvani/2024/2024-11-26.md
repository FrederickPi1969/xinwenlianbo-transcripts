The Nation is observing Constitution Day today. The government has announced year-long celebrations beginning today to commemorate the 75th year of the adoption of the Constitution of India. The year-long celebrations across the country will continue till the 26th of November, 2025. On this day in 1949, the Constituent Assembly of India adopted the Constitution which came into effect on 26 January, 1950.
On the occasion of Constitution Day, a grand event will be organised at the Central Hall of Samvidhan Sadan in Delhi, where President Droupadi Murmu will address members of both Houses of Parliament. Vice-President Jagdeep Dhankhar, Prime Minister Narendra Modi, and Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla, will grace the occasion.
Our correspondent reports that the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment has announced on the 19th of November 2015 that the Government would celebrate 26 November every year as Constitution Day.

“The celebrations are being conducted under the campaign tag line Hamara Samvidhan, Hamara Swabhimaan. These celebrations aim to honor the contribution of the Constitution makers while reiterating the core values enshrined in it. On the occasion of Constitution Day, millions across India will read the Preamble together today from schools to offices, and from cities to villages. A dedicated website, constitution75.com, has also been created to enable citizens to engage with the Constitution’s legacy through interactive activities and resources. Citizens can upload videos of them reading Preamble on the campaign website and get the certificate of participation. With Anand

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will participate in the Constitution Day celebrations at the Supreme Court this evening. He will also address the gathering on the occasion. The programme is being organised by the Supreme Court of India. Mr Modi will also release the Annual Report of the Indian Judiciary 2023-24. Union Law Minister Arjun Ram Meghwal, Chief Justice of India Justice Sanjiv Khanna and others judges of the apex court will be present on the occasion.

The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) has given its approval for the PAN 2.0 Project of the Income Tax Department. The financial implications for the Project will be one thousand 435 crore rupees. Briefing reporters after the Union Cabinet meeting, Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said that the project will have robust cyber security measures. He said that the existing system will be completely upgraded.

Mr Vaishnaw also clarified that the existing Permanent Account Number will be the same.

The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) has approved investment of one thousand 939 crore rupees  for construction of the Heo Hydro Electric Project in Shi Yomi District of Arunachal Pradesh. The estimated completion period for the project is 50 months. Briefing the media after the Union Cabinet meeting, Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw also informed that the CCEA also gave its nod to the investment proposal for construction of the 186 MW Tato-I Hydro Electric Project in Shi Yomi District of Arunachal Pradesh with an outlay of 1750 crore rupees.

The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) has approved three projects of the Ministry of Railways with a total cost of around 7 thousand 927 crore rupees. These projects include the 160 kilometer long Manmad – Jalgaon fourth line, the 131 kilometer long  Bhusawal-Khandwa third and fourth lines and the 84 kilometer long Prayagraj – Manikpur third Line.

Prasar Bharati, RailTel, and PlayboxTV have collaborated to launch the Freedom Plan on RailWire Broadband. Chief Executive Officer of Prasar Bharati, Gaurav Dwivedi, and Chairman and Managing Director of RailTel, Sanjai Kumar, unveiled the plan at an event in New Delhi yesterday. Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Dwivedi said the WAVES OTT platform represents a transformative leap for Prasar Bharati as the nation embraces the digital revolution. He emphasized that the collaboration aims to redefine digital engagement, bridge the gap between traditional broadcasting and modern streaming, and empower people with seamless connectivity and premium entertainment. Mr. Dwivedi said, Prasar Bharati is mandated by law to reach citizens and added that the public broadcaster has kept pace with technology by launching an OTT platform.

The Supreme Court has directed the Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM) to consider relaxing the restrictions on physical classes for schools, colleges, and other educational institutions in Delhi-NCR. These restrictions were imposed last week due to severe air pollution. A bench of Justices Abhay S. Oka and Augustine George Masih said that a large number of students do not have air purifiers at home and, therefore, there may not be a significant difference between children staying at home and those attending schools. The apex court ordered that a decision be taken by the CAQM in this regard by today.

Union Rural Development Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan has inaugurated 227 Gender Resource Centres in 13 states under the Nayi Chetna 3.0 campaign. On the occasion, he asserted that the government is working towards the social, economic, political, and educational empowerment of women. Mr Chouhan urged everyone to raise their voices to eradicate gender-based violence in the country.

National Milk Day will be celebrated in New Delhi today to honour the 103rd birth anniversary of Dr. Verghese Kurien, fondly remembered as the Father of the White Revolution in India. The Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying said, the event will highlight the pivotal contributions of the dairy sector to India’s economy and nutritional security.
On this occasion, Union Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying Minister Rajiv Ranjan Singh will confer the prestigious National Gopal Ratna Awards. This mega event will see countrywide participation including farmers, milk federations and dairy co-operatives.
Gujarat, considered the cradle of India’s White Revolution, has witnessed significant growth in milk production over the years.

“The state contributes 7.49 per cent to India’s total milk production. During the last 22 years, milk production in the state has increased by 119.63 lakh metric tons, with an average increase of 10.23 percent which is more than the country’s average growth in milk production. Amul Federation, which was started with only 6 member unions and a turnover of Rs. 49 crore, currently has 18 member unions in Gujarat. Through these 18 member unions, Amul Federation collects more than 3 crore litres of milk from across the state every day and prepares various products from it which are sold across India and in about 50 different countries. President Droupadi Murmu has urged citizens to imbibe the Constitutional ideals into their behaviour, fulfill their fundamental duties and work towards achieving the national goal of a developed India by 2047. The President said this while addressing a grand event on the Constitution Day organised at the Central Hall of Samvidhan Sadan in New Delhi.

On the completion of 75 years of adoption of the Constitution of India, President Murmu described it as a living and progressive document through which the country is achieving the goals of social justice and inclusive development.

The President said the ideals of justice, liberty, equality and fraternity mentioned in the Preamble of the Indian Constitution give opportunities to citizens to prosper and take care of each other in the society.

In her address, the President recalled the contribution of 15 women members of the Constituent Assembly in framing the Constitution. She also stated the nation will celebrate its 75th anniversary of being a Republic on the 26th of January next year and such occasions give the countrymen the opportunity to make better plans for the future.

Speaking on the occasion, Vice-President Jagdeep Dhankhar said, this momentous day marks a historic milestone as the country celebrates 75 years since it adopted its Constitution, a remarkable achievement for the world’s largest and most dynamic democracy.

In his address, Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla  said the Indian Constitution is the result of years of penance, sacrifice, ingenuity and strength of people of the country. Mr Birla added that the Constitution has been a driving force for social and economic change in the country.

During the event, the President released a commemorative Coin and a Stamp dedicated to the 75th anniversary of adoption of the Constitution of India. Books titled “Making of the Constitution of India: A Glimpse” and “Making of the Constitution of India and its Glorious Journey” were also released. The Constitution in Sanskrit and Maithili languages were also released. A Booklet dedicated to the Art of Constitution of India was also unveiled during the event.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi, several Union Ministers, Leaders of Opposition in both Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha, Members of Parliament and other dignitaries attended the event. Our correspondent reports that the President led the ceremonial reading of the Preamble of Indian Constitution on this occasion. More in this report:

“Constitution Day was first celebrated in 2015, when NDA Government decided to mark the day as a tribute to Dr B R Ambedkar who played an important role in its framing as the Chairman of the Drafting Committee. The constituent assembly took two years, eleven months and seventeen days to accomplish the task of drafting the historic document. On 26th November 1949, the Constituent Assembly of India adopted the Constitution which came into effect on 26th January 1950. The Constitution declares India a sovereign, socialist, secular and democratic republic, assuring its citizens justice, equality and liberty and endeavours to promote fraternity. It is the longest written constitution of any sovereign country in the world. The Indian constitution has stood the test of the time as India has been able to foster a vibrant democracy at its heart. With Dipendra Kumar, Bhanupratab Singh, Akashvani News, Delhi.”
