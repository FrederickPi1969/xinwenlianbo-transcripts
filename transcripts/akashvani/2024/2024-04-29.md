## Midday News

As the voting day approaches, Leaders from various political parties are leaving no stone unturned to woo the voters. Prominent faces of BJP, Congress, Left, TMC, Samajwadi Party, Bahujan Samaj Party, Shiv Sena, NCP, JDU, RJD and others are engaged in hectic campaigning in different parts of the country.

Prime Minister and Senior BJP leader Narendra Modi has emphasised that he is working 24X7 with a vision to make India a developed nation by 2047. Addressing a public rally at Bagalkote in Karnataka  today, he said that BJP works for the development and Congress works for its vote bank.

Mr. Modi accused the Congress Government in Karnataka of doing appeasement politics. He said, the Congress government has changed the image of Bengaluru from IT hub to Tanker hub due to water woes. Mr. Modi called upon people to be careful about fake social media posts spreading rumours against the BJP.

Congress President Mallikarjuna Kharge will address public meetings in Raichur, Yadgir, Kalaburgi districts today. AICC general secretary Priyanka Gandhi will also address a rally in Sedam for the party’s Gulbarga candidate. We have more from our correspondent;

Senior Congress leader Rahul Gandhi criticized the BJP government for the surge in inflation and unemployment across the country. Speaking at the election rally in Patan in North Gujarat, today, he accused the NDA government of writing off substantial amounts of corporate loans and doing injustice to farmers, tribals and small labourers. He said 1 per cent of people in the country control 40 per cent of the wealth.

Mr. Gandhi said that once Congress comes to power, it will conduct a caste-based census to assess the representation of each community in the country and waive off the Farmers’ loans. On the other hand, Gujarat BJP President CR Patil is campaigning in favour of party candidates.

The Congress today released four more candidates for the Lok Sabha polls. Punjab Congress President Amrinder Singh Brar will contest from Ludhiana Lok Sabha seat while party leader Sukhjinder Singh Randhawa will fight from Gurdaspur. Former Punjab Minister Vijay Inder Singla will contest from Anandpur Sahib Lok Sabha seat.

Today is the day of high profile nominations in Uttar Pradesh. Senior BJP leaders and Union Ministers Rajnath Singh and  Smriti Irani filed their nominations today.

Rajnath Singh filed his nomination from Lucknow parliamentary constituency. He is contesting third time from this constituency. Senior BJP leader and Chief Minister of Uttarakhand Pushkar Singh Dhami and Chief Minister of Uttar Pradesh Yogi Adityanath accompanied him during nomination. Meanwhile, campaigning has also intensified in the state for the next phase of election. Senior BJP leader and Chief Minister of Madhya Pradesh Mohan Yadav will address a rally in Firozabad district.

Samajwadi Party Chief Akhilesh Yadav will address rallies in Etah, Hathras and Agra districts today. Bahujan Samaj party supremo Mayawati will hold a rally in Badayun district.

The Aam Aadmi Party’s transgender wing today staged a protest in the national capital against the arrest of Delhi Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal. The protest was led by AAP leader Dilip Pandey. Efforts to combat the forest fires in Uttarakhand are underway with NDRF personnel, along with the Fire and Forest Departments, making significant progress in controlling the blaze across Garhwal and Kumaon divisions.

Local communities are also lending their support to the firefighting efforts. In Nainital district’s Maheshkhan area alone, 300 fire watchers, including 30 NDRF personnel, have been deployed to control the fire incidents.

Chief Minister Pushkar Singh Dhami has assured that all possible measures are being taken to tackle the escalating incidents of forest fires. On the other hand, authorities have taken swift action by arresting eight individuals involved in the forest fire incidents, with legal actions initiated against them under relevant sections of the Indian Forest Act- IFA.

A Maoist has been killed in an encounter with security forces this morning in Bastar division of Chhattisgarh. The body of the slain Maoist has been recovered.

The encounter took place in Peselpad forest in Kistaram area of ​​Sukma district when a joint team of District Reserve Guard Bastar Fighters and CRPF’s Cobra battalion was conducting a search operation based on intelligence inputs about the presence of Maoists in the area. After the encounter, weapons and other Naxalite material have also been recovered from the spot.

Still with Chhattisgarh, eight people have died and 21 injured in a road accident in Bemetara district. According to police officials, about 30 people were returning to their village in a vehicle after attending a family function. Their vehicle collided with another vehicle near Kathia village. In the incident, eight people died on the spot. The dead include five women and three children. Four seriously injured people have been referred to AIIMS Raipur.

In the Kashmir Valley, the death toll in yesterday’s tragic Sonamarg road accident in Ganderbal district has risen to five. According to officials, another body was retrieved this morning from Nallah Sindh in Kullan area of Ganderbal following an accident near Gagangeer in Sonamarg.

The incident unfolded when a vehicle carrying passengers met with a fatal accident near Gagangeer, resulting in the loss of five lives.

Union Health Ministry held a review meeting with states and UTs to assess the present situation in the country amid reports regarding detection of avian influenza virus in cattle and milk in various States of USA.

The ministry of Mines today organized a summit to foster collaboration, sharing knowledge and driving innovation in the field of critical mineral beneficiation and processing in New Delhi. Addressing the inaugural session of the two-day summit, Secretary of Ministry of Mines VL Kantha Rao pointed out that India’s rapid economic growth and ambition for clean energy is the need of the hour.

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has forecast that heat wave to severe heat wave conditions will continue to prevail over parts of Gangetic West Bengal, Odisha, Bihar and Jharkhand for the next three days. Heat wave conditions are likely over isolated pockets of Rayalaseema, Interior Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Kerala and East Uttar Pradesh.

## Evening News

Intense campaigning continues for third phase of Lok Sabha Elections to be held in 95 constituencies in 10 States and 2 Union Territories on the 7th of next month. Key leaders of various political parties are holding multiple rallies covering several constituencies in various states in a single day.

Prime Minister and Senior BJP leader Narendra Modi today held public meetings at Bagalkote in Karnataka and Solapur and Satara in Maharashtra today. In his Solapur rally, the Prime Minister said, nobody in the country can change the constitution or end reservation in the country. He said, even if Baba Saheb himself come and ask to end reservations or to change the Constitution, it would not happen. Mr. Modi accused the opposition of spreading lies about changing the constitution and ending reservation. In Satara rally, Mr Modi said, NDA government used technologies such as social media to connect to the masses and to garner feedback for welfare various schemes of the Government and the opposition is circulating fake videos.

Senior Congress leader Rahul Gandhi today addressed public meetings in Patan, Gujarat and Bilaspur city of Chhattisgarh. In Bilaspur he said, this Lok Sabha election is not just an election but a fight between two ideologies.

Senior BJP Leader Amit shah held two rallies in Bihar’s Begusarai and Madhubani today. Congress President Mallikarjuna Kharge addressed a rally in Gurmitkal in Yadgir district. He spoke about the public distress due to price rise and unemployment in the country. He said that the Congress party is pro poor and will provide one lakh rupees to a woman in every household if the INDI alliance comes to power in the Centre. AICC General Secretary Priyanka Gandhi blamed the Central Government for price rise and unemployment in the country. She addressed a public rally in Sedam in Gulbarga Parliamentary constituency in Karnataka today.  In Uttar Pradesh, Samajwadi Party Chief Akhilesh Yadav addressed a political rally in Etah, Hathras and Agra today.

Bahujan Samaj Party Supremo Mayawati attacked BJP in a rally in Badayun district today. She said that BJP has politicised all the central agencies and they are not going to win this time.

In Bihar, despite sweltering heat, star campaigners are holding back-to-back election rallies. JD(U) President and Chief Minister Nitish Kumar today campaigned in Alamnagar in Madhepura constituency and Beldaur in Khagaria Lok Sabha constituency. Mr. Kumar enumerated development works undertaken during NDA rule in Bihar. JD(U) President said the Double Engine of the NDA government can change the future of the state.

LJP (Ramvilas) Chief, Chirag Paswan held a roadshow in Muzaffarpur. RJD Chief Lalu Prasad first time descended for campaigning in Chhapra where he addressed a public meeting. In a brief address to RJD supporters, Lalu said Saran Lok Sabha seat has been the land of his political genesis and he has done a lot of work for the development of the region.

Leaders of Grand Alliance like Congress State President Akhilesh Prasad Singh, Vikash Sheel Insan Party Chief Mukesh Sahni and leaders of Left parties also held road shows, door to door contact programmes and public meetings today.

The notification for the sixth phase of Lok Sabha elections has been issued today. Polling in this phase will be held on the 25th of next month in 57 Lok Sabha seats in six States and one Union Territory. All seven seats in Delhi, all ten seats in Haryana, fourteen seats in Uttar Pradesh, eight seats each in Bihar and West Bengal, six seats in Odisha, and four seats in Jharkhand, will go to polls in the sixth phase.

Today was also the last day for withdrawal of names for the fourth phase. A total of 96 Lok Sabha seats spread over nine States and a Union Territory will go to polls in this phase on the 13th of next month. Voting for all 25 Parliamentary seats of Andhra Pradesh, 17 seats of Telangana, 13 seats of Uttar Pradesh, 11 seats of Maharashtra, 8 seats each of Madhya Pradesh and West Bengal, 5 seats of  Bihar, 4 seats each of Jharkhand and Odisha and one seat of Jammu and Kashmir will be held on 13th May in this phase.

In Telangana, 525 candidates remained in the election fray after withdrawal of nominations was over by 3PM today.

In Manipur, re-polling in six polling stations of Outer Manipur Parliamentary Constituency will be held tomorrow. Five polling stations in Ukhrul district and one in Senapati will go to re-polling tomorrow from 7 AM to 4 PM. The Election Commission said, the voting process in these polling stations conducted on April 26 has been treated as void and directed for re-polling.

A BJP delegation today met the Election Commission of India (ECI) over the issues of deep fake videos and violence in West Bengal during election. Briefing the media after meeting the ECI, senior party leader and Union Minister Ashwani Vaishnaw said, the Congress party is misleading the people through posting deep fake videos on its social media platforms. He added, Trinamool Congress is continuing to practice violence in West Bengal, which is intolerable.

On the other hand, the Congress also approached the ECI today over the alleged misquoting of their Lok Sabha manifesto by the BJP. Talking to media after meeting the Election Commission, senior party leader Supriya Shrinate alleged that the BJP has been falsely attributing declarations that are not part of their manifesto. She said, the party has also objected on the use of religious symbols during political campaigns.

A two-member delegation of CPI(M) also met the Election Commission in New Delhi today. It highlighted various complaints lodged by the party against BJP leaders for inciting communal tensions, spreading falsehoods, and distorting facts to polarize the electorate.

In our series, covering profiles of key constituencies across States and Union Territories during the Lok Sabha elections, today we bring a report on Sambhal Lok Sabha constituency in Uttar Pradesh.

Sambhal Lok Sabha constituency includes five assembly constituencies: Sambhal, Chandausi, Asmoli, Kundarki and Bilari. There are 16 lakh 11 thousand 419 voters on this seat, will decide the political fate of 12 candidates in the upcoming polls on May 7. Sambhal Lok Sabha seat is also considered to be the stronghold of Samajwadi Party. Samajwadi Party founder late Mulayam Singh Yadav has been MP from this seat twice, and Ram Gopal Yadav once. This time, SP had fielded Shafiq ur Rehman Barq from Sambhal Lok Sabha seat, but he died due to illness, after which the party fielded his grandson Zia-ur-Rehman Barq, who is currently an MLA from Kundarki Assembly. While, BJP has once again fielded Parmeshwar Lal Saini, who was runner-up in the 2019 Lok Sabha elections. BSP has fielded Chaudhary Shaulat Ali as its candidate. Apart from this, four candidates from other parties and five independent candidates are also in the electoral fray. Om Awasthi, Akashwani News, Lucknow.

The UGC has taken this decision following feedback from the candidates. UGC added that the UGC-NET will be conducted in OMR mode across the nation on a single day.

In Kenya, more than 40 people have been killed in Mai Mahiu area after a dam burst today, pushing the death toll from devastating floods to more than 120. Kenyan authorities are struggling to cope with the chaotic conditions.

Heavy rains have been lashing East Africa in recent weeks as the El Nino weather pattern exacerbates seasonal rainfall.

Elsewhere in the region, nearly 100,000 people have been displaced in Burundi while at least 58 people have died in Tanzania and several thousand rendered homeless.

In Jammu and Kashmir, the 275 Km long Jammu-Srinagar National Highway has been closed for vehicular traffic today following multiple landslides triggered by overnight rains in many parts of the Union Territory. Our Correspondent reports, the strategic, and the only all-weather highway linking Kashmir with the rest of the country, was blocked at Mehar, Gangroo, Mom Passi and Kishtwari Pather in Ramban district. The rains are continuing, hampering restoration works. Commuters have been advised to avoid the highway.
