Prime Minister Narendra Modi  has highlighted the rapidly changing nature of jobs and the growing opportunities in creative sectors such as gaming, animation  and  filmmaking among others. In his Mann ki Baat programme on Akashvani, the Prime Minister underscored the immense potential of India’s creative talents and called on creators to participate in the 25 Challenges under the theme ‘Create in India’ which is  being organized by the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting.

Earlier, on 22nd of August this year, Union Minister for Information and Broadcasting Ashwini Vaishnaw had unveiled the Create in India Challenge – Season One at New Delhi. These challenges will serve as a precursor to the upcoming World Audio Visual and Entertainment Summit or (WAVES), aligning with Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s vision of “Design in India, Design for the World”. According to the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting, World Audio Visual and  Entertainment Summit will be  organized from 5th to 9th February next year to propel India’s Media and  Entertainment Industry and boost Creator Economy.

All arrangements have been made for the third and final phase of assembly elections in Jammu and Kashmir. Elaborate security arrangements have been put in place for a smooth, peaceful and incident free polling. Forty Assembly constituencies spread over 7 districts will go to polls in this phase tomorrow. Out of 40 seats, 16 are in Kashmir Division, while 24 are in Jammu region. Campaigning for third phase of assembly elections ended yesterday evening. In Bihar, swollen rivers including Kosi, Gandak, Bagmati, Mahananda and Kamal Balan are wreaking havoc of flood after breaching embankments at several places and inundating more than hundred villages. After the massive discharge of water from Kosi and Gandak barrages in river systems, several low-lying areas in Supaul, Saharsa, Araria, Kishanganj, Madhubani, West Champaran, East Champaran and Gopalganj are affected due to flood. More than four lakh people in 16 districts are reeling under the fury of flood. In Kosi region, rail services have been affected due to flood near Indo-Nepal border. More from our correspondent:

“Water level of Kosi, Gandak, Bagmati, Mahananda are rising rapidly due to continuous heavy rainfall in catchment areas in Nepal. Massive pressure being witnessed on different embankments along Gandak, Kosi and Bagmati rivers. More than 6 incidents of breach have been occurred in West Champaran Sitamarhi, Sheohar, Darbhanga and Supaul districts. Houses and farmlands have been submerged due to breach and spread of flood water. Twenty four different teams of NDRF and SDRF have been deployed for relief and rescue operations. The Katihar rail division has short terminated different trains due to flood and overtopping of water on rail tracks in Jogbani near Indo Nepal border.”

The Centre has assured all possible assistance to Bihar government to tackle the flood crisis. Union Minister of State for Home Affairs Nityanand Rai reviewed the flood situation at Patna yesterday. Mr Rai said, 11 teams of NDRF have been deployed in different parts of the state and eight teams are kept in reserve. He said, the Centre is in constant touch with Bihar authorities. The Union Minister said, if required, Air Force will help in airlifting of flood victims and air dropping relief materials.

Israel has launched a series of air raids on Houthi targets in Yemen, marking a new front in the ongoing Middle East conflict. Israeli military confirmed the strikes, stating they were in response to the recent missile attacks by the Iran-aligned Houthi militants against Israel over the past two days. More from our correspondent:

“The Israeli Air Force targeted military installations belonging to what they termed the “Houthi terrorist regime” in Yemen. Among the targets were power plants and a seaport, which Israel claims were used by the Houthis to transfer Iranian weapons to the region, as well as military supplies and oil. The strikes have resulted in at least four deaths and 29 injuries, according to a statement from the Houthi-run Health Ministry. Residents of the port city of Hodeidah reported widespread power outages following the bombing. This military action comes in the wake of increased Houthi aggression towards Israel, with the group repeatedly firing missiles and drones at Israeli targets since the outbreak of the Gaza war on October 7th. The Houthis claim that these attacks are in solidarity with Palestinians. In their most recent offensive, the Houthis stated they had launched a ballistic missile on Saturday towards Ben Gurion International Airport near Tel Aviv, which Israel reported intercepting. Another Houthi missile was intercepted by Israel on Friday.”

The Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) has arrested 26 key operatives of a highly organized cyber crime network involved in fraudulent activities targeting victims globally. CBI arrested these accused after conducting coordinated multi-city searches at 32 locations to dismantle a major technology enabled crime network as part of larger ongoing OPERATION CHAKRA-III. More from our correspondent:

The CBI has arrested 10 key operatives are from Pune, five from Hyderabad and 11 from Visakhapatnam. CBI has also seized over 950 items including electronic devices, mobile phones, laptops, financial information, communication records and incriminating material used by the cyber crime network for criminal activity and to defraud victims. The team also recovered over 58 lakh rupees in cash, three luxury vehicles and locker keys from the accused during the searches. With Rahisuddien Anupam Mishra for Akashvani News Delhi.
