## Morning News

In Chhattisgarh, 29 Maoists have been killed in an encounter with security forces in Kanker district of Bastar division today. Three jawans of Border Security Force-BSF, including an inspector, have been injured in this gun battle.

The joint team of Border Security Force and District Reserve Guard was on a search operation on the basis of intelligence inputs about the presence of Maoists in this area. The encounter between security forces and Maoists took place this afternoon in the forest of Hapatola under Chhotabethia police station area of Kanker district in Bastar division. The firing lasted for about two and a half hours. A large number of Maoists have been killed in this encounter. So far, bodies of 29 Maoists have been recovered from the spot.
