## Morning News

The counting of votes for the assembly elections in Jammu and Kashmir and Haryana concluded last night.

The Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) secured a majority in the 90-member Legislative Assembly inHaryana, winning 48 seats. With the win, the party has got historic third consecutive term. On the other hand the Indian National Congress captured 37 seats. The Indian National Lok Dal (INLD) won 2 seats, and among independent candidates, 3 emerged victorious.

In Jammu and Kashmir, the National Conference-Congress alliance is set to form the next government, surpassing the halfway mark with 48 seats in the 90-member Assembly. The National Conference secured 42 seats, while its alliance partner,the Indian National Congress, won 6 seats. The BJP won 29 seats.

The Jammu &Kashmir Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) secured 3 seats, while the Jammu & Kashmir People’s Conference, Communist Party of India (Marxist), and the AamAadmi Party each won 1 seat. Independents claimed 7 seats.

The prominent winners in the Union Territory, former Chief Minister and National Conference leader Omar Abdullah won from both the Budgam and Ganderbal constituencies. In a significant setback, PDP leader Iltija Mufti, daughter of party chief Mehbooba Mufti, was defeated in Bijbehara. BJP state president Ravinder Raina also lost in Nowshera.

As the National Conference and Congress alliance has got majority in Jammu and Kashmir, political activities for formation of government will increase in the Union Territory in the coming days. National Conference Vice President Omar Abdullah has said that in the coming days meeting of National conference and Congress Alliance’s newly elected MLAs will be held to elect the leader of legislature party. However, his father and National Conference President Farooq Abdullah has already declared that Omar Abdullah would be the Chief Minister. People in the Jammu & Kashmir are eager to see the formation of new government as elections in Union Territory were held after the gap of ten years, and there was good participation of people across J&K in this assembly polls. With Sushil Chandra Tiwari, Diwaker, Akashvani News, Srinagar.

The World Health Organization, WHO has declared that this year India has eliminated trachoma as a public health problem. With this, India has become the third country in the Southeast Asia Region to achieve this milestone. The WHO officially certified India during its Regional Committee meeting in New Delhi yesterday.

Trachoma is a contagious bacterial infection that affects the eyes and spreads through the eyes, eyelids, nose, or throat secretions of infected people.

Scientists David Baker, John Jumper, and Demis Hassabis have won the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for breakthroughs in predicting and designing the structure of proteins. The Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences today announced half of the prize to David Baker for computational protein design and the other half jointly to John Jumper and Demis Hassabis for protein structure prediction. The Nobel Committee for Chemistry, in a statement, said David Baker built entirely new kinds of proteins, and John Jumper and Demis Hassabis developed an artificial intelligence (AI) model to solve a 50-year-old problem: predicting proteins’ complex structures.Mr. Baker works at the University of Washington in Seattle, United States, while Mr. Hassabis and Mr. Jumper work at Google DeepMind, the tech giant’s AI lab, in London.
