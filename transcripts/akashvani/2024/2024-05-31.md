## Morning News

The polling stage for Lok Sabha elections is reaching its completion as the seventh and final phase of the elections begins tomorrow in 57 Lok Sabha constituencies across eight states and union territories. In this phase, 13 Lok Sabha seats in Uttar Pradesh, 13 seats in Punjab, nine seats in West Bengal, eight seats in Bihar, six seats in Odisha, four seats in Himachal Pradesh, three seats in Jharkhand, and one Parliamentary Constituency in Chandigarh will go to polls. The voting will begin at 7 AM.

Political fate of prominent leaders including senior BJP leaders Narendra Modi and Anurag Singh Thakur and Congress leader Vikramaditya Singh as well as TMC leader Abhishek Banerjee will be sealed in the EVMs.

Elaborate arrangements have been made by the Election Commission to ensure smooth and transparent voting in these areas. Basic facilities ranging from shade, drinking water, ramps, toilets, wheelchairs to electricity will be in place to ensure that every voter, including elderly and persons with disabilities, can cast their vote with ease. State Chief Electoral Officers, District Election Officers and State machineries have been advised to take adequate measures to manage hot weather conditions. Security has also been beefed up in the polling areas to avoid any untoward incident and ensure free, and fair polls. Talking to Akashvani News, Himachal CEO Manish Garg said

In Punjab, all arrangements have been made for Loksabha election to be held tomorrow. People will get a chance to choose their candidate in a multi cornered contest and a total of 328 candidates, including 169 independents, are in the fray, which is the highest number so far.

After a long time, there is no political alliance this time in elections. In such a situation, multi cornered contests are taking place in all 13 Lok Sabhconstituencies. If we talk about voting percentage, The highest turnout of about 71 percent was recorded in the 1967 Lok Sabh election, This time the Election Commission has set a target of crossing 70 percent and to achieve this, several campaigns have been run for voter awareness under sveep activities for the last two and a half months. Election officials are hopeful that the voting percentage will increase tomorrow. Shishu Sharma Shantal, Akashvani News, Jalandhar

In Jammu and Kashmir, the election authorities have put in place adequate arrangements for counting of votes for the 4-Udhampur-Kathua Parliamentary Constituency. Akashvani Jammu Correspondent reports that there are 12 candidates in the fray for the Udhampur Parliamentary Constituency. The counting of votes will be held at Government Degree College Kathua, secured with multi-tier security and round-the-clock CCTV surveillance to ensure a free and fair election process.

In Jharkhand  all preparations have been completed for the final phase of polling   in three constituencies Godda, Dumka and Rajmahal tomorrow .Polling will be from 7 am to 5 pm. Over 53 lakh 24 thousand electorate will decide the political fortune of 52 candidates.Chief Electoral Officer K Ravi Kumar said  polling parties are being dispatched to their respective booths. For most sensitive areas polling parties have been instructed to reach before sunset.Over fifty thousand jawans have been deployed in respective areas to ensure trouble free elections . Jawans of security forces held a flag march in sensitive areas to instill confidence among the electorate. The Chief Electoral officer said a number of measures have been taken at each booth in view of intense heat.

Election Commission has refuted the rumours being spread ahead of the final phase of voting for the 2024 Lok Sabha elections. Talking to Akashvani, Chief Election Commissioner Rajiv Kumar said that the rumour regarding the increase in percentage of voter turnout was spread across the country. Dismissing those claims, Mr Kumar said no one can increase voter turnout in the country as the candidates and agents were provided with the copy of form 17C which has records of  voter turnout. On the rumour regarding vote tampering, Mr Kumar said, Election Commission has updated all the information about voter turnout data and EVMs on its official website.

Senior BJP Leader and Prime Minister Narendra Modi began his meditation at the Vivekananda memorial at Kanyakumari last evening. Before beginning the meditation he paid obeisance at the Bhagavathi Amman Temple at the shore town. He was given a purna kumbh honour at the temple. He later reached the Vivekananda memorial situated at the Bay of Bengal by M.L.Vivekananda ship. Mr.Modi will meditate till 1st of June.

In Karnataka, Hassan JD(S) Member of Parliament facing sex abuse charges Prajwal Revanna was arrested in Bengaluru International airport last night after his arrival from Munich. The Special Investigation Team took Prajwal Revanna in custody and will be producing him before the court and interrogate him about the sexual abuse charges against him. The Special Investigation Team was formed after sleaze video clips of the sexual acts went viral involving Prajwal Revanna and after the accused had left for overseas on April 26th. Prajwal Revanna had filed anticipatory bail pleas at the Principal city and Sessions Court for Elected Representatives and is posted for hearing today.

In Chhattisgarh, security forces have arrested 16 Maoists from different places of Bijapur district.

Of these, 11 Maoists have been arrested from Gorna Padiyarpara area under Bijapur police station area, while, five Maoists have been arrested from Bhusapur area under Usur police station area.

Police claimed that the arrested Maoists were involved in cases of IED blast and digging up of roads to prevent movement of security forces in the area.

In Manipur, the flash flood continues to wreak havoc. Imphal City has been badly affected by the flood. Most of the roads leading to Imphal town have been submerged. The Rajbhavan and surrounding areas have also been submerged in the water. The Khwairamband Ima Market and other markets look deserted as water inundated the area.

The Manipur Police, Army, Assam Rifles, NDRF and SDRF have been pressed into rescue operations. The Loktak Development Authority also joined in the rescue works and around 800 persons have been shifted to safer areas. In Senapati district, the dead body of a woman who was drowned in the river has been recovered. Three temporary camps have already been opened in the district for the flood affected families.

The State Government announced a general holiday for all Government offices including PSUs, autonomous bodies, banks and financial institutions for today and tomorrow considering the situation in the State.

India Meteorological Department (IMD) has forecast that severe heat wave conditions over Northwest and Central India will start to reduce gradually from today. These regions include parts of Punjab, Haryana, Chandigarh, Delhi, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Jharkhand and Odisha. A gradual fall in maximum temperatures by 2 to 4 degrees Celsius is expected over Northwest and Central India during the next 4 days. Though, Madhya Pradesh, Vidarbha and Uttarakhand will continue to face heat wave conditions today.

Meanwhile, the IMD has also forecast that Southwest Monsoon has advanced over Kerala and most parts of Northeast India. The weather agency said that widespread to moderate rainfall, thunderstorm, lightning and gusty winds will descend over Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Meghalaya, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, Tripura, and Sikkim during next 6 days.

In Jammu and Kashmir, the death toll following yesterday’s accident has mounted to  twenty two. The mishap occurred  when an over- loaded bus, carrying pilgrims hailing from Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan to Shiv Khori in Reasi district, rolled down into a deep gorge in Akhnoor area of Jammu district.  69 others were injured following the accident. Immediately after the accident, residents of the area, Akhnoor, Police and Army from the nearby Tanda Unit rushed to the spot and launched rescue operation. All the injured were shifted to Sub District Hospital Akhnoor from where the critically injured were referred to GMC Hospital, Jammu. In the meantime ,District Magistrate  Jammu,  has ordered a Magisterial inquiry to investigate the cause of accident.

Sri Lanka has urged Russia to immediately stop sending Lankan ex-military personnel, now serving as mercenaries to  the military operations against Ukraine.

Sri Lankan State Minister of Foreign Affairs Tharaka Balasuriya said  that the government has made an official request in this regard till a decision is made after a meeting between Russian diplomats and Lanka’s Defence Ministry. He said that 455 complaints have been received so far with regard to Sri Lankans who have been fighting for Russia. So far, 16 Sri Lankans have gone missing while 37 were reported injured, he added.

Foreign Ministers of the NATO member states will hold a two-day informal meeting in Prague  to discuss support for Ukraine and preparations for the July NATO summit in Washington.

In World Olympic Boxing Qualifier in Bangkok, Thailand, India’s Ankushita Boro in 60kg category entered into the Quarter Finals with 4-1 win over Kazakh pugilist. Nishant Dev in 71 kg also entered the quarterfinals with a convincing 5 nil win over Peerapat Yeasungnoen of Thailand.

Earlier, Arundhati Choudhary registered a convincing victory in 66 kg weight category to reach the pre-quarter-finals.

Delhi Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal today said that national capital is facing water shortage due to higher demand of water in the hot summer days. He has urged the neighboring BJP ruled states Haryana and Uttar Pradesh to cooperate in fulfilling the water requirement of Delhi in this crisis situation.

In a social media post, Mr. Kejriwal said, this time the entire country is experiencing unprecedented heat leading to reduced water and electricity supply across the country. If BJP asks its governments in Haryana and UP to provide water to Delhi for a month, it would provide great relief to the people and would be highly appreciated. Mr Kejriwal also said that BJP’s protests against AAP on the issue of water shortage would not solve this crisis.

Meanwhile, BJP today held a protest march in front of Delhi Secretariat over the water crisis situation in the national capital. BJP candidate from New Delhi Lok Sabha seat, Bansuri Swaraj alleged that AAP government has created an artificial water crisis. She said, Delhi Jal Board was in the profit of 600 crores rupees in 2013 and currently, it is in the loss of 73 thousand crore rupees.

In Manipur, the flash flood continues to wreak havoc and Imphal City has been badly affected by the flood. Three persons have  died in different flood related so far while one person is missing. The State Water Resources Minister said that more than one lakh eighty eight thousand persons have been affected by the flash flood in Manipur. The repairing of river bank, tackling of breaches and overflow of key rivers flowing in Imphal Valley in continuing.The Chief Minister N. In Bihar, 29 people have died and more than 200 fell ill due to heat stroke. Officials sources said the deaths have occurred in the last 24 hours. Maximum 12 deaths occurred in Aurangabad district where mercury has soared to above 48 degree Celsius. Six deaths have been reported in Bhojpur district whereas four each in Rohtas and Jehanabad. One each casualty  reported from Buxar, Arwal and Nalanda districts where one polling staff and two homeguard jawans in deputed in election duty succumbed to death due to heat stroke. In Jehanabad four teachers were declared brought dead at a hospital after losing consciousness due to heat stroke.

The Met department has predicted intense heatwave conditions for South Western and South Central regions of the state for next 24 hours. In the past 48 hours Northern and Eastern parts of the state have received pre monsoon showers giving much needed reprieve from scorching heat.

In Odisha, 14 people died of suspected heat stroke in Sundergarh district as the mercury shot up in most parts of the eastern state. As per reports, 10 persons died at the Rourkela Government Hospital within a span of six hours last afternoon. 4 persons died at Sundergarh district headquarters hospital on account of heatwave. Odisha is going through an intense heatwave condition.

Keeping view of the on-going severe heatwave conditions in the national capital, a special red zone has been designated at the Ram Manohar Lohiya Hospital (RML) for heatstroke patients in Delhi. Talking to media, RML Head of Emergency Medicine, Dr. Seema Balkrishna Wasnik said that the red zone contains inflatable tubs filled with ice and water to help bring down  the patient’s temperature as soon as possible. She added the patients under critical conditions are intubated and put on a ventilator for immediate treatment. Dr Wasnik noted that swift treatment to heatstroke is vital as it brings down chances of mortality.
