## Morning News

The 2nd India-Singapore Ministerial Roundtable (ISMR), aimed at further strengthening trade and economic ties between the two countries, will be held in Singapore today. Four union ministers – Nirmala Sitharaman, Piyush Goyal, S. Jaishankar and Ashwini Vaishnaw, will attend the meet. During their visit, the ministers will interact with their Singaporean counterparts and leadership.

The ISMR is a unique mechanism established to set a new agenda for India-Singapore bilateral relations. Its inaugural meeting was held in New Delhi in September 2022. This second meeting will enable both sides to review various aspects of their strategic partnership and identify new avenues to further elevate and broaden it.  Singapore has been a major source of foreign direct investment for India. In 2023-24, Singapore was the largest source of these inflows with 11.77 billion dollars of investments into Indian markets. The cumulative inflow of FDI from Singapore from April 2000 to March 2024 was 159.94 billion dollars. In bilateral trade, Singapore was the sixth largest global trade partner of India in 2023-24. Jigyasa, Akashvani News, Delhi

Union Minister for Commerce and Industry Piyush Goyal discussed bilateral trade and investment issues with the global business leaders in Singapore yesterday. In a statement, Ministry of Commerce and Industry said, during the meeting, key discussions were held to explore and enhance investment opportunities between India and Singapore, focusing on mutual growth and collaboration across diverse sectors. The Ministry said that the focus was on exploring investment opportunities in India, leveraging the country’s economic growth, and fostering collaboration across various sectors.

Maharashtra Cabinet chaired by Chief Minister Eknath Shinde has approved the implementation of a revised National Pension Scheme (NPS) for state government employees on the lines of the Centre’s Unified Pension Scheme (UPS). The revised National Pension Scheme of the state government will be implemented from March 1, 2024.

As per the revised national pension scheme, the employees will get pension equal to 50 per cent of their last salary plus dearness allowance and 60 percent of the family pension.  Under UPS employees will get 50 percent of average of the last 12 months’ salary.  The revised National Pension Scheme of the Maharashtra government will be implemented from Ist March, 2024.  Employees retired before March 2024 can also opt for this revised NPS after refunding 60% of lump sum amount received under NPS to the state government. They will also have to return the pension amount received from the annuity service provider to the Maharashtra Government. More than 13 lakh employees are expected to be benefitted. Jeevan Bhawasar, Akashvani News, Mumbai.

World Health Organization launched a six-month global strategic and response plan to stop the deadly outbreak of Mpox. This plan aims to curtail human-to-human transmission of Mpox through coordinated global, regional, and national efforts. Starting  from September 2024 to February 2025, and envisions a 135 million U.S dollars funding, the plan includes strategic vaccination efforts for people at highest risk to interrupt transmission chains.

Russia unleashed a massive drone and missile barrage throughout Ukraine this morning that appeared to target energy infrastructure. The massive missile and drone attack targeted multiple Ukrainian cities, including Kyiv, causing power outages and explosions. Media reports said, at least three people were killed in explosion. Kyiv Mayor Vitali Klitschko, first said several districts in the capital were without power. On the other hand, as many as five people were killed and 13 others were wounded during an overnight Ukrainian strike on Russia’s Belgorod.

The US Open tennis tournament 2024 will kick off in New York tonight. All eyes will be on Sumit Nagal and Rohan Bopanna as they lead the Indian challenge. Sumit Nagal is set to become the first Indian man since 2019 to compete in the singles event of all four Grand Slam tournaments in a year. Nagal, 72nd in the tennis rankings, reached the second round at the Australian Open before crashing out of the first rounds in the French Open and Wimbledon Championships this year.
