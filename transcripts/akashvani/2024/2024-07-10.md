## Morning News

On the second leg of his two nation tour, Prime Minister Narendra Modi arrived in Vienna yesterday on a two-day visit to Austria. He was given a warm welcome from members of Indian community upon his arrival.

“Prime Minister Modi arrived in Austria from Moscow after a two-day visit to Russia, where he held 22nd India-Russia annual summit with President Putin. It is the first visit by an Indian prime minister to Austria in 41 years.  Mr Modi will call on Austrian President Alexander Van der Bellen, and hold talks with Chancellor of Austria Karl Nehammer today. Prime Minister Modi and Austrian Chancellor will also address business leaders from India and Austria. Mr Modi will also interact with members of the Indian community in Vienna. CHIRAG JHA, AAKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

Mr Modi attended a dinner hosted by Austrian Chancellor last night. In a social media post, Prime Minister Modi thanked Chancellor Nehammer for the warm welcome. Mr Modi said, he is looking forward to their discussions today. PM Modi said, the two nations will continue working together to further global good.

Foreign Secretary Vinay Kwatra says, Russia has agreed to India’s demand to ensure early release and return to home of  the Indian nationals working in the Russian military as support staff.

Mr Modi raised the issue during his informal talks with Russian President Vladimir Putin over dinner at the Russian leader’s dacha or country home. The Foreign Secretary said, the two sides will work on how expeditiously the Indians can be brought back home. Mr Kwatra said, India anticipated the number of its nationals serving in the Russian military to be roughly between 35 to 50 out of which 10 were already brought back.

Professor Soumya Swaminathan has been appointed as the Principal Advisor at the Union Ministry of Health and Family Welfare for the National Tuberculosis Elimination Programme on a pro bono basis.

The Ministry of Health and Family Welfare in a statement said that as the Principal Advisor, Prof. Swaminathan will provide technical advice on the overall strategy to achieve program goals, suggest policy directions and necessary course corrections for optimal outcomes, and advise on research strategy.

## Midday News

The Supreme Court today said that a Muslim woman is entitled for maintenance from her husband under Section 125 of the Code of Criminal Procedure. The bench comprising Justices BV Nagarathna and Augustine George Masih dismissed a petition in the case, in which the man filed a plea against the direction to pay interim maintenance to his divorced wife under Section 125 CrPC.

The bench also ruled that if, during the pendency of the application, the woman gets divorced, then she can take recourse to the (Protection of Rights on Marriage) 2019 Act. The bench said that the Muslim Women (Protection of Rights on Divorce) Act 1986 would not prevail over the secular law. The bench stated that the remedy under the 2019 Act is in addition to the remedy under Section 125 CrPC.

In another decision Apex Court today held that the suit filed by the West Bengal against the Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) is maintainable. The suit was filed for allegedly proceeding with probes and filing FIRs in cases of post-poll violence in West Bengal without getting the state government’s consent as required under the law. A Bench, headed by Justice BR Gavai, rejected the argument raised by the Union government questioning the maintainability of the original suit filed by the plaintiff state under Article 131 of the Constitution.  The top court ordered the matter to be further listed on August 13 for framing of issues in the suit.

The Commission, citing media reports, said that the state government has taken the decision to divert over 14 thousand crore rupees from the sub plans to allocate funds to Five Guarantee Schemes.

Mr. Vaishnaw highlighted that only under exigencies, the trip duration exceeds the prescribed hours.

The Minister added that the recruitment process for 18 thousand running staff is currently in process. The Minister asserted that the attempt to demoralise railway families with fake news will fail.
