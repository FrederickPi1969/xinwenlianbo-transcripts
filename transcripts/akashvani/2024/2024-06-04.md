## Morning News

The counting of votes for the election to the 18th Lok Sabha began this morning amid tight security. The counting of votes also started for the Assembly polls in Andhra Pradesh and Odisha and bye-elections to 25 Assembly Constituencies. The counting began at 8 AM.

On preparations made for counting, Chief Election Commissioner (CEC) Rajiv Kumar said,around 10.5 lakh counting  booths have been set up across the country. Each booth will have 14 tables. There are observers and micro-observers to man the booths.

“The counting of postal ballots will start first across all counting centres. After half-an-hour of the start of the postal ballot count, the counting of votes recorded in the electronic voting machines will commence. For the smooth conduct of the electoral process, three-tier security systems have been set up at multiple counting centres in various cities. The counting trends and results, as per data entered by Returning Officer or Assistant Returning Officers for Parliamentary Constituencies and Assembly Constituencies will be available on the Election Commission Website as well as on the Voter Helpline App. The voting for the 543-member Lok Sabha was conducted over seven phases, starting from 19th April to 1st June. However Surat seat in Gujarat was won by BJP uncontested. Over 64 crore voters participated in this polls including 31 crore women. The counting of votes will bring an end to a marathon polling exercise which stretched over 80 days. ANUPAM MISHRA, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) is seeking a third straight term in office, while the Congress party-led opposition bloc I.N.D.I.A is hoping to upstage them.

Chief Election Commissioner Rajiv Kumar said that India has created a world record with 64.2 crore voters participating in the Lok Sabha Polls. Briefing media in New Delhi,  Mr Kumar said that the number is 1.5 times the voters of all G7 countries – the US, the UK, France, Germany, Italy, Japan and Canada. In Odisha, out of total of 147 Assembly seats, results of 69 seats have been announced.  BJP has bagged 49 seats and is leading in 29 seats while Biju Janata Dal has won 37 seats and is leading in 14 seats.

Prime Minister and senior BJP leader Narendra Modi today said it is certain that the NDA will form the government at Centre for the third consecutive term. Addressing party workers at BJP Head Quarter, Mr. Modi said people have shown full faith in the BJP and NDA in this polls.

Party President J P Nadda said that after the formation of the NDA, it is for the first time in the history of Indian politics that an alliance would form a government for the third time in a row.

Congress president Mallikarjun Kharge has termed the result of Lok Sabha polls as the victory of the people and democracy. Breifing media in New Delhi, Mr Kharge said, no party has been given majority in this polls. He said, they accept the mandate of people. He added that Bharat Jodo Nyay Yatra connected lakhs of people with the Congress.

Senior party leader Rahul Gandhi said, this election was not only against one political party but against the Agencies. He said, these are the institutions that are captured by BJP. Mr Gandhi said, this election was for saving the constitution.

Indian benchmark indices witnessed massive slump today. In the intraday trade, both indices were down 8 percent, which was the biggest single-day fall since  February 2022. At close, Sensex recorded a massive loss of 4,390 points at 72,079, while the Nifty 50 ended with a cut of 1,379 points at 21,884. Meanwhile, BSE Midcap index ended with a deep loss of 8.07 per cent after falling as much as 12 per cent during the session. Similarly, BSE Smallcap index closed with a loss of 6.79 per cent.

Visit of Rashtrapati Bhavan in New Delhi will remain closed for the general public from tomorrow to 9th June. The President house in statement said, the visit of general public will be closed due to preparation for forthcoming event of swearing-in-ceremony of the Council of Ministers at Rashtrapati Bhavan.
