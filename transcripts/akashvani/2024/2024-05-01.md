## Midday News

Union Home Ministry has termed emails regarding bomb threats to different schools in the National Capital and adjoining areas as a hoax and appealed parents and students not to panic. The Ministry said, Delhi Police and Security agencies are taking necessary steps in the matter. The Delhi Police has informed that so far nothing suspicious has been found in the search and further investigation is underway.

Delhi Lieutenant Governor, Vinai Kumar Saxena spoke to Delhi Police Commissioner Sanjay Arora and sought a detailed report into the issue. Mr Saxena directed Delhi Police to identify the culprits and ensure there are no lapses. He said, the miscreants and culprits will not be spared. Mr Saxena also visited several schools which received bomb threats. He told the media that Delhi police acted swiftly in the matter.

Talking to reportes Special CP of Delhi police Ravinder Yadav said that event if it is a hoax call we cannot take chance.

Earlier, Delhi Education Minister Atishi said, the Delhi government is in constant touch with the Police and the schools. Several schools in Delhi NCR had received email of bomb threat this morning.

In the third phase of Lok Sabha Elections, 93 seats spread over 10 states and a Union Territory will go to polls on 7th of this month. Originally, voting was scheduled to be held on 94 Parliamentary Constituencies, however, after elections in Betul Lok Sabha seat of Madhya Pradesh rescheduled from second to third phase, BJP Candidate in Surat Constituency of Gujarat being elected unopposed and revised polling schedule in Anantnag-Rajouri parliamentary constituency of Jammu and Kashmir, the total seats now going to poll are 93.

In this phase, 25 seats in Gujarat, 14 in Karnataka, 11 in Maharashtra and ten seats in Uttar Pradesh will go to polls. Besides, voting will also be held on nine seats in Madhya Pradesh, seven in Chhattisgarh, five in Bihar, Four seats each in Assam and West Bengal, two seats each in Goa and Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu.

With this phase, voting will be completed in 16 States including all Northeastern states and four Union Territories of Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Puducherry, Lakshadweep and Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu.

The Election Commission of India, ECI has revised the polling schedule of Lok Sabha elections in the Anantnag-Rajouri parliamentary constituency of Jammu and Kashmir from the third to the sixth phase. The voting in the Anantnag-Rajouri seat will now be held on 25th of May instead of 7th May, as scheduled earlier. The ECI said, the decision has been taken after representations from different political parties of the Union Territory regarding various logistic, communication and natural barriers turning out as hindrance during campaigning leading to lack of fair opportunities for the contesting candidates.

Chief Electoral Officer Mr. Praveen Gupta informed that re-polling will be held tomorrow from 7 am to 5 pm at the booth located in room number 1 of polling station number 195.

In our series, covering profiles of key constituencies across States and Union Territories during the Lok Sabha elections, today we bring a report on Banaskantha Lok Sabha constituency in Gujarat.

“Banaskantha Loksabha constituency is one of the significant Loksabha seats of North Gujarat. It comprises seven assembly constituencies of Banaskantha district which shares its border with Rajasthan and Pakistan. The constituency has seen a mix of representatives from different political parties over the years. The seat was once used to be the stronghold of Congress party, however from the last two terms, BJP candidates had won from the seat. It is the only constituency in Gujarat in this election where the Congress and BJP have fielded two women candidates against each other in this election. Rekha Ben Chaudhari from BJP will face Geni Ben Thakor of Congress in these polls. There are a total of 19 lakh 61 thousand voters in this Loksabha constituency who will decide the political fate of the 12 candidates in the fray which include six independent candidates. Prime Minister Narendra Modi will address his first election rally in Gujarat for the upcoming polls at Vav in Banaskantha district today. Aparna Khunt, Akashvani News, Ahmedabad.”

The Gross Goods and Services Tax (GST) revenue collections for April this year have recorded the highest ever collection of two lakh ten thousand crore rupees. Union Finance Ministry said, this represents a significant 12.4 per cent year-on-year growth, driven by a strong increase in domestic transactions and imports. It said, out of the total collection in April this year, Central Goods and Services Tax is over 43 thousand crore rupees, State Goods and Services Tax is over 53 thousand crore, Integrated Goods and Services Tax is over 99 thousand crore and cess is over 13 thousand crore rupees.

The Ministry said, after accounting for refunds, the net GST revenue for April 2024 stands at one lakh 92 thousand crore rupees, reflecting an impressive 15.5 per cent growth compared to the same period last year.
