## Midday News

The filing of nominations for the first phase of Lok Sabha elections will end this evening. While, in Bihar, the last date for filling nominations is tomorrow.

The elections will be held in 102 Lok Sabha seats in 17 States and four Union Territories in the first phase. Thirty-Nine seats of Tamil Nadu, 12 seats of Rajasthan, eight seats of Uttar Pradesh, six seats of Madhya Pradesh, five seats each in Uttarakhand, Assam and Maharashtra, four seats of Bihar, three seats of West Bengal, two seats each in Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya and one seat each in Chhattisgarh, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tripura, Anadaman and Nicobar Islands, Jammu and Kashmir, Lakshadweep and Puducherry will go to the polls in the first phase.

The scrutiny of nominations will take place on tomorrow and the last date for the withdrawal of candidatures is Saturday. In Bihar, the scrutiny of nominations will be done on the Saturday and the last date for withdrawal of candidature is 2nd of the next month. Voting will be held on 19th of April.
The notification for the second phase of Lok Sabha election will be issued tomorrow. Elections will be held in 88 Lok Sabha seats in 12 States and Union Territories along with one seat of outer Manipur in this phase. Twenty seats of Kerala, 14 seats of Karnataka , 13 seats of Rajasthan, eight seats each of Uttar Pradesh and Maharashtra, seven seats of Madhya Pradesh, five seats each in Assam and Bihar, three seats each of West Bengal and Chhattisgarh and one seat each in Manipur, Tripura and Jammu and Kashmir will go to polls in this first phase. Nominations can be filed till 4th April. Voting for this phase will be held on 26th April.

The Press Information Bureau has started a microsite, pib.gov.in/elect2024/index.aspx having several features as one stop facilitation portal for mediapersons reporting for General Election. The portal has characteristics like a Digital Flip Book which encompasses various interesting analytics and data rich features. The media persons can utilize this information for writing their articles. Useful links are provided through which the journalists can navigate to relevant parts of the Election Commission website.

The Election Commission is leveraging Technology with 27 IT Apps and Systems during the Elections. Our correspondent reports that use of technology through apps and portals has made the process easier for candidates, people as well as for officials who are closely monitoring the electoral process.

Through online Suvidha Portal, candidates are filing nomination and Affidavit. Candidates are also using the portal for seeking permissions for meetings, rallies etc. It also ensures Level Playing Field – First in First Out. Through Voter Helpline App VHA people can apply forms online, view polling booth details and download E-EPIC Election photo identity card. C-Vigil App is used for recording, reporting, and resolving violation. The response will be in 100-minute timeline and complaints can be made anonymously. The Candidate Affidavit is available on affidavit portal and KYC App. Candidates with Criminal Antecedents must publish the information publicly. It is mandatory for Political parties to publish information publicly across designated channels. ANUPAM MISHRA, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.

Odisha is going for simultaneous Lok Sabha and Assembly polls in the 2024 general elections. There are a total of 21 Lok Sabha and 147 Assembly constituencies which will go for polls in the 4th, 5th, 6th and 7th phase of the ensuing general elections. Our correspondent has the details.

The Gazette notification for the 4th phase of polls will be issued on 18th of April, followed by 26th April for 5th, 29th of April for 6th and 7th May for 7th phase of polls. The polling in Kalahandi, Nabarangpur, Berhampur and Koraput Lok Sabha seats will be held in the fourth phase that is 13th of May, followed by Bargarh, Sundargarh, Balangir, Kandhamal and Aska in 5th phase on 20th of May, Sambalpur, Keonjhar , Dhenkanal, Cuttack, Puri and Bhubaneswar in 6th phase  on 25th of May and Mayurbhanj, Balasore, Bhadrak, Jajpur, Kendrapara and Jagatsinghpur Lok Sabha seats in the 7th phase of elections on 1st of June. The Assembly constituencies coming under respective Lok Sabha constituencies will also go for voting simultaneously along with the Lok Sabha polling. The Election Commission of India has made all preparations for the simultaneous Lok Sabha and Assembly elections in Odisha. A total of about 3 crore 35 lakh voters have been registered for the ensuing elections who will cast their votes in 37,809 booths across the state.  There will be a triangular contest this time among Biju Janata Dal, Bharatiya Janata Party and Congress party. So far only BJP has declared the name of candidates for 18 of the 21 Lok Sabha seats, while BJD and Congress are expected to come up with their nominees shortly. Prakash Dash, Akashvani News, Bhubaneswar

In Bihar, Chirag Paswan led Lok Jan Shakti Party (R) has declared Arun Bharati as a nominee from Jamui parliamentary seat. He is brother-in-law of Chirag Paswan. Mr. Paswan allotted him the party symbol today. Chirag Paswan has represented Jamui Lok Sabha seat in 2014 and 2019.

In the 18th Lok Sabha elections, he has decided to contest from Hajipur parliamentary constituency.

Delhi BJP President Virendra Sachdeva and other party leaders today met Delhi Police Commissioner Sanjay Arora and filed a police complaint regarding alleged official letters coming from the Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal when he is in the  custody. Speaking to media, Mr. Sachdeva said, the BJP has demanded an inquiry against the alleged official letters. He said, such letters cannot be released from the jail according to the law.

Meanwhile, in a video message, wife of Mr. Kejriwal, Sunita today said, Mr. Kejriwal will reveal about the money trail of the alleged liquor scam in the court tomorrow.

In Maharashtra, Senior BJP leader and Union Minister Nitin Gadkari, filed his nomination for Nagpur Lok Sabha Constituency, in Nagpur Collectorate today. On this occasion Chief Minister Eknath Shinde, Deputy Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis, State BJP President Chandrashekhar Bawankule were mainly present.

Shiv Sena (UBT) has announced a list of 17 candidates for Lok Sabha elections in Maharashtra. Party has again given opportunity to its sitting MP Arvind Sawant from South Mumbai, Rajan Vichare from Thane, Vinayak Raut from Ratangiri-Sindhudurg, Omprakash Nimbalkar from Osmanabad and Sanjay Jadhav from Parbhani. But its alliance partner Congress has expressed displeasure over announcement of candidates from Some of Mumbai’s Constituency and Sangli Seat.

Air Chief Marshal VR Chaudhari has said that Balakot like operations have shown that given the political will, aerospace power can be effectively carried out beyond enemy lines. Mr Chaudhari said this in his inaugural address at the 15th Jumbo Majumdar International Seminar on Aerospace Power in Future Conflicts in New Delhi today.

The Air Chief said, militarisation and weaponisation of space has become an inevitable reality as nations increasingly rely on space-based assets for building strategic advantage.

In Chhattisgarh, six Maoists, including two female rebels, were killed in a gun-battle with security forces in the tribal Bastar division today. Details from our correspondent:

A joint team of CRPF, CoBRA battalion and District Reserve Guard was on a search operation in the jungles of Basaguda area of  Bijapur district.  An encounter took place  near Chipurbhatti.  Six Maoists killed in the exchange of fire. Police officials confirmed the incident.  Bodies of 6 Maoists, along with large number of arms and ammunition recovered from the spot. Vikalp Shukla, Akashavani News, Raipur.

The Enforcement Directorate (ED) today called West Bengal Minister Chandranath Sinha at the CGO Complex in Kolkata. Recently, a search interrogation were conducted by the Directorate at his house at Bolpur in Birbhum district in connection with teacher recruitment scam. The ED had confiscated various documents, mobile phone and 41 lakhs rupees in cash.

The Enforcement Directorate (ED) carried out raids at various places in Punjab and Chandigarh this morning. As per reports, the ED teams raided Punjab’s senior IAS and Excise and Taxation Commissioner Varun Roojam’s residence in sector 20 Chandigarh.

India has beaten rest of the Asian markets by attracting the highest foreign funds flow in March. Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) pumped 3.63 billion dollars into Indian equities in their biggest buying binge since December 2023. Domestic institutions continued to be net buyers, investing around 52,467 crore rupees in the market to hit a four-year high. The achievement defies geopolitical crises and concerns that the higher interest rate regime will continue for some more time.

Outside India, South Korea, Taiwan and Indonesia were the investment destinations for FIIs, while they chose to pull out money from markets in Japan, Malaysia, the Philippines, Thailand, Vietnam and Sri Lanka.

President Droupadi Murmu today accepted credentials from the envoys five nations at a ceremony held at Rashtrapati Bhavan. The Envoys are, Ambassador of the Philippines , Uzbekistan, Belarus, High Commissioner of Kenya and the Ambassador of Georgia.

Senior IPS officer Sadanand Vasant Date has been appointed as the Director General of the National Investigation Agency NIA. The 1990-batch Indian Police Service officer is currently serving as Chief of Maharashtra Anti-Terrorism Squad. The Ministry of Personnel said, the Appointments Committee of the Cabinet has approved the appointment of Mr Date for a tenure up to his superannuation on December 31, 2026.

Karnataka Energy Minister K J George has assured uninterrupted power supply during the summer months. In a statement released to the media persons, Mr George has informed that adequate steps are taken to meet the power demand. He informed that the Government has decided to import 2.5 lakh tonnes of coal from Indonesia to help thermal power plants to generate additional 600 to 800 MW of energy in addition to 3400 MW generated now.

In IPL cricket, Sunrisers Hyderabad will take on Mumbai Indians at the Rajiv Gandhi International Stadium in Hyderabad this evening. The match will start at 7.30 PM. Both the teams are aiming for their first victory in the tournament.

Yesterday, reigning champions Chennai Super Kings beat Gujarat Titans by 63 runs at the M A Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai.

The filing of nominations for the first phase of Lok Sabha Elections along with single-phase Assembly polls in two north-eastern states ended this evening. In Bihar, the last day for filling of papers for the first phase of parliamentary Elections is tomorrow. In the first phase, 102 Lok Sabha seats in 17 States and four Union Territories along with 32 Assembly constituencies in Sikkim and 60-member House in Arunachal Pradesh will go to polls on 19th of next month. Among states going to polls in a single phase for the Lok Sabha polls are Tamil Nadu, Arunachal Pradesh, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Lakshadeep, Anadaman and Nicobar Islands and Puducherry.

The scrutiny of nominations will take place tomorrow. The last day for the withdrawal of candidatures is Saturday.

Several prominent candidates across the country filed their nomination for the first phase of Lok Sabha polls on the last day today.

Senior BJP leader and Union Minister Nitin Gadkari, filed his papers for Nagpur Lok Sabha Constituency in Maharashtra today.

In Tripura, BJP nominee from Tripura West Lok Sabha constituency, Biplab Kumar Deb and his nearest rival from Congress Ashish Saha today filed their nominations in Agartala.

In Madhya Pradesh, on the last day, prominent candidates of all major parties including BJP and Congress submitted nominations. More from our correspondent:

“BJP candidates filed nomination papers today in Jabalpur, Chhindwara and Balaghat. Many senior leaders including Chief Minister Dr. Mohan Yadav and State BJP President Vishnudutt Sharma were present during nominations. The Chief Minister held a road show in Balaghat and addressed a public meeting in Chhindwara. In the last Lok Sabha Elections, BJP had won all the 28 seats except Chhindwara out of total 29 seats in the State.

Meanwhile, Congress today released the list of star campaigners for Madhya Pradesh. The list includes the names of 40 leaders including Congress National President Mallikarjun Kharge, Sonia Gandhi, Rahul Gandhi and former Chief Ministers of Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan and Chhattisgarh. Sanjeev Sharma, Akashvani News, Bhopal.”

In Jammu and Kashmir, eight candidates filed nominations from the Udhampur Lok Sabha constituency going to polls in the first phase.

In Assam, 38 candidates have filed nomination papers for the first phase. Five seats will be covered in this phase.

In Sikkim, Incumbent State Chief Minister and ruling Sikkim Krantikari Morcha leader Prem Singh Tamang, and former Chief Minister and Sikkim Democratic Front leader Pawan Kumar Chamling have filed their papers today for the State Assembly election.

In Bihar, the filing of nomination for the four parliamentary constituency segments of Gaya, Aurangabad, Nawada and Jamui in the first phase of Lok Sabha Elections will end tomorrow. The scrutiny of nominations will take place on Saturday and the last date for withdrawal of candidature is 2nd of the next month. However, voting in the state will be held on 19th of April. The Election Commission of India extended the last date of nomination in the state due to festival of Holi and Bihar Foundation Day.

“Among prominent candidates, BJP nominee Sushil Kumar Singh from Aurangabad and RJD candidate Shrawan Kumar Kushwaha from Nawada have filed their nominations. From NDA Hindustani Awam Morcha leader Jitan Ram Manjhi will file a nomination from Gaya and BJP nominee Vivek Thakur from the Nawada Lok Sabha seat. In the Grand Alliance, the formal announcement of candidates for the first phase has not taken place. RJD candidates Kumar Sarvjeet, Archana Ravidas and Abhay Kushwaha who got party symbols from the RJD President Lalu Prasad are likely to submit their nomination papers for Gaya, Jamui and Aurangabad parliamentary constituencies. Dharmendra Kumar Rai, Akashvani News, Patna”.

The notification for the second phase of Lok Sabha election will be issued tomorrow. 88 Lok Sabha seats in 12 States and Union Territories along with the remaining part of the Outer Manipur Lok Sabha constituency will go to polls in this phase on 26th of next month. Last date for filing of nominations is 4th of April.

The Biju Janata Dal (BJD) today announced the names of candidates for the Lok Sabha and Assembly Elections in Odisha. BJD President and Chief Minister Naveen Patnaik declared the names of 15 candidates out of the 21 Lok Sabha seats in the State.

The Odisha Chief Minister will contest from the Hinjili Assembly segment in Ganjam district. The BJD has fielded party leader Pranab Prakash Das from the Sambalpur Lok Sabha seat against Union Minister and BJP candidate Dharmendra Pradhan. The BJD has announced the names of 72 candidates for the 147 Assembly seats in Odisha.

Lok Sabha and Assembly polls in the state will be held simultaneously in four phases on 13th, 20th and 25th of May and 1st of June.

Shiv Sena (UBT) has announced a list of 17 candidates for Lok Sabha Elections in Maharashtra. Party has again given opportunity to its sitting MP Arvind Sawant from South Mumbai. Other candidates including Rajan Vichare from Thane, Vinayak Raut from Ratangiri-Sindhudurg, Omprakash Nimbalkar from Osmanabad and Sanjay Jadhav from Parbhani seat. But its alliance partner Congress has expressed displeasure over announcement of candidates from some of the Mumbai’s Constituencies and Sangli Seat.

The BJP today announced that Independent MP Navneet Rana will be the party’s candidate from the Amravati Parliamentary constituency in Maharashtra while BJP leader Govind Karjol will contest from the Chitradurga Lok Sabha seat in Karnataka.  Haryana Chief Minister Nayab Singh Saini will contest bypoll to Karnal Assembly seat.

The Party also announced the names of ten candidates for the Andhra Pradesh Assembly Elections. Party leader YS Chowdary will fight from the Vijayawada Assembly seat while P Vishnu Kumar Raju has been fielded from the Visakhapatnam North seat.

Meanwhile, Aam Aadmi Party’s lone Lok Sabha member from Punjab and Jalandhar MP Sushil Kumar Rinku and party’s MLA  Sheetal Angural joined BJP at party headquarters in New Delhi today.

On the other hand, a Congress Central Election Committee meeting is underway in New Delhi to finalise the names of remaining candidates for the Lok Sabha polls.

The Election Commission today issued show cause notices to BJP MP Dilip Ghosh and Congress leader Supriya Shrinate for their alleged objectionable remarks. The notice to Mr. Ghosh has been issued for his comments against West Bengal Chief Minister Mamata Banerjee. In its notice to Mrs Shrinate for her remarks against BJP Lok Sabha candidate Kangana Ranaut, the poll body said the Congress leader’s comment is undignified, in bad taste and prima facie violative of the provisions of the Model Code of Conduct. The Commission has asked the two leaders to submit their reply by Friday.

In our ongoing series focusing on state profiles during the Lok Sabha polls, we will now go over to Himachal Pradesh. Voting in the state will be held in four Lok Sabha constituencies, along with by-election to six Assembly constituencies in a single phase on the 1st of June.

“Bharatiya Janata Party has declared its candidates for  six Assembly seats including all the four parliamentary constituencies of the state, whereas the Congress party has not been able to declare the name of any candidate so far. Bharatiya Janata Party has fielded Union Minister and sitting MP Anurag Singh Thakur from Hamirpur parliamentary constituency consecutively for the fifth time, while famous film actress Kangana Ranaut has been made the Party candidate from Mandi Lok Sabha constituency. Similarly, sitting MP from Shimla Lok Sabha constituency, Suresh Kashyap, while Dr. Rajeev Bhardwaj from Kangra has been made the BJP candidate. By-Elections have also been declared in six Assembly constituencies in the State. It is noteworthy that in the Elections held on the 27th of February for a Rajya Sabha seat, six MLAs of the Congress Party and three independent MLAs had voted for the winning BJP candidate Harsh Mahajan, due to which the Congress government in the State was in trouble. The  Assembly Speaker had disqualified these rebel Congress MLAs for violating the whip. All these former MLAs have left Congress and joined BJP, which has declared them as its candidates from their respective constituencies. Ritesh Kapoor, Akashvani News, Shimla”.

The Delhi High Court today refused to grant immediate interim relief to Delhi Chief Minister and Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) leader Arvind Kejriwal in the money laundering case related to the alleged liquor policy scam. The Court issued notice to the Enforcement Directorate (ED) on a plea moved by Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal challenging his arrest by the agency earlier this month and six-day of remand to ED in the case. The court has asked the Enforcement Directorate to file its response to AAP leader’s petition by 2nd April. The next hearing in the matter will take place on 3rd of April.

Delhi Lieutenant Governor Vinai Kumar Saxena assured the citizens of the national capital that the Delhi government will not be run from jail. His remarks came on the backdrop of statements by AAP leaders that party leader Arvind Kejriwal will continue as Chief Minister even if he is behind bars. Mr Saxena was replying to a question during a private TV Channel Summit in New Delhi.

India today lodged a strong protest over the remarks made by the Spokesperson of the US State Department on Delhi Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal’s arrest. In a statement, the Ministry of External Affairs said states are expected to be respectful of the sovereignty and internal affairs of others in diplomacy. The Ministry said, this responsibility is even more so in the case of fellow democracies and it could otherwise end up setting unhealthy precedents.
Meanwhile, India today summoned the acting Deputy Chief of Mission of the US Embassy in New Delhi over remarks made by the US on Kejriwal’s arrest.

External Affairs Minister Dr S Jaishankar today called on Malaysian Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim in Kuala Lumpur. In a social media post, Dr Jaishankar said Prime Minister Ibrahim’s vision for stronger India-Malaysia ties, both in traditional and new-age areas, will help craft a more ambitious agenda for the relationship.

The External Affairs Minister had a meeting with his Malaysian counterpart Mohamad Haji Hasan. They exchanged views on enhancing collaboration in political, economic, defence, digital, startup, consular and P2P linkages.

Ukraine Foreign Minister Dmytro Kuleba will be arriving in New Delhi tomorrow for a two-day official visit to India at the invitation of External Affairs Minister Dr S Jaishankar. During his visit, Mr Kuleba will have several engagements, including official meetings with the External Affairs Minister and Deputy National Security Adviser.

Chief of Army Staff General Manoj Pande today stressed that self-reliance is a strategic imperative for the future. He stressed on the importance for the country to be able to source the requirement for sustenance in terms of ammunition. He was speaking at a Summit organised by a private news channel. General Pande said adequate emphasis is being laid on developing own capabilities in the Defence sector.

Responding to a question, the Army Chief said India is prepared in every manner and levels of operational preparedness is of a very high order. He said India has its response mechanism firmly in place.

Domestic benchmark indices ended on a positive note today amid mixed global cues

“At close, the Sensex was up 526 points at 72,996, and the Nifty climbed 119 points at 22,123. In the forex market, the Indian rupee today ended at 83 rupees and 37 paise against the US dollar. Gold prices at Multi Commodity Exchange for April contracts were trading at 66,422 rupees per 10 gram. And in intra-day trade, Brent crude was trading at 85 dollars and 53 cents per barrel, when reports last came in. Arjun Choudhary, for Akashvani News.”
