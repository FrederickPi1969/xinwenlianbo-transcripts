## Morning News

Former US President Donald Trump has been officially nominated as the Republican presidential candidate. His nomination became official after he secured majority of votes from Republican National Committee delegates in Milwaukee last night. This marks Mr Trump’s third consecutive run after winning in 2016 and losing to President Joe Biden in 2020. US Presidential election is scheduled in November this year.

Mr Trump announced Ohio Senator JD Vance as his vice presidential running mate. 39-year old Vance brings to the ticket an experience in the US military. He is a former venture capitalist and author of “Hillbilly Elegy”, an autobiography. By picking Senator Vance, Mr Trump is showing he’s really focusing on key states in the Midwest that are up for grabs in the next election, like Pennsylvania, Michigan, and Wisconsin. Mr Vance’s selection comes at a turbulent time for the campaign, following an assassination attempt on Mr Trump at a rally on Saturday.

External Affairs Minister Dr. S. Jaishankar will embark on a two-day official visit to Mauritius beginning today. During the visit, the Minister will call on Prime Minister of Mauritius Pravind Jugnauth and will hold bilateral meetings with other senior ministers of Mauritius. He will also engage with other prominent Mauritian leaders.

According to the Ministry of External Affairs, this visit will provide an opportunity for both sides to comprehensively take stock of various facets of the bilateral relationship. The Ministry said, the visit underscores the importance of the India Mauritius relationship, and is a reflection of India’s ‘Neighbourhood First Policy’, Vision SAGAR, and commitment to the Global South. It also reaffirms the continued commitment of both countries to strengthen the multi-dimensional bilateral relationship and deepen close people-to-people ties.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah will chair the 7th apex-level meeting of the Narco-Coordination Center on Thursday in New Delhi. Mr. Shah will launch the National Narcotics Helpline ‘MANAS’ (Madak Padarth Nisedh Asuchna Kendra) and inaugurate the Narcotics Control Bureau’s zonal office at Srinagar. Mr Shah will also release the Bureau’s ‘Annual Report 2023’ and Compendium on ‘Nasha Mukt Bharat’.

This meeting will focus at coordinating and synergizing the efforts of various central and state government agencies involved in countering drug trafficking and abuse in India.

The Supreme Court has dismissed a review petition filed against its January 3 verdict refusing to form any SIT or group of experts to conduct an investigation into the Adani-Hindenburg issue.  After perusing the review petition, a bench comprising Chief Justice of India DY Chandrachud and Justices JB Pardiwala and Manoj Misra said that there is no error apparent on the face of the record.

In its January 3 order, the Supreme Court had said that reports prepared by third-party organisations such as the Organized Crime and Corruption Reporting Project and Hindenburg Research cannot be regarded as a conclusive proof. It had said that the reliance placed by PIL litigants on newspaper articles or reports by third-party organisations does not inspire confidence to question the comprehensive investigation undertaken by the SEBI. The apex court said that the facts in the case did not warrant a transfer of investigation from the SEBI and asked the market regulator to take its investigation to a logical conclusion in accordance with the law.

The Supreme Court has issued notice on a fresh batch of petitions filed by the National Testing Agency, seeking the transfer of pleas pending before the Rajasthan High Court related to the alleged NEET-UG paper leak, to the apex court. A bench comprising Chief Justice D Y Chandrachud, Justice J B Pardiwala, and Justice Manoj Misra issued the notice to the petitioners before the Rajasthan High Court and tagged the transfer petitions with the already pending matters in the issue. However, the Bench refused to pass a formal order staying the proceedings pending before the Rajasthan High Court.

## Midday News

Five security personnel lost their lives in an encounter with terrorists in Doda district of Jammu and Kashmir. The troops of Rashtriya Rifles and Special Operations Group (SOG) of Jammu and Kashmir Police launched a joint cordon and search operation at Dhari Gote in the Dessa forest area last evening following specific input about the presence of terrorists. In the ensuing gunfight,  five security personnel, including an officer and a SOG jawan were critically injured and later succumbed to injuries. Lieutenant Governor of Jammu and Kashmir Manoj Sinah has paid tributes to the brave soldiers.

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh has said the nation stands firmly with the families of the soldiers who sacrificed their lives in the line of duty in Doda. In a social media post, Mr. Singh said the counter-terrorist operations are underway, and Indian soldiers remain committed to eliminating the scourge of terrorism and restoring peace and order in the region.

Union Minister Dr. Jitendra Singh in a social media post, urged people to join together to defeat the nefarious designs of the enemy and maintain peace and harmony in the Kashmir Valley.

Army Chief General Upendra Dwivedi has said, the Army stands firm with the bereaved families in the hour of grief.

The Government has notified the fifth Positive Indigenisation List (PIL) consisting of 346 items to minimize imports and boost self-reliance in defence. These include strategically important Line Replacement Units, spares, components, and raw materials, with import substitution value worth over one thousand crore rupees. The Defence Ministry said these items will only be procured from the Indian industry. The Ministry added that more than 12,300 items have been indigenized in the last three years. As a result, the Defence Public Sector Undertakings have placed orders on domestic vendors totaling over seven thousand five hundred crore rupees.

The Centre has asked States and Union Territories that police stations and the place under the control of the police department should not be designated as a place for examination of witnesses. Union Home Secretary Ajay Bhalla has written a letter to all the State Chief Secretaries and UTs in this regard. All the police stations have been asked to identify a designated place for the examination of witnesses through audio-video electronic means under the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023.

The three new criminal laws namely, the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita and the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam have come into force from the 1st of this month replacing the Indian Penal Code, 1860.
