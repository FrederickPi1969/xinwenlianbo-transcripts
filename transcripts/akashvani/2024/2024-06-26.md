## Morning News

The Speaker for the 18th Lok Sabha will be chosen today. Senior BJP MP Om Birla, who was also the Speaker in the last Lok Sabha, is the NDA nominee for the post this time as well. On the other hand, the Opposition INDIA bloc has fielded Congress MP K Suresh for the post. Both the candidates yesterday filed their nominations for the Speaker’s post for which the election is scheduled to be held today.

Despite hectic parley, the government and Opposition failed to reach a consensus for the post. Parliamentary Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju said, Lok Sabha speaker should be elected unanimously and unopposed. He said, the government talked to the floor leaders of the opposition parties to build consensus on the name of Lok Sabha speaker. He said, Congress led opposition have come up with conditions and demanded the Deputy Speaker post.

Talking to media outside Parliament yesterday, Union Minister Piyush Goyal said senior Union Ministers Rajnath Singh, Amit Shah and J P Nadda met with Congress leader KC Venugopal and DMK MP TR Baalu to build consensus on the selection of Lok Sabha Speaker.

Talking to media outside the Parliament, Congress MP Rahul Gandhi said that the Opposition is ready to extend support to the NDA nominee for the speaker post provided that the Deputy Speaker post must be given to the opposition.

Congress MP Rahul Gandhi will be the Leader of Opposition, LoP in the Lok Sabha. Party General Secretary KC Venugopal informed this to media in New Delhi. He said Congress Parliamentary Party chairperson Sonia Gandhi has written to Pro-tem Speaker Bhartruhari Mehtab informing that Rahul Gandhi will be the Leader of Opposition in the House.

Meanwhile, a meeting of the Floor leaders of INDIA alliance was also held in the residence of Congress President Mallikarjun Kharge yesterday.

External Affairs Minister Dr S Jaishankar has said, Africa will continue to be at the top of India’s priorities. Addressing the Africa Day celebrations in New Delhi yesterday, Dr Jaishankar said, India’s partnership with Africa is beyond strategic concerns and economic benefits. He stated that Prime Minister Narendra Modi redefined India’s engagement in Africa by outlining its 10 guiding principles.

On the trade and economic front, India is the fourth largest trading partner for Africa with a bilateral trade of about 100 billion dollars and cumulative investments of more than 75 billion dollars.

Union Minister Dr. Jitendra Singh has expressed confidence that India’s Nuclear Power generation capacity will increase by around 70 percent in the next five years. He said this while convening a high-level meeting to review the 100 days Action Plan of the Department of Atomic Energy in New Delhi. Dr Singh highlighted India’s progress in nuclear energy and renewable energy in the energy landscape. He also took stock of the already operational projects and gave directions for upcoming plans.

Another accused in the NEET-2024 exam scam has been arrested by Latur police. The name of the accused is Sanjay Jadhav. Two out of four accused have been arrested so far. A case has been registered against a total of four people in the Shivajinagar police station at Latur. Jaleel Khan Pathan, a teacher at Latur Zilla Parishad School, was arrested on June 23rd.

The exam admit cards of 12 students who appeared in the NEET exam have been found in the mobile phones of the accused Sanjay Jadhav and Jaleel Khan Pathan. Meanwhile, accused Sanjay Jadhav was produced before the court yesterday and he has been remanded police custody till 2nd July.

On the recent steps taken by the centre in connection with NEET-UG examination, Dr. Neeraj Gupta Gupta, Vice Chancellor Gujarat University said that steps taken by the Central Government this time will improve of the examinations pattern in the future.

Chancellor of Bareilly International University, Keshav Kumar Aggarwal appealed to the students to be patient and said that the Government is taking appropriate decisions in the interest of the students.

In Madhya Pradesh, the ministers of the state would bear the expenses of income tax and would not take any financial assistance from the government. Chief Minister Dr. Mohan Yadav has said that now, there will be no financial burden on the state.

The Chief Minister further said that it was also decided that out of the assistance given in case of martyrdom of any soldier of the state, 50 percent of the total amount would be given to the wife and the remaining 50 percent to the parents of the martyr.

The Supreme Court is scheduled to hear Delhi Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal’s plea today challenging a Delhi High Court order which paused the bail granted to him by a lower court.

On June 20th, a Delhi court granted bail to Kejriwal, who was arrested on March 21 by the Enforcement Directorate (ED), and ordered his release on a personal bond of 1 lakh rupees. However, ED moved the Delhi High Court, challenging the bail order.

RBI Governor Shaktikanta Das has said that India is at the threshold of a major structural shift in its growth trajectory and is moving towards 8 percent GDP growth in a sustained manner. Addressing the 188th AGM of Bombay Chamber of Commerce and Industry in Mumbai yesterday, Mr. Das said, India’s growth story has been and will be a multi-sectoral growth.

Minister of Road Transport and Highways, Nitin Gadkari today said that the Global Navigation Satellite System for toll collection will play a pivotal role, being effective, viable, and environmentally sound. He said this while addressing an International Workshop in New Delhi on Global Navigation Satellite System based electronic toll collection in India.

In Jammu and Kashmir, in a major boost to the pilgrimage to Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Shrine, the much-awaited helicopter service from Jammu to Vaishno Devi via Panchhi Helipad has commenced from yesterday. The service will be available daily, with 04 flights operating throughout the day. The flight will operate only when a minimum of four pilgrims have booked the service. The Shrine Board has come up with two packages for the pilgrims desirous of availing the facility, which are same day return (SDR) package that would cost 35,000 rupees per pilgrim and next day return (NDR) package that would cost 60,000 rupees per pilgrim.

The US Court has accepted Assange’s 62 months prison time in the United Kingdom as his sentence.

The BWF US Open Badminton began in Fort Worth Convention Center, Texas last evening. The tournament is witnessing a watered-down field because the top players from across the globe have opted to focus on the Paris Olympics.

In Women’s doubles, Treesa Jolly and Gayatri Gopichand will clash with P. Hsieh and E. Hung of Chinese Taipei in round of 16 this afternoon.

## Midday News

Senior BJP MP Om Birla was today elected as the Speaker of the 18th Lok Sabha for the second consecutive term. He was elected with voice vote as the opposition did not press for voting in the House for its candidate Congress MP K. Suresh. After his election, Prime Minister Narendra Modi, Leader of Opposition Rahul Gandhi and Parliamentary Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju escorted Mr. Birla to the chair of the Speaker. Earlier, when the House met this morning, Prime Minister Modi and 12 other NDA leaders moved the motions to elect Mr. Birla as the Speaker. From the Opposition side, Shiv Sena (UBT) leader Arvind Sawant, Supriya Sule of NCP (Sharad Pawar) and Anand Bhadauriya of Samajwadi Party moved motions in favour of Mr. Suresh.

In his address, Mr. Om Biral thanked all the members of the House for giving him the opportunity to work as the Speaker of the House again and showing trust in him. He asserted that enriching Parliamentary values and democratic traditions will be his priorities. He said that the 18th Lok Sabha is the world’s largest celebration of democracy as over 64 crore voters participated in the elections with great enthusiasm. Mr. Birla said in the last decade, the expectations, hopes and aspirations of the people have increased and it is the responsibility of the MPs to make collective efforts to fulfil their aspirations in an effective manner. The Speaker  underlined that the path of debate and discussion should be adopted in the house instead of  stalemate. He said the means of protest should be as per the dignity of the House. Mr. Birla added the chair takes stern action only to maintain the dignity and decorum of the House.

Earlier, Congratulating Mr. Birla on his election, the Prime Minister said, history is being made today with the re-election of Mr. Birla for the second time. He said the house looks forward to his guidance in the next five years. He said, Mr. Birla’s works as Parliamentarian should be an inspiration for the new members. He also said, the previous Lok Sabha passed several path-breaking legislations under the Chairmanship of Mr. Birla. Mr. Modi added that the previous Lok Sabha witnessed record productivity of 97 percent under the Chairmanship of Mr. Birla.

Leader of Opposition Rahul Gandhi also congratulated Mr. Birla saying that this House represents the people’s voice. He assured Mr. Birla of the opposition’s support in the smooth functioning of the House. He said, cooperation happens on the basis of trust, and voices of opposition should be allowed to be raised. He added that the Government may have political power, but opposition also represents the voice of the people.

Samajwadi Party leader Akhilesh Yadav expressed hope that the opposition will get ample time to express their voice.

TMC MP Sudhip Bandhopadhya raised the issue of suspension of opposition MPs in the 17th Lok Sabha. He said the legislation should be passed in the House with proper discussion. Shiv Sena (UBT) leader Arvind Sawant hoped that the house would discuss all the major issues of people’s interest in the 18th Lok Sabha. NCP (Sharad Pawar) MP Supriya Sule congratulated Speaker Birla and said that he did a tremendous job as a speaker in his previous tenure. Shrirarn Barne  of Shiv Sena (Shinde group) hoped that Mr Birla would take everyone together in running the house. LJP (R) leader and Union Minister Chirag Paswan said the election is over and now members should focus on work related to people’s affairs. Apna Dal leader and Union Minister Anupriya Patel also lauded Mr Birla for smoothly conducting the house in the 17th Lok Sabha. RJD leader Abhay Sinha, Harsimrat Kaur Badal of Akali Dal, Mithun Reddy of YSRCP, Sunil Tatkare of NCP, Asaduddin Owaisi of AIMIM, N K Premchandran of RSP, Chandrashekhar of Azad Samaj Party and others also expressed their views on the occasion.

Union Home Minister, Amit Shah has congratulated Om Birla  on being re-elected as the Speaker of the Lok Sabha. In a social media post, Mr. Shah said that Mr Birla’s  exemplary commitment to strengthening  parliamentary democracy will reinforce the glory of Indian  Constitution and the values enshrined in it. Mr. Shah  extended his best  wishes to Mr Birla  for his  next tenure.

India has asserted that as the United Nations turns 80 next year, it is high time to reform the Security Council by expanding its permanent and non-permanent categories to enable it to manage today’s global conflicts effectively. Minister in India’s Permanent Mission to the UN, Pratik Mathur said this while delivering India’s statement at the UN General Assembly debate on the Annual Report of the Security Council. He said, since performance assessment has become one of the focus areas at the UN as part of the discussions on global governance reforms, the Security Council, too, needs to prove its credibility and improve its performance.

Mr. Mathur noted that annual reports of the Security Council must inform, highlight and analyse measures it has decided upon or taken to maintain international peace and security during the reporting period.

An angry crowd broke through police lines to storm Parliament in the capital Nairobi, vandalising the interior and setting parts of the complex on fire. The ceremonial mace, symbolising the authority of the legislature, was stolen. To control the mob, Police opened fire, killing at least five people.

In an address last evening, President William Ruto said all means will be deployed to thwart any attempts by dangerous criminals to undermine the security and stability of our country.

Protests against an unpopular finance bill, which includes several tax rises, have been ongoing for days. But they escalated yesterday as MPs passed an amended bill.
