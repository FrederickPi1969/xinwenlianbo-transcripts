## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi says, big fish is facing the law today because of government’s zero-tolerance policy towards corruption. In an interview to a news agency, Mr. Modi said that when the big fish is being caught, questions are being raised by vested interests. Highlighting on the investigations by state machineries, he said that aim of the government is based on ‘zero tolerance’ and ensure evidence based action and not on perception.

Prime Minister said that under the Atmanirbhar Bharat vision, the aim is to promote indigenous talent and increase employability. Mr. Modi said, over 38 lakh crore rupees have been transferred to people through the Direct Benefit Transfer.  The Prime Minister emphasized that ‘nation first’ is at the centre of government’s every decision.

Political parties have intensified campaigning for seventh and last phase of Lok Sabha elections.

Prime Minister and Senior BJP leader Narendra Modi will address an election meeting today at the Dumka Airport Ground in Jharkhand. The meeting is a part of the Vijay Sankalp Yatra. Mr. Modi will address a public rally in favour of BJP candidate Sita Soren from Dumka parliamentary seat. She is the daughter-in-law of Jharkhand Mukti Morcha (JMM) chief Shibu Soren. Sita Soren is pitted against Nalin Soren of JMM.

BJP President JP Nadda will address three election meetings tomorrow in Deoghar, while Senior Congress leader Rahul Gandhi will address election rallies on May 30.

Polling will be held in three constituencies Godda, Dumka and Sahibganj of Santhal region of Jharkhand during the final phase of elections on June 1. The JMM had won their lone Sahibganj seat in 2019 Lok Sabha elections.

Prime Minister will address two rallies in Barasat and Jadavpur Lok Sabha constituencies in West Bengal today. Mr. Modi will also hold a road show in Kolkata this evening. The road show will start from ShyamBazar Five Point Crossing and end at the ancestral house of Swami Vivekananda.

In Bihar, Leaders of NDA and Grand Alliance and other parties are leaving no stone unturned to ensure victory of their candidates.

Senior BJP leader and Chief Minister of Uttar Pradesh Yogi Adityanath will address back-to-back three rallies in Fatuha in Patna Sahib and Digha in Pataliputra Lok Sabha constituencies. He is also scheduled to hold a rally at Barhara under Arrah Lok Sabha constituency.

JD (U) President and Bihar Chief Minister Nitish Kumar will conduct a road show from Sarmera to Nagarnausa in Nalanda Parliamentary constituency  today. He will also hold a public meeting in Asthawan in Nalanda Parliamentary constituency.

Department of Telecommunications and Ministry of Home Affairs has taken action to protect citizens from potential SMS fraud through the Sanchar Saathi initiative. The Ministry of Communications said, it was noted that more than ten thousand fraudulent messages were sent using eight SMS Headers for sending fraudulent communications in the past three months. It said, the Principal Entities which were owner of these eight SMS Headers were blacklisted. ⁠All the 73 SMS Headers and one thousand 522 SMS content templates owned by these Principal Entities have been blacklisted.

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has said that Northwestern and Central India to get respite from heatwave in three days owing to a fresh western disturbance. IMD DG Mrutyunjay Mohapatra said that this weather system is likely to trigger thunderstorm activity in northwest India and bring rain to the western Himalayan region.

IMD has said that conditions are favourable for onset of monsoon over Kerala and North-Eastern states in next five  days. Monsoon has already advanced over South, East, West Central and North East Bay of Bengal along with some parts of Arabian Sea. The Weather department has forecast above normal rainfall over the country as a whole during the monsoon season this year.

Meanwhile, Deep Depression, remnant of Cyclonic Storm Remal over Bangladesh moved east-northeastwards with a speed of 15 kilometer per hour and lay centered over Bangladesh during last night. The system is likely to move east-northeastwards, and gradually weaken into a Depression today. Owing to the conditions, heavy to very heavy rainfall is likely over eastern districts of Sub-Himalayan West Bengal today.

Global investment bank Goldman Sachs has raised India’s GDP growth forecast to 6.7 percent for calendar year 2024. The  latest data released by the bank said, they expect a sustained growth momentum with additional fiscal space on account of a bumper dividend transfer from the RBI. The financial company said that in India, growth momentum remains strong and core inflation is expected to bottom out in April-June. The report also came on a day when the IMD confirmed its forecast of above-average monsoon rains this year which are expected to spur production in the agricultural sector that was hit by erratic weather last year.

The National Investigation Agency (NIA) has arrested five individuals in a major operation against an organized human trafficking and cyber fraud syndicate. The joint operation with state police forces spanned six states and Union Territories, leading to searches at 15 locations yesterday. The arrested individuals, from Vadodara, Gopalganj, southwest Delhi, Gurugram, and Chandigarh, were part of a network that lured Indian youth abroad with false job promises. Once overseas, the victims were forced into illegal activities in fake call centers in places like Laos and Cambodia.

Today is Menstrual Hygiene Day, a global movement for shattering stigmas and advocating for accessible menstrual hygiene management for all. This year’s theme, “Period Friendly World” emphasizes the collective effort required to ensure everyone who menstruates has the resources and knowledge to manage their periods with dignity. The day was initiated by German-based NGO-WASH United in 2013 to address the staggering reality that millions around the world lack access to safe and affordable menstrual products, sanitation facilities, and proper hygiene education.

Union Health Secretary Apurva Chandra has said that the India’s core values and ethos of “Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam” aligns with the World Health Assembly’s (WHA) theme of “All for Health, Health for All”. Addressing the Committee A of World Health Assembly at Geneva in Switzerland, Mr Chandra said that India embodied the spirit of “One World, One family” as it helped the world during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## Evening News

Campaigning for seventh and final phase of Lok Sabha elections reaches its peak with only three days left for polling. Voting in this phase will be held for 57 Parliamentary Constituencies across seven states and a Union Territory on the 1st of June. A total of 904 candidates are in the fray in this phase.

Prime Minister and senior BJP leader Narendra Modi addressed a public meeting in North 24 Parganas district in support of Barasat and Basirhat Lok Sabha constituencies of West Bengal today. Speaking on the occassion, Mr Modi said, there is zero tolerance for corruption under his rule.

The Prime Minister said, Congress and I.N.D.I.A. block do not care for common people and they only cares for themselves. He said people of Matua community in West Bengal are being given citizenship under CAA. Mr. Modi said, women are being empowered under his government.

Normal life has come to a grinding halt in Northeast India due to extremely heavy rainfall under the impact of Cyclone Remal.

The Cyclone wreaked havoc in parts of the Northeast today, killing at least 23 people in Mizoram, three persons in Assam and one in Meghalaya. The rains, accompanied by gusty winds, uprooted trees, electric poles, triggered landslides and disrupted power and internet services in the region.

In Mizoram, at least 23 people were killed in a massive landslide triggered by incessant rain after which a stone quarry collapsed at a place between Melthum and Hlimen on the outskirts of the state capital Aizawl. Nine persons were reportedly missing after the incident. Mizoram Chief Minister Lalduhoma announced an ex-gratia of four lakh rupees each for the next of kin of those who lost their lives.

President Droupadi Murmu and Vice President Jagdeep Dhankar have expressed grief at the loss of lives in Mizoram.

In Assam, three persons were killed while 17 others were injured. The deaths occurred in Kamrup, Kamrup (Metro) and Morigaon districts. Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma expressed grief over the loss of lives. He directed the Chief Secretary to ensure all necessary assistance is provided to the affected families and timely treatment for the injured.

In Meghalaya, a wall collapsed in East Jaintia Hills district killing one person.
