Have you ever thought how difficult it can be for senior citizens and people with disabilities to go to the voting booth and cast their votes? Standing in long lines and dealing with hot weather can be really tough. So, what are the options available to them? In a step towards ensuring an inclusive democracy, the Election Commission of India has unveiled the ‘Vote from Home’ initiative for senior citizens and Persons with Disability.

With an estimated 82 lakh senior citizens and over 88 lakh Persons with Disability gearing up to cast their votes, the ‘Vote from Home’ ensures that no citizen is left behind in the electoral process. The ‘Vote from Home’ provision is available to voters above 85 years and Persons with Disability with a benchmark disability of 40 percent or more. A dedicated polling team, comprising two election commission officials, a videographer, and security personnel, will visit eligible voters’ homes to facilitate the voting process. In tandem with this initiative, the Election Commission is promoting the Saksham App, designed to empower person with disability in the electoral process. The user-friendly app offers features such as voter registration assistance, polling station location services, and voting guidance. With ‘Vote from Home’ and the Saksham App, every voter can contribute to the democratic process.

The External Affairs Ministry has said, India is distressed at the escalating tensions in West Asia and their potential to fuel further violence and instability. Replying to a query during media briefing in New Delhi this afternoon, Ministry spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal said, India has noted with concern the Israeli attack on Iranian diplomatic premises in Syria on the first of April.

Mr. Jaiswal said, the first batch of people under the G2G agreement has gone to Israel. He stressed that India is conscious of their safety and has urged Israeli authorities to ensure their safety and wellbeing.

Replying to a question on China claiming Arunachal Pradesh, the spokesperson said, Arunachal Pradesh is an inalienable part of India and will remain so.

On India-Bangladesh ties, Mr Jaiswal said bilateral relations are very strong. He said both nations have a very comprehensive partnership that spans across sectors.

On the two Indians who were reported missing after the Taiwan earthquake, Mr Jaiswal said they are now safe and contact has been established with them.
