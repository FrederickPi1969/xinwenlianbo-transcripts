Rashtrapati Bhavan will auction selected gift items next month, presented to the President and former Presidents on various occasions, through an online portal called E-Upahaar. India’s foreign exchange (Forex) reserves rose to all time high of 670.86 billion dollars for the third consecutive week as on 19th of July. According to the data released by RBI, the reserves surged by 4 billion dollars during the week. In the preceding two weeks, the Forex reserves rose by a cumulative 14.9 billion dollars. India’s Current Account Deficit declined to 23.2 billion dollars or point 7 per cent of the GDP during 2023-24.

India and the United States have signed a Cultural Property Agreement to repatriate stolen Indian artifacts from the US. Under the agreement, both countries will work together to also prevent and prohibit illicit trade, import, export and trafficking of archaeological and ethnological materials. This is India’s first-ever Cultural Property Agreement with the US.

Addressing a press conference in New Delhi, Culture and Tourism Minister Gajendra Singh Shekhawat termed the signing as historic and said that it will strengthen the partnership between the two nation, fostering inter-cultural growth.

Meanwhile, the US’s Ambassador to India, Eric Garcetti said that this is an important step to give India its due share on becoming the US’s 30th bilateral Cultural Property Agreement partner.

US Vice President Kamala Harris has officially declared her candidacy for the Presidential elections by signing the necessary forms. In a social media post, Ms Harris reiterated that she would work hard to earn every vote.

The US presidential election to be held on 5th of November will see Ms Harris as the Democratic nominee, following US President Joe Biden’s endorsement. Former US President Barack Obama also publicly endorsed Ms Harris for the US presidential post after Joe Biden exited the race recently.
