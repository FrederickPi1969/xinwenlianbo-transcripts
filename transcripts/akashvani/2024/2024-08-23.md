## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi wrapped up his historic visit to Poland after holding meetings with his Polish counterpart Donald Tusk, and President Andrzej Sebastian Duda in Warsaw yesterday. Mr Modi described his visit to Poland as a special one, and called the central European nation India’s valued friend. In a social media post, Prime Minister Modi said, this visit gave an opportunity to deepen cooperation with a valued friend. He said, our friendship can certainly contribute to a better planet. Mr Modi also thanked the Polish people and Government for their warmth.

Addressing the media after holding talks with his Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk at the Federal Chancellery, PM Modi highlighted that it was a significant day for India-Poland relations as the last visit of an Indian Prime Minister to Poland took place in 1979. Mr Tusk, on the other hand, termed it as a great privilege to host Prime Minister Modi on the eve of the 70th anniversary of the establishment of diplomatic relations between the two countries. PM Modi also invited companies from Poland to join the ‘Make in India, Make for the World’ initiative.

Both countries decided to transform the bilateral relationship into a strategic partnership during his landmark Warsaw visit. Prime Minister Modi and Polish President Andrzej Sebastian Duda held discussions on deepening the India-Poland partnership across a wide range of sectors, including food processing, Artificial Intelligence, energy and infrastructure.

The Prime Minister also met with a group of prominent Polish Indologists including Prof. Maria Christopher Byrski, Prof. Monika Browarczyk and Prof. Przemyslaw Szurek.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi also met the President of the Kabaddi Federation of Poland, Michal Spiczko and another board member of the federation Anna Kalbarczyk in Warsaw yesterday. During the meeting, Prime Minister Modi discussed on how to further popularise Kabaddi in Poland, including ensuring more tournaments between Indian and Polish players.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will arrive in Kyiv today on a day-long visit to Ukraine. Mr Modi will hold one-on-one and delegation-level talks with President Volodymyr Zelenskyy and the conversation is expected to largely focus on finding ways to resolve the conflict in Ukraine. Ahead of his much-anticipated visit to the conflict-torn country, the Prime Minister said, India firmly believes that no problem can be solved on the battlefield and it is ready to extend all possible cooperation for the restoration of peace and stability in the region.

After concluding his trip to Warsaw, Prime Minister Modi is travelling to the Ukrainian capital Kyiv onboard a train under tight security and the journey will take around 10 hours. Mr Modi’s visit to Ukraine comes six weeks after he travelled to Moscow.

This will be the first visit by an Indian Prime Minister to Ukraine after the establishment of diplomatic relations between the two countries in 1992. Mr Modi and Mr  Zelenskyy will hold discussions on the entire gamut of bilateral relations, ranging from economic and business linkages, agriculture, infrastructure, health and education, pharmaceuticals, defense, culture, and people-to-people ties. This visit is taking place against the backdrop of the ongoing conflict in Ukraine, which will also form part of the discussions. India has consistently advocated for diplomacy and dialogue to reach a negotiated settlement. Mr Modi’s landmark visit to Ukraine will help further consolidation and expansion of bilateral ties.With Anupam Mishra , this is Anand Kumar for Akashvani News.

The United Nations Secretary-General has expressed hope that Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s visit to Ukraine will contribute to ending the war, his Spokesperson Stephane Dujarric said. Asked for a reaction to Prime Minister Modi’s visit to Ukraine amid the war, Dujarric said, we have seen a lot of heads of state and government travel to the region and we hope that all of these visits will bring us closer to an end of the conflict in line with the General Assembly resolutions, international law, and the territorial integrity.

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh arrived in Washington yesterday evening on a four-day visit to the United States at the invitation of the US Secretary of Defence Lloyd Austin. During the visit, Mr Singh will hold a bilateral meeting with his US counterpart and also meet the US National Security Advisor Jake Sullivan. The Defence Minister will also chair a high-level round table meeting with the US defence industry on the ongoing and future defence collaborations. India and the United States have hailed the progress on bilateral defence initiatives following the fifth annual India-US 2+2 Ministerial Dialogue in November 2023.

India and the United States have entered into a bilateral, non-binding Security of Supply Arrangement that will enable both the countries to acquire the industrial resources they need from one another to resolve unanticipated supply chain disruptions to meet national security needs, the Pentagon said in a statement yesterday. Through the System of Systems Analysis, the US and India agree to provide reciprocal priority support for goods and services that promote the national defence, the US Department of Defense said in a statement.

Union Minister of Civil Aviation Rammohan Naidu has launched the guidelines for seaplane operation under Regional Connectivity Scheme -UDAN. The guidelines prioritize the safety and security of seaplane operations and clearly outline the responsibilities of each stakeholder, from the operators to the regulatory bodies. The guidelines ensure seamless and efficient rollout of seaplane operations across the nation. Speaking on the occasion in New Delhi, Mr. Naidu said that the guidelines aim to connect remote areas of the country and make travel more accessible for all. He added that after the success of RCS-UDAN, the government is considering to propose RCS-UDAN 2.0 for the next 10 years.

The Union Minister said that the integration of seaplane into the existing aviation system will provide last-mile connectivity to the isolated regions.

The first National Space Day is being celebrated across the country today. President Droupadi Murmu will inaugurate the main function, which will be held at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi. The theme for this year’s celebration is Touching Lives while Touching the Moon: India’s Space Saga, which emphasizes the profound impact of space exploration on society and technology. On the occasion of National Space Day, the President will honour the winners of Robotics Challenge and Bhartiya Antriksh  Hackathon.

On this day last year, Chandrayaan-3 Mission accomplished safe and soft landing of the Vikram Lander on the lunar surface. With this achievement, India has joined the elite group of Spacefaring nations becoming only the fourth nation to land on the surface, and the first nation to land near the south pole of the Moon. The soft landing was followed by a successful deployment of the Pragyaan Rover. The landing site was named as Shiv Shakti Point and 23rd August was declared as the National Space Day. The National Space Day recognizes significant achievements in space exploration and highlights advancements in space technologies. India’s foray into space research started in the early 1960’s and progressed steadily by developing niche technologies PALLAVI PARMAR FROM AKASHVANI NEWS DELHI.

Vice-President Jagdeep Dhankhar will visit Ahmedabad & Gandhinagar in Gujarat today. During his day-long visit, the Vice-President will address the students and faculty members at the National Forensic Sciences University in Gandhinagar. Later in the day, Mr. Dhankhar will attend the Dharma Dhamma International Conference at Gujarat University in Ahmedabad. Gujarat governor Acharya Devvrat and Chief Minister Bhupendra Patel will also present on the occasion.

Paris Olympics silver medallist Neeraj Chopra finished second in the men’s javelin throw event at the Lausanne Diamond League 2024 competition in Switzerland last night. The star Indian athlete produced his season-best throw of 89.49 metre to finish second behind Grenadian ace, Peters Anderson who reached 90.61 metre throw  in his final attempt.  Anderson, a bronze medalist at the 2024 Paris Games, dominated the event from the start and finished with a record throw. Germany’s Julian Weber finished third with his best throw of 87.08 metre.

In wrestling, India’s four women wrestlers clinched gold medal, yesterday, at Under-17 World Championships in Amman, Jordan and at least two more are in gold medal contention. India, with back to back medals, will be favorites to win the women’s wrestling team title.

Aditi Kumari in 43kg, Neha in 57kg and Pulkit in 65kg and Mansi Lather in 73kg won world titles in their respective categories with remarkable ease. Kajal in 69kg and Shrutika Shivaji Patil in 46kg will compete in the gold medal bouts today.

Khelo India will organise a webinar today to help the athletes downloading their certificates from DigiLocker. The exclusive webinar by Khelo Bharat titled “How to download Khelo India certificate from DigiLocker” will begin at 3:00 PM.

## Midday News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi reached Kyiv today on a day-long visit to Ukraine after completing his historic visit to Poland. Prime Minister Modi reached the Ukrainian capital onboard a train, taking a journey of around 10 hours under tight security. The Indian community accorded a very warm welcome to Mr. Modi on his arrival. Later, Prime Minister Modi paid floral tributes at the statue of Mahatma Gandhi in Kyiv.

During his visit to Ukraine, Mr Modi will hold one-on-one and delegation-level talks with President Volodymyr Zelenskyy. The two leaders will hold discussions on the entire gamut of bilateral relations, ranging from economic and business linkages, agriculture, infrastructure, health and education, pharmaceuticals, defense, culture, and people-to-people ties. The visit is taking place against the backdrop of the ongoing conflict in Ukraine, which will also form part of the discussions.

Ahead of his visit to the conflict-torn country, the Prime Minister had said, India firmly believes that no problem can be solved on the battlefield and it is ready to extend all possible cooperation for the restoration of peace and stability in the region.

This is the first visit by an Indian Prime Minister to Ukraine after the establishment of diplomatic relations between the two countries in 1992.

Earlier, during his two day official visit to Poland, Prime Minister Modi held meetings with his counterpart Donald Tusk, and President Andrzej Sebastian Duda in Warsaw.

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh has said, India and the US are natural allies and are destined to be strong partners. The Minister was addressing the Indian diaspora in Washington on the first day of his official visit to the United States. Mr Singh reached Washington last evening on a four-day visit to the United States at the invitation of the US Secretary of Defence Lloyd Austin.

The Defence Minister said, it is heartwarming to see how the Indian community continues to cherish and uphold their cultural values and maintain their connection to their roots.

After Mr Singh’s arrival, India and the US signed the Security of Supplies Arrangement document (SOSA), and Memorandum of Agreement regarding the Assignment of Liaison Officers. With the signing of SOSA, India and the US agreed to provide priority support for goods and services that promote national defence.

During his visit, Mr Singh will hold a bilateral meeting with his US counterpart Lloyd Austin and National Security Advisor Jake Sullivan.

The country is celebrating its first-ever National Space Day today. On this day last year, Chandrayaan-3 Mission accomplished a safe and soft landing of the Vikram Lander on the lunar surface. With the achievement, India has joined the elite group of Spacefaring nations becoming only the fourth nation to land on the surface, and the first nation to land near the south pole of the Moon. The President, Vice President and Prime Minister greeted fellow citizens on the occasion.

The theme for this year’s celebration is Touching Lives while Touching the Moon: India’s Space Saga, which emphasizes the profound impact of space exploration on society and technology.

Speaking at the main function organized on the occasion at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi this morning, President Droupadi Murmu said the Indian Space Research Organisation ISRO has made important contributions not only to the space sector but also to the socio-economic development of the country.

Minister of State for Space, Dr Jitendra Singh said, the Narendra Modi government has broken the shackles of the past to liberate the Space Sector.

ISRO chairman S Somanath said, there is a lot of enthusiasm among people on National Space Day.

On the occasion, President Murmu presented prizes to the winners of ISRO’s Robotics challenge and Bharatiya Antariksh Hackathon.

In his message on the occasion, Vice President Jagdeep Dhankhar said, the day is a reminder of Bharat’s remarkable journey in the space sector propelled by the spirit of Atmanirbharata. The Vice President conveyed best wishes to the space scientists and hoped that their innovation and vision will propel Bharat to even greater heights and continue to inspire humanity.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi said, the countrymen recall with great pride the nation’s achievements in the space sector. The Prime Minister said, his Government has taken a series of futuristic decisions relating to this sector and will do even more in the times to come.

The government has banned 156 medicines including antibiotics, painkillers, and multivitamins after a review found that they posed health risks. The Health Ministry in a notification said that the 156 fixed dose combination Medicines are irrational combinations. The Ministry said that the use of these drugs are likely to involve risk to human beings whereas safer alternatives are available. It also added that the decision has been taken after the matter was examined by an expert committee appointed by the government and the Drugs Technical Advisory Board.

The Centre has approved the release of 40 crore rupees in advance to Tripura in view of the flood situation in the state. The amount released as the central share from the State Disaster Response Fund is intended to provide relief to the affected people. In a social media post, Union Home Minister Amit Shah said 11 National Disaster Response Force teams, three columns of the Army and four helicopters of the Air Force deployed by the Centre are already assisting the state government in relief and rescue operations.

Meanwhile, Tripura Chief Minister Dr. Manik Saha undertook the aerial survey of the flood affected areas in the state today. The rescue operations are underway in flood-hit Gomati and South Tripura districts.

“Reciprocating to the Centre’s financial assistance, Chief Minister Manik Saha has expressed gratitude to Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Home Minister Amit Shah for the much needed support in the time of grief due to floods. The CM said the constant support from the Centre is helping and motivating Tripura to firmly fight in this tough situation. Meanwhile, the torrential rains caused damage to a big stretch on the Assam-Agartala NH-8 at Atharamura hills, prompting the Khowai district magistrate to stop vehicular movement. The National Highway authorities who visited the affected site today, have suspected an earthquake effect in the area coupled with heavy rains. Kunal Shinde, Akashvani News, Agartala.”

In Uttarakhand, four Nepalese workers were killed in a landslide amid heavy rain near Fata in Rudraprayag district. The State Disaster Response Force has reached the spot and started a rescue operation.

The Badrinath National Highway in Chamoli district is blocked for movement of vehicles due to landslides at various places.

Meanwhile, the Met Department has issued an orange alert for heavy rainfall at some places in Dehradun and Bageshwar district today.

The Supreme Court today adjourned the hearing of Delhi Chief Minister and Aam Aadmi Party National Convenor Arvind Kejriwal’s pleas challenging his arrest and seeking bail in the case registered by the CBI over the alleged Delhi Liquor Policy scam. The hearing has been adjourned till 5th of September after the CBI sought time to file a counter-affidavit in the matter.

In response to media queries, MEA Spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal said, the Ministry has seen reports regarding the Guru Granth Sahib seized by the Qatari authorities and the demand of its release by the Sikh community. The Spokesperson said, India will continue to follow up the matter with Qatar authorities with high priority and hope for early resolution.

Several people are feared dead as an Indian-registered bus plunged into the Marsyangdi river in Nepal.

“An Indian number plate bus plying on the Prithviraj highway fell into the Marsyangdi River near Ambukhaireni in Tanahun district of Gandaki Province. Deputy Superintendent of Police of Tanahun, Deepak Kumar Rai, informed that the UP number plate bus, which was going to Kathmandu from Pokhara, fell into the river. According to him, there were Indian tourists on the bus. Now the police team is reaching the spot and rescuing the passengers. According to sources, 16 injured individuals have been rescued so far. However, the total number of passengers has not been ascertained yet. The passengers were returning to Kathmandu early this morning after staying at Majheri Resort in Pokhara. Sweta Singh, Akashvani News, Kathmandu.”

The Education Ministry has directed all states and union territories to implement the Guidelines on School Safety and Security-2021. The guidelines were developed by the Ministry’s Department of School Education and Literacy in alignment with POCSO guidelines. They contain provisions for fixing the accountability of school management regarding the safety and security of children studying in Government, Government-aided, and Private Schools. Additionally, the guidelines provide measures for preventive education, accountability of various stakeholders, reporting procedures, concerned legal provisions, support and counseling, and a safe environment.
