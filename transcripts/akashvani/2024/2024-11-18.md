19th G20 Summit got underway at the Museum of Modern Art in the Brazilian city of Rio de Janeiro this evening with focus on fight against hunger, poverty and inequality.

Several world leaders and representatives of international organizations are participating in various sessions and deliberating on the priority areas of this year’s declaration. The theme of G20 summit this year is “Building a just and sustainable world”.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi took part in in the plenary session of the Summit, on “Social Inclusion and the Fight Against Hunger and Poverty”. Speaking on the occasion, Mr Modi said, One Earth One Family One Future is as relevant at G20 Brazil summit as it was in the Summit held in Delhi last year. He said the people centric decisions taken at the G20 Summit in New Delhi have been taken forward during Brazil’s Presidency. He called it a matter of satisfaction that G20 prioritised SDG goals and focused on inclusive development, women led development, and youth power. He extended India’s support for Brazil’s initiative for a “Global Alliance against hunger and poverty”.

The Prime Minister stressed that countries of the Global south are most adversely impacted by the food, fuel and fertilizer crisis caused by global conflicts. He pointed out that discussions can only be successful when the challenges and priorities of the Global South are kept in mind.

Earlier, Prime Minister Modi was received by Brazilian President Lula da Silva at the venue.

On the sidelines of the Summit, Prime Minister Modi interacted with several world leaders including US President Joe Biden, Singapore Prime Minister Lawrence Wong and UN Secretary General António Guterres. Later tonight, Prime Minister is scheduled to hold more bilateral meetings.

Prime Minister Modi reached Rio de Janeiro this morning on the second leg of his three-nation tour after concluding his visit to Nigeria.

In the third and final leg, Prime Minister Modi will undertake a state visit to Guyana from 19th to 21st of this month.
