## Midday News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has stressed that the India-China border situation needs to be addressed urgently to put the abnormalities in ties behind. He said, stable and peaceful relations between India and China are important for not just the two countries but the entire region and world.

In an interview to a US magazine, the Prime Minister expressed hope that through positive and constructive bilateral engagement at the diplomatic and military levels, both the countries will be able to restore and sustain peace and tranquility at the borders.

Speaking on the Indian democracy, the Prime Minister said, constantly increasing voter participation in the country is a big certificate for the people’s faith in democracy. He said, as the largest democracy in the world, over 600 million people voted in the General Elections in 2019. He said, over 970 million eligible voters will exercise their franchise in the country in this General Election.

Prime Minister and senior BJP leader Narendra Modi has said that the NDA government at the Centre has made the country much stronger during the past ten years. Addressing an election rally in Rishikesh, Mr Modi said, whenever there has been a weak and unstable government in the country, the enemies have taken advantage and terrorism has spread its roots. The Prime Minister said that his government has implemented one rank one pension scheme for soldiers.

He said, his government at the Centre has also provided made in India bullet proof jackets to the soldiers, saving their lives. The Prime Minister said, today, everything from modern rifles to fighter planes and aircraft carriers are being made in the country itself.

The Prime Minister outlined various government programs aimed at enhancing Uttarakhand’s economic potential and boosting tourism to stimulate job growth. Mr Modi said the BJP government has provided tap water connections to 90 percent families of Uttarakhand.

BJP today released another list of one candidate for the ensuing Lok Sabha polls. The party has fielded Dr. Vinod Kumar Bind from Bhadohi Lok Sabha seat in Uttar Pradesh. Meanwhile, Congress has released a list of two candidates for the ensuing Lok Sabha Elections. Dr Kalyan Kale will contest from Jalna and Dr Shobha Dinesh Bachhav from Dhule, Maharashtra.

In a separate development, Former Congress leader from Gujarat, Rohan Gupta joined the BJP at party headquarters in New Delhi today. He joined the party in the presence of BJP General Secretary Vinod Tawde and party leader Hardeep Puri. Mr Gupta had resigned from Congress on the 22nd of last month.

Imagine a country which just got its independence and is still grappling with the aftershocks of imperialism and partition, what would the first general elections have looked like in such an environment. Let us reel back to the dawn of democracy. A report:

Since the inaugural general elections in 1951-52, India has witnessed a transformative journey in its democratic process. Spearheaded by Sukumar Sen, appointed as the first Chief Election Commissioner, and backed by the Representation of the People Act, the pioneering election laid the groundwork for India’s democratic ethos. The election also known as India’s first democratic leap started on 25th October 1951 and concluded on 21st February 1952. Despite formidable challenges, including logistical hurdles and a vast electorate, the nation navigated through 68 phases of polling across nearly two lakh polling stations, with over 27 thousand designated for women. A total of 53 parties and 533 Independent candidates contested for the 489 seats in the elections. At the time, India’s population was pegged at nearly 36 crore, out of which 17.32 crore were eligible to vote. The inaugural vote cast in Chini, Himachal Pradesh, symbolized a historic shift towards inclusive governance. With a voter turnout of 45.7 percent, the election marked a resounding triumph for democratic ideals. The Indian National Congress emerged as victorious while the Communist Party and the Socialist Party finished second and third respectively, underscoring the nation’s commitment to pluralism. ARPITA CHOWDHURY, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.

In Uttar Pradesh, voting is to be held on eight seats in the second phase of Lok Sabha elections. This also includes the important seat of the religious city of Mathura. In this seat, the BJP has given a ticket to Hema Malini, who has been MP for two consecutive terms. Where as SP-Congress alliance declared Mukesh Dhangar as its candidate and BSP has fielded Suresh Singh. Apart from these, 12 independent candidates are also contesting the election. The notification for the third phase of Lok Sabha election will be issued tomorrow. Elections will be held in 94 Lok Sabha seats in 10 States and two Union Territories in this phase. All 26 seats of Gujarat, 14 seats of Karnataka, 11 seats of Maharashtra, 10 seats of Uttar Pradesh, Eight seats of Madhya Pradesh, Seven seats of Chhattisgarh, Five seats of Bihar, Four seats each in Assam and West Bengal, two seats each in Goa and Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu and one seat in Jammu and Kashmir will go to polls in the third phase.

Notification for the adjourned poll in Betul constituency of Madhya Pradesh will also be issued tomorrow. Election for Betul Parliamentary constituency which was to be held in the 2nd Phase was adjourned due to the death of a contesting candidate of Bahujan Samaj Party.

Nominations can be filed till the 19th of this month. Voting for this phase will be held on 7th May.

In Jammu and Kashmir, one terrorist was killed in an encounter with security forces in Pulwama district today. Police said, security forces launched a cordon and search operation at Frassipora village in Rajpora area following inputs about the presence of terrorists there. The police said, the search operation turned into an encounter after the terrorists fired upon the forces. The identity and group affiliation of the slain terrorist is being ascertained. Arms and ammunition and some incriminating material have been recovered from the site.

The National Investigation Agency (NIA) has arrested a person in a case relating to revival attempts by People’s Liberation Front of India (PLFI). According to the NIA, raids and searches were conducted yesterday by its teams, with the help of local police, in Jharkhand and Assam. The crackdown led to the arrest of Binod Munda from Khunti district of Jharkhand. The accused is an armed cadre of PLFI, a splinter Naxal group, and wanted in four PFLI cases of Jharkhand.

Eight children died and several others were injured when a school bus overturned in Haryana’s Mahendragarh district today. The accident took place near Unhani village. Police said that the bus driver has been arrested. Education minister of Haryana Seema Trikha visited the hospital in Mahendergarh, where 12 children are undergoing treatment. President Droupadi Murmu has expressed grief over the accident.

Eid-ul-Fitr is being celebrated with religious fervour and gaiety across the country today. The festival marks the culmination of the fasting month of Ramzan. The faithfuls dressed in their best offered Eid prayers in Eidgahs and grand mosques. Later they exchanged Eid greetings. In Delhi, the main Eid congregations were held at Jama Masjid, Fatehpuri Masjid and Shahi  Eidgah. Elaborate security arrangements have been made for the occasion.

President, Vice President and Prime Minister have greeted the people on the occasion. In her message, President Droupadi Murmu said, the festival marks the culmination of the period of fasting and prayer during the holy month of Ramzan and gives the message of love and brotherhood. She said, this festival promotes unity, forgiveness and charity.

Vice President Jagdeep Dhankhar said that Eid-ul_Fitr reminds us of the values of compassion, generosity, and unity that lie at the heart of the festival, and resonate with the ethos of our diverse nation. He prayed that Eid may bring joy, fulfillment, and abundant blessings to the lives of all.

In his message, Prime Minister Narendra Modi prayed that this occasion may further spread the spirit of compassion, togetherness and peace.

The weather agency has also predicted rainfall and snowfall over Jammu and Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh and Uttarakhand till tomorrow. Rainfall and snowfall with thunderstorm and lightning will continue during the next 6 days in North east India.
