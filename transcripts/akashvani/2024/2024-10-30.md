The city of Lord Ram, Ayodhya, is ready to witness the grandest-ever Deepotsav today to celebrate the first Diwali after the consecration of Ramlala at the newly constructed Ram Temple. The Uttar Pradesh government will aim for the Guinness World Record by lighting more than 25 lakh diyas on Deepotsav on the ghats of Ayodhya, including the Ram Ki Paidi. Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath, Union Minister Gajendra Singh Shekhawat and state Ministers will be present in the event.

A total of 28 lakh lamps have been arranged at the Ghats of Holy Saryu by the 30 thousand volunteers to make a new world record. A team of 30 people led by Guinness World Records team consultant and the counting committee members of the university have already counted the lamps decorated on 55 ghats. In this year’s Deepotsav, another world record will be attempted. It will be of 1,100 people performing the largest aarti together on the ghats of Saryu. Apart from this, special eco-friendly lamps will illuminate the Ram temple. These lamps are designed to prevent stains from affecting the temple’s structure and will remain lit for extended periods. Preparations were also finalized for the Shobha Yatra, which will take place on the Ram Path today. The Shobha Yatra has 18 tableaus, which are based on the seven chapters of the Ramayana. Artists from six countries and 16 states will participate in cultural programs. The administration is trying to involve everyone directly or indirectly in this Deepotsav, so the facility of Ek Diya Ram Ke Naam is also being provided through the Divya Ayodhya app. Om Awasthi, Akashvani News Lucknow.

In Madhya Pradesh, a unique temple of Goddess Mahalaxmi situated in Ratlam city of the state is decorated with the money and jewellery of local people every year on Diwali. This year. the temple has been decorated with around 4.75 crores rupees.

Ratlam’s Mahalaxmi temple is the only temple of its kind in the country. In this temple, local people donate their savings and jewellery to decorate the temple during the festival of lights.The decoration continues from Dhanteras to Bhaidooj. After that, this material is returned to the respective families. This Diwali, cash worth about 1 crore 47 lakh rupees and gold and silver jewellery worth more than 3 crores have been received for decoration of the temple.It is said that this tradition of offering properties to God, which has been going on since the royal era, is still being followed by locals.For the security of the temple, apart from private guards, police are deployed in the temple premises 24 hours a day. Apart from this, the temple is also being monitored through CCTV. With Aditi Mishra from Ratlam, Sanjeev Sharma, Akashvani News, Bhopal.
