## Morning News

Campaigning for seventh and final phase of Lok Sabha elections will end today. Leaders of various political parties are intensifying the last minute rallies. Voting will be held on Saturday in 57 Parliamentary Constituencies spread over seven states and the Union Territory of Chandigarh. A total of 904 candidates are in the fray in this phase. Thirteen seats each in Uttar Pradesh and Punjab, 9 in West Bengal, 8 seats in Bihar, 6 in Odisha, 4 in Himachal Pradesh, 3 in Jharkhand and a Union Territory of Chandigarh are going to polls on 1st June. Simultaneously, 42 Assembly seats in Odisha will also go to polls. Counting of votes will be taken up on fourth of June.

Prime Minister and senior BJP leader Narendra Modi addressed rallies in West Bengal and Odisha yesterday. In West Bengal, the PM addressed a rally in Mathurapur. He said, everyone looks at India with respect today and this has been made possible because people voted to power a BJP led NDA government at the Centre. Addressing a rally in Balasore, Odisha, the Prime Minister said, after witnessing the joy and excitement among the voters in West Bengal, Jharkhand and Odisha, he is certain that a ‘Majboot Modi Sarkar’ will be formed for the third time at the Centre.

On the last day of  campaigning for Lok Sabha elections, Mr Modi will address a party rally in Hoshiarpur town of Punjab today. Tight security arrangements have been made for this rally.  Besides it the BJP National President, Mr.JP Nadda is also scheduled to address rallies in Amritsar, Faridkot and Roopnagar in favour of party candidates.

On the other hand Senior Aam Aadmi Party leader and state Chief Minister Shri Bhagwant Singh Maan is scheduled to hold road shows in various districts.

Meanwhile Shiromani Akali Dal President Sukhbir Singh Badal campaigned for party candidate in Amritsar last evening.

Today is the last day for the campaigning for 7th and final phase of elections in Uttar Pradesh. Political parties are using all their might to woo the voters.

“The high decibel poll cacophony will fall silent as the hectic campaign for the seventh and final phase of elections on 13 loksabha seats in Uttar Pradesh will end this evening.

Defence Minister and senior BJP leader Rajnath Singh will campaign in Kushinagar, Robertsganj and Chandauli constituencies. While, Chief Minister of Madhya Pradesh Mohan Yadav will hold road shows in Kushinagar and Sonbhadra constituencies. On the other hand, Samajwadi Party Chief Akhilesh Yadav will campaign in Maharajganj and Ghosi constituencies today.  SUSHIL CHANDRA TIWARI AAKASHWANI NEWS LUCKNOW.”

In Jharkhand leaders of various parties are making last ditch effort to woo voters in favour of their party candidates. Visit of Senior Congress leader Rahul Gandhi to Dumka has been cancelled due to unavoidable reasons. Jharkhand BJP Chief Babulal Marandi will hold rallies in Godda and Sahibganj. Chief Minister Champai Soren will address a series of meeting in Godda, Dumka and Sahibganj in  favour of party candidates.

Leaders of RJD and left parties will also hold rallies and road shows today.

Polling will be held in three constituencies Godda, Dumka and Sahibganj of Santhal region of Jharkhand during the final phase of elections on Saturday.

In Bihar, today is the last day of campaigning in eight parliamentary constituencies under seventh phase of Lok Sabha elections. Patna Sahib, Arrah, Pataliputra, Nalanda, Sasaram, Karakat and Buxar Lok Sabha constituencies will go to the polls on 1st of June. Several senior leaders and star campaigners of NDA and Grand Alliance will make a last-ditch effort today to woo the voters in support of their party candidates.

BJP state president Samrat Choudhary will hold public meetings in poll bound areas.

Deputy Chief Minister Vijay Kumar Sinha will hold door to door campaign in Nalanda, Jehanabad, Pataliputra constituencies. LJP (Ramvilas) party Chief Chirag Paswan will also hold public meetings in support of NDA candidates.

On the other hand, RJD chief Lalu Prasad will hold road show and voters outreach programme in Pataliputra constituency. RJD leader Tejashwi Prasad Yadav will take a whirlwind tour of all eight parliamentary constituencies and address public meetings.

In our series of key constituencies for Lok Sabha polls, today we bring you a report on Mandi Parliamentary Constituency in Himachal Pradesh. This constituency, considered the center of Himachal’s politics, is the second-largest parliamentary constituency in the country by area. The Mandi parliamentary constituency includes 17 assembly segments from six districts of the state: Mandi, Chamba, Kullu, Shimla, Kinnaur, and Lahaul-Spiti.

This time, 10 candidates are contesting in the Mandi parliamentary constituency, although the main battle is expected to be between the Congress and the BJP. Interestingly, the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) has fielded famous film actress Kangana Ranaut, who hails from Mandi district, as its candidate. On the other hand, the Congress has chosen Vikramaditya Singh, son of the late former Chief Minister Virbhadra Singh and current MP from this constituency, Pratibha Singh. BJP candidate Kangana Ranaut is campaigning on the achievements of the Modi government over the past ten years and on Modi ki Guarantee. Meanwhile, Congress candidate Vikramaditya Singh is highlighting the work done during the one and a half years of the state government and the development initiatives undertaken by his late father, who served as the state’s Chief Minister six times.  Ritesh Kapoor, Akashvani News, Shimla.

In a major crackdown, Maharashtra government has suspended two senior doctors of the Sassoon General Hospital, Pune, on various charges, including tampering with the blood samples of the minor accused in the Porsche car crash of May 19. While Dr. Ajay Taware and Dr. Shrihari Halnor have been suspended with immediate effect, the Dean of the hospital Dr. Vinayak Kale has been sent on compulsory leave. The action against these doctors came after a special 3-member medical panel headed by Dr. Pallavi Saple carried out a two-day inquiry into the circumstances leading to the change of blood samples and tampering with the blood report of the accused. Both the doctors are currently in police custody till tomorrow.

South West Monsoon is expected to hit Kerala today, two days ahead of the normal onset date of first June. India Meteorological Department (IMD) said that Monsoon can advance over some parts of North-Eastern states by today. We spoke to senior scientist at IMD, R K Jenamani about the arrival of monsoon.

Weather department also forecast that isolated heavy rainfall is expected over Kerala, Andaman and Nicobar Islands and Mahe during next five days beginning tomorrow. Rainfall is also predicted over Tamil Nadu and South Interior Karnataka on the first and second of June. The Weather agency informed that rainfall is over Bihar, Jharkhand, Gangetic West Bengal, Odisha is likely during the next five days.

IMD has said that the severe heatwave conditions prevailing over Northwest and Central India are likely to subside gradually from today. Talking to Akashwani News, IMD Director General, Mrityunjay Mohapatra said that Northwest India is expected to witness isolated to scattered light rainfall accompanied by thunderstorm and lightning till 2nd of June as the fresh Western Disturbances are likely to affect this region today.

The 4th Round of India-Bangladesh Consular Dialogue was held in New Delhi. Joint Secretary, Consular, Passport and Visa Division, Dr. Aman Puri led the Indian delegation, while the delegation from Bangladesh side was led by Director General (South Asia), Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Rokebul Haque.

The two sides held wide-ranging discussions on mechanisms to strengthen coordination and cooperation on consular issues, visa matters, repatriation, MLAT and Extradition cases.

British parliament was dissolved midnight last night, as the five-week campaign period begun leading up to the general election on July 4. As the clock struck one minute past midnight, all 650 seats of members of parliament (MPs) became vacant, marking the official commencement of the electoral process. The election is expected to bring the Labour Party back to power after 14 years of Conservative rule. Prime Minister Rishi Sunak’s election announcement, which took place amidst a heavy downpour, has been seen by many as a shaky start to the campaign.

Ace Indian shuttler P.V. Sindhu will face Carolina Marin of Spain in the women’s singles pre-quarters of the Singapore Open BWF Super 750 badminton tournament, today. She defeated World No. 21 Line Kjaersfeldt in straight games, 21-12, 22-20 to advance to the round of 16 yesterday.

## Evening News

Campaigning ends for seventh and final phase of Lok Sabha elections. 57 Parliamentary Constituencies across seven states and a Union Territory to go to polls on Saturday.

Campaigning has ended for the seventh and final phase of Lok Sabha Elections. With this, curtains have come down on the poll campaign for the General Elections.

57 Parliamentary Constituencies across seven states and a Union Territory will go to polls in this phase on Saturday. These include 13 seats each in Uttar Pradesh and Punjab, 9 in West Bengal, 8 in Bihar, 6 in Odisha, 4 in Himachal Pradesh, 3 in Jharkhand and one seat in Union Territory of Chandigarh. A total of 904 candidates are in the fray in this phase of Lok Sabha polls.

Campaigning has also concluded for assembly election to 42 seats in Odisha along with byelections to six assembly seats in Himachal Pradesh which will be held on the same day.

Top leaders of various political parties today intensified their efforts with last-minute rallies and roadshows.

“Today, on the last day of the Lok Sabha election campaign, senior Bharatiya Janata Party leader and Prime Minister  Narendra Modi addressed an election rally in Hoshiarpur while BJP National President JP Nadda held road shows and public meetings at Amritsar, Faridkot, and Roopnagar. Meanwhile, senior Congress leader Rahul Gandhi reached Khatkarkalan in Nawanshahr. Aam Aadmi Party convener Arvind Kejriwal held a road show in Sangrur, the home town of Chief Minister Bhagwant Singh Mann. Shiromani Akali Dal President Sukhbir Singh Badal also remained busy with the campaign. District Electoral Officers in all the districts have taken several measures at polling stations under the heatwave management system due to scorching heat conditions. Shishu Sharma Santhal, Akashvani News, Jalandhar“

“These 13 seats of the seventh phase are spread over the Purvanchal region of the state. Keeping this in mind, tight security arrangements have been made. Especially in Sonbhadra district, which shares its borders with – Bihar, Jharkhand, Madhya Pradesh and Chhattisgarh. Apart from this special vigilance is being maintained on the borders with Bihar. SSB jawans are conducting intensive checking on the international border of Gorakhpur and Kushinagar districts with the neighbouring country Nepal. The prestige of many big candidates is at stake which include Prime Minister Narendra Modi, Union Ministers Mahendra Nath Pandey, Pankaj Chaudhary and Anupriya Patel, film star Ravi Kishan, the main opposition candidates Ajay Rai, Afzal Ansari, Sushil Chandra Tiwari, Akashvani News Lucknow.”

“Today, senior BJP leader and Union Minister Nitin Gadkari held a rally in the Karsog region of the Mandi parliamentary constituency. Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath addressed a rally in the Kullu region where senior party leader and former Chief Minister Jairam Thakur was also present. Yogi Adityanath also campaigned in the Hamirpur parliamentary constituency alongside BJP candidate and Union Minister Anurag Thakur. On the other side, senior Congress leader and Party’s national general secretary Priyanka Gandhi Vadra held a roadshow in Solan. Chief Minister Sukhvinder Singh Sukhu also accompanied her. Himachal Pradesh is one of those places where there is a direct contest between the BJP and Congress. Durgesh Bhadauria for Akashvani News, Shimla.”

In Jharkhand, now political parties including the BJP, JMM, Congress, RJD and left parties have started door to door campaign, requesting people to cast their votes in favour of their candidates. Jharkhand Chief Electoral Officer K Ravi Kumar said a number of measures have been taken at polling booths in view of intense heat. Heavy deployment of security forces has been made to ensure free and fair polls.

The Election Commission has refuted the rumours being spread ahead of the final phase of voting for the Lok Sabha polls. Talking to Akashvani, Chief Election Commissioner Rajiv Kumar said that the rumour over increasing the percentage of voter turnout data was spread across the country. Dismissing the claims, Mr Kumar said no one can tamper with voter turnout data in the country as the candidates and agents were provided with the copy of form 17C which has the record of voter turnout.

After wrapping up the Lok Sabha elections campaign, Prime Minister Narendra Modi reached Kanyakumari this evening on a three-day visit to Tamil Nadu. Mr. Modi is meditating at Vivekananda Rock Memorial in Kanyakumari. He will meditate at the Memorial till Saturday. Earlier, Mr. Modi offered prayers at the Bhagavathi Amman Temple at the shore town.

In Jammu and Kashmir, at least 20 people lost their lives while 15 others were injured after a bus carrying pilgrims rolled down into a deep gorge in the Tungi Morh area of Akhnoor sub division in Jammu district this afternoon. Akashvani Jammu Correspondent reports that the injured have been shifted to sub district hospital Akhnoor, from where the critically injured were referred to Government Medical College Hospital in Jammu. The bus was on its way from Jammu to Shiv Khori Cave in Reasi district and was carrying pilgrims mostly from Uttar Pradesh.

President Droupadi Murmu, Vice President Jagdeep Dhankar and Prime Minister Narendra Modi have expressed grief at the loss of lives in the accident in Jammu.

Prime Minister Modi also announced an ex-gratia of two lakh rupees to the next of the kin of each deceased and 50 thousand rupees to the injured.

In Chhattisgarh, security forces have arrested 16 Maoists from different places of Bijapur district. Police claimed that the arrested Maoists were involved in cases of IED blast and digging up of roads to prevent movement of security forces in the area.

Vice President Jagdeep Dhankhar has emphasized the need for farmers to embrace technological advancements to enhance every facet of their agricultural operation. The Vice President was speaking at Govind Ballabh Pant University of Agriculture and Technology in Udham Singh Nagar district of Uttarakhand.

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has projected real GDP growth at 7 per cent for the current financial year, with risks evenly balanced.  In its annual report, the central bank said the Indian economy expanded at a robust pace in fiscal year 2023-2024, with real GDP growth accelerating to 7.6 percent from 7 percent in the previous year. According to the annual report, the outlook for the Indian economy remains bright, underpinned by a sustained strengthening of macroeconomic fundamentals, robust financial and corporate sectors, and a resilient external sector.

The income of the RBI in the financial year 2023-24 rose around 17 percent year-on-year due to significant rise in interest income from foreign securities, as per the central bank’s report.

India has expressed concern over the loss of civilian lives in Rafah, the Palestine city in southern Gaza. Briefing media in New Delhi today, Ministry of External Affairs’ spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal said, the Israeli side has already announced an investigation into the incident.

Speaking on Pakistan’s former Prime Minister Nawaz Sharif’s comments on the Lahore Declaration, Mr. Jaiswal said, an objective view is emerging in Pakistan on this particular matter.

Responding to a query on suspended JD(S) MP Prajwal Revanna, Randhir Jaiswal said, the Ministry of External Affairs has initiated action for revocation of his diplomatic passport.

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has announced the onset of Southwest Monsoon over Kerala and its advancement into most parts of Northeast India today. Monsoon has arrived two days ahead of the normal onset date of first June. Talking to Akashwani News, IMD Director General, Mrityunjay Mohapatra said the Monsoon will cover the remaining parts of South India in the next two to three days.

Heavy rain continues to last Kerala with severe waterlogging being reported in many districts. Orange alert has been issued in Alappuzha, Kottayam and Ernakulam while all other districts remain under yellow alert. The State Disaster Management authorities have issued warning of landslides in hilly regions. Fishermen have been asked not to venture into the sea.

On heatwave, the IMD Director General said, intensity of heatwave conditions is likely to reduce from today.

The flash flood continues to wreak havoc in Manipur. Imphal City has been badly affected by the flood. Most of the roads leading to Imphal town have been submerged. The Raj Bhavan and surrounding areas have also been submerged in the water. The Manipur Police, Army, Assam Rifles, NDRF and SDRF have been pressed into the rescue operations. The State Government announced a two-day holiday for all Government offices. The State Higher and Technical Education also ordered the closure of the higher educational institutes till Saturday.

In Badminton, Indian Women’s Doubles pair of Treesa Jolly and Gayatri Gopichand entered the quarterfinals at the Singapore Open today.

The Commonwealth Games bronze medal list duo defeated World Number two, Baek Ha Na and Lee So Hee of South Korea . The Indian pair will take on Kim So Yeong and Kong Hee Yong of South Korea tomorrow for a place in the semi-finals.

In Boxing, Sachin Siwach has entered the quarterfinals of the World Qualification Tournament in Bangkok, Thailand. Sachin defeated Batuhan Ciftci of Turkey with a unanimous 5-nil victory, in the Men’s 57 kg category.
