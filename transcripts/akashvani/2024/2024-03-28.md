## Morning News

The notification for the second phase of Lok Sabha election will be issued today. 88 Lok Sabha seats in 12 States and Union Territories along with the remaining part of the Outer Manipur Lok Sabha constituency will go to polls in this phase on 26th of next month. ## Midday News

The Centre has notified the revised wages in Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act, MGNREGA for 2024-25. The daily wage rate for unskilled manual workers in Haryana will be 374 rupees per day, 356 rupees in Goa, 349 rupees in Karnataka, 346 rupees in Kerala and 322 rupees in Punjab. The Rural Development Ministry said, this notification will come into force from 1st of April this year. The last revision in MGNREGA wages was notified in March last year.

## Evening News

The External Affairs Ministry has said that the recent remarks by the US State Department on Delhi Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal’s arrest are unwarranted. Addressing media in New Delhi this afternoon, Ministry spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal said, any such external imputation on India’s electoral and legal processes is completely unacceptable.

India had lodged its strong objection and protest with the senior official from the US Embassy yesterday with regard to the comments made by the US State Department.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today alleged that to browbeat and bully others is vintage Congress culture. In a social media post referring to the letter written by a group of lawyers to Chief Justice of India DY Chandrachud expressing concern over attempts to undermine the judiciary’s integrity, Mr Modi said five decades ago, Congress had called for a committed judiciary. He claimed that Congress wants commitment from others for their selfish interests but desists from any commitment towards the nation.

Prominent lawyers including Harish Salve, Manan Kumar Mishra, and Pinky Anand have written to the Chief Justice of India. In the letter, they alleged that a vested interest group is trying to pressure the judiciary, influence judicial process and defame courts on the basis of frivolous logic and stale political agendas. They said their pressure tactics are obvious in political cases, particularly those involving political figures accused of corruption.

A Delhi Court has extended the Enforcement Directorate’s (ED) custody of Delhi Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal till the 1st of April in the money laundering case related to the alleged liquor policy scam case. He was arrested on March 21 by the ED.

Special judge Kaveri Baweja of Rouse Avenue Court passed the remand extension order after Kejriwal was produced in court on the expiry of his Six days of ED custody.

Yesterday, Delhi High Court had declined interim relief to Kejriwal and only issued notice on his plea challenging the arrest and remand.

The External Affairs Ministry has reiterated that Arunachal Pradesh was, is and will remain an integral and inalienable part of India.
