In our series of constituency profile for Lok Sabha elections, today we bring you a report on Balangir parliamentary seat in Odisha.

“In 2024 general elections, Odisha is undergoing both Lok Sabha and Assembly elections simultaneously. In such a situation, there is a lot of political sparkling in Balangir Lok Sabha constituency. If we look at the political history of Balangir parliamentary seat, this constituency was once held by Congress, but later BJP took hold and then this seat came into the account of BJD. In the 8 elections held since 1990, BJP won 4 times, which also includes a hat-trick. Congress and BJD also won 2 times each. In 1991 and 1996, Sarat Patnaik of Congress won, while in 1998, Sangeeta Kumari Singh Deo of Balangir royal family opened an account for BJP here and won in 1999 and 2004 elections and scored a hat-trick. Then BJD’s Kalikesh Narayan Singh Deo won the 2009 and 2014 elections. In the 2019 elections, two members of the royal family contested the Balangir Lok Sabha seat from different parties. BJD fielded Kalikesh Narayan Singh Deo, who has been a two-time MP from this seat. Sangeeta Kumari of the royal family had contested against him on BJP ticket. Sangeeta Kumari Singh Deo stopped Kalikesh from scoring a hat-trick in the elections and won. She won with 4,98,086 votes, while BJD’s Kalikesh Narayan Singh Deo got 4,78,570 votes. Balangir Lok Sabha seat is currently held by BJP. There are 7 assembly seats under Balangir parliamentary seat, out of which 2 seats are reserved for Scheduled Castes. Here in the 2019 Assembly elections, BJD won on 4 seats, Congress won on 2 seats and BJP won on one seat. BJP has again fielded Sangeeta Kumari Singh Deo in this Lok Sabha elections while BJD has made Surendra Singh Bhoi, who left Congress to join BJD, as its candidate. Congress has given ticket to actor-turned-politician Manoj Mishra. There are chances of a triangular fight between BJP, BJD and Congress in this Lok Sabha constituency voting for which will be held on 20th of this month. PRAKASH DAS, akashvani news, Bhubaneswar.
