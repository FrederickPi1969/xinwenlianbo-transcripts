## Midday News

Voting is underway for the first phase of general elections. Polling is taking place in 102 Parliamentary constituencies spread over 17 states and four Union Territories. Simultaneous voting is also taking place to elect new legislative assemblies in Arunachal Pradesh and Sikkim in a single phase today.

Voting started in most of the constituencies at 7 this morning and will conclude at 6 in the evening. However, timings vary in North Eastern States and Left-Wing Extremism-affected areas.

In the first phase, voting is being held for all the parliamentary constituencies in 10 States and Union Territories. They are: 39 segments of Tamil Nadu, five in Uttarakhand, two each in Arunachal Pradesh and Meghalaya and one each in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Mizoram, Nagaland, Puducherry, Sikkim and Lakshadweep.

Polling is also being held for 12 seats of Rajasthan, eight seats of Uttar Pradesh, six in Madhya Pradesh, five seats each in Assam and Maharashtra, four in Bihar, three in West Bengal, two seats in Manipur and one seat each in Chhattisgarh, Tripura and Jammu and Kashmir.

A total of 1,625 candidates are in the fray in this phase of Lok Sabha elections. Among them, prominent candidates include, senior BJP leaders and Union Ministers Nitin Gadkari, Sarbananda Sonowal, Kiren Rijiju, Dr Jitendra Singh, Sanjeev Balyan and as well as BJP leaders Tamilisai Soundararajan and Jitin Prasada. Besides, Congress leaders Gaurav Gogoi, Karti Chidambaram and Nakul Nath and DMK’s Dayanidhi Maran are prominent faces whose political fate is being sealed in the EVMs today.

One lakh 63 thousand polling stations have been set up for 16 crore 63 lakh voters in this phase. Around 36 lakh first-time voters are also exercising their franchise. The Election Commission has ensured that basic facilities are in place so that every voter can cast their vote with ease. In this phase, over 14 lakh people aged more than 85 years and around 14 lakh Persons with Disabilities have been provided the option to vote from their homes.

The Indian Railways is operating a record number of additional trains to meet the summer rush. The Railways is operating a record-breaking nine thousand one hundred 11 trips during the summer season to ensure the convenience of passengers and manage the anticipated surge in travel demand during summers.  This marks a substantial rise compared to last year’s summer, where a total of six thousand 369 trips were offered.

Ministry of Railways said the additional trains have been meticulously planned to connect key destinations across the country, ensuring seamless travel on major railway routes.

During the summer season, Zonal Railways have been instructed to ensure the availability of drinking water at Railway stations. Elaborate crowd control arrangements are made at all major and important railway stations.

Iran fired air defence missiles near the central city of Isfahan after spotting drones this morning. The firing raised fears of a possible Israeli strike in retaliation for Tehran’s unprecedented drone-and-missile assault on the country. As per initial media reports, Israeli drones have hit a site in the Isfahan province of Iran, days after Tehran launched a drone strike on Israel in response to an attack at the Iranian embassy in Syria. However, Iran’s news agency said, explosions were heard at Isfahan airport but the cause was not immediately known. American broadcast networks quoting unnamed US officials said Israel carried out the attack. Flights in several Iranian cities were briefly suspended. There were also reports of explosions in Syria and Iraq.

In IPL Cricket, Lucknow Super Giants will take on Chennai Super Kings in Lucknow today. The match will start at 7.30 this evening. Yesterday, Mumbai Indians defeated Punjab Kings by nine runs in Chandigarh.

## Evening News

The stage is all set for the first phase of polling for the Lok Sabha elections tomorrow. A total of 102 Parliamentary constituencies which include 11 Lok Sabha seats reserved for ST and 18 seats reserved for SC will go to polls in this phase. These constituencies are spread over 17 States and four Union Territories. These states are Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, Bihar, Assam, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Uttarakhand, Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur and Meghalaya. The prominent candidates in fray are senior BJP leaders Nitin Gadkari, Sarbananda Sonowal, Kiren Rijiju, Dr Jitendra Singh, Sanjeev Balyan as well as BJP leaders Tamilisai Soundararajan and Jitin Prasada. From Congress leaders Gaurav Gogoi, Karti Chidambaram and Nakul Nath and DMK’s Dayanidhi Maran are prominent faces whose political fate will be sealed in the EVMs. A total of 1,625 candidates are in the fray in this phase.

The voting will start at 7 AM. One lakh 63 thousand polling stations have been set up for 16 crore 63 lakh voters in this phase. Around 36 lakh first-time voters will also exercise their franchise. More from our correspondent:

The Election Commission has ensured that basic facilities like water, sheds, toilets, ramps, volunteers, wheelchairs, and electricity are in place so that every voter can cast their vote with ease. 361 observers have been deployed in different Parliamentary Constituencies to keep a vigil on the polling process. Webcasting will be done in more than 50 percent of the polling stations along with the deployment of micro observers in all polling stations. Over one thousand three hundred inter-state and 162 international Border checkposts are keeping a strict vigil on any illicit flow of liquor, drugs, cash, and freebies. Strict surveillance is also being kept at sea and air routes. With Diwaker, Suparna Saikia, Akashvani News, Delhi.

Three deaf-mute sisters of Dadhkai Panchayat, also known as the silent village of India in the Gandoh area of Doda district are enthusiastically preparing to cast their vote tomorrow and inspiring others to participate in the festival of democracy.   Despite having hearing and speaking disability these three siblings aged between 20 and 24 are upbeat to participate in the polling.

Dadhkai-B is located on the hilltop about 9000 ft above sea level in the Gandoh sub-division of the Doda district. The village is also known as the mute village of India because it houses 84 deaf and dumb persons. Gandoh in the Doda district is a part of the Doda-Udhampur parliamentary constituency which will go to polls tomorrow.

The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) conducted a successful flight test of Indigenous Technology Cruise Missile today. The missile was test-fired from the Integrated Test Range, Chandipur off the coast of Odisha.

According to the Defence Ministry, the flight of the missile was also monitored from the Su-30-Mk-I aircraft of the Indian Air Force. Defence Minister Rajnath Singh has congratulated the DRDO for the successful flight test of the missile.

Food Safety and Standards Authority of India is examining the claim that Nestle added sugar to infant milk. Sources said the findings will be placed before the Scientific panel. The move came following a report of a Swiss investigative organisation that claimed the Swiss multinational company added sugar to its milk formula brand for infants and children of up to two years old.

The External Affairs Ministry today said the Indian deck cadet Ann Tessa Joseph from Kerala, who was among the Indian crew members of the container vessel MSC Aries, has landed safely at the Cochin International Airport this afternoon. In a statement, the Ministry said this was done with the concerted efforts of the Indian Mission in Tehran and the Iranian Government. It said the Indian Mission in Tehran remains seized of the matter and is in touch with the remaining 16 Indian crew members of the container vessel. The crew members are in good health and in contact with their family members in India.

European Union leaders have agreed to impose new sanctions on Iran after its attack on Israel by drones and missiles. The decision came as the EU leaders gathered for a two-day summit in Brussels. The EU leaders in their statement from the summit also urged all parties to exercise the utmost restraint and to refrain from any action that may increase tensions in the region.

Odisha is experiencing extreme heatwave conditions.The maximum day temperature breached 40 degrees Celsius or above at many places in the state today. The capital city Bhubaneswar recorded a maximum temperature of 43.6 degrees Celsius making it the hottest city in Odisha, followed by Jharsuguda with 42.6 degrees Celsius. Cities like Balasore, Hirakud, Chandbali, Keonjhar and Rourkela are also experiencing intense heatwave conditions with mercury crossing 40 degree Celsius during the day.

The Odisha government has already announced the closure of all schools in the state from today till Saturday.

IPL cricket, Mumbai Indians were 173 for 4 in 18.3 overs against Punjab Kings, when reports last came in at Maharaja Yadavindra Singh International Cricket Stadium in Chandigarh.
