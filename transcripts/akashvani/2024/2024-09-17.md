Prime Minister Narendra Modi will launch the Odisha government’s flagship initiative Subhadra scheme in Bhubaneswar today. The Subhadra scheme is the largest women-centric initiative in the state, aimed at supporting over 1 crore women. Eligible beneficiaries between the ages of 21 and 60 will receive 50,000 rupees over a period of five years. The Prime Minister will also lay the foundation stone and dedicate to the nation Railway Projects worth more than 2,800 crore rupees. More from our correspondent:

Prime Minister Narendra Modi is visiting Bhubaneswar today to dedicate various schemes and projects including Subhadra Yojana for the poor women on the occasion of his 74th birthday. The Prime Minister will visit a slum area at Gadakana in the state capital soon after his arrival and interact with 20 beneficiaries of Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY)-Urban. He will also digitally transfer the first installment of the PMAY-Urban to about 10 lakh beneficiaries of 14 states. Elaborate security arrangements have been made at Bhubaneswar in view of the Prime Minister’s visit. PRAKASH DASH/Akashvani News /Bhubaneswar.

On the occasion of the birthday of Prime Minister Narendra Modi, BJP National President Jagat Prakash Nadda will inaugurate a blood donation camp under Seva Pakhwada at the party headquarters in New Delhi today. Mr Nadda will also inaugurate an exhibition based on the life of the Prime Minister.

Home Minister Amit Shah has extended wishes to Prime Minister Modi on the occasion of his 74th birthday. In a social media post, Mr Shah said the Prime Minister has brought positive change in the lives of the citizens through tireless hard work, perseverance, and farsightedness.

The 8th India Water Week 2024 will begin today in New Delhi. President Droupadi Murmu will inaugurate the event at Bharat Mandapam. During the four day event, around four thousand delegates including two hundred from 40 foreign countries will participate. Our correspondent reports that more than one hundred exhibitors and startups will showcase their ideas in the water sector during the exhibition.

The 8th India Water Week event will serve as a global platform to elicit ideas and opinions from global level decision-makers, researchers, experts, innovators and stakeholders in the field of water resources from across the world. The Ministry of Panchayati Raj will participate as a Partner Ministry during the event. The 8th India Water Week will showcase the best practices in water management from Gram Panchayats across the country starting from water conservation, rainwater harvesting, groundwater recharge. With Dipendra Kumar, Pallavi Parmar, Akashvani News Delhi.

All arrangements have been made for first phase of assembly elections in Jammu and Kashmir. Twenty-four assembly seats spread over 7 districts will go to polls in this phase tomorrow. These districts are Anantnag, Pulwama, Shopian, Kulgam, Doda, Ramban and Kishtwar.  While 16 constituencies are in Kashmir Division, eight are in Jammu Division.  Over 23.27 lakh voters, including 5.66 lakh youth, will decide the fate of 219 candidates in the first phase.

In Haryana, 1031 candidates have been left in fray after the withdrawal of nominations for assembly elections. Chief Electoral Officer Pankaj Agarwal said a total of 190 candidates withdrew their nominations on the last day yesterday.

Polling for all 90 seats in the state will take place on October 5. Counting of votes will be taken up on the 8th of October.

Swacchata Hi Seva Abhiyan 2024 has started today and it will continue till the 2nd of the next month. The theme for this year is Swabhav Swachhata-Sanskaar Swacchta. The campaign includes mega cleanliness drives at various sites such as tourist locations, public buildings, commercial areas, water bodies, zoos, sanctuaries, and community and public toilets through shramdaan and collective action. All central government ministries and states are planning various citizen engagement activities across the country. More from our correspondent:

This year’s campaign is built around three key pillars under the whole of society approach. These are, Cleanliness of Target Units (CTUs) Swachhata Mein Jan Bhaagidari, and SafaiMitra Suraksha Shivir. Various cultural events, rallies, marathons, artistic Waste to Art installations, and community discussion will also be organised to deepen ownership and responsibility for cleanliness. The campaign will engage citizens, industries, NGOs, development organisations, urban local bodies, panchayats and various stakeholders nationwide. Under the whole of government approach, all states, Union Territories, Chief Ministers, Central Ministries and local bodies will also participate to ensure a nationwide movement for cleanliness. Saklen Akhtar, Akashvani News Delhi.

In Madhya Pradesh, Prime Minister Jan Aushadhi Kendras are being started simultaneously in all the district hospitals today on the occasion of Swachhata Hi Seva Pakhwada. More from our correspondent:

Governor Mangubhai Patel and Chief Minister Dr. Mohan Yadav will inaugurate the operation of Pradhan Mantri Jan Aushadhi Kendras in all the district hospitals on the first day of Swachhata Hi Seva Pakhwada. This step is an important effort to provide affordable and quality generic medicines to the citizens of the state. At present, more than 500 Jan Aushadhi Kendras are functioning in various districts of Madhya Pradesh. In these centers, patients are getting medicines at 50% to 90% less price than branded medicines. Apart from this, there will also be a Griha Pravesh program in 51 thousand houses of Madhya Pradesh. -Sanjeev Sharma, Akashvani News, Bhopal.

West Bengal Chief Minister Mamata Banerjee has said that the state government has accepted three out of four demands of the protesting junior doctors. Addressing the media after meeting the junior doctors at her residence at Kalighat last night, Ms. Banerjee said, in view of their demand, Kolkata police commissioner Vineet Goyal will be removed. She said, the changes in the police force will be made effective through a notification this afternoon. She said, DC North is also being transferred and Director, Medical Education DME and Director, Health Services DHS are also being removed. Ms. Banerjee said, one demand is already in sub-judice status. The Chief Minister urged the junior doctors to join their duty. Junior doctors in Kolkata are holding protests over a rape-murder case, demanding justice.

The second edition of Naval Commanders’ Conference of 2024 will begin today in New Delhi. The Conference is the apex level biannual event facilitating deliberations on significant strategic, operational and administrative issues between the Naval Commanders. The Defence Ministry said, the four day conference will commence with the inaugural address by the Chief of Naval Staff.

An e-auction of gifts received by Prime Minister Narendra Modi will begin today. The ten-day long celebrations of Ganesh Utsav in Maharashtra will end today after immersion of the Ganesh idols. Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation has made elaborate arrangements for the Ganpati Visarjan on the occasion of Anant Chaturdashi. More from our correspondent:

A total of 12,000 BMC officers and employees have been deployed, and 71 control rooms have been established to monitor the festivities. Additionally, 7 hundred 61 lifeguards and 48 motorboats are stationed along the seashores. For the collection of flowers offered to the idols, 1 hundred & 63 Nirmalya Kalash and 2 hundred &74 Nirmalya vehicles are on standby. There are also 127 mobile washrooms available for devotees attending the procession. In order to promote eco-friendly immersion, the BMC has set up 204 artificial lakes. Devotees can find information and locations of these lakes by scanning a QR code. Bhavana Gokhale AIR,Mumbai

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today launched the Odisha government’s flagship women-centric initiative, Subhadra Yojana, besides other railway and national highway projects in Bhubaneshwar today. The Prime Minister, who turns 74 today, received a rousing traditional welcome upon arrival at Biju Patnaik International Airport, with tribal folk dances and drumbeats. Thousands of supporters greeted him on his birthday.

Addressing the gathering at Janata Maidan in Bhubaneswar after inaugurating the projects, the Prime Minister said, more than 1 crore women will be able to take advantage of ‘Subhadra Yojana’. Mr Modi said, development of a nation relies on the participation of Women and the increased  potential of women will be the core mantra of Odisha’s development.

The Prime minister said, the RBI digital currency pilot project has also been linked to this yojana. This is the first such initiative.

Mr Modi said, Modi government 3.0 is completing 100 days today with a large number of projects and schemes to its credit. He said approval has been given for construction of 3 crore pucca houses to the homeless people, pucca roads to 25000 villages, 75,000 more seats in medical colleges, new pension scheme for government employees, minimum support price to the tune of 2 lakh crore rupees, first job and first salary to youths in private sector and many more in the first 100 days of his government.

Soon after his arrival, Mr Modi visited a slum area at Gadakana in Bhubaneswar and interacted with 20 beneficiaries of Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana-Urban. He said, a sister in one of the families gave him Khiri to eat. He said, he missed his mother.

The Prime Minister also dedicated to the nation and laid the foundation of railway projects worth 2,800 crore rupees and national highway projects worth more than 1,000 crore rupees. He also released the first installment of assistance of 3180 crore rupees to nearly 10 lakh beneficiaries under PMAY-Gramin of around 14 states.

President Droupadi Murmu and Vice President Jagdeep Dhankhar have extended wishes to the Prime Minister Narendra Modi on his birthday. The President said, Mr Modi has increased the prosperity and prestige of the country through his personality and work. She also wished that his innovative efforts with the spirit of nation first may pave the way to make India a developed nation. The President wished him a long, healthy, and happy life.

The Vice President said, through his governance for over two decades and as Prime Minister over a decade, Mr Modi has aroused the soul of the country, stimulated its cultural values, nurtured its civilisational ethos, and firmly set it on the unprecedented developmental trajectory towards Viksit Bharat. Mr Dhankhar wished him a good health.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah has said that projects worth 15 lakh crore rupees were sanctioned or initiated during the 100 days of Modi 3.0 government. Briefing media in New Delhi today, Mr. Shah said several decisions were taken to boost the economy, creating world class infrastructure, strengthening the security ecosystem, enhancing ease of living and making life better for the poor and middle class.

On the occasion, Home Minister Amit Shah and Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw today launched a booklet and eight flyers on the works done by the government.

Mr. Shah also appreciated the Digital India initiative, saying that many countries want to learn from India and make it a strong pillar of their growth. Mr Shah expressed hope that the country will emerge as a big player in the semiconductor sector.

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh today asserted that the government is committed to make India self-reliant in the defence sector. Lauding the 100 days of the Narendra Modi government, Mr Singh said, the Ministry has given approval for the purchase of over 1.5 lakh crore rupees defence items in the last three months. He said, the government has included over 501 items in the positive indigenisation list. The Minister highlighted measures taken by the government for the welfare of people of all sections of the society adding that the government is marching towards the goal of making India a developed nation by 2047.

Replying to a question on Leader of Opposition Rahul Gandhi’s criticism of the government, Mr Singh said the Congress leader has no credibility. He alleged that Mr Gandhi goes to other countries and tries to bring disrepute to the country’s image which is an irony.

President Droupadi Murmu has stressed the need to initiate a people’s movement to achieve the goals of water conservation, improved management, development and distribution. Inaugurating 8th India Water Week at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi today, the President emphasized the importance of united efforts to tackle the pressing issue of water conservation and sustainability. She noted there are many ancient practices in the country on water resources management which are still relevant and they should be applied in their modern form.

During the four day event, around four thousand delegates including two hundred foreign delegates from 40 countries will participate and more than one hundred exhibitors and startups will showcase their ideas in the water sector.

The Aam Aadmi Party today announced that senior party leader Atishi will be the new Chief Minister of Delhi. She was elected as the leader of the AAP Legislative Party during the party MLAs meeting in New Delhi. Talking to media after the meeting, City Minister Gopal Rai said it has been unanimously decided that until the next assembly elections, Atishi will be Chief Minister. He said the AAP wants the assembly election in Delhi to be held in October- November this year. Mr. Rai accused the BJP of conspiring to finish the AAP.

After coming out of jail, Delhi Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal had announced two days ago that he would resign from his post. Mr. Kejriwal is scheduled to meet Delhi Lieutenant Governor V.K. Saxena this evening to tender his resignation.

Speaking to media, Chief Minister Designate Atishi thanked the party chief and said that she will continue to work hard and let the schemes continue for the people of Delhi.

The Supreme Court today directed Wikipedia to remove the name of the victim from its pages in the alleged RG Kar Medical College Hospital rape-murder case in Kolkata. A Supreme Court bench headed by Chief Justice of India D.Y. Chandrachud issued this direction in the suo motu case taken by the Apex Court regarding the crime.

The Supreme Court also observed that revelations in the status report filed by the Central Bureau of Investigation were disturbing. However, the Court declined to disclose the details provided by the CBI, stating that such disclosures might hinder the investigation.

Today is Vishwakarma Jayanti. Prime Minister Narendra Modi has extended greetings to the citizens on the occasion. In a social media post, Mr Modi saluted the skilled and hardworking craftsmen and creators associated with construction and creation. The Prime Minister expressed confidence that their contribution will be unparalleled in the accomplishment of the resolution of a developed and self-reliant India.

In Peru, at least 15 people were killed, and more than 3,000 hectares of cultivated land and natural areas were scorched due to widespread forest fires in various parts of the country. Peruvian Prime Minister Gustavo Adrianzen urged farming communities yesterday to cease burning grasslands in the Andean and Amazonian regions, as these practices have sparked the deadly wildfires.

In the Asian Champions Trophy 2024, Indian men’s Hockey team will take on hosts China in the summit clash at Hulunbuir this afternoon. The match will start at 3:30 PM Indian Standard Time.

Mr Modi also laid the foundation stone for National Highway Projects worth more than one thousand crore rupees. Besides, the Prime Minister launched ‘SUBHADRA’, the flagship Scheme of Odisha government. It is the largest, single women-centric scheme and is expected to cover over one crore women.

The Prime Minister released the first installment to nearly 10 lakh beneficiaries of Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY)-Gramin across the country. He took part in Grih Pravesh celebrations for 26 lakh beneficiaries of PMAY (Gramin and Urban) and handed over house keys to them.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi-led NDA government completed 100 days of its third successive term at the Centre today.

Speaking at an event at Bhubaneswar in Odisha today, Prime Minister Modi said, big decisions have been taken for the empowerment of the poor, farmers, youth and women during the period. Mr Modi listed out the achievements of the past 100 days.

On the occasion, several Union Ministers held press conferences in the National Capital to highlight the initiatives taken during the period.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah said, projects worth 15 lakh crore rupees were sanctioned or initiated during the past 100 days.

Speaking on One Nation One Election, the Home Minister said, it will be implemented by the government in this term. He said, census work will be initiated soon.

Talking exclusively to Akashwani News, Union Minister for Parliamentary Affairs Kiren Rijiju said, of the 15 lakh crore rupees sanctioned for the welfare of people, the government will spend three lakh crore rupees towards Nari Shakti.

In an interview to DD News this evening, Information and Broadcasting Minister, Ashwini Vaishnaw highlighted several big decisions taken by the NDA government in the last 100 days, which will bring positive transformation among every section of the society.

Minister for Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying Rajiv Ranjan (Lalan) Singh said, the country has secured the second spot globally in fish production, a record achievement following a substantial investment of 38 thousand 572 crore rupees in various schemes.

Arvind Kejriwal has resigned as Chief Minister of Delhi. Mr. Kejriwal met Delhi Lieutenant Governor VK Saxena this evening and submitted his resignation.

Meanwhile, Senior Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) leader Atishi met Lieutenant Governor Saxena this evening and staked claim to form the government. AAP had earlier announced that Atishi will be the new Chief Minister of Delhi. She was elected as the leader of AAP Legislative Party during party MLAs meeting.

Making the announcement after the meeting, City Minister Gopal Rai said the party wants the assembly election in Delhi to be held in October-November this year. Mr. Rai accused the BJP of conspiring to finish the AAP.

On the other hand, BJP took a dig at AAP. Talking to media, Delhi BJP Chief Virendra Sachdeva said just changing the face will not change the party’s character.

In Jammu and Kashmir, the stage is set for the first phase of assembly elections tomorrow. Twenty-four assembly constituencies spread over seven districts will go to polls in this phase. Of these, eight seats are in three districts of Jammu region and 16 are in four districts of Kashmir valley. Over 23 lakh voters will decide the fate of 219 candidates, including 90 independents. Elaborate security arrangements have been made in the Union Territory for the assembly polls.

Polling for the second phase, in the Union Territory, will take place on the 25th of September and the third phase will be held on the 1st of October.

In Haryana, campaigning is gaining momentum for single-phase assembly elections. Senior BJP leader and Home Minister, Amit Shah addressed a poll rally in Bhiwani today. He criticised the Congress saying, that the party is against reservation for Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes and Backward Classes. Voting for the 90-member state assembly will be held on the 5th of October.

IPS officer Manoj Kumar Verma has been appointed as the new Commissioner of Kolkata Police today. Verma, a 1998 batch IPS officer, was serving as Additional Director General (ADG), law and order of West Bengal. Verma replaces Vineet Kumar Goyal, who has been transferred to the role of ADG and IGP of Special Task Force of West Bengal Police. The transfers come in wake of the rape and murder of a trainee doctor at RG Kar Medical College Hospital in Kolkata last month.

Ten-day long Ganesh Chaturthi festival culminated, this evening, on the occasion of ‘Anant Chaturdashi’, with the immersion of Lord Ganesha’s idols in water bodies across Maharashtra and other parts of the country. Our correspondent reports that Mumbai came alive as lakhs of devotees thronged roads in the city for the immersion processions.

Idols from homes and Ganesh mandals were taken for immersion amidst rhythmic beating of drums and the jubilant chants of ‘Ganapati Bappa Morya, Pudhchya Varshi Lavkar Ya”. The visarjan processions of the famous Lalbaugcha Raja  and Mumbaicha Raja are one of the most iconic sights of the city. Lakhs of devotees eagerly waited on the streets for the final ‘darshan’ of the deity. Mumbai police have beefed up security and have made elaborate arrangements to ensure smooth flow of traffic. Prarthna, Akashvani News, Mumbai”

Union Minister for Environment, Forest and Climate Change, Bhupender Yadav, has said that developing countries need more than five trillion dollars to meet their climate goals by 2030. Addressing the 19th edition of the CII-ITC Sustainability Summit today in New Delhi, the Minister emphasized the need for financial support and technology transfer to combat climate change. He said, India is one of the few countries that have fulfilled all the indices of the Paris Agreement before time.

India has sent 53 tonnes of emergency flood relief assistance worth one million dollars to Myanmar under Operation Sadbhav. Typhoon Yagi caused loss of hundreds of lives, and massive destruction of property in the neighbouring country.

Operation Sadbhav is part of broader efforts to support the people of Myanmar in line with India’s longstanding ‘Act East’ and ‘Neighbourhood First’ policies.

At least 18 people have lost their lives so far in flooding caused by Storm Boris in Central and Eastern Europe.

In Poland, four people have died due to flood-related incidents. A month-long state of natural disaster has been declared in the areas worst hit by flooding.

In Romania, seven people have died, and a state of emergency has been declared for one of the worst-affected regions till the 16th of next month.

In the Czech Republic, three people have died, and more than 13,000 people have been evacuated.

In Austria, four people have died, and thousands of households were without electricity and water in Lower Austria.

In Lebanon, dozens of people were wounded today when pagers used for communication exploded across the country. Lebanon’s state news agency reported blasts in the southern suburbs of Beirut and several other areas. Multiple news agencies are reporting that the pagers belonged to members of the Lebanese terrorist group Hezbollah. Iranian media reported that Iran’s ambassador to Lebanon, Mojtaba Amani, was also injured in one of the blasts. Lebanon’s crisis operations center called on all medical workers to report to their respective hospitals to help cope with the influx of wounded people requiring urgent care.

In Hockey, India lifted the Men’s Asian Champions Trophy for the record fifth time after defeating the hosts China 1-Nil at Hulunbuir this evening. In a high-intensity clash, India’s Jugraj Singh slotted home the only goal of the game in the fourth quarter. Team India, who were the defending champions, are the most successful team in the competition with five titles. The Men in Blue were playing in their sixth Asian Champions Trophy hockey final from the eight editions held so far. China had made their maiden appearance in the final.
