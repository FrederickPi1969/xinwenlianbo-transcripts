## Midday News

Campaigning has gained full momentum for the fourth phase of Lok Sabha elections as only two days are left for it to end. Voting in the fourth phase will take place on the 13th of this month in 96 seats spread across 9 states and a union territory. Voting across all 25 Parliamentary seats in Andhra Pradesh, 17 seats in Telangana, 13 seats in Uttar Pradesh, 11 seats in Maharashtra, 8 seats each in Madhya Pradesh and West Bengal, 5 seats in Bihar, 4 seats each in Jharkhand and Odisha and one seat in the UT of Jammu and Kashmir will be held in this phase.

Prime Minister and Senior BJP leader addressed a rally in Nandurbar in Maharashtra. He said, NDA Government has provided pucca houses, electricity connection, tapped water connection, gas connection and other facilities in tribal areas of the country. Mr Modi said, the NDA Government has constructed around 1.25 lakh pucca houses in Nandurbar under the PM Awas yojana and free ration for than 12 lakh people in the district to fight malnutrition. He also said that the NDA facilitated tapped water connection to more than 30 thousand villages in Maharashtra, including one hundred and eleven villages in difficult terrains of Nandurbar.

He attacked the Congress for neglecting the tribal areas of the country and diseases. He added that the NDA took significant efforts to fight the disease.

Chief Minister Eknath Shinde, Deputy Chief Minister Devendra Fadanvis and others were present at the rally.

The Prime Minister will also address two rallies at Narayanpet and Hyderabad in Telangana today. The meeting to be held this evening at LB Stadium in Hyderabad will be the last poll rally of Mr Modi in Telangana. Later in the evening, Prime Minister Modi will begin a two-day campaign trail in Odisha. He will hold a roadshow in about two and half kilometers at Bhubaneswar this evening.

Re-polling is being held today at four polling centres of Betul Lok Sabha constituency in Madhya Pradesh. Voting began at 7 am and will continue till 6 pm. There are 3 thousand 37 voters at these four polling centres.

Indian Coast Guard has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Hindalco Industries for manufacturing and supply of indigenous marine-grade aluminium for construction of ships. The Indian Coast Guard fleet is presently operating 67 ships with aluminium hull with capability of operating in shallow waters. Defence Ministry said, to further boost coastal security, Indian Coast Guard has planned to induct more such vessels.

IMD has said that rainfall along with thunderstorm and lightning is likely to prevail in West Bengal, Sikkim, Bihar, Odisha and Jharkhand today and will continue till the next two days. Heavy rainfall is also likely over Northeast India today.

The Election Commission has dismissed the allegation raised by the Congress President Mallikarjun Kharge about the mismanagement and delay in release of voter turnout data. In a letter to the Congress President, the Commission said that the allegations are unwarranted, without facts and reflective of a biased and deliberate attempt to spread confusion. He had earlier alleged discrepancies in voter turnout data released by the Commission and non-publication of registered voters in a post on social media. The poll body remarked that the trend of irresponsible statements attacking or attempting to degrade the credibility of the elections in terms of men and material by a national political party is disconcerting. It said utterances from President of the party attacking the very credibility of the electoral steps and processes can have a negative impact on voter participation. The Commission advised the Congress president to exercise caution and refrain from making such statements.

Opposition parties’ bloc I.N.D.I. alliance delegation today met the Election Commission of India. After the meeting, Congress leader Abhishek Manu Singhvi said, more than one thousand 500 complaints have been raised against the Prime Minister and Senior BJP leader Narendra Modi and senior party leader Amit Shah but no action has been taken. He also raised objection to the delay in releasing the voter percentage data.

Delhi Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal was released from Tihar Jail this evening after being granted interim bail by Supreme Court till 1st June in excise policy case. The apex court asked the AAP chief to surrender on 2nd June, a day after the polling for the seven-phased Lok Sabha elections is scheduled to end. Delhi Chief Minister  had sought a release from jail to campaign for the ongoing Lok Sabha polls. The Supreme Court also pointed out the Enforcement Directorate’s delay in arresting him. A bench, comprising Justices Sanjiv Khanna and Dipankar Datta, said that the central probe agency had registered an Enforcement Case Information Report against Arvind Kejriwal in August 2022, but arrested him on 21st March, this year.

Aam Aadmi Party has hailed the Supreme Court’s decision of granting interim bail to Delhi Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal as victory of democracy and the Constitution. Congress leader Pawan Khera also welcomed the judgement of the Supreme Court and expressed hope that the former Chief Minister of Jharkhand Hemant Soren will also get justice. BJP leader Manjinder Singh Sirsa said that the apex court’s judgement clearly says that Kejriwal has been granted bail only for elections and he will have to go back to jail after 1st of next month.

In Chhattisgarh, twelve maoists were killed in an encounter with security forces in Bijapur district today. The bodies of the killed maoists have been recovered from the spot.

According to senior police officials, the encounter with the Maoists took place in the forest near Pedia village in Gangalur area when a joint team of District Reserve Guard and Special Task Force was conducting a search operation in the area.  After an encounter, that lasted for about 4 hours, apart from the bodies of twelve Maoists, a large quantity of weapons and explosives have also been recovered from the area.

Two security personnel have also been injured in this encounter.

Department of Telecommunications (DoT) has issued directions to telecom service providers for pan India blocking of 28 thousand 200 mobile handsets, which were misused in cybercrimes. It has also directed to carry out immediate reverification of 20 lakh mobile connections linked to mobile handsets and disconnect failing re-verification.

The Ministry of Home Affairs and State Police in an analysis found that these mobile handsets were misused in cybercrimes. The DoT further analysed and found that a staggering 20 lakh numbers were used with these mobile handsets.

India will participate at the 77th Cannes Film Festival in France which will be held from 14th to 25th of this month. The Corporate Indian Delegation consists of representatives from Government of India, State Governments and members of the industry, who will showcase India’s creative economy in the leading film market of the world.

India and Moldova today signed an agreement in New Delhi on visa waiver for diplomatic and official passports. This agreement will allow holders of diplomatic and official passports of either country to travel to the other, without a visa. External Affairs Ministry, Secretary (West), Pavan Kapoor and Moldova’s Ambassador, Ana Taban signed the agreement.

The United Nations today warned that aid for the Gaza Strip could grind to a halt, as Israeli troops took their ground war with Palestinian fighters into Rafah. Israeli tanks captured the main road dividing the eastern and western sections of Rafah which is a key aid corridor for Gaza Strip. Israel has ordered civilians out of the eastern part of Rafah, forcing tens of thousands of people to seek shelter outside the city. United Nations aid agencies said, supplies were already running short and aid operations could halt within days.
