External Affairs Minister Dr. S. Jaishankar will attend the 23rd Meeting of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) Council of Heads of Government in Islamabad today. He reached the Pakistan capital yesterday on a two-day visit to the neighboring country. The SCO meeting is being held under Pakistan’s chairmanship, and Dr. Jaishankar will represent India at the event. India remains actively engaged in the SCO format, participating in various mechanisms and initiatives within the organization. The SCO Council of Heads of Government meeting is held annually and focuses on the organization’s trade and economic agenda.

Dr. Jaishankar had earlier remarked that his Pakistan visit is for a multilateral event and ruled out the possibility for bilateral talks. This is the first high-level visit of an Indian Foreign Minister to the neighbouring nation in nine years.

The External Affairs Minister pointed out that disruptions such as extreme climate events, supply chain uncertainties, and financial volatility are already affecting growth and development worldwide. The Minister also voiced concern over the debt terming it a serious concern. He stressed that cooperation between nations must be founded on mutual respect, sovereign equality, and recognition of territorial integrity.

Dr. Jaishankar also underscored the relevance of India’s global initiatives within the SCO framework. He highlighted India’s leadership in the International Solar Alliance, which promotes renewable energy, and the Coalition for Disaster Resilient Infrastructure, which aims to mitigate the impact of climate change. He also highlighted the importance of Mission LiFE, Yoga and millets while noting India’s advancement in digital public infrastructure.

The Supreme Court today criticised the Haryana and Punjab state governments for non-compliance in one of its earlier orders related to air pollution due to stubble burning. ## Evening News

The government today gave nod to increase the Minimum Support Prices (MSP) for rabi crops for marketing season 2025-26. Information And Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw briefed media about the Cabinet decisions taken under the chairmanship of Prime Minister Narendra Modi in New Delhi. He said, government has increased the MSP of Rabi Crops to ensure remunerative prices to the growers for their produce.

Mr. Vaishnaw said, the government also approved construction of Varanasi-Pandit Deen Dayal Upadhyaya multi-tracking project including a new rail-cum-road bridge across the Ganga River in Uttar Pradesh. The Minister said, this project will ease connectivity, minimize logistics cost, reduce oil imports and lower Carbondioxide (CO2) emissions. The project will generate direct employment for about ten lakh human-days during construction. He said the project cost will be two thousand 642 crore rupees and will be completed in four years.

The Union Cabinet approved three percent increase in Dearness Allowance (DA) for  Central Government employees and Dearness Relief (DR) for pensioners. Mr Vaishnaw said, increase in DA and DR will benefit over 49 lakh central government employees and over 64 lakh pensioners. The combined impact on the exchequer on account of both DA and DR will be over nine thousand 448 crore rupees per annum.
