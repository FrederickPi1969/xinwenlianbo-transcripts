## Midday News

Prime Minister and senior BJP leader Narendra Modi today participated in a roadshow in Palakkad in Kerala as part of the NDA election campaign. This was Mr Modi’s first campaign event in Kerala after the announcement of the General Elections. BJP state president K. Surendran and NDA candidates from Palakkad and Ponnani Lok Sabha constituencies were in an open vehicle along with the Prime Minister.

After the roadshow, Mr Modi proceeded to Salem in Tamilnadu where he is addressing a public meeting. He said, NDA has got new energy following the seat-sharing deal with PMK.

The Congress Working Committee meeting is underway in New Delhi to approve the party’s manifesto for the Lok Sabha polls. The meeting is being attended by party President Mallikarjun Kharge, former Presidents  Sonia Gandhi and Rahul Gandhi, and other senior leaders including  Priyanka Gandhi Vadra, P Chidambaram, Digvijaya Singh, Ajay Maken and K C Venugopal.

The Congress Central Election Committee is also expected to be held this evening to finalize the name of candidates for the Lok Sabha election.

The notification for the first phase of Lok Sabha election will be issued tomorrow. Elections will be held in 102 Lok Sabha seats in 17 States and Four Union Territories in this phase. Thirty-Nine seats of Tamil Nadu, 12 seats of Rajasthan, eight seats of Uttar Pradesh, six seats of Madhya Pradesh, five seats each in Uttarakhand, Assam and Maharashtra, four seats of Bihar, three seats of West Bengal, two seats each in Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya and one seat each in Chhattisgarh, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tripura, Anadaman and Nicobar Islands, Jammu and Kashmir, Lakshadweep and Puducherry will go to polls in this first phase. Nominations can be filed till 27th of this month. Voting will be held on 19th April.

“As country braces for mega General elections in coming days, the Election Commission,  which referred to its record as ‘Gold Standard’ is committed to conducting Lok Sabha polls fairly, freely, while also ensuring safety.  The commission has laid out plans to address all existing challenges or those which may occur during the elections. Muscle power along with the misuse of money, spread of misinformation, and violations of the Model Code of Conduct remains the main hurdle. To tackle this issue, the poll body is deploying enough Central Armed Police Force in areas prone to incidents. Round the clock Integrated control rooms are being set up across districts. Webcasting facility will be put in place at sensitive polling booths. Efforts also include executing pending Non-Bailable Warrants, collecting arms, and monitoring individuals with a history of trouble. Countrywide checkpoints are being set up, while drones will be used for border surveillance. District and state administrations have been instructed to ensure fairness, including randomizing polling staff.  This is Durgesh Bhadauria for Akashvani News, New Delhi.”

Rajya Sabha Chairman Jagdeep Dhankhar today administered oath to the re-elected Rajya Sabha Member Sanjay Singh in the Parliament House. Aam Aadmi Party leader Sanjay Singh is in judicial custody in connection with a money laundering case linked to Delhi excise policy case.

Union Minister and Rashtriya Lok Janshakti Party Chief Pashupati Kumar Paras today resigned from the Union Cabinet. This came following the NDA’s seat- sharing agreement in Bihar for Lok Sabha elections in which Mr. Paras’s party was not given any seat to contest the poll. Talking to media in New Delhi, Mr. Paras announced his resignation alleging that injustice has been done to his party.

Meanwhile, Raj Thackeray-led Maharashtra Navnirman Sena is expected to join NDA Alliance. Mr. Thackeray today held a meeting with Senior BJP leader and Union Minister Amit Shah as well as BJP General Secretary Vinod Tawde in New Delhi.

Political activities have intensified in Rajasthan after the announcement of Lok Sabha poll schedule. Chief Electoral Officer of the State Praveen Gupta told Akashvani News that all efforts are being made to effectively enforce Model Code of Conduct in Rajasthan.

BJP today came down heavily on AAP convenor and Delhi Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal for repeatedly skipping the Enforcement Directorate’s (ED) summons linked with the Delhi Excise Policy. Talking to media in New Delhi, Party Spokesperson Sambit Patra said, there is enough evidence against him in this case. Referring to a recent judgement of a Delhi court, Mr Patra said the summons against Mr Kejriwal were legal. He accused the Chief Minister of making excuses to avoid the summons. The BJP Spokesperson also mentioned the recent arrest of BRS leader K Kavitha in the alleged liquor scam case to target Mr Kejriwal.

Meanwhile, AAP leader Atishi accused the Centre of using the ED as a political weapon. She refuted the claims raised by the ED, that Mr Kejriwal had accepted bribes of 100 crore rupees in this case. Ms Atishi said that the claims have already been dismissed by the Supreme Court.

In Maharashtra, four naxals have been killed in a gunbattle between security forces and naxalites inside the Repanpalli forests of Gadchiorli district this morning. Police said, security teams led by Additional SP, Special Operations Yatish Deshmukh encountered the gunfire from the Naxals during an early morning search operation. Security forces retaliated strongly by a counter fire. Police said, a credible intelligence was received yesterday afternoon about the presence of Naxals in the area. Search and anti naxal operation is underway in the area. Details are awaited.

Jharkhand Governor C. P. Radhakrishnan has been given the additional charge of Telangana Governor and the Lieutenant Governor of Puducherry. The appointment comes after President Droupadi Murmu accepted the resignation of Dr. Tamilisai Soundararajan. Yesterday, Dr. Soundararajan resigned from the post of Governor of Telangana and Lieutenant Governor of Puducherry and there are reports that she may contest Lok Sabha polls from Tamil Nadu.

Cricketer Pulkit Narang, athletes Mohammad Nurhasan and Himanshu were among the 19 prominent sportspersons felicitated today by the Indian Air Force for their outstanding performance at national and international sporting events. Indian Air Force Chief VR Chaudhari felicitated these sportspersons who are associated with the IAF at the function in New Delhi. Addressing the gathering on the occasion, the Chief of Air Staff lauded IAF air warriors for their stellar achievements in sports. He further advised them to continue to work hard and strive for greater laurels.

Sergeant Pulkit Narang was honoured for representing India at International forum in 2022 and 2023 and Sergeant Mohd Nurhasan and LAC Himanshu were felicitated for clinching gold in the National Open Athletics Championship 2023. T Das, Marush PS, Bandaru Krishna and Kushal Kumar were among the 19 sportspersons who had won medals at national and international level in Gymnastics, Weightlifting, Wushu, Ultra Marathon, Cycling, Handball and Other sports.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi appreciated the Bulgarian President Rumen Radev on expressing gratitude to the Indian Navy for successfully carrying out a rescue operation on the hijacked Bulgarian ship. Replying to the message of the Bulgarian President in a social media post today, the Prime Minister said, India is committed to protecting freedom of navigation and combating piracy and terrorism in the Indian Ocean region. He further expressed happiness over the safe homecoming of seven Bulgarian nationals.

The National Commission for Women, NCW and the Railway Protection Force have signed an MoU in New Delhi to prevent and combat human trafficking. Speaking on the occasion, Chairperson of NCW Rekha Sharma said, Railway Protection Force personnel deployed at Railway stations are playing a crucial role in the crime against women and children. She said, 70 per cent cases of human trafficking are related to women and Girl child.

The Director General of Railway Protection Force, RPF Manoj Yadava said, the overall objective of this MoU is to provide safe and secure travel experience to women. He said, the RPF will ensure that the Railway network is not allowed to be used for any atrocities against women including trafficking.

Raksha Mantri Rajnath Singh held a telephonic conversation with US Secretary of Defence Lloyd Austin. Both Ministers briefly discussed a range of bilateral, regional security and defence cooperation issues. They reviewed the recent bilateral events such as the INDUS-X Summit held in New Delhi last month and the bilateral Tri-Service exercise Tiger Triumph.

The US Secretary of Defence appreciated the important role being played by the Indian Navy in conducting anti-piracy operations in the Indian Ocean Region.

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has forecast moderate to heavy rainfall in Odisha, Chhattisgarh, Gangetic West Bengal and Jharkhand today. It said, rainfall accompanied by thunderstorms, lightning, and hail is expected over parts of East, Central, and adjoining peninsular India till Thursday, with peak intensity of rainfall and hailstorms today.

In Nagaland, the 3rd edition of North East Games 2024 kick started with a glittering opening ceremony at Regional Centre for Sporting Excellence in Sovima last evening. Nagaland Chief Minister Neiphiu Rio officially declared open the 3rd North East Games 2024.

The 2024 edition of the Swiss Open Super 300 Badminton tournament will begin today in Basel, Switzerland. In Men’s Singles category, India’s Lakshya Sen, seeded seventh, will start his campaign against Leong Jun Hao of Malaysia in the round of 32, while Kidambi Srikant will face Wang Tzu-wei of Taiwan. In Women’s Singles, fourth seeded P V Sindhu is scheduled to face Germany’s Yvonne Li in the round of 32.

In Women’s doubles, the sixth-seeded duo of Ashwini Ponnappa and Tanisha Crasto will take on Indonesia’s Meilysa Trias Puspitasari and Rachel Allessya Rose, while another Indian pair of Treesa Jolly and Gayatri Gopichand, will lock horns with Annie Xu and Kerry Xu of the USA. The tournament will culminate on Sunday.
