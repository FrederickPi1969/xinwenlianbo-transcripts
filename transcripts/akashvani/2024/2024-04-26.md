## Midday News

Brisk polling is being reported in the second phase of Lok Sabha elections today. Eighty eight Parliamentary Constituencies spread over 12 States and the Union Territory of Jammu and Kashmir are being covered in this phase. These include, all twenty seats in Kerala, 14 in Karnataka, 13 in Rajasthan, eight each in Uttar Pradesh and Maharashtra, six in Madhya Pradesh, five each in Assam and Bihar, three seats each in West Bengal and Chhattisgarh and one seat each in Manipur, Tripura and Jammu and Kashmir.

Voting started in most of the constituencies at 7 this morning and will conclude at 6 in the evening. However, timings vary in North Eastern States and Left-Wing Extremism-affected areas.

Some of the prominent candidates whose political fate are being sealed in the EVMs today include Union Minister Rajeev Chandrasekhar, Tejasvi Surya, Hema Malini, Arun Govil and Om Birla from BJP. Rahul Gandhi, Shashi Tharoor, DK Suresh from Congress and former Karnataka chief minister HD Kumaraswamy of JDS.

A total of one thousand 202 candidates along with four candidates from the Outer Manipur Lok Sabha constituency are in the fray in this phase.

Over 16 lakh Polling officials have been deployed across 1.67 lakh polling stations. Nearly 16 crore voters are eligible to exercise their franchise in the second phase.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has asked people to cast their votes in record numbers in the second phase of the Lok Sabha elections today. In a social media post, the Prime Minister urged young and women voters to turn up at the polling booths in great numbers. He said, a high voter turnout strengthens the country’s democracy.

The filing of nominations has begun for the fifth phase of Lok Sabha election with the issuance of notification today. 49 Lok Sabha seats in six States and two Union Territories will be covered in this phase. 14 seats of Uttar Pradesh, 13 seats of Maharashtra, seven seats of West Bengal, five seats each of Odisha and Bihar, three seats of Jharkhand and one seat each in Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh will go to polls in this fifth phase.

Scrutiny of nomination papers for the fourth phase of Lok Sabha elections will take place today. The filing of nominations for this phase ended last evening. A total of 96 Lok Sabha seats spread over nine States and a Union Territory will go to polls in this phase on the 13th of next month. The last day for the withdrawal of candidature for this phase is Monday.

Campaigning for the remaining phases of general elections has gained momentum with senior leaders of major political parties crisscrossing the country to garner votes in support of their candidates.

Prime Minister and Senior BJP leader Narendra Modi today addressed a public rally at Malda in West Bengal. He said, appeasement is the common thing between TMC and Congress, for which they can do anything and everything. He alleged that these parties want to revoke each and every decision taken by the NDA government for the safety of the country.

Addressing a public meeting in Araria parliamentary constituency in Bihar, Prime Minister Modi said this election is to make India strategically and economically developed. He alleged that RJD and Congress alliance in Bihar neither cares about the constitution of India, nor about democracy.

Trinamool Congress President and West Bengal Chief Minister Mamata Banerjee will participate in two public meetings at Ghatal and Jhargram parliamentary constituencies today.

On the other hand Samajwadi Party Chief Akhilesh Yadav will address a rally in Etah district. BSP leader Aakash Anand will be addressing the gatherings in Ambedkar Nagar and Kaushambi LokSabha constituencies. On the other hand, Congress leaders Jitu Patwari, Vivek Tankha and  Arun Yadav will campaign in Burhanpur today.

The Supreme Court of India today rejected the pleas seeking 100 per cent cross-verification of Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs) data with Voter Verifiable Paper Audit Trail (VVPAT) records. The verdict was delivered by a bench of Justices Sanjiv Khanna and Dipankar Datta. Though the cases were reserved for orders on April 18, they were listed again on April 24 as the Bench wanted some technical clarifications from the Election Commission.

The Congress has announced the first list of eight candidates for Loksabha polls in Haryana. The party is contesting on nine out of 10 Lok Sabha seats in the state. The Aam Aadmi Party, a constituent of the opposition I.N.D.I.A bloc, will contest from Kurukshetra seat.

The Congress has fielded Kumari Selja from Hisar reserved constituency. Deepender Singh Hooda, will contest from the Rohtak. The Congress is yet to announce its candidate for the Gurugram constituency.

India-South Korea Consultations on Disarmament and Non-Proliferation were held in Seoul today. External Affairs Ministry said the two sides discussed developments in the field of disarmament and non-proliferation rating to nuclear, chemical and biological domains. They also discussed regional non-proliferation issues, outer space security- related matters, conventional weapons including AI in the military domain and multilateral export control regimes.

The Indian delegation was led by Joint Secretary (Disarmament and International Security Affairs), Ministry of External Affairs, Muanpuii Saiawi.

In IPL Cricket, Kolkata Knight Riders will take on Punjab Kings at the Eden Gardens in Kolkata at 7:30pm. Last night, Royal Challengers Bengaluru defeated Sunrisers Hyderabad by 35 runs at Rajiv Gandhi International Stadium in Hyderabad last night. Chasing the target of 207 runs, Sunrisers Hyderabad managed to score only 171 for 8 in the stipulated 20 overs.

The compound mixed team of Jyothi Surekha Vennam and Abhishek Verma assured India of a fourth medal in the ongoing Archery World Cup, advancing into the final in Shanghai, China today.

The world No. 2 Indians dropped just five points to prevail over their Mexican opponents Andrea Becerra and Lot Maximo Mendez Ortiz 155-151 in the semi-final.

## Evening News

Moderate to brisk polling has been reported in the second phase of Lok Sabha elections today. Eighty eight Parliamentary Constituencies spread over 12 States and the Union Territory of Jammu and Kashmir went to poll in this phase. These include, twenty seats in Kerala, 14 in Karnataka, 13 in Rajasthan, eight each in Uttar Pradesh and Maharashtra, six in Madhya Pradesh, five each in Assam and Bihar, three seats each in West Bengal and Chhattisgarh and one seat each in Manipur, Tripura and Jammu and Kashmir.

The polling for the Betul (ST) Parliamentary Constituency of Madhya Pradesh which was scheduled in this phase has been postponed to the third phase due to the demise of the BSP candidate.

Voting started in most of the constituencies at 7 this morning and concluded at 6 in the evening.

Our correspondents from different parts of the country reported that long queues of voters were witnessed at various polling booths. Special enthusiasm was seen among youth and women voters.

A total of one thousand 202 candidates along with four candidates from the Outer Manipur Lok Sabha constituency were in the fray in this phase.

In Chhattisgarh, peaceful polling took place in three constituencies – Kanker, Mahasamund, and Rajnandgaon. According to the data received so far, around 73 per cent of voting has been recorded.

“Voters shown unprecedented enthusiasm in the second phase of polling. The three Lok Sabha constituencies of the state where voting took place today are affected by the problem of Left-Wing Extremism. However, voters cast their votes ignoring the threat and fear of Naxalites and expressed their commitment to democracy. In many places, the bride and groom and their family members took an active part in this great festival of democracy. The credit for this unprecedented voting  goes to the tight security arrangements as well as campaign run by the Election Commission to make voters aware and motivating them to vote. Vikalp Shukla, Akashvani News, Raipur.”

In Uttar Pradesh, polling on 8 seats of second phase of general elections competed peacefully. More than 52 percent polling was recorded at these seats. Highest 61.89 percent polling recorded in Amroha while Mathura has the lowest turn out of 46.96 percent.

“Barring some incidents of glitches in EVMs overall polling was peaceful in the second phase of elections. Heavy security arrangements were in place on all the polling booths of these seats which are spread over western UP and Braj region. Especially in Amroha people were very enthusiastic in using their franchise, some even came to polling station amidst much fanfare. First time voters, Divyangs and Women arrived in large numbers at the polling booths to cast their votes. Today’s polling will decide fate of many political stalwarts including senior BJP leader and former Union Minister Mahesh Sharma, Film stars Hema Malini and Arun Govil, former BSP MP and Congress-SP alliance candidate Danish Ali and Senior RLD Leader Rajkumar Sangwan. Sushil Chandra Tiwari, Akashvani News, Lucknow.“

In Karnataka, election in the 14 constituencies concluded without any untoward incident. The state registered an overall polling percentage of 64.55 percent as of 5 pm.

“The heat,dust and long queues did not discourage the voters to come out and vote in several booths of the 14 constituencies that went to polls today. There were celebrities, political leaders, technocrats and ex-servicemen who stood along with others in the line, waiting for their turn to cast their vote. There were also the newly wed couples, senior citizens, and youth who came out with enthusiasm. A hospital in Bengaluru facilitated voting for 41 of its inpatients with the help of election authorities. Several hotels in Bengaluru served free masala dosa, ladoo and cool drinks for free or at discounts to the voters. Online taxi service providers had offered free service to the aged and specially abled to go to vote. Sudhindra, Akashvani News, Bengaluru.”

In Assam, the second phase voting for the five parliamentary constituencies’ passes off peacefully with a voter turnout around 70.68 percent till the time of filing of this report.

“Assam voted in the 2nd phase of parliamentary elections peacefully today for the Darrang-Udalguri, Nagaon, Diphu, Silchar and Karimganj constituencies. In this phase 61 candidates, including two women were in fray. Karimganj Parliamentary constituency has the highest 24 candidates while Diphu has five candidates. Chief Electoral Officer, Assam informed that voting were held at 9133 polling stations amid tight security and webcasting in around 55 percent polling stations. Polling officials are bringing back the EVMs  to the strong rooms with GPS enabled tracking vehicles after the successful conclusion of voting. Aminul Haque Jwadder, Akashvani News, Guwahati.”

In Kerala polling for all 20 constituencies was held today in single phase. The state had recorded a polling of over 70 percent as per official sources till 7 this evening.

“High polling was reported in Kerala today inspite of the soaring temperature and heat wave alert in the State. Since morning most of the polling booths had seen large queue of voters. Polling was very peaceful in the State except few technical issues which was rectified later. Kerala had witnessed high intense campaigns in the last months as the  three major fronts, BJP led NDA, Congress led UDF, CPI(M) led LDF had left no stone unturned for ensuring victory for their sides. 194 candidates are in fray for the 20 loksabha seats in Kerala. Kerala had grapped national attention with the presence of Senior BJP candidates like Rajiv Chandrashekhar, V. Muraleedharan, K.Surendran and prominent congress leaders like Shasi Tharoor, Rahul Gandhi. Mayusha for Akashavani News from Thiruvananthapuram.”

Voting for Lok Sabha elections have now been completed in the States of Kerala, Rajasthan, Tripura, Tamil Nadu, Sikkim, Manipur, Meghalaya, Nagaland, Mizoram, Arunachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Puducherry, Lakshadweep and Andaman and Nicobar Islands.

Odisha continued to sizzle as the mercury soared past 40 degrees Celsius in almost all places across the state today. Mercury soared beyond 44 degrees Celsius at Angul, Bolangir, Boudh, and Nuapada, making these the hottest places in the state. Most of the Western Odisha towns recorded a temperature of 43 degrees Celsius or more. Coastal pockets were spared the heat, although humidity levels rose, making life uncomfortable. Our correspondent reports that Met Office has issued a Red alert for several districts.

“Last week, the mercury soared to 45 degrees Celsius in different places in the state. The Bhubaneswar Met office has predicted that there will be no change in the weather conditions and a gradual rise in the maximum temperature by 2 to 3 degrees in several parts of the state over the next four days. The Met Office, in this regard, issued a Red warning to several districts. SN Pattnaik, Akashvani News, Cuttack.”

In a close match, the world No.2 Indian team triumphed over their Mexican opponents, Andrea Becerra and Lot Maximo Ortiz, 155-151. Vennam and Verma will face the lower-ranked Estonian siblings, Robin Jaatma and Lisell Jaatma in the final clash tomorrow.
