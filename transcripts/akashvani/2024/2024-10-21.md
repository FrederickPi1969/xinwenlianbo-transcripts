In Jammu and Kashmir, a Kashmiri doctor and six construction workers were killed by terrorists when they opened fire at a tunnel construction site in Gagangir area of Ganderbal district. The Employees’ Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) has added 18 lakh 53 thousand net members in the month of August this year. The Regional Connectivity Scheme – Ude Desh Ka Aam Nagrik completes eight years of its launch today. The scheme was launched on this day in 2016 by the Ministry of Civil Aviation. The UDAN scheme focuses on improving unserved air routes in under-served regions of the country and fulfilling the aspirations of the common citizens. Under it, the first UDAN flight was inaugurated by Prime Minister Narendra Modi on 27th of April 2017.

Since the launch of the scheme, over 600  UDAN routes have been operationalized. and more than one crore forty four lakh passengers have travelled by flights. This demonstrates the success of the scheme in enhancing air travel accessibility. The scheme has so far connected 34 States and Union territories  across the length and breadth of the country. Under the scheme, ten airports have also been operationalized in the Northeast region in addition to two heliports. RCS-UDAN is not only providing last mile connectivity to Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities but also stands as a prominent contributor to the tourism sector. The scheme  has become a game changer for many airports by triggering air connectivity and regional growth. With Anand Kuamr, this is Bhanu Pratap Singh, Akashvani News Delhi.

Maldivian President Dr Mohamed Muizzu has decided to introduce India’s Unified Payment Interface in Maldives. The President’s office announced that the decision was taken on the recommendation of the Cabinet.  Indian tourists will be able to make transactions in Indian rupees on UPI-enabled apps without going through the hassle of converting the  currency.

The decision of introducing UPI is expected to boost the Maldivian economy by increasing financial inclusion, enhancing transaction efficiency, and strengthening digital infrastructure. In addition, Indian tourists travelling to Maldives will benefit by using UPI-enabled apps without needing currency conversion. Dr. Muizzu plans to establish a consortium involving banks, telecom companies, and fintech firms to oversee the implementation. This move aligns with India’s broader efforts to share its developmental experiences globally. Dr. Ahmed Muyeen Farooqi, Colombo.

Lavrov made the remarks during an interview with the state-owned TASS news agency yesterday. Odisha is gearing up to tackle possible disasters due to the severe cyclonic storm ‘Dana’ which is heading towards its coast and likely to make landfall on Thursday morning. The state government has cancelled leaves of all officers and staff in view of the impending cyclonic storm and asked them to remain alert at their headquarters. The exact location of the landfall of the severe cyclonic storm is yet to be predicted by IMD.

The Odisha government has planned for massive evacuation of people living in low lying areas of coastal districts as the severe cyclonic storm ‘Dana’ is likely to make a landfall on Thursday morning off Odisha coast. District and concerned departments have been instructed to operate control rooms on a 24×7 basis. Cyclone shelters are being kept in readiness, while provision of drinking water during and lighting arrangements are being done. Fishermen have been warned not to venture into the sea off Odisha Coast from  today till Saturday. However, the public has been advised to be alert but need not panic. Meanwhile, the IMD has predicted rainfall in most of the coastal districts of Odisha from today due to the cyclonic formation. Prakash Dash, Akashvani News, Bhubaneswar.

The severe cyclonic storm ‘Dana’ is expected to make a landfall between Odisha’s Puri and West Bengal’s Sagar Island on the intervening night of Thursday and Friday with a wind speed of about 120 km per hour. The India Meteorological Department (IMD), in its latest cyclone bulletin, said the well-marked low-pressure area over east-central Bay of Bengal will intensify into a depression by tomorrow morning.

Talking to Akashvani News, IMD scientist, Dr R K Jenamani said, the depression will then become a cyclonic storm by Wednesday over east-central Bay of Bengal.

Thereafter, it will cross north Odisha and West Bengal coasts between Puri and Sagar Island during the night of Thursday and early morning on Friday as a severe cyclonic storm.

The IMD has forecast extremely heavy rainfall in most coastal districts and some interior areas of Odisha and moderate rainfall over the southern districts of West Bengal from Wednesday to Friday.

Union Cabinet Secretary, Dr. T.V. Somanathan today chaired a meeting of National Crisis Management Committee to review preparedness for the impending cyclone. During the meeting, he assured the Government of Odisha and West Bengal that all Central agencies are on full alert and will be available for assistance. Dr. Somanathan advised the states of Andhra Pradesh, Chhattisgarh and Jharkhand also to be prepared to handle any situation due to heavy rainfall.

During the meeting, the Chief Secretaries of Odisha and West Bengal apprised the Committee of the preparatory measures being taken in view of the cyclonic storm. National Disaster Response Force has kept 14 teams in West Bengal and 11 teams in Odisha on standby for deployment. Rescue and relief teams of the Army, Navy and Coast Guard along with ships and aircraft have been kept in readiness.
