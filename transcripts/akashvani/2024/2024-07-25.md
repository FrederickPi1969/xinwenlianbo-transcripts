## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi said India remains committed to elevating the bilateral ties with UK. After meeting visiting UK Foreign Secretary David Lammy in New Delhi last evening, Mr Modi also welcomed the desire to conclude a mutually beneficial  Free Trade Agreement. In a social media post, Mr Modi said that he appreciates the priority accorded by his UK counterpart Keir Starmer to broaden and deepen the Comprehensive Strategic Partnership between the two countries.

The Ministry of External Affairs said that Mr. Lammy held bilateral consultations with External Affairs Minister Dr S Jaishankar. Both Ministers reviewed the Roadmap 2030 agreed between the Prime Ministers during the India-UK Virtual Summit 2021.

“The two sides appreciated the substantial progress made in the India-UK Foreign Trade Agreement negotiations and looked forward to its early conclusion to achieve a mutually beneficial FTA. They also agreed to deepen defence and security cooperation, in the Indo-Pacific and beyond, and to enhance capacities to address growing threats from non-state actors. During the meeting, Dr. Jaishankar and Mr. Lammy also exchanged views on regional and global issues of mutual interest, including the Russia-Ukraine conflict, the situation in West Asia and the Red Sea, the Commonwealth and the UN Security Council. PALLAVI PARMAR, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.”

Mr. Lammy also met with National Security Adviser Ajit Doval.  The visit comes within the first month of the formation of the UK’s new government. It is reflective of the commitment of both sides to further deepen and diversify the India-UK Comprehensive Strategic Partnership.

Union Minister for Women and Child Development Annapurna Devi has said that the Gender Budget allocation towards  welfare and empowerment of women in 2024-25 is 3.3 lakh crore rupees. Interacting with media in New Delhi, the Minister said that the allocation is 37.3 per cent higher than the financial year  2023-24. She said that this reflects the government’s resolve towards women empowerment. She added that in line with the resolve taken by Prime Minister Narendra Modi to build a developed India, the Government is moving from Women’s Development to Women Led Development.

Union Railway Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw announced that the central government has allocated a budget of 15 thousand 940 crores rupees for Maharashtra for the financial year 2024-25, marking a 13.5 percent increase compared to the budget under the previous NDA government.

Mr Ashwini Vaishnaw also said that the total budgetary allocation for Railways in Andhra Pradesh is a staggering amount of 9,151 crore rupees for the year 2024-25. Addressing the media, the Minister stated that the budget allocations to Andhra Pradesh have been witnessing sustained increase with current year’s allocation being almost ten times the average allocation done during the period of 2009-14 for the combined.

In Punjab, Railways has unveiled a significant enhancement in its infrastructure and development projects for Punjab, reflecting a robust commitment to modernizing the rail network and bolstering regional connectivity. Our correspondent reports that the budget allocation for Punjab’s railways has seen a tremendous increase. The annual average budget outlay has surged from 225 crore rupees during 2009- 2014 to an impressive 5,147 crore rupees for the 2024- 25 fiscal year.

Minister of State for Electronics and Information Technology Jitin Prasada has said that the Government is aware of the need to create guardrails to ensure that Artificial Intelligence, AI, is safe and trusted. In a written reply in the Lok Sabha yesterday, Mr Prasad said that  the Central Government has notified the Information Technology  Rules,  in 2021 which were subsequently amended in 2022 and 2023.

The Minister added that the IT Rules, 2021, cast specific legal obligations on intermediaries, including social media intermediaries and platforms, to ensure their accountability towards safe and trusted internet including removal of the prohibited misinformation and deep fakes.

Jammu Kashmir has not witnessed a single incident of organised stone pelting and organised Hartal since 2023 and the foreign tourist’s arrival has increased by 2.5 times in the Union Territory. The overall Law and Order scenario in Jammu and Kashmir has seen remarkable improvement after abrogation of Article 370.

In a written reply in the Rajya Sabha, Minister of State in Home Nityanand Rai said that after abrogation of Article 370, the Union Territory of Jammu and Kashmir has witnessed an era of peace, progress and prosperity. Schools, colleges, universities, hospitals and other public institutions are functioning efficiently without any strikes or any kind of disturbances during the last four years. Due to improved law and order, Jammu and Kashmir recorded 2.11 Crore tourist visits in 2023.

Government has  clarified that there is no proposal to discontinue NEET (UG) and restore state-wise entrance examinations at present amid controversies over the medical examination. This was stated by Minister of State for Education, Dr Sukanta Majumdar in a written reply in the Rajya Sabha yesterday.

In Ladakh, 58 Panchayats have achieved the status of being ‘TB Mukt’ under the nationwide initiative launched by Prime Minister Narendra Modi. The announcement came after rigorous verification process conducted by the Central TB Division and the Ministry of Panchayati Raj.

“Launched on World TB Day in Varanasi on March 24, last year, the ‘TB Mukt Panchayat’ initiative seeks to raise awareness about the challenges posed by Tuberculosis. It is  aimed at empowering Panchayati Raj Institutions to combat Tuberculosis effectively. The impressive achievement of 58 Panchayats in Ladakh reflects the region’s unwavering commitment to public health and the relentless efforts of its local leaders and health workers. This milestone is part of a larger national success, with 17,490 Gram Panchayats across India qualifying for the ‘TB Mukt Panchayat’ status for the year 2023. ANAYAT ALI, FOR KARGIL AKASHVANI NEWS, .”

## Midday News

The discussion on the Union Budget 2024-25 resumed in both Houses of Parliament,  today. Union Minister Gajendra Singh Shekhawat, Jitendra Singh, Arjun Ram Meghwal and Kirtivardhan Singh laid papers on the table.

In Lok Sabha, Congress and TMC today objected to the comments made by BJP member Abhijit Gangopadhyay during his speech on the discussion over the General Budget in the house yesterday. The House had witnessed heated arguments between him and Congress MP Gaurav Gogoi. When the Lower House met this morning, members from Congress and TMC raised the issue demanding action against the member. Parliamentary Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju said remarks made by the MP are not appropriate and against the  dignity of the House. Speaker Om Birla said, a warning has been given to the MP over his comments.  He urged the members to maintain dignity in the House.

Participating in the discussion, Randeep Singh Surjewala of Congress raised the issues of farmers, poor and unemployment. He said that no provisions were made in the Budget for waiving of the farmers’ loans. He also said the government is not providing adequate MSP on foodgrains to the farmers. Discussion is underway.

Parliamentary Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju today hit out at the opposition leaders accusing that they insulted the mandate of the country. Addressing a press conference in New Delhi, Mr Rijiju said, the country wants to see that the discussion on the budget takes place in a good and meaningful manner. He added that the way the leaders of some parties commented and the way they gave speeches, have insulted the House by lowering it dignity. He called upon the opposition parties to respect the mandate of the people. Quoting Prime Minister Narendra Modi, Mr Rijiju said the elections are over and now the focus should be to work towards a Viksit Bharat and hold constructive discussion on the Union Budget.

He alleged that the opposition leaders did not say anything on the budget,but  they just indulged in politics and abused the Prime Minister.

The Parliamentary Affairs Minister said, the budget should be discussed in the budget session. He questioned the opposition running away from discussing the provisions in the Budget made for farmers and tribal people.

Union Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution Minister Pralhad Joshi today demanded the resignation of Karnataka Chief Minister Siddaramaiah on the alleged Mysore Urban Development Authority scam.

Mr Joshi was talking to media outside the Parliament House. He alleged that the Chief Minister is directly involved in the scam and the matter should be handed over to the CBI.

Minister of State for Petroleum and Natural Gas Suresh Gopi has said that the total sales of CNG vehicles has increased over the past few years. He said, the total number of vehicles using CNG registered till 22nd of this month is over 72 lakh 10 thousand across the country. In a written reply in the Lok Sabha today, Mr Gopi said, establishment of Compressed Natural Gas CNG stations is part of the development of City Gas Distribution network and it is carried out by the entities authorised by Petroleum and Natural Gas Regulatory Board. He said, the authorised entities develop the CNG infrastructure in their Geographical Areas as per their Minimum Work Plan and techno-commercial feasibility.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will visit Kargil War Memorial tomorrow on the occasion of the 25th Kargil Vijay Diwas. He will pay homage to the soldiers who made  supreme sacrifice in the line of duty.  Mr. Modi will also virtually carry out the first blast of the Shinkun La Tunnel Project.  This 4.1 Kilometer long twin tube tunnel will be constructed at an altitude of around Fifteen thousand 800 feet on the Nimu-Padum-Darcha Road to provide all weather connectivity to Leh. Once completed, it will be the highest tunnel in the world. Shinkun La tunnel will not only ensure swift and efficient movement of our armed forces and equipment but also foster economic and social development in Ladakh.

A day ahead of the 25th anniversary of Kargil Vijay Diwas, war veterans, relatives of defence personnel and local people attended a gathering at Lamochen point in Drass to commemorate the historic win of the country in the 1999 Kargil war.

The War Memorial in Drass is all set to host the grand celebration of the Rajat Jayanti of Kargil Vijay Diwas.

Information and Broadcasting Minister, Ashwini Vaishnaw today inaugurated India’s 500th Community Radio Station through video conferencing in presence of Minister of State for Information and Broadcasting Dr. L. Murugan and Chief Minister of Mizoram Lalduhoma. The station, Apna Radio 90.0 FM is a station run by Indian Institute of Mass Communication, Aizawl.

On the occasion, Mr Vaishnaw announced the winners of 10th National Community Radio Awards.

In Jharkhand a draft of the voter’s list has been published today. Jharkhand Chief Electoral Officer K. Ravi Kumar said this voter list has been displayed by Booth Level Officers at all polling stations.

He appealed to all the voters to go to their polling stations to check their names in this draft voter list, if there is any discrepancy, then immediately inform your Booth Level Officer. People can check their name from home through the online mediums of Election Commission like Voter Helpline App or Voter Service Portal.

The Rouse Avenue Court in Delhi today extended the judicial custody of Delhi Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal till 8th of August  in connection with the excise policy case. Mr. Kejriwal faced the setback in the Central Bureau of Investigation’s or CBI’s complaint in the case. He was produced before the court via video conference from Tihar Jail.

Earlier, this month, the Supreme Court had granted interim bail to Mr. Kejriwal on 12th of July in the money laundering case filed by the Enforcement Directorate concerning the now-scrapped excise policy. However, the Chief Minister remained in Tihar jail as he was separately arrested by the CBI in a corruption case related to the alleged scam.

President Droupadi Murmu today taught the students of the ninth class of Dr. Rajendra Prasad Kendriya Vidyalaya in President’s Estate, New Delhi, on completion of her two years of presidency. During her interaction with the students, she discussed about the different problems arising in the modern world. Pointing towards the grim challenges posed by climate change and global warming, the President asked students to follow the 3R rule, reduce, reuse, and recycle to protect the environment.

The President also asked students to inform people about these problems and contribute towards making this world a better place.

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has forecast extremely heavy to very heavy rainfall over Western and Central parts of the country for the next few days. The IMD said that Gujarat, Konkan, Goa, and Maharashtra will continue to witness extremely heavy rainfall till tomorrow and heavy to very heavy rainfall thereafter. Isolated places of Coastal Karnataka and Odisha are also expected to witness very heavy rainfall till tomorrow.

In Delhi, the weather department has forecast a generally cloudy sky with moderate rain till 28th of this month.

Maharashtra is currently grappling with severe rainfall, particularly in the districts of Mumbai, Pune, Thane, Palghar, Kolhapur and Raigad. More from our Mumbai correspondent:-

Heavy rains have disturbed normal life in Pune. Some areas in pune have experienced torrential rainfall exceeding 400 mm. Mumbai too has faced heavy downpours, with certain areas recording over 200 mm of rain in the past 24 hours. Mumbai has faced significant waterlogging, disrupting bus routes and causing delays in suburban train services. Few rivers in raigad districts are flowing above danger level and Pune-Raigad road is closed due to a landslide. In response, local authorities have declared a holiday for schools in Pune, Thane, Palghar, and Raigad districts and several other places. Maharashtra Chief Minister, Eknath Shinde, has announced the deployment of two NDRF teams to Pune for rescue operations, with additional support from the Army in affected areas. To ensure public safety, citizens have been urged to remain indoors unless absolutely necessary.  As per records of the state government, 97 people have lost lives and 132 are injured due to rain related incidents in this monsoon season in Maharashtra. Jeevan Bhawasar, Akashvani News, Mumbai.

In Gujarat, Orange alert of heavy rainfall has been issued for Surat, Navsari, Valsad, Daman and Dadra nagar haveli. Limkheda in Dahod district recorded the highest rainfall of nearly 32 mm. of rainfall during the last 6 to 12 hours. Several places in Kaprada and Dharampur of Valsad district recorded more than 29 mm.of rainfall resulting in road water logging. More than 4000 people have been shifted to safer places across 7 districts, while 535 persons have been rescued.

In the Paris Olympics 2024, Indian archers are competing in the individual ranking round in Les Invalides in Paris.

The women’s ranking round is underway and will be followed by the men’s ranking round, starting at 5:45 p.m. IST. Archers Deepika Kumari, Ankita Bhakat, and Bhajan Kaur are representing India in the women’s individual event.

External Affairs Minister Dr S Jaishankar today reached Vientiane, Laos for a three days visit to take part to participate in the Foreign Ministers’ Meetings under the ASEAN union. Dr Jaishankar said through a social media post that he is looking forward to further deepening India’s ties with ASEAN as India marks a decade of Act East Policy.

The External Affairs Minister is also expected to hold bilateral meetings with his counterparts from other countries in Vientiane, on the margins of the ASEAN-related meetings.

The External Affairs Ministry said that the visit underlines India’s deep engagement and importance to the ASEAN-centred regional architecture along with its strong commitment to ASEAN unity, ASEAN centrality, the ASEAN Outlook on the Indo-Pacific (AOIP) and taking forward the ASEAN-India Comprehensive Strategic Partnership.

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu addressed the U.S. Congress in Washington, D.C. this morning. In his speech, he sought to strengthen U.S. support, particularly for weapons supplies, amid increasing international criticism after over nine months of conflict in the Palestinian enclave. He also mentioned a possible future partnership between Israel and the Arab nations to broaden the West Asia security alliance

Netanyahu said that the anti-Israel protesters stand with Hamas and “should be ashamed of themselves.Hamas-led fighters triggered the war on 7th October  by storming into southern Israel, killing 1,200 people and taking 250 captives, according to Israeli tallies.
