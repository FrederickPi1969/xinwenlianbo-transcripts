## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has left on a two-day visit to Russia beginning today to attend the 16th Brazil, Russia, India, China and South Africa (BRICS) Summit in Kazan. The Summit is themed on Strengthening Multilateralism for Just Global Development and Security. Speaking to Akashvani Correspondent in Kazan, India’s Ambassador to Russia, Vinay Kumar said, the summit will provide an important platform for leaders to discuss key global issues. It will also assess the progress of initiatives launched by BRICS.  Mr. Kumar said, Prime Minister Modi will hold bilateral meeting with Russian President Valadimir Putin.

The notification for the second phase of Assembly elections will be issued today in Jharkhand. Thirty-eight constituencies will go to polls in this phase on 20th of November.

29th October is the last date of filing of nominations. Candidates can file their nominations from 11 AM to 3 PM. Scrutiny of nominations will be done on 30th October. Last date for withdrawal of candidature is 1st November. Assembly elections in Jharkhand are being held in two phases. The second phase of polling will take place on 20th November, while the poll is for the first phase is on 13th November. Counting of votes will be taken up on 23rd November.

Meanwhile, nomination is picking up for the first phase of polls. 25 candidates have so far filed their nomination papers. Last date of making nominations is 25th October.With K K Lal from Jharkhand, Ashish Abraham, Akashvani News, Delhi.

The notification for assembly elections in Maharashtra will be issued today. A single phase polling for all 288 assembly seats in the state will be held on 20th November. The last date for filing nominations is 29th October and scrutiny of nominations will be done on 30th October. The last date for withdrawal of nominations is 4th November.

“Out of a total 288 seats, Maharashtra has 234 general constituencies. There are 25 ST seats and 29 SC seats. Maharashtra has a total of over 9 crore 63 lakh voters, of which 4 crore 97 lakh are male and 4 crore 66 lakh are female voters. The bypoll for the Nanded Lok Sabha seat will also be held on 20th November. Considering that six Maharashtra parliamentary constituencies were among the top 10 urban Lok Sabha seats with the lowest voter turnout in the country, the Election Commission has made targeted campaigns and efforts to increase the voting percentage in the coming polls. Prarthna, Akashvani News, Mumbai.”

President Droupadi Murmu will confer the 5th National Water Awards 2023 in New Delhi today. Union Jal Shakti Ministry has announced 38 winners in nine categories. This includes Best State, Best District, Best Village Panchayat, Best Urban Local Body, Best Water User Association and Best Civil Society.

In the category of Best State, the first prize has been conferred upon Odisha, with Uttar Pradesh securing the second position, and Gujarat and Puducherry jointly securing the third position. The National Water Awards focus on the good work and efforts made by individuals and the organizations across the country in attaining the government’s vision of a Jal Samridh Bharat. Each award winner will be conferred with a citation and a trophy as well as cash prizes in certain categories. Government has undertaken a comprehensive campaign to spread awareness about water management and water conservation on a national level. With Dipendra Kumar, Anupam Mishra, Akashwani News Delhi.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah will inaugurate the 14th All India Civil Defence and Home Guards conference at Mahatma Mandir in Gandhinagar today. Mr. Shah who is on a visit to Gujarat will also attend the 60th foundation day of the National Dairy Development Board (NDDB) in Anand. The Minister will also launch three new projects of NDDB on the occasion.

Later, Mr. Shah will attend the legislative drafting training worksop at Gujarat Assembly and will address the legislators.

Our correspondent reports that the 14th India Civil Defence and Home Gurads conference is being organised after the gap of 19 years.

More than 60 senior officials and nearly 1200 members of civil defense and home guard from various states and union territories will participate in the conference. Director General of Civil Defense and Home Guard Vivek Srivastav said the conference will mainly discuss the draft Civil Defence bill and Model Home Guard Bill along with the policy of both the forces. Apart from this, the conference will also discuss the scope of modern technology to bring more efficiency in the functioning of both the forces.

At present, there are more than 4.5 lakh Home Guard 6 lakh Civil defence guards in the country. Aparna Khunt, Akashvani News, Ahmedabad.

Various issues were discussed including the justice for the victim of the R G Kar Medical College incident. Following the meeting with the Chief Minister, the junior doctors have withdrawn their hunger strike. Chief secretary and home secretary were present in the meeting which was telecast live.

The two day national level Amaravati Drone Summit 2024 will begin today in Andhra Pradesh capital Amaravati. Chief Minister N Chandrababu Naidu and Union Minister for Civil Aviation K Ram Mohan Naidu will participate in the inaugural function.

“Over 1000 delegates and exhibiters will participate in the flagship Amaravati Drone Summit being organized by Andhra Pradesh Drone Corporation. This summit will bring together representatives from Civil Aviation Ministry, Defense sector, Confederation of Indian Industry, Research scholars and entrepreneurs in the field. The summit aims to attract investments in drone manufacturing, create a platform to foster discussions on future of drone technology and strategies for building a drone eco-system through innovation hubs. The summit will feature India’s largest drone show with over 5000 drones on the banks of River Krishna in Vijayawada. The Andhra Pradesh government aims to leverage this summit to position the state as the drone capital of the country. Sai Sistla, Akashvani news, Vijayawada.”

In view of the deteriorating Air quality in Delhi and the NCR region, Stage-2 of Graded Response Action Plan (GRAP) Action Plan invoked across the National Capital Region from 8 AM today. The decision was taken during the meeting of the sub-committee of the Commission for Air Quality Management in NCR and Adjoining Areas in New Delhi. The Committee has urged citizens to avoid dust generating construction activities during October to January. It has also asked to avoid the open burning of solid waste and biomass.

An 11-point action plan will be applicable under Stage 2 of GRAP with immediate effect in the entire NCR. Under the action plan, the agency will carry out mechanical and vacuum sweeping and water sprinkling of the identified roads on daily basis.

The severe cyclonic storm ‘Dana’ is heading towards Odisha coast as the cyclonic circulation is gaining strength in Bay of Bengal. Though the Indian Meteorological Department (IMD) is yet to predict the exact location of the landfall of the severe cyclonic storm, many weather models have said it is expected to make landfall between Bhitarkanika and Dhamra off Odisha coast on the intervening night of Thursday and Friday with a wind speed of about 120 km per hour.

“The Odisha government has announced a goal of ‘100 percent evacuations’ from potentially impacted areas likely to be badly affected due the severe cyclonic storm ‘Dana’.The severe cyclonic strom is expected to make a landfall in between Bhitarkanika and Dhamra off Odisha coast on the intervening night of Thursday and Friday. Rainfall activity is set to commence from tomorrow, with IMD issuing a red alert for coastal districts, including Mayurbhanj, Keonjhar, Balasore, and Bhadrak, on Thursday. The Odisha government has directed district administrations to ready over 800 multipurpose cyclone shelters equipped with essential facilities like electricity, food, and water. The IMD has warned of potential flash floods, with very heavy rainfalls in Mayurbhanj, Cuttack, Jajpur, Balasore, Bhadrak, Kendrapara, Khordha, Keonjhar, Puri and Jagatsinghpur from Thursday. Prakash Dash, Akashvani News, Bhubaneswar.”

In the Men’s T20 Emerging Teams Asia Cup Cricket, India A defeated United Arab Emirates by seven wickets in Al Amerat yesterday. Electing to bat first, UAE were all out for just 107 runs with 19 balls remaining. Rahul Chopra emerged as the top scorer for UAE who made 50 off 50 balls. In response to this, Abhishek Sharma’s 58 off 24 balls helped the young India A in chasing the total easily in 11th over, along with seven wickets in hand. For India A, Rasikh Salam took three wickets by just giving 15 runs in his two-over spell. He was declared Player of the Match for his economic bowling figures.

All-rounder Washington Sundar has been added to Indian Men’s Cricket Team for the remainder of the Test series against New Zealand. Sundar will join the squad for the upcoming 2nd and 3rd Test Match in Pune and Mumbai.

Indian Women’s Cricket Team skipper Harmanpreet Kaur has been named in the ICC Women’s T20 World Cup 2024 Team of the Tournament. She is the only player from India in the 12-member squad announced by the ICC, which includes players from seven countries. Despite India’s disappointing campaign in the recently held T20 World Cup, Harmanpreet Kaur was the standout performer for the Women in Blue.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi held  a bilateral meeting with Russian President Vladimir Putin in Kazan on the sidelines of the 16th BRICS Summit this afternoon.

During his meeting, Prime Minister Modi said, his recent visits to Russia reflect their close coordination and deep friendship. He said, the relations between the two countries will get better with the opening of the new consulate in Kazan.

Speaking on the occasion, President Putin welcomed the Indian government’s decision to open its Consulate in Kazan.

On the Russia-Ukraine conflict, Prime Minister Modi reiterated India’s stance that the issue should be resolved in a peaceful manner.

Later, External Affairs Ministry’s Spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal informed that during the meeting, Mr. Modi appreciated the Russian chairmanship of BRICS and conveyed his best wishes for the success of the summit. He added that the two leaders reviewed the entire spectrum of the India-Russia relationship. They also shared perspectives on regional and global issues, including the ongoing conflict in Ukraine. India and Singapore have agreed to enhance industry cooperation, especially in niche domains such as automation and artificial intelligence. Both ministers expressed satisfaction with the growing defence cooperation between the two countries. Both sides agreed to boost defence cooperation in emerging areas like cybersecurity. They also agreed to extend the bilateral agreement on joint military training for the next five years. During the meeting, the Defence Minister of Singapore acknowledged that India is a strategic voice for Asia’s peace and stability.

Dr. Ng Eng Hen also called on President Droupadi Murmu at Rashtrapati Bhavan today. Welcoming Dr. Hen, the President said that India and Singapore have a rich history of bilateral cooperation. President Murmu expressed happiness that the relationship has been elevated to a Comprehensive Strategic Partnership. She also emphasized the need for closer collaboration between the defence research and development teams of both countries to benefit from the latest expertise and technological advancements in the defence domain.

The severe cyclonic storm ‘Dana’ is gaining strength in the Bay of Bengal and is heading towards the Odisha coast at 4 km per hour, with a landfall expected on the intervening night of this Thursday and Friday. ‘Dana’ is anticipated to make landfall between Bhitarkanika in Kendrapara and Bhadrak or Balasore off the Odisha coast. The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has said that the depression formed over the east-central Bay of Bengal is likely to move west-northwestward and intensify into a cyclonic storm by tomorrow morning over the east-central Bay of Bengal. Thereafter, it is likely to intensify into a severe cyclonic storm over the northwest Bay of Bengal by Thursday morning. The severe cyclonic storm is expected to strike the Odisha coast with wind speeds of 100 to 110 km per hour, gusting up to the 120 km per hour at the time of landfall.

“The Odisha government has started evacuations from potentially impacted areas likely to be badly affected by ‘Dana’. Six IAS officers have been given the charge of relief and rescue operations for six districts likely to be more affected by the severe cyclone. All schools, colleges and universities in 14 coastal and neighboring districts will remain closed for three days from tomorrow, while all scheduled examinations have also been cancelled. The state government has also cancelled leaves of all the doctors. The East Coast Railways has cancelled 198 trains for three days from tomorrow. Over 500 multipurpose cyclone shelters equipped with essential facilities have been readied in the coastal districts. The state government has asked the Centre for more NDRF teams to deploy in red zone districts. Fishermen have been banned from entering sea from tomorrow.  Prakash Dash, Akashvani News, Bhubaneswar.”
