## Midday News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that India has emerged as the world’s third largest startup ecosystem with over 1.25 lakh startups and 110 unicorns. Addressing the ‘Startup Mahakumbh’ at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi today, he said, India’s Startup ecosystem is flourishing at an unprecedented pace backed by the innovative spirit of country’s youth power. Elaborating on space startups, Mr Modi said that Indian startups are working in more than 50 areas in the space sector, including the launch of the space shuttle.
The Prime Minister said that today, when the country is working on the roadmap for a developed India by 2047, this Startup Mahakumbh is of great importance. He said, in the last decade, India has seen as to how it has made strides in the IT and software sector  and now, people are seeing the trend of innovation and start-up culture in India.
Mr Modi mentioned that Startup India initiative has given a  platform to innovative ideas and  connected them to funding. He added that India’s youth has chosen the  path of being job creator rather than job seeker.
The Prime Minister asked the entrepreneurs to be vigilant about filing their patents quickly.  He said, Startups have made a turnover of over 20 thousand crore rupees over  GeM portal which is a big achievement. He said that the Startup revolution is being led by small cities and that too in a wide range of sectors including agriculture, textiles, medicine, transport, space, yoga and ayurveda.
The Prime Minister expressed delight that more than 45 percent of start-ups in the country are being led by women. Mr Modi emphasized the importance of the culture of innovation for humanity, not just for Viksit Bharat.

The Election Commission has issued notification for the first phase of Lok Sabha elections today. The elections will be held in 102 Lok Sabha seats in 17 States and Four Union Territories in this phase. Thirty-Nine seats of Tamil Nadu, 12 seats of Rajasthan, eight seats of Uttar Pradesh, six seats of Madhya Pradesh, five seats each in Uttarakhand, Assam and Maharashtra, four seats of Bihar and three seats of West Bengal will go to the polls in this first phase. Polls will also be held in two seats each in Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya and one seats each in Chhattisgarh, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tripura, Anadaman and Nicobar Islands, Jammu and Kashmir, Lakshadweep and Puducherry in this first phase.
Nominations can be filed till 27th of this month. However, for Bihar, the last date for filing nominations is the 28th of this month. The scrutiny of nominations will be done on the 28th of this month, and the last date for the withdrawal of candidatures is March 30th. Whereas in Bihar, the scrutiny of nominations will be done on the 30th of this month and the last date for withdrawal of candidature is April 2. Voting will be held on April 19.

In Rajasthan, the process of filing nominations has started today with the issuance of poll notifications for 12 Lok Sabha constituencies in the state which are going to poll in the first phase. The  filing of nominations will continue till March 27 in Sri Ganganagar, Bikaner, Churu, Nagaur, Sikar, Jhunjhunu, Alwar, Jaipur, Jaipur Rural, Bharatpur, Dausa and Karauli-Dholpur constituencies. District election officers have imposed Section 144 in the respective districts. No person, Political parties, organisation will be able to hold a procession, rally or a public meeting without valid permission. Five persons including the candidate- will be allowed in the returning officers’ room during filing the nomination. For the total 25 Lok Sabha seats in the state, BJP has declared 15 candidates while Congress has announced their candidates on 10 seats. Brainstorming is underway on for both the parties to announce the candidates for the remaining seats.-Jitendra Dwivedi, Akashwani News, Jaipur

In view of the Lok Sabha elections and Assembly polls in four States, the Election Commission of India has notified the category of electors mentioned for the purpose of facility of voting through postal ballot as absentee voters in the category of persons employed on essential services. The list includes the employees of Metro, Railways, BSNL, Electricity and Health Departments, Fire Services, Postal Services, Aviation, Disaster Management, Doordarshan and Akashvani and media persons authorised by the Commission for polling day coverage among others. The Commission said that as per the provisions of the Representation of the Prople Act, 1951 and on due consideration of the matter, it has notified the persons employed in the essential services as a class of persons  to cast  vote by postal ballot on being on duty on the day of poll in the current General Elections and Legislative Assemblies of Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal  Pradesh, Odisha and  Sikkim, and bye-elections in 26 Assembly Constituencies in 13 States.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah has expressed confidence that the National Democratic Alliance (NDA) will win over 400 seats in the Lok Sabah election and the BJP itself cross the 370 seats mark. Speaking to a  private television channel, Mr. Shah today said, the Narendra Modi government has taken several steps to uplift the poor by providing them basic amenities.  He said, Indian economy scaled a new height in the last ten years by becoming the fifth largest economy in the world.

Lok Janshakti Party (Ram Vilas) Chief Chirag Paswan has announced that he will contest the Lok Sabha elections from Hajipur seat in Bihar. Out of the 40 Parliamentary Constituencies in the State, as per the agreement, LJP (Ram Vilas) will contest five seats as part of NDA ally. Talking to reporters in New Delhi, Chirag Paswan said that discussion on the selection of the candidates is going on within the party and within four to five days, the name of the candidates on the remaining seats will be announced.

External Affairs Minister Dr S Jaishankar has termed the upcoming 18th edition of the Lok Sabha elections as the largest electoral logistics exercise that this planet has ever witnessed. In a virtual statement on the Third Edition of the Summit for Democracy organised by the Republic of Korea in Seoul, Dr. Jaishankar said that the country today has 1.4 million women constituting the largest cohort of elected women representatives at the grassroots level in any part of the world. He said, the government’s recent decision to extend thirty-three percent reservation for women in both parliament and state legislatures recognises the fundamental principle that women’s voices are indispensable in shaping the nation’s destiny.
The Minister said introduction of electronic voting machines is a testament to the country’s commitment to transparency, efficiency, and the sanctity of the democratic process.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will pay a two-day state visit to Bhutan from tomorrow.
During the visit, Mr Modi will receive audience with the King of Bhutan Jigme Khesar Namgyel Wangchuck and the Fourth King of Bhutan Jigme Singye Wangchuck. He will also hold talks with his Bhutanese counterpart Tshering Tobgay. The Ministry of External Affairs said, the visit is in keeping with the tradition of regular high-level exchanges between India and Bhutan and the Government’s emphasis on its Neighbourhood First Policy.

The European Union and the United Nations said Hong Kong’s new national security bill is deeply worrying and could erode fundamental freedoms in the China-ruled city. UN High Commissioner for Human Rights Volker Turk in a statement said that it is alarming that such consequential legislation was rushed through the legislature despite serious concerns raised about the incompatibility of many of its provisions with international human rights law.
The European Union said in a separate statement that it was concerned about the potential impact on the rights and freedoms of the people of Hong Kong.

Former US President Donald Trump’s legal team has filed a brief in the Supreme Court in his bid for criminal immunity, arguing that a former president enjoys “absolute immunity from criminal prosecution for his official acts.” The case is to be argued before the justices on April 25.

Back home,  Prime Minister Narendra Modi has extended greetings to the nation on the occasion of Navroz today. In a social media post on X, he expressed hope that the coming year would be filled with success and growth and enhance the bond of unity in society. Mr. Modi wished joy and well-being for all.

In West Bengal, Income Tax Department is carrying out raids at the house of Swarup Biswas at New Alipur in Kolkata. Swarup Biswas is the brother of state Minister Arup Biswas.
Income Tax team is also carrying out raids in Behala, Pornorshri and other areas of Kolkata. They are also searching the houses of officials of Eden Real Estate and Multicon Real Estate.

India Meteorological Department (IMD) has forecast moderate rainfall in Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand and Bihar today. It said, moderate rainfall accompanied by thunderstorms, lightning, and gusty winds is expected over many parts of India, including the East, Central, and adjoining peninsulas. The winds gusting up to 40 kilometers per hour will also likely to prevail over East Uttar Pradesh today.

Heavy rainfall occurred in several parts of Odisha during the past 24 hrs. The Met department said thunderstorm with lightning and gusty surface wind speed reaching 30-40 kmph very likely to occur at one or two places over the districts of Balasore, Bhadrak, Jajpur, Kendrapada, Jagatsinghpur, Cuttack, Gajapati, Khordha, Nayagarh, and Puri. It has issued an Orange Warning for these districts. Heavy rains accompanied by strong winds uprooted huge trees in Dhenkanal town.
The Bhubaneswar Met Centre has issued advisory to keep watch on weather and take safe shelter during the thunderstorm activity to protect themselves from lightning.

In Swiss Open Badminton, Lakshya Sen, PV Sindhu, Kidambi Srikant, Kiran George, Priyanshu Rajawat, and other elite shuttlers will start their campaigns today.
Earlier in the Men’s Singles Qualifiers, Sathish Kumar Karunakaran defeated Uriel Canjura of El Salvador, while Sameer Verma beat Coelho de Oliveira of Brazil.
In the Women’s Doubles category, India’s Gayatri Gopichand and Treesa Jolly have advanced to the pre-quarters yesterday. They triumphed over the American duo of Kerry Xu and Annie Xu in the Round of 32.
