## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will hold talks with Crown Prince of Abu Dhabi, Sheikh Khaled bin Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan in New Delhi today. Both leaders are expected to hold discussions on a wide range of areas of bilateral cooperation between India and UAE.

Sheikh Khaled bin Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan reached New Delhi last evening on his first visit to India as the Crown Prince of Abu Dhabi. He is accompanied by several Ministers from the UAE government and a business delegation.

Tomorrow, the Crown Prince of Abu Dhabi will visit Mumbai to participate in a Business Forum, in which business leaders from both countries will participate.

“India and UAE share historically close and friendly ties. In recent years, the Comprehensive Strategic Partnership between India and UAE has deepened in a wide range of areas including political, trade, investment, connectivity, energy, technology, education, and culture. The Crown Prince’s visit will further reinforce strong India-UAE bilateral relations and open avenues for partnership in new and emerging areas. India-UAE trade reached 85 billion dollars in 2022-23 making UAE, India’s third largest trading partner for the year 2022-23 after China and US. With Anand Kumar, Suparna Saikia, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar will attend the first India-Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) Foreign Ministers’ Meeting in Riyadh today. This historic gathering aims to strengthen ties between India and the Gulf nations.

“The inaugural GCC Foreign Ministers  meeting will focus on enhancing cooperation across various sectors, including trade, investment, and energy. With the GCC region housing nearly 8.9 million Indian expatriates and serving as a major trading partner, this diplomatic engagement holds significant importance for India’s foreign policy. During his visit, Dr. Jaishankar is also expected to hold bilateral talks with his counterparts from GCC member countries, further solidifying India’s relationships in the region. Dr. Jaishankar visited key cultural sites in Riyadh on the first day of his two day tour to SAUDI ARABIA, emphasizing the historical ties between India and the kingdom. The minister toured the Saudi National Museum, King Abdulaziz Foundation for Research and Archives (DARAH) and Murabba Palace. This is Vinod Kumar for Akashvani news from Dubai.”

The 54th GST Council meeting will be held in New Delhi today.

Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman will chair the meeting.

The last GST Council meeting was held in June this year in which the council had recommended a uniform rate of 12 percent GST on all steel, iron, and aluminum milk cans. The council had also recommended that services provided by the Indian Railways to the common man, including the sale of platform tickets, facility of retiring rooms and waiting rooms, and battery-operated car services, will not attract any GST.

Foreign investors pumped nearly 11,000 crore rupees into the Indian capital markets in the first week of September. As per the depositories data, Foreign Portfolio Investors or FPIs, invested 10,978 crore rupees into equity markets and 367 crore rupees into the debt markets, taking the total net investment into the Indian capital markets to 11,345 crore rupees this month.

This comes after three consecutive months of net inflows into the Indian markets.

The Yogi Adityanath-led Uttar Pradesh government will host Semicon India 2024 at the India Expo Mart in Greater Noida from September 11 to 13. This event, which will be organised in collaboration with SEMI, Messe Muenchen India, and the Electronic Industries Association of India, aims to elevate India’s semiconductor sector and establish the country as a significant global contender. Prime Minister Narendra Modi will officially inaugurate the event on September 11, with Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath also in attendance.

The Headquarters Integrated Defence Staff will conduct maiden Combined Operational Review and Evaluation Programme for officers of Tri-Services in Delhi from today. The five-day development cum orientation programme has been designed for Major General and equivalent officers from the three services, along with officers from the Ministries of Defence, External Affairs, and Home Affairs.

Congress has announced nine more candidates for the ensuing Haryana assembly polls. The party has fielded former MP Brijendra Singh from Uchana Kalan assembly seat against JJP leader Dushyant Chautala. Mohit Grover will contest from Gurugram seat. Earlier, the party had declared 32 candidates for the 90 member state assembly.

A young male patient, who recently travelled from a country currently experiencing Mpox transmission, has been identified as a suspect case of Mpox. Health Ministry informed that the patient has been isolated in a designated hospital and samples from the patient are being tested to confirm the presence of Mpox. It added that the patient is currently stable. The Ministry further said that the case is being managed in line with established protocols, and contact tracing is ongoing to identify potential sources and assess the impact within the country.

The ministry assured that the country is fully prepared to deal with such isolated travel related case and has robust measures in place to manage and mitigate any potential risk.

Sanjeev Sharma, Akashvani News, Bhopal.”

## Midday News

The Supreme Court today directed the Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) to submit a fresh report by 17th September on the probe in the rape and murder case of a postgraduate medic at Kolkata’s RG Kar Medical College and Hospital.

A bench headed by Chief Justice D Y Chandrachud perused the report filed in a sealed cover by Solicitor General Tushar Mehta, appearing for the CBI.

The Bench, also comprising Justices J.B. Pardiwala and Manoj Misra posted the matter for further consideration on 17th of September.

The apex court also directed a senior officer of the West Bengal government’s Home department and a senior CISF officer to ensure all three companies of the CISF, which are deputed for security at the RG Kar Hospital, are given accommodation. It also directed that all requisition, security gadgets needed by the CISF should be handed over to it today.

The CBI said, it will get a separate forensic study done by the AIIMS and other central labs. Solicitor General, Tushar Mehta appeared for the central agency, whereas Senior Advocate Kapil Sibal represented the West Bengal Government.

In Jammu and Kashmir, two terrorists have been killed during an operation by Army against an infiltration bid along the Line of Control (LoC) in Rajouri district today. The gunfight broke out late last night in the Laam sector of Nowshera when Army troops guarding the LoC, picked up terrorist movement trying to infiltrate and challenged them. Massive search operation is underway as it is suspected that at least two more terrorists were hit in the crossfire. The area has been illuminated and kept under strict surveillance. The Army has recovered large quantity of weapons as search ‘Operation Kanchi’ continues.

The 54th GST Council meeting is underway in New Delhi under the chairmanship of Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman. Minister of State for Finance Pankaj Chaudhary and Finance Ministers of States are present in the meeting.

The last GST Council meeting was held in June this year in which the council had recommended a uniform rate of 12 percent GST on all steel, iron, and aluminum milk cans. The council had also recommended that services provided by the Indian Railways to the common man like platform tickets, retiring and waiting rooms and battery-operated car services will not attract any GST.

The Indian Army and Indian Air Force today signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Gati Shakti Vishwavidyalaya to enhance the skills and capability building of personnel in logistics operations. Speaking on the occasion, Railways Minister, Ashwini Vaishnaw said the  Vishwavidyalaya will serve as a vital partner in empowering the Armed Forces with cutting-edge logistics education, research and innovation.

The Centre has asked the States and the Union territories for the screening and testing of all suspect cases of Mpox. Union Health Secretary Apurva Chandra issued an advisory to the States in view of the WHO’s declaration of Public Health Emergency of International Concern related to Mpox.

In its advisory, the Ministry has stressed to create awareness about Mpox and timely reporting and adopting preventive measures to stop undue panic among the masses. The Ministry also advised for the review of public health preparedness particularly at health facility level at the State and Districts by the senior officials.

Union Health Secretary Apurva Chandra today released the annual publication Health Dynamics of India (Infrastructure and Human Resources) 2022-23 in New Delhi. The publication offers comprehensive data on health infrastructure and human resources as of March 31st of each year. This data is important for stakeholders in the health sector, as it supports effective planning, monitoring, and management of health infrastructure across the country.

On the Occasion, Mr Chandra stated that the annual publication is a valuable document furnishing the much needed information on manpower and infrastructure within the National Health Mission. He said the data is immensely helpful in understanding the requirements of the states, their priority areas and formulating policies and targeted campaigns.

The joint military exercise of the Indian and American armies began today at Mahajan Field Firing Range in Bikaner, Rajasthan. The military exercise began with a parade ceremony this morning. About 1200 soldiers of the South Western Command of the Indian Army and the US military are participating in this military exercise. For next 15 days, soldiers of both the countries will practice several war strategies. More from our correspondent:-

For the first time in this military exercise, America’s High Mobility Artillery Rocket System is being demonstrated. Its range is 310 kilometers. The aim of the military exercise is to increase the internal capacity of both the armies and strengthen the possibilities of working together when needed. During the joint exercise, the soldiers of both the countries will envisage many types of military strategies and practice them jointly. This military exercise is considered to be important in view of the growing bilateral defense relations between India and US. Jitendra Dwivedi, Akashvani News, Jaipur

In Sri Lanka, the National Election Commission summoned all the candidates contesting the presidential election in the island nation and their representatives for a discussion today. The meeting focused on the activities related to the upcoming presidential election. In addition, the poll body also emphasised on door to door campaigning restrictions. The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has issued a red alert for several districts in Odisha, warning of extremely heavy to very heavy rainfall. The weather system lying in Bay of Bengal is expected to intensify into a deep depression while moving towards north Odisha and West Bengal coasts leading to increased rainfall and potential flooding. Coastal districts of Odisha, including Puri, Ganjam, and Khordha, are very likely to receive heavy rainfall and storm surges. Local authorities have activated emergency response plans and are monitoring the situation closely. Residents are urged to follow safety guidelines, avoid low-lying areas, and stay updated on the latest weather forecasts.

The India Meteorological Department has also forecast extremely heavy rainfall over Coastal Andhra Pradesh and Yanam today and heavy rainfall in Telangana, South Interior Karnataka, Kerala and Mahe till tomorrow. The weather department has also predicted very heavy rainfall over East Madhya Pradesh and Chhatisgarh till tomorrow. Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, and Tripura will also witness rains during next 3 days.

In the US Open Tennis, Jannik Sinner clinched his second Grand Slam victory of the year by defeating Taylor Fritz 6-3, 6-4, 7-5 in the Men’s Singles final last night. The world number one created history by becoming the first Italian man to win the US Open title. He had previously triumphed at the Australian Open earlier this year. The victory marked Sinner’s 55th match win and sixth title of the season, further solidifying his position at the top of the rankings.

In Kerala, two indigenously designed and constructed Anti-Submarine Warfare Shallow Watercrafts built for the Indian Navy were launched at the Cochin Shipyard this morning. This is the fourth and fifth anti-submarine warfare shallow watercrafts being built for the Navy. They will be christened INS Malpe and INS Mulki, once commissioned into the Indian Navy. The vessels are capable of conducting anti-submarine operations in coastal waters,low intensity maritime and mine laying operations, besides sub-surface surveillance and search and rescue operations.

India and the UAE today signed five agreements in various areas, including nuclear energy and petroleum.

These include an MoU on the Barakah Nuclear Power Plant Operations and Maintenance between Emirates Nuclear Energy Company and Nuclear Power Corporation of India Limited. An agreement for a long-term LNG supply between Abu Dhabi National Oil Company (ADNOC) and Indian Oil Corporation Limited was also signed. An MoU was signed between ADNOC and India Strategic Petroleum Reserve Limited.

The MoUs were inked following delegation-level talks between Prime Minister Narendra Modi and the Crown Prince of Abu Dhabi, Sheikh Khaled bin Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan, in New Delhi. The two leaders expressed satisfaction with the substantial progress achieved in recent years in the India-UAE Comprehensive Strategic Partnership and discussed opportunities to further widen and deepen the partnership in all areas of bilateral cooperation.

Later, President Droupadi Murmu met the Crown Prince of Abu Dhabi at Rashtrapati Bhavan. Our correspondent reports that the Crown Prince also visited Rajghat to pay tribute to Mahatma Gandhi.

“India and the UAE share a multifaceted relationship in which sustainability partnership forms a key pillar. This special bond is highlighted through a unique tradition of tree planting by UAE leaders at Rajghat in New Delhi. Today, Crown Prince Sheikh Khaled Bin Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan has planted an Amaltas sapling. He is the third generation leader from the UAE to plant a sapling at Rajghat. This marks the first time in the history of Rajghat that three generations of leaders from a single nation have planted trees honouring the legacy of Mahatma Gandhi.Suparna Saikia, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

External Affairs Minister Dr. S. Jaishankar has reiterated India’s support for ceasefire in Gaza strip as soon as possible. Speaking at the inaugural India-Gulf Cooperation Council Joint Ministerial Meeting for Strategic Dialogue held in Riyadh today, Dr. Jaishankar also reiterated New Delhi’s support for a two-state solution to end the current Israel-Palestinian conflict.

The meeting was chaired by Qatari Prime Minister Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani. It was attended by foreign ministers of Gulf Cooperation Council member countries along with External Affairs Minister Jaishankar.

Vinod Kumar, Akashvani News, Dubai.”

The Goods and Services Tax (GST) Council in its 54th meeting in New Delhi today decided to slash the GST on cancer drugs by reducing the rate from 12 percent to 5 percent. The meeting was chaired by Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman and attended by Minister of State for Finance Pankaj Chaudhary along with Finance Ministers of several states. Briefing the media in the National Capital, Finance Minister Sitharaman said, GST on selected snacks has been reduced from 18 percent to 12 percent.

The Minister said that funds given for research to universities established by central and state governments, and those that have obtained income tax exemption, are exempted from GST. She added that the Group of Ministers on GST rate reduction for medical health insurance will submit its report by the end of October this year.

The GST Council has also agreed to form a Group of Ministers on compensation cess. Ms. Sitharaman mentioned that the Group of Ministers on rate rationalization and real estate submitted status reports today, including a report on online gaming and casinos. She also noted that revenue from online gaming has increased by 412 percent, reaching 6 thousand and 909 crore rupees in six months.

The Supreme Court today stayed the Allahabad High Court order asking the Uttar Pradesh government to prepare a revised list of 69,000 assistant teachers selected through the Assistant Teachers Recruitment Examination in 2019. The top court also stayed the High Court verdict setting aside the selection lists of assistant teachers issued by the state authorities in June 2020 and January 2022, which included 6 thousand 800 candidates. A bench comprising Chief Justice D Y Chandrachud and Justices JB Pardiwala and Manoj Misra also issued notices to the state government and others including the secretary of the Uttar Pradesh Basic Education Board on a plea filed by multiple individuals. The bench said it will fix the plea for hearing in the week commencing the 23rd of this month.

The Defence Ministry has signed a contract with Hindustan Aeronautics Limited for 240 AL-31FP aero engines for Sukhoi-30MKI aircraft at a cost of over 26 thousand crore rupees. According to the Ministry, these aero engines will be manufactured by the Koraput Division of HAL and enhance the operational capability of the Sukhoi-30MKI fleet for defense preparedness. HAL will supply 30 aero engines annually, with the supply of all 240 engines expected to be completed over the next eight years. This will give a boost to the Atmanirbhar Bharat initiative of the government as HAL will seek support from the country’s defense manufacturing ecosystem, involving MSMEs and both public and private industries.

Jitendra Dwivedi, Akashvani, Jaipur.”

The Union Health Ministry has said that the presence of Mpox Clade 2 has been confirmed in an isolated patient, and the case has been verified as a travel-related infection. The individual, a young male who recently traveled from a country experiencing ongoing Mpox transmission, is currently isolated at a designated tertiary care isolation facility.
