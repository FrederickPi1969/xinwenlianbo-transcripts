## Morning News

At the Paris Olympics 2024, Neeraj Chopra has clinched Silver medal in Javelin throw and the Indian hockey team claimed a second successive Olympic bronze medal for the first time in 52 years.

As the sun set over the gargantuan Stade de France, Indian athletics’ superstar stepped out to defend his javelin gold. But Neeraj Chopra was up against a glittering field and found himself on the back foot early as Pakistan’s big hope Arshad Nadeem hurled a jaw-dropping throw, with his javelin falling 92.97 metres away and setting an Olympic record. Neeraj hurled a massive throw of 89.45m, not enough to give him the gold again but enough to make history. Neeraj became the first Indian track and field athlete to win back to back Olympic medals with this silver win and adding to his already astonishing legacy.

In hockey, India rode captain Harmanpreet Singh’s penalty corner conversions to beat Spain 2-1 and clinch a historic second successive Olympic bronze.

The two medals opened a floodgate of emotions back home as well as stadia here in Paris thronged by Indian fans. SHAURYA CHAUDHARY, AKASHVANI NEWS, PARIS”

The unique registered investor base of National Stock Exchange (NSE) has crossed the 10-crore mark for the first time. It took over 25 years for the registered investor base to hit the 4-crore mark in March 2021. The subsequent 1-crore increments have come through at an accelerated pace, taking on an average of about 6-7 months. The last crore was added in just over five months, the NSE said in a release. During this period, daily new unique investor registrations have averaged between 50 thousand to 78 thousand. The investor base has seen more than 3 times jump in the last five years, facilitated by rapid growth in digitisation, rising investor awareness, financial inclusion, and sustained market performance.

The Carriage of Goods by Sea Bill was introduced in the Lok Sabha today. The bill was introduced by the Minister of Ports, Shipping and Waterways Sarbananda Sonowal. The bill aimed to provide the responsibilities, liabilities, rights and immunities attached to carriers with respect to the carriage of goods by sea. While introducing the Bill, the Minister said, imperative changes are required as the Indian Carriage of Goods by Sea Act, 1925 is almost 100 years old. He said the bill seeks to empower the government to issue directions to comply with the international convention, and the changing global scenario.

The Railways (Amendment) Bill 2024 was introduced in the Lok Sabha today. The bill was introduced by the Railways Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw. The bill seeks to amend the Railways Act 1989. While introducing the bill, the Railways Minister said, this bill will enhance the power of the Railways Board and the efficiency of the Railways will improve.

The Bill proposes to simplify the legal framework by incorporating the proposals of the Indian Railway Board Act, 1905 in the Railways Act, 1989.

The Supreme Court today allowed the bail plea of former Delhi Deputy Chief Minister and Aam Aadmi Party leader Manish Sisodia in the alleged  liquor policy scam case.

The bench of Justices BR Gavai and KV Viswanathan allowed the bail applications filed by Sisodia in both the CBI and ED cases. The court said, to keep Sisodia under custody for an unlimited period of time in the hope of a speedy completion of trial would result in a grave violation of the fundamental right to personal liberty under Article 21. The court also observed that the appellant had deep roots in the society and hence he was not a flight risk. The two judge bench, which reserved the judgment on August 6, disagreed with the findings of the trial court and the High Court that the delay in trial was attributable to Sisodia. The Court asked Sisodia to furnish bail bonds for a sum of 10 lakh rupees with two sureties of the like sum and to surrender his passport. The CBI had arrested Sisodia under the Prevention of Corruption Act on February 26 last year.

Both the Houses of Parliament were adjourned sine die today. The Budget Session of Parliament began on 22nd of the last month and it was due to conclude on Monday. Speaker Om Birla today said, 15 meetings were held in this session which lasted for 115 hours. He said, 12 government bills were introduced during the session of which four were passed by the Lok Sabha. The Finance Bill, the Appropriation Bill, the Jammu and Kashmir Appropriation Bill, and the Bhartiya Vayuyan Vidheyak were passed in the Lower House. The Speaker said, Lok Sabha witnessed 136 percent productivity during the session.

Rajya Sabha Chairman Jagdeep Dhankhar said, around 22 hours discussions were held on Union Budget in the Upper House. He said, the House sat for 90 hours during the session.
