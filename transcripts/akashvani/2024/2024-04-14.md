## Midday News

The BJP today released its manifesto called “Sankalp Patra’ for the upcoming Lok Sabha elections at party Headquarters in New Delhi. It promises Uniform Civil Code, One-Nation One-Election and a ‘single electoral roll’. It was released in the presence of senior BJP leader and Prime Minister Narendra Modi along with party president JP Nadda and senior party leaders Rajnath Singh, Amit Shah and Nirmala Sitharaman.

In the  Sankalp Patra the BJP has promised to continue providing free ration for the next five years under PM Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana and expanding the Ayushman Bharat Yojana to cover senior citizens. The Sankalp Patra focuses on making three crore Lakpati Didis by empowering rural women and promises to make electricity bills zero with PM Surya Ghar Yojana. The party has promised to make the nation the third-largest economy in the world and a global manufacturing hub.

In its election manifesto, the BJP said Gaganyaan will be launched, which will be Bharat’s first human spaceflight mission, to land an astronaut on the Moon. The party has further promised to establish Bharatiya Antariksha Station and operationalise a second launch complex for the country. The manifesto also promises to launch a National Atmospheric Mission called Mausam to make Bharat ‘weather-ready’ and ‘climate-smart’.

On maintaining peace in the Northeast, BJP in its ‘Sankalp Patra’ said the party will continue efforts to address the issues in the disturbed areas and remove AFSPA in a phased manner. The Party has promised to further work towards resolving inter-state border disputes amongst the Northeastern States through sustained efforts.

Speaking on the occasion, senior Party leader and Prime Minister Narendra Modi said that BJP will work to implement One-Nation One-Election.

Mr Modi said Sankalp Patra empowers youth, women, farmers and the poor who are the four strong pillars of a developed nation. He added that Sankalp Patra focuses on the dignity of life and quality of life.
Mr Modi said, the party has decided to bring senior citizens above 70 years of age and the transgender community under the ambit of Ayushman Bharat Yojana.

Meanwhile, the Congress has alleged that the BJP manifesto is a pack of lies. Briefing media in New Delhi, party leader Supriya Shrinate said plans for providing employment have not been included in the party’s manifesto. She said the BJP manifesto is silent on issues such as legal guarantee for MSP, and bringing down inflation.

The Nation is paying tributes to Dr. B.R. Ambedkar on his birth anniversary. They also greeted the nation on this occasion.
In a message, the President said, Babasaheb Ambedkar, a pioneer of social change and a versatile personality, made exceptional contributions to the country and society as a jurist, educationist, economist, social-reformer and political leader.

Mr Dhankar also urged the people to embrace the ideals laid by Dr. Ambedkar and work towards realizing his vision of a just and equitable society.

The Prime Minister said with advancement of Dr. Ambedkar’s vision, the new image of India is entirely different today.

The Ministry of External Affairs today expressed serious concern over the escalating tensions between Israel and Iran. The Ministry said that the country is seriously concerned about the escalation of hostilities between Israel and Iran, which threatens peace and security in the region.

The Ministry also highlighted that it is closely monitoring the evolving situation and the embassies in the region are in close touch with the Indian community. Calling for an immediate de-escalation, the Ministry emphasised the importance of maintaining security and stability in the region. It further urged both the nations to step back from violence and return to the path of diplomacy.

As already reported, air raid sirens sounded across Israel earlier this morning after Iran launched hundreds of drones, ballistic missiles and cruise missiles. The attack marked the first time Iran has launched a direct military assault on Israel, despite decades of enmity dating back to the country’s 1979 Islamic Revolution.

The Israeli military’s spokesman, Rear Admiral Daniel Hagari, said Iran fired scores of drones, cruise missiles and ballistic missiles – with the vast majority intercepted outside Israel’s borders. He said warplanes intercepted over 10 cruise missiles alone, also outside Israeli airspace.

Israel reopened its airspace after a seven-hour closure prompted by a barrage of Iranian missiles and drones targeting the country overnight. The announcement came from the Israeli airports authority, which warned of possible disruptions to flight schedules departing from Tel Aviv, advising travelers to verify their flight times. In a coordinated response, Jordan, Iraq, and Lebanon also announced the reopening of their airspace today.

The Indian embassy in Israel has urged its citizens living in the country to stay calm and adhere to the safety protocols issued by the local authorities.  The embassy said it was closely monitoring the situation and was in touch with both the authorities and the diaspora members to ensure the safety of Indian citizens.

In the series of key Lok Sabha constituencies of the country, let us talk about Jabalpur Lok Sabha seat of Madhya Pradesh.

The cultural capital of Madhya Pradesh with 52 ponds and many cultural and historical heritages, Jabalpur’s Lok Sabha seat is unreserved and one of the important seats of the state.

There are eight assembly constituencies in Jabalpur Lok Sabha seat. More than 18 lakh 83 thousand voters will use their democratic rights in this seat. Of these, around 9 lakh 57 thousand are male and 9 lakh 26 thousand are female voters while 97 voters belong to the third gender.

A total of 19 candidates are in the fray in Jabalpur. BJP has given ticket to Ashish Dubey while Congress has made Dinesh Yadav its candidate. Along with BSP and many local parties, 7 independent candidates are also in the fray.

In the last Lok Sabha elections, BJP candidate Rakesh Singh had defeated Congress candidate Vivek Krishna Tankha by a margin of around 4.5 lakh votes.

With a history of Gondwana and Kalchuri period and famous for the manufacturing units of the Defence Ministry Jabalpur seat has been held by BJP for the last 27 years. Sanjeev Sharma, Akashvani News, Bhopal.

In Jammu and Kashmir the  mountainous  district Doda is on the brink of making a history by welcoming over three dozen voters above the age of 100 years. There are 37 voters in the district who are above the age of 100 years, and are ready to vote during these elections. The oldest among these stands at the age of 108 years. The district election officer has made special arrangements for them, Palkis (Palanquins) will be provided to them so that they come and vote. The election commission of India allows old people to cast their votes at their homes, and for that purpose teams of ECI officials will be visiting their homes to help them cast their vote in advance. But in Doda district, these above-100-years-old voters have decided not to cast their votes in their homes but to reach the nearby polling stations and cast votes on April 19 along with other voters.
