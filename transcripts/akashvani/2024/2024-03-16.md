## Morning News

The Election Commission will announce the schedule for General Elections 2024  at Vigyan Bhawan in New Delhi this afternoon. Along with Lok Sabha polls, the Commission will also declare the schedule for assembly polls to be held in some states. The term of the current Lok Sabha is going to end on the 16th of June this year. In 2019, the Lok Sabha elections were held in seven phases during April and May. It is expected this time as well, that polling will be conducted in several phases in April and May.

In the last Lok Sabha elections, the BJP had registered a resounding victory bagging 303 seats out of 543 Parliamentary constituencies in the country. So far, the ruling party has announced the names of 267 candidates while the main opposition Congress has declared the names of 82 candidates for the Lok Sabha elections. Left, TMC, Aam Aadmi Party and some other parties have also announced the names of their candidates for several Lok Sabha seats.

Senior BJP leader and Prime Minister Narendra Modi will hold public meeting at Nagarkurnool district in Telangana today as part of the party’s campaign for Lok Sabha Elections. He is on a two-day visit to the State. Our Correspondent reports that the BJP has already announced names of 15 out of 17 candidates for the Lok Sabha polls.

“Prime Minister Narendra Modi has been in the Telangana for the second time in the past ten days. He addressed public meetings in Adilabad and Sanga Reddy earlier during this month. A day after holding a mega road show at Malkajgiri last evening, Mr Modi is addressing the people at the Vijaya Samkalp Sabha in one of the SC constituencies in the state this morning. He will also address another public meeting at Jagityal on Monday. Lakshmi Akashvani News From Nagarkurnool.”

Mr Modi will visit Karnataka this afternoon, where he will address a public rally in Kalaburagi district. He will hold another public meeting in the state at Shivamogga district on Monday.

The Prime Minister is holding mega road shows and rallies in southern states as part of the party’s election campaign for Lok Sabha polls.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah has said that electoral bonds were introduced to end the influence of black money in Indian politics. Noting that he fully respects the Supreme Court order on electoral bonds, Mr Shah said, he feels that instead of completely scrapping the electoral bonds, it should have been improved. He was speaking at a programme organised by a media group in New Delhi yesterday.

Mr Shah said, out of the total of 20 thousand crore rupees of electoral bonds, the BJP has got approximately six thousand crore rupees. He raised the question that where did the bonds for 14,000 crore rupees go. He said, TMC has got 1,600 crore rupees, Congress got 1,400 crore rupees, BRS got 1,200 crore rupees, BJD got 750 crore rupees and DMK got 639 crore rupees.

The Home Minister said, once the accounts are settled the Opposition will not be able to face the public and media.

On One Nation One Election, Mr Shah said, the idea behind it is that elections are held again and again in this country and the people are busy in elections and this leads to repeated expenditure. He said, due to the model code of conduct, many development works come to a halt. He added that one nation, one election is a solution for this.

‘CAA-2019’ Mobile App for making applications under Citizenship (Amendment) Act, 2019 has become operational. The Home Ministry said that applicants can download the app from Google play store. It can also be downloaded from website – indiancitizenshiponline.nic.in.

The Defence Ministry has signed contract worth over two thousand 890 crore rupees with Hindustan Aeronautics Limited for Mid Life Upgrade MLU of 25 Dornier Aircraft of Indian Navy in New Delhi. The MLU for Dornier Aircraft includes an upgrade to incorporate state-of-the art Avionics Systems and Primary Role sensors. The upgrade would significantly enhance the operational capability of the Dornier aircraft to perform Primary Roles of Maritime Surveillance, Coastal Surveillance, Electronic Intelligence and development of Maritime Domain Awareness. This upgrade will also enable Indian Navy Dorniers to carry out Secondary Roles of Search and Rescue, Medical and Casualty evacuation and Communication Link.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has reached out to citizens through an open letter and urged them for feedback on shaping India’s future as Viksit Bharat. Mr Modi said that during the last ten years, the nation has taken huge leaps and witnessed tremendous growth. He highlighted that the nation is moving forward by taking both tradition and modernity hand in hand, with a rejuvenation of India’s rich national and cultural heritage.

Mr Modi mentioned that pivotal schemes and movements such as Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana, Ayushman Bharat, Matru Vandana Yojana, implementation of GST, abrogation of Article 370, Nari Shakti Vandan Act and the inauguration of a new Parliament building have transformed the nation. The Prime Minister said that this transformation was possible only due to the trust that the people had placed in him.

Union Minister for Women and Child Development and Minority Affairs Smriti Irani said, Reform, Perform, Transform, and Inform will make India into a Viksit Bharat. She addressed the Viksit Bharat Ambassador Meet at the National Stock Exchange in Mumbai yesterday. During the meeting, she highlighted 10 years of accomplishments of the government in various sectors. She further said that the country has witnessed growth in all sectors, highlighting women-led growth in entrepreneurship and equal opportunities with the benefits of various bank schemes.

Union Road Transport and Highways Ministry has approved 188 crore 95 lakh rupees for upgradation of ropeway work at Ujjain in Madhya Pradesh. This amount will be used to develop, operate and maintain the existing ropeway between Ujjain Junction Railway Station and Mahakaleshwar Temple.

“The proposed ropeway will help in movement, especially during the peak pilgrimage season and reduce the travel time to 7 minutes. The ropeway will facilitate 64 thousand pilgrims every day. Union Minister Nitin Gadkari in a social media post said that it will promote tourism and provide eco-friendly means of transportation while creating new employment opportunities. Madhya Pradesh Chief Minister Dr Mohan Yadav expressed his gratitude towards Mr Gadkari. He said that this is a commendable decision and will give impetus to the development of Ujjain along with the convenience of the devotees of Shri Mahakal. Sanjeev Sharma, Akashvani News, Bhopal.”

Telangana government has constituted a three-member Committee to examine and advise the government to resolve the problems of government teachers and employees. Chief Minister A. Revanth Reddy has formed the panel after reviewing the petitions and memoranda submitted by the employees’ unions.

The Committee will be headed by State Planning Board Vice Chairman G Chinna Reddy.

The Enforcement Directorate has arrested BRS MLC, former MP and daughter of former Chief Minister K Chandrasekhar Rao, K Kavitha in Hyderabad last evening in connection with an alleged money laundering case linked to the Delhi liquor policy scam. Her advocate said they will move the Supreme Court against the arrest.

The party has termed it as political conspiracy and also called for protests in all districts of the state against the alleged illegal arrest of BRS leader Kavita.

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu approved an attack plan targeting Rafah, the southernmost city in Gaza. Mr. Netanyahu made the decision after a meeting of Israel’s war cabinet to discuss a new proposal from Hamas for a ceasefire. As a first stage, Hamas has proposed to release the Israeli women, children, elderly and sick people it is holding hostage in exchange for the release of hundreds of Palestinian prisoners, including some convicted of multiple murders of Israelis. Mr Netanyahu’s office said, the truce proposal presented by Hamas was based on unrealistic demands. The Israeli Prime Minister will, however, dispatch another delegation to Qatar for talks on a possible hostage deal, fostering hopes for a ceasefire.

Hamas, the terrorist organisation seized about 250 Israeli and foreign hostages when it launched an attack on Israel in October last year, killing about 1,200 people, mostly civilians, leading to subsequent war. About half of the hostages were released during a week-long truce in November; Israel believes about 130 of the captives remain in Gaza and that 32 are dead.

In Badminton, ace Indian shuttler Lakshya Sen has stormed into the Men’s Singles semifinals of All England Open Championships in Birmingham. Commonwealth Games gold medallist Lakshya defeated Lee Zii Jia of Malaysia 20-22, 21-16,21-19 in the quarterfinals last night. After losing the first set 20-22, he won the second set 21-16 and claimed the third set 21-19 to wrap up the 71-minute encounter. World number 18 Lakshya will take on World number 9 Jonatan Christie of Indonesia this evening.

In the Women’s Premier League (WPL) Cricket, Royal Challengers Bangalore have stormed into the final defeating defending champions Mumbai Indians by five runs in a thrilling Eliminator match in Delhi last night. With the win, Royal Challengers Bangalore have set up the title clash with the Delhi Capitals in New Delhi tomorrow.

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has forecast rainfall and thunderstorms over East and Central India for the next four days. The IMD said that widespread rainfall with thunderstorms, lightning and gusty winds of up to 40 kilometres per hour will occur over parts of West Bengal, Jharkhand, Odisha, Vidarbha, Chhattisgarh and Madhya Pradesh till the 20th of this month. The weather agency expects the maximum temperature over parts of South India to remain unchanged during the next three to four days.

Delhi’s Rouse Avenue court today granted bail to Delhi Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal against arrest for skipping Enforcement Directorate’s (ED) repeated summons in a case related to the alleged excise policy scam. The Court asked Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) leader to furnish a personal bond of 15,000 rupees and a security bond of one lakh rupees for the bail.

The Delhi Chief Minister violated Section 174 of the Indian Penal Code. The section deals with disobeying a legal order to be at a certain place in person or by agent.

Mr. Kejriwal appeared before the court following the Enforcement Directorate’s complaints that the AAP Chief was not complying with agency’s summons in connection with the case. The ED also told the Court that the Chief Minister skipped eight summons issued under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act. The next hearing in the case will be held at 10 AM on the 1st of April.

The Centre has declared the Jammu and Kashmir Liberation Front, JKLF (Mohd. Yasin Malik faction) as an unlawful association for another five years. Union Home Minister Amit Shah said, the banned outfit continues to engage in activities that foment terror and secessionism in Jammu and Kashmir. He said, anyone found challenging the security, sovereignty and integrity of the nation will face harsh legal consequences.

Mr Shah said, the Narendra Modi Government has also designated the Jammu and Kashmir Peoples Freedom League as an unlawful association for five years. He said, the organisation threatened India’s integrity by promoting, aiding and abetting the secession of Jammu and Kashmir through terrorism. The Home Minister said, the Modi government will remain unsparing to people and organisations involved in terror activities.

Raksha Mantri Rajnath Singh has approved the extension of resettlement facilities to cadets who are invalidated from military training on medical grounds during any military training. The Defence Ministry said, cadets and their parents have been demanding such resettlement opportunities for decades. The Ministry said, every year, young cadets at military academies undergo academic and military training with the prime objective of being commissioned as officers in the Armed Forces. As per the rules, such a cadet is treated as an officer only after commissioning. During Military Training, some cadets are invalidated on medical grounds.

The Raksha Mantri has cleared another proposal of the Department of Ex-Servicemen Welfare, allowing the extension of benefits of schemes run by the Directorate General Resettlement. This will help 500 cadets who have been boarded out on medical grounds to access the schemes and ensure a brighter future for them.

India has said, it strongly condemns ‘all’ forms of religiophobia and has warned against dividing the United Nations into religious camps. This was stated by India’s Permanent Representative to the UN, Ruchira Kamboj while addressing the UN General Assembly during the adoption of a resolution ‘Measures to combat Islamophobia’ at its 78th session. Ms. Kamboj said, India, as a proud champion of pluralism, firmly upholds the principle of equal protection and promotion of all religions and all faiths.

A mass evacuation has begun in Ukraine’s Sumy region near the Russian border due to intense shelling in the area. The military administration of Ukraine said on Telegram that over 180 residents near the Velikopysarska community were evacuated over the past three days. It added that a total of 4,500 residents have been evacuated from 22 villages. According to regional administration, three people were killed and 13 wounded over the past five days in the tense region.
