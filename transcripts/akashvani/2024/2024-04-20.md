## Midday News

After the completion of first phase of General elections, campaigning has intensified for the second phase of Lok Sabha polls. In the second phase, polling will be held in 88 Parliamentary seats in 12 States and Union Territories along with the remaining part of the Outer Manipur Lok Sabha constituency on 26th of this month.
Twenty seats of Kerala, 14 seats of Karnataka, 13 seats of Rajasthan, eight seats each of Uttar Pradesh and Maharashtra, seven seats of Madhya Pradesh, five seats each in Assam and Bihar, three seats each of West Bengal and Chhattisgarh and one seat each in Tripura and Jammu and Kashmir will go to polls in this second phase.

Senior leaders of various political parties are addressing rallies across the country in their bid to woo the voters.

Prime Minister and Senior BJP leader Narendra Modi addressed election rallies in Nanded and Parbhani in Maharashtra. Mr Modi expressed happiness over high voter turnout in the first phase of Lok Sabha polls yesterday. He said, maximum voting must be ensured during this ongoing festival of democracy in the country. He highlighted the NDA government’s achievements, including providing toilets to millions of women, facilitating bank accounts for the poor, and introducing Digital India and UPI technology. Mr Modi said, next 25 years will be years of India’s greatness in the world. He said, his Government is actively tackling poverty and ensuring fair distribution of ration. He also pledged to continue and complete pending projects if re-elected for a third term. He listed the measures taken for the welfare of farmers.
Prime Minister Modi will also hold public meeting in Chikkaballapur and Bengaluru in Karnataka later in the day. Chief Minister of Karnataka and Congress leader Siddaramaiah is campaigning in Mysore and Mandya constituencies while Deputy Chief Minister of Karnataka D K Shivakumar is campaigning in Mandya today.

Congress leader Rahul Gandhi addressed election rally at Bhagalpur in Bihar. He said, if the INDIA bloc government comes into power, they will end the Agniveer scheme. He said, they will work for the welfare of poor and tribal community.

Chief Justice of India Dr DY Chandrachud has said that the enactment of new criminal laws clearly indicates that the country is changing and moving forward. Addressing a conference in New Delhi today, Dr Chandrachud said that with the enactment of three criminal laws – Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, Bharatiya Nagrik Suraksha Sanhita and the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, the country is set for a significant overhaul of its criminal justice system.

A Delhi court today reserved an order on the regular bail plea filed by former Deputy Chief Minister of Delhi and AAP leader Manish Sisodia in the money laundering and corruption cases connected to the liquor policy case. The AAP leader is presently in judicial custody in both cases.

Special judge Kaveri Baweja of Rouse Avenue Court said that the order will be pronounced on the 30th of this month. He was denied bail by the trial court, Delhi High Court and the Supreme Court in both ED and CBI cases.

Earlier, the AAP leader withdrew the interim bail plea moved by him in both cases to campaign for the Lok Sabha polls. He has been in custody since February last year.

In Odisha, the death toll has risen to seven in the tragic boat mishap in Jharsuguda district. The ferry, carrying over 50 people, capsized in the Mahanadi river near Lakhanpur in Jharsuguda district yesterday. Rescue operations are underway to find the missing people. All the occupants on the boat were from neighbouring Chhattisgarh. They were visiting Kharseni, situated in the Ambabhona block of the Bargarh district in Odisha.

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has forecast severe heatwave conditions in isolated pockets of Odisha and Gangetic West Bengal till Monday. Heat Wave conditions are also very likely to prevail in some pockets of Jharkhand for the next two to three days. IMD said that hot and humid weather is very likely to prevail over Telangana, Coastal Andhra Pradesh, Yanam, Rayalaseema, Kerala, Mahe, Sub-Himalayan West Bengal and Sikkim till Tuesday.

The IMD further said that light to moderate rainfall and snowfall accompanied by thunderstorms, lightning and gusty winds is very likely over Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh, Gilgit, Baltistan, Muzaffarabad, Himachal Pradesh, Punjab, Haryana, and Rajasthan till next few days. Rainfall and snowfall accompanied by isolated thunderstorms, lightning and gusty winds are likely to prevail over Northeast India till Wednesday. The IMD said Delhi will witness strong surface winds and generally cloudy sky with drizzle for the next few days.

Four Indian women freestyle wrestlers including Vinesh Phogat, Anshu Malik, Mansi and Reetika stormed into the semi-finals of Asian Olympic Qualifiers at Bishkek in Kyrgyzstan today. With the win, they are one win away from securing a Paris Olympics quota.

Vinesh Phogat pinned her Cambodian opponent Samnang Dit in the women’s 50kg category.
Anshu Malik defeated Kalmira Bilimbekova of Kyrgyzstan 12-1 in the women’s 57 kg category.

Mansi registered a come-from-behind victory over Irina Kuznetsova of Kazakhstan in women’s 62 kg category.

Reetika defeated China’s Juan Wang in women’s 76 kg category.

In IPL Cricket, Delhi Capitals will take on Sunrisers Hyderabad in Delhi this evening. The match will start at 7.30 PM. Yesterday, Lucknow Super Giants defeated Chennai Super Kings by eight wickets in Lucknow.

## Evening News

Campaigning for the remaining phases of General Elections has intensified with the completion of the first phase yesterday. Senior leaders and star campaigners of all political parties including Grand Alliance and NDA are holding multiple rallies across the country to woo the voters. With just four days left for campaigning for the second phase, leaders are leaving no stone unturned.  In this phase the lok sabha seats going to polls are: all twenty in Kerala, 14 out of 28 in Karnataka, remaining 13 seats in Rajasthan, eight seats each in Uttar Pradesh and Maharashtra, seven seats in  Madhya Pradesh, five seats each in Assam and Bihar, three seats each in West Bengal and Chhattisgarh and one seat each in Tripura and Jammu and Kashmir.

Prime Minister and Senior BJP leader Narendra Modi addressed election rallies in Nanded and Parbhani in Maharashtra and Chikkaballapur and Bengaluru in Karnataka today. He said, he has come to present his report card and also to seek the blessings of the people. Prime Minister said, SC, ST and OBC communities have benefited from  the policies of his Government, and over 25 crore people were brought above the poverty line. He once again expressed his commitment to work 24 by 7 for 2047 Viksit Bharat. Speaking on his 10 years of governance, PM Modi said, today India is the fifth largest economy, there is record foreign investments, exports and manufacturing sectors are growing rapidly and it is a major exporter of mobile phones.

Former Prime Minister and JD (S) leader Deve Gowda who spoke on the occasion said that the corrupt Congress Government had emptied the coffers when it left the Government in 2014.

On the other hand, Chief Minister of Karnataka Siddaramaiah alleged that the Surya Ghar Muft Bijli programme as promised in the BJP manifesto has put the burden on the poor as they have to bear the cost of the solar panels.

The Employees’ Provident Fund Organisation, EPFO has added 15.48 lakh net members in the month of February this year. EPFO’s provisional payroll data released today, indicates, around 7.78 lakh new members have been enrolled during the month. Gender-wise analysis of payroll data unveils that over two lakh new members are females, which is indicative of a broader shift towards a more inclusive and diverse workforce. A noticeable aspect of the data is the dominance of the 18 to 25 years age group, constituting a significant 56.36 per cent of the total new members added in February. It indicates that majority of individuals joining the organized workforce are youth, primarily first-time job seekers.
