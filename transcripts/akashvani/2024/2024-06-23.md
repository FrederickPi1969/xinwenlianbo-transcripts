The CBI has registered an FIR and started investigation in connection with the alleged paper leak in the NEET-UG examination. According to  CBI sources, the FIR was registered on the complaint of Director of the Department of Higher Education, Ministry of Education. Special teams have been formed by the CBI to investigate the matter. Special CBI teams are being sent to Patna and Godhra where local police cases have been registered.

The allegations in the FIR states that the NEET-(UG) 2024 Examination was conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) on 5th of last month and over 23 lakh candidates appeared in the examination. The complaint also states that certain isolated incidents occurred in a few states during the conduct of the NEET(UG) 2024 examination.

The Education Ministry has requested the CBI to conduct a comprehensive investigation into the entire gamut of alleged irregularities. It includes conspiracy, cheating, impersonation, breach of trust, and destruction of evidence by candidates, institutes, and middlemen, including attempted irregularities. The Ministry has also requested the CBI for investigating the role of public servants connected with the conduct of the examination.

The CBI is also taking steps to take over the cases filed across the country in the matter for comprehensive probe and unearthing larger conspiracy. Yesterday, the Ministry of Education had entrusted the matter of alleged irregularities in NEET (UG) Examination 2024 to the CBI for a comprehensive investigation.

On the recent steps taken by the Union Education Ministry in connection with NEET-UG examination, University Grants Commission (UGC) Chairman Mamidala Jagadesh Kumar said that the Government is taking proactive steps to protect the student’s future. Talking to Akashvani News, UGC Chairman said that the Government has taken several steps in the last few days to maintain the credibility of examinations in the country. He said that a committee has also  been formed consisting of eminent experts.

Delhi University Vice-Chancellor Professor Yogesh Singh also lauded the government’s steps saying that it was necessary. Talking to Akashvani News, Professor Singh said that he believes that the government will not allow anyone to play with a students’s future. He said that all people should have faith in the government.

The Economic Offence Unit (EOU) of the Bihar police today said, the NEET-UG examination paper was leaked by a professional interstate organized gang from Hazaribagh in Jharkhand. Thirteen members of this organized gang have been arrested by SIT which is investigating the case. In a press statement issued today, the EOU said, SOP was not followed at the examination centre in Hazaribagh in Jharkhand which led to the question paper leak. The SIT has also interrogated four NEET aspirants who are among the 15 suspected candidates in the case. The EOU said, the case will now be handed over to the CBI for further investigation.

In a related development, some local villagers attacked a CBI team in Kasiadih village in Nawada district of Bihar which had gone there for probing the UGC-NET exam paper leak case. Superintendent of Police of Nawada Ambrish Rahul said the incident took place as local residents misunderstood the CBI team as fake and surrounded them. Mr. Rahul said four people have been arrested in the attack case and full police protection has been provided to the team.

A re-test of all the one thousand 563 candidates, who got  grace or compensatory marks in the NEET-UG examination was held today. According to the Education Ministry,the  retest was conducted at seven centres across the states of Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, Haryana, Meghalaya and Chandigarh. Out of the total of the one thousand 563 candidates, 813 candidates appeared for the retest while 750 did not appear for the retest.

Home Minister, Amit Shah today chaired a high-level meeting to review the preparedness of flood management in New Delhi. He also reviewed long-term measures to formulate a comprehensive and far-reaching policy to mitigate the menace of floods in the country. Mr. Shah said that under the leadership of Prime Minister Narendra Modi, the country’s disaster management is moving forward with a zero casualty approach.

“During the meeting, Mr. Shah directed the concerned departments to conduct a detailed study of the recent floods in Sikkim and Manipur and submit a report to the Ministry of Home Affairs. He said, at least 50 large ponds in the Northeast should be constructed so that the water of the Brahmaputra River could be diverted and stored in those ponds. He said, this will help agriculture, irrigation and tourism benefiting the local economy. Home Minister appealed to the States and Union Territories to timely implement the advisories issued by the National Disaster Management Authority for flood management. Bhupendra Singh, for Akashwani News, Delhi”

Mr. Shah also reviewed the preparations to deal with Glacial Lake Outburst Flood. He also emphasized on optimum use of satellite imagery provided by the Indian Space Research Organization by various agencies for flood and water management. He directed the India Meteorological Department and the Central Water Commission to complete the process of recalibrating all the equipment used in flood forecast. He also directed to ensure that floodgates of all the major dams are in good condition. He said that the flood monitoring centres of the CWC should be in accordance with the country’s requirements and of international standards. He directed that efforts should be made to upgrade the forecast system of water level of rivers for better flood management.

The first session of the 18th Lok Sabha will commence tomorrow. During the session, the oath and affirmation of newly elected members will take place. Senior BJP MP Bhartruhari Mahtab, who has been appointed as the Protem Speaker, will administer the oath to the newly elected Lok Sabha members. The election of the new Lok Sabha Speaker will be held on Wednesday. President Droupadi Murmu will address the joint sitting of both houses of Parliament on Thursday. The session of the Rajya Sabha will commence on Thursday and will conclude on the 3rd of July.

The Special Task Force of the West Bengal Police has arrested a college student and detained five others from Panagarh area in Paschim Bardhaman district for their alleged links with a Bangladesh-based terror outfit. A senior officer said in Kolkata today, the youth, a second-year Computer Science student, was picked up from his Panagarh residence last evening for his alleged links with the banned Islamist organisation Shahadat-e al Hiqma of Bangladesh. Based on inputs provided by him during his interrogation, STF slooths nabbed five more persons from Nababghat area in the same district. A case has been filed  against the arrested youth under UAPA and several clauses of the IPC and he’s been sent to 14-days STF custody by Durgapurs ACJM court.

The Indian Space Research Organisation, ISRO has said that the third and final landing experiment of its Reusable Launch Vehicle ‘Pushpak’ was successful today. The landing experiment was conducted at the Aeronautical Test Range in Chitradurga in Karnataka today. The ISRO tweet on social media site X says RLV LEX-03 which stands for Reusable Launch Vehicle Landing Experiment 3, re-demonstrated the autonomous landing capability under more severe wind conditions.

Pushpak used multi-sensory fusion including inertial sensor, radar altimeter, flush air data system, Pseudolite system and NavIC navigation system.

Union Minister of New and Renewable Energy, Prahlad Joshi has lauded the effort of ISRO for completing the final trial of the Reusable Landing Vehicle ‘Pushpak’. In a social media post mentioned to ISRO, Mr. Joshi said that ISRO’s relentless pursuit of innovation continues to propel India to new heights in space exploration.

In the Archery World Cup Stage 3, Dhiraj Bommadevara won the bronze in the men’s individual recurve event as well as in the mixed team event with Bhajan Kaur, in Antalya, Turkey, taking the country’s medal tally to four in the process.

Trailing 0-2 after conceding the opening set, the Indian team produced a flawless round to bring it level and seal a stunning 5-3 (35-38, 40-39, 38-37, 38-38) win over their Mexican opponents Alejandra Valencia and Matias Grande.

India Women beat South Africa by six wickets in the third and final One-Day International at the M. Chinnaswamy Stadium in Bengaluru this evening to register a 3-0 series clean sweep.

The star spin all-rounder Deepti Sharma took two wickets for 27 to restrict South Africa women to a 215 total and then in-form Smriti Mandhana scored 90 runs off just 83 balls to power India women to an easy win in the third ODI game.
