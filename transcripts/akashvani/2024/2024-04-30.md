## Midday News

Political parties have intensified their poll campaigns for the third phase of Lok Sabha elections. Prime Minister and Senior BJP leader Narendra Modi today said that people are witnessing the difference between 10 years of Modi government and 60 years of Congress rule. Addressing a public meeting at Madha in Maharashtra, he said that while Prime Ministers during the Congress rule chanted the phrase gareebi hatayenge, it is the NDA Government that lifted 25 crore people out of poverty in the last ten years.

Mr. Modi said that in 2014, there were about 100 irrigation projects that were stuck for decades and the NDA Government has already executed 63 of them. Attacking NCP(SP) chief Sharad Pawar, Mr. Modi said that when Mr. Pawar was the Agriculture Minister in the Centre, the Fair and Remunerative Price, FRP of sugarcane was 200 rupees but during the 10 years of NDA rule, the FRP of sugarcane has been increased to 340 rupees per quintal.

Mr. Modi also held a public meeting at Dharashiv in Osmanabad LS constituency. Later in the day, Mr. Modi will address rallies at Latur in Maharashtra.

In Chattisgarh, Congress President Mallikarjun Kharge will address a public meeting in Champa Nagar under Janjgir Champa Lok Sabha constituency of the state this afternoon.

Senior Congress leader Rahul Gandhi has assured of financial assistance schemes for women and youth if his party is voted to power. Addressing a rally at Bhind in Madhya Pradesh today, he alleged that BJP is working against the interests of poor people.

Senior BJP leader and Union Minister Amit Shah has accused Congress of spreading misinformation that the BJP will end reservations after crossing 400 seats. Addressing media persons in Guwahati today, he said that these claims are baseless and unfounded. Mr. Shah assured that BJP supports reservations for Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and Other Backward Classes. He claimed that the BJP is getting a great response across the country.

In Manipur, re-polling is underway in six polling stations of Outer Manipur Parliamentary Constituency. Re-polling is being held today in Five polling stations in Ukhrul district and one in Senapati from 7 AM to 4 PM.  Earlier, the Election Commission of India announced that the voting process in these polling stations conducted on April 26 has been treated void and directed for re-polling.

In Karnataka, the sitting JD(S) MP from Hassan, Prajwal Revanna was today suspended from the party over explicit videos allegedly showing him sexually assaulting multiple women that went viral. Prajwal is also contesting from Hassan in this election. He is the grandson of JD(S) supremo and former Prime Minister Deve Gowda and son of Holenarasipur MLA H D Revanna. Based on a complaint by a victim, a police complaint is lodged against Prajwal Revanna and the state government has ordered a SIT probe into the allegations. Before the police complaint was lodged, Prajwal is said to have left for Germany on Saturday morning.

In another development, National Commission for Women (NCW) has demanded swift action from Karnataka DGP in the alleged sexual abuse case involving JDS leader Prajwal Revanna. Taking suo motu cognizance in this matter, NCW Chairperson Rekha Sharma has written a letter to Karnataka DGP and asked to apprehend the accused. She also asked to submit a detailed report within three days. Ms. Sharma condemned the alleged sexual abuse saying these events endanger women’s safety and perpetuate a culture of violence.

In our series, covering profiles of key constituencies across States and Union Territories during the Lok Sabha elections, today we bring you a report on Porbandar Lok Sabha constituency in Gujarat.

Porbandar Lok Sabha constituency, located in western part of Gujarat, is renowned for the birthplace of Mahatma Gandhi. Established in 1977, it has been a significant political seat in the Saurashtra region.

Over the years, Porbandar has been represented by various Members of Parliament (MPs) from various parties like Congress, Janata Dal, and BJP. Rameshbhai Dhaduk from BJP was elected from this seat in both 2014 and 2019 Lok Sabha polls. In this election, BJP has fielded a senior leader and Union Minister, Mansukh Mandaviya, against Senior Congress leader Lalit Vasoya. There are total 11 candidates are in the fray on this seat, including six independent candidates whose fate will be decided by 17 lakh 18 thousand voters in the constituency.  Aparna Khunt, Akashvani News, Ahmedabad.

Admiral Dinesh Kumar Tripathi has taken over as the Chief of the Naval Staff today. He has succeeded Admiral R Hari Kumar. Talking to reporters in New Delhi after assuming the charge,  Admiral Tripathi said, the Indian Navy should remain operationally ready at all times to deter potential adversaries at sea in peace and to win the war at and from the sea. He also stressed that he will strengthen the ongoing efforts of the Indian Navy towards ‘Aatamnirbharta’  by introducing new technologies and becoming an important pillar of national development.

Admiral Tripathi has served as the Flag Officer Command-in-Chief, Western Naval Command. A Communication and Electronic Warfare specialist, he has served on frontline warships of the Navy as Signal Communication Officer and Electronic Warfare Officer, and later as the Executive Officer and Principal Warfare Officer of Guided Missile Destroyer INS Mumbai. He commanded the Indian naval ships Vinash, Kirch and Trishul.  Admiral Tripathi is a recipient of the Ati Vishisht Seva Medal and the Nausena Medal for devotion to duty.

At least seven Maoists are reportedly killed in the encounter with security personnel in Bastar division of Chhattisgarh.

According to sources, this encounter is going on in the forests of Abujhmad of Narayanpur district.  Based on intelligence inputs about the presence of Maoists in this area, a joint team of District Reserve Guard and Special Task Force was sent to the area.

Fierce gun battle is going on between security forces and Maoists in the forests. Security forces are carrying out a search operation in the area.

During the meeting, they reviewed the entire gamut of multi-faceted bilateral relations  and discussed avenues to further deepen the India-UAE Comprehensive Strategic Partnership.

Due to heavy snowfall in the tribal district of Lahaul-Spiti in Himachal Pradesh, day to day life has been affected. The vehicular movement has come to a halt between Manali-Leh highway beyond Baralacha and between Atal Tunnel and Dhundi due to snow accumulation.

Approximately 6,000 tourists stranded at Atal Tunnel and Dhundi have been safely evacuated overnight as part of a rescue operation. The risk of landslides has increased in the valley due to this snowfall, and the road is blocked near Sissu due to landslides, with traffic being diverted via Teling. Furthermore, the Chamba-Bharmour national highway has been closed due to landslides this morning, with efforts to reopen it underway at a war footing. The weather in the state is clear today; however, in the past days, areas at higher altitudes experienced snowfall, while a decrease in temperature was recorded in other regions due to rain and hailstorms. The unseasonal rain and snowfall have caused damage to crops, also heightened the risk of landslides in the valley. With Ritesh Kapoor from Shimla, Chirag Jha, Akashvani News, Delhi.

Sri Lanka’s Cabinet has approved a plan to renovate the Kankesanthurai port in the Northern part of the island. The project will be executed under an Indian financial grant. Last year, ferry services between India and Sri Lanka commenced with a direct passenger ship service connecting Nagapattinam in Tamil Nadu to Kankesanthurai port in Sri Lanka.

“With Sri Lankan Cabinet approving the plan to renovate the Kankesanthurai port in northern part of the island, connectivity between the two countries is likely to improve further. Indian government had recently decided to fully fund the Kankesanthurai Port development project as it had faced considerable delays already. Initially approved as a credit line based project in 2017, India’s pledge to cover the entire estimated cost of 61.5 million dollars under a grant given the significance of the project has been welcomed in Sri Lanka. India and Sri Lanka have been actively implementing the joint vision document under which connectivity is an important facet. Dr. Ahmed Muyeen Farooqi, Akashvani News, Colombo”
