## Morning News

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh called on the Russian President Vladimir Putin in Moscow yesterday. During the meeting, Mr Singh mentioned the friendship between India and Russia, saying New Delhi has always stood by its Russian friends and will continue to do so in future. The Defence Ministry said in a statement, the two leaders discussed various issues of bilateral defence cooperation. They expressed that partnership between the  two nations hold immense potential and combined efforts will pave way for remarkable outcomes.

Earlier, the Defence Minister co-chaired the 21st meeting of India-Russia Inter-Governmental Commission on Military and Military Technical Cooperation with his Russian counterpart, Andrey Belousov in Moscow. Making an initial remarks the Defence Minister emphasised that India-Russia relationship is very strong, and has lived up to the responsibilities of a special and privileged strategic partnership.

The Government has evacuated 75 Indian nationals from Syria, following the recent developments in the West Asian country. The evacuees include 44 ‘zaireen’ from Jammu and Kashmir who were stranded at Saida Zainab. All Indian nationals have safely crossed over to Lebanon and will return by the available commercial flights to India.
External Affairs Ministry said in a statement the evacuation was coordinated by the embassies of India in Damascus and Beirut. The evacuation was put into effect following New Delhi’s assessment of the security situation and the requests from Indian nationals in Syria.
The Ministry stressed that the Government accords highest priority to the safety and security of Indian nationals abroad. Indian nationals remaining in Syria have been advised to stay in touch with the Indian Embassy in Damascus on its emergency helpline number +963 993385973 and email ID (hoc.damascus@mea.gov.in) for updates.

In Syria, Mohamed al-Bashir has been appointed as the caretaker Prime Minister of the transitional government. In a televised statement, Mr Bashir said, he will lead the interim authority till 1st of March, next year with the backing of the former rebels who toppled President Bashar al-Assad regime a few days ago. Al-Bashir ran the rebel-led Salvation Government before the 12-day lightning offensive swept into Damascus.
Mohammed al-Bashir, a figure little known across most of Syria, previously ran an administration in a small pocket of the northwest controlled by rebels.

China has decided to impose visa restrictions on US personnel over interference in Hong Kong affairs. The decision comes in response to the recent US announcement to impose visa restrictions on officials of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region government. Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson Mao Ning made the announcement yesterday at a regular press briefing in Beijing.  She said, the US has interfered in China’s internal affairs and violated the principles of international law and basic norms governing the international relations.

US President-elect Donald Trump has named Republican Party activist Harmeet Dhillon to be the Assistant Attorney General for civil rights. Making the announcement, Mr. Trump said, in her new role at the Department of Justice, Harmeet will be a tireless defender of American Constitutional Rights, and will enforce Civil Rights and Election Laws fairly and firmly. He also added that throughout her career Harmeet has stood up consistently to protect the country’s cherished Civil Liberties.
