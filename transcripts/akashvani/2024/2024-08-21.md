## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will embark on a three day visit to Poland and Ukraine today. In Warsaw, Mr Modi will be accorded a ceremonial welcome. The Prime Minister will hold talks with his Polish counterpart Donald Tusk and will call on President Andrzej Duda. Mr Modi will also interact with the Indian community, business leaders and prominent Indologists.

“This will be the first visit by an Indian Prime Minister to Poland in the past 45 years. The visit is taking place at a time when both nations are marking the 70th anniversary of the establishment of diplomatic relations. Mr Modi will also visit memorials that commemorate the special relation of Poland with Jamnagar and Kolhapur. The Prime Minister will undertake an official visit to Ukraine on 23rd of this month on the invitation of Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy. This will be the first visit by an Indian Prime Minister to Ukraine after establishment of diplomatic relations between the two countries in 1992. The Prime Minister’s engagements in Kyiv will touch a range of aspects of bilateral ties including political, trade, economic, investments, education, cultural, people-to-people exchanges, humanitarian assistance and others. Mr Modi will also interact with the Indian community, including students. With Anupam Mishra, Pallavi Singh Parmar, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has cancelled the advertisement related to lateral entry in the bureaucracy following the directions from the Department of Personnel and Training. Earlier yesterday, the government asked the UPSC to cancel the advertisement related to lateral entry in the bureaucracy. This comes a few days after UPSC issued an advertisement to appoint 45 Joint Secretaries, Directors, and Deputy Secretaries in Central Ministries, which was questioned by the opposition parties.  Responding on the issue, Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said that the decision to align lateral entry with principles of reservation shows Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s commitment to social justice.

Employees’ Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) has added 19.29 lakh net members in June this year registering a growth of 7.86 percent in comparison to same month of the last year. Ministry of Labour and Employment said that the surge in membership can be attributed to numerous factors, including increased employment opportunities. The data indicates that around 10.25 lakh new members have enrolled during June this year. A noticeable aspect of the data is the dominance of the 18-25 age group, constituting a significant 59.14 per cent of the total new members added in June this year. The payroll data highlights that approximately 14.15 lakh members exited and subsequently rejoined EPFO. This figure depicts year over year growth of 11.79 per cent compared to June last year.

BJP has appointed party leader Ram Madhav and Union Minister G Kishan Reddy as election in-charge for the upcoming Jammu & Kashmir Assembly elections. The Union Territory will go to polls in three phases on 18th and 25th September and 1st October.

Jammu and Kashmir has witnessed a significant increase of over 93 thousand new voters, due to corresponding rise in the number of young and women voters. The number of polling stations across the union territory has also gone up by 209. This came to fore as Special Summary of Photo Electoral Rolls with qualifying date July 1, 2024 was successfully completed in all 20 districts of J&K. As per the Special Summary Revision-2024 completed with publication of the final electoral roll, the total number of electors stand at 88.03 Lakhs. These include 44.89 lakh males, 43.13 lakh females and 168 third gender electors. Chief Electoral Officer, PK Pole appealed to all eligible voters across Jammu and Kashmir to exercise their franchise on the polling days in their respective constituencies and strengthen democracy in the Union Territory.

In Uttar Pradesh, a Sultanpur district court has ordered UP Police to arrest and produce Aam Aadmi Party MP Sanjay Singh in the court on 28th of this month in a two-decade-old case of road blockade protest. The court took strong objection on Sanjay Singh’s failure to surrender after his conviction and avoiding case hearings. Earlier, on 13th of this month, a non-bailable warrant was issued against Singh, SP leader Anoop Sanda, and four others, with the hearing scheduled for yesterday. The accused however did not appear before the court. In 2001, Sanjay Singh and Anoop Sanda, then a leader of the Democratic Socialist Party, along with their supporters, blocked a flyover in Sultanpur as a protest on power cut issues. Sultanpur’s special Court convicted all six accused on 23rd of January, last year.

India has called on Japan to remove regulatory bottlenecks that exist in sharing of critical technologies. At third ‘2+2’ dialogue in New Delhi yesterday, India and Japan agreed to prepare a new framework for security cooperation in line with the desire to further expand the overall strategic ties. Indian delegation at the talks was headed by External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar and Defence Minister Rajnath Singh. Japanese team was led by Foreign Minister Yoko Kamikawa and Defence Minister Kihara Minoru. In his remarks, Dr Jaishankar said both sides discussed the unfolding new avenues for technology, research and industrial cooperation including in the field of defence. Dr Jaishankar said that a free, open and rules-based Indo-Pacific is the top priority for both the countries.

He also announced India’s decision to establish a new consulate in the Japanese city of Fukuoka. Japanese foreign minister said, India and Japan reaffirmed their commitment to object to unilateral attempts to change the status quo by force and uphold the free and open international order based on the rule of law.

Vice President Jagdeep Dhankhar will address the inaugural session of 19th CII India Africa Business Conclave in New Delhi today. The theme of the three-day conclave is Creating One Future. The conclave focuses on areas such as Trade, Investment, Infrastructure, Healthcare and Technology. The conclave began yesterday. More than 11 hundred international delegates from sixty five countries out of which 47 are African nations are participating in the conclave.

A two-day EU-India Track 1.5 Conference will begin today in New Delhi. India and the European Union will host the regional conference to discuss current and emerging threats in online radicalisation and find ways to jointly counter the exploitation of online spaces by extremist and terrorist actors. The conference will bring together experts, policymakers, academia and law enforcement officials from South Asia and Europe, who are at the forefront of the fight against digital extremism. The conference aims to enhance counter-terrorism dialogues and partnerships in the region, consistent with the EU’s Indo-Pacific Strategy, which foresees a deeper and stronger engagement with partners that share common goals and face common challenges.

India’s Designated Authority for Cross-Border Trade has approved an additional 251 MW of power exports from 12 hydropower projects in Nepal. For the first time, Nepal would be exporting power to Bihar through a medium-term power sales agreement. This takes the total allowed quantum from 690 MW from 16 projects to 941 MW from 28 projects. In October 2021, India approved 39 MW of power exports from Nepal to India for the first time. In less than 3 years, this figure has grown by more than 24 times. Nepal first began its power exports by selling in the Day Ahead Market of the Indian Energy Exchange. India has also opened the provision of counting hydropower imports from Nepal as a part of the Hydropower Purchase Obligation  for buyers in India, which further incentivises buyers to purchase power from Nepal. The Agreement for Long-Term Power between India and Nepal envisages the sale of up to 10,000 MW of power from Nepal to India in the next 10 years.

Indian Ambassador Dr. Suhel Ajaz Khan presented his credentials  to President Dr. Rashad Al-Alimi in Aden today, marking the first visit by an Indian ambassador to Yemen in nearly a decade. This historic occasion reinforces diplomatic ties between India and Yemen, with Ambassador Khan emphasizing India’s commitment to supporting Yemen’s peace, stability, and development. President of Yemen Al-Alimi expressed his desire to further strengthen bilateral relations, recalling Yemen’s longstanding ties with India. In addition to his meeting with President Al-Alimi, Dr. Khan is scheduled to engage with various local interlocutors, government officials, and members of the Indian community during his visit.

Orange alert has been issued in Pathanamthitta, Kottayam, Idukki, Malappuram, Kozhikode and Wayanad districts. The International Cricket Council (ICC) has moved the ninth edition of Women’s T20 World Cup to the United Arab Emirates (UAE) from Bangladesh, while the Bangladesh Cricket Board (BCB) will continue as the official host of the event. The tournament will take place across the two venues in the UAE – Dubai and Sharjah – from October 3 to 20. ICC Chief Executive Geoff Allardice confirmed the decision in a statement, expressing disappointment that the tournament could not be hosted in Bangladesh as originally planned.

In Wrestling, India’s Ronak Dahiya clinched a bronze in the Greco-Roman 110 kg category at the ongoing U-17 World Wrestling Championships in Amman, Jordan. The current world number 2, Ronak defeated Turkiye’s Emrullah Capkan 6-1 in the bronze medal match. Earlier, Ronak had lost the semi final bout by 0-2 against his Hungarian opponent Zoltan Czako.

International Tennis Integrity Agency (ITIA) has relieved men’s world number one player, Jannik Sinner of any period of suspension or ban in the case of two positive dope tests. The independent tribunal of ITIA, accepted Sinner’s explanation that his two anti-doping tests resulted positive from an over-the-counter spray, that included the prohibited substance clostebol.
