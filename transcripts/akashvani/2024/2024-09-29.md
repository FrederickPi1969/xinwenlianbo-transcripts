In Bihar, several low lying areas have been submerged due to massive water discharge from two biggest barrages on  Gandak and Kosi rivers. Over the past few days catchment areas of Nepal and Northern Bihar are witnessing heavy rainfall following which Gandak, Kosi, Mahananda and their tributaries are swollen. Flash flood alert has been issued in 13 districts. According to Disaster Management Department, One lakh forty one thousand people have been affected due to flood in 20 blocks in the state. All the 56 sluice gates of Kosi barrage in Supaul have been opened to ease the pressure. More than six lakh cusecs of water have been released from Kosi barrage which is highest in 56 years. Traffic has been stopped on Indo-Nepal road till further orders in view of high pressure and overtopping of water in Birpur. From Gandak barrage more than 5.5 lakh cusecs of water has been released. The instant impact is being witnessed in Supaul, Saharsa, Madhubani, Katihar, Araria, West Champaran, East Champaran and Sitamarhi districts where flood waters spread in several areas. More from our correspondent:

“The water resources department has set up a special war room at Patna to monitor and redress issues related to flood. This will work round the clock for next 72 hours. All flood divisions under department are vigilant and patrolling is being carried out on embankments. Villagers from low lying and embankments area are being evaluated to safer places. Eight rivers including Gandak, Kosi, Bagmati, Kamla Balan are flowing above danger level at several places. Dharmendra Kumar Rai, Akashvani News, Patna.”

External Affairs Minister Dr S Jaishankar has stated that Pakistan’s policy of cross-border terrorism will never succeed and actions will certainly have consequences. He said this while addressing the 79th session of the UN General Assembly in New York last night. Dr Jaishankar said the issues to be resolved between the two countries are the vacation of illegally occupied Indian territory by Pakistan and the abandonment of its long-standing attachment to terrorism.

The External Affairs Minister criticised the neighbouring country, saying that Pakistan’s misdeeds have affected others as well, especially the neighbourhood.

The External Affairs Minister said the world cannot be fatalistic about the continuation of violence on a large scale. He said that whether it is the war in Ukraine or the conflict in Gaza, the international community seeks urgent solutions.

Dr Jaishankar called for reforming multilateralism, saying the urgency of this call is highlighted by the theme of the UNGA session – Leaving No One Behind. He said that an effective, efficient, and more representative UN is essential.

Following the death of Hezbollah leader Hassan Nasrallah in a strike by Israeli Defence Forces, Israel’s  Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu issued a warning to Iran and asserted that those who target Israel will face consequences. He said no location in Iran or the Middle East is beyond Israel’s reach.

Mr Netanyahu said, Israel settled accounts with someone who was responsible for the murders of countless Israelis and many nationals of other countries. He emphasised that the killing of Hassan Nasrallah was crucial in achieving the goal of returning Israeli hostages.

Twin bomb blasts rocked Mogadishu and the Middle Shabelle region of Somalia Yesterday. As per police reports, at least six people lost their lives and 10 others were injured in the deadly explosions.

The blasts occurred in two separate locations – one near the National Theatre in Mogadishu, about a kilometre from the president’s office, and another in a livestock market in Jowhar city. It was not immediately clear who had carried out the attacks.

In Cricket, Bangladesh will resume their first innings at 107 for three against India on the third day of the second Test match at Green Park Stadium in Kanpur this morning.

The Day 2 of the test match was called off without a ball being bowled due to rain. For Bangladesh, Mominul Haque, with 40 runs, and Mushfiqur Rahim, with six runs, were at the crease when stumps were drawn on day 1.

Gulveer Singh, a 26-year-old Indian athlete, won the gold medal in the men’s 5,000 meters race at the World Athletics Continental Tour in Japan yesterday. He set a new national record with a time of 13 minutes and 11.82 seconds. This broke his own previous record of 13 minutes and 18.92 seconds, which he had set earlier this year.

The IMD also predicted light to moderate rainfall over Goa, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Gujarat & Uttar Pradesh for the next two days. The Weather agency said that light to moderate rainfall is also expected over Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh, Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan and Delhi for the upcoming two days.

World Heart Day is being celebrated across the globe today. Every year on 29th of September, this day is observed to raise awareness about cardiovascular diseases (CVDs) and promote heart health globally. The day serves as a reminder for individuals, governments, and health organizations to take proactive measures in preventing heart-related conditions, which remain the leading cause of death worldwide. This year’s theme is Use Heart for Action which encourages individuals to take proactive steps in caring for their hearts and promoting a heart-healthy environment in their communities.

To mark this occasion, Union Minister of Minority Affairs and Parliamentary Affairs Kiren Rijiju flagged off a 3 km marathon from India Gate to Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi today. He also extended his wishes to the public on the occasion of World Heart Day and urged them to promote heart health.

## Midday News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today laid the foundation stone and dedicated to the nation various development projects worth over 11,200 crore rupees in Maharashtra through video conferencing. The Prime Minister also inaugurated the Pune Metro section between District Court and Swargate.

Speaking on the occasion, the Prime Minister said, the projects unveiled today will give a boost to urban development and significantly add to ‘Ease of Living’ for the people of Maharashtra.

On revamping of the Solapur Airport, Mr Modi said this will significantly enhance connectivity in the area and boost tourism, particularly religious tourism in Pandharpur.

The Prime Minister also inaugurated the Bidkin Industrial Area. He said the project will attract huge investment and create thousands of employment opportunities in the area.

Prime Minister Modi addressed the 114th edition of Mann Ki Baat on Akashvani today. Speaking during his monthly broadcast, the Prime Minister said, India has become a manufacturing powerhouse due to the Make in India initiative. He said the poor, the middle class and MSMEs are getting a lot of benefit from this campaign and it has provided an opportunity to people of every class to showcase their talent. Mr Modi urged the people to buy Made in India products during the coming festive season.

The Prime Minister pointed towards the changing dynamics of jobs with the emergence of new sectors. He said, the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting has started 25 challenges under the theme, ‘Create in India’ in order to promote talent and creativity.

During the programme, the Prime Minister highlighted the success of Swachh Bharat Mission which is going to complete 10 years on the 2nd of next month. Mr Modi urged the people to take part in the cleanliness campaign.

Encouraging people to participate in the mega plantation drive ‘Ek Ped Maa Ke Naam’ campaign, the Prime Minister said, it leads to amazing results for the entire society which is truly inspiring.

Prime Minister Modi also stressed on the importance of water conservation in his monthly broadcast.

Mr Modi emphasized on the mantra of Vikas Bhi Virasat Bhi. He mentioned the return of around 300 ancient artifacts by the United States during his recent visit to the US.

Highlighting the importance of mother tongue, Prime Minister appreciated the efforts made to preserve languages especially Santhali language. He said, a campaign has been started to give a new identity to Santhali with the help of digital innovation.

In today’s Mann Ki Baat, Prime Minister Modi said this episode is especially emotional for him, as the program is completing 10 years on the 3rd of October. Mr. Modi said, crores of listeners of Mann Ki Baat have been companions in this journey who provided information from every corner of the country. The Prime Minister appreciated the people associated with Akashvani, Doordarshan, and Prasar Bharati saying with their tireless efforts, Mann Ki Baat has reached this important milestone. He also thanked TV channels, print media, and Youtubers who broadcast Mann Ki Baat and programmes on it.

Concluding his address, Mr Modi greeted people for the upcoming festivals, beginning Navratri.

Union Minister for Information and Broadcasting Ashwini Vaishnaw today addressed the 10th edition of the lecture series organized by Sankalp Trust Foundation in New Delhi. Mr Vaishnaw said, India has witnessed high growth and low inflation as opposed to other countries. He further spoke about four pillars of economic growth which are Investment in Infrastructure, Inclusive Development, Manufacturing and Simplification of laws.
