## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi who is on an official visit to Poland will hold delegation level talks with his Polish counterpart Donald Tusk in Warsaw  today. The two leaders are expected to review bilateral relations and exchange views on a range of issues. The meeting between the two sides will also offer an opportunity to explore other avenues. Mr Modi will also call on President of Poland Andrzej Duda. Prior to that, Prime Minister Modi will be accorded ceremonial welcome. He will also lay a wreath at the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier. The Prime Minister will also interact with business leaders and Polish influencers. Last night, addressing the Indian diaspora in Warsaw, Mr Modi said, today’s India seeks to connect with everyone and wants the development and welfare of all. He said, for decades, India had a policy of maintaining distance from other countries, but the policy of today’s India is to remain close to all nations. The Prime Minister stated that India’s stance is clear that this is not an era of war and India believes in dialogue and diplomacy to resolve conflicts.

The Prime Minister reached Warsaw last evening in the first leg of his two-nation visit to Poland and Ukraine. In a social media post, he said, this visit will add momentum to the India-Poland friendship and benefit the people of these two nations. Our correspondent reports that this is the first visit by an Indian Prime Minister to Poland in the past 45 years.

Poland is an important partner for India in Central Europe. The bilateral trade between India and Poland is substantial and it is of the order of six billion dollar which makes Poland India’s largest trading partner in Central and Eastern Europe. India’s investment into Poland is  estimated at around three billion dollar and  the Polish investment into India is about one billion dollar. A number of Indian companies have active business presence in Poland which are engaged in various sectors including IT , electronics  pharmaceuticals, steel, metals and chemicals. Nearly 30 companies of Poland are engaged in various business activities in India. Anand Kumar for Akashvani News.

In the second and final leg of his two nation tour, the Prime Minister will reach Ukraine tomorrow on the invitation of Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy.

Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology has asked the social media platforms operating in India to comply with a recent Supreme Court order related to the identity of the deceased in RG Kar Medical College incident. The Supreme Court in its order has directed that all references to the name of the deceased, along with photographs and video clips depicting the deceased, be promptly removed from all social media platforms and electronic media.

In light of this order, the Ministry said that failure to comply with the Supreme Court’s order may result in legal consequences and further regulatory action.

In the wake of the recent case of sexual assault of two minor girls in a private school in Ghatkopar in Thane district of Maharashtra, the state government yesterday approved the appointment of an administrator. The Government Resolution, GR has also been issued making new arrangements to ensure the safety of school students in the backdrop of recent incidents.

The GR mandates the installation of CCTV cameras at all prime locations in all the schools across the state within a period of one month. On the lines of Vishakha guidelines, schools are to form ‘Students Security Committees’ within a week. To review all these arrangements, their implementation, and their effectiveness, the government has approved a committee with the School Education Commissioner as its chairperson, along with other members. It has also been stated that any bad incident involving a school student must be reported to the concerned education officer within 24 hours. Any delay in this would invite serious action. Ankita Apte, Akashvani News, Mumbai.

To prevent the menace of child marriage in the state, the Assam Government has decided to introduce a new bill to abolish the Assam Muslim Marriages and Divorce Registration Act and Rules of 1935. The new bill replacing it will be tabled in the Assembly in the next session. Earlier this year, the state Cabinet decided to abolish the Act, and yesterday’s meeting authorized the Repealing Bill required to implement this decision.

Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma stated that the Cabinet has taken a significant step to ensure justice for the daughters and sisters of Assam by adding additional safeguards against child marriage.

The autumn session of the Assam Legislative Assembly will begin today. This is the first session after the Lok Sabha polls and State government is scheduled to introduce several key bills. The five day long session is scheduled to conclude on 30th August.

Madhya Pradesh has achieved a significant milestone by becoming the first state in India to formalize rules allowing the digital issuance of summons and warrants. This pioneering initiative permits the use of electronic methods such as email, messaging apps like WhatsApp and text messages in its judicial processes.

The Madhya Pradesh Home Department has officially published the Madhya Pradesh Electronic Processes (Issuance, Service and Execution) Rules, 2024 in the Madhya Pradesh Gazette. Appreciating this initiative, Madhya Pradesh Chief Minister Dr Mohan Yadav said that this development, sanctioned under the country’s new law, the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita 2023, aims to accelerate judicial procedures, fortify legal proceedings and enhance the efficiency of the judicial system.

According to the gazette notification, these new rules apply when the concerned individual uses messaging apps such as WhatsApp or other digital communication methods. For individuals unfamiliar with digital communication services, summons and warrants will continue to be served in the conventional manner.Sanjeev Sharma, Akashvani News, Bhopal

With the IMD forcasting an alert for extremely heavy rainfall for today, the state machinery is on its toes to mitigate the impact and provide relief to the people. The chief minister has also directed all the medical officers to visit relief camps and do health check ups.

The Czech police evacuated 582 people from the vicinity of a chemical plant in the country’s northwest region after an unexploded bomb was found there yesterday. The Czech refining and petrochemical company said that an unexploded aerial bomb from World War II was found during excavation work in a remote part of the Orlen Unipetrol plant near Litvinov. The police said that the bomb weighing 250 kg will remain in place until August 27. A crisis team is now meeting to decide on the next course of action.

The Kerala government has announced a cash reward of 2 crore rupees for former Indian Hockey goalkeeper PR Sreejesh, honouring him for winning Bronze medal at Paris Olympics. The decision was taken during a cabinet meeting presided over by Chief Minister Pinarayi Vijayan yesterday.

The Kerala Government said, the ‘Great Wall’ of Indian Hockey, PR Sreejesh, made India and Kerala proud by securing his second Olympic medal at the 2024 Paris Olympics.

Meanwhile, the Indian Men’s Hockey Team received a grand welcome by fans and Odisha government when it arrived at the Bhubaneswar airport yesterday. The Team was honoured with a thrilling open-top bus parade in the city for their back-to-back Olympic glory.

The Government has said the flood situation in Bangladesh is not due to release of water from the Indian dam on Gumti River in Tripura. In a statement issued today, Ministry of External Affairs said, concerns are being expressed in Bangladesh that the current situation of flood in districts on the eastern borders of Bangladesh has been caused by opening of the Dumbur dam upstream of the Gumti River in Tripura. The Ministry termed this factually incorrect, pointing out that the catchment areas of Gumti river have witnessed heaviest rains in the last few days and the flood in Bangladesh is primarily due to waters from these large catchments downstream of the dam.

The Ministry said it remains committed to resolving issues and mutual concerns in water resources and river water management through bilateral consultations and technical discussions.

The first meeting of the Joint Parliamentary Committee on the Waqf (Amendment) Bill 2024 is underway in New Delhi. Officials from the Ministry of Minority Affairs and Ministry of Law and Justice are briefing the Members about the various amendments proposed in the draft law.

The Waqf (Amendment) Bill, 2024 is intended to overcome the shortcomings and to enhance the efficiency of the administration and management of the waqf properties.
