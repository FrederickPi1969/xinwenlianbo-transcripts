The total foodgrain production in the country is estimated at over three thousand 288 lakh tonnes in 2023-24. The production of Wheat is estimated at over one thousand 129 lakh tonnes which is higher by over 23 lakh tonnes from previous year’s wheat production. The production of Tur is estimated at around 34 lakh tonnes, which is marginally higher by 0.73 Lakh tonnes from last year’s production.          The Ministry said, this Advance Estimates of area, production and yield includes Kharif, Rabi and Summer season. World Environment Day is celebrated to raise awareness, promote action, and encourage global cooperation on     environmental issues. It is a global event led by United Nations to encourage awareness and action to protect the environment. This day was initiated by United Nations General Assembly in 1972 during the Stockholm Conference on the Human Environment. To mark this event, the first World Environment Day was celebrated a year later, in 1973. Since then, it has been celebrated every year on 5th of June. This is Pallavi Parmar for Akashvani News, Delhi.
