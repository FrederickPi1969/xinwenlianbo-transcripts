## Morning News

President Droupadi Murmu  will leave on a three-nation visit to Algeria, Mauritania and Malawi today morning. This will be the first ever visit by the President of India to the three African nations and comes a year after the African Union was made a permanent member of G 20 during India’s Presidency. During her visit, President Droupadi Murmu will hold bilateral meetings and interact with Indian diaspora. A report from our correspondent covering President’s visit:

“During the three day visit in Algeria,  President Droupadi Murmu will hold bilateral meetings with her counterpart, Abdelmadjid Tebboune and other major leaders. The President will also visit important cultural and historical sites of Algeria like Maqam Echahid, the martyrs memorial, Roman Ruins, Mausoleum, Hamma Garden etc.President is also scheduled to  address the India-Algeria Economic Forum and the Sidi Abdellah Science and Technology Pole University. On the second leg of the visit, President Droupadi Murmu will visit Mauritania on 16th  October and in the final leg , President will visit Malawi from from 17th to 19th  October. During the visit to these countries, the President is scheduled to hold bilateral meetings with the leadership and will also interact with key trade and industry leaders and the Indian diaspora. The three nation visit by President Droupadi Murmu aims to expand and strengthen India’s ties with the countries in Africa. Mayusha for Akashvani News from Delhi.

Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla will lead a Parliamentary delegation to the 149th Assembly of the Inter-Parliamentary Union (IPU) scheduled to be held in Geneva from today till the 17th of this month. Mr. Birla will address the assembly on the theme “Harnessing science, technology and innovation for a more peaceful and sustainable future.” He will participate in the meetings of the IPU’s Governing council and will meet the Indian Diaspora in Geneva, tomorrow. Besides this he will also meet his counterparts from other Parliaments on the sidelines of the Assembly.

The Delegation comprises of Rajya Sabha deputy chairman Harivansh Singh, Secretary General of both the houses and various other Members of Parliament.

The IPU has 180 member parliaments and 15 associate members. Members include parliaments from large countries like China, India, and Indonesia, as well as smaller countries like Cabo Verde, San Marino, and Palau.

The International Kullu Dussehra Festival will begin today in Himachal Pradesh. The week-long Dussehra celebrations will be inaugurated by Governor Shiv Pratap Shukla. A grand chariot procession of Lord Raghunath will be taken out on the occasion. This festival symbolizes the divine culture of Kullu. More from our correspondent:

The International Dussehra Festival is beginning today in Kullu, Himachal Pradesh. In Kerala, Vidyarambham ceremonies are being held across the state today. Vidyarambham symbolises a child’s first step towards knowledge and learning. Many people also start taking dance and music lessons on this day. A report by our correspondent:

” Hundreds of tiny tots were initiated into the world of letters at several places across the state as part of Vidyarambham ceremonies this morning. At many locations, teachers, authors, scholars or a senior member of the family led the initiation ceremonies, by writing on the child’s tongue with a honey dipped golden ring or making the child write on a rice- filled platter. Besides households, Vidyarambham ceremonies were held at Thunchan Parambu at Tirur and also at several temples including Panachikkadu, Chottanikkara, Paravur and Attukal. Over the years, the ceremony has acquired a secular dimension in Kerala, with people belonging to other religious beliefs also opting to initiate children into the world of letters on Vidyarambham day.” Raj Mohan, Akashvani News, Kochi.

Amid the escalating tensions in West Asia, Permanent Mission of India to United Nations said that India, as a major troop-contributing country, aligns itself fully with the joint statement issued by the 34 United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon (UNIFL).

In Pakistan, Punjab government has imposed Section 144 in Rawalpindi till October 17 to maintain the law and order situation during the upcoming Shanghai Cooperation Organisation summit to be held in Islamabad on 15th and 16th of this month.

Section 144 of Pakistan allows the government to prohibit various forms of political assemblies, gatherings, sit-ins, rallies, demonstrations, and similar activities for a specified period. In Rawalpindi, this restriction will be in effect for a week till October 17.

Meanwhile, in the first such visit in nearly a decade, External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar will travel to Islamabad to attend the SCO Summit this month.

In Men’s Cricket, India defeated Bangladesh by 133 runs in the third and final T20 International match and clinched the three match series by a margin of 3-0 in Hyderabad yesterday. We have more from our correspondent:

In the third and final T20 International match of the series, India defeated Bangladesh by 133 runs. India posted a mammoth total of 297 runs, This was the second highest innings total in T20Is, behind Nepal’s 314 for three against Mongolia last year. India comfortably moved past its previous best T20I score – a Rohit Sharma-inspired 260 for six against Sri Lanka back in 2017.

Chasing 298 runs, the visitors were restricted to 164 for seven wickets in the stipulated 20 overs. For Bangladesh, Towhid Hridoy unbeaten with 63 runs was the top scorer. For hosts, Ravi Bishnoi scalped three wickets and Mayank Yadav took two wickets.

Earlier, opting to bat first, Sanju Samson scored 111 in 47 balls and brought up his maiden T20I hundred. Captain Suryakumar Yadav scored 75 runs off 35 balls. The winning margin of 133 runs, is also India’s third-biggest win by runs in men’s T20 Internationals. Sanju Samson was declared Player of the Match and Hardik Pandya was declared Player of the Series. PAYAL SHARMA, AKASHVANI NEWS.

Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk has announced plans to temporarily suspend the right to apply for asylum. The measure comes as a part of a new governmental strategy which is aimed at drastically decreasing irregular migration and all the negative consequences that it brings to the country. During a speech at a political convention in Warsaw yesterday, Prime Minister Tusk emphasised the need for European recognition of Poland’s decision explaining that the right to asylum is being exploited by hostile nations and human traffickers. He said, Poland must regain full control over who enters the country assuring that the government would minimise illegal migration. Mr Tusk also stressed that Poland will not adopt any EU policies that jeopardise its security. He further emphasised the importance of integration for those wishing to stay in Poland long-term, citing Germany’s past failure to prioritise this in its migration policy.
