## Midday News

Campaigning for second phase of Lok Sabha polls is in full swing, set to conclude tomorrow. Leaders of various political parties are holding multiple rallies across the country to woo the voters. Polling for this phase will take place in 89 constituencies of 13 states and union territories.

Prime Minister and senior BJP leader Narendra Modi has accused Congress of doing appeasement and vote bank politics. Addressing an election rally at Uniara in Tonk-Sawai Madhopur Lok Sabha constituency in Rajasthan today, Mr. Modi alleged that during the Congress rule in 2004 and 2011, attempts were made to reduce the reservation for SC-ST and give it on the basis of religion. He said that Congress had conspired to divide the reservation on the basis of religion in Andhra Pradesh and Karnataka too. He accused Congress of playing with the spirit of the Constitution. Mr. Modi assured that reservation for SC -ST and backward classes will neither end nor be allowed to be divided on the basis of religion.

The Prime Minister also appealed to the voters to strengthen democracy by voting in maximum numbers.

He will also address election rallies in Sakti of Janjgir-Champa Lok Sabha constituency and Dhamtari of Mahasamund Lok Sabha constituency this afternoon. Mr. Modi is on a two-day visit to Chhattisgarh from today. During his visit, he will address three public meetings in various Loksabha constituencies of Chhattisgarh. Tomorrow, he will hold a public meeting in Ambikapur of Surguja Lok Sabha constituency.

Union Minister and senior BJP leader Amit Shah will take part in a road show in Bengaluru this evening which will traverse from Bommanahalli near Hosur road to IIMB on Bannerghatta Road. Congress General Secretary Priyanka Gandhi Vadra will be addressing two public rallies in Chitradurga and Bangalore South Parliamentary constituencies today. Aam Aadmi party state President Mukhyamantri Chandru will share the dais on this occasion.

High pitched political battle is going on in Uttar Pradesh today. Senior BJP leader and Union Minister Rajnath Singh today campaigned in Gautam Budh Nagar district. Addressing a large gathering, he said that Noida which was earlier infamous as a corruption centre of Uttar Pradesh has now became the business centre of the state. He said, now UP is known for its law and order, development, investment and good governance.

On the other hand, Western UP is going to witness high octane campaigning today and Meerut city is the battleground. The top leaders of three major political parties in the state will be in Meerut today. Samajwadi Party Chief Akhilesh Yadav will address a political gathering in Siwaal Khas area of Meerut district today. He will also campaign in Aligarh district. Bahujan Samaj Party Supremo Mayawati is scheduled to address a rally in Meerut district this afternoon.

At the same time senior BJP leader and Chief Minister of Uttar Pradesh Yogi Adityanath will also hold a road show.

Earlier, he addressed a rally in Amroha district of Western Uttar Pradesh. He will also address a rally in Baraout area of Baghpat district this afternoon.

The Directorate General of Civil Aviation has asked airlines to ensure that children up to the age of 12 years are allocated seats with their parents and guardians who are travelling on the same PNR.

According to the DGCA’s statement, various instances came to its notice wherein children below the age of 12 years were not seated along with their parent or guardian. It said, the existing air transport circular has been suitably modified.

The European Union, EU has adopted new visa rules that will allow frequent travellers from India to apply for multiple-entry Schengen visas with longer validity, easing travel to 29 European countries. Announced by the EU delegation to India and Bhutan yesterday, the rules will make it easier for regular Indian travellers to get the visa under the new cascade regime. The two-year visa will normally be followed by a five-year visa, if the passport has sufficient validity remaining. It added that during the validity period of these visas, holders will enjoy travel rights equivalent to visa-free nationals.

In a significant move, the Ministry of External Affairs has issued directions to the passport offices to issue a revocation order to individuals from Goa and Daman & Diu, who had their Indian passports revoked after acquiring Portuguese citizenship.

This decision could offer relief to many who were rendered ineligible to apply for Overseas Citizenship of India (OCI) due to the previous passport requirements.

Goa Chief Minister Pramod Sawant has said, the Ministry of External Affairs circular on revocation certificate to be a valid document in lieu of the surrender certificate for obtaining Overseas Citizen of India card would be a relief to thousands of Goans.

The Central government has rejected the claims of mandatory transaction of money in Aadhaar banking at least once in a month. Branding the claims as fake, the PIB Fact Check Unit has denied the fact that new updates by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has made it mandatory for people to transact money through Aadhaar every month. It added that failure to do so will not lock the customer’s Aadhaar transaction facility as claimed.

PIB clarified that no such guidelines have been issued for account holders for mandatory Aadhaar Enabled Payment System (AePS) transactions every month to keep the services active.

Archery World Cup 2024 will begin today with qualification round in compound 50m event at Shanghai in China. The three-time Olympian Deepika Kumari will spearhead the Indian challenge at the Archery World Cup. India has sent a 16-member archery team – eight men and eight women – for the first stage of the World Cup which will be held between 23rd to 28th of this month.

Deepika Kumari, who topped India’s archery selection trials in March, will feature in the women’s recurve archery. Dhiraj Bommadevara, who has obtained India’s only archery quota for the Paris 2024 Olympics and Tokyo Olympians, Tarundeep Rai and Pravin Jadhav will lead India’s charge in men’s recurve.

In IPL Cricket, Chennai Super Kings will take on Lucknow Super Giants at the M. A. Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai today. The match will start at 7.30 PM.
