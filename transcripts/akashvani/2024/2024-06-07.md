## Morning News

As the National Democratic Alliance gears up to form the government, political activity has gained momentum in the national capital with leaders of the BJP and JD(U) holding separate meetings. Today, the NDA parliamentary party meeting will take place in New Delhi. Newly elected MPs of BJP, TDP, JD(U), LJP (R), Shiv Sena (Shinde Faction), JD(S), and other constituent parties of NDA will attend the meeting.

A JD(U) Parliamentary party meeting is also scheduled to be held this morning ahead of the NDA parliamentary party meeting.

BJP President J P Nadda and Union Minister Rajnath Singh have been given the responsibility to hold discussions with NDA partners over the cabinet formation. Yesterday, Mr Nadda held a long meeting with senior BJP leaders and Union Ministers in New Delhi.

In Odisha, the race for the first BJP chief minister is heating up with at least half-a-dozen names doing the rounds. The state leaders of the party including all 20 newly elected MPs are camping in Delhi and likely to meet the Central leaders today on the issue. The Central BJP leadership will take opinions of the MPs and other leaders before taking a final call on the name of the new chief minister of Odisha in the parliamentary board meeting.

“Amid speculations over who would be the first BJP chief minister of Odisha,  the party’s parliamentary board is likely to take a call on this in two to three days. Prime Minister and senior BJP leader Narendra Modi during a number of election rallies in Odisha had said that the new BJP chief minister would take oath on 10th of June at Bhubaneswar. The front runners include six-time MP and former Tribal Affairs Minister Jual Oram, National Vice  President Baijayant Panda, Union Minister Dharmendra Pradhan and senior MLA Mohan Charan Majhi. The name of former Lieutenant Governor of Jammu and Kashmir and former Comptroller and Audit General of India Girish Chandra Murmu is also considered as a front runner for the coveted post.

Aam Aadmi Party has said that it will contest the Delhi Assembly elections alone. A meeting of all the party MLA was held at the Chief Minister’s residence in the national capital today. Talking to reporters after the meeting, Delhi Minister Gopal Rai said, the INDIA bloc was established specifically for the Lok Sabha election. He said, Aam Aadmi Party fought the Lok Sabha elections together with Congress but in Delhi Assembly Elections, there will be no alliance.

The notification for election to three seats of Rajya Sabha in Kerala was issued yesterday. The tenure of CPI leader Binoy Biswam, CPI (M) leader Elamaram Kareem and KC (M) leader Jose K Mani is ending on 1st of July.

Notification for bypoll to one seat from Maharashtra in the Rajya Sabha was also issued yesterday. The seat has been vacant due to resignation of NCP leader Praful Patel.

Nominations can be filed for the seats in Kerala and Maharashtra till 13th of this month. Polling will be held on 25th June.

Meanwhile, nine members of the Rajya Sabha have been elected to the Lok Sabha in the recently concluded General Elections.

The BJP refuted the claims made by Congress leader Rahul Gandhi over the stock market scam saying he is trying to deceive market investors. Briefing media in New Delhi, senior BJP leader Piyush Goyal claimed that people have gained even during the election results day. He further added that foreigners have incurred losses while Indian investors have benefited. He said that when the UPA government was in power 10 years ago, India’s market capital at that time was 67 lakh crore rupees and today the market capital has increased to 415 lakh crore rupees.

Earlier, Rahul Gandhi has demanded a Joint Parliamentary Committee (JPC) investigation into the stock market crash of 4th of June. Addressing media at party headquarters in New Delhi yesterday, Mr Gandhi said the retail investors have lost 30 lakh crore rupees due to the market crash.

The National Testing Agency has said that the increase in cutoff of NEET (UG) reflects the competitive nature of the examination and the higher performance standards achieved by the candidates this year. NTA said this in a clarification released in light of recent queries raised by the candidates regarding the NEET (UG) results. It further added that due to the differences in the old and new editions of the NCERT textbook, the Subject Experts held that two options be taken as correct in place of one option. Out of the 67 candidates who got full marks, 44 are on account of the revision in one answer key of Physics and six are on account of compensatory marks for loss of time.

The Calcutta High Court has ordered that the central and state government have to jointly work to stop post poll violence in West Bengal. A division bench of Justices Kausik Chanda and Apurba Sinha Ray was hearing a public interest litigation on the issue yesterday. The PIL was filed by an association of lawyers in the court after 11 people lost their lives in the post poll violence in the state. The court ordered that if the state police failed to stop then central forces can intervene.

The National Health Authority has achieved a significant milestone with the generation of over three crore tokens for Out-Patient Department (OPD) registrations through the ABHA-based Scan and Share service. Union Health Ministry said, the ABHA-based Scan and Share service enables patients to conveniently register for OPD appointments by scanning a QR code displayed at the OPD registration counter.

The Reserve Bank of India will announce its second bi-monthly policy of the current fiscal year today. RBI’s Monetary Policy Committee’s 3 day meeting began on 5th of June. In the last meeting in April, the RBI had kept the repo rate unchanged at 6.5 per cent. The RBI kept the standing deposit facility (SDF) rate at 6.25 per cent and the marginal standing facility (MSF) rate and the Bank Rate at 6.75 per cent.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has extended an invitation to Prime Minister Narendra Modi to visit Ukraine at a convenient time. The invitation came during a phone conversation between the two leaders, where Zelenskyy congratulated Prime Minister Modi on his recent election victory and wished for the swift formation of the government.

India Meteorological Department (IMD) has issued yellow alert warning for rainfall, thunderstorms and lightning over many parts of country including Central and Southern regions for the next four days.

“With the further advancement of southwest monsoon, light to moderate rainfall accompanied by thunderstorm and lightning is expected over Konkan, Goa, Maharashtra, Marathwada, Saurashtra, Kutch, Coastal Andhra Pradesh, Yanam, Rayalaseema, Telangana and Karnataka during the next four days. The IMD has also forecast widespread rainfall accompanied by thunderstorms and lightning over North East region, West Bengal and Sikkim during the same period. Rainfall and thunderstorms are likely at a few places over Northwest India today. The weather department said that similar conditions are also likely to occur over Jammu, Kashmir, Ladakh, Gilgit, Muzaffarabad, Himachal Pradesh and Uttarakhand for the next two to three days. On heatwave, the IMD said that severe heat wave conditions are likely to prevail over some parts of Uttar Pradesh till Monday. Heat wave conditions are very likely in Madhya Pradesh, Jharkhand, Punjab, Haryana, Bihar and Odisha till the next two to three days.

On to sports, In the ICC Men’s T20 World Cup Cricket, hosts United States stunned the former champions Pakistan by five runs in a thrilling Super Over last night.

In the first big upset of this T20 World Cup, USA pulled off a massive victory against Pakistan after their encounter ended in a tie at the Grand Prairie Stadium in Dallas, last night. Electing to field first, newcomers USA restricted Pakistan to 159 for 7 and then matched the score with loss of 3 wickets in the stipulated 20 overs. In the Super Over, USA scored 18 for loss of 1 wicket and Pakistan could manage only 13 runs for one. In the second match yesterday, Scotland defeated Namibia by five wickets at Kensington Oval in Barbados. Chasing the victory target of 156, Scotland overhauled the target with 5 wickets in hand and 9 balls to spare. Earlier, Namibia scored 155 for 9 in stipulated 20 overs. Canada will take on Ireland at the Nassau County International Cricket Stadium in New York this evening.

In badminton, India’s ace shuttler Lakshya Sen will take on Anders Antonsen of Denmark in the Men’s singles quarter-final at the Indonesia Open Badminton in Jakarta, Indonesia this afternoon. Sen defeated Kenta Nishimoto of Japan, in straight games 21-9, 21-15, in the pre-quarters yesterday.

Indian star football player Sunil Chhetri’s last game for India ended in a goalless draw against Kuwait yesterday. The 0-0 at the Salt Lake dented India’s hopes of qualifying for the third round of the FIFA World Cup qualifiers.

India are second in the table with 5 points, ahead of Kuwait and Afghanistan on four points, but with a draw enough for Kuwait and a win for Afghanistan enough, India will need a result against Qatar to progress.

Russian President Vladimir Putin has said that Russia could deploy conventional missiles within striking distance of the United States and its European allies if they allowed Ukraine to strike deeper into Russia with long-range Western weapons. In his first face-to-face meeting with senior editors of international news agencies since the war in Ukraine began, Mr. Putin said the West was wrong to assume Russia would never use nuclear weapons. He said the Kremlin’s nuclear doctrine should not be taken lightly.

When asked about NATO Chief Jens Stoltenberg’s calls to allow Ukraine to use Western weapons to strike Russian territory, Putin differentiated between different missiles but warned allowing Kyiv to strike Russia with ever more powerful weapons.

## Evening News

Mr Modi to take oath as Prime Minister for the third term on Sunday.

Prime Minister-designate Narendra Modi met President Droupadi Murmu this evening and staked claim to form the government. Mr Modi will take the oath for the third term at Rashtrapati Bhawan on Sunday evening. The President will administer the oath to Narendra Modi and his new cabinet.

Speaking outside Rashtrapati Bhavan, Mr Modi stated that NDA will form a strong, stable and growth-oriented government. He said, the stable government will prove beneficial for India and its economy, as well as the youth.

Mr Modi said, people have given the NDA government one more chance and this 18th Lok Sabha is a House of new and youthful energy. He said his government will fulfill the hopes and aspirations of the people of the country.

Before that, an NDA delegation led by BJP President JP Nadda also met the President and submitted a letter stating that Narendra Modi had been elected leader of the BJP Parliamentary Party. Letters of support from NDA constituent parties were also handed over to the President.

Earlier in the day, NDA Parliamentary Party unanimously elected Narendra Modi as its leader. Senior BJP Leader, Rajnath Singh moved a proposal to elect Mr Modi as NDA leader and it was supported by the leaders of all NDA allies. BJP leaders Amit Shah and Nitin Gadkari seconded the proposal. Mr. Modi was also elected as the leader of BJP in Lok Sabha, and BJP parliamentary party.

TDP Chief Chandrababu Naidu said, the country has witnessed remarkable progress and transformation under the visionary leadership of Narendra Modi. Mr Naidu said, India is having a right leader at the right time.

Bihar Chief Minister and JD (U) President Nitish Kumar said, they will work together under Mr Modi’s leadership. Mr Kumar said, Mr Modi has served the country and whatever is left he will fulfill in the new term.

Maharashtra Chief Minister and Shiv Sena (Shinde group) leader Eknath Shinde, LJP (R) Chief Chirag Paswan, NCP leader Ajit Pawar, Jan Sena Chief Pawan Kalyan, Apna Dal (S) leader Anupriya Patel and  JD (S) leader H D Kumaraswamy also supported the proposal.

Speaking on the occasion, Mr. Modi said, NDA is not a group of parties that have come together for power but it is an alliance committed to work with the spirit of nation first. He said, NDA is the most successful alliance in India’s history and our aim will be to reach unanimity in all decisions.

RLD Chief Jayant Chaudhary, several Union Ministers including Piyush Goyal and  Anurag Singh Thakur were among others present in the meeting.

Later, after being elected as NDA leader, Narendra Modi met BJP veterans Lal Krishna Advani and Murli Manohar Joshi and took their blessings. Mr. Modi also met with former President Ram Nath Kovind at his residence in New Delhi.

Vice President Jagdeep Dhankhar today highlighted that it is after many years that a Prime Minister has got a third term. At the closing ceremony of the Rajya Sabha Internship Programme at Vice-President’s Enclave, the Vice President urged the gathering to always ensure that Parliament is the greatest temple of democracy, and a place for debate, dialogue, discussion and deliberation.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today said that every moment of his life is dedicated to upholding the noble values enshrined in the Constitution of India, given by Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar. In a social media post, Mr. Modi said, it is only due to the Constitution that a person like him, born into poverty and in a backward family, is able to serve the nation.

In the Bastar division of Chhattisgarh, five Maoists were killed in an encounter today. Their bodies have been recovered from the spot.

Police said, the encounter took place on the border of Narayanpur and Dantewada district. After receiving intelligence inputs about the presence of Maoists in the forest of Gobel, a joint team of District Reserve Guard-DRG and ITBP had set out on a search operation. During the search operation, an encounter took place in the jungles of Gobel. In the gun battle, three jawans of DRG were injured.

The National Investigation Agency (NIA) has attached the property of a member associated with Popular Front of India (PFI) in connection with murder of a Hindu Front leader in Coimbatore in 2016. According to the NIA, the property of accused Subair has been attached on orders of the NIA Special Court, Poonamallee, Chennai. Subair, along with other co-accused were involved in the gruesome killing of C Sasikumar who was the spokesperson of Hindu Front, Coimbatore.

The Government has imposed a curfew in Jiribam, a border town in Manipur, following a decline in law and security conditions since last night.

The situation arose after a person, who had gone to his farm, was found dead with wounds inflicted by a sharp object yesterday. Additionally, some houses belonging to two different communities were also burned down last night by miscreants.

The district administration imposed a curfew from this morning in the town and security forces and district level officers have been instructed to protect lives and properties. The security forces have evacuated villagers of violence prone areas of the town to safer locations today.

The Congress has slammed the government’s move to relocate the statues of Mahatma Gandhi, Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, and Dr. Bhimrao Ramji Ambedkar from their original positions in the Parliament House complex.

Briefing media in New Delhi today, Congress leader Pawan Khera said, the countrymen want to know what is the real intention behind shifting of statues of great leaders. He said, the government has to answer about this in the Parliament and outside the Parliament.

However, Lok Sabha Secretariat has denied the allegations of shifting of the statues. It said, the statues are being installed systematically and respectfully inside the Parliament House complex.

The RBI has said, it is considering to set up a Digital Payments Intelligence Platform to mitigate payment fraud risks. In this regard, the central bank has constituted a committee under the Chairmanship of former MD and CEO of National Payments Corporation of India, Mr. A P Hota to examine its various aspects. The Committee is expected to give its recommendations within two months.

RBI has said that even though the payment ecosystem takes various measures to protect customers from digital frauds, there is a need for network-level intelligence and real-time data sharing across payment systems to prevent such frauds.

The Reserve Bank has proposed to bring an auto-replenishment facility for UPI Lite wallet.  Accordingly, if the balance in the UPI wallet goes below a threshold amount set by the customer, the fund will move from his/her account to the wallet without additional authentication or pre-debit notification.

RBI has said that related guidelines in this regard will be issued shortly.

India’s foreign exchange reserves have surged by 4.83 billion dollars to touch a new lifetime high of 651.5 billion dollars in the week ended 31st May.

Addressing a post policy press conference in Mumbai today, RBI Governor Shaktikanta Das said, the country’s external sector remains resilient. Pointing to improvements in the current account deficit, external debt to GDP ratio and net international investment position, Mr. Das said the apex bank is confident of comfortably meeting the external financing requirements.
