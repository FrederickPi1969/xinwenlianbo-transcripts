## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will share his thoughts with the people of the country and abroad in the ‘Mann Ki Baat’ programme on Akashvani at 11 AM today. Steel production in India, driven by robust demand for government infrastructure projects, is projected to surpass 300 million tonnes (MT) by 2030. Addressing the Bharat Chamber of Commerce in New Delhi, Steel Secretary Nagendra Nath Sinha said current steel demand remains strong with infrastructure push from the government and expecting a growth of about 10 per cent. Mr. Sinha further said that amid the strong GDP growth and continued emphasis on infrastructure from both government and private sectors, steel demand will continue to stay strong.

The Income Tax Department has said that over 5 crore Income Tax Returns (ITRs) have been filed for the current assessment year, representing an 8 percent increase from the previous year. In a social media post, the department said that it received over 28 lakh ITRs just yesterday.

## Midday News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today raised concerns over the drug abuse in the country saying that the government is taking effective steps to fight it. Addressing the nation, in his 112th Mann Ki Baat programme on Akashvani this morning, Prime Minister Modi said the government has taken a  special initiative called MANAS, which is a step in the fight against drug abuse.

He said the Helpline and Portal of MANAS were launched recently and a toll-free number-1933-has been issued. Mr Modi said anyone can call this number to get the necessary advice or information related to rehabilitation.

The Prime Minister said every family is worried that their child might get caught in the grip of drugs. He urged them to engage in making India ‘drugs free’ by making full use of the MANAS Helpline.

Prime Minister Modi expressed pride that Charaideo Maidam of Assam is being included in the UNESCO World Heritage Sites. The Prime Minister highlighted that it will be the 43rd site of India in the list, and the first Cultural World Heritage site from the Northeast. Elaborating on why it is such an important place, Mr Modi said Charaideo was the first capital of the Ahom dynasty.

The Prime Minister termed it as a great feat for the Ahom Empire to sustain from the 13th century till the beginning of the 19th century. He urged people to include Charaideo Maidam in their future travel plans.

Mr Modi noted that a country can progress only by taking pride in her culture. Highlighting the efforts being made in India in this regard, he mentioned Project PARI, which stands for Public Art of India. The Prime Minister observed that Project PARI is becoming a great medium to bring emerging artists on one platform to popularise public art. He said that the paintings made on roadsides, walls, and underpasses are made by the same artists who are associated with PARI.

Speaking about the handloom industry, Mr Modi lauded 250 women of Rohtak district of Haryana for joining the UNNATI Self Help Group and spreading the magic of colours on clothes. The women joined the Self Help Group and received training in block printing and dyeing. Mr Modi also stated that many people who never used Khadi products before are today wearing Khadi with great pride. He said the turnover of Khadi Village Industry has crossed 1.5 lakh crore rupees for the first time.

In this Mann Ki Baat episode, the Prime Minister spoke to the winners about their experiences during the Olympiad. Aditya Venkat Ganesh and Siddharth Chopra  said they were interested in Maths since childhood. Mr Modi wished them the best for their future and lauded them for bringing glory to the country.

International Tiger Day will be celebrated across the world tomorrow. The Prime Minister said tigers have been an integral part of Indian culture and people in the villages around forests know how to live in harmony with the tiger. He said unprecedented efforts are being made for the protection of tigers in case conflict arises between tiger and man. He said “Kulhadi Band Panchayat” is one such effort of public participation.

The “Kulhadi Band Panchayat” campaign  started from Ranthambore of Rajasthan. Mr Modi said the local communities have taken an oath that they will not go to the forest to cut trees so that a better environment can be created for the tigers.

Ace Indian Shooter Manu Bhaker opened medals tally for India at the Paris Olympics with a bronze in Women’s 10 meter air pistol event today. With the feat, Manu became the first Indian woman ever to win an Olympic medal in Shooting competition. Here is more from our correspondent about all the happenings at the Paris Games:

“India’s first medal success at Paris 2024 came away from the French capital as Manu Bhaker laid to rest the ghosts of her Tokyo misadventure in sublime fashion to shoot her way to a bronze medal in the women’s 10m air pistol event today at Chateauroux. Bhaker, who had also come third in qualifying yesterday, notched up 221.7 points to finish behind the Korean duo of Y.J Oh and Y.J Kim who won the gold and silver. Manu became the first Indian woman shooter to win an Olympic medal and the first in the sport to finish on the podium since 2012. It was a poignant moment for Bhaker who brought joy to the billions back home. Here’s Manu speaking to Akashvani:

The boxer was second best in the first round in her 50 kg round of 32 bout against the dogged German Maxi Kareena Kloetzer but came back strongly to win by a unanimous decision. She will face top seed Wu Yu in the round of 16.
