## Morning News

External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar inaugurated India’s first overseas Jan Aushadi Kendra in Mauritius yesterday. He was joined by Mauritius Prime Minister Pravind Kumar Jugnauth during the inauguration.

In a social media post, Dr Jiashankar said this Aushadi Kendra is the delivery of the promise made by Prime Minister Narendra Modi earlier this year. He further said that the India-Mauritius health partnership project will supply cost-effective, Made-in-India medicines to augment public healthcare and enhance well-being.

Earlier, Dr. Jaishankar inaugurated the Mediclinic project at Grand Bois in Mauritius with Indian grant assistance, calling it the newest expression of our friendship. Speaking at the inauguration of the project, Dr. Jaishankar said, the initiative will provide secondary health care for 16,000 people in the Grand Bois area. Expressing pride, the External Affairs Minister said that health is a priority and all of us have become health conscious.

Indian Navy’s warship INS Teg has rescued nine crew members including 8 Indians who went missing after an oil tanker capsized off the coast of Oman. The Comoros-flagged Oil Tanker MV Prestige Falcon had a total of 16-member crew including 13 Indians and three Sri Lankans. The search for the remaining survivors continues.  The Indian navy warship and a P-8I maritime patrol aircraft have been deployed for search and rescue efforts in Oman after the Oil Tanker capsized about 25 Nautical Miles South East of Ras Madrakah. Indian Embassy said, it is in constant touch with the Omani authorities and the search and rescue operation is being coordinated by the Oman Maritime Security Centre and the Indian Navy is conducting operations in the region.

In a coordinated sea-air operation, the Indian Coast Guard (ICG) has successfully rescued a stranded Indian Fishing Boat (IFB) Aashni, with 11 crew onboard, about 80 nautical miles from Kochi, Kerala, amid heavy rain and challenging weather conditions. Defence Ministry in a statement said that the vessel was in a critical condition due to flooding from a hull rupture near the keel and loss of propulsion, which posed a threat to the safety of the crew. An ICG Dornier aircraft on maritime surveillance located the distressed fishing boat. A technical ICG team embarked onto the distressed boat, carried out de-flooding operations, and provided necessary assistance.

In Maharashtra, 12 Maoists were killed in an anti-naxal operation by Gadchiroli police yesterday evening at Etapalli block along Gadchiroli-Chhattisgarh border.

Naxalites opened fire on the police party which was carrying out anti- naxal operation in the forest of Vandoli village in Etapalli block of Gadchiroli.  Superintendent of Gadchiroli police Nilotpal informed that in the counter fire, 12 Naxalites were killed  during encounter.  Post encounter, the police have recovered bodies of 12 naxalites. They have also recovered  automatic weapons and ammunitions . In this six hour longe gun battle, one Police Sub Inspector and a constable have been injured and they have been shifted to Nagpur for treatment. Meanwhile, Chief Minister  Ekanth Shinde has congratulated Gadchiroli Police for this successful operation and said that this action will be a decisive blow to Naxal activities in Gadchiroli district .Home Minister and Deputy Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis also announced a reward of 51 lakh Rupees for the Gadchiroli police for their act of bravery. With Report of Jayant Nimgade from Gadchiroli Dhanannjay Wankhede, Akashvani Nagpur.

The Haryana government will provide 10 percent horizontal reservation to Agniveers in recruitment to certain posts in police, mining and jail departments. Chief Minister Nayab Singh Saini made this announcement at a press conference in Chandigarh yesterday.

Haryana Chief Minister said that Agniveers seeking deployment in government departments, boards and corporations will be given priority in matrix scores. Mr Saini said that if Agniveers are given more than 30 thousand rupees per month salary in any industrial unit, then the government will give an annual subsidy of 60 thousand rupees to that industrial unit. Apart from this, the Chief Minister also announced that if any Agniveer sets up his own enterprise, he will be given an interest-free loan of up to 5 lakh rupees. He said, Agniveers will also be given arms license on priority basis. If someone meets with an accident, compensation will  be given under the Road Transport Highway Scheme of the Government of India and free treatment will be provided.

The book “Wings to our Hopes – Volume I,” which compiles 75 significant speeches from the first year of President Droupadi Murmu’s tenure, will be released in New Delhi today. Union Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan will release the e-book in the presence of Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw. The Publication Division has published this book. The book will be released in Hindi and English.

Karnataka Government is decided to put on hold the draft Bill that ensured Job quota for Kannadigas in the private sector after industry bodies and experts expressed their reservations. The Karnataka State Employment of Local Candidates in the Industries, Factories and Other Establishments Bill 2024 was passed in the Cabinet meeting on Monday. It proposed the appointment of Kannadigas to 50 percent of management positions and 75 percent of non management positions and 100 percent reservation for Group C and Group D posts in the private sector. However, Chief Minister has posted on X that the bill will be put on hold and passed after wider consultations.

“The Karnataka State Government had to step back on the Kannadigas quota bill after industry backlash. The Industry leaders Kiran Mazumdar Shaw and Mohandas Pai expressed their displeasure over the quota bill on social media. The IT industry body Nasscom had warned that the proposal will erode technology hub status of Karnataka and reverse the progress made so far. The Federation of Karnataka Chamber of Commerce and Industries had sought a meeting with the Chief Minister to reconsider the proposed bill. After the anguish expressed by the private sector industries, the Chief Minister has said that the proposed Bill is temporarily put on hold and will be implemented after consultations. Sudhindra, Akashvani News, Bengaluru.”

Supreme Court is scheduled to hear today a batch of petitions related to NEET-UG 2024 controversy. On July 11, the top court had adjourned till today the hearing on the pleas including those seeking cancellation of the exam, re-test and probe into alleged malpractices in the conduct of NEET-UG 2024, as the responses of the Centre and the National Testing Agency, NTA were yet to be received by some parties. A bench of Chief Justice D Y Chandrachud and Justice J B Pardiwala and Manoj Mishra will hear over 40 petitions. These petitions also include those filed by the NTA seeking transfer of cases pending against it in various high courts on the NEET-UG row to the Supreme Court to avoid multiplicity of litigations.

It said, orange alert for very heavy rainfall has been issued in Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, Karaikal, Kerala, Mahe, Coastal Andhra Pradesh and Yanam today. Another batch of 4,383 pilgrims left Bhagwati Nagar Yatri Niwas base camp in Jammu for the Kashmir valley to perform pilgrimage to the Amarnath Cave Shrine. The pilgrims left the base camp in a cavalcade of 157 vehicles early this morning. The batch included 3,222 Men, 1071 Women, 8 Children, 67 Sadhus & 15 Sadhvies. Of these 1701 pilgrims left for Baltal base camp at 3:14 AM and 2682 for Pahalgam base camp  at 4 AM from where they will proceed for their further journey to the holy cave shrine.

US President Joe Biden has tested positive for Covid-19 following his first event in Las Vegas. President Biden is vaccinated and he is experiencing mild symptoms. White House Press Secretary Karine Jean-Pierre said that Mr Biden will be returning to Delaware where he will self-isolate and will continue to carry out all of his duties fully during that time.

Belarus will introduce a visa-free policy for 35 European countries. The policy will come into effect from tomorrow and will last till 31st of December this year. Citizens from 35 countries, including members of the European Union and the United Kingdom, can stay in Belarus for 30 days at a time without a visa.

Belarusian government said, President Alexander Lukashenko supports citizens of 35 European countries to temporarily enter and stay in Belarus through road and rail checkpoints without a visa to further demonstrate Belarus’s commitment to openness, peace and good neighbourliness, and to simplify personnel exchanges.

India’s final contingent for the upcoming Paris Olympics has been approved by the sports ministry. The contingent will have  117 athletes accompanied by 140 support staff and officials. The games will begin from the 26th of this month to the 11th of next month.

Athletics will make for the biggest group in the contingent with 29 names followed by shooting 21 and hockey 19. Table tennis will be represented by eight players while Badminton will feature seven competitors, including two-time Olympic medallist P. V. Sindhu.

In Swedish Open Tennis, India’s number one ranked player Sumit Nagal will face world number 36 Mariano Navone of Argentina in the Men’s Singles Round of 16 today. The match will be played at the Centre Court of Bastad Tennis Stadium in Sweden this afternoon. Nagal had defeated Swedish ‘Elias Ymer’ 6-4, 6-3 in Round of 32 clash on Tuesday.

In the latest ICC Men’s T20 Batting rankings released yesterday, India’s Suryakumar Yadav has maintained his second position, while Yashasvi Jaiswal secured sixth spot. Ruturaj Gaikwad slipped one place to eighth in the batting rankings.

The Supreme Court says any order for conducting the NEET-UG 2024 afresh has to be on the concrete footing. The Supreme Court today started hearing more than 40 petitions alleging irregularities and malpractices in the conduct of the medical entrance exam NEET-UG 2024.

The Court will resume the hearing in the afternoon. More than 23. 33 lakh students had taken the test on May 5 at 4,750 centres in 571 cities, including 14 overseas.

## Evening News

At least two people have lost their lives and more than 20 were injured in a rail accident in Gonda district of Uttar Pradesh this afternoon. 21 out of the total 23 coaches except engine of Chandigarh Dibrugarh express derailed in the accident on Gonda-Gorakhpur route near Mankapur station. The Ministry of Railways has ordered a high-level inquiry into the Gonda train derailment incident. This inquiry will be apart from the Commissioner of Railway Safety enquiry. The Ministry also announced an ex-gratia of ten lakh rupees each to the family of the deceased, 2.5 lakh rupees for grievous injury, and 50 thousand rupees for minor injuries. Teams of NDRF and SDRF reached the spot and the relief and rescue work is almost complete now. CPRO of North Eastern Railway told Aakashvani News that all the passengers of the Chandigarh Dibrugarh express have been shifted to Mankapur station via buses and other vehicles. A special train will take them to their respective destinations very soon. Investigation on the causes of the accident has started. He said that some people suspected the that loco pilot heard a loud sound before the derailment.

Relief commissioner office of Uttar Pradesh informed that no one is now trapped in the coaches which derailed this afternoon. Only the clearance and Restoration work of the rail track is left which will be completed very soon. Railway has release helpline numbers. The numbers are 8957409292 for Lucknow and 8957400965 for Gonda.

Government today said that the UNICEF report which highlights that India has a high number of zero-dose children depicts an incomplete picture of the country’s immunization data.
