## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will be on a day-long visit to Hazaribagh in Jharkhand today. During his visit, Mr Modi will lay the foundation stone, and inaugurate several development projects worth over 83 thousand crore rupees in the state. It includes, the launch of ambitious Dharti Aaba Janjatiya Gram Utkarsh Abhiyan, with a total outlay of over 79 thousand crore rupees. Our correspondent has the details:

“Among the various projects to government’s efforts to empower tribal communities and enhance rural infrastructure in Jharkhand, the Dharti Aaba Janjatiya Gram Utkarsh Abhiyan is pivotal. The initiative aims to foster holistic development in tribal villages, bringing transformative changes to the socio-economic landscape of the region. The Abhiyan will cover around 63 thousand villages in 549 districts benefitting, over 5 crore tribal people. Related Ministries and Department would be responsible for implementation of schemes under Development Action Plan for Scheduled Tribes in next 5 years. The Prime Minister will also inaugurate 40 Eklavya Schools and lay foundation stone of 25 Eklavya Schools worth over two thousand 800 crore rupees and will interact with the beneficiaries of different schemes. Later, Mr Modi will also address public meeting at Gandhi Maidan, during the concluding ceremony of BJP’s Parivartan Yatra in the state. With KK Lal’s report from Hazaribagh, Tripti Srivastava, Akashvani News.”

The Nation is paying homage to the Father of the Nation Mahatma Gandhi on his 155th birth anniversary today.

President Droupadi Murmu and Prime Minister Narendra Modi paid tributes to Bapu on his birth anniversary. In her message, President Droupadi Murmu called upon the people to resolve to imbibe the values of truth, non-violence, love and purity. The President also called upon the people to strive to continuously advance the development of the country and society with the idea of the India of Mahatma Gandhi’s dreams.
Prime Minister Modi in a social media post, said, Bapu’s life and ideals based on truth, harmony and equality will always remain an inspiration for the countrymen.

A series of functions are being organized across the country and by Indian Missions abroad. A Sarva Dharma Prarthana Sabha is being held at Bapu’s Samadhi, Rajghat in the National Capital. President Droupadi Murmu, Vice President Jagdeep Dhankhar, Prime Minister Narendra Modi, Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla, Union Ministers, and Leader of Opposition in Lok Sabha Rahul Gandhi paid floral tributes to Mahatma Gandhi at Rajghat.

The Nation is also remembering former Prime Minister Lal Bahadur Shastri on his 120th birth anniversary today. Vice President, Prime Minister, Lok Sabha Speaker, Union Ministers and Leader of Opposition in Lok Sabha paid floral tributes to Lal Bahadur Shastri at his samadhi Vijay Ghat in Delhi.

Paying tributes to the former Prime Minister, Mr Modi said, Lal Bahadur Shastri dedicated his life for the soldiers, farmers and self-respect of the country.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will participate in the Swachh Bharat Diwas programme in New Delhi this morning on the occasion of 155th birth anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi. The event marks the completion of 10 years of the Swachh Bharat Mission, one of the most significant mass movements for cleanliness.

During the programme, the Prime Minister will launch and lay the foundation stone for several projects related to sanitation and cleanliness, worth over nine thousand six hundred crore rupees.

“The Swachh Bharat Diwas programme will showcase India’s decade-long sanitation achievements and those in the recently concluded Swachata Hi Seva campaign. It will also include nationwide participation from local government bodies, women’s groups, youth organizations and community leaders, ensuring that the spirit of Sampoorna Swachhata reaches every corner of India. Transformation of around 6.5 lakh Cleanliness Target Units has been achieved. Nearly one lakh SafaiMitra Suraksha Shivir camps have also been organised, benefitting over 30 lakh Safai Mitras. Over 45 lakh trees have been planted under the Ek Ped Maa ke Naam campaign. Anupam Mishra, Akashvani News, Delhi.”

Madhya Pradesh Chief Minister, Dr. Mohan Yadav will today launch development projects worth 685 crore rupees in the state under Swachh Bharat and Amrit Yojana. Prime Minister Narendra Modi will virtually participate in the Swachh Bharat Diwas event.

“Swachhata Hi Seva program to be held today at Kushabhau Thackeray International Convention Center, Bhopal. On this occasion, virtual Bhoomi Pujan will also be done for Sagar’s sewerage scheme worth about 300 crore Rs and water supply schemes of Seoni-Malwa  and Chhindwara the chief municipal officers who have done excellent work in Swachhata Hi Seva Abhiyan-2024 will also be honoured. Sanjeev Sharma, Akashvani News, Bhopal”.

The Centre has released over five thousand eight hundred fifty crore rupees to 14 flood-affected states. Maharashtra received one thousand four hundred ninety-two crore rupees, Andhra Pradesh over one thousand crore rupees, Gujarat 600 crore rupees and Telangana nearly four hundred seventeen crore rupees among others. These states have been affected due to extremely heavy rainfall, floods, and landslides during the south-west monsoon this year.

Iran launched a massive missile attack on Israel last night raising fears of all-out war in West Asia region. Iran claimed that the missile attacks were in response to the recent killing of senior leaders of armed groups Hamas and Hezbollah.

Israeli military said, around 180 missiles were fired, most of which were intercepted. It said, no casualties have been reported from last night’s attack adding that no more threats were seen in Israel’s airspace.
Prime Minister of Israel Benjamin Netanyahu has warned Tehran that it made a big mistake and will pay for it. Israel’s allies have condemned the attack. Talking to reporters, US President Joe Biden pledged unwavering support for Israel.

The United Nations and European Union have reiterated called for a ceasefire, with the UN Security Council due to meet today over the issue.
Meanwhile, in another development, at least six people were killed and nine wounded in a suspected terror attack involving shooting and stabbing in Tel Aviv last night. The attack took place moments before Iran launched a barrage of missiles. Israeli police said, at least two gunmen were involved in the attack and both the terrorists were neutralised.

On the other hand, according to Lebanese authorities, cross-border fire between Israel and Hezbollah continued, with more than 50 people killed by Israeli strikes.

Meanwhile, in view of the prevailing situation in the West Asia region, the Embassy of India in Tel Aviv has advised Indian Nationals in Israel to exercise caution, avoid unnecessary travel within the country and stay close to safety shelters. The Embassy said, it is closely monitoring the situation and is in regular touch with the Israeli authorities to ensure safety of all Indian Nationals.  In case of any emergency, people can contact 24×7 helpline of the Embassy, +972-547520711, +972-543278392 and email, cons1.telaviv@mea.gov.in.

External Affairs Minister Dr S Jaishankar has said that India is very much concerned at the possibility of broadening of the conflict in West Asia. Speaking at the Carnegie Endowment in the United States last night, he called Hamas’ attack on Israel on 7th October last year a terrorist attack. Speaking on India’s position in the Russia-Ukraine conflict, Dr Jaishankar said, New Delhi is playing its role in the resolution of the conflict by assisting in communication between the two sides.

Referring to the evolving situation in the neighbourhood, Dr Jaishankar said that India’s relationship with its neighbours today is much stronger since independence.

Earlier, External Affairs Minister, Dr S Jaishankar held talks with US Secretary of State Antony Blinken in Washington DC. The two leaders followed up on the Quad meetings and bilateral talks held recently at Delaware in US. The discussions also covered deepening bilateral cooperation, situation in West Asia, recent developments in the Indian subcontinent, the Indo-Pacific and Ukraine.
