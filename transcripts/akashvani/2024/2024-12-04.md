Indian Space Research Organisation will launch PSLV C59 carrying European Space Agency’s PROBA 3 satellite from the Sathish Dhawan Space Centre at Sriharikota at 4.08 PM today. Proba 3 is a commercially dedicated mission of the NewSpace India limited to ISRO. PSLV C59 will inject the spacecraft into a highly elliptical orbit to demonstrate precise formation flying.

“The process of liquid fuelling is being carried out and the health of the rocket is being checked out continuously by the ISRO scientists at Sriharikota. Proba 3 which is a 144 meter long spacecraft  weighing 3550 kilos has two spacecraft which are Coronagraph Space Craft and the Occulter SpaceCraft which will  be launched together in a stacked configuration.

The Coronagraph space craft weighing 310 kilos  has an imaging investigation of the Corona which is the outer layer of the Sun. The Occulter space craft weighing 240 kilos has a payload to take a little more of the science experiment and will conduct digital absolute Radiometer Science Experiment. Proba 3 will function as an orbital lab demonstration acquiring data, proximity operations and formation flying vehicle validating metrology sensors and control algorithms opening up new methods of mission control. Joy, AKASHVANI NEWS, Chennai.”

Navy day is being celebrated today. The day is celebrated on 4th of December every year to recognize the achievements and role of the Indian Navy.

President Droupadi Murmu will attend the Navy day celebrations as Chief Guest at the Blue Flag Beach in Puri, Odisha this afternoon. The event will demonstrate aerial manoeuvres by MiG-29K and Hawk fighter jets, combat exercises by MARCOS, submarine operations, amphibious landings, and rocket firing from warships.

“Indian Navy Day is celebrated annually on 4th of December commemorating the Navy’s decisive Operation Trident during the 1971 war, where the Indian missile boats inflicted significant damage on Karachi Harbor. This year the event will feature an operational demonstration on Puri’s Blue Flag beach which will be attended by President Droupadi Murmu who is also the Supreme Commander of Indian Armed Forces. This event will showcase Indian Navy’s combat readiness and advanced capabilities under the theme ‘ Combat Ready, Credible, Cohesive, Future Ready Force’. The Navy’s operations span anti-piracy missions, humanitarian assistance, and global maritime exercises, reinforcing its role as a blue-water force. President Droupadi Murmu, who is on a five-day visit to her home state Odisha since yesterday, will be visiting the 12th Century Sri Jagannath Temple in Puri and offer prayers to the Lords and also grace the 75th anniversary celebration of Gopabandhu Ayurveda Mahavidyalaya in Puri before joining the Navy Day celebration this afternoon. Prakash Dash, Akashvani News, Bhubaneswar.”
