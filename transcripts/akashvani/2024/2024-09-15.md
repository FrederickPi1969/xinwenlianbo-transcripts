## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will be on a three-day visit to Jharkhand, Gujarat and Odisha beginning today. He will also lay the foundation stone and dedicate various Railway Projects worth more than 660 crore rupees to the nation. Mr Modi will also distribute sanction letters to 20 thousand Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana- Gramin beneficiaries in Tatanagar.

Union Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan, Assam Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma and Jharkhand BJP Chief Babulal Marandi will be present on the occasion. Mr Chouhan said, Prime Minister will also transfer more than five crore rupees directly to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries through a single click. Mr. Chouhan added that a housewarming ceremony for the newly constructed houses of 46,000 beneficiaries will also be held.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah has emphasised the need for increased cooperation and coordination between NIA and State Anti-terrorism Squads to strengthen the counter-terrorism architecture. He said this while chairing the second day of the two-day National Security Strategies Conference – 2024 in New Delhi yesterday.

In West Bengal, Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) has arrested former principal of RG Kar Medical College and Hospital Sandip Ghosh and former Officer-in-Charge of Tala Police Station Abhijit Mondal in connection with the rape and murder of a Kolkata doctor. They were arrested yesterday on charges of tampering with evidences and misleading the probe, which is being carried out into the gruesome incident at the state-run hospital, last month. Two more Kolkata Police officials are under the CBI scanner. The probe agency is also questioning a Sub-Inspector of Tala Police Station regarding the case. Sandip Ghosh is also accused of being involved in causing considerable delay in registration of FIR. While the trainee doctor’s body was found on the morning of 9th August, the police took over 12 hours to convert the case of unnatural death into an FIR.

In Uttar Pradesh, at least 7 people died when a three-storey building collapsed yesterday evening in Meerut district. The incident occurred in Meerut’s Zakir Colony in the Lohiya Nagar area, claiming the lives of seven people, including a young girl and two adolescents. Meerut District Magistrate Deepak Meena said that there were around 14 to 15 people in the building when the incident happened. Rescue teams, including NDRF and SDRF, have successfully pulled out 12 people from the debris. However, three other people are still trapped beneath the rubble. Heavy rain hindered the rescue operation. Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath has taken cognizance of the incident and monitoring the rescue operation.

Information and Broadcasting Ministry has instituted a new section as part of the International Film Festival of India (IFFI) 2024 for young filmmakers called the “Best Debut Indian Film Section 2024.” The 55th IFFI is slated to be held in Goa from November 20th to 28th. Doordarshan completes 65 years of its glorious journey today. Established on 15th September 1959, Doordarshan marked the beginning of India’s broadcasting era. The then President, Rajendra Prasad, inaugurated the first broadcast on that day. Today, Doordarshan remains India’s largest broadcasting entity, with a vast network of studios and transmitters. It provides television, online, and mobile services, reaching both metropolitan and regional areas, as well as international audience.

Doordarshan has delivered content to generations and continues to play a vital role in creating awareness. Despite limited resources and technology, shows like Ramayana, Mahabharat, Hum Log, Buniyaad, Malgudi Days, and Udaan set a benchmark for quality content.

Kerala is celebrating Thiruonam today, the most auspicious day of Onam, the biggest cultural festival of the state. The festival marks the home coming of the legendary ruler of the state, King Mahabali and showcases the unique customs, traditions and cuisine of the state. Keralites across the globe irrespective of religious affiliations is celebrating the festival in full vigour.

Onam , the harvest festival of kerala is celebrated in the Malayalam month of Chingam.It is believed the state had its golden period during the reign of King Mahabali and his annual visits to his subjects marks the Onam celebrations.Onam is associated with the famous onasadya,pookkalam, onakodi,ujyallattam , pulikalli, vallamkalli,  etc. As Kerala is celebrating its biggest cultural festival, streets are crowded and people are in total festive mode.  Onam is also the time of get-togethers , exchange of well wishes and gifts.This year the  official onam celebration by the State Govt was cancelled on account of the Wayanad landslide disaster. Onam is being celebrated this time with full vigour with solidarity to Wayanad as it is also a symbol of hope and overcoming all odds into a prosperous future. Mayusha  for Akashvani News form Thiruvananthapuram.

India Meteorological Department (IMD) has issued a red alert for extremely heavy rainfall in parts of Chhattisgarh and Jharkhand today. A similar alert has been issued for East Madhya Pradesh for tomorrow.

According to the IMD, very heavy rainfall is also expected in East Madhya Pradesh, Bihar, and Odisha. Additionally, parts of Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, and Tripura will experience heavy rainfall. The IMD reports that the deep depression over Gangetic West Bengal is anticipated to move slowly westward across the region and maintain its intensity as a deep depression until the afternoon. The IMD, predicts a moderate risk of flash floods in several watersheds and neighborhoods in Bihar, Odisha, Gangetic West Bengal, and Jharkhand. The department advises fishermen not to venture into the northern Bay of Bengal and along the coasts of Bangladesh, West Bengal, and Odisha until tomorrow morning.
