## Midday News

India and Bhutan today exchanged several agreements of mutual interest to further strengthen country’s unique ties with Himalayan nation. The documents were exchanged between India and Bhutan in the presence of Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Prime Minister of Bhutan Tshering Tobgay in Thimphu. Mr Modi reached Paro International Airport this morning on a two-day state visit to Bhutan. Prime Minister Modi was warmly received by his Bhutan counterpart Tshering Tobgay at the airport. He was also accorded a ceremonial welcome on his arrival. A report from our correspondent:

“Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s visit to Bhutan will give new boost to bilateral relationship by focusing on developmental partnership. India is currently engaged in capacity development of Bhutan and assisting several projects in the sectors including Hydroelectric, digitization, financial inclusion, health and education.  India is providing support to various digital programmes like E-Business National Single Window, Bhutan Integrated Taxation System, Integrated Citizen Services, and Digital School to enable Bhutan becoming a smart, connected and inclusive society. Bhupendra Singh, Akashwani News, New Delhi. “

During the visit, Prime Minister Modi will have an audience with King of Bhutan Jigme Khesar Namgyel Wangchuck, and his father Jigme Singye Wangchuck, the Fourth King of the Himalayan nation in Thimphu. The Prime Minister will inaugurate the Gyaltsuen Jetsun Pema Mother and Child Hospital in Thimphu. It is a state-of-the-art hospital, built with the assistance of India in Bhutan Capital.

Delhi Chief Minister and Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) leader Arvind Kejriwal has withdrawn the petition from the Supreme Court against his arrest by the Enforcement Directorate (ED) in connection with the excise policy-linked money-laundering cases. According to sources, the AAP leader will now approach a lower court, in accordance with the protocol.

It comes hours after the apex court denied any relief to Bharat Rashtra Samithi (BRS) leader, K Kavitha earlier in the day. She was arrested in the same case last week. During the hearing of her matter, the special bench of Justices Sanjiv Khanna, MM Sundresh and Bela Trivedi said, it cannot allow people to directly approach the top court for bail just because they are politicians or can afford to approach the top court directly.

The same bench was assigned to hear the plea of Delhi Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal.

Yesterday, the ED team reached the Chief Minister’s residence and carried out searches. Following the searches, the agency arrested the AAP leader from his residence in the National Capital. The arrest came hours after the Delhi High Court refused to grant protection to the AAP leader from any coercive action by the agency.

The ED had named him in the Delhi liquor policy-linked money laundering case and had sent nine summons to him to appear before it. However, Mr Kejriwal skipped all summons terming them illegal and politically motivated.

On the arrest of Delhi Chief Minister, BJP Spokesperson Sambit Patra has said that Mr. Kejriwal skipped nine summons issued to him and he must respect the summons.

Aam Aadmi Party leader Atishi alleged that the top leaders of her party have been arrested on false charges. She claimed that for the first time, a sitting Chief Minister has been arrested by the Government. The Aam Aadmi Party members have staged protest in different parts of Delhi today.

Congress leader K C Venugopal has alleged that the Central government is misusing the enforcement agencies and the Congress party stands with Aam Aadmi Party and Arvind Kejriwal.

The special session of Delhi Assembly which was scheduled for today has been cancelled. The next sitting of the House will be held on 27th March. This comes after the arrest of Delhi Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal.

The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) successfully executed a landing mission for its Reusable Launch Vehicle (RLV) named “Pushpak” this morning from the Aeronautical Test Range at Chalakere in Chitradurga in Karnataka. In a social media post, ‘ISRO said that it has nailed the precise landing of the winged launch vehicle RLV in autonomous mode. ISRO Chairman S Somanath congratulated the team for the flawless execution of maneuvering, high-speed landing and energy management of the spaceship in a fully autonomous mode. We have a report:

“In this second test today on the Launch Vehicle in Challakere, ISRO successfully tested the brake parachute, landing gear brakes, nose wheel steering system, on board navigation hardware and software and its capability to withstand conditions created by high velocity landing. A Chinook helicopter carried the scaled down version of Reusable launch vehicle tied to a string and dropped mid air at the height of 4.5 kms altitude. The 6.5 meter long winged vehicle descended towards the Earth at a speed of 350 kmph. At such a high velocity and altitude, the spacecraft Pushpak autonomously approached the runway with cross-range corrections. It landed precisely on the runway and came to a halt using its brake parachute and other mechanisms. The successful test today is an important step towards building India’s futuristic Reusable Launch vehicle that can launch many satellites, thereby cutting cost. Sudhindra, Akashvani News, Bengaluru”

Information and Broadcasting Minister Anurag Singh Thakur has congratulated ISRO scientists for outstanding accomplishment with the successful autonomous landing of Pushpak.

World Water Day is being celebrated today. The day is observed every year on 22nd of March, to honor the importance of freshwater. World Water Day brings people, organizations, and governments together to confront urgent water challenges and strive toward guaranteeing that everyone has access to clean water and sanitation through awareness-raising events, educational programs, and community-driven campaigns.

The theme for this year is ‘Water for prosperity and peace’. It emphasises the critical role water plays in fostering cooperation and stability around the world.

In a video message on social media post on the occasion, Prime Minister Narendra Modi said that the government is committed to work towards sustainable water management.

## Evening News

King of Bhutan Jigme Khesar Namgyel Wangchuck today conferred Bhutan’s highest civilian award,  the Order of the Druk Gyalpo, on Prime Minister Narendra Modi in Thimpu. Speaking on the occasion, Mr Modi said, it is a very big day in his life. He said, every award is special, but when one receives an award from another country, it shows that both countries are moving on the right path. He said, this honour is not his personal achievement, but the honour of India and 140 crore Indians.

Prime Minister Modi is the first foreign leader to be given this prestigious award. The citation of the Order states that the award recognises Prime Minister Modi’s personal accomplishments and leadership, and his contributions to strengthen the bonds of friendship between India and Bhutan. It also honours India’s rise as a global power under his leadership, and celebrates Bhutan’s special bond with India.

Prime Minister Modi also held talks with the King of Bhutan this afternoon at Tashichhodzong Palace, in Thimphu. Both leaders renewed their commitment to the special and unique India-Bhutan friendship. They reviewed the bilateral ties and discussed ways to take them to new levels.

Earlier in the day, India and Bhutan exchanged several agreements of mutual interest on energy, trade, connectivity, space and agriculture to further strengthen their partnership. The documents were exchanged between India and Bhutan in the presence of Prime Minister Modi and his Bhutanese ounterpart Tshering Tobgay in Thimphu. Mr Modi said, India and Bhutan enjoy long-standing and exceptional ties.

Our correspondent reports that Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s visit to Bhutan will give a new boost to the bilateral relationship by focusing on developmental partnerships.

The Rouse Avenue Court in Delhi today remanded Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal to Enforcement Directorate custody till the 28th of March in excise policy case. Special CBI  judge Kaveri Baweja passed the order. The ED had sought 10 days of custody for Kejriwal who was produced before the Delhi Court this afternoon following his arrest yesterday. The ED said that Kejriwal was directly involved in framing the now-withdrawn liquor policy. Earlier, the Delhi high court had denied him interim protection from arrest.

Kejriwal today withdrew his plea against arrest by the Enforcement Directorate in the excise policy-linked money laundering case from the Supreme Court. It came hours after the apex court denied any relief to Bharat Rashtra Samithi leader, K Kavitha. She was arrested in the same case last week.

Following the arrest of Delhi Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal, the opposition has sought the Election Commission’s interference. An all-party opposition delegation, which met with the poll body today, said that they had presented evidence of agencies’ alleged misuse against the opposition. They emphasized that this is the first instance in the history of independent India where a sitting Chief Minister has been arrested.

Leaders from the Congress, TMC, DMK, Left, Samajwadi Party and others were part of the delegation.

The Election Commission is all set to conduct the 2024 General Election. The poll body has deployed a strong team of 2,100 observers to make sure the elections are free and fair. Amongst a host of responsibilities they have been entrusted with, these observers will supervise District Election Officers and Returning Officers.  A report:

” The primary focus of these observers will be to prevent any instances of coercion, inducement, or intimidation during the electoral process. They will also supervise the deployment of security forces and ensure their randomization. To uphold the fairness of the election proceedings, they have also been entrusted with overseeing the randomization of personnel and voting machines. To enhance accessibility and transparency, these observers are required to remain within their designated Parliamentary Constituencies and make their contact details readily available through various platforms, including websites and media outlets. They have been instructed to conduct extensive visits to polling stations. This will be done to assess and address any potential challenges or issues that may arise during the election period. In an effort to streamline the electoral process, the observers will also engage in meetings with political parties and candidates to facilitate cooperation and resolve any hurdles. This is Durgesh Bhadauria for Akashvani News, New Delhi.”

The UN General Assembly has approved the first resolution on Artificial Intelligence, AI. The resolution gives global support to an international effort to ensure the powerful new technology benefits all nations, respects human rights and is safe, secure and trustworthy.

The resolution, sponsored by the United States and co-sponsored by 123 countries was adopted by consensus with a bang of the gavel and without a vote. It means the resolution has the support of all 193 U.N. member nations.

Over the past few months, the United States worked with more than 120 countries at the United Nations including Russia, China and Cuba to negotiate the text of the resolution that was adopted yesterday.
