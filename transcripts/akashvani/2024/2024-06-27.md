## Morning News

President Droupadi Murmu will address the joint sitting of Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha in the Parliament today. Following the President’s address, a motion of thanks will be moved in both the Houses of Parliament which will be discussed by the members. The first session of the 18th Lok Sabha began last Monday and the Rajya Sabha session will begin today.

Yesterday, Om Birla was elected the new speaker of the Lok Sabha, for the second consecutive term. The Senior BJP MP was elected with voice vote against the opposition’s candidate K. Suresh of the Congress. Prime Minister Narendra Modi, Leader of Opposition Rahul Gandhi and Parliamentary Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju escorted Mr. Birla to the chair of the Speaker after being elected. Mr Birla, said in his address that enriching Parliamentary values and democratic traditions will be his priorities.

Congratulating Mr. Birla on his election, Prime Minister Narendra Modi said, history is being made with the re-election of Mr. Birla for the second time.

Leader of Opposition Rahul Gandhi also congratulated Mr. Birla saying that this House represents the people’s voice. Leaders across party lines congratulated Mr Birla on being elected the Speaker for the second time.

A team of CBI reached Oasis School in Hazaribagh, Jharkhand yesterday, to investigate the NEET paper leak case. The CBI sleuths interrogated the Principal of the school about various aspects related to the paper leak. After that the CBI took the Principal with them for further questioning. The Principal was the city coordinator of schools of Hazaribagh for the NEET examination. The CBI team also quizzed the officials of SBI Hazaribagh where the question papers were stored.

The Economic Offence Unit of  Bihar had earlier said that a partially burnt question paper seized from the Khemnichak play school in Patna, matched with the question paper received from the National Testing Agency and the series of that question paper had been sent to the examination centre at the Oasis School in Kallu Chowk, Hazaribagh.

A Delhi court yesterday sent Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal to three-day CBI custody, hours after he was arrested by the agency in connection with a money laundering case. Earlier, CBI, before producing Kejriwal in the Rouse Avenue Court, arrested him formally. In a related development, Mr Kejriwal withdrew his plea filed before the Supreme Court challenging the interim stay ordered by the Delhi High Court on his release on bail in the money laundering probe related to now-scrapped Delhi Excise Policy.

In Nagaland, polling for the Urban Local Bodies Elections to three Municipalities and 21 Town Councils was held yesterday. A tentative poll percentage of over 83 was recorded. Despite disruption reported in a polling station, voting went off peacefully. This was first-ever municipal elections in the state to be held with 33 per cent reservation for women. Voting took place in 420 polling stations under 214 wards with 523 candidates in fray. The counting of votes will take place on 29th June.

This Urban local bodies election is historic for the State of Nagaland as the polling were conducted after a gap of 20 years, with 33 per cent women reservation.

Veteran BJP leader Lal Krishna Advani was hospitalised late night yesterday at the All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS) in Delhi following some old age-related complications. AIIMS official said, Mr Advani is stable and under observation. The 96-year-old leader has been kept under the supervision of doctors of the geriatric department.

The junior doctors in Telangana called off their three-day strike after the state government issued an order allocating over 200 crore rupees to the Directorate of Medical Education for civil works at three major colleges in the state. The Junior Doctors held day-long discussions with the Health Department officials and Health Minister C. Damodar Raja Narasimha regarding their demands after which they called off the strike.

Defence Research and Development Organisation, DRDO handed over the Medium Range-Microwave Obscurant Chaff Rocket to the Indian Navy at a ceremony held in New Delhi yesterday. Microwave Obscurant Chaff is a niche technology developed by DRDO’s Defence Laboratory in Jodhpur. It obscures radar signals and creates a microwave shield around platforms and assets, and reduced radar detection. Special type of fibres have been assembled in the medium range chaff rocket. The rocket forms microwave obscurant cloud in space when fired, spreading over a sufficient area.

The Spectrum auction held to meet new spectrum requirement of Telecom Service Providers and for renewal of expiring licenses for continuity and growth of mobile services has concluded successfully. Ministry of Communications said all the available spectrum were put to auction. This year the auction has seen activity in 900MHz, 1800MHz, 2100MHz and 2500 MHz bands.

Minister of Communications Jyotiraditya Scindia in a statement said, Spectrum auction 2024 was part of a continuous allocation process that is transparent, robust and progressive.

In Bolivia, armoured vehicles and troops of the Bolivian Armed Forces pulled back from the presidential palace in La Paz, hours after an attempted coup last evening. Bolivian police arrested the rebel military commander General Juan Jose Zuniga, who led the failed coup attempt. The presence of armed troops generated a strong response from citizens, who gathered on the streets surrounding Plaza Murillo, the epicentre of Bolivia’s political power, to denounce the military move. President Luis Arce condemned the coup attempt and called on citizens to mobilise and defend democracy. He also named a new army commander who ordered troops to withdraw.

In a growing concern, NASA astronauts Sunita Williams and Butch Wilmore are stuck at the International Space Station (ISS) as the spacecraft in which they had to return suffered a small helium system leak on its way up. Engineers continue to fix the Boeing Starliner spacecraft that will take them back to Earth. Originally slated to spend eight days on the orbiting space laboratory, the astronauts reached the International Space Station (ISS) on 6th June.

In the ICC Men’s T20 Cricket World Cup, South Africa thrashed Afghanistan by 9 wickets in the first semi-final at the Brian Lara Stadium in Trinidad this morning. South Africa overhauled the petty target of 57 in just 8.5 overs. Proteas scored 60 for the loss of one wicket to enter a World Cup final. ## Midday News

President Droupadi Murmu today asserted that the government is fully committed to fair investigation into the recent incidents of paper leaks and ensured that strict punishment will be given to the culprits. Addressing the joint sitting of both Houses of Parliament this morning, the President said it is important to rise above the party politics and undertake concrete measures nationwide to check such incidents.

President Murmu said the government is working towards major reforms in examination-related bodies, their functioning, and all aspects of the examination process. She said her government is working to create an environment to enable youth of the country to dream big and achieve them.

In her address, the President said that Jammu and Kashmir has given a befitting reply to India’s enemies as the Union Territory registered record voter turnout in the recently concluded Lok Sabha polls.

President Murmu said the new government is going to present its first General Budget in this Parliament Session, which will be an effective document of its far-reaching policies and futuristic vision and reforms will be fast-tracked.

Speaking in the Parliament, the President said that the country has risen from the 11th position to become the fifth largest economy in ten years.

Listing out the priorities accorded to the agriculture sector, the President said agro-based industries, dairy, and fishery-based industries are being expanded in villages. She added that a major problem of small farmers is related to storage and the centre has commenced work on a scheme to create the world’s largest storage capacity in the cooperative sector.

In her nearly hour-long address, President Murmu also highlighted the government initiatives for infrastructure development, saying that over three lakh 80 thousand kilometres of village roads have been constructed in the last ten years under the Prime Minister Gram Sadak Yojana. She said the government is also working on the High-Speed Rail Ecosystem between Ahmedabad and Mumbai and it has decided to conduct feasibility studies for bullet train corridors in the North, South, and East of the country.

On the development of North Eastern States, the President said the government is working to make this region a strategic gateway under its Act East Policy.

President Murmu added that a semiconductor plant is being established in Assam at a cost of 27 thousand crore rupees. She asserted that the North-East will also be a hub of Made in India chips. Highlighting the initiatives taken by the government for the North East region, the President observed that the Centre is working for lasting peace in the region and several problems related to the area have been addressed in the last ten years. President Murmu added that phase-wise repealing of Armed Forces (Special Powers) Act (AFSPA) is underway in areas with unrest after making rapid progress in the region.

Talking about the Bharatiya Nyay Sanhita, which will come into force from the first of next month, the President underlined that now justice will get priority over punishment, which also conforms to the spirit of the Constitution.

On women empowerment, the President said, a new era of women-led development has started citing the enactment of Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam for reservation of women in Lok Sabha and State Assemblies. She said, the majority of the four crore PM Awas houses have been allotted to women beneficiaries during the last 10 years.

Talking about the modernization of Armed Forces, President Murmu said many reforms have been introduced in the defence sector in the last 10 years. In her address, President Murmu also talked about the initiatives taken in the education sector and said the government is working towards establishing a Digital University.

She also commended the Indian players for winning a record number of medals at global platforms. Ahead of the Paris Olympics, she said, India is proud of every athlete representing the country at the Olympics.

The President called for collective denouncement of every attempt to undermine the credibility of Indian democracy saying one should remember the times when ballot papers were snatched and looted. She added that to ensure the sanctity of the electoral process, it was decided to use Electronic Voting Machines and the EVMs have passed every test, from the Supreme Court to the people’s court, in the last few decades. The President said, Constitution of India has stood up to every challenge and every test in the past decades. Referring to the imposition of Emergency in 1975, she said, it was the biggest and darkest chapter of a direct attack on the Constitution. Pointing towards the responsibility of the Members of Parliament, the President highlighted opposition to policies and obstruction of parliamentary functioning are two different things.

Our correspondent reports that during the President’s address some opposition MPs created noisy scenes over the issues related to NEET examination, Manipur violence and other issues.

Chairman Jagdeep Dhankhar announced that Union Minister JP Nadda has been appointed as Leader of the House. Later, the House was adjourned for the day.

The Opposition today criticized President Droupadi Murmu’s address to the joint sitting of Parliament. Talking to media outside Parliament, Congress MP Tariq Anwar said the address ignored the real issues including poverty, inflation, and unemployment and focused only to highlight the government’s achievements. Samajwadi Party chief Akhilesh Yadav said the Government talks about being the world’s fifth-largest economy, but there is widespread unemployment and farmers are suffering, while only a select few are benefiting.

The US House of Representatives has passed a bipartisan resolution in support of democracy and human rights in Pakistan’s general elections and called for a thorough and independent investigation. The resolution titled Expressing support for democracy and human rights in Pakistan was introduced by Congressman McCormick of Georgia and Congressman Kildee of Michigan and co-sponsored by over 100 colleagues.

The resolution passed with 98 per cent voting in its favour. It urged US President Joe Biden to collaborate with Pakistan in upholding democracy, human rights, and the rule of law.
Reacting to it, Pakistan’s Defence Minister criticised the resolution and said it stems from an incomplete understanding of the political situation and electoral process in the country.

The general elections in Pakistan, held on 8th of February this year, were marred by the allegations of irregularities, with incidents of internet shutdown, rigging and violence being reported from different parts of the country.

Kenya’s President William Ruto has said, the finance bill that sparked nationwide deadly protests, would be withdrawn. President Ruto declined to sign the highly contentious bill and has sent it back to the Parliament for amendments. His decision comes after nationwide protests intensified against the highly unpopular finance bill killing at least 22 people while dozens more have been injured.

The bill aims to raise taxes on a wide range of items, which they argue will exacerbate the economic hardships faced by citizens.
