The torrential rains have caused widespread travel disruption, particularly at Dubai International Airport which was forced to shut down operations for a period due to flooding, and many passengers missed their flights. Roads leading to the airport remain flooded and impassable. Dubai-based airline Flydubai has partially resumed flights. However, many other flights have been cancelled due to the ongoing weather conditions and road closures.

IMD has also forecast isolated to scattered rainfall over Punjab, Haryana, Chandigarh, Delhi during the next three days. The IMD has forecast severe heatwave conditions over Odisha and Gangetic West Bengal during the next four days. Heat Wave conditions are also likely over Jharkhand, north Konkan, Saurashtra, Madhya Maharashtra, Coastal Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Tamil Nadu and Puducherry.
