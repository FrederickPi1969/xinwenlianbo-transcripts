## Morning News

Today is National Unity Day. Every year on 31st October the country commemorates the birth anniversary of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel who is a pivotal figure in India’s struggle for independence and the first Deputy Prime Minister and Home Minister of the country. National Unity Day serves as a reminder of his efforts to unite the diverse princely states into a single nation and fosters a spirit of solidarity among the people of the country. Renowned for his exceptional leadership and unyielding commitment to national integration, Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel is fondly remembered as the Iron Man of India.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi offered floral tribute at the Statue of Unity in Ekta Nagar, Kevadia, Gujarat this morning. Mr Modi administered the Ekta Diwas pledge.

This year’s Ekta Divas Parade comprises 16 marching contingents from 9 States and 1 UT Police, 4 Central Armed Police Forces, NCC and a marching band. Special attractions of the parade include Hell March contingent of NSG, daredevil show by BSF and CRPF women and men bikers. A show on combination of Indian Martial Arts by BSF, piped band show by school children, and ‘Surya Kiran’ flypast by Indian Air Force, are also the attractions of the parade. Aparna Khunt, Akashvani News, Ahmedabad.

President Droupadi Murmu paid floral tribute at the statue of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel at Patel Chowk, New Delhi, this morning. Vice President Jagdeep Dhankhar also paid floral tribute on the occasion.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah is on a two-day visit to Gujarat today. In the morning, Mr. Shah  will inaugurate Sri Gopalananda Swami Yatrik Bhawan at the famous Kashtabhanjan Hanumanji Temple in Salangpur, located in Botad. Built at a cost of 200 crore rupees, the new guest house will be equipped with all with state-of-the-art facilities and will have 1100 rooms.

Tomorrow, Mr. Shah will inaugurate the state’s largest waste-to-energy plant at the Pirana waste dumping site in Ahmedabad. Built at a cost of 375 crore rupees on PPP model, the plant has the capacity to generate 15 mega watt of energy from 1 thousand metric tonnes of solid waste per day.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that the inauguration of C-295 aircraft manufacturing complex in Vadodara is watershed moment for India’s defence and aerospace journey. Sharing his article on social media platform LinkedIn, Mr Modi said, the speed of execution is breathtaking – from foundation stone to operational facility in just two years. He said, this is a clear manifestation of a new work culture and the capabilities of the people of India.

The Prime Minister also listed several accomplishments in the Defence sector. He said, Defence production has soared to 1.27 lakh crore rupees in 2023-24. Defence exports have skyrocketed from one thousand crore rupees in 2014 to 21 thousand crore rupees today. Mr Modi said, more than 12 thousand 300 items have been indigenised in just three years. Over seven thousand 500 crore rupees invested in domestic vendors by Defence Public Sector Undertakings. He said, 25 per cent of defence Research and Development budget dedicated to industry-led innovation.

Government has notified additional installment of Dearness Allowance to Central Government employees and Dearness Relief to Pensioners due from 1st July this year. Union Cabinet has approved three per cent additional installment of Dearness Allowance and Dearness Relief to Central Government employees and pensioners. Central Government Pensioners or Family Pensioners will become entitled to higher amount of Dearness Allowance and Dearness Relief from the existing rate of 50 per cent to 53 per cent of the basic pension or family pension.

Tomorrow is the last day of withdrawal of candidature for the second phase of Assembly elections in Jharkhand. Six hundred thirty four candidates have filled nominations for this phase. Thirty eight constituencies will go to polls in the second phase phase on the 20th November while the first phase of polling will be undertaken  on  13th November   in Forty three constituencies. Counting of votes will be on 23rd November.

Illegal materials and cash worth over 122 crore have been seized from different parts of the state since the announcement of the assembly elections. Twenty nine FIRs have been lodged for violation of model code of conduct. Joint operation has been launched in bordering areas of UP, Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh and Odisha to nab maoists. Festival of lights Diwali is being celebrated across the country with traditional fervour and gaiety today. The festival, which marks the victory of good over evil, is celebrated by lighting up houses, temples and other public places. People also worship the Goddess of wealth Lakshmi on the occasion.

President Droupadi Murmu, Vice President Jagdeep Dhankar and Prime Minister Narendra Modi have greeted the citizens on the occasion of Deepawali. In her message, the President said that Deepawali is a festival of happiness and enthusiasm and it symbolises the victory of knowledge over ignorance and good over evil. The President said that on the auspicious occasion of Deepawali, we should illuminate our conscience, adopt virtues of love and compassion, and promote social harmony.

In his message, Vice President Jagdeep Dhankar said that Deepawali is celebrated with reverence and joy not only in India but also by the Indian diaspora across the world. Mr. Dhankar said that the festival symbolizes the timeless victory of light over darkness, hope over despair and knowledge over ignorance.

In a social media post, Mr. Modi wished for a healthy, happy, and prosperous life for all citizens. The Prime Minister expressed hope that everyone may thrive with the blessings of Maa Lakshmi and Lord Shri Ganesha.

Uttar Pradesh witnessed the Grand Deepotsav on the banks of Saryu river in Ayodhya. Union Minister of culture and tourism Gajendra Singh Shekhawat, Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath and other dignitaries along with thousands of devotees were present at the occasion. Two Guinness World Records were set on on the banks of Saryu river in the holy city with over 25 lakh earthen lamps or diyas lit and 1,121 ‘vedacharyas’ or teachers of religious texts performing ‘aarti’ simultaneously.

“Ayodhya city brightened up with the largest ever display of 25 Lakh 12 thousand 5 hundred 85  earthen lamps on the 55 ghats of Saryu river and created a world record yesterday evening. Thousands of volunteers lit the lamps along with the bank of Saryu River through lanes of the city and at houses. Whole city was illuminated with the lights and colorful decorations on the theme of Ramayana. This year another world record was created, in which 1,121 batuks performed the largest aarti together on the ghats of Saryu. This was 8th Deepotsav in a row at Ayodhya after Yogi Adityanath came into power but first since the consecration  ceremony of lord Rama at the Ram janmabhoomi Temple. A grand drone and laser show was also organized to mark the occasion. SUSHIL CHANDRA TIWARI, AKASHWANI NEWS, LUCKNOW.”

Indian Railways is running 7 thousand 296 special trains to cater the rush of passengers during the festive season of Deepawali and Chhath Puja. This year, the Railways is operating around 2 thousand 800 more trains in comparison to 4 thousand 500 operated last year for the ease of passengers.

To effectively deal with the high passenger volumes during the festive season, the Railway has taken several steps for better crowd management and providing seamless services to the passengers at major Railways Stations of Delhi. A designated holding area has been set up at the stations, equipped with additional ticket counters. Automatic Ticket Vending Machines, Enquiry Counters, catering services, drinking water, and mobile toilets have also been set up to cater the requirement of the passengers. Talking to Akashvani News, Divisional Railway Manager, Sukhwinder Singh said that sale of platform tickets at major Railway Stations including New Delhi, Old Delhi  and Anand Vihar Stations has been banned til 7th of November.

In Madhya Pradesh, Divisional Railway Manager of Bhopal Division, Devashish Tripathi inspected the crowd management and arrangements made at ticket booking counters at Bhopal Railway Station and  assessed the facilities available for passengers. Apart from this, coach guidance display boards have been installed to assist passengers, making it easier for passengers to locate their coaches. ‘May I Help You’ desk has also been set up. West Central Railway Zone Public Relations Officer BN Gupta told Akashvani that GRP and RPF are being deployed on both sides of the train for the convenience of passengers.

In Hylo Open 2024 badminton tournament in Germany, Indian shuttlers Ayush Shetty and Sathish Kumar Karunakaran advanced to the men’s singles pre-quarterfinals.

Ayush Shetty beat Joakim Oldorff of Finland in the first-round match while Sathish Karunakaran defeated compatriot Chirag Sen in a hard-fought match at Saarbrucken yesterday.

Earlier, India’s Tharun Mannepalli lost to Collins Valentine Filimon of Austria. In the women’s singles, India’s Keyura Mopati lost to Anna Siess Ryberg of Denmark.

In the U23 World Championships, being played in Tirana, Albania,  Indian wrestler Mansi Ahlawat  has won the bronze medal in the women’s  59kg category while in 65kg category, Manisha Bhanwala missed out in the repechage round match yesterday.

## Midday News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today emphasized the spirit of unity and warned  countrymen against divisive powers that aim to break the integrity of the nation. Addressing the Rashtriya Ekta Diwas celebrations in Ekta Nagar, Kevadiya in Gujarat, on the birth anniversary of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, the Prime Minister underscored that the spirit of national unity is crucial for achieving the vision of Viksit Bharat over the next 25 years. He stated that during the last ten years, his government has been constantly strengthening the spirit of One Nation in all its policies and decisions.

Emphasizing national unity, the Prime Minister said there are several challenges before the country that are posing a threat to the unity and security of the nation. The Prime Minister urged citizens to remain vigilant and to embrace the spirit of India’s unity in diversity.

Prime Minister Modi said, new history is being scripted in the country and India is emerging as a global leader. He highlighted that India today is a country which is progressive, inclusive, sensible and on the path of rapid development despite unstable global situations.

Recalling the valuable contribution of Sardar Patel toward unifying the country, PM Modi said that the 150th birth anniversary of Sardar Patel would be celebrated throughout the country over the next two years.

Earlier in the morning, the PM offered floral tribute to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel at the Statue of Unity in Ekta Nagar. He also administered the Ekta Diwas pledge to the people and witnessed the Ekta Diwas Parade.

President Droupadi Murmu and Vice President Jagdeep Dhankhar today paid floral tributes to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel at his statue at Patel Chowk in New Delhi on his birth anniversary.

Members of Parliament, former Members of Parliament and other dignitaries today paid floral tributes at the portrait of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel in the Central Hall of Samvidhan Sadan, on the occasion of his Birth anniversary.

He said the government will create a North East that is strong and prosperous not only naturally and culturally but also economically.

India has extended its support for a Cuban resolution at the United Nations calling for an end to the long-standing US economic, commercial, and financial embargo on Cuba. During a recent debate, India’s First Secretary at the UN, Sneha Dubey, underscored the damaging effects of the embargo on Cuba’s economy and people. She stated that, as the world’s largest democracy with a commitment to multilateralism, India stands with this assembly in its unambiguous rejection of domestic laws having extraterritorial impact.

In a vote held by the UN General Assembly, 187 nations backed the resolution, with only the United States and Israel opposing it.
