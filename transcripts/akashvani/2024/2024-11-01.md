## Midday News

Union Home Minister Amit Shah inaugurated Gujarat ‘s largest waste to energy plant at Pirana in Ahmedabad City today. The plant has been established on Public-Private Partnership, PPP model at a cost of Rs. 375 crore. Our correspondent reports that this will be Ahmedabad’s first waste to energy plant which will prove to be a big step towards green future. We have a report :

“The state’s largest Waste to energy plant which has been set up at Piranha landfill in Ahmedabad is a joint partnership between Ahmedabad Municipal corporation and the Jindal urban waste management company . The plant which was set up on experimental basis was inaugurated officially today. Nearly 1 thousand metric tonnes of solid waste will be processed in the plant per day generating 15 mega watt of energy. The plant will not only ensure green and clean future to Ahmedabad city but will also prove to be a model initiative for effective waste management in urban areas. Aparna khunt Akashvani News Ahmedabad.”

The new train ticket advance reservation booking rules have come into force from today. The Railways has reduced the existing time limit for advance reservations in trains from 120 days to 60 days. However, all the bookings done upto 31st October this year will remain intact.

According to the Railways Ministry, 60 day booking window will help in reducing the chances of ticket hoarding, ensuring more tickets for passengers. The Ministry said, it was observed that about 21 percent of reservations done for 61 to 120 day periods were getting cancelled.

Certain daytime express trains like the Taj Express and Gomti Express will continue to follow the lower time limits for advance reservations. The Ministry added that the 365-day advance reservation booking limit for foreign tourists remains unchanged.

Indian and Chinese troops have started patrolling at the Line of Actual Control (LAC) in eastern Ladakh region. According to the sources of the Indian Army, patrolling has resumed in the Demchok area which had been halted for nearly four and a half years after a clash broke out between the two armies in April 2020. Patrolling in Depsang will resume soon. Our correspondent has filed this report.

“Both nations reached a consensus on disengagement after a series of diplomatic and military-level talks last month. Yesterday, on the occasion of Diwali, Indian and Chinese soldiers exchanged sweets at various points, including Chushul Moldo, Daulat Beg Oldi, Hot Spring, KK Pass, and Kongkla. The disengagement process began on the 22nd of last month, with both armies removing temporary tents and dismantling structures at the Depsang and Demchok areas. As per the plan, India and China have agreed to communicate in advance when conducting patrols in sensitive areas.”
