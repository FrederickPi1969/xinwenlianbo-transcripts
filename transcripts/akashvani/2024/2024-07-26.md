## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will visit Kargil War Memorial on the occasion of the 25th Kargil Vijay Diwas today. Mr Modi will pay homage to the soldiers who made the supreme sacrifice in the line of duty. In a social media post, the Prime Minister said, it is a very special day for every Indian. He said, it is a day to pay respect to all those who protect the nation.

“On the 25th Kargil Vijay Diwas, Prime Minister Narendra Modi will be the Chief Guest at the event, where he will lay a wreath at Kargil War Memorial to honor the martyrs of the 1999 War. The Prime Minister’s visit also includes the virtual first blast of the Shinkun La Tunnel Project. This ambitious project features a 4.1 km long twin-tube tunnel at an altitude of approximately 15,800 feet on the Nimu-Padum-Darcha Road. Upon completion, the Shinkun La Tunnel will be the highest tunnel in the world, providing all-weather connectivity to Leh. This infrastructure development will enhance the movement of armed forces and promote economic and social development in Ladakh. Akshvani News Anayat Ali from Kargil War Memorial Drass.”

BJP President and Union Health and Family Welfare Minister Jagat Prakash Nadda has said that the Kargil War was a conflict of strategic significance in which our soldiers faced severe conditions and fought valiantly against Pakistan.

Addressing a Mashal Rally in New Delhi, yesterday, Mr. Nadda stressed that despite the challenges, the Indian Army’s strategic brilliance led to the triumph and the unfurling of the tricolor on Kargil’s peak.

He said, under the leadership of Prime Minister Narendra Modi, the army has been provided with double-lane roads connecting to the border. He added that presently, the army can reach from Arunachal Pradesh to Ladakh within 36 hours. On the occasion, Mr. Nadda also inaugurated an exhibition based on the army and paid homage to the brave soldiers.

## Midday News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi today paid tribute to the heroes of the Kargil War on the occasion of the 25th anniversary of Kargil Vijay Diwas in Drass today.

Addressing the gathering, Prime Minister Modi said that this great land of Ladakh is witnessing the 25th anniversary of Kargil Vijay Diwas. He said Kargil Vijay Diwas tells us that the sacrifices made for the nation are immortal.

Critizising Pakistan over the recent terror attacks in Jammu and Kashmir, PM Modi said Pakistan had failed in all its nefarious attempts in the past but was trying to keep itself relevant with the help of terrorism and proxy war.

PM Modi said he wants to tell the patrons of terrorism that their nefarious intentions will never succeed.  He added that Indian soldiers will crush terrorism with full force and the enemy will be given a befitting reply.

Lauding the reforms in the defence sector, Prime Minister said India’s defence production has crossed 1.5 lakh crore. He added that India is making its mark as an arms exporter as well, contrary to its past image of a country that was counted as an arms-importing one.

Prime Minister also criticized the oppoisition for speading misinformation regarding  Agnipath Scheme  and said it is one of the crucial reform and will increase the strength of the country. The Prime Minister pointed out that those misleading the youth of the nation today had no regard for the armed forces in the past.

Recalling the days of Kargil war, the Prime Minister said he salute the brave sons of the country who made the supreme sacrifice to protect the motherland.

He reminded that in a few days from now on 5th of August, the abrogation of Article 370 will complete 5 years and the Jammu & Kashmir of today is talking about a new future full of dreams.

Underlining the developments taking place in Ladakh, the Prime Minister said through Shinkun La Tunnel, the union territory will remain connected with the whole country throughout the year, in every season. He said this tunnel will open doors of new possibilities for the development and better future of Ladakh.

After the event, Prime Minister Narendra Modi carried out the first blast at the Shinkun La Tunnel Project in Ladakh virtually. The Shinkun La Tunnel Project consists of a 4.1 km long Twin-Tube tunnel which will be constructed at an altitude of around 15,800 feet on the Nimu – Padum – Darcha Road to provide all-weather connectivity to Leh.

Lieutenant Governor of Ladakh, Brig (Dr) B D Sharma, Union Minister of State for Defence, Sanjay Seth, Chief of Defence Staff, Gen Anil Chauhan and the Chiefs of Army Staff of the three armed forces were present on the occasion.

President Droupadi Murmu has expressed gratitude and paid tribute to the armed forces on Kargil Vijay Diwas. In a social media post, President Murmu acknowledged the extraordinary valour of Indian armed forces. She remembered and honoured who made the supreme sacrifice while protecting Mother India on the peaks of Kargil in 1999.

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh paid rich tributes to the armed forces personnel who fought valiantly in extremely harsh conditions to defend the nation’s territory during the Kargil war 25 years ago.

Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said that under the leadership of Prime Minister Narendra Modi, India is becoming self-reliant in the defence sector. Talking to media in New Delhi, Mr Vaishnaw said, in the last ten years, 70 per cent of defence equipment has been produced in India. He said, the country is exporting the defense equipments to 80 countries and it has exported defense worth about 25 thousand crore rupees.

Health and Family Welfare Minister today said India has become the dispensaries of the World, adding that it is producing world most effective and cheapest medicines. Replying to supplementaries during the Question Hour, Mr. Nadda said, the government has taken several steps to strengthen the pharmaceutical industries in the country. He said, the government is promoting Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises to include any pharmaceutical manufacturing unit with a turnover of less than 500 crores that requires technology and quality upgradation.

US senator Marco Rubio introduced a bill in American Congress today that aims to strengthen the US-India partnership and counter the growing influence of China in the Indo-Pacific region. This is for the first time such an India-centric bill has been introduced in the US Congress. The bill proposes to treat India on par with its allies like as Japan, Israel, Korea and NATO. Among other things, the bill would set a Statement of Policy that the US will support India in its response to growing threats to its territorial integrity, provide necessary security assistance to India, and cooperate with India with respect to defense, civil space, technology, medicine and economic investments.

“Nearly seven hundred years old, Moidams are hollow vaults of brick, stone and contain the remains of kings and royals. The inclusion of Moidams was decided unanimously by the World Heritage Committee meeting held in New Delhi.
