## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will participate in Veer Baal Diwas, a nationwide celebration honouring children as the foundation of India’s future at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi today. He will also address the gathering on the occasion.

In 2022, Mr Modi had announced that 26th December will be marked as Veer Baal Diwas to pay tribute to the courage of the Sahibzades, the sons of Guru Gobind Singh and their quest for justice.

On the occasion, the Prime Minister will launch Suposhit Gram Panchayat Abhiyan. It aims at improving the nutritional outcomes and well-being by strengthening implementation of nutrition related services and by ensuring active community participation. Around three thousand 500 children, along with the Pradhan Mantri Rashtriya Bal Puraskar awardees and dignitaries, will attend the event. Various initiatives will also be run across the nation to engage young minds, promote awareness about the significance of Veer Baal Diwas and foster a culture of courage and dedication to the nation. A series of online competitions, including interactive quizzes, will be organized through the MyGov and MyBharat Portals. Interesting activities like storytelling, creative writing, poster-making among others will be undertaken in schools, Child Care Institutions and Anganwadi centres. With Dipendra Kumar, Adarsh Akashvani News Delhi.

In Madhya Pradesh, Chief Minister Dr. Mohan Yadav will participate in the Veer Bal Diwas program organized at Bharat Bhavan in Bhopal today.

“On Veer Bal Diwas, a state-level program organized at Bharat Bhavan in Bhopal will include the presentation of Kirtan Jatha and a film based on the sacrifice of Sahibzadas. Various activities centered on ‘Veer Bal Diwas’ are also being organized at every district headquarters of the state.

Punjabi Sahitya Academy under the Madhya Pradesh Culture Department is organizing ‘Veer Bal Diwas’ week from 22 to 29 December. The Academy is organizing events like short film screening, light and sound show, drama presentation and Veer Ras Kavi Sammelan centered on four Sahibzadas in nine districts of the state. Sanjeev Sharma, AKASHVANI News, Bhopal.”

President Droupadi Murmu will confer the ‘Pradhan Mantri Rashtriya Bal Puraskar’ on 17 children for exceptional achievements in seven categories in New Delhi today. Children from 14 states and union territories are being honored with the Bal Puraskar for their achievements in art and culture, bravery, innovation, science and technology, social service, sports, and environment. Each awardee will receive a medal, certificate, and citation booklet.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will distribute over 58 lakh property cards under the SVAMITVA scheme tomorrow. Mr Modi will also interact with beneficiaries of the scheme during the event. Under the scheme, a record of rights will be handed over to every property owner in the Village Abadi or inhabited areas.

The Ministry of Panchayati Raj said that the SVAMITVA scheme will give the landowners a proof of ownership which can be used to acquire credit by enabling monetization of their properties.

Talking to media in New Delhi, Panchayati Raj Secretary Vivek Bhardwaj highlighted that around 3 lakh 17 thousand villages have been surveyed through drone inspection under the scheme and over 1 lakh 37 thousand crore square meter residential land has been mapped. He added that around 2 crore property cards have been created so far, marking a major milestone in the adoption of the scheme.

Celebrated Malayalam writer and filmmaker MT Vasudevan Nair, often referred to as MT, passed away at a private hospital in Kozhikode last night at the age of 91. MT had been ailing for some time and was hospitalized since the 15th of this month.

Kerala government has declared State mourning today and tomorrow. All government functions scheduled to be held today have been deferred.

“A prolific and versatile story teller, M T Vasudevan Nair strode the Malayalam literary and cinematic  world like a colossus.  His works extended beyond novels and short stories and included screen plays and direction of films. MT’s works explored the complexities of human emotions and societal issues, touching upon themes of love, betrayal, alienation and struggles, set against Kerala’s cultural landscape. His writings continue to inspire readers and film makers, going beyond regional and linguistic barriers, making him one of the most influential literary figures in the country. Raj Mohan, Akashvani News, Kochi.”

The Joint Committee of Parliament on the Waqf (Amendment) Bill, 2024 today held meetings with the principal secretaries and revenue secretaries of the Department of minority affairs of Karnataka, Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh. Talking to media after the meeting in Delhi, Chairperson of the committee Jagadambika Pal said that during the meeting the committee tried to know about the registered and unregistered properties and the status of the Waqf properties in these states.

Mr. Pal said, the panel will call other organizations and state government officials and will go on tours to West Bengal, Bihar and Uttar Pradesh. He also said that the committee has conducted around 20 tours including Kolkata, Patna and Lucknow and the members also intend to visit Jammu and Kashmir to hold discussions with the stakeholders.

The Waqf (Amendment) Bill, 2024, aims to bring significant reforms, including digitisation of records, stricter audits, increased transparency, and legal mechanisms to reclaim illegally occupied Waqf properties. The Bill is aimed at reforming the management and regulation of Waqf properties across the country.
