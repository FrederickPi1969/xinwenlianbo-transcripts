## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will inaugurate SEMICON India 2024 in Greater Noida today. The theme of the three day event is “Shaping the Semiconductor Future”. The conference will showcase India’s semiconductor strategy and policy which envisions making India a global hub for semiconductor. Our correspondent reports that top leadership of global semiconductor giants will participate in the programme, with more than 250 exhibitors and 150 speakers.

SEMICON India will witness participation of top leadership of global semiconductor giants. It will bring together global leaders, companies, and experts of the semiconductor industry. The conference will witness participation of more than 250 exhibitors and 150 speakers. India is committed to build the semiconductor manufacturing system and is leading the technology revolution with focus on electronics. Semiconductors have applications in nearly all sectors, including communication, defence, automobiles and computing devices. India has a big role to play in the growth of global semiconductor sector and Semicon India will offer a platform to deliberate on various aspects shaping the semiconductor ecosystem in India. With Anand Kumar, Suparna Saikia, Akashvani News, Delhi.

Earlier, yesterday, the Prime Minister chaired the Semiconductor Executives Roundtable at his residence in New Delhi. He stressed that India has the capability to become a trusted partner in a diversified semiconductor supply chain. He said semiconductor is the basis of the Digital Age and the day is not far when the semiconductor industry will be the bedrock for even the basic necessities. The Prime Minister emphasised that democracy and technology together can ensure the welfare of humanity. He assured the leaders that the Indian government will follow a predictable and stable policy regime and will continue to support the industry at every step.

Top Semiconductor CEOs spoke to media after meeting the Prime Minister and praised him for his leadership. Kurt Sievers, CEO NXP Semiconductors said he is yet to meet a political leader with such a deep expertise in the semiconductor industry like Prime Minister Modi.

Bob Pragada, CEO, Jacobs said Mr. Modi is elevating India on the global scale and it is what both India and the world needs.

Luc Van Den Hove, CEO IMEC said he is very impressed with the leadership shown by Prime Minister Modi which shows a strong commitment to make India a powerhouse in the area of semiconductor technology.

The second Asia Pacific Ministerial Conference on Civil Aviation will begin at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi today. The International Civil Aviation Organization and the Ministry of Civil Aviation are co hosting the two day event.

India is the fastest growing aviation market in the world and is currently the third largest in the domestic segment. In the last decade, the number of aircraft in India increased from 400 to more than 800 and airports grew exponentially from 74 to 157. The government’s initiatives like UDAN (Ude Desh ka Aam Nagrik) have enhanced regional connectivity, ensuring that even remote areas are integrated into the aviation network.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will release an installment of 2 thousand 745 crore rupees to lakhs of beneficiaries of Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana in Jamshedpur, Jharkhand on 15th of this month. Union Minister for Rural Development Shivraj Singh Chouhan said this during a meeting of Rural Development Ministers of States through video conferencing yesterday. Mr Chouhan informed a large number of people will participate in the programme and lakhs of people will join the programme online. He said, acceptance letters will also be distributed to all the targeted beneficiaries.

The Bhartiya Janta Party (BJP) has announced that the party has enrolled over two crore new members in just eight days since the launch of its national membership drive on the 2nd of this month. Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated the drive by becoming the first member. In a statement, BJP General Secretary Vinod Tawde said, BJP Chief JP Nadda, Union Home Minister Amit Shah and General Secretary (Organisation) B L Santosh reviewed the ongoing membership drive with the office bearers of nine States separately.

The Aam Aadmi Party released the third list of eleven candidates last night for the ensuing Haryana Assembly Elections. The party has fielded Pravin Guskhani from Garhi Sampla-Kiloi assembly seat while Mahender Dahiya will contest from Jhajjar seat. Bheem Singh Rathi has been declared the party candidate from the Radaur seat and Amar Singh from Nilokheri seat. Earlier in the day, the party released its second list of nine candidates. On Monday, the party had announced the first list of 20 candidates.

Russia has launched its largest naval drill in decades, involving all 5 fleets. The “Ocean 2024”, which began yesterday, will be held simultaneously in the Pacific and Arctic Oceans, as well as the Mediterranean, Caspian and Baltic Seas. Russian President Vladimir Putin made the announcement during a virtual meeting, stressing the need for the country to be prepared for any scenario amid growing geopolitical tensions.

The exercises will involve more than 400 battleships, submarines, and support vessels from the auxiliary fleet, along with over 120 aircraft and more than 90,000 personnel.

China is also participating in the drills, contributing four vessels and 15 aircraft from the People’s Liberation Army. Mr. Putin noted that representatives from 15 other nations were invited to observe the drills.

US Republican Presidential candidate, Donald Trump and Democrat Kamala Harris participated in a live television debate hosted by ABC Networks. Vice President, Kamala Harris began by promoting her vision of an opportunity economy, while former President Donald Trump focused on linking Ms Harris to issues related to undocumented immigrants.

When asked whether Americans are better off now than they were four years ago, Ms Harris discussed her plans to support families and small businesses, criticizing Mr Trump for prioritizing tax cuts for billionaires and large corporations.

Mr Trump countered by denying claims that he intended to implement a nationwide sales tax, emphasizing instead his plan to raise tariffs on foreign imports.

The debate took place eight weeks before the election day across the US.

Newly elected UN General Assembly, UNGA President Philemon Yang has called for deeper international cooperation to address pressing global challenges such as climate change, escalating conflicts and a slowdown in sustainable development. Mr Yang said this in his opening remarks at the commencement of the 79th session of UNGA in New York yesterday. Hailing from Cameroon, Mr. Yang was sworn in at the closing of the last session of the UNGA yesterday.

International crude oil prices crashed to near three-year low yesterday, with the benchmark Brent crude futures sinking below 70 dollars a barrel for the first time since December 2021. The drop came shortly after the Organisation of Petroleum Exporting Countries and its allies (OPEC+) slashed its global oil demand forecast for 2024 and 2025.

Brent crude futures dropped by 2.65 dollars to 69.19 dollars a barrel. US West Texas Intermediate crude settled down 4.31 percent to 65.75 dollars a barrel.

The India Meteorological Department, IMD has forecast very to extremely heavy rainfall over parts of Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Vidarbha, Uttar Pradesh and Uttarakhand today, as the well-marked low-pressure area over north-eastern Madhya Pradesh and surrounding region is expected to re-intensify into a depression.

The weather department has also predicted heavy rainfall in north eastern states West Bengal, Bihar and Chhattisgarh for next few days.

In Telangana, the authorities have issued the second flood warning in Bhadradri Kothagudem district following the water level in River Godavari crossing the 48 feet mark at Bhadrachalam last evening. The District authorities informed that the discharge in the river down the stream was recorded at 11.82 cusecs.

An Inter-Ministerial Central Team (IMCT) will visit Telangana today to assess the damage caused by the recent floods and heavy rain. The Ministry of Home Affairs constituted the IMCT with six officials. Joint Secretary in the Home Ministry and adviser at the National Disaster Management Authority Colonel Kirti Pratap Singh will lead the team. The other members of the team include officers from Union Ministries of Agriculture, Finance, Roads and Highways, Rural Development and National Remote Sensing Centre.

In Hockey, defending champions India will take on last edition’s runners-up, Malaysia, in their third match of Men’s Asian Champions Trophy tournament at  Hulunbuir, in China, today.

The Harmanpreet Singh-led team currently leads the points table with six points.

The 45th FIDE Chess Olympiad will begin Hungary’s capital Budapest today. The 12-day biennial event will feature over 1,800 players, with 193 national teams registered in the open section and 181 teams competing in the women’s section.

India will enter the open section as the second-seeded team, just behind the USA.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that this is the era of silicon diplomacy and India will take every step to become the semiconductor power house. He said, the government is focussing on increasing the number of chip production in the country to equip every device with Indian-made chips. Prime Minister Narendra Modi has emphasized that climate change and energy transitions are global concerns, and international partnership is critical for promoting the impact of green hydrogen on decarbonization. Addressing the International Conference on Green Hydrogen virtually today, the Prime Minister highlighted that scaling up production, minimizing costs, and building infrastructure can progress faster through cooperation. He also emphasised that there is a growing realisation that climate change is not just a matter of the future as its impact is being felt here and now. Mr. Modi said that energy transition and sustainability have become central to global policy discourse. Highlighting that India is committed to creating a cleaner and greener planet, Mr. Modi noted that the country is the first among G20 nations to fulfill its Paris commitments on green energy.

Mr. Modi also outlined that India’s non-fossil fuel capacity increased by nearly 300 percent over the last ten years, and the country’s solar energy capacity increased by over 3,000 per cent during the same period. He said that Green hydrogen can help in decarbonizing industries that are difficult to electrify.  India has already launched the National Green Hydrogen Mission in 2023.

## Evening News

The Cabinet has given nod to providing health coverage to all senior citizens above 70 years of age irrespective of income under Ayushman Bharat PM Jan Arogya Yojana. Briefing media after the Cabinet meeting this evening in Delhi, Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said, the senior citizens will get the facility of free treatment up to 5 lakh rupees.

Senior citizens aged 70 years and above who are already availing benefits of other public health insurance schemes such as Central Government Health Scheme, Ex-Servicemen Contributory Health Scheme, Ayushman Central Armed Police Force  may either choose their existing scheme or opt for AB PMJAY. Senior citizens who are under private health insurance policies or Employees’ State Insurance scheme will also be eligible to avail benefits under AB PM-JAY.

The Cabinet also approved PM Electric Drive Revolution in Innovative Vehicle Enhancement, PM E-DRIVE, Scheme with an outlay of 10,900 crore rupees over a period of two years.

The Government has also approved an outlay of 12 thousand 461 crore rupees for 31,350 Mega Watt hydropower projects.  ‘Mission Mausam’ was also approved to create a more weather-ready and climate-smart Bharat with an outlay of two thousand crore rupees over two years. It is envisaged to be a multi-faceted and transformative initiative to tremendously boost India’s weather and climate-related science, research, and services.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has expressed confidence that India is going to play a major role in driving the global Semiconductor industry. The Prime Minister was speaking after inaugurating SEMICON India 2024 in Greater Noida today. He said, the government is focussing on increasing the number of chip production in the country to equip every device with Indian-made chips.
