## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has successfully concluded his historic two-nation visit to Poland and Ukraine. In a social media post, Mr Modi on his Ukraine visit said, he came to the great nation with the aim of deepening friendship between the two countries. Mr Modi added that he held productive talks with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy during their bilateral talks in Kyiv. The Prime Minister said that India firmly believes that peace must always prevail always in the region.
During the bilateral talks yesterday, Prime Minister Modi invited President Zelenskyy to visit India. Mr Modi also told Mr Zelenskyy that both Ukraine and Russia should sit together without wasting time to find ways to end the ongoing war and that India had been on the side of peace since the beginning of the conflict in February 2022.

Speaking to the media after the meeting, the Ukrainian President said that India can be a key influence in the global diplomatic efforts to end the war between his country and Russia.

The Ukrainian President proposed India among a handful of Global South countries for hosting the second Ukraine peace summit and conveyed his idea to Mr Modi. Mr Modi’s nearly nine-hour visit to Ukraine came six weeks after he held summit talks with Russian President Vladimir Putin.

During his two-day official visit to Poland, Prime Minister Modi held meetings with his counterpart Donald Tusk, and President Andrzej Sebastian Duda in Warsaw. In a social media post, Mr Modi described his visit to Poland as a special one, and called the central European nation India’s valued friend.

The United States has said that Prime Minister Modi’s visit to Kyiv can contribute to achieving a peaceful resolution of conflict in Ukraine aligned with President Zelenskyy’s vision. US National Security Communications Advisor, John Kirby said, India is a strong partner for the United States, and Prime Minister Modi’s visit and conversation with President Zelenskyy can be helpful in getting an end to the conflict.

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh and US Secretary of Defense Lloyd Austin reviewed the existing defence cooperation activities and discussed ways to deepen it further during a bilateral meeting at Pentagon last night. They held wide-ranging discussions on bilateral defence cooperation, industrial collaboration, regional security and other international issues. Mr Singh reached Washington on Thursday on a four-day visit to the United States.

During the meeting with his US counterpart, Mr Singh highlighted the various co-development and co-production opportunities in India in the areas identified in the India-US Defence Industrial Cooperation Roadmap, which was adopted last year. In a statement, the Defence Ministry said, both the Ministers appreciated the progress made in operationalising the Indo-Pacific Maritime Domain Awareness, a Quad initiative and lauded the ongoing efforts by India to enhance the Maritime Domain Awareness for the partners in the Indian Ocean Region.

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh and US Secretary of Defense Lloyd Austin commended the efforts of the India-US Defence Acceleration Ecosystem (INDUS-X) for establishing the Defence Innovation bridge between the two countries.

The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) has asked Food Business Operators (FBOs) and e-commerce FBOs to remove all claims regarding A1 and A2 types of milk and milk products from all packaging. FSSAI’s instructions came after it found that several FBOs were selling milk and milk products such as ghee, butter, and curd under the A1 and A2 classification using FSSAI license numbers. The food regulator added that the A1 and A2 differentiation of milk is essentially linked to a difference in the structure of protein, and therefore, any A2 claims on milk fat products are misleading and not in conformance with the provisions of the FSS Act 2006 and its regulations. FSSAI further said that the standards for milk, as specified in the Food Safety and Standards (Food Product Standards and Food Additives) Regulations, 2011, do not mention or recognize any differentiation of milk based on A1 and A2 types.

The Ministry directed the institutes to embrace India’s rich cultural tapestry and design appropriate dress code for the Convocation Ceremony based on local traditions of the State in which the Institute is located.

India’s first reusable hybrid rocket Rhumi-1 was launched from Chennai by Space Zone India, in collaboration with martin group. The rocket was launched using a mobile trajectory while carrying a payload of 50 pico satellites and three cube satellites. The sounding rocket flew at an altitude of about 35 kms and returned to earth after conducting several experiments within seconds.

Dr. M. Annadurai, former Director of ISRO Space Centre who led the expedition said that the rocket used a combination of liquid and solid fuel propellants to enhance efficiency and minimize operational costs.

The Union Cabinet has approved the Unified Pension Scheme for government employees, which provides for an assured pension. Addressing the media after the Cabinet meeting in New Delhi this evening, Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said that the first pillar of the scheme ensures 50 percent of the average basic pay drawn over the last twelve months prior to superannuation for a minimum qualifying service of 25 years. The second pillar guarantees that 60 percent of the pension is assured for the family after the employee’s demise. Mr. Vaishnaw said  about 23 lakh central government employees would benefit from this scheme.

Mr. Vaishnaw said that the Cabinet, chaired by Prime Minister Narendra Modi, also approved the Bio E3-Biotechnology for Economy, Environment, and Employment policy to foster high-performance biomanufacturing. The Cabinet also approved the continuation of three umbrella schemes, which have been merged into a unified central sector scheme named Vigyan Dhara, with an outlay of 10,579 crore rupees.
