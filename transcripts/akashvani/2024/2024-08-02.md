## Morning News

President Droupadi Murmu will preside over a two-day Conference of Governors at Rashtrapati Bhavan commencing from today. This is the first Conference of Governors to be presided over by the President.

Governors of all States will attend the Conference. Vice President Jagdeep Dhankhar, Prime Minister Narendra Modi, and Several Union Ministers including Amit Shah, Dharmendra Pradhan, Shivraj Singh Chouhan, Ashwini Vaishnaw and Dr Mansukh Mandaviya will also attend the conference. The vice chairman and CEO of NITI Aayog and other senior officials from the PMO, and Cabinet Secretariat in the Home Affairs Ministry will also participate.

Our correspondent reports that the agenda of the Conference includes the implementation of three criminal laws, reforms in higher education and accreditation of universities, and the development of focus areas.

The CBI has filed its first chargesheet against 13 accused in NEET-UG entrance examination paper leak case in Bihar. The agency said, it has filed cases against mastermind of the paper leak scam Manish Prakash, Sikander Yadvendu and 11 others under sections 120-B, 201, 409, 380, 411, 420 and 109 of the IPC. In the NEET-UG paper leak case the Economic Offence Unit (EOU) of Bihar police had busted an organized inter-state gang. Initially the case was registered at Shastri Nagar Police Station in Patna in May this year but was later transferred to the CBI on 23rd June. CBI has utilized advanced forensic techniques, artificial intelligence, CCTV footages, tower location analysis and other tools to gather evidence against the accused.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi  met the speaker of the House of Representatives of Japan Nukaga Fukushiro and his delegation of Japanese Parliament and business leaders. During the meeting, the Prime Minister highlighted the conducive business environment and reforms undertaken in India for more investment and technology from Japan. He assured the government’s full support to the visiting delegation for these endeavours.

The meeting underscored the robust India-Japan Special Strategic and Global Partnership, highlighting key areas of collaboration and mutual interest with focus on people to people cooperation.

India and Vietnam have signed nine agreements to deepen comprehensive strategic partnership and enhance cooperation in the fields of customs, agriculture, legal, radio and television broadcast, culture, and tourism. This was stated by Secretary East of Ministry of External Affairs Jaideep Mazumdar in New Delhi. Briefing media on the State Visit of Prime Minister of Vietnam Pham Minh Chinh to India, he said, Prime Minister Narendra Modi held talks with his Vietnamese counterpart Pham Minh Chinh in New Delhi. He said, both the leaders agreed to further deepen India-Vietnam relationship by increasing economic, strategic and cultural ties. He said, Vietnam is a rapidly growing economy and India-Vietnam bilateral trade has reached 15 billion US dollars. He said, both the countries emphasized on increasing bilateral trade.

Lok Sabha has approved demands for grants of the Ministry of Education. Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan said budgetary allocation for Education has increased over the years as it is only by providing quality education that India will achieve the target of 30 trillion dollar economy by 2047. Mr Pradhan was replying to the discussion on demands for grants of Education Ministry  in the House. He elaborated that 4 Lakh 30 thousand crore rupees was being spent on education in 2013-14, which has now increased to 9 lakh 19 thousand crore. Mr Pradhan also informed that there has been a 78 percent increase in budgetary allocation for higher education since 2013-14.

The Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE) under Kushal Bharat Viksit Bharat campaign, felicitated trainees and beneficiaries of Skill India Mission in Lucknow yesterday. Union Minister of State, MSDE Jayant Chaudhry was the chief guest at the event. Addressing the gathering, Mr. Chaudhry highlighted that government is focusing on skill development and has made it a part of school curriculum in a way that the youth can develop employable skills at an early age. The Unified Payments Interface (UPI)-based transactions surged in the month of July, reaching Rs 20.64 lakh crore rupees, shows the data released by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI). The report further reads, the UPI transactions have a surge 35 percent year on year.

According to the data, The total UPI transaction count went up almost 4 per cent (month-on-month) to 14.44 billion in July. The average daily transaction volume stood at 466 million, UPI is now adding up to 60 lakh new users every month, fuelled by the RuPay credit card on UPI and its launch in foreign countries.

The Goods and Services Tax – GST revenue has increased by 10.3 per cent to over 1 lakh 82 thousand crore rupees last month, the third-highest collection overall. According to the data released by the Ministry of Finance, the net Goods and Services Tax (GST) collection after adjusting refunds was around one lakh 60 thousand rupees in July, which is a growth of 14.4 per cent. The total refunds decreased by 19.4 per cent to 16 thousdand 283 crore. The finance ministry said, the main driver for the GST collection growth has been domestic transactions in goods and services which grew by 8.9 per cent to 1 lakh 34 thousand rupees. The revenue from imports have increased by 14.2 per cent to 48 thousnad 39 crore rupees.

Delhi University Vice Chancellor Professor Yogesh Singh has said that the University will use Class XII marks to fill any remaining vacant seats after the regular admission rounds are completed. The announcement comes as the University Grants Commission issued a standard operating procedure (SOP) for Central Universities to ensure all seats in UG and PG courses are filled. The UGC noted that keeping the seats vacant for an entire academic year is not only a waste of resources but also results in the denial of quality higher education to many students.

In Kerala, search operations are on war footing in the multiple landslides hit Wayanad. The massive disaster that struck on Tuesday had reportedly claimed nearly 300 lives so far. A report from our correspondent:-
