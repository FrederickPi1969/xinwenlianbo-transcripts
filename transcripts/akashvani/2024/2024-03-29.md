## Morning News

The External Affairs Ministry has said that the recent remarks by the US State Department on Delhi Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal’s arrest are unwarranted. Addressing the media in New Delhi yesterday, Ministry spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal said any such external imputation on India’s electoral and legal processes is completely unacceptable.

India lodged its strong objection and protest with the senior official from the US Embassy yesterday with regard to the comments made by the US State Department.

The spokesperson highlighted that India is proud of its independent and robust democratic institutions and is committed to protecting them from any form of undue external influences.

A Delhi Court has extended the Enforcement Directorate (ED) custody of Delhi Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal till the 1st of April in the money laundering case related to the alleged liquor policy scam case. He was arrested on the 21st of this month by the ED.

Special judge Kaveri Baweja of Rouse Avenue Court passed the remand extension order after Kejriwal was produced in court on the expiry of his six-day ED custody.

The External Affairs Ministry has reiterated that Arunachal Pradesh was, is and will remain an integral and inalienable part of India. Ministry spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal was responding to media queries in New Delhi yesterday.

The combined Index of Eight Core Industries of the country witnessed a three-month high growth of 6.7 per cent in February this year, in comparison to the same period last year. The index had grown by 4.1 per cent in January this year and 4.9 per cent in December last year.

The scrutiny of nominations was held yesterday for the first phase of Lok Sabha Elections along with single-phase Assembly polls in two north-eastern states. The filing of papers for this phase ended on Wednesday. In Bihar, the last day for filling of papers for the first phase of parliamentary Elections was yesterday. In the first phase, 102 Lok Sabha seats in 17 States and four Union Territories along with 32 Assembly constituencies in Sikkim and 60-member House in Arunachal Pradesh will go to polls on the 19th of next month. Among the states going to polls in a single phase for the Lok Sabha polls are Tamil Nadu, Arunachal Pradesh, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Lakshadeep, Anadaman and Nicobar Islands and Puducherry. Tomorrow is the last day for the withdrawal of candidature.

In Maharashtra, after the scrutiny, nominations of 110 out of 181 candidates were found valid for the five Lok Sabha constituencies going to polls in the first phase.

In Arunachal Pradesh, after the scrutiny of nomination papers, in total, 168 nominations were found valid for 58 Assembly Constituencies and the scrutiny of two Assembly seats was adjourned till 11 am today.

For two parliamentary constituencies, 15 nominations were found valid. There are eight candidates for the Arunachal West Parliamentary Constituency including the nominations of BJP candidate and Union Minister Kiren Rijiju and Congress nominee Nabam Tuki. In the Arunachal East Parliamentary seat, papers of 7 candidates were found valid.

The notification for the second phase of Lok Sabha elections was issued yesterday. 88 Lok Sabha seats in 12 States and Union Territories along with the remaining part of the Outer Manipur Lok Sabha constituency will go to polls in this phase on the  26th of next month.

Nominations can be filed till the 4th of April. The scrutiny of nominations across all States and Union Territories will be done on the 5th of April except for Jammu and Kashmir, where scrutiny will be held on the 6th of April.

Jailed gangster-turned-politician, Mukhtar Ansari died due to cardiac arrest at the Banda Medical College Hospital in Uttar Pradesh last night. He was admitted to the hospital yesterday after his condition allegedly deteriorated when he fainted inside the district jail where he was lodged.

State Director-General of Police Prashant Kumar said that prohibitory orders under CrPC Section 144 have been imposed across Uttar Pradesh following Ansari’s death. Security has been tightened in Ghazipur, Mau, Varanasi and other districts in the state as a precautionary measure. Police conducted flag march in Firozabad.

The five-time MLA from Mau district in eastern Uttar Pradesh was in more than 60 criminal cases in the state and in many other places. He was in custody since 2005. He contested elections twice as Bahujan Samaj Party candidate.

Russia has vetoed a UN resolution which ended the monitoring of United Nations’ sanctions against North Korea over its nuclear programme. The move sparked the western accusations, saying, Russia is trying to protect its weapon purchases from North Korea for use in its war against Ukraine, which violates the United Nations sanctions. The vote in the 15-member council, with 13 in favor, has no impact on the actual sanctions against North Korea. However, Russia’s veto will halt its work when its current mandate expires at the end of April. China abstained from voting.

Russia’s UN Ambassador Vasily Nebenzia accused the expert committee of increasingly becoming a plaything in the hands of Western approaches, reprinting biased information and analyzing newspaper headlines and poor-quality photos.

Meanwhile, US Deputy Ambassador Robert Wood has warned that a Russian veto would encourage North Korea to continue endangering global security through the development of long-range ballistic missiles and efforts to evade sanctions.

In Palestine, President Mahmoud Abbas has approved the formation of a new government. The newly elected government, which consists of 23 Ministers, will be led by Prime Minister Mohammad Mustafa. He will also serve as the Foreign Minister. The official Palestinian news agency, WAFA, reported that in a cabinet statement addressed to President Abbas, Prime Minister Mustafa said the first national priority is an immediate ceasefire in Gaza and a complete Israeli withdrawal from the enclave, in addition to allowing humanitarian aid to enter in large quantities and reaching all areas.

The Centre has extended the Armed Forces (SpecialPowers) Act (AFSPA) in eight districts of Nagaland and three districts of Arunachal Pradesh for six months with effect from the 1st of next month. It has also been extended to 21 police stations in five other districts of Nagaland and three police stations in another district of Arunachal Pradesh. The AFSPA has been in force in certain districts and police station areas in Nagaland and Arunachal Pradesh for many years now and it is extended periodically.

It is also armed with Active Electronically Scanned Array Radar, Electronic Warfare Suite, beyond-visual-range missiles and Air-to-Air  refuelling.

In Miami Open Tennis, top seeded, India’s Rohan Bopanna and his Australian partner Matthew Ebden stormed into the Men’s Doubles Final last night. They defeated the fourth seeded pair of Marcel Granollers of Spain and Horacio Zeballos of Argentina in straight sets, 6-1, 6-4.

External Affairs Minister Dr S Jaishankar is holding a meeting with his Ukrainian counterpart Dmytro Kuleba in New Delhi. Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that Digital Public Infrastructure is a big requirement in itself and the government is extending the digital facilities to all the villages. Interacting with philanthropist Bill Gates, he said, technology can play a big role in agriculture, health and education sectors.

On Artificial Intelligence or AI, Mr. Modi said, if we rely on AI out of laziness, then it is the wrong path. He added that if used as a magic tool then AI will perhaps lead to grave injustice. He said, AI is a very good tool, but if it is given to some one without proper training, it is likely to be misused. He suggested that there should be clear watermarks on AI-generated content, so that nobody is misguided.

The Prime Minister said, the Government is committed to ensuring that emerging talent from within the community can continuously contribute and enhance its value to foster trust in technology among people.

The government has ordered mandatory declaration of stock position of wheat. Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution Ministry today said Traders/Wholesalers, Retailers, Big Chain Retailers and Processors in all States and Union Territories have to declare their stock position of wheat with effect from 1st April till further orders. It said, the decision has been taken in order to manage the overall food security and to prevent hoarding and unscrupulous speculation.

In Bihar, the opposition Grand Alliance today announced the seat sharing agreement for the 40 parliamentary constituencies among Congress, RJD and Left Parties.

The seat sharing was announced by leaders of Grand Alliance in Patna in a joint press conference.  Barring some seats Congress and RJD will contest on the same seats which were contested by these parties in 2019 Lok Sabha elections.

Out of 26 the RJD candidates have already filed nominations for the four Lok Sabha seats of Aurangabad, Nawada, Jamui and Gaya in the first phase.

Congress has retained its Kishanganj seat, the lone Lok Sabha seat won by Grand Alliance in Bihar in 2019 parliamentary elections. At least ten people have been killed after a passenger vehicle en route to Srinagar from Jammu veered off the road and plunged into a deep gorge near Battery Cheshma in Ramban district along the Jammu-Srinagar national highway today. Local authorities swiftly arrived at the accident site. However, due to the challenging terrain, darkness, and persistent rainfall in the area, rescue efforts couldn’t be immediately undertaken.

President Droupadi Murmu has expressed grief on the loss of lives in the accident.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has expressed grief on the accident in Ramban, Jammu and Kashmir and announced an ex-gratia of two lakh rupees from Prime Minister’s National Relief Fund to the next of kin of each deceased. The injured would be given 50 thousand rupees.

Jammu and Kashmir Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha has also expressed shock and grief over the loss of lives.
