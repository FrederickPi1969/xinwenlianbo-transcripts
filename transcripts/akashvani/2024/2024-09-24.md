## Morning News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has said that Digital Public Infrastructure should be a bridge and not a barrier. Speaking at the Summit of the Future at the United Nations General Assembly last night, Prime Minister Modi added that for global good, India is ready to share its Digital Public Infrastructure. PM Modi said that for safe and responsible use of technology, balanced regulation is needed. He added that the world wants such a global digital governance in which sovereignty and integrity remain intact.  Mr. Modi also said that the success of humanity lies in collective strength and not on the battlefield.

Highlighting the threats on peace and security, PM Modi said that while on one hand, terrorism continues to be a serious threat to global peace and security. On the other hand, cyber, maritime and space are emerging as new theatres of conflict. On all these issues, PM stressed that global action must match global ambition.

Talking about reforms of multi lateral institutions, PM said that Global institutions need reforms as reforms are the key to relevance. PM also called the African Union’s inclusion as a permanent member in the G20 an important step in this direction. PM Modi also stated that for India, ‘One Earth One Family One Future’ is a commitment.

“On the last day of his US visit, Prime Minister Narendra Modi addressed the Summit of the Future at the United Nations General Assembly at UN Headquarters.

In his address, Prime Minister highlighted India’s vision for shaping a sustainable world for future generations and called for urgent reform of the institutions of global governance, including the UN Security Council.

On the sidelines of the summit, Prime Minister Modi met Mr. To Lam, General Secretary of the Communist Party of Vietnam and State President of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam. Mr. Modi reiterated his sympathies and solidarity with Vietnam in the face of loss and damage caused by Typhoon Yagi earlier this month.

PM also held talks with President Volodymyr Zelenskyy of Ukraine. Prime Minister reiterated India’s clear, consistent and constructive approach in favour of a peaceful resolution of the conflict through diplomacy and dialogue as well as engagement between all stakeholders. From New York HIMANI RANA, AKASHVANI NEWS.”

PM Modi also met the Prime Minister of Armenia Nikol Pashinyan and interacted with Secretary of State of the Holy See Cardinal, Pietro Parolin.

Foreign Secretary Vikram Misri has said that Prime Minister Narendra Modi had a very intense and successful visit to the United States. Briefing media in New York, Mr. Misri said, the Community event held during the Prime Minister’s visit showed the kind of connect that the Indian American community has built in the US. Mr. Misri added that the visit had a very strong technology element to it, which is reflected in the Quad Joint Statement, in the US India Bilateral Factsheet, in the CEO Roundtable discussion and meeting with the CEO of Holtec International.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi left for New Delhi this morning after concluding his successful and substantial three-day visit to US.

During her visit, the Finance Minister will attend the 9th Annual Meeting of the Board of Governors of Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank (AIIB) in Samarkand tomorrow and on Thursday. He will interact with Bharatiya Janata Party workers of Jaipur district in Jaipur today. He will also inspect the railway track protection system – KAVACH by traveling in the train loco from Sawai Madhopur to Sumerganj Mandi.

“The railway track between Kota to Sawai Madhopur in Kota railway division has become the first track equipped with the track protection system KAVACH 4.0. It has been installed in a record time. KAVACH is the Railways’ automatic train protection system, which has been developed indigenously. KAVACH provides the ability to prevent train collision and automatically detects and prevents head-on, rear and side collision. Through this, the loco pilot will get in-cab signaling, which will make train operation possible even during fog. Jitendra Dwivedi, Akashwani News, Jaipur.”

Employees’ Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) has recorded an all time highest addition of around 20 Lakh net members in July this year, registering a growth of 2.43 percent in comparison to the same month the last year. Labour and Employment Minister Dr. Mansukh Mandaviya said this while releasing EPFO’s provisional payroll data of July in New Delhi yesterday. He said that it is the highest recorded increase since payroll data tracking began in April 2018. The data indicates that around 10.52 lakh new members have enrolled during June this year. The highest growth was observed in the 18-25 age group, with around 9 lakh net additions.

The government also released a revolving fund worth 2500 crore rupees for 4.3 lakh Self Help Groups, benefitting 48 lakh members. Celebrating the strength of Nari Shakti, Bank loans worth five thousand crore rupees were released to support 26 lakh members across 2.35 lakh Self help groups. The Mudra loan limit has been increased from 10 lakh to 20 lakh rupees to empower women entrepreneurs. Pallavi Parmar for Akashvani News, Delhi.

## Midday News

After concluding his successful three day visit to the US, Prime Minister Narendra Modi is now on his way to New Delhi. During a hectic visit, Prime Minister Modi attended the Quad Leaders’ Summit, a large Indian-American community event, addressed the United Nations Summit of the Future and held multiple bilateral meetings with heads of several other countries. Our correspondent covering the Prime Minister’s visit has filed a detailed report:

Prime Minister Modi began his visit with a meeting with the US President, Joe Biden. In a special gesture, President Biden hosted the meeting at his home in Wilmington, Delaware. The 2 agreements signed during the meeting include Indo-Pacific Economic Framework Agreement and India-US Drug Policy framework. In his address at the Quad Leaders Summit, PM Modi said, Quad is committed for a free, open, inclusive and prosperous Indo Pacific. Prime Minister stressed that Quad is here to stay, to assist, to partner and to complement.

At the Cancer Moonshot event, PM Modi has said that India will contribute 40 million vaccine doses for Indo-Pacific countries under GAVI-the Vaccine Alliance and QUAD initiatives. On the sidelines of the summit, Prime Minister Modi also held bilateral meetings with Japanese Prime Minister Fumio Kishida and Australian Prime Minister, Anthony Albanese.

On the second day of his visit, PM attended a massive Indian-American Community event at Nassau Coliseum in Long Island, New York.  Lauding the Indian diaspora’s role in India-US bilateral relations, PM Modi said that their skills and talents are unmatched. At the event, Prime Minister Narendra Modi also gave the PUSHP mantra for making India a developed nation.

Later in the day, Prime Minister interacted with tech industry leaders in New York in a Roundtable anchored by the MIT, School of Engineering. The tech-roundtable primarily focused on Artificial Intelligence; Biotechnology and Semiconductor technologies. Prime Minister highlighted the economic transformation happening in India, particularly in electronics and information technology manufacturing, semiconductors, biotech and green development and invited the tech leaders for investment in India in these sectors.

The roundtable followed by PM’s back-to-back bilateral engagements with PM of Nepal, KP Sharma Oli, Crown Prince of Kuwait, Sheikh Sabah Khaled Al-Hamad Al-Sabah and Palestinian President, Mahmoud Abbas.

On the third and day of his visit, speaking at the Summit of the Future at the United Nations General Assembly,  PM Modi added that Digital Public Infrastructure should be a bridge and not a barrier. Mr. Modi also said that the success of humanity lies in collective strength and not in the battlefield.

Talking about reforms of multilateral institutions, PM said that Global institutions need reforms as reforms are the key to relevance.

On the sidelines of UNGA, PM Narendra Modi also held talks with President Volodymyr Zelenskyy of Ukraine in New York. Prime Minister conveyed that India remains open to provide all support within its means to facilitate a lasting and peaceful resolution of the conflict. Thus concluding a very intense and successful visit to the United States. From New York, Himani Rana, Akashvani News.

External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar held a bilateral meeting with Bangladesh Foreign Affairs Advisor, Touhid Hossain on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly in New York today. This marks the first high-level engagement between India and Bangladesh’s interim government, led by Muhammad Yunus after Sheikh Hasina’s ouster as Prime Minister, last month.

The meeting focused on strengthening the bilateral ties between the two neighbouring countries and addressed mutual interests.

President Droupadi Murmu has stressed that Audit needs to keep up with technological evolution in order to be able to perform its oversight functions effectively. The President said, the mandate of public sector audits has expanded beyond traditional auditing and it also include assessing the effectiveness of public welfare schemes and projects, ensuring that they serve all citizens equitably. President Murmu said this after inaugurating the 16th Assembly of the Asian Organization of Supreme Audit Institutions (ASOSAI) in New Delhi today. The President said that timeliness is equally important for effective auditing of public finance. She said, the Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) of India plays a key role in ensuring transparency and accountability in the country’s public finance.

The four-day conference will have a series of discussions on the evolving role of Supreme Audit Institutions in fostering transparency and accountability.

In Jammu and Kashmir, all arrangements are in place to ensure free, fair, and smooth polling in the second phase of the assembly elections tomorrow. Twenty Six assembly constituencies spread over six districts will go to polls in this phase. The voting will be held from 7 AM to 6 PM. The key candidates include former Chief Minister Omar Abdullah, PCC UT Chief Tariq Hamid Qarrah, and Apni Party President Altaf Bukhari.

A total of the 3,502 polling stations across all 26 constituencies have been equipped with 100 percent webcasting facilities, and all minimum assured basic amenities are being provided for the convenience of voters. The third phase of elections covering the remaining 40 assembly segments will be held on the First of October. Counting of votes will take place on 8th October.

The Chief Election Commissioner Rajiv Kumar along with Election Commissioners – Gyanesh Kumar and Sukhbir Singh Sandhu is holding a review meeting with the Commissioners of all divisions, Zonal IGs, District Election Officers of all districts, all DIGs, and all Superintendents of Police of the Jharkhand in Ranchi today. Earlier, the commission instructed central and state enforcement agencies to ensure an inducement free elections. The commission also instructed Chief Electoral Officer of the state and State Police Nodal Officer and the nodal officer of Central Armed Police Forces for a smooth and peaceful conduct of assembly elections in the state.

Talking to reporters in Ranchi, Mr. Sarma said that if the current government were serious about the common people of the state, Mr. Soren should have taken steps much earlier, not just before the elections.

The Karnataka High Court has rejected a petition filed by Chief Minister Siddaramaiah challenging the Governor’s sanction to prosecute him in the MUDA land allotment case.

The single Judge bench of Justice M Nagaprasanna pronounced that the Governor can make an independent decision and that his order to prosecute the Chief Minister does not suffer from non-application of mind. On August 16, Governor Thawarchand Gehlot had sanctioned proceedings against the CM under the Prevention of Corruption Act and the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita. Siddaramaiah had challenged the order on August 19. The anti corruption activists T J Abharam, Snehamayi Krishna and Pradeep Kumar had sought permission from the Governor to begin prosecution alleging that CM Siddaramaiah’s wife Parvathi received 14 housing sites from MUDA in exchange for a 3.16 acre plot of land that MUDA land development agency in Mysuru had acquired in 2021. They alleged that this resulted in a loss of 55.80 crore rupees to the state exchequer.

Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman has reached Tashkent, Uzbekistan to attend the 9th Annual Meeting of Board of Governors of Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank (AIIB). She will attend the meeting tomorrow. India is the second largest shareholder of the bank. The multilateral discussions will centred around a broad spectrum of important global issues relevant to the development agenda. Ms Sitharaman will also sign the Bilateral Investment Treaty between India and Uzbekistan.

In a dramatic escalation of tensions in West Asia, Israeli airstrikes targeting Hezbollah positions across Lebanon have resulted in at least 492 deaths, according to the Lebanese health ministry. This marks the deadliest day of conflict in the region in almost 20 years. More from our correspondent:

The Israeli military reports striking 1,600 Hezbollah assets, including what they describe as “cruise missiles,” rockets, and explosive warheads. Israeli officials claim these munitions were stored in civilian homes, a tactic that has long been a point of contention in conflicts involving militant groups. The death toll from Monday’s strikes alone – nearly 500 people, including dozens of children represents approximately half the number of Lebanese casualties from the entire 34-day war between Israel and Hezbollah in 2006. It remains unclear how many of those killed were Hezbollah militants. Residents in targeted areas received evacuation warnings through various means, including text messages from Israel and calls from automated numbers. Vinod Kumar, Akashvani News, Dubai.”

In Chengdu Open Tennis the Men’s Double Finals, the clashis underway  between the third seeded Indo-French pair of Yuki Bhambri and Albano Olivetti and the top-seeded French pair Sadio Doumbia and Fabien Reboul. The Bhambri-Olivetti pair defeated second seeded doubles pair of Ivan Dodig and Rafael Matos in the semifinal 6-3, 7-6 yesterday.

Domestic benchmark indices recovered from their earlier losses to trade in positive territory in the afternoon trade session. Meanwhile, Sensex had reached a fresh high at 85,077 and the Nifty also reached peak of 25,984 in the early trade today.

The Supreme Court today directed the States and the Union Territories to implement the ‘Centre’s guidelines on safety and security of children in schools’. The Apex Court also asked the National Commission for Protection of Child Rights to coordinate with states and union territories and monitor the implementation of the guidelines. A bench of justices BV Nagarathna and N Kotiswar Singh noted the Centre has notified the guidelines on the safety and security of children in schools and directed the Union Government to send the copies to the Chief Secretaries or equivalent officers.

The top court’s direction comes in the wake of an application, filed by an NGO, on recent sexual assault incidents of children in some schools, including Badlapur in Maharashtra.
