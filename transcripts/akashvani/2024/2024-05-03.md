## Midday News

With only three days left for canvassing, the election campaign for the third phase of Lok Sabha polls in the country has reached a crescendo. The top leadership of all major parties are conducting whirlwind tours to cover as much ground as possible before the polling on Tuesday. Voting in this phase will be held for 93 seats in 10 states and a Union Territory.

Prime Minister and Senior BJP leader Narendra Modi addressed a public meeting at Bardhaman-Durgapur and Krishnanagar constituencies in West Bengal today. Mr Modi said, he wants women to be more independent. He vowed to make three crore Lakhpati didis. The Prime Minister said, he wants Durgapur to be known as industrial city not only in India, but across the world. He said, if India gets developed then it will benefit every citizen of the country and the income of people in the state will increase.

Hitting out at the opposition, Mr Modi said, the people of Left, TMC, and Congress don’t have a vision for development. He said, Opposition only knows how to divide the society for the sake of votes. Mr. Modi said his government has given various welfare schemes to West Bengal.

Later in the day, he will address public meeting at Bolpur constituency.

Mr Modi will hold a poll rally at Singhbhum and Ranchi in Jharkhand this evening. He will also hold a road show in Ranchi from Birsa Munda airport to New Market in Ratu Road, where he will meet several party leaders and workers. Prime Minister Modi attend a public meeting in Palamu Lohardaga constituency in the state tomorrow.

Senior BJP leader Amit Shah will address public meetings today at Mapusa in Goa and Ratnagiri and Sangli in Maharashtra.

Senior Congress Senior leader, Rahul Gandhi will hold a rally at Pune in Maharashtra later in the day.

BJP Tamil Nadu President K.Annamalai will address a public meeting at Garaga in Dharwad in Karnataka.

In Uttar Pradesh, senior BJP leader and Madhya Pradesh Chief Minister Mohan Yadav will address a rally in Sambhal district while party leader and Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath will also hold rallies in Sambhal and Bareilly districts.

Congress leader Priyanka Gandhi will hold a roadshow today at Fatehpur Sikri in the state.
Samajwadi Party Chief Akhilesh Yadav will do door to door campaign and address a political gathering in Etawah district today.

“The Haveri Lok Sabha constituency was formed after adding five Assembly seats of Haveri district and three constituencies of Gadag district. There are 1982 polling booths set up for the Phase III May 7th election. In this election former Chief Minister of Karnataka Basavaraj Bommai is the BJP candidate and Anandswamy Gaddadevaramath is the Congress candidate. There are 14 candidates in fray including five Independents. In the 2019 Lok Sabha election, Shivakumar Udasi from BJP had defeated the Congress candidate D R Patil. Nagaland will be conducting the Urban Local Bodies (ULB) elections in three Municipal Councils and 36 Town Councils on 26th of June. This will be the first ever municipal elections in the state with 33 percent reservation for women. The last ULB election was held in 2004 without the reservation of seats for women.

The State has a total of 418 wards spread over three Municipal Councils and 36 Town Councils, out of which 142 wards are reserved for women.

As per the Election schedule, nominations can be filed from 7th to 11th of next month, while scrutiny of valid nomination papers will be carried out on 13th of June.

The last date for withdrawal of candidature has been fixed on 20th June. Counting of votes and declaration of results will be done on 29th of June.

The Enforcement Directorate has attached assets worth over 205 crore rupees in the Chhattisgarh liquor scam case. The assets include  18 movable and 161 immovable properties. In the attached properties, 14 properties are of former IAS officer Anil Tuteja and 115 properties of Anwar Dhebar. Around two thousand crore rupees liquor scam ran between 2019 and 2022  by an alcohol syndicate led by Anwar Dhebar.   There are allegations that bribes were collected from the distillers for each case of liquor procured from them by the Chhattisgarh State Marketing Corporation Limited. The bribes were taken from distillers to allow them to make a cartel and have fixed market share.

World Press Freedom Day is being observed today. The day recognises the vital role that a free and independent press plays in upholding the public’s right to information and holding those in power accountable.

The theme for this year is “A Press for the Planet: Journalism in the Face of the Environmental Crisis.” In his message, on the occasion, United Nations General Secretary, António Guterres said Journalists have a key role in informing and educating the public about the world’s major environmental and climate emergency.

In Brazil, at least 30 people were killed and several others injured after a dam collapsed in Rio Grande do Sul state. The dam collapsed after several days of heavy rainfall caused massive flooding. According to reports, about 15,000 residents have fled their homes while thousands of people are without power and clean water across the state. Brazilian President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva visited the region, promising help from the central government.

World Press freedom Day is being observed today. The day was first established by the United Nations General Assembly in 1993 on the recommendations of the UNESCO General Conference. The day is observed to mark the anniversary of the Windhoek Declaration, a statement of free press principles put together. Here is more in this report:

“Awareness of all aspects of the global environmental crisis and its consequences is essential to build democratic societies. Journalistic work is indispensable for this purpose. In the context of the world’s triple planetary crisis -climate change, biodiversity loss, and air pollution- Misleading and false information can undermine international efforts to address them. It can lead to a lack of public and political support for climate action and effective policies. To achieve sustainable development, it is necessary for journalists to report accurately, timely, and comprehensively on environmental issues. On the other hand this also requires a comprehensive strategy to prevent and protect the journalists from crimes committed against them. Ensuring the rights to freedom of expression, freedom of scientific research, and access to key sources of information are also essential among others. TRIPTI SRIVASTAVA, NEWS DESK.”
