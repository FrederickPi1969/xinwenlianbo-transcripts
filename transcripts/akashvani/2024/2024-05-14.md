## Midday News

Prime Minister and senior BJP leader Narendra Modi today filed his nomination from Varanasi Lok Sabha seat. Varanasi will go to polls in the seventh and final phase of general elections on June 1.

Earlier this morning, Mr Modi offered prayers at Dashashwamedh Ghat on the banks of Ganga river in Varanasi. He also performed aarti at the ghat amid chanting of Vedic mantras. The Prime Minister also sought blessings of Kaal Bhairav, who is considered the Kotwal of Kashi.

Leaders of alliance partners like Chandra Babu Naidu, Eknath Shinde, Jitan Ram Manjhi, Sanjay Nishad, Ramdas Athawale along with BJP national president JP Nadda and senior party leaders including Rajnath Singh and Hardeep Singh Puri were also present on the occasion. The four proposers of Mr. Modi included Pandit Ganeshwar Shashtri, Baijnath Patel, Lalchand Kushwaha and Sanjay Sonkar.

Congress candidate Ajay Rai, who is the Opposition INDI alliance joint nominee is contesting against Mr. Modi. The UP Congress chief, Rai was also fielded by the Congress against Mr. Modi in Varanasi in the 2014 and 2019 Lok Sabha polls, but he had finished third both times. In the 2019 polls, PM Modi had defeated the Samajwadi Party’s Shalini Yadav by over 4.79 lakh votes.

Congress President Mallikarjun kharge will participate in the nomination of party candidate Virendra Chaudhary from Maharajganj constituency. Mr Khadge will address a political rally in Gorakhpur district also. BJP national president JP nadda and Chief Minister of Uttar Pradesh Yogi Adityanath will a address political rally in Amethi, while Congress General Secretary Priyanka Gandhi Vadra will also campaign in Amethi today. Congress leader Rahul Gandhi and Samajwadi party chief Akhilesh Yadav will hold a joint rally in favour of alliance candidate in Jhansi.

The Samajwadi Party Chief will also address a political rally in Jalaun district. Bahujan Samaj party Chief Mayawati will also hold a rally in Jhansi.

Union Home Minister and senior BJP leader Amit Shah will address two public meetings in West Bengal today. He will address a gathering in Bongaon Lok Sabha constituency this afternoon. Later, he will hold another public meeting at Amta for party’s candidate from Uluberia Lok Sabha seat.

In Bihar, campaigning is underway in full swing for remaining phases of Lok Sabha elections.

Senior NDA leaders from BJP, JD(U), Lok Janshakti Party (Ramvilas), Hindustani Awam Morcha and Rashtriya Lok Morcha are holding public meetings and rallies in poll bound areas.

Due to demise of former Deputy Chief Minister Sushil Kumar Modi, star campaigners of BJP have cancelled their today’s party programmes.

From Grand Alliance, RJD leader Tejashwi Prasad Yadav along with Vikas Sheel Insaan Party Chief Mukesh Sahni are holding election rallies in poll bound areas. Leaders of Bihar Pradesh Congress Committee and party candidates are also busy campaigning in different constituencies.

BJP is fighting on all seven seats. AAP contesting on four seats and Congress on three. A total of 162 candidates are in the fray in the national capital.

With Diwaker, Anupam Mishra, Akashvani News Delhi.“

In our series of key constituencies for Lok Sabha polls, today we bring you a report on Bargarh parliamentary seat in Odisha.

“Bargarh is one of the 21 Lok Sabha constituencies in Odisha and comprises seven legislative assembly segments. It is a general category parliamentary seat. It comprises the entire Bargarh and Jharsuguda districts of Odisha. In 2019 polls, BJP had won the Lok Sabha seat, while the ruling BJD in the state had won all the assembly segments namely Padampur, Bijepur, Bargarh, Attabira, Bhatli, Brajarajnagar and Jharsuguda. Polling for this Lok Sabha seat will be held on 20th of this month in the fifth phase of the ongoing general elections and result will be declared on 4th of June. There are about 13 lakh voters in the parliament seat who will cast their vote in 1397 polling booths. The BJP registered a surprise victory from the Bargarh Lok Sabha constituency in 2019 when Suresh Pujari defeated BJD stalwart Prasanna Acharya by a margin of over 63,000 votes. What was particularly noteworthy in the previous election here was that the voters made a clear distinction between the state and parliamentary polls. While the BJD swept all seven assembly segments in 2019 elections when Odisha went for simultaneous Lok Sabha and Assembly polls, the BJD MP candidate lost to the BJP, as voters chose to support Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s bid for re-election. Since the constituency came into being in 2008, neither the BJD nor the BJP have repeated MP candidates from Bargarh. This time too, both parties have changed their candidates.This time, the BJP has given the ticket to senior party leader and former MLA from Padmapur Pradip Purohit, while Parineeta Mishra from the BJD is the other top contender here. Congress has fielded former MP from Bargarh Sanjay Bhoi.  Prakash Dash Akashvani News Bhubaneswar.  “

India has sent the second tranche of Humanitarian Assistance including 40 tonnes of medicines, medical supplies and other equipment in a special Indian Air Force plane to assist the flood victims in Kenya. India has sent tents, blankets, power generation sets, basic sanitary utilities, hygiene kits, baby food, malaria and dengue diagnostic kits and anti-venom treatment that can be readily deployed on the ground.

First tranche of assistance was sent in Naval Ship Sumedha and was handed over to the Government of Kenya on 10th of this month. It included food, relief and medicine supplies for flood victims.

India’s domestic coal stock has surged by 26 per cent this year to reach 149 million tonnes. Coal Minister Pralhad Joshi in a social media post informed that country’s production grew by 7.31 per cent in April. He also said that the dispatch to power sector has increased by 5.43 per cent Year-on-Year. Mr. Joshi also asserted that the country is ready to meet the peak demand, which is expected to rise to 240 Gigawatt during the day and 235 Gigawatt in the evening in the month of June.

The Ramcharitmanas, Panchatantra and Sahṛdayāloka-Locana have been included in UNESCO’s Memory of the World Asia-Pacific Regional Register. The Culture Ministry said, this inclusion is a proud moment for India, a confirmation of the country’s rich literary heritage and cultural legacy. The Ministry said, it also signifies a step forward in global cultural preservation efforts, highlighting the importance of recognizing and safeguarding the diverse narratives and artistic expressions that shape the shared humanity. It said, by honouring these literary masterpieces, society not only pays homage to the creative genius of their creators but also ensures that their profound wisdom and timeless teachings continue to inspire and enlighten future generations.

The maximum temperature rose by three degree celsius yesterday and highest temperature 43.7 degree celsius was recorded at Abohar town. Met office has advised people to stay updated with the latest weather advisories from time to time.

“As per met office prediction the weather conditions will be mainly dry during the coming days. Maximum temperatures are very likely to rise by about 4 degree celsius. As a result, temperatures over some parts of southern Punjab could reached in between 44 to 46 degree celsius. Similarly, temperatures over the northern parts are expected to range from 41 to 44 degree celsius. This may lead to heat wave conditions in the state during 16th to 18th May. The met office has suggested measures for public to stay hydrated, avoid prolonged exposure to the sun, especially during peak hours, wear light clothing and keep heads covered when under direct sun. The Home Ministry has advised the people to beware of fraudsters posing as officials of Police, Central Bureau of Investigation, Narcotics Department, Reserve Bank of India, Enforcement Directorate and other Law Enforcement Agencies. According to the Ministry, a large number of complaints are being reported onthe National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal regarding intimidation, blackmail, extortion and digital arrests by cyber criminals posing as government officials.

The Ministry said, several victims have lost large amounts of money to such criminals. It has advised that the citizens should immediately report the incident on cybercrime helpline number 1930 or www.cybercrime.gov.in for assistance.

The Centre has extended the ban imposed on the Liberation Tigers of Tamil Eelam (LTTE) for five more years. The Ministry of Home Affairs in its notification said, the central government has taken the decision as LTTE continues to pose a threat to the sovereignty and territorial integrity of India. The government had declared the LTTE an unlawful association in May 2019 and banned it for five years.

In Pakistan-occupied Kashmir, the situation continues to worsen as fresh violence erupted due to clashes between protestors and authorities. Four people were killed and more than 100 injured following violent clashes which comes amid a wheel-jam strike that started on Friday. Thousands of people are on the streets, to protest against the high prices of basic essentials like flour and the severe power cuts in the area. Following the intense violence over the weekend, the authorities imposed Section 144, shutting down schools and also suspending mobile communications in the area. Pakistan has been witnessing extremely high inflation and dismal economic growth for more than two years due to rising energy costs.
