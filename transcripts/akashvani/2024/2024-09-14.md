## Morning News

Campaigning is in full swing for the first phase of the Assembly elections in Jammu and Kashmir. Twenty-four Assembly constituencies will go to polls in this phase on 18th of this month. Top leaders of major political parties are leaving no stone unturned to woo the voters.

Prime Minister and senior BJP leader Narendra Modi will address an election rally in Doda district today. The Congress and National Conference are also campaigning for their party candidates.

Jammu and Kashmir BJP Chief Spokesperson, Sunil Sethi addressed a press conference in Jammu yesterday. He accused National Conference and Congress of bringing gun culture in Kashmir and promoting separatism and terrorism. He further added that Prime Minister Narendra Modi established peace in the Union Territory and ensured all round development in Jammu and Kashmir.

Former Chief Minister and Democratic Progressive Azad Party Chairman, Ghulam Nabi Azad addressed three public gatherings at Dooru, Qazigund and Devsar in South Kashmir yesterday. Speaking at the rallies, Mr Azad strongly asserted his commitment to protect the rights of the people of Jammu and Kashmir.

Polling in the Union Territory is being held in three phases. Voting for the second phase will take place on the 25th of this month and for the third phase on 1st of October.

Meanwhile, after the scrutiny of nomination papers for the final phase of the Assembly polls yesterday, the candidature of 449 contestants was found valid. The last date for withdrawal of candidature is the 17th of September.

Election campaign has also picked up pace for single-phase assembly polls in Haryana. Voting for the 90-member Assembly will be held on the 5th of October.

Prime Minister and senior BJP leader Narendra Modi will launch the  party’s campaign in the state today. He will hold an election rally at Theme Park of Kurukshetra this afternoon. It will be the first election rally of Mr Modi in Haryana. Congress and other regional parties are also making efforts to woo voters.

After scrutiny of nomination papers yesterday in the state, candidature of 1,561 contestants was found valid out of 1,747. The nominations can be withdrawn till the 16th of this month.

The Centre has revised wheat stock holding limits for traders, wholesalers, big chain retailers, and processors to check its price rise and hoarding. In a statement, the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution said, the revised stock limits on wheat for traders and wholesalers will now be two thousand metric tonnes as against three thousand metric tonnes earlier. For processors, the limit has been reduced to 60 per cent from the earlier limit of 70 per cent of their monthly installed capacity multiplied by the remaining months of the 2024-25 fiscal. The stock limits have been kept unchanged for individual retailers who continue to stock up to 10 tonnes of wheat. Big chain retailers can store wheat up to 10 metric tonnes in each outlet.

Government has removed the minimum export price of 950 dollar per tonne for issuing registration-cum-allocation certificates for export of basmati rice. In a social media post, Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal hailed the decision saying that the export of high-quality rice will boost farmers’ earnings. He said, this will also boost export and also increase the income of farmers. Mr Goyal said, the decision taken by Narendra Modi’s government shows sensitivity towards the farmers.

The Government has increased customs duty on crude oil to 20 per cent and refined sunflower oil to 32.5 per cent. These changes will come into effect from today. According to a notification issued by the Finance Ministry, the basic customs duty on crude palm, soybean and sunflower seed oil has been increased from nil to 20 per cent.

In Jammu and Kashmir, two Army personnel lost their lives and two others were injured in an encounter with terrorists in Kishtwar district. Acting on a tip-off, security forces yesterday launched a joint cordon and search operation in the Naidgham area in Chhatroo belt of Kishtwar district. During the operation, an exchange of fire broke out between security forces and hiding terrorists at Pingnal Dugadda forest area in the upper reaches of Naidgham village in which four soldiers received bullet injuries. Later, two personnel succumbed to injuries while receiving treatment. The other two are stated to be in stable condition. The search operation is in progress and more details are awaited.

Another encounter broke out between terrorists and security forces in Chak Tapper area of Pattan in north Kashmir’s Baramulla district last night. As per the police sources, the gunfight was going on till reports last came in.

The National Investigation Agency (NIA) has conducted searches at several locations in Punjab in connection with its probe into the attack on the Indian High Commission in Canada allegedly by pro-Khalistan supporters. The searches were conducted yesterday in Moga, Amritsar, Gurdaspur and Jalandhar. The target locations were connected with the accused Khalistani leader Amarjot Singh and his associates.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah inaugurated the 7th National Security Strategies Conference in New Delhi yesterday. He launched the Director Generals and Inspector Generals of Police Conference Recommendations Dashboard. During the two-day conference, the government will outline a roadmap to address emerging national security challenges with the top police leadership from states, Union Territories, Central Armed Police Forces, and Central Police Organisations.

Union Minister of Ports, Shipping and Waterways, Sarbananda Sonowal virtually laid the keel of the country’s largest dredger to be built at the Cochin Shipyard, marking a significant milestone in enhancing India’s maritime capabilities. The Trailing Suction Hopper Dredger, christened Godavari, is being built for the Dredging Corporation of India. The vessel has a hopper capacity of 12,000 cubic meters and will be built in collaboration with Royal IHC, of the Netherlands.

The Centre, in a significant move, has decided to completely remove the floor price on export of Basmati rice to boost its export. The step has been taken in response to the ongoing trade concerns and adequate domestic availability of rice. A floor price of 1200 dollar per metric ton was introduced in August 2023 as a temporary measure in view of the rising prices of rice and a tight supply position in domestic markets. The floor price was later rationalised to 950 dollars per metric ton in October 2023.
