## Midday News

Prime Minister Narendra Modi and his Bhutanese counterpart Tshering Tobgay inaugurated Gyaltsuen Jetsun Pema Mother and Child Hospital in Thimphu this morning. The state-of-the-art hospital has been built with the assistance of the Government of India.

The development of the 150 – bedded Hospital has been supported by India in two phases. The newly constructed Hospital would add value to the quality of mother and child health services in Bhutan. The Ministry of External Affairs said, the Gyaltsuen Jetsun Pema Wangchuk Hospital stands as a shining example of India-Bhutan partnership in health-care.
After the inauguration, Prime Minister left for New Delhi. In a special gesture, Bhutan King Jigme Khesar Namgyel Wangchuck accompanied by Prime Minister Tobgay accorded a warm send off to Mr Modi at the airport.

In a social media post, Mr Modi said, this has been a very special Bhutan visit and he had the opportunity to meet the King, PM Tobgay and other distinguished people of Bhutan. He said, the talks will add even more vigour to the India-Bhutan friendship. Mr Modi also expressed gratitude to have been conferred the Order of the Druk Gyalpo. He thanked people of Bhutan for their warmth and hospitality. Mr Modi said, India will always be a reliable friend and partner for Bhutan.

Yesterday, Prime Minister Modi announced assistance of ten thousand crore rupees to Bhutan over next five years. Mr Modi assured Bhutan’s top leadership that India will stand by the Himalayan Kingdom in its quest for development. The two countries also signed agreements in the fields of energy, trade, digital connectivity, space and agriculture, and finalised the MoU on the establishment of rail links between the two nations.

Informing about Prime Minister’s economic aid to Bhutan, Foreign Secretary Vinay Mohan Kwatra said, the assistance also includes India’s support to Bhutan’s economic stimulus package.

External Affairs Minister Dr S Jaishankar today reached Singapore on a three day visit. He began his visit by paying homage to Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose and the brave Indian National Army soldiers. In a social media post, Dr Jaishankar said, the INA Marker in Singapore recognizes their deep patriotism and indomitable spirit that remain an inspiration for generations to come.

The terror attack in Russia on concert venue, Crocus City Hall at Moscow has received international condemnation with the United Nations Chief and the UN Security Council calling for justice.

The Islamic State Khorasan (ISIS-K) has claimed responsibility for the attack on the concert venue.

United Nations Secretary-General Antonio Guterres expressed shock at the attack and condemned it in the strongest possible terms.

The United Nations Security Council has called on all states to cooperate actively with the Government of the Russian Federation, as well as all other relevant authorities to bring to account those responsible for the terrorist attack.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has condemned the terror attack in a Moscow concert. The US, EU, UK, France, Spain and UAE also expressed shock.

French President Emmanuel Macron has said, France expresses its solidarity with the victims, their loved ones and all the Russian people.

Spain said it was shocked at events in Moscow, while Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni said it was an odious act of terrorism and expressed her full solidarity with the affected people and the victims families.

Over 60 people were killed and more than 145 injured when gunmen opened fire in a concert hall near capital Moscow last night.

In Punjab, more than 20 people have been killed after consuming spurious liquor in several villages of Sangrur district. Six more victims succumbed in hospitals today. A special police team is investigating the matter and eight persons have been arrested so far. More from our correspondent:

The series of deaths due to alleged spurious liquor, started from Dirba area of Sangrur district, is not stopping and now this havoc is being seen in Sunam area of the same district. Today, with the death of 6 more persons, the death toll in Sunam and Dirba has reached twenty. About sixteen victims are undergoing treatment in various hospitals and some of them are critical. Meanwhile, the entire opposition is demanding the resignation of Chief Minister Bhagwant Singh Mann and Excise Minister Harpal Singh Cheema over the incident. Shishu Sharma Shantal, Akashvani News, Jalandhar

Delhi’s Rouse Avenue Court today extended the custody of Bharat Rashtra Samithi (BRS) leader K Kavitha to Enforcement Directorate (ED) till 26th of this month in the money laundering case. Special CBI judge Kaveri Baweja passed the order after the BRS leader was produced in court on the expiry of her ED remand in the alleged liquor policy scam. The central probe agency had sought five more days of remand of Ms Kavitha in the court.

This comes a day after the Supreme Court refused to entertain Kavitha’s bail plea in the case and asked her to move the trial court.

In some election news, around one crore 80 lakh first time voters aged between 18 to 19 years will exercise their franchise in the ensuing Lok Sabha polls. Several social media campaigns like IVote4Sure are underway to encourage the young and first time voters. A Report:

This time around 19 crore 74 lakh voters are in the age group of 20 to 29 years. Election Commission said, over 13.4 lakh advance applications have been received from people aged over 17 years. The poll body is collaborating with National icons like Sachin Tendulkar and Rajkummar Rao to enhance youth participation in the festival of democracy. Social media campaigns and radio engagement also aims to increase the participation. To address barriers in youth participation, the Commission is implementing Turnout Implementation Plan. ANUPAM MISHRA, AKASHVANI NEWS, DELHI.

Nomination filing for first phase of election has started in West Bengal. The last date of filing nomination is 27th March. Expenditure observers have already taken their charge. More from our correspondent:

“West Bengal will witness Lok Sabha election in seven phases for all its forty two constituencies. Under the first phase on 19th April vote will be taken in Alipurduar – Coochbehar and Jalpaiguri constituencies. There are more than seven crore electors in the state, The percentage of first time voters is 2.07. There will be eighty thousand five hundred thirty polling stations out of which nearly six thousand will be managed by women and twenty four polling stations will be managed by divyang jans. Kalyan Laha, Akashvani News, Kolkata.”

In Tripura, electioneering is gradually picking up in the run-up to the first two phases of Lok Sabha election. Tripura West parliamentary seat will go to polls on April 19 while the Tripura East will go to poll on April 26. Our correspondent reports:

Major political parties are presently canvassing through door-to-door campaigns. Also, election machinery has started conducting training programs for polling staff in different districts. Senior BJP leader and Chief Minister Dr. Manik Saha participated in the campaigning for his party candidate Biplab Kumar Deb who is contesting from Tripura (West) Lok Sabha seat. INDIA alliance leaders from Congress and CPIM also organsied door to door campaigning in several areas at Agartala and neighbouring Sepahijala district. CPI (M) candidate and former MLA Ratan Das has filed his nomination paper for the bye-poll to Ramnagar assembly seat which goes to poll on 19th of April. The BJP has fielded Agartala Mayor, Deepak Majumder as its candidate from this seat. The bye-election to Ramnagar assembly seat has been necessitated due to the death of sitting BJP MLA Surajit Dutta. KUNAL SHINDE, AKASHVANI NEWS, AGARTALA.”

Arunachal Pradesh will go for simultaneous polls for its two  Lok Sabha and 60 Legislative Assembly seats in a single phase on April 19 next month. A report:

The notification for the elections was issued on 20th of this month, the last date of nomination is on March 27. As the term of the last Legislative Assembly ends on June 2 this year, the counting of votes for the Assembly election will be held on June 2 and counting for the two parliamentary seats will be done along with the rest of the country on June 4. As per electoral role published in January this year, Arunachal Pradesh has 8,82,816 voters, The state has more female voters than male. There will be 2,226 polling stations. Rakesh Doley, Akashvani, Itanagar.”

Six former Himachal Pradesh MLAs, who were disqualified as Congress legislators joined BJP in New Delhi today in the presence of Union Minister Anurag Singh Thakur and former Himachal Pradesh Chief Minister Jai Ram Thakur. The former MLAs are Sudhir Sharma, Ravi Thakur, Inder Dutt Lakhanpal, Devendra Bhutto and Chaitanya Sharma.

External Affairs Ministry today summoned German Deputy Chief of Mission in New Delhi to convey India’s strong protest on their Foreign Office Spokesperson’s comments on India’s internal affairs. It said India sees such remarks as interfering in the judicial process and undermining the independence of India’s judiciary.

External Affairs Ministry said India is a vibrant and robust democracy with rule of law. It said law will take its own course in the matter, as in all legal cases in the country, and elsewhere in the democratic world. It termed the biased assumptions made on this account as unwarranted.

The Central Bureau of investigation CBI is conducting searches at various locations including in Kolkata in connection with TMC leader Mahua Moitra cash for query case. Sources said, CBI is also conducting searches at the premises of Moitra in Kolkata and other cities.

The CBI had registered an FIR against the former TMC MP on the directives of the Lokpal, which has instructed the agency to file a report within six months.

In Chhattisgarh, two jawans of Bastar fighters were injured in a blast of IED planted by Maoists in Dantewada district last night. Our correspondent reports that a joint team of CRPF, Bastar fighters and District Reserve Guard were on a search operation Kirandul area when the bomb exploded.  Injured jawans were referred to the state capital Raipur. Meanwhile, an encounter is going on in Pidia area of Bijapur  district between security forces and Maoists.
