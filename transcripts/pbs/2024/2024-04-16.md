## Study links petrochemical plants in Louisiana to premature and low-weight births

Those facilities provide many of the essentials of modern life, plastics, fertilizer, and even medical equipment. But that production also comes with serious risks. The area has also long been known as Cancer Alley because of the high rates of cancer among people who live near those plants.

He had stopped breathing. And -- and, of course, just me being new to the experience, it was very overwhelming.
