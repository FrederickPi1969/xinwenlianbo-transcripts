## Idaho ranchers torn between hunting and deterrents for gray wolves harming their livestock

The reintroduction of the gray wolf in America is considered one of the great conservation victories of recent decades.

But now more than 50 wildlife groups are asking the federal government to put the wolves back on the endangered species list.

In many ways, the story of the wolf is our story, and our right and freedom to be on the land is just as important as that of the wolf. So, we have always taken that relationship seriously.

They had done a wonderful thing by allowing wolves back onto the landscape. It really started to right many areas in our homelands that were sick and ailing. It brought some species back, the scavenger species that relied on kills by wolves.

As the wolf population rebounded, the federal government removed it from the endangered species list, first in Idaho and Montana in 2011, then across the entire lower 48 states earlier this year.
