## Trump's AI chip deal sparks legal questions and national security concerns

It has huge implications for the semiconductor industry and actually all of high tech, as well as the export control regime as well.

## Ahead of summit, Trump says Russia will face consequences if Putin won't end Ukraine war

President Trump today issued a new threat to punish Russia if it doesn't end the war in Ukraine just two days before a high-stakes summit in Anchorage with Russian President Vladimir Putin. Mr. Trump also spoke with European leaders, including Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy, who said that Mr. Trump agreed to their principles on the best way to try and end the bloody 3.5-year war.

As Ukrainian soldiers today struggle to hold the front line, 5,000 miles away, President Trump lobbed a new threat at Russian President Putin.

Will Russia face any consequences if Vladimir Putin does not agree to stop the war after your meeting on Friday?

Donald Trump, President of the United States: Yes, they will, yes.

There will be -- I don't have to say. There will be very severe consequences.

If the first one goes OK, we will have a quick second one. I would like to do it almost immediately, and we will have a quick second meeting between President Putin and President Zelenskyy and myself if they'd like to have me there, if the second meeting takes place.
