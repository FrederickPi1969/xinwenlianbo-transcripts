Tonight, she returns with a conversation with someone who has spent his life considering such questions.

## Epidemiologist analyzes Trump administration's strategies against bird flu

Yes, so livestock vaccination actually has been a strategy that's been seriously talked about for a while now. And like any policy decision, there's pros and there's cons.
