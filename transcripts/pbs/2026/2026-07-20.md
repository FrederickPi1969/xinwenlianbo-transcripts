## Test results create more confusion around cyclosporiasis outbreak

Federal health officials are still trying to determine the source of a multistate outbreak of cyclospora infections likely linked to shred iceberg lettuce. There may be more than 7,000 cases of cyclosporiasis nationwide, the largest recorded outbreak in U.S. history.

A third sort of thing you can do is, farmer's markets or local farmers often sidestep these complex bagged lettuce supply chains, which is a partial fix and not risk-free, but it's something that you can do.

This all ends when this contaminated product is out of the supply chain, and that's going to take time.
