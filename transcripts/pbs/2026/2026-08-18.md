## Scientists explore new ways to target mosquitoes as warming climate expands threat

Because they're all male mosquitoes, and males don't bite.

The common name for this mosquito is the yellow fever mosquito, and yellow fevers are really nasty.

His team breeds this specific species of mosquito in their lab and ships them to control districts across the country. These males carry a naturally occurring bacteria called Wolbachia, which makes them incompatible with females they try to mate with.

Overall, temperatures are going up and the atmosphere is becoming wetter. Is that a good thing for mosquitoes in terms of their ability to survive?

For these mosquitoes, unfortunately, yes. It's exactly what they like, a nice warm, humid environment.
