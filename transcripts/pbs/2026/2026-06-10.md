## How the loss of USAID has weakened the fight against Ebola

The Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of the Congo and Uganda is escalating quickly. More than 120 people have died and health officials have reported over 630 cases.

Not with the kind of scale and level of organization that would have been the case before.
