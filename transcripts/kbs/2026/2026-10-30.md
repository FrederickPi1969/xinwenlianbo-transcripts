## Special Counsel Appeals Seoul Mayor&#039;s Acquittal in Opinion Poll Payment Case

The special counsel team in charge of a wide-ranging investigation centering on former first lady Kim Keon-hee has appealed the acquittal of Seoul Mayor Oh Se-hoon in a case involving payments for opinion polls that allegedly constituted illegal political donations.
Special counsel Min Joong-ki’s team said Friday that it is challenging last Friday’s ruling from the Seoul High Court, arguing that the court wrongly dismissed key circumstantial evidence supporting the charges.
It said crimes such as bribery and illegal political contributions are often carried out through secret agreements and private transactions, making it necessary to assess circumstantial evidence alongside direct testimony.
The team maintained that it could be established not just through political broker Myung Tae-kyun’s testimony, but through objective evidence, that businessman Kim Han-jung made the payments after Oh requested the opinion polls and asked for someone else to cover the costs.
Oh was accused of commissioning ten opinion polls from Myung ahead of the 2021 Seoul mayoral by-election and of having his longtime political supporter Kim Han-jung pay 33 million won, roughly 25-thousand U.S. dollars.
But the appellate court acquitted Oh, Kim Han-jung and former Deputy Mayor Kang Cheol-won, overturning the lower court’s guilty verdict, saying Myung’s testimony was the only direct evidence on key issues but was difficult to trust and insufficiently corroborated.
The Supreme Court is expected to rule as early as late January.

## S. Korean Army Names First Female ‘Top Heligun of the Year’

The South Korean Army has named its first female “Top Heligun.”
The Army Aviation Command on Friday announced at a ceremony that Maj. Pyeon Yeon-ju, an AH-64E Apache Guardian pilot from the 901st Aviation Battalion, had become the first woman to receive the prestigious honor.
She and Chief Warrant Officer Song Young-il achieved the highest scores in the 2026 Army Aviation Gunnery Competition and were both named the 28th Top Heligun award winners.
The two winners competed as command pilots of separate Apache helicopters.
This marked Song’s second career Top Heligun title — he also won the presidential award in 2015 on a 500MD helicopter — putting him in rare company.
A Top Heligun signifies the best aerial shooter among combat helicopter pilots, equivalent to fighter pilot “Top Gun” honors.
Pyeon said she was honored to become the first female Top Heligun and credited the achievement to the support she received from her fellow unit members, “who worked tirelessly behind the scenes.”
Meanwhile, the 901st Aviation Battalion was named the top attack helicopter unit and received a commendation from the minister of national defense, defending its title for the sixth consecutive year, and the 60th Aviation Battalion took top honors in the maneuver helicopter category.
A total of 25 battalions and over 200 elite pilots participated in this year’s event.
Since its inception in 1989, the Army Aviation Gunnery Competition has been held annually to assess the combat capabilities of the country’s air forces and strengthen their ability to conduct missions in combat.
