## S. Korea Loses to Japan in Super Round of Baseball at 2026 Asian Games

South Korea lost to Japan in the Super Round of baseball at the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games.
The national team suffered a 5-0 shutout defeat against Japan on Friday in their opening Super Round match of the tournament at Toyohashi Municipal Baseball Stadium in Nagoya.
The four-time defending champion, consisting of star players from the Korea Baseball Organization, managed just four hits against the Japanese team consisting of amateur players.
South Korea and Japan had each won all three preliminary games to advance to the Super Round, also joined by Chinese Taipei and China.
South Korea earlier won a group stage match against Chinese Taipei, while Japan defeated China in their group.
Should Japan win against Chinese Taipei and South Korea defeat China on Saturday, the two sides will face off again on Sunday for the gold medal.

## Sunny Skies Expected Nationwide Sunday, Except for Rain in Jeju

Sunny skies are forecast nationwide on Sunday, the final day of the four-day Chuseok holiday.
Jeju Island, however, will see cloudy, overcast skies, with some areas expected to receive up to 10 millimeters of rain, according to the Korea Meteorological Administration.
Morning lows around the country will range between 13 and 20 degrees Celsius, before daytime highs rise to between 25 and 28 degrees.
The weather agency advises the public, particularly in inland regions, to pay attention to potential health impacts from the wide temperature gap between day and night.

## S. Korea Wins First Asian Games Gold Medal in Men's 3x3 Basketball

South Korea has won its first Asian Games gold medal in men's 3x3 basketball.
The South Korean team — consisting of Lee Ju-yeong, Kim Seung-woo, Lee Dong-geun and Ku Min-gyo — defeated Qatar 19-10 in the final match of the event at Nagoya's Kinjo Futo Station Square Venue on Friday.
Each team in 3x3 basketball consists of four players, including a substitute.
The game is played on a court half the size of a traditional basketball court, with two points given for shots from behind the two-point line and one point for those inside the line.
The sport became an official Asian Games event at the 2018 Jakarta-Palembang Asian Games, where South Korea won the silver medal.
At the 2022 Asian Games in Hangzhou, South Korea placed fourth.
The latest gold medal is Team Korea's 13th at the Aichi-Nagoya Games and 800th in the history of its participation at the Asiad since 1954.
The men's basketball team earlier claimed gold in the traditional 5x5 event for the first time in 12 years.

## Rival Parties Clash over Delayed Onsite Investigation of DMZ Explosions

The ruling and opposition parties continue to clash over the government's handling of recent landmine explosions in the Demilitarized Zone(DMZ) between the two Koreas, which injured three South Korean soldiers.
Main opposition People Power Party floor leader Jeong Jeom-sig criticized the government on social media on Saturday for not promptly carrying out an onsite investigation, referring to President Lee Jae Myung's earlier pledge to honor the injured servicemen.
In a separate social media post, PPP Rep. Bae June-young pointed out that Monday's explosions in the DMZ occurred two days prior to Lee's address to the UN General Assembly.
Bae questioned whether the administration was concerned that the president's speech would fall flat in the event North Korea was behind the landmine incident.
Ruling Democratic Party spokesperson Park Sung-joon countered the criticism, stating that the government is doing all it can to prevent another incident in the DMZ.
Park argued that the PPP's conspiracy theories constitute a grave threat that undermines South Korea's national security.
On Monday, three South Korean soldiers were injured in suspected landmine explosions on the south side of the Military Demarcation Line within the DMZ, including a reconnaissance battalion commander who suffered an amputated ankle.

## Saturday Holiday Traffic Expected to Surge at 5 PM

Traffic congestion continues Saturday on expressways across the country as travelers hit the roads again the day after Chuseok.
According to the Korea Expressway Corporation, nationwide traffic volume is expected to peak around 5 p.m.
As of 2:00 p.m. Saturday, the estimated travel time from the southern port city of Busan to Seoul had reached seven hours and 30 minutes, six hours and 20 minutes from the southwestern city of Gwangju, and four hours 50 minutes from the eastern coastal city of Gangneung.
The drive time from Seoul to Busan was five hours and ten minutes, three hours and 20 minutes from Gwangju, and two hours and 40 minutes to Gangneung, according to traffic authorities.
Approximately five-point-nine million vehicles were expected to be on the move today, 520-thousand of which were likely to drive toward the capital region, while 380-thousand leave the area.
Highway tolls will be exempt until Sunday, the last day of the four-day holiday period.

## Lee Regrets Ukraine&#039;s Disclosure of North Korean POW Transfer

President Lee Jae Myung has expressed regret after Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy revealed in a speech at the United Nations that two North Korean prisoners of war(POWs) were sent to South Korea.
During his state visit to Mexico on Thursday, Lee posted on social media that it was deeply regretful Zelenskyy revealed the POW transfer, calling it a violation of Seoul's and Kyiv's non-disclosure agreement.
Lee stated that they had agreed to conduct the transfer behind closed doors, considering its impact on diplomacy, national security, and the security environment on the Korean Peninsula.
Lee also pushed back against members of South Korea's conservative opposition who criticized the nondisclosure, calling those who prioritize personal gain over the lives of others and national security "murderers" and "traitors."
A high-level Seoul official said Kyiv had notified them in advance that the transfer would be included in Zelenskyy's speech to the UN General Assembly, but did not share details of how the matter would be disclosed.
The two North Korean soldiers were captured by Ukrainian forces in January 2025, after Pyongyang deployed thousands of troops to Russia's Kursk region in support of Moscow's military offensive in Ukraine.

## Lee Calls for Restarting FTA Negotiations with Mexico

Anchor: South Korean President Lee Jae Myung has highlighted the need for a free trade agreement with Mexico. At a business forum in Mexico City on Friday, Lee said a comprehensive trade pact will further boost bilateral economic cooperation.
Kim Bum-soo has more on the third day of Lee's state visit to the Latin American country.
Report: South Korean President Lee Jae-myung called for a swift resumption of free trade negotiations with Mexico.
He made the call during the South Korea-Mexico Business Forum in Mexico City on Friday, the third day of his state visit.
[Sound bite: President Lee Jae Myung (Korean-English)]
"Numerous Korean companies, including SK, Kia, Samsung, and LG, take pride in having made significant contributions to Mexico’s economic development and job creation by investing in its dynamic industrial landscape. ... Despite these remarkable collaborative achievements, the economic relationship between the two nations has yet to progress to a comprehensive trade agreement."
South Korea and Mexico launched FTA talks in 2006, but negotiations have since stalled.
[Sound bite: President Lee Jae Myung (Korean-English)]
"Given the complementary industrial and economic structures of both countries, a trade agreement between South Korea and Mexico will undoubtedly provide a solid foundation for advancing economic cooperation to the next level. ... I hope that by organically combining and strengthening South Korea’s cutting-edge technological competitiveness with Mexico’s excellent manufacturing base and local component ecosystem, companies from both countries can expand beyond North America into the global market."
[Sound bite: Official welcoming ceremony before S. Korea-Mexico summit]
The day prior, Lee and Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum held summit talks, agreeing on the need to restart negotiations while boosting economic cooperation.
[Sound bite: Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum (Spanish-English)]
"Economic relations between our nations maintain extraordinary dynamism. In 2025, bilateral trade reached 27 billion dollars. Korea is one of the five main trading partners of Mexico in the world, and Mexico is the main trading partner of Korea in Latin America. Likewise, Korean investment accumulated since 2006 amounts to more than eleven-point-five billion dollars through more than two-thousand companies."
[Sound bite: President Lee Jae Myung (Korean-English)]
"In order to boost bilateral trade and investment, we agreed to establish an institutional foundation and strengthen financial and supply-chain ties. In addition to seeking new opportunities for cooperation, both countries reached an agreement in principle to conclude negotiations on the revision of the agreement on the promotion and reciprocal protection of investments, which have been underway for seven years."
After addressing this year's UN General Assembly in New York and holding a brief summit with the U.S. President Donald Trump, Lee flew to Mexico, marking the first state visit to the country by a South Korean president in 16 years.
Wrapping up his seven-day trip, Lee will return home on Sunday.
