## Prosecution Service Abolished after 78 Yrs., 2 New Agencies to Take over Separated Powers

The Prosecution Service that has held authority over both investigations and indictments has been abolished after 78 years under the Lee Jae Myung administration's reforms, with the separated powers taken over by two new agencies.
Under relevant legal amendments and enactments effective on Friday, the Public Prosecution Service will be limited to indictments and maintaining them against suspects, and the Serious Crimes Investigation Agency will investigate seven categories of major crimes.
The categories include corruption, economic crimes, defense acquisition, drug offenses, crimes against state security, such as insurrection and foreign aggression, cybercrimes and distortion of the law.
The national police will be in charge of most of the other crimes, but the new agency will handle those classified as serious crimes, such as child abuse, domestic violence, sexual crimes, sexual crimes against minors, stalking, abuse of those with disabilities and seniors.
The Corruption Investigation Office for High-ranking Officials will have authority over both investigations and indictments in cases involving judges, prosecutors, and high-level police officers.
The reforms are centered on separating prosecutors' authority over investigations and indictments, after the state agency was cricitized for wielding excessive power and accused of political bias, targeted investigations and other systemic abuse of authority.

## Gas, Diesel Prices Decline on Friday

The average retail price of gasoline and diesel at gas stations nationwide declined on Friday.
According to the Korea National Oil Corporation’s price information system, Opinet, as of 9 a.m., the national average price of a liter of gasoline was one-thousand-857-point-58 won, or roughly one U.S. dollar and 36 cents, down zero-point-14 won compared to the previous day.
The average price of diesel also fell zero-point-56 won to one-thousand-843-point-one won per liter.
In Seoul, the average price of gasoline rose zero-point-01 won to one-thousand-903-point-64 won per liter, and that of diesel fell zero-point-76 won to one-thousand-885-point-56 won per liter.

## Lee Requests Confirmation Hearing for Nominee to Head New Investigation Agency

President Lee Jae Myung has approved a request for a parliamentary confirmation hearing for his nominee to head the new Serious Crimes Investigation Agency.
Chief presidential spokesperson Kang Yu-jung on Friday announced the move, which comes nearly a month after Interior Minister Yun Ho-jung recommended Kim Ji-yong's nomination on September 7.
Lee ordered additional vetting of the former prosecutor amid opposition from the ruling Democratic Party, following claims that Kim was against prosecutorial reforms and previously belonged to a faction close to former President Yoon Suk Yeol.
In a post on X on Thursday, Lee said the additional vetting disproved the claims and stressed that the reforms should not focus on settling personal grievances or serving the interests of specific individuals or groups.
The new investigation agency and the Public Prosecution Service, which will be limited to handling indictments, are set to launch on Friday under the administration's reforms.
