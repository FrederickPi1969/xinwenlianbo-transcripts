## 5 Former Military Commanders Sentenced to Prison for Roles in 2024 Martial Law Incident

Five former military commanders involved in the 2024 December 3 martial law incident were all found guilty and sentenced to prison terms on Monday.

## Seized Substances Reveal Evidence of Domestic Synthetic Drug Production

Two chemical intermediates used to make synthetic cannabinoids have been identified in seized substances, providing evidence of domestic drug manufacturing in South Korea.
The National Forensic Service, under the Ministry of the Interior and Safety, said Monday that it had detected two previously unidentified intermediates produced during the manufacturing process and determined their chemical structures.
The two substances were found while analyzing three samples seized in recent drug manufacturing and distribution cases.
The forensic service named the two indazole-based synthetic cannabinoid analogues as M-4en-PINAC and M-4en-PINAC 2-isomer, respectively, under the international standard naming system.
The intermediates can be converted into synthetic cannabinoids through additional chemical reactions, making them a key indicator of illicit drug manufacturing, and they are also classified as controlled substances under South Korea’s Narcotics Control Act.
The forensic service said the findings show that synthetic cannabinoids, which had previously been mainly imported as finished products, are now being synthesized and manufactured domestically, suggesting that drug crime could expand beyond distribution to include direct production.

## Semiconductor-Led Exports Rose in 2024, but Consumption Generated More Jobs

Exports accounted for a larger share of the country’s economy in 2024, driven by increased semiconductor shipments, but exports generated only about half the jobs that consumption generated for an equal amount of demand.
According to the Bank of Korea’s 2024 Input-Output Tables, released Monday, exports accounted for 31-point-eight percent of final demand in 2024, up two-point-four percentage points from a year earlier.
The share of consumption fell from 48 percent to 47 percent, while that of investment declined from 22-point-six percent to 21-point-two percent.
The central bank attributed the increased prominence of exports to a sharp rise in exports of computers and electronic and optical equipment, including semiconductors.
Exports also accounted for a larger share of employment, with jobs directly and indirectly generated by export demand rising to 22-point-two percent of total full-time-equivalent employment in 2024 from 20-point-seven percent a year earlier.
However, for every one billion won in final demand, consumption generated nine-point-seven jobs, compared with seven-point-nine for investment and five for exports, meaning the number of jobs generated by exports was roughly half that generated by consumption.

The government has been conducting a national farmland survey to curb real estate speculation, but it has faced backlash from farming communities who argue that the crackdown neglects the realities of rural life.

The film, starring Choi Min-sik as the senior intern and Han So-hee as the young female entrepreneur running a fashion startup, explores lessons that can be shared between different generations.
“Intern” broke the 47-day consecutive box office chart-topping run by Christopher Nolan’s epic “The Odyssey,” which has seen a total of eleven-point-two million admissions to date.

## Exports during First 20 Days of September Break $70 Billion for First Time

Exports during the first 20 days of September have surpassed 70 billion U.S. dollars for the first time, jumping nearly 80 percent year-on-year on the continued upswing in semiconductor shipments.
Provisional data from the Korea Customs Service on Monday showed that exports rose 78-point-three percent from a year earlier to 71-point-four billion dollars between September 1 and 20, setting a record for the 20-day period.
Shipments of semiconductors surged 259-point-four percent to a fresh high of 34-point-one billion dollars, comprising a record 47-point-eight percent of total exports for the first 20 days of the month.
Imports increased 26-point-seven percent to 48-point-four billion dollars, with inbound energy shipments, including crude oil, rising 27-point-nine percent.
Exports exceeded imports, resulting in a record trade surplus of 23 billion dollars for the 20-day period.
