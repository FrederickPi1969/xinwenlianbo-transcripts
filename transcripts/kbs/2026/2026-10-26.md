## BOK: AI, Semiconductor-Led Growth Deepen Youth Employment Woes

The Bank of Korea(BOK) says the quality of youth employment is deteriorating amid an economic growth model centered on semiconductors and the spread of artificial intelligence(AI).
In a report released Monday, the central bank said youth employment is being affected by a combination of industrial restructuring, AI-driven technological changes and a rigid labor market.
The BOK found that since 2023, more people aged 15 to 29 have lowered their job expectations and entered the secondary labor market, consisting of temporary and daily positions, rather than waiting for regular jobs in the primary market.
It attributed the trend partly to the shift toward IT manufacturing, which generates relatively few regular jobs, and to the spread of generative AI, which has strengthened employers’ preference for experienced workers while reducing demand for entry-level tasks typically performed by young people.
The report said these changes are creating structural barriers to entry into AI-exposed industries, including finance and insurance, professional and scientific services, and information and communications.
The BOK said the proportion of young workers moving from the secondary to the primary labor market fell from the eight percent range in the years 2014 to 2019 to the five percent range in 2023 and 2024, warning that limited opportunities to build skills and move into better jobs could delay improvements in youth employment even if labor demand recovers.
The central bank also identified South Korea’s rigid labor market as a barrier to youth employment, noting that countries with stronger employment protection tend to have lower youth employment rates and that South Korea ranked tenth among 23 major economies on the index measuring protection against individual dismissal of regular workers.
