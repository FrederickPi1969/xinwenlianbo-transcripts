He also noted that foreign capital outflows following the U.S. rate hike and a potential slowdown in artificial intelligence(AI) investments could further exacerbate this volatility.

Ahead of Thursday’s plenary session, the main opposition People Power Party decided to allow members to vote freely on Kim’s appointment.
If President Lee Jae Myung approves Kim’s appointment, Kim will become the first Supreme Court justice to take office since the launch of the incumbent administration.

Officials at the company are also suspected of forging or manipulating safety inspection documents following the police investigation.
