## All 5 Microsatellites Deployed by Nuri Successfully Make Contact with Ground Stations

NEONSAT-4 made contact with the King Sejong Station at 2:42 p.m.

At 4:18 p.m., NEONSAT-2 made contact with the King Sejong Station, followed by NEONSAT-5 at 5:52 p.m.
The five NEONSAT microsatellites were deployed for the country’s first domestically launched Earth observation constellation.
For the next three years, they will conduct Earth observation missions for national security and disaster response.

## S. Korea Successfully Launches Nuri Rocket for Fifth Mission

Anchor: South Korea has successfully launched its homegrown Nuri rocket from the Naro Space Center for the fifth time. The rocket deployed five microsatellites for the country’s first domestically launched earth observation constellation. All five microsatellites succeeded in making contact with ground stations.
Report: The 200-ton Nuri rocket blasted off from the Naro Space Center in Goheung, South Jeolla Province, at 12:25 p.m. Wednesday.
According to the Korea Aerospace Administration, or KASA, Nuri’s first stage separated about two minutes after liftoff, with the payload fairing splitting away above the atmosphere approximately two minutes later.
Thirty seconds after that, the second stage separated.
KASA Administrator Oh Tae-seog said the rocket successfully executed all planned flight procedures, including fairing separation and third-stage engine combustion.
He confirmed that data transmitted from Nuri to ground control showed the rocket entered its target sun-synchronous orbit, successfully deploying its primary payload of five NEONSAT microsatellites.
Of the ten secondary CubeSat payloads, nine were deployed.
This marks Nuri’s fifth launch, following a successful mission in November 2025 that deployed 13 satellites into orbit.

## Ex-President Yoon Acquitted on Appeal over Free Opinion Polls

The Seoul High Court on Wednesday found both Yoon and Myung not guilty of violating the Political Funds Act, saying there was no evidence that Yoon, his wife and Myung had reached an agreement or held consultations to commission or receive the polls for free.
The court said it was therefore difficult to establish that Yoon had intended to accept the polling services as a political donation, rejecting the lower court’s finding that both sides had implicitly agreed to that.
Yoon was originally indicted on allegations that he and former first lady Kim Keon-hee received 58 free opinion polls worth about 270 million won, or some 200-thousand U.S. dollars, from Myung between June 2021 and March 2022.
The lower court found Yoon guilty in connection with 14 polls that Myung had delivered to the couple directly and sentenced the former president to two years in prison, while sentencing Myung to a year and a half, but found the couple could not be held responsible for the remaining 44 polls.
The special counsel team had sought four years in prison for Yoon and three years for Myung.
The former first lady also faced charges but was acquitted in the district court and again in the high court, both of which concluded that Myung provided the polls unilaterally in the absence of any prior requests or consultations.
