## Audit Finds Gov’t Improperly Intervened in 2023 YTN Stake Sale

The state auditor says the government improperly intervened in the 2023 sale of YTN shares by forcing state-run institutions to change an agreement on the sale.
The Board of Audit and Inspection(BAI) said Monday that the trade, industry and energy ministry and the Korea Communications Commission(KCC) under the Yoon Suk Yeol administration infringed on institutions’ autonomy and limited participation by large companies and other potential bidders.
According to the audit, KEPCO KDN and the Korea Racing Authority had initially agreed to jointly sell 29-point-99 percent of their combined 30-point-95 percent stake in cable channel YTN, with the aim of maximizing proceeds while allowing large companies and other potential buyers to participate in the bidding.
But after then-KCC Chairman Lee Dong-kwan called for all the shares to be sold in September 2023, the trade ministry instructed the institutions to sell all 13 million shares, resulting in only three bidders.
The BAI concluded that the ministry and the KCC had improperly forced the two institutions to change a legitimately established agreement, violating the autonomy in decision-making guaranteed under the Act on the Management of Public Institutions.
However, the BAI determined that there was no problem with the requirement for the highest bidder or the calculation of the expected price, saying the shares were not sold below market value.
