## S. Korea Accepts Defeat in Elliott Case after 8 Years

The government has decided to accept an adverse ruling in its international investor-state dispute with U.S. private equity firm Elliott Investment Management regarding the 2015 merger of Samsung C&T and Cheil Industries, acknowledging liability for compensation totaling around 153 billion won, or 114 million U.S. dollars, including principal and interest.
The justice ministry said Wednesday that it will not file another lawsuit seeking to overturn the London-based arbitral tribunal’s September 30 ruling, following a review of the ruling, consultations with relevant government agencies and outside experts, and consideration of further legal costs.
The dispute began in July 2018, when Elliott filed an investor-state dispute settlement claim under the South Korea-U.S. Free Trade Agreement(FTA), accusing the Park Geun-hye administration of causing losses for the firm by putting pressure on the National Pension Service, then a major stakeholder, to vote in favor of the merger.
The Hague-based Permanent Court of Arbitration ruled in June 2023 that the government was liable and ordered it to pay Elliott about 48-point-49 million U.S. dollars plus interest, which the government sought to nullify, arguing a jurisdiction violation under the FTA when the tribunal classified the pension service as a “de facto state agency.”
After a series of court proceedings, the case was referred back to the arbitral tribunal in Britain, which again found on September 30 that there was a causal link between the government’s actions and Elliott’s losses.
A justice ministry official said the amount and specific payment arrangements were still under discussion.

The tech-heavy KOSDAQ went up 13-point-07 points, or one-point-76 percent, to open at 756-point-90.

## Team Probing June 3 Ballot Shortage Begins Counting Center Inspection

A special counsel team investigating the June 3 election ballot shortage has begun inspecting ballots and surveillance footage at a ballot-counting center in Seoul's Songpa District.
Special prosecutor Lee Tae-han and his team of investigators entered the SK Olympic Handball Gymnasium around 8:30 a.m. on Wednesday.
The team will inspect approximately two-point-47 million ballots currently stored in the gymnasium, which served as a ballot-counting center on June 3, as well as CCTV footage.
The investigators plan to look for fabricated ballots, errors related to the equipment used for ballot counting and signs of intrusion in the surveillance footage.
On Tuesday, investigators searched the gymnasium for election-related documents and CCTV footage.

## Guryong Village Fire Extinguished after Nearly 13 Hours

Fire authorities have extinguished the fire that broke out Tuesday afternoon in the last remaining shantytown of Seoul’s affluent Gangnam District.
Authorities said the blaze at Guryong Village was extinguished at around 4:39 a.m. Wednesday, nearly 13 hours after it had broken out the previous day, and that they had lifted a Level One alert.
More than 230 personnel and 110 pieces of equipment were mobilized for the firefighting efforts.
A woman in her 30s suffered a minor injury to her leg, and 77 displaced families evacuated to temporary shelters.
Area residents said they'd heard several loud bangs that sounded like gas cylinders exploding.
Police, fire departments and officials from the Korea Electrical Safety Corporation plan to conduct a joint on-site inspection at 10 a.m. Wednesday to determine the cause of the fire.
