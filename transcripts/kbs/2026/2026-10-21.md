## Alleged Arsonist in Seoul's Yeomsak Area Suspected of Premeditation

The suspect accused of arson in two separate fires in Seoul's Yeoksam area on Tuesday had ended his home rental contract effective on the day of the fires, prompting the police to probe whether the alleged crime was premeditated.
KBS has learned that the man in his 70s, identified by the surname Lee, had notified his landlord about a month earlier that he intended to end the lease effective October 20, despite a remainder of the contract period.
The suspect, who was apprehended near Dongmyo Station at around 4 p.m. Tuesday, claimed that he had planned the alleged arson from three days earlier after being cheated by a patent firm over a patent he registered in 2000.
Lee, meanwhile, was convicted in 2023 of special intimidation and sentenced to two years and six months, after he poured gasoline over workers at two patent companies and threatened them with a lighter and scissors the previous year.
The fires at two patent law firms on Tuesday have left 19 people injured.
