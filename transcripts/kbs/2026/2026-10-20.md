## US Reaffirms Fulfillment of Conditions Must Precede OPCON Transfer

The United States has reaffirmed that a set of conditions agreed between Seoul and Washington must first be met before proceeding with the planned transfer of wartime operational control(OPCON) to South Korea.
In a response to a written inquiry by Voice of America(VOA) that was published on Tuesday, the U.S. Department of War said the OPCON transfer remains as a procedure based on the allies' mutually agreed conditions.
The department said the two sides continue to share the view that the fulfillment of the necessary conditions should precede the transition, and that neither side would predict any decisions or outcomes regarding the procedure.
The VOA report said, however, that the department did not explain what U.S. Forces Korea Commander Gen. Xavier Brunson meant last week, when he said that the conditions have changed, and that the allies therefore have to uphold new standards.
The report comes after President Lee Jae Myung on Monday stressed the need for South Korea to swiftly regain the wartime OPCON from the U.S. and said the allies' discussions are fundamentally leaning toward returning the operational control.
There has been an apparent rift between Seoul and Washington over the timing of the conditions-based transfer, with Seoul seeking to accelerate the transition so it can be completed before Lee's term ends in June 2030.

## Average Gas, Diesel Prices Continue Sliding Tuesday

The average retail price of gasoline and diesel at gas stations nationwide went down again on Tuesday.
According to the Korea National Oil Corporation’s price information system, Opinet, as of 9 a.m., the average price of a liter of gasoline was one-thousand-856-point-01 won, or around one U.S. dollar and 40 cents, down zero-point-23 won compared to the previous day.
The average price of diesel also fell zero-point-59 won to one-thousand-842-point-03 per liter.
The average price of gasoline in Seoul dropped one won to one-thousand-901-point-39 won per liter, and that of diesel fell one-point-87 won to one-thousand-883-point-23 won per liter.

## Trump Uncertain about Meeting with N. Korea's Kim during China Trip

U.S. President Donald Trump expressed uncertainty about meeting with North Korean leader Kim Jong-un during his visit to China next month.
Trump was asked by a reporter at the White House on Monday about a potential meeting during the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit in Shenzhen, China, to which he said, "I don't know," and "I'm not sure that anybody's asking for a meeting."
The president, however, said his relationship with Kim was "very good," and that the two got along "very well."
Trump's latest comment appears to mark a shift in tone from his previous overtures to the North Korean leader, dating back to August, when he posted photos on social media of interactions with Kim during his first term.
Later the same month, Trump scaled back Washington's annual military exercise with Seoul, and when asked if he expected to meet Kim within the year, he said: "I will be."
In early October, State Department spokesperson Tommy Pigott said Trump doesn't view talks with North Korea as a "concession," and that he is open to meeting with anyone for America's interests.
