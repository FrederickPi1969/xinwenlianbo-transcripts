## N. Korea Tests 2 Types of Submarine-Launched Strategic Cruise Missiles

North Korea says it test-fired two types of submarine-launched strategic cruise missiles in the East Sea on Wednesday.
The North's state-run Korean Central News Agency(KCNA) reported on Thursday that the test was carried out by the Missile Administration and overseen by Jang Chang-ha, the head of the bureau, and Kim Jong-sik, first vice department director of the Workers' Party Central Committee.
The missiles were launched to inspect the mobilized posture of underwater combat systems, the KCNA said, and contributed to enhancing navy's performance of strategic missions.
The test did not have any adverse impact on the security of neighboring countries, according to the news agency.
This comes after the North test-fired tactical ballistic missiles from the Pyongyang area toward the East Sea on Monday.
The South Korean military, meanwhile, has not released details regarding Wednesday's missile firings.
