## Semiconductor Jobs Up 15% in 8 Years, New Hires Down 22%

The number of paid jobs in semiconductor manufacturing has increased by nearly 15 percent over the past eight years to a record high, but the number of newly created jobs has fallen by about 22 percent.
According to data released Sunday by the Korean Statistical Information Service, paid jobs in semiconductor manufacturing reached a record 174-thousand in the first quarter, up 14-point-five percent, or 22-thousand, from the same quarter in 2018, when the data was first compiled.
Semiconductor manufacturing jobs fell one-point-eight percent in the first quarter of 2024 but rose one-point-two percent last year and two-point-four percent this year, marking two consecutive years of growth.
However, the increase has not translated into a significant rise in new hires.
Newly filled jobs, including replacement positions created by employee departures and new positions generated through business establishment or expansion, totaled 29-thousand in the first quarter, the third‑lowest level on record for the period and down 21-point-six percent, or eight thousand, from 37-thousand in 2018.
Experts attribute the relatively weak improvement in hiring, despite a recovery in the semiconductor industry, to the sector's low employment‑generating effect.

## KTX, Train Ticket Booking Window Extended to 2 Months

The booking window for KTX and other train tickets will be extended from one month to two months before departure.
The Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport said Sunday that the change will take effect October 1 to make rail travel more convenient for both domestic and international passengers.
Foreign tourists have faced difficulties planning domestic travel because flights can be booked two months in advance, while train tickets have only been available one month ahead.
As the number of foreign visitors continues to rise, the ministry also plans to expand language support on its rail ticketing website from the current seven languages to 10 by the end of the year, adding French, German and Mongolian.
A total of six-point-69 million foreign visitors used the rail network through August, a 68 percent increase from the same period last year.
The ministry is also developing a system to support global mobile payment services such as WeChat Pay and Alipay, in addition to overseas credit cards including Visa, Mastercard and UnionPay, which are already supported.

## Police Nab 1,863 in Special Crackdown on Local Corruption

Police have arrested one-thousand-863 people in a special crackdown on corruption involving local government officials and businesses, with 27 of them formally detained.
The National Office of Investigation under the Korean National Police Agency on Sunday announced the results of the crackdown, which ran from March through September 16.
Abuse of authority – including exerting undue pressure or accepting money in connection with official duties – accounted for the largest share, with 841 people, or 45-point-one percent, followed by financial corruption involving the diversion of public funds at 814, or 43-point-seven percent.
Public officials made up the largest group, at 803, while 703 people, including private‑sector business representatives, were also caught.
Police said they uncovered numerous cases in which officials with licensing and permitting authority colluded with private operators seeking business interests, often through brokers.
The agency added that it will analyze the results and improve its response system before launching a new crackdown on local corruption and collusion in November.
