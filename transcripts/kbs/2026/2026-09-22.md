## Lee Approves Appointment of 3 Cabinet Ministers

President Lee Jae Myung has approved the appointment of three Cabinet ministers.
Chief presidential spokesperson Kang Yu-jung said Tuesday that the president approved the appointment of Lee Hyoung-il as the deputy prime minister and finance minister, Kang Shin-chul as the defense minister, and Hong Jee-sun as the land and transport minister.
The president, who departed on Monday for a weeklong trip to New York for attendance at the United Nations General Assembly and Mexico for a state visit, likely gave his authorization after landing in New York.
Out of the president's six Cabinet nominees, Gender Equality Minister nominee Yong Hye-in resigned amid criticism for insisting that she keep her proportional representation seat in parliament, even after she assumes the Cabinet role.
Justice Minister nominee Kim Seung-won stepped down as he faced backlash over allegations that he had illegally lobbied the drug safety ministry to approve a clinical trial for a COVID-19 drug in 2021.
The National Assembly has yet to finalize its report on the confirmation hearing of SMEs and Startups Minister nominee Lee So-young.

## Lee Arrives in New York to Attend UN General Assembly

President Lee Jae Myung has landed in New York, the first stop in his weeklong trip that includes attendance at the United Nations General Assembly, followed by a state visit to Mexico.
National security adviser Wi Sung-lac said on Monday prior to Lee’s departure that the president will speak at the 81st session of the UN General Assembly on Tuesday afternoon local time.
He is expected to propose UN reforms with the aim of resolving global issues, explain how Seoul can contribute to those efforts, and present a strategy to achieve peaceful coexistence on the Korean Peninsula.
Lee will meet with UN Secretary-General António Guterres to request the UN’s interest and cooperation to achieve peace on the peninsula, and also likely make contact with state leaders, including U.S. President Donald Trump.
The talks with Trump, if held, would come as the two sides have yet to reach agreement on Seoul’s pledged investment in America and the country’s decision on troop deployment to Strait of Hormuz is still pending.
The South Korean leader will attend the Korea Investment Summit on Thursday to promote Seoul’s AI policy and capital market reforms to global investors.
