## KMA: Rain to Continue on Jeju, Spread Elsewhere Except Capital Area

Rain is forecast to persist on the southernmost island of Jeju on Sunday, with wet weather expected to expand across the country, excluding the capital area.
According to the Korea Meteorological Administration, Jeju is expected to get up to 50 millimeters of rainfall, the inland regions of Daegu and the Gyeongsang provinces up to 20 millimeters, and the Jeolla region and southern parts of Gangwon Province up to 10 millimeters.
Precipitation will vary significantly by region, with many areas experiencing a temporary lull.
Morning low temperatures on Sunday will range from ten to 20 degrees Celsius, while daytime highs peak between 22 and 28 degrees.
The low in Seoul will hover around 15 degrees before warming up to a high of 26 degrees.

## Assembly Speaker Cho Meets Croatian Leaders in Zagreb

National Assembly Speaker Cho Jeong-sik held a series of meetings with Croatian leaders in Zagreb to boost bilateral energy and defense cooperation.
Cho held separate meetings on Friday with President Zoran Milanovic, Parliament Speaker Gordan Jandrokovic and Prime Minister Andrej Plenkovic, according to his office.
Cho's visit to Zagreb comes as Croatia has become the fourth European country to acquire the South Korean Chunmoo multiple launch rocket systems, following Poland, Estonia and Norway.
South Korean defense firm Hanwha Aerospace last month signed a deal to export 18 Chunmoo units to Croatia in an arms contract worth some 640 billion won, or roughly 477 million U.S. dollars, marking the first major arms deal between the two countries.
Cho is on a nine-day, four-nation trip to Italy, Vatican City, Croatia and Greece.

## S. Korea Unveils 50 Additional Photos of Hypersonic Glide Missile Test

South Korea on Friday unveiled additional photos of its first hypersonic glide missile test.
A series of 50 images from Thursday's missile test released by the presidential office captured the weapon soaring up from the launch pad off the country's west coast and purportedly striking its maritime target.
The missile launch and additional publicity came amid heightened inter-Korean tensions over North Korean landmine explosions on the south side of the Demilitarized Zone last month, which injured South Korean soldiers.
Boosted into the upper atmosphere, the Hypersonic Glide Vehicle, or HGV, glides toward its target at hypersonic speeds, penetrating enemy air defenses with unpredictable flight paths.
The top office earlier said the HGV is a game-changing strategic asset capable of launching surprise precision strikes against enemy targets.

## ARTEX Developer Makes AI Agent Closed-Source After S. Korean Bank Hack

The Chinese developer of the ARTEX artificial intelligence(AI) cybersecurity tool has said they will no longer publicly develop and update the agent after it was linked to a recent series of cyberattacks against South Korean banks.
According to Reuters on Saturday, ARTEX developer "Autumn-27" posted on the GitHub platform two days earlier that the project will be converted to closed-source and no longer updated "given the misuse of the tool."
The developer said no further versions will be publicly released, nor will any maintenance support be provided.
The suspension, however, is unlikely to block additional cyberattacks using ARTEX already in circulation.
ARTEX is an open-source AI agent designed to automate network penetration testing via connection to external ⁠large language models, such as ChatGPT, Claude and DeepSeek.
U.S. cybersecurity firm CrowdStrike said this week that the suspect behind the hacking of South Korean banks between late September and early October had likely used the ARTEX AI agent and Anthropic's Claude Code.

## S. Korea&#039;s Ambassador to Ukraine Returns Home amid Dispute over N. Korea POWs

South Korea’s ambassador to Ukraine has returned home, recalled in protest over Kyiv disclosing confidential details about the transfer of North Korean prisoners of war.
Seoul's foreign ministry said Ambassador Park Ki-chang returned on Saturday morning, adding that it would continue consultations with Kyiv.
President Lee Jae Myung previously posted on social media that both sides agreed to keep the POW handover confidential at Ukraine's request, but Kyiv violated the agreement by unilaterally publicizing the matter.
Lee warned of “additional measures” if Ukraine continued to deny the agreement.
Prior to recent events, the last time Seoul recalled an ambassador over a diplomatic dispute was in 2008, when Japan claimed South Korea's easternmost Dokdo islets in a teaching guide for a middle school textbook.

## British Man Arrested in UK after Allegedly Stabbing Acquaintance in Seoul

A British man who fled to his home country after allegedly stabbing a woman in Seoul has been arrested by law enforcement officials.
The Korean National Police Agency announced in a Saturday press notice that the British National Extradition Unit apprehended the suspect in the United Kingdom on Thursday.
South Korean authorities intend to seek the man's extradition.
The suspect, in his 30s, is accused of attempted murder for allegedly stabbing a female acquaintance in the neck at a university campus in Seoul on September 29, before fleeing South Korea.
The victim was said to have suffered minor injuries.
The KNPA issued an Interpol Red Notice and verified that the suspect had returned to the U.K.

## &#039;LA:ON&#039; K-Culture and Lifestyle Zone Opens in Los Angeles

A global K-zone designed to promote Korean culture — ranging from K-pop, beauty products, food, fashion, and language to traditional attire — has opened in Los Angeles.
