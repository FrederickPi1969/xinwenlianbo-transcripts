If the bill is signed into law, the special counsel team will look into 12 cases, including those involving President Lee Jae Myung and former President Moon Jae-in.
The team could comprise up to 309 members, including 20 dispatched prosecutors and 210 civil servants.

## KMA: This Year’s Record El Niño Expected to Peak in December

The national weather agency forecasts that this year’s El Niño is developing into the strongest on record and will likely peak in December, bringing warm, wet weather to South Korea.
According to the Korea Meteorological Administration(KMA), as of October, the sea surface temperature in the El Niño monitoring area was two-point-six degrees Celsius higher than average.
The figure surpasses previous strong El Niño events in 1997 and 2015, when readings were two-point-four degrees and two-point-three degrees higher than average, respectively.
From August to October this year, the average exceeded the long-term average by two-point-four degrees, the highest for the three-month period since records began in 1950.
The KMA confirmed that El Niño has been forming since May.
This naturally recurring phenomenon of warming waters in parts of the tropical Pacific Ocean generally strengthens in winter, and as a result, a strong high-pressure system forms east of South Korea.
The KMA predicted that this system will pump warm, wet air into the country, leading to unseasonably mild temperatures through January and unusually high amounts of rain or snow through next month.
The KMA is also warning of sudden cold spells as Arctic sea ice and Eurasian snow cover complicate El Niño’s winter impact.
