## Kim Yo-jong Warns of 'New Response Measures' along Border, Loudspeaker Broadcasts

The influential sister of North Korean leader Kim Jong-un has warned that "new response measures" were prepared for duty along the inter-Korean border and that loudspeaker propaganda broadcasts would be on standby within the next few days.
According to the North's state-run Korean Central News Agency on Thursday, Kim Yo-jong said the "permanent project" for the border within the regime's sovereign territory would certainly continue.
Kim said fortification of the border would substantially reduce the risk for inter-Korean military clashes and help resolve many complex issues, but warned any rash acts by the South Korean military or even a "mistake" would be met with consequences.
The warning comes after Kim's earlier threat to resume the propaganda broadcasts this month in protest of the South Korean military's warning shots against the North Korean troops' infringement of the military demarcation line.
The regime leader's sister also slammed Kim Byung-joo, special presidential adviser for defense policy, for being "nonsensical" and "irresponsible," after he demanded an apology for the North's shamelessness following last month's mine blast inside the demilitarized zone.
She then accused the Army general-turned-lawmaker of shifting blame over the explosion that injured three South Korean soldiers and denied Pyongyang's responsibility.
