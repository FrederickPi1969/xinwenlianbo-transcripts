## S. Korea Defeats Japan to Win Fourth Straight Gold Medal in Asiad Football

South Korea has defeated rival Japan to win its fourth consecutive Asiad gold medal in men’s football at the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games.
The national team beat Japan 1-0 at Toyota Stadium on Saturday, after midfielder Eom Ji-sung scored in the 61st minute.
South Korea continued to struggle against Japan’s offensive pressure after finishing the first half with a 0-0 draw, until Eom broke the deadlock by netting a cross from fellow midfielder Yang Min-hyeok in the penalty area.
It was Team Korea’s third straight meeting with Japan in a men’s football final at the Asian Games since 2018.
South Korea has now won four consecutive Asiad football titles, following victories at the 2014 Incheon, 2018 Jakarta-Palembang and 2022 Hangzhou Games.
It is South Korea’s sixth gold overall, the most for any country in men’s football at the Asian Games.

## Lee Woo-seok Claims 2nd Archery Gold at Asian Games

South Korean archer Lee Woo-seok has won his second gold medal at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games.
Lee defeated Japan’s Junya Nakanishi 6-2 in set points in the final of the men’s individual recurve event on Saturday at the Okazaki Chuo Sogo Park Multipurpose Square in Okazaki, Aichi Prefecture.
The victory gave Lee his second gold of the Games after he teamed up with Kim Woo-jin and Kim Je-deok to win the men’s team event on Friday.
Lee, who won gold in the men’s team and mixed team events at the 2022 Hangzhou Asian Games and in the men’s team event at the 2024 Paris Olympics, has now claimed his first individual gold medal at a major international multi-sport event.

## Teenage Sailor Choi Ji-un Wins AG Gold Medal in Dinghy Sailing Race

South Korea's teenage sailor Choi Ji-un has won the gold medal in the boys' dinghy event in sailing at the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games.
The 15-year-old claimed the title after earning the lowest 25 net penalty points from competing in 12 races at Kaiyoh Yacht Harbor that ran from Tuesday through Saturday.

## Avg. Prices of Gasoline, Diesel Drop for 20th Consecutive Week

The average prices of gasoline and diesel in South Korea have fallen for the 20th consecutive week amid a recovery in crude shipments from the Middle East and the resumed operation of Saudi Arabia's East-West oil pipeline.
The Korea National Oil Corporation’s price information system showed on Saturday that the average price of gasoline in the fifth week of September fell 40 won from the previous week to one-thousand-857-point-six won per liter, or around one U.S. dollar and 38 cents.
The average price of diesel also dropped zero-point-one won to one-thousand-843-point-three won per liter.
The decline, however, was limited due to the tight global supply of petroleum products.
The South Korean government maintains price caps on refiners' supplies of gasoline and diesel to gas stations at one-thousand-784 won per liter and one-thousand-773 won per liter, respectively, to minimize the impact of global oil price fluctuations on domestic prices.

## KMA: Dryness Alerts Issued in Seoul for First Time This Year

Dryness alerts have been issued in Seoul for the first time this fall with an inflow of cold and dry winds.
The Korea Meteorological Administration says dryness advisories were issued for Seoul's northeastern, southwestern and northwestern areas as of 10 a.m. Saturday.
A dryness advisory is issued when the effective humidity level of wood and other flammable materials is expected to remain below 35 percent for at least two consecutive days, prompting caution against the increased risk of wildfires.
The weather agency advised the public to watch for temperature fluctuations from day to night, with the mercury in Seoul spiking from the season's lowest ten-point-two degrees Celsius Saturday morning to 23 degrees in the afternoon.
The humidity level is expected to rise starting Sunday, with rain forecast for the capital region, northern part of Gangwon Province and along the east coast in North Gyeongsang Province and is likely to spread nationwide by Monday.

## N. Korea Fires Ballistic Missile toward East Sea after Kim Yo-jong Rejects Dialogue

South Korea’s Joint Chiefs of Staff(JCS) says North Korea has fired a ballistic missile toward the East Sea on Saturday morning.
The JCS said it detected the missile launch from the Wonsan area on the North’s east coast at around 6:30 a.m.
The military is currently analyzing details of the missile, including its specifications and flight range.
The JCS said the military has reinforced its monitoring and vigilance, while South Korea, the United States and Japan are closely sharing information on North Korea’s ballistic missile activities and maintaining a full defense posture.
The latest launch comes three days after the South Korean military demanded an apology from North Korea over recent land mine explosions within the Demilitarized Zone(DMZ) that seriously injured three South Korean soldiers.
On Friday, Kim Yo-jong, the influential sister of North Korean leader Kim Jong-un, criticized President Lee Jae Myung’s Armed Forces Day address, in which he pledged to continue taking steps to ease military tension between the two Koreas, calling it “the pinnacle of a farce.”
Kim also dismissed Unification Minister Chung Dong-young’s proposal for inter-Korean dialogue as a “delusion not even worth responding to."
Saturday’s launch marks North Korea’s 15th ballistic missile launch this year and comes 13 days after its previous one.
