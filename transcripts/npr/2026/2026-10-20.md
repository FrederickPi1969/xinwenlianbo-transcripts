## Former U.S. ambassador on Trump's recent moves to embrace Russia

STEVE INSKEEP, HOST:

Let's ask Michael McFaul about this. He was the U.S. ambassador to Russia during the Obama administration and is now a professor of political science and a Hoover senior fellow at Stanford University, where it is ridiculously early, and yet he's gotten up to talk with us. Ambassador, welcome.

When questioned in a very famous summit with Putin, his first one in 2018, in Helsinki, he sided with Putin and he didn't side with our own intelligence community about whether there was interference in our elections.

Everybody should want to see the end of this barbaric war that Putin started. But this week felt different in both tone and actions.
